For most Cornell students, leave the car at home. A resident parking permit costs $752.98 for 2026-27 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)), before gas, insurance or repairs. A car pays off mainly if you live off campus far from a bus line, drive to a weekly job, or head home where no bus goes.

```glance
- A resident permit works out to $2.06 a day, before gas and insurance.
- First-years ride TCAT free to Aug. 31, 2027; later years pay $200 for OmniRide or ride free evenings.
- A light year of Ithaca Carshare errands costs $386.96, about half the permit.
- Bring a car only for an off-bus-line home, a weekly job or a drive home no bus covers.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Cornell's families page says first-years are [discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits are available](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). You ride TCAT free at all hours, so a permit mostly buys a parked car. Check Carshare's license and under-21 rules below before you count on it. Plan on the bus for your first year and decide again before the August permit sale for your second.
```

```reader "If you're living off campus"
You can't buy a resident permit. Your campus option is the student commuter (B) permit, and the car also needs a place to sleep at home. Price both: a monthly garage space costs more than $2,000 a year beyond a spot in your lease (see the table below).
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
A full assistantship on the Ithaca campus earns a free Outer Tier A or E permit, and the E permit also requires work in its zone. Resident students can't get the free permits, so in Cornell housing you pay the full resident rate. PhD students and graduate assistants also get a free OmniRide bus pass. That makes the no-car side of your budget close to $0.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Ithaca Carshare accepts a valid foreign license. If you've held one in the last two years, you must send an official driving record, and an International Driving Permit doesn't count as one. Some countries don't issue driving records, and [applications from those countries can't be approved](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility). Check this before you count on Carshare in place of a car.
```

## What does a car cost at Cornell in 2026-27?

Where you live sets which permit you buy. Cornell housing residents buy the resident permit priced above; off-campus students buy the student commuter (B) permit for $359.77 (Cornell Transportation). Resident permits come in four areas: FH for Hasbrouck Apartments, ND for North Campus, SW for South Campus and WD for West Campus. Each works in its own signed area, plus North Campus A Lot after 2:30 p.m. The B permit works in B Lot by the Vet College at all times and in A Lot after 2:30 p.m., and only commuters can buy it.

Permits are sold first come, first served, and annual permits are valid through Aug. 15 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Resident and commuter permits are both sold in limited numbers. The North Campus ND permit is capped and limited to North Campus residents of Cornell housing. A permit bought late is prorated by the day, from Sept. 1 for resident permits and from Aug. 16 for commuter permits. Return it by March 31 for a prorated refund; after that, Cornell gives none.

Two rules apply even if you never buy a permit. Every student car, motorcycle and scooter must be [registered with Transportation Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus) through MyParking.Cornell.edu. And parking without a permit or payment costs [$50 a ticket](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-08-01/citation-fines-increasing-september-1), plus $10 if it goes unpaid for 10 days. Without a permit, you pay by the hour or day through the ParkMobile app, with a [$0.45 fee on every transaction](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Daily Decision zones in outer lots are open to anyone, at prices that change with demand.

Put in everyday terms, the resident permit is $62.75 a month (752.98 / 12) or $2.06 a day (752.98 / 365). It is the price of 502 TCAT cash fares (752.98 / 1.50). The commuter permit works out to $29.98 a month. For permit types, lots and sale-day steps, see the [student parking permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/).

## What does a car cost beyond the permit?

Gas, insurance, maintenance and registration make up the rest of the bill, set by your car, your driving record and your insurer. Ask your insurer for a quote at your Ithaca address before you decide, and if you're on a parent's policy, ask what keeping the car in Ithaca adds. The calculator below takes your own figures for each.

Off campus, the car also needs a place to sleep. A City of Ithaca residential street permit costs [$45 a year, valid to July 31](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance), and it is voluntary for residents of permit blocks. Your landlord can't buy it for you. A 24/7 monthly space at the [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage), 118 Dryden Road in Collegetown, costs $232 a month. For Collegetown and downtown options, see [parking in Collegetown and downtown Ithaca](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/).

Winter adds rules, not fees, unless you break them. From Dec. 1 to April 1, when snow is falling or forecast, overnight parking on campus is limited to listed areas from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)). Resident-permit holders stay in their own lots. The city runs odd/even street parking from Nov. 1 to April 1 when snow removal is needed. A car left on a city street over winter break can be ticketed, towed and impounded as abandoned. The [student guide to having a car at Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/having-a-car-at-cornell-student-guide/) covers winter bays, breaks and move-in.

## What does getting around without a car cost?

Less than the permit, unless you need a car most days. TCAT is free for first-years: fall entrants ride at all hours from registration to Aug. 31 of the next year, and spring entrants from Jan. 1 to Dec. 31 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). After that, registered students ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends. Daytime weekday rides cost $1.50 cash, or $200 a year for an OmniRide pass, Cornell's discounted unlimited pass. The pass pays for itself at 134 paid rides (200 / 1.50 = 133.3). The [TCAT guide for Cornell students](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) has routes and hours.

For the trips a bus can't do well, such as a big grocery run, there is Ithaca Carshare, a nonprofit car-sharing service you book by the hour. Cornell subsidizes student memberships, and the two student plans differ by how much you drive ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)):

- **Just In Case:** $30 a year plus $9 an hour, for under 6 hours a month.
- **It's My Car:** $310 a year plus $6 an hour, for over 6 hours a month. Gas, maintenance and insurance are included.

Both plans add a one-time $20 fee per driver and 42 cents a mile in a car ([Ithaca Carshare rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)). Hourly and mileage charges also carry 8% sales tax and a 12% rental tax. Before tax and mileage, Just In Case is cheaper below 93.3 hours a year ((310 - 30) / (9 - 6)). Cornell lists [four pickup spots on campus and four in Collegetown and nearby](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing). The service paused in May 2023 when its insurer left the market and returned in March 2024 with 12 cars ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/03/25/ithaca-carshare-officially-returns-to-operation)).

**Watch out:** Carshare isn't open to every student. You must be 18, with a full license held at least two years and no suspension in three years. Drivers under 21 need a spotless record and their own insurance policy. Drivers 18 to 20 also send a consent form signed by the policy owner, such as a parent, and a declarations page showing at least $300,000 in liability per accident. Plans, eligibility and the plan calculator are in the [Ithaca Carshare guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/).

## A year with a car vs. a year without

Say you live in Cornell housing and need a car 3 hours and 10 miles a month for nine months. That is 27 hours and 90 miles a year. On Just In Case it costs $386.96: $30 + $20 + (27 x $9 + 90 x $0.42) x 1.20 ([Ithaca Carshare rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)). The same year with your own car starts at $752.98 for the permit, before any gas or insurance.

Spent on It's My Car instead, after its fee and sign-up, the resident permit's price buys 70.5 hours of driving ((752.98 - 310 - 20) / 6), or 58.7 hours once the 20% tax is added (422.98 / 7.20). That is about six and a half hours a month for nine months, with gas and insurance already covered. ## What about getting home for breaks?

If home is New York City, the bus does the job without a car. Cornell's Campus-to-Campus (C2C) bus to Manhattan costs [$187 round trip with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates), including a temporary $6 fuel and toll surcharge each way. Four C2C round trips come to $748 (4 x 187), almost exactly the resident permit. OurBus has run four to six buses a day from campus to Manhattan since Tuesday, Sept. 1, 2026, stopping at the Ithaca Commons, West Campus and North Campus ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk)). Its fares change with demand, so check the date you want.

If home is a drive no bus serves, a car can earn its keep on those trips alone. If you fly instead, long-term parking at Ithaca Tompkins International Airport costs [$40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking), which belongs in the car column.

## When does a car make sense at Cornell?

- **If you live in Cornell housing and stay near campus,** skip the car. The permit costs more than a year of Carshare errands, and TCAT runs to downtown and the Ithaca Mall.
- **If you're a first-year,** skip it. Your bus is free, and Cornell discourages first-year cars.
- **If you live off campus on a bus line,** skip it unless you have free parking at home and a reason to drive weekly.
- **If you live off campus away from a bus line or drive to a weekly job,** run the calculator. The $359.77 B permit plus a free spot at home is the cheapest way to keep a car here.
- **If home is a drive no bus covers,** a car can pay off, especially if you go home often.
- **If you're under 21 or newly licensed,** check Carshare's rules above first. If it won't take you, your options are the bus or your own car.

## Mistakes that cost money

- Buying the car before the permit. Resident permits are limited, and a permit is recalled if you lose eligibility.
- Skipping plate registration because you don't have a permit. Cornell requires it anyway.
- Pricing only the permit. Get the insurance quote first; it can change the answer.
- Leaving the car on a city street over winter break.
- Missing March 31 if you sell the car or go abroad in spring. After that date, no permit refund.

## FAQ

**Is a motorcycle or scooter cheaper to park?** Yes. The motorcycle (M) permit is $120.01 a year and covers motorcycles, scooters and mopeds. It prorates by the month, and Cornell gives no refunds on it.

**Can I keep the car on campus over winter break?** Yes, if you live in Cornell housing. Leave it in your resident permit area. B permit holders follow the overnight winter rules in their lot.

## What to do next

Get an insurance quote for your Ithaca address and enter it in the calculator. If the car still wins, register your plate in MyParking.Cornell.edu before the August permit sale. If it doesn't, join Carshare before your first grocery run.
