Most Cornell students get home by bus and around town on foot and TCAT, the local bus. The cheapest seats to New York City ran [$25 to $43 one way](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/) on Nov. 8, 2026, and first-years ride TCAT free. Five choices matter: bus, airport, car, bus pass and breaks.

```glance
- Commercial buses to New York start at $25 to $43.98; Cornell's C2C bus is $93.50 with a NetID.
- Ithaca's airport has two nonstops, to JFK and Washington Dulles; Syracuse has 27 destinations and an OurBus link.
- A resident parking permit is $752.98 a year, so most first-years leave the car home.
- Book Thanksgiving travel by mid-November; no classes run Nov. 25 to 29, 2026.
```

## What are the five decisions and when does each come due?

| Decision | When it comes due | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| NYC by C2C or a commercial bus | Every break; Thanksgiving by mid-November | C2C is $93.50 with a NetID; commercial floors start at $25 to $43.98 |
| Ithaca, Syracuse or another airport | When you buy the flight | Ithaca has 2 nonstops; Syracuse has 27 destinations |
| Car or no car | Before the August permit sale | Resident permit $752.98 a year; Carshare student plans from $30 a year |
| OmniRide or pay per ride | When your free first-year pass ends | Break-even at 134 paid daytime rides |
| Home or stay for a break | Winter break housing applications by Dec. 1 | $125 a week or $500 for the full break |

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your Cornell ID rides TCAT free at all hours until Aug. 31 of next year, so skip the OmniRide pass. Cornell discourages first-year cars. For New York, compare C2C with the commercial lines before you book.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or spring entrant"
A spring entrant's free TCAT pass runs [Jan. 1 to Dec. 31 of the same year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), not to August, with no extensions. Transfers ride free at any time, according to the Cornell Daily Sun; confirm with Transportation Services at 607-255-4600.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
No direct bus runs from Ithaca to JFK or LaGuardia, so ride into Manhattan and transfer. Leave a long gap between landing and your bus, because a missed C2C bus forfeits the fare. Ithaca Carshare does not accept an International Driving Permit as a driving record.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
You pay public fares. C2C is open to everyone at $101 one way, and a child 2 or older needs a paid seat. Ithaca's airport is about 10 minutes from downtown, but Syracuse and Elmira need a rental car or a bus.
```

## How do I get from Ithaca to New York City by bus?

Take a commercial bus if price decides it, and C2C if you carry two big suitcases or want a Cornell-run coach. C2C costs [$93.50 one way with a NetID and $101 without](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Since Nov. 4, 2026, [every rider needs a new account](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4); sign in with Cornell single sign-on to get the NetID fare.

| Bus | Ithaca stops | One way | Bags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cornell C2C | North Campus, Sage Hall, B Lot | $93.50 NetID | 2 suitcases below, 2 small carry-ons |
| OurBus | Downtown, West Campus, North Campus | From $33 | 1 large bag guaranteed |
| Greyhound | 131 E. Green St., 121 Jessup Rd., 240 West Ave. | From $43.98 | 1 carry-on and 1 checked bag |
| FlixBus | Same stops as Greyhound | $27 on one Nov. 7 listing | Not listed |

Sources: [C2C policies](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations), [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) and Wanderu, read Nov. 8, 2026. From Nov. 9, OurBus boards downtown at [235 S. Cayuga St.](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/).

Four round trips a year cost $748 on C2C ($187 each) and $264 at the OurBus floor ($33 each way). Floors are the cheapest seat on the cheapest day, so a peak day costs more. The [NYC bus guide](/cornell/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/) compares every carrier and stop.

## Which airport should I use: Ithaca, Syracuse or Elmira?

Fly into Ithaca if your route goes through JFK or Dulles, and into Syracuse if you want more flights. The Ithaca airport's [airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines) lists only Delta to JFK and United to Dulles, and its terminal sits about 10 minutes from downtown. Syracuse lists [nonstop service to 27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/), and OurBus runs there from campus for $46.70 to $62.10 each way.

Parking for a week costs $40 at Ithaca's long-term lot ([airport parking](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking)). TCAT Route 32 runs to the airport on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends ([airport transit page](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation)). No bus meets a 5:30 a.m. flight, so prebook a taxi from the airport's [taxi page](https://flyithaca.com/taxis).

## Do I need a car at Cornell?

Probably not as a first-year. [Cornell's family page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) says first-years are discouraged from bringing one. A resident permit costs $752.98 a year, about $2.06 a day, and a commuter B permit costs $359.77 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Four C2C round trips cost almost the same as one permit.

If you need a car a few times a year, Ithaca Carshare's student plans cost $30 or $310 a year plus an hourly rate, with the $30 plan cheaper below about 93 hours ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)).

```reader "If you're a student with a car on campus"
Register your plate in MyParking.Cornell.edu, even for short-term parking. From Dec. 1 to Apr. 1, when snow is forecast, overnight parking outside your permit area is limited to listed bays. Over winter break, leave the car in your permit area, never on a city street.
```

```reader "If you're a grad or professional student"
PhD students and graduate assistants get a free OmniRide pass, so the 6 p.m. limit does not apply. A full assistantship can also bring free outer-tier permits, unless you live in university housing, so check before you pay for a commuter permit.
```

## How does TCAT work and when is it free?

First-years, transfers, PhD students and graduate assistants ride free at any hour. Everyone else registered at Cornell rides free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend, and pays a per-ride fare otherwise ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). A farebox glitch let every student ride free until Cornell fixed the eligibility list on Oct. 16, 2024, according to the [Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free).

The OmniRide pass costs $200 a year and pays off after 134 paid daytime rides, which is $200 divided by the fare, rounded up ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

```reader "If you're renting off campus"
You cannot buy a resident permit, only the commuter B permit. Routes 14S and 83 to West Hill and Cayuga Heights have been [suspended since fall 2023](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation), so do not plan a commute on them.
```

## What about bikes, walking and rides?

Collegetown to central campus is a short walk, and the Commons is about 2 miles. Ithaca Bikeshare charges [$1.50 to start plus $0.25 a minute](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org). Campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far)), so register and lock. For late nights, prebook a taxi or use Uber and Lyft, which are limited outside downtown. See the [Uber, Lyft and taxi guide](/cornell/getting-around/uber-lyft-taxi-ithaca-cornell/).

## How do I plan for breaks and move-in?

Book Thanksgiving travel by mid-November, because the Wednesday before the holiday is a peak day. Cornell's calendar has [no classes Nov. 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). Winter break housing costs $125 a week or $500 for the full break, with applications due Dec. 1 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)).

For move-in, print your Move-In Ticket from the Housing and Dining Portal after Aug. 1. Your timeslot is for arrival and check-in only, not unloading ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Assuming TCAT is free all day after first year.** Upper-years pay for weekday daytime rides.
- **Booking C2C without a new account.** A plain account pays the public fare.
- **Waiting at the old OurBus stop.** It moved on Nov. 9.
- **Leaving a car on a city street over winter break.** It can be ticketed and towed.
- **Cutting it close for a dawn flight.** No bus meets a 5:30 a.m. departure.

## What to do next

Pick your route home and book it by mid-November for Thanksgiving. If you plan to fly, compare airports before you buy a ticket. If you are weighing a car, price the permit against the bus and Carshare first.
