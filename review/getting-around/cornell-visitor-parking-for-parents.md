Visitors pay to park on weekdays at Cornell and park free on weekends at three named sites ([Visit Cornell](https://www.cornell.edu/visit/parking/)). The main visitor lot, at the Tang Welcome Center, costs $1.50 an hour or $10 a day.

```glance
- Weekday visitor parking requires payment, by ParkMobile or prepaid online.
- Weekend parking is free at Toboggan Lodge and two garages.
- The Tang Welcome Center lot is Zone 4119 in ParkMobile.
- A campus ticket for no payment is $50.
```

## Where can a visitor park at Cornell?

On weekdays, every visitor needs to pay. Prepay online at cornell.clickandpark.com with your visit access code, or pay through ParkMobile. On weekends, parking is free at Toboggan Lodge (38 Forest Home Dr.), the Forest Home Drive garage and the Hoy Road garage ([Visit Cornell](https://www.cornell.edu/visit/parking/)).

## How do I pay for visitor parking?

Use ParkMobile. Text PARK to 77223 or use the app. The minimum purchase is 1 hour and the maximum is 8 hours or by the day ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)). Visitors with a Visit Cornell confirmation email can prepay through it. The page lists no price for that option.

The Tang Welcome Center pay station was out of service as of Sept. 8, 2026, so pay by phone ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)).

Example: a parent visits on a weekday for 4 hours at Tang. That is $6.00 at $1.50 an hour, plus one $0.45 fee, or $6.45 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)).

## What do visitors need to know about student permits?

Cornell sells student permits to students, for the areas listed on its [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). Visitors pay by the hour or day instead. Our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) explains the student options.

## What are the risks of parking without paying?

An unpaid space draws a $50 ticket, and a fire lane or accessible space draws a higher fine plus a tow. Those amounts are from Cornell's 2023 schedule ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/)), so confirm them with Transportation and Delivery Services.

## What if I am visiting without a car?

You do not need one. Our [parents and visitors guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-parents-visitors/) covers TCAT, taxis and the C2C bus, and the [getting to Ithaca guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/) covers flights and buses.

## What to do next

Decide whether your visit falls on a weekday or a weekend. For a weekend, use Toboggan Lodge or one of the two garages. For a weekday, prepay with your access code or pay through ParkMobile.
