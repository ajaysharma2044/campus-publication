Cornell residence hall mail goes to the service center for your area of campus. North Campus students use the Robert Purcell Community Center, 107 Robert Purcell Community Center, Ithaca, NY 14853, with a name and NetID on the label ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/toni-morrison-hall)). Other areas have their own centers, listed below.

```glance
- Address mail to your service center with your name and NetID, not to your room.
- Collegetown and South Campus halls use the Cascadilla Hall service center at 115 Cascadilla Place.
- Food delivery apps need your hall's street address, not the service center.
- Ship boxes home at move-out with Big Red Shipping and Storage, (607) 524-3130.
```

## What is the mailing address for a Cornell residence hall?

The North Campus format has five lines: your name, your NetID, 107 Robert Purcell Community Center, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/toni-morrison-hall)). A NetID is the short login that identifies you at Cornell.

Collegetown-area and South Campus halls route mail elsewhere, so use the table to find your center.

| Where you live | Where your mail goes |
|---|---|
| North Campus halls | Service center, 107 Robert Purcell Community Center |
| Cascadilla Hall, 115 Cascadilla Place | South Campus Service Center, inside Cascadilla Hall |
| Schuyler House, 112 Sage Place | 115 Cascadilla Place |
| 301 College Avenue | South Campus Service Center in Cascadilla Hall |
| 112 Edgemoor Lane | Willard Straight Hall Service Center |

Source: Cornell Student and Campus Life hall pages for [Cascadilla](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/cascadilla-hall), [Schuyler House](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house), [301 College Avenue](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue) and [112 Edgemoor](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/112-edgemoor).

Published pages for the other halls were not part of this guide's research. Ask your residence hall director or check your hall's page on the Student and Campus Life site for its center and address format.

## How do you pick up a package?

The Cornell pages in this guide's research do not state the pickup steps, such as how you are notified or what ID the counter asks for. Ask the service center for your hall, since each posts its own hours. Cascadilla's center showed the pattern in August 2026: open 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Aug. 18 to 22 and 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Aug. 23, and closed on Aug. 17 ([Cornell move instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)). Hours outside move-in week differ.

Schuyler House residents have a longer walk. The Schuyler service center closed on Aug. 21, 2023, and residents were sent to Cascadilla Hall, which one resident put at about 10 minutes each way and hilly. A Cornell official cited low package volume as the reason ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2023/08/schuyler-house-service-center-closed-after-move-in-residents-directed-to-cascadilla-hall)).

## What about delivery apps and packages for move-in?

Do not send food delivery to the service center. For delivery apps, use your hall's street address instead, for example Toni Morrison Hall, 18 Sisson Place ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/toni-morrison-hall)).

Before move-in, ship only what you cannot bring. The move-in rules for unloading and parking are in the [move-in day guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/). Parents sending boxes should read the sender's side in our [care package guide](/cornell/parents/cornell-care-package-ideas-what-to-send-student/), which covers address format and weight limits.

## How do you ship things home at move-out?

Cornell's contracted vendor is Big Red Shipping and Storage, run by Student Agencies. It picks up free from a dorm room, apartment or house, and you book at bigredboxes.com or call (607) 524-3130 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)). Shipping is priced at FedEx rates set at pickup. Prices and storage options are in the [move-out storage and shipping guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).

If you stay in Ithaca during a break, ask your service center about mail handling before you leave. Break rules are in the [guide to staying in Ithaca over breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).

```reader "If you're a transfer or upper-level student in Collegetown"
You live outside North Campus, so the Robert Purcell address does not apply. Cascadilla Hall's service center handles all Collegetown-area halls, and 301 College Avenue and Schuyler House mail both route there.
```

## What to do next

1. Find your hall in the table above and copy the address with your NetID.
2. Use your hall's street address for food delivery.
3. Visit your service center in the first week to learn its hours.
