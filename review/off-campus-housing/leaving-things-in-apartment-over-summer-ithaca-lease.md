```glance
- On a 12-month lease the apartment is yours through the last day, so your belongings can stay.
- Cornell housing offers no storage, and property left past check-out is treated as abandoned.
- A landlord can deduct the cost of moving and storing what you leave after the lease ends.
- A friend who stays in your room counts as a sublet at Ithaca Renting, paid or not.
```

On a 12-month lease, yes: your belongings can stay, because you rent the apartment through the last day of the term. The answer changes if your lease ends before you return, if someone else moves in, or if you live in a Cornell room, which has [no storage space](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027).

## Can you leave belongings in a 12-month apartment?

Yes, unless your lease says otherwise. The limit is your lease. Cornell's list of lease terms to check names a clause on [restrictions, including storage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease), next to smoking, noise and pets. Find that clause and read it. If the clause is silent about a basement, porch or storage room, ask in writing before you leave anything there. The [lease-reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) walks through the full list of clauses.

Cornell's [tenant checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) says that when you go away you should tell your contacts, forward your mail, set up your utilities, and clean and secure the apartment. It also says to avoid overcrowding. Take photos of the room as you leave, so you have a record of its condition.

Ithaca Renting adds a rule for its tenants: [keep the electricity on](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), with the fridge running at the least, even if you are away. If you leave a fridge or freezer with food in it, you need power through August.

## What happens in a Cornell residence hall?

The rules are stricter. Under the [2026-27 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027), storage space is not available, and property not removed within 24 hours of check-out is treated as abandoned. The license charges $60 a day for occupancy outside your assigned period, so you cannot pay to stay in the room. Graduating seniors get an extension through the day after Commencement at no charge. Pickup companies and self-storage are the options.

## What if your lease ends before you come back?

Check the end date. Most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), but some end sooner: every lease at [Dryden South ends May 31, 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961). Once the lease ends, you have no right to leave anything inside. The landlord can deduct the cost of [moving and storing belongings left after the lease](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) from your security deposit, and the landlord has 14 days after you move out to return the rest with an itemized list ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). The [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) and the [move-out guide](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/) cover how to protect the deposit.

```reader "If your lease ends May 31"
You have two months with no lease to cover June and July. Either store everything, or find a subletter whose dates match yours. A sublet at a building that ends May 31 cannot run past that date, so check the main lease first.
```

## What if someone stays in your room?

Then it is a sublet, even if the person pays nothing. Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page lists a [$20 fee if anyone uses the apartment over the summer](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), even if no money changes hands. Its [downtown policy](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) requires a Sublet Request Form in the same case. A sublet at either needs approval, and a downtown sublet runs at least 2 months. Leaving your couch and desk for a subletter can save you the cost of storage. The [guide to finding a summer sublet](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers the subtenant's side, and the [how-to for subletting](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) covers the landlord's consent.

## What does keeping the apartment cost?

Rent is due for every month on the lease, and [Cornell financial aid does not cover rent](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in months you are not registered. Take a Collegetown 4-bedroom with a [2027-28 median of $1,246.25 per bed](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). June and July together cost $2,492.50 (2 months at that rate). That is the price of keeping a room full of things, with or without a subletter to share it.

## What to do next

Read the restrictions clause today. Then pick one: leave things in a lease that runs through summer, sublet furnished, or move them to storage before the end date. Email your landlord to confirm in writing, and keep the reply.
