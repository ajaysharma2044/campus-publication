```glance
- Collegetown is 0.1 to 0.5 miles from campus, uphill from its lower blocks.
- Downtown and Fall Creek are 20 to 30 minute uphill walks, so plan on the bus.
- Ithaca Renting's downtown median per bed runs $370 to $400 a month under Collegetown for studios and 1-bedrooms.
- Listing walk times vary, so check any building's distance on a map before you sign.
```

If you want to walk to class, live in Collegetown: Cornell lists it at [0.1 to 0.5 miles, a walk of up to a quarter hour](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods), uphill from the lower blocks. Fall Creek, downtown and South Hill add a bus ride, and Lansing and West Hill need a car or a long bus trip.

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Grad housing is mostly off campus, so distance matters more for you than for a sophomore. Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments sit on North Campus, a [10-minute walk to central campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-students/hasbrouck-apartments), with 338 units. Outside it, Cornell lists Cayuga Heights and Cornell Heights at 1 to 4 miles.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Walkability costs money. The farther you go from Ho Plaza, the lower the posted price, as the table below shows. If you want the lowest rent and still no car, pick a downtown or Fall Creek address that sits on a frequent bus line, and budget your bus rides using the commute tool near the end of this page.
```

## Which neighborhoods can I walk from?

Cornell's own neighborhood guide gives distances and walk times for each area. A reading of the Ithaca marketplace inventory adds the median of each building's lowest listed price by area.

| Area | Distance and walk (Cornell) | Median of cheapest listed spot |
|---|---|---|
| Collegetown | 0.1 to 0.5 miles; up to a quarter hour; uphill from lower Collegetown | $997.50 (74 buildings) |
| Belle Sherman and East Hill | 1 to 2 miles (Belle Sherman); 0.75 to 2 miles (East Hill) | Not listed |
| Cayuga Heights and Cornell Heights | 1 to 4 miles | $862.50 (28, Cornell Heights and North Campus edge) |
| Fall Creek | 0.5 to 1 mile; 20 to 30 minute uphill walk; frequent bus | $828.33 (10) |
| Downtown and The Commons | 0.5 to 1.5 miles; 20 to 30 minute uphill walk | $875 (44) |
| South Hill | 1.5 to 3 miles; 15-minute downhill walk to downtown, then transit | $730 (8) |
| West Hill | 4 miles; rural; not practical on foot | Not listed |
| Lansing | 5 to 10 miles; bus or car recommended | Not listed |

Source: [Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) and the [Ithaca off-campus listings](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing). Listing prices mix bases, so use the last column to compare areas, not to budget.

Cornell's page gives the same walk time for Belle Sherman and East Hill, so the table keeps only their distances. Measure the walk from the exact address on a map.

By distance alone, per the [same listings](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing), the median cheapest spot is $1,000 within 0.5 straight-line miles of Ho Plaza (71 buildings), $900 from 0.5 to 1.0 miles (76) and $837.25 beyond one mile (38). The median falls $100 from the first band to the second, and the savings come with a bus ride.

## Which Collegetown buildings are closest?

The closest buildings state their distances in feet or minutes. These are the figures on each listing, which you should confirm on a map.

- **Collegetown Center, 151 Dryden Rd.** [150 feet to Cornell's entrance](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=4). Studios and 1-bedrooms run $1,695 to $2,545 per bed for 2027-28. It has a concierge desk and laundry on the sixth floor.
- **210 Dryden Rd.** [200 feet from Cornell's entrance](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=18). Rooms in a shared suite start at $695 per bed for 2027-28, the cheapest walkable bed on Ithaca Renting's list.
- **Collegetown Court, 208 Dryden Rd.** [One block from Cornell](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=5), with studios from $1,565 per bed.
- **Collegetown Plaza, 111 Dryden Rd.** [Two minutes from Cornell](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=1), from studios to 6-bedrooms.
- **407 College Ave.** The listing says a [4-minute walk to the Oval](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/407-college-ave-collegetown-s-best-kept-secret-237329), with units from 2 to 4 bedrooms.
- **Dryden South, 205 Dryden Rd.** A ["one minute walk" per its listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), at $1,395 to $1,990 per person, furnished.
- **Ravenwood Apartments, 516 University Ave.** [One block from the edge of West Campus](https://www.travishyde.com/west-campus/ravenwood), with a 15-minute walk on one room listing.

Collegetown Center, Court, Plaza and 210 Dryden form the Dryden Road cluster, each listed within a block of the campus gate by [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). Each distance comes from that building's own page, linked above and in the sources list, so confirm it on a map.

The marketplace's own walk-time field does not always fit the map. It prints "30+ mins" for 102 Highland Pl, which sits about 0.36 straight-line miles from Ho Plaza, per the [inventory](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing). Treat that field as a default, not a measurement.

## What about downtown and Fall Creek?

Downtown rents run lower, and you give up the walk. Ithaca Renting's median per-bed asking rent downtown is [$1,395 for a studio, $1,625 for a 1-bedroom and $992.50 for a 2-bedroom](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), against $1,765, $2,025 and $1,377.50 in Collegetown, per its [Collegetown list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). That is $370 and $400 a month less for studios and 1-bedrooms.

Cornell puts downtown at a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk. Commons West at 107 E State St lists a back-door bus stop with [buses every 15 to 20 minutes to Cornell and Ithaca College](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=7). The longer the trip, the more the bus decides whether the saving is real. The [guide to areas](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) compares the three in detail.

## How far is too far to walk?

Use Cornell's own housing as a yardstick. Schuyler House on Sage Place is a Cornell residence hall that the university describes as a [15 to 20 minute walk to Central Campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house), with 3 to 11 TCAT buses an hour and a ride of about 5 minutes. Sheldon Court sits above Collegetown Bagels, and a Sun report puts the nearest dining hall a [12-minute walk away](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court).

That gives a working rule. A walk of 15 minutes or less is one you can make daily in snow. Past 20 minutes, most people take the bus, so a building's bus stop matters as much as its distance. At Cayuga Place downtown, the leasing site says the main bus route to Cornell takes [10 to 15 minutes depending on traffic](https://livehhcayuga.com/), a time that does not count the wait.

Hills change the numbers. Cornell says lower Collegetown is uphill to campus, and the downtown and Fall Creek walks are uphill too. A map shows distance but not slope, so walk the route once before you sign.

## Can I get by on the bus?

Yes, if your building sits near a frequent line. New students ride TCAT free with a Cornell ID, per [Cornell Transportation Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). After the first year, riders without a pass ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend, and otherwise pay the $1.50 cash fare, according to [the Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). Cornell Transportation lists a free OmniRide pass for PhD students and graduate assistants.

Upper-years should read the [no-car guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-upper-years-no-car/) and the [bus pass guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) before they count on a bus. The [no-car start page](/start/upper-year-no-car/) has the reading order, and the [guide for off-campus renters](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/) covers winter, parking and late nights.
