If a roommate leaves a joint lease, you and the tenants who stay owe the full rent until the landlord releases the leaver or a replacement signs. Cornell says most local leases make housemates [jointly and severally liable](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire). Find a replacement fast and get the landlord's approval in writing.

## Who pays when a roommate leaves a joint lease?

All of you do. "Jointly and severally" means each tenant is responsible for the housemates' rent and damages for the whole term. The landlord can collect from whoever is easiest to reach, and the Cornell Law tenants' guide says [each tenant can be held for the full rent](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease). The landlord does not have to split the bill into shares.

The leaver is still on the lease, so they still owe too. Moving out does not end the lease. Only a written release, a sublet or an assignment changes who is liable, and the landlord has to agree to each one.

| Situation | Who owes the landlord | What ends it |
|---|---|---|
| Roommate leaves, nothing signed | Every tenant, including the leaver | Landlord's written release, or a new lease for the unit |
| Replacement sublets the room | Every tenant, including the leaver | The sublet ends, or the landlord releases the leaver |
| Replacement takes over by assignment | The remaining tenants and the new tenant | The assignment itself, once the landlord consents |
| Landlord re-rents the unit | The old lease ends and offsets damages | A new lease, under [RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) |

Source: Cornell Law Tenants' Rights Guide and New York Real Property Law sections 226-b and 227-e.

## How much more do you pay when one tenant leaves?

You split the same rent among fewer people. Take a Collegetown 4-bedroom at the 2027-28 median of [$1,246.25 per bed per month](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). The unit costs $4,985.00 a month. With four tenants, each pays that median. After one leaves, three pay $1,661.67 each ($4,985.00 divided by 3).

## Can you make the roommate who left pay their share?

Yes, but you could have to sue. The Cornell Law guide says a tenant who overpays can [sue a co-tenant for contribution](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease). Contribution means the leaver repays the part of the rent that was theirs.

Small claims court hears these cases. Town and village courts take claims up to $3,000. Ithaca City Court takes claims up to $5,000 and sits at 118 East Clinton Street ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Keep proof: texts agreeing on the split, rent payments and the date the roommate left.

Start with a written note that names the amount and the date it was due. If the leaver ignores it, the Community Dispute Resolution Center mediates roommate disputes at 607-273-9347 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources)).

## How do you replace a roommate who left?

Ask the landlord first, in writing. The landlord's consent decides which route you take.

### A sublet

A subtenant lives in the room and pays rent, and the leaver stays on the lease. In a building with four or more residential units, [New York law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) gives the tenant a right to sublet with the landlord's advance written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. The request goes by certified mail and must include the written consent of any co-tenant or guarantor. That means you, the remaining tenants, have to sign off on the replacement.

The landlord has 10 days to ask for more information and a month to answer. Silence counts as consent. In a building of three or fewer units, the lease controls instead ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

Ithaca Renting has its own rules. Collegetown sublets must be registered and approved, cost a $15 fee, go only to Cornell students, and run at least one month, with no Airbnb use ([Ithaca Renting Collegetown](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Downtown sublets run at least two months, need the agreement of all named tenants and do not relieve the tenant of lease duties ([Ithaca Renting downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)). The [sublet guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) walks through the full process.

### An assignment

An assignment hands the leaver's place to a new tenant. After it, the original tenant is no longer liable for the new tenant's rent or damage. The Cornell Law guide says assigning beats subletting when the leaver does not plan to move back. The catch is that the landlord [can refuse without cause](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). If the refusal is unreasonable, the tenant's only remedy is release from the lease on 30 days' notice.

### A new occupant who is not on the lease

A friend can move in without signing the lease, but only as an occupant. Under [RPL 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F), tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the number of tenants on a multiple-tenant lease, so one in for one out fits the count. The tenant must give the occupant's name within a month of move-in or of the landlord asking. An occupant has no right to stay if the tenants leave, and the landlord has no claim on them for rent. You stay liable for the whole unit.

### Letting the landlord re-rent

If the leaver goes with nothing signed, [RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) requires the landlord to take reasonable steps, in good faith, to re-rent at the lower of fair market value or the lease rate. A new lease ends the old one and offsets damages. A lease clause waiving this duty is void. Whether the duty reaches one empty bed in a unit where others remain is a question for the Tenants Advocacy hotline, 607-301-1560.

## What happens to the security deposit?

The landlord holds one deposit for the unit. New York caps it, plus any rent paid in advance, at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). The landlord must return what is left, with an itemized statement, within two weeks after the tenant vacates, or forfeits the right to keep any of it. That clock runs from when the tenants leave the unit.

Whether the leaver's share of the deposit comes back when they go depends on the landlord. Ask for the answer in the release or assignment paperwork. The [deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) covers the deductions.

## What mistakes do remaining tenants make?

- **Treating the empty room as the leaver's problem.** The landlord can collect from any tenant, so a missed payment hits all of you.
- **Letting a friend move in with no paperwork.** Without the landlord's approval, a sublet without the written steps is a substantial breach.
- **Taking a check from a "subtenant" and refunding the extra.** A student subletting once received a $3,850 check, sent refunds to two accounts, and the check never cleared ([University of Illinois Police](https://police.illinois.edu/fake-sublessee/)).
- **Skipping the roommate agreement next time.** Cornell asks housemates to settle in advance who finds a replacement if someone leaves. Settle it before you sign.

## FAQ

**Can the landlord evict everyone if the leaver's share goes unpaid?** Rent unpaid for 5 days draws a certified-mail notice, and the tenant then has two weeks to pay or leave before a filing ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/eviction-proceedings)). Pay the shortfall while you sort out the leaver.

**Who can I call for help?** Off-Campus Living gives one-on-one consultations and its line is 607-255-2310 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease)). The free Tenants Advocacy hotline is 607-301-1560.

## What to do next

Read your lease's sublet and assignment clauses before the next rent date. Then email the landlord a request to release the leaver and approve a replacement. Send the certified-mail sublet request at the same time so the landlord's answer clock starts ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).
