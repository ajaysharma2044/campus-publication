```glance
- Big Red Shipping and Storage ships at FedEx rates set at pickup and posts no shipping price.
- Uncle Marty's ships by FedEx, DHL and USPS, and sells box bundles from $74.99.
- OurBus charges $30 for each bag past the first, prepaid. C2C allows two suitcases.
- Book before May 1 for a guaranteed delivery slot at Uncle Marty's.
```

The cheapest way home depends on volume. A few bags ride a bus for a flat fee, while a box load ships at a carrier's rate. Big Red Shipping and Storage (BRSS), Cornell's contracted vendor, [calculates shipping at pickup at current FedEx rates](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) and posts no price list for it.

## What are your options for getting belongings home?

| Option | What it carries | Posted cost | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| BRSS shipping | Boxes and bags collected from your room | FedEx rate set at pickup | Several fees are unposted |
| Uncle Marty's shipping | Boxes you drop off or have collected | Carrier rates; bundles $74.99 to $499.99 | Carrier rates not posted |
| Bus, as luggage | Suitcases and small bags | OurBus extra bag $30 each | Space limits at peak times |
| Flying | Checked and carry-on bags | Set by your airline | Airline fees not in this guide |

Source note: [BRSS](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing), [Uncle Marty's bundles](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/move-out-bundles), [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university). The [move-out options guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/) puts these next to storage.

## How does BRSS shipping work?

BRSS is a division of Student Agencies, and the ship-home service runs through FedEx. Cornell says pickup is [free at your dorm, apartment or house](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), and you must be there when the driver arrives.

The price list names the fees that can raise your bill: [overweight, oversized or fragile items, late reschedules, missed appointments and boxes that are not BRSS boxes](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing). It does not post amounts for them. Ask for a written total on the phone before the appointment. Demand is heavy: Robert Purcell Community Center handled [nearly 28,000 packages in two weeks in 2022](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes), and the same report says carriers have raised their rates.

## What does Uncle Marty's offer?

Uncle Marty's is a [FedEx, DHL and USPS authorized shipper](https://business.tompkinschamber.org/list/member/uncle-marty-s-printing-shipping-storage-more-12929) that has been in Collegetown since 2011. Its [box bundles](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/move-out-bundles) run from $74.99 for a dorm room to $499.99 for a whole house. Curbside delivery is free on orders over $150, and an order placed before May 1 gets a guaranteed delivery slot. The shop does not post carrier shipping rates, so get a quote at the counter. Its [storage page](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage) lists a free in-store drop-off.

## How much can you carry on a bus?

If you take a bus home, check the bag limit before you pack. Prices and limits differ by line.

| Bus line | Free allowance | Extra bags |
|---|---|---|
| C2C (Cornell's campus-to-campus bus) | 2 suitcases below deck and 2 small carry-ons | A bike must be in a bike box and counts as 1 suitcase |
| OurBus | 1 suitcase up to 50 lb plus 1 handbag | $30 each, prepaid |
| Greyhound | 1 carry-on and 1 checked bag | The Flexible fare adds a second checked bag |

Source note: [C2C rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations), [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university), [Greyhound](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny).

Peak weeks are tighter than the table suggests. The Cornell Daily Sun reports that OurBus guarantees one large bag per ticket, and at peak move-in a second large bag effectively takes a second seat. Two extra bags on OurBus cost $60. The [New York City bus guide](/cornell/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/) compares the lines and fares.

## What about flying home?

Airlines set their own baggage fees, and this guide has no fee table for them. Call the airline before you pack: Delta baggage is 1-800-325-8224 and United baggage is 1-800-335-2247, both listed by [Ithaca Tompkins International Airport](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). The [airport and rides guide](/cornell/getting-around/flying-home-from-cornell-airports-rides/) covers getting to the terminal with your bags.

```reader "If you're an international student"
BRSS lists extra fees for international shipments and customs paperwork and posts no amounts. Ask for those in writing, and ask the carrier who pays import duties at the other end. The [international students' arrival guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-cornell-international-students/) covers the trip in the other direction.
```

## What goes wrong, and how do you avoid it?

- **Missing the pickup.** BRSS needs you present, and a missed appointment carries a fee. Items left in a room after the buildings closed at 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026, were donated.
- **Using your own boxes.** BRSS lists non-BRSS boxes as an extra fee. Buy its boxes, or ask first.
- **Waiting for May.** Slots are limited, so book before finals.
- **Assuming a flat price.** Shipping is set by weight and size at pickup. Weigh your boxes.

## What to do next

Sort your belongings into what rides with you, what ships and what stays. Call BRSS for a written shipping total and ask Uncle Marty's for a quote on the same boxes. If family sends a package the other way in August, the [care package guide](/cornell/parents/cornell-care-package-ideas-what-to-send-student/) covers what to put in it.
