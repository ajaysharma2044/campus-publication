Wari Cooperative is a Cornell-owned co-op at 208 Dearborn Pl. for 10 undergraduate women of color. It has eight singles and one double, and no house meal plan (Cornell Housing). It takes transfer and upper-level women.

## What is Wari Cooperative?

A co-op is a university-owned house run by its residents. Cornell lists Wari at 208 Dearborn Pl. with 10 undergraduate women of color in 8 singles and 1 double ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/wari-cooperative)). It is the smallest of the eight co-ops.

Cornell's diversity timeline dates the house to 1968 ([Cornell Diversity](https://diversity.cornell.edu/about-us/our-historic-commitment)). A 2001 Sun article quoted a resident who gave 1967, so the year is not settled in every source. Go by 1968, which is Cornell's own record. Cornellians credits Irene Smalls '71 as Wari's founder ([Cornellians](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/cbaa-50th/)).

## What does Wari cost?

For 2026-27 a double is $4,981 per semester, or $9,962 for two semesters. A single is $5,640 to $6,014 per semester, which is $11,280 to $12,028 for the year ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

Since eight of Wari's nine rooms are singles, the single rate is the one that matters. Even the top Wari single, at $6,014, costs $858 per semester less than a hall double at $6,872. Over two semesters that is $1,716. A hall double costs $13,744 for the year, and the Wari double is $3,782 less (Cornell Housing).

There is no house meal plan. Cornell's co-op overview and the Wari page agree on this, while a 2023 Sun article called Wari one of the co-ops with a plan. Go by Cornell's pages ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). For leases, see [Is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/) and [cheapest off-campus housing near Cornell](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/).

## How do you get into Wari?

Each co-op runs its own selection, and selection starts in early November and early February (Cornell Housing). The Sun reported in 2019 that Wari used interviews to choose residents ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/11/open-house-day-highlights-unique-qualities-of-cornell-co-ops/)). Write to waricoop@cornell.edu, or to co-ops@cornell.edu or 607-255-3445, for the current steps.

Rising sophomores must state their co-op plan on the 2027-28 application by March 15, 2027 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). A co-op counts as affiliated housing, explained on our [affiliated housing page](/start/affiliated-housing/). To live off campus instead, check the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What should you watch?

The houses are old. The Sun reported in October 2024 that four of the eight co-ops had floors collapse in recent years ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/)). Ask what has been repaired at Wari.

All eight co-ops share one license, with no security deposit and a full-time student rule. See [Cornell co-ops compared](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-all-eight-compared/) and our guide to [co-ops and Greek houses](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/).
