```glance
- Cornell's Hasbrouck charges $75 more a month for a furnished 1-bedroom, $900 over 12 months.
- Furnished is standard at Ithaca Renting, Dryden South, Cityview and Maplewood.
- Unfurnished units cost you a summer of storage or a haul out of town.
- No Ithaca source prices a full set of furniture, so price a bed and desk yourself.
```

Choose furnished if you will live in Ithaca one year or less, arrive from far away, or sublet in summer. Choose unfurnished if you will stay several years and already own furniture. The price gap is small: Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments charge [$1,490 for a furnished 1-bedroom and $1,415 unfurnished](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates).

```reader "If you're an international student"
Furnished is the practical choice. You arrive without a car or a lease history, and a furnished unit saves a shopping trip in your first week. Pick a building that also includes utilities, such as Maplewood. The [international student guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-international-students/) covers renting from abroad.
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar or postdoc"
Furnished is nearly the only workable choice for a stay of months, because you cannot sell furniture at the end. The [scholar housing guide](/cornell/housing/visiting-scholar-postdoc-furnished-housing-ithaca/) lists furnished options for short stays.
```

## How much more does furnished cost?

The gap is small in every pair below.

| Comparison | Furnished | Unfurnished | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hasbrouck 1-bedroom, 12-month contract | $1,490 | $1,415 | $75 a month, $900 a year |
| Hasbrouck family 2-bedroom townhouse | $1,530 | $1,475 | $55 a month, $660 a year |
| Cayuga Place furniture package | $75 per bedroom | None | $75 a month, $900 a year |
| Farm St 1-bedrooms, Fall Creek | $1,545 (312 Farm St) | $1,535 (310 Farm St) | $10 a month, from different units |

Source: [Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), [Cayuga Place](https://livehhcayuga.com/), and the Farm St listings, [furnished](https://www.ithacarents.com/4385-cute-furnished-1-br-apt-in-fall-creek-that-is-closest-to-cornell) and [unfurnished](https://www.ithacarents.com/4563-unfurnished-sun-filled-1br-in-fall-creek-1e-walk-to-cornell--commons).

At [Cayuga Place](https://livehhcayuga.com/), the furniture package is an add-on, so you can compare renting furniture with buying it.

The Farm Street pair is not a clean comparison, because the two units differ in utilities. The [furnished unit](https://www.ithacarents.com/4385-cute-furnished-1-br-apt-in-fall-creek-that-is-closest-to-cornell) includes utilities except water, WiFi and trash, and the [unfurnished one](https://www.ithacarents.com/4563-unfurnished-sun-filled-1br-in-fall-creek-1e-walk-to-cornell--commons) includes heat and gas. Both charge $33 a month per person for water.

Furnished does not mean pricier per bed. The [Prospect St house](https://www.ppmhomes.com/listings/detail/3f1544f9-76c1-4375-91f6-b2400a52dbbf) works out to $650 per bedroom ($3,900 divided by 6) and the [Hudson St house](https://www.ithacarents.com/4516-6-bedroom-house-for-27/28-school-year) to $695 ($4,170 divided by 6), both below the [Collegetown median](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) of $800 for a 6-bedroom bed. Check utilities before you compare, because the Prospect St tenants pay them.

## What does unfurnished cost you at the end of the lease?

Storage, or a trip home. Cornell residence halls do not offer storage, per the [2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028), so the storage market is private. Four options, each with a posted price:

| Service | Price | Term |
|---|---|---|
| Big Red Shipping and Storage | Small box $39; large box $49; small item $49; medium item $75 | 3 to 4 months, with pickup and delivery |
| Uncle Marty's | $15 per item a month (up to 50 pounds); $35 large | Monthly |
| Storage Squad | $45 for a standard item such as a cube fridge | 4-month summer term |
| Bolt Storage, 1401 Dryden Rd | 5x5 drive-up $84 a month; 10x10 $147 | Month to month; insurance required |

Source: [Big Red Shipping and Storage](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing), [Uncle Marty's](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage), [Storage Squad](https://storagesquad.com/summer-storage/cornell-university/) and [Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny).

A student leaving ten small boxes with [Big Red Shipping and Storage](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) pays $390 ($39 times 10). Three regular items at [Uncle Marty's](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage) cost $45 a month, or $135 over three months. A 5x5 unit at [Bolt](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny) for the same three months costs $252 ($84 times 3), and you do the hauling. Cornell's own page for Big Red Shipping and Storage lists no prices, so use the vendor's price list and book early.

A furnished unit avoids those costs, but a furnished summer sublet is easier too. The [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers how to hand a unit to a subtenant, and the [move-out storage guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/) compares the storage vendors in more detail.

## What about the cost of furnishing an empty apartment?

No Ithaca source prices a full set of furniture, so there is no total here. Price a bed, mattress, desk, chair and sofa at a store near you. Then multiply the monthly gap in the table above by your lease length and compare the two figures. Owning pays off only if you keep the furniture for the years it takes to cover that gap, and storage costs come on top.

## How do I judge what "furnished" includes?

The word covers very different things, so ask for a list. [210 Dryden Rd](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=18) lists a mini-fridge and fan in each room. [Whiton Hall](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=11) lists a mini-refrigerator and microwave in each furnished room. [Ivy Ridge Townhomes](https://ivyridgetownhomes.com/) in Varna lists a queen bed, four-drawer dresser, desk and chair per bedroom, plus a dining table, couch, living room chair and coffee table. Ravenwood's units come with TVs.

Cornell's checklist says to send the landlord an inventory and photos after move-in, and to buy renter's insurance. A written inventory protects your deposit, because a landlord cannot deduct for damage that was there at move-in if the record shows it, and it settles who owns a couch if a housemate leaves.

Ask also what is not on the list, and budget for bedding and kitchen items in either case.

## Which should I choose?

Match the decision to your stay.

**Choose furnished** if the lease is 12 months or less, you are moving a long way, or you will sublet in summer. Confirm in writing which items are included. Cornell's [lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) tells renters to find the extra costs, including furnishings, and the [checklists page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations) says to send the landlord an inventory and photos after move-in.

**Choose unfurnished** if you will stay two or more years, already own furniture, and live near enough to move it yourself. A unit like Summerhill's, with a washer and dryer in each, suits that plan.

For a month-by-month budget either way, the [monthly cost guide](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/) adds utilities and food. The checklist tool below tracks the move-in steps.
