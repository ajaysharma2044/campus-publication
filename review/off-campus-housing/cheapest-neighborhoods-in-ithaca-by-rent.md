Fall Creek and Cornell Heights have the cheapest whole apartments within walking distance of Cornell. A one-bedroom asks a median $1,467.50 a month in Fall Creek and $1,515 in Cornell Heights, against $1,585 in Collegetown (EduExplained Ithaca apartment inventory). If you need only one bed, a room in a large shared unit is cheapest, from $695 a month.

## Which Ithaca neighborhood has the cheapest rent?

Fall Creek has the lowest median one-bedroom rent of the neighborhoods near campus, then Cornell Heights, then Collegetown. Downtown is the most expensive of the four. Fall Creek sits below campus toward Cayuga Lake. Cornell Heights is the hillside across the Fall Creek gorge, north of campus. Collegetown is the student district just south of campus.

## What does a cheap apartment in Fall Creek or Cornell Heights cost?

The cheapest Fall Creek one-bedroom asks $890 to $900 a month at 802 N. Cayuga St., listed on the [Ithaca College marketplace](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing). CSP Management lists a one-bedroom at [909 N. Cayuga St.](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/public/rentals/21495) for $1,400 a month, available Jan. 8, 2027. True Renew Homes asks $1,535 a month for an unfurnished one-bedroom at [310 Farm St.](https://www.ithacarents.com/4563-unfurnished-sun-filled-1br-in-fall-creek-1e-walk-to-cornell--commons), plus $33 a month per person for water and an $82 yearly recycling fee.

Fall Creek is 0.5 to 1 mile from campus, a 20- to 30-minute uphill walk, with frequent buses ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)).

Cornell Heights listings sit 0.29 to 0.89 mile from Ho Plaza in a straight line, so many are walkable. Travis Hyde Properties runs several of the cheaper ones. A 411-square-foot one-bedroom at [934 Edgecliff](https://www.travishyde.com/west-campus/934%20edgecliff), 934 Stewart Ave., asks $1,305 a month. Lakeland Apartments at 319 Highland Road asks $1,420 for a one-bedroom and $1,005 for a studio ([Travis Hyde availability](https://www.travishyde.com/availability)). A private one-bedroom at 512 Wyckoff Road asks $950.

The area's new building, [The Pinnacle](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/the-pinnacle-237571) at 121 Lake St., lists one-bedrooms at $2,700 to $2,800 a month. That is why Cornell Heights' range runs so wide.

## Why is downtown more expensive than Collegetown?

Downtown's median is high because its new buildings dominate the listings. [The Dean](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/the-dean-237348), at 401 E. State St., lists one-bedrooms from $2,350 to $2,500 a month. Seneca Way, at 140 Seneca Way, lists them from $2,377 to $2,665. [Ironworks Ithaca](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/ironworks-ithaca-236868), at 430 W. State St., lists studios from $2,072. Travis Hyde's [Gateway Commons](https://www.travishyde.com/downtown-ithacaf4ba8d70/gateway%20commons), 311 E. Green St., asks $2,430 for a one-bedroom.

Older downtown buildings still run cheap. A 387-square-foot one-bedroom at [Westminster Hall](https://www.travishyde.com/downtown-ithacaf4ba8d70/westminster%20hall), 321 N. Tioga St., asks $1,435 a month. Center Ithaca, at 171 E. State St., lists one-bedrooms from $1,435 to $1,845. If you want downtown on a budget, look at older walk-ups and converted buildings, not the new towers.

At a single landlord the picture flips. Ithaca Renting prices downtown beds 20% to 28% below the same unit sizes in Collegetown ([Collegetown vs downtown rent per bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/)). The two fit together: the new towers lift downtown's median, while its older stock stays cheap.

## Do you save money by living farther out?

You save on rent in Lansing, but you pay in time and bus fare. Lansing West, at 700 Warren Road, lists one-bedrooms from $1,302 a month, two-bedrooms from $1,625 and three-bedrooms from $2,072 ([RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx)). Its listing offers rent free until January 2027 to applicants who apply by Nov. 14, 2026, and move in by Dec. 1, 2026. Beer Properties' [Pine Hill Apartments](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/pine-hill-apartments-99962), at 709 Triphammer Road, asks $1,390 to $1,490 for a one-bedroom. Cornell puts Lansing 5 to 10 miles from campus and recommends a bus or a car.

Farther out is not always cheaper. Springwood Townhomes on South Hill, at 123 E. King Road, asks $1,350 to $1,475 for a one-bedroom. On West Hill, which Cornell calls rural and 4 miles out, one-bedrooms run $1,200 at 101 Westhaven Road and $1,650 at 738 Elm St. Ext. In Varna, [Evergreen Pointe](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/evergreen-pointe-236735), at 1061 Dryden Road, asks $2,495 to $2,525 for a one-bedroom.

Lansing West's $1,302 one-bedroom is $283 a month below the $1,585 Collegetown median. A TCAT ride costs $1.50. TCAT's fare page lists the monthly pass at $45.00 and its where-to-buy page at $46.00; go by $46.00 ([TCAT](https://www.tcatbus.com/ride/fare-pass-options/)). That leaves $237 a month, or $2,844 over a 12-month lease. Registered Cornell students ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends ([Cornell Transportation Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-pri)).

## FAQ

**Are these prices what tenants actually pay?**
No. They are asking rents printed on listing pages in fall 2026. Most listings print no lease year, and some landlords change prices as units fill.

**Why isn't Northside or Cayuga Heights in the table?**
The inventory has too few priced whole-unit listings placed in those areas to give a median. Search the Cornell and Ithaca College marketplaces by street to price them yourself.

## What to do next

Decide first whether you want a whole apartment or a single bed, because the cheapest neighborhood differs. For a one-bedroom, start with Travis Hyde, CSP Management and True Renew Homes in Fall Creek and Cornell Heights. For a bed, compare Ithaca Renting's large Collegetown units with its downtown three-bedrooms.
