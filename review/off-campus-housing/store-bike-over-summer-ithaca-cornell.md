Keep your bike indoors: in your own apartment, with a friend, in a storage unit or at Uncle Marty's, which charges [$60 a month for a bicycle](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage). Leaving it locked at a campus rack all summer is the one option to skip.

## Where can you store a bike over summer?

| Option | Cost | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Your own apartment on a 12-month lease | Included in your rent | Check the lease for a storage clause first |
| A friend's apartment in Ithaca | Free | Agree on who owns the risk if it is damaged |
| Uncle Marty's | $60 a month; curbside pickup $25 | Treated as a giant item; one-month minimum, then billed every two weeks |
| Self-storage unit | From $84 a month for a 5x5 at Bolt Storage | You provide the transport; measure the bike first |
| Take it home | A bus fare plus a bike box | C2C counts a boxed bike as 1 suitcase |

Source note: [Uncle Marty's](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage), [Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny), [C2C rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).

Big Red Shipping and Storage, Cornell's vendor, picks up from your room, but its [price list](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) names no bike in any item tier. Call 607-524-3130 and ask what it charges. Until it answers, treat the price as unknown.

## Can you leave a bike locked at a campus rack?

No. Cornell says to park only at [designated bike racks](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus), and it impounds bikes that are abandoned, blocking a path or locked to a railing, signpost, tree or pole. A bike left all summer reads as abandoned. Theft is the second risk. Campus police counted [51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far), worth at least $41,177. Four bikes came back intact. Cornell advises a U-lock.

Indoor storage in campus buildings is allowed only with the written permission of the building coordinator. A residence hall is not a bike room either. A summary of Cornell's housing rules says bikes belong in external racks or sprinklered bike rooms, so ask Housing before you wheel one in.

## How much does a summer of bike storage cost?

Summer storage runs four months, May through August. At [Uncle Marty's rate](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage), that is $240 (4 × $60), plus $25 for curbside pickup. A 5x5 unit at Bolt's Dryden Road site costs $336 over the same period (4 × $84), which only makes sense if the unit also holds your boxes. If you can leave the bike in a friend's apartment, the cost is a thank-you.

## How do you take a bike home on a bus?

Pack it in a bike box. C2C, Cornell's campus-to-campus bus, accepts a bike [only in a bike box, and counts it as one suitcase](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). Each passenger gets 2 suitcases below deck, so a boxed bike leaves room for one more. Other lines do not post bike rules in the sources this guide uses, so call the line before you buy a ticket. On campus, every TCAT bus has a front bike rack at no extra fare, but that is for a ride across town.

## What should you do before you store it?

Register the bike. Registration is free through Cornell Commuter and Parking Services, and Cornell urges it but does not say it is required. Lock the frame and a wheel when you park it anywhere. Cornell's cycling page does not cover winter riding, storage or repair, and it recommends a local bike shop for repairs. A shop can also tune the bike before you put it away. The [bike rules and theft guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-bike-ebike-scooter-rules-theft/) covers registration and locks in full.

```reader "If you live in a co-op"
Wait Avenue Cooperative at 302 Wait Ave. lists [free summer storage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/wait-avenue-cooperative) for its residents. Ask your own house whether it offers the same.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for summer"
You may not need to store the bike, since you can ride it. If you left it at home, [Ithaca Bikeshare](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org) charges $1.50 to unlock plus $0.25 a minute, or a monthly plan. The [Bikeshare cost guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-bikeshare-cost-cornell-students/) shows when each pays off.
```

## What about winter?

The same options work for a winter break. Riding through the winter is a different question, covered in the [winter biking guide](/cornell/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/). The [breaks guide](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) says who can stay in Cornell housing.

## What to do next

Pick the cheapest indoor option you can reach. Call BRSS for a bike quote, and decide before the buildings close.
