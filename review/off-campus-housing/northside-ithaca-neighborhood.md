Northside is the City of Ithaca neighborhood south and west of Cascadilla Creek, running down to Washington Park and Route 13, according to the Ithaca Times as relayed by the Ithaca Voice ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2015/07/often-overlooked-ithacas-northside-residents-try-building-clout/)). Cornell's neighborhoods page does not list it, so check each address on a map before you rely on a listing's name.

## Is Northside the same as Fall Creek?

No, but landlords blur them. Landlords on Lake Avenue tell renters the area is Fall Creek, the Ithaca Voice reported in 2015 (Ithaca Voice). Cornell describes Fall Creek as 0.5 to 1 mile from campus with a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)), but Northside has no Cornell walk time. Fall Creek is the nearer area.

## What is Northside like?

Residents describe it as one of the city's least affluent neighborhoods, with public housing and refugee families, according to the Ithaca Voice's 2015 reporting. Our [neighborhood comparison](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) covers the three areas students pick most.

## How much does rent cost?

No source gives a Northside rent. Two datapoints help:

- RentCafe lists an average of about $2,488 for a two-bedroom of 913 square feet in Northwest Ithaca, the closest published area, though RentCafe states no borders ([RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments-for-rent/northwest-ithaca-ny/2-bedroom/)).
- A three-bedroom at 508 W. Clinton St. lists $2,000 a month for a lease starting Sept. 25, 2026, with the resident paying electricity, gas, heat, trash and water ([Ithaca Rents](https://www.ithacarents.com/3099-three-bed-apt,-downtown-ithaca-2,000/-avail-fro)).

Lambrou Real Estate lists houses on West Court Street and apartments on North Fulton Street without printing rents, with driveways and detached garages at the West Court houses ([Lambrou Real Estate](https://www.lambrourealestate.com/apartments)). Ask each landlord for the price in writing, and whether it is per bed or per unit.

## How far is it from campus?

The listings above sit 1.2 to 1.4 miles from Ho Plaza in a straight line, and the hill between adds to the walk. That is farther than Cornell's 1 mile for the Commons. Plan on a bus, a bike or a car.

Ithaca CarShare serves Northside, along with downtown, Fall Creek, Collegetown and East Hill, with about 20 vehicles that include maintenance and insurance ([Ithaca CarShare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org)). The student It's My Car plan costs $310 a year plus $6 an hour, and Just In Case costs $30 a year plus $9 an hour ([Ithaca CarShare students](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)). Our [CarShare cost guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/) shows when each plan wins. For buses and bikes, see [getting around as an off-campus renter](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/).
