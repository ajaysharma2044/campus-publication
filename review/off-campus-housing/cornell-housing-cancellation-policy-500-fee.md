Returning Cornell juniors and seniors pay $500 to cancel a campus housing license between Feb. 1 and March 31. Through Jan. 31 the fee is $50, and it climbs to $2,000 by July ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-license-cancellation-policy)). First-years, new transfers and second-years can't cancel at all except in a few narrow cases.

```glance
- Juniors and seniors: cancel a 2027-28 room by Jan. 31, 2027, and the fee is $50.
- After that it climbs in four steps to half the fall room rate.
- Going away or graduating? Cancel free by June 15 (fall) or Dec. 1 (spring).
- First-years, new transfers and second-years can't cancel to move off campus.
```

## How much does it cost to cancel Cornell housing?

The fee depends only on the date Cornell receives your request. A housing license is Cornell's word for its room contract: unlike a lease, it gives you the right to use a room, not to rent one from a landlord. Returning juniors and seniors, who have already met Cornell's two-year live-on requirement, pay on the schedule below, set out in the [2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) for rooms picked in fall 2026.

The first step is the one to plan around. Say you sign a Collegetown lease in February and drop your campus room the same week: that costs $450 more than cancelling in January (Cornell Housing and Residential Life).

## What does "half the fall room rate" come to?

For a residence hall double, half the fall room rate is $3,436: Cornell charges $6,872 per semester for a double in 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)), and $6,872 divided by two is $3,436. That is almost 69 times the January fee. The same charge applies if you never show up: students who don't check in by the first day of classes, and don't arrange a late arrival, lose the room and are billed half the fall rate through the bursar, Cornell's student billing office.

## When can I cancel without paying anything?

You can cancel penalty-free if you are leaving Ithaca for an approved reason and you file on time. Cornell names the reasons: study away from Ithaca (Cornell in Washington, Cornell in Rome, a Cornell global program, the Engineering Co-op, an academic internship or ILR in New York City), a medical, voluntary or required leave of absence, deferring admission, withdrawing, or graduating.

The deadlines are June 15 before a fall semester and Dec. 1 before a spring semester; the timeline below gives the 2027-28 dates. A request that arrives late costs a flat $500 ([2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

## Who is not allowed to cancel?

First-years, new transfer students and second-years can't cancel to move off campus. Cornell requires them to live in university housing, so the license holds unless the student is no longer attending, has an approved exemption, or, for second-years only, was accepted to an approved Greek house, affiliated house or university co-op before the rising sophomore selection deadline. Transfers admitted as second-years count as second-years, and the [transfer housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/) explains how new transfers are assigned rooms.

The cost of ignoring the rule is the whole year. Cornell says students who neither live on campus nor get an exemption "will still be billed for the full year" ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)). An exemption has to be requested through the housing application; see [how exemptions work](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

```reader "If you're a sophomore"
Your 2026-27 room can't be cancelled to move off campus. A 2027-28 room you chose for junior year falls under the junior and senior schedule, because you will have met the two-year requirement. That makes it a cheap backup through January while you look for a lease; after that, holding both gets expensive. The [sophomore off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) covers the lease side.
```

## Can I cancel after I move in?

Only for a few reasons, and only with Cornell's approval. Once you occupy the room, the license binds you for the whole term. Cornell can let you out if you withdraw, take a leave, get a medical withdrawal, start an academic internship, graduate or study in absentia, which means earning credit while away from Ithaca. If Cornell approves, you pay a daily rate for the days you lived there and must move out and return your keys within 24 hours.

Moving off campus mid-year doesn't qualify. Cornell's Sorority and Fraternity Life office says spring releases go only to students who are graduating, going abroad or leaving, and that moving off campus or into a fraternity small residence "is not an extenuating circumstance" ([Cornell Sorority and Fraternity Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities)). Housing and Residential Life can grant other releases in exceptional cases, at its sole discretion, with a prorated refund minus fees. Subletting is not a way around it: the license lets only you and your assigned roommates live in the room.

If you've already signed an apartment lease and need out of that instead, the rules are New York tenant law, not Cornell's; see [breaking a lease early in Ithaca](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).

## Why did Cornell start charging this fee?

To free rooms for students on the waitlist. Housing and Residential Life told The Cornell Daily Sun that campus housing isn't guaranteed after two years and that the new policy lets students give up rooms so others can move off the waitlist. The schedule is new for licenses beginning in August 2026. For 2025-26, juniors and seniors couldn't cancel at all except for a leave, medical withdrawal, internship, study in absentia or other grounds Cornell approved ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)).

The waitlist explains the shape of the fee. The low January price frees rooms early, when waitlisted students can still use them. The steep summer prices discourage students from holding a room as insurance into August. Room selection for 2026-27 ran Oct. 2 to Oct. 8, 2025, so juniors and seniors now choose campus rooms in the same weeks many Collegetown leases sign. Read [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) before you lock in both.

```reader "If you're a parent paying the bill"
Late fees and no-show charges go to the student's bursar account, the same bill as tuition. If your student is choosing between a campus room and a lease, ask which one they will keep before January ends.
```

## FAQ

**How do I submit a cancellation?**
Through the Housing & Dining Portal, on the day you decide, since the date sets the fee. For questions, Housing Operations is at 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, housing@cornell.edu, weekdays 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life directory](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory)).

**Does joining a co-op or Greek house let me cancel?**
For second-years, yes, if the acceptance came before the rising sophomore selection deadline. Juniors and seniors follow the fee schedule either way. Costs and timelines for those houses are in the [co-ops and Greek houses guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/).

## What to do next

If you're a junior or senior holding a campus room and a lease is in the works, decide before the January deadline. If you're leaving Ithaca for a semester, file before the deadline for the term you'll miss. Either way, run your date through the calculator above before you submit.
