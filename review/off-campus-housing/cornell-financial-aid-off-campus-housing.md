```glance
- Cornell calculates your financial need the same way whether you live on or off campus.
- Off campus, the Bursar stops billing housing; extra aid comes back as a refund for living costs.
- Aid will not pay a security deposit, first or last month's rent, or summer rent.
- Ask Financial Aid, 203 Day Hall, for your off-campus housing and dining budget before you sign.
```

Moving off campus does not change how Cornell calculates your financial need, and your family contribution stays the same. What changes is the bill: the Bursar stops charging for housing, you pay your landlord directly, and any aid left after your Cornell charges comes back to you as a refund for rent and food ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).

## Does my Cornell financial aid go down if I live off campus?

No. Cornell calculates your need "exactly the same way as if you lived on-campus" ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)). Your need is the gap between Cornell's estimated cost and what your family is expected to pay. Your cost of attendance, the university's full estimate of a year's expenses, does change when you move out, because housing leaves your Cornell bill.

If next year's package is smaller than this year's, our guide to [why a Cornell aid offer comes in lower](/cornell/money/cornell-financial-aid-decreased-why-aid-offer-lower-than/) covers the reasons.

## How does the money reach my landlord?

It goes through you. Off campus, the Bursar, Cornell's billing office, keeps charging tuition, fees and anything else billed through the university. It no longer charges rent. You pay rent, utilities, trash and cable to those companies yourself.

If your aid is larger than your Bursar charges, Cornell sends you the difference as a refund for off-campus living costs. If your charges are larger than your aid, your family contribution covers the gap, along with Cornell Card charges and your off-campus bills.

Cornell's refund schedule does not have to match your landlord's due date. If you plan to pay August or January rent from a refund, ask your landlord in writing, before you sign, whether a late first payment is allowed.

Say your aid totals $85,000 for the year and you are in an endowed college. Cornell's latest posted budget, for 2025-26, shows the swap ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-to-attend)).

## How much does Cornell budget for off-campus rent?

Ask the Office of Financial Aid before you sign a lease. Cornell tells students to find housing that costs less over 12 months than its "off-campus housing and dining budget," but it does not publish that figure ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).

What Cornell does publish is its standard undergraduate budget: for 2025-26, housing at $13,744 and food at $7,604 ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-to-attend)). Both lines assume standard housing. The budget leaves out "premium on-campus or off-campus housing options," and need-based aid does not stretch to cover them. Use the housing line as a yardstick, not as Cornell's off-campus allowance. Spread over a 12-month lease, it is $1,145.33 a month.

A bed in a Collegetown six-bedroom runs a median of $800 a month for 2027-28 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)), which is $4,144 a year under the housing line. A single in a suite at Dryden South, 205 Dryden Road, costs $1,395 per person a month, with heat and hot water included and leases ending May 31, 2027 ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)). That is $2,996 a year over the line. A Collegetown one-bedroom alone, at the $2,025 median, is $10,556 over.

The aid office also tells students to count trash, cable, internet and utilities, which on-campus rates include. ## What won't Cornell financial aid pay for?

Three costs fall outside your aid. Cornell will not pay the costs of securing future housing, which means your security deposit and your first and last month's rent. It will not pay rent for months when you are not a registered student. And the Office of Financial Aid will not step in if you try to break a lease or a campus housing contract mid-year.

The summer rule hits hardest on a 12-month lease. Say you rent the $800 Collegetown bed above ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)) on a lease that starts Aug. 1. Two summer months you are not registered cost $1,600 that aid will not cover. Signing costs another $1,600 for first and last month, plus the deposit.

If you need out of a lease, our guide to [breaking a lease early in Ithaca](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/) covers subletting and your rights under New York law.

```reader "If you're a parent paying the bills"
What changes for you is timing. On campus you pay the Bursar each semester. Off campus, cash goes out at lease signing, months before any refund arrives, and again each summer. See our [guide for parents of off-campus students](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-for-parents/).
```

With the example above, a $9,600 bed leaves $11,748 of the $21,348 housing and food budget for a year of food and utilities before you pay more than on campus. A $16,740 single leaves $4,608. ## How does it work for graduate students?

Cornell's 2026-27 graduate budget for Ithaca sets housing at $19,962 and food at $5,768 a year ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students)). The Graduate School says those allowances cover "comfortable, but modest, housing" ([Cornell Graduate School](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/tuition-rates/cost-of-attendance/)). Unlike the undergraduate off-campus budget, this number is published.

```reader "If you're a graduate student"
Your $19,962 housing allowance is $1,663.50 a month over 12 months. Whether housing shows up on your Bursar bill depends on your university housing plan and program; off campus, you pay the landlord. A Collegetown one-bedroom at the $2,025 median is $24,300, or $4,338 over. Routine transportation, including parking, sits in your personal allowance, not housing.
```

## Can I ask for more aid if Ithaca rent costs more?

Not for the rent alone. Cornell's budget covers standard housing, so a pricier apartment does not raise your aid. If your family's finances have changed, an appeal is the route. Our guide to [Cornell financial aid appeals](/cornell/money/cornell-financial-aid-appeal-success/) explains what the office reviews.

## Who to contact and how to estimate your refund

Before you sign a lease, email finaid@cornell.edu or call (607) 255-5145 and ask for your off-campus housing and dining budget. You can also visit 203 Day Hall, Monday through Thursday, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. and 2 to 4 p.m. ([Cornell Office of Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).

To estimate your refund, take your Bursar statement and subtract the housing and dining you will not buy from Cornell. Compare what remains with your aid. Leave books, personal and travel costs out of that comparison.
