```glance
- Landlords in buildings of four or more units cannot unreasonably refuse a sublet. Elsewhere, your lease decides.
- Send the request by certified mail with six items, including your housemates' written consent.
- The landlord has 10 days to ask questions and a month to answer. Silence counts as consent.
- You stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage. Only an assignment ends that.
```

You need your landlord's written consent to sublet. In a building with four or more units, New York law says the landlord [cannot unreasonably withhold it](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), and silence for a month after your request counts as a yes. In a smaller house, your lease decides.

## Does your landlord have to agree?

Count the residential units in your building first. The count decides what rights you have.

| Your building | Your right to sublet | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| 4 or more residential units | A legal right; consent cannot be unreasonably withheld | Real Property Law 226-b; a lease clause waiving it is void |
| 3 or fewer units | No statutory right | [Your lease](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) |
| Public housing, units with statutory admission criteria, co-op proprietary leases | Excluded from 226-b | Program rules or the proprietary lease |

Source note: [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) and the [Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).

A common belief is that a landlord can always say no. In a building of four or more units, that is wrong. You still have to ask in writing. The right limits how a landlord can refuse. It does not let you skip the request. The [legal explainer](/cornell/housing/can-landlord-refuse-sublet-new-york-ithaca/) covers what counts as an unreasonable refusal.

Cornell's lease checklist lists the [ability to sublet and its conditions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) as terms to read before you sign. Off-Campus Living answers lease questions at 607-255-2310.

## Should you sublet or assign your lease?

A sublet keeps you as the tenant. An assignment hands the whole lease to someone else. The difference is who owes the rent if things go wrong.

| | Sublet | Assignment |
|---|---|---|
| Who stays liable to the landlord | You, for the subtenant's rent and damage | Generally not you |
| Can the landlord refuse? | Not unreasonably, in 4+ unit buildings | Yes, without cause |
| Your remedy if the refusal is unreasonable | Sublet as requested; costs and attorney fees if the landlord acted in bad faith | Release from the lease on 30 days' notice, and nothing more |
| Fits | A summer, a semester abroad, an internship | Leaving Ithaca for good |

Source note: [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) and the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).

If neither works, the third route is a written early-termination agreement with the move-out date and the amount owed. The Cornell Law guide calls it the simplest route. If you leave anyway, the landlord must try in good faith to [re-rent the unit](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) at the lower of market or lease rent. That case has its own page: [breaking an Ithaca lease early](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).

## How do you ask for consent, step by step?


1. **Read the lease.** Find the sublet clause and any listed fee. In a small house this clause is the whole rule.
2. **Get co-tenant and guarantor consent.** The [statute](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) wants their written consent inside the request. On a joint lease, Cornell says each housemate is [responsible for the others' rent and damages](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire), so they have a stake in who moves in. A parent who co-signed is a guarantor and signs too. The [roommates guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) explains joint liability.
3. **Find a subtenant and write the sublease.** Put in the dates, rent, deposit and who pays utilities.
4. **Mail the request.** Use certified mail, return receipt requested. Email is a courtesy copy, not a substitute.
5. **Answer follow-ups fast.** The landlord's answer clock restarts from the date you supply extra information.
6. **Keep the receipt.** It proves when the clock started if the landlord stays silent.

The request must contain six items, per [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B):

- the term of the sublease;
- the subtenant's name and addresses;
- your reason for subletting;
- your address during the sublet;
- the written consent of any co-tenant or guarantor;
- a copy of the proposed sublease.

## What are the 10-day and 30-day clocks?

The landlord can ask for more information [within 10 days](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). The answer is due within 30 days of your request, or of the extra information, whichever is later. A landlord who never answers is deemed to have consented.

Say a junior leaving for a spring semester abroad mails the request on Oct. 1. The landlord can ask for more information until Oct. 11. If the landlord asks on Oct. 8 and the junior replies on Oct. 15, the answer is due by Nov. 14. With no answer by then, the sublet is approved ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). A request mailed in December could still be open when the flight leaves.

The statute does not list reasons a landlord may give for refusing. If you get a refusal, call the Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline at [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) before you sublet anyway.

```reader "If you're going abroad or away for a semester"
Mail the request two to three months before you leave, so both clocks run out while you are still in Ithaca. Put your address abroad in the letter, because the statute asks where you will live during the sublet. Get housemates' written consent first. If no subtenant turns up, your share is still due. A five-month semester at the [Collegetown 4-bedroom median of $1,246.25 per bed for 2027-28](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) is $6,231.25 (5 months at that rate), and your housemates are jointly liable for it.
```

```reader "If you're a senior whose lease runs past graduation"
Most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), so a May graduate pays June and July unless someone takes the room. At the Collegetown median above, that is $2,492.50 (2 months). Mail the request by mid-March so the landlord's clock ends before May. If you are leaving for good, ask about an assignment, which ends your liability, though the landlord can refuse it without cause. Waiting until May means about 40 days of legal clocks eat most of June.
```

```reader "If you're an alum who owns an Ithaca rental"
In a building of four or more units you can ask for more information in the first window and must answer in writing in the second. Silence is consent. Refuse only for a reason you can defend: a tenant facing an unreasonable refusal can sublet anyway and recover costs and attorney fees if you acted in bad faith. A lease clause waiving these rules is void. In a house of three or fewer units, your lease sets the rule.
```

## What does it cost to sublet?

A landlord can charge a sublet fee [only if the lease allows it, and it must be reasonable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). A background or credit check on the subtenant is allowed. State law caps that check at the [lesser of $20 or actual cost](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A), and the landlord must waive it if the applicant brings a check from the past 30 days.

Listing is free. Sublets were [free to list on Cornell's service in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/), when landlords paid $30. The Graduate School calls the marketplace [free to anyone with Cornell credentials](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). No Ithaca landlord publishes a standard sublet fee or a typical summer sublet rent, so ask your landlord for the fee in writing and compare asking prices on the listing sites.

## Where can you list a sublet?

Start with the [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/), where only people who prove Cornell affiliation can view and contact subletters. The Off-Campus Living email list reaches Cornell NetID holders: send a message with the subject "Join" and a blank body, per [Cornell's listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Both Cornell channels screen out most strangers. The [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) covers every channel, and the [summer housing guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) shows what subtenants look for.

## What happens after the subtenant moves in?

You are still the tenant. If the subtenant stops paying, the landlord comes to you, and your claim is against the subtenant. Ithaca City Court hears small claims [up to $5,000](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). Before you leave, Cornell's [tenant checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) says to notify contacts, forward mail, sort out utilities, and clean and secure the apartment. Take dated photos at handover. If the landlord docks your deposit for damage the subtenant caused, the photos are your record, and the [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) explains the 14-day return rule.

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Handing over keys on a verbal "sure."** A sublet without the written steps is a [substantial breach of the lease](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), which puts your deposit and your next reference at risk.
- **Mailing the request too late.** Both clocks plus a follow-up run past six weeks. A request sent in late April can fail to clear before a June 1 start.
- **Skipping a housemate's consent.** The request must include it.
- **Choosing an assignment because it sounds cleaner.** The landlord can refuse it without cause, and your only remedy is release on notice.
- **Paying a sublet fee the lease never mentions.** It is allowed only if the lease lists it.
- **Assuming Cornell will step in.** Cornell Financial Aid [will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in a mid-year lease break.

## FAQ

**I hold a Cornell housing license, not a lease. What do I do?** Cornell's license deadlines apply. For 2026-27, Cornell cancels without penalty for study away, leave or an internship if you ask by [June 15 for fall or Dec. 1 for spring](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027). Later requests cost $500.

**Does a dispute with my landlord affect my visa?** Cornell Graduate School staff say a landlord dispute [does not affect international students' visa status](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/) or standing.

**Who do I call with a lease question?** Off-Campus Living at 607-255-2310. This page is general information, not legal advice.

## What to do next

Count your units, read the sublet clause, collect your housemates' signatures and mail the request. Then run the clock with the tool above.
