The cheapest large buildings are the Ithaca Renting properties on Dryden Road, where beds in big units start at $695 a month for 2027-28. The newest buildings on College Avenue cost far more, and several print no price. The table below puts them side by side ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## Which buildings charge by the bed?

Ithaca Renting runs Plaza, Center, Court, Aces and the Dryden Road houses, and it is the one landlord that posts a full per-bed price list. Its median per bed for 2027-28 is $800 in a 6BR, $1,246.25 in a 4BR, $1,377.50 in a 2BR and $2,025 for a 1BR alone. The bigger the unit, the cheaper the bed ([2027-28 dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)).

Rent in these buildings includes heat, hot water and furniture. Electricity is extra except in the 210 Dryden Rd. shared suites on floors two and three. Parking is a separate full-year license, because Ithaca Renting sells no monthly or semester parking ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The Cornell marketplace lists Plaza surface parking at $2,450 a year ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-plaza-236537)).

The buildings differ in feel. Plaza is a nine-story building described as relatively social and mostly undergraduate ([Ithaca Renting, Plaza](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=270)). Center is pitched to graduate students and "the more mature student," with a concierge desk from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. ([Ithaca Renting, Center](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=461)).

## What do the newer buildings offer?

Dryden South at 205 Dryden Rd. prices by the person. Its ten four-bedroom suites ran $1,360 to $1,575 per person for 2027-28 and were all marked rented. Two studios were still available at $1,990 and $2,190 ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/)).

The Ivy, at College Avenue and Dryden Road, is the priciest. A 3BR at $5,500 to $5,800 works out to $1,833.33 to $1,933.33 per person. Nearly all units were leased before construction finished, and its developers include Nick Robertson and Charlie O'Connor of Modern Living Rentals ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)).

Catherine Commons and Collegetown Terrace both list a price header with no period. Ask for a written per-bed quote before you compare either with a per-bed building. Their listings are on the Cornell marketplace ([Catherine Commons](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/catherine-commons-236692), [Collegetown Terrace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-terrace-236694)).

## Which buildings are already full for 2027-28?

On Nov. 10, 2026, 14 of the 67 rows on Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page were marked sold out, including the 8BR and 14BR houses. The Student Agencies Building shows "All units HAVE BEEN leased" and offers an early-access waitlist for 2027-28 ([The Student Agencies Building](https://www.thestudentagenciesbuilding.com/)). All ten Dryden South suites are rented too. For when to start, read [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
Decide group size before you pick a building. A group of six fits Aces or Plaza at the lowest rents. A group of two or three belongs in Center, Court or Dryden South, where per-person rent runs higher.
```

```reader "If you're a parent comparing with Cornell's bill"
Compare per person per year, and add electricity and parking to every Collegetown quote. The [per-bed price guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/) sets those totals against Cornell's residence hall rates.
```

## What about Cornell's own building in Collegetown?

301 College Ave. is Cornell housing, not a lease. It offers studios to six-bedroom units with kitchenettes, shared laundry, and a housing license through Cornell ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue)). It is open to transfer and upper-level students through Cornell's housing selection.

3. Check each building's certificate before you sign.
