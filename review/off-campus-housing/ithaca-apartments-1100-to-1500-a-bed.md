For $1,100 to $1,500 a month, you can rent a bed in a two- to five-bedroom Collegetown share, a downtown studio, or a small studio or one-bedroom near campus on your own. Over a 12-month lease, that budget comes to $13,200 to $18,000 a year, against $13,744 for a 2026-27 Cornell residence hall double ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What does $1,100 to $1,500 a bed get you in Ithaca?

It buys the middle of the market, above the big group houses and below the new Collegetown towers. It lands in three places:

- **A two- to five-bedroom share in Collegetown**, the dense rental neighborhood at the southwest edge of campus. Most of the listings in this guide are this kind.
- **A studio downtown**, near the Ithaca Commons, about 0.8 to 0.9 miles from campus in a straight line.
- **A studio or small one-bedroom alone**, in older houses on Stewart Avenue, Osmun Place and Fairmount Avenue, or in complexes north of campus.

The Cornell double covers the academic year and includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month (Cornell housing rates). Most Ithaca leases run a full year. Spread over 12 months, the double's $13,744 equals $1,145.33 a month. If you sublet the summer and pay 10 months yourself, the break-even rises to $1,374.40.

## What do these prices leave out?

Most leave out at least one bill that a Cornell double covers.

- **Electricity.** Most Ithaca Renting Collegetown tenants pay it. Downtown, it is included only at the three buildings named above. CSP's 408 Stewart Ave. and 116 Osmun Place and PPM's 308 Fairmount Ave. include it.
- **Internet and trash.** Eddygate charges them as yearly fees. CSP includes them at 408 Stewart Ave., 116 Osmun Place and unit 2A at 400-404 Stewart Ave.
- **Parking.** Ithaca Renting sells parking only as a separate full-year license, with no monthly or semester option. Its lots on the 100 block of Dryden Road, the 300 block of College Avenue and the 700 block of East Buffalo Street hold more than 100 vehicles.
- **The summer.** Ithaca Renting's Collegetown leases run one year and start in June, July or August, on fixed dates for each apartment. Its Collegetown sublets must be registered, cost a $15 fee, and go only to Cornell students.

For a line-by-line monthly budget with power, bus fare and trash tags, see [what a month off campus costs at Cornell](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/).

Signing costs real money up front. In New York, a security deposit plus any rent paid in advance cannot be more than one month's rent ([N.Y. General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). At $1,250 a month, that caps what a landlord can hold at $1,250. ## What if this range doesn't fit?

Below $1,100, the answer is a bigger group. Six-bedroom and ten-bedroom Collegetown shares and rooms in shared suites run $775 to $895 at the median for 2027-28 ([EduExplained Collegetown rent dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)). The trade-offs of each size are in [Collegetown rent prices per bed](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/).

Above $1,500, you reach Collegetown's newest buildings and its studios. At The Ivy, at 325 College Ave., three-bedrooms rented for $5,500 to $5,800 a month for leases that began Aug. 17, 2026 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)). Split three ways, that is $1,833.33 to $1,933.33 a person. Ithaca Renting's cheapest Collegetown studio, at Collegetown Court, starts at $1,565 for 2027-28.

How every way of living in Ithaca compares over a full year, from co-ops to one-bedrooms, is in the [EduExplained housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/).

## FAQ

**Is a per-bed price the same as a per-person price?**
Yes, for one person in one bedroom. Ithaca Renting and Dryden South quote per person. PPM Homes, CSP Management and Travis Hyde quote the whole apartment, so a $4,045 three-bedroom is $1,348.33 a person. Check which one a listing uses before you split it with friends.

**Why do some listings show no lease year?**
Many listings on the Ithaca College and Cornell marketplaces, including the PJ Apartments, Goldenrod and Delaware Ave. listings above, print a price with no lease year or period. Ask the landlord which year the price covers and what it includes before you compare it with a 2027-28 price.

## Before you sign

Decide whether $1,100 to $1,500 is rent alone or rent plus utilities, and how many roommates you want. Cornell advises seeing a place in person before you sign ([Cornell rental listings](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)), and read the whole lease before you pay anything.
