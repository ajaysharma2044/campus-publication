```glance
- Ask your landlord in writing before anyone moves in. With four or more units, consent can't be unreasonably refused.
- You stay liable for the rent even after a subtenant moves in.
- Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace lists sublets and checks that viewers are affiliated with Cornell.
- A Cornell residence hall license cannot be sublet at all.
```

If you rent off campus and go abroad for a semester, you can sublet your room, but only with your landlord's written consent, and you stay responsible for the rent. In a building of four or more units, the landlord cannot unreasonably refuse ([Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).

## Can you sublet while you are abroad?

Yes, if your lease and the law allow it. A sublet means you rent your space to someone else for part of your lease while the lease stays in your name. In New York, the right to sublet belongs to tenants in buildings with four or more residential units. In a building with three or fewer units, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease), so read the sublet clause before you promise anyone a room.

Cornell's own advice is blunt. Most leases require landlord approval to sublet, and the original tenant can remain responsible ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus)). That is the part students miss. A subtenant who stops paying is your problem first.

If you live in a Cornell residence hall, none of this applies. The 2027-28 housing license says only the student and Cornell-assigned roommates can live in the room, and it bars any attempt to assign or transfer it ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

## How do you ask your landlord for permission?

Send a written request by certified mail with a return receipt. It must list the dates of the sublet, the subtenant's name and addresses, your reason, your own address while you are away, and a copy of the proposed sublease. If a co-tenant or guarantor must consent, include that too ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).

Then two clocks start. The landlord has 10 days to ask for more information. The landlord must answer within 30 days of the later of your request or the extra information. If the landlord says nothing, [the law treats silence as consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B).

## When should you start looking for a subtenant?

Start as soon as Education Abroad accepts you. For Fall 2027 and the 2027-28 year, applications opened Nov. 10, the first-round deadline is Jan. 7 and decisions come Jan. 29. For Spring 2028, the first-round deadline is June 1 and decisions come July 13 ([Cornell Education Abroad](https://abroad.globallearning.cornell.edu/apply/deadlines)).

Timing matters because the pool of subtenants shifts by semester. Cornell Abroad's director told The Cornell Daily Sun in 2016 that most students go abroad in spring, and that some students drop out of study abroad when they cannot find a subletter ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/20/students-housing-search-hampers-study-abroad)). If you are leaving for spring, you are competing with many others for the same fall-returning students.

| Your semester abroad | Application decision | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2027 or the full 2027-28 year | Jan. 29 (first round) | Mail the landlord request soon after acceptance |
| Spring 2028 | July 13 (first round) | Draft the sublet request over the summer |

Source: Cornell Education Abroad deadlines.

## Where do you find a subtenant?

Use the channels that verify Cornell ties. Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace, run through College Pads, lets only people who prove Cornell affiliation view and contact landlords and subletters. The Off-Campus Living email list also carries sublets and roommate posts, and it requires a NetID ([Cornell Rental Listings & Resources](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)).

Some landlords handle it for you, and their rules can be stricter than the law. Ithaca Renting's Collegetown sublets must be [registered and approved, go to Cornell students only, and run one per term](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), with no Airbnb and no stays under a month. Its [downtown policy](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) sets a two-month minimum, requires every named tenant to agree, and says a sublet does not relieve you of lease obligations.

## What should the sublease say?

Put it in writing. Cornell's tenant page tells you to get a written sublease, and the Cornell Law guide says that an unpaid subtenant can be pursued for breach of contract in small claims, city, town or supreme court ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). A written agreement gives you something to point to. A promise over text does not.

Cover these points:

1. The exact start and end dates, and who pays for utilities.
2. The monthly rent and the day it is due, with payment sent to you or the landlord as the landlord directs.
3. Who holds any deposit, and who is responsible for damage.
4. The rule for guests, keys and moving out.

A landlord can charge a sublet fee only if the lease allows it and the fee is reasonable ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). Ithaca Renting lists its Collegetown sublet fee as $15 on one line and a $20 fee elsewhere on the same page, so ask which applies before you pay.

## What can go wrong?

Three problems come up most.

- **The subtenant stops paying.** You owe the landlord. Collect rent up front when you can, and use a written sublease so you can pursue the money later.
- **The landlord says nothing.** In a covered building, silence after the deadline counts as consent, but keep your certified mail receipt as proof.
- **You skip the request.** A sublet without the required steps is a substantial breach of the lease ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).

Cornell's list of questions for housemates asks what happens if a housemate takes leave, transfers or studies abroad. It gives no answer ([Cornell Housemate Questionnaire](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)). Settle it with your housemates before you sign the lease, not after you are accepted abroad.

```reader "If you're on a joint lease with housemates"
Everyone on the lease is on the hook for the whole rent, so your housemates need to agree to the sublet in writing, and the landlord will often ask for that too. See [roommates and joint liability](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) for how that responsibility works.
```

```reader "If you haven't signed your next lease yet"
Ask about sublets before you sign. The list of lease terms Cornell tells you to check includes the ability to sublet and its conditions. [When to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) covers timing, and a lease that starts after you return avoids the problem entirely.
```

```reader "If you'd rather leave the lease"
If you will not return to the apartment at all, an assignment can serve you better than a sublet, because it hands the whole lease to someone else. The Cornell Law guide generally prefers assigning in that case. See [breaking a lease early](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).
```

## What to do next

Check your lease for the sublet clause and count the units in your building. Mail the certified request as soon as you have a subtenant and dates.
