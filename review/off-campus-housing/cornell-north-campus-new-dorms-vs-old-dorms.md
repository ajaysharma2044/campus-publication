```glance
- Two North Campus halls pair suites with air conditioning: Ganędagǫ: and Toni Morrison, both opened in 2021.
- Three newer halls from 2022 are corridor-style: McClintock, Hu Shih and Ruth Bader Ginsburg.
- All residence halls share one rate table, so a double costs $13,744 a year in any of them.
- First-years cannot rank halls; Cornell assigns them by random auto-allocation.
```

Cornell's North Campus has two kinds of newer hall and many older ones. Ganędagǫ: and Toni Morrison are suite-style with air conditioning, and the 2022 halls are corridors. The older halls are corridors too, mostly without air conditioning. Room rates do not change by building, so the choice is about layout and comfort, not price ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is the difference between a suite and a corridor?

A corridor hall has rooms along a hallway and shared bathrooms. A suite groups several bedrooms around a shared space. Cornell lists the layouts by hall ([Cornell residence hall configurations](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/residence-hall-configurations)):

- **Suite:** Ganędagǫ:, Toni Morrison, Language House and Anna Comstock Hall.
- **Apartment:** the Townhouse Apartments.
- **Corridor:** Akwe:kon, Balch, McClintock, Clara Dickson, Court-Kay-Bauer, High Rise 5, Hu Shih, Jameson, Loving House, Low Rises 6 to 10, Mary Donlon, Mews, McLLU, Risley and Ruth Bader Ginsburg. Ecology House is a corridor with private baths.

Newer does not mean suite. Toni Morrison and Ganędagǫ: opened in 2021 with 800 beds, and McClintock, Hu Shih and Ruth Bader Ginsburg added 1,200 beds for fall 2022, all part of a 6.5-year North Campus project ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2022/08/cornell-welcomes-students-marks-milestone-residential-life)).

## Which North Campus halls have air conditioning?

Five buildings have room or temperature control, and many do not. Cornell's hall pages show the following:

| Group | Halls |
|---|---|
| Air conditioning or temperature control | Toni Morrison, Ganędagǫ:, Balch (thermostat in each room), Townhouses (manual), Ecology House (manual) |
| Air filtration, air conditioning not stated | Court-Kay-Bauer, Mews |
| Not stated | McClintock, Hu Shih, Ruth Bader Ginsburg |
| No air conditioning | Risley, Comstock, Dickson, Donlon (lounge and library cooled), Low Rises 6 to 10, Jameson, High Rise 5, Akwe:kon |

Source: Cornell hall pages, such as [Mary Donlon Hall](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates/residence-halls/mary-donlon-hall), as of Nov. 10, 2026.

At least 2,384 of the 5,659-plus listed residents, about 42%, live in buildings with no room air conditioning, a sum of the resident counts on [Cornell's hall pages](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates-campus-housing). For the three 2022 halls, the pages do not say, so ask Cornell Housing at housing@cornell.edu or 607-255-5368 before you count on it.

## How big are the halls?

Size shapes the feel. Ganędagǫ: holds 564 residents and Toni Morrison 306. Among the 2022 halls, Ruth Bader Ginsburg holds 504, Hu Shih 429 and McClintock 311. The older halls range from Risley with 194 (opened 1913) and Balch with 230-plus (1929) to Clara Dickson with 460-plus (1946) and Mary Donlon with 450-plus (1961) ([Cornell housing pages](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates-campus-housing)).

Balch deserves a second look. It closed for more than two years for its first full renovation in six decades. North Balch reopened in August 2024, with an elevator in each of four units, new windows, HVAC vents and a thermostat in each room for the first time ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/peek-inside-balch-hall-renovations-near-completion)).

## What do residents say?

Early residents of the two 2021 halls liked the spacious rooms, hardwood floors, air conditioning and heating. They reported flooding shower drains and heavy, always-shut suite doors that made the halls less social, and all said the positives outweighed the negatives ([The Cornell Daily Sun, Oct. 13, 2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/from-leaky-showers-to-lonely-suites-student-residents-voice-concerns-about-new-north-campus-dorms/)). That is five years old. The fact set holds no newer resident reporting, so ask a current resident before you rank a hall.

## Can you choose between them?

The answer changes with your year. A first-year cannot. Unless you apply to a program house, your hall comes from random auto-allocation, and students do not rank halls ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)). Our [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) lists the deadlines.

A sophomore can. The upper-level options on North Campus are Balch, Clara Dickson's north wing, Ganędagǫ:, Toni Morrison and the Townhouse Apartments, and Dickson is half first-years and half sophomores ([Cornell upper-level housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduate-campus-housing)). Our [sophomore start page](/start/sophomore/) covers the 2027-28 selection steps, and the [residential requirement guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) covers leaving campus.

## Does a newer hall cost more?

No. Residence halls, program houses and West Campus Houses share one rate table. A double costs $13,744 a year, a single $15,566, a triple $12,644 and a super single $16,595. A Sun column argued in April 2026 that all dorms should keep costing the same and that Cornell should narrow the gap between single, double and triple rates ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/unequal-vs-inequitable-housing-why-all-dorms-should-cost-the-same)).

## Which halls stay open over winter break?

Several newer halls stay open. For 2026-27 the list includes Ganędagǫ:, High Rise 5, HILC, Hu Shih, Loving House, Mews, Toni Morrison and the Townhouses. HILC and Loving House stay open free, and the others charge $125 per week or $500 for the full break, with applications from Oct. 1 to Dec. 1 ([Cornell winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). The corridor halls Balch, Dickson and Donlon are not on that list. Our guide to [staying in Ithaca over breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) covers food and transport.

## What about move-in?

First-years moved in on Mon., Aug. 17, 2026, with transfers on Tue., Aug. 18 and upper-level students on Aug. 21 and 22. Ganędagǫ: residents unload at the Robert Purcell Community Center lot; the pages the fact set drew on do not name an unloading zone for Toni Morrison, so check the Move-In Ticket, which shows your assigned parking area ([Cornell move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)). Our [move-in day guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) has the full plan.

```reader "If you're a sophomore"
Ranking is real for you, so decide between suite and corridor. If you want air conditioning and a suite, Ganędagǫ: and Toni Morrison are your two options on North Campus. If you want a renovated corridor with a thermostat in each room, rank Balch.
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
Rate and meal plan rules do not change with the hall. The differences are air conditioning, layout and winter break access, so ask your student which of those matters most.
```
