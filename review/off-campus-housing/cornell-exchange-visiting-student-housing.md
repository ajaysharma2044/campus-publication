- A deposit plus prepaid rent is capped at one month's rent.

If you are at Cornell for one semester, look for a furnished unit offered as a sublet or a semester-only row, because a standard Ithaca lease runs 12 months. Ithaca Renting lists [semester rows for spring and fall 2027](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), and Cornell's marketplace lists sublets to people who prove Cornell affiliation.

## Can exchange students live in Cornell housing?

Cornell does not post whether exchange or visiting undergraduates can get a residence hall room. Ask the exchange coordinator at your home school and Housing Operations at 607-255-5368 before you search the open market. If you are a scholar or postdoc, the [scholars housing guide](/cornell/housing/visiting-scholar-postdoc-furnished-housing-ithaca/) covers the International Scholar House and Hasbrouck options, which are for scholars.

## Where do I find a one-semester rental?

Start with Cornell's own channels. The Off-Campus Housing Marketplace shows listings and subletters only to people who [prove Cornell affiliation](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). The Off-Campus Living email list covers housing, roommates and sublets and needs a NetID. Admitted students without one forward the acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu.

Then check these:

- **Semester rows.** Ithaca Renting tags some Collegetown rows as spring or fall semester 2027 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Furnished units and heat and hot water come with its properties.
- **Sublets.** Modern Living Rentals advertises sublet and roommate openings from $900 per person, without naming the period ([Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/)). Ask which months the price covers.
- **Furnished suites.** Dryden South at 205 Dryden Road lists furnished suites from $1,395 per person a month on 12-month leases that end May 31, 2027 ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)).

For the sublet route itself, the [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers the summer market and the [sublet how-to](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) walks through the steps.

## What can a landlord charge a short-stay renter?

The same limits apply to you as to anyone. Under [General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), a security deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent. A landlord who asks you to prepay the whole semester is asking for more than the cap. Cornell International Services also says you do not need an SSN to rent ([Cornell Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students)). ## What if my stay is shorter than the lease?

You have three routes, and the first costs the least. In the simplest, you agree an early end in writing with the landlord, with the move-out date and any money owed. Termination fees can be treated as liquidated damages, which means a pre-set charge a court can refuse to enforce ([Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

The second is a sublet. In a building with four or more units, you can sublet with the landlord's written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. You send the request by certified mail, the landlord has 10 days to ask for more information and 30 days to answer, and silence counts as consent ([Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). You stay liable for the rent. In buildings with three or fewer units, the lease controls. Track the clocks here:

The third is leaving. If you go early, the landlord must in good faith take reasonable steps to re-rent the unit, and a new lease offsets what you owe ([Real Property Law 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E)). The [early exit guide](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/) has the full picture.

## What to do next

1. Ask your exchange coordinator whether Cornell housing is open to you.
2. Search the Cornell marketplace and Ithaca Renting for semester-only rows.
3. Get the end date, deposit and sublet terms in writing before you pay.
