Cornell's eight co-ops are small houses where residents run the household themselves. Members cook together, elect officers and keep the house going, and they pay $3,487 to $4,981 per semester for a double in 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is a Cornell co-op?

A co-op is housing that the residents help operate. Cornell owns eight: Prospect of Whitby, 660 Stewart, 302 Wait Avenue, Triphammer Cooperative, Redbud in Von Cramm Hall, Wait Terrace, Wari Cooperative and Watermargin. They count as second-year housing under the residential requirement, so a sophomore can meet it in a co-op. Houses range from 10 to 35 members, and the eight together hold 158 residents ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). The [co-ops and Greek houses guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/) compares all eight, and the [exemptions guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) explains how a co-op satisfies the rule.

## How are co-ops governed?

Residents govern the house. There is no live-in staff. Officers and liaisons are elected, and an Inter-Cooperative Union has one representative from each house ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/11/open-house-day-highlights-unique-qualities-of-cornell-co-ops/)). Julie Paige, the Director of Off-Campus, Cooperative, and Graduate Living, runs the program, and you can reach the co-op office at co-ops@cornell.edu or 607-255-3445 (Cornell Student & Campus Life).

Cornell sets the money rules. It required co-ops to standardize room rates within and across houses, so Triphammer, which once priced by room size, now follows Cornell's table ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/)).

## What about meals and cooking?

Four houses run a house meal plan: Whitby, Triphammer, Watermargin and 660 Stewart (Cornell Student & Campus Life). Sources disagree on a fifth, so check a house page before counting on a plan. Each house works differently:

- **Triphammer** has 20 residents and mandatory vegetarian dinners Sunday through Thursday. Residents take one cook group a week ([Triphammer page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/triphammer-cooperative)). Cook groups there have three or four members ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/triphammer-co-op-fosters-community-and-growth-cornellians)).
- **Redbud** has 34 residents, the most, and serves nightly shared dinners ([Redbud page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/redbud-cooperative-von-cramm-hall)).
- **660 Stewart** has 25 residents and an optional house meal plan five nights a week with one cooking night, plus a mandatory staples program ([660 Stewart page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/660-stewart-avenue)).
- **Prospect of Whitby** has 19 residents and an all-vegetarian house meal plan ([Whitby page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/prospect-whitby)).
- **Wait Avenue Cooperative** has 14 residents and no house meal plan, but staples are provided ([Wait Avenue page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/wait-avenue-cooperative)).

Cornell's pages do not publish chore schedules. Ask the house during the mosey what the weekly work is, and write down who does what before you accept a room.

## How do you join a co-op?

You go through a "mosey," the co-op word for the selection process, a term suggested as an alternative to "rush." Applicants attend events and a cook group, and the final pick is by vote or lottery. Selection starts in early November and early February, and each house runs its own. In 2023-24, the fall mosey ran from an open house on Nov. 4, 2023 to decisions on Nov. 29, and the spring one ended with decisions on Feb. 28, 2024 ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/news-events/news/co-op-mosey-2024)). A 2023 Sun report said the lottery is weighted by financial need and diversity factors ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/06/underserved-and-minority-students-find-belonging-and-support-in-co-op-housing/)).

<!-- ad-break -->

Rising sophomores who plan to join a co-op must affirm that plan by March 15, or they complete the requirement on campus. A Cornell license applies: it is a revocable license, not a lease, running from 9 a.m. Aug. 21, 2026 to 2 p.m. May 23, 2027. You must be registered full time, at least 12 credit hours, and there is no security or key deposit ([Cornell terms and conditions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/co-op-housing-2026-2027-terms-and-conditions)).

Some houses take graduate students: Wait Avenue Cooperative, Wait Terrace, Triphammer, Watermargin and Redbud list them as eligible. Prospect of Whitby, Wari and 660 Stewart do not ([Redbud page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/redbud-cooperative-von-cramm-hall)).

## What should you watch for?

The houses are old. The Sun reported in 2024 that four of the eight co-ops had floors collapse in recent years, and that Watermargin needs about $500,000 for a roof, or $21,739.13 per resident across its 23 residents (500,000 / 23) ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/)).
