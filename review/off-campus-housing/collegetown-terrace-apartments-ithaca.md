Collegetown Terrace is a furnished apartment and townhome complex at 115 S. Quarry St. where you sign a lease for your own bed, not the whole unit. Its website lists rates starting at $605 but gives no period, so ask for a written quote before you compare it with other buildings ([Collegetown Terrace](https://www.collegetownterrace.com/)).

## What is Collegetown Terrace, and who is it for?

It is a residential complex of studios and 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments and townhomes. The site shows the logo of The Scion Group, but the Cornell marketplace listing does not name an owner or manager. Because leases are per person and roommate matching is available, it suits a student who wants a private bedroom without finding a full group first.

## How far is Collegetown Terrace from campus?

The marketplace listing puts it 0.6 miles from campus and shows a 19-minute commute. That is farther than the Dryden Road buildings. A 2012 Sun story described Collegetown Terrace's rents of $1,000 to $1,200 as being far from campus ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2012/10/01/as-collegetown-housing-rush-intensifies-students-sleep-outside-rental-agency/)). ## What does it cost?

Collegetown Terrace does not post a price table. The header range tops out at $2,265, and each plan is marked "Call For Price." A Sun opinion piece in 2022 reported studios at up to $1,795 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/)). That figure is four years old, so do not use it as a current price.

To judge a quote, compare it per bed with the buildings in the [Collegetown rent-per-bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/) and in [Collegetown rent prices per bed](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/). Ask which utilities the rent covers, because the listing says only that utilities are included.

## How do you get a spot?

New applicants can lock in a rate by Nov. 15, 2026 ([Collegetown Terrace](https://www.collegetownterrace.com/)). The site gives no lease start or end dates, so ask the leasing office, at 607-367-6933 as listed on the Cornell marketplace, for the dates before you apply. Collegetown's popular units go early, and one developer's Ivy building leased nearly every unit before it was built ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)).

## What should you watch for?

On a per-person lease, ask whether you are responsible only for your own bed's rent if a roommate leaves. Read the whole lease first: the [guide to reading an Ithaca lease](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) explains the clauses, and the [landlord reviews guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-landlords-to-avoid-collegetown-reviews/) covers what tenants report in Collegetown.
