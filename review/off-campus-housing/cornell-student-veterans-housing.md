Student veterans do not have to live on campus. Cornell exempts anyone who served in active military, naval or air service and was discharged under conditions other than dishonorable. You must [request the exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) in the Housing application before you sign a Cornell license, and it applies to one academic year at a time.

## Who counts as a veteran for the exemption?

Cornell's policy covers service in active military, naval or air service with a discharge other than dishonorable. It includes international students who served in their home country's military. It does not apply to ROTC participants (Cornell Student & Campus Life).

Cornell says supporting documentation can be required, so upload your discharge paperwork with the request or ask Housing Operations what it wants first. The [exemptions guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) lists every exemption, and the [off-campus quiz](/tools/can-i-live-off-campus-cornell-quiz/) checks your case.

## What is Veterans House?

Veterans House is a program house, a themed residence for undergraduates with a shared interest or background. It opened in fall 2021 at 625 University Avenue, a former Alpha Tau Omega fraternity house, with 26 undergraduates, 20 of them student veterans ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2021/09/new-program-house-opens-creating-community-among-student-veterans)). Cornell lists it with 26 residents, including one resident adviser.

Beginning in 2027, Veterans House will be based at [112 Edgemoor Lane](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/112-edgemoor), a building that houses 20 residents in all-single rooms with shared single-sex bathrooms. Cornell's 2027-28 selection page also lists Veterans House at 112 Edgemoor Lane. Confirm the address on your license before you plan a commute.

The rooms are super singles, and Cornell's configuration page says window air conditioning is available on request ([Cornell program houses](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/loving-house)). Veterans House has 12-month occupancy, so you keep your room over summer and winter break.

## How do I apply to Veterans House?

Veterans House needs an additional application beyond the main Housing application. For 2027-28 selection, rising juniors and seniors applied Sept. 1 to Sept. 27, 2026, and priority program house selection ran on Oct. 1, 2026 ([Cornell fall selection page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)). Program house beds can run out in later stages, so apply in the first window.

## What does Veterans House cost?

Room price follows room type. A super single costs $16,595 for 2026-27, or $8,298 per semester ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). With the cheapest allowed meal plan, the Collegetown plan at $7,056, and the $50 annual fee, the year comes to $23,701 ($16,595 + $7,056 + $50). Compare that with a bed off campus before you commit; the [off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/) maps the market.

```reader "If you're a veteran with a spouse or children"
You are exempt as a student with legal dependents as well as a veteran. Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments hold family units at $1,475 to $1,530 a month on 12-month contracts, and they sit under graduate and professional housing. Cornell's pages do not say whether an undergraduate family may apply, so ask Housing Operations. See the [family housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-student-family-housing-ithaca/).
```

## Can I stay in Ithaca over breaks?

Yes, if your housing stays open. Veterans House is on Cornell's winter break list for 2026-27, and it is included in the license ([Cornell winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). Cascadilla Hall is also open. The [breaks guide](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) lists dining and transit during closures.

## What if I am called to active duty during a lease?

A tenant can end a lease by written notice after active duty starts. The termination takes effect 30 days after the next rent due date ([Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). Give written notice with a copy of your orders, and keep a dated copy. Housing Operations at 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, handles exemptions and licenses.

## What to do next

1. Gather your discharge paperwork and request the exemption in the Housing application.
2. Decide between Veterans House and an apartment using the totals above.
3. Apply to Veterans House in the first fall window if you want a bed.
