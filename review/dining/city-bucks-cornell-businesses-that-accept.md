City Bucks are a balance on your Cornell ID that pays for food at 12 businesses off campus, including 7-Eleven, D.P. Dough, Wings Over Ithaca, Tops and P&C Fresh ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). The table below gives every address and phone number.

Three sit in Collegetown: 7-Eleven and D.P. Dough on College Avenue, and Wings Over Ithaca on Dryden Road. Monks on the Commons is downtown. The rest are a bus ride away, along Elmira Road, Pine Tree Road, South Meadow Street and North Triphammer Road.

Ithacatogo.com, a delivery service, also takes City Bucks ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). Its site lists hours of 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. every day but gives no delivery fee or order minimum ([Ithaca To Go](https://ithacatogo.com/)), so check the total at checkout.

## What is not on the list?

Wegmans and Trader Joe's are not on Cornell's list ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). Tops and P&C Fresh are the two grocery stores that take City Bucks, so a student without a car who wants groceries on this balance has two options. GreenStar's Collegetown store at 307 College Ave appears in some Cornell material but not on the 12-business list, so ask City Bucks at citybucks@cornell.edu before you spend there. Hours and menus are not part of Cornell's list, so call the business before a long trip.

City Bucks do not work on campus. For campus food, the balance to use is Big Red Bucks, which the [off-campus plan guide](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) compares with cooking.

## How do City Bucks work?

Every registered student has an account, and so does every faculty or staff MealChoice holder. You turn it on by adding money at get.cbord.com/cornell ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). Scan your Cornell ID at the business, and the balance pays. Four rules matter.

- **Tax.** Sales tax applies to every purchase. Adults 21 and over can buy alcohol, and tobacco and lottery tickets are not allowed.
- **Whole purchase.** A purchase is declined unless your balance covers all of it ([Cornell Dining FAQ](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks/city-bucks-faqs)).
- **Deposit limit.** Bursar billing for City Bucks is disabled if deposits top $500 in the last 30 days.
- **Disputes.** Take a charge problem to the business within two weeks, with your receipt.

Your balance carries from semester to semester while you are registered. Graduating seniors should spend it or visit the Dining Office before the last day of spring classes. Balances of $35.00 or more are refunded. The Collegetown Meal Plan includes $436 in City Bucks per semester ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## Can I use City Bucks over winter break?

Yes, and it matters. Cornell Dining is shut from Dec. 25, 2026, to Jan. 1, 2027, and Cornell says to use City Bucks off campus or for delivery during that stretch. You can add money while the dining system is closed ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining)). The wider break picture is in [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).
