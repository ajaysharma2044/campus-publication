```glance
- Door prices include tax: breakfast, lunch, dinner and Sunday brunch each have a posted rate.
- Paying in Big Red Bucks lowers every price, and BRBs carry no sales tax.
- Off-Campus Value works out to $17.50 a meal, so it beats the door only at dinner or brunch.
- Anyone can eat in a dining room, with BRBs, CornellCard or a credit card.
```

In a Cornell dining room, a walk-in pays $11.90 for breakfast, $16.80 for lunch Monday to Saturday, $20.80 for dinner and $18.55 for Sunday brunch, tax included. Big Red Bucks (BRBs) lower each price ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

## How much does a dining-room meal cost without a plan?

| Meal | Door price, tax included | Price in BRBs | Staff (MealChoice) | Children |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $11.90 | $10.80 | $10.70 | $5.95 |
| Continental breakfast at the Houses | $8.55 | $7.75 | $7.70 | $4.30 |
| Lunch, Monday to Saturday | $16.80 | $15.25 | $9.50 Mon-Fri; $15.10 Sat | $8.40 |
| Dinner | $20.80 | $18.85 | $18.70 | $10.40 |
| Sunday brunch | $18.55 | $16.85 | $16.70 | $9.30 |

Source: Cornell Dining meal plans and rates, 2026-27.

Big Red Bucks are a dollar-for-dollar balance on your ID card. They carry no sales tax, while a credit card, CornellCard or MealChoice purchase adds 8% New York State sales tax ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/january-morrison-dining-reduced)). That is why the door price runs higher than the BRB price.

## Who can eat without a plan?

Everyone. Cornell lets anyone eat in a residential dining room and accept a meal swipe, BRBs, CornellCard or a credit card, plus MealChoice for staff ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). Kosher holiday meals and some unit specials cost more, and the five West Campus House dining rooms hold House Dinners for House members on Wednesdays from 6 to 7 p.m. Shabbat, holiday and special meals at 104West! carry extra charges, and reservations go through kosher.scl.cornell.edu ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

The GET app can show a barcode in place of your ID at the door checker. Guests who carry cash can use the cash-to-card kiosk in the Statler Hotel ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/contactless-payment)).

Cafes and food courts price by the item. A February 2025 Sun column put Trillium entrees at about $7 to $14 ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus)).

## When is a plan cheaper than paying at the door?

Only when you eat enough of the meals the plan covers. Here are two weekly habits at the door prices above, over an 18-week term ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

- Five lunches a week: 5 × $16.80 × 18 = $1,512.00.
- Three dinners a week: 3 × $20.80 × 18 = $1,123.20.

Now take Off-Campus Value, the entry plan for students who live off campus. It costs $912.30 a semester for 45 meals plus $150 in BRBs ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Subtract the BRBs, add $25 for half the yearly $50 fee, and divide by 45 meals: ($912.30 − $150 + $25) ÷ 45 = $17.50 a meal. That is more than a door lunch or breakfast, and less than a door brunch or dinner. So Off-Campus Value pays off if most of your 45 meals are dinners or brunches, and not otherwise.

Bear Traditional works out to $12.51 a meal if you use every one of its 14 weekly swipes, but it is not open to every student. The full break-even math, plan by plan, is in [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/). If you would rather cook, compare in [meal plan or cook yourself](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/).

Cornell's own food estimate for undergraduates is $7,604 a year, billed by Cornell, and its cost-of-attendance page gives no separate off-campus food figure ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend)). For a full monthly budget, see [Cornell cost of living: student monthly budget](/cornell/money/cornell-cost-of-living-ithaca-student-monthly-budget/).

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar or postdoc"
Staff and faculty can hold MealChoice, which gives a $9.50 weekday lunch, a free 11th lunch on a punch card and 10% off breakfast and dinner in dining rooms and off cafe purchases ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/staff-faculty-meal-plans)). Ask Cornell Dining whether your appointment qualifies. If your housing has a kitchen, see [furnished housing for visiting scholars and postdocs](/cornell/housing/visiting-scholar-postdoc-furnished-housing-ithaca/).
```

```reader "If you're a graduate student"
The Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan costs $50 a semester, returns it as BRBs and adds a 5% discount at four cafes, with no admin fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)). Pay door prices in dining rooms.
```

## What to do next

Add up how many dinners and brunches you eat in a dining room each week. If you eat few dinners and brunches there, the door price costs less than Off-Campus Value. Check the current prices on the [Cornell Dining rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates) before the semester starts.
