The easiest halal meal at Cornell is at Morrison Dining on North Campus, where a dedicated halal and kosher station runs at lunch and dinner every day ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). Keeton House on West Campus serves halal on request, and 104West! also serves it. Cornell also lists a halal food truck.

## Where can I eat halal on campus?

Cornell's halal page names three on-campus routes, and the special diets page adds a fourth. Holidays can change service, so check Dining Now before a trip.

| Place | What Cornell lists | When |
|---|---|---|
| Morrison Dining, North Campus | Dedicated halal and kosher station | Lunch and dinner, every day |
| Keeton House Dining Room, West Campus | Halal on request | Dinner seven days a week; lunch and brunch Saturday and Sunday |
| Willard Straight Hall | Take Us Home items that are halal certified | Pre-order through the GET app |
| 104West! | Serves halal, Star K kosher and Seventh-day Adventist diets | Check Dining Now |

Source: Cornell Dining halal resources and food allergies and special diets pages.

Morrison and Keeton are two of Cornell's ten residential dining rooms, so a swipe covers a meal at either ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). Take Us Home is a grab-and-go counter, and you pre-order through GET Order at get.cbord.com/cornell or the CBORD GET app, with no fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/order-ahead)).

Dining rooms also carry labels for pork and alcohol, which helps when you are reading an unfamiliar dish ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/halal-resources)). The Dining Nutrition Office, at 108 Appel Commons, takes questions at (607) 255-9280 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

## What does Cornell do during Ramadan?

Cornell's halal page lists extended hours, take-out containers and pre-fast boxes for Ramadan, and it links to the 2026 details ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/halal-resources)). The page does not give dates or locations for the next Ramadan, so call the Dining Nutrition Office at (607) 255-9280 a few weeks ahead to learn which rooms will run extended hours.

## Where can I eat halal off campus?

Halal Brothers, a Mediterranean food truck, is on Cornell's approved food truck list as of May 2026 ([Cornell EHS](https://ehs.cornell.edu/campus-health-safety/event-management/approved-food-truck-and-trailer-vendors)). A March 2025 Sun columnist recommended it and wrote that it stays open to 2 a.m. on weekends and 10 p.m. on weekdays ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)). Those hours are the writer's claim, and Cornell does not post the truck's location, so check where it is parked before a late trip.

A September 2025 Sun column on late-night food listed a halal chain among its wishes for Collegetown ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/rgcm36ewemrx)). That tells you the late-night options are thin. Plan your late meal before the dining rooms close.

Cornell's pages do not list halal grocery stores or halal butchers in Ithaca. If you cook, call the stores you plan to use and ask about halal meat before you count on them. For prices and stores, read [cheap groceries in Ithaca](/cornell/money/cheap-groceries-ithaca-ny-students/).

## What should I ask Cornell Dining?

Ask four things before you commit to a plan. Which dining room is closest to your hall? Is halal available at the meals you eat most? How does the kitchen keep halal food apart from pork? What changes during Ramadan? The Dining Nutrition Office is the right place for all four. The pages cited here do not describe how the kitchen keeps halal and pork apart, so ask the office directly.

## What to do next

Look at the Morrison and Keeton pages on [Dining Now](https://now.dining.cornell.edu/), call (607) 255-9280 with your questions, and eat once at the Morrison station before you sign up for a plan.
