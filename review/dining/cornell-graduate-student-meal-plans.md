```glance
- Four full plans are open to graduate students, from $2,489 to $3,802 per semester for 2026-27.
- Bear Basic beats a door lunch only if you use about 6.7 of its 7 weekly meals.
- If you cook at home, take the grad plan and use City Bucks at Tops or P&C Fresh.
```

Most graduate students should take the $50 Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan, which has no fee and gives a 5% discount at four cafes ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)). A full plan beats paying at the door only if you eat nearly every meal in a dining room.

## Which meal plans can graduate students buy?

Two kinds are open to you: the grad plan and the four full plans ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)). Here is how they compare for 2026-27.

| Plan | Price per semester | Meals | Big Red Bucks | Admin fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan | $50 | None | $50 | None |
| Bear Basic | $2,489 | Up to 7 a week | $500 | $50 a year |
| Bear Choice | $3,075 | Up to 10 a week | $500 | $50 a year |
| Bear Traditional | $3,528 | Up to 14 a week | $400 | $50 a year |
| Unlimited | $3,802 | All meals in service periods | $400 | $50 a year |

Source: [Cornell Dining graduate plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans) and [undergraduate plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), 2026-27. The yearly fee is not refundable and does not apply to the graduate plan.

Big Red Bucks (BRBs) are a dollar-for-dollar balance on your ID card. They are tax-free and buy food at Cornell Dining locations. A swipe is one meal in a residential dining room, the halls where one swipe buys a full meal.

## What does the $50 graduate plan give you?

You pay a flat amount each semester and get the same amount back in BRBs, so you are prepaying food you would buy anyway. The plan adds a 5% discount at Atrium Cafe, Big Red Barn, Bear Necessities and the Veterinary Medicine Cafe, and it has no administrative fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)). Spend $200 at those four cafes and the discount saves $10.00.

BRBs are not refundable, so buy only what you will spend. Cornell's pages disagree on the last day to use them: the graduate page says May 31, 2027, and the undergraduate page says May 27. Spend yours by the earlier date ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

Big Red Barn is the cafe in the graduate and professional community center, and the Sun described it as popular with graduate students, with hot-pressed sandwiches and soups ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/)). Atrium Cafe is in Sage Hall and the Veterinary Medicine Cafe is in Schurman Hall ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/cafes-food-courts-coffeehouses)).

## When does a full plan beat the door price?

Rarely, and the math shows why. A walk-in pays $16.80 for lunch Monday to Saturday and $20.80 for dinner, with tax included ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

To compare, take each plan's price, subtract its BRBs (worth $1 each), add $25 for half the yearly fee, and divide by the weekly meals over an 18-week term, using the prices in the table ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). This assumes you use every meal.

- Bear Traditional: a net $3,153 over 252 meals (14 a week for 18 weeks) is $12.51 a meal.
- Bear Choice: a net $2,600 over 180 meals is $14.44 a meal.
- Bear Basic: a net $2,014 over 126 meals is $15.98 a meal.

A plan meal costs less than the door price ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)) only if you use enough of them (net cost above divided by the door price, per week). Bear Basic needs about 6.7 lunches a week or 5.4 dinners a week. Bear Choice needs 8.6 lunches or 6.9 dinners. Bear Traditional needs 10.4 lunches or 8.4 dinners. Weekly swipes that you skip are lost when the week ends on Wednesday, so a plan punishes an irregular schedule.

If you eat five lunches a week on campus, paying the lunch door price ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)) costs $84.00 a week, or $1,512.00 over 18 weeks. That is less than any full plan costs in the table above ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)).

```reader "If you cook at home"
Take the grad plan and skip the rest. Every registered student also has a City Bucks account, a separate balance for participating off-campus businesses, and it works at Tops Friendly Markets (2300 North Triphammer Road) and P&C Fresh (315 Pine Tree Road) ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). Wegmans and Trader Joe's are not on the list. For a monthly food budget, see [Cornell grad student off-campus budget](/cornell/money/cornell-grad-student-off-campus-budget-monthly-rent-ithaca/).
```

```reader "If you're on campus all day, most days"
Lab and teaching schedules can make Bear Basic or Bear Choice work, but only if you reliably eat dinner in a dining room five or six times a week. If your schedule varies, the grad plan plus door prices on the heavy days costs less.
```

## How do you sign up?

Cornell's graduate plans page has the enrollment steps, and the details are not repeated in our sources. For questions, contact the Dining Contracts Office at 1508 Clara Dickson Hall, (607) 255-5952, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/contact-cornell-dining)).

## So which should you choose?

Take the graduate plan if you eat on campus a few times a week or mostly cook. Price a full plan only if you eat dinner in a dining room at least five times a week, and compare against the door prices above ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)). Everyone else is better off at the door.
