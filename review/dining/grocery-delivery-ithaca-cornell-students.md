For restaurant food, Ithaca To Go delivers and accepts City Bucks ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). For groceries, Cornell lists no delivery service, so read any app's fees at checkout and compare them with a bus ride to Wegmans.

## Which delivery and pickup options exist?

| Option | What it covers | Cost shown in our sources | How you pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ithaca To Go | Restaurant delivery, 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily per its site | Delivery fee, service fee and minimum not listed | City Bucks, per Cornell |
| GET Order | Pickup at 12 campus cafes and Jansen's Market | No fee | Big Red Bucks, MealChoice or credit card |
| Meals on the Move | Breakfast pickup, Monday to Friday in class weeks | One meal swipe | Your meal plan |
| TCAT bus to Wegmans | You shop in store | $1.50 a ride | Cash, card or app |
| Ithaca Carshare | You drive and shop | $30 a year plus $9 an hour | Membership |
| Grocery delivery apps | Not covered by Cornell's pages | Fees and minimums not in our sources | Card |

Source: [Ithaca To Go](https://ithacatogo.com/), [Cornell Dining GET Order](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/order-ahead), [Meals on the Move](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/meals-move), [Downtown Ithaca Alliance](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/) and [Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters). Ithaca To Go's page carries a 2024 copyright footer, so confirm its hours in the app.

## Can you pay for delivery with City Bucks or Big Red Bucks?

City Bucks, a separate balance on your Cornell ID for participating off-campus businesses, can pay for delivery from participating restaurants, and Ithacatogo.com accepts them. Sales tax applies ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). Big Red Bucks are for Cornell Dining locations only and do not pay for delivery from outside businesses.

If you add City Bucks through the bursar bill, Cornell disables bursar billing after more than $500 in deposits within 30 days ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks/city-bucks-faqs)).

## How do grocery delivery apps compare?

Cornell's pages list no fees for any grocery app and do not say which stores deliver to Cornell addresses, so the app is your only source for fees. Open the app you like, enter your address and add a typical cart. Then read the item prices, the delivery fee, the service fee and the tip.

Here is a fair comparison. A trip to Wegmans on the bus is two rides at $1.50, and Wegmans is open from 6 a.m. to midnight daily ([Wegmans](https://www.wegmans.com/stores/ithaca-ny)). Delivery wins when your total fees are low, you are sick or you cannot carry the load. The bus wins on cost. For store prices, see [Wegmans vs. Aldi vs. Price Chopper](/cornell/money/wegmans-vs-aldi-vs-price-chopper-ithaca-cheapest-grocery/).

Delivery can add up. A March 2025 Sun columnist said she spent about $400 extra on delivery and restaurant meals when the dining halls did not fit her vegetarian diet ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)).

## What do students use instead of delivery?

Most pick a weekly trip or buy staples within walking distance. [Cheap groceries in Ithaca](/cornell/money/cheap-groceries-ithaca-ny-students/) lists the stores, and Cornell's order-ahead pickup at campus cafes works for lunch.

## What to do next

Check one cart on the app you prefer, compare its total with a bus round trip, and decide which trips are worth the fee.
