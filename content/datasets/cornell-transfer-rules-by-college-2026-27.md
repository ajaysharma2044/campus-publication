Each Cornell college sets its own transfer rules, and they differ more than any one-line summary suggests. **Only CALS (3.0, or 3.5 for Biological Sciences) and AAP (2.7) print a minimum GPA.** Hotel (3.3) and ILR (3.4) give what admitted transfers usually have, Human Ecology and Brooks call 3.5 competitive, and Arts and Sciences and Engineering give no GPA figure at all. Cornell's own FAQ says admitted transfers are "generally" 3.3 or better ([FAQ 325](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/)).

The table puts nine colleges side by side on eight rules: the GPA figure and what kind of number it is, the credit cap and the exam-credit sub-cap, the minimum time at Cornell, standing limits, prerequisite courses, closed majors, the deadline the college page prints, and whom to contact. Every row links the college page it came from, and every rule a college page leaves out says "not stated."

Use it if you are:

- **A sophomore applicant from a four-year college.** Read the prerequisites column first and take those courses this year, for letter grades. CALS and Human Ecology refuse pass/fail. Global Development (CALS) takes sophomores only.
- **Thinking about entering as a junior.** Look at standing limits. CALS bars anyone with more than four semesters at a four-year school, Hotel bars current juniors, and Engineering takes juniors only in their first semester of junior standing. Juniors also face longer course lists in ILR and Human Ecology.
- **At a community college.** In CALS, an associate's degree means junior standing, with no option to enter as a sophomore. Cornell cannot deny credit because a course was taken at a community college or online, but it must be about 80% similar to the Cornell course and graded C or better ([Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services)).
- **Aiming at Engineering or computer science.** Engineering has the only fixed course block: missing MATH 1910, MATH 1920, PHYS 1112 with 1110, or CHEM 2090 with 2091 means the file is not reviewed. Its cap is 66 credits, the highest, but no more than 33 per full-time year.
- **A New York resident.** Five of the nine colleges (CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks, Dyson) charge NY residents $49,816 in 2026-27 tuition, against $73,946 at the four endowed colleges, a gap of $24,130 a year ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters)).

## What the table shows

- **GPA figures come in four kinds.** Minimum: CALS 3.0 (Biological Sciences 3.5), AAP 2.7. Typical of admits: Hotel 3.3, ILR 3.4. Competitive: Human Ecology 3.5, Brooks 3.5. None: Arts and Sciences, Engineering. Dyson's 3.5 rests on a search snippet of a 2019 file. A 3.0 clears CALS's floor but sits below every typical or competitive figure.
- **The cap is 60 credits almost everywhere.** Arts and Sciences, CALS, Hotel, ILR and Brooks print 60. Engineering allows 66, Architecture 64 ([Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services)). Human Ecology and Dyson print no cap of their own, so the registrar's 60 applies.
- **Four colleges limit exam credit inside the cap.** CALS 15 (AP), Hotel 15 (AP, IB, GCE), Human Ecology 15 (pre-college credit), ILR 12 (AP).
- **Four college pages print a residence floor.** Arts and Sciences: 60 credits and four semesters. ILR: 60 credits and four full-time semesters. Brooks: four semesters on the Ithaca campus. Engineering: 60 credits. Architecture needs 90 credits at Cornell under the registrar's rule.
- **Only four college pages print March 15.** Engineering, Hotel, ILR and AAP do. Human Ecology and Brooks describe rolling review with no date, and Arts and Sciences, CALS and Dyson give none. The central deadline covers every college: March 15, 2027 for the application and fee, April 15, 2027 for everything else ([Undergraduate Admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- **Three colleges close or limit majors.** AAP takes no external B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027. CALS closes Global and Public Health Sciences to transfers and sends AEM applicants to Dyson. Engineering admits you to one major, with no undecided option, and you stay in it at least a year.

## Method

The steps follow the topic brief, sections 2.4, 2.6, 2.7, 10.4 and 10.11. Every value was checked against its page on 2026-11-11.

1. **List the colleges.** Take the nine undergraduate colleges and schools with external transfer rules: Arts and Sciences, CALS, Engineering, Hotel, Human Ecology, ILR, AAP, Brooks and Dyson. That gives 9 rows.
2. **Open each college's own transfer page** (the source_url column) and record its rules in the words the page uses.
3. **Classify the GPA figure.** `minimum` is a floor the page says applicants must meet. `typical` is what admitted transfers usually have. `competitive` is a figure the page says helps. `not stated` means the page gives no GPA. `unverified snippet` means the figure was seen only in a search result.
4. **Record the credit cap and any exam-credit sub-cap** from the college page. Where the page is silent, add the registrar's university rule in brackets: up to 60 credits including exam credit, Engineering 66, Architecture 64 ([Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services)).
5. **Record the minimum time at Cornell** the same way. The registrar's university floor is 60 credits at Cornell (Architecture 90).
6. **Record standing limits, prerequisite courses, closed or limited majors, and the deadline exactly as the college page prints it.** A college that prints no deadline is marked "not stated," with a note that the central March 15 deadline applies.
7. **Record the admissions contact** from the college page. Where only an after-admission office is listed, the cell says so and dates it.
8. **Mark weak evidence.** Any value seen only in a search snippet carries "(search snippet)" in its cell.
9. **Add tuition.** `tuition_basis` marks the five contract colleges (CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks, Dyson) and the four endowed ones. `ny_resident_tuition` is the 2026-27 figure a New York resident pays: $49,816 at contract colleges, $73,946 at endowed. Non-residents pay $73,946 everywhere ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters)).
10. **Count across rows** for the summary above: GPA kinds (2 minimum, 2 typical, 2 competitive, 2 not stated, 1 snippet), caps printed by the college (6 of 9), exam sub-caps (4 of 9), residence floors on the college page (4 of 9), March 15 printed (4 of 9).

**Credit loss under the cap (brief 10.4).** With a 60-credit cap, 120 credits to graduate and a four-semester residence floor, at 15 credits a semester: 60 credits earned elsewhere lose nothing and finish in 8 semesters in all. 75 lose 15 (9 semesters), 90 lose 30 (10 semesters), 105 lose 45 (11 semesters). In Engineering, two full years elsewhere at 33 credits a year reach the 66 cap exactly. This model assumes every credit is accepted up to the cap; real evaluations are case by case.

Sample size: 9 colleges; 8 read from a current college page, 1 (Dyson) from a 2019 file seen as a search snippet.

## Caveats

- **Nine rows, one date.** Each row is one college's rules as its page read on 2026-11-11. Colleges revise these pages between cycles. The Arts and Sciences page still carried 2025-26 dates, and the ILR guidelines are the fall 2026 edition, updated 2026-02-10.
- **A GPA figure is not an admit line.** Only CALS and AAP print a floor. The typical and competitive figures describe admitted students or what helps, and no college publishes transfer admit rates.
- **Dyson's row is the weakest.** Its GPA and exam-credit cells come from a 2019 file seen only as a search snippet. Treat them as leads and confirm with Dyson.
- **Snippet-only cells are labeled.** The A&S major rule, the CALS Biological Sciences prerequisites and the Brooks exam-credit rule each carry "(search snippet)."
- **Contacts dated 2024 are after-admission offices.** Engineering, Brooks and Dyson rows list offices from a 2024 Tatkon Center page ([Tatkon Center](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/college-school-links)).
- **Two open disputes.** Whether Engineering requires CS 1110 or CS 1112 as a fifth core course is unresolved; the default reading is the four math, physics and chemistry courses. Whether Hotel transfers must send a resume is unresolved; sending one costs nothing.
- **Credits are evaluated after admission.** Every cap is a ceiling. The college decides course by course, needs a C or better and about 80% similarity, and most colleges will not pre-approve courses.
- **Not in the brief:** an external transfer page for Dyson or the Bowers College of Computing and Information Science; AAP's list of open external programs besides the B.F.A.; course prerequisites for AAP and Dyson; Human Ecology's and Dyson's own credit caps; transfer admit rates by college or major.
