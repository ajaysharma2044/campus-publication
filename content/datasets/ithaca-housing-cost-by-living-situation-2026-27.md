A year of housing near Cornell costs one person **$5,530** at the cheapest (a co-op triple for two semesters) and **$28,104** at the dearest typical figure in this table (a Collegetown 1BR alone at RentCafe's average). The line to measure against is Cornell's 2026-27 residence hall double: [$13,744 for the academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included. Eleven of the other 27 options come in under it, almost all of them big shared units or co-ops.

Over a 12-month lease, the double equals **$1,145.33 a month** per person. If you sublet the summer and pay only 10 months, it equals $1,374.40.

Each row is one way of living, priced per person per year, with the months it covers, what the price includes, and the gap to $13,744 (a minus sign means cheaper than the double). Ranges keep their low and high ends.

Use it if you are:

- **A parent** comparing Cornell's bill with a lease. Off-campus rows count 12 months; the double counts two semesters. A 4BR share at the Collegetown median is $14,955 a year (row 11); a 1BR alone is $24,300 (row 8).
- **A sophomore** choosing group size for junior year. Per-bed rent falls as the group grows: $21,180 for a Collegetown studio, $9,600 for a share of a 6BR (rows 7 and 13).
- **On a tight budget.** The cheapest beds are the co-op triple ($5,530), co-op doubles ($6,974 to $9,962), downtown 3BR and 5BR shares ($9,540) and the Collegetown 10BR ($9,300).
- **A grad student.** Hasbrouck runs $16,260 to $17,880 a year with utilities (rows 4 to 6). The 2026-27 Ithaca grad budget for housing is [$19,962 a year](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students), or $1,663.50 a month (19,962 / 12).
- **Coming with a partner or children.** Family units are priced per household, not per person, so they are not rows here. Hasbrouck's family 2BR apartment is $1,500 a month ($18,000 a year); the family 2BR townhouse is $1,475 unfurnished or $1,530 furnished ($17,700 or $18,360) ([Cornell rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). A whole Ithaca 2BR at RentCafe's average is $29,508 a year (2,459 x 12); a 3BR is $36,468 (3,039 x 12).
- **In a co-op or Greek house.** Co-op rows 2 and 3 use Cornell's semester rates. Greek house prices are not published, so they are not in the table.

## Method

These are the steps from the topic brief, sections 2.3, 10.3, 10.7, 10.9, 10.10 and 10.12. Every price was read or checked on 2026-11-08. They are posted rates and asking rents, not what anyone paid.

1. **Set the benchmark.** Cornell's 2026-27 residence hall double: $6,872 a semester, 6,872 x 2 = $13,744 a year per student. Per month: 13,744 / 12 = $1,145.33; over 10 months, 13,744 / 10 = $1,374.40.
2. **Cornell co-ops.** From [Cornell's rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), the double rates across all eight co-ops run $3,487 (Red Bud in Von Cramm) to $4,981 (660 Stewart, Wari) a semester. Two semesters: 3,487 x 2 = $6,974 and 4,981 x 2 = $9,962. Cheapest triple, Red Bud: 2,765 x 2 = $5,530.
3. **Hasbrouck.** Same page, 12-month contracts: studio 1,355 x 12 = $16,260; unfurnished 1BR 1,415 x 12 = $16,980; furnished 1BR 1,490 x 12 = $17,880.
4. **Collegetown per bed.** On [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) (67 unit rows on 2026-11-08), keep rows with a printed price range and a 2027-2028 tag. Drop semester-only, sold-out, unpriced and "available now"-only rows. That leaves 38 rows. Take each row's midpoint, (low + high) / 2. Take the median of the midpoints for each unit size, or the mean where a size has only two rows (3BR, 5BR). Multiply by 12. Low and high are the lowest and highest range endpoints for that size, times 12.
5. **Downtown per bed.** The same steps on [Ithaca Renting's downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/): 20 priced rows across six sizes, mostly 2026-27, 12-month leases. (Whiton Hall's room row was not used.)
6. **The Ivy.** The [Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026) reported 3BR units at $5,500 to $5,800 a month. Split three ways: 5,500 / 3 = $1,833.33 and 5,800 / 3 = $1,933.33; x 12 = $22,000 and $23,200.
7. **RentCafe sharing scenarios.** RentCafe's whole-unit averages for [Collegetown](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/) and [Ithaca](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) (both updated 2026-10-30), split evenly among the people a unit holds, times 12. Example: Collegetown 2BR 3,414 / 2 = 1,707; x 12 = $20,484.
8. **Gap to the double.** Annual cost minus 13,744, for the low, typical and high figure where each exists.

Assumptions, as the brief states them: rent split evenly; 12 months of rent for every off-campus row; no sublet income; deposit refunded; utilities, insurance and furniture not added to the off-campus rows (the double includes utilities).

Sample size: 79 prices. That is 1 double rate, 8 co-op double rates, 1 co-op triple rate, 3 Hasbrouck rates, 38 Collegetown rows, 20 downtown rows, 2 Ivy prices and 6 RentCafe averages. RentCafe does not say how many listings sit behind each average.

## What the numbers say

- **Under the double:** both ends of the co-op double range and the co-op triple; Collegetown 5BR (on a two-row mean), 6BR, 10BR and shared-suite rooms; downtown 2BR, 3BR, 5BR and 6BR shares; and an Ithaca 3BR split three ways at RentCafe's average. That is 11 of 27.
- **Over it:** every Hasbrouck unit, every Collegetown unit of four bedrooms or fewer at the median, downtown studios and 1BRs, The Ivy, and five of the six RentCafe scenarios.
- **Group size moves the number most.** In Collegetown, going from a 6BR share ($9,600) to a 4BR share ($14,955) adds $5,355 a year per person. Living alone in a 1BR ($24,300) costs $10,556 more than the double.
- **Downtown is cheaper per bed at the same landlord.** Collegetown runs 26.5% above downtown for studios, 24.6% for 1BRs and 38.8% for 2BRs ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)). The catch is a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk or a bus.
- **The double includes bills that most off-campus rows do not.** As an upper-bound illustration, an Ithaca household's average $280 a month electricity bill ([EnergySage](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/), updated 2026-11-01) split four ways adds $70 a month. That takes the Collegetown 4BR median from $1,211 over the double to $2,051 over.

## Caveats

- **Asking prices and posted rates, not paid rents.** Landlord prices can change until a lease is signed.
- **One landlord for 58 of the 79 prices.** Rows 7 to 21 are all Ithaca Renting. Several sizes rest on one to three rows (Collegetown 3BR, 5BR and 10BR; downtown 1BR, 3BR, 5BR and 6BR).
- **Different years in one table.** Cornell, downtown and Ivy rows are 2026-27; Collegetown Ithaca Renting rows are 2027-28. Downtown rows are mostly 2026-27 units still listed in November, possibly discounted. RentCafe pages were updated 2026-10-30.
- **Periods differ by design.** Cornell rows cover two semesters. Off-campus rows count 12 months, as most local leases run. Collegetown Ithaca Renting lease length and The Ivy's lease length are not in the brief, so 12 months is the brief's assumption there.
- **RentCafe counts only buildings with 50 or more units**, which leaves out the small landlords and houses that dominate Collegetown. Its listing counts are not stated, and its Collegetown square footages (148 sq ft studio, 550 sq ft 2BR) look implausible. There is no Collegetown 3BR average.
- **The Ivy's prices are two figures from one news report** (Dec 2, 2025), before any discount. One group was offered 20% off for early interest.
- **Utilities, insurance, furniture, parking and transit are not added** to any off-campus row. Inclusions differ row by row; see the "included" column.
- **Low and high in the per-bed rows are the extreme range endpoints** for that size, not a typical spread.
- **Not in the brief:** Greek house costs in dollars; a 2026-27 Cornell meal plan price; which four co-ops have a meal plan; Cornell's single, suite and program-house rates applied per person here (they are in brief 2.3 but not in the planned rows); Maplewood prices; Fall Creek, Lansing and East Hill per-bed medians (only single listings exist); 10-month lease prices; what tenants actually paid; Cornell's 2027-28 rates, which post in March 2027.
