A new Cornell transfer must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), and that year is billed at **$95,218 to $99,169 for an endowed or non-New York student** and **$71,088 to $75,039 for a New York resident in a contract college**, before aid, at 2026-27 prices. The spread comes from the room: a triple is the cheapest hall row, a super single the most expensive. A co-op double cuts the housing line to **$6,974 to $9,962 a year**, against $13,744 for a hall double.

The table lists 13 options: five hall rooms with their meal plans, each billed at both tuition rates, and eight co-op doubles with what each saves on housing. Every price is from Cornell's official 2026-27 lists, checked 2026-11-11.

Use it if you are:

- **A sophomore transfer from a four-year college.** Your room is assigned by random auto-allocation from your preferences, so you do not fully pick your row. Plan on the double ($96,318 endowed) and treat the triple and super single as the floor and ceiling.
- **A parent.** The hall double with the Unlimited plan is the number to budget: $96,318 endowed or $72,188 NY contract. Add the Student Health Plan ($3,751 in year one) unless your child waives it with comparable coverage.
- **On a tight budget.** A Redbud co-op double saves $6,770 on housing alone against a hall double. With endowed tuition and fees, that is $74,970 + $6,974 = $81,944 before food; ask the co-op what meals cost.
- **A New York resident at a contract college.** Your tuition is $49,816, not $73,946, so every hall row is $24,130 lower. The double comes to $72,188.

## What the table shows

- **Room choice moves the hall bill by $3,951.** The triple ($95,218 endowed) and the super single ($99,169) are 4.1% apart. Tuition is 75% to 78% of every endowed row (73,946 / 99,169 to 73,946 / 95,218).
- **The meal plan follows the hall.** Most halls put transfers on Unlimited or the West Campus House Meal Plan, both $7,604 a year. The six named South Campus halls use the Collegetown plan at $7,056, so a double there is $548 cheaper.
- **Co-ops are the cheapest rooms on the price list.** Every co-op double costs less than the hall triple. Savings against a hall double run from $3,782 (660 Stewart, Wari) to $6,770 (Redbud).
- **Health insurance is a separate bill.** It sits outside both the table and Cornell's cost of attendance (see the line under the table).

## Method

All prices are Cornell's 2026-27 rates, read on 2026-11-11.

1. **Tuition.** Endowed or non-New York: $73,946. New York resident at a contract college: $49,816 ([Cornell Chronicle, 2026-03-23](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters)).
2. **Mandatory fees.** $1,024 ($424 activity plus $600 health fee) ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend)). Tuition plus fees: 73,946 + 1,024 = $74,970 endowed; 49,816 + 1,024 = $50,840 NY contract.
3. **Housing.** Per-year hall rates from the [housing rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates): triple or one-room quad $12,644; double $13,744; single $15,566; super single $16,595. A quad is billed like a double and a townhouse double like a single, so they are not separate rows.
4. **Meal plan.** Hall residents are auto-enrolled. Unlimited or House Meal Plan: $7,604 a year. Collegetown plan in the named South Campus halls: $7,056 ([meal plans page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans); [transfer families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies)).
5. **Billed year, hall rows.** Tuition plus fees plus housing plus meal plan. Triple: 74,970 + 12,644 + 7,604 = $95,218. Double, South Campus: 74,970 + 13,744 + 7,056 = $95,770. Double: 74,970 + 13,744 + 7,604 = $96,318. Single: 74,970 + 15,566 + 7,604 = $98,140. Super single: 74,970 + 16,595 + 7,604 = $99,169.
6. **NY contract rows.** Subtract the tuition gap, 73,946 - 49,816 = 24,130, from each: $71,088; $71,640; $72,188; $74,010; $75,039.
7. **Check against Cornell.** Add books ($1,234) and personal ($2,182) to the double: 96,318 + 3,416 = $99,734 endowed and 72,188 + 3,416 = $75,604 NY contract. Both match Cornell's published cost of attendance.
8. **Co-op rows.** Take each co-op's per-semester double rate from the housing rates page and multiply by two. Redbud 3,487 x 2 = $6,974; Triphammer and Watermargin 4,317 x 2 = $8,634; Prospect of Whitby 4,318 x 2 = $8,636; 302 and 308 Wait 4,715 x 2 = $9,430; 660 Stewart and Wari 4,981 x 2 = $9,962.
9. **Savings.** Subtract each yearly housing figure from the hall double's $13,744. Redbud 6,770; Triphammer and Watermargin 5,110; Prospect of Whitby 5,108; 302 and 308 Wait 4,314; 660 Stewart and Wari 3,782. Hall rows use the same subtraction, so rooms dearer than a double show a negative number.
10. **Health plan.** Fall entrant: $1,705 (August 1 to December) + $2,046 (spring) = $3,751 ([Student Health Benefits](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27)). Kept off the table because it can be waived.

Sample size: 13 room options from Cornell's official 2026-27 price lists (5 hall rooms, 8 co-op doubles). These are list prices, not observations of what students paid.

## Caveats

- **2026-27 prices, not 2027-28.** A transfer entering in fall 2027 will pay 2027-28 rates, which Cornell has not yet announced. The last tuition increase was 3.76%; that is not a forecast.
- **Before aid.** These are billed prices. Grants can cover much of them; see the aid guide below.
- **You do not pick the hall row.** Halls are assigned by random auto-allocation from your preferences, except Program Houses. Co-ops are among the upper-level areas where transfers can be placed, but no count of co-op places for new transfers is published.
- **Co-op rows are housing only.** Co-op rows carry no meal plan and no billed total; ask the co-op what meals cost. Compare co-op and hall rooms on the housing columns.
- **Small fees left out.** The $50 yearly meal plan administrative fee is not in the billed totals, which keeps them matched to Cornell's own cost of attendance.
- **Cheaper hall meal plans are not shown.** Bear Traditional ($7,056) exists, but transfers in halls are auto-enrolled in Unlimited, House or Collegetown, so only those appear.
- **Sample size.** 13 list prices from one academic year. No observation of what transfers actually paid.
