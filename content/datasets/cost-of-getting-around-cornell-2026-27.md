A year of getting around Cornell in 2026-27 costs **$264 to $948** in these four profiles, depending on whether you have a car, buy a TCAT pass and which bus you take to New York. The cheapest is a first-year with no car riding OurBus at its floor fare. The dearest is an upper-year with an OmniRide pass who takes Cornell's Campus-to-Campus (C2C) bus four times. A resident parking permit alone is **$752.98**, or **$2.06 a day**, and **four C2C round trips cost about the same as one permit** ($748).

These are scenarios built from posted prices, not a record of what students spend. Every input, step and assumption is in the table, so you can swap in your own number of trips.

Use it if you are:

- **A first-year** deciding whether to bring a car. Your TCAT rides are free until Aug 31, 2027, so the car is the only big cost you can add.
- **Past first year without a car**, deciding whether the $200 OmniRide pass or Carshare is worth it.
- **Keeping a car on campus**, and want the permit priced against the alternatives.
- **Paying your own way**, where the gap between C2C and a commercial bus is the biggest line you control.
- **A parent** budgeting the year, or **an off-campus renter** pricing the $359.77 commuter permit.

## The four profiles at a glance

| Profile | Year total | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First-year, no car | $264.00 | Free first-year TCAT + 4 NYC round trips on OurBus at $33 |
| 2. Upper-year, no car, OmniRide | $416.00 to $948.00 | $200 OmniRide + 4 NYC round trips: FlixBus floor $416, OurBus floor $464, C2C $948 |
| 3. Upper-year with a resident permit | $832.98 | $752.98 permit + two weeks of ITH long-term parking ($40 each), before gas, insurance and tolls |
| 4. No car, Carshare | $714.00 | It's My Car $310 + $20 sign-up + 20 hours at $6 + OurBus floor $264 |

The same 20 hours on Ithaca Carshare's Just In Case plan would make profile 4 **$494.00** (row 29), because that plan is cheaper below about 93 hours a year.

## Method

Every step comes from the topic brief, sections 3.4, 3.5, 10.13, 10.14, 10.17 and 10.18. Prices were read on 2026-11-08. No inflation adjustment.

1. **Read the inputs.** Cornell's [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students): resident permit $752.98, commuter (B) $359.77. Cornell's [bus passes page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes): free unlimited TCAT for new first-years, OmniRide $200 a year, cash fare $1.50. [C2C rates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates): NetID round trip $187. Bus floors: [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) from $33, [FlixBus](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/new-york-ny) $27, and the six-carrier mean average fare of $61.91 from [Wanderu](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/). [ITH long-term parking](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking): $40 a week. [Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters) student plans: It's My Car $310 a year + $6 an hour, Just In Case $30 a year + $9 an hour, $20 sign-up ([rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)).
2. **Fix the scenario.** Four round trips to New York City a year.
3. **NYC travel alone.** C2C 4 x 187 = $748.00. OurBus floor 4 x 66 = $264.00. FlixBus floor 4 x 54 = $216.00. At the six-carrier mean average, 4 x 2 x 61.91 = $495.28.
4. **Profile 1**, first-year, no car, OurBus floor: $0 TCAT + $264 = $264.00.
5. **Profile 2**, upper-year, no car, OmniRide: 200 + 264 = $464.00 with OurBus; 200 + 216 = $416.00 with FlixBus; 200 + 748 = $948.00 with C2C.
6. **Profile 3**, upper-year with a resident permit who drives home and parks at ITH for two weekly stays: 752.98 + 2 x 40 = $832.98, before gas, insurance and tolls.
7. **Profile 4**, no car, 20 hours of It's My Car (an assumed figure) plus OurBus floor: 310 + 20 + 20 x 6 + 264 = $714.00. Variant on Just In Case: 30 + 20 + 20 x 9 + 264 = $494.00.
8. **Permit per period.** 752.98 / 12 = $62.75 a month; 752.98 / 365 = $2.06 a day. Commuter: 359.77 / 12 = $29.98 a month.
9. **Permit in TCAT rides.** 752.98 / 1.50 = 502 cash rides.
10. **OmniRide break-even.** 200 / 1.50 = 133.3, so the 134th paid daytime weekday ride makes the pass pay. Over an assumed 30-week academic year, 134 / 30 = 4.47 paid rides a week. A public monthly pass at $45 for an assumed 9 months is $405, against $200.
11. **Carshare break-even.** 30 + 9h = 310 + 6h, so 3h = 280 and h = 93.3 hours a year. Below that, Just In Case is cheaper.
12. **Permit in Carshare hours.** 752.98 - 310 - 20 = $422.98 left for driving; / 6 = 70.5 hours. With the 20% in taxes on usage applied to the hourly charge (an assumption), 6 x 1.20 = $7.20 and 422.98 / 7.20 = 58.7 hours.
13. **C2C against the permit.** 748 / 752.98 = 0.993.
14. **Permit plus one ticket.** 752.98 + 50 = $802.98.
15. **Thanksgiving.** Applying the +33.3% Thanksgiving-week premium measured on three other Ithaca routes to one OurBus floor round trip: 66 x 1.333 = $87.98, or $21.98 more.

Sample size: 18 input prices (rows 1 to 18). Two inputs are themselves samples: the $61.91 mean covers 6 carriers on one aggregator, and the +33.3% premium covers 3 routes and 51 priced dates. Rows 19 to 42 are arithmetic on those inputs.

## What the numbers say

- **The car is the big line.** $752.98 is $62.75 a month or $2.06 a day, the price of 502 TCAT cash rides. One ticket for parking without a permit or payment takes it to $802.98.
- **C2C costs about a permit a year.** Four NetID round trips are $748, 99.3% of the resident permit. The same four trips at bus floors are $216 to $264, and $495.28 at the six-carrier average fare.
- **OmniRide pays from the 134th paid ride.** That is about 4.5 daytime weekday rides a week over a 30-week year. Evenings after 6 p.m. and weekends are free anyway, so most riders who stick to those hours should pay cash.
- **Pick the Carshare plan by hours.** Below 93.3 hours a year, Just In Case ($30 + $9 an hour) beats It's My Car ($310 + $6 an hour). The permit's price would buy 58.7 to 70.5 hours of It's My Car driving.
- **Thanksgiving costs more.** If NYC follows the +33.3% seen on three other routes, one OurBus floor round trip goes from $66 to $87.98.

## Caveats

- **Scenario arithmetic, not a measurement.** No student's spending was recorded. The profiles assume four NYC round trips; change that number and every bus line scales with it.
- **Floors are not what you pay.** The $33 and $27 fares are the cheapest seats advertised on one day. Booked fares near a break run closer to the $61.91 average or above it.
- **Assumptions are labeled.** Twenty Carshare hours, a 30-week academic year, 9 months of a monthly pass, the 20% tax applying to the hourly charge only, and the Thanksgiving premium applying to NYC are all assumptions, not sourced figures.
- **Undated pages.** Cornell's permit, pass and C2C pages, the ITH parking page and the Carshare pages are undated; all were read as current on 2026-11-07 or 2026-11-08. The TCAT monthly pass price comes from a search summary of a page marked updated 2026-09-04 that could not be opened directly.
- **Permits are limited.** Resident and commuter permits are first come, first served, and ND is capped to North Campus residents. The families page says first-years are discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits exist.
- **What it does not cover:** gas, insurance, tolls and car payments for profile 3; Carshare mileage (42 to 52 cents) and taxes in profile 4's total; last-mile rides to and from bus stops; airfares; bag fees; storage and break housing.
- **Not in the brief:** a profile for an off-campus renter with the commuter permit or the $45 city residential permit; what students actually spend on travel in a year; how many trips home a typical student takes; NYC fares for Thanksgiving week; a current annual Ithaca Bikeshare price (the site lists day $25 and month $60); the OurBus student discount amount; gas and insurance costs for Ithaca.
