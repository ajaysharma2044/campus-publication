On Nov 8, 2026, the cheapest one-way bus seat out of Ithaca was **$8 to Binghamton, $10 to Syracuse and $17 to Rochester**. Farther out, the lows were **$36 to Newark Penn Station, $41 to Philadelphia, $44 to Boston and $49 to Washington DC**. The OurBus run to **Syracuse airport (SYR) costs $46.70 to $62.10**, which is **$0.91 to $1.21 a mile, 4 to 5 times** what the regional buses charge per mile at their floor.

Use this table if your home is somewhere other than New York City, if you fly out of Syracuse, if you are an international student routing through Newark or Toronto, or if every $10 counts. Cornell's Campus-to-Campus bus runs only to Manhattan, so none of these routes have a C2C option ([C2C rates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates)). For Ithaca to New York City itself, see the [pillar guide](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/).

What the numbers say:

- **Short regional hops are cheap.** Binghamton daily lows averaged $12.13 over 15 travel dates, Rochester $21.29 over 17, Syracuse $26.00 over 12 ([CheckMyBus Binghamton](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/binghamton-ny), [Rochester](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/rochester-ny), [Syracuse](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/syracuse-ny)).
- **The airport bus is the dear one per mile.** Regional floors ran $0.15 (FlixBus to NYC) to $0.23 (Rochester) a mile; the SYR airport bus ran $0.91 to $1.21. Round trip to SYR: $93.40 to $124.20 ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny)).
- **Thanksgiving week costs about a third more.** On the floor fare, Binghamton rose 27.9%, Rochester 34.2% and DC 37.9% in Thanksgiving week (Nov 23 to 27; Nov 21 to 27 for Rochester), mean +33.3%. DC floors averaged $93.40 that week against $67.71 earlier in November ([CheckMyBus DC](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/washington-dc)).
- **A transfer can save money.** Newark Penn via NYC (OurBus about $32 plus Greyhound about $5) was about $37 on Wed Nov 11, $9 to $12 under the direct FlixBus that day, for about 50 more minutes and a Manhattan change ([CheckMyBus Newark](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/newark-nj)).
- **Toronto prices are the weakest numbers here.** Every Toronto figure comes from undated search summaries ($43 to $170), so check the operator's site before you plan around them.

## Method

Every step comes from the topic brief (sections 3.2, 10.8, 10.9, 10.10 and 10.16). Anyone can redo it with the pages linked in each row.

1. **Day-by-day lows.** On 2026-11-08, open the CheckMyBus page for each route and record the "lowest fare" listed for each travel date. Skip dates with no fare. Syracuse gave 12 dates (Nov 6 to 26), Binghamton 15 (Nov 7 to 27), Rochester 17 (Nov 3 to 27), DC 19 (Nov 7 to 28; Nov 13, 22 and 28 had no fare).
2. **Mean and median.** Binghamton: 182 / 15 = $12.13, median $13. Rochester: 362 / 17 = $21.29; sorted, the 9th of 17 values is $21 (our median). Syracuse: 312 / 12 = $26.00, median $26. DC: 1,415 / 19 = $74.47; sorted, the 10th of 19 values is $75 (our median).
3. **Thanksgiving-week premium.** Split each route's lows into the weeks before and Thanksgiving week. Binghamton: before (Nov 7 to 20, 10 values) 111 / 10 = $11.10; week (Nov 23 to 27) 71 / 5 = $14.20; 14.20 / 11.10 = 1.279, +27.9%. Rochester: before (Nov 3 to 20, 11 values) 209 / 11 = $19.00; week (Nov 21 to 27, 6 values) 153 / 6 = $25.50; +34.2%. DC: before (14 values) 948 / 14 = $67.71; week 467 / 5 = $93.40; +37.9%. Mean of the three: 100.0 / 3 = 33.3%. Syracuse was not split because its page lists $41 and $12 for Nov 8.
4. **Newark Penn by weekday.** Record the direct FlixBus range for Sun Nov 8 to Thu Nov 12 and take each day's midpoint: 53.5, 49, 46.5, 47.5, 45.5. Mean 242.0 / 5 = $48.40, median $47.50. Sunday minus Thursday: 53.5 - 45.5 = $8.00 (17.6%). Add the lowest single date, $36 on Wed Nov 18 (25.6% under the mean). Via NYC on Nov 11: $37 against $46 to $49 direct, a saving of $9 (19.6%) to $12 (24.5%).
5. **SYR airport.** Read the OurBus route page: 51.4 miles, fares from $46.70, most $46.70 to $62.10, 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min. Add the CheckMyBus reverse-direction sample for Sun Nov 8 (three direct trips at $48, $51, $51).
6. **Cost per mile.** Divide each fare by the route distance the operator or aggregator page gives: SYR airport 46.7 / 51.4 = $0.909 and 62.1 / 51.4 = $1.208; Binghamton 8 / 38.4 = $0.21 and 16 / 38.4 = $0.42; Rochester 17 / 74.3 = $0.23 and 30 / 74.3 = $0.40; NYC 27 / 174.8 = $0.15. Airport against the Rochester floor: 0.91 / 0.23 = 4.0 and 1.21 / 0.23 = 5.3. Two values are our own division on the same basis: SYR reverse sample 48 / 51.4 = $0.93 and 51 / 51.4 = $0.99; Boston 44 / 330 = $0.13 and 101 / 330 = $0.31, using the planner's driving distance because no bus mileage is in the brief.
7. **Boston, Philadelphia, Toronto and the operator pages.** Record what each page states (FAQ range, sampled dates, "from" fare, times), mark rows from undated search summaries as undated, and do not average them.
8. **Travel times** are converted to minutes (for example 1 h 25 min = 85).

Sample: 88 fare readings across 16 rows, the sum of the sample_size column. That is 63 dated daily lows on four CheckMyBus routes (12 + 15 + 17 + 19), 6 Newark Penn readings, 3 Boston dates, 3 SYR airport trips, 2 Philadelphia fares, 2 transfer fares, 1 OurBus SYR page reading, 4 undated single-page readings and 4 undated Toronto summaries.

## Caveats

- **Floors, not fares paid.** Every "low" is the cheapest seat a page advertised for that date, not a fare anyone booked. Expect to pay more if you book late or travel on a peak day.
- **One capture day.** All dated rows were read once, on 2026-11-08, for travel 2 days to 3 weeks out. Prices for the Thanksgiving week may rise further as the dates get closer.
- **Small samples on the long routes.** Boston rests on 3 dated fares plus a FlixBus FAQ range; Philadelphia on 2 fares. The DC (n = 19), Rochester (17), Binghamton (15) and Syracuse (12) rows are the strongest.
- **Toronto is flagged.** All four Toronto figures come from undated search summaries. Treat $43 to $170 as a rough guide only. The DC and Philadelphia OurBus "from" fares, the Trailways SYR fare and the OurBus Boston planner range are undated too.
- **Thanksgiving premium covers three routes.** The +33.3% mean comes from Binghamton, Rochester and DC (51 priced dates). The "before" sets mix weekdays and weekends unevenly. Applying it to other routes is an assumption.
- **Mileage.** Per-mile figures exist only where a page gave the route distance. Boston uses a planner's driving distance of about 330 miles, so its per-mile figure is approximate. For NYC, Greyhound gives about 227 miles against OurBus's 174.8, which would make NYC per-mile figures about 23% lower.
- **Newark Penn is not Newark Airport.** The only Newark Airport fare in the brief is a cheapest $82 on a February 2026 date, about 10 buses a day and 4.5 to 5 hours (search summary, [CheckMyBus](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/newark-nj-airport)); it is too old to sit in this table.
- **Planner pages can mean the wrong Cornell.** Rome2rio results for "Cornell" may point to a different town; check the origin.
- **Not in the brief:** route mileage for Syracuse city, Newark, Philadelphia, DC and Toronto; bus mileage to Boston; dated Toronto fares; the operator of the $41 Philadelphia fare and the count of Philadelphia dates priced; total times for the two transfer rows; whether the Philadelphia OurBus route is running now; border documents for Toronto; bag fees on these routes; Thanksgiving-week fares for Boston, Philadelphia, Newark and Toronto.
