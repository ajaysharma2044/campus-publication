Collegetown leases in the Cornell Daily Sun's record are signed **8 to 16 months before an August move-in, mostly 10 to 11 months ahead**. That means September to October of the year before, which for a junior-year lease is the fall of sophomore year. The one exception is a fallback: a 14-person group whose Greek housing plans fell through started hunting in February and signed **at most about 6 months** ahead.

This is the record behind the [when-to-sign guide](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/), which turns it into a decision. The [lease timeline tool](/tools/when-to-sign-lease-timeline-cornell/) applies the same window to your year and term.

Nine dated cases from six [Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/) stories, signings from April 2017 to December 2025. On 2026-11-08, nine months before an August 2027 start, [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) already marked **14 of 67** Collegetown rows for 2027-28 sold out.

Use it if you are:

- **A sophomore** planning junior year. If you want a popular Collegetown unit, the record puts your signing in September to November of this year.
- **A first-year.** You cannot sign for sophomore year without an approved exemption, but the junior-year search starts about a year from now. Start thinking about housemates.
- **A transfer** who arrives after the fall rush. Case 3 shows a large group still finding a Collegetown house from a February start.
- **A parent** wondering why your child is asked to commit, and sometimes pay first month's rent and a security deposit at signing ([209 Stewart Ave's 2026-27 listing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095), for one), 10 or 11 months before moving in.

## What the record shows

- **5 of 9 cases fall at 10 or 11 months.** That is September or October for an August start.
- **2 of 9 are big houses signed 15 and 16 months ahead**, in April and May 2017 for August 2018. Large group houses go first.
- **A new building leased early too.** Nearly all 52 units at The Ivy (325 College Ave) were leased by December 2025, at least 8 months before leases began on 2026-08-17.
- **The rules did not visibly move the window.** Cases signed after the City's 120-day notice rule took effect on 2022-05-31 (cases 6 and 9) fall in the same range as those before it, as do all cases after the 2013 60-day rule.

## Method

1. **Collect.** Take every first-person or named lease signing with a stated month in a Cornell Daily Sun story about the Collegetown leasing rush. Six stories qualify, dated 2018-11-07, 2019-09-19, 2021-10-13, 2022-08-04, 2022-09-12 and 2025-12-02. They yield nine cases.
2. **Date the signing.** Use the month the story gives (a day where it gives one: 2019-09-12 for Ithaca Renting's opening, 2021-09-19 for case 7). Where the story gives only an upper bound (The Ivy, leased by the story date) or only the month the search began (case 3, a February start), record that in the qualifier.
3. **Set the move-in.** Take August of the move-in year, the month most Ithaca 12-month leases start. The Ivy's stated start, 2026-08-17, is also August.
4. **Count.** Count calendar months from the signing month to August of the move-in year: April 2017 to August 2018 = 16; May 2017 to August 2018 = 15; February to August = 6; September to the following August = 11; October to the following August = 10; December 2025 to August 2026 = 8.
5. **Qualify.** Mark each count "about" (month precision), "at most" (case 3, where the search began in February, so signing came 6 or fewer months ahead) or "at least" (The Ivy, signed by December 2025). Case 1 uses the article's own figure of 16.
6. **Summarize.** Count cases by months ahead: 6 or fewer (1), 8 or more (1), 10 (2), 11 (3), 15 (1), 16 (1).

Sample size: 9 cases from 6 stories.

## Caveats

- **Nine cases, one outlet.** Every case comes from the Cornell Daily Sun. This is a dated press record, not a survey and not a measured median.
- **Reporters seek out early signers.** Stories about the rush quote the people who signed early or camped out, so the record leans early.
- **Month precision.** Most signings are dated to the month, so each count can be off by up to a month. Two cases are bounds, not points (case 3 at most 6, case 9 at least 8).
- **Collegetown only.** The record covers Collegetown houses and buildings. Downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and leftover units can lease later.
