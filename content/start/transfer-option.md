You applied to Cornell as a first-year and got a Transfer Option instead of admission. It is an invitation to apply for sophomore transfer after one full-time year at another school. It is not an offer of admission, and it cannot be deferred ([Arts and Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process)). The decisions in front of you: which courses and grades to aim for this year, when to file the Transfer Option application, and what to line up in case the answer is no.

## Read in this order

1. [Transferring to Cornell: the complete guide for fall 2027](/transfer/transferring-to-cornell-guide/): the whole transfer process, from eligibility to move-in; read it first to see where your route fits.
2. [Cornell Transfer Option: is it guaranteed, and what now?](/transfer/cornell-transfer-option-guide/): your guide, covering the year elsewhere, the separate application and the decision; read it as soon as you accept the offer.
3. [What GPA you need to transfer to Cornell, by college](/transfer/cornell-transfer-gpa-requirements-by-college/): the grade figure each college gives; read it before you register for your first term elsewhere.
4. [Transferring into Cornell Arts and Sciences: the rules](/transfer/cornell-arts-and-sciences-transfer-requirements/): the A&S course and grade conditions in full; read it at the start of each term if your offer names A&S.
5. [Cornell transfer timeline: your dates, Nov 2026 to Dec 2027](/tools/cornell-transfer-timeline/): choose Transfer Option to get your own dated list; use it before winter break.
6. [Do AP and IB credits count when you transfer to Cornell?](/transfer/cornell-transfer-ap-ib-exam-credit/): what happens to your exam scores and the written request you must make; read it once you are admitted.
7. Cornell transfer housing: on campus, exemptions, leases (in the housing section): the year on campus you will owe as a sophomore transfer; read it the day you are admitted.
8. [Making friends as a Cornell transfer: what worked](/transfer/making-friends-as-a-cornell-transfer/): how other transfers found their people; read it over the summer before you arrive.

## Dates to watch

- October 1, 2026: FAFSA opens for 2027-28; file the CSS Profile as soon as possible after it ([aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)).
- Winter 2027: the Transfer Option page says "Application Available Winter 2027" ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants)).
- March 15 (2026 pattern): Transfer Option application and aid forms due; the Mid-Term Report may come later ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants)).
- Mid-April onward (2026 pattern): Transfer Option decisions roll in, most in late April or early May.
- Within ten days of getting your NetID: apply for housing through the Housing and Dining Portal.
- Last day of instruction in your first Cornell semester (Dec 7 in 2026): deadline to request AP, IB or other exam credit in writing from your college registrar.
