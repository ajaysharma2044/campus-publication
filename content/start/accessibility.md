You have a mobility or other disability and work through Student Disability Services (SDS). You may need a unit with step-free access and a commute you can manage up Ithaca's hills, an accessible parking permit, wheelchair access on an intercity bus, a reduced fare or help on move-in day, and you face the same snow and early flights as everyone else with fewer fallbacks. Each topic below has its own reading order, in the order you will hit the questions.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [Cornell student parking permits: cost, rules, who qualifies](/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/). How the accessible (AP) permit works and why SDS comes first; read it in July, before the permit sale.
2. [Cornell C2C bus: who can ride, fares and how to book](/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/). Booking C2C with a wheelchair or a service animal, and the phone call that has to come before the trip; read it before you book any break trip.
3. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/). When you ride free, when a fare applies and how to ask for the reduced fare; read it before your first week of classes.
4. [Cornell move-in day parking: where to unload and park](/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/). Where to unload, where a taxi or Uber drops you and when staff help ends; read it the week before move-in.
5. [Cornell to Ithaca airport: TCAT 32 and 72, taxis, parking](/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/). Which flights a bus can reach and when you need a taxi instead; read it before you book a flight.
6. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The whole topic: buses home, airports, TCAT and cars, for every trip the pages above do not cover.

Not in the brief: paratransit in Tompkins County, and the accessibility features of OurBus, Greyhound and FlixBus. No guide covers them yet; ask SDS and the carrier directly.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- **Before Tue Aug 4, 2026, 9 a.m.:** contact SDS about the AP permit before student permit sales open, first come first served ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)).
- **Mon Aug 17, Tue Aug 18, Fri Aug 21 to Sat Aug 22:** North Campus move-in days (year inferred as 2026) ([North Campus move instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).
- **Nov 4, 2026:** the new C2C reservation system starts, and everyone needs a new account ([Cornell announcement](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4)).
- **Ten business days before any C2C trip:** call to arrange wheelchair access ([C2C additional information](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-additional-information)).
- **Dec 1 to Apr 1:** Cornell winter overnight parking rules ([winter and break parking rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)).

## Read in this order: Housing

1. [Off-campus housing at Cornell: the 2026-27 guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/). The whole topic, from whether you may live off campus to leases, deposits and renewals; read it first, before you start searching.
2. [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown Ithaca: where to live](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/). Which neighborhoods leave you an uphill walk to campus and how each one commutes; read it before you choose where to look.
3. [Collegetown landlords: how to check one before you sign](/housing/how-to-check-an-ithaca-landlords-record/). How to check a landlord and a building before you commit; read it once you have a shortlist.
4. [When an Ithaca landlord won't fix heat, mold or bed bugs](/housing/ithaca-landlord-wont-fix-heat-mold-bed-bugs/). What to do when a repair stalls, and who to call; read it after you move in, or the day something breaks.

Not in the brief: reasonable accommodations or modifications from a private landlord, fair-housing rules and counts of accessible units. No guide covers them yet; ask SDS before you sign.

## Dates to watch: Housing

- **Before you start searching:** register with Student Disability Services ([Cornell graduate housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing)).
- **Late August to September:** Collegetown leasing for the following year opens ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/12/hurt-for-housing-begins-early-causing-stress-for-students/)).
- **Sept 15:** Ithaca's heat season starts ([Cornell Law tenants advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).
- **July 31:** most Ithaca leases expire, and the next one starts on or about August 1 ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing)).
- **Early August (Aug 4 in 2026):** buy the AP permit alongside Cornell's student permit sales, after contacting SDS ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)).
