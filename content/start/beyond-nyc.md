Home is Boston, Philadelphia, New Jersey, Washington DC or Toronto, not New York City, and Cornell's own C2C bus only runs to Manhattan. The decision in front of you is which direct bus or connection gets you home without a car, where in Ithaca it leaves from, and which date to book before Thanksgiving prices climb. Read these in order; each guide carries the fares, times and sources.

## Read in this order

1. [Cornell to Boston, Philadelphia, DC or Toronto by bus](/getting-around/ithaca-bus-to-boston-philadelphia-dc-toronto/). Your main guide: every route beyond NYC, direct or with a transfer, and what to skip. Read it first, before you book anything.
2. [Ithaca to JFK, LaGuardia or Newark airport without a car](/getting-around/ithaca-to-jfk-lga-newark-airport-bus/). The New Jersey trip, including why a Newark Penn ticket is not a Newark Airport ticket. Read it if home is in New Jersey or your flight leaves from a New York airport.
3. [Where intercity buses stop in Ithaca and at Cornell](/getting-around/ithaca-intercity-bus-stops-cornell-downtown/). Which stop your carrier uses on campus and downtown. Read it once you have picked a carrier, and again before you leave, since a stop moves on Nov 9.
4. [Cornell Thanksgiving travel: when to book and what it costs](/getting-around/cornell-thanksgiving-break-bus-tickets-when-to-book/). The short break, its peak days and when to buy. Read it now if Thanksgiving is not booked.
5. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The pillar: every way in and out of Ithaca on one page, flights included. Read it when you want a plan B.

## Dates to watch

- Mon Nov 9, 2026: OurBus's downtown Ithaca stop moves to 235 S. Cayuga St.
- Tue Nov 17 and Wed Nov 18, 2026: the cheapest dates seen to DC, Philadelphia and Newark in the Nov 8 fare sample.
- Thanksgiving week, Nov 23 to 27, 2026 (Thanksgiving Day is Thu Nov 26): fares run higher, and the Wednesday before is a peak day.
- Return weekend after Thanksgiving: storm risk on the way back.
- Last day of December finals: a peak travel day.
