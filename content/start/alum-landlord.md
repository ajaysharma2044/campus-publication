You're a Cornell alum, maybe a former Collegetown tenant, who owns a rental near campus or is thinking of buying one. Before you lease to students you need the City and state duties, the student leasing calendar and what the market charges. The decision in front of you: whether the rules and the rents make the property worth running, and how to run it without a costly mistake.

This page is general information, not legal advice.

## Read in this order

1. [Off-campus housing at Cornell: the 2026-27 guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/): how your tenants search, sign and move, from their side. Read it first, so the rest of the path makes sense.
2. [Renting to Cornell students: an Ithaca landlord checklist](/housing/becoming-ithaca-landlord-student-rental-rules/): the Certificate of Compliance, fees, safety duties and what students expect. Read it before you buy or list.
3. [Ithaca's 120-day lease renewal rule and the waiver](/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/): when you may offer a renewal, show the unit or re-let it. Read it before your first leasing season.
4. [Ithaca Good Cause Eviction: renewals and rent increases](/housing/ithaca-good-cause-eviction-rent-increase-students/): whether your building is covered and what counts as an unreasonable increase. Read it before you set next year's rent or decline a renewal.
5. [Ithaca security deposit rules: the cap, 14 days, banned fees](/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/): what you may collect, how to hold it and when it goes back. Read it before you write your first lease.
6. [Ithaca renter rules: trash tags, snow, noise and parties](/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/): which duties stay with the owner and which a lease can pass to tenants. Read it before your first winter.
7. [City of Ithaca or not: why your rental's address matters](/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/): which rules follow the property line, from occupancy to inspections. Read it before you choose a property.
8. [Collegetown rent in 2026: one-bedroom and per-bed prices](/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/): what comparable units ask per bed and per unit. Read it when you price a unit or check a purchase against the rent it can bring.

## Dates to watch

- **Before your current Certificate of Compliance expires:** apply through OpenGov and pay the fee; one 30-day extension is allowed ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance); [fees](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/147/Certificate-of-Compliance-Fees)).
- **120 days before you renew, show or re-let:** written notice to current tenants, unless they initialed the waiver; for a July 31 lease end one reading lands on about April 2, and when the count starts is unresolved ([Ithaca City Code](https://ecode360.com/27390826)).
- **Sept 15 to May 31:** Ithaca's heat minimums apply ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).
- **30 to 90 days before lease end:** Good Cause renewal or increase notices ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal)).
- **By Aug 1 each year:** DHCR usually publishes the CPI figure behind the Good Cause rent standard ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal)).
- **Within 14 days of move-out:** return the deposit with an itemized statement, so by mid-August for a July 31 exit ([NY General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
