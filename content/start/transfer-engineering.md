You want a B.S. from Cornell Engineering, or computer science, which comes through either Engineering's B.S. or the Arts and Sciences B.A. ([Cornell CS](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/bachelor-arts-computer-science/apply)). Engineering asks for a fixed block of math, physics and chemistry courses before it reads an application, admits you straight into one major, and sets its own credit caps and exam score thresholds ([Engineering requirements](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements)). The decisions in front of you are whether that course block will be done or planned in time, which major to name, and how much of your credit will count.

## Dates to watch

- Fall 2026: have MATH 1910, MATH 1920, PHYS 1112 with 1110 and CHEM 2090 with 2091 finished, in progress or planned ([Engineering requirements](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements)).
- March 15, 2027: Transfer Common App, fee or waiver, CSS Profile and FAFSA due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- April 15, 2027: Mid-Term Report, recommendations and all other required materials due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- Right after admission: send the course description form with the Enrollment Response Form; Engineering emailed admitted transfers from early June in 2024 ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- Mid-August (fall 2026 pattern: optional MATH CASE Thursday Aug 20, 9 to 11 a.m., Olin Hall 155): Engineering transfer orientation ([Engineering orientation](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/)).
- Last day of instruction in your first Cornell semester (Dec 7 in 2026): deadline to request AP, IB or other exam credit in writing from the college registrar ([registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services)).
