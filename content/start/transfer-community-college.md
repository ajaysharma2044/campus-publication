You are at a two-year college, often working long hours, sometimes the first in your family at college, and Cornell can look out of reach or unaffordable. Cornell says it has no preference by type of school ([Cornell FAQ](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/)). The decisions in front of you are whether a pathway fits or you apply openly, which courses to take so they transfer, and whether to apply after two semesters or four.

## Dates to watch

- October 1, 2026: FAFSA opens for 2027-28; file the CSS Profile as soon as possible after it ([aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)).
- January 2027: applications open for the SUNY Broome to Cornell online Bachelor of Professional Studies path, a separate online program with a first cohort in August 2027 ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/06/cornell-sce-suny-broome-create-transfer-pathway-new-online-bachelors-degree)).
- March 15, 2027: Transfer Common App, fee or waiver, CSS Profile and FAFSA due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- April 15, 2027: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- Late April through June 2027: rolling decisions; send syllabi when your college asks and keep them until your Cornell degree audit is right.
