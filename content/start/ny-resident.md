You live in New York and you are aiming at CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks or Dyson, the Cornell colleges where New York residents pay contract tuition; many of you are at a SUNY or CUNY school now. The decisions in front of you are which college fits both your subject and your bill, whether a SUNY pathway helps you, and which aid forms to file, HESC included, before March 15, 2027.

## Read in this order

1. [Transferring to Cornell: the complete guide for fall 2027](/transfer/transferring-to-cornell-guide/): the whole process from eligibility to move-in; read it first for the shape of the year.
2. [NY resident transfers: Cornell contract college tuition](/transfer/cornell-contract-colleges-ny-resident-transfer/): which colleges are the state ones, what you pay at each and how residency is decided; read it before you choose a college.
3. [Cornell transfer cost 2026-27: $95,218 to $99,169 billed](/transfer/cornell-transfer-cost-first-year/): the billed cost of the required on-campus year, line by line; read it right after the tuition page to see the whole bill.
4. [Cornell financial aid for transfers: need-based, no merit](/transfer/cornell-financial-aid-transfer-students/): the CSS Profile, FAFSA and HESC, the codes and what aid covers by income; read it before you file.
5. [Transferring into Cornell CALS: majors, courses and GPA](/transfer/cornell-cals-transfer-requirements/): majors, required courses and why you cannot add AEM later; read it if CALS is on your list.
6. [Transferring into Cornell ILR: courses, GPA and essays](/transfer/cornell-ilr-transfer-requirements/): the four required courses and the essays; read it if ILR is on your list.
7. [Transferring into Cornell Human Ecology: courses and GPA](/transfer/cornell-human-ecology-transfer-requirements/): course credits by major and the design supplements; read it if Human Ecology is on your list.
8. [Cornell Brooks School transfer: courses, GPA and timing](/transfer/cornell-brooks-school-transfer-requirements/): the courses Brooks wants and when to apply; read it if public policy or health care policy is your subject.
9. [Cornell Dyson transfer requirements: courses, GPA, dates](/transfer/cornell-dyson-transfer-requirements/): the route into AEM, which runs through Dyson and not CALS; read it if business is your subject.
10. [Is there a guaranteed transfer to Cornell? Every pathway](/transfer/cornell-guaranteed-transfer-pathways/): what the TC3 and SUNY Broome agreements promise and what older SUNY agreements may no longer cover; read it before you plan around one.

## Dates to watch

- October 1, 2026: FAFSA opened for 2027-28; file the CSS Profile as soon as possible after it ([aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)).
- March 15, 2027: Transfer Common App and fee or waiver due, and the CSS Profile and FAFSA; New York residents also file with HESC at hesc.ny.gov, code 0174 for contract colleges or 0175 for endowed ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants); [aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)).
- Early April 2027: official aid offers, tentatively, with 2027-28 prices confirmed before then ([aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)).
- April 15, 2027: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and recommendations due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- Early to mid-June 2027 (2024 pattern): CALS, ILR and Brooks email admitted transfers; Human Ecology asks admits to verify their major June 8 to 30.
- Early July 2027: the fall tuition bill arrives, due before registration ([finances](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances)).
