You are a veteran, or you have been out of school two or more years, and you are older than the typical transfer. Your questions are whether military training or old courses earn credit, who writes your recommendation when no recent professor knows you, whether to use the GI Bill or a Cornell aid package, and where you will live and fit in.

## Read in this order

1. [Transferring to Cornell: the complete guide for fall 2027](/transfer/transferring-to-cornell-guide/): the whole process from eligibility to move-in; read it first for the shape of the year.
2. [Veterans and older students transferring to Cornell](/transfer/veterans-transferring-to-cornell/): your guide, from the Veterans Admissions Team to military credit, the GI Bill and housing; read it right after the main guide.
3. [How many credits transfer to Cornell? Caps and evaluation](/transfer/cornell-transfer-credit-how-many-credits-transfer/): the credit caps and the course-age limit that matter if your college work is years old; read it before you count on any credit.
4. [Cornell transfer application: every piece and who does it](/transfer/cornell-transfer-application-materials/): every form, and the personal or employer recommendation you can send in place of the Academic Evaluation; read it in January.
5. Veterans House Cornell: rooms, cost and how to apply: the Program House you can ask for inside your housing application, with an extra application; read it before you get your NetID.
6. [Cornell transfer orientation and move-in, day by day](/transfer/cornell-transfer-orientation-move-in/): what happens in August and which optional events you can skip; read it once you are admitted.
7. [Making friends as a Cornell transfer: what worked](/transfer/making-friends-as-a-cornell-transfer/): the groups and habits transfers say helped; read it before your first semester.

## Dates to watch

- Before you apply: contact the Veterans Admissions Team ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- March 15, 2027: Transfer Common App, fee or waiver, CSS Profile and FAFSA due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- April 15, 2027: all other materials due, including your recommendation and the Mid-Term Report ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- Within ten days of getting your NetID: apply for housing, add the Veterans House extra application if you want it ([housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)).
- Mid-August 2027 (2026 pattern: August 18 for transfers): move-in and New Student Orientation ([Tatkon Center](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation)).
