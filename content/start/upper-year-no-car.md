You're a sophomore, junior or senior with no car: you walk most places, ride TCAT and take the bus home for breaks. Your first-year bus pass is gone, so you're deciding what to pay for TCAT and whether Carshare or bikeshare fills the gaps. If you're moving off campus, there's a second decision: which neighborhood you can reach on foot or by bus, with groceries and winter hills in the picture. Each topic below has its own path; start with housing if a lease is on the table.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- **Aug 31:** last year's first-year TCAT privileges and OmniRide passes expire ([Cornell bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).
- **Nov 4, 2026:** C2C's new reservation system starts; every rider needs a new account ([Cornell FCS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4)).
- **Nov 9, 2026:** downtown stops move: OurBus to 235 S. Cayuga St, and TCAT's East Seneca stops reshuffle ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/)).
- **Thanksgiving week (Nov 23 to 27, 2026):** OurBus names the Wednesday before Thanksgiving a peak day ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269)).
- **Dec 23 and Jan 6, 2027:** last C2C departure on the "2026" booking link, then first departures on the "2027" link ([C2C](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)).
- **Mar 31:** last day for an OmniRide refund, less $40 ([Cornell bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).
