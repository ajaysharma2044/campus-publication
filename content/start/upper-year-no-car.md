You're a sophomore, junior or senior, on campus or in Collegetown, with no car. You know the campus, walk most places and take the bus home, but your first-year bus pass is gone and you may be paying your own way. The decision in front of you: what to pay for TCAT, whether Carshare or bikeshare fills the gaps, and how to get home for breaks without overpaying.

## Read in this order

1. [Getting around Cornell after first year without a car](/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-upper-years-no-car/): your whole year in one guide. Read it first.
2. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/): when you ride free, when you pay, and which routes to use. Read it before you decide on a pass.
3. [Ithaca Carshare for Cornell students: plans, cost, rules](/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/): the two student plans and who qualifies. Read it when you need a car for a grocery run, an errand or a trip out of town.
4. [Ithaca Bikeshare cost for Cornell students, 2026 prices](/getting-around/ithaca-bikeshare-cost-cornell-students/): what a short hop costs. Read it if you'd ride for trips the bus doesn't cover well.
5. [Uber, Lyft or taxi in Ithaca: what works and when](/getting-around/uber-lyft-taxi-ithaca-cornell/): late nights, early flights and the times rides are hard to get. Read it before you count on one at 2 a.m.
6. [NYC to Cornell by bus: C2C, OurBus, FlixBus, Greyhound](/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/): every way to the city compared. Read it before you book a break trip.
7. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the full topic, from airports to parking. Read it when a question falls outside this path.

## Dates to watch

- **Aug 31:** last year's first-year TCAT privileges and OmniRide passes expire ([Cornell bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).
- **Nov 4, 2026:** C2C's new reservation system starts; every rider needs a new account ([Cornell FCS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4)).
- **Nov 9, 2026:** downtown stops move: OurBus to 235 S. Cayuga St, and TCAT's East Seneca stops reshuffle ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/)).
- **Thanksgiving week (Nov 23 to 27, 2026):** OurBus names the Wednesday before Thanksgiving a peak day ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269)).
- **Dec 23 and Jan 6, 2027:** last C2C departure on the "2026" booking link, then first departures on the "2027" link ([C2C](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)).
- **Mar 31:** last day for an OmniRide refund, less $40 ([Cornell bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).
