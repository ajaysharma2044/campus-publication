You are coming to Ithaca with a spouse, partner or child: most often as a grad or professional student on one stipend, sometimes as an undergraduate transfer. You need a family-sized place on a 12-month lease, a school district, health coverage for the people you bring and a way for your partner to get around. If you are a grad student, the choice is Hasbrouck or a private rental; if you are a transfer, Hasbrouck is not open to you, so the first questions are the one-year on-campus rule and what an exemption takes.

## Read in this order: Off-campus housing

1. [Off-campus housing at Cornell: the 2026-27 guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/): the whole topic on one page, from rules to rents to renewals. Read it first for the map.
2. [Cornell family housing in Ithaca: costs and options](/housing/cornell-student-family-housing-ithaca/): Hasbrouck family units against private two- and three-bedrooms, who may live with you, schools and your partner's commute. Read it next; it is written for you.
3. [Where Cornell grad students live in Ithaca, and when to look](/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/): the neighborhoods grad households choose and the grad leasing calendar. Read it before you start searching.
4. [City of Ithaca or not: why your rental's address matters](/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/): which rules and protections follow the address once you look past the city line toward Lansing or Cayuga Heights. Read it before you shortlist a house or complex.
5. [Ithaca Good Cause Eviction: renewals and rent increases](/housing/ithaca-good-cause-eviction-rent-increase-students/): what protects a family that wants to stay a second or third year. Read it before your first renewal notice.
6. [Eviction or a vacate order in Ithaca: what happens next](/housing/ithaca-eviction-vacate-order-what-to-do/): the court process and where to get help. Read it if a notice ever arrives; with children in the home, know it before you need it.

## Dates to watch: Off-campus housing

- March to April: start looking for an August start (grad timing); Hasbrouck takes applications on a rolling basis.
- April (April 6 in 2026; confirm the 2027 date with Cornell): Cornell housing application due, including the dependents or married exemption for undergrads; an exemption lasts a single academic year, so reapply each year.
- On move-in: tell the landlord the name of any occupant not on the lease within 30 days.
- 30 to 90 days before your lease ends: the window for a Good Cause renewal notice.
- July 31: most Ithaca leases end.
- By August 1: DHCR usually publishes the CPI figure behind the Good Cause rent standard.

3. [Cornell two-year housing requirement: who is exempt](/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/): the one-year on-campus rule for new transfers and how an individual exemption is requested. Read it before you sign any lease.
4. Cornell family housing in Ithaca: costs and options (in the housing path above): family units against private rentals, schools and your partner's commute. Read it once you know whether you must live on campus.
5. [Veterans and older students transferring to Cornell](/transfer/veterans-transferring-to-cornell/): the rules that change for student veterans and students returning after time away, including housing. Read it if either describes you.

## Dates to watch: Transfer

- Within ten days of getting a NetID: apply for housing through the Housing and Dining Portal; any exemption request goes with the housing application.
- Mid-June: housing confirmations or a place on the waitlist; June 15 is the no-penalty date to cancel a fall housing license for leave or deferral (2026 pattern).
- July or August: Student Health Plan dependent coverage starts, from July or from August, with a different rate for each.
- July 31 (2026 pattern): Student Health Plan waiver deadline for fall new admits; after it, waivers lock.
- August 1: Student Health Plan coverage starts for new fall students.
