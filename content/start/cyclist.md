You ride a bike, an e-bike or an e-scooter to class on a hilly campus, maybe all year. Theft is common, and winter riding has no official guidance. The decisions in front of you: how to register, lock and store what you ride, whether to keep riding when the snow comes, and when Bikeshare or a bus with a bike rack does the job better.

## Read in this order

1. [Biking at Cornell: e-bike rules, scooters and theft](/getting-around/cornell-bike-ebike-scooter-rules-theft/): registration, where you may lock up, the e-bike and scooter limits, battery charging and how bikes get stolen. Read it before your first week of riding.
2. [Biking in Ithaca in winter: what it's like at Cornell](/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/): what riders say about snow, salt, the Slope and the bridges. Read it in early November, before the first snow.
3. [Ithaca Bikeshare cost for Cornell students, 2026 prices](/getting-around/ithaca-bikeshare-cost-cornell-students/): what a ride, a day and a month cost, and where you may leave the bike. Read it if you're deciding between your own bike and a shared one.
4. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/): when you ride free and which routes climb the hill, so you can put the bike on the front rack. Read it before the first icy morning.
5. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the full topic, including taking a bike home on the C2C bus. Read it when a question falls outside this path.

## Dates to watch

- **Mid to late Aug (North Campus move-in Mon Aug 17, Tue Aug 18, Fri Aug 21 to Sat Aug 22; year inferred as 2026):** register the bike and buy a U-lock before classes start ([Cornell move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).
- **Nov 4, 2026:** C2C's new reservation system starts; every rider needs a new account, including anyone booking a bike box home ([Cornell FCS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4)).
- **Dec 23 and Jan 6, 2027:** last C2C departure on the "2026" booking link, then first departures on the "2027" link ([C2C](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)).
- **Three times a year:** TCAT changes schedules (the fall 2025 schedule ran to Jan 11, 2026); check your bus-and-rack route each time ([TCAT](https://tcatbus.com/about/about-tcat/)).
- **Mid-May (2 p.m. Sun May 17, 2026):** buildings close for move-out and unclaimed items are donated; move the bike out of the bike room before then ([Cornell storage and shipping](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)).
