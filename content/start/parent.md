You have no NetID, so you see Cornell from the outside: you pay public fares when you come for move-in, Family Weekend, Commencement or Reunion, and you cannot see the Cornell housing marketplace when your student starts looking off campus. You are often the one paying, for trips home, for the rent and the deposit, and sometimes as a co-signer, and you want to compare that cost with Cornell's bill and know the building and the landlord are sound. Each topic below has its own path; read the guides in order, and each one has the details and its sources.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [Visiting Cornell without a car: a parent's guide](/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-parents-visitors/). Your guide: airport, buses, taxis and parking for a visit, by reason for coming. Read it first, before you book flights or a hotel.
2. [ITH, SYR or ELM: which airport to fly into for Cornell](/getting-around/ith-syr-or-elm-which-airport-for-cornell/). The three airports side by side, including the ride from each one. Read it while you compare flights.
3. [Cornell to Ithaca airport: TCAT 32 and 72, taxis, parking](/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/). Getting between the Ithaca airport and campus. Read it once you have chosen ITH, and before you book an early flight out.
4. [Cornell move-in day parking: where to unload and park](/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/). Unloading, then where to move the car. Read it in the weeks before move-in.
5. [Cornell C2C bus: who can ride, fares and how to book](/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/). Cornell's New York City bus, which you can ride too. Read it if you, or your student, are travelling to or from the city.
6. [Parking in Collegetown and downtown Ithaca: 2026 rates](/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/). Garages, meters and what is closed. Read it before you drive into town for dinner or a hotel.
7. [Uber, Lyft or taxi in Ithaca: what works and when](/getting-around/uber-lyft-taxi-ithaca-cornell/). What works without a car, and when it gets thin. Read it if you are not renting one.
8. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The pillar: every way in and out of Ithaca on one page. Read it when you want the full picture or a backup plan.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- After Aug 1: the Move-In Ticket can be printed from the Housing and Dining Portal.
- Mon Aug 17, Tue Aug 18 and Fri Aug 21 to Sat Aug 22: North Campus move-in (the brief infers the year as 2026).
- Nov 4, 2026 onward: the new C2C booking system needs a new account, even for past riders.
- Nov 9, 2026: downtown intercity stops move, with OurBus going to 235 S. Cayuga St.
- Mid-May: buildings close for move-out (2 p.m. Sun May 17 in 2026), and Commencement hotels often sell out.

Not in the brief: the 2027 dates for Family Weekend, Commencement and Reunion.

## Dates to watch: Housing

- September to November of sophomore year: groups that want Collegetown sign for junior year, and the first month and deposit may be due at signing.
- Jan 31: the last day returning juniors and seniors can cancel a Cornell license for $50; it rises to $500 from Feb 1.
- Apr 6 (in 2026): the Cornell housing application is due, including exemption requests.
- On or about Aug 1: most leases start; the landlord must offer a move-in inspection before your student moves in.
- July 31: most leases end, and the deposit or an itemized statement is due within 14 days of moving out.

Not in the brief: the 2027 date for the Cornell housing application.
