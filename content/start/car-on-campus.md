You live in university housing on North, West, South or at Hasbrouck, and you have a car here or are deciding whether to bring one. The questions in front of you: is a permit worth buying, where can the car sit overnight once snow season starts, and what happens to it over breaks. These pages answer them in the order you will hit them.

## Read in this order

1. [Having a car at Cornell: permits, winter, breaks, costs](/getting-around/having-a-car-at-cornell-student-guide/). Your guide to the whole year with a car; read it first, before the permit sale.
2. [Cornell student parking permits: cost, rules, who qualifies](/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/). Which permit you can buy, what it costs and how the sale morning works; read it in July.
3. [Cornell and Ithaca winter parking: odd/even, bays, breaks](/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/). Where the car can stay overnight from November and where to leave it over winter break; read it in late October.
4. [Winter storms and Cornell travel: driving, buses, flights](/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/). What driving the hills and highways is like in a storm and when to change plans; read it before you drive home for Thanksgiving.
5. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The whole topic, for the trips where the car stays parked: buses, airports and TCAT.

## Tools

## Dates to watch

- **Tue Aug 4, 2026, 9 a.m.:** student permit sales open, first come first served ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)).
- **Aug 16:** the new permit year starts, valid Aug 16, 2026 to Aug 15, 2027 ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)).
- **Nov 1:** the city odd/even on-street parking season begins, to Apr 1 ([City of Ithaca code](https://ecode360.com/8395630)).
- **Dec 1 to Apr 1:** Cornell winter overnight parking rules ([winter and break parking rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)).
- **Mar 31:** last day to return your permit for a prorated refund ([student parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)).
