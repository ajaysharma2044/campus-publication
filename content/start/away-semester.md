You're a junior or senior on a 12-month Ithaca lease, or a student holding a Cornell housing license, and you'll study abroad, take leave or do a semester internship. Your housemates may stay behind on a joint lease. The decision in front of you: sublet, assign, negotiate a way out or cancel your Cornell license, and what each one leaves you owing while you're gone.

## Read in this order

1. [Off-campus housing at Cornell: the 2026-27 guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/): the whole topic in one place. Skim it first so the lease terms in the next guides make sense.
2. [Breaking an Ithaca lease to study abroad or leave Cornell](/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/): your options side by side, from a sublet to a written release. Read it as soon as your away term is confirmed.
3. [How to sublet an Ithaca apartment and get landlord consent](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/): the written request, the landlord's clocks and finding a subtenant. Read it two to three months before you leave.
4. [Finding off-campus roommates at Cornell: joint liability](/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/): what your housemates owe if your share goes unpaid. Read it with them before you send any request.
5. [Renters insurance off campus at Cornell: need and cost](/housing/cornell-off-campus-renters-insurance-cost/): whether your policy covers a subtenant and what you leave behind. Read it before you hand over keys.
6. [Moving out of an Ithaca apartment: inspection to deposit](/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/): the inspection, storage and the deposit return. Read it the month your lease or sublease ends.

## Dates to watch

- **Two to three months before you leave:** send the sublet request by certified mail, return receipt ([N.Y. Real Property Law 226-B](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).
- **Early April:** Storage Scholars asks for bookings ([Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny)).
- **Dec 1 (spring term; June 15 for fall):** last day to cancel a 2026-27 Cornell license penalty-free for study away, leave or an internship ([Cornell 2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027)).
- **June 15, 2027 or Dec 1, 2027:** the same deadlines for a 2027-28 license ([Cornell 2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).
- **July 31:** most Ithaca leases end; the deposit is due back within 14 days of moving out ([N.Y. General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
