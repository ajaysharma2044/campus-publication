You're working, interning or doing research in Ithaca from May to August. You either keep your 12-month lease, sublet someone else's place, or bridge the gap between a dorm and an off-campus lease, and your lease, bus pass and parking permit each end on a different summer date. The decisions in front of you: where you sleep in June and July, what to store or keep, how you'll get around once term ends, and what to renew before the August dates.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/): whether your pass still covers you over the summer and which routes to use. Read it first, before you plan a summer commute.
2. [Cornell move-out: storage, shipping and loading zones](/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/): storage, shipping and the building-closing deadline. Read it in March, since Storage Scholars advises booking summer storage by early April.
3. [Ithaca Bikeshare cost for Cornell students, 2026 prices](/getting-around/ithaca-bikeshare-cost-cornell-students/): what a month of bikeshare costs. Read it in May if you'd ride to a summer job or lab.
4. [Ithaca Carshare for Cornell students: plans, cost, rules](/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/): the two student plans and who qualifies. Read it if your summer job or errands need a car now and then.
5. [Cornell student parking permits: cost, rules, who qualifies](/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/): when your permit ends and when the next one goes on sale. Read it in July if you keep a car on campus into the fall.
6. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the full topic, from airports to parking. Read it when a question falls outside this path.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- **Early April:** book summer storage; Storage Scholars advises booking by then ([Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny)).
- **Mid-May (2 p.m. Sun May 17, 2026):** buildings close for move-out; items left behind are donated ([Cornell housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)).
- **June:** heat can pull TCAT buses from service, as it did in June 2025 ([Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/06/tcat-reduces-service-due-to-overheating-buses)).
- **Aug 15:** old student parking permits expire ([Cornell parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)).
- **Aug 16:** the new permit year starts, valid Aug 16, 2026 to Aug 15, 2027 ([Cornell FCS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)).
- **Aug 31:** the previous year's first-year TCAT privileges and OmniRide passes expire ([Cornell bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

## Dates to watch: Housing

- **Mid-May (2 p.m. Sun May 17, 2026):** dorm buildings close ([Cornell housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)).
- **May 31:** some leases end (Dryden South leases end May 31, 2027) and Ithaca's heat season ends ([Dryden South listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961); [Cornell Law tenant guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).
- **June 15:** penalty-free deadline to cancel a fall Cornell housing license ([Cornell housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027)).
- **July 31:** most Ithaca leases end, and City residential parking permits expire ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)).
- **About Aug 1:** most new 12-month leases start ([209 Stewart listing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095)).
- **Mid-August:** your deposit is due back within 14 days of moving out, so by mid-August for a July 31 exit ([NY GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
