You are a junior, senior or grad student on a 10- or 12-month lease in Collegetown, downtown, Fall Creek or West Hill, most likely ending July 31, and you are deciding whether to renew, move or leave Ithaca. Two sets of choices sit in front of you: the lease itself (renewal, rent increases, repairs, the deposit and moving out), and how you get to class and, if you keep a car, where it sleeps, since campus resident permits are not open to you. Each topic below has its own path; start with housing if a renewal offer or a repair is on your mind.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [Off-campus at Cornell: commute, buses and parking by area](/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/): your guide, area by area. Read it first, ideally before you sign a lease or decide whether the car comes to Ithaca.
2. [Parking in Collegetown and downtown Ithaca: 2026 rates](/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/): street zones, city garages and the residential permit. Read it if the car is coming, before you pick where to keep it.
3. [Cornell and Ithaca winter parking: odd/even, bays, breaks](/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/): which side of the street, and what to do with the car before a break. Read it before November if you park on a city street.
4. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/): when you ride free, when you pay, and whether a pass is worth it. Read it when you plan your daily commute, and again when routes change.
5. [Ithaca Carshare for Cornell students: plans, cost, rules](/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/): the no-car option for grocery runs and day trips. Read it if you are deciding whether to leave the car at home.
6. [Biking at Cornell: e-bike rules, scooters and theft](/getting-around/cornell-bike-ebike-scooter-rules-theft/): what you can ride on campus and how to keep the bike. Read it before you commit to riding up the hill.
7. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the whole topic, including buses home and airports. Read it when your next break comes up.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- **Nov 9, 2026:** downtown stops move; TCAT's East Seneca stops are reshuffled ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/)).
- **Nov 1 to Apr 1:** the city's odd/even on-street parking season, when activated ([Ithaca code](https://ecode360.com/8395630)).
- **Before any break:** move the car off the street.
- **Mar 31:** last day for a prorated Cornell permit refund ([Cornell parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)).
- **July 31:** the city residential parking permit expires; renew for the new lease year ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)).
- **Early Aug (Tue Aug 4, 2026, 9 a.m.):** Cornell student permit sales open; commuter permits prorate daily from Aug 16 ([Cornell](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)).

## Read in this order: Housing

1. [Off-campus housing at Cornell: the 2026-27 guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/): the whole topic on one page, from rents and leases to repairs and moving out. Read it first for the map.

3. [Ithaca's 120-day lease renewal rule and the waiver](/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/): when your landlord may ask you to renew or show your unit, and what the first-page waiver gives up. Read it as soon as a renewal offer arrives.
4. [Ithaca Good Cause Eviction: renewals and rent increases](/housing/ithaca-good-cause-eviction-rent-increase-students/): whether your building is covered and how big an increase can be. Read it before you answer a renewal with a higher rent.
5. [When an Ithaca landlord won't fix heat, mold or bed bugs](/housing/ithaca-landlord-wont-fix-heat-mold-bed-bugs/): the steps from a written request to an inspection, and why not to withhold rent first. Read it the first time a repair stalls.
6. [Ithaca renter rules: trash tags, snow, noise and parties](/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/): the City rules that land on tenants, including where cars may sit on the lot. Read it in your first weeks and again before the first snow.
7. [Renters insurance off campus at Cornell: need and cost](/housing/cornell-off-campus-renters-insurance-cost/): what a policy covers and what it costs. Read it before move-in or at renewal.
8. [Ithaca security deposit rules: the cap, 14 days, banned fees](/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/): what a landlord may hold and deduct. Read it before you sign a renewal and again before you leave.
9. [Moving out of an Ithaca apartment: inspection to deposit](/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/): the inspection, the photos and the 14-day clock. Read it a month before your lease ends.
10. [Eviction or a vacate order in Ithaca: what happens next](/housing/ithaca-eviction-vacate-order-what-to-do/): the court process, a City vacate order and where to get help. Read it if a notice ever arrives.
11. [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown Ithaca: where to live](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/): rent, noise and the trip to campus, area by area. Read it if you are deciding whether to move next year.

## Dates to watch: Housing

- **Sept 15:** Ithaca's heat season starts (to May 31) ([Cornell Law tenants' advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).
- **About December:** the 120-day rule's sponsor put the renewal decision for August leases here (his example) ([Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/)).
- **30 to 90 days before your lease ends:** the Good Cause renewal notice window ([NY RPL 216](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/216)).
- **Two weeks before move-out:** schedule the final inspection and change your address ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out)).
- **July 31:** most leases end; City residential parking permits expire ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)).
- **By mid-August:** your deposit or an itemized statement is due, 14 days after a July 31 exit ([NY GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
