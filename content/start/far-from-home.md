Your home is in the Midwest, South or West, a bus does not reach it, and every break means a flight from Ithaca (ITH), Syracuse (SYR) or a New York City airport. The decision in front of you is which airport to fly from, how to get there from campus, and when to book, with luggage as a real cost and Thanksgiving short enough that a full travel day each way counts. Read these in order; each guide carries the details and its sources.

## Read in this order

1. [Flying home from Cornell: airports, rides, bags, timing](/getting-around/flying-home-from-cornell-airports-rides/). Your guide: the whole trip from dorm to gate, by airport and by break. Read it first, before anyone buys a ticket.
2. [ITH, SYR or ELM: which airport to fly into for Cornell](/getting-around/ith-syr-or-elm-which-airport-for-cornell/). The airport choice side by side. Read it while your family compares flights.
3. [Syracuse airport to Cornell: OurBus, car service, driving](/getting-around/syracuse-airport-to-cornell-ourbus-car-service/). Getting between campus and SYR. Read it once SYR is on the table.
4. [Cornell to Ithaca airport: TCAT 32 and 72, taxis, parking](/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/). Getting between campus and ITH, including early flights. Read it before you pick a departure time.
5. [Cornell Thanksgiving travel: when to book and what it costs](/getting-around/cornell-thanksgiving-break-bus-tickets-when-to-book/). The short break and its peak days. Read it now if Thanksgiving is not booked.
6. [Cornell move-out: storage, shipping and loading zones](/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/). What to do with the things that won't fit in a checked bag. Read it before May finals.
7. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The pillar: every way in and out of Ithaca on one page. Read it when you want the full picture or a plan B.

## Dates to watch

- Oct 1 to Dec 1: winter break housing applications, if you decide to stay.
- Thanksgiving Day, Thu Nov 26, 2026: the Wednesday before is a peak travel day.
- Return weekend after Thanksgiving: storm risk on the way back.
- December finals: the Student Assembly has funded free airport charters in past finals weeks; none found for fall 2026 as of Nov 8.
- Mid-May: buildings close for move-out (2 p.m. Sun May 17 in 2026).

## When you're done

If you are flying out for winter break, read [Getting home for Cornell winter break, 2026-27](/getting-around/cornell-winter-break-travel-home-2026/); if you are staying, read [Staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).
Everything else on buses, parking and getting around town is on the [getting around index](/getting-around/).
