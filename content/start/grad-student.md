You are a PhD student, a graduate assistant, or a master's or professional student. The undergraduate residential requirement does not apply to you, so you most likely rent off campus, in a later market than undergraduates, and you probably want somewhere quiet and away from undergraduate Collegetown. Two decisions follow: where to live and on what lease, and how you get to campus from there (a free or paid TCAT ride, a car and a permit, or Carshare). Each topic below has its own path; read the housing one first if you have not signed yet.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the whole topic on one page, from airports and buses to permits. Read it first for the map.
2. [Cornell grad students: free TCAT, parking and what you pay](/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-cornell-grad-students/): what changes for PhD students, assistants and professional students, case by case. Read it next; it is written for you.
3. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/): who rides free, which routes serve the grad neighborhoods, and passes for a partner. Read it before your first commute.
4. [Cornell student parking permits: cost, rules, who qualifies](/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/): which permits an assistantship covers and what everyone else pays. Read it before permit sales open in August.
5. [Ithaca Carshare for Cornell students: plans, cost, rules](/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/): the two student plans and who can join. Read it if you are weighing Carshare against owning a car.
6. [Cornell C2C bus: who can ride, fares and how to book](/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/): personal and department-billed fares, and booking for NYC or Weill trips. Read it before your first work trip.
7. [Parking in Collegetown and downtown Ithaca: 2026 rates](/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/): garages, meters and the city residential permit. Read it if your apartment has no parking.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- Aug 16: the permit year starts (free A or E permits with a full assistantship).
- Fall registration: your OmniRide turns on; activation can take 24 hours.
- Nov 1: the city's odd/even on-street parking season begins (to Apr 1, when activated).
- Nov 4, 2026: the new C2C reservation system launched; every rider needs a new account.
- Mar 31: the last day for a refund on a paid OmniRide or permit.
- Aug 31: OmniRide passes expire; renew for the next year.

## Dates to watch: Housing

- March to April: start looking for an August lease; Hasbrouck takes rolling applications on 12-month contracts.
- June to July: a few places remain for grad students.
- About August 1: most 12-month leases start; most end July 31.
- About April 2: 120 days back from a July 31 lease end (one reading of the City's renewal notice rule; the count start is unverified).
- 30 to 90 days before your lease ends: the Good Cause renewal notice window.
- 14 days after you move out: your deposit is due back, so by mid-August for a July 31 exit.
