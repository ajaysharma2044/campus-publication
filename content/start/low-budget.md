You pay your own way or rely on aid, as an upper-year undergrad or a grad on a thin stipend, and every $10 matters. Two decisions sit in front of you: the cheapest workable place to live, knowing that once you leave campus aid does not cover the deposit, the first month or summer rent, and the cheapest way to get home and around Ithaca, including whether a short break is worth the trip at all. Read each path in order; each guide carries the numbers and their sources.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [The cheapest ways to get home and around Cornell](/getting-around/cheapest-ways-to-get-around-cornell/). Your guide: the lowest-cost way to do every trip, from the bus home to the ride across campus. Read it first, before you book anything.
2. [Cornell Thanksgiving travel: when to book and what it costs](/getting-around/cornell-thanksgiving-break-bus-tickets-when-to-book/). The short break where waiting costs the most. Read it now if Thanksgiving is not booked.
3. [Staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks: housing, food, rides](/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/). What staying costs against the fare home. Read it before you decide to go, and before winter break housing closes.
4. [Ithaca Bikeshare cost for Cornell students, 2026 prices](/getting-around/ithaca-bikeshare-cost-cornell-students/). Plans, prices and the low-income option. Read it if buses don't go where or when you need.
5. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The pillar: every way in and out of Ithaca on one page. Read it when you want the full picture or a plan B.

## Dates to watch: Getting around

- Oct 1 to Dec 1: winter break housing applications, if you decide to stay.
- About two weeks before each break: the booking window Wanderu claims is cheapest.
- Thanksgiving week, Nov 23 to 27, 2026: when the lowest advertised bus fares ran 28% to 38% above earlier November days in a Nov 8 sample; OurBus names the Wednesday before Thanksgiving a peak day.
- December finals: the Student Assembly has funded free airport charters in past finals weeks; none found for fall 2026 as of Nov 8.
- January: Wanderu showed Ithaca to NYC fares averaging about $41, against about $66 in November.
- Mar 31: last day for an OmniRide refund, if you bought one and ride less than you planned.

## Dates to watch: Housing

- Early November and early February: co-op selection rounds; each house runs its own.
- Fall: the big Collegetown houses sell out first; on Nov 8, 2026, Ithaca Renting showed 14 of 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 sold out.
- Spring: downtown leftovers and Lansing promotions.
- At signing: the first month and a deposit are due, and aid covers neither.
- Each term: an aid refund for living costs, if your aid exceeds your Bursar charges.
- July 31: most leases end; your deposit is due back within 14 days of moving out.
