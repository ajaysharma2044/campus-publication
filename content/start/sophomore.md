You are a second-year living in a residence hall, a co-op or a Greek house this year, and your junior-year lease is free to sign. The catch is timing: the Collegetown rush for that lease runs in the fall of your sophomore year, often during prelims, so the decisions on where, with whom and when come before you feel ready. Read these in order and you will have made them.

## Read in this order

1. [Off-campus housing at Cornell: the 2026-27 guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/): the whole market on one page. Read it first, in your first weeks back in the fall.
2. [Cornell sophomores: how to get a good junior-year lease](/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/): your plan for this year, step by step. Read it straight after the guide.
3. [Who can live off campus at Cornell: the rule and exemptions](/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/): why your lease must start junior year, not this year. Read it before you sign anything.
4. [When to sign a Collegetown lease, and when waiting is fine](/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/): whether you need to move in the fall rush or can wait for spring. Read it by September.
5. [Finding off-campus roommates at Cornell: joint liability](/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/): how to pick a group and what you owe for each other. Read it before your group settles on a size.
6. [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown Ithaca: where to live](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/): the neighborhoods side by side. Read it once you know your group.
7. [Collegetown rent in 2026: one-bedroom and per-bed prices](/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/): what a bed costs by unit size. Read it before you tour.
8. [Collegetown landlords: how to check one before you sign](/housing/ithaca-landlords-to-avoid-collegetown-reviews/): the checks to run on a landlord and a building. Read it once you have a shortlist.
9. [Reading an Ithaca lease before you sign: what to check](/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/): the clauses and fees to look for. Read it with the lease in front of you.
10. [Ithaca's 120-day lease renewal rule and the waiver](/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/): what the waiver on page one means. Read it before you initial anything.

## Dates to watch

- **Late August to September:** Collegetown leasing for the next year opens as students arrive ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/12/hurt-for-housing-begins-early-causing-stress-for-students/)).
- **Early October:** Cornell's fall room selection for the next year (Oct 2 to 8 in 2025) and prelims ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)).
- **Early November:** the co-op selection round ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)).
- **Jan 31:** last day to cancel a junior-year Cornell housing license for $50, for 2027-28 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).
- **February to April:** the later search for downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing; second co-op round in early February ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)).
- **On or about Aug 1 before junior year:** the lease starts; take the landlord's move-in inspection ([NY General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
