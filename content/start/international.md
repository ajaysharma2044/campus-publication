You are coming to Cornell from another country, with no US citizenship or approved permanent residency, probably no car, no SSN and often no US credit history. If you are transferring from another college, your first deadline is aid: the CSS Profile is due March 15, and missing it closes Cornell aid for good; after admission you move your F-1 or J-1 record to Cornell. Then come the same choices every international student faces: where to live, how to get here, and whether to go home or stay in Ithaca when breaks are too short for the trip.

## Read in this order: Getting around

1. [JFK to Cornell for international students, plus breaks](/getting-around/getting-to-cornell-international-students/): your guide to the whole trip, from landing to campus and through each break. Read it first, before you book a flight.
2. [Ithaca to JFK, LaGuardia or Newark airport without a car](/getting-around/ithaca-to-jfk-lga-newark-airport-bus/): how to link a New York airport with Ithaca in both directions. Read it while you compare flights.
3. [ITH, SYR or ELM: which airport to fly into for Cornell](/getting-around/ith-syr-or-elm-which-airport-for-cornell/): whether to skip New York and connect to a closer airport, and what each one costs you in rides and risk. Read it before you pick the last leg.
4. [NYC to Cornell by bus: C2C, OurBus, FlixBus, Greyhound](/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/): which bus to book from the city, where it leaves and how much slack to leave after landing. Read it once your flight is booked.
5. [Staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks: housing, food, rides](/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/): break housing, what stays open and how to get around a quiet town. Read it in early fall, before the housing window closes.
6. [Winter storms and Cornell travel: driving, buses, flights](/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/): what to do when snow cancels your bus or flight on the way back. Read it before you book the January return.
7. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the full topic, from TCAT to parking, for everything after you arrive. Read it in your first week on campus.

- Nov 26, 2026: Thanksgiving Day; some students from far away stay in Ithaca.

## Dates to watch: Housing

- Up to 120 days before your program starts: the earliest your visa appointment can be.
- March to April (grad students) or the fall before (undergrads who want Collegetown): search and sign.
- About Aug 1: when most Ithaca leases start; confirm your move-in date and what is furnished before you fly.
- On arrival: take the move-in inspection and photos, and buy renters insurance.
- Winter break: ask housing@cornell.edu if you need a campus option over the break.
- Aug 31 of the following year: your free first-year TCAT rides end if you started in the fall.

## Dates to watch: Transfer

- October 1, 2026 onward: file the CSS Profile as soon as possible.
- March 15, 2027: the application, the $85 fee and the CSS Profile; miss the aid deadline and Cornell aid is closed for your whole undergraduate career.
- April 15, 2027: all other materials, with official English translations of anything not in English.
- Early April 2027: aid offers, "tentatively," for those already admitted.
- Late April through June 2027: decisions; then the F-1 or J-1 SEVIS transfer, and a housing application within ten days of getting your NetID.
- Mid-August (2026 pattern: Aug 18 for transfers): move-in and orientation.
