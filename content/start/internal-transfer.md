You are already enrolled at Cornell and want to move to another Cornell college, including if you came to Cornell as an external transfer and now want to switch. Moving between colleges is internal transfer, a separate track with its own deadlines and GPA rules, not the Transfer Common App process that shows up first when you search. The decision in front of you is which college you can still apply to, whether you are eligible, and which deadline you are working toward.

## Read in this order

1. [Transferring between Cornell colleges: rules by college](/academics/how-hard-is-it-to-transfer-colleges-within-cornell/): the guide for your situation, how internal transfer works across the colleges; read it first, before you pick a target college.
2. [Transferring into Cornell Arts and Sciences: the rules](/transfer/cornell-arts-and-sciences-transfer-requirements/): the A&S rules, including when a student who transferred into A&S may move to another college; read it if you are leaving A&S or applying into it.
3. [Cornell Hotel School transfer: courses, GPA and dates](/transfer/cornell-hotel-school-transfer-requirements/): the Hotel School's courses and dates, and who is not eligible for its internal process; read it before the Hotel office hours if Hotel is your target.
4. [Can you transfer to Cornell for spring? Fall only now](/transfer/cornell-spring-transfer-fall-only/): why spring entry is closed to students from other schools, and why that rule is not yours; read it if a search told you spring is impossible.

## Dates to watch

- November 18, 2026: Hotel School internal transfer office hours ([Hotel School](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/)).
- December 1, 2026: internal transfer deadline for spring entry at the Hotel School (11:59 pm ET) and AAP ([Hotel School](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/); [AAP](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/)).
- Early January 2027: Hotel School decisions for spring entry ([Hotel School](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/)).
- May 1, 2027 (2026 pattern; recheck when the 2027 dates appear): internal transfer deadline for fall entry at the Hotel School, AAP and Arts and Sciences ([A&S](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process)).
- Late June 2027: Hotel School decisions for fall entry ([Hotel School](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/)).

## When you're done

Apply through the college you want, not through the Transfer Common App, and confirm its current deadline on that college's page.
For the external transfer rules this track replaces, see the [transfer guide](/transfer/transferring-to-cornell-guide/) or the topic page at [/transfer/](/transfer/).
