You are staying in Ithaca for Thanksgiving, winter break or both: home is far, the trip costs too much, or work or research keeps you here. The decisions in front of you are whether to apply for winter break housing, how you will get groceries and errands done while dining is closed and buses may run holiday service, and where a car can sit safely while campus empties. Read these in order; each guide carries the details and its sources.

## Read in this order

1. [Staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks: housing, food, rides](/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/). Your guide: break housing, food, mail and getting around while campus is closed. Read it first, before the housing deadline.
2. [Cornell and Ithaca winter parking: odd/even, bays, breaks](/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/). Where a car can stay over break and where it gets towed. Read it before campus empties if you have a car here.
3. [Ithaca Carshare for Cornell students: plans, cost, rules](/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/). A car by the hour for grocery runs and errands. Read it early, since joining takes time.
4. [Uber, Lyft or taxi in Ithaca: what works and when](/getting-around/uber-lyft-taxi-ithaca-cornell/). What to expect from a ride when the town is quiet. Read it before you count on one.
5. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/). Which buses reach the stores and when you ride free. Read it before the first break week.
6. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/). The pillar: every way in and out of Ithaca on one page. Read it when you want the full picture or decide to travel after all.

## Dates to watch

- Oct 1 to Dec 1: winter break housing applications.
- Nov 25 to 29, 2026: Thanksgiving break, with Thanksgiving Day on Thu Nov 26.
- Dec 1: Cornell winter overnight parking rules start (to Apr 1).
- Sun Dec 20, 2026, 2 p.m.: undergraduate residences close for anyone without approved winter break housing.
- Dec 23 to Jan 7: no winter-break mail or packages.
- Tue Jan 19, 2027, 9 a.m.: residences reopen; spring term begins Jan 25, 2027.
