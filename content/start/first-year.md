You started at Cornell this fall, you live in university housing, most likely on North Campus, and you probably have no car. You ride TCAT free with your Cornell ID, but you don't yet know the stops, the booking links or the peak days. The decisions in front of you: how you get home for Thanksgiving and winter break and back, and whether a car is worth bringing at all.

## Read in this order

1. [Cornell first-year transportation: buses, home, airports](/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-first-year/): your whole first year in one guide. Read it first, this week.
2. [NYC to Cornell by bus: C2C, OurBus, FlixBus, Greyhound](/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/): which bus fits your side of Manhattan, your bags and your budget. Read it before you book any trip home.
3. [Cornell C2C bus: who can ride, fares and how to book](/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/): the new account, the student fare and the cancellation rules. Read it if C2C is your pick, before you sign in.
4. [Is TCAT free for Cornell students? Hours, routes, passes](/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/): what your free pass covers, which routes you'll use and when it ends. Read it whenever you're getting off campus for the first time.
5. [Cornell Thanksgiving travel: when to book and what it costs](/getting-around/cornell-thanksgiving-break-bus-tickets-when-to-book/): peak days and how late booking costs you. Read it now; Thanksgiving week is close.
6. [Getting home for Cornell winter break, 2026-27](/getting-around/cornell-winter-break-travel-home-2026/): finals-week departures, the C2C booking links and break housing. Read it right after you book Thanksgiving.
7. [Getting to Ithaca and around Cornell, 2026-27](/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/): the full topic, from airports to parking to move-out. Keep it for anything the first six don't cover.

## Dates to watch

- **Nov 9, 2026:** the OurBus downtown Ithaca stop moves to 235 S. Cayuga St ([Finger Lakes 1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/)).
- **Nov 23 to 27, 2026:** Thanksgiving week; OurBus names the Wednesday before Thanksgiving a peak day ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269)).
- **Dec 1:** deadline to apply for winter break housing ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)).
- **Dec 23:** last C2C departure on the "2026" booking link ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)).
- **Jan 6, 2027:** first C2C departures on the "2027" booking link ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)).
- **Aug 31, 2027:** your free first-year TCAT pass ends ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

Also plan around these: Cornell's Thanksgiving break runs Nov 25 to 29, 2026 ([Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)), and undergraduate residences close Sun Dec 20, 2026 at 2 p.m. ([winter closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice)); check that notice before you book a December bus.
