You have four semesters, an associate's degree or about 60 credits done, and you would arrive at Cornell as a junior, a path Cornell discourages for students coming from four-year schools ([Cornell FAQ](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/)). The decision in front of you is whether to enter as a junior or sooner, which colleges still take you, and how many credits you lose. As a junior you are exempt from the residential requirement ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)), so housing becomes a private lease you look for after an April to June admission.

## Dates to watch

- October 1, 2026: FAFSA opens for 2027-28; file the CSS Profile as soon as possible after it ([aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)).
- March 15, 2027: Transfer Common App, fee or waiver, CSS Profile and FAFSA due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- April 15, 2027: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts due ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).
- Late April through June 2027: rolling decisions; start the lease search as soon as you are admitted, since desirable Collegetown leases can go up to 16 months ahead ([Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)).
- Early July: the fall tuition bill (2024 example: generated July 12, due August 7).
- Last day of instruction in your first Cornell semester (Dec 7 for fall 2026 entrants): deadline to request AP, IB or other exam credit in writing from your college registrar.
