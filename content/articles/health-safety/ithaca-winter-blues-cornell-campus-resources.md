If the dark Ithaca winter is pulling your mood down, start with Cornell Health: $20 light boxes, Let's Talk drop-ins and a 24-hour line at 607-255-5155 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/02/winter-blues-sad-and-the-benefits-of-light-therapy); [Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)). Light, movement and people are the cheap first steps.

## Why does winter feel so heavy in Ithaca?

Less light and more cold. On Dec. 21, 2026, the sun rises at 7:32 a.m. and sets at 4:36 p.m., for 9 hours and 4 minutes of daylight ([U.S. Naval Observatory](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=2026-12-21&coords=42.44,-76.50&tz=-5)). By Jan. 25, 2027, the first day of spring classes, the sun rises at 7:26 a.m. and sets at 5:11 p.m., which is 9 hours and 45 minutes of daylight. That is 41 minutes more than on the shortest day (from the same source).

Cold adds to it. The 1991-2020 normals put January's mean temperature at 22.8F ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)). Winter 2025-26 was the coldest in over a decade, with about 19 days that never rose above freezing from Jan. 23 to Feb. 11 and a low of -7F on Jan. 30 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/5z6poblqwoea)).

## What does Cornell Health offer?

Cornell Health covers light, someone to talk to and advice at any hour.

- **Light boxes.** The Cornell Health Pharmacy sells them for $20. Writing in The Sun, a Cornell Health psychiatric nurse practitioner advises a 10,000-lux box (lux measures brightness) for 30 minutes soon after waking, ideally before noon, set off to the side at the distance its instructions give, 12 to 24 inches for many boxes. If you have a diagnosed condition, ask a Cornell Health clinician before you buy one.
- **Let's Talk.** Free, confidential drop-in talks with a Cornell Health counselor, open to all Cornell undergraduate, graduate and professional students. No appointment is needed. Visits are first-come, first-served and last about 20 to 25 minutes. Cornell Health calls them informal consultations, not regular counseling, and not for urgent concerns ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/mental-health-care/lets-talk)).
- **An advice line.** Cornell Health runs a 24-hour advice line at 607-255-5155 ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)).

Let's Talk runs Monday through Friday during the semester, in campus buildings and by Zoom. Busy sessions can run out of time before everyone is seen, so arrive early or come back the next weekday ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/mental-health-care/lets-talk)). ## What can you do for free or cheap?

Get outside in the morning light when you can, and move. A day pass or membership at [Cornell Fitness Centers](https://scl.cornell.edu/recreation/fitness-centers/student-memberships-day-passes) gets you indoors on the worst days; our [winter activities guide](/cornell/daily-life/winter-activities-ithaca-cornell-students/) lists the prices. Our [winter biking guide](/cornell/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/) covers riding in the cold.

Dress so you can go outside: our guide to [what to wear for an Ithaca winter](/cornell/daily-life/what-to-wear-ithaca-winter-cornell/) covers the coat, boots and gloves, and help paying for them.

## Who needs extra planning?

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your first Ithaca winter lands in your second semester, when the days are still short. Add a standing weekly plan, like a gym slot or a club meeting, before the cold arrives. Our [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) has the broader checklist.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
If you come from a warmer or sunnier place, the first winter can hit hard. Cornell Health's resources above are open to you. Our [international start page](/start/international/) covers arrival, clothing and help.
```

## When should you call someone?

Call [Cornell Health's advice line](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety) at 607-255-5155 if your mood is making it hard to sleep, study or eat, or if you feel unsafe. It runs 24 hours a day. Do not wait for spring to feel better first. In a crisis, call 911 ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/mental-health-care/lets-talk)).

## What to do next

Ask the Cornell Health Pharmacy about a light box, pick a Let's Talk time that fits your week, and put the advice line number in your phone.
