Theft dominates Ithaca's crime statistics. In 2024, City of Ithaca police reported 1,183 index crimes, the seven offenses New York uses to gauge crime, including 958 larcenies and 72 violent crimes ([New York State DCJS](https://data.ny.gov/Public-Safety/Index-Crimes-by-County-and-Agency-Beginning-1990/ca8h-8gjq)). Judge a building by its safety record, its locks and your route home.

```glance
- Cornell's online safety tool shows sprinklers, alarms and extinguishers for City of Ithaca rentals.
- The free Blue Light bus on Route 92 runs 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. every night.
- Campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024, so lock bikes with a U-lock.
- Newer buildings add keyless entry, card access and cameras; ask what yours has.
```

## How much crime do Ithaca and Cornell police report?

The seven index crimes are murder, rape, robbery, aggravated assault, burglary, larceny and motor vehicle theft. City of Ithaca police and Cornell Police report their monthly totals to the state, and the counts include only crimes reported to police ([New York State DCJS](https://data.ny.gov/Public-Safety/Index-Crimes-by-County-and-Agency-Beginning-1990/ca8h-8gjq)). The city figures cover the whole City of Ithaca.

| Index crimes reported | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| City of Ithaca police, all | 1,316 | 1,256 | 1,183 |
| City of Ithaca police, violent | 101 | 56 | 72 |
| City of Ithaca police, larceny | 996 | 1,033 | 958 |
| City of Ithaca police, burglary | 195 | 155 | 137 |
| Cornell Police, all | 273 | 256 | 296 |
| Cornell Police, larceny | 245 | 237 | 278 |

Source: [Index Crimes by County and Agency, New York State DCJS](https://data.ny.gov/Public-Safety/Index-Crimes-by-County-and-Agency-Beginning-1990/ca8h-8gjq).

Larceny was 81% of the city's index crimes in 2024 (958 of 1,183) and 94% of Cornell Police's (278 of 296). Violent crime in the city rose from 56 in 2023 to 72 in 2024, still below the 101 of 2022 ([New York State DCJS](https://data.ny.gov/Public-Safety/Index-Crimes-by-County-and-Agency-Beginning-1990/ca8h-8gjq)).

On campus, the federal Clery Act requires Cornell to publish an Annual Security Report every year, titled Campus Watch ([Cornell Public Safety](https://publicsafety.cornell.edu/clery/annual-security-report)). The 2026 edition covers 2023 through 2025 and counts crimes reported on campus, in certain Cornell-owned buildings, in student organization spaces and in public areas accessible from campus. On-campus reports of rape dropped from 28 to six, reports of fondling from 22 to seven and reports of dating violence from 35 to 14, while stalking reports rose from 22 to 31 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/2026-annual-security-report-released-reported-on-campus-rape-dating-violence-decreases-while-stalking-increases)).

The same report counts arson on campus and in campus housing at 16 in 2023, three in 2024 and seven in 2025. Liquor-law violations were the most common disciplinary issue, with 396 referrals in 2025. Hazing is a new figure in the 2026 edition, added under the federal Stop Campus Hazing Act: 18 incidents in 2025. The report lists two hate crimes for 2025, one vandalism motivated by race and one intimidation motivated by ethnicity ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/2026-annual-security-report-released-reported-on-campus-rape-dating-violence-decreases-while-stalking-increases)).

## How do I check how safe a building is?

Use the online tool at safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu, which Cornell built in partnership with the City of Ithaca Building Division. It shows fire protection such as sprinklers, extinguishers, and smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, plus construction type, applicable building code and property use, for City of Ithaca rentals, and its data updates daily ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)). It covers City rentals only.

Cornell began rating City rentals for safety in September 2017, on a scale of one to five stars ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/289146)). Today each property's rating shows four checks: a current Certificate of Compliance (the City's inspection certificate), a building code of 2003 or later, meeting minimum requirements and exceeding them. Fire extinguishers are required for a valid Certificate of Compliance. The rating is not an assessment of the unit's quality, the landlord or the safety of the neighborhood ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing Safety](https://safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu/default)).

Multiple dwellings, meaning buildings of three or more units or units with six or more unrelated people, must pass a Certificate of Compliance inspection, which covers structure, fire protection, exits, electrical work and fire walls ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)). To check a building's status, call the City Building Division at 607-274-6508.

## How do I get home safely at night?

Ride Route 92, the Blue Light bus, which runs every night from 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round ([Cornell Family Resource Hub](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation)). Students beyond first year ride TCAT free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free)), so the late ride costs nothing. The [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) has hours and routes.

Walking from North Campus to Collegetown takes 15 to 20 minutes through the Arts Quad, according to [The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/10/embrace-your-inner-intellectual-go-on-a-midnight-walk). After 2:30 a.m., book a taxi or a ride; the [Uber, Lyft and taxi guide](/cornell/getting-around/uber-lyft-taxi-ithaca-cornell/) lists the options.

## What security features do Collegetown buildings have?

They vary, so ask. Collegetown Center at 151 Dryden Rd. has a concierge desk that staffs the building from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=461)). Cornell's 301 College Avenue installed card access and new locks before it opened in fall 2026, and early key problems were later resolved ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)).

In a rental house, a landlord generally cannot require a duplicate key unless the lease reserves one, and a tenant cannot change locks without consent ([Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access)). Ask about locks before you sign.

## Is bike and property theft a problem?

Yes for bikes. Campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts worth at least $41,177 in fall 2024, with one arrest and four bikes recovered intact ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far)). Cornell also points students to GradGuard renters insurance, which covers theft, fire and vandalism ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices)).

## Who do I call in an emergency?

Call 911 for an emergency or a crime in progress. For non-emergencies, call Ithaca police at 607-272-3245 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/234/Ithaca-Police-Department)) or Cornell Police at 607-255-1111 ([Cornell Public Safety](https://publicsafety.cornell.edu/)).

In an alcohol or drug emergency, Cornell's Good Samaritan Protocol protects both the caller and the person who needs help, according to [Cornell's off-campus living page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices).

## Where can I find crime statistics?

Cornell posts each year's security report on its [Clery Act page](https://publicsafety.cornell.edu/clery/annual-security-report). For a paper copy, ask the Public Safety Communications Center at G2 Barton Hall or email the Clery Compliance Office at clerycompliance@cornell.edu, according to [The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/2026-annual-security-report-released-reported-on-campus-rape-dating-violence-decreases-while-stalking-increases). For recent incidents, the Cornell Police [daily crime log](https://publicsafety.cornell.edu/clery/daily-crime-log/) lists crimes from the last 60 days in the areas the security report covers or within the department's patrol area, entered within two business days of a report.

For the city, Ithaca police post annual reports, the latest for 2025, on the [City of Ithaca website](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/691/Year-End-Reports-Budget). The state's open-data site adds each police agency's index crime counts every year ([New York State DCJS](https://data.ny.gov/Public-Safety/Index-Crimes-by-County-and-Agency-Beginning-1990/ca8h-8gjq)).

## What to do next

1. Look up your building in the safety tool.
2. Ask the landlord about locks, entry and cameras in writing.
3. Plan your late-night route and save the Route 92 hours.
4. Register your bike and buy a U-lock.
