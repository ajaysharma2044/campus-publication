```glance
- Most transfers enroll during add/drop, at the start of the term, because credit evaluations finish only after final transcripts arrive.
- Clear the Re-Entry Term Checklist in Student Center first, or an enrollment hold blocks you.
- Fall 2026 dates: classes began Aug 24, add deadline Sep 8, drop and grading-basis deadline Oct 19.
- Some courses your college pre-enrolls you in cannot be dropped; a greyed box means ask your college registrar.
- AP, IB and A-Level credit is not automatic: request it in writing by the last day of instruction of your first semester (Dec 7 in 2026).
- 52.9% of Cornell's 2,391 class sections in fall 2025 had fewer than 20 students; 186 had 100 or more.
```

Most new Cornell transfers build their first schedule during add/drop, the opening days of the term, because college registrars [finish credit evaluations only after final transcripts arrive](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). Before you can enroll, complete the [Re-Entry Term Checklist in Student Center](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/creating-your-schedule), or an enrollment hold stops you. In fall 2026 the add deadline was [September 8 and the drop deadline October 19](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), so check your schedule against both.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You arrive with up to 60 credits, so nothing is lost to the cap, but each course still has to pass your college's review. Most colleges emailed new transfers in June in 2024; send any syllabi yours asks for, and keep every syllabus until your degree audit shows the credit. Activate your NetID and Two-Step Login early, clear the Re-Entry Term Checklist, and plan a backup for each class, since popular sections fill before add/drop. The costly miss: no written request for AP or IB credit by the last day of instruction (December 7 in 2026). That credit is then lost, and you may sit a course you had already placed out of.
```

```reader "If you're entering as a junior"
You must earn at least 60 credits at Cornell, so a wrong first schedule costs more. Most colleges [cap transfer credit at 60, exam credit included](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services); arrive with 90 and 30 are lost, ten semesters of college in all. A heavier load does not shorten that, because colleges usually require four semesters in residence too. Ask your college registrar which transferred courses meet major prerequisites before add/drop, not after. Arts and Sciences sends a [tentative evaluation once you accept and deposit](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits); read it line by line. The mistake that costs a semester: taking a Cornell course that repeats a transferred one. You get credit for only one.
```

## Why transfers enroll during add/drop

Each Cornell college, not the university, evaluates your credit. It needs your final spring transcript, and often your syllabi, before it knows which Cornell courses you still need. That is why the Tatkon Center says transfers [usually enroll during the add/drop period](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment) at the start of the term.

Some colleges pre-enroll transfers in some or all of their courses over the summer. A few of those courses cannot be dropped. If the drop box is greyed out or you get a consent error, that is the sign; write to your college registrar instead of retrying.

The catalog is large: [more than 9,000 course offerings](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). Cornell's Undergraduate Education office advises transfers to [understand how their credits are processed, meet their advisor early and use pre-enroll](https://undergrad.cornell.edu/students/academic-planning/external-transfer-students) when it is open to them.

## Credit, exam credit and the degree audit

A transferred course counts when the Cornell department that owns the matching course finds it [about 80% similar in scope, content and learning outcomes](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), with a grade of C or better. Most colleges accept up to 60 credits, including exam credit; Engineering takes up to 66 and Architecture 64. Your Cornell transcript shows only your old school's name and the total transfer credits. Your old grades do not enter your Cornell GPA.

Writing courses can be the hardest to carry over. A 2016 [College Confidential poster](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/cornell-transfer-courses/1840413) who moved from UVA warned that writing seminars were harder to transfer, and found intro biology and psychology easier. The post is secondhand and hedged; treat it as one data point.

Exam credit is a separate request. AP, IB or A-Level credit your old school accepted [does not carry over automatically](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/). Send your scores (College Board code 2098) and make a [written request to your college registrar by the last day of instruction](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) of your first Cornell semester. Where an exam and a transfer course overlap, only the course counts.

Quarter-system credit converts at two-thirds. In Arts and Sciences, only quarter courses of [4 credits or more meet requirements](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits).

Example: a sophomore transfer into Arts and Sciences from a quarter-system school brings 45 quarter credits. At two-thirds, that is 30 semester credits, under the 60 cap. A 3-credit quarter course among them adds 2 semester credits but cannot meet an Arts and Sciences requirement, so plan the first schedule as if that requirement is still open.

Then watch the degree audit. In the Sun's [January 2023 story on spring transfers](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/), Abbey Yang '25 said her advisor told her biology credits should count, but her degree audit (DUST) did not show them yet. Keep every syllabus until the audit is right.

Once you are enrolled, a course taken elsewhere needs [prior approval](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Arts and Sciences allows up to 12 summer credits elsewhere and no outside courses during fall or spring.

## Full classes

Popular classes may be full by the time a transfer can enroll. Spring 2023 transfers told the Sun they worried about [closed classes at add/drop](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/), because current students had pre-enrolled in November. In our count of dated transfer accounts (the Sun, College Confidential and Cornell pages), closed classes, credit doubts and a lagging audit came up in 4 sources from 2016 to 2025.

Our advice: put two or three sections or substitute courses in Scheduler for every class you need. Go to your advisor in the first week with the list. If a pre-enrolled course is wrong for you and will not drop, the registrar is the only fix.

## How Cornell academics feel to transfers

Most classes are small. In fall 2025 Cornell ran [2,391 class sections with a 12 to 1 student-faculty ratio](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf). The shares below are our math from the Common Data Set counts.

| Section size | Sections, fall 2025 | Share (our math) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 to 19 students | 1,266 | 52.9% |
| 20 to 49 students | 644 | 26.9% |
| 50 to 99 students | 295 | 12.3% |
| 100 or more students | 186 | 7.8% |

The big lectures are where transfers feel the jump. In the same count, rigor, size, cold and geography came up in 5 sources from 2018 to 2024:

- Eve Iulo, who entered as a transfer, wrote about [imposter feelings in a lecture of more than 100](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/persona-non-grata-actually-you-do-belong) after a hometown class of 73, and argued that everyone feels it.
- Alexia Kim, a Human Ecology sophomore transfer, named [organic chemistry prelims and 12-page essays](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/02/kim-what-it-means-to-be-a-transfer-at-cornell/) as the rigor jump in 2019. Prelims are Cornell's midterm exams.
- Jessica Silverman, who came from UCLA to Human Ecology, described [switching from quarters to semesters](https://hecec.human.cornell.edu/?p=8161).
- One spring 2023 transfer planned an 18-credit semester, per the [same Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). Full-time status starts at [10 credits](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf), so a lighter first term is allowed.

Undergraduate Education tells transfers to [expect different teaching and grading](https://undergrad.cornell.edu/students/academic-planning/external-transfer-students) and to use tutoring early. The support Cornell names for transfers: [advisors in each college, the Tatkon Center, the Learning Strategies Center, the Writing Centers and CAPS](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies).

```reader "If you're applying to Engineering or computer science"
You are admitted into one major and [must stay in it for at least one academic year](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/150-as-a-transfer-student-does-it-matter-which-engineering-major-i-apply-to/). Engineering gives exam credit only at its thresholds: [a 5 on AP Calculus BC, AP Chemistry and AP Physics C Mechanics](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements), and the PHYS 1110 lab is still required. For course credit, send detailed syllabi and a certified official transcript. With a 4 on BC, take the optional MATH CASE (August 20, 2026, 9 to 11 a.m., Olin Hall 155, no calculators). The [advising drop-in](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/) ran 8 a.m. to noon that day in Rhodes 180. A C- earns no credit. Skipping the CASE with a 4 means retaking calculus.
```

```reader "If you're coming from a community college"
Cornell [cannot deny equivalency because a course was taken at a community college or online](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services); the test is the same 80% match and a C or better. Math below precalculus, study skills and remedial courses never transfer, so do not count them in your plan. Four community college graduates profiled in 2024 described [feeling behind and imposter feelings](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell) at first. The same story names a CALS transfer student success navigator, with similar roles in ILR and Human Ecology, plus PAVER and the CC STEP group. Use them in week one.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
Credit from abroad is [generally accepted from institutions your Ministry of Education recognizes](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services); remedial, ESL and international military courses never transfer, and the Literatures in English department reviews English courses. Send IB, A-Level and French Baccalaureate transcripts [to your college registrar](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). Like other transfers, expect to build your schedule at add/drop. The costly miss: leaving the Re-Entry Term Checklist for arrival week. The hold then keeps you out of enrollment while the add window runs, and it lasted 15 days in fall 2026.
```

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the Re-Entry Term Checklist.** The enrollment hold blocks you in your window, and the add period lasted only 15 days in fall 2026.
- **Assuming you can drop a pre-enrolled course.** Some cannot be dropped online; a greyed box sends you to the registrar.
- **Missing the exam-credit request.** No written request by the last day of instruction (December 7 in 2026) means no AP or IB credit, and possibly a repeated course.
- **Retaking a course you transferred in.** You [receive credit for only one](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) of the two.
- **Throwing out syllabi.** If the degree audit lags, the syllabus is your evidence.
- **Not checking before the drop deadline.** After October 19 (2026), the grading basis is locked as well.
- **Picking an Engineering major casually.** You keep it for at least one academic year.

## FAQ

**Can I take a summer course near home after my first Cornell year?** Only with [prior approval](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Arts and Sciences allows up to 12 summer credits; Engineering allows summer transfer credit only with [fewer than 12 Cornell credits that term](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements).

**Will my old GPA follow me?** No. The [Cornell transcript lists only your old school and total transfer credits](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and your transfer grades stay out of your Cornell GPA.

**How many credits do I need to be full time?** Ten, per the [2025-26 Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf).

**Can I switch to another Cornell college later?** Arts and Sciences lets external transfers [apply to move only after two semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), and the [Hotel School's internal process](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) excludes students who transferred in.

**Who do I ask about a course that did not transfer?** Your college registrar or student services office, listed in the table above. The [department that owns the matching Cornell course](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) decides from the syllabus.
