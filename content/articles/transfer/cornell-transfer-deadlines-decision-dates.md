```glance
- March 15, 2027: the Transfer Common App, the $85 fee or waiver, any portfolio, and the CSS Profile and FAFSA.
- April 15, 2027: everything else (College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report, transcripts).
- Fall entry only: there is no spring transfer deadline.
- Decisions roll from late April through June; Hotel decides in May and June; most Transfer Option decisions land late April or early May.
- After a yes: housing within ten days of your NetID, a tuition bill in early July, a health plan waiver by July 31 (2026 pattern).
```

For fall 2027 entry, Cornell's transfer deadline is [March 15 for the application, the fee or fee waiver and any portfolio, and April 15 for all other required materials](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). US and international aid forms are also due March 15, 2027. Decisions come out on a rolling basis from late April through June, in the Applicant Portal.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You can't apply from your first college semester; Cornell takes students with [two to four semesters done](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/). So you apply this spring. Submit the Common App, fee and any portfolio by March 15, 2027, with the CSS Profile and FAFSA. Then chase the April 15 pieces: your registrar or dean completes the College Report, one instructor writes the Academic Evaluation, and you fill in the Mid-Term Report yourself. The costly slip is sending only the March 15 pieces and leaving the file incomplete. After a yes, apply for housing within ten days of your NetID; you must live on campus for a year.
```

```reader "If you're a junior-entry transfer"
Your deadlines are the same March 15 and April 15, 2027. Your clock after admission is tighter. As a matriculating junior you are exempt from the one-year housing rule, so you will likely rent. Desirable Collegetown leases sign [up to 16 months before move-in](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), and your decision may not arrive until June. Start reading listings while you wait. Check eligibility before March: Cornell [discourages junior transfer from four-year schools](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) because of the roughly 60-credit cap, and [CALS bars students](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) with more than four semesters at a four-year school.
```

```reader "If you're a community college transfer"
The central dates apply to you: March 15, 2027 for the application and aid forms, April 15 for the rest. The TC3 to CALS pathway gives ["priority consideration,"](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students) not a guarantee, so file by March 15 like everyone else. If the $85 fee is a burden and you apply for aid, the [fee waiver](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) is applied automatically, with no documents. The CSS Profile fee is waived for family income up to $100,000. Ask one instructor for the Academic Evaluation in February so it lands before April 15. The costly mistake is skipping the aid forms because Cornell looks too expensive: aid meets 100% of demonstrated need.
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
You use the [Transfer Option application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), not the regular Common App, with your earlier portal login and no fee. In 2026 it was due March 15, with aid forms the same day and the Mid-Term Report allowed later. For fall 2027 the page shows only "Application Available Winter 2027," so plan on March 15, 2027 until it says otherwise. Decisions roll from mid-April; most arrive in late April or early May, and some wait for final grades. In [Arts and Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), a semester under 3.5 or any grade below B- misses the stated condition. Keep a fallback school for your second year.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
March 15, 2027 is your one chance at Cornell aid. File the CSS Profile (code 2098) by then, or you [cannot receive undergraduate aid later](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international). There is generally no CSS fee waiver for you. Application dates match everyone else's: March 15 and April 15, with decisions late April through June. After admission, agree a SEVIS release date with your current school's international office. Cornell issues the I-20 [two to three weeks after that date](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students), once funding is proven. A June decision leaves a short summer, so gather funding documents before you hear.
```

```reader "If you're a parent of a transfer"
Your deadline is March 15, 2027, when the [CSS Profile and FAFSA](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) are due with the application. The decision comes between late April and June. Then the dates turn into bills. Fall tuition is billed in early July and due before registration; the [fall 2024 bill](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances) was generated July 12 and due August 7. You see e-bills only if your student invites you to Cashnet. Waive the Student Health Plan by July 31 (2026 pattern) if your family plan covers your student, or $1,705 is billed for fall.
```

## The two application deadlines

Cornell splits the transfer application across two dates. Both apply to every college for fall 2027 entry, and [applications are considered for the fall semester only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

| Due | What | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| March 15, 2027 | Transfer Common App with the essay (250 to 650 words) and the college's Writing Supplement | You |
| March 15, 2027 | $85 application fee or fee waiver | You |
| March 15, 2027 | Portfolio or design supplement (Architecture, Art, Landscape Architecture, Fashion Design, Design and Environmental Analysis) | You |
| March 15, 2027 | CSS Profile (code 2098) and FAFSA (002711); NY residents also file with HESC | You and your family |
| April 15, 2027 | College Report (academic and disciplinary standing) | An official at your current school |
| April 15, 2027 | Academic Evaluation | One instructor of a full-credit college course |
| April 15, 2027 | Mid-Term Report, with official or estimated spring grades | You |
| April 15, 2027 | Official transcripts from every college, plus the final high school transcript | Your schools |

The aid deadline comes from the [financial aid office](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants), which lists March 15, 2027 for fall 2027 transfers. FAFSA opened October 1, 2026, and the office asks for both forms as soon as possible. The CSS Profile fee is waived automatically for US families with adjusted gross income up to $100,000.

SAT and ACT scores are [neither required nor expected](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/), so there is no test date to plan around.

Two college pages add their own notes. ILR asks for the [Mid-Term Report by March 15 if possible](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines), and no later than April 15. Human Ecology's [transfer page](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) describes rolling review with no deadline; the central admissions page still applies to every college, so file by March 15 anyway.

## When decisions come out

Decisions are rolling, and each Cornell page words the window a little differently. Read them together: decisions run from April into June, and silence in April is not a rejection.

| Page | What it says |
|---|---|
| [Admissions transfer page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) | Late April through June |
| [Enrollment FAQ](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/427-when-are-transfer-admission-decisions-released/) | Rolling, beginning in April |
| [Hotel Administration](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) | Rolling in May and June |
| [Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) | Rolling from mid-April; most late April or early May; some wait for final grades |
| [Common Data Set 2025-26](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) (D9) | Closing date March 15; notification June 15; reply by July 1; rolling |

The Common Data Set dates are a reporting form's fixed fields, not a promise that every decision waits until June 15. Its reply date for transfers is July 1.

You see the decision in the [Applicant Portal](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), whose login arrives by email after you submit the Common App. Arts and Sciences produces its [tentative credit evaluation only after you accept and deposit](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), so you commit before seeing it.

Aid timing has its own line. The aid office says a Cornell Aid Estimate arrives soon after admission, and official offers go out [tentatively in early April](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) for students already admitted. It asks families to answer document requests fast so the estimate arrives before the deposit is due. That advice has history: in spring 2021, [admitted first-years and transfers had to commit by May 3](https://cornellsun.com/2021/05/14/admitted-students-await-financial-aid-offers-weeks-after-commitment-deadlines/) before many aid offers came, The Cornell Daily Sun reported, and some students waited on the phone more than two hours.

The housing clock matters most. New transfers who are not exempt must live on campus for a year, and the [2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/12-month-undergraduate-housing-license-2026-2027) says they cannot cancel unless they leave the university or win an exemption. Activate the NetID the day your admission materials explain how.

The health plan clock costs money. Full-time students are enrolled in the Student Health Plan unless they waive it with comparable coverage. A new fall 2026 student was billed [$1,705 for fall and $2,046 for spring](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27), $3,751 in all. After the waiver deadline, waivers lock.

Example: a sophomore applies to Hotel Administration on March 10, 2027 and files the CSS Profile and FAFSA the same week. The College Report and Academic Evaluation arrive by April 15. Hotel decides in May or June, so a quiet April means nothing. Say the yes comes June 5. The NetID arrives a few days later, which starts the ten-day housing clock. A room confirmation or waitlist place follows from mid-June. The tuition bill comes in early July. If a family plan covers the student, the health plan waiver goes in before July 31. Move-in, on the 2026 pattern, is mid-August.

## Dates for international applicants

The application dates do not change: [March 15 and April 15, the $85 fee, and decisions late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). The aid date is the one with no second chance. File the CSS Profile as soon as possible after October 1, 2026, and by March 15, 2027. Admission is need-aware for international applicants who ask for aid.

English test scores follow the [proficiency page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/resources/submit-english-language-proficiency): you may self-report with the application, and official scores are required if you are admitted. Its minimum competitive scores are TOEFL iBT 100 for exams before January 2026, IELTS Academic 7.5 and Duolingo 130.

The visa step comes after admission. You take the acceptance letter to your current school's international office, agree a SEVIS release date, and Cornell issues the I-20 [two to three weeks after that date](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students), once funding is proven. A student graduating from the current school must have the release date entered within the 60-day grace period.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending only the March 15 pieces.** The College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts are due April 15. Without them the file is incomplete. The College Report is a standing form from your school's official, not a recommendation, and the Mid-Term Report is yours to fill in.
- **Treating April 15 as the aid deadline.** The CSS Profile and FAFSA are due March 15, 2027. An international applicant who misses it loses Cornell aid for every undergraduate year.
- **Planning around a spring start.** External transfer is fall only, though older guidelines and aggregator pages still list a spring date. Waiting for one costs a full term.
- **Using the regular Common App as a Transfer Option holder.** It is the wrong route, and it charges the $85 fee the Transfer Option application does not.
- **Missing the ten-day housing window.** In 2016, transfers who applied after the July 1 housing deadline then in force were among about 30 students placed in [converted North Campus lounges](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/03/transfer-students-forced-to-live-in-north-campus-lounges/), the Sun reported. The ten-day rule is today's version of that deadline.
- **Letting the health plan bill go through.** Miss the July 31 waiver (2026 pattern) with family coverage in place, and $1,705 is billed for fall.
- **A junior waiting for the decision to look for housing.** Desirable Collegetown leases go up to 16 months ahead, so a June admit finds much of the stock signed.

## FAQ

**Is there a spring transfer deadline?** No. Cornell considers external transfer applications [for the fall semester only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), so the next entry after fall 2027 is fall 2028.

**Can I send the Mid-Term Report before my spring grades exist?** Yes. You list your spring courses with [official or estimated grades](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and upload an explanation if no grade can be given yet.

**Do I need SAT or ACT scores by a deadline?** No. Transfer applicants are [not required or expected](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/) to send them; self-report AP, IB or A-Level results in the Common App.

**I'm in my first college semester. Can I apply by March 15?** Only if that March falls after your first semester ends. Cornell does not accept applicants [in their first college semester](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), so a fall 2026 first-year applies in spring 2027.

**When will 2027-28 prices be known?** The trustees set them in late March; the [2026-27 rates were approved March 23, 2026](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). The aid office says rates are [confirmed before the early April 2027 offers](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants).

**How long do Transfer Option holders wait?** Decisions roll [from mid-April](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants); most come in late April or early May, and some wait for final grades.
