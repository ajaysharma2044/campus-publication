```glance
- The required on-campus year bills $95,218 (triple) to $99,169 (super single) at 2026-27 endowed or out-of-state prices, before aid.
- New York residents in CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks or Dyson pay $49,816 tuition, so the same year bills $71,088 to $75,039.
- The Student Health Plan adds $3,751 for a fall entrant's first year unless waived with comparable coverage (deadline July 31 in 2026).
- A co-op double costs $6,974 to $9,962 a year for the room, against $13,744 for a hall double.
- Two years at 2026-27 rates: $199,468 full cost (endowed) or $151,208 (NY contract), before aid.
- Fall 2027 entrants pay 2027-28 prices, set before April 2027 aid offers; grants cover tuition for families under $125,000 with typical assets.
```

At 2026-27 prices, a new Cornell transfer's required year on campus bills $95,218 to $99,169 for tuition, fees, room and meal plan, at the [endowed and out-of-state tuition of $73,946](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). A New York resident in a contract college pays $49,816 tuition, so the same year bills $71,088 to $75,039. With books and personal costs, Cornell's [cost of attendance](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend) is $99,734 or $75,604, before need-based aid and before the health plan.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer"
You must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), so room and meal plan are not optional. Budget $96,318 billed for a hall double with the Unlimited or House plan at endowed tuition. Apply on the Housing and Dining Portal within ten days of getting your NetID; there is [no application fee and no damage deposit](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process), and confirmations start in mid-June. Rooms are assigned by random auto-allocation, so you do not fully pick your price row. The costly mistake is signing a Collegetown lease for the required year: new transfers cannot cancel the housing license, so you would pay rent twice.
```

```reader "If you're a parent of a transfer"
Budget $96,318 billed for a hall double at endowed or out-of-state tuition, or $72,188 for a New York resident at a contract college. Add Cornell's estimates of $1,234 for books and $2,182 for personal costs. The first bill arrives in early July through Cashnet, and you will not see it [unless your student invites you](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances). Housing follows in July or August. If your family plan covers your student, file the health plan waiver before the deadline (July 31 in 2026); missing it puts $1,705 on the fall bill. The Cornell Installment Plan spreads payments.
```

```reader "If you're transferring on a tight budget"
Apply for aid even if the sticker price looks impossible. For 2026-27, grants cover tuition for families up to $125,000 with typical assets, and the full cost of attendance up to $75,000. The $85 application fee is [waived automatically](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) if it is a burden and you plan to apply for aid. The CSS Profile fee is [waived automatically up to $100,000](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) in adjusted gross income. A co-op double saves $3,782 to $6,770 a year on the room. The health plan sits outside the cost of attendance, so waive it if you have comparable coverage, or $3,751 lands on your bill.
```

```reader "If you're a New York resident aiming at a contract college"
At CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks or Dyson you pay $49,816 tuition for 2026-27, $24,130 less than the $73,946 endowed rate. Your required year bills $71,088 to $75,039, and two years of full cost come to $151,208. The same subject at an endowed college (Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Hotel or AAP) costs the full $73,946, even for New Yorkers. File with HESC alongside the CSS Profile and FAFSA, using [code 0174 for contract colleges or 0175 for endowed](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). The costly mistake is applying to an endowed college without noticing the gap.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
You pay $73,946 tuition at every Cornell college, so your required year bills $95,218 to $99,169 before aid. Admission is need-aware for you. The [CSS Profile is due by March 15, 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international), generally with no fee waiver, and an applicant who skips that deadline cannot receive Cornell aid later. Aid above tuition and fees is taxable, with 14% withheld unless a tax treaty applies. The health plan rule covers you too: enroll, or waive with proof of comparable coverage.
```

```reader "If you're a junior-entry transfer"
Entering as a junior [exempts you from the one-year housing rule](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), so your fixed bill is $74,970 in tuition and fees at endowed rates for 2026-27. Housing is your choice: a large-building Collegetown studio averaged $2,006 a month in October 2026, against about $1,527 a month for a hall double over nine months. Off-campus meal plans start at [$1,000 a year for Just Bucks](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). The costly mistake is arriving with too many credits: under [Arts and Sciences rules](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits), 90 credits means 30 lost and ten semesters instead of eight, about $74,970 more in tuition and fees.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer with a partner or children"
Cornell's family apartments at Hasbrouck are [graduate-only](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) and closed to undergraduate transfers. Individual exemptions from the one-year rule are requested through the housing application, one year at a time; do not sign a lease before one is granted. Health plan dependent rates for 2026-27: a spouse, partner or one child costs [$4,092 from July or $3,751 from August](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27). Two or more children cost $8,184 or $7,502. A large-building Collegetown two-bedroom averaged $3,414 a month in October 2026.
```

## What the year costs, line by line

Tuition rose [3.76% for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters): $73,946 at the endowed colleges and for non-New Yorkers anywhere, $49,816 for New York residents at the five contract colleges.

| Item | Per year, 2026-27 | On the Cornell bill? |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition, endowed college or non-NY resident | $73,946 | Yes |
| Tuition, NY resident at a contract college | $49,816 | Yes |
| [Mandatory fees](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend) (student activity $424, Cornell Health $600) | $1,024 | Yes |
| [Room](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), hall triple to super single | $12,644 to $16,595 | Yes |
| [Meal plan](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), Unlimited or House | $7,604 | Yes |
| Meal plan, Collegetown (South Campus halls) | $7,056 | Yes |
| Meal plan administrative fee, once a year | $50 | Yes |
| Books (Cornell's estimate) | $1,234 | No |
| Personal (Cornell's estimate) | $2,182 | No |
| [Student Health Plan](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27), fall entrant | $3,751 | Yes, unless waived |

The room rate includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month. The contract colleges are CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks and Dyson. Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Hotel and AAP are endowed and charge $73,946 to everyone.

## Billed cost by room

Transfers in campus housing are [automatically enrolled](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies) in the Unlimited plan or the House Meal Plan, depending on the hall; both cost $7,604. The cheaper Collegetown plan applies in 112 Edgemoor, Cascadilla, Schuyler, Sheldon Court, 301 College Ave and 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive. Our sums below add tuition, the $1,024 in fees, the room and the meal plan, the same lines Cornell uses for its billed total.

Every row differs by $24,130 between the columns, the tuition gap. The cheapest and dearest rooms are $3,951 apart. The $96,318 double row matches Cornell's own [billed total](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend). Neither includes the $50 meal plan fee.

You do not fully choose your row. Halls are filled by [random auto-allocation](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process) from your preferences. Program Houses are the only places you pick by name. Transfers go to upper-level areas: [Program Houses, South Campus, the West Campus House System and the co-ops](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates).

Example: a sophomore from Ohio enters Arts and Sciences and lands in a West Campus double. Tuition $73,946, fees $1,024, room $13,744 and the House plan $7,604 make $96,318. The $50 meal plan fee brings it to $96,368. Without a health plan waiver, add $3,751: $100,119 billed in year one, before aid.

## The co-op rooms

Cornell's eight cooperative houses set their rates from each house's operating costs, well below the halls. The [2026-27 rate page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) lists co-op doubles at $3,487 to $4,981 a semester. The yearly figures and savings are our doubling of those rates.

These are room rates only. The Redbud triple is cheaper still: $2,765 a semester, $5,530 a year.

Example: a New York resident admitted to ILR gets a Redbud double. Tuition $49,816, fees $1,024 and the room $6,974 come to $57,814 before food. In a hall double, the same student would pay $64,584 for tuition, fees and room, plus $7,604 for the required meal plan.

## Health insurance: $3,751 unless you waive

Full-time, degree-seeking students must [enroll in the Student Health Plan or waive it](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/health-community/health-insurance) with proof of comparable coverage. Anyone who does nothing is enrolled and billed. Waivers are reverified each semester. The plan sits outside Cornell's cost of attendance, and the $600 Cornell Health fee is a separate charge.

| Who is covered, 2026-27 | Cost |
|---|---|
| New fall 2026 student, from August 1 | $1,705 for fall, then $2,046 for spring ($3,751) |
| New spring 2027 student | $2,046 for six months |
| Spouse, partner or one child | $4,092 from July, or $3,751 from August |
| Two or more children | $8,184 from July, or $7,502 from August |

The [waiver deadline](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/node/179) was July 31, 2026 for new fall students and is January 21, 2027 for new spring students. After it, waivers lock and only a prorated termination is possible.

## When the bills arrive

Cornell bills through Cashnet e-bills. Fall tuition is billed in [early July and is due before registration](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances); spring tuition in January. In the 2024 example on that page, the fall bill was generated July 12 and due August 7.

Fall housing appears on the [July or August Bursar bill](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process), spring housing in December. The meal plan is [billed in July and January](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). Payment tools include the Cornell Installment Plan and an elective Tuition Refund Plan.

Fall 2027 entrants will pay 2027-28 prices, which are not yet published. The aid office says rates are [confirmed before the early April 2027 aid offers](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). If the next rise matched this year's 3.76%, endowed tuition would go up about $2,780; that is our arithmetic, not a forecast.

## Two years, and what changes in year two

Arts and Sciences requires [four semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits), so a sophomore transfer pays for at least two years. At 2026-27 rates, two years of Cornell's full cost of attendance come to $199,468 at endowed tuition and $151,208 at NY contract tuition, before aid and increases. Tuition alone is $147,892 or $99,632.

Year two can be off campus. A hall double works out to about $1,527 a month over the nine-month academic year. A large-building Collegetown studio averaged [$2,006 a month on October 30, 2026](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/), a one-bedroom $2,342 and a two-bedroom $3,414; RentCafe counts only buildings of 50 or more units. Desirable Collegetown leases leave the market [up to 16 months before move-in](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), so start the year-two search in your first fall.

## What aid covers

Cornell meets [100% of demonstrated need](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) with grants, work-study and reasonable loans, and gives no merit aid. For 2026-27, families earning up to [$125,000 with typical assets get grants covering tuition](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). Up to $75,000, grants, scholarships and work-study cover the full cost of attendance. The Unlimited meal plan is ["fully covered by financial aid for eligible students"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans).

US transfers file the CSS Profile and FAFSA by [March 15, 2027 for fall 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). A Cornell Aid Estimate arrives soon after admission. Grant aid beyond tuition and mandatory fees is taxable.

## Common mistakes

- **Reading the wrong school's cost page.** Cornell College in Mount Vernon, Iowa, publishes transfer and cost pages that crowd the same searches. Its prices have nothing to do with Ithaca.
- **Missing the health plan waiver.** Doing nothing enrolls you and bills $1,705 for fall, then $2,046 for spring.
- **Signing an apartment lease for the required year.** The [2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/12-month-undergraduate-housing-license-2026-2027) binds new transfers, who cannot cancel unless they leave Cornell or win an exemption. A $2,006 studio on top of a $13,744 hall double is pure loss.
- **Leaving the payer out of Cashnet.** The fall bill is generated in July and due before registration; a parent who was never invited never sees it.
- **Committing before the aid estimate arrives.** In spring 2021, admitted first-years and transfers had to [commit before many aid offers came](https://cornellsun.com/2021/05/14/admitted-students-await-financial-aid-offers-weeks-after-commitment-deadlines/), and one student described her offer as mostly loans. Answer document requests fast so the estimate reaches you first.
- **Skipping the CSS Profile as an international applicant.** Missing March 15 closes Cornell aid for your whole undergraduate career.
- **Arriving with more than 60 credits.** Arts and Sciences accepts at most 60 and still requires four Cornell semesters, so each extra 15 credits adds a paid semester.

## FAQ

**Is there a housing application fee or deposit?** No. For 2026-27 the housing application has [no fee and no damage deposit](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process).

**Does Cornell give transfers merit scholarships?** No. Aid is need-based only, and Cornell [meets 100% of demonstrated need](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) for admitted undergraduates.

**Is the $600 Cornell Health fee the same as health insurance?** No. The fee sits inside the $1,024 in mandatory fees; the Student Health Plan is a separate charge outside the [cost of attendance](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend).

**Can I leave campus housing if I find a cheaper place?** Not as a new transfer without an exemption. You are exempt if you lived on campus two years elsewhere or enter as a junior or senior, per the [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).

**Does West Campus cost more than South Campus?** The room rate follows room type, not area. West Campus houses require the $7,604 House plan; the six named South Campus halls use the $7,056 Collegetown plan.
