```glance
- Transfers moved in Tuesday, August 18, 2026, one day after first-years (August 17); expect the same pattern in mid-August 2027.
- Family Orientation was the same day, August 18; families get the details in early May.
- New Student Orientation ran August 18 to 23, 2026, with transfer small groups; New Student Convocation was 6 p.m. August 18 at Schoellkopf Field.
- Required: your college's programs, Community at Cornell, Orientation Leader meetings and Cornell's Caring Community; everything else is optional.
- Engineering adds its own transfer days (August 18, 20 and 21 in 2026), including an optional MATH CASE exam.
- Classes began Monday, August 24, 2026; most transfers enroll during add/drop, with the add deadline September 8.
```

In fall 2026, Cornell moved transfers into their rooms on [Tuesday, August 18, the day after first-years](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation), and New Student Orientation ran August 18 to 23. Transfers attend the [same required programs as first-years](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation), plus small groups and socials of their own. Fall 2027 dates are not posted yet, so plan on the 2026 pattern: arrive in mid-August, about a week before classes.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer living on campus"
Your move-in slot is the one you picked in the Housing and Dining Portal after you signed your license. You can [change it until August 1](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process). In 2026 transfers moved in August 18, then went straight into orientation through August 23. Go to your college's program, Community at Cornell, the Orientation Leader meetings and Cornell's Caring Community; those are required. Go to the transfer small groups too. The costly mistake is skipping the Re-Entry Term Checklist in Student Center: it puts a [hold on your enrollment](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/creating-your-schedule) the week you need to add classes.
```

```reader "If you're a parent of a transfer"
Book travel for the transfer day, not the first-year day. In 2026 that was [August 18, the same day as Family Orientation](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation); first-years moved in August 17. Cornell sends [family orientation information in early May](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation). New Student Convocation was at [6 p.m. that evening at Schoellkopf Field](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429). If your student is in Engineering, the college ran a [family briefing from 3 to 4:30 p.m. in Statler Auditorium](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/). Returning students arrived August 21 and 22, so the first move-in days are the quieter ones.
```

```reader "If you're an Engineering or CS transfer"
Duffield runs its own transfer program on top of university orientation, and in 2026 it [listed nothing as mandatory](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/). Three items still matter. The advising drop-in (Thursday, August 20, 8 a.m. to noon, Rhodes 180) is where you sort out credit and schedule questions. The MATH CASE (same day, 9 to 11 a.m., Olin Hall 155, no calculators) is optional but strongly encouraged if you scored a 4 on AP Calculus BC, which earns no Engineering credit. The Transfer Welcome Lunch was Friday, August 21, 1 to 2:30 p.m., Upson 116. Skip the CASE with a BC 4 and you may retake MATH 1910.
```

```reader "If you're a junior transfer living off campus"
Junior entrants are [exempt from the on-campus requirement](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), so there is no Cornell move-in slot for you. Orientation still applies: transfers attend the [same required college and university programs](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation) as everyone else, and in 2026 they began August 18. Time your lease start so you are in Ithaca by then. The mistake that costs most is treating orientation as optional because you are not in a residence hall: the transfer small groups are where an off-campus junior meets other transfers before classes scatter everyone.
```

```reader "If you're a veteran or non-traditional transfer"
Cornell says non-traditional students [may skip some optional orientation events](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation). The required list still applies: your college's program, Community at Cornell, Orientation Leader meetings and Cornell's Caring Community. You will not be alone: [23 transfer veterans entered in fall 2026](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429). Cornell lists [support for non-traditional students, veterans and student families](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation/beyond-first-week) beyond the first week. If you live on campus, Veterans House is a [Program House you can choose by name](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process) on the housing application.
```

The fall 2027 schedule is not there yet. Two things change around these dates. Your student health plan coverage starts [August 1 for new fall students](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27), at $1,705 for the fall unless you waive it. ### Ignore two kinds of stale dates

The Tatkon Center's transfer [arrival page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/your-arrival) still says new students move in August 19 and 20. It carries no year and is from an earlier fall; the 2026 transfer day was August 18. Trust the NSO page and the Chronicle over it.

Search results for "Cornell transfer orientation" also mix in [Cornell College in Mount Vernon, Iowa](https://www.cornellcollege.edu/admissions/transfer-students/transfer-of-academic-credit.shtml), a different school. If a page mentions Mount Vernon or Iowa, it is not about Ithaca.

## Which programs are required and which are optional

Cornell's [transfer orientation page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation) puts transfers through the same programs as first-years. It sorts them like this.

| Program | Required? | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Your college's orientation programs | Required | Run by your college: advising, degree rules, credit questions |
| Community at Cornell | Required | University program for all new students |
| Orientation Leader meetings | Required | Small groups led by student mentors |
| Cornell's Caring Community | Required | University program for all new students |
| Transfer small groups and socials | Transfer track | Part of NSO for transfers, per the [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies) |
| Other NSO events | Optional | Non-traditional students may skip some of them |

New Student Convocation opens the week for all new students; in 2026 it was [6 p.m. on August 18 at Schoellkopf Field](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429), the evening of transfer move-in. Orientation Leaders are current students who mentor new ones. The transfer small groups are the one part of NSO built only for you. In a [May 2025 Cornell Daily Sun letter](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you), Rachel Eisenhart, an ILR transfer in the Class of 2027, wrote that transfers sit in limbo between first-years and experienced students and repeat freshman small talk. She also wrote that within weeks you feel like a regular sophomore.

## Placement exams before classes

The CASE exams (Cornell Advanced Standing Exams) run [in person before the term](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment) in Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics and Physics. Language placement is online, for Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Spanish.

Take a CASE when an exam score falls short of credit. Engineering's case is the clearest: AP Calculus BC needs a 5 for MATH 1910 credit, so a 4 earns nothing. The 2026 MATH CASE was [Thursday, August 20, 9 to 11 a.m., in Olin Hall 155](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/), with no calculators.

Example: an Engineering transfer arrives with a 4 on AP Calculus BC and no college course that matches MATH 1910. The AP score earns nothing. A CASE [may give credit or placement](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and it costs two hours on Thursday morning. Skip it and MATH 1910 goes on the first-semester schedule.

## Course enrollment during orientation week

Most transfers [enroll during add/drop at the start of the term](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment), not over the summer. Registrars finish credit evaluations only after final transcripts arrive. Your college may pre-enroll you in some or all courses, and some of those cannot be dropped. A greyed-out box or a consent error means ask your college registrar.

College emails went out from early to mid-June on the [2024 version of the college links page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/college-school-links); Engineering's start in early June. The [2026-27 calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar) put the add deadline at September 8 and the drop and grading-basis deadline at October 19.

Undergraduate Education's advice for external transfers is to [meet your advisor early](https://undergrad.cornell.edu/students/academic-planning/external-transfer-students) and learn how your credits are processed. Orientation week is when both happen. The full enrollment process is on our [course enrollment guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-course-enrollment-academics/).

## After orientation: the first six weeks

Cornell's [beyond-the-first-week page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation/beyond-first-week) lists two events for new students in the first six weeks: the Tatkon Block Party and CU Downtown. It points transfers to their academic advisor, the Dean of Students, Career Services, the Office of Academic Diversity Initiatives and residence hall staff.

The Tatkon Center is the office for new students, at [600 Thurston Avenue in South Balch Hall](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students), phone (607) 255-5808. Jessica Silverman, a UCLA transfer to Human Ecology, [recommended the Tatkon Center](https://hecec.human.cornell.edu/?p=8161), CUeLinks and West Campus house dinners.

Two Sun pieces show why the first weeks matter. Spring 2023 transfers told the paper that [college-specific orientation socials helped](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). In fall 2020, transfers [described feeling disconnected](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/), without the dorm community first-years had; a transfer GroupMe had more than 350 members.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking travel for first-year move-in.** In 2026 that was August 17; transfers moved in August 18. Plan the trip around the 18th.
- **Missing the August 1 timeslot change.** After that date your [move-in slot is fixed](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process), whatever your flight says.
- **Leaving the Re-Entry Term Checklist undone.** It triggers an enrollment hold in the week you need to add classes. Spring 2023 transfers told the Sun that [popular classes were already full](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/) when they could enroll; a hold makes that worse.
- **Skipping the MATH CASE with a 4 on AP Calculus BC.** Engineering gives no credit for it, so the cost is a full semester of MATH 1910.
- **Trusting the undated arrival page.** Its August 19 and 20 dates are from an earlier year.
- **Missing the exam-credit request.** External transfers must ask the college registrar in writing for AP, IB or other exam credit by the [last day of instruction of the first semester](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), December 7 in 2026. Miss it and the credit is gone.

## FAQ

**Is transfer orientation separate from first-year orientation?** No. Transfers attend the same New Student Orientation and required programs, with [transfer small groups and socials](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies) added.

**When will fall 2027 orientation dates come out?** Families get orientation information [in early May](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation), and the dates go up on the [NSO page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation).

**Can I choose my move-in time?** Yes, within transfer day: you pick a timeslot in the Housing and Dining Portal after signing your license, [changeable until August 1](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process).

**When is the first break, for booking a trip home?** In 2026-27 fall break ran [October 10 to 13 and Thanksgiving break November 25 to 29](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar).

**Where do I go with a question during orientation week?** Your college's student services office handles credit and enrollment; Engineering's is adv_engineering@cornell.edu or (607) 255-7414, per the [college links page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/college-school-links). For anything else, the Tatkon Center.
