```glance
- The year: two full-time semesters elsewhere; summer does not count. Arts and Sciences wants a 3.5 every semester and no grade below B-.
- The application: only offer holders use the Transfer Option application, with old portal credentials and no fee.
- Deadline: March 15 for the application and aid forms (2026 pattern); the page says "Application Available Winter 2027."
- Decisions: rolling from mid-April, most by late April or early May; some wait for final grades.
- Fallback: keep your sophomore year at your current school open until Cornell decides.
```

A Cornell Transfer Option is not guaranteed admission. Arts and Sciences calls it [an invitation to apply for sophomore-year transfer after one year of full-time study elsewhere, "not an offer of admission,"](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) and it cannot be deferred. Do the full year, meet your college's conditions, and file the separate Transfer Option application by [March 15 (the 2026 date), with no application fee](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants).

```reader "If you're holding an Arts and Sciences Transfer Option"
Your conditions are the clearest of any college. Take two full-time semesters or three quarters at a regionally accredited, degree-granting school. Keep a 3.5 GPA every semester with no grade below B-. Take broad liberal arts and sciences courses, not business, engineering or pre-professional ones, and fit in a writing-intensive composition course ([A&S](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process)). A&S will not pre-approve your courses, so check the list yourself. Summer classes do not count toward the year. The costly mistake is one B- or a 3.4 semester: that misses the stated bar, and a rescue is not promised. Your A&S extras on the application: none.
```

```reader "If your Transfer Option is for CALS, Dyson or the Hotel School"
Cornell's central Transfer Option page leaves the conditions to each college. Get them in writing from the college before your spring schedule locks: CALS at cals_admissions@cornell.edu or 607-255-2036 ([CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants)), Hotel at sha_admissions@cornell.edu ([Nolan School](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/)). For context, CALS sets a 3.0 minimum for all transfers and 3.5 for Biological Sciences. Your extras: CALS wants a resume, course schedule, AP scores and two essays; the SC Johnson College of Business wants a resume.
```

```reader "If your Transfer Option is for Human Ecology or ILR"
Human Ecology asks Transfer Option applicants for a resume, AP scores, a 600-word essay and a design supplement; ILR asks for AP scores ([Cornell Admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants)). The bars these colleges set for all transfers are useful targets: Human Ecology calls a 3.5 competitive, and most ILR transfer admits have at least a 3.4. Both want letter grades in the courses that match your major. The mistake that costs most is writing the 600-word essay the week of March 15. Draft it over winter break.
```

```reader "If you're the parent paying for this"
Budget two years: the year at the other school, then a Cornell sophomore year billed at $96,318 for 2026-27 at endowed tuition, or $72,188 for a New York resident in a contract college ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend)). File the CSS Profile and FAFSA with your student by March 15, 2027. Aid is need-based only; families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get tuition covered by grants. Keep a deposit in place at the current school for sophomore year until Cornell decides, which can run into May.
```

## What the Transfer Option is, in Cornell's words

A Transfer Option is an offer some first-year applicants get instead of admission. Arts and Sciences says it goes to [a select group of first-year applicants](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) as an invitation to apply for sophomore transfer after one year of full-time study elsewhere. It names a college, and that college reads your second application.

Three things follow from the wording:

- **It is not admission.** A&S says so directly. Cornell's [Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) never calls it guaranteed and publishes no count of offers.
- **It cannot be deferred.** A gap year does not meet it. You need two full-time semesters (or three quarters) at another school starting this fall.
- **It is a sophomore route.** You arrive after one year, not two.

It is also not a community college pathway or a general guarantee program. Those are separate routes with their own wording.

## Is it guaranteed? What students and consultants say

Our count of student and consultant pieces on this question finds six from 2017 to 2026, and they disagree. On College Confidential's [decision-night thread of March 26 and 27, 2020](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/cornell-class-of-2024-transfer-option/2087946), posters disagreed on whether the offer was guaranteed. Some cited a CALS FAQ saying applicants who miss the requirements are reviewed with the regular transfer pool.

Richard Ballard spent his first year at Ithaca College on a Transfer Option and is now a CALS junior. He wrote in the [Cornell Daily Sun in 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/from-both-sides-of-the-hill-an-ic-transfers-perspective) that the offer was described to him inconsistently, as guaranteed and then not. He feared the second application would be as hard as the first.

The consultancy Koppelman Group wrote in [2023](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2023/7/3/using-the-cornell-transfer-option-read-this-first) that it has never seen one of its own students denied after completing all the requirements. It also flagged the denial reasons it collected from Reddit as uncertain.

Our advice: treat it as a strong but conditional invitation. Meet every condition, and keep a real plan for sophomore year if it does not convert.

## The year elsewhere: what counts

Arts and Sciences publishes the most complete conditions. Other colleges set their own.

| Condition | Arts and Sciences rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Two full-time semesters or three quarters | Fall 2026 and spring 2027 at one school |
| School | Regionally accredited, degree-granting | A four-year college or university |
| Summer | Does not count | Summer classes cannot replace a semester |
| Courses | Broad liberal arts and sciences; not business, engineering or pre-professional programs | Skip a pre-business or nursing track |
| Writing | At least one writing-intensive composition course, encouraged | Take it in the fall if you can |
| Grades | 3.5 GPA each semester, no grade below B- | A 3.6 fall and a 3.4 spring misses it |
| Pre-approval | A&S cannot pre-approve courses | Match the rules yourself |
| Major | Shifting into a more selective or sequential major may hurt | Stay near the interest you applied with |

Source: [Arts and Sciences admissions requirements](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process).

The grade rule is per semester, not cumulative. A strong spring cannot repair a weak fall.

Example: an A&S holder at a state university earns a 3.7 in fall 2026 with four As and one B. In spring 2027 the midterm grades sit at a B+ average with one C+. That C+ is below the B- floor. Finishing the term with it would miss the stated condition. The best move is office hours and a tutor before finals, plus a confirmed sophomore-year spot at the current school.

For CALS, Dyson, Hotel, Human Ecology and ILR, ask the college for its written conditions. As a reference point only, Koppelman Group [wrote in 2023](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2023/7/3/using-the-cornell-transfer-option-read-this-first) that most CALS Transfer Option majors need a 3.0 and Biological Sciences a 3.5. Those match the [3.0 minimum and 3.5 for Biological Sciences](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) that CALS sets for every transfer applicant. CALS also wants letter grades, with no pass/fail.

## How to apply under the Transfer Option

You do not use the regular transfer route. [Only students who received a Transfer Option offer](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) use the Transfer Option application. You sign in with the applicant portal credentials from your first-year application. Everyone else applies through the Transfer Common App.

A Reddit commenter in [January 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qbvg0g/snapshots/2026-01-16T10%3A01%3A13.578438Z) said the Transfer Option application is the full Common App again with slight changes. Follow the instructions on Cornell's page, not the thread.

| | Transfer Option application | Regular transfer application |
|---|---|---|
| Who uses it | Only Transfer Option offer holders | Every other transfer applicant |
| How you sign in | Your earlier applicant portal credentials | A new Transfer Common App account |
| Fee | None | $85, or a fee waiver |
| Main deadline | March 15 (2026 pattern) | March 15, 2027 |
| Other materials | Mid-Term Report may come later | April 15, 2027 |
| Decisions | Rolling from mid-April | Late April through June |

Sources: [Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants); [transfer applicants page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

Every Transfer Option applicant sends:

```figure

rows:
  - label: The Transfer Option application, signed in with your old portal credentials (no fee)
  - label: Official transcripts from every college you attended
  - label: Your final high school transcript
  - label: The College Report, completed by an official at your current school
  - label: The Mid-Term Report, which you fill in yourself with spring grades (it may arrive after March 15)
  - label: Your college's extras (table below)
```

The College Report is a standing form from your dean or registrar, not a recommendation. The [Mid-Term Report](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) is yours: you list your spring courses with official or estimated grades.

### Extras by college

| College on your offer | Extra materials |
|---|---|
| Arts and Sciences | None |
| Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS) | Resume, course schedule, AP scores, two essays; Landscape Architecture adds a portfolio via SlideRoom |
| Human Ecology | Resume, AP scores, a 600-word essay, a design supplement |
| SC Johnson College of Business (Dyson, Hotel) | Resume; no interview |
| Industrial and Labor Relations (ILR) | AP scores |

Source: [Cornell's Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants).

## Deadlines and decisions

For fall 2026 entry the Transfer Option application and the aid application were both due [March 15, 2026](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants). The page now carries a link reading "Application Available Winter 2027" for the next cycle. Plan on March 15, 2027 and check the page when it opens.

US aid forms for fall 2027 transfers have a fixed date: the [CSS Profile (code 2098) and FAFSA (002711) by March 15, 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). New York residents also file with HESC, using 0174 for contract colleges or 0175 for endowed ones. The CSS fee is waived for families with adjusted gross income up to $100,000.

Decisions roll [from mid-April, with most in late April or early May](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants). Some wait for final spring grades.

## Plan your fallback

If the invitation does not turn into admission, you have no other Cornell offer. The year-one school is your fallback, so keep it intact:

1. Register for fall 2027 classes there as if you were staying.
2. Sign up for sophomore housing there, and learn its cancellation terms.
3. Pay any deposit it requires for sophomore year, knowing a Cornell decision may come in May.
4. Keep your aid application current at that school too.

Some Cornell decisions wait for final grades, so you may hold both plans into May. A deposit you lose at the old school costs less than a fall with no school.

## After you're admitted: the sophomore transfer year

Once admitted, you follow the same path as any sophomore transfer. Arts and Sciences requires [at least 60 Cornell credits and four semesters in residence](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). One year elsewhere plus four Cornell semesters is the usual four years.

**Housing.** New transfers [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Apply on the Housing and Dining Portal [within ten days of receiving your NetID](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process); there is no fee and no deposit. Transfers may be placed on West Campus, South Campus, North Campus or in Program Houses. That wording describes a required room, not a named building or a guarantee; the only building you pick by name is a Program House. Confirmations start in mid-June.

**Cost.** Endowed tuition is [$73,946 for 2026-27, and New York residents in contract colleges pay $49,816](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). The contract colleges are CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks and Dyson. Families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get tuition covered by grants, and up to $75,000 the full cost.

**Arrival.** In 2026, [transfer move-in and Family Orientation were August 18](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation), a day after first-year move-in. New Student Orientation ran August 18 to 23 and includes transfer small groups. **Friends.** Transfer Option admits [found first friends in a large Discord of other Transfer Option students](https://admissions.cornell.edu/node/6748) over the summer, in an admissions student Q&A. In a [2020 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/), an ILR Transfer Option student from Penn State and a student from Maryland both urged new transfers to message classmates and step outside their comfort zone. Join the Discord as soon as you are admitted.

**Credits.** Each college evaluates your year-one credits after you commit. A [Her Campus Cornell article](https://www.hercampus.com/school/cornell/transfer-option-blessing-disguise/) says Cornell reviewed the author's syllabi and approved most of her classes after her Transfer Option year. Save every syllabus.

## Common mistakes

- **One B- or a 3.4 semester on an Arts and Sciences offer.** It misses the [stated condition](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Posters in 2020 cited a CALS FAQ saying such applicants are reviewed with the regular transfer pool, which is a far harder file to win.
- **Taking a gap year.** The offer cannot be deferred. A year off forfeits it.
- **A business, engineering or pre-professional schedule on an A&S offer.** It is off the stated plan, and A&S will not pre-approve it.
- **Applying through the regular Transfer Common App.** Wrong route, and an $85 fee the Transfer Option application does not charge.
- **No fallback at the year-one school.** If the invitation does not convert, there is no other Cornell offer, and you may have given up your sophomore room and classes there.
- **Skipping the March 15 aid forms.** The aid deadline is the same as everyone's. Late forms can leave you choosing without an aid estimate.
- **Missing the ten-day housing window after your NetID.** It is the only timing Cornell gives for transfer housing. In 2016, when placement went by application date, [about 30 transfers began fall in converted North Campus lounges](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/03/transfer-students-forced-to-live-in-north-campus-lounges/).

## FAQ

**Can I go to community college for the Transfer Option year?** Arts and Sciences asks for a [regionally accredited, degree-granting institution](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) and full-time study in broad liberal arts and sciences. Confirm with the college named on your offer before you enroll.

**Can I switch colleges or majors on the Transfer Option?** The offer names a college. A&S warns that moving toward a more selective or sequential major may hurt your application.

**Do I need to send SAT or ACT scores again?** No. Cornell says the SAT and ACT are [neither required nor expected for transfer applicants](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/).

**Will Cornell meet my financial need as a Transfer Option admit?** Cornell meets [100% of demonstrated need for admitted undergraduates](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) with grants, work-study and reasonable loans. You get a Cornell Aid Estimate soon after admission if your forms are in.

**Who gets the bills once I enroll?** Fall tuition bills in early July and spring in January, as [Cashnet e-bills](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances). Parents see them only if the student invites them.
