```glance
- Military training, ACE military credit and CLEP earn no Cornell credit; only college courses with a C or better can count.
- College courses seven or more years old are generally not eligible for transfer credit.
- Out of school two or more years? A personal or employer reference can replace the professor's Academic Evaluation.
- Veterans choose between the GI Bill (Yellow Ribbon spots for undergraduates are unlimited) and a Cornell aid package.
- Student veterans are not required to live in campus housing; Veterans House is there if you want it.
- Fall 2027 deadlines: March 15, 2027 for the application and aid forms, April 15 for everything else.
```

Veterans and older students apply through the same Transfer Common App as everyone else, by [March 15, 2027 for fall 2027](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), with a dedicated Veterans Admissions Team to help. Cornell is [not typically able to award credits for military training](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans), and college courses [seven or more years old are generally not eligible](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), so your recent college coursework is what transfers. Veterans then choose the GI Bill or a Cornell aid package, and [are not required to live on campus](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs).

```reader "If you're a veteran"
Email the Veterans Admissions Team at veteranadmissions@cornell.edu before you apply; the [veterans page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans) sends fee and document waiver requests there too. Do not count on credit for your service: the [Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) marks ACE military credit "No". File the CSS Profile and FAFSA by March 15, 2027 anyway, because the [Veterans FAQ](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs) lets you choose between your GI Bill and a financial aid package, and you can only compare if you have both. The Student Veteran Advisor is at sva@cornell.edu. Housing is your call: the dorm rule is waived for student veterans, and [Veterans House](/housing/veterans-house-cornell/) is a Program House you request by name.
```

```reader "If you're an older student who has been out of school"
Two rules decide your application. First, the professor's Academic Evaluation can be replaced: applicants out of school two or more years [may substitute a personal or employer recommendation](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Second, old credits fade: courses [seven or more years old are generally not eligible](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), though the department can decide otherwise. A course from 2018 will likely not count for fall 2027 entry. If you have fewer than 12 recent college credits, take a term or two at a community college first. If you are at least four years past high school and need to keep working, compare the [online part-time SCE degree](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/06/cornell-sce-suny-broome-create-transfer-pathway-new-online-bachelors-degree).
```

```reader "If you're transferring with a partner or children"
Cornell's family apartments at Hasbrouck are [graduate-only and not open to undergraduate transfers](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). Student veterans are not required to live on campus; other new transfers must, unless Housing grants an exemption you [request through the housing application](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) while it is open. Do not sign an Ithaca lease until that answer is in writing. Budget health cover too: adding a spouse, partner or one child to the Student Health Plan costs [$4,092 from July or $3,751 from August](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27) for 2026-27, and two or more children cost $8,184 or $7,502. Once you arrive, the Tatkon Center points [student families](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation/beyond-first-week) to support offices.
```

## Who counts as a transfer, at any age

Age does not change the rules. You are a transfer applicant if you hold a high school diploma or equivalent and have [at least 12 semester hours of college credit after high school, or full-time enrollment at another institution](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Exam credit such as AP does not count toward the 12. Anyone who already holds a bachelor's degree cannot transfer in for a second one.

You will be older than most classmates. The average Cornell undergraduate is 19.7, and [0.6% are 25 or older](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) (fall 2025). That is about 97 of 16,138 undergraduates, by our math. Veterans are a visible group within the transfer class: the Chronicle counted [603 transfers and 23 transfer veterans](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429) arriving in fall 2026.

## What credit you get

Cornell credits college courses, not experience. Each college evaluates your transcript case by case after admission. A course needs a [C or better and must closely parallel a Cornell course](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/327-how-are-my-transfer-credits-evaluated/). Most colleges accept up to 60 credits, and you must still earn [at least 60 credits at Cornell](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) (Engineering caps transfer credit at 66, Architecture at 64).

| What you bring | Cornell credit? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Military training | Not typically awarded | [Admissions, veterans page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans) |
| ACE military credit recommendations | No | [Common Data Set 2025-26](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) |
| CLEP exams | No | [Common Data Set 2025-26](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) |
| Work, life or military experience (Engineering) | No | [Engineering requirements](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) |
| Work or military training (ILR) | No | [ILR guidelines, fall 2026](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) |
| International military courses | No | [University Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) |
| College course, C or better, under 7 years old | Yes, if about 80% similar to a Cornell course | [University Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) |
| College course 7 or more years old | Generally no; the department may decide otherwise | [University Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) |

Two details help returning students. A course cannot be refused [because it was taken at a community college or online](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). And the US school must be regionally accredited, with a syllabus and an official transcript for each course.

Military service still matters in admission, just not as credit. The [veterans page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans) calls military training and experience among the many factors in review.

Example: a veteran applying for fall 2027 took 30 community college credits in 2017 and 2018, then 24 credits online in 2025 and 2026. The 2017 and 2018 courses will be around nine years old at entry, so they are generally out. The 24 recent credits can count if each earned a C or better and matches a Cornell course. At a college that needs 120 credits, that leaves 96 to earn in Ithaca: about seven semesters at 15 credits each, by our math. If the departments accepted the 30 older credits too, 66 would remain: five semesters.

## How the application differs

The pieces are the same as for any transfer. Two of them change for you.

**The Academic Evaluation.** It normally comes from an instructor of a full-credit college course. If you have been out of school two or more years, you [may substitute a personal or employer recommendation](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). A commanding officer or supervisor who saw your work fits the second kind. One exception to watch: ILR's fall 2026 guidelines require [one academic recommendation from a college professor](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf), so ILR applicants should ask the Veterans Admissions Team which rule applies.

**Help with waivers.** The [veterans page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans) names a Military/Veteran Admissions and Enrollment Services team and asks veterans to email veteranadmissions@cornell.edu about application fee or document waivers. The $85 fee is also waived automatically when it is a burden and you plan to apply for aid.

Everything else follows the [standard checklist](/transfer/cornell-transfer-application-materials/): the essay of 250 to 650 words, a college-specific Writing Supplement, the College Report, transcripts from every college attended, your final high school transcript, and a Mid-Term Report if you have spring courses. No SAT or ACT is required. Those materials are due [April 15, 2027](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and decisions roll from late April through June.

## GI Bill or Cornell aid

Cornell [allows military students to choose between a financial aid package and their GI Bill](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs). The same FAQ encourages you to apply for financial aid even when you plan to use Post-9/11 benefits. Do that, and decide when you can see both offers. The FAFSA for 2027-28 [opened October 1, 2026](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants), and Cornell's aid route meets [100% of demonstrated need](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

| | Post-9/11 GI Bill route | Cornell aid route |
|---|---|---|
| What pays | VA benefits plus the Yellow Ribbon supplement | Grants, work-study and loans from Cornell's aid office |
| Yellow Ribbon spots | [Unlimited for undergraduates](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs) | Not used |
| Federal aid (Pell, loans) | [Still eligible](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans) | Included in the package |
| Forms | VA paperwork, plus a [change-of-school form](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs) if you used benefits before | [CSS Profile and FAFSA by March 15, 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) |
| Who to ask | Student Veteran Advisor, sva@cornell.edu, 607-255-0308 | finaid@cornell.edu |

The [veterans page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans) adds that students with Post-9/11 benefits are not required to use them at Cornell.

For scale, endowed-college tuition is [$73,946 for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), and New York residents in a contract college pay $49,816. The [aid guide for transfers](/transfer/cornell-financial-aid-transfer-students/) covers the income thresholds and codes.

## Housing: optional for veterans, required for most others

Most new transfers must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). The exemptions are two years of on-campus living at a previous school, or entering as a junior or senior. Age alone is not one of them.

Veterans are different. Cornell's [Veterans FAQ](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs) says student veterans are not required to live in campus housing and that the first-year residency requirements are waived. To be safe, also request the exemption [through the housing application](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) while it is open, and keep the FAQ handy.

If you want campus housing, Veterans House is one of the Program Houses, the only halls you [choose by name](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process) rather than through random allocation. Apply within ten days of getting your NetID. Our [Veterans House guide](/housing/veterans-house-cornell/) covers rooms, cost and the extra application.

## Orientation and support after you arrive

Transfers get the same orientation as first-years, and [non-traditional students may skip some optional events](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation). The required parts are your college's programs, Community at Cornell, Orientation Leader meetings and Cornell's Caring Community. Transfer move-in was August 18 in 2026; the [orientation guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-orientation-move-in/) has the day-by-day.

After week one, the Tatkon Center lists [support for non-traditional students, veterans and student families](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation/beyond-first-week), along with your academic advisor, the Dean of Students and Career Services.

## The online degree as an alternative

If moving to Ithaca full time does not fit, Cornell's School of Continuing Education runs a part-time online Bachelor of Professional Studies with [its own admissions process](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Applicants must be [at least four years beyond high school and bring 45 to 60 transferable credits](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/06/cornell-sce-suny-broome-create-transfer-pathway-new-online-bachelors-degree). Applications open January 2027 for a first cohort in August 2027, and classes are mostly asynchronous. It is a different degree from the residential colleges, so choose it for flexibility, not as a side door into them.

## Common mistakes

- **Counting on military or ACE credit.** Cornell gives none, so every planned credit has to come from college courses. At 15 credits a semester, a 30-credit gap is two extra semesters of tuition, by our math.
- **Building a plan on courses from 2018 or earlier.** Courses seven or more years old are generally not eligible. Retake the prerequisites your college lists, or ask the department to review them.
- **Asking an old professor who barely remembers you.** If you have been out two or more years, a supervisor or commanding officer who knows your work can write instead (check ILR's professor rule).
- **Skipping the aid forms because you have the GI Bill.** You can only choose the better route if you filed the CSS Profile and FAFSA by March 15, 2027.
- **Signing a family lease before housing is settled.** Non-veteran transfers are bound by the one-year rule, and a new transfer [cannot cancel a housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/12-month-undergraduate-housing-license-2026-2027) unless they leave Cornell or win an exemption.
- **Expecting family housing on campus.** Hasbrouck is graduate-only.

## FAQ

**Does Cornell take the Post-9/11 GI Bill and Yellow Ribbon?** Yes. Cornell [participates in the Yellow Ribbon Program](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans), with an unlimited number of undergraduate spots.

**Can I still get Pell Grants or federal loans while using VA benefits?** Yes. Veterans who receive education benefits are [still eligible for federal aid, loans and Pell Grants](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans).

**Is there an age limit for transferring?** No. The rules are the 12-credit minimum and no prior bachelor's degree. Cornell takes transfers who have [completed two to four semesters elsewhere](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), and first-semester students cannot apply.

**Who do I talk to about credit before I apply?** [Undergraduate Admissions answers credit questions](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment) before you enroll, and veterans can start with the Veterans Admissions Team. Official evaluations happen only after admission, by your college.

**Can I enter as a junior to graduate faster?** Sometimes, but Cornell [discourages junior transfer from four-year schools](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) because the [60-credit cap](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) means credits are lost. See the [credit guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-credit-how-many-credits-transfer/).
