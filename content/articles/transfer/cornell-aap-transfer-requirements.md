```glance
- B.Arch.: AAP takes no external transfer applications for fall 2027.
- B.F.A.: at least ten artworks through SlideRoom, a 3 to 5 minute recording and an artist statement, all by March 15, 2027.
- Urban and Regional Studies: no portfolio; application, AAP personal statement and at least one recommendation.
- GPA floor: 2.7 cumulative, in good standing. Admitted Cornell transfers are "generally" 3.3 or better.
- Second deadline: April 15, 2027 for transcripts and reports; decisions late April through June.
- Credit: judged only after an offer; you get two weeks to send syllabi and coursework. Cap 60 credits (64 in Architecture).
```

Cornell's College of Architecture, Art, and Planning (AAP) is [not accepting external transfer applications for the fall 2027 B.Arch.](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/), so an outside applicant's real choices are the B.F.A. in Art and the B.S. in Urban and Regional Studies. Both need a [2.7 cumulative GPA](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/), an AAP personal statement and a recommendation; the B.F.A. adds a portfolio of [at least ten artworks through SlideRoom](https://aap.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/art). Everything AAP reviews first is due March 15, 2027.

```reader "If you're hoping to start in spring"
External transfer into AAP is fall only. Cornell's admissions page says applications are [considered for the fall semester only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), so the next door opens in August 2027, with the portfolio due March 15, 2027. AAP did take spring transfers once: it was one of five colleges that enrolled [72 spring 2023 transfers](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). The December 1 spring deadline on AAP's page is the [internal transfer date](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/), for students already enrolled at Cornell. The costly mistake is leaving your school in December expecting a January start. Stay, finish your studio work and build the portfolio for March. More in [our spring transfer guide](/transfer/cornell-spring-transfer-fall-only/).
```

```reader "If you're entering as a junior"
Count credits before you count portfolio pieces. The Registrar caps transfer credit at [60 in most colleges and 64 in Architecture](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and you need at least 60 Cornell credits (90 in Architecture). A B.F.A. applicant arriving with 75 credits loses 15. Cornell's own FAQ [discourages transferring in or after the junior year](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) from a four-year school for exactly this reason. One upside: transfers matriculating as juniors are [exempt from the residential requirement](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) if they request it in the Housing application, so start looking at Collegetown leases the day you are admitted.
```

```reader "If you're applying from outside the US"
File the CSS Profile by March 15, 2027, the same day as your portfolio. Cornell's international aid page says an applicant who misses that deadline [cannot receive undergraduate aid later](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international), and admission is need-aware for international students. AAP is an endowed college, so tuition is [$73,946 for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) whatever your residency. Documents not in English need [official English translations](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), which a teacher or school official may provide. Studio courses taken abroad can be shown with a portfolio during the two-week credit window after an offer. See [our international transfer guide](/transfer/international-transfer-to-cornell/) for F-1 steps.
```

## Which AAP programs take external transfers for fall 2027

Cornell's [transfer requirements page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/college-and-school-transfer-admissions-requirements) lists three AAP majors: Architecture (B.Arch.), Art and Urban and Regional Studies. For fall 2027, only two of them are open to outside applicants.

| Program | Open to external transfers, fall 2027 | Portfolio | Extra pieces | Credit cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture (B.Arch.) | [No](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) | Required when open | Video interview, per the [central page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/college-and-school-transfer-admissions-requirements) | [64 credits](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) |
| Art (B.F.A.) | Yes | At least ten artworks, SlideRoom | 3 to 5 minute recording; artist statement | 60 credits |
| Urban and Regional Studies (B.S.) | Yes | None | AAP personal statement; recommendation | 60 credits |

AAP's B.Arch. statement names the fall 2027 cycle only. Apply to the B.F.A. or Urban and Regional Studies because you want that degree, not as a side door. Moves between AAP majors go through [a change of major form](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/), so plan on finishing the degree you were admitted to.

AAP is small. In fall 2021 it expected [15 of Cornell's 674 new fall transfers, plus 1 in spring](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf), the fewest of the eight colleges in that count. CALS expected 243.

## What AAP asks of every transfer applicant

AAP's floor is [good academic standing and a cumulative GPA of at least 2.7](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/). That is a minimum, not a target. Cornell says admitted transfers university-wide [generally have a 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/), and it weighs the essay, your current grades and professor recommendations most. Compare college by college in [our GPA guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-gpa-requirements-by-college/).

AAP sets a GPA floor and a materials list, not a prerequisite course list. Unlike Engineering or Human Ecology, it does not screen out applicants for a missing course. Your courses matter later, when AAP matches them for credit.

| B.F.A. portfolio, recording and artist statement | You, through SlideRoom | March 15, 2027 | [AAP Art](https://aap.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/art) |

## The B.F.A. portfolio, piece by piece

AAP's Art page is specific. You submit [high-quality images or videos of at least ten artworks](https://aap.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/art).

- **Mix:** up to six pieces may respond to AAP's optional prompts. At least four must be work you already made, on your own or in class.
- **Media:** any medium. The transfer page names photography, watercolor, jewelry or furniture design, sculpture and painting as examples.
- **Recording:** a 3 to 5 minute interview recording, video or audio.
- **Artist statement:** this is your AAP Writing Supplement.
- **Upload:** Cornell AAP SlideRoom only. AAP accepts no portfolio sent another way, and none after March 15.
- **Authorship:** all work must be your own, with no AI-generated artwork, images, video or audio passed off as yours.

Reviewers judge six qualities: form, content, rigor, drive, empathy and passion. AAP defines rigor as a strong link between form and content, shaped by critical thought. Choose pieces that show a line of thinking, not just range.

## Urban and Regional Studies: no portfolio

The portfolio rule on AAP's transfer page covers [the B.Arch. and B.F.A.](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) only. An Urban and Regional Studies applicant sends the Common App, the AAP personal statement and at least one recommendation, plus Cornell's standard reports and transcripts.

That makes the personal statement the main place to show fit. For internal applicants AAP wants it to cover interest in AAP, possible professional goals and how AAP helps reach them; the same outline works for an outside applicant. Tie your courses so far to the planning questions you want to study.

## The B.Arch. if it reopens

The closure covers fall 2027 only. When the B.Arch. takes transfers, the central page asks for [a portfolio and a video interview](https://admissions.cornell.edu/college-and-school-transfer-admissions-requirements). AAP's transfer page adds that B.Arch. applicants [need not submit architectural models or drawings](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/); summer program or internship work may go in.

The credit math is different too. The degree takes [154 credits over five years](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), with up to 64 transferred and at least 90 earned at Cornell.

## How AAP counts your credits

AAP [does not evaluate transfer credit until after an offer](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/). Admitted students then have two weeks to send syllabi and sample coursework. For studio and visual representation courses, a portfolio may stand in for the syllabus.

The Registrar's rules then apply. A course must be [about 80% similar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) in scope and outcomes to a Cornell course, and the Cornell department that owns it decides from the syllabus.

Example: a B.F.A. applicant finishes four semesters at a state university with 62 credits, including two drawing studios and a sculpture course. The 60-credit cap means 2 credits cannot count. For the studios, AAP lets a portfolio stand in for a syllabus. Two weeks is short, so have the images and syllabi ready before decisions arrive in late April.

## Already at Cornell? The internal route

Current Cornell students use a separate internal application. Deadlines are [December 1 for spring and May 1 for fall](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/). The 2.7 GPA rule applies. First-year students can apply at the end of their second semester at the earliest; applications after the first semester are denied. Internal applicants email the portfolio as a PDF to aap-admissions@cornell.edu instead of using SlideRoom. Decisions come after final grades.

## What it costs

AAP is an endowed college. Tuition is [$73,946 for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), with no New York resident discount. Families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get grants covering tuition, and up to $75,000 the full cost, per the same Chronicle story. Full budgets are in [our first-year cost guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-cost-first-year/).

## Common mistakes

- **Applying to the B.Arch. as an outside student for fall 2027.** AAP is not taking those applications. You lose the $85 fee and a cycle.
- **Treating April 15 as the portfolio date.** The portfolio is due March 15, and AAP accepts none late. Missing it ends a B.F.A. application for the year.
- **Sending fewer than ten works, or emailing them.** External portfolios go through SlideRoom only.
- **Filling the portfolio with prompt pieces.** At most six of the ten can answer prompts; at least four must be existing work.
- **Letting the two-week credit window pass.** AAP judges credit only from the syllabi and coursework you send in those two weeks; a studio course with nothing on file has nothing to match.
- **Arriving with more than 60 credits.** Everything above the cap is lost, 15 credits for a 75-credit junior.
- **International applicants skipping the CSS Profile.** Missing March 15 closes Cornell aid for the whole degree.

## FAQ

**Does AAP interview transfer applicants?** The B.F.A. asks for a [3 to 5 minute recorded interview](https://aap.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/art) with the portfolio. The B.Arch. calls for a video interview on the [central page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/college-and-school-transfer-admissions-requirements), but it is closed to outside applicants for fall 2027.

**Can I meet with AAP before I apply?** Yes. Email aap-admissions@cornell.edu to [set up a meeting](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) about the programs and the transfer process.

**Do I need SAT or ACT scores?** No. Transfer applicants self-report AP, IB or A-Level results in the [Common App](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

**Can a first-semester college student apply?** No. Cornell takes transfers who have [completed two to four semesters elsewhere](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), so apply in the spring of your first year for sophomore entry.

**Who handles my enrollment after I'm admitted?** AAP student services, at aap-studentservices@cornell.edu. See [our orientation guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-orientation-move-in/) for the summer steps.
