```glance
- Cornell says admitted transfers "generally" have a 3.3 or better; your college's number matters more.
- Hard floors: CALS 3.0 (Biological Sciences 3.5) and AAP 2.7. Engineering states no minimum.
- Typical or competitive: ILR admits mostly 3.4+; Human Ecology, Brooks and Dyson point to 3.5.
- Transfer Option in Arts and Sciences: 3.5 every semester and no grade below B-.
- Internal-transfer rules (3.0 in A&S, Hotel and Dyson) are for current Cornell students, not you.
- Fall 2025: 864 of 7,381 transfer applicants admitted (11.7%).
```

Cornell says the transfers it admits [generally have a 3.3 GPA or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). That is a typical figure, not a cutoff. You [apply to one college](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and its number ranges from a 2.7 minimum in AAP to 3.5 in Dyson, Human Ecology and CALS Biological Sciences.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You apply in the spring of your first year, by [March 15, 2027](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Compare your GPA to your target college's figure, not to 3.3. A 3.2 clears the [CALS 3.0 floor](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) but not Biological Sciences at 3.5. A 3.4 matches [ILR's typical admit](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines) but sits under the 3.5 that [Human Ecology](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) and [Dyson](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/) name. Cornell's FAQ says coursework and essays matter most. The costly mistake is a weak spring term. Your Mid-Term Report shows those grades, so keep them up through April.
```

```reader "If you're a junior-entry transfer"
Your GPA covers four semesters, so one bad term moves it less. Eligibility moves it more. [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) bars anyone with more than four semesters at a four-year school. [Hotel](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) and [Dyson](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/) bar current juniors and seniors. Cornell's [junior-transfer FAQ](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) discourages the move from a four-year school because of the roughly 60-credit cap. Juniors also need the longer course lists: [ILR](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines) wants two composition courses and both micro and macroeconomics. The mistake that costs most is a strong GPA sent to a college that cannot take you.
```

```reader "If you're a community college transfer"
Cornell states no preference by type of school, and [about a quarter of its transfers](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) come from community colleges. The same 3.3 signal applies to you. The TC3 Sustainable Farming and Food Systems A.A.S. asks for a 3.0 and B or better in every transfer course, and in return gives [priority consideration](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students) for CALS Agricultural Sciences, not a guarantee. Take courses for letter grades: [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) and Human Ecology do not accept pass/fail. In CALS an associate's degree fixes you at junior standing.
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
Your number is stricter than everyone else's. Arts and Sciences asks for a [3.5 GPA in each semester and no grade below B-](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) during the year elsewhere. A cumulative 3.5 does not meet it if one term was 3.4. The offer is an invitation to apply, not admission, and it cannot be deferred. Other colleges set their own conditions; a consultant, [Koppelman Group](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2023/7/3/using-the-cornell-transfer-option-read-this-first), says most CALS Transfer Option majors need 3.0 and Biological Sciences 3.5. Apply through the [Transfer Option application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), which has no fee, and keep a backup school for year two.
```

```reader "If you're an Engineering or computer science transfer"
Duffield [states no minimum GPA](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements). That does not make it easy. The gate is the course block: MATH 1910 and 1920, PHYS 1112 with PHYS 1110, and CHEM 2090 with the CHEM 2091 lab, done, in progress or planned. Missing any one means the application is not reviewed. For credit later, Engineering needs a C, not a C-. You are admitted to one major and stay in it for a full year. Computer science majors also enroll in Arts and Sciences, and the CS department keeps [a transfer credit form for each college](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/undergrad/csmajor/transfercredits). That B.A. route falls under the Arts and Sciences GPA signal.
```

## GPA by college: minimum, typical and competitive

Cornell's colleges use three kinds of GPA language. A minimum is a floor below which you should not expect review. A typical figure describes who got in. A competitive figure is what the college says strengthens a file. Read each row for its kind.

| College | GPA figure | Kind | Other grade rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| University-wide | [3.3 or better, "generally"](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) | Typical | Called "selective and highly competitive" |
| Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS) | [3.0 overall; 3.5 for Biological Sciences](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) | Minimum | Letter grades, C or better, for credit |
| Industrial and Labor Relations (ILR) | [Most admits have at least 3.4](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines) | Typical | C floor for transfer credit |
| Human Ecology | [3.5 or higher, mostly As and Bs](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) | Competitive | Letter grades only, no pass/fail |
| Hotel Administration (Nolan) | [No minimum; admits generally 3.3+](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) | Typical | Coursework and essay weigh most |
| Applied Economics and Management (Dyson) | [Should have at least 3.5](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/) | Guideline | B or better in calculus, microeconomics, statistics and writing |
| Brooks School of Public Policy | [No minimum; 3.5+ "increases competitiveness"](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/undergraduate/apply/transfer-applicants/external-transfers/) | Competitive | As and B+s in the prep courses help |
| Architecture, Art and Planning (AAP) | [2.7 cumulative and good standing](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) | Minimum | No external B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027 |
| Engineering (Duffield) | [None stated](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) | None | Missing a core course means no review |
| Arts and Sciences, Transfer Option | [3.5 each semester](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) | Condition | No grade below B- |

Arts and Sciences publishes no GPA figure for regular external applicants. The 3.5 rule above binds only Transfer Option holders. Regular applicants fall under the university's 3.3 signal.

## What a GPA does and does not decide

The same FAQ that gives the 3.3 figure says the [most important factors](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) are your essay on interest in the program, your grades at your current school and professor recommendations. Activities and the high school record are read too. Coursework and essays matter most.

That makes the course list as important as the number. Dyson wants calculus, microeconomics, statistics and a writing course, [each at B or better](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/). [Human Ecology](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) wants up to 23 sophomore credits depending on the major, including 8 in lab science and 6 in writing. Brooks wants 15 letter-graded credits across writing, microeconomics, statistics or calculus, and sociology. A high GPA built on other courses can still leave a file short.

The odds are steep at any GPA. In fall 2025, [7,381 people applied and 864 were admitted](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf), an 11.7% rate by our division of the Common Data Set counts. Cornell does not break that rate out by college, so no GPA figure can tell you your chances in one college.

Example: a sophomore has a 3.4 after two semesters, with intro economics, statistics, a composition course and biology with lab. That matches the typical ILR admit and clears the CALS 3.0 floor. It sits below the 3.5 that Human Ecology calls competitive and Dyson names as its guideline. CALS Biological Sciences is out at a 3.5 minimum. The strongest fits on grades alone are ILR and most CALS majors. The essay and the course list then decide between them.

Example: a Transfer Option holder in Arts and Sciences earns a 3.7 in the fall and a 3.4 in the spring. The year averages above 3.5. It still misses the [each-semester rule](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Arts and Sciences states the condition and stops there. In a [2020 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/cornell-class-of-2024-transfer-option/2087946), posters cited a CALS FAQ saying applicants who miss requirements are reviewed with the regular transfer pool.

## Internal-transfer GPAs are a different rule

Search results for "cornell transfer gpa" mix in rules for current Cornell students who want to switch colleges. Those numbers are lower and do not apply to you as an outside applicant.

| College | Internal-transfer GPA (current Cornell students) | External applicant figure |
|---|---|---|
| Arts and Sciences | [3.0 cumulative and 3.0 in 12 credits that semester](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) | 3.3 university signal; 3.5 each semester on a Transfer Option |
| Hotel Administration | [3.0 Cornell GPA](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/); transferees from other schools not eligible | No minimum; admits generally 3.3+ |
| Dyson | [3.0 minimum, 3.3+ competitive](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/) | 3.5 guideline |
| AAP | [2.7 cumulative](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) | 2.7 cumulative |

Once you are in, the internal route matters again. An external transfer can apply to move colleges [only after two semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), and Arts and Sciences strongly discourages it. Hotel's internal route excludes students who transferred in from another school. Pick the college you want to finish in.

## Common mistakes

- **Aiming at a single number for all of Cornell.** A "3.75 competitive" figure appears on a third-party site with no source. Applying to CALS Biological Sciences with a 3.3 because a blog said 3.3 costs the [$85 fee](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) and a cycle.
- **Reading an internal-transfer rule as yours.** Dyson's 3.0 minimum is for current Cornell students. Outside applicants are told to have at least 3.5.
- **Averaging the Transfer Option year.** Arts and Sciences counts each semester. One 3.4 term or one C+ misses the stated condition, whatever the yearly GPA.
- **Taking prerequisites pass/fail.** CALS and Human Ecology want letter grades, and Dyson wants B or better in four named courses. A pass in statistics does not count.
- **Reading "no minimum" as easy.** Engineering states no GPA floor, but one missing core course means the file [is not reviewed at all](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements).
- **Sending a strong GPA to a closed door.** CALS bars more than four semesters at a four-year school; Hotel and Dyson bar current juniors; AAP takes no external B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027.

## FAQ

**Do I need the SAT or ACT to transfer?** No. Cornell says test scores are [neither required nor expected](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/) of transfer applicants; your college grades carry that weight.

**Does a community college GPA count less?** Cornell says it has [no preference by institution or type](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). In a [2014 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/what-are-my-chances-for-transferring-to-cornell/1642313), one poster claimed community college grades run about a point high; that is one unverified opinion, not Cornell policy.

**Does my high school record still matter?** Yes, but less. Cornell reads it alongside your college work, and you send a final high school transcript covering grades 9 to 12 by [April 15](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

**Is the Transfer Option guaranteed if I hit 3.5 every term?** Arts and Sciences calls it an invitation to apply, not an offer of admission. The [Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) makes no promise of admission, so keep a fallback school.

**When are spring grades due?** The Mid-Term Report, which you fill in yourself with official or estimated grades, is due [April 15](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). The application itself is due March 15.
