```glance
- Not automatic: AP, IB and A-Level credit your old school gave does not carry over; Cornell's own rules apply.
- Each college sets the score: Engineering wants 5s on AP Calculus BC, Chemistry and Physics C Mechanics; ILR wants a 5 on AP Microeconomics.
- Caps: CALS, Hotel, Dyson and Human Ecology take at most 15 exam credits, ILR 12; most colleges count them inside the 60-credit transfer cap (Engineering 66).
- Dyson gives no exam credit for calculus, statistics, micro or macro; Brooks takes IB Higher Level only.
- Send AP scores with code 2098 and a written request to your college registrar by the last day of classes of your first term (Dec 7 in fall 2026).
- Exam plus matching college course: only the course counts.
```

No. AP, IB and A-Level credit that your current school accepted [does not transfer to Cornell automatically](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/); your Cornell college applies its own score rules, which can differ from your old school's. To get any of it, have your scores sent and make a [written request to your college registrar by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
A 4 your first college credited may earn nothing here: ILR takes AP Microeconomics only with a 5 (https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf). At sophomore entry you are well under the 60-credit cap, so claim every exam that meets the score. Send AP scores to code 2098 during the summer, then file the written request with your college registrar in your first fall. In the 2026-27 calendar that deadline was December 7 (https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). Credit by exam requires both steps by that date, and without it you may sit through a course you already passed by exam.
```

```reader "If you're an Engineering or computer science applicant"
Duffield accepts no exam credit below its threshold: AP Calculus BC needs a 5 (it meets MATH 1910), AP Chemistry a 5, and AP Physics C Mechanics a 5, which earns PHYS 1112 but leaves the PHYS 1110 lab required (https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements). Exam credit counts toward Engineering's 66-credit transfer cap. With a 4 on BC, take the optional MATH CASE during orientation; in 2026 it ran Thursday, August 20, 9 to 11 a.m. in Olin Hall 155, no calculators (https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/). Skipping it with a 4 means taking MATH 1910 again.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
IB, A-Level and French Baccalaureate results do not go through the College Board. Send those transcripts straight to your college registrar (https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). The same first-semester deadline and written request apply to you. Check your college's IB rule before you count on a diploma's worth of credit: Brooks takes only Higher Level exams (https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/current-students/undergraduates/course-enrollment-information/pre-college-credits/), and Hotel gives HADM 1410 plus 3 elective credits for a 7 in IB Economics (https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). University courses abroad are judged separately as transfer work; credit generally comes from Ministry-of-Education-recognized institutions, and ESL courses never count (https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
Some colleges want your AP scores in the Transfer Option application itself: CALS, Human Ecology and ILR list them among the extras (https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants). Credit your year-elsewhere school gave for those exams still does not carry over; Cornell rereads the scores after you enroll. In Arts and Sciences, AP or IB can place you out of only one of the two First-Year Writing Seminars (https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), so take the writing-intensive composition course A&S encourages in your year away. Then file the written request by the last day of classes in your first Cornell fall.
```

## Why your old school's AP credit does not carry over

Exam credit is [not automatic; Cornell's Courses of Study rules apply](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/). The line on your current transcript settles nothing; Cornell needs the scores themselves. Three more rules shape what survives:

- **Departmental exams from your old school do not count.** Credit a prior school awarded [solely on its own departmental exam is not awarded](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/326-does-the-credit-i-received-by-examination-at-my-previous-college-or-university-transfer/).
- **A matching college course wins.** Where an exam and a transfer course overlap, [only the transfer course counts](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).
- **CLEP is out in Arts and Sciences.** The A&S registrar [does not accept CLEP](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits).

## The rules by college

Most colleges count exam credit inside the overall transfer cap: up to [60 credits including exam credit in most colleges, 66 in Engineering and 64 in Architecture](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).

| College | Most exam credit | Score rules on the college's page |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering (Duffield) | Inside the 66-credit transfer cap (with GCE and CASE) | [AP Calculus BC 5 for MATH 1910; AP Chemistry 5; AP Physics C Mechanics 5 for PHYS 1112, lab still required](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) |
| ILR | [AP up to 12, inside 60](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) | AP Microeconomics needs a 5 |
| Hotel (Nolan) | [AP, IB and GCE up to 15, inside 60](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) | AP Micro 5 meets HADM 1410; IB Economics 7 gives HADM 1410 plus 3 elective credits; AP English 5 or IB English HL 7 meets a First-Year Writing Seminar; AP Statistics not accepted |
| Dyson (AEM) | [15 test credits (AP, IB, GCE, A-level)](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/) | No exam credit for calculus, statistics, microeconomics or macroeconomics |
| CALS | [AP up to 15, inside 60](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) | Test plus transfer credit up to 60 |
| Human Ecology | [15 pre-college credits](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) | AP or IB cannot be relied on for most requirements |
| Brooks | 60 in all | [IB Higher Level only; AP and IB statistics earn nothing](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/current-students/undergraduates/course-enrollment-information/pre-college-credits/) |
| Arts and Sciences | 60 in all | [AP or IB places out of only one of two First-Year Writing Seminars](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) |

**Dyson** says applicants [must take calculus, statistics, microeconomics and macroeconomics, or a more advanced equivalent, in college](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/). A 5 on AP Calculus does not excuse you from college calculus.

**Human Ecology** tells applicants [not to count on AP or IB](https://human.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/transfertips). Finish its [23 credits of sophomore prerequisites](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) in college instead.

## How to claim the credit, step by step

```figure

  - {label: "Take a CASE exam or language placement before classes if you want placement"}

  - {label: "Deadline: the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester"}

```

1. **Send the scores.** AP scores go from the College Board to [Cornell's code, 2098](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). The registrar awards the credit. IB, A-Level and French Baccalaureate transcripts go to your college registrar. Transfer Option holders applying to CALS, Human Ecology or ILR also [include AP scores in the application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants).
2. **Ask in writing.** External transfers [submit a written request to the college registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Each college runs its own student services office; the [Tatkon transfer pages](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment) list them.
3. **Beat the deadline.** The request is due by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester. In the 2026-27 calendar, fall instruction began August 24 and the [last day of fall instruction was December 7, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). A fall 2027 entrant uses the date on the 2027-28 calendar.
4. **Ask questions in the right place.** Before you enroll, Undergraduate Admissions answers credit questions; after, your [college registrar does](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). CALS [cannot give an official credit evaluation before admission](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), and Engineering [offers no pre-evaluation](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements).

Transfers usually [enroll during add/drop](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment), because registrars finish evaluations only after final transcripts arrive. The add deadline in fall 2026 was [September 8](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). File the exam request early in the term, so the credit lands before you pick spring courses.

## Placement without AP: CASE and language exams

If your score misses the line, departmental [Cornell Advanced Standing Exams (CASE) may give credit or placement](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Transfers can take CASE exams [in Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics and Physics, in person before the term](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). Online language placement covers Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Spanish.

In Engineering, CASE credit counts toward the [66-credit cap](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) like AP credit. Duffield strongly encourages the [optional MATH CASE](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/) for anyone with a 4 on AP Calculus BC (August 20 in 2026).

## Worked examples

Example: a sophomore applying to Engineering has a 5 on AP Calculus BC, a 4 on AP Chemistry and a 5 on AP Physics C Mechanics. The BC score meets MATH 1910. The Chemistry 4 earns nothing, so CHEM 2090 with the 2091 lab must be done in college. The Physics 5 earns PHYS 1112, but the PHYS 1110 lab is still required. All of it counts inside the 66-credit cap, under the [Duffield rules](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements).

Example: an ILR applicant's first school gave credit for a 4 on AP Microeconomics and for four other AP exams. At Cornell the Micro 4 earns [nothing, since ILR needs a 5](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf). However many credits the other four exams add up to, ILR counts no more than 12.

Example: an Arts and Sciences junior arrives with 60 credits of college coursework and five AP 5s. The 60 college credits already fill the [60-credit cap](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and exam credit sits inside it. The AP scores add no credits, but they can still place the student out of one of the two writing seminars.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming the credit came with you.** It did not. Without a request, you can sit a full Cornell course in a subject you passed by exam, a semester's seat spent for nothing.
- **Missing the first-semester deadline.** The written request is due by the [last day of instruction](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) of your first term. Credit by exam requires the scores sent and the request filed by that date.
- **Sending scores to your old school's code.** Cornell's College Board code is [2098](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment). Scores sent elsewhere never reach the registrar.
- **Skipping the MATH CASE with a 4 on BC.** In Engineering, that means retaking MATH 1910, a full course you might have tested out of on August 20.
- **Applying to Dyson on AP calculus or micro.** Dyson [requires those courses in college](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/), whatever your scores.
- **Taking a college course that repeats a strong AP exam.** Where the two overlap, [only the course counts](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). The exam credit vanishes, and the course uses up room under your cap.

## FAQ

**Does credit my old school gave for its own placement exam transfer?** No. Credit awarded [solely on a departmental exam is not awarded](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/326-does-the-credit-i-received-by-examination-at-my-previous-college-or-university-transfer/).

**Can I get a credit decision before I apply?** Not an official one. CALS [cannot evaluate credit before admission](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), Engineering offers [no pre-evaluation](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements), and A&S gives a [tentative evaluation only after acceptance and deposit](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits).

**What if I take a Cornell course that matches my AP exam?** At Brooks, a [Cornell course that overlaps an exam forfeits the exam credit](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/current-students/undergraduates/course-enrollment-information/pre-college-credits/). Ask your college registrar before you enroll in the course.

**Can I make up a missed score with a summer course elsewhere?** Yes, with [prior approval before you take it](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Arts and Sciences allows [up to 12 summer credits](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits); Engineering allows summer transfer credit only when you carry [fewer than 12 Cornell credits that term](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements).
