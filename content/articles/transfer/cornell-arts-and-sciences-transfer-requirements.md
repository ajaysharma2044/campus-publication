```glance
- Apply by March 15, 2027 for fall 2027 entry; the rest of the file is due April 15, 2027. There is no spring entry.
- A&S accepts up to 60 transfer credits. You still need 60 Cornell credits and four semesters in residence.
- Sophomore entry loses nothing. A junior with 90 credits loses 30 and spends ten semesters in college.
- Full-time liberal arts credit can fill A&S requirements and count toward the 100 A&S credits. Part-time, summer and CLEP credit cannot.
- Transfer Option holders need a 3.5 every semester elsewhere and no grade below B-.
- To move to another Cornell college later, you wait two semesters, and Hotel will not take you.
```

You apply to Cornell's College of Arts and Sciences through the Transfer Common App by [March 15, 2027](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), for fall entry only. A&S takes up to 60 credits from your old school, and you then need [at least 60 Cornell credits and four semesters in residence](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Apply after your first year if you can: up to 60 credits, nothing is lost.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer"
Apply in the spring of your first college year. Cornell takes students with [two to four semesters done](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), and first-semester students cannot apply. You enter A&S, not a major: students with [fewer than two years elsewhere](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) are not admitted directly to one. Take full-time, letter-graded liberal arts courses this year, including a writing course. Those credits can fill A&S requirements. The costly mistake is a summer or part-time course meant to fill a requirement. A&S treats it as a current student's outside course, so it meets no requirement and adds nothing to the 100 A&S credits.
```

```reader "If you're a junior-entry transfer"
Cornell [discourages transfer](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) during or after junior year at a four-year school. The 60-credit cap is why. Arrive with 90 credits and 30 are lost. You still owe four Cornell semesters, so college takes ten. That extra year cost $74,970 in [2026-27 endowed tuition and fees](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), before housing and aid. If you still apply, A&S expects the [intended major's prerequisite courses](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Find them on the department's own site before your last semester elsewhere, while you can still take them.
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
Your offer is an invitation to apply for sophomore transfer, [not admission](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), and it cannot be deferred. A&S wants two full-time semesters or three quarters at a regionally accredited degree-granting school; summer does not count. Take broad liberal arts and sciences, not business, engineering or pre-professional courses. Keep a 3.5 GPA every semester with no grade below B-. A&S will not pre-approve your courses. Apply through the [Transfer Option application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) with your old portal login, not the Common App. There is no fee, and A&S asks for no extra essays or resume. One B- breaks a stated condition, so guard every grade.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
File the CSS Profile by March 15, 2027 if you might ever want aid. Skip it and you can never get [Cornell aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international) as an undergraduate. Admission is need-aware for you. Courses from a Ministry-of-Education-recognized university generally transfer; [ESL and remedial courses never do](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Literatures in English reviews English courses, which decides whether one fills a writing seminar. Cornell's "minimum competitive" scores are [TOEFL iBT 100](https://admissions.cornell.edu/resources/submit-english-language-proficiency) (5.0 for exams taken from January 2026), IELTS Academic 7.5 and Duolingo 130. Non-English documents need official English translations, which a teacher or school official may provide.
```

```reader "If you're a current Cornell student switching colleges"
Moving into A&S from another Cornell college is a separate process. In the 2026 cycle you needed a [3.0 cumulative GPA](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) and a 3.0 in 12 credits the semester you applied. The earliest point is after your second semester; the latest is the end of your first junior semester. Seniors and second-semester juniors cannot apply. Applications go through internaltransfer.cornell.edu/apply, and the 2026 deadline was May 1. Do not use the Common App or the March 15 date: that is the external route.
```

## Who can apply

You need a high school diploma or equivalent, plus 12 semester hours of college credit earned after high school or full-time enrollment at another college. Exam credit does not count toward the 12 ([Cornell admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)). Students still in high school apply as first-years, whatever their dual-enrollment credits. Bachelor's degree holders are not eligible.

A&S uses Cornell's [central transfer dates](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Cornell says admitted transfers [generally have a 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). The same page names the essay on your interest in the program, your college grades and professor recommendations as what matters most.

A&S takes fewer transfers than CALS or ILR. Cornell's newest count by college is its 2021 profile: [102 of 674 fall 2021 transfers entered A&S](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf), against 243 for CALS and 116 for ILR. Across all colleges, [7,381 people applied and 864 were admitted](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) for fall 2025, an 11.7% admit rate.

## Sophomore or junior: what the 60-credit cap does

A&S accepts up to 60 credits, based on your [full-time semesters elsewhere](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits). You need 120 credits to graduate, and 60 of them plus four semesters must be at Cornell. The table below assumes 15 credits a semester and every course accepted up to the cap; real evaluations go course by course.

| Credits before Cornell | Accepted | Lost | Cornell semesters | Total semesters of college |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 (two semesters) | 30 | 0 | 6 | 8 |
| 45 (three semesters) | 45 | 0 | 5 | 8 |
| 60 (four semesters) | 60 | 0 | 4 | 8 |
| 75 | 60 | 15 | 4 | 9 |
| 90 | 60 | 30 | 4 | 10 |

Overloading at Cornell does not shorten the 90-credit case, because the four-semester rule still holds. Exam credit sits [inside the 60](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), so AP credit fills the cap faster.

Standing also decides the major. Students with [fewer than two years elsewhere](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) are not admitted directly to a major. Junior applicants need the [intended major's prerequisite coursework](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), listed on each department's site.

## How A&S counts your old courses

A&S issues a tentative credit evaluation only [after you accept and pay the deposit](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits). It wants courses "substantially equivalent" in content and rigor, and compares content hours, assessments and textbooks. Departments then decide whether a course counts toward a major or minor.

| Rule | What A&S does |
|---|---|
| Minimum grade | C or higher; pass/fail only with a written statement that the grade was C or better |
| Cap | Up to 60 credits, based on full-time semesters elsewhere |
| Requirements | Full-time liberal arts and sciences credit can meet them, including a First-Year Writing Seminar and language |
| The 100 A&S credits | The same full-time liberal arts credit counts toward the 100 A&S credits you need of 120 |
| Part-time, summer, high school, or after your offer | Treated under current-student rules: meets no requirement, adds nothing to the 100 |
| Quarter courses | Converted at two-thirds; only courses of 4 or more quarter credits meet requirements |
| CLEP | Not accepted |
| Your old school's departmental exams | [Not awarded](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/326-does-the-credit-i-received-by-examination-at-my-previous-college-or-university-transfer/) |
| Your transcript | Shows the school's name and total credits; your transfer GPA stays out of the Cornell GPA |

Each row comes from the [A&S registrar's transfer credit page](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits) unless linked. Some summaries say transfer credit counts only toward the 120 and never toward distribution requirements. Trust the A&S registrar page, then check your degree audit (DUST) once you arrive. A spring 2023 transfer, Abbey Yang '25, told the Sun her advisor said her biology credits should count, but [DUST did not yet show them](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). Keep every syllabus until the audit is right.

University rules sit on top. A course must be about [80% similar in scope and content](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) to its Cornell match. It cannot be refused for being at a community college or online. Courses seven or more years old are generally out.

Example: a hypothetical sophomore from a quarter-system school brings twelve 4-credit courses and three 3-credit courses. That is 57 quarter credits, or 38 semester credits at two-thirds. The twelve 4-credit courses can fill requirements. The three 3-credit courses add 6 credits but fill none.

### The writing seminar limit

AP or IB scores can [place you out of only one of the two First-Year Writing Seminars](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). A full-time college writing course can fill one through transfer credit. Take your writing courses full-time and keep the syllabi.

### Exam credit is a separate request

AP or IB credit your old school accepted [does not carry over automatically](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/). Send your scores and a written request to your college registrar by the [last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Where an exam and a transfer course overlap, only the course counts.

## The application, step by step

```figure
kind: steps
title: Fall 2027 entry to A&S, in order
rows:
  - label: "First year elsewhere: full-time, letter-graded liberal arts courses, including writing"
  - label: "March 15, 2027: Transfer Common App, $85 fee or waiver, essays; CSS Profile and FAFSA"
  - label: "April 15, 2027: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report, all transcripts"
  - label: "Late April to June 2027: rolling decision in the Applicant Portal"
  - label: "After you accept and deposit: tentative A&S credit evaluation"
  - label: "Last day of classes in your first Cornell semester: written request for exam credit"
```

1. **By March 15, 2027:** the Transfer Common App, the [$85 fee or a waiver](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), a 250 to 650 word essay and the college Writing Supplement. US applicants file the [CSS Profile and FAFSA](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) the same day. The SAT and ACT are [not required](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/).
2. **By April 15, 2027:** the College Report, which an official at your school completes; an Academic Evaluation from a college instructor; your own Mid-Term Report with spring grades; and every transcript.
3. **Late April through June 2027:** decisions roll out in the Applicant Portal. A quiet April is not a denial.

## The Transfer Option in A&S

Some first-year applicants get a Transfer Option instead of admission. A&S calls it [not an offer of admission](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). The A&S conditions: a full-time liberal arts year, a 3.5 each semester and no grade below B-. Moving toward a more selective or sequential major may hurt you.

A&S asks Transfer Option applicants for [no college extras](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), unlike CALS or Human Ecology. In the 2026 cycle the deadline was March 15, and Cornell says the 2027 application opens in winter 2027. Decisions roll from mid-April, mostly in late April or early May. Once in, you need the same 60 Cornell credits and four semesters.

Richard Ballard spent his Transfer Option year at Ithaca College and is now a CALS junior. He wrote in the Sun in 2025 that the option was [described to him as guaranteed and then not](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/from-both-sides-of-the-hill-an-ic-transfers-perspective), and that he feared the second application would be as hard as the first. Keep a sophomore-year fallback at another school.

## Computer science through A&S

Cornell's CS major comes as an Engineering B.S. or an A&S B.A. Transfers [apply to one of the two colleges and request CS](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/undergrad/csmajor/transfercredits). Engineering admits you straight into one major, and it will not review an application missing its required math, physics and chemistry courses. In A&S, a sophomore enters the college rather than the major, under the two-year rule above.

## Moving to another college after you arrive

External transfers in A&S can apply to another Cornell college [only after two semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). You still need four semesters in residence overall and at least two in A&S. Credits are re-evaluated when you move, so check your DUST report first. A&S strongly discourages the move. The [Hotel School's internal process](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) excludes anyone who came from another institution.

## Common mistakes

- **Transferring with 90 credits.** 30 credits are lost and college takes ten semesters, about $74,970 more in 2026-27 tuition and fees.
- **Filling a requirement with a summer or part-time course.** It meets no requirement and adds nothing to the 100 A&S credits, so you retake the subject at Cornell.
- **Counting on AP for both writing seminars.** Only one can go.
- **Taking 3-credit quarter courses for requirements.** Only 4-credit or larger quarter courses fill them.
- **Missing the exam credit request.** After the last day of first-semester classes, the AP or IB credit is gone.
- **One B- in a Transfer Option year.** It breaks a stated A&S condition.
- **Planning a switch into Hotel.** External transfers are ineligible, and every other switch waits two semesters.

## FAQ

**Does a New York resident pay less in A&S?** No. A&S is an endowed college, so 2026-27 tuition is [$73,946 for everyone](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters).

**Will a community college course count?** It can. The registrar says equivalency [cannot be denied](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) because a course was at a community college or online; it still needs a C and about 80% similarity.

**Can I take summer courses elsewhere once I'm at Cornell?** Up to 12 summer credits, with approval first, and no outside courses during fall or spring. Summer sessions [do not count toward residence](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits).

**Where do enrolled A&S students send outside-credit requests?** The Application for Credit from Other Institutions goes to [as-studentserv@cornell.edu or KG17 Klarman Hall](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits).

**How does an international student move an F-1 record to Cornell?** Your current school's advisor enters a release date in SEVIS. Cornell issues the I-20 [within two to three weeks of that date](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students), after proof of funding.

**Do I need an interview for A&S?** No. Cornell's admissions page requires one [only for Architecture](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).
