```glance
- Same dates as everyone: application and $85 fee by March 15, 2027; all other materials by April 15; decisions late April through June.
- Aid is need-aware for international applicants, and the CSS Profile is due March 15, 2027. Skip it and Cornell aid is closed for your whole degree.
- Cornell meets 100% of demonstrated need for admitted aid applicants, with no merit, athletic or talent aid. Grant money above tuition and fees has 14% withheld unless a tax treaty applies.
- You pay non-New York tuition at every college: $73,946 for 2026-27, and $96,318 billed for a double with the Unlimited meal plan.
- Credit from a Ministry-of-Education-recognized university abroad usually counts; ESL and remedial courses never do. Most colleges cap transfer credit at 60.
- F-1 students: your current school releases your SEVIS record, then Cornell issues the I-20 within two to three weeks.
```

International students apply to transfer to Cornell through the same Transfer Common App, with the same [March 15 and April 15 deadlines and $85 fee](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). The difference is money: admission is need-aware, and the CSS Profile is due [by March 15, 2027 for fall 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international), with no second chance later. If your family needs any aid in any year at Cornell, file it now.

```reader "If you're an international student at a US college"
You keep your F-1 status by moving your SEVIS record, not by applying for a new visa. After admission, take your Cornell acceptance letter to your current school's international office, agree a release date, and Cornell issues the I-20 [within two to three weeks after that date](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students), once you show proof of funding. The costly mistake is skipping the CSS Profile to look like a full-pay applicant. Do that and you [cannot receive Cornell undergraduate aid later](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants), even if your family's money changes in year three.
```

```reader "If you're applying from a university abroad"
[23 fall 2021 transfers](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf) came from institutions outside the US. Credit is [generally accepted from Ministry-of-Education-recognized institutions](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), but ESL, remedial and international military courses never transfer. Send official English translations of any non-English record; Cornell says a [teacher or school official may translate](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). A-Level and IB transcripts go to your college registrar after admission. For English, Cornell lists an IELTS Academic [minimum competitive score of 7.5](https://admissions.cornell.edu/resources/submit-english-language-proficiency) and Duolingo 130. The costliest mistake is counting on your ESL courses for credit.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying the bill"
Budget for non-New York prices at any Cornell college: tuition is [$73,946 for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), and a double with the Unlimited plan bills [$96,318 for the year](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend). The Student Health Plan comes on top unless waived: $1,705 for a new student's fall and $2,046 for spring. The first-year room is required and assigned by Cornell, with no housing fee or deposit. The fall tuition bill arrives in [early July](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances), and Cashnet e-bills reach a parent only if your student invites you. File the CSS Profile with your student by March 15, 2027. International families generally get no CSS Profile fee waiver, and a missed deadline closes Cornell aid for every undergraduate year.
```

```reader "If you can pay the full cost"
Skipping the CSS Profile sends your application in without an aid request, which counts because international admission is [need-aware](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). Skip it only if you are sure: an international applicant who misses the aid deadline cannot get Cornell undergraduate aid later. At 2026-27 prices the required on-campus year runs $95,218 to $99,169 billed, plus books, personal costs and health insurance. Prices for 2027-28 come out in late March 2027, after the March 15 aid deadline.
```

## Who counts as international

Cornell uses one test. You are an international applicant if you [do not have US citizenship or approved permanent residency](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). A pending green card still counts as international. Undocumented students, with or without DACA, are treated as domestic for admission and aid.

You also need to be a transfer at all. Cornell's [transfer page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) asks for at least 12 semester hours of college credit after high school, or full-time enrollment at another institution. Students who already hold a bachelor's degree cannot apply.

The pool is small. Of [674 fall 2021 transfers](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf), 86 were international, or 12.8%, the latest official breakdown. Overall, fall 2025 drew [7,381 transfer applicants, 864 admits and 625 enrollees](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf), an 11.7% admit rate by our math (864 divided by 7,381) for all transfers, domestic and international together.

## How to apply, step by step

You file the same application as domestic transfers, plus translations, an English score and the CSS Profile.

1. **Pick one college.** You apply to a Cornell college, not to the university, and each sets its own courses and GPA signals. Admitted transfers ["generally" have a 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/).
2. **October 1, 2026 onward: start the CSS Profile.** Use Cornell's [code 2098](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international). Upload documents through IDOC only when asked.
3. **Book the English test.** Scores are in the table below.
4. **March 15, 2027.** Transfer Common App, $85 fee or waiver, any portfolio, and the CSS Profile.
5. **April 15, 2027.** Everything else: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report, every college transcript and your final high school transcript, with [official English translations](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) of any non-English document.
6. **Late April through June.** Decisions roll out. The official aid offer comes "tentatively" in early April for applicants admitted by then.

SAT and ACT scores are neither required nor expected of transfers. You self-report AP, IB and A-Level results in the Common App's Academic History.

### English proficiency

The international transfer page [points to Cornell's Standardized Testing Policy](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). If English is not your first language, the [English proficiency page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/resources/submit-english-language-proficiency) asks for a score. US citizens are exempt, as is anyone who studied at least four years at a school that teaches in English.

| Test | Score Cornell lists | How to send |
|---|---|---|
| TOEFL iBT, taken January 2026 or later | 5.0 minimum, 5.5 recommended | ETS, code 2098 |
| TOEFL iBT, taken before January 2026 | 100 minimum competitive | ETS, code 2098 |
| IELTS Academic | 7.5 minimum competitive | Select Cornell University in the IELTS portal |
| Duolingo English Test | 130 minimum competitive | Share Score in your Duolingo account |
| Cambridge C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency | 191 minimum competitive | Select Cornell University when sharing results |

You can self-report a score in the Common App for the first review. If you are admitted, you must send official scores from the testing agency.

## Financial aid: need-aware, and now or never

Seven rules, from Cornell's [international aid page](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international) and [international transfer page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants):

| Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Admission is need-aware | Asking for aid is part of the admission decision |
| 100% of demonstrated need met | If you applied for aid and are admitted, Cornell meets your need |
| CSS Profile by March 15, 2027 | The only window; code 2098 |
| No aid if you miss the deadline | You cannot receive undergraduate aid later, in any year |
| Generally no CSS fee waiver | Your family pays the College Board fee |
| No merit, athletic or talent aid | All Cornell aid is need-based |
| 14% withholding | Grant money above tuition and fees is taxable, with 14% withheld unless a tax treaty applies |

Domestic transfers are admitted need-blind; international ones are not. That makes the CSS Profile a real choice. If there is any chance your family will need help over two or more years, file it. A stronger-looking full-pay application does not reopen the door later.

Forums add little: a [College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/does-cornell-university-provide-decent-financial-aid-packages-for-international-transfer-students/1919903) asking whether Cornell aids international transfers well drew no substantive answers. For all transfers in fall 2021, 32.7% received Cornell need-based grants, averaging [$44,712](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf); that count covers domestic and international transfers together.

Example: an international transfer's grant covers tuition and fees and leaves $10,000 toward housing and meals. That $10,000 is taxable, so 14%, or $1,400, is withheld unless a treaty between the US and the student's country applies.

## What the year costs

You pay the endowed, non-New York rate at every Cornell college. That includes CALS, ILR, Human Ecology, Brooks and Dyson, where only New York residents get the $49,816 rate. Tuition for 2026-27 is [$73,946](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters).

New transfers live on campus for their first year unless exempt, so the room and meal plan are part of the bill. Room rates come from the [2026-27 housing rate page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates); the Unlimited and House plans cost [$7,604 a year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). Mandatory fees are [$1,024](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend). Added up, before aid:

## Will your credits from abroad count?

Each Cornell college evaluates transfer credit [case by case, after admission](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/327-how-are-my-transfer-credits-evaluated/), and may ask for syllabi. Courses need a C or better and must closely parallel Cornell courses. For coursework from outside the US, the registrar adds these rules:

| Coursework | Counts? |
|---|---|
| Courses from a Ministry-of-Education-recognized institution | Generally accepted |
| ESL courses | Never |
| Remedial courses | Never |
| International military courses | Never |
| English courses taken abroad | Reviewed by the Department of Literatures in English |
| Courses seven or more years old | Generally not, unless the department decides otherwise |
| Quarter-system credits | Multiplied by two-thirds |

Source: [Credit Evaluation Services, Office of the University Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).

The cap is the other limit. Most colleges accept up to 60 credits, Engineering 66 and Architecture 64. You must earn at least 60 credits at Cornell (90 in Architecture). A student who arrives with more than 60 loses the rest.

### A-Levels, IB and other exams

Exam credit your old university accepted does [not carry over automatically](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/); Cornell applies its own rules. A-Level, IB and French Baccalaureate transcripts go to [your college registrar](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment), not to admissions. You must send scores and make a written request by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester. That was December 7 in fall 2026. Where an exam and a transfer course overlap, only the course counts.

## After you're admitted: moving your F-1 record

Cornell International Services lists the steps on its [F-1 transfer page](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students):

```figure

title: Moving an F-1 SEVIS record to Cornell
rows:
  - {label: "Take your Cornell acceptance letter and any Cornell transfer forms to your current school's international office"}
  - {label: "Agree a release date; your advisor enters it in SEVIS"}
  - {label: "Send proof of funding to Cornell admissions"}
  - {label: "Cornell admissions issues your I-20 within two to three weeks after the release date"}
  - {label: "Arrive in Ithaca and complete the online Required Status Activation"}
```

The record moves to Cornell on the release date, so pick it around your travel and work plans. If you are graduating from your current school, the release date must be in SEVIS before your 60-day grace period ends. J-1 students follow similar steps with a 30-day grace period. Do not walk into the International Services office to activate status; Cornell asks you to do it online.

Then housing. Apply through the Housing and Dining Portal [within ten days of getting your NetID](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process). There is no fee or deposit. New transfers must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). The exceptions: you lived on campus two years at your previous school, or you enter as a junior or senior. In 2026, transfers moved in on [August 18](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation), the same day as Family Orientation.

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the CSS Profile to look full-pay.** Cost: no Cornell aid in any later year, even after a family job loss.
- **Filing the CSS Profile after March 15.** It shares the application's deadline, and late means never.
- **Assuming a grant above tuition is tax-free.** 14% is withheld on the part above tuition and fees unless a treaty applies: $1,400 on $10,000.
- **Counting ESL or remedial courses toward your degree.** None transfers, so a student who planned on them needs those credits again at Cornell.
- **Sending A-Level or IB results to admissions only.** Credit requests go to the college registrar by the last day of your first semester's classes, or the credit is not awarded.
- **Budgeting at the New York rate for CALS or ILR.** The $49,816 rate is for New York residents; you pay $73,946, $24,130 more for 2026-27.
- **Forgetting the health plan.** $3,751 for a new student's first year at 2026-27 rates if you do not waive it.

## FAQ

**Can I apply for aid in my second year at Cornell instead?** No. International applicants who do not apply for aid by the deadline [cannot receive undergraduate aid later](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants).

**Do I need a TOEFL if my US college teaches in English?** The exemption is [four years at a school](https://admissions.cornell.edu/resources/submit-english-language-proficiency) where English is the primary language of instruction. One or two years of college in English alone falls short of that.

**When do I get my Cornell I-20?** Within two to three weeks after your current school's release date, once Cornell admissions has your proof of funding, per [International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students).

**Can I start in January?** No. External transfers are [considered for the fall semester only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

**Does applying for aid hurt my chances?** It is part of the decision, since international admission is [need-aware](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international). If you are admitted after applying for aid, Cornell meets 100% of your demonstrated need.

**Will my transfer grades count in my Cornell GPA?** No. The [registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) lists only your old institution's name and total transfer credits on the Cornell transcript.
