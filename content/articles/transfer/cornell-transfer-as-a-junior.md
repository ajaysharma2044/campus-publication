- 60 credits or fewer: nothing lost. 75 credits: 15 lost, 9 semesters. 90 credits: 30 lost, 10 semesters.
- An extra year costs $74,970 in 2026-27 endowed tuition and fees, before housing and aid.
- CALS bars more than four semesters at a four-year school; Hotel bars current juniors; Engineering takes first-semester juniors only.
- Junior entrants are exempt from the one-year on-campus rule, so you rent; Collegetown studios in large buildings average $2,006 a month.
- Apply by March 15, 2027; decisions come late April through June, after the best leases are signed.

Usually no, if you are at a four-year school: Cornell's admissions FAQ [discourages junior-year transfer](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) because the roughly 60-credit cap means many credits are lost. At 15 credits a semester, a student who arrives with 90 credits keeps 60, loses 30 and spends ten semesters in college, under the [Arts and Sciences rules](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits). Junior entry works when you bring 60 credits or fewer (an associate's degree, or exactly two years), your college takes juniors, and its junior prerequisites are done.

```reader "If you're finishing sophomore year at a four-year college"
You would enter as a junior, and [Cornell itself suggests](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) finishing where you are and considering Cornell for graduate study. Count the credits you will hold by the end of this spring, AP included. At 60 or fewer, nothing is lost. Past 60, each extra 15 credits adds a semester, because you still owe four semesters and 60 credits at Cornell. Check standing first: [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) will not take you if you will have more than four semesters at a four-year school, and Hotel will not take current juniors. If you do go, you are exempt from the housing rule, so start the lease search the week you are admitted. The costly mistake: applying with 90 credits and expecting to graduate on time.
```

```reader "If you're finishing an associate's degree"
An associate's degree or about 60 credits is the cleanest junior entry: up to 60 credits nothing is lost under the cap. In CALS, an associate's degree or four semesters means junior standing, and you cannot choose sophomore entry instead. Cornell says it has [no preference by school type](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) and that about a quarter of transfers come from community colleges. A community college or online course [cannot be refused for being one](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), but it needs a C or better and about 80% overlap with a Cornell course. The costly mistake: taking courses pass/fail. CALS and Human Ecology give no credit for them.
```

```reader "If you're aiming at Engineering as a junior"
[Duffield](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) takes juniors only in their first semester of junior standing, and it admits you straight into one major that you keep for a year. The block of MATH 1910, MATH 1920, PHYS 1112 with PHYS 1110 and CHEM 2090 with CHEM 2091 must be finished or in progress, or the file is not reviewed. Engineering caps credit at 33 per full-time year and 66 in all, so two full years elsewhere fill the cap exactly. You still need 60 credits at Cornell. Chemical Engineering asks applicants to come as sophomores if possible. The costly mistake: a C- in a course you want credited; Engineering's floor is a C.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year deciding whether to wait"
Apply this spring instead. Cornell takes students with two to four semesters done, and a first-semester student cannot apply, so spring of your first year is the earliest window. Sophomore entry loses no credits for most students and keeps college at eight semesters. It also opens doors that close for juniors: Global Development in CALS takes sophomores only. The trade-off is housing: sophomore entrants must live on campus for one year, where a double costs [$13,744 for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). The costly mistake: waiting a year for a stronger GPA, then arriving with 90 credits.
```

## Why Cornell warns against junior transfer

The FAQ's reasoning is credit math. Most Cornell colleges [allow up to 60 transfer credits](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and they also set a floor on work done at Cornell: at least 60 credits there, and in most colleges four semesters in residence. Engineering's cap is 66 and Architecture's 64, with 90 credits required at Cornell for Architecture.

So the cap and the floor work together. A student with 90 credits cannot finish in two years at Cornell, because only 60 come in and 60 more must be earned on campus. Taking 20 credits a semester does not help, since the four-semester residence rule still applies.

Exam credit sits inside the cap in most colleges. A student with AP credit therefore hits 60 sooner. AP, IB and A-Level credit your old school accepted [does not carry over automatically](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services): send the scores and make a written request to your college registrar by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester.

## Credits lost and semesters in total

The table uses Arts and Sciences rules: 60 credits at most transferred, 120 to graduate, 60 and four semesters at Cornell. It assumes 15 credits a semester in both places and every credit accepted up to the cap. Real evaluations are case by case, so treat it as the best outcome.

| Credits before Cornell | Semesters elsewhere | Credits accepted | Credits lost | Semesters at Cornell | Total semesters |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 2 | 30 | 0 | 6 | 8 |
| 45 | 3 | 45 | 0 | 5 | 8 |
| 60 | 4 | 60 | 0 | 4 | 8 |
| 75 | 5 | 60 | 15 | 4 | 9 |
| 90 | 6 | 60 | 30 | 4 | 10 |
| 105 | 7 | 60 | 45 | 4 | 11 |

Our method: accepted is the lower of credits earned and 60; Cornell semesters are (120 minus accepted) divided by 15, never fewer than four.

The extra time has a price. At 2026-27 rates, endowed tuition is [$73,946 a year](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) and mandatory fees [$1,024](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend). Our sum: an extra year costs $74,970 before housing and aid. For a New York resident in a contract college, tuition is $49,816, so our extra-year figure is $50,840.

Quarter credits convert at two-thirds. Ninety quarter credits equal 60 semester credits, exactly the cap.

Example: a student finishing five semesters at a four-year school holds 75 credits, 15 of them from AP exams. Applying to Arts and Sciences, at most 60 count, so 15 are lost before any course is even tested against the 80% rule. Sixty credits remain, which is four semesters at 15 credits. College runs nine semesters in all, and the extra term costs about $37,485 in endowed tuition and fees, our half of $74,970.

## Which Cornell colleges take juniors

You apply to [one college and usually one major](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and each sets its own standing rules. The admissions page asks for two to four semesters done elsewhere ([FAQ 330](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/)).

| College | Junior entry? | What juniors need | Cap and residence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS) | Yes; four semesters or an associate's means junior standing, with no sophomore option. More than four semesters at a four-year school: not eligible | Major foundation courses; 3.0 GPA minimum, 3.5 for Biological Sciences; Global Development takes sophomores only | 60, test plus transfer, AP up to 15; 60 at Cornell |
| Engineering (Duffield) | First semester of junior standing only | Math, physics and chemistry block; major list | 33 a year, 66 total; 60 at Cornell |
| Hotel Administration (Nolan) | Rising juniors may apply; current juniors and seniors may not | Microeconomics, a writing course, plus high school language, chemistry and precalculus | 60, exam credit up to 15; 60 at Cornell |
| Industrial and Labor Relations (ILR) | Yes | 2 composition, micro and macroeconomics, math, 1 science-and-technology course, 2 ILR elective credits | 60, AP up to 12; four semesters and 60 credits at Cornell |
| Human Ecology | Yes | Sophomore list plus microeconomics, a design studio, and organic chemistry for pre-health | 60; 60 at Cornell |
| Arts and Sciences | Yes | The intended major's prerequisites | 60; 60 credits and four semesters at Cornell |
| Brooks School of Public Policy | Yes | 15 prep credits: writing, microeconomics, statistics or calculus, sociology | 60; four semesters on the Ithaca campus |
| Architecture, Art and Planning (AAP) | No external B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027 | Portfolio for B.F.A.; 2.7 GPA minimum | Architecture 64; 90 at Cornell |

Sources: [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), [Engineering](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements), [Hotel](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/), [ILR guidelines for fall 2026](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf), [Human Ecology](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer), [Arts and Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), [Brooks](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/undergraduate/apply/transfer-applicants/external-transfers/), [registrar caps](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).

Two rules catch juniors most. CALS counts semesters, not credits: a fifth semester at a four-year school ends eligibility. Hotel's line is your standing now: a sophomore finishing the year may apply, a junior may not.

Grades matter alongside standing. Cornell says admitted transfers are [generally 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). ILR's admits typically have at least 3.4; Human Ecology calls 3.5 competitive.

## The application dates for fall 2027

Cornell takes external transfers for fall only. The Transfer Common App, the $85 fee or waiver and any portfolio are due [March 15, 2027](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). The College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts are due April 15. Decisions roll from late April through June.

## Housing: exempt, so you rent

New transfers live on campus for one academic year. Students [matriculating as a junior or senior](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) are not subject to that requirement, and neither are those who lived on campus for two years at their old school. The [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) says exemption requests go through the housing application while it is open, and not after a housing license has been signed.

The catch is timing. Desirable Collegetown leases [leave the market up to 16 months before move-in](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), according to a 2018 Sun report. A junior admitted between late April and June is shopping after most of that stock is signed.

The rents, from [RentCafe's Collegetown page](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/) updated October 30, 2026, cover buildings of 50 or more units only.

| Option | Price | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Collegetown studio | $2,006 a month | RentCafe average, large buildings, Oct 30, 2026 |
| Collegetown one-bedroom | $2,342 a month | same |
| Collegetown two-bedroom | $3,414 a month | same |
| Collegetown, all rentals | $2,815 a month | same |
| Cornell hall double (for comparison) | $13,744 a year, about $1,527 a month over nine months | [2026-27 housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) |

Start on [Cornell's off-campus listing board](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/listing) the day you are admitted. A two-bedroom split two ways is $1,707 each by our division, close to the hall double's monthly cost.

Example: a junior admitted in May wants to live alone near campus. A studio at the RentCafe average costs $24,072 over a 12-month lease, our multiplication. The same student sharing a two-bedroom pays $20,484 a year for their half. Both sit above a hall double's $13,744, which is the comparison sophomore entrants face, but the hall double comes with a required meal plan.

## Meal plans off campus

## Community college juniors

The associate's route avoids the credit trap, since 60 credits is exactly the cap. The [Cornell Chronicle](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell) reported in 2024 that Cornell admits about 100 community college students a year, and that CALS has a transfer student success navigator, with similar roles in ILR and Human Ecology.

There is no general guaranteed transfer. The newest pathway, from TC3 to CALS Agricultural Sciences, gives ["priority consideration"](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students) with a 3.0 GPA and a B or better in every transfer course. Its [expedited route](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/signature-pathway-program) runs one year at TC3, two semesters plus summer, then four semesters at Cornell.

Cost is less of a wall than it looks. For 2026-27, families earning under $125,000 with typical assets get [tuition covered by grants](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), and under $75,000 the full cost. File the CSS Profile and FAFSA by March 15, 2027, the same day as the application.

Pick courses with the [80% rule](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) in mind. Math below precalculus, study skills, orientation and remedial courses never transfer. Courses seven or more years old are generally not eligible.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying with 90 credits and planning on four years.** You lose 30 credits and spend ten semesters in college; the extra year is $74,970 in 2026-27 endowed tuition and fees.
- **Applying to CALS after a fifth semester at a four-year school.** CALS rules you ineligible, and the $85 fee is gone.
- **Assuming your AP credit is already counted.** It sits inside the 60 cap, and Cornell grants it only on a written request by the last day of your first semester's instruction.
- **Waiting for the decision before looking at apartments.** Desirable Collegetown leases go up to 16 months ahead; a June admit competes for what is left, at averages of $2,006 to $3,414 a month.
- **Taking prerequisites pass/fail.** CALS and Human Ecology give no credit, and a C- fails in Engineering.
- **Skipping the junior course list.** ILR juniors need two composition courses and both micro and macroeconomics, not the sophomore list.

## FAQ

**Can I still graduate in four years total?** Only if you bring 60 credits or fewer under the 60-credit cap, since you must earn [60 credits and spend four semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Above 60, every 15 credits adds a semester.

**Will my transfer GPA count at Cornell?** No. The Cornell transcript [shows only your old school's name and total transfer credits](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and transfer grades stay out of the Cornell GPA.

**Can I retake a course I already passed elsewhere?** Yes, but you get credit for [only one of the two](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).

**When will I know which credits transfer?** After admission. Each college evaluates case by case; Arts and Sciences issues a [tentative evaluation](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits) only after you accept and deposit.

**Can I switch Cornell colleges later?** In Arts and Sciences, external transfers may apply to move only [after two semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), and the college strongly discourages it for them.
