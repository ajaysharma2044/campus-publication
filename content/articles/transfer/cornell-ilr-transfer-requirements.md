```glance
- Fall entry only; the application and $85 fee are due March 15, 2027, everything else by April 15.
- Most admitted ILR transfers have a 3.4 cumulative GPA or higher; that is typical, not a minimum.
- Sophomores need 4 courses first: composition, intro economics, math, a science-and-technology course.
- Three essays: 350 words (Cornell), 650 (ILR) and 650 (personal statement). No interviews.
- ILR counts up to 60 transfer and exam credits, AP capped at 12; AP Microeconomics needs a 5.
- NY residents pay $49,816 tuition for 2026-27; everyone else pays $73,946.
```

ILR admits external transfers for the fall only, and most admitted transfers have [at least a 3.4 cumulative college GPA](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf). Finish ILR's prerequisite courses, write its three essays, and submit the Transfer Common App by [March 15, with the remaining materials by April 15](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). For fall 2027 entry, that means March 15 and April 15, 2027.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
Apply in the spring of your first year. Cornell takes students with [two to four semesters done](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), and a first-semester student cannot apply. This year, take one composition course, one intro economics course, math up to statistics or calculus, and one science-and-technology course, all for letter grades. Ask the professor who knows your work best for the one academic recommendation ILR requires. Send the Mid-Term Report by March 15 if you can; estimated grades are allowed. After admission you live on campus for one academic year. The costly mistake: sending only the March 15 pieces and leaving the College Report and transcripts unrequested until April.
```

```reader "If you're entering as a junior"
Your list is longer: two composition courses, both microeconomics and macroeconomics, math, one science-and-technology course and at least two courses toward ILR elective credits. ILR counts at most 60 transfer and exam credits and requires four full-time semesters at Cornell. A student with 90 credits loses 30 and spends ten semesters in college, by our count. Cornell's admissions office [discourages junior-year transfer](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) from a four-year school for exactly that reason. You are exempt from the one-year campus housing rule, so start a lease search as soon as you are admitted. The costly mistake: arriving with 75 or more credits and assuming they all count.
```

```reader "If you're at a community college"
Cornell has no preference by school type, and [about a quarter of its transfers](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) come from community colleges. A course [cannot be refused credit](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) because it was taken at a community college or online, but it needs a C or better and must closely match a Cornell course. An associate's degree plus AP credit can pass ILR's 60-credit cap, and AP counts inside it. Cornell aid covers tuition for families earning up to [$125,000 with typical assets](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) in 2026-27. The costly mistake: skipping the aid forms because Cornell looks too expensive.
```

```reader "If you're a New York resident"
ILR is a contract college, so New York residents pay [$49,816 in tuition for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) instead of $73,946. The Admissions Office sets your residency at admission, and it needs [a year of continuous New York residence](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/ny-state-residency/application-guidelines) before registration. Besides the CSS Profile and FAFSA, file with HESC using [code 0174 for contract colleges](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). HEOP applicants to ILR [email stateprograms@cornell.edu](https://oadi.cornell.edu/node/50) instead of sending a certification form. The costly mistake: comparing ILR with an endowed college on tuition alone. The gap is $24,130 a year.
```

## Who ILR takes, and how many

ILR is a large door for transfers. About [one-third of ILR graduates are transfers](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines), per the school. The last official count by college, from July 2021 deposit data, expected [116 fall 2021 transfers in ILR plus 13 for spring](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf). That was 17.2% of the 674 fall 2021 transfers, our share from those counts, second only to CALS.

You apply to ILR itself, not to Cornell in general. You must have a high school diploma and [at least 12 semester hours of college credit after high school, or full-time enrollment elsewhere](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Students who already hold a bachelor's degree cannot apply. The one current community college pathway, from TC3, leads to [CALS Agricultural Sciences](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students), not ILR, so ILR applicants from any school use the open application.

Spring entry is gone. ILR's [fall 2026 guidelines](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) list fall entry and a March 15 deadline only. Spring transfers did exist recently: [72 enrolled across five colleges, ILR among them, in spring 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). Ignore any October 15 spring date in older ILR guidelines.

## The courses to finish before you apply

ILR's course list depends on the standing you would enter with. Take every course for a letter grade with a C or better, since [ILR's credit floor is a C](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf).

| Area | Sophomore entry | Junior entry |
|---|---|---|
| Writing | 1 composition or writing course | 2 composition courses |
| Economics | 1 introductory economics course | Both microeconomics and macroeconomics |
| Math | Math up to statistics or calculus | Math up to statistics or calculus |
| Science | 1 science-and-technology course (or a language course) | 1 science-and-technology course |
| ILR electives | Optional; language classes count | At least 2 elective courses; language classes count |

Source: [ILR transfer guidelines, fall 2026](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf), updated February 10, 2026.

Pick microeconomics as your intro economics course if you can. It counts for sophomores and is half of the junior requirement.

Example: a sophomore applicant has English 101, Principles of Microeconomics, Introduction to Statistics and General Biology, all with grades of B or better. That covers every sophomore line in the table. A second applicant took macroeconomics but no writing course. They should take composition in the spring term, where it appears on the Mid-Term Report with an estimated grade.

## GPA and what else admissions reads

ILR's number is a 3.4. Its guidelines say most admitted transfers have [at least a 3.4 cumulative GPA](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf). That is a typical figure, not a cutoff. Across all of Cornell, admitted transfers are ["generally" 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/).

The same Cornell FAQ names what weighs most: the Writing Supplement showing interest in the program, grades at your current college, and professor recommendations. Activities and the high school record are read too. Overall, coursework and essays matter most.

ILR offers [no interviews](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf), so the essays carry your case.

## The three essays and the other pieces

ILR's fall 2026 guidelines list [three pieces of writing](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf):

| Piece | Length | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell supplement | 350 words | Community prompt |
| ILR Writing Supplement | 650 words | ILR's own supplement |
| Personal statement | 650 words | Personal essay |

Cornell's admissions page frames the main essay as [250 to 650 words on how continuing at a new institution helps your future goals](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). The ILR supplement is where the interest in the program shows. Use it to name the ILR courses or fields that fit what you already study.

The rest of the file, per the [admissions page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants):

- **College Report.** Your registrar or dean confirms your standing. You request it in the Applicant Portal by naming the official.
- **Academic recommendation.** ILR requires one from a college professor.
- **Mid-Term Report.** You fill it in yourself, with official or estimated spring grades. ILR wants it [by March 15 if possible and no later than April 15](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf).
- **Transcripts.** Official transcripts from every college, plus the final high school transcript.
- **Fee.** $85, or a waiver claimed in the Common App with no documents sent.

## How your credits count

ILR evaluates credit after admission, course by course. Its [fall 2026 rules](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf):

| Rule | ILR |
|---|---|
| Transfer and exam credits counted | Up to 60 |
| AP credit within the 60 | Up to 12 |
| Minimum grade | C |
| AP Microeconomics | Needs a 5, even if your first school took a 4 |
| Schools accepted | Non-profit, regionally accredited, degree-granting |
| Never counted | MOOCs; credit for work or military training |
| Required at Cornell | 60 credits and four full-time semesters |

Cornell-wide rules apply on top. The department that owns the matching Cornell course judges whether yours is [about 80% similar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) from the syllabus. Keep every syllabus. Courses seven or more years old are generally not eligible.

AP credit [does not carry over automatically](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/), even when your old school accepted it. Send scores and a written request to the college registrar [by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). In fall 2026 that day was [December 7](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar).

The cap decides how long college takes. The table below is our model, assuming 15 credits a semester and every credit accepted up to 60.

| Credits before Cornell | Counted | Lost | Cornell semesters, at least | Total semesters, at least |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 60 | 0 | 4 | 8 |
| 75 | 60 | 15 | 4 | 9 |
| 90 | 60 | 30 | 4 | 10 |

Example: a community college graduate brings a 64-credit associate's degree and 9 AP credits. ILR counts 60 in all, so at least 13 credits are lost. Where an AP exam and a college course overlap, [only the course counts](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), so the AP credit may add nothing.

## What the first year costs

ILR is a contract college. Tuition for 2026-27 is [$49,816 for New York residents and $73,946 for everyone else](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). Fall 2027 entrants pay 2027-28 prices, which the trustees set in late March.

Sophomore transfers must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Juniors are exempt. Here is the billed year with a hall double and the Unlimited meal plan, from [Cornell's cost page](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend):

| 2026-27, before aid | NY resident | Non-NY resident |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Billed: tuition, fees, double, Unlimited plan | $72,188 | $96,318 |
| Cost of attendance, with books and personal | $75,604 | $99,734 |

Aid is need-based, and ILR transfers follow [the same policies as first-years](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf). For 2026-27, families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get [grants covering tuition](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). Up to $75,000, grants and work-study cover the full cost of attendance.

## What ILR transfers say about arriving

Rachel Eisenhart, an ILR transfer in the Class of 2027, wrote in [a 2025 Sun letter](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you) that transfers repeat freshman small talk and sit between first-years and experienced students. Her advice was to reuse what you learned in your first year and keep old friends while making new ones. She says you feel like a regular sophomore within weeks.

In [a December 2020 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/), an ILR Transfer Option student who came from Penn State urged new transfers to message classmates and step outside their comfort zone. That fall's transfer GroupMe had more than 350 members.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating 3.4 as a floor or a ticket.** It is typical, not a minimum, and coursework and essays weigh most. Finish the course list before worrying about a tenth of a point.
- **Sending only the March 15 pieces.** The College Report, recommendation and transcripts come by April 15. A missing piece leaves the file incomplete through the decision window.
- **Counting AP credit your first school accepted.** ILR caps AP at 12 credits and needs a 5 on AP Microeconomics. A 4 your old college took can mean retaking the course at Cornell.
- **Applying as a junior with 75 to 105 credits.** By our model that loses 15 to 45 credits and adds one to three semesters. By our math, each extra year costs $74,970 in non-resident tuition and the $1,024 mandatory fees for 2026-27, or $50,840 at the New York rate.
- **Taking prerequisites pass/fail.** ILR's floor is a C. Cornell counts a pass only with [a written statement that it equals a C or better](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), which your school may not issue.
- **Leaving out HESC.** New York residents who file only the CSS Profile and FAFSA are not considered for state aid.

## FAQ

**Is there a minimum GPA for ILR transfers?** No published minimum. Most admitted transfers have [at least a 3.4](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf), and Cornell-wide admits are ["generally" 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/).

**Can I apply from my first college semester?** No. Cornell takes transfers who have [completed two to four semesters](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), so apply in the spring of your first year.

**Do I need the SAT or ACT?** No. Self-report AP, IB or A-Level scores in the Common App instead, per the [admissions page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

**Who do I contact at ILR?** The fall 2026 guidelines list ims25@cornell.edu and a mailing address of [410 Thurston Avenue, Ithaca NY 14850](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf).

**I hold an ILR Transfer Option. What do I add?** The Transfer Option application asks ILR holders for [AP scores on top of the standard materials](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), with no application fee. The [Transfer Option guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-option-guide/) covers the rest.

**Does ILR help transfers after they arrive?** Yes. Cornell's 2024 profile of community college transfers names [a transfer support role in ILR](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell), similar to CALS's transfer success navigator.
