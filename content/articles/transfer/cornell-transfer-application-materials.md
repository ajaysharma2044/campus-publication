```glance
- March 15, 2027: the Transfer Common App, the $85 fee or waiver, any portfolio, and the CSS Profile and FAFSA.
- April 15, 2027: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and every official transcript.
- You write the 250 to 650 word essay, your college's Writing Supplement and the Mid-Term Report.
- Your school's official files the College Report; one instructor writes the Academic Evaluation.
- No SAT or ACT. Extras by college: ILR's three essays, portfolios for art and design, a resume for AEM and Hotel.
- Admissions weighs your essay, current college grades and professor recommendation most; admits are "generally" 3.3 or better.
```

A Cornell transfer application has five core pieces beyond your transcripts: the essay and Writing Supplement (you), the Mid-Term Report (you), the College Report (an official at your school) and the Academic Evaluation (one instructor). The application, the $85 fee and any portfolio are due [March 15, and everything else by April 15](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), which for fall 2027 entry means March 15 and April 15, 2027. Some colleges add essays, portfolios or a resume, so check your college's row in the table below.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You can apply only after your first college semester; Cornell takes students with [two to four semesters done](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/). Line up two people in January: an official for the College Report and a professor for the Academic Evaluation. The Mid-Term Report covers your spring courses, and you fill it in yourself with official or estimated grades. The costly mistake is treating March 15 as the only date. That date covers the application, fee and portfolio. The College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts are due April 15, and a file missing them is incomplete.
```

```reader "If you're a junior-entry transfer"
Admissions [discourages junior transfer](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) from a four-year school, because most colleges accept about 60 credits. Check eligibility before you write a word. CALS bars anyone with [more than four semesters at a four-year school](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants). Hotel bars [current juniors and seniors](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). Junior lists are longer too: ILR juniors need two composition courses, micro and macroeconomics, math, a science course and two ILR elective credits ([ILR guidelines](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines)). ILR also requires four full-time semesters and 60 credits at Cornell, whatever you bring.
```

```reader "If you're a community college transfer"
Cornell says it has no preference by school type and that [about a quarter of transfers come from community colleges](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Your Academic Evaluation comes from an instructor of a full-credit college course, so pick one from a course that counts, not a remedial one. The $85 fee is waived automatically when it is a burden and you apply for aid, with [no documents sent](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). The CSS Profile fee is waived for families with [adjusted gross income up to $100,000](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). The mistake that costs most: skipping the aid forms because Cornell looks too expensive.
```

```reader "If you're a veteran or non-traditional transfer"
If you have been out of school two or more years, you may replace the instructor's Academic Evaluation with a [personal or employer recommendation](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). The same page names a dedicated Veterans Admissions Team; contact it before you apply. Do not build the application around military training. ILR does not accept credit for [work or military training](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines), and Engineering lists [life, work or military experience](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) as ineligible. In fall 2026, [23 transfer veterans entered Cornell](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429).
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
Same dates and the same $85 fee, with decisions [late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). File the CSS Profile (code 2098) by March 15, 2027 if you will ever want aid: applicants who miss it [cannot receive undergraduate aid later](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international), and admission is need-aware. Non-English documents need official English translations, which a teacher or school official may provide. If English is not your first language, Cornell's general English-proficiency page lists "minimum competitive" scores of [TOEFL iBT 100 (exams before January 2026; 5.0 on the new scale for later exams), IELTS Academic 7.5 or Duolingo 130](https://admissions.cornell.edu/resources/submit-english-language-proficiency), and says applicants with four years at an English-language school may be exempt. Self-reported scores may be used for initial review; admitted applicants send official results.
```

## Every piece, who completes it, and when

You apply through the Transfer Common App. Adding Cornell [opens the program-specific materials](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) for the one college and major you choose. After you submit, Cornell emails a login for the Applicant Portal, where you track each item and later see your decision.

| Piece | Who completes it | Due (fall 2027 entry) | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer Common App, with college and major | You | March 15, 2027 | The application itself |
| Essay, 250 to 650 words | You | March 15, 2027 | One set prompt (below) |
| Writing Supplement | You | March 15, 2027 | A college-specific essay |
| $85 fee or fee waiver | You | March 15, 2027 | Waiver claimed in the Common App, no documents |
| Portfolio or design supplement | You | March 15, 2027 | Only for the programs listed below |
| College Report | An official at your current school | April 15, 2027 | Confirms academic and disciplinary standing |
| Academic Evaluation | One instructor of a full-credit college course | April 15, 2027 | The recommendation |
| Mid-Term Report | You | April 15, 2027 | Your spring courses with official or estimated grades |
| College transcripts | Every college you attended | April 15, 2027 | Official copies |
| Final high school transcript | Your high school | April 15, 2027 | Grades 9 to 12 |
| AP, IB or A-Level scores | You, self-reported | With the application | In Common App Academic History |
| CSS Profile and FAFSA | You and your family | March 15, 2027 | Only if you want aid |

Every row except the aid forms comes from the [admissions transfer page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

## The three forms people mix up

**The College Report is not a recommendation.** It confirms your academic and disciplinary standing. You request it in the Applicant Portal by naming an official at your current school, and [that official completes it](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants). That is usually your registrar or a dean.

**The Academic Evaluation is the recommendation.** It comes from an instructor of a full-credit college course. If you have been out of school two or more years, a [personal or employer recommendation](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) can take its place. ILR states its rule the same way: [one academic recommendation from a college professor](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines). AAP asks for [at least one recommendation](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/).

**The Mid-Term Report is yours.** It is required if you are taking spring courses. You list each course with an official or estimated grade, and you [upload an explanation](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) for any course that cannot carry a grade yet. No professor signs it. ILR asks for it [by March 15 if possible](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines), and no later than April 15.

## The essay and the Writing Supplement

Every applicant writes one essay of 250 to 650 words on this prompt: "How does continuing your education at a new institution help you achieve your future goals?" ([Cornell admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants)). You also write the Writing Supplement for the college you apply to.

The supplement matters more than its length suggests. Cornell's FAQ lists the essay showing interest in the program, the Writing Supplement, first among [the most important factors](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Human Ecology's tips page says to [tailor the essay](https://human.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/transfertips) to the college. Consultant guides that count Cornell's essays in characters do not match Cornell's word limits; use the words.

ILR asks for three pieces in its [fall 2026 guidelines](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf): a Cornell supplement of 350 words on a community prompt, an ILR supplement of 650 words, and a personal statement of 650 words. Plan those as three separate drafts, not one essay cut three ways.

Example: a sophomore applying to ILR writes the 650-word personal statement on why they are changing schools, the 650-word ILR supplement on a labor or policy interest, and the 350-word community piece. That is 1,650 words across three prompts, all due March 15, 2027. ILR also wants the Mid-Term Report by that date if possible, so spring grades go in early too.

## College extras: portfolios, supplements and resumes

| College or program | Extra piece | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ILR | 350-word Cornell supplement, 650-word ILR supplement, 650-word personal statement | [No interviews offered](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) |
| Architecture (AAP) | Portfolio plus interview | AAP [takes no external B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) |
| Art (AAP) | Portfolio | Through SlideRoom, due March 15 |
| Landscape Architecture (CALS) | Portfolio | Through SlideRoom |
| Fashion Design | Portfolio plus Design Index | Due March 15 |
| Design and Environmental Analysis; Fashion Design Management | Design supplement | Due March 15 |
| Applied Economics and Management (Dyson) | Resume or CV | Listed on the admissions page |
| Hotel Administration | Resume or CV | Pages disagree (below) |
| Engineering | Course description form | After admission, with the Enrollment Response Form |

The portfolio and resume rows come from the [admissions transfer page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), which also says an interview is required only for Architecture.

**The Hotel resume conflict.** The admissions page lists a resume as required for Hotel Administration. The [Hotel School's own page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) lists resumes for first-year applicants, not for external transfers. Send one. An unneeded resume costs nothing; a missing required one could leave the file incomplete.

**Engineering's after-admission form.** Admitted Engineering transfers send a [course description form](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) with the Enrollment Response Form. Engineering credit needs a detailed syllabus and a certified official transcript, so save every syllabus now.

## Tests, translations and what you can skip

The SAT and ACT are [neither required nor expected](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/) for transfers. Self-report AP, IB or A-Level results in the Common App Academic History; whether they earn credit is decided after admission.

Non-English documents need official English translations, and a teacher or school official may translate them. The [admissions page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) describes no separate credential evaluation.

Interviews are not part of most applications. Architecture is the one program the admissions page says requires one, and it is closed to external transfers for fall 2027.

## What admissions weighs most

Cornell's FAQ names the Writing Supplement, your academics at your current college and professor recommendations as the main factors. It also reads extracurriculars and your high school record, but says [coursework and essays matter most](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Admitted transfers "generally" have a 3.3 or better.

Your college's number is the one to judge against. CALS sets a [3.0 minimum, 3.5 for Biological Sciences](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants). Most ILR admits have [at least a 3.4](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/programs/undergraduate-admissions/apply-ilr/transfer-guidelines). AAP requires good standing and [at least a 2.7](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/). Engineering states [no minimum](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) but will not review an application missing any of its required math, physics or chemistry courses.

## Common mistakes

- **Asking a professor for the College Report.** It is a standing form from a school official. A file without it on April 15 is incomplete, however good the recommendation.
- **Waiting for a professor to file the Mid-Term Report.** You complete it. Unfiled, it leaves your file incomplete on April 15.
- **Treating March 15 as the finish line.** It covers the application, fee and portfolio only. Four pieces and every transcript are due April 15.
- **Missing the March 15 aid date.** For an international applicant, a missed CSS Profile [ends Cornell aid for the whole degree](https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international). For a US applicant, the [CSS Profile and FAFSA](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) are due the same day as the application; the FAFSA opened October 1, 2026.
- **A generic Writing Supplement.** It is the first factor Cornell names. A reused first-year essay wastes the one piece that shows interest in the program.
- **Contacting faculty during admissions.** Human Ecology's tips page tells applicants [not to](https://human.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/transfertips).

## FAQ

**I got a Transfer Option. Do I use the Common App?** No. Only offer holders use the [Transfer Option application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), with their earlier portal login and no fee. It asks for the same transcripts, College Report and Mid-Term Report, plus college extras.

**Can a teaching assistant or employer write my Academic Evaluation?** It must come from an instructor of a full-credit college course. An employer or personal reference is allowed only if you have been out of school two or more years ([admissions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants)).

**What if a spring course has no grade by April?** List an estimated grade on the Mid-Term Report and upload a short explanation for any course that cannot be graded yet.

**When will I hear back?** Decisions roll from [late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and the Hotel School decides in [May and June](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). Silence in April is not a denial.

**Can I apply with a bachelor's degree already done?** No. Students who already hold a bachelor's degree [are not eligible as transfers](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).
