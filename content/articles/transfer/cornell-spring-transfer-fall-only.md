```glance
- No external spring entry: Cornell considers transfer applications for the fall semester only.
- For fall 2027, the application, $85 fee and any portfolio are due March 15, 2027; everything else by April 15.
- Spring transfer did exist: 88 students in spring 2021 and 72 (from 584 applications) in spring 2023.
- ILR's October 15 spring date (2022) and the housing page's "spring transfers" line are out of date.
- Already at Cornell? Hotel and AAP take spring internal transfers by December 1, 2026.
- Waiting until fall costs no extra time up to 60 credits; past 60, every 15 credits adds a semester.
```

No. Cornell takes external transfer students [for the fall semester only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), so the next door open to you is fall 2027, with the application due March 15, 2027. The only spring moves left are internal: current Cornell students can switch into the Hotel School or AAP for spring by December 1.

```reader "If you're hoping to start in January"
There is no spring 2027 application, so do not leave your current school in December. Stay enrolled, take the courses your target college lists as prerequisites, and keep the grades up: admitted transfers are [generally 3.3 or better](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Submit the Transfer Common App, fee and any portfolio by March 15, 2027, and the College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts by April 15. The CSS Profile and FAFSA are [also due March 15](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). Decisions roll from late April through June. The costly mistake is trusting an October or November "spring deadline" on an old page, then missing March 15 and losing a full year.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year elsewhere aiming at sophomore entry"
Spring was never your best door. Cornell takes students who have finished [two to four semesters](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/) elsewhere, and a first-semester student cannot apply at all. Apply this spring for August 2027. You arrive with about 30 credits, all under the 60-credit cap, and finish in eight semesters total. One upside of fall entry: you join other transfers at August orientation instead of starting mid-year.
```

```reader "If you're already at Cornell and want another college"
Spring moves exist for you. The [Hotel School](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) takes internal applications by December 1 at 11:59 pm ET, with decisions in early January; you need a 3.0 Cornell GPA and must be a first- or second-year. [AAP](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) also takes spring internal applications by December 1, with a 2.7 GPA and a PDF portfolio. If you came to Cornell as an external transfer, Hotel's internal track is closed to you, and A&S makes you wait two semesters.
```

## What Cornell says now

Every current admissions and college page says fall only. The central [transfer applicants page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) says applications are considered for the fall semester only. The [2025-26 Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) lists fall as the only transfer entry term. [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), Engineering, Hotel, Human Ecology and [ILR's fall 2026 guidelines](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) say the same.

So the dates that matter are the fall ones:

## Why you keep seeing spring dates

Spring transfer was real until recently, and old pages still say so. Four sources keep it alive.

| What you saw | Where it comes from | What is true for 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| An October 15 spring deadline | ILR's 2022 transfer guidelines, since taken down | ILR's [fall 2026 guidelines](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) list fall entry and March 15 only |
| A November 15 spring deadline | A college-search aggregator, not Cornell | No Cornell page gives a November date for external transfers |
| "Spring transfers" live on campus for the spring plus the next year | Cornell's own [transfer housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) | The housing wording is older than the admissions rule; admissions decides who enters when |
| News of spring transfers arriving in January | The Sun's [2023 story](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/) on the spring 2023 class | That intake no longer runs |

Trust the admissions page over the housing page. Admissions controls the entry term; the housing rule only says where a spring arrival would live.

## How big spring entry used to be

Spring classes were always small next to fall. Cornell's [2021 transfer profile](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf) counted 88 spring 2021 transfers against 674 for fall 2021. CALS took 29 of them, Hotel 21 and A&S 15.

In spring 2023, 72 new transfers enrolled out of 584 applications, across five colleges: CALS, A&S, ILR, SC Johnson and AAP. Shawn Felton, Executive Director of Undergraduate Admissions, gave those figures to [the Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). Those students were admitted around November. For scale, 625 transfers enrolled in fall 2025, per the [Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf).

Starting mid-year was not easy. The same Sun story found spring 2023 transfers worried about full classes, because current students had pre-enrolled in November. Abbey Yang '25 said her advisor told her biology credits should count, but her degree audit did not yet show them. Cynthia Cui '25 found Ithaca more rural and much colder than UC Santa Barbara.

## First Year Spring Admit is a different program

First Year Spring Admit (FYSA) is for first-year students, not transfers. It has its own orientation, and its students move in shortly before January, as a [2021 Sun column](https://cornellsun.com/2021/04/12/so-youve-been-accepted-to-cornell-as-a-spring-admit-read-this/) explains. Cornell offers it to some first-year applicants. You cannot apply to it as a transfer.

If you are still in high school, you are a first-year applicant even with college credits. If you have [12 or more semester hours](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) of college credit after high school, you apply as a transfer, for fall.

## What waiting until fall costs you

For most readers, nothing in total time. The credit cap decides it. Arts and Sciences accepts at most [60 transfer credits](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits), requires 120 to graduate, and requires at least 60 credits and four semesters at Cornell. Most other colleges cap at 60 as well.

Our model below uses those A&S rules, assumes 15 credits a semester and assumes every credit is accepted up to the cap. Real evaluations are case by case.

| Credits when you enter | Credits lost | Cornell semesters needed | Total semesters of college |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 0 | 6 | 8 |
| 45 | 0 | 5 | 8 |
| 60 | 0 | 4 | 8 |
| 75 | 15 | 4 | 9 |
| 90 | 30 | 4 | 10 |

Example: a sophomore had 45 credits in December 2026 and hoped to start in January. Spring entry would have meant five Cornell semesters, eight in total. Instead the student takes 15 more credits and enters in August 2027 with 60. Nothing is lost, four Cornell semesters remain, and the total is still eight.

Example: a junior had 75 credits in December 2026. Spring entry would have meant 15 credits lost and nine semesters in total. Another full semester at the current school brings 90 credits, 30 lost and ten semesters. That extra semester is the real cost of waiting. At 2026-27 endowed tuition of [$73,946](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) plus [$1,024 in mandatory fees](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend), one more Cornell year runs $74,970 before housing and aid; half of that, $37,485, is our per-semester figure.

Cornell itself [discourages transfer](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) for students finishing their junior year at a four-year school, because so many credits are lost. If you are near 75 credits, weigh finishing where you are.

## Spring options that do exist

| Route | Who can use it | Spring deadline | Key rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel internal transfer | Current Cornell first- and second-years | December 1, 2026, 11:59 pm ET | 3.0 Cornell GPA; ECON 1110 or equivalent; essay up to 650 words; not open to students who transferred in from another school |
| AAP internal transfer | Current Cornell students | December 1, 2026 | 2.7 cumulative GPA; portfolio as a PDF |
| A&S internal transfer | Current Cornell students | May 1 (2026 date); no December date on the page | 3.0 cumulative and 3.0 in 12 credits that semester; apply at internaltransfer.cornell.edu/apply |
| External transfer | Students at other colleges | None | Fall only; March 15, 2027 |

The Hotel details come from the [Hotel School's requirements page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/), which also lists office hours on November 18, 2026. AAP's rules are on its [transfer page](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/). The A&S rules, and its two-semester wait for students who entered Cornell as external transfers, are on the [A&S admissions page](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process).

## If spring entry ever returns

The housing page already says what would apply. A spring transfer would [live on campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) for that spring plus the next full academic year, three semesters instead of two. The Student Health Plan for a new spring 2027 student is [$2,046 for six months](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27) unless waived. Watch the [admissions page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) for any change, not the housing page. For what spring housing looks like for students who do arrive mid-year, see [spring transfer housing](/housing/cornell-spring-transfer-housing-whats-left-when/).

## Common mistakes

- **Planning around October 15 or November 15.** No spring application exists. If that plan makes you miss March 15, 2027, you lose a full year.
- **Withdrawing in December for a January start.** You lose a semester of credit and stand out of school until August.
- **Reading the housing page as proof spring is open.** Its "spring transfers" line is stale; the admissions page decides.
- **Applying to FYSA as a transfer.** It is a first-year program; a transfer application goes in for fall.
- **Piling credits past 60 while you wait.** Under A&S rules each 15 credits past 60 is lost and adds a semester, about $37,485 at 2026-27 endowed tuition and fees by our count.
- **Following external rules when you are already at Cornell.** Internal moves run on December 1 and May 1, not the Common App's March 15.

## FAQ

**When did Cornell stop taking spring transfers?** Spring 2023 is the latest spring class with published numbers: [72 students](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/). Every current page lists fall only.

**Can I apply in the fall semester to start in fall 2027?** You apply in the spring of 2027, by March 15, while still enrolled. You must have finished [two to four semesters](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/) elsewhere by entry.

**Do I need a Mid-Term Report if I apply in spring?** Yes, if you have spring courses. You fill it in yourself with official or estimated grades by [April 15](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants); ILR asks for it by March 15 if possible.

**Can an external transfer switch colleges in their first spring at Cornell?** Not into A&S's process before two semesters at Cornell, and never through Hotel's internal track, per the [A&S page](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) and the [Hotel page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/).

**Is it easier to get in for spring?** There is no spring round to compare. In spring 2023, 72 enrolled from 584 applications; in fall 2025, 864 of 7,381 transfer applicants were admitted, or [11.7%](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf).
