```glance
- Finish your major's course list first: for Design + Environmental Analysis it is 23 credits (lab science 8, writing 6, statistics 3, precalculus or calculus 3, psychology 3).
- Competitive applicants have a 3.5 or higher with As and Bs; Cornell admits transfers "generally" at 3.3 or better.
- No pass/fail courses; at most 15 pre-college credits (AP and similar) count.
- Pre-med and health applicants need two semesters of inorganic chemistry with labs; juniors add organic chemistry.
- Apply by March 15, 2027 (application, $85 fee or waiver, design supplement); other materials by April 15.
- New York residents pay $49,816 tuition in 2026-27, against $73,946 at Cornell's endowed colleges.
```

To transfer into Cornell's College of Human Ecology, finish the required courses for your intended major for a letter grade, and aim for the college's competitive mark: [a GPA of 3.5 or higher, with As and Bs](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer). The college reviews on a rolling basis, but Cornell's own deadline for fall entry is [March 15 for the application, fee and any design supplement, and April 15 for everything else](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). That means March 15, 2027 and April 15, 2027 for fall 2027.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You can apply in the spring of your first year, once you have 12 semester hours after high school. Use this year to finish your major's sophomore list from the table below, all letter-graded. For Design + Environmental Analysis that is 23 credits: two lab sciences (8), two writing courses (6), statistics (3), precalculus or calculus (3) and intro psychology (3). The costly mistake is a pass/fail course: [Cornell does not accept Pass/Fail coursework](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer), so a required course taken that way does not count. You will live on campus for [one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates).
```

```reader "If you're applying as a junior"
Juniors complete the whole sophomore list plus their major's junior additions, such as microeconomics and a 2D and 3D design studio for Design + Environmental Analysis, or organic chemistry with labs for pre-health ([college page](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer)). Cornell admissions [discourages transfer applications from students completing or past their junior year](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) at a four-year school, because most colleges accept [at most 60 transfer credits](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Arrive with 90 and 30 are lost. Matriculating juniors are [exempt from the on-campus year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), so plan a lease early.
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
Your offer is an invitation to apply after a year elsewhere. Arts and Sciences calls it [not an offer of admission](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). You use the [Transfer Option application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) with your old portal login, and pay no fee. Human Ecology asks for a resume, your AP scores, a 600-word essay and a design supplement. The fall 2026 deadline was March 15, 2026; the page now reads "Application Available Winter 2027." Decisions roll from mid-April, most in late April or early May. Each college sets its own GPA and course conditions, so take the sophomore list below for grades and email humec_admissions@cornell.edu with questions.
```

```reader "If you're a New York resident"
Human Ecology is one of Cornell's five contract colleges. New York residents pay [$49,816 in tuition for 2026-27, against $73,946](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) at the endowed colleges. With a hall double and the Unlimited meal plan, the required on-campus year bills [$72,188 before aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend), against $96,318 for a non-resident. File with HESC using [code 0174 for contract colleges](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants), along with the CSS Profile and FAFSA, by March 15, 2027. The mistake that costs most is applying to an endowed college for a similar subject: $24,130 a year more in tuition.
```

## Who can apply

You count as a transfer if you have a high school diploma and either [at least 12 semester hours of college credit earned after high school, or full-time enrollment elsewhere](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Exam credit does not count toward the 12. Students still in high school apply as first-years, whatever their dual-enrollment credits. Bachelor's degree holders cannot apply.

Human Ecology adds two conditions of its own. Your courses must come from [an accredited two- or four-year college that gives letter grades](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer), and none may be pass/fail. High school biology and chemistry or physics must be done, either in high school or as college courses.

## The courses you need, by major

You apply to one major, and each major has its own list. Juniors complete the sophomore column too. All of these come from the [Human Ecology transfer page](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer); credits are semester credits.

| Major | Sophomore entry | Juniors also add |
|---|---|---|
| Design + Environmental Analysis | Lab science (biology, inorganic chemistry or physics) 8; writing 6; statistics 3; precalculus or calculus 3; intro psychology 3 | Intro microeconomics 3; studio in 2D and 3D design 3 |
| Fashion Design | Lab science 8; writing 6; precalculus or calculus 3; intro psychology 3 | Statistics or computer science 3; design or studio art; intro microeconomics 3; intro anthropology or sociology 3 |
| Fashion Design Management | Lab science 8; writing 6; calculus 3 to 4; intro psychology 3; intro microeconomics 3 | Statistics or computer science 3; intro anthropology or sociology 3 |
| Fiber Science | Inorganic chemistry with labs 8; writing 6; calculus 3 to 4; intro psychology 3 | Intro physics with lab 8; statistics 3; intro microeconomics 3; computer science 3 |
| Human Biology, Health and Society | Inorganic chemistry with labs 8; general biology with lab 3 or more; writing 6; calculus or statistics 3 to 4; one intro social science 3 | Biology with lab 8 or more; organic chemistry with labs 8; intro nutritional sciences 3; a second intro social science 3 |
| Human Development | Intro biology with lab (human-biology focus) 3 to 4; a second lab science 3 to 4; writing 6; precalculus or calculus 3; intro psychology or human development 3 | A human development course 3; intro anthropology or sociology 3 |
| Nutritional Sciences | Inorganic chemistry with labs 8; writing 6; calculus or statistics 3 to 4; one intro social science 3 | Biology with lab 8 or more; organic chemistry with labs 8; intro nutritional sciences 3; a second intro social science 3 |

"Intro social science" means macroeconomics, microeconomics, psychology, anthropology or sociology. The two writing courses are English composition or writing-intensive courses. For Nutritional Sciences and Human Biology, Health and Society juniors aiming at the Didactic Program in Dietetics, the college also recommends microbiology.

### Pre-med, health and dentistry

Students on a health track carry extra chemistry. Sophomores need [a two-semester general inorganic chemistry sequence with labs](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer), equivalent to Cornell's CHEM 2070/2071 and 2080/2081. Juniors also need a two-semester intro biology sequence with labs and organic chemistry equivalent to CHEM 1570, 3570, 3580 and 3600.

### Precalculus, AP and quarter credits

Three rules trip up applicants:

- **Precalculus.** College-level precalculus is an admissions requirement, but [precalculus credits do not transfer](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer). It opens the door and adds nothing to your credit total.
- **AP and IB.** Do not plan to fill most of the list with exam credit. Placing out does not automatically meet a Human Ecology requirement, and [at most 15 pre-college credits](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer) from AP or other sources may apply.
- **Quarter schools.** Quarter credits convert at .67, so a 3-credit quarter course becomes 2 semester credits. Count before you assume a list is complete.

Example: a student on a quarter calendar takes two 4-credit writing courses. At .67 each, that is about 5.4 semester credits, short of the 6 the list asks for. A third writing course closes the gap.

## What GPA is competitive

The college's own line: the most competitive applicants are full-time students with [a 3.5 or higher, with As and Bs](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer), in a curriculum parallel to Human Ecology's. Applicants with a 3.5 or above, with As and B+s, in the required courses are more competitive still.

Neither is a cut-off. Cornell says admitted transfers university-wide are ["generally" 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). The same page names the essay, your current grades and professor recommendations as what matters most. Across Cornell in fall 2025, [7,381 people applied to transfer and 864 were admitted](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf), an 11.7% rate by our division. No per-college rate is published. The last official count by college, from [July 2021 deposit data](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf), showed 68 transfers expected in Human Ecology that year (59 fall, 9 spring).

Compare the other colleges on [our GPA-by-college page](/transfer/cornell-transfer-gpa-requirements-by-college/).

## Design majors: the supplement

Design + Environmental Analysis, Fashion Design and Fashion Design Management require [a design supplement reviewed by design faculty](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer). Without it, the college will not consider the application. Re-applicants submit a new one. It goes in by the [March 15 deadline](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), along with the application and fee.

## The application, step by step

```figure
kind: steps
title: Applying to Human Ecology for fall 2027
rows:
  - label: "Fall 2026 to spring 2027: finish your major's course list, all letter-graded"
  - label: "Transfer Common App: add Cornell, choose Human Ecology and one major"
  - label: "March 15, 2027: application, $85 fee or waiver, design supplement; CSS Profile, FAFSA and HESC"
  - label: "April 15, 2027: College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report, all transcripts"
  - label: "Late April to June 2027: rolling decision in the Applicant Portal"
  - label: "June: verify your major with the college (June 8 to 30 in the 2024 pattern)"
```

1. **Apply through the Transfer Common App.** Adding Cornell [opens the college's own questions](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Write the 250 to 650-word essay on how a new school helps your goals, plus the Human Ecology Writing Supplement. The [college's tips page](https://human.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/transfertips) says to tailor that essay to the major.
2. **March 15, 2027.** The application, the $85 fee or a waiver, and any design supplement.
3. **April 15, 2027.** The College Report (an official at your school confirms your standing), the Academic Evaluation (from an instructor of a full-credit course), your Mid-Term Report (you fill it in yourself) and official transcripts from every college plus high school.
4. **Wait.** Decisions arrive [late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Human Ecology reviews on a rolling basis and asks you to tell it about any pending deadline at another school.
5. **After admission.** In 2024 the college asked admitted transfers to [verify their major between June 8 and 30](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/college-school-links); questions go to humec_students@cornell.edu.

The tips page also says not to contact faculty during admissions. Appointments go through humec_admissions@cornell.edu; the office is in Martha Van Rensselaer Hall.

## How your credits count after you arrive

Most Cornell colleges accept [up to 60 transfer credits, exam credit included](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and you need at least 60 credits at Cornell. A course needs a C or better and about 80% overlap with the Cornell course. The transfer GPA does not enter your Cornell GPA.

AP, IB and A-Level credit is a separate request. Send scores and write to your college registrar by [the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), or you lose it.

A sophomore with 30 credits or a junior with 60 loses nothing and finishes in 8 semesters. At 75 credits, 15 are lost and college takes 9 semesters; at 90, 30 are lost and it takes 10. Real evaluations go course by course.

## What two Human Ecology transfers said

Alexia Kim, a Human Ecology sophomore who came from Case Western as what the piece calls a guaranteed transfer, wrote in [The Cornell Daily Sun in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/02/kim-what-it-means-to-be-a-transfer-at-cornell/) about culture shock, homesickness and a campus big enough to still need maps. Kim called it "Freshman Year 2.0" and listed organic chemistry prelims and 12-page essays among the demands she now faces.

Jessica Silverman, who transferred from UCLA, wrote on the [Human Ecology blog](https://hecec.human.cornell.edu/?p=8161) about moving from quarters to semesters. Silverman recommended the Tatkon Center, CUeLinks and West Campus house dinners.

## Common mistakes

- **Taking a required course pass/fail.** The college does not accept it, so the course does not count and you may be missing a requirement in March.
- **Counting on AP to cover the list.** Only 15 pre-college credits apply, and placing out does not meet a requirement automatically.
- **Skipping the design supplement.** For the three design majors, the application is not considered at all.
- **Reading "rolling" as "no deadline."** The college page lists none, but Cornell's March 15 and April 15 dates still apply.
- **Pre-health with one semester of chemistry.** Health applicants need the full two-semester inorganic sequence with labs before sophomore entry.
- **Arriving with 90 credits.** Thirty are lost under the 60 cap, and the extra year costs $74,970 at endowed rates before housing and aid: [$73,946 tuition](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) plus [$1,024 in fees](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend) for 2026-27, our sum.

## FAQ

**Does a precalculus course count toward the credits I transfer?** No. The college requires college-level precalculus but says [those credits do not transfer](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer).

**Can I take a required course over the summer or online?** Cornell says equivalency [cannot be denied because a course was online or at a community college](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), as long as it is letter-graded, C or better and from an accredited school.

**Do I need test scores?** No. SAT and ACT scores are not required; you [self-report AP, IB or A-Level results](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) in the Common App.

**Can I email a professor in the major to show interest?** The college's [tips page](https://human.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/transfertips) asks applicants not to contact faculty during admissions. Use humec_admissions@cornell.edu instead.

**Is there an application fee waiver?** Yes. Cornell [applies the waiver automatically](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) when the $85 fee is a burden and you plan to apply for aid; you send no documents.
