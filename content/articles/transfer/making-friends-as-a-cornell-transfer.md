```glance
- Social connection came up in 7 of 7 dated first-hand transfer accounts from 2018 to 2025 (our count); it is the most common worry, not a rare one.
- What worked, in students' own accounts: a summer group chat, orientation transfer small groups, college socials, West Campus house dinners, Program Houses and a few clubs.
- Pick a Program House by name on the housing application; it is the one place you choose your building. More than 500 first-year and transfer students live in Program Houses.
- Transfers move in a day after first-years (August 18 versus August 17 in 2026); orientation ran August 18 to 23.
- The first six weeks hold the Tatkon Block Party and CU Downtown; Cornell lists 1,000-plus student organizations.
- Fraternities and sororities count 23% of men and 19% of women (fall 2025); about four in five students are not members.
```

Transfers who made friends fastest joined a summer group chat, the transfer small groups at [New Student Orientation](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies), a house with shared dinners, and one or two clubs in the first weeks. Feeling left out at first is the most common transfer theme: it appears in all 7 dated first-hand accounts from 2018 to 2025 in our count. A 2025 transfer, Rachel Eisenhart, wrote that [you feel like a regular sophomore within weeks](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you).

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You will live on campus for one academic year, in upper-level housing where Cornell [groups transfer rooms together whenever possible](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Apply within ten days of getting your NetID and list a Program House if one fits you; it is the only building you choose by name. A West Campus house brings the [House Meal Plan](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) ($7,604 for 2026-27) and its house dinners, which one UCLA transfer recommended. Then go to the transfer small groups at orientation. The costly mistake is skipping orientation week, the one time every new transfer is in the same place. Eisenhart's 2025 advice: reuse what your first year taught you, and keep old friends while you make new ones.
```

```reader "If you're a junior-entry transfer living off campus"
Juniors are exempt from the residential requirement, so most rent in Collegetown and lose the built-in hall community. That is the setup that went worst in past accounts: a 2018 transfer, Lauren Jung '21, [felt nervous living off campus](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus). Replace the hall with a schedule: the orientation transfer groups, your college's socials, and two clubs. A meal plan keeps you in dining halls where friends eat; the [Off-Campus Value plan](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) costs $1,824.60 a year, with 45 meals and $150 Big Red Bucks a semester. You are not unusual: [45% of undergraduates](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) lived off campus or commuted in fall 2025.
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
Once your Transfer Option application is admitted, use the summer to find your class's transfer group chat. In 2020 an ILR Transfer Option student from Penn State [urged new transfers to message classmates](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/). Expect upper-level housing grouped with other transfers where possible, not the first-year halls of the class you first applied with. The mistake that costs you most is skipping orientation events because you already know Cornell from visits.
```

```reader "If you're a community college transfer"
Cornell's profile of four 2024 community college graduates quotes one, who worked 40-plus hours a week while in school, on [feeling behind and imposter feelings](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell). The same story names supports built for you: a CALS transfer student success navigator, with similar support in ILR and Human Ecology, plus PAVER and the CC STEP group. If your college has a navigator, meet them in the first week, not after the first prelim. Cornell says [about a quarter of transfers](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) come from community colleges, so others in your orientation group have the same background. The costly mistake is assuming you are the only one and staying quiet.
```

```reader "If you're a veteran or older transfer"
Cornell's undergraduates averaged 19.7 years old in fall 2025, and [0.6% were 25 or older](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf). Your shortcut is Veterans House, a Program House you can [choose by name](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process) on the housing application. 23 transfer veterans entered in fall 2026, per the [Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429). [Non-traditional students may skip some optional orientation events](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation), and the Tatkon Center lists support for veterans and student families after week one. The mistake: skipping the required events too, which are where transfers meet each other.
```

## Why transfers feel behind at first

Transfers are a large group, not a handful. Cornell says [almost 15% of new incoming students are transfers](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process); 625 enrolled in fall 2025, per the [Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf). The problem is timing and place. First-years arrive together, live together on North Campus and bond in their first weeks. Transfers arrive a year or two later and are spread across West, South and Program Houses.

Our count of seven dated first-hand pieces from 2018 to 2025 (hand-picked, not random; Sun news skews negative and Cornell blogs positive) shows which worries recur:

The accounts describe the same feelings in different years:

- **2019.** Alexia Kim, a Human Ecology sophomore who transferred from Case Western, called it ["Freshman Year 2.0"](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/02/kim-what-it-means-to-be-a-transfer-at-cornell/): culture shock, homesickness, and a campus so big Kim still needed maps.
- **2020.** Fall 2020 transfers [felt disconnected](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/), with no dorm community like first-years had; the Sun described Will Bodenman '23 arriving not knowing a soul.
- **2023.** Eve Iulo, who entered as a transfer, wrote about [imposter feelings in a 100-plus student lecture](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/persona-non-grata-actually-you-do-belong) after a hometown class of 73, and argued everyone feels it.
- **2025.** Eisenhart, an ILR transfer in the Class of 2027, described repeating freshman small talk, feeling like a small fish again, sitting between first-years and experienced students, and missing admitted-student celebrations.

## What worked: the ways in, by who said them

| What worked | Who said it, and when | What they did or advised |
|---|---|---|
| A transfer group chat | Fall 2020 transfers, [Sun, December 2020](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/) | A transfer GroupMe had 350-plus members |
| Messaging classmates | An ILR Transfer Option student from Penn State and a student from Maryland, 2020 | Message classmates; step outside your comfort zone |
| College socials at orientation | Spring 2023 transfers, [Sun, January 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/) | College-specific orientation socials helped |
| West Campus house dinners, Tatkon, CUeLinks | Jessica Silverman, UCLA to Human Ecology, [about 2024](https://hecec.human.cornell.edu/?p=8161) | Recommended all three |
| A summer visit and old friends | Rachel Eisenhart, ILR, [2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you) | Visit Ithaca in summer (Cascadilla Gorge, Collegetown coffee, a campus tour); keep old friends while making new ones |

Advice that follows from the table: start before you arrive. Two of the five routes (the group chat and the summer visit) begin before move-in, and the college socials happen at orientation.

## Step by step, from admission to week six

```figure

title: When to make your moves, fall 2026 pattern

  - {label: "Join the transfer group chat", date: "Summer"}
  - {label: "First-year move-in", date: "August 17, 2026"}
  - {label: "Transfer move-in", date: "August 18, 2026"}
  - {label: "New Student Orientation with transfer small groups", date: "August 18 to 23, 2026"}
  - {label: "Tatkon Block Party and CU Downtown", date: "First six weeks"}
```

1. **Housing.** Apply [within ten days of receiving your NetID](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process). Halls are filled by random auto-allocation; Program Houses are the exception, so this is your one social choice on the form.
2. **Summer.** Find your class's transfer chat. Fall 2020 transfers described a GroupMe with 350-plus members.
3. **Move-in and orientation.** In 2026 transfers moved in August 18 and [New Student Orientation ran August 18 to 23](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation). Orientation includes [transfer small groups and social events](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies), plus your college's required programs. Details: [transfer orientation and move-in](/transfer/cornell-transfer-orientation-move-in/).
4. **The first six weeks.** The [Tatkon Center lists the Block Party and CU Downtown](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation/beyond-first-week) in this window. Cornell names [1,000-plus student organizations](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies); pick two and go back to them every week.
5. **Somewhere to land.** The [Tatkon Center](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students) sits in South Balch Hall, 600 Thurston Ave: open until 11 p.m. Sunday through Thursday (Wednesday closes 5 to 6 p.m.), until 5 p.m. Friday, closed Saturday; (607) 255-5808.

Example: a sophomore transfer into ILR, assigned a West Campus double. The House Meal Plan is [required in the West Campus houses](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) at $3,802 a semester for 2026-27, so house dinners are already paid for. Going three nights a week costs nothing extra and puts the same faces at the table each time. Add the college's orientation social and one club, and that is three repeat groups by mid-September.

## Where you live shapes who you meet

Cornell's [transfer housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) places transfers in upper-level areas: Program Houses, South Campus, the West Campus House System and co-ops. Here is how each played out in students' accounts.

| Where | What it means socially | What students reported |
|---|---|---|
| West Campus House System | Required House Meal Plan ($7,604 a year, 2026-27) with house dinners | Recommended by a UCLA transfer (about 2024); in 2009, 26.4% of West Campus transfers said housing hindered their transition |
| Program Houses | Themed community you choose by name; more than 500 first-year and transfer residents | Akwe:kon, Loving House, Risley, Veterans House and nine others |
| South Campus (Collegetown) | Collegetown Meal Plan ($7,056 a year, 10 meals a week) | In 2018 Eitan Wolf '21 in Schuyler House called it nearly a mile from central campus |
| North Campus | Mostly first-years | About 30 transfers housed in lounges there in 2016 found it hard to meet peers |

The 2018 Schuyler account matters for food. Wolf said a 7-meal plan [kept him from eating with friends on West](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus). A 2025 Ithaca College to Cornell transfer saw the other side: the [unlimited meal plan and residency requirement keep students on campus](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/from-both-sides-of-the-hill-an-ic-transfers-perspective).

### The transfer house Cornell had, and what replaced it

Cornell once ran a transfer program house. After it ended in 2008, [transfers were placed across North Campus, Collegetown and West Campus](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2008/09/without-transfer-center-students-placed-on-north-in-c-town-and-on-west). A Student Assembly survey of 527 students the next spring measured the difference:

| 2009 survey result | Former transfer-house residents | Transfers after it closed |
|---|---|---|
| Rated first-year living positive | 88% | 36.7% |
| Called a transfer program essential | 89.3% | 51% |

The survey, [reported by the Sun in April 2009](https://cornellsun.com/2009/04/24/study-finds-transfers-feel-marginalized-campus), recommended an optional transfer house. Today's closest version is the rule that transfer rooms are grouped together whenever possible. In 2016 the Sun reported [about 30 transfers in converted North Campus lounges](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/03/transfer-students-forced-to-live-in-north-campus-lounges/), some five to a lounge. The residential requirement now covers transfers who arrived in fall 2022 or later.

## Greek life, clubs and what most students do

Fraternity and sorority members were [23% of men and 19% of women](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf) in fall 2025. That makes Greek life one option, not the default: roughly four in five students are not members. Members who live off campus have their own [SFL Supplemental meal plan](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), $4,335.02 a year for 127 meals a semester. For everyone else, clubs carry the load: Cornell counts 1,000-plus organizations, and clubs run through the 2018 to 2026 accounts of what worked.

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the transfer small groups at orientation.** They are the one time every transfer is in the same room; miss them and you rebuild that group one person at a time.
- **Leaving the housing form at defaults.** Halls are random; a Program House is a chosen community. Apply within ten days of your NetID, as Cornell advises, and list the Program House you want.
- **Picking food over friends.** Wolf's 7-meal plan in 2018 kept him from dinner with friends on West; a West Campus house dinner is already in the $7,604 House Meal Plan.
- **Treating the first six weeks as optional.** The Block Party and CU Downtown fall in this window, while everyone else is still meeting people too.
- **Cutting ties at the old school.** Eisenhart advised keeping old friends while making new ones; a support system in week one beats none.
- **Reading the feeling as failure.** Cat Huang, a 2019 Student Assembly transfer representative, wrote that [transferring out is more common than assumed](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/03/guest-room-transfer-in-transfer-out) and that first-year housing may be a cause. Talk to an advisor before deciding.

## FAQ

**Is there a transfer student club or a transfer-only dorm?** No transfer-only residence exists today; transfer rooms are [grouped together whenever possible](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Orientation runs [transfer small groups and social events](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies).

**Do I have to live on campus for two years like first-years?** No. New transfers [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), and juniors or students with two years on campus elsewhere are exempt; a 2025 Sun column's "two-year" wording describes the first-year rule.

**Do transfers go to the same orientation as first-years?** Yes, the same required college and university programs, plus a transfer track; [non-traditional students may skip some optional events](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation).

**Who can I talk to if I feel isolated?** Your academic advisor, the Dean of Students and residence staff are [named for new students](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation/beyond-first-week) after the first week. Cornell's [transfer families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/first-year-families/transferfamilies) adds CAPS, the counseling service.

**How long does it take to feel settled?** Eisenhart wrote that you feel like a regular sophomore within weeks; Kim, in 2019, still needed maps but wrote of gratitude and perspective too.
