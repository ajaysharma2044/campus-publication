```glance
- Dyson (Applied Economics and Management) takes external transfers for fall only; fall 2027 applications are due March 15, 2027.
- Required college courses: calculus, microeconomics, statistics and one writing or English composition course, each with a B or better.
- AP and IB scores never replace calculus, statistics, microeconomics or macroeconomics at Dyson.
- Admitted transfers "generally" have at least a 3.5 college GPA; current juniors and seniors cannot apply.
- Dyson accepts up to 60 transfer credits, at most 15 of them from AP, IB or other tests.
- Dyson is a contract college: New York residents paid $49,816 tuition for 2026-27, everyone else $73,946.
```

To transfer into Cornell's Dyson School, you apply to it directly on the Transfer Common App by March 15, 2027 for fall 2027, as a first-year, sophomore or rising junior at your current school. Dyson wants [college calculus, microeconomics, statistics and a writing or composition course, each with a B or better, and says admitted transfers generally have at least a 3.5 GPA](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/). AP and IB scores do not replace those courses, so build them into your college schedule now.

```reader "If you're hoping to start at Dyson in January"
There is no January start. Cornell considers external transfer applications [for the fall semester only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), so the next entry is fall 2027, with the application due March 15, 2027. Spring entry did exist: [72 transfers enrolled in spring 2023 across five colleges](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/), the SC Johnson College among them. That is why older pages still mention it. Use spring 2027 at your current school to finish whichever of calculus, microeconomics, statistics and writing you lack, with a B or better. The costly mistake is leaving your school in December expecting a January start: you lose a semester and still apply in March.
```

```reader "If you're finishing your second year"
Apply this spring or not at all. Dyson takes [rising juniors but not current juniors or seniors](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/), so a fall 2027 application in your fourth semester is your last chance. Dyson counts up to 60 transfer credits. Cornell requires [at least 60 credits earned at Cornell](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), so anything past 60 is lost. Entering as a junior [exempts you from the one-year on-campus rule](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), which means you choose between a Collegetown lease and campus housing right after an April to June decision. Start the lease search the day you are admitted.
```

```reader "If you're at a community college"
Cornell says it has no preference by type of school and that [about a quarter of its transfers come from community colleges](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). A course [cannot be refused credit because it was taken at a community college or online](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), but it must be about 80% similar to the Cornell course. Take Dyson's four required courses for a letter grade and earn a B or better in each. If you finish an associate's degree in May 2027, you apply in spring 2027 as a rising junior; credits past 60 do not count. The one current community college pathway, from TC3, [leads to CALS Agricultural Sciences](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students), not to Dyson.
```

```reader "If you're the parent paying for it"
Dyson is a contract college, so residency matters. For 2026-27, [a New York resident paid $49,816 in tuition and a non-resident $73,946](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). With a double room, the Unlimited meal plan and fees, the required first year billed [$72,188 for a New York resident and $96,318 for everyone else](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend). Aid is need-based only: file the CSS Profile and FAFSA [by March 15, 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). Fall tuition is billed in early July through Cashnet, and [a parent sees the bill only if invited](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances). Waive the health plan if your family coverage qualifies, or it adds [$1,705 for fall](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27).
```

## Where Dyson sits and how you apply

The Dyson School grants the B.S. in Applied Economics and Management (AEM). It is part of the SC Johnson College of Business, alongside the Nolan School of Hotel Administration, and its transfer rules live on the [college's AEM requirements page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/).

You apply to one Cornell college through the [Transfer Common App](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and for AEM that college is Dyson. CALS is not a side door. [CALS says AEM applicants apply through Dyson, and transfers into CALS cannot change to or add AEM](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants) later.

Dyson's transfer class is small. In fall 2021, the newest by-college count, Dyson expected [54 of Cornell's 674 fall transfers and none in spring](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf). That is 8.0% (our math). Across all colleges, Cornell admitted [864 of 7,381 transfer applicants for fall 2025](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf), an 11.7% rate (our math).

## Who can apply

Two sets of rules apply. Cornell's central page requires a high school diploma plus [at least 12 semester hours of college credit after high school, or full-time enrollment elsewhere](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). AP-type exam credit does not count toward the 12. Cornell takes students who have finished [two to four semesters elsewhere](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), and a student in a first college semester cannot apply. Dyson then narrows it to first-years, sophomores and rising juniors at their current school.

| Where you are in spring 2027 | Can you apply to Dyson for fall 2027? |
|---|---|
| First college semester (started January 2027) | No: Cornell requires you to be past your first semester |
| Second semester of your first year | Yes |
| Sophomore, including a rising junior finishing year two | Yes |
| Current junior or senior | No: Dyson excludes them |
| Already hold a bachelor's degree | No: Cornell does not admit transfers for a second bachelor's |

Cornell's FAQ already [discourages transfer during or after the junior year](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) at a four-year school, because of the roughly 60-credit cap. Dyson makes it a hard stop.

## The courses Dyson requires

Dyson lists four college courses. Each needs a [B or better](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/). The same page says Dyson "does not accept advanced testing credit for calculus, statistics, microeconomics or macroeconomics," so you take them in college or take a more advanced equivalent.

| Course | Grade needed | Does AP or IB replace it? |
|---|---|---|
| Calculus | B or better | No |
| Microeconomics | B or better | No |
| Statistics | B or better | No |
| One writing or English composition course | B or better | No: it must be a college course |
| Macroeconomics (not on the required list) | No admission rule | No |

Three habits keep these courses usable. Take them for a letter grade, since a pass mark cannot show a B. Take real calculus: [math below precalculus never transfers](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). And keep every syllabus, because the Cornell department that owns the matching course decides from it whether yours is about 80% similar. Courses seven or more years old are generally not eligible.

Example: a hypothetical first-year with a 5 on AP Calculus AB and a 5 on AP Microeconomics, now at a state university. Neither score counts at Dyson. A safe spring 2027 schedule includes college calculus and college microeconomics, with statistics in fall 2026 or spring 2027. A B- in any of the four falls short of Dyson's B.

## The GPA Dyson expects

Dyson's page says admitted external transfers [generally have at least a 3.5 GPA](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/), and its FAQ on the same page calls 3.5 a minimum and asks for good academic standing in every previous semester. Treat 3.5 as the line to clear, not a number to approach. Cornell-wide, admitted transfers are [generally 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/), so Dyson asks for more than the average college.

If business is the goal, compare Dyson with the two colleges business-minded transfers also weigh.

| | Dyson (AEM) | Hotel (Nolan) | ILR |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPA signal | At least 3.5 expected | [No minimum; admits generally 3.3 or better](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) | [Most admits 3.4 or more](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf) |
| Economics required | Microeconomics, B or better | One college microeconomics course | One intro economics course (sophomores) |
| Other required courses | Calculus, statistics, writing | College writing, plus language, chemistry and precalculus (high school or college) | Writing, math, a science-and-technology course |
| Junior entry | Rising juniors only; current juniors excluded | Rising juniors only; current juniors excluded | Allowed, with a longer course list |
| Tuition type, 2026-27 | Contract | Endowed | Contract |

## What to send, and when

The central [admissions page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) sets two deadlines. By March 15, send the application, the $85 fee or fee waiver, and any portfolio. Everything else is due April 15. The same page lists a resume or CV as required for AEM applicants.

1. **Common App and essays.** A 250 to 650 word essay on how a new institution helps your goals, plus Cornell's college-specific Writing Supplement for Dyson.
2. **Resume.** Required for AEM. Interviews are required only for Architecture.
3. **College Report.** You request it in the Applicant Portal; an official at your school fills it in. Due April 15.
4. **Academic Evaluation.** From an instructor of a full-credit college course. Due April 15.
5. **Transcripts.** Every college attended, plus your final high school transcript. Due April 15.
6. **Mid-Term Report.** You list your spring courses with official or estimated grades. Due April 15.
7. **Aid forms.** The CSS Profile and FAFSA are due [March 15, 2027 for fall 2027 transfers](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). The FAFSA opened October 1, 2026.

Transfer Option holders use a separate application with [no fee](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants). For the SC Johnson College, the extra item is a resume, with no interview.

Decisions arrive [late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Admitted transfers apply for housing within ten days of getting a NetID. In the 2024 cycle, [Dyson met transfers in July](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/college-school-links) to review credit evaluations; its student services office is Dyson_OSS@cornell.edu. Transfers moved in on [August 18 in 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation), a day after first-years.

## How many credits carry over

Dyson accepts [up to 60 transfer credits, with at most 15 from AP, IB, GCE or A-level tests](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/). Cornell also requires [at least 60 credits earned at Cornell](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), and sets a C as the university's minimum grade for transfer credit.

| Credits earned before Cornell | Credits Dyson can accept | Credits lost |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 30 | 0 |
| 45 | 45 | 0 |
| 60 | 60 | 0 |
| 75 | 60 | 15 |
| 90 | 60 | 30 |

Our model assumes every course is accepted up to the cap; real evaluations go course by course. Test credit sits inside the 60, so AP credit fills the cap sooner. At 15 credits a semester, every 15 lost credits is a semester of college you repeat.

## What the first year costs

New transfers must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). The exceptions: you lived on campus two years elsewhere, or you enter as a junior or senior. Dyson charges the contract-college rate, so a New York resident pays far less tuition.

| Billed for 2026-27 | New York resident at Dyson | Non-resident or international |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Mandatory fees | $1,024 | $1,024 |
| Double room | $13,744 | $13,744 |
| Unlimited meal plan | $7,604 | $7,604 |
| Total billed | $72,188 | $96,318 |

Sources: [tuition](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) and [cost of attendance](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend). Books ($1,234) and personal costs ($2,182) come on top. So does the [Student Health Plan](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27) at $1,705 for a new student's fall and $2,046 for spring, unless you waive it. 2027-28 prices are confirmed before aid offers go out in early April 2027.

Aid is need-based only. For 2026-27, families earning [up to $125,000 with typical assets had tuition covered by grants](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), and families up to $75,000 had the full cost covered. Applicants for New York's HEOP program who apply to Dyson email stateprograms@cornell.edu instead of sending a certification form.

## If you are already at Cornell

Moving into Dyson from another Cornell college is a separate internal process. Dyson sets a [Cornell GPA minimum of 3.0 and calls 3.3 or higher competitive](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/). That matches 2019, when the Sun reported that [successful internal applicants to Dyson usually had 3.3 or higher](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/26/internal-transfers-finding-a-new-home-within-cornell/). An external transfer admitted to CALS still cannot switch to AEM.

## Common mistakes

- **Counting on AP Calculus or AP Micro.** Dyson gives no exam credit for either. Finding out in March costs a full year, because the course has to be finished by fall and the next deadline is a year away.
- **Applying to CALS as a back door to AEM.** CALS transfers cannot change to or add AEM, so you would graduate in a different major.
- **Waiting until junior year.** Current juniors cannot apply. Missing your fourth-semester window closes Dyson for good.
- **Settling for a B- in a required course.** Dyson's floor on all four courses is a B.
- **Planning a spring start.** Fall only. Skipping March 15 while waiting for a spring application costs a year.
- **Leaving off the resume.** The central admissions page lists it as required for AEM, and it costs you nothing to send.
- **Treating March 15 as an application-only date.** The CSS Profile and FAFSA are due the same day; late forms can delay your aid estimate past the point you must commit.

## FAQ

**Can I apply to Dyson and the Hotel School at the same time?** No. You apply to [one Cornell college](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), so choose the one whose courses you have finished.

**Does Dyson interview transfer applicants?** No. Cornell's admissions page requires interviews only for Architecture, and the [Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants) lists no interview for the SC Johnson College.

**Do I need macroeconomics before I apply?** It is not on Dyson's required list. If you take it, take it in college: Dyson gives [no test credit for macroeconomics](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/).

**Will an online statistics course count?** It can. Cornell [cannot deny equivalency because a course was online](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) or at a community college, as long as it is about 80% similar and from a regionally accredited school.

**When will I hear back?** Decisions roll from [late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Silence in April is not a denial.
