```glance
- NY residents pay $49,816 tuition in 2026-27 at CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks and Dyson; everyone else, and every endowed college, is $73,946.
- The gap is $24,130 a year: the required transfer year bills $72,188 against $96,318 with a hall double and the Unlimited plan.
- Residency is set when you are admitted and needs one year of New York residence before you register.
- File HESC with code 0174 (contract) or 0175 (endowed) alongside the CSS Profile and FAFSA, due March 15, 2027.
- HEOP applicants to a contract college email stateprograms@cornell.edu instead of sending the certification form.
- Seven of ten fall 2021 transfers (472 of 674) entered a contract college.
```

If you live in New York, five Cornell colleges charge you [$49,816 tuition for 2026-27, against $73,946](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) at Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Hotel and AAP. The five are CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, the Brooks School and Dyson, and the saving is $24,130 a year.

```reader "If you're a New York resident aiming at a contract college"
Your tuition is $49,816 for 2026-27 at CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks or Dyson. Your required on-campus year bills $72,188 with a hall double and the Unlimited plan, and two years of full cost of attendance come to $151,208 at 2026-27 prices. File HESC at hesc.ny.gov with code 0174, plus the CSS Profile (2098) and FAFSA (002711), by March 15, 2027 ([aid page](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)). The costliest mistake: picking an endowed college for a subject a contract college also teaches, which adds $24,130 a year. The second: applying to CALS for business and planning to add AEM later. [CALS bars that](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants); AEM applicants apply through Dyson.
```

```reader "If you're a community college transfer"
In fall 2021, 34 of the 96 community college transfers came from New York schools ([IRP profile](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf)). If that is you, a contract college charges you $49,816 tuition for 2026-27, not $73,946. The one current pathway into a contract college is TC3's Sustainable Farming and Food Systems A.A.S. to CALS Agricultural Sciences: a 3.0 GPA and B or better earns ["priority consideration,"](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students) not a guarantee. Apply elsewhere too. In CALS, an associate's degree means junior standing, and you cannot choose sophomore entry. SUNY Broome's new agreement leads to an online part-time degree, not a residential college.
```

```reader "If you're transferring on a tight budget"

Families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get [tuition covered by grants](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) at either rate; up to $75,000, the full cost is covered. The [CSS fee is waived](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) automatically up to $100,000 in adjusted gross income. ```

```reader "If you're the parent of a transfer"
Budget $72,188 billed for a New York contract-college year with a hall double and the Unlimited plan, or $96,318 at an endowed college ([cost table](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend)). Add books ($1,234), personal costs ($2,182) and $3,751 for the health plan unless your coverage waives it. Fall tuition bills arrive in early July, and [Cashnet e-bills](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances) reach parents only if the student invites them. Your New York residency counts: Admissions sets the student's status at admission, based on one year of residence before registration. If you move to New York later, the student applies to the Bursar by May 1 for fall, and the change is not retroactive.
```

## Which Cornell colleges are contract colleges

Contract colleges charge New York residents a lower rate; endowed colleges charge everyone the same. The [2026-27 budget story](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) names the contract five, and non-New Yorkers pay the endowed rate at any college.

| College | Type | NY resident tuition 2026-27 | Non-NY tuition 2026-27 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS) | Contract | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Human Ecology | Contract | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Industrial and Labor Relations (ILR) | Contract | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Brooks School of Public Policy | Contract | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Dyson School (Applied Economics and Management) | Contract | $49,816 | $73,946 |
| Arts and Sciences | Endowed | $73,946 | $73,946 |
| Engineering | Endowed | $73,946 | $73,946 |
| Nolan School of Hotel Administration | Endowed | $73,946 | $73,946 |
| Architecture, Art and Planning (AAP) | Endowed | $73,946 | $73,946 |

Two traps sit in that table. Dyson and the Hotel school both belong to the SC Johnson College of Business, yet only Dyson charges the resident rate. And computer science has no contract route: transfers reach it through [Engineering or Arts and Sciences](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/node/17), both endowed.

Both rates [rose 3.76% for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). For 2025-26 they were [$48,010 and $71,266](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2025/03/board-trustees-approves-2025-26-budget-parameters). Prices for 2027-28, the year a fall 2027 transfer starts, are not set yet; the aid office says they are [confirmed before the early April 2027 offers](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants).

## What the required year costs

New transfers live on campus for their first academic year unless exempt, so the first bill includes a room and a meal plan. Fees are [$1,024 a year](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend), a hall double is [$6,872 per semester](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) ($13,744 a year), and the Unlimited plan is [$3,802 per semester](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) ($7,604 a year).

## Who counts as a New York resident

Residency ["is determined at the time of your admission by the Admissions Office"](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/ny-state-residency/application-guidelines). It rests on a New York domicile, your permanent home, and "continuous residence in NYS for at least one year immediately preceding registration."

If your family moves to New York after you enroll, the status can change, but slowly. After the first semester you apply to the University Bursar with documents. The [residency FAQ](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/ny-state-residency/residency-faq) describes two routes: parents who establish a New York domicile, or a financially independent student who documents independence and residence. Applications are due May 1 for fall and November 1 for spring. Approval is not retroactive and starts the semester after it is granted.

## Aid forms: HESC, CSS and FAFSA

Every US transfer files the CSS Profile (code 2098) and FAFSA (002711) by [March 15, 2027 for fall 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). New York residents also file with HESC at hesc.ny.gov. The code depends on the college: 0174 for a contract college, 0175 for an endowed one. File it even if you apply to an endowed college.

The resident rate matters less if your family qualifies for large grants. Cornell meets [100% of demonstrated need](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get [tuition covered by grants](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). Up to $75,000, grants, scholarships and work-study cover the full cost of attendance. The $24,130 gap bites hardest for families above those lines.

## EOP and HEOP: a different step for contract colleges

HEOP is New York's opportunity program. For fall 2027, applicants to [CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks or Dyson email stateprograms@cornell.edu](https://oadi.cornell.edu/node/50). Applicants to AAP, Arts and Sciences, Engineering, or the SC Johnson College with the Nolan School use the HEOP Transfer Certification form instead. Both groups file the CSS Profile and FAFSA and submit 2025 financial documents. Questions go to the Office of Academic Discovery and Impact at 607.255.3841.

## Admission rules at the five contract colleges

A lower price does not mean an easier door.

| College | GPA signal | Main rule for transfers |
|---|---|---|
| CALS | 3.0 minimum; 3.5 for Biological Sciences ([CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants)) | Associate's degree means junior standing; Global and Public Health Sciences takes no transfers; no AEM |
| Human Ecology | 3.5 or higher is competitive ([Human Ecology](https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer)) | 23 credits of listed sophomore courses; no pass/fail |
| ILR | Most admits have at least 3.4 ([ILR guidelines](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf)) | Writing, economics, math and science courses; one professor's recommendation |
| Brooks | No minimum; 3.5 "increases competitiveness" ([Brooks](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/undergraduate/apply/transfer-applicants/external-transfers/)) | 15 prep credits: writing, microeconomics, statistics or calculus, sociology |
| Dyson | Admits "generally" have 3.5 or more ([Dyson](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-aem/application-requirements/)) | Calculus, microeconomics, statistics and a writing course at B or better; no exam credit for the calculus, statistics or economics courses; current juniors cannot apply |

Switching from an endowed college into a contract college later is slow. Arts and Sciences lets external transfers apply to another Cornell college only [after two semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), and strongly discourages it.

## How many transfers go to contract colleges

In fall 2021, the newest by-college count Cornell has published, 472 of 674 transfers (70.0%, our math from the [IRP profile](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf)) entered the contract colleges counted in that profile. CALS alone took 243, ILR 116, Human Ecology 59 and Dyson 54. On the endowed side, Arts and Sciences took 102, Hotel 48, Engineering 37 and AAP 15. That year, 33.5% of transfers had New York residency.

## SUNY and community college pathways

Cornell admits about [100 community college transfers a year](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell) (Cornell Chronicle, 2024). Two current agreements involve New York community colleges.

- **TC3 to CALS.** Tompkins Cortland's Sustainable Farming and Food Systems A.A.S. leads to CALS Agricultural Sciences. A 3.0 GPA and B or better in transfer courses earns "priority consideration." The plan is one year at TC3, two semesters plus a summer, then four semesters at Cornell ([CALS Signature Pathway Program](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/signature-pathway-program)).
- **SUNY Broome to SCE.** Business A.S. students can move credits into Cornell's online, part-time Bachelor of Professional Studies. It needs 45 to 60 transferable credits and four years past high school. Applications open January 2027, and the first cohort starts August 2027 ([Chronicle, June 2026](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/06/cornell-sce-suny-broome-create-transfer-pathway-new-online-bachelors-degree)). It is not admission to a residential contract college.

## Common mistakes

- **Trusting the $48,414 in some search snippets.** The 2026-27 resident rate is $49,816. Budgeting from the lower number leaves you $1,402 short a year.
- **Choosing an endowed college for a subject a contract college also teaches.** That costs a New York family $24,130 a year in tuition, $48,260 over two years at 2026-27 rates.
- **Applying to CALS to reach AEM.** [CALS transfers cannot change to or add AEM](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants). For business, apply through Dyson.
- **Skipping HESC or using the wrong code.** Cornell's aid page tells New York residents to file it with the CSS Profile and FAFSA by March 15. Use 0174 for a contract college, 0175 for an endowed one.
- **Sending the HEOP certification form for a contract college.** CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks and Dyson want an email to stateprograms@cornell.edu.
- **Counting on a residency change mid-year.** The Bursar approves changes only after the first semester, and never retroactively, so a family that moves after admission pays $73,946 for at least one semester.

## FAQ

**Do I pay the resident rate if I transfer to Engineering or Arts and Sciences?** No. Endowed colleges charge [$73,946 to everyone](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters) in 2026-27, New Yorkers included.

**Does the resident rate apply to housing and meals?** No. Rooms and meal plans cost the same at every college: a hall double is [$13,744 a year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) in 2026-27.

**Do my SUNY or CUNY credits transfer?** Credit is judged course by course after admission, and most colleges [cap transfer credit at 60](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). See our [credit guide](/transfer/cornell-transfer-credit-how-many-credits-transfer/).

**Is CALS easier to get into because it takes the most transfers?** CALS took 243 of 674 fall 2021 transfers, but it still sets a [3.0 minimum GPA](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), 3.5 for Biological Sciences. Cornell publishes no admit rate by college.

**Can I appeal my aid offer if our finances change?** Yes. Email finaid@cornell.edu with your name and admissions ID, per the [aid page](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants).
