```glance
- Fall entry only. For fall 2027: application, $85 fee and portfolio due March 15, 2027; everything else April 15, 2027.
- Fall 2025: 7,381 applied, 864 admitted (11.7%), 625 enrolled. Admitted transfers "generally" have a 3.3 or better.
- You apply to one college. Its courses, GPA signal and credit cap (60 in most, 66 in Engineering) decide your file.
- CSS Profile and FAFSA are due March 15, 2027. Aid is need-based only and meets 100% of need.
- New transfers live on campus one year: $96,318 billed for 2026-27 (double, meals, tuition) before aid; $72,188 for a NY resident at a contract college.
- Nothing is guaranteed: the Transfer Option and the TC3 pathway are invitations to apply, not admission.
```

You transfer to Cornell by applying to one of its undergraduate colleges through the Transfer Common App, and only for fall: for fall 2027 the application, fee and any portfolio are due [March 15, 2027, and every other piece by April 15](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). For fall 2025, [864 of 7,381 transfer applicants were admitted](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf), 11.7% by our division, and admitted students [generally have a 3.3 GPA or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Once admitted, you apply for housing within ten days of getting your NetID, live on campus for a year unless exempt, and build your schedule after your college evaluates your credits.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer from a four-year college"
You are the typical case. Apply in the spring of your first year, because [first-semester students cannot apply](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/). Take your target college's sophomore courses now, for letter grades. Submit by March 15, 2027 and finish the College Report, Academic Evaluation and Mid-Term Report by April 15. Up to 60 credits, nothing is lost. After admission you must live on campus for one year: apply for housing within ten days of your NetID. The costly mistake is assuming your AP credit carries over. It does not, and you must [request it in writing](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) by the last day of classes of your first Cornell semester.
```

```reader "If you're entering as a junior"
Cornell [discourages junior transfer from a four-year school](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) because most colleges cap transfer credit at 60. With 90 credits you lose 30 and spend ten semesters in college; the extra year costs $74,970 in 2026-27 endowed tuition and fees. Overloading cannot shorten it, since you still need four Cornell semesters. CALS bars anyone with more than four semesters at a four-year school, and Hotel bars current juniors. You are [exempt from the housing rule](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), so you will rent. Desirable Collegetown leases sign up to 16 months ahead, so start looking the day you are admitted.
```

```reader "If you're at a community college"
Cornell has no preference by school type and says [about a quarter of transfers come from community colleges](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Credit cannot be refused because a course was at a community college or online, but it must be about 80% equivalent and a C or better. In CALS an associate's degree means junior standing, with no sophomore option. TC3's farming A.A.S. earns ["priority consideration"](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students) for CALS Agricultural Sciences, not a guarantee. Apply to backup schools, and apply for aid: grants cover tuition for families earning up to $125,000.
```

```reader "If you're holding a Transfer Option"
It is [an invitation to apply after one full-time year elsewhere](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), not admission, and it cannot be deferred. Arts and Sciences wants a 3.5 every semester and no grade below B-, in broad liberal arts courses. Apply through the Transfer Option application with your old portal login, not the regular Common App; there is no fee. In 2026 it was due March 15, with decisions from mid-April. File aid forms by March 15 too, and keep a second-year plan at your current school in case it does not convert.
```

```reader "If you're applying to Engineering or computer science"
Engineering [will not review your file](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements) unless MATH 1910, MATH 1920, PHYS 1112 with PHYS 1110, and CHEM 2090 with CHEM 2091 are done, in progress or planned. Engineering publishes no GPA minimum. You are admitted to one major and must keep it for a year. Credit is capped at 33 per full-time year and 66 in all, with a C (not C-) floor. AP Calculus BC, Chemistry and Physics C Mechanics count only with a 5. Computer science comes through Engineering (B.S.) or Arts and Sciences (B.A.).
```

```reader "If you're an international applicant"
Your dates and $85 fee match everyone else's, but admission is need-aware. File the CSS Profile by March 15, 2027 if you will ever want aid: an international applicant who skips that deadline [cannot receive Cornell undergraduate aid later](https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). There is generally no CSS fee waiver, and 14% is withheld from aid above tuition and fees unless a tax treaty applies. Non-English documents need official translations. ESL and remedial courses never transfer. You pay $73,946 tuition at every college, contract colleges included. After admission, move your F-1 or J-1 record through Global Learning.
```

```reader "If you're hoping to start in January"
External transfer is [fall only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) now. Older college guidelines, aggregator sites and Cornell's own housing page still mention spring transfers, but every current admissions and college page says fall. Do not leave your school in December. Apply by March 15, 2027 for fall 2027 and use the spring term for prerequisites. Watch your credit count: one more semester elsewhere can push you past the 60-credit cap. Spring switches exist only for current Cornell students changing colleges.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Apply for aid; domestic admission is need-blind. In 2026-27, families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets [get grants covering tuition](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters), and up to $75,000 the full cost of attendance. The $85 fee is waived automatically if it is a burden and you plan to apply for aid. The CSS fee is waived for incomes up to $100,000. EOP and HEOP applicants add [one form or email](https://oadi.cornell.edu/node/50). The cheapest required-year room is a co-op double at $6,974 to $9,962 a year. Ignore "Cornell transfer scholarship" results: they are Cornell College in Iowa.
```

```reader "If you're a New York resident"
Five colleges are contract colleges: CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks and Dyson. There, NY residents paid $49,816 tuition in 2026-27 against $73,946 at Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Hotel and AAP, a $24,130 gap every year. File HESC with the CSS Profile and FAFSA, using [code 0174](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants) for a contract college or 0175 for an endowed one. HEOP applicants to contract colleges email stateprograms@cornell.edu. A CALS transfer cannot later add Applied Economics and Management; apply to Dyson for that.
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
Budget $96,318 billed for the required year at 2026-27 endowed prices with a double and the Unlimited plan, or $72,188 for a New York resident at a contract college, before aid. Fall tuition is [billed in early July](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances); only parents the student invites to Cashnet see the e-bill. Housing bills in July or August, meals in July. Waive the Student Health Plan by the deadline (July 31 in 2026) if your family plan covers your student, or $1,705 is billed for fall. Rooms are required and assigned, not chosen. Transfers moved in on August 18 in 2026, a day after first-years, with Family Orientation the same day.
```

```reader "If you're a veteran or have been out of school for years"
Out of school two or more years, you may [replace the instructor's Academic Evaluation](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) with a personal or employer recommendation. Cornell [generally cannot award credit for military training](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans), and courses seven or more years old are generally ineligible, so plan on more Cornell semesters. Veterans choose between GI Bill benefits and a Cornell aid package. Admissions has a Veterans Admissions Team; contact it before applying. Veterans House is a Program House you can request by name. Twenty-three transfer veterans entered in fall 2026.
```

```reader "If you need disability accommodations"
Register with [Student Disability Services](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process) (607-254-4545, sds_cu@cornell.edu) before you submit the housing application, which is due within ten days of your NetID. Accessible housing runs through that registration. Individual exemptions from the one-year residential requirement are requested through the housing application while it is open, one academic year at a time. A medical leave cancels a fall housing license without penalty until June 15 under the [2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/12-month-undergraduate-housing-license-2026-2027); after that the fee is $500.
```

```reader "If you're bringing a partner or children"
Cornell's family housing at Hasbrouck is [for graduate students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) and is not open to undergraduate transfers. Unless you enter as a junior or lived on campus two years elsewhere, the one-year rule applies. Request an individual exemption through the housing application before you sign any lease, because a new transfer cannot cancel a housing license. [Student Health Plan dependent rates](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27) for 2026-27 for a spouse, partner or one child: $4,092 from July or $3,751 from August. Two or more children cost $8,184 or $7,502.
```

```reader "If you're already at Cornell and want another college"
That is internal transfer, a separate process with no Common App and no March 15 date. [Hotel](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) and [AAP](https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/) take internal applications by December 1 for spring and May 1 for fall. [Arts and Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process) asks for a 3.0 cumulative GPA and a 3.0 in 12 credits that term. If you came to Cornell as an external transfer, Arts and Sciences makes you wait two semesters, and Hotel's internal process excludes you.
```

## Now (fall 2026): check that you can apply

Cornell defines a transfer as someone with a high school diploma or equivalent plus [at least 12 semester hours of college credit earned after high school, or full-time enrollment at another institution](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). AP and similar exam credit does not count toward the 12 hours.

Who cannot apply as a transfer:

- **First-semester college students.** Cornell accepts students who have [completed two to four semesters elsewhere](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/). If you started college this fall, your earliest application is March 15, 2027, for sophomore entry.
- **High school students with dual-enrollment credit.** They apply as first-years, whatever their college credits.
- **Bachelor's degree holders.** They [cannot enroll for a second bachelor's](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).

Two groups apply differently. International applicants are anyone without US citizenship or approved permanent residency; pending residency counts as international. Undocumented students, with or without DACA, are [treated as domestic applicants for admission and aid](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). They are considered for Cornell aid but not federal aid.

## Now: pick the year you enter

Most Cornell colleges [accept up to 60 transfer credits](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), including exam credit, and require at least 60 credits at Cornell. Engineering takes up to 66 and Architecture up to 64, with 90 at Cornell. Arts and Sciences also requires [four semesters in residence](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). So credits past 60 do not shorten your time at Cornell; they are simply lost.

Admissions [discourages junior-year transfer from a four-year school](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) for exactly this reason and suggests finishing there and considering Cornell for graduate study.

| Credits before Cornell | Accepted (60 cap) | Lost | Semesters at Cornell | Total semesters of college |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 30 | 0 | 6 | 8 |
| 45 | 45 | 0 | 5 | 8 |
| 60 | 60 | 0 | 4 | 8 |
| 75 | 60 | 15 | 4 | 9 |
| 90 | 60 | 30 | 4 | 10 |
| 105 | 60 | 45 | 4 | 11 |

Our model, using the Arts and Sciences rules (120 credits to graduate, [at most 60 transferred](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits)) and 15 credits a semester. Real evaluations go course by course, so treat it as the best case. Exam credit sits inside the cap in most colleges, so AP credit fills it sooner.

Example: you finish three years elsewhere with 90 credits. Sixty transfer and 30 are lost. You still need 60 Cornell credits over at least four semesters, so college takes ten semesters. That extra year costs $74,970 in 2026-27 endowed tuition and fees alone ($73,946 plus $1,024), before housing and aid.

Quarter-system credits convert at two-thirds: 90 quarter credits equal 60 semester credits, exactly the cap.

Standing rules differ by college. In [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), two semesters mean sophomore standing. Four semesters or an associate's degree mean junior standing, and a junior-eligible student cannot choose sophomore entry. Engineering takes [sophomores in either semester and juniors in their first semester](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements). The junior route has its own guide: [Transferring to Cornell as a junior](/transfer/cornell-transfer-as-a-junior/).

- **AAP** is not taking external B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027.

**What admissions weighs most.** The [admissions FAQ](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) names the essay showing interest in your program, your grades at your current school and your professors' recommendations. Extracurriculars and the high school record are also read. It prefers no type of school. CALS says transfers are [nearly one-third of its students](https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants), and ILR says about one-third of its graduates transferred in.

The CSS Profile fee is waived automatically for US families with adjusted gross income up to $100,000.

Cornell [meets 100% of demonstrated need](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) with grants, work-study and reasonable loans, and admission is need-blind for US applicants. ## The Transfer Option and the "guaranteed" pathways

There is no general guaranteed transfer to Cornell. Three things get called guarantees, and none is one.

| Route | Who it is for | What it promises | Key condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer Option | First-year applicants who got the offer | An invitation to apply for sophomore transfer, "not an offer of admission" | A&S: 3.5 each semester, no grade below B-, full-time liberal arts year |
| TC3 to CALS (2025) | Tompkins Cortland Sustainable Farming and Food Systems A.A.S. students | "Priority consideration" for CALS Agricultural Sciences | 3.0 GPA and B or better in all transfer courses |
| SUNY Broome to SCE (2026) | SUNY Broome business A.S. students | Credit into Cornell's online, part-time Bachelor of Professional Studies | 45 to 60 transferable credits; at least four years past high school |

Sources: [Arts and Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), [the Transfer Option page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), [CALS News](https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students), [Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/06/cornell-sce-suny-broome-create-transfer-pathway-new-online-bachelors-degree).

**Transfer Option holders** apply only through the [Transfer Option application](https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants), with their earlier portal login and no fee. For fall 2026 it was due March 15, 2026, with aid forms the same day; the Mid-Term Report could come later. The page now shows "Application Available Winter 2027." Extras vary: CALS wants a resume, course schedule, AP scores and two essays; Human Ecology a resume, AP scores, a 600-word essay and a design supplement; Arts and Sciences none. Decisions roll from mid-April, most in late April or early May. A summer year or a gap year does not meet the A&S condition. Richard Ballard, who did his Transfer Option year at Ithaca College, [wrote in 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/from-both-sides-of-the-hill-an-ic-transfers-perspective) that it was described to him inconsistently as guaranteed and not. Ballard feared the second application would be as hard as the first. Full steps: [The Cornell Transfer Option](/transfer/cornell-transfer-option-guide/).

**The TC3 route** plans one year at TC3 (two semesters plus summer), then four semesters at Cornell. The announcement's words are "priority consideration"; headlines calling it a direct path overstate it.

**The SUNY Broome route** leads to an online degree, not to a residential college in Ithaca. Applications open January 2027 and the first cohort starts August 2027.

**Community college students** have no preference against them. The [Cornell Chronicle reported in 2024](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell) that about 100 community college transfers are admitted each year, and that CALS, ILR and Human Ecology have staff dedicated to transfer students' success. Older agreements with SUNY Broome (2017), SUNY Orange (2014) and the Pathways to Success colleges (2006 to 2008) are not current pathways; CALS admissions (cals_admissions@cornell.edu, 607-255-2036) can tell you what applies now. Every route is compared in [Is there a guaranteed transfer to Cornell?](/transfer/cornell-guaranteed-transfer-pathways/) and [Community college to Cornell](/transfer/community-college-to-cornell/).

## Late April to June: the decision and the bill

Decisions are rolling. The admissions page says [late April through June](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants); the FAQ says [beginning in April](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/427-when-are-transfer-admission-decisions-released/); Hotel decides in May and June. Silence in April is not a rejection. The Common Data Set lists June 15 as the notification date and July 1 as the reply date.

If you applied for aid, a Cornell Aid Estimate arrives soon after admission, and official offers come tentatively in early April. Answer document requests fast so the estimate reaches you before you commit. ## Within ten days of your NetID: housing

New transfers must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). You are exempt if you lived on campus for two years at your previous school or you matriculate as a junior or senior. Exemptions are requested [through the housing application while it is open](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), one year at a time.

Apply on the Housing and Dining Portal [within ten days of receiving your NetID](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process). There is no fee and no deposit. You request a room type and roommate preferences; halls are then assigned by random auto-allocation. Program Houses are the one place you choose by name, and more than 500 first-year and transfer students live in them. Transfers "may be placed" in West Campus houses, South Campus, North Campus and Program Houses, grouped together when possible. The wording describes a requirement and a placement, not a guarantee.

A meal plan comes with the hall: Unlimited or the West Campus House plan at $7,604 a year, or the Collegetown plan at $7,056 in South Campus halls such as Cascadilla and Sheldon Court.

From mid-June you get a room, or a place on the guaranteed waitlist with weekly updates. Sign the license and pick a move-in timeslot, which you can change until August 1. The license then binds you: a new transfer [cannot cancel unless leaving Cornell or granted an exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/12-month-undergraduate-housing-license-2026-2027). Do not sign a private lease for this year.

The ten days matter because of history. In 2016, [about 30 transfers started the year in converted North Campus lounges](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/03/transfer-students-forced-to-live-in-north-campus-lounges/), some five to a lounge, and Cornell said placement went by application date. In 2018, transfers learned [only on June 22](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus) whether they would be housed. Both predate the current one-year rule, which covers transfers arriving from fall 2022 on. Placement areas, roommates and co-ops are covered in [Cornell transfer housing](/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/).

## June and July: credit, bills and the health plan

**Credit evaluation.** Each college evaluates your courses case by case after admission and needs your final spring transcript, so evaluations finish late. The [University Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) sets the rules:

- A course must be about 80% similar in scope and content to a Cornell course; the Cornell department that owns that course decides from your syllabus.
- The minimum grade is a C; a major may require up to a B.
- A course cannot be refused because it was taken at a community college or online.
- Courses seven or more years old are generally ineligible.
- Never transferable: orientation, study skills, math below precalculus, remedial and ESL courses, MOOCs, and credit a prior school gave for its own departmental exam.
- Your Cornell transcript shows only your prior school's name and total transfer credits; your old GPA does not enter your Cornell GPA.

Keep every syllabus until your Cornell degree audit shows the credit. Caps and examples: [How many credits transfer to Cornell?](/transfer/cornell-transfer-credit-how-many-credits-transfer/)

**Exam credit is separate.** AP, IB or A-Level credit your old school accepted [is not automatic at Cornell](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/); Cornell's own tables apply. Engineering needs a 5 on AP Calculus BC, Chemistry or Physics C Mechanics. ILR needs a 5 on AP Microeconomics where your first school took a 4. Have scores sent (College Board code 2098) and send a written request to your college registrar by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester. More in [Do AP and IB credits count when you transfer to Cornell?](/transfer/cornell-transfer-ap-ib-exam-credit/)

**College emails.** In 2024, [most colleges emailed](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment/college-school-links) admitted transfers in early to mid-June about credit and enrollment. Human Ecology asked students to verify their major between June 8 and 30, and Dyson met transfers in July.

**Bills.** Fall tuition is [billed in early July and due before registration](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/finances); the 2024 bill was generated July 12 and due August 7. Parents see the Cashnet e-bill only if the student invites them. Housing bills in July or August, meals in July. The Cornell Installment Plan spreads payments.

**Health plan.** Every full-time student is enrolled in the Student Health Plan unless they waive it with comparable coverage. For 2026-27 a new fall student paid [$1,705 for fall and $2,046 for spring](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27), $3,751 in year one by our addition. The waiver deadline for fall 2026 admits was [July 31, 2026](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/node/179); after it, waivers lock. This plan is separate from the $600 Cornell Health fee inside your mandatory fees.

**International students** start the F-1 or J-1 record transfer through [Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students) once admitted.

## August: move-in and orientation

In 2026, transfers [moved in on August 18](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation), the day after first-years, and New Student Orientation ran August 18 to 23. Family Orientation was also August 18, and family information [arrives in early May](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/orientation). The [Cornell Chronicle counted 603 new transfers](https://news.cornell.edu/node/333429) that fall, 23 of them veterans. Expect fall 2027 to follow a similar pattern.

Transfers attend the same required programs as first-years: their college's programs, Community at Cornell, Cornell's Caring Community and Orientation Leader meetings, plus transfer small groups and socials. Non-traditional students may skip some optional events.

Engineering ran its own [transfer program in 2026](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/advising/new-engineering-students/orientation/transfer-student-orientation/): a resource fair and family briefing on August 18, an advising drop-in and an optional math placement exam on August 20, and a Transfer Welcome Lunch on August 21. The exam is strongly encouraged if you scored a 4 on AP Calculus BC. What happens each day: [Cornell transfer orientation and move-in](/transfer/cornell-transfer-orientation-move-in/).

**Friends.** Social connection is the theme students raise most. First-years bond in their halls months before you arrive, and transfers are spread across campus.

- In December 2020 [the Sun reported](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/09/lack-of-connection-marks-transition-to-cornell-for-transfers/) that transfers felt scattered without a dorm community. A transfer GroupMe had more than 350 members, and two students urged messaging classmates and stepping outside your comfort zone.
- Rachel Eisenhart, an ILR transfer in the Class of 2027, [wrote in 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you) that transfers repeat first-year small talk and sit between first-years and upperclassmen. The advice was to keep old friends while making new ones and to visit Ithaca in the summer. Eisenhart wrote that after a few weeks you feel like a regular sophomore.
- Jessica Silverman, who moved from UCLA to Human Ecology, [recommended](https://hecec.human.cornell.edu/?p=8161) the Tatkon Center, CUeLinks and West Campus house dinners.
- Four 2024 community college graduates profiled by the Cornell Chronicle described feeling behind and doubting they belonged after working 40-plus hours a week.

What follows from all of it: request a Program House or West Campus if you want a ready-made community, join the transfer group chat before August, and go to the transfer small groups at orientation. More in [Making friends as a Cornell transfer](/transfer/making-friends-as-a-cornell-transfer/).

**Year two.** After the required year you may rent. Desirable Collegetown leases [leave the market up to 16 months before move-in](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/) (Sun, 2018), so the search starts in your first fall. RentCafe's Collegetown average for buildings of 50 or more units was [$2,006 a month for a studio and $2,342 for a one-bedroom](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/) (updated October 30, 2026). A hall double works out to about $1,527 a month over nine months by our division. Off campus, a meal plan is optional: Bear Basic costs $4,978 a year for up to 7 meals a week, and Off-Campus Value $1,824.60 for 45 meals. Exempt juniors face the same market on arrival, after an April to June admission.

**Switching colleges later.** External transfers in Arts and Sciences can [apply to another Cornell college only after two semesters at Cornell](https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process), and A&S strongly discourages it. Hotel's internal process [excludes students who came from other institutions](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). For everyone else, Hotel wants a 3.0 Cornell GPA and AAP a 2.7. Each college's rules are in [How hard is it to transfer colleges within Cornell?](/academics/how-hard-is-it-to-transfer-colleges-within-cornell/)

## Common mistakes

- **Planning on spring entry.** There is none for external transfers. Leaving your school in December costs a semester, and missing March 15 costs a year.
- **Calling a pathway a guarantee.** The Transfer Option and the TC3 route promise consideration. Without backup applications, a denial leaves you with nothing for the fall.
- **Sending only the March 15 pieces.** The College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts are due April 15. Without them your file is incomplete.
- **Filing aid forms on April 15.** The CSS Profile and FAFSA are due March 15, 2027. For international applicants, missing it ends Cornell aid for the whole undergraduate career.
- **Applying to Engineering without the full course block.** The application is not read.
- **Transferring with 75 to 105 credits.** You lose 15 to 45 credits and add one to three semesters, about $74,970 a year in endowed tuition and fees.
- **Assuming AP credit carries over.** Miss the written request in your first semester and the credit is gone.
- **Taking prerequisites pass/fail or at a C-.** No credit in CALS or Human Ecology; no credit in Engineering below a C.
- **Missing the ten-day housing window, or signing a lease anyway.** You cannot cancel the housing license, so a lease means paying twice.
- **Not waiving the health plan when your family plan covers you.** $1,705 is billed for fall.

## FAQ

**Can I transfer to Cornell after one semester?** No. Cornell takes students who have [completed two to four semesters elsewhere](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/), so a first-semester student applies in the spring for the following fall.

**Is there a spring transfer deadline?** No. External transfers enter in the [fall only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants), and the deadline for fall 2027 is March 15, 2027.

**Is Cornell easier to get into as a transfer?** The rate is higher: 11.7% of transfer applicants were admitted in fall 2025 against 8.4% of first-years, per the [Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf). The pool is different, and your college's courses decide whether you are reviewed at all.

**What GPA do I need to transfer to Cornell?** Admitted transfers are [generally 3.3 or better](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). CALS sets a 3.0 minimum, ILR's admits mostly have 3.4 or higher, Human Ecology calls 3.5 competitive and AAP's minimum is 2.7.

**Do I need the SAT or ACT?** No. Test scores are [neither required nor expected](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/322-are-standardized-tests-required-for-transfer-applicants/); you self-report AP, IB or A-Level results in the Common App.

**Can I transfer to Cornell from a community college?** Yes. Cornell says [about a quarter of its transfers](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/) come from community colleges, and it prefers no type of school.

**Can I graduate in four years total as a transfer?** Yes, if you arrive with 60 or fewer accepted credits and finish four semesters at Cornell. Past the [60-credit cap](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services), the excess is lost.

**Is my housing guaranteed?** Cornell requires new transfers to live on campus for one year and says they [may be placed](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process) in upper-level halls, with a guaranteed waitlist for students without a room in mid-June. Apply within ten days of your NetID.

**Does Cornell give transfer scholarships?** No merit scholarships. Aid is need-based and [meets 100% of demonstrated need](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). The [transfer scholarships](https://www.cornellcollege.edu/financial-assistance/scholarships-awards/transfer-scholarships.shtml) in search results belong to Cornell College in Iowa.

**Does Cornell give credit for military training?** Generally not, per the [admissions veterans page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/veterans). Veterans choose between GI Bill benefits and a Cornell aid package; the student veteran advisor is at sva@cornell.edu, per the [veterans FAQ](https://veterans.cornell.edu/faqs). More in [Veterans and older students transferring to Cornell](/transfer/veterans-transferring-to-cornell/).

**How do I move my F-1 visa record?** Cornell's Office of Global Learning handles [F-1 transfers into Cornell](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/f-1-transfer-students), with a separate page for J-1 students. See [International students transferring to Cornell](/transfer/international-transfer-to-cornell/).
