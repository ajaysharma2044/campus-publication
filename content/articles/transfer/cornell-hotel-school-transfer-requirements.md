```glance
- Who can apply: first-year and sophomore college students with at least 12 credits; current juniors and seniors cannot.
- Deadline: March 15, 2027 for fall 2027 entry (fall only); decisions roll out in May and June.
- Required courses: 3 units of a foreign language, 1 of chemistry, 1 of precalculus, plus college microeconomics and a Knight-standard writing course.
- Grades: no minimum GPA; admitted transfers generally have a 3.3 or better.
- Credit: up to 60 transfer credits, with AP, IB or GCE capped at 15; AP Microeconomics 5 counts for HADM 1410, AP Statistics counts for nothing.
- Already at Cornell: the internal route closes December 1, 2026 for spring and May 1 for fall, and excludes anyone who transferred in.
```

To transfer into Cornell's Nolan School of Hotel Administration, apply through the Transfer Common App by [March 15 for fall entry](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) while you are a first-year or sophomore elsewhere. Have college microeconomics and a writing course that meets Knight Institute standards done or under way. Nolan sets no GPA floor, but its admits generally have a 3.3 or better, and decisions arrive in May and June.

```reader "If you're a sophomore-entry transfer from a four-year college"
Apply in the spring of your first year: Cornell takes students with two to four semesters done and not first-semester students (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/). Take microeconomics and the writing course this year, for letter grades. Submit the application, the $85 fee or waiver by March 15, 2027 and the College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts by April 15 (https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). As a sophomore you lose no credits under the 60 cap, and you live on campus for a year. The costly mistake: sending only the March 15 pieces and leaving the file incomplete.
```

```reader "If you're at a community college"
Cornell says it has no preference by type of school and that about a quarter of transfers come from community colleges (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Your microeconomics and writing courses count if they are about 80% similar to Cornell's, graded C or better, and from a regionally accredited school; credit cannot be refused because a course was at a community college or online (https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Apply during your first or second year. Nolan does not take current juniors, so if you have finished four semesters, confirm your standing with Nolan admissions before paying the fee. Keep every syllabus.
```

```reader "If you're a Transfer Option holder"
Use the Transfer Option application with your earlier portal login, not the regular Common App; there is no application fee (https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants). The page lists a resume as the extra item for SC Johnson College of Business, Nolan's parent college. The deadline was March 15 in 2026, and the page says the next application opens in winter 2027. Decisions roll from mid-April, mostly in late April or early May. The Transfer Option is an invitation to apply, not admission, so finish Nolan's microeconomics and writing courses during your year away and keep a fallback school.
```

```reader "If you're already at Cornell and want Hotel"
You use the internal route, not the March 15 one (https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). You need a Cornell GPA of 3.0, ECON 1110 or an equivalent microeconomics course, a full-time 12-credit term, and first- or second-year standing. The essay runs up to 650 words. Spring entry closes December 1, 2026 at 11:59 pm ET, with decisions in early January; fall entry closes May 1, with decisions by late June. Optional office hours run November 18, 2026. If you came to Cornell from another school, you cannot apply internally.
```

## Who can apply

Nolan considers [first-year and sophomore applicants](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) with at least 12 college credits. Sophomores applying in spring enter as juniors, so "rising juniors" qualify. Current juniors and seniors do not, and neither does anyone who already holds a bachelor's degree.

That is stricter than Cornell overall. The central admissions FAQ [discourages junior-year transfers](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/) from four-year schools; Nolan bars them outright. A first-semester college student cannot apply anywhere at Cornell yet, so the realistic window is the spring of your first or second college year.

Entry is [fall only](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) for external transfers. The current cycle is fall 2027.

## The courses you need before you apply

Nolan lists five prerequisites. Three can come from high school, two must be college courses.

| Requirement | Amount | High school counts? | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foreign language | 3 units | Yes | High school or college |
| Chemistry | 1 unit | Yes | High school or college |
| Precalculus | 1 unit | Yes | High school or college |
| Microeconomics | 1 course | No | Must be college-level |
| Writing | 1 course | No | Must meet Knight Institute requirements |

Source: the [Nolan application requirements page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/), 2026-27.

The Knight Institute runs Cornell's First-Year Writing Seminars, so pick a real composition or writing-intensive course, not a business-communication elective. Take both college courses for a letter grade and aim for at least a C, the [university's floor](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) for transfer credit.

The factors Nolan names are your college coursework, the application essay (part of the Writing Supplement) and recommendations from your professors. Hotel or restaurant work is not on that list, and [interviews are not offered](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). The essay is where you show why hotel administration, and why now.

## Grades: what 3.3 means

Nolan sets [no minimum GPA](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). Its admitted students generally have at least a 3.3, the same figure Cornell gives for [all admitted transfers](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/). Treat 3.3 as typical, not as a cutoff: a 3.3 with strong prerequisites and a focused essay is a real application, and a 3.9 without microeconomics is not.

For scale, Cornell's latest count by college is the 2021 profile: Nolan expected [48 fall and 21 spring transfers](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf), 69 of 762 that year. Spring entry has since closed. Across all colleges, 864 of 7,381 transfer applicants were admitted for fall 2025, our 11.7% from the [2025-26 Common Data Set](https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf).

## The application, step by step

1. **By March 15, 2027.** Submit the Transfer Common App with Cornell and Hotel Administration chosen, the 250 to 650 word essay, Nolan's Writing Supplement, and the [$85 fee or a waiver](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).
2. **By April 15, 2027.** Name an official at your school for the College Report in the Applicant Portal. Ask one instructor of a full-credit course for the Academic Evaluation. Send every college transcript and your final high school transcript. Fill in the Mid-Term Report yourself with your spring grades.
3. **Aid, also by March 15.** US applicants file the [CSS Profile and FAFSA by March 15, 2027](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants). EOP and HEOP applicants to Nolan also send a [HEOP Transfer Certification form](https://oadi.cornell.edu/node/50).
4. **May and June.** Nolan releases decisions on a rolling basis. Silence in April is normal.

### The resume question

Cornell's [central transfer page](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) lists a resume for Hotel Administration. Nolan's own page lists a resume only for first-year applicants, not transfers. Send a one-page resume anyway: an extra document costs nothing, a missing one could.

## How your credits count

Nolan accepts [up to 60 transfer credits](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/), and AP, IB or GCE exam credit counts for at most 15 of them. Credits above 60 are lost.

Exam credit lands in free electives, with these exceptions:

| Exam score | What it earns at Nolan |
|---|---|
| AP Microeconomics 5 | HADM 1410 |
| IB Economics 7 | HADM 1410 plus 3 elective credits |
| AP English 5 or IB English HL 7 | A First-Year Writing Seminar |
| AP Statistics, any score | No credit |

Source: [Nolan application requirements](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/), 2026-27.

None of it is automatic. Credit your old school gave for AP [does not carry over by itself](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/). Send scores and a written request to the college registrar by the [last day of instruction](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services) of your first Cornell semester; that day was December 7 in fall 2026, per the [academic calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). Where an exam and a transfer course overlap, only the course counts. So a 5 in AP Micro plus a college micro course gives you one HADM 1410, not two.

Example: a sophomore applicant with 30 college credits and 12 AP credits, all accepted, arrives with 42. That is under both caps, so nothing is lost. The same student with 60 college credits would have no room left for the AP credit.

After admission, send your final transcript to the Hotel registrar and syllabi for any course you want counted toward the core. ## Cost and housing for a Nolan transfer

Nolan is one of Cornell's endowed schools, so New York residents get no tuition break: [$73,946 for 2026-27](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters). Our sum of that tuition, $1,024 in fees, a double and the Unlimited meal plan comes to $96,318 billed for the year, before aid. Aid is need-based only, and the $85 application fee is [waived automatically](https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants) when it is a burden and you apply for aid. Families earning up to $125,000 with typical assets get grants covering tuition; up to $75,000, the full cost.

Sophomore entrants must [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). A double is $6,872 per semester for 2026-27. Students who enter as juniors are exempt and can rent.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying as a current junior.** Nolan will not consider it, and the $85 fee is gone.
- **Skipping college microeconomics because you have AP Micro.** The page asks for a college-level course. The AP 5 earns HADM 1410 after admission; it is not the college course.
- **Counting on AP Statistics.** It earns no credit at Nolan, so do not count it toward your 15 exam credits.
- **Treating March 15 as the only deadline.** The College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts are due April 15; a missing one leaves the file incomplete.
- **Missing the exam credit request.** Without a written request by the last day of your first Cornell semester, the AP credit is forfeited.
- **Entering another Cornell college first and planning to switch.** Students who came from another school cannot use Nolan's internal process. If Hotel is the goal, apply to Hotel from the start.
- **Paying for a private lease in year one.** Sophomore entrants are bound by the one-year housing rule, so a lease on top doubles the rent.

## FAQ

**Is Hotel easier to get into than the rest of Cornell?** Cornell's admit figures are university-wide, 11.7% for fall 2025 transfers. Nolan's typical 3.3 matches the [university's figure](https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/), so plan on the same bar.

**Does my old GPA follow me to Cornell?** No. Your Cornell transcript shows only your previous school's name and the total transfer credits, and the [transfer GPA is not part of your Cornell GPA](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).

**Do I need hospitality experience?** No. [Nolan's page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/) names coursework, the essay and professor recommendations as factors, not work in hotels or restaurants.

**Can I start at Nolan in January?** Not as an external transfer: Nolan's March 15 deadline is for fall only. Current Cornell students can apply internally for spring by December 1.

**Who do I ask before applying?** Nolan admissions at sha_admissions@cornell.edu or +1 (607) 254-4228, in 248 Sage Hall, per the [requirements page](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/).

**Will I fit in arriving a year late?** Rachel Eisenhart, an ILR transfer, wrote in the Sun that transfers repeat first-year small talk and feel in limbo at first, and that you feel like a regular sophomore within weeks ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you)).
