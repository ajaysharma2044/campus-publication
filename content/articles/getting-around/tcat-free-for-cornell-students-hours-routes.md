```glance
- Free at all hours: first-years with a Cornell ID (fall entrants to Aug 31, spring entrants to Dec 31), PhD students and graduate assistants.
- Everyone else registered: free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday.
- Weekday daytime without a pass: $1.50 a ride, or OmniRide at $200 a year, which pays off after about 134 paid rides.
- Routes students use: 81 and 82 on campus, 10 to the Commons, 30 to the mall, 32 and 72 to the airport, 90, 92 and 93 at night.
- From Mon Nov 9, 2026, most downtown routes board at a new East Seneca stop, about 60 yards east.
- Track buses in Bus Tracker or MyStop, not only Google Maps.
```

TCAT is free at all hours for Cornell first-years, PhD students and graduate assistants, and your Cornell ID is the pass ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). Every other registered student rides free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend, and pays $1.50 a ride or $200 a year for OmniRide on weekday days. The routes most students need are the campus-only 81 and 82, Route 10 to the Commons and the night routes in the 90s ([Sustainable Cornell](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting)).

```reader "If you're a first-year"
You ride free at any hour with your Cornell ID, from fall registration until Aug 31, 2027, with no extensions for any reason. Do not buy OmniRide; you already have it. The routes you will use are the 81 and 82 around campus, the 10 to the Commons and the 92 Blue Light bus at night. The mistake that costs you is assuming the free pass carries into sophomore year. From Sept 1, 2027, a weekday ride before 6 p.m. costs $1.50.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or spring entrant"
A spring entrant's free pass runs Jan 1 to Dec 31 of the same year, not to Aug 31. A spring 2027 entrant loses it on Dec 31, 2027. Transfers ride free any time in their first year on campus, per [Cornell Student Disability Services](https://sds.cornell.edu/accommodations-services/transportation); if the farebox rejects your ID, call Transportation Services at 607-255-4600. Buying OmniRide while the free pass covers you wastes $200, refundable only until Mar 31 less $40.
```

```reader "If you're an upper-year without a car"
You ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend. Weekday daytime rides cost $1.50 each. OmniRide costs $200 a year and pays off after about 134 paid daytime rides, about 4.5 a week over a 30-week year (our assumption). Below that, pay per ride. The costly mistake is believing TCAT is still free all day for everyone. That ended in mid-October 2024, when Cornell updated the eligibility list after a farebox glitch had let all students through.
```

```reader "If you're a grad or professional student"
PhD students and graduate assistants get OmniRide free, so the 6 p.m. limit does not apply to you. Do not pay $200 for a pass you already have. Master's and professional students without an assistantship follow the student rules: the [Johnson MBA page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/) says TCAT is free the first year, then free after 6 p.m. weekdays and all weekend. A spouse or partner with a Cornell ID can buy the OmniRide student pass at 116 Maple Ave, good for night routes 90 and 92.
```

```reader "If you rent off campus"
Your commute is likely Route 10 from downtown or Collegetown, which stays on North Tioga St after the Nov 9, 2026 stop change. If you ride a different route from East Seneca St, your stop moves about 60 yards east on Monday. West Hill and Cayuga Heights renters should not plan around routes 14S or 83: both were suspended in fall 2023 and the board [extended the suspension in April 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/).
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Ride in the free hours when you can: after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends. On weekday days, pay the $1.50 cash fare until you are sure you will pass about 134 paid rides a year; only then is the $200 OmniRide cheaper. If you buy it and ride less, a refund before Mar 31 still costs $40. If family visits, children under 12 ride [free with a paying adult](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). Student government asked Cornell in March 2026 to fund free passes for all undergraduates; in April the president [sent the request back for further work](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026/returned-president).
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
A fall entrant's free first-year pass runs to Aug 31, so the summer after first year is covered. Upper-years keep free rides after 6 p.m. weekdays and on weekends. OmniRide expires Aug 31 and is prorated monthly, so a pass bought for a summer job costs less than $200. Heat can pull buses: on June 23, 2025 several overheated or lost air conditioning, and TCAT [cut service the next day](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/06/tcat-reduces-service-due-to-overheating-buses). On hot days, check Bus Tracker before you leave.
```

```reader "If you need accessibility accommodations"
When a fare applies, riders with disabilities pay [$0.75 instead of $1.50](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/); tell the driver before you tap. First-years and PhD students ride free at all hours anyway. In snow, TCAT switches to [snow routes chosen by dispatch](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/01/tcat-prepares-to-swerve-away-winter-storm-conditions) and posts detours online, so check Bus Tracker or MyStop before you leave. With a mobility-affecting disability, temporary or permanent, apply to [Student Disability Services](https://sds.cornell.edu/accommodations-services/transportation): options include a free bus pass and CULift, Cornell's free paratransit shuttle on campus and to nearby housing. CULift runs 7:45 a.m. to 10 p.m. on weekdays in the fall and spring semesters; book by 10 a.m. the business day before. For off-campus trips, TCAT's ADA paratransit, run by Gadabout, is booked through TCAT at 607-277-9388, ext. 450.
```

## Who rides free, and when

Your Cornell ID works as the pass on the farebox. What it buys depends on your status, per [Cornell's bus pass page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes):

| Who you are | Free hours | Outside free hours |
|---|---|---|
| New first-year, fall entrant | All hours, fall registration to Aug 31 of the next year | Not applicable |
| New first-year, spring entrant | All hours, Jan 1 to Dec 31 of the same year | Not applicable |
| Transfer, first year | All hours, for the first year on campus | Not applicable |
| PhD student or graduate assistant | All hours (free OmniRide) | Not applicable |
| Any other registered student | After 6 p.m. weekdays; all day Saturday and Sunday | $1.50 a ride, or OmniRide at $200 a year |
| Spouse or partner | None by default | Can buy the OmniRide student pass at 116 Maple Ave for night routes 90 and 92 |

The first-year pass has no extensions for any reason. Cornell's wording is that new students "automatically get OmniRide privileges for unlimited TCAT rides." [Student Disability Services](https://sds.cornell.edu/accommodations-services/transportation) adds that the free first-year pass covers transfer students too. Call Transportation Services at 607-255-4600 if your ID is rejected as a transfer.

The rule is older than most students think. Cornell ended free passes for continuing students in [2007](https://cornellsun.com/2007/04/25/university-changes-free-bus-pass-policy/). Later, a farebox ID glitch let every student ride free at all hours. Cornell updated the eligibility list on Oct 16, 2024, and non-first-years started paying again.

## OmniRide or pay $1.50 a ride

OmniRide costs [$200 a year, prorated monthly](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). You buy it on MyParking.Cornell.Edu, which shows the current price at purchase. Activation can take up to 24 hours. You can refund it until Mar 31, less $40. It expires Aug 31 of the next year.

The math is simple, because evening and weekend rides are free anyway. Only weekday rides before 6 p.m. count.

Example: a junior lives downtown and takes Route 10 to campus and back on all five weekdays, both before 6 p.m. That is 10 paid rides a week, or $15 a week in cash. The junior passes 134 rides in about 14 weeks, so OmniRide is the better buy.

Example: a sophomore in Collegetown walks to class and rides only on two rainy weekdays, round trip. That is 4 paid rides a week, or 120 over an assumed 30-week year. At $1.50, that is $180, so cash beats the $200 pass.

If you ride a lot on one weekday, TCAT caps fares at [$3 a day](https://www.masstransitmag.com/technology/fare-collection/fare-collection-equipment/news/55324585/tompkins-consolidated-area-transit-inc-tcat-tcat-and-genfare-introduce-tap-to-pay-fare-collection-daily-fare-capping): pay with the same contactless card, phone or rechargeable T-card, and every ride after the second is free for the rest of that service day.

## How to pay when your ID fails

Cornell IDs use a magstripe that works poorly with TCAT's fareboxes, according to the [Sun's 2024 report](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). If the farebox rejects your ID during free hours, the general manager said drivers override it. Outside free hours, pay the [$1.50 cash fare](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

[Downtown Ithaca lists the ways to pay](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/):

1. Exact-change cash.
2. Tap a contactless card or phone.
3. The TFare app; its QR code expires after a minute, so open it at the door.
4. A rechargeable T-card, or a disposable one from local retailers.

The same page lists a $0.75 fare for seniors 60 and up and riders with disabilities, and free rides for children under 12 with a paying adult.

Lost your ID? The Registrar is at 607-255-4232. TCAT answers at 607-277-RIDE, Monday to Friday 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

## The routes students actually use

Cornell's [bus commuting page](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting) names the routes that matter for students. The night routes come from the [bus pass page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

| Route | Where it goes | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 81, 82 | Campus only | North, West and central campus hops |
| 83 | Campus only | Suspended since Oct 2023; ride 10 or 31 instead |
| 10 | Downtown and campus | Commons, Collegetown and class |
| 15 | Southside Shopper | Groceries and shopping |
| 30 | Ithaca Mall | The mall and shopping |
| 32 and 72 | Airport and Commons | ITH: 32 on weekdays, 72 on weekends |
| 90, 92, 93 | Night routes | Late nights; 92 is the Blue Light bus |

The [Blue Light bus runs nightly on Route 92](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation), 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round. For every upper-year, those hours fall inside the free window.

For the airport, the [airport's own page](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation) says Route 32 runs weekdays and Route 72 on weekends. Pay the driver as on any route. Our [airport guide](/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/) covers taxis and early flights.

Routes 14S and 83 serve West Hill, Cornell and Cayuga Heights. Both were suspended in fall 2023 for lack of drivers and buses, and [TCAT's board extended the suspension indefinitely on Apr 25, 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/).

Every TCAT bus carries a [front bike rack at no extra fare](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall). Use it for the climb from downtown to campus.

The City of Ithaca and TCAT reshuffle the East Seneca St stops on Nov 9, 2026, [to ease congestion](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/) after the Seneca Street garage closed in the spring.

| Stop from Nov 9, 2026 | Where | Routes |

| East Seneca stop 1 | About 60 yards east, between Dewitt Mall and Tompkins Financial | 11, 11S, 13, 14, 15, 17, 20, 21, 31W, 40, 43, 51, 52, 65, 67 |
| East Seneca stop 2 | East Seneca at North Tioga, the former intercity spot | 30, 30W, 90 |

OurBus leaves East Seneca the same day for [235 S. Cayuga St](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/). TCAT posts updates on its website, Bus Tracker and the MyStop app. For intercity buses, see [where intercity buses stop](/getting-around/ithaca-intercity-bus-stops-cornell-downtown/).

## Tracking buses and living with delays

Late buses are the most repeated TCAT complaint in the coverage: our tally counts 10 distinct articles and documents from 2019 to March 2026. A Cornell transportation official wrote in 2025 that [nearly a third of students](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall) use TCAT as their main way to campus.

- In 2022, student Coco Wen '26 described a long wait because Google Maps showed the wrong time, then paying for an Uber ([Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/06/tcat-implements-massive-service-cut-due-to-driver-shortage/)).
- In 2023 the Sun reported [overcrowding and hourly service](https://cornellsun.com/2023/02/10/tcat-service-cuts-have-caused-lasting-trouble-for-cornell-community-and-ithaca-residents/) on some routes.
- TCAT pulled its electric buses in April 2024; by March 2025 it had put [5 donated 2010 buses](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/04/tcat-begins-using-donated-buses-as-agency-seeks-to-stabilize-service) from Albany's CDTA into service while 7 electric buses were still off the road.
- Student Assembly Resolution 69, adopted Mar 26, 2026, calls the buses late and unreliable and asks Cornell to fund [free passes for all undergraduates and graduate workers](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026). The president [returned it for further work](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026/returned-president) on Apr 24, 2026, noting that the current Bus Pass Program Agreement runs through June 30, 2027.

What to do about it: use Bus Tracker or MyStop, not only Google Maps. In snow, TCAT runs [snow routes chosen by dispatch](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/01/tcat-prepares-to-swerve-away-winter-storm-conditions) and posts detours online. In summer, heat can pull buses: on June 24, 2025, TCAT [reduced service](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/06/tcat-reduces-service-due-to-overheating-buses) and kept the frequent routes 10, 30, 81 and 82 running first.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming TCAT is free all day after first year.** A weekday ride before 6 p.m. costs $1.50, every time.
- **Buying OmniRide while a free pass covers you.** First-years, transfers, PhD students and graduate assistants waste $200, and a refund before Mar 31 still costs $40.
- **Riding fewer than about 134 paid trips on OmniRide.** At 4 paid rides a week, cash costs about $180 over a 30-week year against $200.
- **Thinking a spring entrant's pass runs to Aug 31.** It ends Dec 31; the next weekday daytime ride costs $1.50.
- **Waiting at the old East Seneca stop after Nov 9, 2026.** Fifteen routes now board about 60 yards east.
- **Trusting Google Maps alone.** The 2022 student who did so ended up paying for an Uber.
- **Planning a West Hill commute on 14S or 83.** Both have been suspended since fall 2023; one rider's workaround meant up to a 55-minute unsheltered wait.

## FAQ

**Do I still need to swipe my ID during free hours?**
Yes, the ID is your pass. If the farebox rejects it after 6 p.m. or on a weekend, drivers override it, per [TCAT's general manager in 2024](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free).

**Does TCAT run on Thanksgiving Day?**
No. TCAT runs every day except six holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas ([Cornell Sun, July 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/07/service-changes-for-july-4-holiday-weekend-tcat-buses-curbside-recycling-city-offices-will-be-closed-on-independence-day)). Our [guide to staying over breaks](/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) covers food and rides.

**Can my spouse or partner ride free?**
No, but a student's spouse or partner with a Cornell ID can buy the OmniRide student pass at [116 Maple Ave](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), good for unlimited rides on night routes 90 and 92.

**Can I bring my bike on the bus?**
Yes. Every TCAT bus has a [front rack at no extra fare](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall).

**Do Cornell staff ride free?**
Yes. Since [Oct 22, 2024](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2024-10-22/unlimited-tcat-access-faculty-staff), faculty and staff ride TCAT free in Tompkins County with a Cornell photo ID, seven days a week. Students are not covered by that change.

**Is OmniRide cheaper if I buy it in spring?**
Yes. The [$200 price is prorated monthly](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) and every pass expires Aug 31, so a later purchase costs less. The current price shows at purchase.
