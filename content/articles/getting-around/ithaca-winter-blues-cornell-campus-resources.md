If winter gray hits your mood, start with Cornell Health. Its pharmacy sells light boxes for $20, and the campus offers Let's Talk, a drop-in counseling service ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/02/winter-blues-sad-and-the-benefits-of-light-therapy)). Light, movement and people are the cheap first steps.

## Why does winter feel so heavy in Ithaca?

Less light and more cold. On Dec. 21, 2026, the sun rises at 7:32 a.m. and sets at 4:36 p.m., for 9 hours and 4 minutes of daylight ([U.S. Naval Observatory](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=2026-12-21&coords=42.44,-76.50&tz=-5)). By Jan. 25, 2027, the first day of spring classes, the sun rises at 7:26 a.m. and sets at 5:11 p.m., which is 9 hours and 45 minutes of daylight. That is 41 minutes more than on the shortest day (from the same source).

Cold adds to it. The 1991-2020 normals put January's mean temperature at 22.8F ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)). Winter 2025-26 was the coldest in over a decade, with about 19 days that never rose above freezing from Jan. 23 to Feb. 11 and a low of -7F on Jan. 30 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/5z6poblqwoea)).

## What does Cornell Health offer?

Three things show up in the sources behind this page.

- **Light boxes.** The Cornell Health Pharmacy sells them for $20. The Sun reports using one for 30 minutes soon after waking, before noon, 12 to 24 inches from your face, at up to 10,000 lux, a measure of light brightness.
- **Let's Talk.** A drop-in counseling service, described in the same story.
- **An advice line.** Cornell Health runs a 24-hour advice line at 607-255-5155 ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)).

Hours and locations for Let's Talk are not in the sources behind this page, so check Cornell Health's website or call the advice line. Whether a light box suits you is a medical question, so ask a Cornell Health clinician before you buy one for a diagnosed condition.

## What can you do for free or cheap?

Get outside in the morning light when you can, and move. A Cornell Fitness Centers day pass costs $10 and a fall membership costs $115, bought in the FusionGO app ([Cornell Fitness Centers](https://scl.cornell.edu/recreation/fitness-centers/student-memberships-day-passes)). Our [winter biking guide](/cornell/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/) covers riding in the cold.

Dress so you can go outside. Cornell Health advises layers, a water-resistant jacket and boots, and covering hands and ears (Cornell Health cold-weather page above). If cost is the barrier, the Access Fund covers up to $100 for boots or $150 for a coat, hat and gloves ([Graduate School tips](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-winter-clothing-resources)).

## Who needs extra planning?

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your first Ithaca winter lands in your second semester, when the days are still short. Add a standing weekly plan, like a gym slot or a club meeting, before the cold arrives. Our [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) has the broader checklist.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
If you come from a warmer or sunnier place, the first winter can hit hard. Cornell Health's resources above are open to you. Our [international start page](/start/international/) covers arrival, clothing and help.
```

```reader "If you stay over break"
Break is the darkest and quietest stretch, with dining shut and fewer people around. Make a plan for meals and company before the halls empty. See [staying in Ithaca over breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) and the [staying over break start page](/start/staying-over-break/).
```

## When should you call someone?

Call [Cornell Health's advice line](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety) at 607-255-5155 if your mood is making it hard to sleep, study or eat, or if you feel unsafe. It runs 24 hours a day. Do not wait for spring to feel better first.

## What to do next

Walk to Cornell Health soon to ask about a light box and Let's Talk hours, and put the advice line number in your phone.
