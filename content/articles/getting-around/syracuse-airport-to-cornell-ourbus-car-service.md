```glance
- OurBus runs Cornell to Syracuse Hancock (SYR) six days a week; posted fares are $46.70 to $62.10 each way, Sunday cheapest (Nov 8, 2026).
- The ride takes about 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min; Ithaca stops are on Jessup Rd, across from Baker Flagpole and the Cayuga St Garage.
- At SYR, follow Ground Transportation signs to the south taxi queue for the OurBus pickup.
- Landing when no bus runs: prebook Big D's Limousines, 607-273-3030 ext 2; it prices each trip by quote.
- Driving is about 50 to 65 miles and 1 h 15 min to 1.5 h; SYR garage parking for a pickup is $10 for the first hour.
- The free Student Assembly charters run to the airports after finals, not back; sign-up is on Campus Groups.
```

Take OurBus. It runs between Cornell and Syracuse Hancock International Airport [six days a week](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk), with posted fares of [$46.70 to $62.10 each way and Sunday the cheapest day](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny) as of Nov 8, 2026. If you land when no bus runs, prebook the car service Cornell lists, [Big D's Limousines at 607-273-3030 ext 2](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources), or have someone drive.

```reader "If you're flying home to the Midwest, South or West"
SYR is your hub: it advertises [nonstop service to 27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/), against two from Ithaca. Book the OurBus seat once your flight is fixed, and pick a bus that leaves well after you land. Sunday departures are the cheapest. A round trip by bus costs about $93 to $124 at the posted fares. One suitcase up to 50 lb rides free; a second is [$30 and must be prepaid](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university). The costly mistake is a tight connection: OurBus says to allow [at least 2 hours between the bus's scheduled arrival and your flight's boarding time](https://www.ourbus.com/faq).
```

```reader "If you're an international student landing at SYR"
You will reach SYR on a connection, so the last leg is the one most likely to run late. Book an OurBus departure with a wide gap after your scheduled landing. In a [2019 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/), Jaewon Sim '21 described missing a campus bus by five minutes as a freshman and now triple-checks the bus schedule; Ludovica Stilli '22 said "timing is everything"; and Pia Bocanegra '19 said she now packs extra clothes and toiletries in her carry-on. If you land late at night, arrange Big D's before you fly. Do not book a train to "Syracuse": the Amtrak station is a different place from the airport.
```

```reader "If you're a parent picking up or dropping off"
Cornell's family page says flights into SYR [need a rental car or a bus](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). The drive is about 1 h 15 min each way. Pickup parking in the SYR garage is [$10 up to an hour and $13 for one to three hours](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/). If your student flies alone, OurBus at $46.70 to $62.10 saves you a 2.5 to 3 hour round trip, and it stops on campus. Expect evening and early-morning delays from [I-81 northbound roadwork near the airport since May 3, 2026](https://cnysignal.com/hancock-airport-adds-flights-and-a-construction-zone-for-summer-2026-plan-accordingly).
```

```reader "If you're paying your own way"
The direct OurBus is the cheapest door-to-campus option at $46.70 to $62.10, and Sunday is the cheapest day. A Sunday Nov 8 sample also showed a [transfer option for $32 that takes about 3 hours](https://www.checkmybus.com/syracuse-ny-airport/ithaca-ny). [OurBus Select](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269) takes $15 off each of your next 10 tickets, up to $150. The separate [student discount](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/ourbus-student-discount/) gives 10% off your first ride booked with a .edu email, up to $15, on a booking of $25 or more. On the way out after finals, watch Campus Groups for the free Student Assembly charter to SYR.
```

## How the OurBus airport route works

OurBus expanded its campus service to Manhattan to seven days a week from Sept 1, 2026, and the same [Sun report](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk) says it runs Cornell to Syracuse six days a week, stopping at the airport and at Destiny USA. OurBus is a [broker that owns no buses](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel) and has served Cornell since 2017.

The [Ithaca to SYR route page](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny) lists 51.4 miles and a ride of 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min. It drops at SYR Terminal A. In the Ithaca-bound direction, the [Syracuse Airport to Ithaca page](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/syracuseairport-to-ithaca) tells riders to follow Ground Transportation signs to the south taxi queue for the pickup.

| Ithaca stop on the SYR route | Where exactly |
|---|---|
| North Campus | Jessup Rd, behind Ganedago Hall (to SYR) or in front of Robert Purcell Community Center (from SYR) |
| West Campus | West Ave, across from Baker Flagpole |
| Downtown | Cayuga St Garage |

Downtown, OurBus moved its city stop to [235 S. Cayuga St beside the South Cayuga garage on Monday, Nov 9, 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/). Check the exact curb on your ticket.

From SYR, the last bus in a Sunday, Nov 8 sample left at 8:00 p.m. ([CheckMyBus](https://www.checkmybus.com/syracuse-ny-airport/ithaca-ny)). The booking page shows each day's exact times.

## Car service and Uber

Cornell's travel page lists [Big D's Limousines](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources) for Syracuse to Ithaca trips, at 607-273-3030 ext 2. Cornell says its listings are a courtesy, not an endorsement. Big D's [own site](https://bigdslimos.com/) books a trip once you have a quote and sign its service agreement, and it lists a second number, 315-730-5931. Cornell's MBA page on living in Ithaca says ground transport from SYR [must be arranged in advance](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/).

Big D's is the answer for a late-night landing or a group splitting one car. For a solo rider, Uber's SYR page puts the average UberX fare to Ithaca at [$120 over the last 12 months](https://www.uber.com/global/en/r/airports/syr/taxi/).

## Driving from SYR to Cornell

Distance depends on the source: OurBus says 51.4 miles, [Cornell conference services](https://conferenceservices.cornell.edu/node/196) about 60, and the [Ithaca College family page](https://www.ithaca.edu/orientation/family-and-guest-resources/transportation) about 65 miles and 1 h 15 min. Plan on 1 h 15 min to 1.5 h.

An old Cornell lab conference document routes drivers [I-81 south to the US-11 exit toward Homer and Cortland](https://www.classe.cornell.edu/rsrc/Home/Research/ERL/XDL11Travel/Syracuse.doc), about 1.5 hours. It predates the current roadwork. CNY Signal reported [I-81 northbound roadwork near the airport from May 3, 2026](https://cnysignal.com/hancock-airport-adds-flights-and-a-construction-zone-for-summer-2026-plan-accordingly), affecting evening and early-morning travel, and a consolidated rental car facility under construction.

Parking for a pickup or a trip, on [SYR's rate page](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/):

| SYR lot | Up to 1 h | 1 to 3 h | 3 to 6 h | 6 to 24 h (daily max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garage | $10 | $13 | $15 | $22 |
| Blue Lot | $7 | $9 | $11 | $16 |
| Red and Purple economy | $6 | $8 | $9 | $14 |

Pre-booked parking adds a non-refundable $2.99 fee. A week in economy at the daily max comes to about $98 (7 x $14). [Ithaca's airport](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking) charges $40 a week for long-term parking.

Example: a parent drives up to meet a 2 p.m. arrival and waits two hours in the garage. Parking is $13, and the round trip from campus is about 2.5 to 3 hours of driving. The same student alone on OurBus pays $46.70 to $62.10.

## The free Student Assembly charters

These charters run from campus to the airports after finals. They do not bring you back to Cornell.

- May 2024: Resolution 76 put [$18,000 toward 20 free shuttles](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) to SYR and Ithaca. Undergraduates only, first come first served, pickups at Baker Flagpole and RPCC. Groups registered by May 12 at 11 p.m., and buses ran from 4 a.m. May 15 to 3:30 p.m. May 18.
- Fall 2025: [Resolution 18](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026), adopted Nov 13, 2025, moved $7,853.32 for finals-week charters to both airports.
- Spring 2026: [Resolution 83](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026), adopted Apr 16, 2026, moved $10,668.87. Free for undergraduates, sign-up through Campus Groups, first come first served.

## The Syracuse Amtrak station is not the airport

Amtrak stops at the [William F. Walsh Regional Transportation Center, 1 Walsh Circle, Syracuse NY 13208](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources). The airport is at [1000 Col. Eileen Collins Blvd, Syracuse NY 13212](https://www.syrairport.org/), phone 315-454-3263. A ticket "to Syracuse" on a train or a city bus does not reach the terminal.

## When snow hits SYR

Snow can stop the Syracuse leg too. In a January 2019 storm, Cornell's own bus service [cancelled its Syracuse-to-Cornell run](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/) that weekend, and the governor banned buses from most highways from Saturday at 3 p.m. until the storm ended. One student pushed his Syracuse-bound flight to Monday.

If a storm is forecast for your landing day, change the bus early. OurBus lets you cancel or reschedule until 60 minutes before departure. Cancel more than 24 hours ahead and the ticket price goes back to the card you paid with; inside 24 hours it becomes OurBus credit. Booking and facility fees are not refunded ([OurBus FAQ](https://www.ourbus.com/faq)). Keep the Big D's number in your phone.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking the bus too close to the landing.** A delayed flight makes you miss it, and OurBus does not refund a missed bus. Unless it can move you to a later bus that day, the $62.10 seat is lost.
- **Bringing two big suitcases without paying.** A second bag is $30 if prepaid, and at peak move-in times the [Sun found](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes) two large bags effectively mean two seats.
- **Booking "Syracuse" instead of the airport.** City buses and Amtrak stop in the city; you still need a ride to campus or the terminal.
- **Counting on a free charter to bring you back.** The Student Assembly charters run to the airports after finals only.
- **Leaving a car at SYR for a week.** About $98 in economy, against $40 at Ithaca's airport.
- **Taking a car when the bus runs.** UberX from SYR to Ithaca averages $120, against $48 to $51 for the bus on a Sunday.
- **Landing late with nothing booked.** If your flight lands after the day's last bus, you need a car, and Big D's needs a call ahead. Book it before you fly.

## FAQ

**Does OurBus wait if my flight is late?**
No. OurBus says it [cannot hold a bus at a stop for a late passenger](https://www.ourbus.com/faq), because it runs to a timetable. If you miss it, OurBus can move your ticket once to the next bus with a free seat that day, but only while your original bus is still on its trip. There is no refund for a missed bus, so book a later bus rather than a tight one.

**How much luggage can I bring on the airport bus?**
One suitcase up to 50 lb in the hold and one handbag on your lap ride free. Extra bags are [$30 each and must be prepaid](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university) during booking.

**Should I fly into SYR or Ithaca instead?**
Ithaca has two scheduled nonstops, JFK and Washington Dulles, so one cancellation can strand you. SYR lists [27 nonstop destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/) but adds the bus ride. The airport comparison guide below works through the trade-off.

**Which airlines fly to SYR?**
The [destinations list](https://syrairport.org/destinations/) includes American, Delta, United and JetBlue, plus leisure carriers such as Allegiant, Breeze, Sun Country and Frontier.

**Who do I call at the airport about parking?**
SYR's main line is 315-454-3263 and its [parking office is 315-455-3550](https://www.syrairport.org/).
