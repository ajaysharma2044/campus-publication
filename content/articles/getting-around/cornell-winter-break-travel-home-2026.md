- Dec. 19, the last day of finals, is a peak bus day, so book the trip out early.

Cornell's undergraduate residences close at [2 p.m. on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, and reopen at 9 a.m. on Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). Most students ride a bus to New York City. Book the trip out early, and plan the trip back with a spare day for snow.

## Which dates set your trip?

Fall finals run Dec. 11 to 19, and Dec. 19 is the last day of the term ([Cornell Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)). Anyone still in a room after the 2 p.m. closing pays $60 per hour or part hour, with no proration. To leave later, such as for a late flight, send a request through the Housing and Dining Portal with your name, room and reason by Monday, Dec. 7. Only students approved for winter break housing can stay. Meal plans end with brunch on closing day and resume with lunch the day the halls reopen.

OurBus names the last day of December finals as one of three peak days of the year, with the Wednesday before Thanksgiving and the Friday spring break begins ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269)). In 2026 that is the Saturday that ends finals.

On the way back, you can move in any time before classes start with an arrival timeslot in the portal ([closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice)). Spring term begins Jan. 25 on the registrar's calendar, so plan your return for that date.

## Should you ride C2C or a commercial bus?

For price, commercial. Cornell's own Campus to Campus coach, C2C, costs $93.50 one way with a NetID and $101 for the public ([C2C rates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates)). Six commercial carriers advertised New York seats from $25 to $43 in one November sample ([Wanderu](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/)). Since Sept. 1, OurBus has run 4 to 6 buses a day to Manhattan, seven days a week ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk)).

| | C2C | OurBus | Greyhound or FlixBus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campus stops | North Campus, Sage Hall, B Lot | North and West Campus, plus downtown | North and West Campus, plus 131 E. Green St. |
| NYC end | Weill Cornell and East 44th St. | GW Bridge, Hudson Yards, Madison Ave. | Port Authority and 12 other stops |
| Bags | 2 suitcases below, 2 small carry-ons | 1 suitcase up to 50 lb | 1 carry-on and 1 checked bag |
| If you cancel | Fee per seat | Credit, not cash | Check the fare type |

Sources: Cornell [C2C policies](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations), [OurBus at Cornell](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university) and [Greyhound](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny).

OurBus's downtown stop moved to 235 S. Cayuga St. on Nov. 9 ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/)). Every carrier's stops and prices are compared in our [NYC bus guide](/cornell/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/).

Say a first-year rides C2C both ways. That is [$187 round trip with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Two OurBus seats at the $25 floor cost $50, a gap of $137. The floor is the cheapest seat on the cheapest day, not a promise for the peak day.

### How does C2C booking work this winter?

C2C moved to a new booking system on Nov. 4. Everyone needs a new account, including past riders, and signing in with Cornell single sign-on gets the NetID fare ([Cornell FCS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4)). The full rules are in our [C2C guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/).

Two links split the season. "2026 Travel Reservations" covers departures through Dec. 23 and changes to existing bookings. "2027 Travel Reservations" covers trips from Jan. 6, when the new system's first trips run ([C2C page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)). A round trip over break is two bookings in two systems.

1. Book the trip out on the 2026 link, for the last day of finals or closing day if you can.
2. Book the trip back on the 2027 link, for a date after the halls reopen.
3. Board 10 minutes early with a photo ID that matches the booking.
4. If you miss the bus out, cancel the return yourself. C2C keeps the missed fare and leaves the other leg booked.

## How do you fly home?

Ithaca's airport has two scheduled nonstops, Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles ([Ithaca airport](https://flyithaca.com/airlines)). Syracuse has nonstop service to 27 destinations ([Syracuse airport](https://www.syrairport.org/)), and OurBus runs from campus to its Terminal A for $46.70 to $62.10 each way ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny)). Our [flying home guide](/cornell/getting-around/flying-home-from-cornell-airports-rides/) compares the airports.

| Getting to the plane | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ITH by TCAT 32 (weekdays) or 72 (weekends) | Pay the driver by cash, card or Tcard | Exact change if you pay cash |
| ITH by taxi | On request | Three firms, open around the clock; prebook late flights |
| ITH long-term parking | $40 a week | $160 for four weeks |
| SYR by OurBus | $46.70 to $62.10 each way | Pickups at North and West Campus |

Source: Ithaca airport [public transportation](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), [taxi](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) and [parking](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking) pages.

Student Assembly paid $7,853.32 for free finals-week charters to the Ithaca and Syracuse airports in fall 2025 ([Student Assembly](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026)). No fall 2026 charter has been announced, so check Campus Groups before you count on one.

## How do you get back in January?

The return is cheaper than the trip home. Wanderu puts January fares to NYC at about $41 on average, against about $66 in November ([Wanderu](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/)).

Snow is the risk. In January 2019, Cornell's own bus cancelled its Syracuse-to-Cornell run during a storm ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/)). Our [winter storms guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/) covers what to do on the day.

Plan a margin. Say a sophomore plans to return on Sunday, Jan. 24, and a storm is forecast. Moving to Thursday, Jan. 21, still falls after the reopening and leaves three days of slack before the first day of classes (dates from the [registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)).

## What about your car and your room?

```reader "If you have a car on campus"
Students in university housing can leave a car in their permit area over break, and Cornell warns that a car on a city street can be ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned ([Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)). The city's odd/even rule runs from Nov. 1 to Apr. 1 ([City code](https://ecode360.com/8395630)). C2C riders get free B Lot parking by emailing c2cbus@cornell.edu with plate and dates ([Cornell FCS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488)).
```

Before you leave, unplug appliances except the fridge, take valuables, passports and medications, and empty community fridges.

## Can you stay in Ithaca instead?

Winter break housing is open to students returning to campus housing for spring. It costs $125 per one-week option or $500 for the full break, and applications close Dec. 1 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). See [staying in Ithaca over breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) for food, rides and open buildings.

## FAQ

**Can I take C2C between Dec. 24 and Jan. 5?** C2C's 2026 trips run through Dec. 23 and its 2027 trips start Jan. 6, so plan on a commercial bus for those dates.

**Can my parents ride C2C with me?** Yes. C2C is open to the public, and the new system books several passengers on one reservation.

**Can I change a C2C date if my exam moves?** Yes, up to 8 hours before departure in the reservation system. Inside 8 hours, a change costs $30 per seat.

**Do I need to take everything home?** Take valuables, passports and medications. Keep other belongings in your share of the room if you return to it in spring.

## What to do next

Book the trip out for the end of finals, then the trip back for after the halls reopen. Add the Dec. 7 late-departure deadline from the [closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice) to your calendar if your flight leaves after the 2 p.m. closing.
