- Finals end Sat Dec 19, which OurBus names as a peak travel day: book that bus or flight now.
- C2C: book trips through Dec 23 on the "2026" link and trips from Jan 6 on the "2027" link; nothing runs Dec 24 to Jan 5.

Cornell's undergraduate residences close at [2 p.m. on Sunday, December 20, 2026, and reopen at 9 a.m. on Tuesday, January 19, 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). Most students go home by bus to New York City, on Cornell's C2C or a commercial carrier; students from beyond the bus routes fly from Ithaca, Syracuse or a NYC airport. Book the trip out for December 19 or 20 now, and plan the trip back for the week of January 19, with a spare day for snow.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Finals end [Dec 19](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar) and your hall closes at 2 p.m. on Dec 20, so you have about a day to leave. Book now. For C2C, make a [new account](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4) and sign in with Cornell single sign-on, or you pay $101 instead of [$93.50](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). The ride home goes on the 2026 link and the ride back on the 2027 link: two separate bookings. Your first-year pass covers TCAT to the bus stop until [Aug 31](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). The costly mistake: if you miss the bus out, C2C [keeps the full fare](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) and leaves the return booked.
```

```reader "If you fly home"
ITH has two scheduled nonstops, [Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles](https://flyithaca.com/airlines), so one cancellation can mean a long wait. Syracuse has [27 nonstop destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/), and OurBus runs there from North and West Campus for [$46.7 to $62.1 each way](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny). Fly out by Dec 20, the day halls close. Do not book a 5:30 a.m. departure without a [taxi booked ahead](https://flyithaca.com/taxis): no bus runs that early. Coming back, land a day or two before classes start on Jan 25. Snow at Syracuse in December 2016 cancelled flights for up to two days.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Most long-haul trips start from a NYC airport or Dulles, so leave a big buffer between the bus or ITH flight and your international flight. [A 2019 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/) describes a 2 p.m. JFK landing and a sprint for a 4:30 p.m. bus. Take your passport and medications with you; the closing checklist names both. If you stay, [winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing) costs $125 a week or $500, the application closes Dec 1, and mail stops Dec 23. Residences reopen Jan 19. In January 2019 a storm left Singaporean students stranded in NYC, offered housing by their compatriots at NYU and Columbia.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Skip C2C unless you need its two free suitcases or an East Side drop. On Nov 8, six carriers advertised NYC seats from [$25 to $43](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/), against $93.50 on C2C. Wanderu put January fares at about $41 on average, against about $66 in November, so the return should cost less than the trip out. OurBus gives [credit, not cash](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel) for cancellations, so buy only dates you will use. Finals-week airport charters have been free for undergraduates; watch Campus Groups. If your round trip costs more than $500, the full-break housing fee may be cheaper: apply by Dec 1.
```

```reader "If you have a car on campus"
Leave the car in your own permit area for the break. [Cornell allows that](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) for students in university housing, and warns that a car on a city street can be ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned. The city's [odd/even rule](https://ecode360.com/8395630) runs Nov 1 to Apr 1, from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. If you drive to ITH instead, long-term parking costs [$40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking). If you ride C2C, email c2cbus@cornell.edu with your plate and dates for [free B Lot parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488). The costly mistake is a Collegetown street spot over break. Not confirmed: what a tow and impound costs.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or start in spring"
If you arrive in January, C2C trips from Jan 6, 2027 book only on the "2027" link, [c2cbus.transportation.cornell.edu](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service). A spring entrant's free TCAT pass runs [Jan 1 to Dec 31](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) of the same year. You arrive inside the campus winter overnight rules, Dec 1 to Apr 1: when snow is forecast, park from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. only in the listed lots. Spring entrants' own move-in dates are not on the closing page, so ask Housing. A fall transfer going home in December follows the first-year plan.
```

## The dates that set your trip

The university calendar and the housing closing notice set the trip. Fall finals run [December 11 to 19, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), and December 19 is the last day of the term. The [2026 Winter Closing Checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice) closes undergraduate residences at 2 p.m. on Sunday, December 20. A resident still inside after that pays $60 per hour or part hour, and the charge is not prorated. Fall meal plans end with brunch that day.

If your last exam or your flight keeps you past 2 p.m., ask for a late departure through the Housing and Dining Portal by Monday, December 7, with your name, room number and reason. Only students approved for winter break housing may stay on.

Residences reopen at 9 a.m. on Tuesday, January 19, 2027. You may move back any time before classes start (the notice says January 26; the registrar's spring term begins January 25), but you need an arrival timeslot in the portal. Meal plans resume with lunch on January 19.

OurBus names [the last day of December finals](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269) as one of its three peak days of the year, with the Wednesday before Thanksgiving and the Friday spring break begins. In 2026 that is Saturday, December 19. Book Sunday the 20th early too: it is the last day anyone can leave from the halls.

## The bus to New York: C2C or a commercial carrier

Most students go home by bus. Cornell's own Campus-to-Campus coach, C2C, runs to Manhattan from three campus stops. Since September 1, 2026, OurBus has run [4 to 6 buses a day](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk) from campus to Manhattan, seven days a week. Greyhound and FlixBus stop at North Campus (121 Jessup Rd), West Campus (240 West Ave) and downtown at 131 E Green St, per [Greyhound's route page](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny). From November 9, OurBus's downtown stop moves to [235 S. Cayuga St](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/).

### How C2C works this winter

C2C moved to a [new booking system on November 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4). Everyone needs a new account, past riders included. Sign in with Cornell single sign-on to get the $93.50 fare.

Then pick the right link. On the [C2C page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service), "2026 Travel Reservations" covers departures through December 23, 2026 and any change to an existing booking. "2027 Travel Reservations" covers departures from January 6, 2027. No departures are listed from December 24 to January 5. A round trip home for the break is two bookings in two systems. The ride takes about 4.5 to 5 hours from B Lot, the last Ithaca stop.

1. Book the trip out on the 2026 link, for Dec 19 or 20 if you can.
2. Book the trip back on the 2027 link, for a date from Jan 19.
3. Check that day's times on the live departure table; the schedule changed on Aug 1, 2026.
4. Board 10 minutes early with a photo ID that matches the booking.
5. If you miss the bus out, cancel the return yourself. C2C keeps the missed fare and does not cancel the other leg.

Example: a first-year from the Upper East Side rides C2C both ways for $187 with a NetID. The same student paying the OurBus floor of $25 each way would spend $50, a gap of $137. The floor is the cheapest seat on the cheapest day, not a promise for December 19.

## Flying home

Ithaca's airport has two scheduled nonstops: [Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). The Newark flight ended in 2025. Syracuse has [nonstop service to 27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/). OurBus runs from campus to Syracuse's Terminal A with about three daily departures, for [$46.7 to $62.1 each way](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny); holiday timings may vary. Pickups are North Campus behind Ganedago Hall, West Ave across from Baker Flagpole, and the Cayuga St Garage.

| Getting to the plane | Cost | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| ITH by TCAT 32 (weekdays) or 72 (weekends) | $1.50, or free on a first-year pass | No bus meets dawn flights |
| ITH by taxi | Not published | Prebook for early and late flights |
| ITH, car parked for the break | $40 a week, long-term lot | About $160 for four weeks, our arithmetic |

The airport lists three taxi firms on its [taxi page](https://flyithaca.com/taxis): Ithaca Airport Cab (607-252-9052), Collegetown Cab (607-588-8888) and Ithaca Dispatch (607-277-7777). They run around the clock, and the airport says to prebook for late flights. ITH long-term parking is $8 a day or $40 a week, per its [parking page](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking). Not confirmed: what a 2026 taxi from campus to ITH costs.

The Student Assembly moved [$7,853.32 for free finals-week charters](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026) to the Ithaca and Syracuse airports in fall 2025. Not confirmed: whether a fall 2026 charter runs; no resolution had been posted as of November 8.

In a 2017 [College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/air-transportation-to-and-from-ithaca/1892899), posters said ITH's flights are limited and suggested Syracuse as a backup airport. One described December 2016 snow at Syracuse cancelling flights for up to two days.

## Coming back in January

The return is cheaper than the trip home, but it runs into the snow. Wanderu showed [January Ithaca-to-NYC fares averaging about $41](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/), against about $66 in November, and claims booking about 14 days ahead is cheapest. Those are an aggregator's figures, not a fare table.

January storms have stopped travel before. In January 2019, the [Cornell Daily Sun reported](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/) that the bus operators cancelled all departures after noon on Saturday, Cornell's own Syracuse-to-Cornell run was cancelled, and the governor banned buses on most highways. Singaporean students stranded in NYC were offered housing by their compatriots at NYU and Columbia.

Operators cancel early and add earlier trips when snow is forecast. Before a December 2019 storm, a student forecaster [told the Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/30/under-heavy-snow-forecast-university-mum-on-class-cancellation) to return by Saturday night or early Sunday. The same logic applies in January: you have from January 19 to 24 to get back, so aim for the front of that week.

Example: a sophomore from Philadelphia plans to return on Sunday, January 24. A storm is forecast for that weekend. Moving the trip to Thursday the 21st still lands inside the Jan 19 reopening, and leaves three days of slack before classes on January 25.

## Your car and your room over break

Students in university housing may [leave cars in their permit area](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) over break. Resident permit holders (FH, ND, SW and WD) park in their own lots in winter as usual. Everyone else follows the winter overnight rule from December 1 to April 1: when snow is forecast, park from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. only in designated areas such as the A Lot west and mid bays, the B Lot east and west bays, or the Forest Home garage.

City streets are the trap. The [city's odd/even rule](https://ecode360.com/8395630) runs November 1 to April 1, from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., and a car can be towed after 72 hours. Cornell warns that a car left on a city street over winter break may be treated as abandoned, ticketed, towed and possibly impounded. If you take C2C, email c2cbus@cornell.edu with your plate and dates for [free B Lot parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488).

Before you leave your room, the closing checklist says to unplug appliances (except fridges), take valuables, passports and medications, lock the windows and empty community fridges. Keep your keys if you return in spring.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking the January C2C trip on the 2026 link.** That link covers departures only through December 23; the return goes on the 2027 link.
- **Signing in to C2C with a plain account.** You pay $101 instead of $93.50, $7.50 more each way.
- **Waiting to book December 19 or 20.** OurBus names December 19 a peak day. Cheap seats go first: Thanksgiving-week floors ran about a third higher than earlier November dates in a November 8 sample (no December sample exists).
- **Missing the bus out and forgetting the return.** C2C keeps the $93.50 and the return leg stays booked unless you cancel it; cancelling inside 8 hours costs $30 a seat instead of $10.
- **Staying in the room after 2 p.m. on December 20 without approval.** The charge is $60 per hour or part hour.
- **Leaving the car on a city street.** It can be ticketed, towed and impounded under the odd/even rule.
- **Flying back on January 24 with no slack.** One cancelled ITH flight or a SYR snow delay of up to two days puts you past the first class on January 25.

## FAQ

**Can I take C2C between December 24 and January 5?** No departures are listed on [either booking link](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service) for those dates, so use a commercial bus.

**Can my parents ride C2C with me?** Yes. C2C is [open to the public](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4) at $101, and the new system books several passengers on one reservation.

**What if my last exam is Saturday night and my bus is Monday?** Request a late departure in the Housing and Dining Portal by [December 7](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). Without approval, staying past 2 p.m. on December 20 costs $60 an hour.

**Can I change a C2C date if my exam moves?** Yes, up to 8 hours before departure, in the reservation system only. Inside 8 hours a change costs [$30 per seat](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).

**Is there a winter break airport shuttle?** Not confirmed for 2026. The free finals-week charters to ITH and SYR ran in fall 2025; watch Campus Groups.

**Do I need to take everything home?** No, if you return to the same room in spring. Take valuables, passports and medications, and keep belongings inside your share of the room.
