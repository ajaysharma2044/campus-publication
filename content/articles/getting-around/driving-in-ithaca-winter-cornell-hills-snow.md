```glance
- Ithaca averages 62.9 inches of snow a year, and January is the snowiest month.
- Cornell's winter overnight rule starts Dec. 1: park in designated areas from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m.
- City odd/even parking starts Nov. 1 and applies from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. when the city calls it.
- Small residential streets are plowed last, so leave extra time and do not count on a clear street.
```

Park where the plows can reach, and leave early. Ithaca averages 62.9 inches of snow a year ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)), and two overnight parking rules, one from Cornell and one from the city, decide where a car can sit when it falls.

## How much snow does Ithaca get?

January is the peak. The 1991-2020 normals give these monthly totals ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)).

| Month | Average snowfall |
|---|---|
| November | 4.6 in. |
| December | 12.2 in. |
| January | 16.6 in. |
| February | 14.5 in. |
| March | 12.0 in. |
| April | 2.8 in. |

Source: Northeast Regional Climate Center, Ithaca 1991-2020 normals.

The first inch of snow now falls on Nov. 25 in a normal year, five days later than under the previous 30-year normal ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/17/delayed-first-snowfall-in-ithaca-reflects-changing-climate-patterns/)). Winter 2025-26 ran 3.1F below normal, the coldest in over a decade, with a low of -7F on Jan. 30 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/5z6poblqwoea)).

Daylight is short too. On Dec. 21, 2026, the sun sets at 4:36 p.m. and rises at 7:32 a.m. ([U.S. Naval Observatory](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=2026-12-21&coords=42.44,-76.50&tz=-5)). Plan on driving home in the dark.

## Where can you park overnight in winter?

Two rules overlap, and neither is a suggestion. On campus, from Dec. 1 to Apr. 1 and when snow is falling or forecast, overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. is limited to designated areas, open to all registered vehicles. The areas include the west and mid bays of A Lot, the east and west bays of B Lot at the Vet College, the Forest Home garage and the Hoy Road garage except its roof ([Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)). The posted fine for breaking the winter rules is $50 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/)).

On city streets, the odd/even rule runs Nov. 1 to Apr. 1, from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. Park on the odd side on odd dates and the even side on even dates. The city enforces it only when needed, such as snow events, and sends alerts by TC SIREN or by texting SIREN to 67283. Tickets run $15, $30 and $45 for repeats ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). A car unmoved for 72 hours can be towed ([City code](https://ecode360.com/8395630)). The full rules, with campus lots and break parking, are in our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/).

Collegetown renters have a third option. The Dryden Road Garage, at 118 Dryden Road, is open around the clock and costs $232 a month. Its uncovered 4th and 5th floors close from 2 a.m. to 8 a.m. from November through April ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)). Other garages and permits are in our [Collegetown and downtown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/).

## How do the plows treat the hills?

The city plows about 65 miles of road. Emergency lanes come first, then the highest-volume roads, with steep roads prioritized. Small residential streets come last, and restoring parking lanes can take weeks ([City of Ithaca plowing guide](https://cityofithacany.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12559)). On campus, the A and B lots are cleared first after heavy snow (Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services, winter rules page above).

Salt has limits. A pound melts 46 pounds of ice at 30F but barely 5 pounds at 10F, and campus uses more than 2,500 tons in a typical winter ([Cornellians magazine](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/cold-comforts-of-a-big-red-winter)). On the coldest mornings, the road can stay slick after a plow has passed.

The sources behind this page set no tire or chain rule for Ithaca roads. Ask your insurer or a local tire shop about winter tires, and check [511NY](https://www.511ny.org), the state's travel information map, before an out-of-town drive.

## What happens on a storm day?

Cornell has closed for snow. In January 2026, it closed the Ithaca and AgriTech campuses from noon Sunday, Jan. 25, to noon Monday, Jan. 26. Residence halls stayed open and status posted at emergency.cornell.edu ([Cornell Emergency Management](https://emergency.cornell.edu/?p=5006)). The city declared a state of emergency, and TCAT delayed Monday service to 10 a.m. ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/01/cornell-announces-ithaca-and-agritech-campuses-will-reopen-at-noon-on-monday)). Break-travel storms are covered in our [winter storms guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/).
