```glance
- Ithaca averages 62.9 inches of snow a year, and January is the snowiest month.
- Cornell's winter overnight rule starts Dec. 1: park in designated areas from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m.
- City odd/even parking starts Nov. 1 and applies from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. when the city calls it.
- Small residential streets are plowed last, so leave extra time and do not count on a clear street.
```

Leave early, favor main roads over the small side streets the city plows last, and park where the plows can reach. Ithaca averages 62.9 inches of snow a year ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)), and two overnight parking rules, one from Cornell and one from the city, decide where a car can sit when it falls.

## How much snow does Ithaca get?

January is the peak, with 16.6 inches in a normal month ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)); our [Ithaca winter survival guide](/cornell/daily-life/ithaca-winter-survival-guide-cornell/) has the month-by-month table.

The first inch of snow now falls on Nov. 25 in a normal year, up from Nov. 20 under the previous 30-year normal ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/17/delayed-first-snowfall-in-ithaca-reflects-changing-climate-patterns/)). Winter 2025-26 ran 3.1F below normal, the coldest in over a decade, with a low of -7F on Jan. 30 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/5z6poblqwoea)).

Daylight is short too. On Dec. 21, 2026, the sun sets at 4:36 p.m. and rises at 7:32 a.m. ([U.S. Naval Observatory](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=2026-12-21&coords=42.44,-76.50&tz=-5)). Plan on driving home in the dark.

## Where can you park overnight in winter?

Cornell limits overnight parking to designated areas from Dec. 1 to April 1 when snow falls or is forecast, and the city's odd/even rule runs Nov. 1 to April 1, from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., when the city calls it ([Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules); [City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). Our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) has the lots, fines, alerts and garages.

## How do the plows treat the hills?

The city plows about 65 miles of road. Emergency lanes come first, then the highest-volume roads, with steep roads prioritized. Small residential streets come last, and restoring parking lanes can take weeks ([City of Ithaca plowing guide](https://cityofithacany.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12559)). On campus, the A and B lots are cleared first after heavy snow (Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services, winter rules page above).

Salt has limits. A pound melts 46 pounds of ice at 30F but barely 5 pounds at 10F, and campus uses more than 2,500 tons in a typical winter ([Cornellians magazine](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/cold-comforts-of-a-big-red-winter)). On the coldest mornings, the road can stay slick after a plow has passed.

New York allows studded tires on a private car only from Oct. 16 to Apr. 30 ([N.Y. Vehicle and Traffic Law 375](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VAT/375)). Ask your insurer or a local tire shop about winter tires, and check [511NY](https://www.511ny.org), the state's travel information map, before an out-of-town drive.

## What happens on a storm day?

Cornell has closed for snow. In January 2026, it closed the Ithaca and AgriTech campuses from noon Sunday to noon Monday, Jan. 26. The city declared a state of emergency, and TCAT delayed Monday service to 10 a.m. ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/01/cornell-announces-ithaca-and-agritech-campuses-will-reopen-at-noon-on-monday)). Break-travel storms are covered in our [winter storms guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/).

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Cornell discourages first-years from bringing a car, though it offers limited permits ([Cornell Family Resource Hub](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation)). Winter is the best argument for leaving it home. Our [car guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-parking-guide-campus-collegetown-ithaca/) and the cost breakdown in [should I bring a car](/tools/should-i-bring-a-car-cornell-ithaca-cost/) weigh it.
```

```reader "If you rent off campus"
Your street is not a campus lot, so the city's odd/even rule is the one that tows you. Pick a spot you can reach on the correct side by 7 p.m. each night when snow is forecast, or rent a garage space.
```
