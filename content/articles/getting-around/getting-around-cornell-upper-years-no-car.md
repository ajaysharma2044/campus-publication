```glance
- After first year, TCAT is free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend; a daytime weekday ride costs $1.50.
- OmniRide costs $200 a year and pays off after about 134 paid daytime rides, roughly 4.5 a week.
- Late at night, the Blue Light bus (Route 92) runs 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. every night, in your free hours.
- Ithaca Carshare for students: $30 a year plus $9 an hour, or $310 a year plus $6 an hour; under about 93 hours a year, take the $30 plan.
- Ithaca Bikeshare: $1.50 to start a ride plus $0.25 a minute, so a 20-minute ride is $6.50; day pass $25, month $60.
- A year with OmniRide and four NYC round trips: $416 to $464 by commercial bus, $948 by Cornell's C2C (our math).
```

After your first year, TCAT is still free for Cornell students [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), and a weekday daytime ride costs $1.50. Buy the $200 OmniRide pass only if you take more than about 134 paid daytime rides a year; otherwise pay per ride, walk, and use Ithaca Carshare and Bikeshare for the trips a bus does not cover. For home, the cheapest commercial seat to NYC costs about a third of the C2C fare, so four round trips plus OmniRide come to $416 to $464 a year, against $948 with C2C.

```reader "If you're a sophomore or junior in campus housing"
Your first-year pass ended on Aug 31. Daytime weekday rides are now $1.50, and evenings and weekends stay free. If you walk to class and ride only in bad weather, pay per ride: two daytime rides a week for 30 weeks is $90, well under the [$200 OmniRide](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Do your errands on Saturday and Sunday, when every route is free. For a grocery or Target run by car, the [$30 Just In Case Carshare plan](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters) is the cheaper one below about 93 hours of driving a year. The costly mistake is assuming your ID still works at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday: it does not, and you will need $1.50 in exact change or a card.
```

```reader "If you're renting in Collegetown"
Central campus is a [15 to 20 minute walk](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/10/embrace-your-inner-intellectual-go-on-a-midnight-walk), so most of your trips cost nothing. You pay $1.50 only when you bus up the hill on a weekday before 6 p.m. Below about 134 such rides a year, skip OmniRide. Late at night, the free Blue Light bus on Route 92 runs until 2:30 a.m. Collegetown has Carshare pods, so a grocery trip on the [Just In Case plan](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters) costs $9 an hour plus mileage and taxes. The mistake that costs you most is a bike locked to a railing or with a cheap lock: campus police reported [51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far).
```

```reader "If you're renting downtown, in Fall Creek or on West Hill"
The Commons is about 2 miles from campus, about 40 minutes on foot by our estimate, so you will ride daily. Two daytime rides each weekday for 30 weeks is 300 rides, or $450 in cash. OmniRide at $200 is the clear buy. Route 10 stays on North Tioga Street after the [Nov 9, 2026 downtown stop changes](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/). On West Hill, do not plan around Routes 14S or 83: both have been [suspended since fall 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/).
```

```reader "If you're a PhD student or graduate assistant"
Most of this page's money math does not apply to you. Cornell gives PhD students and graduate assistants a [free TCAT OmniRide pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so you ride free at all hours. Master's and professional students follow the upper-year rule: free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and on weekends, $1.50 otherwise, or $200 for OmniRide. Carshare's student prices apply to grad students too, because Cornell subsidizes [annual memberships for undergraduates and graduate students](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters). The mistake is buying OmniRide when you already qualify for the free pass: $200 you did not need to spend.
```

```reader "If you're paying your own way"
Your cheapest year has three habits. Ride TCAT only in the free hours or walk, which costs $0. Book the cheapest commercial seat home, with floors of [$25 to $43 to NYC](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/) on Nov 8, 2026, instead of C2C at $93.50. Take the $30 Carshare plan only if you need a car a few times a year. Four OurBus round trips at the floor and no pass comes to $264 a year by our math. If your income is at or below 150% of the federal poverty guideline, Bikeshare runs an [Easy Access plan](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org) through GO ITHACA. The costly mistake is booking a peak-day bus late.
```

## What changed after your first year

First-years ride TCAT free at any hour with a Cornell ID. For fall entrants the pass runs from [fall registration to Aug 31 of the next year](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting), with no extensions for any reason. Spring entrants keep it from Jan 1 to Dec 31.

After that, you get free, unlimited TCAT after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends, as long as you stay registered. A ride at any other time is $1.50. Your other choice is the discounted annual OmniRide pass at $200.

If you remember the ID working all day, it did, by accident. Until mid-October 2024, a farebox glitch let every student ride free, and Cornell then updated the eligibility list on Oct 16, 2024, [per TCAT's general manager](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). The same report says drivers override rejected IDs during the free hours. Outside them, you pay $1.50.

You can pay the fare with [exact-change cash, a tapped card or phone, the TFare app or a T-card](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). The TFare QR code expires after a minute, so open it at the stop, not at home.

## OmniRide or pay per ride

The pass costs $200 a year, prorated monthly, and you buy it on MyParking.Cornell.Edu, which shows the current price at purchase. It can take up to 24 hours to activate. You can get a refund until Mar 31, less $40, and the pass expires on Aug 31 of the next year, per [Cornell's bus pass page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

The break-even is simple: $200 divided by $1.50 is 133.3, so the 134th paid daytime ride is where the pass wins. Over a 30-week academic year (our assumption), that is about 4.5 paid rides a week. Evening and weekend rides do not count, because they are free anyway.

| Your weekday daytime riding | Paid rides a year (30 weeks) | Cash at $1.50 | With OmniRide | Pick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 a week | 30 | $45 | $200 | Pay per ride |
| 2 a week | 60 | $90 | $200 | Pay per ride |
| 4 a week | 120 | $180 | $200 | Pay per ride, barely |
| 5 a week | 150 | $225 | $200 | OmniRide |
| 10 a week (to class and back daily) | 300 | $450 | $200 | OmniRide |

Example: a junior renting near the Commons takes Route 10 to campus and back every weekday before 6 p.m. That is 10 paid rides a week. At $1.50, the pass pays for itself in about 13.4 weeks (134 / 10), and the full year costs $450 in cash against $200.

Example: a sophomore on West Campus walks to class and rides only on rainy weekdays, about twice a week. That is 60 rides, or $90 for the year. Buying OmniRide would waste $110.

## Using the free hours

Plan your errands for the free windows: weekdays from 6 p.m. and anytime on Saturday and Sunday. Cornell names the routes students use most on its [bus commuting page](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting).

| Route | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| 81, 82 | Campus-only circulators |
| 10 | Downtown and campus |
| 15 | Southside Shopper |
| 30 | Ithaca Mall |
| 32 | The airport route |
| 90, 92, 93 | Night routes |

A Saturday grocery or mall trip on Route 15 or 30 costs you nothing. Every TCAT bus also has a [front bike rack at no extra fare](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall), so you can ride downhill and bus back up.

Downtown stops shift on Mon Nov 9, 2026. East Seneca stop 1 moves about 60 yards east, between Dewitt Mall and Tompkins Financial. Routes 30, 30W and 90 use East Seneca at North Tioga, and [Route 10 stays on North Tioga](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/). Updates appear on TCAT's site, Bus Tracker and the MyStop app.

## Late nights and the gaps

The Blue Light bus [runs nightly on Route 92, 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). Those hours fall inside your free window, so it costs nothing. TCAT drivers told the Sun in 2025 that the late-night Route 90 Cornell run [goes from packed to near empty fast](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/behind-the-wheel-meet-the-tcat-drivers-who-keep-ithaca-moving).

After 2:30 a.m., or off the night routes, your choices are a ride-hail, a taxi or your feet. Visit Ithaca's 2026 guide says [Uber and Lyft are limited outside downtown](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/). In a 2022 Sun article, a student worried about a [20 to 30 minute walk home after a 10:30 p.m. class](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/06/tcat-implements-massive-service-cut-due-to-driver-shortage/) during that year's service cuts.

Keep a taxi number in your phone. The airport's taxi page lists [Ithaca Dispatch at 607-277-7777 and Collegetown Cab at 607-588-8888](https://flyithaca.com/taxis). Call ahead rather than counting on a car at 2 a.m.

Not confirmed: current Ithaca taxi fares and Uber wait times; the newest sourced fare samples are from 2017.

## When TCAT runs late

Late buses are the top complaint in our tally of student and news sources on Ithaca travel: 10 distinct sources from 2019 to March 2026 raise it. All 8 of TCAT's electric buses were pulled in April 2024, and [5 donated 2010 buses entered service in March 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/04/tcat-begins-using-donated-buses-as-agency-seeks-to-stabilize-service). The Sun reported [overcrowding and hourly routes](https://cornellsun.com/2023/02/10/tcat-service-cuts-have-caused-lasting-trouble-for-cornell-community-and-ithaca-residents/) in 2023. In March 2026 the Student Assembly's [Resolution 69](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026) called the buses late and unreliable. It urged Cornell to fund free passes for all undergraduates and graduate workers. The president returned it on Apr 24, 2026. Not confirmed: any change to the free-hours rule since then.

Three habits help:
1. Track buses in Bus Tracker or MyStop, not only Google Maps. In 2022 a student waited because Google Maps times were wrong, [then paid for an Uber](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/06/tcat-implements-massive-service-cut-due-to-driver-shortage/).
2. For a class or exam, leave one bus earlier than you think you need.
3. In snow, expect dispatch-chosen snow routes and check Bus Tracker before you walk to the stop.

If your ID fails or goes missing, the Registrar is at 607-255-4232 and TCAT at 607-277-RIDE (Mon to Fri 7:30 to 5:00), both listed on [Cornell's bus pass page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

## Ithaca Carshare for errands and moving days

Ithaca Carshare is the local car share. Cornell subsidizes annual student memberships, and there are [four pickup spots on campus plus four in Collegetown and nearby](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing).

| Student plan | Yearly fee | Per hour | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Just In Case Annual | $30 | $9 | Under about 93 hours a year |
| It's My Car Annual | $310 | $6 | Over about 93 hours a year |

Both student plans are [$45 off the community price](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters); monthly plans get no discount. On top, you pay a one-time, non-refundable $20 per driver, mileage of 42 cents for a car, 44 for the van and 52 for the truck, plus 8% New York sales tax and a 12% rental tax. A full-day rate caps hourly charges at 10 hours in 24, except on the highest-demand cars, per the [rates page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates).

The 93-hour line comes from setting the plans equal: $30 + $9h = $310 + $6h, so h = 93.3 hours a year.

Example: a junior makes two 2-hour grocery runs a month for 8 months, 32 hours in all. Just In Case costs $30 + $20 + 32 x $9 = $338 before mileage and taxes. It's My Car would cost $310 + $20 + 32 x $6 = $522.

```tool ithaca-carshare-plan-calculator```

Check eligibility before you plan on it. You must be at least 18, with a full license held 2 or more years and no suspension in 3 years. Under 21, you need a spotless record, [your own insurance policy with at least $300,000 per accident in liability](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility), and the Younger Driver Insurance Consent Form. A foreign license needs an official driving record; an International Driving Permit is not accepted. Questions go to (607) 277-3210.

## Ithaca Bikeshare and your own bike

On Ithaca Bikeshare's electric bikes, pay as you go is [$1.50 to start a ride plus $0.25 a minute, plus tax](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org). A Day Pass is $25 for unlimited trips up to 2 hours each, and a Monthly pass is $60 with 30 free minutes a day. Ending a trip outside a hub adds $1.50.

By our math, a 10-minute ride is $4.00 and a 20-minute ride is $6.50 before tax. Twenty-two 10-minute weekday rides in a month would cost $88 pay as you go, so the $60 monthly pass wins once you ride most weekdays.

With your own bike, theft is the cost to plan for. Campus police reported [51 bike and scooter thefts worth at least $41,177](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far) in fall 2024, with one arrest. Cornell advises registering your bike and using a U-lock. After a $400 bike was taken from a Carpenter rack, its owner moved to a cable, a U-lock and two AirTags, the Sun reported. Cornell's bike network has more than 1,000 parking spots and about 7 miles of bike lanes, per its [assistant director of transportation](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall).

## Getting home without a car

For NYC, you choose between Cornell's own coach and the commercial lines. C2C costs [$93.50 one way, $187 round trip, with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates), and the public pays $101. It runs to the Upper East Side and East Midtown and carries [2 suitcases below plus 2 small carry-ons per rider](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).

| Carrier | One way, Ithaca to NYC | Where you board in Ithaca | Bags |
|---|---|---|---|
| C2C (NetID) | $93.50 | North Campus, Sage Hall, B Lot | 2 suitcases plus 2 carry-ons |
| OurBus | From $33 | North Campus, West Campus, downtown | 1 large bag guaranteed |
| FlixBus | $27 for Sat Nov 7, 2026 | 121 Jessup Rd, 240 West Ave, 131 E Green St | Not in this table |
| Greyhound | From $43.98 | 121 Jessup Rd, 240 West Ave, 131 E Green St | 1 carry-on plus 1 checked bag free |

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming your ID still rides free in the daytime.** Each weekday ride before 6 p.m. is $1.50, and at 10 rides a week that is $450 a year.
- **Buying OmniRide and then walking everywhere.** At two paid rides a week, you spend $200 for $90 of riding. A refund before Mar 31 still costs you $40.
- **Paying cash past about 134 daytime rides.** Every ride after that is $1.50 the pass would have covered.
- **Trusting Google Maps over Bus Tracker or MyStop.** A student in 2022 waited on wrong Google Maps times and ended up paying for an Uber.
- **Applying to Carshare under 21 without your own insurance.** Without a policy carrying $300,000 per accident in liability, you cannot be approved.
- **Paying for C2C by habit.** Four C2C round trips cost $748; four OurBus round trips at the floor cost $264, a $484 difference.
- **Booking the Wednesday before Thanksgiving late.** It is one of OurBus's three named peak days, when cheap seats go first.

## FAQ

**Can I buy OmniRide partway through the year?** Yes. The pass is [prorated monthly](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), and MyParking.Cornell.Edu shows the current price when you buy. Allow up to 24 hours for it to activate.

**Is TCAT free on holidays and over breaks?** Cornell's page states the free hours without a break exception, but fewer buses may run. Not confirmed: TCAT's 2026 holiday schedule, including Thanksgiving Day.

**Can my spouse or partner get a TCAT pass?** Yes, for night riding: spouses and partners can buy OmniRide at 116 Maple Ave for the night routes, per [Cornell's bus pass page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Ask Transportation Services at 607-255-4600 which routes it covers.

**Can I take a Carshare car out of town for a day?** The full-day rate caps hourly charges at 10 hours per 24, except on the highest-demand cars, per the [rates page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates). Mileage still runs at 42 cents a mile for a car.

**Can I get to the Ithaca airport by TCAT?** Yes: the airport's transit page lists [Route 32 on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), and weekend rides fall in your free hours. More on the [airport guide](/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/).

**Can I bring a bike on the bus home?** On C2C, only in a bike box, which counts as one of your two suitcases, per the [C2C rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).
