After first year, TCAT buses are still free for Cornell students [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). A daytime weekday ride costs $1.50. Walk when you can, buy the $200 OmniRide pass only if you ride more than 133 paid times a year, and use Carshare or Bikeshare for the rest.

```glance
- Past first year, TCAT is free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend; daytime weekday rides are paid.
- OmniRide pays for itself on the 134th paid daytime ride, about 4.5 rides a week over 30 weeks.
- Carshare's cheaper annual plan changes at about 93 hours of driving a year.
- A car-free year costs $264 to $948, depending mostly on which bus you take to New York.
```

```reader "If you're a sophomore or junior in campus housing"
Your first-year pass has ended. Evenings and weekends stay free, but weekday daytime rides now cost money, and your ID will not work at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday, so carry a card or exact change. If you walk to class and ride only in bad weather, pay per ride. Two daytime rides a week over the school year comes to $90, well under the pass. Do errands on Saturday and Sunday, when every route is free.
```

```reader "If you're renting in Collegetown"
A Sun writer put the walk from North Campus to Collegetown through the Arts Quad at [15 to 20 minutes each way](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/10/embrace-your-inner-intellectual-go-on-a-midnight-walk), so most trips cost nothing. You pay only when you bus up the hill on a weekday before 6 p.m., and below the break-even you skip OmniRide. Collegetown has Carshare cars for grocery runs. Lock a bike to a real rack with a U-lock, because [campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far).
```

```reader "If you're renting downtown, in Fall Creek or on West Hill"
Downtown is about 2 miles from campus, so you will ride daily. Two daytime rides each weekday over the school year is 300 rides, which is $450 in cash, so OmniRide is the clear buy ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). After the [Nov. 9, 2026 downtown stop changes](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/), Route 10 stays on North Tioga Street. On West Hill, do not plan around Routes 14S or 83, which are [suspended indefinitely](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/).
```

```reader "If you're a PhD student or graduate assistant"
Cornell gives PhD students and graduate assistants a [free OmniRide pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so you ride free at all hours and the pass math below does not apply. Master's and professional students follow the upper-year rule. Buying OmniRide when you already qualify for the free pass wastes the fee.
```

```reader "If you're paying your own way"
Ride TCAT only in the free hours, walk the rest, and book the cheapest commercial seat home instead of Cornell's own bus. Bikeshare runs an [Easy Access plan](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org) for GO ITHACA members at or below 150% of the federal poverty guideline.
```

## What changes with TCAT after first year?

First-years ride free at any hour with a Cornell ID. For fall entrants the pass runs from registration to Aug. 31 of the next year, with no extensions. Spring entrants keep it from Jan. 1 to Dec. 31 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

After that, you ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends as long as you stay registered ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free)). Any other ride is a paid fare. If you remember your ID working all day, that was a glitch: until mid-October 2024 a farebox error let every student ride free, and Cornell updated the eligibility list on Oct. 16, 2024, according to TCAT's general manager. During free hours, drivers override a rejected ID.

You can pay with [exact-change cash, a tapped card or phone, the TFare app or a T-card](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). The TFare QR code expires after a minute, so open it at the stop. Hours, routes and holiday notes are in the [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/).

## Is the OmniRide pass worth $200?

Only if you take more than 133 paid daytime rides a year. OmniRide costs $200 a year, prorated monthly, and you buy it on MyParking.Cornell.Edu. It can take up to 24 hours to activate, you can get a refund until Mar. 31 less $40, and it expires Aug. 31 of the next year ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

The math is the pass price divided by the fare, which is 133.3, so the 134th ride is where the pass wins ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). Over a 30-week school year that is about 4.5 paid rides a week. Evening and weekend rides do not count, because they are free already.

| Weekday daytime riding | Paid rides a year | Cash fare total | Best pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 a week | 30 | $45 | Pay per ride |
| 2 a week | 60 | $90 | Pay per ride |
| 4 a week | 120 | $180 | Pay per ride, by a small margin |
| 5 a week | 150 | $225 | OmniRide |
| 10 a week (to class and back daily) | 300 | $450 | OmniRide |

Say you live near the Commons and ride Route 10 to campus and back every weekday before 6 p.m. That is 10 paid rides a week, and the pass pays for itself in 13.4 weeks (134 divided by 10, per [Cornell's pass rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). Now say you live on West Campus and ride only in rain, about twice a week. Cash is $90 for the year, so the pass would waste $110.

## Which routes and hours are free?

Plan errands for weekdays after 6 p.m. and anytime on Saturday and Sunday, when upper-year riders pay nothing ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free)). Cornell's [bus commuting page](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting) names the routes students use most.

| Route | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| 81, 82 | Campus-only circulators |
| 10 | Downtown and campus |
| 15 | Southside Shopper |
| 30 | Ithaca Mall |
| 32 | The airport route |
| 90, 92, 93 | Night routes |

A Saturday grocery or mall trip on Route 15 or 30 costs nothing. For cheaper ways to cover the rest of your trips, see the [cheapest ways to get around Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/cheapest-ways-to-get-around-cornell/).

## How do I get home at night?

The Blue Light bus [runs nightly on Route 92 from 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). Those hours fall inside your free window, so it costs nothing.

After 2:30 a.m., or off the night routes, you need a taxi, a ride-hail or your feet. The airport's taxi page lists [Ithaca Dispatch at 607-277-7777 and Collegetown Cab at 607-588-8888](https://flyithaca.com/taxis). Save one number in your phone, call ahead, and ask the company for the fare when you book.

## What if TCAT runs late?

Three habits help. Track buses in the TCAT Bus Tracker or the MyStop app rather than only a map app. Leave one bus earlier for a class or an exam. In snow, check Bus Tracker before you walk to the stop.

If your ID fails or goes missing, the Registrar is at 607-255-4232 and TCAT is at 607-277-RIDE (Mon. to Fri., 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.), both listed on Cornell's bus pass page.

## Is Ithaca Carshare worth it for errands and moving days?

Yes, for a few trips a year. Cornell subsidizes annual student memberships, each [$45 off the community price](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters), and monthly plans get no discount.

| Student plan | Yearly fee | Per hour | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Just In Case Annual | $30 | $9 | Under about 93 hours a year |
| It's My Car Annual | $310 | $6 | Over about 93 hours a year |

You also pay a one-time $20 sign-up per driver, mileage of 42 cents for a car, 44 for the van and 52 for the truck, plus 8% New York sales tax and a 12% rental tax. A full-day rate caps hourly charges at 10 hours in 24, except on the highest-demand cars ([rates page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)). The 93-hour line comes from setting the plans equal: $30 + $9h = $310 + $6h, so h = 93.3.

Say you make two 2-hour grocery runs a month for 8 months, 32 hours in all. Counting the yearly fee, the sign-up charge and 32 hours at each plan's rate, Just In Case costs $338 and It's My Car costs $522, before mileage and taxes. More plan detail is in the [Carshare guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/).

Check [eligibility](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) first. You must be at least 18, with a full license held 2 years or more and no suspension in 3 years. Under 21, you need your own insurance policy with at least $300,000 per accident in liability. A foreign license needs an official driving record, because an International Driving Permit is not accepted. Questions go to (607) 277-3210.

## Should I use Ithaca Bikeshare or my own bike?

Bikeshare suits short trips and your own bike suits daily riding. On Ithaca Bikeshare's e-bikes, pay as you go is [$1.50 to start a ride plus $0.25 a minute, plus tax](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org). A Day Pass is $25 for unlimited trips up to 2 hours each, and a Monthly pass is $60 with 30 free minutes a day. Ending a trip outside a hub adds a fee.

With your own bike, theft is the cost to plan for. Campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts worth at least $41,177 in fall 2024, with one arrest. Registration, locks and campus e-bike limits are in the [bike rules guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-bike-ebike-scooter-rules-theft/).

## How do I get home without a car?

Cheapest first: for New York City, Cornell's own bus (C2C) costs [$93.50 one way, or $187 round trip, with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) starts at $37.40 one way, and [FlixBus](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/new-york-ny) showed $27 for Nov. 7, 2026. These are floors, so a peak day costs more.

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Assuming your ID still rides free in the daytime.** At 10 paid rides a week that adds up to a full year of fares.
- **Buying OmniRide and then walking everywhere.** At two paid rides a week the pass costs more than the $90 of fares it replaces.
- **Paying cash past the break-even.** Every ride after the 134th is money the pass would have covered.
- **Applying to Carshare under 21 without your own insurance.** Without the $300,000 policy you cannot be approved.
- **Paying for C2C by habit.** Four OurBus round trips at the floor are $484 less than four on C2C.

## FAQ

**Can I buy OmniRide partway through the year?** Yes. The pass is prorated monthly, and MyParking.Cornell.Edu shows the current price when you buy.

**Is TCAT free on holidays and over breaks?** Weekday evenings and weekends stay free under the upper-year rule. Check TCAT's posted holiday schedule before you travel.

**Can I take TCAT to the airport?** Yes. Cornell lists [Route 32 as the airport route](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting). See the [airport guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/).

## What to do next

Count your weekday daytime rides for a typical week and compare the total with the 134-ride line. Then decide on OmniRide by Mar. 31, the [refund cutoff](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). If you want a car for a few trips, apply to Carshare before you need it.
