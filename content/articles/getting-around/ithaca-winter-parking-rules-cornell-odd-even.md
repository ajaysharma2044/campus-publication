```glance
- Campus, Dec 1 to Apr 1: from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. on snow nights, park only in the nine designated areas, such as A Lot's west and mid bays, B Lot and the Forest Home garage.
- Resident permits (FH, ND, SW, WD): park in your own lot all winter and leave the car there over break.
- City streets, Nov 1 to Apr 1: odd/even parking from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. when the city turns it on; towing is possible after 72 hours.
- Never leave a car on a city street over a break: it can be ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned.
- The campus winter-rules fine is $50; a fire lane or accessible space costs $150 plus a tow.
- Driving to the C2C bus? Email c2cbus@cornell.edu for free B Lot parking on your travel dates.
```

From Dec 1 to Apr 1, Cornell limits overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. on snow nights to [nine designated lots, bays and garages](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules), and students with a resident permit simply stay in their own lot. Off campus, Ithaca's [odd/even rule runs Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m.](https://ecode360.com/8395630), and a car left on a city street over break can be towed as abandoned. If you leave town, the safe places are your campus permit area or an off-street spot.

```reader "If you're a student with a car in university housing"
Your $752.98 [resident permit](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) (FH, ND, SW or WD) is your winter answer. Cornell says these permit holders [park in their lots as usual](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) all winter, and students in university housing may leave the car in their permit area over winter break. The trap is a night outside your area. Your permit also works in A Lot after 2:30 p.m., but on a snow night stay in A Lot's west or mid bay. The costliest mistake: leaving the car on a Collegetown street over break, where it can be towed as abandoned.
```

```reader "If you're an off-campus renter"
Campus rules are not your main risk; the city's are. From Nov 1 to Apr 1, when the city activates [odd/even parking](https://ecode360.com/8395630), you move the car between 7 p.m. and 6 a.m. to the side that matches the date. Towing is possible after 72 hours. Before any break, get the car off the street: a lease spot, a friend's driveway or a garage. The $45 [city residential permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) runs to July 31 and only the resident can get it, not your landlord. Snowbanks [buried Collegetown cars in 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/), so dig out before the date flips.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over a break"
Staying means your car stays too, and break nights still count. On campus, winter break housing costs [$125 a week or $500 for the full break](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing), and the application closes Dec 1, the same day the campus winter parking rule starts. If you hold a resident permit, keep the car in that area. If you don't, park overnight only in a designated winter area, and pay in commuter lots on weekdays from 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., which [applies during breaks](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Off campus, odd/even still applies every night it is active.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or spring entrant"
A January arrival lands inside both seasons: the city's started Nov 1 and campus rules run from Dec 1 to Apr 1. Register your plate at MyParking.Cornell.edu first, which is [required even for short-term parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus). Designated winter areas are open to all registered vehicles overnight. A permit bought mid-year is [prorated daily](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) (resident from Sept 1, commuter from Aug 16), and Mar 31 is the last day to return one for a refund. The costly mistake: a first snowy night in the wrong lot, at $50.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
With a full assistantship on the Ithaca campus, you get free Outer Tier A and E permits as a [non-resident commuter](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students); without one, the B commuter permit is $359.77. Neither is a resident permit, so on a snow night from Dec 1 to Apr 1 you leave campus by 5 p.m. or park in a designated area, such as A Lot's west and mid bays or B Lot's east and west bays. Most grad students rent, so the city's odd/even rule is the one you meet every night. Before a conference or a holiday trip, move a street-parked car off the street.
```

## The campus rule, Dec 1 to Apr 1

Cornell's [winter parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) sets the rule. From Dec 1 to Apr 1, when it is snowing or snow is forecast, overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. is allowed only in designated areas. Those areas are open to all registered vehicles.

The page ties the rule to both the dates and the snow. It does not say whether a dry night inside those dates is exempt. Our advice: treat every night from Dec 1 to Apr 1 as a snow night, because a wrong guess costs $50.

These are the nine designated winter areas, in the page's own order:

| Designated area | What part of it |
|---|---|
| A Lot (North Campus) | West and mid bays |
| B Lot (Vet College) | East and west bays |
| Crescent Lot | North bay |
| Wilson Lab lot | West bay |
| Alumni Field | East end, near Wing Drive on Tower Road |
| Forest Home garage | All levels |
| Hoy Road garage | All levels except the roof |
| Myron Taylor Hall lot | Northwest end |
| Morrison Hall lot | The lot |

Resident-permit holders in FH, ND, SW and WD are the exception: they [park in their own lots as usual](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules). After heavy snow, crews clear A and B lots first, so those two are your best bet the morning after a storm.

Cornell posts a winter parking notice each year; the [2023 one went up Nov 16](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-11-16/winter-parking-transportation-information). Look for this year's in mid-November.

## Paying for a night on campus

Being in a designated area does not make the space free. Every car must be registered at MyParking.Cornell.edu, and parking is [by permit or payment](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus).

Without a permit for that lot, pay by ParkMobile: text PARK to 77223 or use the app, with a [$0.45 fee per transaction](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Most lots' parking day ends at 7:30 a.m., and B Lot uses a 24-hour day. Multi-day purchases are allowed in most locations.

Enforcement runs whenever the university is open. It pauses on official closed staff holidays until the next business day, per the [winter rules page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules). No-parking, fire lane, reserved, service and accessible rules are never suspended.

## The city rule: odd/even from Nov 1 to Apr 1

Ithaca's code sets on-street [odd/even parking from Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m.](https://ecode360.com/8395630) The city turns it on and off by weather. When it is on, you may not park on the odd-numbered side on even dates, and the reverse. In plain terms, on Feb 14 you park on the even side, which is how [FingerLakes1 described the rule in effect](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/02/14/ithaca-sidewalk-snow-rules-in-effect/) that day in 2026.

Cornell's own summary says the city enforces it [when snow removal is needed](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules). The code allows ticketing, and towing is possible after 72 hours. Snow emergency routes have their own separate rule, also with towing.

Plows set the pace. Two sources describe residential streets being plowed last: a [2014 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/traveling-to-and-from-cornell/1599797) and the [Sun's 2022 winter-driving report](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/). The 2022 Sun report also described Collegetown street cars buried by plow banks.

Not confirmed: whether a $45 city residential permit changes anything under the odd/even rule.

## Over a break: where the car can sit

The [winter rules page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) puts it bluntly. A car left on a city street over winter break may be treated as abandoned, ticketed, towed and possibly impounded.

Winter break is long. In 2025-26, undergraduate residences closed at 2 p.m. on Sun Dec 21 and recess ran to Jan 13, 2026, per the [winter closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). That is more than three weeks, far past 72 hours. The 2026-27 dates were not posted on Nov 8.

| Where you leave it | Over break | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Your resident permit area (FH, ND, SW, WD) | Allowed; the policy applies year-round | [Cornell winter rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) |
| B Lot, riding C2C | Free on your travel dates if you email c2cbus@cornell.edu with plate and dates (since Oct 2024) | [C2C notice](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488) |
| B Lot, with a B commuter permit | The permit is valid in B Lot at all times; break-long stays are not addressed | [Student permits](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) |
| B Lot, temporary parking | Buy up to 14 days at a time | [C2C FAQ](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions) |
| City street | Ticket, tow and possible impound as abandoned | [Cornell winter rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) |
| City garage | $2.25 an hour, $12 daily maximum at Green and Cayuga, $18 at Dryden Road | [City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) |

Not confirmed: whether city garages or a B commuter permit allow a car to stay for the whole break, and what a tow and impound cost. Ask TDS at 607-255-4600 before you leave.

Example: a junior in Collegetown with a street-parked car flies home Dec 19 and returns Jan 12. That is 24 days on the street, past the 72-hour mark eight times over. By our math, 24 days at the $12 garage maximum would run $288, if a garage allows it. Riding C2C instead, the junior could leave the car in B Lot and email for free parking on the travel dates. A friend's driveway costs nothing.

## What a mistake costs

Cornell raised its fines on [Sept 1, 2023](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-08-01/citation-fines-increasing-september-1), the first increase since 2014. Permit and payment violations make up about 85% of tickets. ## Common mistakes

- **Leaving a car on a Collegetown street over break.** It can be ticketed, towed and impounded as abandoned. Three weeks away is far past the 72-hour line.
- **Parking in a non-designated lot on a snow night.** $50 for the winter-rules ticket, from Dec 1 to Apr 1.
- **Assuming a dry forecast means no rule.** The page links the rule to snow without defining it, so a wrong guess still costs $50.
- **Forgetting that breaks are paid weekdays.** Commuter lots charge Mon to Fri, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., including holidays and breaks.
- **Parking on the wrong side when odd/even is active.** A ticket, and a tow if the car sits.
- **Leaving the car in a fire lane or accessible space to dodge the plow.** $150 and an automatic tow; those rules are never suspended.

## FAQ

**Does a resident permit let me park anywhere on campus overnight in winter?** No. It covers your own area (FH, ND, SW or WD) plus A Lot after 2:30 p.m., per the [student permit page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). Elsewhere on a snow night, use a designated area.

**Can I park in the Hoy Road garage roof overnight?** No. The Hoy Road garage is a designated area on [all levels except the roof](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules).

**Do the winter rules apply over Thanksgiving?** Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Nov 26, before the campus rule starts on Dec 1. The city odd/even season has been running since [Nov 1](https://ecode360.com/8395630), so a street-parked car still needs moving when the city activates it.

**Who do I call about a winter ticket on campus?** Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services, 116 Maple Avenue, 607-255-4600, open Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to noon in person and by phone, per the [TDS short-term parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).

**Can I get a refund if I park off campus for spring?** Return the permit by Mar 31 for a [prorated refund](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students); there are no cash refunds.
