Winter in Ithaca is long, but students find plenty to do in it. Greek Peak, a ski area, hosts Cornell's skiing physical education course, the campus fitness centers sell day passes, and a Saturday winter market runs indoors. This page gathers what the sources behind it support, and says where to look for the rest.

## What is the snowy season like?

January averages 16.6 inches of snow and a mean temperature of 22.8F, and the year averages 62.9 inches ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)).

## Where can you ski or skate?

Skiing is the best-documented option. Greek Peak hosts Cornell's skiing physical education course and offered college discounts on Monday nights in 2023 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/01/winter-sports-organizations-aim-to-engage-cornellians-in-cold-weather-activities)). The same story covers student winter sports groups. Lift prices and this winter's discounts are not in the sources behind this page, so check Greek Peak's website before you go.

Skating has deep roots on campus. Beebe Lake hosted ice carnivals until Lynah Rink opened in 1957 ([Cornellians magazine](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/cold-comforts-of-a-big-red-winter)). Public skating hours at Lynah Rink are not in the sources, so ask Cornell Athletics or look at Campus Groups.

## What can you do indoors on campus?

Cornell Fitness Centers sell student memberships. For 2026-27, the academic-year membership is $180, a fall membership is $115 through Jan. 15, 2027, a half-fall membership is $60 and a day pass is $10. You buy in the FusionGO app ([Cornell Fitness Centers](https://scl.cornell.edu/recreation/fitness-centers/student-memberships-day-passes)). A spring-only price is not on the page; ask the front desk. Two semesters at the academic-year price work out to $90 each.

## Is there anything to do on a Saturday?

The Ithaca Farmers Market runs a Winter Market indoors at Triphammer Marketplace, 2255 North Triphammer Rd., on Saturdays from 10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. Last winter it ran Jan. 3 to March 28, 2026. The 2026-27 schedule was not posted on the market's page when this guide was updated, so confirm the dates there ([Ithaca Farmers Market](https://ithacamarket.com/winter-market)).

## What should you wear?

Wind chill is the number to watch. At 0F with a 15 mph wind, it feels like -19F, and exposed skin can freeze in 30 minutes ([National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-wind-chill-chart)). Cornell Health recommends layers, a water-resistant jacket and boots, and covering hands and ears ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)).

```reader "If you're an international student"
Cornell's international office says down coats are warmest, wool is next and leather is not warm enough. Mittens beat gloves, and insulated hiking boots with thick treaded rubber soles work best ([Cornell Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/student-resources)). If boots and a coat are a stretch, the Access Fund covers up to $100 for boots or $150 for a coat, hat and gloves, and free clothing closets exist at Catholic Charities of Tompkins/Tioga and the Women's Opportunity Center ([Graduate School tips](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-winter-clothing-resources)). Our [start page for international students](/start/international/) covers the rest of arrival.
```

## How do you get there without a car?

After first year, TCAT is free on weekday evenings and all weekend, which covers most outings ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). For a trip out of town, Ithaca Carshare's student plan costs $30 a year plus the hourly rate ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)); our [Carshare guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/) breaks down the plans. The [car-free guide for upper-years](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-upper-years-no-car/) covers more backups, and winter riding is in our [winter biking guide](/cornell/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/).
