```glance
- Cheapest seat: six carriers advertised NYC-Ithaca floors of $25 to $43 one way for the week of Nov 8, 2026; their average fares ran $45 to $82.
- Cornell's C2C: $93.50 one way with a NetID, $101 for anyone else, about three times the cheapest commercial seat.
- Pick by your NYC end: C2C boards on the Upper East Side and in East Midtown; OurBus, FlixBus and Greyhound board on the West Side.
- All four stop on North Campus, and the three commercial lines also stop on West Campus, so most students never need downtown.
- Since Nov 4, 2026 every C2C rider needs a new account; trips through Dec 23 and from Jan 6, 2027 book on separate links.
- Book Thanksgiving now: the Wednesday before it (Nov 25, 2026) is a peak day.
```

For most students the best bus from NYC to Cornell is OurBus, FlixBus or Greyhound: floor fares ran [$25 to $43 one way on Nov 8, 2026](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/), and all three stop on campus. Pay for Cornell's Campus-to-Campus bus ([$93.50 with a NetID, $101 without](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates)) when you start on the East Side, carry two big suitcases, or want a seat Cornell runs. Book any of them early for break weeks.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
You live on North Campus, and every operator stops there. C2C boards at [217 Jessup Road](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations); Greyhound and FlixBus at [121 Jessup Rd](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny). For C2C, sign in with Cornell single sign-on, or you pay $101 instead of $93.50. Thanksgiving break runs [Nov 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), and OurBus names that Wednesday a [peak day](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269), so book this week. The mistake that costs most: missing a C2C bus. Once it leaves its last stop, the [full fare is kept](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).
```

```reader "If you're an upper-year without a car"
You likely pay your own way now, so the yearly total matters. Four NYC round trips cost $748 on C2C at the NetID fare, against $264 at OurBus's "from $33" and $216 at FlixBus's $27 (our math from the posted and sampled fares below). That gap is $484 to $532 a year. C2C still wins if you live near Weill or Grand Central. The costly mistake is assuming the cheap seat will be there late: averages ran $45 to $82 on the [Nov 8 Wanderu sample](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/).
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Take the floor fare and book early. On Nov 8, 2026 floors were OurBus $25, Coach USA $28.98, Megabus $29, FlixBus $30, Greyhound $37 and Adirondack Trailways $43 ([Wanderu](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/)). Wanderu claims booking about 14 days ahead is cheapest and showed January averaging about $41 against about $66 in November. OurBus Select takes [$15 off each of your next 10 tickets](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269). Pack one bag: a second costs [$30 prepaid on OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university). Only buy what you will ride, since OurBus cancellations return [credit, not cash](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel).
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
You will likely land at JFK or Newark and ride up the same day. Leave a wide gap between landing and the bus. In a [2019 Cornell Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/), one student sprinted from a 2 p.m. JFK landing to a 4:30 p.m. bus, and another missed a campus bus by five minutes. A missed C2C bus forfeits the $93.50 fare; a missed OurBus leaves you with credit and a rescheduling fee. Bring a photo ID that matches the booking name, which [C2C requires](https://c2cbus.ipp.cornell.edu/Start.aspx), and keep spare clothes in your carry-on.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
You have no NetID, so C2C costs you [$101 one way or $202 round trip](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). It is [open to the public](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), and one reservation can hold several passengers. Children 2 and up need a paid seat; under-2s ride free on a lap, and [anyone 12 or under needs an adult](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). A family of three pays $303 each way on C2C (our math). The same three seats at a $33 OurBus floor cost $99, so price both before you book.
```

```reader "If you're a grad or professional student"
Travel for approved Cornell business, billed to a department, costs [$86 one way on C2C](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) instead of $93.50. No PCards are accepted. If your work is at Weill, C2C drops you at 1300 York Ave, the door you need. The mistake to avoid: a Weill CWID is not recognized by the booking system, so request a NetID from the IT Service Desk first. Personal trips get the regular $93.50 NetID fare.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or spring entrant"
A January arrival books C2C on a different link. Trips from Jan 6, 2027 use the ["2027 Travel Reservations" link](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service); the "2026" link covers only departures through Dec 23, 2026. No C2C trips are listed from Dec 24 to Jan 5. Spring term begins [Jan 25, 2027](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). January storms are the risk. In Jan 2019 the operators [cancelled all buses departing after noon Saturday](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/), and the governor banned buses on most highways. Arrive a few days early.
```

The commercial floors come from one [Wanderu reading on Nov 8, 2026](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/) for that week. Our math on those six carriers: the floors have a median of $29.50, and the averages a median of $63.18. Coach USA and Megabus had the lowest averages, about $45. Greyhound and FlixBus had the highest.

C2C prices do not move with demand. Each fare already includes a temporary [$6 fuel and toll surcharge](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) with no end date. Round trips cost exactly double; there is no round-trip discount. The NetID fare saves $7.50 a trip over the public fare.

How C2C compares, by our math: $93.50 is 2.83 times OurBus's "from $33" and 3.46 times FlixBus's $27 seat. Against a typical commercial average ($61.91, the mean of the six), C2C costs about 1.5 times as much. Travel times overlap, so neither side is clearly faster.

Ignore the $75 C2C fare that still ranks in search. That price is from a [2011 Cornell Chronicle story](https://news.cornell.edu/node/269896).

Example: a North Campus first-year going home to Midtown West for Thanksgiving. Two OurBus seats at the $33 floor cost $66. The same round trip on C2C is $187, so OurBus saves $121. If break-week floors rise by a third, as they did on three other Ithaca routes sampled Nov 8, the OurBus trip comes to about $88. That rise is an assumption for NYC; no NYC break-week fares were sampled.

## Where you get off at Cornell

Every operator now reaches campus, which saves a TCAT ride with luggage.

- **C2C** stops at North Campus (217 Jessup Road, shelters in front of Robert Purcell Community Center), Sage Hall (114 Feeney Way, across from Statler Hall) and B Lot (930 Campus Road, by the Vet College), [15 minutes apart](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule).
- **Greyhound and FlixBus** stop at North Campus (121 Jessup Rd, south side near Robert Purcell), West Campus (240 West Ave, in front of Mennen Hall) and downtown (131 E Green St, curbside in front of Urban Outfitters), per [Greyhound](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny).
- **OurBus** has run [4 to 6 campus-to-Manhattan trips a day, seven days a week, since Sept 1, 2026](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk), from the Commons, West Campus and North Campus. Before that, campus stops ran mostly around major breaks. OurBus says [most buses make two Cornell stops](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university), West Campus and North Campus; your ticket names the exact curb. From Mon Nov 9, 2026 its downtown stop moves from East Seneca St to [235 S. Cayuga St](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/), beside the Cayuga garage.

Not confirmed: whether Greyhound, FlixBus and Megabus also leave Green Street on Nov 9. Their pages still list 131 E Green St, so check your ticket. Also not confirmed: where Coach USA and Megabus board in NYC, and whether they stop on campus.

The old bus terminal at 710 W State St is closed. Cornell's families page still names it.

## Booking C2C after the Nov 4 switch

C2C moved to a [new browser-based booking system on Nov 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4). Schedules, rates and stops did not change. The steps:

1. Make a new account, even if you have ridden before. Sign in with Cornell single sign-on for the NetID fare; a standard account pays the public fare.
2. Use the right link. Departures through Dec 23, 2026, and changes to any existing booking, go through "2026 Travel Reservations." Departures from Jan 6, 2027 use ["2027 Travel Reservations"](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service).
3. Pick a seat. The new system adds seat selection and lets one booking hold several riders.
4. Check that day's times on the [live schedule](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule). It lists NYC departures from Weill at 5:45, 7:45, 9:45 and 11:45 a.m., then 1:45, 3:45 and 5:45 p.m., with Cornell Club pickups 45 minutes later. Not every slot runs every day.
5. Be at the stop 10 minutes early with a photo ID matching the booking.

| C2C fee or rule | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cancel 8 or more hours ahead | $10 per seat |
| Cancel inside 8 hours | $30 per seat |
| Change date or time inside 8 hours | $30 per seat per change |
| Change only the stop, same bus | Free |
| Miss the bus | Full fare kept; your return leg stays booked |
| Wheelchair access | Call 607-254-8747 at least 10 business days ahead |

Source: [C2C policies](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). You cancel only inside the reservation system, never by phone or email. If you leave a car at Cornell for the trip, email c2cbus@cornell.edu with your plate and dates for [free B Lot parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488).

If a C2C trip is full, the office says to check other times and that it may add trips when feasible.

## Luggage

Luggage is where C2C earns its price. C2C allows [2 suitcases below deck plus 2 small carry-ons](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) per passenger, with a coat closet for garment bags. A bike rides only in a bike box, counted as one suitcase.

OurBus includes [one suitcase up to 50 lb in the hold and one handbag](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university). Each extra bag is $30 and must be prepaid during booking. The Cornell Sun reported in 2024 that extra bags go only if space remains, so at peak move-in two large bags [effectively means two seats](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes). Greyhound includes one carry-on (16 by 12 by 7 inches, up to 25 lb) and one checked bag; its Flexible fare adds a second checked bag.

Example: a student moving back in January with two large suitcases. On OurBus that is a $33 floor seat plus $30 for the second bag, $63, if space remains. On C2C both bags are included in $93.50. The $30.50 gap is the price of a guaranteed spot for the second bag.

## When to book

Book break trips as soon as you know your dates. OurBus names three [peak days](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269): the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, the last day of December finals and the Friday spring break begins. For 2026-27 those fall around these dates, from the [academic calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar):

Thanksgiving break runs Nov 25 to 29, 2026, so the Sunday return (Nov 29) is crowded too. Fall finals run Dec 11 to 19, and spring break Mar 27 to Apr 4, 2027. Wanderu's cheapest November date was Nov 19, 2026.

Storms add a second reason to plan the return. In the Dec 1 to 2, 2019 storm, OurBus cancelled trips, one ShortLine bus slid off the road, and one student who rode C2C said that without it he [would not have reached campus until Wednesday](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/12/before-classes-were-cancelled-cornellians-attempted-to-return-to-ithaca-at-all-costs). If snow is forecast for your return, go a day early.

## What riders say

The Cornell Sun's [2025 student guide](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/busses-trains-and-planes-getting-to-and-from-cornell), by Melanie Delfosse, suggests comparing buses on Wanderu and staying calm when they run late. It also reports riders left at stations and long delays.

In the Sun's [fall 2023 OurBus feature](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel), Rena Weintraub '27 described an Ithaca-NYC bus that arrived more than 4 hours late, with Wi-Fi and air conditioning that were not working. Maia Mehring '27 saw a ticket holder turned away from an overbooked bus. The same feature praised how easy OurBus is to book and reschedule.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking C2C with a plain account.** You pay $101 instead of $93.50, $7.50 more each way.
- **Taking C2C out of habit.** It costs $60.50 to $66.50 more each way than the cheapest commercial seat, or $484 to $532 over four round trips (our math).
- **Missing the C2C bus.** The full fare is kept once it leaves the last stop, and your return stays booked; cancel it yourself or lose that fare too.
- **Cancelling C2C late.** Inside 8 hours the fee is $30 a seat instead of $10.
- **Bringing a second big bag on OurBus unpaid.** It costs $30 prepaid, and at peak it may not fit at all.
- **Waiting for Thanksgiving.** The Wednesday before (Nov 25, 2026) is a named peak day, and floors on other Ithaca routes ran about a third higher that week.
- **Going to the wrong downtown curb.** From Nov 9, 2026 OurBus boards at 235 S. Cayuga St, not East Seneca, and nobody boards at 710 W State St.

## FAQ

**Is C2C only for Cornell students?** No. It is [open to the public](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), including family and visitors, at $101 one way.

**Can I ride C2C to Boston or Philadelphia?** No. The [rates page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) lists only the Ithaca-NYC route.

**Does OurBus have a student discount?** It has a verification-based student discount with [no amount stated](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269), plus OurBus Select at $15 off each of 10 tickets.

**How many OurBus trips run from NYC each day?** Its [route page](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) showed 9 buses a day between Ithaca and NYC, the first at 5:00 a.m. and the last at 11:55 p.m.

**What if I arrive at Cornell late at night?** Plan the last mile before you board. OurBus's co-founder told the [Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk) that a 20-minute walk from downtown matters with luggage at early or late hours, so pick a campus stop when you can.
