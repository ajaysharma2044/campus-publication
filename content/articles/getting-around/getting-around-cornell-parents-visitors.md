```glance
- Fly into Ithaca (ITH) if you can: Delta flies from JFK and United from Washington Dulles, and the airport is about 10 minutes from downtown.
- From the airport, TCAT Route 32 (weekdays) or 72 (weekends) costs $1.50 an adult; children under 12 ride free with a paying adult.
- From New York City, the Cornell C2C bus is open to families at $101 one way per seat; every rider age 2 and up needs a paid seat.
- If you do drive onto campus, the Tang Welcome Center lot is $1.50 an hour or $10 a day through ParkMobile.
- Book hotels early for Family Weekend and Commencement (May 28 to 30, 2027); Cornell says they often sell out.
- Ignore three stale tips: the 710 W State St bus terminal, the Seneca Street garage and free Saturday parking are all gone.
```

You can do a Cornell visit without a car if you fly into Ithaca, which is [about 10 minutes from downtown](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/), and then use TCAT buses at [$1.50 a ride](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/) plus a prebooked taxi. From New York City, Cornell's Campus-to-Campus bus is [open to family and visitors](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4) at [$101 one way](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Book your hotel first, because Cornell's families page says they [often sell out for Family Weekend and Commencement](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation).

```reader "If you're flying in for move-in"
Your student's Move-In Ticket prints from the Housing and Dining Portal [after August 1](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus). It shows the parking area. Arriving by Uber or taxi, go to A Lot; arriving by bus, go straight to the building. The timeslot covers check-in only, not unloading. In the 2026 cycle, staff unloading help ended at 8 p.m. Monday, 11 a.m. Tuesday and 5 p.m. Friday and Saturday. The costly mistake is landing late on a Tuesday and hauling every box yourself. Book the ITH flight that lands the day before, and prebook a cab: [Ithaca Airport Cab is 607-252-9052](https://flyithaca.com/taxis).
```

```reader "If you're coming from New York City"
Take the C2C if you want a Cornell-run coach that drops you on campus. One reservation can [hold several passengers](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), and everyone needs a new account in the system that launched November 4, 2026, past riders included. Each seat is $101 one way or $202 round trip. Your student's $93.50 NetID fare does not cover you. Be at the stop [10 minutes early](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). Commercial buses cost less: OurBus advertises [from $33](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) and Greyhound [from $43.98](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny).
```

```reader "If you're visiting for Family Weekend or Commencement"
Book the room the day you know the date. Cornell's families page says hotels [often sell out](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) for both weekends. Family Weekend 2026 ran [September 25 to 27](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/family-events/family-weekend), and Cornell says the 2027 dates will be announced in early 2027. In 2026, registration opened [August 3](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/family-events/family-weekend/family-weekend-faq), and Cornell suggested arriving Thursday evening or Friday morning. Commencement weekend 2027 is [May 28 to 30](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). Visit Ithaca says a car is [not necessary for a family weekend](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/), but Uber and Lyft are limited outside downtown. A hotel downtown or near campus keeps you within TCAT range. A hotel out by a highway exit leaves you paying for a taxi every trip.
```

```reader "If you're an alum back for Reunion"
Reunion 2027 runs [June 10 to 13](https://alumni.cornell.edu/come-back/reunion/). Between events, TCAT costs $1.50 a ride, and the Blue Light bus covers late nights. The C2C bus leaves Manhattan from the Cornell Club at 6 East 44th Street. Your student fare ended with graduation, so expect the [$101 public fare](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Check the Reunion pages for your class's schedule before you book.
```

```reader "If you're traveling with young children"
On C2C, a child under 2 rides free [on a lap](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). Age 2 and up pays the full $101, with no child discount. A stroller or backpack that takes a seat counts as a passenger. Anyone 12 or under travels with an adult. On TCAT, children under 12 ride [free with a paying adult](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). A family of two adults and two kids under 12 pays $3 a trip. The costly mistake is booking C2C seats for the adults only and finding a 3-year-old has no seat.
```

## Which airport to fly into

Ithaca's own airport is the easy choice when the fares work. It has two scheduled airlines: [Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). That means every connection runs through one of those two hubs.

Syracuse has more choice, [nonstop service to 27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/). It is an hour or more away, and Cornell's families page says flights into [Syracuse, Elmira or Binghamton need a rental car or a bus](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). Without a car, the bus is OurBus. It runs from SYR Terminal A to campus in [1 hour 25 minutes to 2 hours 5 minutes](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny), with most fares $46.70 to $62.10 and Sunday the cheapest day.

Elmira is about [36 miles away](https://www.airport.guide/kelm/ny/tompkins-co/ithaca). Chemung County's C TRAN runs Route 30, a commuter bus from Elmira to Ithaca, at [$6 for the full trip](https://ridectran.com/en/fares). It starts at the Elmira Transit Center and [does not stop at the airport](https://ridectran.com/wp-content/uploads/CTRAN-Schedule30-Final-PRD2.pdf), which sits at [276 Sing Sing Road in Horseheads](https://www.chemungcountyny.gov/850/Elmira-Corning-Regional-Airport). Fly there only if you plan to rent a car.

| Airport | Flights | Getting to campus without a car | Cost each way |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ithaca (ITH) | Delta to JFK, United to Dulles | TCAT 32 or 72 to the Commons, then a campus bus; or a taxi | $1.50 a bus ride per adult; taxi by quote |
| Syracuse (SYR) | 27 nonstop destinations | OurBus, Terminal A to North Campus, West Campus or the Cayuga St Garage | $46.70 to $62.10 a seat |
| Elmira (ELM) | [Allegiant and Delta](https://www.chemungcountyny.gov/850/Elmira-Corning-Regional-Airport) | Rental car; the C TRAN Route 30 bus leaves from Elmira, not the terminal | Car or taxi; C TRAN $6 from Elmira |
| NYC airports | Everything | Bus to Manhattan, then C2C or a commercial bus | $101 C2C; OurBus from $33 |

Our advice: for a fall or spring weekend, fly ITH when the fare gap is small. With two airlines, a cancelled flight at ITH leaves few rebooking options, and that matters most in winter. From December to March, price Syracuse too, and compare the full door-to-door cost.

## From the Ithaca airport to campus

TCAT Route 32 runs on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends, both [between the airport and Ithaca Commons](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation). Pay the driver with exact cash, a credit or debit card, a digital wallet, a Tcard or the TFare app. From the Commons, Route 10 is the [downtown and campus route](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting).

Buses do not meet every flight. The terminal is open 3:30 a.m. to midnight, and taxis run [24 hours a day](https://flyithaca.com/taxis). The airport tells travelers to prebook a cab for late flights. Three companies are listed:

- Ithaca Airport Cab, 607-252-9052 (all payment types, no card fee)
- Collegetown Cab, 607-588-8888 (online booking and an app)
- Ithaca Dispatch, 607-277-7777

Ithaca Airport Cab also takes texts, and Collegetown Cab's website and app give quotes. Ask for the airport-to-campus price and say how many bags you have.

## Taking the C2C bus from New York City

The Campus-to-Campus bus is Cornell's own coach between Ithaca and its New York City campuses. It is [open to the public](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), family and visitors included. The new booking system adds seat selection and lets one reservation hold several passengers.

**Stops.** In Manhattan, the bus leaves from Weill Cornell Medicine at 1300 York Avenue and from the Cornell Club at 6 East 44th Street, [per Cornell's stop list](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations). In Ithaca, it stops at North Campus (217 Jessup Road), Sage Hall (114 Feeney Way) and B Lot. The ride takes about 4.5 to 5 hours.

**Rules that catch families.** These come from the [C2C policies page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations):

- Each passenger gets 2 suitcases below deck plus 2 small carry-ons.
- Cancelling costs $10 per seat at least 8 hours ahead and $30 per seat inside 8 hours. You cancel only in the reservation system, not by phone or email.
- Be at the stop 10 minutes early. Once the bus leaves its last stop, the fare is gone.

Example: two parents and a 3-year-old ride from the Cornell Club to Sage Hall for Family Weekend. Three paid seats at $101 is $303 one way, or $606 round trip. If they also bring a 1-year-old on a lap, that child rides free. A booking change inside 8 hours of departure would add $30 per seat.

The C2C is not the cheapest way up. OurBus, Greyhound and FlixBus all run between Manhattan and Ithaca for less, and they drop you on campus too. Greyhound and FlixBus stop downtown at [131 E. Green Street](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny) and on campus at 121 Jessup Road and 240 West Avenue. From November 9, 2026, OurBus's downtown stop is [235 S. Cayuga Street](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/), beside the South Cayuga garage.

## Getting around Ithaca once you're here

TCAT is the city bus, and you pay the public fare: your student's Cornell ID does not cover you. The [fares by age](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/):

| Rider | TCAT fare per ride |
|---|---|
| Adult | $1.50 |
| Senior 60 and over, or a rider with a disability | $0.75 (tell the driver before you tap) |
| Child under 12 with a paying adult | Free |

Pay with exact cash, a contactless card or phone, the TFare app or a T-card. The TFare QR code expires after a minute, so open it at the door.

At night, the Blue Light bus runs on Route 92 [from 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation).

For door-to-door rides, Visit Ithaca says Uber and Lyft are [limited outside downtown](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/). Save a taxi number before dinner, not after.

Example: two parents and a 10-year-old stay downtown for a Saturday. They ride to campus and back, then to dinner and back: four rides. At $1.50 per adult and free for the child, that is $3 a ride and $12 for the day.

## If you rent a car anyway: where to park

**On campus.** The visitor lot is the Tang Welcome Center, ParkMobile Zone 4119, at [$1.50 an hour or $10 a day](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Its pay station has been out of service since September 8, 2026, so pay through the ParkMobile app or text PARK to 77223. Each transaction adds a $0.45 fee. Most campus lots charge Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Residence-hall areas need a permit or payment around the clock. Parking without a permit or payment costs [$50](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-08-01/citation-fines-increasing-september-1), and an accessible space or fire lane costs $150 plus an automatic tow. For Family Weekend 2026, Cornell said it would [make certain lots free](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/family-events/family-weekend/family-weekend-faq) and email parking details to registrants in September.

**Downtown.** City garages charge [$2.25 an hour](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328). The daily maximum is $12 at Green Street and Cayuga Street and $18 at Dryden Road in Collegetown. Street meters with pay stations charge $2.50 an hour. The Cayuga Street Garage at 245 S. Cayuga Street has [more than 300 spaces](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/), about 5 minutes' walk from the Commons.

## Move-in and move-out

**Move-in.** North Campus move-in in 2026 ran [Monday, August 17, Tuesday, August 18, and Friday to Saturday, August 21 to 22](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus). After unloading, cars move to 3-hour short-term parking in the area on the Move-In Ticket. The Jessup Road north-side unloading spaces allow no overnight parking. Families arriving without a car can still use the help: go to A Lot by Uber or taxi, and arrive well before staff unloading help ends that day. Ignore the "August 2021 Arrival Instructions" page that still turns up in search, and watch the current move-in pages for the 2027 dates.

**Move-out.** In spring 2026, residence halls closed at [2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), and anything left in a room after that was donated. Spring 2027 finals run [May 15 to 22](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). A parent flying in without a car cannot drive a room's worth of things home. Cornell points students to Big Red Shipping and Storage, its contracted vendor, which offers [storage and shipping with free in-room pickup](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options). Slots are limited, so book before finals.

## Stale advice to ignore

Three things you may read elsewhere are out of date:

- **The bus terminal at 710 W State St.** It is [closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_Bus_Station), though Cornell's families page still names it. Greyhound and FlixBus board on Green Street, and OurBus on South Cayuga Street from November 9, 2026.
- **The Seneca Street garage.** It has been [closed since spring 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/). Use Green Street or Cayuga Street.
- **Free Saturday parking downtown.** It [ended with the 2026 city budget](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2025/11/12/free-saturday-parking-to-end-in-ithaca-in-2026/); Sunday is still free. Visit Ithaca's May 2026 family guide still says the garages cost about $2 an hour and are free on weekends. The city's own notice wins.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking the hotel last.** Cornell says hotels often sell out for Family Weekend and Commencement. A room out of town turns every outing into a taxi fare.
- **Flying into Elmira expecting an airport shuttle.** The C TRAN bus to Ithaca leaves from Elmira, not the terminal, and the drive is about 36 miles. Reserve the rental car with the flight.
- **Booking C2C seats for adults only.** Every child age 2 and up needs a $101 seat, and a stroller that takes a seat counts as a passenger.
- **Counting on Uber at night.** It is limited outside downtown. Prebook a taxi for a late flight or a late dinner.
- **Parking at the Tang Welcome Center without ParkMobile.** The pay station is out of service, and an unpaid space costs $50.
- **Sending your student to 710 W State St.** That terminal is closed, and the bus leaves from Green Street, South Cayuga Street or campus instead.

## FAQ

**Can I ride the C2C if I am not a Cornell student or employee?** Yes. It is open to the public at $101 one way, and you book a standard account in the [new reservation system](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4).

**Can my student book my C2C seat on their account?** One reservation can hold several passengers, but the NetID fare of $93.50 is for students, faculty and staff.

**Is there a rental car counter at the Ithaca airport?** Yes. The airport's car-rental page lists [Avis, Budget and Hertz](https://flyithaca.com/car-rentals). Avis and Budget share a local office at 607-257-0441, open 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Saturdays and 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sundays; Hertz is 607-257-8677, open 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Saturdays and 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Sundays. For a late flight, the page says to sign up for a Fastbreak or Preferred membership first so you can pick up the car after hours. The airport [recommends renting](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith) for a longer stay or a group.

**Can I bring a wheelchair on the C2C?** Yes, with notice. Call 607-254-8747 at least 2 weeks, or 10 business days, [before the trip](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-additional-information).

**Does my student ride TCAT free?** First-years ride free at all hours with a Cornell ID [until August 31](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). After the first year, rides are free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend.
