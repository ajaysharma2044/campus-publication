Yes. If you live on campus and are returning to campus housing for spring, apply for [winter break housing between Oct 1 and Dec 1](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing): it costs $125 per one-week option or $500 for the full break, in one of ten open buildings. Everyone else must be out by [2 p.m. on Sunday, Dec 20, 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice), the day meal plans stop, so plan to cook and to ride TCAT in its free hours.

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over a break"
Apply in the Housing and Dining Portal by Dec 1; nothing on Cornell's pages says late applications are taken. Cornell offers two one-week options, one right after fall term ends and one right before spring term starts, so pick the $500 full break if you will stay for the stretch in between. Order anything you need shipped to arrive before Dec 23, when campus mail and packages stop until Jan 7. Stock up before brunch on Dec 20, the last meal-plan meal until Jan 19. The costliest mistake is assuming TCAT runs normally on holidays: it does not run on Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's Day.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Thanksgiving ([Nov 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)) is too short for most trips abroad, and winter break may be too. Apply for break housing by Dec 1; it is $125 a week or $500 for the whole break. If you leave for part of it, Cornell's [C2C bus to NYC](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service) makes its last trip on Dec 23 and restarts on Jan 6, on separate booking links. Want a car for groceries? Ithaca Carshare [does not accept an International Driving Permit](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) as a driving record and cannot approve drivers from some countries. Ask in November, not in the first week of break.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
For winter break, compare $500 for the full break with a round trip home. On Nov 8, 2026 the cheapest [Ithaca to NYC bus seats](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/) started at $25 to $43 one way across six carriers. If home is near NYC, going costs less than a month of housing plus your own groceries. If you stay, ride TCAT in the [free hours](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) (weekdays after 6 p.m. and all weekend) instead of paying $1.50 a daytime ride. A Carshare Just In Case plan is [$30 a year for students](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters) plus $9 an hour.
```

```reader "If you're far from home and fly"
When home takes a full day each way, a five-day Thanksgiving is often not worth the flight, and some students [stay in Ithaca](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/16/students-share-upcoming-thanksgiving-break-plans). If you stay, Thanksgiving Day has [no TCAT service](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/07/service-changes-for-july-4-holiday-weekend-tcat-buses-curbside-recycling-city-offices-will-be-closed-on-independence-day). For winter, decide by Dec 1, before you know your December flight will hold. If your flight leaves after the [2 p.m. close on Dec 20](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice), request a later departure in the portal by Dec 7. Staying without one costs $60 an hour.
```

## Who can stay over winter break and what it costs

Winter break housing is [open to students returning to on-campus housing for spring](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing). Applications open Oct 1 and close Dec 1. The fee is flat: $125 per one-week option, or $500 for the full break.

For 2026-27, the open buildings are Ganędagǫ: Hall, High Rise 5, HILC/Low Rise 8, Hu Shih, Loving House, Mews, Toni Morrison, Townhouse Apartments, Cascadilla and Veterans House, per the [same page](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing).

The two one-week options are one week after the fall semester ends and one week before the spring semester starts; both together cost $250 (our arithmetic). The residences are closed for 30 days, from Dec 20 to Jan 19, so anyone staying the whole time should take the $500 full-break rate. Students not living in an open building can apply only for the full break.

The [winter closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice) sets the rules for everyone else. All undergraduate residences close at 2 p.m. ET on Sunday, Dec 20, 2026. Only residents with prior approval for winter break housing may enter residential buildings. Staying late costs $60 per hour or fraction of an hour, with no proration. To leave later for a reason such as a flight, submit a request through the Housing and Dining Portal by Monday, Dec 7.

Off-campus renters need no winter housing application, but the city's street parking rules below still apply to them.

Not confirmed: whether approved students stay in their own room or move into one of the ten open buildings. Ask your Residence Hall Director or House Assistant Dean, whom the closing notice names as the contacts.

## Food when the dining halls close

Meal plans [end with brunch on Dec 20, 2026 and resume with lunch on Jan 19, 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). The winter housing page adds that [dining is usually closed the week the university is closed](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing). Plan on buying and cooking your own food for a month.

Not confirmed: whether any dining hall serves approved winter residents, and Cornell Dining's Thanksgiving 2026 hours.

Thanksgiving has worked differently. In 2022, one student told the [Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/16/students-share-upcoming-thanksgiving-break-plans) about staying in Ithaca for the dining hall's Thanksgiving meal because home was far. In 2013, a [Sun writer](https://cornellsun.com/2013/12/01/oh-thanksgiving-in-ithaca-stranded-and-starved) described closed dining and no TCAT on the holiday. Check this year's dining hours before you count on a meal.

## Getting around while you're here

Your Cornell ID is your bus pass. First-years, including transfers, ride TCAT free at all hours until Aug 31. After first year, rides are [free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes); a weekday daytime ride is $1.50. PhD students and graduate assistants get a free OmniRide pass.

TCAT does not run every day. A 2025 [Cornell Sun notice](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/07/service-changes-for-july-4-holiday-weekend-tcat-buses-curbside-recycling-city-offices-will-be-closed-on-independence-day), following a TCAT press release, lists six days with no service: Independence Day, New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Thanksgiving and Christmas. Over break that means Nov 26 and Dec 25, 2026 and Jan 1, 2027.

Not confirmed: TCAT's reduced schedules on the other days of Thanksgiving week and winter break. TCAT posts them on its own site; check before you plan a trip.

### Ithaca Carshare for grocery runs

Cornell subsidizes [student memberships](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters): Just In Case is $30 a year and It's My Car is $310 a year, each $45 off the community price. Monthly plans get no discount. You also pay a one-time, non-refundable $20 per driver. On the [rates page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates), Just In Case driving costs $9 an hour and It's My Car $6, with mileage at 42 cents for a car. The bill adds 8% New York sales tax and a 12% rental tax.

Example: an upper-year staying the whole winter break takes two grocery trips a week, each two hours and 10 miles. One trip is $18 in time plus $4.20 in mileage, about $26.64 with both taxes if they apply to the full charge. Eight trips over four weeks run about $213, plus the $30 plan and the $20 sign-up. Route 30 to the Ithaca Mall on a Saturday costs nothing.

Join before break. The [eligibility rules](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) require age 18 or older and a full license held two or more years. Drivers under 21 need their own insurance with at least $300,000 per accident in liability. Foreign licenses need an official driving record, and an International Driving Permit does not count.

## Staying for Thanksgiving

Thanksgiving break is five days, Nov 25 to 29. The winter break housing page does not mention Thanksgiving, and no Thanksgiving housing application appears there.

Not confirmed: whether every residence hall stays open over Thanksgiving 2026. Ask your Residence Hall Director before you plan to stay.

The hard day is Thursday, Nov 26. TCAT runs no buses, so plan any trip for Wednesday or Friday, or keep a taxi number. In 2022, students told the [Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/16/students-share-upcoming-thanksgiving-break-plans) about other ways to spend it: one stayed for the dining hall meal, and another spent the holiday with a friend's family in Cleveland because home was a full day away. The 2013 writer wrote that Thanksgiving airfares run two to three times normal, and that family income decides who goes home.

Example: a low-budget sophomore whose family lives near NYC weighs Thanksgiving. The cheapest Ithaca to NYC seats [started at $25 to $43 one way on Nov 8, 2026](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/), before Thanksgiving-week demand. A round trip at those floors is $50 to $86. Staying costs no housing fee, but it means buying food and getting through a day with no buses.

## If you keep a car here

Students in university housing [may leave a car in their permit area](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) over break; the policy applies year-round. From Dec 1 to Apr 1, when snow is falling or forecast, overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. is limited to designated areas. FH, ND, SW and WD resident permit holders park in their own lots as usual.

Never leave a car on a city street over winter break. The same Cornell page warns it may be treated as abandoned, ticketed, towed and possibly impounded. The city's odd/even street rule runs Nov 1 to Apr 1 when snow removal is needed.

## Common mistakes

- **Missing the Dec 1 deadline.** Cornell's page gives no late option, and without approval you must be out by 2 p.m. Dec 20 or pay $60 an hour.
- **Expecting a package over break.** Campus mail and packages stop Dec 23 and resume Jan 7, so anything shipped in those 15 days waits until January.
- **Planning on dining halls.** Meal plans stop after brunch Dec 20 and restart at lunch Jan 19, a month of buying your own food.
- **Counting on a bus on Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's Day.** TCAT runs none, and a taxi is the only fallback.
- **Paying $1.50 a ride when you could wait.** After first year, a Saturday or evening trip is free; four weekday daytime round trips cost $12.
- **Leaving your car on a Collegetown or downtown street.** It can be towed and treated as abandoned.
- **Joining Carshare on the day you need it.** Approval needs a driving record, and drivers under 21 need their own insurance policy first.

## FAQ

**Can I stay in my room over winter break if I live off campus next semester?** No. Winter break housing is [only for students returning to on-campus housing for spring](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing).

**Who do I ask about my winter housing?** The [closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice) sends approved residents to their Residence Hall Director or House Assistant Dean.

**Can I leave for part of winter break and come back?** Partly. The $125 one-week options cover only the week right after fall term or the week right before spring term; the full break is $500. Cornell's C2C bus to NYC runs to [Dec 23 and resumes Jan 6](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service), with separate booking links for each side of the gap.

**Is TCAT still free for first-years over break?** Yes. Fall entrants' privileges run [to Aug 31 of the next year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so break weeks are covered on every day TCAT runs.

**What if my flight out is late on Dec 20?** Request a later departure in the Housing and Dining Portal [by Dec 7](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice), giving your name, room number and reason.
