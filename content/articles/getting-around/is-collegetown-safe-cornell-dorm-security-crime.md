Cornell and the City of Ithaca publish fire and building safety data for rentals, and a free late-night bus runs until [2:30 a.m.](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) The sources behind this guide contain no crime statistics for Collegetown, so judge a building by its safety record, its locks and your route home.

```glance
- Cornell's online safety tool shows sprinklers, alarms and extinguishers for City of Ithaca rentals.
- The free Blue Light bus on Route 92 runs 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. every night.
- Campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024, so lock bikes with a U-lock.
- Newer buildings add keyless entry, card access and cameras; ask what yours has.
```

## How do I check how safe a building is?

Use the online tool at safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu, which Cornell Off-Campus Living and the City Building Division run. It shows fire protection such as sprinklers, extinguishers, and smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, plus building type and use, for City of Ithaca rentals, and it updates daily ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)). It covers City rentals only. It does not measure landlord quality or neighborhood safety.

Cornell launched 1-to-5 star fire and building safety ratings in September 2017, built from public Building Division data ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/289146)). The current page shows no star scale, so read the listed features instead.

Every City rental must pass a Certificate of Compliance inspection, which covers structure, fire protection, exits, electrical work and fire walls ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)). To check a building's status, call the City Building Division at 607-274-6508.

## How do I get home safely at night?

Ride Route 92, the Blue Light bus, which runs every night from 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round ([Cornell Family Resource Hub](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation)). Students beyond first year ride TCAT free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free)), so the late ride costs nothing. The [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) has hours and routes.

Walking from central campus to Collegetown takes 15 to 20 minutes through the Arts Quad, according to [The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/10/embrace-your-inner-intellectual-go-on-a-midnight-walk). After 2:30 a.m., book a taxi or a ride; the [Uber, Lyft and taxi guide](/cornell/getting-around/uber-lyft-taxi-ithaca-cornell/) lists the options.

## What security features do Collegetown buildings have?

They vary, so ask. Collegetown Center at 151 Dryden Rd. has a concierge desk that staffs the building from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=461)). Collegetown Crossing at 307 College Ave. lists 24 security cameras ([Urban Ithaca](https://www.urbanithaca.com/)). Cornell's 301 College Avenue installed card access and new locks before it opened in fall 2026, and early key problems were later resolved ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)).

In a rental house, a landlord generally cannot require a duplicate key unless the lease reserves one, and a tenant cannot change locks without consent ([Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access)). Ask about locks before you sign.

## Is bike and property theft a problem?

Yes for bikes. Campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts worth at least $41,177 in fall 2024, with one arrest and four bikes recovered intact ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far)). Cornell advises registering your bike and using a U-lock. Cornell also points students to GradGuard renters insurance, which covers theft, fire and vandalism ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus)).

## Who do I call in an emergency?

Call 911 in an alcohol or drug emergency. Cornell's Good Samaritan Protocol protects the caller, according to [Cornell's off-campus living page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus). Cornell Health's 24/7 advice line is 607-255-5155 for health questions.

## Where can I find crime statistics?

The sources behind this guide do not include Collegetown crime counts or campus security reports. Ask Cornell's Off-Campus Living office where to find current safety reports, and compare what you learn with the building's own features.

## What to do next

1. Look up your building in the safety tool.
2. Ask the landlord about locks, entry and cameras in writing.
3. Plan your late-night route and save the Route 92 hours.
4. Register your bike and buy a U-lock.
