Collegetown's closest paid parking is the city's Dryden Road Garage: $2.25 an hour, an $18 daily maximum or $232 a month ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)). Downtown's Green Street and Cayuga Street garages charge the same rate, capped at $12. Street spots cost $2.50 an hour. A $45 city permit covers some residential blocks; our [residential permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-residential-parking-permit-streets-how-to-apply/) shows how to apply.

```glance
- Dryden Road Garage is open 24/7 and caps a day at $18; downtown garages cap it at $12.
- Street meters run 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Monday to Saturday. Sunday is free.
- The city permit is for residents. You apply online or by mail; your landlord cannot.
- From Nov. 1, the city can turn on odd/even overnight parking.
```

## How much does parking cost in Collegetown?

Collegetown has three kinds of paid parking: a city garage, city street spaces and Cornell lots. The city raised its rates on Jan. 5, 2026 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328)). Before that, garages and streets both cost $1.50 an hour and were free from 6 p.m. Friday to 8 a.m. Monday ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2025/11/free-saturday-parking-in-ithaca-poised-to-end/)). Council approved Saturday fees on Nov. 5, 2025. Older guides that quote the old rate are out of date.

| Where | Hourly rate | Daily maximum | Longer stays | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dryden Road Garage | $2.25 | $18 | $71 a week, $232 a month | Collegetown; 24/7; 210 spaces |
| Green Street Garage | $2.25 | $12 | $39 a week, $117 a month | Downtown; 120 E. Green St. |
| Cayuga Street Garage | $2.25 | $12 | $108 a month standard | Downtown; 245 S. Cayuga St.; run by Premium Parking |
| Seneca Street Garage | Closed |  |  | Closed April 10, 2026 |
| Street ParkMobile and pay-station zones | $2.50 |  |  | Enforced 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Monday to Saturday |
| Coin meters farther out | $1.50 |  |  | Rate unchanged "for now" |

Source: City of Ithaca [rate notice](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) and garage pages. The Cayuga monthly rate is from the city's [2026 budget proposal](https://cityofithaca.org/DocumentCenter/View/20272/2026-Final-Budget-Proposal-20251107?bidId=), so confirm it with Premium Parking.

## Which garage works for Collegetown or downtown?

Use the [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage), at 118 Dryden Road. The city recommends its monthly permit for Collegetown residents. Monthly permits come in 30-day blocks with no guaranteed space, and a Worker's Special costs $185 a month. In winter, from November to April, the uncovered 4th and 5th floors are closed from 2 a.m. to 8 a.m., and cars must move every 24 hours. A merchants' survey reported by the [Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/278473) found about a third of the ramp's 210 spaces empty in the daytime. The Chronicle reported this in October 1998.

Example: a renter keeps a car at Dryden Road for nine months. At the monthly rate ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)) that is $2,088. The daily maximum for a 30-day month would be $540, so the monthly permit wins after 13 days.

Downtown, use Cayuga Street or Green Street. The Green Street Garage is [free on Sundays unless an event is on](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/716/Green-Street-Garage), its special events rate is $5 per entry, and cars there must also move every 24 hours. The Seneca Street Garage [closed on April 10, 2026](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/05/seneca-street-parking-garage-closed-for-good-officials-weigh-next-steps/) after debris fell, and the city made the closure permanent in May. Do not send anyone there. On Sept. 2, 2026, Common Council authorized the Ithaca Urban Renewal Agency to begin seeking redevelopment proposals for the site, with a taxable residential project as the goal ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/09/05/ithaca-advances-seneca-street-garage-redevelopment-public-safety-facility-alternatives-amid-budget-pressure/)).

## How do I get the city's $45 residential permit?

Residents of permit blocks apply to the City Clerk online or by mail, with their vehicle registration and proof of residency, such as a lease signed by both tenant and landlord ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/711/Residential-Parking-Permits)). A landlord cannot get the permit for a tenant, and every permit expires July 31, with no partial-year price ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)). The system left out central Collegetown when it began in 1998 ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/278473)), so confirm your block with the Clerk first; the [residential permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-residential-parking-permit-streets-how-to-apply/) has each step.

## Can I park on campus if I live in Collegetown?

Yes, but not with a resident permit. Cornell's [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) lists what is left, and our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) covers each option in full.

| Option | Cost | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| B commuter permit | $359.77 a year | B Lot always; A Lot after 2:30 p.m. |
| Free A or E permit | Free with a full assistantship on the Ithaca campus | Outer Tier lots; E needs work in its zone |
| Daily Decision | As little as $1 a day in select zones | Outer lots, first come |
| ParkMobile by the hour | $0.45 fee per transaction, plus the lot rate | Most lots |

B Lot, at [930 Campus Rd.](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule) by the Vet College, runs a [24-hour parking day](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Riders of Cornell's C2C bus can [buy temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions). Commuter lots charge Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., including breaks ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)). In 2023, permits for the Williams Street lot at Stewart Avenue, about $700, [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students).

## What are the rules for parking overnight in Collegetown?

Your choices, in order of certainty, are your lease spot, the Dryden Road Garage, then a city street.

On streets, the city's [odd/even rule](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations) runs from Nov. 1 to April 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., and the city turns it on only when needed. Park on the odd side on odd dates and the even side on even dates. A car that sits 72 hours counts as abandoned and can be towed. The city also has a 24-hour move rule Monday to Friday. Text SIREN to 67283 for alerts. The odd/even ticket is $15, then $30 and $45 for repeats ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). The 2027 city budget plans higher fines, worth about $381,000 a year ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/11/common-council-approves-2027-budget-city-will-remain-below-tax-cap/)). Our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) has the break rules.

For a stay of days rather than a night, the city says its garages offer no long-term parking and refers drivers who need it to Premium Parking on Cayuga Street, across from the Hotel Ithaca, at (607) 256-7275 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). Premium Parking is the operator the city's [rate notice](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) names for the Cayuga Street Garage.

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Sending visitors to the Seneca Street Garage.** It is closed for good.
- **Counting on a free Saturday.** Saturdays are paid since 2026.
- **Asking the landlord for the city permit.** The Clerk will not issue it to one.
- **Leaving a car on a street over break.** It can be towed as abandoned.
- **Paying for a B permit with a full assistantship.** Free A and E permits come with it.

## FAQ

**Is parking free on Sundays in Ithaca?** Street meters are enforced [Monday to Saturday](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/705/Parking-Information), so Sunday is free on the street. Among the garages, Green Street is free on Sundays except on event days. At Dryden Road and Cayuga Street, check the sign at the entrance before you leave the car.

**Where does a friend park when visiting?** If your block is in the permit system, the city sells a [$10 visitor pass](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance). Otherwise use the Dryden Road Garage.

**What happens if I park at Cornell without paying?** Our [parking tickets guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-parking-tickets-fines-how-to-appeal-cornell/) lists Cornell's fines and how to appeal.
