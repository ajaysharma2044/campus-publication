Collegetown's closest paid parking is the city's Dryden Road Garage, at $2.25 an hour with an $18 daily maximum or $232 a month ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)). Street spots cost $2.50 an hour. A $45 city permit covers some residential blocks.

```glance
- Dryden Road Garage is open 24/7 and caps a day at $18.
- Street meters run 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Monday to Saturday. Sunday is free.
- The city permit is for residents. You apply in person; your landlord cannot.
- From Nov. 1, the city can turn on odd/even overnight parking.
```

## How much does parking cost in Collegetown?

Collegetown has three kinds of paid parking: a city garage, city street spaces and Cornell lots. The city raised its rates on Jan. 5, 2026 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328)). Before that, garages and streets both cost $1.50 an hour and were free from 6 p.m. Friday to 8 a.m. Monday. Council voted on Nov. 5, 2025 to end free Saturdays ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2025/11/free-saturday-parking-in-ithaca-poised-to-end/)). Older guides that quote the old rate are out of date.

| Where | Hourly rate | Daily maximum | Longer stays | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dryden Road Garage | $2.25 | $18 | $71 a week, $232 a month | Collegetown; 24/7; 210 spaces |
| Green Street Garage | $2.25 | $12 | $39 a week, $117 a month | Downtown; 120 E. Green St. |
| Cayuga Street Garage | $2.25 | $12 | $108 a month standard | Downtown; 245 S. Cayuga St.; run by Premium Parking |
| Seneca Street Garage | Closed | n/a | n/a | Closed April 10, 2026 |
| Street ParkMobile and pay-station zones | $2.50 | None stated | None | Enforced 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Monday to Saturday |
| Coin meters farther out | $1.50 | None stated | None | Rate unchanged "for now" |

Source: City of Ithaca [rate notice](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) and garage pages. The Cayuga monthly rate is from the city's 2026 budget proposal, so confirm it with Premium Parking.

## Which garage works for Collegetown?

Use the Dryden Road Garage, at 118 Dryden Road. The city recommends its monthly permit for Collegetown residents. Monthly permits come in 30-day blocks with no guaranteed space, and a Worker's Special costs $185 a month. In winter, from November to April, the uncovered 4th and 5th floors are closed from 2 a.m. to 8 a.m., and cars must move every 24 hours. A Cornell survey of merchants found about a third of the ramp's 210 spaces empty in the daytime ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/278473)).

Example: a renter keeps a car at Dryden Road for nine months. At the monthly rate ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)) that is $2,088. The daily maximum for a 30-day month would be $540, so the monthly permit wins after 13 days.

Downtown, use Cayuga Street or Green Street. The Green Street Garage is [free on Sundays unless an event is on](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/716/Green-Street-Garage). The Seneca Street Garage at 202 E. Seneca St. [closed on April 10, 2026](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/05/seneca-street-parking-garage-closed-for-good-officials-weigh-next-steps/) after debris fell, and the city made the closure permanent in May. Do not send anyone there.

## How do I get the city's $45 residential permit?

The City Clerk sells an annual [residential permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) to residents of blocks in the permit system. It is voluntary: residents of a permit block are not required to buy one, but city parking rules still apply.

```figure

  - {label: "Bring your vehicle registration and proof of residency: deed, lease, license, or a utility or phone bill"}
  - {label: "Apply in person at the City Clerk, 108 E. Green St.; first come, first served"}
  - {label: "Pay for the permit; a visitor pass costs $10"}
  - {label: "Display it on the driver-side rear window, never the windshield"}
  - {label: "Renew after July 31; there is no partial-year price"}
```

The rules that trip students up:

- **You apply, not your landlord.** The city does not issue permits to landlords for tenants.
- **One plate per permit.** A new car needs a new permit.
- **July 31 ends it.** A permit bought in spring still expires on July 31 at full price.
- **Replacement is free** if you return the remnants; otherwise it costs the full price again.

New York State law, [Vehicle and Traffic Law 1640-e](https://nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VAT/1640-E*2), sets the zone on the city's east side and excludes streets inside Cornell. It requires short-term parking of at least 120 minutes in the permit area and keeps at least 20% of spaces open to nonresidents. The system began in 1998, and the Chronicle reported that it then left out R-3 blocks, including central Collegetown. The city's permit page does not list which blocks are in today, or the permit hours for each block. Call the City Clerk with your address before you count on a street spot. Renters should also read the city's [trash tag, snow and noise rules](/cornell/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/).

## Can I park on campus if I live in Collegetown?

Yes, but not with a resident permit. Cornell's [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) lists what is left, and our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) covers each option in full.

| Option | Cost | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| B commuter permit | $359.77 a year | B Lot always; A Lot after 2:30 p.m. |
| Free A or E permit | Free with a full assistantship on the Ithaca campus | Outer Tier lots; E needs work in its zone |
| Daily Decision | As little as $1 a day in select zones | Outer lots, first come |
| ParkMobile by the hour | $0.45 fee per transaction, plus the lot rate | Most lots |

B Lot, at 930 Campus Rd. by the Vet College, runs a 24-hour parking day. Riders of Cornell's C2C bus can [buy temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions). Commuter lots charge Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., including breaks ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)). In 2023, permits for the Williams Street lot at Stewart Avenue, about $700, [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students).

## What are the rules for parking overnight in Collegetown?

Your choices, in order of certainty, are your lease spot, the Dryden Road Garage, then a city street.

On streets, the city's [odd/even code](https://ecode360.com/8395630) runs from Nov. 1 to April 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., and the city turns it on only when needed. Park on the odd side on odd dates and the even side on even dates. A car that sits 72 hours counts as abandoned and can be towed. The city also has a 24-hour move rule Monday to Friday. Text SIREN to 67283 for alerts. The odd/even ticket is $15, then $30 and $45 for repeats ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). The 2027 city budget plans higher fines, worth about $381,000 a year ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/11/common-council-approves-2027-budget-city-will-remain-below-tax-cap/)). Our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) has the break rules.

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Sending visitors to the Seneca Street Garage.** It is closed for good.
- **Counting on a free Saturday.** Saturdays are paid since 2026.
- **Asking the landlord for the city permit.** The Clerk will not issue it to one.
- **Leaving a car on a street over break.** It can be towed as abandoned.
- **Paying for a B permit with a full assistantship.** Free A and E permits come with it.

## FAQ

**Is parking free on Sundays in Ithaca?** Street meters are enforced [Monday to Saturday](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/705/Parking-Information), so Sunday is free. Check the sign where you park.

**Where does a friend park when visiting?** If your block is in the permit system, the city sells a visitor pass. Otherwise use the Dryden Road Garage.

**What happens if I park at Cornell without paying?** It is a $50 ticket. Our permit guide lists Cornell's fines.
