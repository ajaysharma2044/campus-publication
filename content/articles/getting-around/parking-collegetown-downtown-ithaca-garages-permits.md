```glance
- City garages cost $2.25 an hour in 2026, capped at $12 a day downtown (Green, Cayuga) and $18 at Dryden Road in Collegetown.
- Street parking in ParkMobile zones is $2.50 an hour; coin meters farther out are $1.50. Saturday is no longer free.
- The Seneca Street garage has been closed since spring 2026. Use Cayuga Street (245 S. Cayuga, 300+ spaces) or Green Street.
- A city residential permit is $45 a year, valid to July 31. You apply yourself at City Hall, 108 E Green St; a landlord cannot.
- On campus, off-campus students buy a B commuter permit ($359.77 a year) or pay Daily Decision from about $1 a day.
- From Nov 1 to Apr 1 the city's odd/even rule governs street parking overnight; never leave a car on a city street over break.
```

Since January 5, 2026, the City of Ithaca charges [$2.25 an hour in its garages and $2.50 an hour at on-street ParkMobile zones](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328), with a daily cap of $12 at the Green and Cayuga garages and $18 at the Dryden Road garage in Collegetown. For a whole day or a night near Collegetown, the Dryden Road ramp is the paid option; downtown, it is Cayuga Street, because the [Seneca Street garage closed in spring 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/). Renters parking on the street need the city's $45 residential permit where their block is in the permit area, and must follow the odd/even rule from November 1.

```reader "If you're renting off campus with a car"
Your home spot and your campus spot are two separate costs. At home, check your lease for a spot; if there is none, the city's [$45 residential permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) covers blocks in the permit system, runs to July 31 and has no partial-year price. You apply with your registration and lease; your landlord cannot. On campus you are not eligible for a resident permit, so buy the [B commuter permit at $359.77](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) or pay Daily Decision by the day. B Lot runs a 24-hour parking day, so it also works for a night on campus. The costly mistake: leaving the car on the street over winter break, where it can be [towed and treated as abandoned](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules).
```

```reader "If you're a student with a car in university housing"
Your resident permit ($752.98 a year) covers your own lot and A Lot after 2:30 p.m., not Collegetown or downtown. For a night out or a shift downtown, the city garages are the cheap certain option: $2.25 an hour, so 4 hours costs $9 and a full day stops at $12 on Green or Cayuga. Collegetown's Dryden Road ramp caps at $18. Do not move the car to a Collegetown street to save a walk in winter: the [odd/even rule](https://ecode360.com/8395630) runs 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. from Nov 1 to Apr 1 when the city activates it. Your campus lot is the safe place to leave it over break.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
Downtown, park at the Cayuga Street Garage, 245 S. Cayuga St, [more than 300 spaces](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/) and five minutes' walk from the Commons. It costs $2.25 an hour and no more than $12 a day. Skip the Seneca Street garage: it is closed. Saturday is a paid day in 2026; a May 2026 [Visit Ithaca guide](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/) still says weekends are free, and the city's notice wins. On campus, the Tang Welcome Center lot is [$1.50 an hour or $10 a day](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options); pay with ParkMobile, since its pay station was out of service as of September 8, 2026.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
Check your assistantship before you pay for anything. A full assistantship on the Ithaca campus, confirmed in Workday, gets you [free Outer Tier A and E permits](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students); without one, the B commuter permit is $359.77 a year. Grads in university housing do not qualify for the free permits. Most grads rent, so the home side matters too: if your apartment has no off-street spot, budget for the $45 city residential permit where your block is in the system, or a city garage. The proposed 24/7 monthly rate at Dryden Road is $232, which is about $2,088 over a nine-month academic year.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over a break"
A break is when cars get towed. Cornell's summary of the city rule says a car left on a city street over winter break [may be treated as abandoned, ticketed, towed and possibly impounded](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules). If you live off campus and stay, move the car to the correct side each night the odd/even rule is active (7 p.m. to 6 a.m., Nov 1 to Apr 1). If you are going away for a few days, a garage is the paid fallback: at Dryden Road, a full day caps at $18, so ten days costs up to $180. Students in university housing can leave a car in their own permit area. The full break rules are on the [winter parking guide](/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/).
```

## What parking costs in Collegetown and downtown in 2026

The city raised its rates on [Monday, January 5, 2026](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328), in a notice posted December 18, 2025. Older guides that still rank in search quote $1 an hour; that number is out of date.

| Where | Rate from Jan 5, 2026 | Daily maximum | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-street ParkMobile and pay-station zones | $2.50 an hour | None stated | Downtown and Collegetown streets |
| Coin-only meters | $1.50 an hour | None stated | Farther out; unchanged "for now," per the city |
| Green Street garage | $2.25 an hour | $12 | Downtown |
| Cayuga Street garage | $2.25 an hour | $12 | 245 S. Cayuga St; managed by Premium Parking |
| Dryden Road garage | $2.25 an hour | $18 | Collegetown; 210 spaces |
| Seneca Street garage | Closed | n/a | Closed since spring 2026 |

Free Saturday parking [ended with the 2026 city budget](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2025/11/12/free-saturday-parking-to-end-in-ithaca-in-2026/), and the same report says Sunday stays free.

Example: a parent drives in for a Saturday and parks downtown from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. At $2.25 an hour, 8 hours would be $18, but the Cayuga or Green cap holds it to $12. The same 8 hours at a $2.50 street zone is $20.

## The garages, one by one

**Dryden Road (Collegetown).** A city-owned ramp off Dryden Road with [210 spaces](https://news.cornell.edu/node/278473). In an informal merchants' survey reported by the Cornell Chronicle (undated), about a third of it sat empty in the daytime. It is the closest paid garage to Collegetown apartments and the campus's south edge, and its $18 daily cap is the highest in the city.

![Dryden Road in Collegetown in 1985, looking north from College Avenue, with parked cars along the curb](2.webp)

**Cayuga Street (downtown).** At 245 S. Cayuga St, with more than 300 spaces, within 5 minutes' walk of the Commons. From November 9, 2026, [OurBus's downtown stop moves to 235 S. Cayuga St](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/), right beside it, which makes it the garage for anyone dropping off or meeting a bus rider.

**Green Street (downtown).** The other open downtown city garage, at $2.25 an hour and a $12 cap.

**Seneca Street.** Closed since spring 2026. The city cited merchant problems after the closure as one reason it moved the East Seneca bus stops on November 9.

## Monthly garage permits

For a car that lives in a garage, the monthly permit is the number that matters. These come from the city's [2026 budget proposal](https://cityofithaca.org/DocumentCenter/View/20272/2026-Final-Budget-Proposal-20251107?bidId=), not a final rate sheet, so confirm the rate before you pay.

| Garage | Proposed 2026 monthly permit |
|---|---|
| Dryden Road, 24/7 | $232 |
| Green Street | $117 (weekly $39) |
| Cayuga Street | $108 standard, $180 premium |
| Worker special | $185 |
| Lost ticket | $20 |

Example: a Collegetown renter with no lease spot keeps a car at Dryden Road from late August to mid-May, about nine months. At the proposed $232 a month that is about $2,088, our multiplication. Paying the $18 daily cap instead would cost $540 for a 30-day month, so the monthly permit pays off after 13 days a month.

## The city residential parking permit, step by step

The city sells a [$45 annual permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) to residents of blocks in its permit system. It is voluntary: the city says residents of a permit block are not required to buy one, but city parking rules still apply to them.

```figure
kind: steps
title: Getting the $45 city residential permit
rows:
  - {label: "Confirm your block is in the permit system (the City Clerk keeps the map)"}
  - {label: "Bring your vehicle registration and proof of residency: lease, deed, license, or a utility or phone bill"}
  - {label: "Apply in person at the City Clerk, 108 E Green Street, first come, first served"}
  - {label: "Pay $45; a visitor pass is $10"}
  - {label: "Display it on the driver-side rear window (driver's window if the rear is tinted), never the windshield"}
  - {label: "Renew after July 31 each year; there is no partial-year price"}
```

The rules that trip students up:

- **You apply, not your landlord.** Landlords may not obtain permits for tenants.
- **One plate per permit.** A new car needs a new permit.
- **July 31 is the end.** A permit bought in May still expires July 31, and the price is the same $45.
- **Replacement.** Free if you return the remnants of the old one, otherwise another $45.

Where the zone is: the state law behind it, [Vehicle and Traffic Law 1640-e](https://nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VAT/1640-E*2), draws it on the city's east side, along streets including East State Street, Stewart Avenue, University Avenue and Linn Street up to the Cayuga Heights line. It excludes streets inside Cornell, and no permit applies on blocks next to commercial or retail property. The same law requires at least 120 minutes of short-term parking for everyone and keeps at least 20 percent of spaces in a permit area open to nonresidents.

Not confirmed: the permit-only days and hours for each block, and whether a Cornell permit counts on Collegetown streets. Ask the City Clerk at 108 E Green St before you count on overnight street parking.

## Parking on campus when you live off campus

Off-campus students cannot buy resident permits. The [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) lists what is left:

| Option | Cost | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| B commuter permit | $359.77 a year, prorated daily from Aug 16 | B Lot always; A Lot after 2:30 p.m. |
| Free A or E permit | $0 with a full assistantship on the Ithaca campus | Outer Tier lots; E needs work in its zone |
| Daily Decision | "As little as $1/day" in select zones | Outer lots, first come |
| ParkMobile by the hour | Varies by lot, plus a $0.45 fee per transaction | Most lots |

Register your plate at MyParking.Cornell.edu first; Cornell [requires it even for short-term parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus).

**Daily Decision.** Demand-priced parking in outer lots, open to all. Cornell's [commuter page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/getting-around/commuter-support-services) prices it from $1 a day ($0.65 permit plus a $0.35 ParkMobile fee). Short-term rates changed August 1, 2026, so check the price in the ParkMobile app.

**Paid hours.** Commuter lots charge [Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. year-round](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options), breaks and holidays included. Most lots' parking day ends at 7:30 a.m.

**B Lot overnight.** B Lot, at 930 Campus Rd by the Vet College, runs on a 24-hour parking day, and multi-day purchases are allowed in most lots. C2C riders can [buy temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions), or park free by [emailing c2cbus@cornell.edu with plate and dates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488) (a 2024 rule).

Permit lots near Collegetown fill fast. In fall 2023 permits for the Williams Street lot at Stewart Avenue, about $700, [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students). Deana Druz '24 called finding a spot a daily "treasure hunt," and one morning, with the lot full, paid at a ParkMobile lot and was late.

## Overnight in Collegetown

Your choices for a night, in order of certainty:

1. **Your lease spot**, if the lease includes one.
2. **The Dryden Road garage**, at $2.25 an hour with an $18 daily cap; the proposed $232 monthly permit is for 24/7 use.
3. **A city street**, by the posted signs, the residential permit where it applies, and from November 1 the odd/even rule.

The [odd/even code](https://ecode360.com/8395630) covers November 1 to April 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., when the city activates it for snow removal: no parking on the odd side on even dates, and the reverse. Towing is possible after 72 hours. In 2022 students told the [Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/) that plow banks buried Collegetown cars parked on the street. The full winter and break rules are on the [winter parking guide](/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/).

## Common mistakes

- **Sending visitors to the Seneca Street garage.** It has been closed since spring 2026; send them to Cayuga Street instead.
- **Counting on a free Saturday.** It ended in 2026. A full Saturday at a street zone costs $2.50 an hour with no cap; a garage stops at $12 or $18.
- **Budgeting $1 an hour from an old guide.** The 2026 street rate is $2.50, two and a half times that.
- **Asking the landlord for the city permit.** The city will not issue it to a landlord; go yourself with your lease.
- **Buying a city permit in spring.** It expires July 31 at the full $45, so a May purchase covers under three months.
- **Paying $359.77 for a B permit with a full assistantship.** Free A and E permits come with it.
- **Leaving the car on a city street over break.** It can be ticketed, towed and impounded as abandoned.

## FAQ

**Is parking free on Sundays in Ithaca?** The [FingerLakes1 report](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2025/11/12/free-saturday-parking-to-end-in-ithaca-in-2026/) on the 2026 budget says Sunday remains free; only free Saturday ended. Check the sign or pay station where you park.

**Where does a friend park when visiting my apartment?** If your block is in the city permit system, a [visitor pass costs $10](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) from the City Clerk. Otherwise, the Dryden Road garage caps a day at $18.

**How do I pay at a city street spot?** In the ParkMobile and pay-station zones, by app or at the station, at [$2.50 an hour](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328). Coin-only meters farther out take coins at $1.50 an hour.

**What happens if I park at Cornell without paying?** The fine is [$50, plus $10 if unpaid in 10 days](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/), under Cornell's schedule from September 2023. See the [student permit guide](/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) for the full list.

**Where do I park to catch the OurBus downtown?** From November 9, 2026, OurBus stops at 235 S. Cayuga St, next to the Cayuga Street Garage, where a day costs no more than $12.
