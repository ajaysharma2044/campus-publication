```glance
- TCAT dispatch chooses snow routes on the day, so a normal route can detour in a storm.
- Check TCAT's website and the Bus Tracker, Transit or MyStop apps before you leave.
- A weekday daytime ride costs $1.50; after first year, evenings and weekends are free.
- TCAT runs no buses on Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's Day.
```

TCAT runs through the winter, but when snow hits, dispatchers send buses on snow routes they choose that day. Detours go up on TCAT's website, and riders are told to check the Bus Tracker, Transit and MyStop apps before leaving ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/01/tcat-prepares-to-swerve-away-winter-storm-conditions)).

## What is a snow route?

A snow route is a planned detour around a road that snow or ice makes unsafe for a bus. TCAT switches these detours on route by route and posts a map for each one. For the 2025-26 winter, it had snow detours ready for six routes ([TCAT](https://web.archive.org/web/20260304083123/https://tcatbus.com/learn/snow-detours/)):

- Route 11 stays on South Aurora Street and uses Hillview Place, skipping Hudson Street between the two.
- Routes 14 and 14S skip Chestnut Street and Campbell Avenue and use Elm Street, Hector Street and Trumansburg Road instead.
- Route 36 skips Lake Street between University Avenue and Falls Street and uses Cayuga Street instead.
- Route 51 skips the loop of Snyder Hill Road, Skyvue Road and Eastern Heights Drive.
- Route 92 skips Stewart Avenue, Thurston Avenue, Fall Creek Drive and Barton Place and uses University Avenue instead.

Stewart and Thurston Avenues were on the list in 2019 too. That January, TCAT's snow plans for lines 51, 92 and 93 avoided stretches of University, Stewart and Thurston Avenues ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/01/tcat-prepares-to-swerve-away-winter-storm-conditions)).

On a snow day, active detours show in the red alert bar on TCAT's home page ([TCAT](https://web.archive.org/web/20260304083123/https://tcatbus.com/learn/snow-detours/)). You can also call TCAT at 607-277-RIDE on weekdays from 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ([Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

## How do big storms change service?

Storms bring delays. In January 2026, a storm closed Cornell's Ithaca campus from noon on Jan. 25 to noon the next day. TCAT delayed its Monday service to 10 a.m. ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/01/cornell-announces-ithaca-and-agritech-campuses-will-reopen-at-noon-on-monday)). Our [closures guide](/daily-life/cornell-snow-days-class-cancellation-how-it-works/) explains who decides.

## Does the winter schedule change?

Yes. Over the 2025-26 holidays, TCAT ran reduced service from Sunday, Dec. 21, through Sunday, Jan. 4. Routes 90 and 92 did not run, Route 81 started later and ran fewer trips, and service ended early on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve. TCAT announced the cuts on Dec. 18, 2025, three days ahead. Its winter-spring schedule started Jan. 11, 2026 ([TCAT](https://web.archive.org/web/20251219220619/https://tcatbus.com/winter-holidays/)).

TCAT runs every day of the year except Independence Day, New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Thanksgiving and Christmas ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/07/service-changes-for-july-4-holiday-weekend-tcat-buses-curbside-recycling-city-offices-will-be-closed-on-independence-day)). For break travel and what to do without buses, see our [winter storms guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/).

## How reliable is TCAT in winter?

Winter is not the only strain. TCAT has run short of drivers and buses in recent years. Route 14S, which ran from West Hill to the southwest retail district, and Route 83, which ran from West Campus to Central Campus and Highland Avenue, were suspended from fall 2023 and the board extended the suspension in April 2024 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/)). Nearly a third of students use TCAT as their main way to campus, according to Cornell's assistant director of transportation ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall)).

The planning rule is simple: leave one bus earlier on snow days, and carry a backup, such as a walking route or a friend with a car.

## What does a ride cost?

The standard fare is $1.50 ([Downtown Ithaca](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/)). New students ride free on their Cornell ID until their pass ends. After that, rides are free on weekdays after 6 p.m. and all weekend ([Cornell MBA page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/)). Compare paying per ride with the annual pass in our [OmniRide calculator](/tools/omniride-break-even-calculator/), and see the [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) for who qualifies.

## How do you stay safe waiting at a stop?

Cold is the other risk. At 0F with a 15 mph wind, the wind chill is -19F and exposed skin can freeze in 30 minutes ([National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-wind-chill-chart)). Cornell Health advises layers, a water-resistant jacket and boots, and covering hands and ears. Its 24-hour advice line is 607-255-5155 ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)).
