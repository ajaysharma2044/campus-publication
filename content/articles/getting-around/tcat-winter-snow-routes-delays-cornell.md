- Check TCAT's website and the Bus Tracker, Transit or MyStop apps before you leave.

TCAT runs through the winter, but when snow hits, dispatchers send buses on snow routes they choose that day. Detours go up on TCAT's website, and riders are told to check the Bus Tracker, Transit and MyStop apps before leaving ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/01/tcat-prepares-to-swerve-away-winter-storm-conditions)).

## What is a snow route?

A snow route is a detour that keeps a bus off roads that are too steep or too slick in a storm. TCAT dispatch decides which routes to use, so no fixed list applies to every storm.

The one documented example on Cornell routes is from January 2019. Routes 51, 92 and 93 detoured around parts of University, Stewart and Thurston Avenues (Cornell Daily Sun, same story).

TCAT does not publish a snow-route map in the sources behind this page. Before a forecast storm, look at TCAT's own site and apps, or call 607-277-RIDE on weekdays from 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ([Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)).

## How do big storms change service?

Storms bring delays. In January 2026, a storm with a forecast of 12 to 18 inches closed Cornell's Ithaca campus from noon on Jan. 25 to noon the next day. TCAT delayed its Monday service to 10 a.m. ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/01/cornell-announces-ithaca-and-agritech-campuses-will-reopen-at-noon-on-monday)). Our [closures guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-snow-days-class-cancellation-how-it-works/) explains who decides.

## Does the winter schedule change?

TCAT publishes schedule changes about three times a year. The latest one found ran through Jan. 11, 2026, and no fall 2026 change notice was posted when this page was updated ([About TCAT](https://tcatbus.com/about/about-tcat/)). Check tcatbus.com for the schedule that applies to the date you travel.

Holidays are the other change. TCAT runs every day of the year except Independence Day, New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Thanksgiving and Christmas ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/07/service-changes-for-july-4-holiday-weekend-tcat-buses-curbside-recycling-city-offices-will-be-closed-on-independence-day)). For break travel and what to do without buses, see our [winter storms guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/).

## How reliable is TCAT in winter?

Winter is not the only strain. TCAT has run short of drivers and buses in recent years. Routes 14S and 83, which serve West Hill, Cornell and Cayuga Heights, were suspended from fall 2023 and the board extended the suspension in April 2024 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/)). Nearly a third of students use TCAT as their main way to campus, according to Cornell's assistant director of transportation ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall)).

The planning rule is simple: leave one bus earlier on snow days, and carry a backup, such as a walking route or a friend with a car.

## What does a ride cost?

The standard fare is $1.50 ([Downtown Ithaca](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/)). New students ride free on their Cornell ID until their pass ends. After that, rides are free on weekdays after 6 p.m. and all weekend ([Cornell MBA page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/)). Compare paying per ride with the annual pass in our [OmniRide calculator](/tools/omniride-break-even-calculator/), and see the [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) for who qualifies.

## How do you stay safe waiting at a stop?

Cold is the other risk. At 0F with a 15 mph wind, the wind chill is -19F and exposed skin can freeze in 30 minutes ([National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-wind-chill-chart)). Cornell Health advises layers, a water-resistant jacket and boots, and covering hands and ears. Its 24-hour advice line is 607-255-5155 ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)).
