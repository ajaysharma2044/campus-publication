Off-campus students get mail at the apartment address, not at a Cornell service center. Use the street address, your unit number and an Ithaca, NY 14850 line, and ask your landlord where carriers leave packages. The [Student Agencies Building](https://www.thestudentagenciesbuilding.com/) at 409 College Avenue lists package service, so check your own listing.

```glance
- Use your street address plus unit number; Cornell service centers serve dorm residents only.
- The Student Agencies Building at 409 College Ave lists package service among its included amenities.
- Change your address with the Post Office two weeks before you move out.
- Uncle Marty's in Collegetown is authorized for FedEx, DHL and USPS.
```

## What address do you use off campus?

Use the apartment's street address and unit number, then Ithaca, NY and the ZIP code. Cornell's residence hall service centers handle mail for hall residents, so they do not apply once you live in a lease.

The ZIP code can mislead you. An "Ithaca, NY 14850" address covers 12 towns and villages plus the City of Ithaca, by the Town of Ithaca's welcome page, so a mailing address does not tell you which city, town or village you live in ([Town of Ithaca](https://townithacany.gov/welcome-new-resident-guide/)). That matters for rules like trash and permits, covered in the [lease guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) and the [off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/).

## How do packages get delivered to an apartment?

Where a carrier leaves a box depends on the building. The inventory behind this guide records one building with package handling:

| Building | Package amenity listed |
|---|---|
| The Student Agencies Building, 409 College Ave | Package service, listed with free amenities ([building site](https://www.thestudentagenciesbuilding.com/)) |

Source: building and marketplace listings. Other buildings can offer parcel rooms or lockers, but their listings do not say so.

For any other address, ask the landlord three questions before you sign: where does a carrier leave a box, are mailboxes numbered by unit, and can you get a key to a shared entry. Published sources for this guide do not cover porch theft or locker systems in Ithaca. If a building has no secure spot, have packages go to a store that accepts parcels, such as Uncle Marty's.

Theft of a delivered package is a renters insurance question. Cornell's preferred insurer, GradGuard, covers theft, fire and smoke damage for students on and off campus ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/campus-living/living-campus)). Costs are in the [renters insurance guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-renters-insurance-cost/).

## Where else can you ship and receive in Collegetown?

Uncle Marty's opened in Collegetown in 2011 and downtown in 2025, and it is an authorized FedEx and DHL shipper and an approved USPS provider ([Tompkins Chamber of Commerce](https://business.tompkinschamber.org/list/member/uncle-marty-s-printing-shipping-storage-more-12929)). For summer storage and move-out shipping Cornell's contracted vendor is Big Red Shipping and Storage, (607) 524-3130, with free pickup at a dorm, apartment or house ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)).

## How do you forward mail at move-out or during a sublet?

Cornell's move-out timeline gives two steps. Two weeks before you leave, change your address with the Post Office. One week before, give your landlord a forwarding address ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out)). The deposit rules and timeline are in the [guide to getting your deposit back](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/).

If you leave for the summer, Cornell's tenant best practices say to notify your contacts and forward your mail before you go. Most leases require landlord approval to sublet ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/campus-living/living-campus)). See the [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) for how that works.

## What to do next

1. Ask the landlord where carriers leave packages.
2. Put your unit number on every order.
3. Set a calendar reminder two weeks before move-out to file your address change.
