Cornell enrolls students in the Student Health Plan (SHP), which costs [$4,092 for 12 months in 2026-27](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27). To keep other coverage instead, waive the plan before your deadline, because waivers lock after it passes. For care, call Cornell Health's 24/7 line at 607-255-5155.

```glance
- The Student Health Plan is $341 a month in 2026-27; new fall students paid for 11 months.
- Spring 2027 new students must waive by Jan. 21, 2027; waivers lock after the deadline.
- Spring 2027 admits submit health requirements between Nov. 17 and Dec. 18, 2026.
- Cornell Health's 24/7 advice line is 607-255-5155.
```

## How much does the Student Health Plan cost?

The plan costs $341 a month in 2026-27, which is the posted yearly price divided by 12. New fall students were billed $1,705 for fall, which covers five months from Aug. 1, and $2,046 for spring ([Cornell Student Health Benefits](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27)). Spring is six months at $341, which is $2,046. Fall plus spring for a new student is $3,751, or 11 months.

## How do I waive the Student Health Plan?

Submit the waiver on Cornell Student Health Benefits before your deadline. New fall-start students had until July 31, 2026. New spring students have until Jan. 21, 2027, and spring reverification is due Jan. 31, 2027 ([Cornell Student Health Benefits](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/node/179)). After the deadline the waiver locks, so a missed date means you keep the plan and the charge.

1. Find your insurer's member ID and plan name before you start.
2. Open the waiver form on the Student Health Benefits site.
3. Submit it before the date that applies to you.
4. Keep the confirmation.

The sources behind this guide list the deadlines but not the rules for which outside plans qualify. Read those rules on the waiver page before you decide, or email Student Health Benefits with your plan details.

## What health requirements must new students meet?

New students submit immunization and tuberculosis (TB) records to Cornell Health. For fall 2026, first-years were due June 10 and transfers July 23 ([Cornell Health, archived](http://web.archive.org/web/20260815222031/https://health.cornell.edu/getcare/healthrequirements/undergrad-grad-prof)). Spring 2027 admits submit between Nov. 17 and Dec. 18, 2026. Missing the date places a hold on your enrollment on Dec. 22, 2026 ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/getcare/healthrequirements/undergrad-grad-prof)).

Required immunizations and TB testing cost $50 to $1,000 at Cornell Health depending on your insurance, and up to $50 out of pocket on the Student Health Plan. Only the QuantiFERON-TB Gold or T-SPOT blood tests are accepted ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/getcare/healthrequirements/undergrad-grad-prof)).

## How do I find a doctor in Ithaca?

Start with Cornell Health, the campus clinic. Its 24/7 advice line is [607-255-5155](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety), and you can also book through the myCornellHealth portal. Cornell Health also has nutritionists; students can call the same number for a nutritionist plan ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food)). For prescriptions and off-campus options, see the [pharmacy guide](/cornell/daily-life/pharmacy-near-cornell-campus-ithaca/).

Cornell Health's pharmacy sells light boxes for $20, which students use for winter low mood ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/02/winter-blues-sad-and-the-benefits-of-light-therapy)). Cold-weather guidance from Cornell Health says to dress in layers and watch for frostbite signs ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/about/news/cold-weather-safety)).

Cornell Health's pages list no off-campus primary care, urgent care or Cayuga Health clinics (its [affordable food page](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food) names Ithaca food resources). If you stay on an outside plan, call the number on your insurance card and ask for in-network doctors near 14850. If you are on the Student Health Plan, call Cornell Health first.

## What to do next

1. Check today's date against your waiver deadline.
2. Decide to keep the Student Health Plan or waive it, and submit.
3. Upload your immunization and TB records by your due date.
4. Save 607-255-5155 in your phone.
