```glance
- TCAT Route 32 links the Commons, central campus and the Ithaca airport (ITH) seven days a week for $1.50.
- Cornell first-years ride free with a Cornell ID; every registered Cornell student rides free on weekends and after 6 p.m. on weekdays.
- The first bus reaches ITH at 7:35 a.m. weekdays and 8:31 a.m. weekends; prebook a taxi for earlier flights.
- Taxis: Ithaca Airport Cab 607-252-9052, Collegetown Cab 607-588-8888, Ithaca Dispatch 607-277-7777; ITH to Cornell cost $15 to $16 (Dec. 2025).
- Driving: long-term parking is $8 a day or $40 a week; short-term is $9.50 a day or $48 a week.
- Free Student Assembly charters to ITH and SYR ran in past finals weeks; none was announced for fall 2026 as of Nov 8.
```

Yes: TCAT [Route 32 runs Monday through Sunday](https://web.archive.org/web/20261004030053/https://tcatbus.com/bus-schedules) between the Commons, central campus and the Ithaca airport, and the adult fare is $1.50. The first bus reaches the terminal at 7:35 a.m. on weekdays. For an earlier flight, book a taxi the day before.

```reader "If you're flying home for every break"
Treat the bus as the daytime option and the taxi as the dawn option. The Student Assembly president said in 2024 that for [5:30 a.m. flights there is "literally no transportation available"](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) beyond paid rides. Call [Ithaca Airport Cab, Collegetown Cab or Ithaca Dispatch](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) a day ahead. ITH has only two scheduled nonstops, [JFK on Delta and Washington Dulles on United](https://flyithaca.com/airlines), so a cancellation can mean a long wait. In finals week, check Campus Groups for a free Student Assembly charter before you pay for a cab.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
ITH connects through JFK or Washington Dulles, and the terminal is open [3:30 a.m. to midnight](https://flyithaca.com). The bus into town takes [exact cash, a credit or debit card, a digital wallet, a Tcard or the TFare app](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), so you do not need US coins. Your free first-year ride needs a Cornell ID; until you have the card, carry $1.50. If you land after the last bus (9:33 p.m. on weekdays, earlier on weekends), the costly mistake is waiting at the curb: prebook a taxi before you fly, since the airport tells late arrivals to.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
You pay the public fare: [$1.50 for adults, $0.75 for seniors 60 and over, and free for children under 12 with a paying adult](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). The airport is [about 10 minutes from downtown](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/). Visit Ithaca says you do not need a car for a family weekend, but [Uber and Lyft are limited outside downtown](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/), so book a cab for the return trip instead of hoping for a rideshare at your hotel.
```

```reader "If you're a student who needs accessibility accommodations"
Every TCAT bus has a [wheelchair lift or ramp](https://web.archive.org/web/20260913070000/https://tcatbus.com/ada/accessibility/), and when a fare applies, riders with disabilities pay the [$0.75 reduced fare; tell the driver before you tap](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). If you cannot ride the bus, TCAT's paratransit, run by [Gadabout at (607) 273-1878](https://web.archive.org/web/20260225180143/https://tcatbus.com/ada/ada-complementary-paratransit/), serves the airport for [$2 one way](https://web.archive.org/web/20260227123641/https://tcatbus.com/ada/ada-complementary-paratransit/ada-paratransit-info-guide/). TCAT must approve your eligibility first, which usually takes one to three weeks, and each ride is booked by the day before. Paratransit keeps bus hours, so a dawn flight still means a taxi: [Collegetown Cab lists wheelchair transportation](https://collegetowncab.com/ithaca/) among its services, so ask for it when you prebook.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your Cornell ID covers TCAT [at all hours until Aug 31 of next year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), Route 32 included, so a daytime flight costs you nothing to reach. The trap is a 6 a.m. flight booked by a parent who assumes the bus runs: it does not. Prebook a cab, or share one with friends on the same flight. After Aug 31, weekday daytime rides cost $1.50.
```

```reader "If you have a car on campus"
Long-term parking at ITH is [$8 a day or $40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking), against [a $14 daily maximum in Syracuse's economy lots](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/), or about $98 for seven days. Use the long-term lot, not short-term ($48 a week). If you would rather leave the car in your campus permit area, a cab is the other way to the terminal.
```

## Taking TCAT Route 32 to the airport

TCAT's fall 2026 schedule calls Route 32 ["Airport via Cornell"](https://web.archive.org/web/20261004030053/https://tcatbus.com/bus-schedules) and runs it from Green Street at the Commons through central campus to the terminal. On campus, the timetable lists the Vet School and Rockefeller Hall on the way out, and Goldwin Smith Hall and the Boyce Thompson Institute on the way back. The bus reaches Rockefeller Hall about 15 minutes after leaving the Commons and the terminal about 20 minutes after that. You pay the driver when you board. Cornell's own route list names Route 32 as the ["Airport route"](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting) alongside its campus routes.

| Rider | Weekdays | Weekends |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell first-year with a Cornell ID | Free | Free |
| PhD student or graduate assistant | Free | Free |
| Other registered Cornell student | $1.50 before 6 p.m., free after | Free |
| Adult visitor | $1.50 | $1.50 |
| Senior 60+ or rider with a disability | $0.75 | $0.75 |
| Child under 12 with a paying adult | Free | Free |

Sources for the table: Cornell's [bus privileges page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) for students, and the [Downtown Ithaca fare guide](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/) for everyone else. If your Cornell ID fails at the farebox, the fallback fare is $1.50.

## Early and late flights

The terminal opens at [3:30 a.m. and closes at midnight](https://flyithaca.com). The buses do not cover those edges. For first flights, the 2024 Student Assembly president said there is [no transportation available](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) beyond paid rides. [Taxis are available at the airport 24/7](https://flyithaca.com/taxis), and the airport asks you to prebook for late flights.

Example: a sophomore on West Campus has a 6 a.m. Delta flight on a Tuesday in December. No bus runs that early, so they book Collegetown Cab online the evening before for a 4:30 a.m. pickup. At the cab's [December 2025 airport rates](https://web.archive.org/web/20251216125647/https://flyithaca.com/ground/taxi-service/), the ride comes to about $18.50: the $15 Cornell fare, $3 for two checked bags and $0.50 for riding before 5 a.m. They confirm the total by phone.

## What a taxi from Cornell to the airport costs

| Company | Phone | Airport to Cornell, one rider | Extra charges |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collegetown Cab | 607-588-8888 | $15 | $1.50 a bag; $1.50 per extra rider; $0.50 a rider from 8 p.m. to 5 a.m. |
| Ithaca Dispatch | 607-277-7777 | $16 | No bag charge; $4 per extra family member; children under 13 free |
| Ithaca Airport Cab | 607-252-9052 | Quote by phone or text | No card fee; takes all payment types |

Collegetown Cab and Ithaca Dispatch fares are from the [airport's taxi page as of December 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20251216125647/https://flyithaca.com/ground/taxi-service/). The current [airport taxi page](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) lists all three firms, which quote prices when you call, and adds that Collegetown Cab books online and by app. Ask for the total when you book a ride from campus.

Uber and Lyft work by app at the airport. In 2017, The Ithacan priced a ride from Ithaca College to the airport at [$17.49 by UberX and $19 by taxi](https://theithacan.org/news/ride-hailing-services-slightly-less-expensive-than-taxis-in-ithaca/). Visit Ithaca's May 2026 guide warns that rideshare is [limited outside downtown](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/), which matters at 4 a.m. on North Campus.

## Driving and parking at ITH

| Time parked | Long-term lot | Short-term lot |
|---|---|---|
| First 30 minutes | Free | Free |
| 31 to 60 minutes | $2 | $2 |
| 3 to 4 hours | $5 | $7 |
| 5 to 24 hours | $8 | $9.50 |
| One week | $40 | $48 |

Rates are from the [airport parking page](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking). Take a ticket at the entrance and pay by card at the exit gate. To pay cash, use the machine inside the terminal before you return to the car. The short-term lot is nearest the terminal, with a small covered section. You may park in either lot for as long as you need. The parking office is at [607-220-4699](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking).

## Free Student Assembly charters in finals week

The Student Assembly has paid for free charters to both airports at the end of recent terms. In May 2024 it moved [$18,000 for 20 shuttles](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/), with pickups at Baker Flagpole and Robert Purcell Community Center (RPCC). Riders registered by group, first come first served.

- Fall 2025: [Resolution 18 moved $7,853.32](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026) for finals-week charters to ITH and Syracuse.
- Spring 2026: [Resolution 83 moved $10,668.87](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026), free for undergraduates, with sign-up on Campus Groups.
- Fall 2026: no resolution or announcement as of Nov 8.

Holiday shuttles are rarer. In 2023 a Swarthout Coaches shuttle ran to ITH over Thanksgiving for [$14 one way or $28 round trip](https://cornellsun.com/2023/11/19/special-thanksgiving-shuttle-connects-cornell-with-ithaca-tompkins-international-airport/). No 2026 version has been announced.

## Common mistakes

- **Planning a dawn flight around TCAT.** No bus runs for 5:30 a.m. departures. The cost is a missed flight, from an airport with two nonstop routes.
- **Waiting for a Route 72 on a weekend.** The [airport's transit page](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation) still lists one, but TCAT's own [fall 2026 schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20261004030053/https://tcatbus.com/bus-schedules) has no Route 72, and TCAT runs the buses. Route 32 runs Saturday and Sunday.
- **Hailing a cab at the curb after a late landing.** The airport asks you to prebook late flights; a cold call can leave you waiting in a terminal that closes at midnight.
- **Parking a trip in the short-term lot.** It costs $1.50 more a day and $8 more a week than long-term.
- **Counting on a fall or Thanksgiving shuttle.** None has been announced for 2026; the 2023 Thanksgiving shuttle ran once.

## FAQ

**How far is the airport from downtown Ithaca?** About [10 minutes by road](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/), per Downtown Ithaca.

**Can I buy a bus card before I land?** Yes. The airport says a [prepaid card can be bought before arrival](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith), and the TFare app works on board.

**What if my car is stuck in the long-term lot past my return date?** You can leave it as long as you need and pay for the extra days. If the car itself is stranded, the airport asks you to [contact it](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking) at 607-220-4699.

**Which airlines fly from ITH?** Delta flies daily to JFK and United to Washington Dulles, per the [airport's airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). There is no Newark flight.

**Can I rent a car at the airport?** Yes. [Avis, Budget and Hertz](https://www.flyithaca.com/car-rentals) have desks there, open until 6 p.m. on weekdays and shorter hours on weekends. The airport suggests renting for [longer stays or groups](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith).

**Do graduate students ride free to the airport?** PhD students and graduate assistants get a [free OmniRide pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Other graduate students ride free on weekends and after 6 p.m. on weekdays.
