```glance
- TCAT Route 32 runs on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends between the Ithaca airport (ITH) and the Commons; the fare is $1.50.
- Cornell first-years ride free with a Cornell ID; every registered Cornell student rides free on weekends and after 6 p.m. on weekdays.
- No bus serves dawn flights. The terminal opens at 3:30 a.m.; prebook a taxi for early or late flights.
- Taxis: Ithaca Airport Cab 607-252-9052, Collegetown Cab 607-588-8888, Ithaca Dispatch 607-277-7777. No fares are posted; an older airport page lists $12 to $15 base plus $1.50 a bag.
- Driving: long-term parking is $8 a day or $40 a week; short-term is $9.50 a day or $48 a week.
- Free Student Assembly charters to ITH and SYR ran in past finals weeks; none was announced for fall 2026 as of Nov 8.
```

Yes: TCAT [Route 32 runs on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation) between the Ithaca airport and the Commons, and the adult fare is $1.50. No current taxi fare is posted anywhere; the airport lists three cab companies, and its older taxi page gives [$12 to $15 as a base fare plus $1.50 a bag](https://flyithaca.com/?p=13). For a flight before the buses start, book a taxi the day before.

```reader "If you're flying home for every break"
Treat the bus as the daytime option and the taxi as the dawn option. The Student Assembly president said in 2024 that for [5:30 a.m. flights there is "literally no transportation available"](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) beyond paid rides. Call [Ithaca Airport Cab, Collegetown Cab or Ithaca Dispatch](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) a day ahead. ITH has only two scheduled nonstops, [JFK on Delta and Washington Dulles on United](https://flyithaca.com/airlines), so a cancellation can mean a long wait. In finals week, check Campus Groups for a free Student Assembly charter before you pay for a cab.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
ITH connects through JFK or Washington Dulles, and the terminal is open [3:30 a.m. to midnight](https://flyithaca.com). The bus into town takes [exact cash, a credit or debit card, a digital wallet, a Tcard or the TFare app](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), so you do not need US coins. Your free first-year ride needs a Cornell ID; until you have the card, carry $1.50. If you land after the last bus, the costly mistake is waiting at the curb: prebook a taxi before you fly, since the airport tells late arrivals to.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
You pay the public fare: [$1.50 for adults, $0.75 for seniors 60 and over, and free for children under 12 with a paying adult](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). The airport is [about 10 minutes from downtown](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/). Visit Ithaca says you do not need a car for a family weekend, but [Uber and Lyft are limited outside downtown](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/), so book a cab for the return trip instead of hoping for a rideshare at your hotel.
```

```reader "If you're a student who needs accessibility accommodations"
When a fare applies, riders with disabilities pay the [$0.75 reduced fare; tell the driver before you tap](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). The bus does not run for early flights, so the fallback is a taxi. Ask the dispatcher for a wheelchair-accessible vehicle when you prebook; the [airport taxi page](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) does not say which of the three firms has one. Not confirmed: whether Tompkins County paratransit serves the airport.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your Cornell ID covers TCAT [at all hours until Aug 31 of next year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), airport routes included, so a daytime flight costs you nothing to reach. The trap is a 6 a.m. flight booked by a parent who assumes the bus runs: it does not. Prebook a cab, or share one with friends on the same flight. After Aug 31, weekday daytime rides cost $1.50.
```

```reader "If you have a car on campus"
Long-term parking at ITH is [$8 a day or $40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking), against [a $14 daily maximum in Syracuse's economy lots](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/), about $98 a week by our count at seven daily maximums. Use the long-term lot, not short-term ($48 a week). The lot states no maximum stay. If you would rather leave the car in your campus permit area, a cab is the other way to the terminal.
```

## Taking TCAT Route 32 or 72 to the airport

The airport describes both routes as running [between the airport and Ithaca Commons](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith). Route 32 runs Monday to Friday; Route 72 runs Saturday and Sunday. You pay the driver when you board. Cornell's own route list names Route 32 as the ["Airport route"](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting) alongside its campus routes, and Route 10 links downtown and campus if you need to change at the Commons.

| Rider | Weekdays (Route 32) | Weekends (Route 72) |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell first-year with a Cornell ID | Free | Free |
| PhD student or graduate assistant | Free | Free |
| Other registered Cornell student | $1.50 before 6 p.m., free after | Free |
| Adult visitor | $1.50 | $1.50 |
| Senior 60+ or rider with a disability | $0.75 | $0.75 |
| Child under 12 with a paying adult | Free | Free |

Sources for the table: Cornell's [bus privileges page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) for students, and the [Downtown Ithaca fare guide](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/) for everyone else. If your Cornell ID fails at the farebox, the fallback fare is $1.50.

## Early and late flights

The terminal opens at [3:30 a.m. and closes at midnight](https://flyithaca.com). The buses do not cover those edges. For first flights, the 2024 Student Assembly president said there is [no transportation available](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) beyond paid rides. All three cab firms run [24 hours a day](https://flyithaca.com/taxis), and the airport asks you to prebook for late flights.

Example: a sophomore on West Campus has a 6 a.m. Delta flight on a Tuesday in December. No bus runs that early, so they book Collegetown Cab online the evening before. Two checked bags add $3 on the old fare page, making about $18 at that page's $15 minimum, before any zone charge. They confirm the total by phone.

## What a taxi from Cornell to the airport costs

| Company | Phone | What the source says |
|---|---|---|
| Ithaca Airport Cab | 607-252-9052 | Takes all payment types, no card fee |
| Collegetown Cab | 607-588-8888 | Online booking and an app |
| Ithaca Dispatch | 607-277-7777 | Largest fleet; [dispatches University Taxi, Yellow Cab and Cayuga Taxi](https://localwiki.org/ithaca/Ithaca_Dispatch) |

The [airport taxi page](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) lists these three firms and no prices. An older, undated airport page gives Collegetown Cab's [$12 base within 1 mile of the airport, $15 minimum beyond, and $1.50 per bag](https://flyithaca.com/?p=13). Campus is more than a mile away, so the $15 minimum is the one that applies. Treat it as old.

The newest dated sample is from 2017. The Ithacan priced a ride from Ithaca College to the airport at [$19 by taxi, $17.49 by UberX and $27.56 by UberXL](https://theithacan.org/news/ride-hailing-services-slightly-less-expensive-than-taxis-in-ithaca/). The same article says the City sets taxi fares in six zones. Ask for the total when you book.

Uber and Lyft work by app at the airport. Visit Ithaca's May 2026 guide warns that rideshare is [limited outside downtown](https://www.visitithaca.com/blog/post/your-guide-to-a-great-college-family-weekend-in-ithaca/), which matters at 4 a.m. on North Campus.

Not confirmed: any 2026 taxi fare from Cornell to the airport.

## Driving and parking at ITH

| Time parked | Long-term lot | Short-term lot |
|---|---|---|
| First 30 minutes | Free | Free |
| 31 to 60 minutes | $2 | $2 |
| 3 to 4 hours | $5 | $7 |
| 5 to 24 hours | $8 | $9.50 |
| One week | $40 | $48 |

Rates are from the [airport parking page](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking), which shows no date. Take a ticket at the entrance and pay by card at the exit gate. To pay cash, use the machine inside the terminal before you return to the car. The short-term lot is nearest the terminal, with a small covered section. The long-term lot has no stated maximum stay, and no holiday pricing is listed. The parking office is at [607-220-4699](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking).

## Free Student Assembly charters in finals week

The Student Assembly has paid for free charters to both airports at the end of recent terms. In May 2024 it moved [$18,000 for 20 shuttles](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/), with pickups at Baker Flagpole and Robert Purcell Community Center (RPCC). Riders registered by group, first come first served.

- Fall 2025: [Resolution 18 moved $7,853.32](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026) for finals-week charters to ITH and Syracuse.
- Spring 2026: [Resolution 83 moved $10,668.87](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026), free for undergraduates, with sign-up on Campus Groups.
- Fall 2026: no resolution or announcement as of Nov 8.

Holiday shuttles are rarer. In 2023 a Swarthout Coaches shuttle ran to ITH over Thanksgiving for [$14 one way or $28 round trip](https://cornellsun.com/2023/11/19/special-thanksgiving-shuttle-connects-cornell-with-ithaca-tompkins-international-airport/). No 2026 version has been announced.

## Common mistakes

- **Planning a dawn flight around TCAT.** No bus runs for 5:30 a.m. departures. The cost is a missed flight, from an airport with two nonstop routes.
- **Waiting for Route 32 on a Saturday.** Weekend service is Route 72, per the [airport's transit page](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation).
- **Hailing a cab at the curb after a late landing.** The airport asks you to prebook late flights; a cold call can leave you waiting in a terminal that closes at midnight.
- **Parking a trip in the short-term lot.** It costs $1.50 more a day and $8 more a week than long-term.
- **Counting on a fall or Thanksgiving shuttle.** None has been announced for 2026; the 2023 Thanksgiving shuttle ran once.
- **Booking one ITH flight with no backup.** Posters on [College Confidential in 2017](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/air-transportation-to-and-from-ithaca/1892899) said ITH has few flights, so a cancellation means a long wait, and kept a backup airport.

## FAQ

**How far is the airport from downtown Ithaca?** About [10 minutes by road](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/), per Downtown Ithaca.

**Can I buy a bus card before I land?** Yes. The airport says a [prepaid card can be bought before arrival](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith), and the TFare app works on board.

**What if my car is stuck in the long-term lot past my return date?** The lot has no stated maximum stay. The airport asks owners of stranded vehicles to [contact it](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking) at 607-220-4699.

**Which airlines fly from ITH?** Delta flies daily to JFK and United to Washington Dulles, per the [airport's airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). There is no Newark flight.

**Can I rent a car at the airport?** Yes. The airport recommends renting for [longer stays or groups](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith) and says several companies operate there, without naming them.

**Do graduate students ride free to the airport?** PhD students and graduate assistants get a [free OmniRide pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Other graduate students ride free on weekends and after 6 p.m. on weekdays.
