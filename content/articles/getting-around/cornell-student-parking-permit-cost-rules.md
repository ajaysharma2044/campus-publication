A Cornell resident parking permit costs $752.98 a year, and a commuter (B) permit costs $359.77 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Limited permits are available to first-years, but Cornell discourages bringing a car, and permits are sold first come, first served.

```glance
- Resident permits cost $752.98 a year. Commuter (B) and accessible (AP) permits cost $359.77.
- Cornell discourages first-year cars, but limited permits are available.
- Permits run Aug. 16 to Aug. 15. Return one by Mar. 31 for a prorated refund.
- Parking without a permit or payment is a $50 ticket.
```

## What does each Cornell student parking permit cost?

The price depends on where you live and how you reach campus. Cornell sets the rate as of July 1, and the current price shows when you buy.

| Permit | Who can buy it | Price | Where it works | Proration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resident (FH Hasbrouck, ND North, SW South, WD West) | Students in university housing in that area | $752.98 a year | Your signed area, plus A Lot after 2:30 p.m. | Daily from Sept. 1 |
| North Campus resident (ND) | North Campus residents of university housing only; extremely limited and capped | $752.98 a year | Your signed area, plus A Lot at all times | Daily from Sept. 1 |
| Student commuter (B) | Commuters only | $359.77 a year | B Lot always, A Lot after 2:30 p.m. | Daily from Aug. 16 |
| Accessible (AP) | Contact Student Disability Services first | $359.77 a year | Permit-restricted spaces except reserved and MT spaces | From Aug. 16 |
| [Motorcycle (M)](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-motorcycle-scooter-bike-parking/) | Motorcycles, scooters and mopeds | $120.01 a year | Spaces signed for M permits | Monthly, nonrefundable |
| Outer Tier A and E | Graduate assistants with a full assistantship; not resident students | Free | Outer lots; E needs work in its zone | Not applicable |

Source: Cornell Transportation, [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students).

The Hasbrouck (FH) permit also includes OmniRide bus privileges. The commuter lots are Vet College B, TRB, South Morrison, North Morrison and BTI. Every permit is valid through Aug. 15, and Cornell recalls a permit if you lose eligibility ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)).

## Can first-years have a car at Cornell?

Yes, but Cornell steers them away from it. The families page says first-years are [discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits are available](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation).

Cornell houses first-years in [North Campus residence halls and program houses](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates-campus-housing), so the resident permit they can buy is ND. ND is extremely limited: sales are capped, and only North Campus residents of university housing can buy it ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Call Transportation and Delivery Services (TDS) at [607-255-4600](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services), Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to noon, before you drive up.

New first-years also [ride TCAT free from fall registration to Aug. 31 of the next year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). If you are unsure a car is worth it, the quiz below weighs it against buses and shared cars. Our [Cornell parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-parking-guide-campus-collegetown-ithaca/) covers the full year with a car.

```reader "If you're in university housing"
Buy the permit for your own area. The winter rule is different for you: resident-permit holders [park in their own lots as usual](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules), and the car can stay in that area over break. The costly mistake is missing the sale morning, because ND sales are capped.
```

```reader "If you live off campus"
You cannot buy a resident permit. Your choices are the B permit or paying by the day. The B permit works in B Lot at all times, which is useful because B Lot runs a 24-hour parking day. Your home street is a separate question with separate city rules, covered in our [Collegetown and downtown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/).
```

```reader "If you're a graduate student"
Check Workday before you pay. With a full assistantship on the Ithaca main campus, you get the free Outer Tier A and E permits, and a free OmniRide pass too. You can also buy faculty and staff permits. Graduate students who live in university housing are not eligible for the free permits. The mistake that costs most is buying a B permit when your appointment makes one free. Our [grad student transportation guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-cornell-grad-students/) covers the rest.
```

```reader "If you need an accessible permit"
Contact Student Disability Services before the August sale, not after. A friend or relative who parks your car in an accessible space without the permit draws a $150 fine and an automatic tow.
```

```reader "If you're a spring transfer"
Cornell's two-year housing requirement does not apply to transfers who lived on campus for two years at their previous school or who enter as juniors or seniors ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates-campus-housing)). If you live off campus, B is your permit. Bought on Jan. 15, it covers 213 of the permit year's 365 days, about $210 at a straight daily share (our math: the B price x 213 / 365). Checkout shows the exact price.
```

## How do I get a Cornell parking permit?

```figure
kind: steps
title: Getting a Cornell student parking permit
rows:
  - label: Register your plate at MyParking.Cornell.edu
  - label: Check which permit your housing or status allows
  - label: Accessible permit only, contact Student Disability Services first
  - label: Be online when sales open, at 9 a.m. in early August
  - label: Pay at myparking.cornell.edu
  - label: Return the permit by Mar. 31 if you no longer need it
```

1. **Register your plate.** Students must [register cars, motorcycles and scooters](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus) in MyParking.Cornell.edu, even for short-term parking.
2. **Check your eligibility.** Resident permits follow your housing area. B is for commuters. AP starts with Student Disability Services. Graduate assistants check their appointment in Workday.
3. **Be online at sale time.** In 2023, Williams Street lot permits, about $700, [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students).
4. **Pay.** The price drops daily after Sept. 1 for resident permits and after Aug. 16 for commuter permits.
5. **Return it on time.** Cornell refunds a prorated amount only if you return the permit by Mar. 31. Motorcycle and temporary permits get no refund, and no refund is paid in cash.

## Can I park on campus without a permit?

Yes. Register your plate, then pay by the hour or day through ParkMobile. Text PARK to 77223 or use the app. The minimum is 1 hour and the maximum is 8 hours or by the day. Each transaction, extensions included, carries a [$0.45 fee](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).

Prices vary by lot. Cornell's [2026 ParkMobile map](https://fcs.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/2026-08/ParkmobileMap26.pdf) puts edge lots such as University Avenue and East Hill Plaza at $0.75 an hour or $3.00 a day. Premium spots near Willard Straight Hall and Alumni Field cost $3.00 an hour or $24.00 a day. The Tang Welcome Center lot is $1.50 an hour or $10.00 a day.

Daily Decision is parking for anyone who needs occasional access. Cornell advertises it at [as little as $1 a day](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/getting-around/commuter-support-services) in select zones.

Pay attention to the hours ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)). Lots in the resident areas need a permit or payment 24 hours a day. Commuter lots charge Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., year-round, including holidays and breaks. Enforcement runs whenever the university is open and pauses only on official closed staff holidays. No-parking, fire lane, reserved, service and accessible rules are never suspended.

## Is a Cornell parking permit worth it?

The resident permit works out to $62.75 a month, or $2.06 a day over 365 days. The commuter permit is $29.98 a month, under $1 a day. Those figures cover the space only, before gas, insurance and tickets. The resident price has barely moved: the Sun printed it at [$752.86 in 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/), 12 cents less.

A permit buys a lot, not a space beside your class. For the full cost of a car against buses and shared cars, see [should I bring a car to Cornell](/tools/should-i-bring-a-car-cornell-ithaca-cost/).

## What are Cornell's parking fines?

Parking without a permit or payment, the most common ticket, costs $50, plus $10 if it is unpaid after 10 days ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/paying-appealing-citations/citation-fee-schedule)). Our [Cornell and Ithaca parking tickets guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-parking-tickets-fines-how-to-appeal-cornell/) lists every Cornell and city fine and how to appeal.

## FAQ

**Can I buy a permit after the August sale?** Yes, while any are left. Cornell sells permits first come, first served, space available, and prices prorate daily after the dates above.

**Does a Cornell permit cover Collegetown streets?** No. A Cornell permit works only in the campus lots listed for it ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Collegetown streets follow city rules, which our Collegetown guide explains.

**Who do I call about a permit?** TDS, at 116 Maple Avenue, 607-255-4600, Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to noon.

## What to do next

Register your plate in MyParking now, because Cornell requires students to register plates ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus)). Decide by July 1, when Cornell sets rates ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)), whether you need the resident permit, the B permit or neither, and watch TDS announcements for the 2027-28 sale date.
