```glance
- ITH has 2 scheduled nonstops: Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles. Newark ended in 2025.
- SYR advertises nonstops to 27 destinations; its average fare was $445 against $571 at ITH (April to June 2026).
- ITH is about 10 minutes from downtown; TCAT Routes 32 and 72 cost $1.50. SYR is 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min by OurBus at $46.7 to $62.1 each way.
- A week of parking: $40 at ITH long-term, $45 at ELM economy, about $98 at SYR economy.
- ELM is about 36 miles from Ithaca: rent a car or take two CTRAN buses ($8.25).
- Rule of thumb: ITH for short trips and JFK or Dulles connections; SYR for more nonstops, a backup, and many-bag trips by car.
```

Fly into Ithaca (ITH) when your trip connects well through JFK or Washington Dulles, the only two places it flies nonstop, per the [airport's airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). Pick Syracuse (SYR) for a nonstop from its [27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/), lower average fares or a fallback if one ITH flight cancels. Elmira (ELM), 36 miles away, works best with a car.

```reader "If you're flying home to the Midwest, South or West"
Count connections, not miles. From ITH you always change planes at JFK or Dulles. SYR has nonstops on [American to Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas and Miami](https://syrairport.org/destinations/), Delta to Atlanta, Detroit and Minneapolis, and United to Denver. Getting to SYR costs $93 to $124 round trip on [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny) from North or West Campus, against $1.50 each way on TCAT to ITH. The mistake that costs most: one late ITH flight with no backup.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Your long-haul flight lands at JFK or Dulles either way, so ITH is often one short hop more. The Saturday JFK arrivals on Nov 7, 2026 carried [LATAM, Air France and KLM codes](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals), so ask whether your airline can ticket through to ITH. The terminal is open [3:30 a.m. to midnight](https://flyithaca.com), and two of those four arrivals were due after 10 p.m. No bus meets late flights: prebook a taxi from the [airport's list](https://flyithaca.com/taxis). Do not book ITH to Newark. That route [ended in 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk).
```

## The three airports side by side

| | Ithaca (ITH) | Syracuse (SYR) | Elmira Corning (ELM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scheduled nonstops | 2: JFK (Delta), Dulles (United) | 27 destinations, about 8 airlines | Allegiant and Delta |
| Distance from Ithaca | About 10 minutes from downtown | About 50 to 65 miles | About 36 miles, 45 minutes |
| Public link to Cornell | TCAT 32 (weekdays), 72 (weekends), $1.50 | OurBus, 6 days a week, $46.7 to $62.1 | CTRAN 20, then 30, $8.25 |
| Week of parking | $40 (long-term) | About $98 (economy, 7 x $14) | $45 (economy) |

ITH sits at 1 Culligan Drive, [about 10 minutes from downtown](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/). United added a [third daily Dulles flight in October 2025](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/News-articles/Ithaca-Tompkins-International-Airport-to-add-third-daily-flight-to-Washington-D.C.-starting-in-October-2025). The airport later said United would [resume that third flight on March 5, 2026](https://1037qcountry.com/news/258852-ithaca-tompkins-international-airport-resumes-third-daily-flight-to-washington-dulles-international-airport/), so treat Dulles as up to three a day. Delta flies JFK. [American left in 2022](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/News-articles/ITH-Seeking-to-Retain-American-Airlines-Following-its-Announcement-to-Suspend-Area-Flights) and Newark ended in 2025.

SYR is the bigger airport. Its [destinations page](https://syrairport.org/destinations/) lists American, Delta, United and JetBlue plus four leisure carriers: Allegiant, Breeze, Sun Country and Frontier. The drive is [about 1 h 15 min and 65 miles](https://www.ithaca.edu/orientation/family-and-guest-resources/transportation) per Ithaca College.

ELM is at 276 Sing Sing Rd in Horseheads. The [Chemung County airport page](https://www.chemungcountyny.gov/850/Elmira-Corning-Regional-Airport) shows Allegiant and Delta.

## How few flights is "few"?

On Saturday, Nov 7, 2026, four planes landed at ITH: JFK at 12:23 p.m., Dulles at 7:02 p.m., JFK at 10:29 p.m. and Dulles at 11:44 p.m. That is our count from the [Avionio arrivals board](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals), merging codeshare rows into one plane each. It matches the two routes on the airlines page.

Four arrivals means a cancellation can push you to the evening or the next day. Delta's ITH to JFK flight 5265 had [several cancelled dates in July 2026](https://www.avionio.com/en/flight/dl-5265), July 6, 7 and 10, among mostly landed flights.

SYR does not make you storm-proof. In a January 2019 storm, one student's flight into Syracuse was cancelled and other flights were sold out until Monday, per the [Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/). Cornell's own bus service cancelled its Syracuse-to-Cornell run that weekend. What SYR gives you is more flights to rebook onto.

## What the ground leg costs

The fare is only half the trip. Add the ride to the airport, or the parking.

**To ITH.** TCAT [Route 32 runs on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), between the airport and the Commons, for $1.50. Pay with exact cash, a card, a digital wallet, a Tcard or the TFare app. No bus meets the first flights. In 2024 the Student Assembly president said that for 5:30 a.m. flights there is "literally no transportation available" beyond costly rides, per the [Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/). The airport lists three taxi firms that run 24/7 and says to prebook for late flights: Ithaca Airport Cab 607-252-9052, Collegetown Cab 607-588-8888 and Ithaca Dispatch 607-277-7777.

**To SYR.** OurBus runs [six days a week](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk). It picks up on [Jessup Road behind Ganedago Hall, on West Ave across from Baker Flagpole, and at the Cayuga St Garage](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny), and drops at Terminal A. Sunday is cheapest and Monday dearest. Each ticket includes [one suitcase up to 50 lb](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university); extra bags cost $30 each and must be prepaid when you book. Cornell lists one car service, [Big D's Limousines at 607-273-3030 ext 2](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources).

**Parking.** ITH long-term is [$8 a day or $40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking); short-term is $9.50 a day or $48 a week. SYR's [daily maximums](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/) are $14 economy, $16 Blue Lot and $22 garage. Seven days at those maximums comes to about $98 and $154. ELM's economy lot caps at [$9 a day and $45 a week](https://www.chemungcountyny.gov/853/Parking), credit card only.

## Which airport for which trip

| Your trip | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Connecting through JFK or Dulles anyway | ITH | One hop from Ithaca; $1.50 bus |
| Home is a nonstop from SYR (Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Detroit, Miami, Minneapolis) | SYR | Saves a connection; worth $93 to $124 by bus |
| Short weekend trip, light bag | ITH | Ten minutes to town, no 1.5-hour leg each way |
| Driving yourself, gone a week | ITH | $40 parking against about $98 |
| Must arrive on a fixed day in winter | SYR, or ITH with an SYR fallback | More flights to rebook onto |
| A 5:30 a.m. departure | Either, with a booked ride | No bus runs that early |
| Cheap Allegiant fare and a friend with a car | ELM | 36 miles; the bus needs a change in Elmira |

Example: a sophomore whose home is a Dallas nonstop from SYR, flying Thanksgiving week. From ITH they connect at JFK or Dulles, and the ground leg is $3 round trip on TCAT. From SYR they fly nonstop on American, and the ground leg is $93 to $124 on OurBus. If the SYR ticket saves more than that gap, $90 to $121, or they hate connections, SYR wins.

Example: a grad student driving to the airport for a seven-day conference. ITH parking is $40. SYR economy parking is about $98, plus about two and a half hours of driving round trip. Unless the SYR fare saves more than $58 plus the gas, ITH wins.

Fares lean toward SYR. From April to June 2026, the average domestic ticket from ITH cost $571.08 with taxes, per the [Bureau of Transportation Statistics](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/AverageFare/). From SYR it was $445.26, about $126 less, and from ELM $457.48. That SYR saving is about what the OurBus round trip costs. Averages mix every route and date, so price your own dates on both before you decide.

## Elmira: when it makes sense

ELM is [about 36 miles and 45 minutes from Ithaca by road](https://www.airport.guide/kelm/ny/tompkins-co/ithaca). Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) says flights into SYR, ELM or Binghamton need a rental car or a bus. At ELM, [Avis, Budget, Enterprise, Hertz and National](https://www.chemungcountyny.gov/854/Ground-Transportation) rent cars next to baggage claim. The county also lists two taxi firms, Bill's Transportation (607-731-8801) and Terps (607-795-4426), plus Lyft, and recommends booking ahead.

The bus takes two Chemung County Transit (CTRAN) routes. [Route 20](https://ridectran.com/wp-content/uploads/CTRAN-Schedule20-Final-PRD2.pdf) links the airport and the Elmira Transit Center for $2.25. [Route 30](https://ridectran.com/wp-content/uploads/CTRAN-Schedule30-Final-PRD2.pdf) runs from the Transit Center to Cornell's Statler Hall and Vet Tower for $6. It leaves for Cornell at 6:15 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. On weekdays, the 2:05 p.m. Route 20 from the airport reaches the Transit Center at 2:40 p.m. The 3:00 p.m. bus then reaches the Vet Tower at 4:35 p.m. Bring exact cash: drivers carry no change.

Fly ELM with a rental car or a ride arranged, or land in time for that afternoon bus.

```reader "If you're a parent or family visitor"
ITH is the easy landing: about 10 minutes from downtown, [TCAT 32 or 72 for $1.50](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), [three taxi firms](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) and Uber or Lyft. [Avis, Budget and Hertz](https://flyithaca.com/car-rentals) rent cars there. Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) warns that SYR and ELM need a rental car or a bus. From ELM, rent a car at the terminal or arrange a ride for the 36 miles. The costly mistake: landing at ELM expecting a shuttle.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
The Johnson School's [Living in Ithaca page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/) puts SYR about 1 h 15 min away with more airlines, and says ground transport must be arranged in advance. If you drive, ITH long-term parking is $40 a week against about $98 at SYR economy. The free finals-week charters to ITH and SYR are for [undergraduates only](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026). For a conference via Dulles or JFK, ITH is usually simpler. Biggest cost: a week in the SYR garage at about $154.
```

## Free airport charters in finals week

The Student Assembly has paid for free charters to ITH and SYR after finals. The spring 2026 run got [$10,668.87 under Resolution 83](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026), free for undergraduates, with sign-up on Campus Groups, first come first served. The 2024 pilot picked up at [Baker Flagpole and RPCC](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/). The fall 2025 run had its own vote: [$7,853.32 under Resolution 18](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026), adopted Nov 13, 2025.

## Common mistakes

- **Trusting one ITH flight for a deadline.** With four arrivals on a Saturday, a cancellation can cost you most of a day. Keep SYR as a backup.
- **Booking a dawn ITH flight with no ride.** No bus runs for 5:30 a.m. departures. Book a taxi the night before.
- **Landing at ITH near midnight without a taxi booked.** The airport tells late arrivals to prebook, and the last Saturday plane on Nov 7, 2026 was due at 11:44 p.m.
- **Parking a week at SYR.** About $98 in economy and $154 in the garage, against $40 at ITH.
- **Booking ITH to Newark.** The route ended in 2025. Rebooking costs you the new fare.
- **Flying into ELM expecting a shuttle.** The bus to Cornell takes two CTRAN routes, and on weekdays you must leave the airport by 2:05 p.m.

## FAQ

**Does American fly from Ithaca again?** No. The current [airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines) lists only Delta and United.

**How do I get from Syracuse airport to campus?** OurBus drops and picks up at Terminal A and stops at North Campus, West Campus and the Cayuga St Garage. Our [Syracuse airport guide](/getting-around/syracuse-airport-to-cornell-ourbus-car-service/) has the details.

**Can I fly to Binghamton or Rochester instead?** Yes. Both are on [Cornell's list of regional airports](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources), which it gives as a courtesy, not an endorsement. From April to June 2026, the average domestic ticket was $402.47 from Rochester and $579.58 from Binghamton, per [federal data](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/AverageFare/).

**Is it cheaper to fly from a New York City airport?** On average, yes. From April to June 2026, the average domestic ticket was $404.23 from LaGuardia, $495.84 from Newark and $497.23 from JFK, per the same [federal data](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/AverageFare/). All three sit below ITH. Add the bus from Ithaca; see [Ithaca to JFK, LaGuardia or Newark](/getting-around/ithaca-to-jfk-lga-newark-airport-bus/).

**Where do I pay for parking at ITH?** Take a ticket at the entrance and pay by card at the exit gate, or with cash at the machine inside the terminal, per the [parking page](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking).
