```glance
- ITH has 2 scheduled nonstops: Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles. Newark ended in 2025.
- SYR advertises nonstops to 27 destinations, including American, Delta, United and JetBlue hubs.
- ITH is about 10 minutes from downtown; TCAT Routes 32 and 72 cost $1.50. SYR is 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min by OurBus at $46.7 to $62.1 each way.
- Parking a car for a week: $40 at ITH long-term, about $98 at SYR economy (7 times the $14 daily max).
- ELM is about 36 miles from Ithaca with no confirmed scheduled bus. Plan on a car.
- Rule of thumb: ITH for short trips and JFK or Dulles connections; SYR for more nonstops, a backup, and many-bag trips by car.
```

Fly into Ithaca (ITH) when your trip connects well through JFK or Washington Dulles, the only two places it flies nonstop, per the [airport's airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). Pick Syracuse (SYR) when a nonstop from its [27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/) saves you a connection, or when you want a fallback if one ITH flight cancels. Elmira (ELM) works only if someone can drive you the 36 miles.

```reader "If you're flying home to the Midwest, South or West"
Count connections, not miles. From ITH you always change planes at JFK or Dulles. SYR has nonstops on [American to Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas and Miami](https://syrairport.org/destinations/), Delta to Atlanta, Detroit and Minneapolis, and United to Denver. Getting to SYR costs $93 to $124 round trip on [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny) from North or West Campus, against $1.50 each way on TCAT to ITH. The mistake that costs most: one late ITH flight with no backup. 2017 posters on [College Confidential](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/air-transportation-to-and-from-ithaca/1892899) booked Thanksgiving flights in June or July and kept a second airport in mind.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Your long-haul flight lands at JFK or Dulles either way, so ITH is often one short hop more. The Saturday JFK arrivals on Nov 7, 2026 carried [LATAM, Air France and KLM codes](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals), so ask whether your airline can ticket through to ITH. The terminal is open [3:30 a.m. to midnight](https://flyithaca.com), and two of those four arrivals were due after 10 p.m. No bus meets late flights: prebook a taxi from the [airport's list](https://flyithaca.com/taxis). Do not book ITH to Newark. That route [ended in 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk).
```

## The three airports side by side

| | Ithaca (ITH) | Syracuse (SYR) | Elmira Corning (ELM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scheduled nonstops | 2: JFK (Delta), Dulles (United) | 27 destinations, about 8 airlines | Allegiant and Delta shown; no official count |
| Distance from Ithaca | About 10 minutes from downtown | About 50 to 65 miles | About 36 miles, 45 minutes |
| Public link to Cornell | TCAT 32 (weekdays), 72 (weekends), $1.50 | OurBus, 6 days a week, $46.7 to $62.1 | None confirmed |
| Week of parking | $40 (long-term) | About $98 (economy, 7 x $14) | Not posted |

ITH sits at 1 Culligan Drive, [about 10 minutes from downtown](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/). United added a [third daily Dulles flight in October 2025](https://flyithaca.com/news/category/Flights). A later airport post spoke of "resuming" that third flight, so treat Dulles as up to three a day. Delta flies JFK. American left in 2022 and Newark ended in 2025.

SYR is the bigger airport. Its [destinations page](https://syrairport.org/destinations/) lists American, Delta, United and JetBlue plus four leisure carriers: Allegiant, Breeze, Sun Country and Frontier. The drive is [about 1 h 15 min and 65 miles](https://www.ithaca.edu/orientation/family-and-guest-resources/transportation) per Ithaca College; an older Cornell lab document put it nearer 1.5 hours.

ELM is at 276 Sing Sing Rd in Horseheads. The [Chemung County airport page](https://www.chemungcountyny.gov/850/Elmira-Corning-Regional-Airport) shows Allegiant and Delta and lists no Ithaca shuttle.

## How few flights is "few"?

On Saturday, Nov 7, 2026, four planes landed at ITH: JFK at 12:23 p.m., Dulles at 7:02 p.m., JFK at 10:29 p.m. and Dulles at 11:44 p.m. That is our count from the [Avionio arrivals board](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals), merging codeshare rows into one plane each. One day is a small sample, but it matches the airlines page.

Four arrivals means a cancellation can push you to the evening or the next day. Delta's ITH to JFK flight 5265 had [several cancelled dates in July 2026](https://www.avionio.com/en/flight/dl-5265), July 6, 7 and 10, among mostly landed flights. Our tally of student and news accounts finds the same point in 7 sources, from 2014 forum threads to a July 2026 flight tracker: ITH flights are few, and one cancellation means a long wait.

SYR does not make you storm-proof. In December 2016 snow cancelled SYR flights for up to two days, per a [2017 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/air-transportation-to-and-from-ithaca/1892899). In January 2019 Cornell's own Syracuse-to-Cornell run was [cancelled in a storm](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/). What SYR gives you is more flights to rebook onto.

## What the ground leg costs

The fare is only half the trip. Add the ride to the airport, or the parking.

**To ITH.** TCAT [Route 32 runs on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), between the airport and the Commons, for $1.50. Pay with exact cash, a card, a digital wallet, a Tcard or the TFare app. No bus meets the first flights. In 2024 the Student Assembly president said that for 5:30 a.m. flights there is "literally no transportation available" beyond costly rides, per the [Cornell Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/). The airport lists three taxi firms that run 24/7 and says to prebook for late flights: Ithaca Airport Cab 607-252-9052, Collegetown Cab 607-588-8888 and Ithaca Dispatch 607-277-7777.

**To SYR.** OurBus runs [six days a week](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk). It picks up on [Jessup Road behind Ganedago Hall, on West Ave across from Baker Flagpole, and at the Cayuga St Garage](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny), and drops at Terminal A. Sunday is cheapest and Monday dearest. Each ticket includes [one suitcase up to 50 lb](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university); extra bags cost $30 each and must be prepaid when you book. Cornell lists one car service, [Big D's Limousines at 607-273-3030 ext 2](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources), with no price posted.

**Parking.** ITH long-term is [$8 a day or $40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking); short-term is $9.50 a day or $48 a week. SYR posts [daily maximums only](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/): $14 economy, $16 Blue Lot, $22 garage. Seven days at those maximums is our math, about $98 and $154; SYR may sell a weekly rate its page does not show.

## Which airport for which trip

| Your trip | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Connecting through JFK or Dulles anyway | ITH | One hop from Ithaca; $1.50 bus |
| Home is a nonstop from SYR (Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Detroit, Miami, Minneapolis) | SYR | Saves a connection; worth $93 to $124 by bus |
| Short weekend trip, light bag | ITH | Ten minutes to town, no 1.5-hour leg each way |
| Driving yourself, gone a week | ITH | $40 parking against about $98 |
| Must arrive on a fixed day in winter | SYR, or ITH with an SYR fallback | More flights to rebook onto |
| A 5:30 a.m. departure | Either, with a booked ride | No bus runs that early |
| Cheap Allegiant fare and a friend with a car | ELM | 36 miles, no bus |

Example: a sophomore whose home is a Dallas nonstop from SYR, flying Thanksgiving week. From ITH they connect at JFK or Dulles, and the ground leg is $3 round trip on TCAT. From SYR they fly nonstop on American, and the ground leg is $93 to $124 on OurBus. If the SYR ticket saves more than that gap, $90 to $121, or they hate connections, SYR wins.

Example: a grad student driving to the airport for a seven-day conference. ITH parking is $40. SYR economy parking is about $98, plus about two and a half hours of driving round trip. Unless the SYR fare saves more than $58 plus the gas, ITH wins.

Not confirmed: current ITH and SYR airfares side by side. A 2014 poster on [College Confidential](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/traveling-to-and-from-cornell/1599797) said flying from NYC was about $200 cheaper than ITH; 2017 posters called ITH pricier than SYR. Price your own dates on both before you decide.

## Elmira: when it makes sense

ELM is [about 36 miles and 45 minutes from Ithaca by road](https://www.airport.guide/kelm/ny/tompkins-co/ithaca). Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) says flights into SYR, ELM or Binghamton need a rental car or a bus. For ELM there is no confirmed bus. A [Busbud listing](https://www.busbud.com/en/poi/elmira-corning-regional-elm-airport/dr3rf6c) shows OurBus at about $58, but the matching OurBus page looks like a 2020-era route. Chemung County Transit runs a [route 30 between Elmira and Ithaca](https://ridectran.com/wp-content/uploads/CTRAN-Schedule20-Final-PRD2.pdf).

Not confirmed: whether CTRAN route 30 stops at the airport, and whether the $58 OurBus trip still runs.

Fly ELM only with a rental car or a ride already arranged.

```reader "If you're a parent or family visitor"
ITH is the easy landing: about 10 minutes from downtown, [TCAT 32 or 72 for $1.50](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), [three taxi firms](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) and Uber or Lyft. The airport names no rental car companies. Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) warns that SYR and ELM need a rental car or a bus. Plan the 36 miles from ELM by car; no shuttle is confirmed. One 2014 parent on [College Confidential](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/traveling-to-and-from-cornell/1599797) found Syracuse better in winter. The costly mistake: landing at ELM expecting a shuttle.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
The Johnson School's [Living in Ithaca page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/) puts SYR about 1 h 15 min away with more airlines, and says ground transport must be arranged in advance. If you drive, ITH long-term parking is $40 a week against about $98 at SYR economy. The free finals-week charters to ITH and SYR are for [undergraduates only](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026). For a conference via Dulles or JFK, ITH is usually simpler. Biggest cost: a week in the SYR garage at about $154.
```

## Free airport charters in finals week

The Student Assembly has paid for free charters to ITH and SYR after finals. The spring 2026 run got [$10,668.87 under Resolution 83](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026), free for undergraduates, with sign-up on Campus Groups, first come first served. The 2024 pilot picked up at [Baker Flagpole and RPCC](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/). No fall 2026 resolution or announcement had appeared as of Nov 8, 2026, and no Thanksgiving shuttle for 2026 either.

## Common mistakes

- **Trusting one ITH flight for a deadline.** With four arrivals on a Saturday, a cancellation can cost you most of a day. Keep SYR as a backup.
- **Booking a dawn ITH flight with no ride.** No bus runs for 5:30 a.m. departures. Book a taxi the night before.
- **Landing at ITH near midnight without a taxi booked.** The airport tells late arrivals to prebook, and the last Saturday plane on Nov 7, 2026 was due at 11:44 p.m.
- **Parking a week at SYR.** About $98 in economy and $154 in the garage, against $40 at ITH.
- **Booking ITH to Newark.** The route ended in 2025. Rebooking costs you the new fare.
- **Flying into ELM expecting a shuttle.** None is confirmed for the 36 miles.
- **Counting on a 2026 holiday shuttle.** None was announced as of Nov 8, 2026.

## FAQ

**Does American fly from Ithaca again?** The current [airlines page](https://flyithaca.com/airlines) lists only Delta and United. A [February 2026 airport post](https://flyithaca.com/?p=1939) mentioned Charlotte service starting Dec 22, but no Charlotte arrivals appeared on the board on Nov 7, 2026.

**How do I get from Syracuse airport to campus?** OurBus drops and picks up at Terminal A and stops at North Campus, West Campus and the Cayuga St Garage. Our [Syracuse airport guide](/getting-around/syracuse-airport-to-cornell-ourbus-car-service/) has the details.

**Can I fly to Binghamton or Rochester instead?** Both are on [Cornell's list of regional airports](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources), which it gives as a courtesy, not an endorsement. Their flights and ground links are not covered here.

**Is it cheaper to fly from a New York City airport?** A 2014 poster said NYC was about $200 cheaper than ITH. You then need a bus to Manhattan; see [Ithaca to JFK, LaGuardia or Newark](/getting-around/ithaca-to-jfk-lga-newark-airport-bus/).

**Where do I pay for parking at ITH?** Take a ticket at the entrance and pay by card at the exit gate, or with cash at the machine inside the terminal, per the [parking page](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking). The long-term lot has no stated maximum stay.
