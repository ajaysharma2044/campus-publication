```glance
- Anyone can ride: C2C is open to the public, family and visitors included. Only the fare depends on who you are.
- One way to NYC: $93.50 with a NetID, $101 for everyone else, $86 for department-paid Cornell business travel.
- Since Nov 4, 2026 every rider needs a new account. Trips through Dec 23 book on the "2026" link; trips from Jan 6, 2027 on the "2027" link.
- Cancel 8 or more hours ahead: $10 a seat. Inside 8 hours: $30. Miss the bus: the whole fare is gone.
- Two suitcases below deck plus two small carry-ons each. Bring a photo ID that matches the booking.
- Wheelchair users call 607-254-8747 at least 10 business days ahead. Riders with a car park free at B Lot by emailing c2cbus@cornell.edu.
```

Anyone can ride Cornell's Campus-to-Campus bus between Ithaca and Manhattan: since the [new reservation system launched on Nov 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), it is open to the public, family and visitors included. Students, faculty and staff pay [$93.50 one way when they sign in with a valid NetID, and everyone else pays $101](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Make a new account first, even if you rode last year, and book on the link that matches your travel date.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Board at North Campus, [217 Jessup Road, in the shelters in front of Robert Purcell Community Center](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations). Make your new account with Cornell single sign-on. A plain account charges $101, not $93.50: $7.50 more every trip. Book Thanksgiving now. A full bus means trying another time; the office [adds trips only "when feasible"](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4). For winter break, the last listed departure is Dec 23 and the first one back is Jan 6, 2027. Get to the stop on time: once the bus leaves its last stop, the $93.50 is gone.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
You can ride without a Cornell connection at [$101 one way or $202 round trip](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). One reservation can hold several passengers, and you pick the seats. Children [under 2 ride free on a lap; from age 2 each child pays the full fare](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). A stroller that takes a seat counts as a passenger. Riders 12 and under travel with an adult. Every adult needs a [photo ID with the name on the reservation](https://c2cbus.ipp.cornell.edu/Start.aspx). The Cornell Club stop is a pickup point only: the club itself is for members and guests.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student or Cornell employee"
A trip billed to your department as approved Cornell business travel costs [$86 one way, against $93.50 for a personal trip](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Procurement cards (PCards) are not accepted. Cornell's travel office calls C2C the [preferred mode between Ithaca and the NYC campuses](https://finance.cornell.edu/travel/transportation), and university pricing needs a profile on the C2C site. If you are linked to Weill, the system does not recognize a CWID, so request a NetID from the IT Service Desk before you book. Each child aged 2 or older needs a paid seat.
```

```reader "If you use a wheelchair or need access help"
Do not book online and hope. Call [607-254-8747 at least two weeks (10 business days) before you travel](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-additional-information) to arrange wheelchair access. [ADA service animals are allowed](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) on board. The other rules still apply: be at the stop 10 minutes early, and cancel or change only in the reservation system. For a stop near parking, B Lot at 930 Campus Road is the last Ithaca pickup, about 30 minutes after North Campus.
```

```reader "If you have a car on campus"
Leave it at B Lot, the last Ithaca stop. Since Oct 10, 2024, [C2C riders are exempt from B Lot parking fees if they email c2cbus@cornell.edu with their plate and travel dates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488). The FAQ also lets riders without a permit [buy temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days at a time](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions), and student SC and B permits are valid there. Send the email before you leave, not from the bus. Board at the B Lot shelter on the lot's southeast side.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
C2C's New York stops are in Manhattan, at [Weill Cornell (1300 York Ave) and the Cornell Club (6 East 44th St)](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations), not at JFK or Newark. Bring your passport or another photo ID in the booking name. Leave a long gap between landing and the bus: a missed C2C keeps the $93.50 fare. Students in a [2019 Cornell Daily Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/) described a 2 p.m. JFK landing with a sprint for a 4:30 p.m. bus, and one missed a campus bus by five minutes. Nothing is listed from Dec 24 to Jan 5.
```

## Who can ride C2C

Per the Oct 22, 2026 notice, C2C [serves the public, including family and visitors](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4). Students, faculty and staff sign in with Cornell single sign-on (their Cornell email). Public riders make a standard account.

There is no separate student fare. Students, faculty and staff all pay $93.50 with a valid NetID on the account, per the [rates page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates).

Cornell started the service in 2004. It runs [seven days a week year-round except holidays, with Wi-Fi, a bathroom and snacks](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting) on board.

## How much it costs

| Who | One way | Round trip |
|---|---|---|
| Public (no Cornell sign-in) | $101 | $202 |
| Student, faculty or staff with a NetID | $93.50 | $187 |
| Cornell or Weill department-paid business travel | $86 | $172 |
| Child under 2 on a lap | Free | Free |
| Child 2 and older | Full fare | Full fare |

All three fares on the [rates page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) already include a temporary $6 fuel and toll surcharge per one-way ticket, with no end date posted. A round trip is exactly two one-way fares, so there is no round-trip discount. C2C runs only between Ithaca and New York City: the rates page lists no Boston or Philadelphia route.

C2C is not the cheapest way to Manhattan. Commercial buses advertise far lower floor fares; our [NYC to Cornell bus guide](/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/) compares them side by side. You pay for an East Side drop, two checked suitcases and a Cornell-run coach.

## How to book, step by step

1. **Make a new account.** [Everyone, including past riders, needs a new account](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4) in the system that went live on Nov 4, 2026. It works in a browser; there is no app. Schedules, rates, stops and fee rules did not change with the switch.
2. **Pick the right link.** The [C2C page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service) has two. "2026 Travel Reservations" covers departures through Dec 23, 2026, plus changes to bookings you already have. "2027 Travel Reservations" covers departures from Jan 6, 2027. No departures are listed between Dec 24 and Jan 5.
3. **Choose the trip and seats.** The new system adds seat selection and several passengers on one reservation.
4. **Check the day's times.** Read the [departure schedule](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule) for your exact date. Not every time slot runs every day.
5. **Arrange extras early.** Email c2cbus@cornell.edu for free B Lot parking. Call 607-254-8747 ten business days ahead for wheelchair access.

Questions go to [c2cbus@cornell.edu or the help portal at tdx.cornell.edu](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources), or by phone to 607-254-8747 (also written 607-254-TRIP). The [office at 209 Solidago Rd is open 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-and-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service).

## Where it stops and when

| Stop | Where to wait | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| North Campus, Ithaca | 217 Jessup Road, shelters in front of Robert Purcell Community Center | First Ithaca pickup |
| Sage Hall, Ithaca | 114 Feeney Way, TCAT shelter near East Ave and Campus Rd, across from Statler Hall | About 15 minutes after North Campus |
| B Lot, Ithaca | 930 Campus Road, shelter on the southeast side of B Lot | About 30 minutes after North Campus |
| Weill Cornell, NYC | 1300 York Ave, main entrance across from 69th St; wait in the lobby | First NYC pickup |
| Cornell Club, NYC | 6 East 44th St between Fifth and Madison | Last NYC pickup |

Addresses are from the [stop locations page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations). The Cornell Club pickup has moved for construction: as of Nov 8, 2026 the [booking site said loading is on Fifth Ave between 43rd and 44th St "until further notice"](https://c2cbus.ipp.cornell.edu/Start.aspx).

The Aug 1, 2026 update [dropped the NYC F Train stop at 64th St and 3rd Ave](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17782) and adjusted times. Some older Cornell pages still list it.

The [schedule page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule) shows North Campus departure slots at 5:30, 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. Weill slots run from 5:45 a.m. to 5:45 p.m. every two hours, and Cornell Club slots from 6:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. The ride takes about 4.5 to 5 hours from the last pickup.

Not confirmed: which slots run on which weekday. Check the live table for your date.

## Bags, kids, pets and ID

The [policies page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) sets these rules:

- **Luggage:** 2 suitcases below deck and 2 small carry-ons per passenger. Garment bags go in the coat closet. C2C is not liable for lost items.
- **Bikes:** only in a bike box, which counts as 1 of your 2 suitcases.
- **Children:** under 2 ride free on a lap. From 2, a paid seat, with no child discount. Anyone 12 or under travels with an adult; the same page says unaccompanied riders under 13 may be refused.
- **Pets:** ADA service animals are allowed. Companion pets stay in a carrier on your lap or at your feet; a carrier on a seat needs a paid seat.
- **ID:** a [photo ID with the name on the reservation](https://c2cbus.ipp.cornell.edu/Start.aspx).

## Changes, cancellations and missed buses

| What you do | When | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Cancel | 8 or more hours before departure | $10 per seat |
| Cancel | Inside 8 hours | $30 per seat |
| Change date or time | 8 or more hours before | Allowed |
| Change date or time | Inside 8 hours | $30 per seat per change |
| Change only the stop, same bus | Any time | Free |
| Give your seat to someone else | Before travel | Passenger substitution allowed |
| Miss the bus | After it leaves its last stop | Full fare kept |

All of it happens [only in the reservation system](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). Phone calls, voicemails and emails do not cancel a seat.

Be at the stop 10 minutes early. [Drivers do not leave early](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions). Once the bus leaves its last stop, your trip is forfeited.

Example: a sophomore books a $187 round trip for Thanksgiving and misses the bus out of Ithaca. The $93.50 outbound fare is gone. The return seat still stands. If they skip it without cancelling, a second $93.50 is lost. Cancelling 8 or more hours ahead costs a $10 fee instead.

## C2C in a snowstorm

In the Dec 1-2, 2019 snowstorm, C2C kept running while OurBus cancelled most of its trips. The [Cornell Daily Sun reported](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/12/before-classes-were-cancelled-cornellians-attempted-to-return-to-ithaca-at-all-costs) that OurBus cancelled most of its trips, and one student took C2C because otherwise he would not have reached campus until Wednesday. That is one storm, not a promise.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing in with a plain account.** You pay $101 instead of $93.50: $7.50 more each way, $15 a round trip.
- **Using last year's login or the wrong link.** Old accounts do not carry over. A Jan 6, 2027 return is not on the "2026" link.
- **Arriving after the bus leaves.** The full fare is kept: $93.50 for a student, $101 for a parent.
- **Forgetting the return leg.** A missed outbound does not cancel it; skip it without cancelling and a second fare is gone.
- **Cancelling late or by email.** Inside 8 hours the fee is $30 a seat, not $10. An email or voicemail cancels nothing.
- **Waiting at the old F Train stop.** It closed on Aug 1, 2026. Use Weill or the Cornell Club.
- **Booking a wheelchair trip online only.** Access needs a call at least 10 business days ahead.

## Old numbers that still show up in search

- **"C2C costs $75."** That was the [2011 fare](https://news.cornell.edu/node/269896). Today's fares are $86 to $101.
- **"C2C needs a Cornell connection."** That comes from a [2005 Chronicle article](https://news.cornell.edu/node/282639), when the standard fare was $99 one way. The Oct 2026 notice says the public can ride.
- **"There is a $50 student fare."** That was a [2020 promotion that ran through April 5](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2020-03-14/c2c-student-discounted-rate-through-april-5). No student-only fare exists now.

## FAQ

**Can I book a seat for a friend who is not at Cornell?** Yes. One reservation can hold several passengers, and anyone not on a Cornell account pays the [public fare of $101](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Each rider shows a photo ID matching the booking.

**Does C2C go to the NYC airports?** No. The only New York stops are [Weill Cornell and the Cornell Club](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations). Our [Ithaca to JFK, LaGuardia or Newark guide](/getting-around/ithaca-to-jfk-lga-newark-airport-bus/) covers the airport buses.

**Can I send a package on C2C?** Only for a Cornell department or Weill account: [$20 a package, up to 8 cubic feet](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-additional-information). Students cannot ship personal boxes this way.

**Can I bring my bike?** Only in a bike box, and it [counts as one of your two suitcases](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).

**What if every bus on my day is full?** Check other departures. The office [may add trips when feasible](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), but nothing guarantees one.
