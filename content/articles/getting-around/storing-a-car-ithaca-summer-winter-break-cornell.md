Over winter or summer break, leave the car in your campus permit area if you live in university housing ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)). If you live off campus, use a driveway, a lease space or a paid lot. Never leave it on a city street.

```glance
- University housing residents can leave a car in their permit area year-round.
- A car on a city street over break can be towed and impounded.
- C2C riders can park in B Lot free if they email Cornell with plate and dates.
- Airport lots and city garages cost money; compare them below.
```

## Which option fits you?

If you hold a resident permit, use your permit area. If you ride Cornell's Campus-to-Campus (C2C) bus, use B Lot. If you rent off campus, use your lease space or a garage you have asked first. If you fly from an airport, price its lot.

| Option | Cost | Best for | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your resident permit area (FH, ND, SW, WD) | No added cost | Students in university housing | Policy covers students in university housing |
| B Lot, riding C2C | Free on travel dates | C2C riders | You must email plate and dates |
| B Lot with a B permit | Your permit | Commuters | Break-long stays are not addressed |
| City garage | $2.25 an hour, $18 a day at Dryden Road | Short trips | City says no long-term parking |
| Dryden Road monthly permit | $232 a month | Collegetown renters | 30-day blocks, no guaranteed space |
| ITH airport lot | $8 a day, $40 a week | Fliers from Ithaca | Rates are posted without a year |
| City street | Ticket, tow, impound | Nobody | Treated as abandoned |

Sources: Cornell [winter parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules), [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) and the [Ithaca airport](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking).

## Can I leave my car on campus over winter break?

Yes, if you live in university housing. Cornell says students in university housing can leave cars in their permit area, and the policy applies year-round. Resident-permit holders in FH, ND, SW and WD park in their own lots as usual. The winter closing checklist says to leave cars only in designated on-campus lots.

All undergraduate residences close Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, at 2 p.m. and reopen Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027, at 9 a.m. ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice)). That is 30 days. Our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) covers the overnight rule from Dec. 1.

If you ride C2C, you can park in B Lot at no charge by emailing c2cbus@cornell.edu with plate and vehicle details ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488)). Riders without a permit can also buy temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days at a time ([C2C FAQ](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions)).

```reader "If you rent off campus"
The campus policy covers students in university housing, so it may not cover you. The student parking page says a B permit is valid in B Lot at all times, but it does not address break-long stays, so ask Transportation and Delivery Services at 607-255-4600. Otherwise use a driveway, a lease space or a garage. The city says its garages offer no long-term parking and refers drivers to Premium Parking, (607) 256-7275 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). Our [Collegetown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/) lists garage prices.
```

## What if I am flying out of Ithaca or Syracuse?

An airport lot is the plain answer for a trip that starts at a gate. Ithaca Tompkins long-term parking costs $8 a day for 5 to 24 hours or $40 a week ([Ithaca airport parking](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking)). The 30 days of winter closing is 4 weeks and 2 days. At those posted rates that is $160 for the weeks plus $16 for the extra days, or $176, if the weekly rate applies to a stay that long. Syracuse charges a daily maximum of $22 in the garage, $16 in the Blue Lot and $14 in the Red and Purple economy lots ([Syracuse airport](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/)).

## Can I leave a car in Ithaca over summer?

Cornell's published rule covers students in university housing and winter break. Move-out ends when buildings close, as at 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, in 2026 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)). Permits run through Aug. 15, so a permit-holder's car is still within its permit year. Cornell does not say whether a car can stay in a permit area once you have moved out of housing, so ask TDS before move-out. Our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) lists permit dates.

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over break"
Your car stays too, and break nights still count. Winter break housing is available for students who apply by Dec. 1. Our [staying over breaks guide](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) covers housing and food.
```
