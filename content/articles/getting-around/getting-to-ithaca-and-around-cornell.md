```glance
- NYC by bus: commercial seats from $25 to $43.98 one way (Nov 8 floors); Cornell's C2C is $93.50 with a NetID, $101 without.
- Flying: ITH has two nonstops (JFK and Washington Dulles); Syracuse has 27 destinations and an OurBus link at $46.70 to $62.10.
- TCAT: free at all hours for first-years, transfers, PhD students and grad assistants; everyone else free after 6 p.m. weekdays and weekends, otherwise $1.50 or a $200 OmniRide pass.
- Car: a resident permit is $752.98 a year, commuter $359.77; first-years are discouraged from bringing one.
- Breaks: no classes Nov 25 to 29, 2026, so book Thanksgiving now; winter break housing is $125 a week or $500, apply by Dec 1.
- New this month: every C2C rider needs a new account (Nov 4); OurBus's downtown stop moves to 235 S. Cayuga St (Nov 9).
```

Most Cornell students get home by bus and get around on foot and TCAT, so a first-year needs no car: TCAT is [free at all hours with a Cornell ID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) until Aug 31 of the next year. To New York City, the cheapest seats on OurBus, FlixBus, Greyhound and others ran [$25 to $43 one way on Nov 8](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/), while Cornell's own Campus-to-Campus (C2C) coach costs [$93.50 with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). Book Thanksgiving now (no classes [Nov 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)), and open a new C2C account first, because [every rider needs one since Nov 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4).

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your Cornell ID rides TCAT free at all hours until Aug 31, 2027, so skip OmniRide. Leave the car at home: Cornell discourages first-year cars, and a resident permit costs $752.98. For NYC, compare C2C ($93.50 from 217 Jessup Road on North Campus, two suitcases below deck) with OurBus, FlixBus and Greyhound from North and West Campus (floors $25 to $43.98 on Nov 8). By our math, four round trips cost $748 on C2C and $264 at the OurBus floor. Book Thanksgiving this week: Wednesday Nov 25 is a named peak day. Make a new C2C account with Cornell sign-on, or you pay $101. The costliest slip: miss a C2C bus and the $93.50 fare is gone, while your return stays booked.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or spring entrant"
Transfers count as first-years for TCAT and ride free at any time, per the [Cornell Sun's November 2024 report](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free); confirm with Transportation Services at 607-255-4600. A spring entrant's free pass runs [Jan 1 to Dec 31 of the same year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), not to August, with no extensions. Spring classes start Jan 25, 2027. Arriving in January, you land inside two winter parking seasons: Cornell's overnight rules (Dec 1 to Apr 1) and the city's odd/even rule (Nov 1 to Apr 1). C2C trips from Jan 6, 2027 book on the separate "2027" link. Do not buy OmniRide while the free pass covers you: $200, refundable only to Mar 31, less $40.
```

```reader "If you're an upper-year without a car"
Since fall 2024 you ride TCAT free only after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend. Daytime weekday rides cost $1.50, or $200 a year for OmniRide, which pays off after about 134 paid rides, roughly 4 or 5 a week. For errands, Ithaca Carshare's student plans cost $30 a year plus $9 an hour or $310 plus $6 an hour; under about 93 hours a year, the $30 plan is cheaper. Bikeshare costs $1.50 a ride plus $0.25 a minute. Late at night, Route 92 (the Blue Light bus) runs 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. The common mistake is believing TCAT is still free all day.
```

```reader "If you're a student with a car on campus"
A resident permit is $752.98 a year, about $2.06 a day, and works in your housing area plus A Lot after 2:30 p.m. Sales for 2026-27 opened Tue Aug 4 at 9 a.m., first come first served; in 2023 one lot sold out within hours. From Dec 1 to Apr 1, when snow is forecast, overnight parking off your permit area is limited to listed bays and garages. Over winter break, leave the car in your permit area, never on a city street: the city can ticket, tow and treat it as abandoned. Fines start at $50; a fire lane or accessible space costs $150 plus an automatic tow.
```

```reader "If you're an off-campus renter"
You cannot buy a resident permit. The commuter B permit is $359.77 a year (B Lot always, A Lot after 2:30 p.m.). At home, the [city residential permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) is $45 a year, valid to July 31; bring your registration and lease to the City Clerk at 108 E Green St, since a landlord cannot get it for you. City garages charge $2.25 an hour, with a $12 daily cap at Green and Cayuga and $18 at Dryden Road. Route 10 links the Commons and campus. Routes 14S and 83 to West Hill and Cayuga Heights have been suspended since fall 2023, so do not plan a commute on them.
```

```reader "If you're a grad or professional student"
PhD students and graduate assistants get a [free TCAT OmniRide pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so the 6 p.m. limit does not apply. A full assistantship on the Ithaca campus also brings [free Outer Tier A and E permits](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), unless you live in university housing. The [Johnson MBA page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/) says TCAT is free in the first year, then free only after 6 p.m. weekdays and all weekend. Department-paid C2C travel costs [$86 one way](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates), against $93.50 personal. Weill staff need a NetID, because the booking system does not recognize CWIDs. Do not pay $359.77 for a commuter permit if your assistantship makes a permit free.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Most international students land at a New York airport and take a bus up, or connect to Ithaca on Delta from JFK or United from Dulles. Ithaca's Newark flight ended in 2025. No direct bus runs from Ithaca to JFK or LaGuardia, so get into Manhattan and board there; Greyhound and FlixBus leave from Port Authority, 625 8th Ave. FlixBus also goes direct to Newark Penn Station ($40 to $60 in a Nov 8 sample). Leave a long gap between landing and the bus: a missed C2C bus forfeits the fare. Ithaca Carshare [does not accept an International Driving Permit](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) as a driving record. If you stay for winter break, apply for break housing by Dec 1 ($125 a week or $500).
```

```reader "If you're flying home within the US"
Ithaca (ITH) is about 10 minutes from downtown but has two scheduled routes, so one cancellation can strand you. Syracuse (SYR) has 27 nonstop destinations, and OurBus runs there from campus six days a week for $46.70 to $62.10 each way. A parked week costs $40 at ITH long-term and about $98 at SYR economy, our seven times its $14 daily maximum. No bus meets a 5:30 a.m. flight, so prebook a taxi. Student Assembly has funded free finals-week charters to ITH and SYR for undergraduates; none was announced for fall 2026 as of Nov 8.
```

```reader "If you're headed to Boston, Philadelphia, NJ, DC or Toronto"
C2C goes only to Manhattan. FlixBus is the main direct operator beyond NYC: Boston in 8 h 05 min to 12 h 05 min (Nov samples $46 to $101), Philadelphia from about $41 to $44, Newark Penn $40 to $60, Washington from $49 to $53 in 10 to 11 hours. For Toronto, OurBus lists fares from $55 (6 h 40 min) and [Greyhound runs two buses a day from about $43](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/toronto-on-ithaca-ny). Amtrak leaves from Syracuse, not Ithaca. Thanksgiving week raised the cheapest DC fares by 37.9% in a Nov 8 sample, so book early.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Take the cheapest commercial bus, not C2C: on Nov 8 the six NYC carriers' cheapest seats ran $25 to $43, and C2C at $93.50 is about three times the cheapest seat. Four NYC round trips cost $216 to $264 at advertised floors against $748 on C2C. Ride TCAT in the free hours (after 6 p.m. weekdays and all weekend) and pay $1.50 otherwise. OurBus cancellations return credit, not cash, so book once your plans are firm. Watch Campus Groups for free Student Assembly airport charters in finals week. Compare a trip home with winter break housing at $125 a week or $500. [Ithaca Bikeshare](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org) has an Easy Access plan for GO ITHACA members at or below 150% of the federal poverty guideline.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over a break"
Winter break housing is open to students returning to campus housing for spring: apply between Oct 1 and Dec 1; it costs $125 per week or $500 for the full break. Ten buildings stay open, among them Mews, Toni Morrison, Hu Shih and Cascadilla. ```reader "If you ride a bike, e-bike or scooter"
```

**The money.** "From" fares are the cheapest seat on the cheapest day, not what most riders pay. On Nov 8, Wanderu's averages ran from $44.54 (Coach USA) to $82.31 (Greyhound). C2C costs about three times the cheapest seat, by our division of $93.50 by fares of $27 to $33. It costs about 1.5 times a typical average fare. Wanderu claims booking about 14 days ahead is cheapest. Trip times overlap: C2C takes about 4.5 to 5 hours from B Lot, and OurBus lists 4 h 10 min to 7 h. Example: you go home to Manhattan four times this year. On C2C that is 4 x $187 = $748, about the price of a resident parking permit. At the OurBus floor of $33 each way it is $264, and at the FlixBus floor of $27 it is $216. Floors understate what most riders pay.

**Booking C2C under the new system.**

1. Make a new account. Sign in with Cornell single sign-on for the $93.50 fare; a standard account pays $101. C2C is open to the public, family included.
2. Pick the link by date: through Dec 23, 2026 on the 2026 link, from Jan 6, 2027 on the 2027 link.

Cancelling costs [$10 a seat 8 or more hours ahead, $30 inside 8 hours](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). Once the bus leaves its last stop, the full fare is kept, and a missed outbound leg does not cancel your return.
6. Driving to the bus: park in B Lot free by [emailing c2cbus@cornell.edu with your plate and dates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488).
7. Wheelchair users call 607-254-8747 at least 10 business days ahead, per the [C2C information page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-additional-information).

## Breaks: when to book, and whether to go home

**Thanksgiving: book now.** There are no classes [Wednesday Nov 25 through Sunday Nov 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar); Thanksgiving Day is Thursday Nov 26. OurBus names [the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, the last day of December finals and the Friday spring break begins](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269) as its peak days. This year that Wednesday is the first day of the break.

In a Nov 8 capture of three Ithaca routes (Binghamton, Rochester and DC; 51 priced dates), Thanksgiving-week floors ran 28% to 38% above earlier November days, a mean of 33.3% by our arithmetic. On the [Washington route](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/washington-dc) the cheapest fares averaged $93.40 that week against $67.71 before it. NYC was not sampled, so treat "a third more" as a guide, not a quote. Wanderu flagged Nov 19 as the cheapest November date to New York.

Example: an OurBus round trip at the $33 floor is $66. A third more is about $88.

## Car or no car at Cornell

- **First-year:** no. The [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) says first-years are discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits exist, and TCAT is free for you.
- **Upper-year on campus:** only if you drive often enough to justify $752.98 a year plus winter rules.
- **Off campus or a grad student:** check whether an assistantship makes a permit free, then compare with Carshare.

**How a permit works.** Register your plate in MyParking.Cornell.edu, which is [required even for short-term parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus). 2026-27 sales [opened Tue Aug 4 at 9 a.m.](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809), first come first served, for permits valid Aug 16, 2026 to Aug 15, 2027. Outside your area, pay by ParkMobile, with a [$0.45 fee per transaction](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).

**Fines.** Parking without a permit or payment costs [$50, plus $10 if unpaid after 10 days](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/). A fire lane, accessible space or life-safety zone costs $150 with automatic towing. Breaking winter rules costs $50.

**Even with a permit, spots are scarce.** In a [2023 Sun story](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students), Williams Street permit holders paid about $700, the lot sold out within hours, and Deana Druz '24 called finding a space a daily "treasure hunt." In a [2016 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/should-i-bring-a-car/1811430), several posters told an incoming first-year to skip the car.

**Carshare instead.** [Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters) runs about 20 vehicles, with four pickup spots on campus and four in Collegetown and nearby. Cornell subsidizes the student plans. You must be 18 with a license held two years; [under 21, you need your own insurance with at least $300,000 per accident liability](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility). It's My Car adds a $20 sign-up per driver, plus 8% sales tax and a 12% rental tax. Under about 93 hours a year, the $30 plan beats the $310 plan, by our comparison of the two price lines.

## TCAT: free, $1.50 a ride, or OmniRide

Your status decides what you pay. These are Cornell's rules on its [bus passes page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

| You are | Free when | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| Fall-entry first-year (and transfers, per the Sun) | All hours, to Aug 31 of the next year | Not applicable |
| Spring entrant | All hours, Jan 1 to Dec 31 | Not applicable |
| PhD student or graduate assistant | All hours (free OmniRide) | Not applicable |
| Any other registered student | After 6 p.m. weekdays, all Saturday and Sunday | $1.50 a ride, or OmniRide at $200 a year |
| Visitor | Children under 12 with a paying adult | $1.50; $0.75 for seniors 60+ and riders with disabilities |

Visitor fares are from [Downtown Ithaca's fare guide](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/). The transfer line comes from the [Sun's November 2024 report](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free), which also explains the change: a farebox glitch had let all students ride free until Cornell updated the eligibility list on Oct 16, 2024.

**OmniRide math.** The $200 pass pays for itself after 134 paid daytime rides, our $200 divided by $1.50. That is about 4.5 rides a week over an assumed 30-week year. It is prorated monthly, sold on MyParking.Cornell.Edu, refundable until Mar 31 less $40, and can take 24 hours to activate.

**Routes students use.** Routes 81 and 82 run on campus. Route 10 links the Commons and campus; it ran every 6 to 12 minutes on weekdays in summer 2024. Route 30 goes to the Ithaca Mall, 15 is the Southside Shopper, and 32 (weekdays) and 72 (weekends) serve the airport. Night routes are 90, 92 and 93; Route 92, the Blue Light bus, runs [6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. nightly, year-round](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). Routes 14S and 83 have been [suspended since fall 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/).

**Reliability.** Late buses are the complaint that comes up most, raised in 10 separate articles and documents from 2019 to March 2026. In 2022, Coco Wen '26 told the Sun about long waits because Google Maps times were wrong, then paying for an Uber ([Sun 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/06/tcat-implements-massive-service-cut-due-to-driver-shortage/)). Student government's [March 2026 resolution](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026) called the buses late and unreliable and asked Cornell to fund free passes for all undergraduates and graduate workers; no outcome has been reported. Track buses in Bus Tracker or MyStop. If the farebox rejects your ID in the free hours, drivers override it; otherwise pay $1.50.

## Walking, bikes, e-bikes and bikeshare

- **On foot:** Collegetown to central campus is [15 to 20 minutes through the Arts Quad](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/10/embrace-your-inner-intellectual-go-on-a-midnight-walk). The Commons is about 2 miles, about 40 minutes at 3 mph by our estimate, mostly uphill coming back.
- **Own bike:** register it free, lock it to a rack with a U-lock, and use TCAT's front racks for the hills.
- **Occasional rides:** Ithaca Bikeshare, at $1.50 to start a ride plus $0.25 a minute.

Cornell [urges free registration](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus) of bikes, e-bikes and scooters. Park only at designated racks; bikes locked to railings, signposts, trees or poles can be impounded. Theft is real: campus police counted [51 bike and scooter thefts worth at least $41,177 in fall 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far). In that story, Reid Fleishman '25 described moving to a cable, a U-lock and two AirTags after a $400 bike was taken.

An e-bike on campus must have a motor of [no more than 750 W, be no wider than 36 inches and have working pedals](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/cycling-campus); an e-scooter must weigh under 100 pounds and top out at 20 mph. Do not charge either battery in a residence room, office or academic building.

[Ithaca Bikeshare](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org) runs pedal-assist e-bikes. A day pass is $25 and a monthly plan $60, and a trip that does not end at a hub costs $1.50 more. A 20-minute trip costs $6.50 before tax, by our arithmetic. No student discount is listed.

## Move-in day: arriving and unloading

1. After Aug 1, print your Move-In Ticket from the Housing and Dining Portal. It names your parking area.

## Winter: storms, snow and the return trip

The two riskiest trips of the year are the return after Thanksgiving and the trip back in January.

- **Dec 1 to 2, 2019:** OurBus cancelled trips, a ShortLine bus slid off the road and its replacement took about 9 hours against the usual 4 to 5, and one student found C2C the only way back, per the [Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/12/before-classes-were-cancelled-cornellians-attempted-to-return-to-ithaca-at-all-costs).
- **January 2019:** Coach USA cancelled New York departures, Cornell's Syracuse run was cancelled, and the governor banned buses on most highways ([Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/)). Singaporean students stuck in New York found beds through the Singapore student associations at NYU and Columbia.

Before a forecast storm, travel Saturday night or early Sunday, as a student forecaster [told the Sun in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/30/under-heavy-snow-forecast-university-mum-on-class-cancellation). That weekend, OurBus cancelled Sunday trips and added Saturday ones, and ShortLine moved departures earlier. The university did not announce a class cancellation before students were already on the road. In January, travel a day early if a storm is forecast.

**Driving and parking in snow.** From Dec 1 to Apr 1, when snow is forecast, overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. is [limited to listed bays and garages](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules): A Lot's west and mid bays, B Lot's east and west bays, the Forest Home garage, Hoy Road garage except the roof, and others. Resident-permit holders stay in their own lots. The city's [odd/even street rule](https://ecode360.com/8395630) runs Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., when activated. Students told the Sun in 2022 about [cars struggling on icy hills and buried by plow banks in Collegetown](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/). On foot, a 2023 student tip was to avoid [the Slope while it is snowing and stay off metal bridge panels](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/01/upperclassman-cornellians-share-how-they-cope-with-harsh-ithaca-winters).

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming TCAT is free all day after first year.** Upper-years without OmniRide pay $1.50 per daytime weekday ride.
- **Booking C2C without a new account or Cornell sign-on.** A plain account pays $101, $7.50 more each way.
- **Booking a January C2C trip on the 2026 link.** It covers departures only through Dec 23.
- **Booking late for peak days.** The Wednesday before Thanksgiving, the last day of December finals and the Friday spring break begins sell out; Thanksgiving-week floors ran about a third higher.
- **Missing or late-cancelling C2C.** A missed bus keeps the $93.50 fare, and your return leg stays booked until you cancel it; cancelling inside 8 hours costs $30 a seat instead of $10.
- **Waiting at the old East Seneca stop for OurBus after Nov 9.** It now boards at 235 S. Cayuga St.
- **Leaving a car on a city street over winter break.** It can be ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned.
- **Parking overnight outside the winter bays when snow is forecast.** That is a $50 winter-rules ticket.
- **Using stale numbers.** C2C at $75 was [2011](https://news.cornell.edu/node/269896); the 710 W State St terminal and ITH's Newark flight are gone; bikeshare prices rose after 2023.
- **Cutting it close.** No transit meets a 5:30 a.m. flight, and storms have cancelled buses and flights after Thanksgiving and in January.
- **Buying OmniRide you will not use.** Fewer than 134 paid daytime rides a year and cash is cheaper; a refund costs $40.
- **Missing the Aug 4 permit sale.** In 2023 one lot sold out within hours.
- **Packing two big bags for OurBus at peak.** One is guaranteed; the second may need a second seat.

## FAQ

**Is C2C only for Cornell students?** No. It is [open to the public, family and visitors included](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), at $101 one way; students, faculty and staff pay $93.50 with a NetID.

**When is Thanksgiving break in 2026?** There are no classes [Nov 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar); Thanksgiving Day is Thursday Nov 26.

**Can freshmen bring a car to Cornell?** Cornell's families page says first-years are discouraged from it, but limited permits exist. The [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) lists no separate first-year permit, so confirm with Transportation Services at 607-255-4600.

**Is there a bus from campus to the Ithaca airport?** Yes: TCAT Route 32 on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends run [between the airport and the Commons for $1.50](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation). Neither meets dawn flights, so prebook a taxi for those.

**Where do intercity buses stop downtown?** Greyhound and FlixBus stop at 131 E Green St; from Nov 9, 2026, OurBus stops at [235 S. Cayuga St](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/). On campus, most carriers board on Jessup Road (North) and West Avenue (West).

**Can I get to Ithaca by train?** Not directly. The nearest Amtrak station is in Syracuse at 1 Walsh Circle, separate from the airport; Cornell lists it among its [travel resources](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources).

**Can I take my bike home on C2C?** Only in a bike box, which counts as one of your two suitcases, per [C2C's rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).

**Can I stay on campus over winter break?** Yes, if you return to campus housing for spring: apply by Dec 1 and pay [$125 a week or $500 for the full break](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing).
