For most students in a shared apartment, Spectrum is the cheapest published choice at about $30 a month for 100 Mbps, while Verizon Fios reaches some addresses with faster plans ([ISP Reports](https://ispreports.org/internet-service-providers-ithaca-ny/)). First check whether your rent already includes Wi-Fi. A landlord's offer can cost more than either provider.

```glance
- Spectrum's 100 Mbps entry plan is the lowest published price.
- Directory prices run up to $120 a month, promotions and regular prices mixed.
- Some rents already include Wi-Fi, such as $850 per room at 209 Stewart Ave.
```

## How do Ithaca internet providers compare?

No official source names Ithaca's providers, so the comparison rests on an internet-provider directory, which does not separate sale prices from regular ones. Ask any provider for the price after the first year.

| Option | Published price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spectrum | About $30 a month for 100 Mbps | Cheapest in the directory |
| East Ithaca listing | $49.99 a month for 1,000 Mbps | Gigabit speed, one listing |
| Verizon Fios | Not priced in the directory | Claimed to reach up to 40% of addresses |
| Range across Ithaca | $30 to $120 a month | Promotions and regular prices not separated |

Source: [ISP Reports](https://ispreports.org/internet-service-providers-ithaca-ny/), an internet-provider directory. A Mbps (megabits per second) is a measure of speed, and 1,000 Mbps is called gigabit.

Over a 12-month lease, the top of the range costs $1,440 (120 times 12), or $40 a month each for three roommates ([ISP Reports](https://ispreports.org/internet-service-providers-ithaca-ny/)).

## Which provider is best for a student apartment?

Pick the cheapest plan that streams for your whole household. A 100 Mbps plan costs the least. If you game or stream on several screens, the $49.99 gigabit listing is the only faster plan with a price in the directory. Confirm which providers serve your address, since Fios is claimed at only up to 40% of addresses. Neighborhood coverage differs, and the [neighborhood guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) and the [off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/) explain the areas.

## When does landlord-included internet beat a provider?

When the rent is close. Several listings include it:

| Place | Internet terms |
|---|---|
| 209 Stewart Ave, Collegetown | WiFi inside $850 per room per month ([marketplace listing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095)) |
| 117 Thurston Ave., Cornell Heights | Heat and Wi-Fi included in $815 per person ([Ithaca Rents](https://www.ithacarents.com/4312-spacious-3-bedroom-in-historical-cornell-heights)) |
| Whiton Hall, downtown | Wi-Fi included in $965 to $1,065 per bed ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)) |

Source: building and marketplace listings, 2026-27 and 2027-28 terms.

Landlord internet that is bundled into rent is harder to price, so compare total rent. The [monthly cost guide](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/) and [utilities cost guide](/cornell/money/ithaca-utilities-cost-winter-heating-bill-apartment-students/) show how the pieces add up.

## What to do before you order

Read the lease for an internet clause ([lease guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/)), ask each provider for a price that includes the second year, and decide which roommate owns the account.

## What to do next

Check your address on each provider's site, then compare the two lowest quotes with any landlord offer.
