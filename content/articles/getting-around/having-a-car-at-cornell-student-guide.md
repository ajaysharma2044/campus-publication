```glance
- A resident permit costs $752.98 a year ($2.06 a day); the commuter (B) permit is $359.77.
- The 2026-27 sale opened Tue Aug 4, 2026 at 9 a.m., first come first served; register your plate in MyParking first.
- Outside your permit area, pay by ParkMobile, with a $0.45 fee on every transaction.
- Dec 1 to Apr 1: resident-permit cars stay in their own lots overnight; everyone else uses the listed winter bays.
- Over winter break, leave the car in your permit area, never on a city street, where it can be towed as abandoned.
- Return the permit by Mar 31 for a prorated refund; after that, none.
```

If you live in Cornell housing and bring a car, buy a resident permit for [$752.98 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), and be online when sales open (the 2026-27 sale began [Tue Aug 4, 2026 at 9 a.m.](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809)). In winter your car stays in your own lot, and over break it stays there too, never on an Ithaca street. Everything else is ParkMobile, a few fines worth knowing by heart, and a Mar 31 refund deadline.

```reader "If you're an upper-year with a car in Cornell housing"
Your permit is FH (Hasbrouck), ND (North), SW (South) or WD (West), at [$752.98](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), valid in your signed area plus North Campus A Lot after 2:30 p.m. Register the plate in MyParking.Cornell.edu, then be online the morning sales open: in 2023 the Williams St lot [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students). Anywhere else on campus, pay by ParkMobile. From Dec 1 to Apr 1 you park in your own lot as usual, and the car can stay there over break. The mistake that costs most: missing the sale morning and paying by the hour all year, or parking without payment at $50 a ticket.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year thinking about bringing a car"
Cornell's families page says first-years are ["discouraged" from bringing a car, but limited permits are available](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). The permit page lists no separate first-year permit, and the ND permit for North Campus is ["extremely limited and capped"](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). You already ride [TCAT free at all hours](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so a $752.98 permit buys mostly a parked car. If you still want one, ask Transportation Services at 607-255-4600 before move-in, and be online at 9 a.m. on sale day. The costly mistake is arriving with a car and no permit: every ticket for no permit or payment is $50.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or arriving in January"
A permit bought after the August sale, while supply lasts, is [prorated daily](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), from Sept 1 for resident permits and from Aug 16 for commuter permits. A January arrival lands inside both winter seasons: the campus overnight rule (Dec 1 to Apr 1) and the city's odd/even street rule (Nov 1 to Apr 1). Register your plate before you drive up, buy the permit for your housing area, and park overnight only in your own lot or a listed winter bay. A permit bought in January can still be returned by Mar 31 for a prorated refund. Overnight parking outside the bays when snow is forecast costs a [$50 winter-rules fine](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).
```

```reader "If you're an off-campus renter with a car"
You cannot buy a resident permit. Your campus option is the student commuter (B) permit at [$359.77](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), about $30 a month, valid in B Lot always and A Lot after 2:30 p.m. At home, the city's residential permit is [$45 a year, valid to July 31](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance); bring your registration and a lease or utility bill to the City Clerk at 108 E Green St. Your landlord cannot get it for you. The street car is the one at risk in winter: odd/even runs [Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m.](https://ecode360.com/8395630), and a car left on the street over break can be towed as abandoned.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student with a car"
A full assistantship on the Ithaca campus can earn you a [free Outer Tier A or E permit](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), as a "non-resident commuter". The same page says resident students are not eligible for the free permits. So if you live in Hasbrouck or other Cornell housing, you buy the $752.98 resident permit like an undergrad. Living off campus with an assistantship, check the free permit in MyParking before you pay $359.77 for a B permit. The E permit also needs work in its zone. The costly mistake is buying a commuter permit you did not need, since refunds stop after Mar 31.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over a break"
Campus enforcement runs [whenever the university is open](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) and pauses only on official closed staff holidays, until the next business day. Fire lanes, accessible spaces and reserved spaces are enforced every day. Commuter lots are paid [Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. year-round, breaks included](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). A car in your resident permit area is fine all break. If you park on a city street, follow odd/even every night from Nov 1, and expect towing to be possible after 72 hours when the rule is active.
```

## Step 1: register the plate before anything else

Every student car, motorcycle and scooter must be [registered with Transportation Services through MyParking.Cornell.edu](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus). That holds even if you only plan to pay by the hour. Do it in July, so sale morning is one click, not a form.

Campus parking is by permit or payment only. Transportation and Delivery Services sits at [116 Maple Avenue, 607-255-4600](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services), open Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to noon in person and by phone.

## Step 2: buy the permit on sale morning

The 2026-27 sale opened [Tue Aug 4, 2026 at 9:00 a.m.](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809) at myparking.cornell.edu, first come first served. Those permits run Aug 16, 2026 to Aug 15, 2027. Resident and commuter permits are [limited and recalled if you lose eligibility](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), for example by moving out of the area.

| Permit | Who can buy it | Price | Where it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resident: FH, ND, SW, WD | Students in Cornell housing in that area | $752.98 a year, prorated daily from Sept 1 | Your signed area, plus North Campus A Lot after 2:30 p.m. (ND: A Lot at all times) |
| ND (North Campus) | North Campus residents only; "extremely limited and capped" | $752.98 | North area and A Lot |
| Student commuter (B) | Commuters only | $359.77, prorated daily from Aug 16 | B Lot always; A Lot after 2:30 p.m. |
| Accessible (AP) | Through Student Disability Services first | $359.77 | Permit-restricted spaces except reserved and MT |
| Motorcycle (M) | Motorcycles, scooters, mopeds | $120.01, prorated monthly | Motorcycle parking; no refund |

All prices are from Cornell's [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), which says rates are set as of July 1 and shows no year on them.

How fast it goes: in fall 2023 the Williams St and Stewart Ave lot permit, about $700, [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students) of the August sale. Our advice: log in at 8:55 a.m. with the plate already registered and a card ready.

## Step 3: use the permit's rules, and ParkMobile for the rest

Your resident permit covers your own area 24/7. Resident areas FH, ND, SW and WD need [a permit or ParkMobile payment at all hours](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Driving to class in a different lot means paying.

How ParkMobile works on campus, from the [short-term parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options):

- Text PARK to 77223 or use the app.
- Each transaction, extensions included, adds a $0.45 convenience fee.
- The minimum buy is 1 hour; the maximum is 8 hours or by the day.
- Most lots' parking day ends at 7:30 a.m.; B Lot uses a 24-hour day.
- Loading areas (Zone 4125) have a 30-minute limit.
- Commuter lots charge Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., including holidays and breaks.

Per-lot hourly rates sit only in a 2026 map PDF, and rates were updated Aug 1, 2026, so check the zone sign before you pay. Students in 2022 told the Sun that [tickets and ParkMobile charges add up](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/).

## Step 4: what the fines cost

Cornell raised fines on Sept 1, 2023, the [first increase since 2014](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).

| Violation | Fine |
|---|---|
| No permit or payment | $50, plus $10 if unpaid after 10 days |
| No parking zone | $50 |
| Winter rules | $50 |
| Over the time limit | $35 |
| Parking on grass | $100 |
| Reserved space or unauthorized permit | $150 |
| Accessible space or aisle, fire lane, life-safety zone | $150 and automatic towing |
| Boot release | $150 |
| Plate not visible | $10 |

The first row is the one that matters: Cornell says about [85% of violations](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-08-01/citation-fines-increasing-september-1) are for no permit or payment.

## Step 5: winter nights, Dec 1 to Apr 1

The campus rule runs [Dec 1 to Apr 1, when it is snowing or snow is forecast](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules). From 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m., cars may park only in designated areas. Holders of FH, ND, SW and WD permits park in their own lots as usual. The rule bites when you leave the car somewhere else overnight.

The designated winter areas are:

- West and Mid bays of A Lot
- East and west bays of B Lot (Vet College)
- North bay of Crescent Lot
- West bay of the Wilson Lab lot
- East end of Alumni Field near Wing Drive on Tower Road
- Forest Home garage, all levels
- Hoy Road garage, all but the roof
- Northwest end of the Myron Taylor Hall lot
- Morrison Hall lot

After heavy snow, A and B lots are cleared first. Cornell posts a winter parking notice each year in mid-November ([the 2023 one](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-11-16/winter-parking-transportation-information) is an example), so read the new one when it lands.

The hills are the other winter problem. In 2022 a student told the Sun a front-wheel-drive car struggled on wet hills, and [another student, who has to park on the street off campus, worried that plowed snow would pile up around his car](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/).

Example: a West Campus resident with a WD permit works late in a lab near Hoy Road on a January night with snow forecast. Outside the WD area, the car needs ParkMobile payment. After 5 p.m., it belongs in the Hoy Road garage (not the roof) or back in the WD lot. Left in an ordinary lot overnight, it risks the $50 winter-rules fine.

## Step 6: the car over breaks

Students in university housing [may leave cars in their permit area over winter break](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules), and Cornell says the policy applies year-round. That is the safe answer for Thanksgiving, winter break and spring break.

The city street is the wrong answer. Cornell warns that a car left on Ithaca streets over winter break may be treated as abandoned, ticketed, towed and possibly impounded. The city's odd/even rule runs [Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m.](https://ecode360.com/8395630): no parking on the odd side on even dates, and the reverse. The city switches it on and off with the weather, and towing is possible after 72 hours.

City rates if you do park downtown: street ParkMobile zones are [$2.50 an hour](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) from Jan 5, 2026, and city garages $2.25 an hour with a $12 daily maximum at Green and Cayuga and $18 at Dryden Road. The city's $45 residential permit is for city residents with a lease or bill in their name, so it does not help a student in campus housing.

## Step 7: driving to the airport or the C2C bus

If you fly from Ithaca, long-term parking at ITH is [$8 a day (5 to 24 hours) or $40 a week](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking). Short-term is $9.50 a day or $48 a week. Take a ticket at the entrance and pay by card at the exit gate, or with cash at the machine inside the terminal first. The long-term lot lists no maximum stay.

At Syracuse, economy parking tops out at [$14 a day; the garage at $22](https://www.syrairport.org/parking/).

| A week of airport parking | Cost |
|---|---|
| ITH long-term, weekly rate | $40 |
| ITH short-term, weekly rate | $48 |
| ITH long-term at the daily rate (7 x $8) | $56 |
| SYR economy at the daily max (7 x $14) | $98 |
| SYR garage at the daily max (7 x $22) | $154 |

The ITH figures are posted; the 7-day lines for the daily rate and SYR are our arithmetic from each airport's daily maximum, so a weekly rate SYR does not show could lower them. The gap between the two airports' parking is $58 a week; a cheaper flight from Syracuse has to beat that plus gas for the drive.

Taking Cornell's Campus-to-Campus bus to NYC? C2C riders park free in B Lot if they [email c2cbus@cornell.edu with the plate and travel dates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488), a rule in effect since Oct 10, 2024. Without that, you can buy [temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions). A resident permit is not listed as valid in B Lot; student B permits are.

## Step 8: Mar 31, the refund deadline

Permits are valid through Aug 15. A [prorated refund](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) needs the permit returned by Mar 31. There is none for motorcycle or temporary permits, and no cash refunds. If you plan to sell the car or leave for a spring abroad, return the permit in time.

## Out-of-state plates and licenses

Students from other states attending school in New York are normally not New York residents and [need not exchange their license](https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/exchange-out-of-state-driver-license), per New York DMV guidance. A vehicle registered in New York needs New York insurance. Foreign students may need an International Driving Permit with the home license. In a [2016 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/should-i-bring-a-car/1811430), one poster said out-of-state plates were fine at Cornell.

## Is the car worth it? The year in dollars

| Option | Cost for 2026-27 |
|---|---|
| Resident permit alone | $752.98 ($62.75 a month, $2.06 a day) |
| Permit plus two weeks of ITH long-term parking | $832.98, before gas, insurance and tolls |
| Permit plus one unpaid-permit ticket | $802.98 |
| No car: Carshare It's My Car for 20 hours, plus four NYC round trips at the OurBus floor | $714.00 |
| No car: four C2C round trips to NYC | $748.00 |

These are our sums of posted prices for one sample year. The bus lines use C2C's [$187 NetID round trip](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) and the OurBus ["from" fare of $33](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) each way on Nov 8, 2026. Floor fares understate what most riders pay; the car lines leave out gas, insurance and tolls.

Example: an upper-year in West Campus housing keeps a car, drives home for breaks, and flies twice from ITH. The year is $752.98 for the permit plus 2 x $40 for two weeks of long-term ITH parking, or $832.98. Then add gas and insurance. That same permit money is about 502 TCAT rides at the [$1.50 cash fare](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

The alternative is Ithaca Carshare. Cornell-subsidized student plans are [Just In Case at $30 a year plus $9 an hour, or It's My Car at $310 a year plus $6 an hour](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters), with a $20 sign-up. Mileage runs [42 to 52 cents, plus 8% sales tax and a 12% rental tax](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates). By our math, the $752.98 a permit costs pays for It's My Car plus about 59 to 70 hours of driving, before mileage. Below about 93 hours a year, Just In Case is the cheaper plan. You need to be [18 or older with a license held 2 or more years](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility).

Our advice: keep the car if you drive home often, or need it weekly for work, groceries or a lab off campus. If it sits in the lot most weeks, the permit alone costs more than a year of carshare hours plus bus fares.

## Common mistakes

- **Missing sale morning.** In 2023 the Williams St lot permit [sold out within hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students). You then pay ParkMobile all year, $0.45 a transaction before the hourly rate.
- **Parking without a permit or payment.** $50 a ticket, $60 if you wait more than 10 days to pay.
- **Using a fire lane or accessible space "for a minute".** $150 plus an automatic tow, enforced even on holidays.
- **Leaving the car on a city street over break.** Ticketed, towed and possibly impounded as abandoned.
- **Parking overnight outside the winter bays when snow is forecast.** $50 under the winter rules, Dec 1 to Apr 1.
- **Paying the SYR garage for a week.** About $154 at the daily maximum, against $40 at ITH long-term.
- **Returning the permit in April.** After Mar 31 there is no refund at all.

## FAQ

**Can I park my resident permit car in a commuter lot during the day?** Not on the permit: resident permits cover your area and A Lot after 2:30 p.m. Commuter lots charge Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. by [ParkMobile](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).

**Is parking enforced on weekends?** Cornell enforces [whenever the university is open](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules), and resident areas need a permit or payment 24/7. Some commuter lots are permit-only on evenings and weekends.

**Can I still buy a permit in October or January?** Yes, while permits last; resident and commuter permits are limited. The price is [prorated daily](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), from Sept 1 for resident permits and Aug 16 for commuter permits.

**What if my permit lot is full?** You park somewhere else and pay ParkMobile; the permit does not reserve a space. A [2022 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/) described a student who parked in an off-campus lot about 15 minutes from where she lived, so build that walk into winter mornings.

**Does my Cornell permit let me park on Collegetown streets?** Not confirmed. Cornell's pages do not say, and Collegetown streets fall under city rules: [$2.50 an hour](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) in ParkMobile zones and odd/even from Nov 1.

**Who do I call about a ticket or a permit problem?** Transportation and Delivery Services, [607-255-4600](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services), Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to noon, or the ticket portal linked from MyParking.
