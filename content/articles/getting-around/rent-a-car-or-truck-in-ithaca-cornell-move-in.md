For a move within Ithaca, the rental with posted prices is an Ithaca Carshare pickup truck or minivan, billed by the hour plus [42 to 52 cents a mile](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates). For a trip out of town, call a rental company; [Avis, Budget and Hertz](https://www.flyithaca.com/car-rentals) rent at the Ithaca airport. Do not plan to arrive at a North Campus dorm in a U-Haul.

```glance
- Ithaca Carshare lists a pickup truck and a minivan, open 24 hours a day.
- A 4-hour truck trip of 20 miles is $46.40 at the Just In Case rate, before taxes.
- Cornell does not permit U-Hauls or trailers on campus; use a car or a Carshare truck for the last stretch.
- Avis, Budget and Hertz rent cars at the Ithaca airport, which recommends a rental for long stays.
```

## Which rental is best for a Cornell move?

It depends on how far you are moving.

- **Within Ithaca or between Collegetown and campus:** use Ithaca Carshare by the hour.
- **A move-in day with a car full of boxes:** drive your own car, or a family car, and follow the unloading rules.
- **A move from out of town with furniture:** rent a truck near home for an apartment move, and ask the landlord where to unload. Cornell turns moving trucks away from dorms.
- **A move after a flight:** rent a car at the airport, since the airport recommends it for longer stays.

## What do Carshare trucks and minivans cost?

Ithaca Carshare lists fuel-efficient cars, a minivan and a pickup truck, with 24/7 access, on a transportation page that Ithaca College publishes ([Ithaca College](https://www.ithaca.edu/orientation/family-and-guest-resources/transportation)). Cornell subsidizes annual memberships for undergraduates and graduate students ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)).

| Student plan | Yearly fee | Per hour |
|---|---|---|
| Just In Case Annual | $30 | $9 |
| It's My Car Annual | $310 | $6 |

Mileage is 42 cents a mile for a car, 44 cents for the van and 52 cents for the truck. You also pay a one-time $20 per driver, 8% New York sales tax and a 12% special rental tax. A full-day rate caps hourly charges at 10 hours in 24, except on the highest-demand cars ([Ithaca Carshare rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)).

Say you rent the truck for 4 hours and drive 20 miles on the Just In Case plan. The hourly charge is 4 x $9 = $36. Mileage is 20 x $0.52 = $10.40. The trip is $46.40 before taxes, plus your yearly fee and sign-up. The [Carshare plan calculator](/tools/ithaca-carshare-plan-calculator/) shows when the $310 plan wins, and the [Carshare cost guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/) explains eligibility.

Carshare has [20 vehicles](https://www.ithacacarshare.org) shared across the city, and the fleet includes one pickup truck, a Toyota Tacoma, and two Dodge Grand Caravans. Book the truck as early as you can, and check availability in the member app before you promise a date to your family.

```reader "If you're under 21"
Carshare requires a spotless record and your own insurance policy with at least $300,000 per accident in liability, plus a consent form ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility)). Apply weeks before move-in, not the week of.
```

## Can I bring a U-Haul to Cornell move-in?

No. Cornell's move-in page says U-Hauls, moving vans and trucks, and trailers are not permitted on campus and will be turned away. Vehicles over 14 feet are not allowed, except SUVs, pickup trucks and standard passenger vans or minivans, so a Carshare pickup or minivan fits the rule ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20261022005051/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus)). Cars check in at an arrival location, then unload in zones assigned on the Move-In Ticket, which prints from the Housing and Dining Portal after Aug. 1 ([North Campus move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)). The unloading steps are in the [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/).

If you need a large truck for a lease move in Collegetown or downtown, ask your landlord where you can park it for the day.

## How much does a pickup truck rental cost per day?

One company's blog lists a pickup-truck rental at $30 to $60 a day ([Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing)). Storage Scholars sells storage, not rentals, so treat that figure as a rough guide and ask a rental company for a quote that includes mileage and insurance. Compare it with the Carshare truck for a short local move: the 4-hour example above comes in under the top of that range.

## Where can I rent a car at the Ithaca airport?

The airport recommends renting for longer stays or groups ([Ithaca Tompkins International Airport](https://www.flyithaca.com/news/traveling-to-and-from-ith)). Avis and Budget share a local office at 607-257-0441, and Hertz is at 607-257-8677 ([airport car rentals](https://www.flyithaca.com/car-rentals)). Weekend counter hours are shorter, so book ahead. For the full airport picture, including TCAT and taxis, see the [airport guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-to-ithaca-airport-tcat-taxi-parking/).

## What about moving out and storage?

Cornell's contracted vendor, Big Red Shipping and Storage, offers pickup, storage and delivery ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)). The [move-out guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/) compares options.

## What to do next

1. Decide your move size: car load or furniture.
2. For a local move, join Carshare before you need it.
3. For an out-of-town move, call two rental companies for quotes and ask whether they allow one-way rentals.
4. Print your Move-In Ticket after Aug. 1.
