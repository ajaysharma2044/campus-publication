```glance
- TCAT is free at all hours with your Cornell ID until Aug 31, 2027. From Sept 1, 2027, weekday daytime rides cost $1.50.
- Skip the car: a resident permit costs $752.98 a year, and Cornell discourages first-years from bringing one.
- To NYC: C2C costs $93.50 one way with a NetID; OurBus, FlixBus and Greyhound start at $25 to $43.98.
- Book Thanksgiving now: break runs Nov 25 to 29, 2026, and Thanksgiving-week floor fares ran about a third higher in a Nov 8 sample.
- Four NYC round trips cost about $264 a year at the OurBus floor fare, against $748 on C2C.
- ITH flies only to JFK and Dulles. TCAT 32 and 72 reach it free for you, but no bus runs for a 5:30 a.m. flight.
```

As a Cornell first-year you ride TCAT free at all hours with your Cornell ID until [Aug 31, 2027](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so you need no car and no bus pass. To get home to NYC, take Cornell's C2C at [$93.50 one way with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates), or [OurBus](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk), which has run 4 to 6 trips a day from campus to Manhattan since Sept 1, 2026, from about $25 to $33. Book Thanksgiving now: OurBus names the Wednesday before it a [peak day](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269).

```reader "If you're from NYC"
Choose the bus by where you are going in Manhattan, and by your bags. C2C leaves 217 Jessup Rd on North Campus and drops at Weill Cornell, 1300 York Ave, or the Cornell Club on East 44th St ([stops](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations)). It carries two suitcases below deck. OurBus and Greyhound are cheaper and go to the West Side: Hudson Yards, the GWB station or Port Authority. OurBus guarantees only [one large bag per ticket](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes). Four round trips cost $748 on C2C against $264 at the OurBus floor fare. The mistake that costs most: signing in to the new C2C system with a plain account and paying $101 instead of $93.50.
```

```reader "If you're flying home"
Ithaca's airport has [two routes](https://flyithaca.com/airlines): Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles. One cancellation can strand you. TCAT Route 32 on weekdays and Route 72 on weekends run to the airport, free with your Cornell ID. No bus meets a 5:30 a.m. flight, so prebook a taxi. Syracuse has [nonstops to 27 destinations](https://www.syrairport.org/), and OurBus runs there from North and West Campus six days a week for [$46.7 to $62.1 each way](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny). Book Thanksgiving flights early and keep a backup airport.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying for the trips"
Budget about $264 a year for four NYC round trips at the cheapest OurBus fare, or $748 on C2C. The gap buys a Cornell-run coach, two checked bags and an East Side drop. Do not buy a bus pass: your student rides TCAT free until Aug 31, 2027. Do not send a car without a real need. A resident permit is [$752.98 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), and the North Campus permit is "extremely limited and capped." For C2C, have your student book under their own Cornell sign-in to get the $93.50 fare. A booking on your own account pays the $101 public fare.
```

```reader "If you're paying your own way"
The cheapest seat to NYC on Nov 8 ran [$25 to $43 across six carriers](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/), against $93.50 on C2C. C2C costs about three times the cheapest seat. Book early and avoid the three OurBus peak days: Thanksgiving week ran about a third higher in a Nov 8 sample. OurBus cancellations give credit, not cash, so buy only trips you will take. A second large bag on OurBus costs $30 prepaid. Free Student Assembly charters to ITH and SYR have run in finals week. If going home costs more than staying, [winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing) is $125 a week or $500, with a Dec 1 deadline.
```

## Getting around campus and town: free TCAT

Your Cornell ID is your bus pass. New first-year students get [unlimited TCAT rides](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) from fall registration to Aug 31 of the next year. For a fall 2026 entrant, that is Aug 31, 2027. The page says there are no extensions for any reason.

That covers every TCAT route, day and night. The ones you will use most:

- **Campus routes** 81, 82 and the 90s, plus Route 10 between campus and downtown ([Cornell sustainability office](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting)).
- **Night routes** 90, 92 and 93.
- **The Blue Light bus** on Route 92, which runs [6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. year-round](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation).
- **Routes 32 and 72** to the airport, covered below.

If the farebox rejects your ID, the fare is $1.50. In free hours drivers override rejected IDs, according to a [Nov 2024 Cornell Sun report](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). If you lose your ID, call the Registrar at 607-255-4232.

Expect some late buses. They are the most common complaint about getting around Ithaca, raised in 10 news stories, columns and resolutions from 2019 to 2026. One student in 2022 waited a long time because Google Maps times were wrong, then paid for an Uber ([Cornell Sun, 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/06/tcat-implements-massive-service-cut-due-to-driver-shortage/)). The Student Assembly called the buses late and unreliable in a [March 2026 resolution](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026). Track buses in TCAT's Bus Tracker or the MyStop app, and leave a margin before exams.

### What changes on Sept 1, 2027

From sophomore year, TCAT is free only after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday. A daytime weekday ride costs $1.50. The other choice is the discounted OmniRide pass at [$200 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), sold on MyParking.Cornell.Edu. By our math, it pays off at 134 paid daytime rides a year ($200 divided by $1.50). Do not buy it this year; your free pass already covers everything. Upper-year choices are in the [guide for students without a car after first year](/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-upper-years-no-car/).

## Why to skip the car

Cornell's families page says first-years are [discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits are available](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). The numbers back that up.

- **The permit.** A resident permit costs [$752.98 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). The North Campus (ND) permit is "extremely limited and capped" and only for North Campus residents.
- **What that buys instead.** By our math, $752.98 is $2.06 a day, or the price of about 502 TCAT cash rides. You ride free anyway.
- **The fines.** Parking without a permit or payment is [$50, plus $10 if unpaid after 10 days](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/). A fire lane or accessible space is $150 with a tow.
- **Winter.** From Dec 1 to Apr 1, overnight campus parking when snow is forecast is [limited to listed lots and garages](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules).

If you still want a permit, a first-year may buy one. On the [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), eligibility for a resident permit turns on living in university housing, not on class year. Permit questions go to Transportation Services at 607-255-4600, open Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to noon.

For the odd grocery run, Ithaca Carshare has student plans, but check its [eligibility rules](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) first. Drivers must be 18 and have held a full, unrestricted license for 2 years. Under 21, you also need a spotless record and your own insurance policy with at least $300,000 per accident in liability. Most first-years are under 21, so check this before you count on it.

## Getting home to NYC: C2C, OurBus or another bus

Example: a North Campus first-year whose family lives near 72nd St and York Ave goes home four times this year with two suitcases. C2C costs 4 x $187 = $748 and drops a few blocks away. OurBus at its $33 floor costs 4 x $66 = $264, but lands on the West Side and guarantees one bag. The $484 difference buys the East Side drop and the second bag. With one small bag, OurBus wins.

## Booking C2C under the new system

C2C moved to a [new reservation system on Nov 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4). Everyone needs a new account, even past riders.

1. **Sign in with Cornell single sign-on.** That gets the $93.50 NetID fare. A standard account pays the $101 public fare.
2. **Use the right link.** Departures through Dec 23, 2026 book on the "2026 Travel Reservations" link. Departures from Jan 6, 2027 book on the "2027" link ([C2C page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service)). The two links skip Dec 24 to Jan 5.
3. **Check the day's times on the live table.** The schedule changed on Aug 1, 2026.
4. **Board 10 minutes early with photo ID** matching your booking. The Ithaca stops run North Campus first, then Sage Hall 15 minutes later, then B Lot 30 minutes later.
5. **Change or cancel only in the system.** The [C2C policies](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) charge $10 a seat to cancel 8 or more hours ahead and $30 inside 8 hours. Phone and email cancellations are not accepted.
6. **If you miss the bus,** the full fare is kept once it leaves its last stop. Missing the outbound leg does not cancel your return; cancel it yourself.

Booking rules and fares in full: [C2C booking guide](/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/).

### Booking OurBus, Greyhound or FlixBus

Compare on an aggregator, then book on the operator's site. "From" prices are the cheapest seat on the cheapest day. On Nov 8 the six-carrier floor ran $25 to $43 and average fares $45 to $82 on [Wanderu](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/).

Two things to know before you pay. OurBus [cancellations give credit, not cash](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel), and rescheduling has a fee. OurBus Select gives [$15 off each of your next 10 tickets](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269).

Check your downtown stop too. From Mon Nov 9, 2026, OurBus boards downtown at [235 S. Cayuga St](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/), beside the South Cayuga garage. Greyhound and FlixBus still list 131 E Green St. The old terminal at 710 W State St is [closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_Bus_Station), though Cornell's families page still names it.

## Thanksgiving and winter break: what to book now

Cornell's [2026-27 calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar) puts Thanksgiving break at Nov 25 to 29, 2026. Fall finals run Dec 11 to 19, and spring term begins Jan 25, 2027. OurBus names three [peak travel days](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269): the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, the last day of December finals and the Friday spring break begins.

Prices rise as those days near. In our Nov 8 reading of daily lowest fares on three Ithaca routes (Binghamton, Rochester and DC, on [CheckMyBus](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/binghamton-ny)), Thanksgiving-week floors ran 28% to 38% above earlier November days. That is a mean of 33.4% across 51 priced dates. At the same premium, a $66 OurBus floor round trip to NYC would cost about $88.

Plan the return as carefully as the trip out. On Dec 1 and 2, 2019, a storm after Thanksgiving [cancelled OurBus trips](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/12/before-classes-were-cancelled-cornellians-attempted-to-return-to-ithaca-at-all-costs). For Eva Zhang '23, a ShortLine bus slid off the road, and the replacement took about 9 hours against the usual 4 to 5. A student forecaster's advice in the [Sun that weekend](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/30/under-heavy-snow-forecast-university-mum-on-class-cancellation) was to come back by Saturday night or early Sunday when a storm is forecast.

## Getting to the airport

| Airport | Flights | Getting there from campus | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ithaca (ITH) | Delta to JFK; United to Washington Dulles | TCAT 32 on weekdays, 72 on weekends, free with your ID; or a taxi | Two routes, so a cancellation means a long wait |
| Syracuse (SYR) | Nonstops to 27 destinations | OurBus six days a week, $46.7 to $62.1 each way, to Terminal A | Snow cancels flights here too |
| NYC airports (JFK, LaGuardia, Newark) | The widest choice | Bus to Manhattan, then transfer | Leave a big buffer after the bus |

Sources: [ITH airlines](https://flyithaca.com/airlines), [ITH buses](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), [SYR](https://www.syrairport.org/), [OurBus to SYR](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny), [Sun on OurBus Syracuse service](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk).

The ITH-Newark flight [ended in 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk). OurBus to SYR picks up on North Campus behind Ganedago Hall, on West Ave across from Baker Flagpole and at the Cayuga St Garage. Sunday is its cheapest day.

Early flights are the gap. The Student Assembly president said in 2024 that for 5:30 a.m. flights there is ["literally no transportation available"](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) beyond costly rides. Prebook a taxi. The [airport lists](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) Ithaca Airport Cab (607-252-9052), Collegetown Cab (607-588-8888) and Ithaca Dispatch (607-277-7777). Text or call for a price; the airport says taxis are there 24/7 and advises prearranging a ride for a late flight.

In finals week, the Student Assembly has paid for free charters to ITH and SYR for undergraduates since May 2024. The fall 2025 run got [$7,853.32, voted on Nov 13, 2025](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r18-2025-2026), and the spring 2026 run got [$10,668.87](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r83-2025-2026), approved on Apr 16, 2026. Sign-up is first come on Campus Groups.

If you need a train, the nearest Amtrak station is in [Syracuse](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources). ## Common mistakes

- **Signing in to C2C with a plain account.** You pay $101 instead of $93.50: $7.50 a trip, $15 a round trip.
- **Booking Thanksgiving late.** Floor fares ran about a third higher in Thanksgiving week, and peak buses sell out.
- **Missing the C2C bus.** The full $93.50 is kept, and the return leg stays booked until you cancel it. Cancelling inside 8 hours costs $30 a seat instead of $10.
- **Going to the wrong downtown stop.** The 710 W State St terminal is closed, and OurBus left East Seneca St on Nov 9, 2026.
- **Bringing a car.** $752.98 for the permit, plus $50 for each ticket without a permit or payment.
- **Assuming free TCAT carries into sophomore year.** From Sept 1, 2027, each daytime weekday ride costs $1.50, or $200 a year for OmniRide.
- **Booking a dawn flight with no ride.** Nothing runs for a 5:30 a.m. flight, so prebook a taxi.

## FAQ

**Can my parents ride C2C with me?** Yes. C2C is [open to the public](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4) at $101 one way, and one reservation can hold several passengers.

**Can I take my bike home on the bus?** On C2C, only in a bike box, which [counts as one of your two suitcases](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). Around Ithaca, every TCAT bus has a [front bike rack](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall) at no extra fare.

**What if a storm cancels my bus back after break?** Operators cancel early and add earlier trips: in 2019 OurBus [cancelled Sunday trips and added Saturday ones](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/30/under-heavy-snow-forecast-university-mum-on-class-cancellation). Rebook on an earlier bus as soon as a storm is forecast.

**How do I get to the Ithaca airport at Thanksgiving?** TCAT 32 on a weekday or 72 on the weekend, free with your ID, or a prebooked taxi for an early flight. In 2023 the airport and Swarthout Coaches ran a one-off campus shuttle for [$14 one way](https://cornellsun.com/2023/11/19/special-thanksgiving-shuttle-connects-cornell-with-ithaca-tompkins-international-airport/), so watch for a repeat.

**Does C2C go to Boston or Philadelphia?** No. The [rates page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) lists only the NYC route.
