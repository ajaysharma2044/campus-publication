On move-in day, unload at your building, then move the car to the 3-hour short-term parking area printed on your Move-In Ticket. Print the ticket from the Housing and Dining Portal after Aug. 1 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).

```glance
- Your timeslot covers arrival and check-in, not unloading.
- Uber and taxi arrivals go to A Lot; bus arrivals go straight to the building.
- Staff unloading help ends early on Tuesday: 11 a.m. for arrivals before 11.
- Move-out has its own list of loading docks and lots.
```

## Where do I unload and park on move-in day?

Each building has its own move-in page. Follow the steps below, then check your building's page.

```figure
  - {label: "Arrive in your timeslot and check in; the timeslot is not for unloading"}
  - {label: "Unload at your building; Uber and taxi riders start at A Lot"}
  - {label: "Move the car to the 3-hour short-term area on the ticket"}
  - {label: "Finish before staff unloading help ends for the day"}
```

| Where you live | Where the car goes | Rule to know |
|---|---|---|
| North Campus | Building unloading zone, then the 3-hour area on your ticket | Jessup Road north-side spaces: no overnight parking |
| Balch Hall | Balch unloading zone, then the 3-hour area | No staff unloading help ([Balch page](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-balch-hall)) |
| West Campus | Queue at the University Avenue Lot | Stewart Ave. paid spaces are free after 6 p.m. and on Sundays |
| South Campus | Far end of the lot | No overnight parking |
| Cascadilla Hall | Williams Street Lot, the upper lot at Williams St. and Stewart Ave. | Sheldon Court and Schuyler House have their own pages ([Cascadilla page](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)) |
| By Uber or taxi | A Lot, North Campus | Then carry bags to the building |
| By bus | Straight to your building | No car to park |

Source: [North Campus page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus) and the building pages. Your ticket wins if anything differs.

## When does staff unloading help end?

The North Campus page lists staff help ending at 8 p.m. on Monday, 11 a.m. on Tuesday for arrivals before 11, and 5 p.m. on Friday and Saturday. The 2026 dates were Aug. 17 (first-years) and Aug. 18 (transfers) ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation)), plus Aug. 21 to Aug. 22 ([North Campus page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).

Tuesday is the trap: a family arriving after 11 a.m. unloads alone. On the Monday, the Cornell Daily Sun counted 3,681 first-years moving in from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/08/this-is-the-place-i-want-to-be-incoming-freshmen-make-the-move-to-campus)). Cornell has not yet announced the 2027 move-in dates, so check the North Campus page when the ticket opens.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Most first-years live on North Campus. Check in during your timeslot, then unload. Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) says first-years are discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits exist. The costly mistake is leaving the car in the unloading space while you set up your room.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or family member"
You park twice: once to unload and once for the rest of the day. After unloading, use the 3-hour area, or the Tang Welcome Center lot, ParkMobile Zone 4119. Its pay station was out of service as of 9/08/2026, so pay by phone. Our [parents and visitors guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-parents-visitors/) covers the rest of the weekend.
```

```reader "If you bring your own car"
Register the plate at [MyParking.Cornell.edu](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus) before you drive up. After the 3-hour period, resident lots need a permit or ParkMobile payment around the clock ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)). The resident permit is $752.98 a year ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)), and our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) lists the options.
```

```reader "If you need accessible parking or help"
The accessible (AP) permit starts with Student Disability Services ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). For housing questions, call the Housing and Residential Life Office at 607-255-5368 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing)). Uber and taxi drop-offs go to A Lot, so plan for the distance.
```

```reader "If you arrive in spring"
Residences reopen Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027, at 9 a.m., and you pick an arrival timeslot in the Housing and Dining Portal ([2026 winter closing checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice)). Winter overnight parking rules apply in January.
```

## What does parking cost around move-in?

Unloading is free. The money starts when the car stays.

| Item | Cost or rule |
|---|---|
| Tang Welcome Center lot (Zone 4119) | $1.50 an hour, $10 a day |
| ParkMobile fee | $0.45 per transaction, extensions included |
| ParkMobile limits | 1-hour minimum; 8 hours or by the day; loading areas (Zone 4125) 30 minutes |
| No permit or payment | $50, plus $10 if unpaid in 10 days |
| Over a time limit | $35 |
| Accessible space, fire lane or life-safety zone | $150 and automatic tow |

Source: [short-term parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options) and the [2023 fine schedule](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).

Example: a parent leaves the car at Tang for a full day. That costs $10.45, the day rate plus one ParkMobile fee ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options)).

## Where do I load at move-out?

Move-out uses a separate list of docks and lots ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/important-loading-and-parking-information)). On North Campus, they are the Mews, Dickson and Donlon docks, the Robert Purcell lot, the Helen Newman lot (ParkMobile) and Jessup Road's north side. On West Campus, they are West Ave. near Baker Tower and Founders and Lyon, Stewart Ave., Mary Ann Wood Dr. and Forest Park Ln. Cascadilla and Sheldon Court use the city garage south of Cascadilla and the Williams Street Garage. Gothics Way and Becker Drive are not accessible.

In spring 2026, buildings closed at 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, and items left behind were donated. Storage and shipping are covered in our [move-out guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).

## What to do next

Print your ticket after Aug. 1, check your building's page, and plan to arrive before staff help ends. If you are coming by bus or plane, start with [getting to Ithaca and around Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/) and the [first-year transportation guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-first-year/).
