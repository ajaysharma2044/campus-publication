```glance
- Print the Move-In Ticket from the Housing and Dining Portal after Aug 1. It names your parking area.
- Your timeslot covers arrival and check-in only, not unloading.
- Unload at the building, then move the car to 3-hour short-term parking in the area on the ticket.
- Uber and taxi arrivals go to A Lot; bus arrivals go straight to the building.
- Staff unloading help ends 8 p.m. Monday, 11 a.m. Tuesday, 5 p.m. Friday and Saturday (North Campus, 2026 dates).
- A family car staying all day: Tang Welcome Center, $1.50 an hour or $10 a day.
```

On move-in day you unload at your building, then move the car to the 3-hour short-term parking area printed on your Move-In Ticket, which you print from the Housing and Dining Portal [after Aug 1](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus). Your timeslot is for check-in, not unloading. Uber and taxi riders get dropped at A Lot, and staff help ends at 5 p.m. on the Friday and Saturday dates.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Most first-years live on North Campus, so the North Campus page is yours. Print the Move-In Ticket after Aug 1. Check in during your timeslot, then unload. Get everything inside before staff help ends: 8 p.m. Monday, 11 a.m. Tuesday for arrivals before 11, 5 p.m. Friday and Saturday. Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) says first-years are discouraged from bringing a car, though limited permits exist. The costly mistake is leaving the car in the unloading space while you set up the room.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or family member"
You park twice: at the building to unload, then somewhere for the rest of the day. After unloading, use the 3-hour area on the ticket, or the Tang Welcome Center lot at [$1.50 an hour or $10 a day](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options). Its pay station was out of service as of 9/08/2026, so pay through ParkMobile, Zone 4119, with a $0.45 fee. On Tuesday, staff help stops at 11 a.m. The costly mistake is parking unpaid in a campus lot: $50 a ticket under the [2023 fine schedule](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).
```

```reader "If you're bringing your own car"
Register the plate at [MyParking.Cornell.edu](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus) before you drive up; Cornell requires it even for short-term parking. On move-in day, unload, then use the 3-hour area on your ticket. After that, resident lots (FH, ND, SW, WD) need a permit or ParkMobile payment 24 hours a day. A resident permit is [$752.98 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). FH, SW and WD permits work in the signed area plus A Lot after 2:30 p.m.; the ND permit works in A Lot at all times. The North Campus (ND) permit is extremely limited and capped to North Campus residents of university-owned housing. The costly mistake is treating the move-in spot as yours: $50 a ticket.
```

```reader "If you need accessible parking or help unloading"
The accessible permit (AP) costs $359.77, and you contact Student Disability Services first, per the [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). It is valid in permit-restricted spaces except reserved and MT spaces. A companion's car without the permit in an accessible space draws a $150 fine and automatic towing. Uber and taxi drop-offs go to A Lot, so plan for the distance to your building. Pick an early timeslot, and avoid Tuesday afternoon: help ends at 11 a.m. Not confirmed: a separate accessible unloading area. Ask Housing at housing@cornell.edu or 607-255-5368 before the day.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or starting in spring"
A fall transfer follows the page for their building and uses the table below. For a spring arrival, residences reopen Tue Jan 19, 2027 at 9 a.m., with arrival timeslots in the Housing and Dining Portal, per the [2026 winter closing checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). January brings winter rules: from Dec 1 to Apr 1, when snow is forecast, overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. is allowed only in [designated areas](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules), such as the west and mid bays of A Lot. The winter-rules fine was $50 in 2023. Not confirmed: spring unloading instructions for new students.
```

The [North Campus page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus) lists Monday Aug 17, Tuesday Aug 18, and Friday Aug 21 to Saturday Aug 22. It prints no year, but the weekdays match 2026.

## What parking costs on move-in weekend

Unloading is free. The money starts when the car stays. Students must register their plates at [MyParking.Cornell.edu](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus), even for short-term parking.

| Item | Cost or rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tang Welcome Center lot (ParkMobile Zone 4119) | $1.50 an hour, $10 a day; pay station out of service as of 9/08/2026 | [Short-term parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options) |
| ParkMobile convenience fee | $0.45 per transaction, extensions included; text PARK to 77223 or use the app | same |
| ParkMobile limits | 1-hour minimum; 8 hours or by the day; loading areas (Zone 4125) 30 minutes | same |
| Resident lots (FH, ND, SW, WD) | Permit or ParkMobile payment 24 hours a day | same |
| No permit or payment | $50, plus $10 if unpaid in 10 days | [Cornell Sun 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/) |
| Over a time limit | $35 | same |
| Accessible space, fire lane or life-safety zone | $150 and automatic towing | same |

The fines are from the schedule effective Sept 1, 2023.

Example: a parent drives up for a full Friday on North Campus, unloads, then leaves the car at Tang Welcome Center for the day through ParkMobile. That costs $10.45: the $10 day rate plus one $0.45 fee. Leaving it in the 3-hour area past its limit risks the $35 fine.

Families staying overnight downtown pay city rates. The [Green Street and Cayuga Street garages](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) charge $2.25 an hour with a $12 daily maximum, effective Jan 5, 2026. The Seneca Street garage has been [closed since spring 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/).

## Arriving without a car

Uber and taxi riders are dropped at A Lot on North Campus. Bus riders go straight to the building, per the [North Campus page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus).

On the bus, bags are the limit. OurBus guarantees one large bag per ticket. The Cornell Sun reported in 2024 that at peak move-in, [two large bags effectively meant buying two seats](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes).

Example: a first-year flies into Ithaca on a Friday with two suitcases and takes a taxi to A Lot. If they reach the building after 5 p.m., when help ends, they carry everything alone.

## Common mistakes

- **Unloading at your timeslot.** The timeslot is for check-in only.
- **Leaving the car in the unloading space.** Move it to the 3-hour area on your ticket. An unpaid campus space costs $50; overstaying a time limit, $35.
- **Arriving Tuesday afternoon.** Staff help ends at 11 a.m. that day.
- **Parking in an accessible space or fire lane to unload faster.** That is $150 plus automatic towing.
- **Following the 2021 arrival page.** It still shows up in search, with old dates and lots.
- **Skipping plate registration.** Cornell requires it even for short-term parking, and a plate not visible was a $10 fine in 2023.
- **Packing for the bus like a car.** Two large bags on OurBus at peak move-in effectively meant two seats in 2024.

## FAQ

**Can we park overnight on Jessup Road?** No. The north-side Jessup Road unloading spaces (for Low Rise #6 only) allow no overnight parking. The South Campus page says the same for its lot.

**Who do I call if the ticket shows the wrong area?** Housing, at housing@cornell.edu or 607-255-5368. Parking questions go to Transportation and Delivery Services at 607-255-4600, staffed 7:30 a.m. to noon on weekdays, per the [short-term parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).

**Is West Campus street parking free on move-in weekend?** Paid spaces on Stewart Ave are free after 6 p.m. and on Sundays, per the [West Campus page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-west-campus).

**Can I keep my car on campus after move-in without a permit?** Only by paying ParkMobile, since resident lots need a permit or payment 24 hours a day. A resident permit is [$752.98 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students).

**Where do we load for move-out?** Move-out uses a separate list of docks and lots, covered in the [move-out guide](/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).
