```glance
- PhD students and graduate assistants get a free TCAT OmniRide pass: free rides at all hours, no $200 to pay.
- A full assistantship on the Ithaca campus gets free Outer Tier A and E permits; grads in university housing do not qualify.
- Master's, MBA and law students without an assistantship: free TCAT as new students, then free only after 6 p.m. weekdays and weekends, else $1.50 a ride or $200 a year.
- No assistantship and a car: the student commuter (B) permit is $359.77 a year, valid to Aug 15.
- C2C to NYC: $93.50 one way with a NetID, $86 on approved department travel; Weill CWIDs do not work.
- A spouse can buy an OmniRide for night routes; children under 12 ride TCAT free with a paying adult.
```

If you are a PhD student or a graduate assistant, the bus is already paid for: Cornell says [registered PhD students and graduate assistants receive free TCAT OmniRide passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). A full assistantship also gets you [free Outer Tier A and E parking permits](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), unless you live in university housing. Everyone else pays for daytime weekday buses after the first year and [$359.77 a year for a commuter permit](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students).

```reader "If you're a PhD student or on a full assistantship"
You ride TCAT free at all hours on the OmniRide pass, so the after-6 p.m. limit does not touch you. Do not buy the $200 pass. If you drive, the parking page lists free Outer Tier A and E permits for a full assistantship on the Ithaca main campus, checked in Workday; E needs work in its zone. The permit is classed "non-resident commuter", so living in university housing rules you out. You may also buy faculty and staff permits. Claim the permit at myparking.cornell.edu. The costly mistake is paying $359.77 for a B permit you could have had free.
```

```reader "If you're a master's, MBA or law student without an assistantship"
The [Johnson MBA page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/) says TCAT is free your first year, then free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend. Daytime weekday rides after that cost $1.50 each, or $200 a year for OmniRide, which pays off after 134 paid rides. A car means the $359.77 B commuter permit or Daily Decision parking from about $1 a day in select lots. The costly mistake in a two-year program is assuming the free pass carries into year two. It ends Aug 31.
```

```reader "If you're moving here with a spouse, partner or children"
With a Cornell ID card, your spouse or partner can buy the OmniRide student bus pass for TCAT night routes from Transportation Services, 116 Maple Ave (607-255-4600). On TCAT, children under 12 ride free with a paying adult. On the C2C bus to NYC, a child under 2 rides free on a lap; from age 2 every child needs a paid seat, and there are no child discounts. For grocery runs with kids, an Ithaca Carshare minivan or a car of your own matters more than the bus.
```

```reader "If you travel to NYC or Weill for work"
Book approved Cornell business travel on a department account: $86 one way against $93.50 personal, a $7.50 saving each way. PCards are not accepted. Cornell's travel office calls C2C the preferred mode between Ithaca and the NYC campuses. If your appointment is through Weill, your CWID will not work in the booking system; request a NetID from the IT Service Desk first. Since Nov 4, 2026 every rider needs a new C2C account.
```

## What is free and what you pay

The rules split on two questions: are you a PhD student or graduate assistant, and is this your first year? This table puts every grad situation in one place.

| You are | TCAT | Campus parking | C2C to NYC, one way |
|---|---|---|---|
| PhD student | Free OmniRide, all hours | B permit $359.77, unless on a full assistantship | $93.50 personal |
| Graduate assistant (full assistantship), living off campus | Free OmniRide, all hours | Free Outer Tier A and E permits | $93.50 personal; $86 department travel |
| Graduate assistant in university housing | Free OmniRide | Not eligible for free A or E | $93.50 |
| New master's, MBA or law student | Free in the first year | B permit $359.77 | $93.50 |
| Second-year master's or professional student | Free after 6 p.m. weekdays and weekends; else $1.50 or $200 a year | B permit $359.77 | $93.50 |
| Spouse or partner | OmniRide for night routes, bought at 116 Maple Ave | Daily Decision zones, open to everyone | $101 public fare |

Sources: [bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes); [Graduate School](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/student-experience/student-perks-and-discounts/); [student parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students); [short-term parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options); [C2C rates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates).

## The bus: who rides free

TCAT is the default. The Cornell ID is the pass. Cornell's rule has three tiers:

1. **New students.** The bus-pass page says "As a New Cornell Student, you'll automatically get OmniRide privileges for unlimited TCAT rides." The privileges expire Aug 31 for fall entrants and Dec 31 for spring entrants, with no extensions.
2. **PhD students and graduate assistants.** Free OmniRide every year you stay registered.
3. **Everyone else, after the first year.** Free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday. Daytime weekday rides cost the $1.50 cash fare, or you buy the [discounted OmniRide for $200 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) at MyParking.Cornell.Edu.

The $200 pass is prorated monthly and expires Aug 31. You can return it for a refund until Mar 31, less $40.

The free first year covers master's and professional students too. Cornell's [Graduate School](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/student-experience/student-perks-and-discounts/) says all registered Cornell students ride every TCAT bus free in their first year.

Why the rules feel slippery: until mid-October 2024 a farebox glitch let every student ride free. After Cornell fixed the eligibility list, non-first-years were denied at fareboxes in daytime hours, per the [Cornell Sun's November 2024 report](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). In free hours, drivers override a rejected ID.

### Should a second-year master's student buy OmniRide?

Divide $200 by $1.50 and you get 134 paid rides. Over an assumed 30-week academic year, that is about 4.5 paid daytime weekday rides a week. Evening and weekend rides are free anyway, so count only weekday trips before 6 p.m.

```tool omniride-break-even-calculator```

Example: a second-year MBA student who rides Route 10 in from downtown four mornings a week and walks home after 6 p.m. pays for about 4 rides a week. Over 30 weeks that is 120 rides, or $180 in cash fares. Cash wins by $20. Add one more daytime ride a week and the pass wins.

### Getting around at night and in a family

The [Blue Light bus on Route 92](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) runs nightly from 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round. A spouse or partner with a Cornell ID card can buy the [OmniRide student bus pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) at 116 Maple Ave, good for unlimited rides on TCAT night routes.

For children, the [Downtown Ithaca fare guide](https://www.downtownithaca.com/five-ways-to-pay-your-tcat-fare/) says riders under 12 go free with a paying adult. The same guide lists the ways to pay: exact cash, a tapped card or phone, the TFare app, or a T-card.

### Is the bus reliable enough to live on?

Student government's [March 2026 resolution](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026) calls the buses late and unreliable and blames inadequate funding. It also asks Cornell to fund free passes for all undergraduates and graduate workers; the president returned it on Apr 24, 2026. The [law school's housing page](https://lawschool.cornell.edu/life-at-cornell-law/housing) says many law students go without a car and find the bus reliable. Track buses in Bus Tracker or MyStop rather than trusting Google Maps times alone.

Renting on West Hill or in Cayuga Heights? Routes 14S and 83, which served those areas and Cornell, have been [suspended since fall 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/). Check the route from a listing to your building before you sign.

## Parking: the free permit and the paid one

The free permit is the biggest grad-only perk. The [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students) gives grad students with a full assistantship on the Ithaca main campus free Outer Tier A and E permits. Eligibility is checked in Workday. The permit is "no fee; non-resident commuter", and E needs work in its zone. Resident students are not eligible for a free A or E permit. Full-assistantship grads may also buy faculty and staff permits.

Without an assistantship, your options are these:

| Option | Price | Where and when | Fine print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free Outer Tier A or E | $0 | Outer Tier lots; E only if you work in its zone | Full assistantship; not in university housing |
| Student commuter (B) | $359.77 a year, about $30 a month | B Lot always; A Lot after 2:30 p.m. | Prorated daily from Aug 16; commuters only |
| Daily Decision | "As little as $1/day" in select zones | Outer lots, first come | $0.65 permit plus $0.35 ParkMobile fee; rates updated Aug 1, 2026 |
| ParkMobile by the hour | Lot rate plus $0.45 a transaction | Commuter lots paid Mon to Fri, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. | Minimum 1 hour |
| Resident permit | $752.98 a year | Your housing area | For students in university housing |

Sources: [student parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students); [Daily Decision](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/getting-around/commuter-support-services); [short-term parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).

All permits run through Aug 15. A prorated refund needs the permit back by Mar 31. Register your plate in MyParking.Cornell.edu even for short-term parking. Parking without a permit or payment costs [$50, plus $10 if unpaid in 10 days](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).

Example: a fifth-year PhD student on a full assistantship who drives in from Brooktondale saves $359.77 on a B permit and $200 on OmniRide. That is $559.77 a year in transport Cornell already covers, if the student claims both.

### Parking at home is a separate problem

[Cornell's postdoc guidance](https://postdocs.cornell.edu/postdoc-life/living-in-ithaca) warns that many Ithaca apartments have no parking. Ask before you sign. Inside the city, a [residential parking permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) costs $45 a year, runs to July 31 and has no partial-year price. Bring your registration and a lease or utility bill to the City Clerk at 108 E Green St. A landlord cannot get one for you.

Winter adds two rule sets. The city's [odd/even rule](https://ecode360.com/8395630) runs Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., when the city activates it, and towing is possible after 72 hours. On campus, from Dec 1 to Apr 1, overnight parking from 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. when snow is forecast is limited to [listed areas](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules) such as the A and B Lot bays and the Forest Home garage. A car left on a city street over winter break can be ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned.

## Do you need a car at all?

For occasional trips, Ithaca Carshare is the middle path between the bus and a car of your own. The [Cornell Sun in 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/19/ithaca-carshare-to-resume-operations-march-2024/) described a grad student who used Carshare as a safety net, moved to Bikeshare when Carshare paused, and planned to buy a car.

Cornell subsidizes annual student memberships, grad students included. The [student plans](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters) are:

```tool ithaca-carshare-plan-calculator```

Example: a law student with a partner and no car books 40 hours a year for grocery and pediatrician trips. Just In Case costs $30 plus $360 in hourly charges, $390 before mileage, taxes and the $20 sign-up. It's My Car costs $310 plus $240, or $550. Just In Case wins by $160.

Check [eligibility](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) first. You need to be 18 with a full license held 2 or more years. Under 21, you need your own policy with at least $300,000 per-accident liability. A foreign license needs an official driving record; an International Driving Permit is not accepted. Carshare has [four campus pickup spots plus four in Collegetown and nearby](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing).

## Trips to NYC and Weill

C2C is Cornell's own coach between Ithaca and Manhattan. The [rates page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates) lists three fares:

| Who books | One way | Round trip |
|---|---|---|
| Public, including a spouse without a NetID | $101 | $202 |
| Cornell student, faculty or staff with a NetID | $93.50 | $187 |
| Department-paid Cornell business travel | $86 | $172 |

A temporary $6 fuel surcharge is already inside each fare. Weill CWIDs are not recognized; Weill staff request a NetID from the IT Service Desk. [Cornell's travel rules](https://finance.cornell.edu/travel/transportation) call C2C the preferred mode between Ithaca and the NYC campuses, and university pricing needs a profile on the C2C site.

The booking system changed on [Nov 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4). Everyone needs a new account. Sign in with Cornell single sign-on to see the $93.50 fare; a standard account pays $101. One reservation can now hold several passengers.

The [policies page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) sets the family rules. A child under 2 rides free on a lap. From age 2 a child needs a paid seat, with no child discount. A stroller that takes a seat counts as a passenger. Cancelling costs $10 a seat at least 8 hours ahead and $30 inside 8 hours.

Example: a PhD student, a partner without a NetID and a 4-year-old ride to Weill. The student pays $93.50. Fares follow each passenger's own eligibility, per Cornell's [booking-system FAQ](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), so the partner and the child each pay the $101 public fare. The trip is $295.50 one way.

Driving to the bus? C2C riders [park free in B Lot](https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488) if they email c2cbus@cornell.edu with the plate and dates.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying OmniRide when you already have it.** A PhD or assistantship makes it free. Paying costs $200, and a refund before Mar 31 still loses $40.
- **Buying a B permit on a full assistantship.** The free A or E permit saves $359.77. Check your assistantship shows in Workday first.
- **Booking work trips as personal fares.** Department-billed C2C is $86; personal is $93.50. That is $7.50 each way, $15 a round trip.
- **Signing in to C2C with a plain account.** You pay the $101 public fare instead of $93.50.
- **Assuming the free pass lasts a two-year program.** It ends Aug 31. Year two costs $1.50 per daytime weekday ride or $200.
- **Signing a lease with no parking, then buying a car.** The postdoc page warns that many apartments have none. Street parking then means winter odd/even rules and a $45 city permit, if you qualify.
- **Parking on campus without paying.** The ticket is $50, plus $10 after 10 days.

## FAQ

**Do postdocs get the free bus pass?** Yes. Cornell's [postdoc office](https://postdocs.cornell.edu/postdoc-life/living-in-ithaca) says postdocs working on the Ithaca campus can enroll in the OmniRide commuter program for unlimited TCAT rides at all hours. Cornell [employees, affiliates included, ride TCAT free](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2024-10-22/unlimited-tcat-access-faculty-staff) with a Cornell photo ID.

**Can I buy a faculty or staff permit as a grad student?** Yes, with a full assistantship on the Ithaca main campus, per the [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). On the [employee fee schedule](https://fcs.cornell.edu/permit_fees), Perimeter permits cost the same as a B permit, Mid permits $574.91 to $752.98 a year and most Central permits $806.37, tax included. You pay by credit card, check or bursar bill, not in payroll installments.

**I live in university graduate housing. Do I get a free permit?** No. The page says resident students are not eligible for a free A or E permit. You can buy a resident permit, $752.98 a year, or a faculty and staff R (North Campus) or WE (West Campus) permit, which costs the same as a B permit on the employee fee schedule.

**Can my partner use Ithaca Carshare on my plan?** A student plan covers up to two drivers, per [Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters). Each driver pays the $20 sign-up and must meet the eligibility rules.

**Does a bike go on the bus?** Every TCAT bus has a [front bike rack at no extra fare](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall). MBA students can shower at Sage Hall, classroom level, per the [Johnson page](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/).
