```glance
- No bus runs from JFK or LaGuardia to Ithaca: get into Manhattan, then take a bus of 4 h 10 min to 7 h.
- Manhattan to Ithaca: from $25 to $43 at the cheapest (Nov 8, 2026 sample), or $93.50 on Cornell's C2C with a NetID.
- Or connect at JFK to Delta's nonstop to Ithaca (ITH); the ITH-Newark flight ended in 2025.
- Landing at ITH late at night: no bus meets you, so prebook one of the three listed taxis.
- Thanksgiving break is Nov 25 to 29, 2026; winter break housing costs $125 a week or $500, apply by Dec 1.
- Spring term starts Jan 25, 2027: come back early, because January storms have stopped buses before.
```

No bus goes straight from JFK to Cornell: get into Manhattan, then ride 4 h 10 min to 7 h to Ithaca on [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny), FlixBus, Greyhound or Cornell's C2C at [$93.50 one way with a NetID](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). The other way is a connection at JFK to the [Delta nonstop to Ithaca](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). Leave a wide gap between landing and the bus, and apply for winter break housing by Dec 1 if you will stay.

```reader "If you're landing at JFK"
There is no direct bus. Plan two legs: JFK into Manhattan, then a bus to Ithaca. A trip planner puts the whole bus route at about 6 h 43 min, for $20 to $80, and the flight option at about 2 h 46 min, for $35 to $270 (Rome2rio estimates, undated). Pick the bus by its Manhattan stop: C2C boards on the East Side (Weill Cornell, the Cornell Club), and OurBus serves Hudson Yards and Madison Ave. The costly mistake is a tight gap. Once a C2C bus leaves its last stop, the $93.50 fare is gone. If you miss an OurBus, you get credit, not cash.
```

```reader "If you're landing at Newark"
FlixBus runs direct to Newark Penn Station, not the airport. Cheap fares there ran $40 to $60 by weekday in a Nov 8, 2026 sample. OurBus also lists an Ithaca-Newark Airport route that stops at the AirTrain; its own pages say P3 in one place and P4 in another, so check your booking. Do not book a flight from Newark to Ithaca. That route ended in 2025. The worst outcome is the one from a 2019 account: landing around 10 p.m. with about 15 hours still to go, seven of them on an airport bench.
```

```reader "If you're connecting to Ithaca (ITH)"
ITH has two nonstops: Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles. That means one cancellation can strand you. The terminal is open 3:30 a.m. to midnight. TCAT Route 32 (weekdays) and Route 72 (weekends) run to the Ithaca Commons for $1.50. On Sat Nov 7, 2026, two of the four arrivals were due after 10 p.m., and no bus meets late flights. Prebook a taxi before you fly: Ithaca Airport Cab 607-252-9052, Collegetown Cab 607-588-8888 or Ithaca Dispatch 607-277-7777.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over winter break"
Apply between Oct 1 and Dec 1. Break housing is open to students returning to on-campus housing for spring. It costs $125 per one-week option or $500 for the full break, a flat fee. Mail and packages stop Dec 23 and resume Jan 7. Dining is usually closed the week the university closes, so stock food before then. C2C to NYC has no trips listed between Dec 24 and Jan 5. The mistake that costs most is missing Dec 1: the page lists no late option.
```

```reader "If you're planning to drive or rent a car"
Whether New York requires an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside your home license is not confirmed here, so ask the state DMV. Ithaca Carshare, the local car-sharing service, is stricter. It needs an official driving record and does not accept an IDP as one. It also says some countries cannot be approved. Drivers must be 18 or older and have held a full license for 2 or more years. Check eligibility before you count on a shared car for groceries or a winter break errand.
```

## From JFK to Cornell, step by step

```figure
kind: steps
title: JFK to Cornell by bus
rows:
  - label: Land at JFK; clear immigration and collect bags
  - label: Get from JFK into Manhattan
  - label: Take a bus to Ithaca from the Manhattan stop you booked (4 h 10 min to 7 h)
  - label: Get off at North Campus, West Campus or downtown Ithaca
```

1. **Before you fly, book the bus with slack.** Cornell does not publish a buffer rule in hours. In the [2019 Cornell Sun accounts](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/), Jaewon Sim '21 advised triple-checking the bus schedule, and Christine Relander advised arriving at the airport early and allowing an extra hour for traffic.
2. **JFK into Manhattan.** No bus runs from JFK or LaGuardia to Ithaca, so this leg comes first. [Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cornell-University/JFK-Airport-Terminal-4) estimates the whole Cornell-JFK trip by bus at about 6 h 43 min. Not confirmed: the cost and time of the JFK-to-Manhattan leg on its own.
3. **Manhattan to Ithaca.** OurBus lists [9 buses a day, the first at 5:00 a.m. and the last at 11:55 p.m.](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny), taking 4 h 10 min to 7 h. Since Sept 1, 2026, OurBus runs [4 to 6 trips a day between Manhattan and campus](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk), seven days a week.
4. **Arrive on campus.** OurBus stops on North Campus, West Campus and at the Ithaca Commons. Greyhound and FlixBus stop at [121 Jessup Rd on North Campus, 240 West Ave on West Campus and 131 E Green St downtown](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny). C2C stops at [North Campus (217 Jessup Road), Sage Hall and B Lot](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations).

Example: a first-year lands at JFK at 2 p.m. on a weekday in August with two big suitcases. In 2019 a student in that spot had to sprint for a 4:30 p.m. bus. An evening bus removes the sprint and absorbs a late landing or a slow immigration line. C2C fits the two bags: [two suitcases below deck plus two small carry-ons](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations). OurBus guarantees [one large bag per ticket](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes); at peak times, two large bags effectively mean two seats.

The floors come from one [Wanderu reading for the week of Nov 8 to 14, 2026](https://www.wanderu.com/en/bus/us-ny/ithaca/us-ny/new-york/): OurBus from $25, FlixBus $30, Greyhound $37. The same reading put carrier averages at $44.77 to $82.31. Floors are the cheapest seat, not what most riders pay.

C2C changed booking systems on Nov 4, 2026. [Everyone needs a new account](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), and you get the $93.50 fare only by signing in with Cornell single sign-on. C2C picks up at the [Weill lobby and the Cornell Club](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations), and the ride takes about 4.5 to 5 hours.

OurBus is cheaper but less forgiving. A cancellation gives [credit, not cash, and rescheduling has a fee](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel) (fall 2023).

## From Newark: Penn Station is not the airport

FlixBus runs direct between Ithaca and Newark Penn Station. Lowest fares in a [Nov 8, 2026 sample](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/newark-nj) ran $47 to $60 on Sunday, $40 to $58 on Monday and $42 to $49 on Thursday, with $36 on Wed Nov 18. Our midpoint for those five days averages $48.40. Going via NYC instead (OurBus about $32 plus Greyhound about $5) saved $9 to $12 on Nov 11, for about 50 more minutes and a Manhattan transfer.

Newark Penn is a train station in the city. Not confirmed: the rail connection and fare from Newark Penn to Newark Airport.

The direct option is [OurBus's Newark Airport route](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-newark-airport-nj). It boards at West Campus across from Baker Flagpole or on North Campus on Jessup Road. One OurBus page says it stops at AirTrain P4, another says P3, so read your ticket.

What no longer works: the Ithaca-Newark flight. It ended on [March 29, 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_Tompkins_International_Airport), and OurBus's co-founder credited part of a spring 2026 ridership rise to that loss.

## Flying into Ithaca instead

[ITH lists two scheduled routes](https://flyithaca.com/airlines): Delta to JFK and United to Washington Dulles, up to three a day. On [one Saturday's arrivals board](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals) (Nov 7, 2026, our count), 4 planes landed: JFK at 12:23 and 22:29, Dulles at 19:02 and 23:44. The JFK flights carried LATAM, Air France and KLM codeshare numbers.

| At ITH | What to know |
|---|---|
| Terminal hours | [3:30 a.m. to midnight](https://flyithaca.com) |
| Bus | [Route 32 weekdays, Route 72 weekends](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation), to the Commons, $1.50 |
| Paying the bus | Exact cash, card, phone wallet, Tcard or the TFare app |
| Taxis | [Three firms, 24/7, prebook for late flights](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) |
| Taxi fares | Not listed now; an [older airport page](https://flyithaca.com/?p=13) gave a $12 to $15 base plus $1.50 a bag |

First-years ride TCAT free with a Cornell ID [from fall registration](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Before that, or if the card fails, the fare is $1.50.

Early flights home have the same gap in reverse. In 2024 the Student Assembly president said that for 5:30 a.m. flights there is ["literally no transportation available"](https://cornellsun.com/2024/05/07/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports/) beyond costly rides.

## What went wrong for students in 2019

Nine international students told the Sun in 2019 how their trips went. The longest ran about 35 hours door to door:

- One landed at Newark around 10 p.m. from Tianjin with about 15 hours still to go, seven of them on an airport bench.
- One landed at JFK at 2 p.m. and sprinted for a 4:30 p.m. bus.
- One missed a campus bus by five minutes.
- A strike cancelled one student's flight to Ithaca, which meant a night at Port Authority.
- One gave a 9-hour flight plus a 5-hour bus as a "technically 14 hours" example, before any buffers.

Pia Bocanegra '19 advised spare clothes and toiletries in the carry-on. The same article noted that an early ticket can be wasted if plans change, and that waiting until March or April can roughly double prices.

## Thanksgiving and other short breaks

Thanksgiving break runs [Nov 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). Spring break runs Mar 27 to Apr 4, 2027. Neither is long enough for most trips home overseas.

Students who stay make other plans. In [2022 Sun reporting](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/16/students-share-upcoming-thanksgiving-break-plans), one student stayed in Ithaca for Cornell Dining's Thanksgiving meal, and another spent it with a friend's family in Cleveland because home takes a full day. A [2013 column](https://cornellsun.com/2013/12/01/oh-thanksgiving-in-ithaca-stranded-and-starved) said TCAT ran no service on Thanksgiving. Not confirmed: TCAT's 2026 holiday schedule.

If you go to NYC or a friend's home, book early. OurBus names the [Wednesday before Thanksgiving and the Friday spring break begins](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269) as peak days. In our Nov 8, 2026 reading of 51 priced dates on three routes (Binghamton, Rochester, DC), the lowest Thanksgiving-week fares ran 28% to 38% above earlier November days, as on the [Binghamton table](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/binghamton-ny).

Not confirmed: which dining halls open over Thanksgiving 2026.

Finals run Dec 11 to 19, 2026. Last year, [undergraduate residences closed at 2 p.m. on Sun Dec 21, 2025](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice). The 2026 closing notice is not out yet.

To stay, apply for [winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing) between Oct 1 and Dec 1. It is open to students returning to on-campus housing for spring. Ten buildings stay open, including Ganędagǫ: Hall, Hu Shih, Mews, Toni Morrison, the Townhouse Apartments and Cascadilla.

| Winter break housing | 2026-27 |
|---|---|
| One week | $125 |
| Full break | $500 flat |
| Applications | Oct 1 to Dec 1 |
| Mail and packages | Stop Dec 23, resume Jan 7 |
| Dining | Usually closed the week the university closes |

Example: an international sophomore weighs one week against the whole break. Four one-week options cost 4 x $125 = $500, the same as the flat fee. So anyone staying four weeks or more should take the full break.

Getting around while you stay: after first year, TCAT is [free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes); other rides cost $1.50.

If you fly home from NYC, C2C bookings split across [two links](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service): the "2026" link covers departures through Dec 23, and the "2027" link covers departures from Jan 6, 2027. Nothing is listed in between. OurBus names the last day of December finals as a peak day.

## Getting back in January

Spring term starts Jan 25, 2027, which leaves room to land early. January storms have stopped travel before. In [January 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/), Coach USA cancelled all NYC departures after noon on Saturday, Cornell's Syracuse-to-Cornell run was cancelled and the governor banned buses on most highways. Singaporean students stuck in NYC found beds through the Singapore student associations at NYU and Columbia.

The return after Thanksgiving carries the same risk. In the [Dec 1 to 2, 2019 storm](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/12/before-classes-were-cancelled-cornellians-attempted-to-return-to-ithaca-at-all-costs), OurBus cancelled trips and one ShortLine replacement took about 9 hours instead of 4 to 5. Before a forecast storm, a student forecaster's advice was to [return by Saturday night or early Sunday](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/30/under-heavy-snow-forecast-university-mum-on-class-cancellation).

January is also cheaper: Wanderu showed Ithaca-NYC bus fares averaging about $41, against about $66 in November.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking a bus two hours after landing.** A missed C2C keeps the full $93.50, and the return leg is not cancelled automatically. A missed OurBus gives credit, not cash, plus a fee to rebook.
- **Booking ITH to Newark.** The route ended in 2025. Connect at JFK on Delta or Dulles on United.
- **Treating Newark Penn as Newark Airport.** The direct FlixBus stops at the train station, so you still owe a transfer.
- **Landing at ITH after dark with no taxi booked.** No bus meets late flights. On Nov 7, 2026 two of the four arrivals were due after 10 p.m.
- **Missing the Dec 1 break housing deadline.** The fee is $125 a week or $500, and Cornell's page lists no late route.
- **Coming back the day before classes in January.** The 2019 bus ban on most highways shows what one storm can do.
- **Signing up for Carshare on an IDP.** It is not accepted as a driving record, and the [$20 sign-up fee](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates) is non-refundable.

## FAQ

**Can I take C2C if I'm not on Cornell's system yet?** Yes. C2C is [open to the public](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4) at $101 one way; signing in with Cornell single sign-on brings it to $93.50.

**Can I cancel a C2C trip if my flight is late?** Only in the reservation system. Cancelling 8 or more hours ahead costs [$10 a seat, and inside 8 hours $30](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).

**Is Syracuse a better airport to fly into?** It has more routes than ITH, and OurBus runs between Cornell and Syracuse airport [six days a week](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk). The [airport guide](/getting-around/ith-syr-or-elm-which-airport-for-cornell/) compares them by trip.

**What if a storm cancels my bus in January?** Operators tend to cancel early and add earlier trips: in 2019 [OurBus cancelled Sunday trips and added Saturday ones](https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/30/under-heavy-snow-forecast-university-mum-on-class-cancellation). Watch the forecast and move your trip up a day.

**Can I ship luggage instead of carrying it?** [Big Red Shipping and Storage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options) is Cornell's contracted vendor (607-524-3130), but it posts no prices. Not confirmed: international shipping rates.
