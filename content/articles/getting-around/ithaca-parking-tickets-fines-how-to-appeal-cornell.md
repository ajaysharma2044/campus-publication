A Cornell ticket for parking without a permit or payment costs $50, and it rises by $10 if you let it sit for 10 days ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-08-01/citation-fines-increasing-september-1)). City tickets range from $15 to $200. Contest either one with the office that wrote it.

```glance
- Cornell and the City of Ithaca write separate tickets. Pay or contest each with its own office.
- Cornell's common ticket is $50 for no permit or payment.
- City fines run from $15 for an overtime zone to $200 for a repeat handicap violation.
- A late city ticket adds a penalty, and more than five unpaid tickets can mean impoundment.
```

## Who wrote my ticket, Cornell or the city?

Look at the ticket. Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services (TDS) writes tickets in campus lots. The City of Ithaca writes tickets on streets, at meters and in its garages. The fines and the offices are different, so the first step is to read the issuer's name.

## How much are Cornell parking tickets?

Cornell raised its fines on Sept. 1, 2023, the first increase since 2014 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-08-01/citation-fines-increasing-september-1)). Permit or payment violations are about 85% of all tickets. The amounts below are from that 2023 schedule, so confirm today's with TDS.

| Violation | Cornell fine |
|---|---|
| No permit or payment | $50, plus $10 if unpaid in 10 days |
| Accessible space or aisle, fire lane, life-safety zone | $150 and automatic tow |
| Reserved space or unauthorized permit | $150 |
| Boot release | $150 |
| Parking on grass | $100 |
| No parking zone | $50 |
| Winter rules | $50 |
| Over the time limit | $35 |
| Plate not visible | $10 |

Source: [Cornell Daily Sun, Sept. 4, 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).

Cornell enforces whenever the university is open. It pauses on official closed staff holidays until the next business day. No-parking, fire lane, reserved, service and accessible rules are never suspended ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules)). Our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) explains how to avoid the most common one, the no-permit ticket.

## How much are City of Ithaca parking tickets?

The city's fines vary by violation, and several rise with repeats within 18 months ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)).

| Violation | City fine |
|---|---|
| Overtime meter | $25 |
| Overtime zone | $15 |
| Odd/even overnight | $15, $30, $45 |
| Residential permit zone | $15, $30, $45 |
| Fire hydrant | $50 |
| Yard parking | $20, $30, $50 |
| Handicap | $100, $150, $200 |
| Abandoned vehicle | $25 |

Source: [City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations). A ticket not paid within 20 days adds a $20 penalty, and more than five unpaid tickets can mean impoundment. The city's 2027 budget plans to raise or add fines for many common violations, worth about $381,000 a year. City Manager Dominick Recckio called it a modernization ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/11/common-council-approves-2027-budget-city-will-remain-below-tax-cap/)). Check the current schedule before you plan around any amount.

## How do I appeal a parking ticket in Ithaca?

Contact the issuing office quickly, before the late fee applies.

- **Cornell ticket:** call TDS at 607-255-4600, Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to noon, or use the TDS ticket portal listed on the [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). TDS is at 116 Maple Avenue ([TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services)).
- **City ticket:** call the Parking Violations Bureau at (607) 274-6581 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)).

Ask each office three things: the deadline to contest, what evidence it accepts, and whether the late fee is paused while a contest is pending. Get the answers in writing.

## What mistakes make a ticket worse?

- **Letting a Cornell ticket sit.** It grows by $10 after 10 days.
- **Ignoring a city ticket.** The late penalty applies after 20 days.
- **Collecting five or more unpaid city tickets.** The car can be impounded.
- **Parking in an accessible space or fire lane.** It draws the highest fine and a tow.

## What to do next

Photograph the ticket today, then call the issuing office before the late fee date. For future trips, register your plate in MyParking and check the posted signs.
