```glance
- There is no Zipcar in Ithaca. The local car share is Ithaca Carshare, a nonprofit with about 20 vehicles.
- Cornell students pay $30 a year plus $9 an hour (Just In Case) or $310 a year plus $6 an hour (It's My Car).
- Add a one-time $20 sign-up, 42 to 52 cents a mile and 20% in taxes on usage.
- Under about 93 driving hours a year (about 78 counting tax), Just In Case is cheaper.
- You must be 18 with a license held 2 years; under 21 needs your own insurance policy with $300,000 liability.
- An International Driving Permit is not accepted as a driving record.
```

Ithaca Carshare is worth it for most Cornell students who drive a few times a month and do not own a car. Pick the Cornell-subsidized Just In Case plan, [$30 a year plus $9 an hour](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters), unless you will drive more than about 78 to 93 hours a year. Check the [eligibility rules](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) before you pay, because under-21s and foreign licenses face extra hurdles.

```reader "If you're an upper-year without a car"
You lost free daytime TCAT after first year, so errands now cost $1.50 a ride before 6 p.m. on weekdays ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). Carshare fills the gaps the bus does not: a big grocery haul, a hardware run, moving a desk. Join Just In Case at $30 a year; a 2-hour, 8-mile grocery run then costs about $25.63 with tax, by our math. Do the trip after 6 p.m. or on a weekend and TCAT is free instead. The mistake that costs most: applying under 21 without your own car insurance policy. Carshare cannot approve you, and the $20 sign-up is non-refundable.
```

```reader "If you're a grad or professional student"
You may live off campus with a partner or kids, and an apartment may come with no parking. Carshare's student plans cover grad students and allow up to two drivers ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)). Not confirmed: whether the second driver must also be a student. If you drive every week, price It's My Car: $310 a year plus $6 an hour, and it wins past about 93 hours a year. A permit is the other benchmark: the $752.98 resident permit alone equals about 59 to 70 Carshare hours, by our math. The costly mistake is buying a car and a permit for trips that add up to a few hours a month.
```

```reader "If you're renting off campus"
Carshare's pods sit in your neighborhoods: Downtown, Northside, Southside, Fall Creek, Collegetown, West End and East Hill ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org)). That beats a $359.77 commuter permit plus a city parking fight if you drive only for errands ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Book the pod nearest your lease, not the campus one. A 4-hour, 20-mile Saturday errand runs about $53.28 on Just In Case and $38.88 on It's My Car with tax, by our math. The mistake: buying a car and a permit for a commute that walking or Route 10 already covers.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca over a break"
Dining is usually closed the week the university closes ([winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)), so groceries matter. Just In Case at $30 a year plus $9 an hour fits a few trips ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)). Four 2-hour, 8-mile runs over winter break cost about $152.53 in your first year, sign-up included, by our math. Join in early December, not the day you need a car. The Carshare office keeps weekday hours only, 9 to 5 ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org)). The mistake: waiting until the closed week, when questions sit until a weekday.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Carshare needs an official driving record from your home country. An International Driving Permit is not accepted as that record, and some countries cannot be approved at all ([eligibility](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility)). Your license must also be at least 2 years old. Ask for your record before you leave home. The page does not name the countries it cannot approve, so call (607) 277-3210 before you pay the non-refundable $20 sign-up. That fee lost is the costly mistake. If you cannot qualify, plan grocery runs in TCAT's free hours: after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend for upper-years.
```

```reader "If you're staying for the summer"
OmniRide passes expire Aug 31 ([Cornell TDS](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)), and a summer job off the bus lines is where a car share earns its fee. Student discounts apply only to annual plans; monthly plans are full community price ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)). Just In Case is $7.50 a month for the community ([rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)), so three summer months cost $22.50, against $30 for the student year. If you will stay in the fall, take the $30 annual. Not confirmed: whether monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.
```

## Is there a Zipcar at Cornell?

No. Cornell's car-sharing page points students to [Ithaca Carshare](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing), the local nonprofit, not Zipcar.

Ithaca Carshare runs about 20 vehicles across the city, with maintenance and insurance included ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org)). The fleet has fuel-efficient cars, a minivan and a pickup truck, with 24/7 access ([Ithaca College](https://www.ithaca.edu/orientation/family-and-guest-resources/transportation)). Cornell lists [four on-campus pickup spots plus four in Collegetown and nearby](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing). Not confirmed: the street address of each pod.

## How much Ithaca Carshare costs a Cornell student

Cornell subsidizes annual memberships for undergraduates, grad students, staff and faculty through its Commuter Alternative Program. Each student plan is $45 off the community price ([students and commuters](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)). Ithaca College and TC3 students qualify too.

| | Just In Case (student) | It's My Car (student) |
|---|---|---|
| Yearly fee | $30 | $310 |
| Per hour | $9 | $6 |
| Per mile | 42 cents car, 44 cents van, 52 cents truck | Same |
| Sign-up, once per driver | $20, non-refundable | $20, non-refundable |
| Taxes on usage | 8% NY sales tax + 12% rental tax | Same |
| Daily cap | Hourly charges stop at 10 hours per 24 | Same |
| Drivers | Up to two | Up to two |

Rates are from the [rates page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates) and the [student page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters), both undated and read in November 2026. The 10-hour daily cap does not apply to the highest-demand cars. The student page lists gas or EV charging, maintenance and insurance as included with It's My Car.

The community plans, for comparison: Just In Case is $7.50 a month or $75 a year, meant for under 6 hours a month. It's My Car is $35 a month or $355 a year, for over 6 hours a month ([rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)). Monthly plans get no student discount.

### What a trip costs

Our math below uses the posted rates, a car at 42 cents a mile, and the 20% tax on both hours and miles. The rates page says both taxes are added to hourly and mileage charges.

| Trip (our examples) | Just In Case | It's My Car |
|---|---|---|
| Grocery run: 2 hours, 8 miles | $25.63 | $18.43 |
| Saturday errands: 4 hours, 20 miles | $53.28 | $38.88 |
| Day trip: 10 hours (the cap), 120 miles | $168.48 | $132.48 |
| A year: 20 hours, 100 miles, with fee and sign-up | $316.40 | $524.40 |

Compare a [$1.50 TCAT ride](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), free for upper-years after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend. A 20-minute [Ithaca Bikeshare](https://www.ithacabikeshare.org) trip is $6.50 before tax.

## Which plan to pick: the 93-hour rule

It's My Car costs $280 more a year and saves $3 an hour. So it pays only past 280 / 3 = about 93 driving hours a year. Count the 20% tax on hours and the line drops to about 78 hours (280 / 3.60). Miles cost the same on both plans, so they do not move it.

- **Under about 78 hours a year:** Just In Case. That is about 39 two-hour trips.
- **Over about 78 hours a year:** It's My Car, once tax counts. Past 93 hours it wins even before tax.
- **Unsure:** start on Just In Case. Guessing low costs you $3 an hour; guessing high costs $280 up front.

Example: a junior in Collegetown, no car, plans two 2-hour, 8-mile grocery runs a month for eight months. That is 32 hours and 128 miles. Just In Case: $30 + $20 + (32 x $9 + 128 x $0.42) x 1.20 = $30 + $20 + $410.11 = $460.11. It's My Car: $310 + $20 + (32 x $6 + $53.76) x 1.20 = $330 + $294.91 = $624.91. Just In Case wins by $164.80.

## Carshare against owning a car

A Cornell resident parking permit costs [$752.98 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students), and a commuter permit $359.77. Those prices buy no gas, insurance or car.

By our yearly math, a student with no car who uses It's My Car for 20 hours and takes four NYC round trips at the $33 OurBus floor spends about $714. That is $310 + $20 + $120 + $264, before taxes on the driving.

## Who can join: the eligibility rules

The [eligibility page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility) sets the bar by age and license.

| Driver | What Carshare requires |
|---|---|
| Everyone | Age 18 or older; full, unrestricted license held 2 or more years; no suspension in 3 years |
| Under 21 | A spotless record; your own insurance policy with at least $300,000 per accident liability; the Younger Driver Insurance Consent Form; your policy's declarations page |
| 21 and over | At most two accidents or violations in 3 years; a DUI or DWI in the last 7 years disqualifies |
| Foreign license | An official driving record; an International Driving Permit is not accepted as a record; some countries cannot be approved |

The page mentions both 2-year and 3-year lookbacks for foreign records. If yours is borderline, call (607) 277-3210 or email info@ithacacarshare.org.

The under-21 rule is the one that stops sophomores. Carshare asks for your own policy with the $300,000 liability limit, so ask your insurer before you apply.

1. **Check eligibility** against the table above, using the [official page](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility).
2. **Apply** at reserve.ithacacarshare.org/orientation and pay the non-refundable $20 per driver ([rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)).
3. **Pick a student annual plan.** The Cornell discount applies only to annual plans ([students and commuters](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)).
4. **Reserve and drive** from one of the four campus pods or four in Collegetown and nearby ([Cornell Sustainability](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing)).

## What students use it for

Groceries, mostly. A 2013-era Cornell report found that 54% of Carshare trips by on-campus Cornell students were grocery runs ([Cornell Sustainability](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/campus-initiatives/transportation/ride-car-sharing)). In 2012-13, 584 Cornell students and 237 employees used the membership incentive ([STARS report, 2013](https://reports.aashe.org/institutions/cornell-university-ny/report/2013-03-08/OP/transportation/OP-T2-37)). Those are the newest usage counts published; treat them as history.

Car share as a safety net is a theme in five sources from 2016 to 2024. In a [2016 College Confidential thread](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/should-i-bring-a-car/1811430), posters told a first-year to skip the car. In the [Sun in 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/19/ithaca-carshare-to-resume-operations-march-2024/), a grad student described using Carshare as a backup, then switching to Bikeshare and planning to buy a car.

Not confirmed: any student review of Carshare since it returned in 2024.

## The 2023 to 2024 shutdown

Carshare stopped in May 2023 when its insurer left the market. A Vermont-based risk retention group could not insure it under New York rules until the governor signed a bill ([Sun, 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/19/ithaca-carshare-to-resume-operations-march-2024/)). It returned in March 2024 with 12 cars, down from 30, with a goal of 20 ([Sun, 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/03/25/ithaca-carshare-officially-returns-to-operation)). Its home page listed about 20 vehicles in November 2026. Any page saying Carshare is closed is out of date.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying under 21 on a parent's insurance.** Carshare needs your own policy with $300,000 liability. Without it you lose the $20 sign-up.
- **Counting on an International Driving Permit.** It is not accepted as a driving record. Get the official record from home first.
- **Buying It's My Car for occasional trips.** Below about 78 to 93 hours a year, it costs up to $280 more than Just In Case.
- **Paying monthly for a school year.** Monthly Just In Case at $7.50 is $90 over 12 months, against $30 for the student annual plan.
- **Driving into the hours TCAT is free.** An upper-year's grocery run after 6 p.m. on a weekday costs $0 on the bus and about $25.63 by car share.
- **Joining the day you need a car.** The Carshare office is open weekdays, 9 to 5, so a weekend problem waits until Monday. Join a week ahead of a break or a move.
- **Trusting old prices.** The 2008 rate of $4.95 an hour still ranks in search; today's student rates are $6 and $9.

## FAQ

**Does Ithaca Carshare work for Ithaca College or TC3 students?** Yes. Ithaca College and TC3 students qualify for the same discounted annual plans ([students and commuters](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)).

**Can I take a Carshare car on a day trip?** Yes. Hourly charges are capped at 10 hours in any 24, except on the highest-demand cars, so a 10-hour, 120-mile day costs about $168.48 on Just In Case by our math ([rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)).

**Can I rent the pickup truck for move-out?** The fleet includes a minivan and a pickup ([Ithaca College](https://www.ithaca.edu/orientation/family-and-guest-resources/transportation)). The truck bills at 52 cents a mile and the van at 44, against 42 for a car ([rates](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates)).

**Is there a low-income plan?** Carshare's [Summer 2024 member handbook](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/wp-content/uploads/IthacaCarshareHandbook.pdf) describes an Easy Access plan at $10 a month plus $6 an hour, with $20 a month in driving credits. Not confirmed: who qualifies for it.

**Can two of us share one membership?** Student annual plans cover up to two drivers ([students and commuters](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)). Each driver pays the $20 sign-up.

**Is gas included?** The student page lists gas or EV charging as included with It's My Car. The mileage charge applies on both plans.
