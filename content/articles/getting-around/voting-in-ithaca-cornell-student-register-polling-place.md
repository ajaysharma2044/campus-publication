Register to vote at the address where you live in Ithaca, because that address decides which local offices you vote for. Deadlines, registration forms and polling places come from the Tompkins County Board of Elections and the New York State Board of Elections, so confirm them there before you register.

```glance
- Your Ithaca address, not your Cornell mailing address, decides your local ward and council.
- Common Council voted 8-2 to opt Ithaca into Good Cause eviction protections in 2024.
- Council passed the 120-day renter notice rule unanimously in 2022.
- Confirm deadlines and polling places with the Tompkins County Board of Elections.
```

## Why does your Ithaca address matter?

An "Ithaca, NY 14850" mailing address covers 9 municipalities by the Town of Ithaca's address page and 12 by its welcome page. The Town of Ithaca wraps around the City like a donut ([Town of Ithaca](https://townithacany.gov/welcome-new-resident-guide/)). Two students with the same ZIP code can live under different local governments. The [City versus Town guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) shows where your apartment sits. The Town's page also has a tool to check an address.

## What do local votes decide for students?

Local votes set rent rules. Common Council voted 8-2 to opt in to Good Cause renter protections, as reported on July 12, 2024 ([WAMC](https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections)). It passed the 120-day notice rule for lease renewals unanimously. Alderperson Patrick Mehler sponsored it ([WSKG](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/)). Council decisions on housing and buses affect your costs, including the [TCAT](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) services you ride.

## How do you register and find your polling place?

The sources for this guide do not include New York registration deadlines, the Tompkins County Board of Elections' contact details, or campus and Collegetown polling places. Start with the New York State Board of Elections and the Tompkins County Board of Elections websites. Bring the address on your lease or housing assignment, since that is the address you will register.

```reader "If you're a first-year in a residence hall"
Your hall's street address is where you live, so use it when you register. Mail comes through a service center, which is a different address.
```

```reader "If you're living off campus or a grad student"
Use the street address and unit number from your lease. See the [off-campus start page](/start/off-campus/) and [grad student start page](/start/grad-student/) for the rest of your move-in tasks.
```

## What to do next

Look up your polling place on the county's site, register at your Ithaca address, and read the [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) for your other first-month tasks.
