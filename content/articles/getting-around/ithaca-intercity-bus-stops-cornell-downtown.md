```glance
- OurBus boards downtown at 235 S. Cayuga St from Mon Nov 9, 2026, beside the Cayuga Street Garage. Its East Seneca St stop ends.
- Greyhound and FlixBus list 131 E Green St downtown, curbside in front of Urban Outfitters. Wait outside, not in the library.
- On campus, OurBus, Greyhound and FlixBus board on Jessup Rd (North) and West Ave (West), each at its own curb.
- Cornell's C2C boards at 217 Jessup Rd, then Sage Hall 15 minutes later, then B Lot 30 minutes later. It has no downtown stop.
- The 710 W State St bus station is closed. Cornell's families page still names it.
- Not confirmed: whether Greyhound, FlixBus or Megabus also move to South Cayuga on Nov 9. Check your ticket.
```

Ithaca has no bus station building any more: intercity buses board at curbs on campus and downtown, and the stop depends on the company. From Monday, Nov 9, 2026, OurBus's downtown stop is [235 S. Cayuga St, beside the South Cayuga garage](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/), while Greyhound and FlixBus still list [131 E Green St](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny). If you live on campus, skip downtown and board on Jessup Road or West Avenue at your operator's exact spot.

```reader "If you're a first-year on North Campus"
Your stop is on Jessup Road, but three companies use three spots. C2C leaves from the shelters at 217 Jessup Rd in front of Robert Purcell. Greyhound and FlixBus use 121 Jessup Rd, on the south side near Robert Purcell. OurBus boards on Jessup Rd behind Ganedago Hall. Read the stop line on your ticket, not the street name. TCAT is [free for you at all hours](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) until Aug 31, 2027, so a downtown departure costs nothing extra to reach. The costly mistake is standing at the wrong Jessup spot as your bus pulls out.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visitor"
Do not send your student to 710 W State St. That terminal is closed, though Cornell's families page still lists it. For a trip that starts downtown, Greyhound and FlixBus board at 131 E Green St, and OurBus at 235 S. Cayuga St from Nov 9, 2026. If you are driving to meet a bus, the Cayuga Street Garage at 245 S. Cayuga St has more than 300 spaces at $2.25 an hour with a $12 daily maximum. The Seneca Street garage has been closed since spring 2026.
```

```reader "If you're an upper-year without a car"
Board on campus if you can. Both campus stops are free to reach on foot, while a weekday daytime TCAT ride downtown costs you $1.50 unless you hold OmniRide. Evening and weekend rides are free after your first year: after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday. West Campus boarding is at 240 West Ave in front of Mennen Hall (Greyhound, FlixBus) or across from Baker Flagpole (OurBus). Arriving on a late bus, plan the last mile: the Blue Light bus, Route 92, runs nightly until 2:30 a.m.
```

```reader "If you're renting off campus"
If you live downtown, the downtown curbs are yours. OurBus moves to 235 S. Cayuga St on Nov 9, 2026, south of the Commons; Greyhound, FlixBus and Megabus list 131 E Green St. In Collegetown you can pick a downtown curb or the West Ave stops on campus, where OurBus, Greyhound and FlixBus all board. If you drive to the bus, the Cayuga Street Garage caps at $12 a day. TCAT downtown stops also change on Nov 9: Route 10 stays on North Tioga St, and routes 30, 30W and 90 move to East Seneca at North Tioga.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer or spring entrant"
Arriving in January, you will board at the same stops as everyone else. The Nov 9, 2026 move is already in place by then. A spring entrant rides TCAT free from Jan 1 to Dec 31 of the same year, so a downtown departure is free to reach. If you take C2C, book on the ["2027 Travel Reservations" link](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service) for trips from Jan 6, 2027; the "2026" link covers only departures through Dec 23, 2026. Older blogs and Cornell's families page still point to the closed 710 W State St station; ignore them.
```

```reader "If you're headed beyond NYC"
Check the Ithaca stop on each route page, because they differ. Megabus's Toronto page lists 131 E Green St. OurBus's DC and Toronto pages still show 130 E Seneca St, the stop that ends on Nov 9, 2026, so expect your ticket to say 235 S. Cayuga St instead and confirm it. OurBus to Newark Airport boards on campus: across from Baker Flagpole or on Jessup Road. C2C goes only to Manhattan, so it is no help here.
```

## Every Ithaca boarding point, by operator

Each company publishes its own stop list, and the same street can mean three different curbs. This is the full map as of Nov 8, 2026.

## North Campus: Jessup Road

Jessup Road is the closest boarding point for most first-years, and it is where people get confused. The three spots sit near Robert Purcell Community Center but are not the same curb.

- **C2C** boards at the shelters at 217 Jessup Rd, in front of Robert Purcell. It is the first stop of each Ithaca departure, per the [C2C schedule](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule).
- **Greyhound and FlixBus** board at 121 Jessup Rd, on the south side near Robert Purcell.
- **OurBus** boards on Jessup Rd behind Ganedago Hall.

Since Tuesday, Sept 1, 2026, OurBus runs [4 to 6 buses a day, 7 days a week, from campus to Manhattan](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk), stopping at North Campus, West Campus and the Commons. Before that, its campus stops ran mostly around major breaks. OurBus's own [Cornell page](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university) says most of its Cornell buses make two campus stops, West and North.

## West Campus: West Avenue

West Avenue has two curbs.

- **Greyhound and FlixBus** board at 240 West Ave, on the west side in front of Mennen Hall.
- **OurBus** boards on West Ave across from Baker Flagpole. Its [Newark Airport route](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-newark-airport-nj) also boards here or on Jessup Road.

C2C does not stop on West Campus.

## Central campus: Sage Hall and B Lot (C2C only)

C2C is Cornell's own coach to Manhattan, and it boards only on campus. After 217 Jessup Rd, the bus calls at Sage Hall, 114 Feeney Way, at the TCAT shelter near East Ave and Campus Rd across from Statler Hall. Its last Ithaca stop is B Lot, 930 Campus Rd by the Vet College, at the shelter on the southeast side of the lot.

The [C2C FAQ](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-frequently-asked-questions) says to arrive at least 10 minutes early, and that drivers do not leave early. Once the bus leaves its last stop, the fare is gone. B Lot is also where C2C riders with a car can park free by request. The fare is [$93.50 one way with a NetID and $101 for everyone else](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates).

Example: a sophomore in Collegetown has a C2C seat on the 11:30 a.m. departure, one of the North Campus times on the [schedule page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule). The same bus reaches Sage Hall at about 11:45 a.m. and B Lot at about noon. Boarding at B Lot buys half an hour, but it is the last stop: miss it and the fare is gone.

## Downtown: Green Street and the new South Cayuga stop

There is no indoor terminal downtown. Buses load at the curb.

**131 E Green St** is the curb in front of Urban Outfitters. Greyhound and FlixBus list it, and so does [Downtown Ithaca's visitor page](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/). Greyhound asks riders not to wait inside the Tompkins County Public Library.

**235 S. Cayuga St** is OurBus's downtown stop from Monday, Nov 9, 2026, beside the South Cayuga garage. It replaces the East Seneca St stop; OurBus's [DC route page](https://www.ourbus.com/amp/routes/cornell-ithaca-to-dc) still shows the old address, 130 E Seneca St. The [City of Ithaca and TCAT notice](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/) says the intercity stop is moving to the 200 block of South Cayuga St to ease congestion and answer merchant complaints since the Seneca Street garage closed in spring 2026. That notice names no operator.

Not confirmed: whether Greyhound, FlixBus, Megabus or Trailways also move to South Cayuga on Nov 9. Only OurBus is named, and their own pages still list 131 E Green St as of Nov 8, 2026. Check the stop on your ticket the day before you travel.

The Cayuga Street Garage next door, at 245 S. Cayuga St, has more than 300 spaces and sits within a five-minute walk of the Commons. City garages charge [$2.25 an hour with a $12 daily maximum](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1328) at Cayuga and Green Street.

## The closed bus station at 710 W State St

Ithaca once had a bus station at 710 W State St. [Wikipedia lists it as closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_Bus_Station), and in October 2018 the Sun reported the [terminal relocating to Green Street](https://cornellsun.com/2018/10/10/ithaca-bus-terminal-relocates-to-new-space-on-green-street/). Cornell's [families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation) still names 710 West State St. Trust the operators' pages instead.

Green Street has its own history. In February 2019 the city [declined to renew OurBus's Green Street boarding permit](https://cornellsun.com/2019/04/14/ourbus-unable-to-renew-permit-to-board-on-green-street-sues-city-of-ithaca/), citing irregular scheduling and late fees, and OurBus sued. Not confirmed: how that case ended.

## Getting to each stop by foot or TCAT

On campus, walk. The C2C, OurBus, Greyhound and FlixBus stops on Jessup Rd and West Ave sit beside the residence halls. Sage Hall is a TCAT stop itself.

Downtown is a different trip. OurBus's co-founder, Axel Hellman, said in the company's announcement, as the Sun reported, that a 20-minute walk downtown [matters with luggage at early or late hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk). Route 10 is the [downtown and campus route](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting) Cornell names. What a TCAT ride costs depends on who you are:

| Rider | TCAT to a downtown stop |
|---|---|
| First-year (fall entrant) | Free at all hours to Aug 31 of the next year |
| Spring entrant | Free Jan 1 to Dec 31 of the same year |
| Upper-year without OmniRide | Free after 6 p.m. weekdays and all weekend; $1.50 cash otherwise |
| Upper-year with OmniRide | Free; the pass is $200 a year |
| PhD student or graduate assistant | Free OmniRide pass |

Source: Cornell's [bus passes page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

On Nov 9, 2026 TCAT's downtown stops shift too, per the [city and TCAT notice](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/03/ithaca-and-tcat-will-move-downtown-bus-stops-nov-9/):

| TCAT stop from Nov 9, 2026 | Where | Routes |
|---|---|---|
| East Seneca stop 1 | About 60 yards east, between Dewitt Mall and Tompkins Financial | 11, 11S, 13, 14, 15, 17, 20, 21, 31W, 40, 43, 51, 52, 65, 67 |
| East Seneca stop 2 | East Seneca at North Tioga, the former intercity spot | 30, 30W, 90 |
| North Tioga St | Unchanged | 10 |

TCAT posts the changes on its website, Bus Tracker and the MyStop app. Not confirmed: 2026 timetables for these routes, so check Bus Tracker for times.

Coming back late, the Blue Light bus on Route 92 runs [nightly from 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m., year-round](https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation).

Example: a first-year on North Campus books an OurBus seat that leaves from downtown, because the campus departures are sold out. The TCAT ride downtown is free for them, but they should board a bus that reaches the Commons well before departure. From Nov 9 the right curb is 235 S. Cayuga St, not the old East Seneca spot or Green Street.

## Common mistakes

- **Going to 710 W State St.** It is closed. You miss the bus and buy a new ticket; OurBus cancellations [give credit, not cash](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel) (fall 2023), and rescheduling has a fee.
- **Waiting at the old OurBus stop after Nov 9, 2026.** The East Seneca St curb is now a TCAT stop for routes 30, 30W and 90. Your OurBus boards at 235 S. Cayuga St.
- **Standing at the wrong Jessup Rd curb.** 217 is C2C, 121 is Greyhound and FlixBus, and behind Ganedago Hall is OurBus. A missed C2C bus keeps the full $93.50 fare.
- **Waiting inside the library on Green Street.** Greyhound tells riders to wait at the curb, not in the Tompkins County Public Library, so you can miss the bus from indoors.
- **Paying for a garage that is closed.** The Seneca Street garage shut in spring 2026. Use Cayuga Street at $12 a day maximum.
- **Assuming the downtown ride is free.** For upper-years without OmniRide, a weekday ride before 6 p.m. is $1.50.

## FAQ

**Is there a waiting room or ticket counter in Ithaca?** No. Every stop on this page is a curb or a shelter, and Greyhound asks riders not to wait inside the library on Green Street.

**Do C2C buses stop downtown?** No. C2C boards only at 217 Jessup Rd, Sage Hall and B Lot, per its [stop locations page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations).

**Where does the bus to Syracuse airport board?** OurBus picks up behind Ganedago Hall, across from Baker Flagpole and at the Cayuga Street Garage, and drops at Terminal A, per its [route page](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny).

**Who do I call about a stop?** Greyhound's Ithaca number is [607-272-7930](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/travel-resources), and OurBus is [1 (844) 800-6828](https://www.downtownithaca.com/getting-around-visit/).

**Where do OurBus buses arrive in New York City?** Its [Ithaca to New York page](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny) lists the GWB Bus Station, Hudson Yards and Madison Ave at 26th to 27th St.
