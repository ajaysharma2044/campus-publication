```glance
- Newark Airport is the only New York-area airport with a direct bus: OurBus boards at West Campus (Baker Flagpole) or North Campus (Jessup Road) and drops at the AirTrain P3 station.
- FlixBus goes direct to Newark Penn Station, not the airport: $40 to $60 one way in a Nov 8, 2026 sample, then $11.95 on NJ Transit with the AirTrain included.
- JFK and LaGuardia: bus to Manhattan first. Subway plus AirTrain to JFK is $11.75; subway plus the free Q70 bus to LaGuardia is $3.
- Flying: Delta runs a daily nonstop from Ithaca (ITH) to JFK. The ITH to Newark flight ended in 2025.
- Missed connections are the costly mistake: a missed C2C keeps the full fare, and a missed OurBus gives credit, not cash.
```

If you are flying out of Newark, take OurBus's direct [Ithaca to Newark Airport route](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-newark-airport-nj) from West or North Campus. For JFK or LaGuardia there is no direct bus: ride to Manhattan and finish by train, or fly Delta's [nonstop from Ithaca to JFK](https://flyithaca.com/airlines). Either way, leave hours of slack, because the bus alone runs [4 h 10 min to 7 h](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny).

```reader "If you're an international student"
Your long-haul flight probably leaves from JFK or Newark. Delta flies ITH to JFK, so ask whether your airline can ticket that leg with the rest; on Nov 7, 2026 the JFK flights carried [LATAM, Air France and KLM codes](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals). By bus, Newark is the simplest: OurBus runs to the airport itself. The costly mistake is a tight gap. Students in a [2019 Cornell Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/) sprinted from a 2 p.m. JFK landing for a 4:30 p.m. bus, and one missed a campus bus by five minutes. Carry spare clothes and toiletries in your cabin bag, as one of them advised.
```

```reader "If you're a domestic student who flies home"
Compare a NYC flight against Syracuse before you book. OurBus runs from campus to SYR for [$46.7 to $62.1 one way](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-syracuse-airport-ny), and SYR has Delta, JetBlue and American routes, including [JFK and LaGuardia](https://syrairport.org/destinations/). If NYC wins on price, Newark is the easy bus. For JFK, Delta's ITH flight avoids Manhattan entirely. Dawn flights are the trap: in 2024 the Student Assembly president [said](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/05/student-assembly-funds-free-charter-buses-to-syracuse-ithaca-airports) there was "literally no transportation available" for 5:30 a.m. flights. Prebook a [taxi](https://flyithaca.com/taxis) the night before.
```

```reader "If you're headed to New Jersey"
New Jersey is the one place FlixBus takes you without NYC. The direct bus to Newark Penn ran [$40 to $60 one way](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/newark-nj) in the Nov 8, 2026 sample, cheapest at $36 on Wed Nov 18, with Sunday the dearest day. Going via Manhattan cost about $37 on Nov 11 for about 50 more minutes. C2C runs only to Manhattan, so it does not help here. The mistake that costs most: buying Newark Penn for a Newark Airport flight and then paying [$11.95](https://www.mta.info/guides/airports/newark-ewr) for the NJ Transit leg you did not plan for.
```

```reader "If you're a parent or visiting family"
You pay public fares. Cornell's C2C bus to Manhattan is [$101 one way](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates), open to anyone, and [every rider needs a new account since Nov 4, 2026](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4), past riders included. Bring [photo ID matching the booking](https://c2cbus.ipp.cornell.edu/Start.aspx). If you are buying your student's ticket home, the direct OurBus to Newark avoids a Manhattan transfer with bags. For JFK, Delta's ITH flight is the low-effort choice. If you are flying out yourself after a visit, ITH long-term [parking](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking) is $8 a day.
```

## Newark Airport: the direct OurBus

OurBus's [Newark Airport route page](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-newark-airport-nj) boards on West Campus at Baker Flagpole (West Avenue, uphill side), on North Campus on Jessup Road, and downtown on S. Cayuga Street. It drops at Newark's AirTrain P3 station, on the south side, at the sign for Hotel Shuttles Zone 1. OurBus confirms exact boarding details when you book, so check your confirmation, then ride the AirTrain to your terminal.

On Nov 11, 2026 the route page listed [fares from $95.70](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-newark-airport-nj), depending on the date and how full the bus is, for a ride of 4 h 20 min to 5 h.

Bags: OurBus includes [one suitcase up to 50 lb in the hold plus one handbag](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university). Extra bags are $30 each and must be prepaid during booking. If plans change, cancellations [return credit, not cash, and rescheduling has a fee](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/11/inside-ourbus-cornellians-preferred-method-of-travel) (fall 2023 report).

## Newark Penn Station is not Newark Airport

FlixBus runs direct from Ithaca to Newark Penn Station, a rail hub in downtown Newark. You still need a train to the airport. Fares on the direct bus clustered between $40 and $60 in a [Nov 8, 2026 sample of one aggregator](https://www.checkmybus.com/ithaca-ny/newark-nj), with Sunday the dearest day.

That is about $37, $9 to $12 less than the direct bus, for about 50 more minutes and a Manhattan transfer (our arithmetic on those fares).

From Newark Penn, the [MTA's Newark airport guide](https://www.mta.info/guides/airports/newark-ewr) gives these steps:

1. Buy an NJ Transit ticket to Newark Airport Station, not to Newark Penn. It costs $11.95 and includes the AirTrain, normally $8.50 alone.
2. Board a Northeast Corridor or North Jersey Coast Line train that stops at Newark Airport.
3. Change to the AirTrain at Newark Airport Rail Station. It takes about 15 to 20 minutes to reach your terminal.
4. On weekdays from 5 a.m. to 3 p.m., shuttle buses replace the AirTrain between the train station and the terminals.

## JFK: bus to Manhattan, then train

No bus runs from Ithaca to JFK. Ride to Manhattan, then take the subway or the Long Island Rail Road (LIRR) to the AirTrain.

Where each Manhattan bus drops you decides the next leg:

- **OurBus**: the [GWB Bus Station, Hudson Yards, or Madison Ave at 26th to 27th St](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny), from $33 on Nov 8, 2026, nine buses a day.
- **Greyhound and FlixBus**: [Port Authority at 625 8th Ave](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny) among 13 stops, including Jamaica, Queens (91-91 144th Pl). Greyhound showed "from $43.98" on Nov 8; [FlixBus](https://global.flixbus.com/bus-routes/bus-ithaca-ny-new-york-ny) lists at least five trips a day.
- **Cornell's C2C**: the [Cornell Club at 6 E 44th St and Weill Cornell at 1300 York Ave](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations). It costs [$93.50 with a NetID or $101 for anyone else](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates), and takes [two suitcases plus two small carry-ons](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) per rider.

From Manhattan, the [MTA's JFK guide](https://www.mta.info/guides/airports/jfk) lists three ways in:

| Route to JFK | Fare for most riders | What you pay |
|---|---|---|
| Subway to Sutphin Blvd-Archer Av-JFK Airport (Jamaica), then AirTrain | $11.75 | $3 subway + $8.75 AirTrain |
| Subway to Howard Beach-JFK Airport, then AirTrain | $11.75 | $3 subway + $8.75 AirTrain |
| LIRR to Jamaica, then AirTrain | $14 or $16 | $5.25 off-peak or $7.25 peak + $8.75 AirTrain |

The Port Authority's [JFK transit page](https://www.jfkairport.com/transportation/public-transport) gives typical times: about 50 minutes by E train from Penn Station, 55 from Grand Central Madison, 60 by A train from below 14th Street, and about 30 by LIRR from Penn Station or Grand Central Madison.

The Greyhound and FlixBus Jamaica stop is [curbside on 144th Place, just off Archer Avenue](https://www.greyhound.com/bus/new-york-ny/queens-jamaica). The JFK AirTrain leaves from Jamaica Station, reached by subway at Sutphin Blvd-Archer Av-JFK Airport.

Example: a hypothetical sophomore with one suitcase takes OurBus at its $33 floor, then the subway and AirTrain for $11.75. That is $44.75 to JFK. The same trip on C2C with a NetID is $93.50 plus $11.75, or $105.25, and buys room for a second suitcase.

## LaGuardia: no direct bus, no Ithaca flight

There is no bus from Ithaca to LaGuardia and no flight from ITH. A Tradewind route [appears to have last been scheduled in April 2026](https://www.airportoverview.com/routes/LGA/ITH) and is not on the airport's airlines page. So ride to Manhattan, then take one of the three routes in the [MTA's LaGuardia guide](https://www.mta.info/guides/airports/laguardia):

| Route to LaGuardia | Fare for most riders | Terminals served |
|---|---|---|
| M60-SBS bus from Broadway at 106th, 116th or 120th St, or along 125th St | $3 | Every terminal |
| Subway to Jackson Heights-Roosevelt Av or 74 St-Broadway, then the free LaGuardia Link Q70 bus | $3 | B and C |
| LIRR to Woodside, then the free Q70 bus | $5.25 off-peak or $7.25 peak | B and C |

For Terminal A, take the airport shuttle from Terminal B. Not every LIRR train stops at Woodside, and the MTA says to allow an hour or more, depending on your route and traffic.

The other way in is Syracuse. Delta flies SYR to LaGuardia and JFK, and JetBlue flies SYR to JFK, per the [SYR destinations page](https://syrairport.org/destinations/). OurBus reaches SYR Terminal A from campus in 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min.

## Flying from Ithaca to JFK

Delta runs a [daily nonstop from ITH to JFK](https://flyithaca.com/airlines), and United flies to Washington Dulles. That is the whole scheduled list. The ITH to Newark flight [ended on March 29, 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_Tompkins_International_Airport), so any older advice to fly ITH to Newark is out of date.

The upside is no Manhattan transfer with bags. The downside is depth. On Saturday Nov 7, 2026, [four aircraft arrived at ITH](https://www.avionio.com/widget/en/ITH/arrivals), two of them from JFK. Delta 5265 from ITH to JFK [was cancelled on July 6, 7 and 10, 2026](https://www.avionio.com/en/flight/dl-5265) among mostly completed flights.

Getting to ITH:

- The terminal is [open 3:30 a.m. to midnight](https://flyithaca.com).
- TCAT [Route 32 runs weekdays and Route 72 weekends](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation) between the airport and Ithaca Commons, $1.50.
- For early flights, prebook a [taxi](https://flyithaca.com/taxis): Ithaca Airport Cab 607-252-9052, Collegetown Cab 607-588-8888, Ithaca Dispatch 607-277-7777.
- Long-term [parking](https://www.flyithaca.com/parking) is $8 a day or $40 a week.

## How much slack to leave

The 2019 Cornell Sun story on [international students' trips](https://cornellsun.com/2019/05/06/international-students-travel-cornell/) is the clearest record of what goes wrong:

- One student landed at Newark around 10 p.m. from Tianjin with about 15 hours still to go, seven of them on an airport bench.
- Another landed at JFK at 2 p.m. and sprinted to make a 4:30 p.m. bus.
- One missed a campus bus by five minutes.
- One counted a 9-hour flight plus a 5-hour bus as "technically 14 hours" once airport and transit time were added.

Ludovica Stilli '22 and Jaewon Sim '21 advised a big buffer between landing and the bus, and double-checking schedules. Pia Bocanegra '19 advised spare clothes and toiletries in the carry-on.

Weather adds risk. In January 2019 Coach USA cancelled all NYC departures after noon on a Saturday, and Singaporean students stuck in NYC found beds through student associations at NYU and Columbia ([Sun, Jan 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/)).

Our advice: for an international departure, book the bus one run earlier than the one that looks right. OurBus's own NYC trip time spans almost three hours, from 4 h 10 min to 7 h.

## When to book

OurBus names three peak days: [the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, the last day of December finals and the Friday spring break begins](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269). Book those early. Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Nov 26. OurBus's downtown Ithaca stop moves to [235 S. Cayuga St from Nov 9, 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/); campus stops are unchanged.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying a Newark Penn ticket for a Newark Airport flight.** You still need NJ Transit to the airport, $11.95, plus 15 to 20 minutes on the AirTrain.
- **Looking for a flight from Ithaca to Newark.** That route ended in March 2025. Delta to JFK is the only NYC flight from ITH.
- **Searching for a direct bus to JFK or LaGuardia.** None exists. On top of the Manhattan bus, budget $11.75 to $16 and 30 to 60 minutes for JFK, or $3 to $7.25 and an hour or more for LaGuardia.
- **Expecting OurBus at your terminal.** It stops at the AirTrain P3 station; ride the AirTrain from there.
- **A tight landing-to-bus gap.** A missed C2C keeps the full $93.50 or $101 fare; a missed OurBus leaves you with credit and a rescheduling fee.
- **Two big suitcases on OurBus.** Only one is included and the second is $30 prepaid. At peak move-in, the [Sun reported](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes) that guaranteeing two large bags effectively meant buying two seats.

## FAQ

**Does the C2C bus go to any airport?** No. Its New York stops are the [Cornell Club and Weill Cornell](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule-stop-locations), both in Manhattan. From there, take the subway and AirTrain to JFK, the subway and free Q70 bus to LaGuardia, or NJ Transit from New York Penn to Newark.

**How do I get from New York Penn Station to Newark Airport?** NJ Transit plus the AirTrain is [$16.80 for most riders](https://www.mta.info/guides/airports/newark-ewr), about an hour from New York Penn. The MTA calls it the quickest route from Midtown.

**Is there an OurBus discount for students?** OurBus Select gives [$15 off each of the next 10 tickets](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269). Separately, OurBus's [Student Only Hot Deal](https://www.ourbus.com/blog/ourbus-student-discount/) takes 10% off the first ride booked with a .edu email address, on bookings of $25 or more, up to $15.

**Where do I board OurBus downtown now?** At 235 S. Cayuga St from Nov 9, 2026. Greyhound and FlixBus still list 131 E Green St.
