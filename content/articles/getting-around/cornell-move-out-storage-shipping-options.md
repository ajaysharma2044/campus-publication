```glance
- Big Red Shipping and Storage (BRSS): $39 a small box, $49 a large box, $49 to $150 per loose item, for a 3 to 4 month term with pickup and delivery included.
- Pickup from your room is free, but you must be there: Housing staff will not let movers in.
- Book by early April. BRSS slots are limited, and the market advice is the same.
- Shipping goes by FedEx at rates set at pickup; no shipping price is posted.
- Clear out before the building closes (2 p.m. Sunday, May 17, in 2026). Unattended items left after that are donated.
- Load at your area's dock or lot: Mews, Dickson and Donlon on North; West Ave and Stewart Ave on West; the Williams Street Garage for Cascadilla and Sheldon.
```

The simplest move-out storage for Cornell residents is Big Red Shipping and Storage, the [vendor Cornell contracts with](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), which picks up from your room free and charges [$39 for a small box and $49 for a large one](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) for a 3 to 4 month term, delivery included. Book in early April, be in the room for your pickup slot, and use the same company's FedEx shipping if you are not coming back. Anything left with no resident present after the building closes (2 p.m. Sunday, May 17, in 2026) is donated.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
This is your first move-out, and North Campus is where most of you start. Cornell itself [provides no storage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), so the choice is BRSS, another company, or taking it home. A typical room on BRSS (six large boxes and a mini fridge) comes to $369 by our arithmetic from its [price list](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing). Book in early April, for a day you will still be in Ithaca. Load the car at the [Mews, Dickson or Donlon docks or the Robert Purcell lot](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/important-loading-and-parking-information). The costly mistake: booking a pickup after you have already left. You must be present, and Housing will not open the door.
```

```reader "If you're flying home"
A year of stuff will not fit in your bag allowance, so you store or ship. BRSS ships by FedEx at [rates calculated at pickup](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing), so ask for a quote before your slot. Storing a large suitcase in Ithaca costs $75 for the summer, and it is waiting in August. A Nebraska student who travels about 15 hours home [shipped extras](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes), the Sun reported in 2024. If you take the bus to an airport, check bag rules first: OurBus guarantees [one suitcase up to 50 lb and charges $30 per extra bag](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university). Not confirmed: airline bag fees.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Store in Ithaca rather than ship abroad. BRSS warns that [international shipments and customs paperwork carry extra fees](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) and posts no amounts. A 3 to 4 month storage term covers the summer. Book the pickup for a day before your flight: you [must be present](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), and nobody can let movers in for you. Keep your passport and documents with you, not in a box. Items left in the room after the 2 p.m. closing are donated, so a missed pickup on your last morning can mean losing everything you meant to store.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
You still leave term housing by the closing Sunday (2 p.m. May 17 in 2026), even if you stay in town. BRSS's [3 to 4 month term](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) fits May to August. If you have space in your summer place, a small self-storage unit is the other route: a 5x5 runs [$64 to $110 a month](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny), about $192 to $330 for three months by our math, and you haul it yourself. Book either one by early April. The costly mistake is assuming summer plans let you stay in your room past the closing: items left behind are donated.
```

```reader "If you're a parent helping with move-out"
Park at the loading spot for your student's area. Cornell lists [loading spots by area](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/important-loading-and-parking-information): North Campus docks at Mews, Dickson and Donlon, the Robert Purcell lot and the Helen Newman lot (ParkMobile); West Ave and Stewart Ave on West Campus; the Williams Street Garage for Cascadilla and Sheldon Court. Arrive well before 2 p.m. on closing Sunday, when unattended items left behind are donated. If your student stores with BRSS, they must be there for the pickup. Housing's line is [607-255-5368](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus).
```

```reader "If you're an upper-year without a car"
You may be moving from a dorm into a Collegetown lease, and a year of boxes is hard to haul on TCAT. BRSS includes [pickup and delivery](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) in its storage price, so nothing needs a car. For a self-move, Ithaca Carshare's Just In Case plan is [$30 a year plus $9 an hour](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters), plus mileage and tax. A pickup-truck rental runs [$30 to $60 a day](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing) on a company blog. The mistake that costs most is waiting until finals to book: slots are limited, and an off-date move with Storage Scholars can carry a surcharge of up to $300.
```

## What Big Red Shipping and Storage charges

BRSS is run by Student Agencies, and Cornell calls it [the most affordable option in Ithaca](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options). That is Cornell's claim; Cornell's page posts no rates. It does describe free in-room pickup and delivery and a climate-controlled facility. BRSS's own [pricing page](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) does, and says all prices include pickup, storage and delivery for a 3 to 4 month term.

A giant box (4 to 6 cubic feet) is priced by its dimensions. Extra fees can apply for overweight, oversized or fragile items, late reschedules, missed appointments and boxes that are not BRSS boxes; the page gives no amounts. Supplies can be [picked up at the 409 College Ave front desk](http://www.bigredboxes.com/) or delivered for a fee. Orders turn "confirmed" 48 hours before the appointment day.

Example: a first-year on North Campus packs six large boxes and stores a mini fridge. At BRSS's prices that is 6 × $49 + $75 = $369 for the summer. Four small boxes alone would be $156. The figure below sets our BRSS totals against the market ranges above.

## Shipping instead of storing

BRSS also ships. Shipping is [calculated at pickup on current FedEx rates](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing), with added fees for international shipments and customs paperwork. The Sun reported in 2024 that carriers had [raised their rates](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes), and that Robert Purcell handled nearly 28,000 packages in two weeks in 2022.

Ship when you are not returning to Ithaca, or when carrying costs more than sending. The free bus allowance is small:

| Bus | Free luggage | Extra bags |
|---|---|---|
| [C2C](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) | 2 suitcases below deck plus 2 small carry-ons | None listed |
| [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university) | 1 suitcase up to 50 lb plus a handbag | $30 each, prepaid at booking |
| [Greyhound](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny) | 1 carry-on and 1 checked bag | Flexible fare adds a second checked bag |

At peak move-out, the Sun reported that two large bags on OurBus are [guaranteed only by buying two seats](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes). C2C's own package service is [$20 a package for Cornell department or Weill accounts only](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-additional-information), so it is not a student option.

Example: a student going home to New York City on OurBus has a third suitcase. Paying the $30 extra-bag fee gets it there the same day. Storing it with BRSS as a medium item costs $75 and keeps it in Ithaca for fall. If the bag is coming back in August anyway, storage saves two bus trips with it.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking a pickup after you leave.** You must be present, and Housing will not let movers in. A missed appointment can draw a [BRSS fee](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing), and whatever is still in the room after 2 p.m. is donated.
- **Waiting until finals to book.** BRSS slots are limited. Storage Scholars charges a surcharge of [up to $300](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing) for moves outside finals or move-in dates.
- **Packing in your own boxes.** Non-BRSS boxes are on the fee list, with no amount posted.
- **Assuming Cornell stores things for you.** It does not. The free storage of 2020 was a [pandemic-era program](https://cornellsun.com/2020/03/13/local-storage-services-left-boxed-in-after-new-cornell-paid-solution/), not a standing one.
- **Carrying three bags onto OurBus.** Only one large bag is guaranteed; each extra is $30 prepaid, and at peak a second large bag may need a second seat.
- **Shipping abroad without a quote.** International shipments add customs fees that BRSS does not publish.

## FAQ

**Do I need storage over winter break?** Not if you return to the same room. The [2026 winter closing checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice) says residences close Sunday, Dec 20, 2026 at 2 p.m. and asks you to take valuables, passports and medications and unplug appliances except fridges.

**Can a friend meet the movers for me?** Cornell's page says the student must be present, and Housing staff cannot give access. Ask BRSS at 607-524-3130 before you plan around anyone else.

**Can I store a mattress or furniture?** Yes with BRSS, at the giant-item price of [$150](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) for a dresser, mattress, futon, desk or couch. A self-storage unit is the other choice for several big pieces.

**Is BRSS really the cheapest?** Cornell says so, but no competitor posts Ithaca prices to check it against. BRSS's box prices land inside the market ranges on the Storage Scholars blog.

**What happens to things I leave behind?** For spring 2026, items left with no resident present after the 2 p.m. closing on May 17 [were donated](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options).
