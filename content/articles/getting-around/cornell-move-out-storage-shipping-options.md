```glance
- Cornell stores nothing over the summer; its contracted vendor charges $49 a large box, pickup and delivery included.
- You must be in your room for any pickup; items left for a crew are donated after closing.
- Six boxes cost $270 to $294 at Big Red, Storage Squad or Uncle Marty's; a shared unit can cost less.
- Book by early April 2027; spring finals run May 15 to 22, 2027.
```

At 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026, Cornell closed its undergraduate residence halls for the summer. Under Cornell's rules, any boxes a student left in a room for a storage company, without being there to hand them over, would be donated to Dump & Run ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out)).

That rule turns a pile of boxes into a deadline. Every spring, Cornell students have to decide where a year of clothes, books, lamps and mini fridges will spend about three months. The university will not hold any of it. Its housing license says storage space for students' belongings "is not available" ([Cornell housing license, 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027)).

So summer storage at Cornell is a private market, and it splits two ways. You can pay a company to carry your boxes out of your room in May and back in August. Big Red Shipping and Storage, Storage Squad, Storage Scholars and Uncle Marty's all do some version of that. Or you can rent a locked unit of your own, at Bolt Storage or another self-storage site, and haul everything yourself. Shipping it home and carrying it on the bus are the other two roads.

Six boxes cost $270 to $294 for the summer at Big Red, Storage Squad or Uncle Marty's, before any extras. A Storage Scholars order can run more than three times that. Four roommates who share a drive-up unit can each pay about $110 (Posted rates, compared below).

## Why Cornell stopped keeping your boxes

Cornell used to store a little for free. Until 2003, a student could leave up to two small boxes in the dorm they would live in the next fall. That spring the university ended the program and handed summer storage to Big Red Shipping and Storage after a bidding process. Patrick Savolskis, then manager of the housing and dining office, told The Cornell Daily Sun that the old service "was quite limited." He said Cornell could not store anything for students moving off campus ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2003](https://cornellsun.com/2003/05/02/students-seek-storage-options)).

Big Red was not a stranger. It is a division of Student Agencies Inc., which was founded in 1894 and calls itself the oldest independent student-run company in the nation. Student Agencies also runs Hired Hands Moving Company and SAI Real Estate, and it employs more than 300 students ([Student Agencies](https://www.studentagencies.com/)). Big Red's own footer dates the storage business to 1986 ([Big Red Shipping & Storage](http://www.bigredboxes.com/)).

Students grumbled about the price. In 2003, a small box dropped at a service center cost $15 for the summer. One student, Dave Wang, told the Sun he would "find any means necessary to avoid using Big Red Storage" (Cornell Daily Sun, 2003).

Competitors followed, and several started in Collegetown. In 2011, two track co-captains in the ILR School, Nick Huber and Dan Hagberg, began picking up classmates' belongings during finals week of their junior year. They ran the business from a Collegetown basement and called it Storage Squad. Its first profit paid their rent ([Cornell Chronicle](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2021/04/ilr-alumni-sell-storage-business-seven-figures)).

Other students tried a sharing model. Ezra Box, started by members of Cornell's International Students Union, matched students who needed space with students who had room in Ithaca for the summer. In its first summer, 2016, it stored 170 boxes for 43 students. A co-founder, Yash Malhotra, said its rates were less than half of Cornell's option ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2017](https://cornellsun.com/2017/05/07/student-run-storage-startup-moves-into-its-second-year/)).

The pandemic showed how big the job is. In March 2020, less than two hours after President Martha E. Pollack suspended classes, two senior Cornell administrators promised free storage in Ithaca for students living on campus. The plan covered five boxes per student, a load worth $185 at Storage Squad's prices, its owner told the Sun ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2020](https://cornellsun.com/2020/03/13/local-storage-services-left-boxed-in-after-new-cornell-paid-solution/)). That spring Storage Squad hired 30 people to move about 40,000 boxes for more than 2,000 students (Cornell Chronicle). The free program was an emergency measure.

Then the founders changed sides of the market. In January 2021 they sold Storage Squad for a seven-figure sum (Cornell Chronicle). The buyer was 1-800-PACK-RAT, a portable storage company in Wake Forest, N.C. ([SpareFoot Blog](https://www.sparefoot.com/blog/bolt-storage-builds-tech-driven-storage-portfolio)). Huber and Hagberg kept their other business, Bolt Storage. Its first site was a 51,000-square-foot building in Ithaca, and the company now runs more than 65 locations in at least 11 states ([Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/about)). Storage Squad still serves Cornell under its new owner. So two of the names on a Cornell student's list, Storage Squad and Bolt, trace back to the same Collegetown basement.

The newest arrival is national. Storage Scholars was founded in 2017 by Sam Chason, then a first-year at Wake Forest University. Mark Cuban took a 10 percent stake on "Shark Tank" in October 2022, and the company says it now serves more than 200 campuses ([Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny)).

## Big Red Shipping and Storage, the default

Cornell's housing office sends students to Big Red first. It calls the company its contracted vendor and says "their prices are the most affordable in Ithaca" ([Cornell Storage & Shipping Options](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)). Every price covers pickup, storage and delivery for one term of three to four months. Extra fees can apply for overweight, oversized or fragile items, late reschedules, missed appointments and boxes that are not Big Red's (BRSS pricing). Big Red's terms put amounts on most of them. Any appointment between May 8 and 28 carries a $25 peak fee, and the busiest move-out and move-in days add $20 more. A box over 50 pounds costs $35 extra for the term, and one over 70 pounds or 6 cubic feet costs $50. Shrink-wrapping a fragile or large item costs $10, and an item that takes more than two people to carry costs $15. Rescheduling within 48 hours costs $25. Cancelling within 48 hours, or missing an appointment after a 15-minute grace period, costs $65. Each box or item is insured for up to $100 against damage from the company's negligence, and extra coverage costs $10 per $100, up to $500 more ([BRSS terms](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_TermsAndConditions)).

Booking runs online. You create an account at bigredboxes.com, pick a time slot and buy boxes and tape. Supplies can be picked up at the 409 College Ave. front desk or delivered for a fee. Every order's status flips to "confirmed" 48 hours before the appointment day, which means you are on the truck list (Big Red Shipping & Storage). Cornell's page adds that Big Red can pack for you, picks up free from a dorm, apartment or house, and stores items in a climate-controlled building. On "Free Box Days," customers with an appointment can pick up free boxes. The phone number is 607-524-3130 (Cornell Storage & Shipping Options).

The flat price is the catch and the appeal. A box costs the same whether you need it held for two weeks or the full three to four months. That suits a student who leaves after finals and comes back for fall move-in. It costs a student with a short gap far more (see the off-campus gap below).

## The other pickup services

**Storage Squad** prices by item for a four-month summer term. A small item, such as a lamp or a mirror, is $36. A standard item, which includes its 24-by-17-by-15-inch Mega Box, a cube fridge or a carry-on, is $45. Large items are $65, extra-large items such as luggage or a bicycle are $75, and a futon, couch or mattress adds $20 to the extra-large price. Boxes, tape and labels are free, and its price calculator counts pickup and delivery as included with storage. The company says most students store four to six boxes plus one or two bigger things and spend about $400. It also sells a "Room Return" plan: up to 25 items picked up and returned for $750. A reservation fee holds your spot ([Storage Squad, Cornell](https://storagesquad.com/summer-storage/cornell-university/)). It is refundable until two weeks before your pickup date or until your free packing kit ships, whichever comes first. The company's terms add $75 to cancel with less than a week's notice or to reschedule with less than 72 hours' notice, $75 for a missed delivery appointment, $25 for a box over 50 pounds, $50 for a fridge that is not fully defrosted, $100 on a Room Return reservation and at least $250 for a pickup or delivery outside its posted dates. Each item is covered for up to $100 against loss or damage from the company's negligence ([Storage Squad terms](https://storagesquad.com/terms-and-conditions/)).

**Storage Scholars** sends a free packing kit and photographs and logs every item in an online portal. It charges $14 to $40 per item per month by size, with a four-month minimum for summer. It also charges up to $300 extra for a pickup outside finals week or a delivery outside move-in week ([Storage Scholars blog](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing)). The same post puts a typical summer order at about $915. At partner schools, Storage Scholars can enter halls with a key so students need not meet the crew (Storage Scholars). Cornell is not on that footing. Its rule, below, says you must be there.

**Uncle Marty's** is the local shop in the group. Marty Johnson and Clark Young own it. It opened in Collegetown in 2011 and added a downtown store in 2025 ([Tompkins Chamber](https://business.tompkinschamber.org/list/member/uncle-marty-s-printing-shipping-storage-more-12929)). It prices storage by the month, not by the summer.

Bundle orders over $150 qualify for local curbside delivery, and ordering before May 1 guarantees a delivery slot ([Uncle Marty's bundles](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/move-out-bundles)).

## Renting a unit yourself

Self-storage is a room you rent and fill. It holds far more than six boxes, and nobody comes to your door. You need a car, a friend with one, or a rental. Ithaca Carshare's Just In Case plan for students costs $30 a year, then $9 an hour plus 52 cents a mile for a truck, before sales and rental taxes ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)).

Bolt Storage, the founders' company, runs three sites near Cornell. Its main one, at 1401 Dryden Road on Route 366, is about six minutes from campus, by Bolt's count. It has drive-up units and indoor climate-controlled units, gated 24-hour access and month-to-month rentals. Bolt says students from Cornell, Ithaca College and Tompkins Cortland Community College use it for summers, semesters abroad and gaps between leases, and that you can rent in May and leave in August ([Bolt Storage, Dryden Road](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny)). Its Pinckney Road site, a few minutes away, has drive-up units only (Bolt Storage, Pinckney Road).

Climate control means the unit stays at a steady temperature all year. Bolt says that protects paper, electronics, instruments and wood furniture from Ithaca's humid summers (Bolt Storage, Dryden Road). If you are storing a laptop, a guitar or a wooden desk, it is worth the extra $12 a month for a 5x5.

Two other sites sit closer to the city. The fairest test is the same load at every company. Take six boxes of two to four cubic feet, the size of Big Red's large box. Storage Squad's Mega Box, at 24 by 17 by 15 inches, is about 3.5 cubic feet. Hold them for one summer, about three months (BRSS pricing; Storage Squad, Cornell).

Big Red, Storage Squad and Uncle Marty's land within $24 of each other, less than one extra item at any of them. So the choice among the three turns on service, not price. Big Red includes the trip from your room both ways, and Storage Squad's price includes pickup and delivery at your dorm. Uncle Marty's is cheapest when you carry boxes to 206A Dryden Road yourself (Storage Squad, Cornell; Uncle Marty's storage).

Add a mini fridge and the order changes. Big Red counts a mini fridge as a medium item at $75, which brings its total to $369 (BRSS pricing). Roommates change the math the most. A 10x10 drive-up unit at Bolt's Dryden Road site costs $441 for three months at $147. Cornell's claim that Big Red is the most affordable in Ithaca holds only in part. Big Red beats Uncle Marty's by $26 once Uncle Marty's adds curbside pickup and delivery. It loses to Storage Squad by $24, and it loses to a shared unit. For a fridge, it costs more than Storage Squad (BRSS pricing; Storage Squad, Cornell; Uncle Marty's storage).

## Shipping it, or carrying it

Shipping makes sense when you are not coming back, or when the bus fee for an extra bag beats three months of storage. Big Red ships through FedEx at rates set at pickup, with extra fees for international shipments and customs paperwork (BRSS pricing). Storage Squad ships boxes home through UPS if you book shipping when you sign up for storage (Storage Squad, Cornell). Uncle Marty's is an authorized FedEx and DHL shipping center and a USPS approved provider (Tompkins Chamber).

Carrying it costs less but holds less.

| Bus | Free luggage | Extra bags |
|---|---|---|
| [C2C](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations) | Two suitcases below deck and two small carry-ons | An item that takes a seat counts as a passenger and needs a paid reservation |
| [OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university) | One suitcase up to 50 pounds and one handbag on your lap | $30 per extra bag, paid in advance |

Sources: C2C Policies and Regulations; OurBus.

Busy travel periods squeeze those limits. The Sun reported in 2024 that at peak times, OurBus guarantees space for two large bags only if you buy two seats ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2024](https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes)). The same story followed Carver Hauptman, a student from Alliance, Neb., whose trip to Ithaca takes about 15 hours across three flights and a bus. He checks one bag and has shipped other belongings to campus.

A student riding OurBus to New York with a third suitcase pays $30 to bring it along. Storing that suitcase with Big Red as a medium item costs $75 and leaves it in Ithaca for fall. If the bag will come back in August anyway, storage saves dragging it on two bus trips. For the full set of routes, see [getting to Ithaca and around Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/), and for airports and bag rules, see [flying home from Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/flying-home-from-cornell-airports-rides/).

```reader "If you're an international student"
Store in Ithaca rather than ship abroad unless you are leaving for good. Big Red's price list warns that international shipments and customs paperwork can carry extra fees, so get a quote before your pickup day. Bolt rents units online and takes payment from anywhere, which helps if a bill comes due while you are home. Book your pickup for a day before your flight, since you must be there for it. Keep your passport and medications in your carry-on, never in a stored box.
```

## Move-out day

Be in your room for every pickup, or lose what you left. In 2026, Cornell also asked every resident to pick a move-out time slot in the Housing and Dining Portal (Cornell Housing).

If you book Big Red or any other company, you must be in your room for the appointment. No housing or university staff member can let a crew into your building or room. If you miss the slot, you reschedule. If you are not there, everything left for the company is donated after the building closes (Cornell Storage & Shipping Options). The housing license adds a broader rule: anything not removed within 24 hours of check-out counts as abandoned, and Cornell can dispose of it and bill you for the removal (Cornell housing license, 2026-27).

Cornell's checklist asks residents to turn in all keys, defrost, unplug and prop open their MicroFridges, and drop usable items at the Project ReCollect collection area in the lobby (Cornell Housing).

Families driving in need the loading zones.

|---|---|---|
| North Campus | Mews, Dickson and Donlon loading docks; Helen Newman lot; Robert Purcell and Appel spaces or ParkMobile; Program House Drive; north side of Jessup Road; Risley, Latino Living Center, Ecology House and Akwe:kon lots | A Lot |
| South Campus | Cascadilla and Sheldon Court: City of Ithaca garage or Williams Street lot; Schuyler House and 112 Edgemoor: building lots | Williams Street lot |
| West Campus | West Ave. across from Baker Tower and Founders and Lyon; Stewart Ave. metered spaces; Mary Ann Wood Drive near Bethe and Keeton | University Avenue lot |

Source: Cornell Housing, Moving Out, May 2026. At EEC, parking is only in designated spaces on West Ave. or Forest Park Ave., and 109 McGraw and Veterans House use their building lots. Gothics Way and Becker Drive above the West Campus loading dock were closed.

August is the same trip in reverse. The guide to [move-in day parking and unloading](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) covers where to pull up when the boxes come back.

```reader "If you're graduating"
You do not need summer storage, but you do need a plan for leaving. Cornell extends graduating seniors' housing through the day after Commencement at no charge and notifies them by email in the spring (Cornell housing license, 2026-27). For 2027, Commencement weekend runs May 28 to 30. Ship what you are keeping, or take it home, rather than paying for a term you will not return from.
```

## The off-campus gap

For Collegetown renters, the lease sets the deadline, not a hall closing. A short gap between leases calls for monthly pricing; a full summer calls for a flat term.

Most Ithaca leases expire on July 31, the Sun reported in 2024 ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing)). Some end earlier: every lease at Dryden South, 205 Dryden Road, expires May 31, 2027 ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)). And new leases do not always start the next day. Leases at The Ivy, the new building at Dryden Road and College Avenue, began on Aug. 17, 2026 ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)).

That last case shows why the pricing model matters. A tenant moving from a lease that ended on that common date into The Ivy had 17 nights to cover. At Uncle Marty's, six boxes for the one-month minimum cost $90. A month in a 5x5 drive-up unit at Bolt costs $84. Big Red's flat summer price for the same six boxes is more than three times as much. For a short gap, a monthly price saves about $200 (Uncle Marty's storage; Bolt Storage, Dryden Road; BRSS pricing).

A student moving from a dorm in May into an apartment that starts Aug. 1 needs about ten weeks. That is close to a full summer, so the flat-term services make sense again. Big Red picks up from apartments and houses, not only dorms (Cornell Storage & Shipping Options). Uncle Marty's inside pickup works only from a ground-floor unit or a building with an elevator, so tenants in a walk-up need its curbside option (Uncle Marty's storage).

Leaving things behind in an apartment costs money too. Cornell's off-campus housing page warns that tenants can be charged for removing abandoned items from the apartment or building ([Cornell Moving In and Out](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/moving-and-out)). New York lets a landlord deduct the cost of moving and storing a tenant's belongings after the lease ends from the deposit ([Cornell Law tenant guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits)). The rest of the move-out, from the inspection to the 14-day deposit clock, is in [moving out of an Ithaca apartment](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/).

The other way to close the gap is not to move at all. Some tenants keep the apartment to the end of the lease and sublet it while they are away, which leaves their things in place. The guide to [subletting your apartment](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) covers landlord consent. If you need a room yourself for May to August, see [finding a summer sublet in Ithaca](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/).

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
Skip the pickup services, which are built for people who leave. Use a month-to-month unit you can reach any day, or move straight into a summer sublet. Bolt's gated sites are open around the clock. Uncle Marty's charges a fee for each visit to stored items, which adds up if you need things often. The [summer in Ithaca start page](/start/summer-in-ithaca/) collects the housing and transit guides for the months you stay.
```

## Winter break is a different problem

Winter break needs no storage company if you return to the same room. Cornell closes undergraduate residences at 2 p.m. on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, and reopens them at 9 a.m. on Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027. Staying past the closing costs $60 per hour, and late-departure requests are due in the Housing and Dining Portal by Monday, Dec. 7. Leave your things in your room, but take valuables, passports and medications, because you will have no access during the break ([Cornell Winter Break Closing Checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-break-closing-checklist)). If you are not going home, see [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).

## The trade-offs

Each model asks you to give something up. Big Red's flat price is simple and includes the trip from your room, but you pay the full term even for a short gap, and a pickup during finals week carries its peak fee. Storage Squad and Storage Scholars price by the item, so a careful packer pays less, but both add costs that sit in the fine print: Storage Squad's late-cancellation and late-reschedule fees and Storage Scholars' off-schedule surcharge.

Self-storage is the cheapest per box for a group, and it is the only option you can reach any day. But it needs a car twice, and you do all the lifting.

The biggest risk is timing, not price. Every pickup service needs you in the room at the scheduled time, during finals week. In 2020, when Cornell's emergency storage plan arrived, scores of students moved up or canceled their bookings, and one competitor stopped taking new orders (Cornell Daily Sun, 2020). Spring move-out is a smaller version of that rush every year, and slots fill. Cornell warns that Big Red has limited time slots (Cornell Storage & Shipping Options), and Storage Scholars tells Cornell students to book by early April (Storage Scholars).

## What comes next

The housing license says residents must leave by 2 p.m. on the last day of the term (Cornell housing license, 2026-27). The registrar lists the last day of spring term as Saturday, May 22, 2027, the final day of exams ([Cornell Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)). Housing sets the exact closing, so check the Moving Out page in April before you book a pickup slot. Posted prices can change before May 2027. Get a written quote for your exact load, compare it with the six-box math above, and book the slot that fits your last exam, not your first one.
