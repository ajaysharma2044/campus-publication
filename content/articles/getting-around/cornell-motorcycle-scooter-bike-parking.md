A motorcycle, scooter or moped at Cornell needs the M permit, which costs $120.01 a year ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Lock a bike to a designated rack.

```glance
- The M permit covers motorcycles, scooters and mopeds and prorates monthly.
- Register motorcycle and scooter plates in MyParking, even for short-term parking.
- Park bikes at designated racks. Locking to railings, trees or poles can lead to impound.
- Do not charge e-bike batteries in residence rooms, offices or academic buildings.
```

## What does motorcycle, scooter and moped parking cost?

The M permit costs $120.01 and is prorated monthly. It is nonrefundable ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)).

The permit is valid only in spaces signed for motorcycle parking. Cornell lists 29 M areas, including spots by Willard Straight Hall, Barton Hall, Malott Hall, Robert Purcell Community Center and Hasbrouck Apartments, and in the Appel Commons lot. If you already have a car permit, you can list the motorcycle's plate on it and park in that permit's spaces ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/motorcycle-motorscooter-mopeds)). Cornell's 2023-24 [traffic and parking rules](https://fcs.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/2023-12/Control%20of%20Traffic%20and%20Parking_complete-2023-24.pdf) bar motorcycles, motor scooters and mopeds from bike racks and campus parking garages.

Cornell treats motor scooters and mopeds as motor vehicles under all vehicle and traffic laws. Every moped must be registered and licensed with New York State ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/motorcycle-motorscooter-mopeds)). Students must also [register the plates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus) of cars, motorcycles and motor scooters in MyParking, including for short-term parking. An e-scooter is a separate category: Cornell defines it as under 100 pounds with a top speed of 20 mph ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus)). Our [permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) lists every permit and the dates.

```reader "If you ride a motorcycle"
Buy the M permit, not a car permit. Because it prorates by the month, a mid-year purchase costs less. Do not expect a refund if you sell the bike.
```

## Where can I park a bike at Cornell?

Park only at designated bike racks. Locking a bike to railings, signposts, trees or poles can lead to impound, and so can blocking a path or leaving a bike abandoned. Indoor storage in campus buildings needs written permission from the building coordinator ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus)). Lock the frame and a wheel.

Improper bike parking carries a fine of up to $50 if paid within 10 calendar days, and $60 after that. Cornell can also impound or boot the bike at the owner's expense. Staff may remove the lock to take it, and Cornell does not pay to repair or replace the lock. Impounded bikes are stored at Cornell Police, and getting one back takes proof of ownership and a release form ([Cornell traffic and parking rules, 2023-24](https://fcs.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/2023-12/Control%20of%20Traffic%20and%20Parking_complete-2023-24.pdf)).

Cornell's assistant director of transportation wrote in the Sun that campus has more than 1,000 bike parking spots, about 7 miles of bike lanes and 2 miles of shared paths ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall)).

```reader "If you live in a residence hall"
University housing requires bikes to be kept in bike rooms, not in resident rooms ([Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/bicycle-registration-storage)). Do not keep a bike in your room, and do not charge its battery there.
```

## Do I need to register a bike?

Yes, if you live in university housing. Housing requires every bike, e-bike and scooter to be registered through Cornell's Bike and Micromobility Registration Tool. You register first, then request bike room access ([Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/bicycle-registration-storage)). Registration is free, and Cornell urges everyone else to register bikes, e-bikes and scooters with Cornell Commuter and Parking Services and with the local municipality ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus)).

It matters for theft: campus police reported 51 bike and scooter thefts in fall 2024, worth at least $41,177 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far)). Cornell advises registering and using a U-lock. Registration also helps at the end of the year. Under the housing rules, bikes still in bike rooms or on bike racks after Commencement are removed and taken to the Lost and Found in Barton Hall. If a bike is registered, Cornell Police send its owner a registered letter at their permanent address ([Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/bicycle-registration-storage)). Our guide to [bike, e-bike and scooter rules and theft](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-bike-ebike-scooter-rules-theft/) covers the full rules.

## What are the e-bike and charging rules?

Cornell defines an e-bike as a motor of no more than 750 watts, no wider than 36 inches, with working pedals ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus)). Do not charge e-bike or e-scooter batteries in residential rooms, offices or academic buildings, according to a [Cornell guidance document](https://fcs.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/2025-02/Micro-Mobility%20E-Bikes_Litium-Ion%20Batteries%20Guidance%20Document%20v.2%20final%2002.09.2024.pdf).

## Where do Bikeshare bikes go?

Ithaca Bikeshare bikes do not have to be returned to a specific hub, but returning one to a Bikeshare hub waives the next fee to start a ride, as the [Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/24/ithaca-bikeshare-a-comprehensive-guide-to-ithacas-newest-transportation-option/) explained at launch in 2023. Current prices and rules are in our [Bikeshare guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-bikeshare-cost-cornell-students/).

## What about winter?

Some M permit areas are not available from Dec. 1 to April 1. Cornell says the list can change, so check the posted signs before you park ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/motorcycle-motorscooter-mopeds)). Campus snow removal covers roads, parking lots, steps and sidewalks. Report unsafe or slippery spots anywhere on campus to Customer Service at 607-255-5322 ([Cornell Facilities](https://fcs.cornell.edu/services/repair-maintenance/snow-removal)). Our [winter biking guide](/cornell/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/) covers riding in the snow.

## What about parking off campus?

Collegetown and downtown streets follow City of Ithaca rules. A vehicle parked on a city street must move at least once every 24 hours, Monday through Friday. One that has not moved in 72 hours can be treated as abandoned and towed at the owner's expense. The city's ticket for parking on a sidewalk is $25 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)). Our [Collegetown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/) covers the garages, permits and overnight rules.

## What to do next

Register your plate or bike, buy a lock, and find the rack nearest your building before the semester's first week.
