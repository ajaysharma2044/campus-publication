The City of Ithaca sells a $45 residential parking permit to people who live on blocks in its permit system. The City Clerk's Office issues it, and you bring your vehicle registration and proof of residency. Renewals can go by mail with the documents and the fee. Every permit expires on July 31 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)). You can apply online or mail the application with a check to the City Clerk's Office ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/711/Residential-Parking-Permits)).

```glance
- The permit costs $45 a year, with no partial-year price.
- Only residents can apply. A landlord cannot get one for a tenant.
- A permit is voluntary and does not guarantee a space.
- Permit hours fall between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. on weekdays, and the city's map shows which blocks are in.
```

## Who can get an Ithaca residential parking permit?

Residents only. You must live at the address, and the permit is tied to one license plate. A landlord cannot obtain one for tenants. The Clerk issues permits first come, first served.

Bring two things:

- A copy of your vehicle registration.
- Proof of residency: a deed, a lease, a license, or a utility or phone bill.

```reader "If you rent"
Your lease is your proof of residency, and the city accepts a lease copy only if both you and your landlord signed it ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/711/Residential-Parking-Permits)). Apply yourself, because the city will not issue the permit to your landlord. Before you sign, ask whether the street is in the system. Our [off-campus renter guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/) shows how parking fits into the commute.
```

## Which Ithaca streets need a residential permit?

The state law behind the system, [Vehicle and Traffic Law 1640-e](https://nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VAT/1640-E*2), lets Ithaca run it on city streets but excludes highways inside Cornell. It describes a perimeter that includes East State Street, Giles Street, Water Street, Seneca Way, East Seneca Street, Parker Street, Terrace Place, Linn Street, University Avenue, Willard Way and Stewart Avenue. The edge of the area is the city line with Cayuga Heights and the Town of Ithaca. No permit applies where the adjacent property is zoned commercial or retail.

The law leaves the permit days and hours to the city's own local law. The city sets them within 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday, excluding holidays, and street signs on each block show its restricted hours. Permit holders are exempt from the 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. and 1 p.m. to 5 p.m. no-parking limits in permit areas ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/732/Hours-System-Alternative-Appeal-Process)).

The system's history shows why Collegetown is a question mark. It began in 1998 at the same price it has today, and it was open only to residents of R-1 and R-2 zones. R-3 residents, including central Collegetown, were left out. A consultant then estimated that up to 450 Cornell-related vehicles parked daily in Belle Sherman and Bryant Park, Collegetown and Cornell Heights ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/node/278473)). To see whether a block is in the system today, and whether permits are left, search the address on the city's [permit map page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/711/Residential-Parking-Permits). Our [Collegetown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/) covers garages and other options if your block is not.

```reader "If you live outside the city line"
The permit is a City of Ithaca program, so it does not cover streets past the city line with the Town of Ithaca and Cayuga Heights. See [city versus town and village renting](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) for how they differ.
```

## What does the permit actually do?

It is voluntary. The [city says](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) residents of a permit block are not required to buy one, but city parking rules still apply to them. State law requires that short-term parking of at least 120 minutes be allowed and that at least 20% of spaces stay open to nonresidents ([Vehicle and Traffic Law 1640-e](https://nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VAT/1640-E*2)). So the permit is not a reserved space.

A permit also does not exempt you from other rules. Permit holders must follow every other city rule, including the odd/even overnight rule, 24-hour limits and loading zones ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/732/Hours-System-Alternative-Appeal-Process)). The odd/even rule runs from Nov. 1 to April 1; read our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/). Snow and trash rules apply too; the [renter rules page](/cornell/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/) lists them. A ticket in a residential permit zone costs $15, rising for repeats ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations)).

## When does it expire, and can I get a partial year?

Every permit expires on July 31 and there are no partial-year permits. A permit bought in spring covers only a few months at the full price. If you arrive in the spring, ask whether the odd/even code at [eCode360](https://ecode360.com/8395630) matters to your block before paying.

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Asking your landlord to apply.** The Clerk will not issue it to a landlord.
- **Putting it on the windshield.** It belongs on the rear driver-side window.
- **Assuming a permit holds a space.** It does not.
- **Buying a new car without a new permit.** The permit is tied to one plate.

## FAQ

**Does a Cornell parking permit work on city streets?** No. Cornell permits cover campus lots. City streets follow city rules.

**Who do I contact?** The City Clerk, 108 E. Green St., Ithaca, NY 14850.

## What to do next

Look up your address on the city's permit map, then apply with your registration and a lease signed by you and your landlord. Apply before the semester starts so you are covered from the first week.
