```glance
- Bear Traditional allows 14 meals a week, Bear Choice 10 and Bear Basic 7.
- At full use a swipe costs $12.51, $14.44 or $15.98, in that order.
- Bear Choice and Bear Basic carry $500 in Big Red Bucks; Bear Traditional carries $400.
- On campus, only transfers and juniors can pick Traditional; Choice and Basic need pre-2021 enrollment.
```

Pick Bear Traditional if you eat 11 or more dining-room meals a week, Bear Choice if you eat 9 or 10, and Bear Basic if you eat 7. Their 2026-27 prices run from $2,489 to $3,528 per semester ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). The cheapest swipe is Traditional, if you use it fully.

## How do the three plans compare?

All three work in Cornell's ten residential dining rooms. A swipe is one entry to a dining room, and Big Red Bucks (BRBs) are dollars on your ID for campus food, worth $1 each and tax-free ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)).

| | Bear Traditional | Bear Choice | Bear Basic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per semester | $3,528 | $3,075 | $2,489 |
| Price per year | $7,056 | $6,150 | $4,978 |
| Meals per week, up to | 14 | 10 | 7 |
| BRBs per semester | $400 | $500 | $500 |
| Guest meals per semester | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Annual fee | $50 | $50 | $50 |
| Who can take it on campus | Upper-level and transfer students | Students enrolled before Summer 2021 | Same as Choice |
| Off campus | See below | All students | All students |

Source: Cornell Dining undergraduate meal plans, 2026-27. The fee is non-refundable and charged once per academic year.

Moving down one step saves real money. Choice costs $906 less a year than Traditional ($7,056 minus $6,150), and Basic costs $1,172 less than Choice ($6,150 minus $4,978). You give up 4 weekly meals for the first step and 3 for the second ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## What does a swipe cost on each plan?

The honest price of a swipe is the plan price minus the BRBs you would spend anyway, plus half the annual fee each semester, divided by the swipes you use. The table assumes 18 weeks a semester, close to the fall term of Aug. 17 to Dec. 20, 2026 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

| Meals eaten per week | Bear Traditional | Bear Choice | Bear Basic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | $35.03 | $28.89 | $22.38 |
| 7 | $25.02 | $20.63 | $15.98 |
| 10 | $17.52 | $14.44 | Over the cap |
| 12 | $14.60 | Over the cap | Over the cap |
| 14 | $12.51 | Over the cap | Over the cap |

The net semester prices behind the table are $3,153 for Traditional ($3,528 - $400 + $25), $2,600 for Choice and $2,014 for Basic ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

The cost per swipe climbs fast when you skip meals. Weekly swipes reset on Thursdays and do not carry over ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). A student on Choice who eats 7 meals a week pays $20.63 a swipe, not $14.44.

## How many meals a week make each plan worth it?

Compare against the cost of buying the same meals without a plan. Cornell's walk-in lunch is $16.80 Monday to Saturday with tax ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). Against that price, you break even at 10.43 meals a week on Traditional, 8.60 on Choice and 6.66 on Basic. Dinner at the door costs more, so these are cautious numbers.

Basic is the easiest to fill, since 6.66 is almost its 7-meal cap. Traditional asks the most, because you must eat 10 of 14 meals every week.

## Who is allowed to buy which plan?

Rules decide more than math. First-years must take Unlimited and cannot reduce or cancel, and West Campus house residents must take the House Meal Plan ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms)). The two-year residential requirement carries a meal plan, so the Bear plans open up after that. If you hope to live off campus sooner, read the [residential requirement exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## How do I change plans?

You can upgrade at any time with a Cornell Dining upgrade request or the Housing and Dining Portal ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). Reductions have deadlines, and changes start on a Thursday. Only eligible students can reduce. Plans last a full academic year, and there are no one-semester plans.

BRBs run out on a date, and Cornell's pages disagree. The undergraduate page says May 27, 2027, and the deadlines page says May 31, 2027. Spend them by the earlier date. The plans also do not cover winter intersession, and Cornell Dining closes from Dec. 25, 2026, to Jan. 1, 2027 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining)).

## What to do next

Count the dining-room meals you ate last week and match the number to the table. If you are between two plans, take the smaller one, since you can upgrade later but cannot reduce after the deadline. For the question of whether to buy a plan at all, read [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).
