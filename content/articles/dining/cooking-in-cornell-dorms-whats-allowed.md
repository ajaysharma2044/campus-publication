Start with your building. Cornell lists shared kitchens in Cascadilla Hall, Sheldon Court and Schuyler House, and kitchenettes with a fridge and microwave at 301 College Ave. ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue)). Your housing license, not this guide, sets the rules for appliances in your room.

## Which Cornell residences have kitchens?

| Residence | Cooking setup |
|---|---|
| Cascadilla Hall | A kitchen, plus a piano room and laundry |
| Sheldon Court | Kitchens, with a TV lounge and laundry |
| Schuyler House | A kitchen on each floor |
| 301 College Ave. | Kitchenettes with a fridge and microwave in studios to six-bedroom units |
| Townhouse Apartments | Full-size refrigerators; kitchen renewals began with units G and H |

Source: Cornell Housing pages for [Cascadilla Hall](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/cascadilla-hall), [Sheldon Court](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/sheldon-court), [Schuyler House](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house) and [301 College Avenue](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue), and the [Townhouse renovation notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/residential-improvement-project-underway).

The Sun reported in September 2026 that the kitchenettes at 301 College Ave. have no stoves ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)). Cornell's housing license says full-size refrigerators are provided in the Townhouse Apartments and at 301 College Ave. ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

Cooperative houses are a different model. 660 Stewart Avenue, for one, lists an optional house meal plan five nights a week with one cooking night ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/660-stewart-avenue)).

## What appliance rules apply in a room?

Cornell's housing pages in our sources do not list which small appliances you can bring to a residence hall room. That means nothing here says a microwave, kettle or hot plate is allowed or banned. Read the academic-year housing license for your building and email your residence hall director before you buy, so you do not carry a banned item up the hill on move-in day.

## Do you need to cook if you live on campus?

Mostly no. Every undergraduate living in on-campus housing who entered Cornell in 2021-22 or later must hold a meal plan ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)), and first-years must take Unlimited ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). Cooking is a supplement for snacks, late meals and weekends. The cost side is in [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/), and if you want a full kitchen, see [is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

## What do students say about cooking in residence halls?

A May 2023 Sun columnist wrote that kitchens in dorms offer accessible ways to cook and store groceries ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/05/03/culinary-autonomy-cooking-for-yourself)). A March 2026 Sun writer could not cook meat in a dorm and bought cheese at the farmers market instead ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/ithaca-farmers-market-winter-edition)). Both are one student's experience.

## What happens to your kitchen gear over breaks?

At winter closing, Cornell's checklist tells students to unplug all appliances except fridges and to empty community fridges. Staff also inspect rooms and can enter for repairs over break ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice)).

If you cannot take a microwave home, Big Red Shipping and Storage prices small items, a category that includes a microwave, at $49 for a three- to four-month term with pickup, storage and delivery ([BRSS](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing)). Options are compared in [move-out storage and shipping](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).
