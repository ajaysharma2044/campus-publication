104West! is the kosher dining room on Cornell's campus. Its food is Star K certified, and a mashgiach, the person who supervises kosher rules, oversees the kitchen ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). It is for students who keep kosher and want a full meal plan dining room that fits.

## What is 104West!?

The official name is 104West! Multicultural Dining Room, and it is one of Cornell's ten residential dining rooms ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). Because it is a residential room, the usual swipe rules apply. Besides kosher food, Cornell says it also serves halal and Seventh-day Adventist diets.

A 2024 Sun roundup described it as the kosher dining hall next to the Center for Jewish Living, open weekdays from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 5 to 7 p.m., and said it takes swipes, Big Red Bucks, Cornell currency and credit cards ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/)). The same piece said reviews call its food among the best on campus. Those hours are from 2024, so check current hours on [Dining Now](https://now.dining.cornell.edu/) before you go.

Cornell places it next to the Center for Jewish Living building on the south edge of West Campus ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/religious-observances-accommodations)).

## What sets 104West! apart from the other rooms?

Cornell's special-diets page names 104West! as the kosher dining room, and it is the room with a mashgiach. Morrison Dining, on North Campus, runs a separate halal and kosher station at lunch and dinner every day, though holidays can change that service ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). Use Morrison if you live on North Campus and want a daily option near your hall. Use 104West! when you want the full kosher setting, including Shabbat meals.

Cornell's housing page lists it, with Keeton House and Okenshields, among the dining choices for 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive, a residence at the base of Libe Slope ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/104-mary-ann-wood-drive)).

## What does it cost to eat at 104West!?

For plan holders, regular meals cost a swipe. Shabbat, holiday and special meals at 104West! can carry extra charges, and you reserve them at kosher.scl.cornell.edu ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Ask Cornell Dining at dining@cornell.edu for the amount when you reserve.

Walk-ins without a plan can pay with Big Red Bucks, MealChoice or a credit card, and Cornell notes that kosher holiday meals can cost more ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). The standard door prices, such as $16.80 for lunch Monday to Saturday, are on the [rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates).

The plan itself works as it does everywhere. The Unlimited plan covers all ten rooms, 104West! included. For whether it pays off, read [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).

## How do I get a spot at 104West!?

Eat with your plan, and reserve Shabbat and holiday meals ahead. If you are off campus and keep kosher, a plan that includes dining-room meals is the only way to swipe in. See [meal plans for off-campus students](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) for the options and the cost of cooking kosher at home instead.

## What should I watch for?

Holidays change service. Cornell lists Passover for April 21 to 29, 2027, on its religious observances page ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/religious-observances-accommodations)). Cornell Dining asks students keeping Passover to contact its Nutrition team for help, so reach out well before the date.

Housing choice also changes your meal plan. Students who move into a co-op cannot cancel their plan but can switch to a similar or smaller one, and students who join a fraternity or sorority cannot cancel either ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)). If you plan to join a house, read [a co-op or Greek house instead of a dorm: how to join](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/) before you sign a lease.

## What to do next

Check current hours on Dining Now, reserve your first Shabbat meal at kosher.scl.cornell.edu, and ask Cornell Dining about the extra charge when you do. If North Campus is closer, compare with [Morrison Dining](/cornell/dining/morrison-dining-cornell/), or look at [Okenshields](/cornell/dining/okenshields-cornell-dining/) on Central Campus.
