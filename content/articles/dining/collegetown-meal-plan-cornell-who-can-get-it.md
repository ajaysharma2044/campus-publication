The Collegetown Meal Plan costs $3,528 a semester and gives up to 10 dining-room meals a week, $500 in Big Red Bucks (BRBs) and $436 in City Bucks. Only residents of six Cornell buildings can buy it ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## What does the Collegetown Meal Plan include?

Cornell renamed the South Campus Meal Plan the Collegetown Meal Plan ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). For 2026-27 it includes the following.

## Who can get the Collegetown Meal Plan?

Residents of these six buildings, and no one else ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

- **Cascadilla Hall,** 115 Cascadilla Place, with 366 residents, singles and doubles, and a kitchen ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/cascadilla-hall)).
- **Sheldon Court,** 420 College Ave., with 164 residents and kitchens ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/sheldon-court)).
- **Schuyler House,** 112 Sage Place, with 125 residents, a kitchen on each floor and a free TCAT bus pass for all residents ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house)).
- **301 College Ave.,** four floors of studios to six-bedroom units with kitchenettes, open to transfer and upper-level students ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue)).
- **104 Mary Ann Wood Drive,** at the base of Libe Slope, where Cornell lists dining at Keeton House, 104West! and Okenshields ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/104-mary-ann-wood-drive)).
- **112 Edgemoor Lane,** with 20 residents, all in singles ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/112-edgemoor)).

Living in Collegetown in a private apartment does not qualify. Students who rent off campus choose from Bear Choice, Bear Basic and the debit plans instead, and the [off-campus plan or cook yourself](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) guide compares them.

Cornell lists the plan alongside Bear Traditional as the upper-level and transfer option, depending on where you live. West Campus House residents are the exception, because they must take the House Meal Plan ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). Students who entered before 2021 and live in these buildings must hold a plan, may switch to the Collegetown plan if eligible, and are enrolled in Unlimited when they sign the housing contract ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

## How does it compare with Bear Traditional and Unlimited?

| Plan | Price per semester | Meals a week | Big Red Bucks | City Bucks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Collegetown | $3,528 | Up to 10 | $500 | $436 |
| Bear Traditional | $3,528 | Up to 14 | $400 | None |
| Unlimited | $3,802 | All meals in service periods | $400 | None |

Source: Cornell Dining undergraduate meal plans, 2026-27.

Per [Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), Collegetown costs the same as Bear Traditional, so you give up four weekly meals and gain $100 more in BRBs and the City Bucks. Against Unlimited, Collegetown is $274 cheaper per semester, which is $548 over the year ($7,604 minus $7,056).

Per meal, the numbers favor Bear Traditional on paper. Using the prices above, take the price, subtract the BRBs and City Bucks (valued at $1 each, a best case since City Bucks work only at partners), add $25 for half the yearly fee, and divide by 180 meals (10 a week over 18 weeks). That is a net $2,617 over 180 meals, or $14.54 a meal. Bear Traditional works out to $12.51 a meal on the same basis, with a net $3,153 over 252 meals. If you will eat in the dining rooms most days, Bear Traditional is the better buy ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). If you eat in Collegetown several times a week, the City Bucks are what you are paying for.

## What can you buy with City Bucks?

City Bucks work at participating off-campus businesses. Near Collegetown, that includes 7-Eleven (409 College Ave.), D.P. Dough (408 College Ave.), Wings Over Ithaca (121 Dryden Rd.) and Papa John's (435 Franklin St.). Two grocers are on the list too, Tops Friendly Markets and P&C Fresh. Wegmans and Trader Joe's are not ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)).

Sales tax applies, students 21 and older can buy alcohol, and tobacco and lottery tickets are excluded ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks)). The balance carries from semester to semester while you are registered. Cornell asks that you raise a dispute with the vendor within two weeks and keep your receipt.

## Where do you swipe if you live in Collegetown?

Cornell's ten residential dining rooms are on North, Central and West Campus, and none is in Collegetown ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). Schuyler House is a 15 to 20 minute walk from Central Campus, with 3 to 11 TCAT buses an hour, per Cornell Housing. Plan your swipes around classes, and use the BRBs at campus cafes between them.

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
Transfer students living on campus may reduce to the Bear Traditional plan only ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). 301 College Ave. is open to transfers, so check the building before you assume Collegetown is available. More on options is in [Cornell transfer housing](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/).
```

```reader "If you're a first-year or sophomore"
Your two-year residential requirement includes a meal plan, and first-years, sophomores and transfers cannot cancel it. If you want to live off campus anyway, read the [residential requirement exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).
```
