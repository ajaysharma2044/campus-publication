On Central Campus, a Cornell meal swipe works at Okenshields in Willard Straight Hall ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/02/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus)) and at the Meals on the Move pickup in the Weill Hall Atrium. Cornell's cafes on Central, from Mann Cafe to Trillium, take Big Red Bucks instead.

## Where can I use a meal swipe on Central Campus?

Cornell lists ten residential dining rooms, and only one is on Central Campus: Okenshields, in Willard Straight Hall ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). A February 2025 Sun column made the same point, calling Okenshields the only Central Campus spot that took swipes. It added that students quoted found the hall far from their classes and crowded, and said plan holders pay thousands of dollars a year yet have few swipe options between classes ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus)). That is the author's argument.

Meals on the Move is the second way. It is a pre-order service where one swipe, deducted on the morning you pick up, buys a meal you ordered ahead.

## What is Okenshields?

Okenshields sits in the basement of Willard Straight Hall. It closed in fall 2020, merged with the Ivy Room and reopened in fall 2021, with a bigger salad bar, a wok station and a vegan and vegetarian section ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2021/10/okenshields-renovations-meet-mixed-reviews-from-students)). Prices, food and complaints are in the [Okenshields guide](/cornell/dining/okenshields-cornell-dining/). The short version: it is the one swipe hall near classes, and the Sun's reporting says it gets crowded at midday.

## How does Meals on the Move work?

You order online, pay one swipe, and pick up the next morning. Cornell lists these rules ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/meals-move)):

The [Meals on the Move page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/meals-move) names Crossings Cafe for North Campus in its introduction and Flora Rose House Dining Room for West Campus in its FAQ, so confirm your pickup site on the ordering page. The pickup window ends at 10:30 a.m., so this fits a student with an early class who wants to carry a meal.

## Where can I use Big Red Bucks on Central Campus?

Big Red Bucks (BRBs) are dollars on your ID, worth $1 each ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). They work at all of Cornell Dining's cafes, food courts and coffeehouses ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/cafes-food-courts-coffeehouses)). Fourteen of the 18 sit on Central Campus.

Willard Straight Hall also holds Straight From The Market, which a 2024 Sun roundup described as a hot bar and salad stop that takes BRBs, with takeout at $8 a pound ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/)). The same story said Gimme! Coffee in Gates takes only cash and credit. The February 2025 Sun column put Trillium entrees at $7 to $14 each, paid in BRBs and not swipes.

Order ahead at many of these spots with the GET app. It accepts BRBs and has no fee, and Libe Cafe, Big Red Barn, Bus Stop Bagels, Cafe Jennie, Goldie's Cafe, Mann Cafe, Martha's Cafe and Mattin's Cafe all offer it ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/order-ahead)).

Take Us Home grab-and-go meals, eight in all as of fall 2026, are sold at 11 locations, including Libe Cafe, Atrium Cafe, Vet Med Cafe, the Dairy Bar, Goldie's, Green Dragon, Mann Cafe, Mattin's Cafe and Trillium. You pay with BRBs, MealChoice or Cornell Card ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/take-us-home-expands-five-new-meals-fall)). Nearly 300 vending machines also take BRBs ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/vending-machines)).

## How do I plan a day on Central Campus?

Check each cafe's hours on Dining Now, since Cornell loads them by script and none of these pages print them. As a sample, a 2024 Sun story gave Mattin's Cafe in Duffield hours of 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Monday to Thursday and 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Friday ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/03/students-express-frustration-with-north-star-dinings-limited-late-night-options)). Those are two years old, so confirm them.
