- Off campus, 1 to 9 dining-room meals a week: Just Bucks costs least.
- At 10 to 12 meals a week, Bear Choice wins; at 13 or 14, Bear Traditional.
- At 15 or more meals a week, Unlimited is cheapest, down to $9.52 a swipe.

Your weekly count of dining-room meals picks the plan. Off campus, 1 to 9 meals a week points to Just Bucks, 10 to 12 to Bear Choice, 13 or 14 to Bear Traditional, and 15 or more to Unlimited. That ranking uses Cornell's 2026-27 prices, 18-week terms and Big Red Bucks spent in full ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## Which Cornell meal plan should I get for the meals I eat?

The plan that costs least is the one whose swipes you actually use. A swipe is one entry to a residential dining room, such as Morrison Dining or North Star. The calculator below shows only the plans you are allowed to buy and ranks them by what your meals cost for a semester.

```tool cornell-meal-plan-calculator-by-meals-a-week```

It asks where you live and who you are, which sets the plans open to you. It asks how many dining-room meals you eat in a typical week, and which meals, since dinner costs more than breakfast. It also asks how many weeks you will eat on campus. Fall meals run from lunch on Aug. 17 to brunch on Dec. 20, 2026, and spring meals from lunch on Jan. 19 to dinner on May 28, 2027 ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)). Each term is about 18 weeks, the default.

A checkbox decides how to count Big Red Bucks, or BRBs. These are dollars loaded on your Cornell ID for Cornell Dining cafes, stores and dining rooms, where 1 BRB equals $1 and is tax-exempt ([Meal plan FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). Leave it ticked if you would spend that money on campus food anyway. Untick it if the bucks would sit unused, and the plan gets no credit for them.

For whether a plan is worth buying at all, rather than which one, see [Is the Cornell meal plan worth it?](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/)

## What does each 2026-27 Cornell meal plan cost?

Undergraduate plans cost $500 to $3,802 a semester for 2026-27 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). A weekly plan gives a set number of swipes each week. A debit plan gives a set number of meals for the whole semester instead. Guest meals, which Cornell calls bonus meals, let you swipe in a friend.

## What does one swipe really cost?

A swipe costs less than its sticker suggests, because part of each plan price is Big Red Bucks you get back as spending money. The math: take the semester price, add half the yearly fee, subtract the bucks, and divide by the swipes. Bear Traditional over 18 weeks is ($3,528 + $25 − $400) / (14 × 18) = $3,153 / 252 = $12.51 a swipe ([Cornell Dining plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

Compare that with paying for a meal without a swipe. Cornell's 2026-27 dining rooms charge $10.80 for breakfast, $15.25 for lunch Monday to Saturday, $18.85 for dinner and $16.85 for Sunday brunch in Big Red Bucks. At the door, tax included, the same meals cost $11.90, $16.80, $20.80 and $18.55 ([Cornell Dining rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

A week of every meal is breakfast, lunch and dinner Monday to Saturday plus Sunday brunch and dinner, or 20 meals. In Big Red Bucks that week costs the six days of three meals plus Sunday brunch and dinner come to $305.10, or $15.26 a meal. At the door it costs $336.35, or $16.82 a meal ([Cornell Dining rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

## Which plans can you choose?

Your housing decides your choices more than your appetite does. Every undergraduate living in on-campus housing must have a plan, a rule that covers students who entered Cornell in 2021-22 or later ([Cornell Dining rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

- **First-years** get Unlimited, with no other option.
- **West Campus House System residents** in Alice Cook, Carl Becker, Hans Bethe, William Keeton and Flora Rose houses get the House Meal Plan and cannot change or cancel it.
- **Residents of six Collegetown halls** (112 Edgemoor, Cascadilla Hall, Schuyler House, Sheldon Court, 301 College Ave. and 104 Mary Ann Wood Dr.) start on Unlimited and can switch to the Collegetown Meal Plan.
- **Other upper-level and transfer students on campus** can choose Unlimited or Bear Traditional. Cornell's FAQ names transfers and on-campus juniors; its rates page says upper-level students. If you're a sophomore, confirm with Cornell Dining before counting on Bear Traditional.
- **Off-campus undergraduates** can buy any traditional plan, a debit plan or nothing. Bear Choice and Bear Basic are open on campus only to students enrolled before summer 2021.
- **Off-campus students who lived in the House System** for at least a semester can add House Affiliate by emailing dining@cornell.edu.
- **Members of recognized fraternities and sororities** can take SFL Supplemental. On campus, that holds only if the chapter requires a plan of at least 7 meals a week.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your choice is how much of Unlimited you use. At 14 meals a week each swipe costs you $13.60 ($3,427 / 252); at 10 a week it is $19.04. Use your 8 guest meals, worth up to $150.80 a semester at the dinner price. The [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) covers the rest of your first semester.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
You start on Unlimited and cannot cancel. You can reduce to Bear Traditional by the semester deadline, which saves $274 a semester if you eat 14 or fewer meals a week.
```

## Unlimited or Bear Traditional: which is better on campus?

Choose Bear Traditional if you eat 14 or fewer dining-room meals a week, and Unlimited at 15 or more. The two plans carry the same $400 in Big Red Bucks, so the gap is the $274 price difference per semester. A 15th meal each week, paid in Big Red Bucks, costs $274.68 a semester (at the average meal price above). That is where the two plans break even ([Cornell Dining plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

In the six Collegetown halls, the Collegetown Meal Plan beats Unlimited at 12 or fewer meals a week if you spend its $436 in City Bucks, and at 11 or fewer if you don't. Each meal a week above its 10 swipes adds $274.68 a semester at Big Red Bucks prices ([Cornell Dining plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

SFL Supplemental's 127 meals a semester work out to about 7 a week. For an off-campus member it is the cheapest choice from about 7 to 12 meals a week. House Affiliate is the best value for anyone who qualifies, at $12.02 a swipe, as long as you eat its 3 weekly meals on West Campus. It stays cheapest from 3 to 12 meals a week ([Cornell Dining plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## Should you buy a plan if you live off campus?

Buy Just Bucks instead of nothing if you eat even one or two dining-room meals a week. Its whole price comes back as Big Red Bucks, so you pay only the fee share, and every meal then costs the Big Red Bucks price instead of the higher door price. At 5 meals a week, 90 meals cost $1,398.40 a semester with Just Bucks ($25 + 90 × $15.26) against $1,513.80 at the door (90 × $16.82). You can add Big Red Bucks at any time at get.cbord.com/cornell once you are on a plan ([Cornell Dining rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

From 10 meals a week, follow the ranking at the top of the page. Cornell Dining calls Unlimited a good value for upper-level students who live off campus; that holds only at 15 or more meals a week. If you mostly cook, compare the plan with groceries in [Off-campus at Cornell: meal plan or cook yourself?](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) Rent and food together decide [whether off-campus living is cheaper than a dorm](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Take the Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan instead of Just Bucks. Its whole $50 price is Big Red Bucks and it carries no administrative fee, so it beats Just Bucks by the fee share at any meal count below 10 a week. It also gives 5% off at the Atrium Cafe, Big Red Barn, Bear Necessities and the College of Veterinary Medicine cafe.
```

## When can you change or cancel your plan?

Reduce or cancel by Wednesday, Sept. 9, 2026, for fall, and reduce by Wednesday, Feb. 3, 2027, for spring ([Cornell Dining deadlines](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms)). After a deadline you can still upgrade, but not reduce. First-year, sophomore and transfer students cannot cancel.

The plan week runs Thursday to Wednesday, and swipes you don't use by Wednesday are lost. Plan changes take effect on Thursdays, and credits are prorated by the week. New York State tax law bars credits for missed meals, for any reason. Unused Big Red Bucks carry from fall to spring, then expire. The [plans page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) sets that date at May 27, 2027, and the [terms page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027) at noon on May 31, 2027. Go by May 27.

## FAQ

### Does financial aid pay for the meal plan?

Cornell Dining says the Unlimited plan is fully covered by financial aid for eligible students. Before you reduce to a smaller plan, ask the Office of Financial Aid how the change affects your aid package. Other costs aid budgets miss are in [hidden costs for first-gen and low-income students](/cornell/money/cornell-first-gen-low-income-hidden-costs/).

### Can I use my swipes or Big Red Bucks for a friend?

Only through guest meals. Swipes and Big Red Bucks are for your own use, and each plan with guest meals carries 4 or 8 a semester.

### Can I buy a plan for one semester?

No. Every plan runs the full academic year and renews automatically for spring. You can cancel spring only if you will be away, for study abroad, an internship, a leave or graduation.

## What to do next

Count your dining-room meals for one normal week, enter them in the calculator, and reduce or switch plans before the semester deadline above. Changes go through the Housing and Dining Portal.
