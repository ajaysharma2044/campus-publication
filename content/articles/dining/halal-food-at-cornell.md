The easiest halal meal at Cornell is at Morrison Dining on North Campus, where a dedicated halal and kosher station runs at lunch and dinner every day ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). Keeton House on West Campus serves halal on request, and 104West! also serves it. Cornell also lists a halal food truck.

## Where can I eat halal on campus?

Cornell serves halal food at four places on campus. Holidays can change service, so check Dining Now before a trip.

| Place | What Cornell lists | When |
|---|---|---|
| Morrison Dining, North Campus | Dedicated halal and kosher station | Lunch and dinner, every day |
| Keeton House Dining Room, West Campus | Halal on request | Dinner seven days a week; lunch and brunch Saturday and Sunday |
| Willard Straight Hall | Take Us Home items that are halal certified | Pre-order through the GET app |
| 104West! | Serves halal, Star K kosher and Seventh-day Adventist diets | Check Dining Now |

Source: Cornell Dining halal resources and food allergies and special diets pages.

Morrison and Keeton are two of Cornell's ten residential dining rooms, so a swipe covers a meal at either ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). Take Us Home items at Willard Straight Hall are halal certified, and you pre-order them on GET ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/halal-resources)). Order at get.cbord.com/cornell or the CBORD GET app, with no fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/order-ahead)).

Dining rooms also carry labels for pork and alcohol, which helps when you are reading an unfamiliar dish ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/halal-resources)). Cornell asks students with dietary needs to work with the Dining Nutrition Office ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

## What does Cornell do during Ramadan?

Each Ramadan, Cornell Dining offers extended hours in select dining rooms, take-out containers, and pre-fast meal boxes you pick up the night before ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/halal-resources)). Email the Dining Nutrition Office at diningnutrition@cornell.edu a few weeks before Ramadan to learn which rooms will run extended hours and where to pick up boxes ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

## Where can I eat halal off campus?

Halal Brothers, a Mediterranean food truck, is on Cornell's approved food truck list as of May 2026 ([Cornell EHS](https://ehs.cornell.edu/campus-health-safety/event-management/approved-food-truck-and-trailer-vendors)). A March 2025 Sun columnist recommended it and wrote that it stays open to 2 a.m. on weekends and 10 p.m. on weekdays ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)). Hours and parking spots change, so check where the truck is before a late trip.

A September 2025 Sun column on late-night food listed a halal chain among its wishes for Collegetown; the columnist had seen a sign there saying a Halal Munchies would be opening ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/rgcm36ewemrx)).

If you cook, call the Ithaca stores you plan to use and ask whether they carry halal meat before you count on them. For prices and stores, read [cheap groceries in Ithaca](/cornell/money/cheap-groceries-ithaca-ny-students/).

## What should I ask Cornell Dining?

Ask four things before you commit to a plan. Which dining room is closest to your hall? Is halal available at the meals you eat most? How does the kitchen keep halal food apart from pork? What changes during Ramadan? Send all four to the Dining Nutrition Office at diningnutrition@cornell.edu ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

## What to do next

Look up Morrison and Keeton hours and menus on [Dining Now](https://now.dining.cornell.edu/), and eat once at the Morrison station before you sign up for a plan.
