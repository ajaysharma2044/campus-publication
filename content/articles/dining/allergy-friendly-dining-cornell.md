Look up your meal in NetNutrition before you eat, then read the label at the station and ask staff if you are unsure. Cornell Dining labels eight major allergens in its residential dining rooms, and its nutrition office answers questions ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

The office's number is (607) 255-9280 ([Contact Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/contact-cornell-dining)). Cornell also warns that contamination risk exists everywhere.

## What do the allergen labels cover?

Residential dining rooms mark milk, egg, fish, tree nuts, peanuts, shellfish, soy and sesame. They also mark gluten and coconut, plus alcohol and pork ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). Cornell Dining's culinary team and managers are trained every year with AllerTrain, and the workforce holds FARECheck Silver, a food-allergy safety certification that requires at least 90% of staff to be trained. Cornell says it received that rating as of November 2025.

Cornell's own disclaimer is blunt: contamination risk exists, and manufacturers can change formulas without notice. That means a label is a starting point. If your reaction would be severe, confirm the ingredient with a staff member each visit, even for a dish you ate last month.

## Which dining room is most allergy-friendly?

Risley Dining sources its food without gluten, peanuts and tree nuts, and it allows coconut. The page says Risley is AllerCheck approved by MenuTrinfo ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). A 2021 Sun column for vegans agreed that Risley had the best dietary variety and described a vegetarian hot station and vegan stir-fry ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/24/dining-halls-as-a-vegan/)). The same writer said Risley was fully gluten-free, which is her claim, so ask staff before you rely on it for celiac disease.

Other rooms handle special diets differently. Morrison Dining has a dedicated halal and kosher station, 104West! is the kosher dining room, and Cornell lists Plant Forward options at every location, with a shared oil sign to look for ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

## What do students report?

Experience varies by year and writer. A 2019 Sun column said dairy-free milk was widely available but often sweetened, and that some items labeled vegan contained dairy in the writer's experience. The writer also said dining workers helped with dietary needs when students were respectful ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/02/inocciduous-thoughts-things-ive-noticed-about-food-on-west-campus)). A 2025 Sun columnist argued the mandatory plan has no reduced option for dietary restrictions and that feedback to the head chef changed nothing ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)). These are opinions, and the labels and office above are the facts.

## What if I am sick and cannot get to the dining room?

A classmate can pick up a tray for you at any residential dining room after you email dining@cornell.edu with your name, card ID and allergies. It counts as one meal off your weekly balance ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). Put your allergies in that email every time.

## What goes wrong, and what fixes it?

Three problems come up. A label is missing: ask staff or skip the item. A menu changes: check NetNutrition again. The room cannot meet your needs: call the Dining Nutrition Office at 108 Appel Commons, or the Dining Contracts Office at (607) 255-5952, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/contact-cornell-dining)). Cornell's pages in this guide do not explain how to get a plan exemption for a medical diet, so ask the contracts office what documents it needs.

If you carry medication, plan refills before the semester. Wegmans' pharmacy is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 9 p.m., Saturday until 6 p.m. and Sunday until 4 p.m. ([Wegmans](https://www.wegmans.com/stores/ithaca-ny)). For nearer options, see [pharmacies near campus](/cornell/daily-life/pharmacy-near-cornell-campus-ithaca/).
