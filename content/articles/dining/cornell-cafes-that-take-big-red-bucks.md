Big Red Bucks (BRBs) work at any Cornell Dining location on campus ([Cornell Dining FAQ](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). Cornell lists 18 cafes, 4 on North Campus and 14 on Central Campus. They do not work at eateries that Cornell Dining does not run.

## How far do Big Red Bucks go?

One BRB equals $1, and BRBs buy food only. They work at Cornell Dining locations and many vending machines, not at eateries outside Cornell Dining ([Cornell Dining FAQ](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). They cover your own food, not a guest's.

BRBs also carry no sales tax. A credit card, CornellCard or MealChoice purchase at Cornell Dining adds 8% New York State sales tax ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/january-morrison-dining-reduced)). So a $10.00 sandwich costs $10.80 on a card and $10.00 in BRBs.

Each plan includes a set amount per semester. Unlimited and Bear Traditional carry $400, Bear Choice and Bear Basic carry $500, and Off-Campus Value carries $150 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). A March 2026 Sun columnist argued that the allotment has stayed flat while prices rose, and said it covers at most about two Trillium visits a week ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/we-want-more-big-red-bucks)). Whether a plan is worth it is covered in [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).

## Which places do not take Big Red Bucks?

Gimme! Coffee in Gates Hall takes cash and credit only, according to a September 2024 Sun guide ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/)). The March 2026 columnist named Gimme! Coffee and Temple of Zeus as popular places that do not accept BRBs, and told readers to verify with Cornell Dining. That is the writer's claim, so ask at the register.

Big Red Food Court on College Avenue is off campus. Its owners planned to accept BRBs, but a University spokesperson told the Sun in February 2025 that it was not eligible, and City Bucks had not yet been applied for ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/13/big-red-food-court-a-new-york-style-dining-experience-set-to-open-on-college-avenue/)). City Bucks is the separate balance for participating off-campus businesses.

## Which cafes are worth a stop?

These are student and Cornell descriptions, with the date of each.

- **Big Red Barn:** a former carriage house popular with graduate students, with hot-pressed sandwiches and soups, open 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. daily in 2024 ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/)).
- **McCormick's at Moakley House:** a golf-course tavern with wings, poutine and burgers, open weekdays 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturdays until 5 p.m. and Sundays 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. in 2024.
- **Crossings Cafe:** a 2022 Sun tip list says to order Crossings smoothies ahead on the GET app ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/07/01/what-i-wish-i-knew-about-campus-food-before-coming-to-cornell/)).
- **Bear Necessities:** the late-night option, listed open to 2 a.m. in fall 2026 on Dining Now.

Take Us Home, Cornell's grab-and-go meal line, offers 8 meals at 11 locations in fall 2026, and 5 of them are new. You can pay with BRBs, MealChoice or a Cornell Card, and the selection varies by site ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/take-us-home-expands-five-new-meals-fall)). If you study in the library, Libe Cafe in Olin and Mann Cafe in Mann sit inside the buildings; see [best places to study at Cornell](/cornell/academics/best-places-to-study-at-cornell/) and [24-hour study spaces for finals](/cornell/academics/cornell-24-hour-study-spaces-finals/).

## What happens to Big Red Bucks over winter break?

Fall BRBs work at open cafes and stores through Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026. Cornell Dining is closed from Dec. 25 through Jan. 1, and from Jan. 4 to 19 some cafes and shops reopen, with fall BRBs still usable ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining)). Plan food for the closed days with [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).

```reader "If you live off campus"
Off-Campus Value gives only $150 in BRBs. Two debit plans sell mostly BRBs: Just Bucks costs $1,000 a year and gives BRBs only, and Flex 10/500 costs $1,348 a year for 10 meals a semester plus $500 BRBs a semester ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Weigh them against cooking in [meal plan or cook yourself](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/).
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
The Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan costs $50 a semester and buys $50 in BRBs plus a 5% discount at four cafes: Atrium Cafe, Big Red Barn, Bear Necessities and the Veterinary Medicine Cafe. It carries no administrative fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)).
```
