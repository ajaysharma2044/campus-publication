Okenshields is the dining hall in Willard Straight Hall, the one residential dining room on Central Campus where a meal swipe works ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). It is for anyone with classes on the Arts Quad or the Ag Quad who wants a full meal between them.

## What is Okenshields?

Okenshields is one of ten Cornell residential dining rooms. It sits in the basement of Willard Straight Hall, the student union on Central Campus ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus)). The other rooms are on North Campus and West Campus, so this is the one students pass between classes.

The hall closed in fall 2020 and was rebuilt after merging with the old Ivy Room. The renovation added a bigger salad bar, a wok station and a vegan and vegetarian section, and scaled back planned seating and a build-your-own bowl ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2021/10/okenshields-renovations-meet-mixed-reviews-from-students)).

## What sets Okenshields apart?

Location is the draw. A Sun column in February 2025 said Okenshields was the only place on Central Campus that took swipes, and that students quoted in it found it far from their classes and crowded ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus)). Treat that as the author's claim, but it matches Cornell's own room list, where Okenshields is the only Central Campus entry.

The food gets a loyal defense. Cornell's official vegan page names Okenshields and Morrison Dining as the two dedicated vegan stations on campus ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/vegan-and-vegetarian-resources-campus)). Items from the Willard Straight Take Us Home counter are halal certified, and you pre-order them through the GET app ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/halal-resources)).

Two Sun columnists argue the hall is underrated. One wrote in September 2025 that its Asian and grill dishes are more consistent than the other halls, naming Thursday pad thai and evening fried chicken ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/abou-alfa-cornells-best-dining-hall)). Another, in March 2026, called it predictable in a good way, with friendly staff and standout soups, including wok daikon, hot and sour, and tom yum ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/a-foodie-s-homage-to-okenshields-why-it-deserves-more-love)).

## What does a meal at Okenshields cost?

Plan holders pay nothing at the door, because a swipe covers the meal. Walk-ins pay the door price, which includes tax. Big Red Bucks (BRBs, dollars on your ID that carry no tax) cost less per meal ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

| Meal | Door price, tax included | Price in BRBs |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $11.90 | $10.80 |
| Lunch, Monday to Saturday | $16.80 | $15.25 |
| Dinner | $20.80 | $18.85 |
| Sunday brunch | $18.55 | $16.85 |

Source: Cornell Dining meal plans and rates, 2026-27.

Prices match every other dining room, so cost does not separate Okenshields from its siblings. The plan rules are the same too. For which plan pays off, read [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).

## How do I get a spot at Okenshields?

You walk in. Cornell says everyone can eat in a residential dining room, and plan holders swipe their ID at the door ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)).

## What should I watch for at Okenshields?

Crowding is the steady complaint. The 2021 renovation story said crowding continued, and it quoted Paul Muscente, an associate director of Cornell Dining, saying the department had fewer staff than it needed ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2021/10/okenshields-renovations-meet-mixed-reviews-from-students)). The 2026 homage listed long noon lines, cramped seating and doubts about utensil cleanliness among its complaints, along with unseasoned green beans and burnt pork. The 2025 defense admitted dry rice, overcooked chicken and a small salad bar.

Go before or after the noon rush if you can. Cornell loads its hours by script, so check the Okenshields listing on [Dining Now](https://now.dining.cornell.edu/) before you walk over.

If the line is too long, Willard Straight has other food. A 2024 Sun roundup described Straight From The Market there as a hot bar and salad stop that takes BRBs, with takeout at $8 a pound ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/)).

## What to do next

Walk over at an off-peak hour and judge the line yourself. If you want a different kind of room, compare it with [Morrison Dining](/cornell/dining/morrison-dining-cornell/) on North Campus or the kosher hall [104West!](/cornell/dining/104west-kosher-dining-cornell/).
