Cornell does not rank its dining rooms, so the best West Campus hall depends on your diet and your schedule. In the Sun's reporting, Bethe's Jansen's Dining Room scored best in one review and Rose led a class survey, while Keeton suits halal and grain-salad eaters. All five rooms take the same swipe ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)).

## What are the five West Campus dining rooms?

Cornell lists five House dining rooms on West Campus: Becker House Dining Room, Cook House Dining Room, Jansen's Dining Room, Keeton House Dining Room and Rose House Dining Room. Each sits inside a House of the West Campus House System, which finished building in 2009 ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2009/08/west-campus-house-system-officially-complete)). Jansen's is the dining room in Hans Bethe House, and Rose is in Flora Rose House.

| House | Dining room | Opened | Residents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alice Cook House | Cook House Dining Room | Aug. 2004 | Nearly 400 |
| Carl Becker House | Becker House Dining Room | Aug. 2005 | 350+ |
| Hans Bethe House | Jansen's Dining Room | 2007 | 350+ |
| William Keeton House | Keeton House Dining Room | Aug. 2008 | 300+ |
| Flora Rose House | Rose House Dining Room | Aug. 2009 | 350+ |

Source: Cornell Housing house pages and Cornell Chronicle. Bethe's count includes McFaddin Hall, which houses about 60 residents when open and [left 2026-27 selection for construction](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/mcfaddin-hall-will-not-house-students-for-fall-2026-due-to-upcoming-construction).

## How do the five rooms differ?

The differences come from student reporting. A Sun review of West Campus dinners in the week of Oct. 24, 2022, scored Bethe an 8 out of 10, Cook 6.6, Rose 6.5, Keeton 5.5 and Becker 2.5 ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/07/a-comprehensive-review-of-west-campus-dining/)). Two reviewers ate for one week, so treat it as a snapshot. Their larger point was that the same hall swung from a 2 on Monday to a 10 on Tuesday. The review also noted that Bethe closes earliest, at 7:30 p.m.

Popularity tells a different story. In the Sun's Sunset Survey for the Class of 2026, Rose led West Campus picks with 28.9% of votes ([The Sun](https://cornell-sun-data.github.io/sunset-survey-2026/at-cornell.html)). The survey ran from April 20 to May 5, 2026, drew 103 responses in total and left every question optional ([Sun methodology](https://cornell-sun-data.github.io/sunset-survey-2026/about.html)).

Diet needs shape the choice more than rank. A 2021 Sun column for vegans praised Keeton's grain salads and called Rose creative but not always vegan ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/24/dining-halls-as-a-vegan/)). Keeton also serves halal on request, at dinner seven days a week and at lunch and brunch on Saturday and Sunday ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

Rose hosts special dinners, including the Cena Romana banquet on Nov. 12, 2026, from 5 to 8 p.m. ([Cornell Dining events](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining)).

A 2025 Sun columnist called Okenshields the best hall and described a West hall as less reliable than it ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/abou-alfa-cornells-best-dining-hall)). One person's pick settles nothing, so eat in two or three rooms before you decide.

## What are House Dinners?

Each house runs House Dinner every Wednesday from 6 to 7 p.m. Only members of that house, its fellows and their guests can eat in during that hour, and the room reopens to everyone at 7 p.m. ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms/west-campus-house-dinners)). If you live elsewhere, plan to arrive after 7 p.m. on Wednesdays.

## What does the House Meal Plan cost?

Every West Campus resident is signed up for the House Meal Plan automatically, and cannot change or cancel it while living there. It costs $3,802 per semester, which is $7,604 a year, and it includes $400 in Big Red Bucks per semester and 8 guest meals ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). The same page lists a $50 yearly fee.

Housing adds to that. Cornell's single room rate is $15,566 and a quad is $13,744 per academic year, and West Campus houses share the residence hall rate table ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). A single plus the House Meal Plan comes to $23,170 ($15,566 plus $7,604). For how that compares with renting, read [is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

## Where do the five rooms sit among all ten Cornell dining rooms?

Cornell runs ten residential dining rooms. Check each room's hours on [Dining Now](https://now.dining.cornell.edu/eateries). The prices are the same everywhere, so the table below compares places and standout features.

| Room | Where | Notable feature |
|---|---|---|
| Morrison Dining | Toni Morrison Hall, North Campus | Dedicated halal and kosher station; vegan station |
| North Star Dining Room | Appel Commons, North Campus | Late Night block, 8:30 to 10:30 p.m. on listed fall 2026 dates |
| Risley Dining | Risley, North Campus | Sources food without gluten, peanuts and tree nuts |
| Okenshields | Willard Straight Hall, Central Campus | Dedicated vegan station |
| Becker House Dining Room | Carl Becker House, West Campus | House Dinner on Wednesdays |
| Cook House Dining Room | Alice Cook House, West Campus | Late Night block, 9 to 10 p.m. on listed fall 2026 dates |
| Jansen's Dining Room | Hans Bethe House, West Campus | Earliest closing of the West rooms in a 2022 Sun review |
| Keeton House Dining Room | William Keeton House, West Campus | Halal on request |
| Rose House Dining Room | Flora Rose House, West Campus | Special dinners such as Cena Romana |
| 104West! | Kosher and multicultural room | Star K kosher certification |

Source: Cornell Dining room list, special diets page and Dining Now.

Meals on the Move lets you order a meal ahead for morning pickup on class days. It costs one swipe, and the West Campus pickup is Rose House Dining Room ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/meals-move)).
