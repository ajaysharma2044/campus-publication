## What is Anabel's Grocery like?

Cornell Dining says Anabel's sells fresh, affordable food and points students to SNAP, the federal food benefit ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/food-security)). Two Cornell pages describe its location differently. Cornell Health says Central Campus, and Cornell Dining says the south edge of campus, but both mean Anabel Taylor Hall ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food)).

Students describe it as small. A 2022 Sun report called it mostly plant-based whole foods, and an Anabel's worker said Collegetown's cheaper options cost about the same ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/)). A 2023 Sun guide to skipping the meal plan said the selection is limited and that the store sits by the TCAT Route 30 bus stop at Anabel Taylor Hall ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/27/obaseki-a-brokies-guide-to-having-no-meal-plan/)). A 2024 guide by Carlin Reyen said it sells at co-op prices and accepts SNAP ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/22/a-cheapskates-guide-to-navigating-cornell)). Prices for the stores around it are in [cheap groceries in Ithaca](/cornell/money/cheap-groceries-ithaca-ny-students/).

## Is Anabel's Grocery still funded?

A February 2025 Sun opinion column said Cornell is ending about $100,000 a year in funding for Anabel's and the Center for Transformative Action after 53 years, effective at the end of fiscal 2027. It cited Dean of Students Marla Love as saying the center no longer fits the original mission of Cornell United Religious Work ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/28/reyen-anabels-defunding-sheds-light-on-a-larger-issue)). The column is the writer's account, and Cornell's pages do not confirm a closing date, so ask Cornell Dining before you rely on the store for next year.

Cornell Health's affordable food page still carries a Nov. 10, 2025 note about reduced prices and a 50% discount during the federal government shutdown. That note is a year old, so do not read it as current pricing ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food)).

## What is the Cornell Food Pantry?

It is a free, confidential pantry at 109 McGraw Place, across from Alice Cook House on West Campus, with food and personal care items for students and employees, per Cornell Health. Cornell Dining lists undergraduates, graduate students, staff and faculty ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/food-security)). Neither page gives hours or enrollment steps, so read the [Cornell Health affordable food page](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food) for current details before you walk over.

## What other free food does Cornell offer?

Swipe Out Hunger lets a meal-plan student donate guest meals from their plan each semester, up to a per-semester limit. Cornell Dining, Swipe Out Hunger and the Dean of Students run it, and the fall 2026 donation window is Sept. 21 to Dec. 8 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/food-security/swipe-out-hunger)). The First Generation and Low-Income office, in Barnes Hall 200 and open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., handles free-meal requests, per the same page.

The Sun's 2024 cheapskate guide also points to free receptions and club listservs for event food ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/22/a-cheapskates-guide-to-navigating-cornell)). Those are the writer's tips, not Cornell programs.

## Can you get SNAP as a student?

Students aged 18 to 49 who are enrolled at least half-time cannot get SNAP unless they meet an exemption. The listed exemptions include working 20 hours a week, caring for a child and being unable to work ([Hunger Solutions New York](https://hungersolutionsny.org/federal-nutrition-programs/snap/snap-eligibility-for-college-students/)). The temporary pandemic-era widening has ended, per a state notice, so check with the state before relying on an old rule.

Tompkins County Department of Social Services is at (607) 274-5680, and you can dial 2-1-1 or text your ZIP code to 898-211 ([Tompkins County](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/News-articles/Tompkins-County-encourages-donations-to-support-residents-facing-SNAP-benefit-delays-as-the-federal-shutdown-continues)). The Ithaca Farmers Market accepts SNAP and EBT, giving $5 and $1 wooden tokens at the market office, plus FreshConnect checks that match $2 for every $2 spent, up to $50 per day, as of July 2023 ([Ithaca Farmers Market](https://ithacamarket.com/ebt-snap)).
