Cornell Dining runs dedicated vegan stations at two dining rooms, Morrison and Okenshields, and says plant-based items appear at every campus eatery ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/vegan-and-vegetarian-resources-campus)). Student columnists in the Sun rate the halls very differently, so use the table below to pick where to eat.

## What does Cornell Dining officially offer vegetarians and vegans?

Cornell's own page names two dedicated vegan stations, at Okenshields and at Morrison Dining. It says vegetarian and vegan items are available at all locations, including cafes, dining rooms and convenience stores. Cafe menu boards mark vegetarian items "VG" and vegan items "VN" ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/vegan-and-vegetarian-resources-campus)).

In the dining rooms, Cornell labels its Plant Forward options at the stations. It also asks diners to look for the shared oil sign ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). Cornell warns that cross-contact can happen and that manufacturers can change recipes without notice, so read labels each visit.

Cornell lists ten residential dining rooms, which are the halls where one swipe buys a meal: Morrison Dining, North Star, Risley, Okenshields, Becker House, Cook House, Jansen's at Bethe House, Keeton House, Rose House and 104West! ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)).

To check dishes, many retail units post menus, allergen and ingredient details on NetNutrition, where you can filter for "vegan" or "vegetarian" ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/vegan-and-vegetarian-resources-campus)). Questions about a specific diet go to Cornell Dining's registered dietitians at 108A Appel Commons, 607-254-2389 or diningnutrition@cornell.edu ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets)).

## Which dining halls do students say work best?

The Sun's student columns span several years, so treat each line as one writer's view in one year.

| Hall | What the writer reported | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Risley | Best variety: a vegetarian hot station, vegan stir-fry and poke | 2021 |
| Keeton | Good grain salads | 2021 |
| Rose | Creative dishes that are not always vegan | 2021 |
| North Star (Appel) | Many stations; a make-your-own bowl; called repetitive | 2021, 2025 |
| Morrison | A plain cheese pizza on one visit; called repetitive | 2025 |
| Okenshields | Renovation added an expanded salad bar, a wok and a vegan and vegetarian section | 2021 |

Source: The Cornell Daily Sun columns of [Oct. 24, 2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/24/dining-halls-as-a-vegan/), [Oct. 27, 2021](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2021/10/okenshields-renovations-meet-mixed-reviews-from-students) and [March 10, 2025](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead).

The 2021 vegan writer also listed misses. A vegan grilled cheese was badly made, vegan waffles disappeared, and some days had thin vegan choices. Her tips were to check the Eatery app menus and to ask the staff. A 2019 column on West Campus food said some items labeled vegan contained dairy ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/02/inocciduous-thoughts-things-ive-noticed-about-food-on-west-campus)). Ask at the station if a label matters to you.

## What do students say is missing?

Variety and flexibility. Sneha Singhi, a junior, wrote in the Sun in March 2025 that Morrison and North Star options were repetitive, that West Campus breakfast was mostly plain vegetables, and that the tofu was soggy. She also wrote that the mandatory plan has no reduced option for dietary restrictions and that her feedback to the head chef changed nothing. She estimated about $400 extra on delivery and restaurant meals ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)).

Opinions also change by year and writer. A February 2019 Sun column said Cornell was better for vegetarians than the writer's former school, with veggie burgers, tofu, seitan and vegan chili at most spots. It named Ten Forward, a fully vegan cafe in the Commons ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/27/dining-hall-diversity-being-vegetarian-at-cornell/)). That column is seven years old, so confirm the cafe is open before you plan a meal around it.

If a plan does not fit your diet, the cost question matters. The break-even math is in [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).

## Where can vegetarians and vegans eat off campus?

Singhi's 2025 column ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)) named Moosewood, Thompson and Bleecker, Dos Amigos, Koko, Zocalo, Halal Brothers, Sangam Indian Cuisine and Plum Tree. Her tip was to ask restaurants to modify dishes.

Moosewood posts weekday lunch from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m., dinner from 5 to 9 p.m., and weekend brunch from 11 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. It is closed Tuesdays ([Moosewood](https://moosewood.com/menu/)). Check the hours of any other spot before a late trip.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Unlimited is required for first-year undergraduates ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Contact Cornell Dining's dietitians before move-in to ask how your hall handles your diet. The [first-year guide](/start/first-year/) covers the rest of the dining rules.
```

```reader "If you live off campus"
You do not need a plan to eat in a dining room, since anyone can pay at the door. Off-Campus Value, the entry-level plan, gives 45 meals a semester ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). If the halls do not suit your diet, compare it with [meal plan or cook yourself](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) and the [cheap groceries guide](/cornell/money/cheap-groceries-ithaca-ny-students/).
```

```reader "If you live in a co-op"
Co-op residents cook some of their own meals. 660 Stewart Avenue lists an optional house meal plan five nights a week with one cooking night ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/660-stewart-avenue)). That gives you control over the menu. See [a co-op or Greek house instead of a dorm: how to join](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/) for the rules.
```

## What to do next

Check NetNutrition on the day you plan to eat and look for the vegan station at Morrison or Okenshields. If the menu falls short, ask the station staff what else they can make, then fall back on a restaurant from the list above.
