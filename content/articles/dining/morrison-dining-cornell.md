Morrison Dining is the dining room in Toni Morrison Hall on North Campus, one of ten Cornell rooms where a meal swipe works ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). It suits North Campus residents, first-years above all, and it has a dedicated halal and kosher station.

## What is Morrison Dining?

Morrison is a residential dining room: a hall where you swipe in once and eat from several stations. It sits inside Toni Morrison Hall at 18 Sisson Place ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/toni-morrison-hall)). The hall opened in 2021 with Ganędagǫ: Hall, and the two together added 800 beds in the North Campus expansion ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2022/08/cornell-welcomes-students-marks-milestone-residential-life)).

The same building holds Crossings Cafe, a smaller stop for coffee and quick food ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/cafes-food-courts-coffeehouses)). Language House also lives in Toni Morrison Hall ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/toni-morrison-hall)), so you will meet language students at the tables.

## What sets Morrison apart from the other dining rooms?

Morrison is the room to pick when your diet has rules. Cornell lists a dedicated halal and kosher station here at lunch and dinner every day, and says holidays can change service ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)). Cornell's official page names only two dedicated vegan stations on campus, at Morrison and at Okenshields ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/vegan-and-vegetarian-resources-campus)).

Morrison hosts the big themed dinners. Cornell Dining lists a take on Indigenous cuisine on Nov. 17, 2026, from 5 to 8:30 p.m., and a Thanksgiving Day Celebration of Gratitude Dinner on Nov. 26, 2026, from 11:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. ([Cornell Dining events](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining)).

Students rate it well. In the Sun's Sunset Survey for the Class of 2026, Morrison took 35.1% of votes for favorite dining spot, ahead of Risley and a spot the survey calls North, at 12.4% each ([The Sun](https://cornell-sun-data.github.io/sunset-survey-2026/at-cornell.html)). The survey drew 103 responses between April 20 and May 5, 2026, and the Sun says that sample does not represent the whole class ([The Sun](https://cornell-sun-data.github.io/sunset-survey-2026/about.html)). A 2022 Sun tip list also points new students to Morrison for Sunday brunch ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/07/01/what-i-wish-i-knew-about-campus-food-before-coming-to-cornell/)).

## What does a meal at Morrison cost?

A walk-in pays the door price, which includes sales tax. Students who pay with Big Red Bucks (BRBs, a dollar-for-dollar balance on your ID that carries no tax) pay less ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

| Meal | Door price, tax included | Price in BRBs |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $11.90 | $10.80 |
| Lunch, Monday to Saturday | $16.80 | $15.25 |
| Dinner | $20.80 | $18.85 |
| Sunday brunch | $18.55 | $16.85 |

Source: Cornell Dining meal plans and rates, 2026-27.

Plan holders pay none of this at the door, because a swipe covers the meal. The Unlimited plan, which first-years must take, costs $7,604 for 2026-27 and covers every dining room ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). For the break-even math on each plan, see [is the Cornell meal plan worth it](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).

## How do I get into Morrison Dining?

Everyone can eat in a residential dining room. Plan holders swipe, and others pay with BRBs, CornellCard or a credit card ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms)). The GET app can show a barcode that works in place of your ID at the door checker ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/contactless-payment)).

You do not choose. Unlimited is required for first-years, and Morrison is the closest room if you live on North Campus. Start with the [first-year guide](/start/first-year/) for the rest of the dining rules.

You need a plan to swipe in, or you pay the door price each time. ## What should I watch for at Morrison?

Lines are the usual complaint. After the Robert Purcell Marketplace Eatery closed in 2022, a student quoted by the Sun said reopening a dining hall would help balance crowding at Morrison and North Star ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/12/01/robert-purcell-marketplace-eaterys-permanent-closing-elicits-student-discussion-around-food-quality-and-dining-hall-preferences/)).

Variety is the other one. A 2025 Sun columnist wrote that Morrison and North Star menus felt repetitive for vegetarians ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead)). That is one writer's view. The plant-forward items Cornell lists at every location are in the [dining options page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets).

Service also changes during breaks. Morrison ran reduced service from Jan. 4 to 12, 2026, for deep cleaning, with door prices of $8 for breakfast and $16 for dinner ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/january-morrison-dining-reduced)). Fall meals end with brunch on Dec. 20, 2026, and Cornell Dining closes from Dec. 25 to Jan. 1 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining)). If you stay in Ithaca, read [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).

Dining Now posts each week's hours. For Nov. 11 to 17, 2026, Morrison serves breakfast from 7 a.m. on weekdays and from 8 a.m. on Saturday, lunch until 2:30 p.m., a late lunch until 4 p.m., Sunday brunch from 10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m., and dinner from 5 to 8:30 p.m. every day ([Dining Now](https://now.dining.cornell.edu/eatery/Morrison-Dining)). Check that page before an evening trip, since the hours are posted by the week.

Check today's hours on Dining Now, then decide which plan you need. If a religious diet drives your choice, compare Morrison with [104West!](/cornell/dining/104west-kosher-dining-cornell/). If a central location matters more, read about [Okenshields](/cornell/dining/okenshields-cornell-dining/).
