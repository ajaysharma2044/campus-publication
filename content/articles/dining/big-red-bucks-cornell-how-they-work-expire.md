A Big Red Buck (BRB) is $1 on your Cornell ID, spent dollar for dollar on food at Cornell Dining and carrying no sales tax ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). Your balance carries from fall to spring, not to the next year. Spend it by the earlier of the two end dates Cornell publishes.

## What are Big Red Bucks?

BRBs are a declining balance: each purchase lowers it. They are food only, and you can buy your own meals with them, not meals for a guest. Cornell's FAQ calls them non-refundable and non-transferable ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)).

Most undergraduate plans include BRBs each semester, and the amount depends on the plan. Cornell also sells BRBs by themselves through debit plans.

| Plan | BRBs per semester |
|---|---|
| Unlimited | $400 |
| Bear Traditional | $400 |
| Bear Choice | $500 |
| Bear Basic | $500 |
| Collegetown Meal Plan | $500, plus $436 in City Bucks |
| SFL Supplemental | $500 |
| Flex 10/500 | $500 |
| Just Bucks | $500, no meals |
| Off-Campus Value | $150 |
| House Affiliate | $100 |

Source: [Cornell Dining undergraduate meal plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), 2026-27. The graduate plan is different: $50 per semester buys $50 in BRBs, with no administrative fee and a 5% discount at the Atrium Cafe, Big Red Barn, Bear Necessities and the Veterinary Medicine Cafe ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans)).

## Where can I use Big Red Bucks?

BRBs work at Cornell Dining locations on campus and in many vending machines. They do not work at eateries that Cornell Dining does not run ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). That covers the ten residential dining rooms, the cafes and food courts, and the convenience stores. Nearly 300 vending machines have BRB readers, and healthy-choice machines stand in Martha Van Rensselaer Hall, Mann Library, Helen Newman and Teagle ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/vending-machines)).

You can pay in BRBs at a dining room instead of swiping. The BRB price is lower than the door price because BRBs carry no tax. Lunch from Monday to Saturday costs $15.25 in BRBs and $16.80 at the door, so BRBs save $1.55, or 9.2% ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). Cornell charges 8% New York sales tax on credit card, CornellCard and MealChoice purchases at Cornell Dining, and none on BRBs ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/january-morrison-dining-reduced)).

You can order ahead and prepay with BRBs through the GET app, at no fee, at spots including Crossings Cafe, Mann Cafe, Bear Necessities and Cafe Jennie ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/order-ahead)).

Some campus-area spots do not take BRBs. A March 2026 Sun columnist named Gimme! Coffee and Temple of Zeus as popular places that do not, which is the author's claim, so ask at the counter ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/we-want-more-big-red-bucks)). When Big Red Food Court opened on College Avenue in 2025, a University spokesperson said it was not eligible for BRBs ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/13/big-red-food-court-a-new-york-style-dining-experience-set-to-open-on-college-avenue/)). Off campus, the tool is City Bucks, covered in the [off-campus plan guide](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/).

## How do I add Big Red Bucks?

You can add BRBs at any time at get.cbord.com/cornell. The account uses your NetID with two-step login, and it can top up automatically when your balance runs low ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/manage-your-account)). Parents can add funds too, using the student's NetID and the name exactly as it appears on the ID card.

## When do Big Red Bucks expire?

The date depends on which Cornell page you read. The undergraduate plans page says BRBs last through May 27, 2027, while the deadlines page, the terms page and the graduate plans page say May 31, 2027, by noon on the terms page ([Cornell Dining deadlines](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms)). Go by the earlier date. The cost of spending early is zero, and the cost of waiting is the balance.

Two exceptions shorten the clock. Students who will not attend in spring lose fall BRBs at the end of the fall semester ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)). Those students must tell the Dining Contracts Office by Jan. 6, 2027, to avoid spring charges.

## What happens to Big Red Bucks over winter break?

Fall BRBs work at open cafes and stores through Dec. 24, 2026. Cornell Dining closes from Dec. 25, 2026, to Jan. 1, 2027, and from Jan. 4 to Jan. 19, 2027, fall BRBs work at the locations that are open ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining)). Your meal plan does not cover winter, so BRBs are what pays for food in the gap. If you stay in town, read [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).

## What if I lose my card or run out early?

Report a lost card to Cornell Dining at dining@cornell.edu or 607-255-8582, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks/city-bucks-faqs)). The barcode in the GET app replaces the physical ID at door checkers and cashiers ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/contactless-payment)).

Running out is the common complaint. The March 2026 Sun column argued the BRB amount has stayed flat while prices rose, and that it covers at most two Trillium visits a week. That is one student's view ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/we-want-more-big-red-bucks)). Count your spending after a month and top up only what you will spend.
