Winter in Ithaca is long, but students find plenty to do in it. Greek Peak, a ski area, hosts Cornell's skiing physical education course, and Cass Park Rink holds several public skating sessions a day. The campus fitness centers sell day passes, and a Saturday winter market runs indoors.

## What is the snowy season like?

January averages 16.6 inches of snow and a mean temperature of 22.8F, and the year averages 62.9 inches ([Northeast Regional Climate Center](https://www.nrcc.cornell.edu/wxstation/ithaca/normal.html)).

## Where can you ski or skate?

Greek Peak hosts Cornell's skiing physical education course ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/01/winter-sports-organizations-aim-to-engage-cornellians-in-cold-weather-activities)). For 2026-27, a one-day lift ticket costs $125, and a night ticket, good from 3:30 p.m. to close, costs $79. On Mondays starting in January, College Night tickets cost $29. The resort has no ticket windows, so buy online before you go ([Greek Peak](https://www.greekpeak.net/lift-tickets/)).

For skating, the City of Ithaca's Cass Park Rink, at 701 Taughannock Blvd., runs several public sessions a day in its 2026-27 season. Night sessions run Fridays from 7:45 to 9:45 p.m. and Saturdays from 8:30 to 10 p.m. Dec. 4 has no public sessions, and Jan. 8, Jan. 15 and Feb. 5 have no Friday night session. An adult session costs $5.25 for City and Town of Ithaca residents with proof of address, such as a lease, and $6.75 for everyone else. Skates rent for $3.75 a pair. Each session holds 120 skaters, so book a spot on Community Pass, up to four days ahead ([Cass Park](https://www.casspark.org/ice-rink.html)).

On campus, skating has deep roots. Beebe Lake hosted ice carnivals until the late 1940s, and Big Red hockey teams played on it until Lynah Rink was built in 1957 ([Cornellians magazine](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/cold-comforts-of-a-big-red-winter)).

## What can you do indoors on campus?

Cornell Fitness Centers sell student memberships. For 2026-27, the academic-year membership is $180, a fall membership is $115 through Jan. 15, 2027, a half-fall membership is $60 and a day pass is $10. You buy in the FusionGO app ([Cornell Fitness Centers](https://scl.cornell.edu/recreation/fitness-centers/student-memberships-day-passes)). Two semesters at the academic-year price work out to $90 each.

## Is there anything to do on a Saturday?

The Ithaca Farmers Market runs Saturday markets into December. Its last outdoor Saturday market of 2026 is Dec. 19, in its Steamboat Landing pavilion, 545 3rd St. That day is also the 29th International Rutabaga Curl: register on site from 10 to 11:30 a.m. for $5, rutabaga included ([Ithaca Farmers Market](https://ithacamarket.com/markets/rutabaga-curl/)).

In winter, the market moves indoors. The Winter Market meets at Triphammer Marketplace, 2255 North Triphammer Rd., on Saturdays from 10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. In 2026, its eighth winter there, it ran Jan. 3 to March 28 ([Ithaca Farmers Market](https://ithacamarket.com/winter-market)).

## What should you wear?

Dress for wind chill: at 0F with a 15 mph wind it feels like -19F ([National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-wind-chill-chart)), and our guide to [what to wear for an Ithaca winter](/cornell/daily-life/what-to-wear-ithaca-winter-cornell/) covers the coat, boots and gloves.

```reader "If you're an international student"
Cornell's international office says down coats are warmest, wool is next and leather is not warm enough. Mittens beat gloves, and insulated hiking boots with thick treaded rubber soles work best ([Cornell Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/student-resources)). If boots and a coat are a stretch, the Access Fund covers up to $100 for boots or $150 for a coat, hat and gloves, and free clothing closets exist at Catholic Charities of Tompkins/Tioga and the Women's Opportunity Center ([Graduate School tips](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-winter-clothing-resources)). Our [start page for international students](/start/international/) covers the rest of arrival.
```

## How do you get there without a car?

After first year, TCAT is free on weekday evenings and all weekend, which covers most outings ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). For a trip out of town, Ithaca Carshare's student plan costs $30 a year plus the hourly rate ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters)); our [Carshare guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/) breaks down the plans. The [car-free guide for upper-years](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-upper-years-no-car/) covers more backups, and winter riding is in our [winter biking guide](/cornell/getting-around/biking-in-ithaca-winter-cornell/).
