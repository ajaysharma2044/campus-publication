Students from other states or countries who attend school in New York are normally not New York residents, so they do not need to exchange their license ([New York State DMV](https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/exchange-out-of-state-driver-license)). A car registered in New York needs New York insurance.

```glance
- Out-of-state students normally keep their home license and need no New York exchange.
- Students from other countries are also normally non-residents, so they keep their home license.
- A New York-registered car needs New York insurance.
- Ithaca Carshare wants a full license held 2+ years.
```

## Do Cornell students need a New York license?

No, if New York is not your home. The state DMV treats students who come to attend school as non-residents, and they can keep the license from their home state ([New York State DMV](https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/exchange-out-of-state-driver-license)). The DMV page says a student is "normally" a non-resident, so ask the DMV if you work in New York, claim it as your home or register a car here.

The state's own rules, not Cornell's, decide this. A student who makes New York home has 30 days from becoming a resident to exchange the old license, and living in the state for 90 days is what the DMV calls presumptive evidence of residency ([New York State DMV](https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/exchange-out-of-state-driver-license)). The exchange happens in person at a DMV office. You surrender the out-of-state license, which must carry a photo, be valid or expired for less than 24 months and have been issued at least 6 months before you apply. You pass a vision test at the office or bring a completed Eye Test Report (form MV-619), and the DMV's online pre-screening tool tells you which forms and documents to bring. You leave with a temporary non-photo document, and the card arrives by mail.

You pay the DMV's fee for a first New York license: $64.25 to $67.50 for a Class D license at age 21 or older, and $76.75 to $80.00 between 18½ and 21 ([New York State DMV](https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/fees-refunds)). Those are the DMV's ranges for counties outside the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District, which does not include Tompkins; the district's own ranges are higher ($73.25 to $77.50 and $85.75 to $90.00). The Ithaca office is the Tompkins County DMV at 301 Third Street, open Monday to Friday from 8:30 am to 12:45 pm and 2:00 pm to 4:30 pm; permit tests run 8:30 am to 12:00 pm and 2:00 pm to 3:30 pm, road tests 8:00 am to 3:00 pm, and the phone is (607) 273-7187 ([Tompkins County](https://tompkinscountyny.gov/dmv)).

## Can international students drive on their home license?

Yes. You can drive in New York State with a valid driver license from another country, and you need a New York license only if you become a New York State resident ([New York State DMV](https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/drivers-from-other-countries)). A student who does become a resident must pass a written test, complete the five-hour pre-licensing course and pass a road test, and the examiner takes the foreign license when you pass. A license printed in a language other than English needs an International Driving Permit or a certified translation at the road test, and the translation must come from a consulate, the U.S. Department of State or another official government agency.

## Do you need a license for Ithaca Carshare?

Yes, and its rules are stricter than the DMV's. Ithaca Carshare requires members to be at least 18 and to have held a full, unrestricted license for 2 or more years with no suspension in the past 3 years ([Ithaca Carshare](https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility)). Members under 21 need a spotless record and their own insurance policy. A foreign license needs an official driving record, and an International Driving Permit does not count as one. See the [Carshare guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-carshare-cornell-students-cost/) for costs.

## Which ID do you need for other tasks?

A driver's license is not the only accepted ID. Alternatives Federal Credit Union accepts a state driver's license, learner's permit, non-driver ID or U.S. passport when you open an account ([Alternatives](https://www.alternatives.org/accounts/open-an-account/)). The [bank guide](/cornell/money/cornell-student-bank-account-best-bank-ithaca-students/) lists the other banks. A City of Ithaca residential parking permit accepts a driver's license with a valid address as proof of residency, though a lease or a utility bill also works ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)).

## Should you bring a car?

If you do, register its plate with Cornell Transportation Services through MyParking.Cornell.edu, including for short-term parking ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus)). The [car guide](/getting-around/cornell-parking-guide-campus-collegetown-ithaca/) and the [cost guide](/tools/should-i-bring-a-car-cornell-ithaca-cost/) help you decide, and the [parking permit guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-student-parking-permit-cost-rules/) covers lots and rates.
