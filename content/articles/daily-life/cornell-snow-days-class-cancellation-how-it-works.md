```glance
- Cornell cancels classes only when it closes the university, and snow closures are rare.
- When Cornell closes, classes and exams are canceled and no extra work is due that day.
- Residence halls stay open during a closure, while libraries and retail close.
- Check emergency.cornell.edu or call 607-255-3377 for status.
```

Cornell does cancel classes for snow, but only when the whole university closes. Under Cornell's inclement weather policy, a closure cancels classes, and students are not expected to attend virtually or finish extra coursework that day ([Cornell Human Resources, Policy 8.2](https://hr.cornell.edu/about/employment-policy-practice/employment-policies/inclement-weather)).

## Who decides, and how do you find out?

The university decides, not individual professors. Status is posted at emergency.cornell.edu, and you can call 607-255-3377 ([Cornell Emergency Management](https://emergency.cornell.edu/?p=5006)). In March 2023, the reopening was announced through CornellALERT ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/13/breaking-news-university-to-close-tuesday-due-to-winter-storm/)).

If the university has not announced a closure, classes are on.

## What does a closure actually close?

It closes classes, exams and events, but not your room. In January 2026, a winter storm closed the Ithaca and AgriTech campuses from noon on Sunday, Jan. 25, with a reopening planned for noon on Monday, Jan. 26. Libraries, the Cornell Store and retail closed, and the residence halls stayed open ([Cornell Emergency Management](https://emergency.cornell.edu/?p=5006)). The governor declared a state of emergency, and some dining locations closed Monday ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/01/cornell-announces-ithaca-and-agritech-campuses-will-reopen-at-noon-on-monday)). The city's TCAT service started late; the details are in our [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/).

The March 2023 closure ran from 3 a.m. on March 14 to 3 a.m. on March 15. The forecast was 9 to 18 inches, and residence halls stayed open while libraries and retail closed (Cornell Daily Sun, March 2023 story above).

## How often does Cornell close for snow?

Rarely, but more than it once did. These closures appear in the records behind this page.

Counting each entry above, that makes six closures from 2017 to 2026, or about one every one and a half years (nine years divided by six).

## Do you make up the work?

Under the current policy, no work is due for the day. In 2020, the university had a different approach that allowed required online makeups, announced by Executive Vice President Joanne DeStefano ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2020/12/02/snow-days-no-more-students-not-warming-to-cornells-new-policy-for-the-coldest-days/)). The HR policy page is undated, so confirm with your instructor whether a makeup applies to your course.

## What if the storm hits on a travel day?

Closures do not stop buses or flights from being canceled. Break-travel storms and what to do are in our [winter storms guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-storms-travel-driving-ithaca/). For the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, which falls inside the break from [Nov. 25 to 29, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), see [when to book Thanksgiving buses](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-thanksgiving-break-bus-tickets-when-to-book/).

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your Cornell ID gets you on TCAT for free, and halls stay open during closures, so a snow day means a free day in your building, not an empty campus. Move your car if the city or campus calls an overnight rule: our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) has the details, and the [first-year guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-first-year/) covers getting around.
```

```reader "If you commute from off campus"
A closure means classes are canceled, not that your street is plowed. The city plows small residential streets last ([City of Ithaca plowing guide](https://cityofithacany.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12559)), so leave extra time.
```
