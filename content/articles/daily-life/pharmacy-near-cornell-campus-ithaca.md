The nearest pharmacy is on campus: the [Cornell Health pharmacy](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy), on Level 4 just inside the Ho Plaza entrance at 110 Ho Plaza. Off campus, the closest chains and Wegmans are a short bus or car ride away. Among the pharmacies we checked, none keeps its counter open past 9 PM, even on weekdays, so "open late" in Ithaca mostly means planning ahead, or using the 24/7 vending machine at Cornell Health for basic over-the-counter items.

## Which one is open late

- **Latest pharmacy counter on weekdays:** Wegmans, until 9 PM. The [store itself](https://www.wegmans.com/stores/ithaca-ny) is open 6 AM to midnight every day, which helps for cold medicine, bandages and snacks after the pharmacy closes, but you cannot pick up a prescription once the counter shuts.
- **Late Saturday:** Walgreens and Wegmans pharmacies both close at 6 PM. Cornell Health closes at 4:30 PM.
- **Sunday:** Cornell Health is closed. Walgreens is open until 5 PM and Wegmans until 4 PM.
- **Any hour, no prescription:** the [vending machine](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy/vending) just inside the Ho Plaza entrance is open 24/7. It sells non-prescription medicines and supplies such as pain relievers, allergy medication, bandages and emergency contraception, and gives out free naloxone spray and fentanyl and xylazine test strips. Products may change.

If you feel sick at night and need a prescription, [Cornell Health's hours page](https://health.cornell.edu/get-care/hours) points to a 24/7 phone line at 607-255-5155 for after-hours care options.

## The Cornell Health pharmacy

It is open to the public, not only students: Cornell's page says [students, faculty, staff and visitors](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy) can use it. Outside prescriptions have a rule, though. [Prescriptions from non-Cornell providers within New York State must be e-scribed to the pharmacy](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy), which appears as "Cornell - zip code 14853" in Surescripts. Out-of-state prescriptions can be dropped off, phoned in, faxed to 607-254-5042 or e-scribed.

- **Insurance:** it accepts the Cornell Student Health Plan, Aetna, Caremark (most plans), Express Scripts, Optum and several others. Cornell asks that your pharmacy insurance card be uploaded in myCornellHealth; you can also bring a card or photo. If your plan is not accepted, you can pay in full and submit the receipt for reimbursement.
- **Student Health Plan copays:** for a 30-day supply, [$12 generic, $40 preferred brand, $60 non-preferred brand](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy).
- **Payment:** students can have charges billed to the Bursar; cards, Apple Pay and personal checks also work.
- **Parking:** if you drive, there are 10 paid spaces reserved for Cornell Health patients, at $3 an hour plus a $0.35 transaction fee.
- **Mail-order medications:** students can have specialty drugs shipped to 110 Ho Plaza, Level 4. The pharmacy messages you through myCornellHealth when one arrives, and you pick it up at Window 3 with ID.
- **Out of stock:** the pharmacy can usually order a medication to arrive the next day.

## Getting there without a car

The Cornell Health pharmacy is at Ho Plaza itself, so from central campus it is a walk and needs no bus. For Wegmans (500 South Meadow St), Walgreens (Pine Tree Rd and S Meadow St) you need a bus or a ride. No TCAT route has been verified for this page: TCAT's route and timetable pages would not load while we researched it, so we are not naming route numbers or frequencies. Enter the pharmacy address in the TCAT trip planner or a transit app and check the current schedule, because routes change each semester. Our [getting around guide](/getting-around/) covers transit in general. If you are ill, a rideshare or a friend with a car is worth the cost over a bus transfer.

## Transferring a prescription from home

1. **Ask your home pharmacy to transfer it, or have your prescriber send it.** Give the new pharmacy your medication name, prescriber and old pharmacy's phone number. A prescriber in New York must e-scribe to Cornell Health; an out-of-state one can phone, fax or e-scribe (see above).
2. **Check that you have refills left.** If you are out, a transfer will not help and you need a new prescription from your prescriber. When you need a renewal at Cornell Health, call 607-255-5155 at least 3 business days before your last dose.
3. **ADHD medication is harder.** Cornell's page says [ADHD medications require thorough diagnosis documentation](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy). Start weeks before you run out.
4. **Out-of-state prescriptions:** Cornell Health lists a [fax line, 607-254-5042](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy), for these.

Moving a prescription out of Cornell Health works the other way. You need refills left, and you request the transfer through myCornellHealth, by phone or at the pharmacy. [All remaining refills move to the new pharmacy and the Cornell Health prescription becomes void](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy). Renewals are not accepted through myCornellHealth; call 607-255-5155. The page also says New York's transfer law has temporarily changed without giving details, so confirm the current rule with the pharmacy.

## If you are...

- **A student on campus with a cold at 10 PM:** use the 24/7 vending machine, or Wegmans for anything else after the pharmacy closes.
- **A student with a monthly prescription:** have your prescriber e-scribe it to Cornell Health before you arrive, and plan pickups for weekdays before 5 PM.
- **A parent visiting:** Wegmans is the easiest to find with a car and has the longest weekday hours.
- **Someone needing medication on a Sunday:** Walgreens until 5 PM, or Wegmans until 4 PM.

## FAQ

**Is there a 24-hour pharmacy in Ithaca?** Among the pharmacies we checked, none. The nearest thing is Cornell Health's 24/7 vending machine for non-prescription items.

**Can non-students use the Cornell Health pharmacy?** Yes, [it is open to the public](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy).

**What if I run out of a medication over break?** Hours around holidays are not on the pages we read, so call 607-255-6976 first. See also [daily life in Ithaca](/daily-life/).
