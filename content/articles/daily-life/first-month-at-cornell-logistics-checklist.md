```glance
- Waive the student health plan by July 31 if your own insurance qualifies, or Cornell bills you for it.
```

At 8 a.m. on Monday, Aug. 17, 2026, orientation leaders with red pom poms started cheering cars onto North Campus. By evening, 3,681 freshmen had moved in. Football players unloaded trunks. One student had driven 12 hours from North Carolina with his father. Midway through the day, the moving team's record for one student's boxes was five ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/08/this-is-the-place-i-want-to-be-incoming-freshmen-make-the-move-to-campus)).

Move-in looks like the start. It is closer to the middle. By the time those boxes went up the stairs, each student had already faced a health deadline, an insurance deadline and a tuition bill. Still ahead were a swim test, a course-change deadline, a textbook opt-out and the first trip home.

The first month at Cornell is a relay of deadlines run by separate offices. Cornell Health, the Bursar, the Registrar, Cornell Dining, The Cornell Store and Transportation Services each own a piece, and each publishes only its own deadlines. Cornell's own [New Student To Do List](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-do-list) has 29 items, from "Activate Two-Step Login" to "Gorge Safety Video." On one clock, they run from the spring the list opens to the first trip home in October.

The stakes are real money and real holds. Take a first-year whose family insurance qualifies but who misses the waiver, then keeps a textbook program worth less than its fee. That student adds $2,000 to the fall bill: the $1,705 fall health plan charge plus the $295 textbook fee ([The Cornell Store](https://www.cornellstore.com/about-textbooks)). A student who skips the health forms loses the ability to change classes. Cornell's 2026 rules called for withdrawing students still out of compliance on Sept. 10 ([Cornell Health, fall 2026](http://web.archive.org/web/20260815222031/https://health.cornell.edu/getcare/healthrequirements/undergrad-grad-prof)). Most of the tasks are free and take minutes. The trouble is doing them in the wrong order.

Class dates below are from the fall 2027 calendar, which Cornell has posted ([Cornell Registrar, 2027-28](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar/2027-2028)). Deadlines Cornell sets each spring, such as health forms and move-in, are the 2026 dates. They show the pattern a fall 2027 student can expect.

## Spring: the To Do List opens

The list goes live in late March. In the 2026 cycle it opened on March 26, after admitted students accepted their offers and got a Cornell NetID, the username for every Cornell system ([New Student Resource Hub](https://scl.cornell.edu/new-student-resource-hub)). Housing applications, health requirements and placement exams follow in late spring. Advising and fall pre-enrollment come in July. Move-in and orientation come in August, on dates that change each year.

The first task unlocks the others. Activate your NetID and Two-Step Login, Cornell's second sign-in check through your phone. Cornell Health's portal will not work without both. It also needs two working days after activation to receive your record from the Registrar ([Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/getcare/healthrequirements/undergrad-grad-prof)). Do this the day the NetID arrives.

Next comes the ID card photo. Upload it through the To Do List well before move-in. The Registrar has no temporary ID cards, and a lost card costs $40 to replace ([Cornell Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/services/id-cards)). Your card is also your bus pass and your meal card, so a late photo slows down both.

## June: health forms, and the hold that follows

Cornell Health's requirements are the first hard deadline. New York State and Cornell require proof of the measles, mumps and rubella shots, plus a meningococcal vaccine or a signed waiver. Every new student also completes an online tuberculosis risk screen. Students the screen flags must submit a blood test. Cornell accepts only the QuantiFERON-TB Gold or T-SPOT test, not a skin test (Cornell Health).

In the 2026 cycle, first-years had until June 10 and transfers until July 23. Then the penalties stacked up (Cornell Health, fall 2026):

- **July 8, 2026:** a hold on any change to class enrollment
- **Aug. 6, 2026:** a second hold on add/drop
- **Sept. 10, 2026:** withdrawal from the university for the fall term

Records must be in English, from a doctor, school or the military. If you can't get shots at home, contact Cornell Health's Requirements Office before the deadline and make a plan to get them on campus. The charge at Cornell Health runs from $50 to $1,000, depending on your insurance. Students on Cornell's own health plan pay up to $50 for the required shots and TB test (Cornell Health).

Cornell says the process exists to comply with state and federal laws and public health guidelines, and it enforces the rule through enrollment (Cornell Health). A hold means you can't add the seminar you wanted on the day add/drop opens. That is why the June deadline matters even to a student who plans to finish the forms in August.

## July: insurance, the bill and your classes

**The health insurance decision.** Every new student is enrolled in Cornell's Student Health Plan (SHP) and billed for it, unless a waiver for other insurance is approved by the deadline. For 2026-27, SHP costs $4,092 for 12 months. A new fall student is billed $1,705 on the fall bill for five months of coverage, then $2,046 in spring ([Cornell Student Health Benefits](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27)). New fall students had until July 31, 2026, to get a waiver approved. A waiver must be approved again every semester. After the deadline, waivers lock, and the only way out is a prorated refund request ([waiver deadlines](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/node/179)). Your plan must meet Cornell's criteria, and submitting a waiver does not guarantee approval.

**The first bill.** The Bursar posted the fall 2026 bill on July 10 and set it due Aug. 7. Cornell bills on the second Friday of each month, with payment due on the seventh of the next month. A late payment brings finance charges and enrollment holds ([Cornell Bursar](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/congratulations-your-acceptance-to-cornell-university)). Two other Bursar tasks sit on the To Do List:

- **The Financial Responsibility Agreement.** You must sign it online. Without it, you can't enroll.
- **Authorized payers.** If a parent pays, invite them through CASHNet, Cornell's billing site. The student stays responsible for the account.

The Cornell Installment Plan splits a semester into monthly payments for a $125 enrollment fee each semester. The fall plan runs Aug. 1 to Dec. 1 (Cornell Bursar). Students on federal aid can also file a Title IV authorization at least two weeks before classes. Without it, federal aid pays only tuition, certain fees, housing and dining.

**Direct deposit.** Set it up before you need it. When the Bursar generates a refund, you have 24 hours to enroll in direct deposit before it mails a check. Direct deposit arrives in 2 to 3 business days, and a check takes 7 to 10 business days after printing ([Cornell Bursar refunds](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/deposit-refunds)). You need a U.S. checking or savings account. Ithaca's banks and their fees are compared in the [student bank account guide](/cornell/money/cornell-student-bank-account-best-bank-ithaca-students/).

**Courses.** July is advising and pre-enrollment (New Student Resource Hub). The To Do List also carries "Assigned College Tasks," which differ by college.

The textbook fee also shows up in July. Every undergraduate is enrolled in the Cornell Academic Materials Program (CAMP), which charges that flat fee each semester for required course materials. In 2026, students got an email with an opt-out link on July 2, and books appeared in Canvas around Aug. 21 (The Cornell Store). Wait to decide: the deadline falls in September, after you see your books.

## Early August: the arrival plan and the car question

Pick a move-in time slot in the Housing and Dining Portal by Aug. 1. In 2025, Cornell required every student to book a slot by that date. The 30-minute slot is your arrival time at check-in, not your unloading time ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus)). Where to unload and park, building by building, is in the [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/).

Then decide about a car. Cornell's own parking page encourages students to walk, bike or ride TCAT, the county bus system, which is free to first-year students. A resident permit costs $752.98 a year. The North Campus permit is "extremely limited" and capped ([Cornell Parking for Students](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). For most first-years the math ends there. The bus costs nothing, and the permit costs as much as about 502 cash bus fares at $1.50 each.

## Move-in day

In 2026, first-years moved in on Aug. 17 and transfers on Aug. 18. Family Orientation ran the same day as transfer move-in. New Student Orientation ran Aug. 18 through 23 ([New Student Orientation](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation)). The 2027 dates are not yet announced. In 2026, move-in fell on the Monday one week before classes began.

The 2026 move-in had one change from earlier years. Students checked in and collected their keys on campus. In earlier years, all students had been sent to check in at The Shops at Ithaca Mall first. Each car also got a card that told volunteers where to direct it (The Cornell Daily Sun). One parent told the Sun the parking was planned "down to a T."

**Your ID card.** If yours isn't ready at check-in, the Registrar issues cards at 245 Day Hall, Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. and 2 to 4 p.m. (Cornell Registrar). Cornell requires you to carry it at all times.

**Your mailing address.** North Campus mail goes through the Robert Purcell Community Center. Address it to your name and NetID at 107 Robert Purcell Community Center, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853. For food and grocery apps, give your hall's street address instead ([Toni Morrison Hall page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/toni-morrison-hall)). Hall pages differ slightly on the format, so check your own hall's page before you order anything.

**Your meal plan.** It starts on its own. Fall 2026 meals began with lunch on move-in day ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)). First-year undergraduates must take the Unlimited plan, at $3,802 per semester for 2026-27. It includes $400 in Big Red Bucks, a declining balance for cafes and convenience stores, plus 8 guest meals a semester ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Cornell has required a meal plan for first- and second-year students since the class that entered in summer 2021. First-years can't reduce or cancel the plan. They can add Big Red Bucks at any time.

**Your prescriptions.** Move them before you run out. The Cornell Health pharmacy is at 110 Ho Plaza, Level 4. A prescription from a doctor elsewhere in New York State must be sent electronically to "Cornell - zip code 14853." An out-of-state prescription can also be phoned, faxed or dropped off. Students can bill it to their Bursar account ([Cornell Health pharmacy](https://health.cornell.edu/services/pharmacy)). Hours and off-campus options are in the [pharmacy guide](/health-safety/pharmacy-near-cornell-campus-ithaca/).

## Orientation week: the swim test and the online modules

New Student Orientation is the busiest week of the month. Required small-group meetings with your Orientation Leader sit beside college sessions and optional events (New Student Orientation).

**The swim test.** Cornell requires every undergraduate to pass a water competency test to graduate. The test asks you to:

- jump or step feet-first into the deep end
- float or tread water for one minute
- turn around in a full circle
- swim 25 yards with any stroke and no time limit
- climb out of the pool

The test is free during orientation in August and January, at the Teagle Hall or Helen Newman Hall pools. Miss it in your first year and at later orientations, and it costs a $100 fee. A student who can't pass takes PE 1100, Beginning Swimming ([Cornell Physical Education](https://scl.cornell.edu/pe/pe/swim-requirement)).

The test is older than most of campus. Colonel Frank Barton started it for men in fall 1905. He argued that a soldier who could not swim was useless to his unit, and military drill was then required of all male students. Dorothy Bateman started the women's test in 1920. The early women's test, in the Sage Hall pool, ended with an instruction to "come up smiling" ([Cornell swim history](https://scl.cornell.edu/pe/pe/swim-requirement/history-swim-requirement)). The current version replaced a 75-yard swim on Nov. 10, 2023, and aims at water safety rather than swimming skill (Cornell Physical Education).

**The online modules.** Several To Do List items are short online courses or videos: alcohol and other drug education, Title IX policies, the Student Code of Conduct, "The Cheating Video" and the "Gorge Safety Video" (New Student To Do List). Finish them before orientation week, when you will have less free time.

**Add/drop opens.** For fall 2027, first-years can start changing their schedules on Aug. 20 at 9 a.m., after every other class year. Classes begin Monday, Aug. 23, 2027 (Cornell Registrar, 2027-28). Have a backup course ready before 9 a.m.

## Week one of classes: the free bus

Your Cornell ID is your bus pass. As a new student, you get unlimited TCAT rides from fall registration to Aug. 31 of the next year. Swipe the card at the farebox. If it is rejected, the cash fare is $1.50. The free ride period has "no extensions for any reason" ([Cornell bus passes](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)), so a first-year never needs to buy one. Routes, hours and what changes after first year are in the [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/).

The free pass has a protest in its history. In 2005, students fought the clearing of Redbud Woods, a stand of trees on West Campus, for a parking lot. The agreement that settled the University Avenue lot gave free bus passes to first-years, then to sophomores too. In April 2007, Cornell announced that from that August only new students would get the free pass. Continuing students got free rides after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend, plus a discounted pass for $200 a year. TCAT then charged $495 for a similar annual pass ([The Cornell Daily Sun, 2007](https://cornellsun.com/2007/04/25/university-changes-free-bus-pass-policy/)). Stephen Golding, then Cornell's executive vice president for finance and administration, told the Sun that surveys showed clear support for keeping a free first-year pass. Most respondents wanted the central administration to pay for it.

That 2007 deal still sets the rules. The discounted pass costs the same $200 it cost 19 years ago (Cornell bus passes).

The system has strained since. Early in 2024, a farebox problem let every student ride free for months. When Cornell updated its rider list on Oct. 16, 2024, swipes from upper-year students started failing without warning. TCAT general manager Matthew Rosenbloom-Jones pointed to Cornell's magnetic-stripe IDs and recommended an app-based pass like Ithaca College's ([The Cornell Daily Sun, 2024](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free)). In March 2026, the Student Assembly unanimously urged Cornell to give TCAT enough money to cover free passes for all undergraduates and graduate workers. The resolution says Cornell pays 15 percent of TCAT's budget while 70 percent of TCAT's service goes to Cornell. The president returned it on April 24, 2026 ([Student Assembly Resolution 69](https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026)).

Week one is also when the rest of daily life gets set up. A gym membership is optional. Cornell Fitness Centers charged $180 for the 2026-27 academic year or $115 for fall, bought through the FusionGO app ([Cornell Recreational Services](https://scl.cornell.edu/recreation/fitness-centers/student-memberships-day-passes)).

## Weeks two to four: the deadlines that cost money

The quiet weeks after orientation hold the deadlines that are easiest to miss.

**The last day to add.** For fall 2027, it is Sept. 7 for regular classes and first-year writing seminars. Labor Day, Sept. 6, 2027, has no classes. The last day to drop a class or change its grading option is Oct. 18 (Cornell Registrar, 2027-28). After the add deadline, your schedule is set except for drops and grading changes.

**The CAMP opt-out.** In 2026, the deadline was Sept. 11 ([CAMP FAQ](https://academicmaterials.cornell.edu/frequently-asked-questions)). Your books appear in each course's Canvas page under "Course Materials," and that is where the "Opt-Out" button sits. Once you opt out, the fee comes off your Bursar account in 5 to 7 business days. Return any print books to The Cornell Store in like-new condition within 14 days, or pay full price. Late opt-outs need a valid academic reason. Opt back in after the deadline and you pay the full fee again. Financial aid includes the fee in your cost of attendance (The Cornell Store).

When CAMP launched in fall 2022, it cost $225 a semester. In its first fall, a little over 10,000 of almost 15,000 eligible students stayed in all term. Cornell estimated average savings of about $145 per student per semester ([Cornell Student and Campus Life, 2023](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/students-see-savings-cornell-academic-materials-program)). The fee has since risen $70 ($295 minus $225), or 31 percent. Add up the price of your required books before the deadline. If they cost less than the fee, opting out saves money. Used and cheaper options are in the [textbook guide](/cornell/money/cornell-textbooks-cheap-where-to-buy-used-ithaca/).

**The meal plan deadline is not yours.** Cornell Dining's fall deadline to reduce or cancel a plan was Sept. 9, 2026, but first-years can do neither (Cornell Dining terms).

**The health deadline's last step.** If you entered "in progress" with a plan to get shots on campus, keep that appointment before the September withdrawal date. Missing it puts the hold back.

## Booking the first trips home

The first breaks come fast. Fall break is Oct. 9 through 12, 2027, and Thanksgiving break is Nov. 24 through 28, 2027 (Cornell Registrar, 2027-28). Book both early, before the peak travel days.

Cornell's own bus to New York City, Campus-to-Campus or C2C, moved to a new reservation system on Nov. 4, 2026. Every rider needs a new account, even past riders ([Cornell Transportation announcements](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/tds-announcements)). The downtown Ithaca stop for OurBus moved on Nov. 9, 2026, to 235 S. Cayuga St., beside the South Cayuga parking garage ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/)). Fares, routes, airports and break dates for a first-year are all in the [first-year transportation guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-first-year/).

One more October task: if you can't go home for winter break, apply to stay. The application opens Oct. 1 and closes Dec. 1. In buildings that stay open, a week before or after the break costs $125, and the whole break costs $500. Students outside those buildings can apply only for the whole break. You must be returning to campus housing in spring ([Cornell winter break housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)).

## If your first month looks different

```reader "If you're an international student"
Two tasks are yours alone. Activate your F-1 or J-1 status online with Cornell International Services no later than 15 days after the start date on your I-20 or DS-2019, and no more than 30 days before your program begins. Miss it and you can lose your immigration status. Bring your I-20 or DS-2019, SEVIS fee receipt, I-94 record, local address and NetID ([status activation](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/status-activation-students)). Prepare, the free international orientation, ran online on July 28 and in person Aug. 14 to 16 in 2026 ([Cornell events](https://events.cornell.edu/event/prepare-international-undergraduate-orientation-fa26)). You don't need a Social Security number to open a bank account ([Cornell International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/getting-started/your-first-week-0)).
```

```reader "If you're starting in spring 2027"
Your clock runs on January. Cornell Health's spring forms open Nov. 17 and are due Dec. 18, 2026, with holds from Dec. 22 and withdrawal on Feb. 3, 2027 (Cornell Health). Your health plan waiver is due Jan. 21, 2027 (waiver deadlines). First-year add/drop opens Jan. 22 at 9 a.m., classes start Jan. 25, and the last day to add regular classes is Feb. 8 ([Cornell Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)). Your free TCAT runs Jan. 1 to Dec. 31, 2027, not to August. Take the swim test at January orientation.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer renting off campus"
Your list adds a lease, utilities and a renter's move-in. Transfers had a later health deadline in 2026, July 23. Transfers entering since fall 2025 must also pass the swim test and take PE (Cornell Physical Education).
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
You can see and pay the bill only after your student invites you in CASHNet, so ask before the July bill posts. Plan to stay through Family Orientation: in 2026 it ended with New Student Convocation at 6 p.m. ([Cornell families page](https://scl.cornell.edu/node/49486)). Three of the charges above are avoidable: the health plan, if your own coverage truly meets Cornell's criteria; the CAMP fee, if your books cost less; and the late swim test fee. ## The bigger picture
```

The first month is hard to see whole because it isn't run by one office. Each piece has its own owner and its own history. The swim test answers to a faculty committee and a 1905 rule (Cornell swim history). The free bus pass answers to a 2005 protest and a 2007 budget decision (The Cornell Daily Sun, 2007). The textbook fee answers to a program launched in 2022 (Cornell Student and Campus Life, 2023). The health forms answer to state law. The To Do List is Cornell's attempt to put them in one place, and it works best for students who read it as a calendar, not a checklist.

Three questions will shape the next class's first month. Will Cornell answer the Student Assembly's call for free bus passes for every student, and move IDs to the app-based system TCAT has asked for? Will the CAMP fee keep rising, after a 31 percent climb since 2022? And when will Cornell post 2027 dates for health forms, insurance waivers and move-in? Watch Cornell Health's page in late spring and the Tatkon Center's hub for move-in. The [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) collects the next guides to read once the first month is done.
