Register to vote at the address where you live in Ithaca, because that address decides which local offices you vote for. New York takes registrations online, by mail or in person, and your application must reach a board of elections 10 days before an election for you to vote in it. Once you are registered, the state's voter lookup shows your polling place.

```glance
- Your Ithaca address, not your Cornell mailing address, decides your local ward and council.
- Register online, by mail or in person; applications must reach a board of elections 10 days before an election.
- Report a change of address at least 15 days before an election.
- The state's voter lookup shows your polling place once you are registered.
```

## Why does your Ithaca address matter?

An "Ithaca, NY 14850" mailing address is shared by the City and the towns and villages around it. The Town of Ithaca counts 9 municipalities on its [address verification page](https://townithacany.gov/address-verification/) and 12 in its [welcome guide](https://townithacany.gov/welcome-new-resident-guide/). The Town wraps around the City like a donut. Two students with the same ZIP code can live under different local governments. The [City versus Town guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) shows where your apartment sits. The Town's address verification tool checks a street address.

## What do local votes decide for students?

Ithaca's Common Council makes rules that reach student renters directly. In 2024 it voted 8-2 to opt Ithaca into Good Cause renter protections ([WAMC](https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections)). In 2022 it voted unanimously to require 120 days' notice before lease renewals ([WSKG](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/)).

## When do you have to register?

New York lets you register up to 10 days before an election to vote in that election ([NYS Board of Elections FAQ](https://elections.ny.gov/frequently-asked-questions)). For the November 3, 2026 general election, mailed and in-person applications had to reach a board of elections by October 24, 2026. The state posts each election's dates on its [deadlines page](https://elections.ny.gov/registration-and-voting-deadlines). Two other clocks matter. A change of address must reach the county board at least 15 days before an election to count for it. A change of party enrollment must arrive by February 14 to vote in that year's primary. You must also have been a resident of New York and of the county for at least 30 days before the election, so register soon after you move in.

## How do you register?

The state offers three routes ([NYS Board of Elections](https://elections.ny.gov/frequently-asked-questions)). Register online through the [state portal](https://elections.ny.gov/register-vote). Register in person at the county board, a DMV office or a state agency registration center. Or mail the state voter registration form to your county board. The online route through MY DMV needs a New York driver's license or non-driver ID ([Tompkins County Board of Elections](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Board-of-Elections/Voter-Information-and-Registrations)). In Tompkins County the paper form, signed and dated, goes by mail or in person to the Board of Elections at 214 W. State St., Ithaca, NY 14850. The office mails a form to anyone who calls 607-274-5521. It does not accept registrations by email or fax. It is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, at (607) 274-5522 ([Tompkins County Board of Elections](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Board-of-Elections)).

To register you must be a US citizen, 18 by the election (you can pre-register at 16 or 17), and not claim the right to vote anywhere else. Being a student does not by itself give you or cost you a voting residence under New York Election Law section 5-104. A board of elections weighs your stated intent together with your conduct and circumstances ([New York State Senate](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ELN/5-104)). A move to a new county means registering in that county. If you move within Tompkins County, send a new registration form with the new address. The county then mails a card with your new polling place ([Tompkins County FAQ](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Board-of-Elections/Frequently-Asked-Questions)).

## How do you find your polling place?

Use the state's [voter lookup](https://voterlookup.elections.ny.gov/). Enter your name, date of birth, county and ZIP code. It returns your poll site for early voting and Election Day, your registration and a mail ballot tracker. It works only once you are registered. Tompkins County links an address lookup map from its [voter information page](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Board-of-Elections/Voter-Information-and-Registrations); the map shows the polling place, district and elected representatives for an address. The county also mails registered voters a postcard around May with their polling place and election district number. First-time voters who registered by mail without verified ID may be asked for ID at the polls. Accepted ID includes a current photo ID or a current utility bill with your name and address ([Tompkins County FAQ](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Board-of-Elections/Frequently-Asked-Questions)). Early voting runs for nine days starting the tenth day before Election Day ([NYS Board of Elections FAQ](https://elections.ny.gov/frequently-asked-questions)). In 2026 it ran October 24 through November 1. Polls on November 3 were open 6 am to 9 pm ([Tompkins County Board of Elections](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Board-of-Elections)).

## What to do next

Register at your Ithaca address through the state portal or the county office. Check the state's deadlines page before the next election. Look up your polling place once your registration is active. Then read the [first-year start page](/start/first-year/) for your other first-month tasks.
