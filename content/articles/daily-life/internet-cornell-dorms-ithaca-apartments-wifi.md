In Cornell housing, internet is part of the room rate: the rate covers gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB per month ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Off campus, about four in ten listings with a stated amenity list include internet, and the rest leave you to set it up and pay for it.

```glance
- Cornell housing rates include internet up to 200 GB per month, with no separate bill.
- A residence hall double costs $13,744 per academic year, utilities and internet included.
- In the inventory, 143 of 366 listings with amenity lists include internet.
```

## How does internet work in a Cornell residence hall?

You pay nothing extra. The room rate covers it, and a residence hall double costs $13,744 per academic year, or $6,872 per semester, with utilities and internet included ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). That makes housing costs easy to compare with an apartment, where internet is its own line. The full comparison is in [Is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

The 200 GB figure is a monthly data allowance, meaning the amount you can download. Graduate students reported the cap as a complaint about Hasbrouck Apartments in a 2020 student assembly report, so heavy users such as streamers should check it ([Graduate and Professional Student Assembly](https://assembly.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/gpci_-_housing_dec_2020.pdf)).

Cornell's housing pages in this guide's research do not describe how Wi-Fi is set up in a room, whether wired ports are available, or whether personal routers and game consoles are allowed. Ask Cornell's IT Service Desk before you buy a router.

## What does internet look like in an Ithaca apartment?

Listings differ. Of 413 buildings in the inventory behind this guide, 366 list their amenities, and 143 of those mention internet, about 39% (143 divided by 366). In Collegetown, 46 of 89 listings with amenity lists mention it ([Off-Campus Housing marketplace](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing)).

Examples show the range:

| Place | Internet |
|---|---|
| Whiton Hall, downtown | Wi-Fi included in $965 to $1,065 per bed for 2026-27 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)) |
| Maplewood Apartments, 121 Veterans Place | Rent includes electricity, trash, internet and cable ([Greystar](https://www.greystar.com/maplewood-ithaca-ny/p_15697)) |

Source: building and marketplace listings.

If internet is not included, you order it from a provider. Setup steps are in the [utilities setup guide](/housing/set-up-utilities-ithaca-apartment-nyseg-internet/), and prices by provider are in the [Ithaca internet comparison](/daily-life/internet-providers-ithaca-compared-cornell-students/).

## What should you check before you sign?

Ask whether internet is in the rent, how fast it is, and who owns the account. A listing that says "high speed internet" does not name a speed. The [lease guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) lists the terms to read, and the cost guide shows how a bill changes your monthly total ([what a month off campus costs](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/), [utilities cost](/cornell/money/ithaca-utilities-cost-winter-heating-bill-apartment-students/)).

```reader "If you're a low-budget student"
Pick a listing that includes internet if the rent is close, because a separate bill adds up over a 12-month lease.
```

## What to do next

Dorm residents need no setup. Off-campus renters should find the internet line in the lease and plan the order two weeks before move-in.
