Many Cornell halls have laundry rooms, but the housing pages do not say what a load costs, so ask your residence hall director. Only the co-ops list free laundry. Lazybones offers pickup laundry if you skip the machines.

```glance
- Cascadilla, Sheldon Court, 301 College Avenue and 1 Forest Park Lane list laundry rooms.
- 112 Edgemoor lists coin-operated laundry, so bring payment.
- Wait Avenue and 660 Stewart Avenue co-ops list free laundry.
- Lazybones offers pickup laundry through Cornell's vendor page.
```

## Is laundry free in Cornell dorms?

Cornell's housing rate covers gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB per month, and the rate page does not list laundry ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). The hall pages used here mention laundry rooms without prices, except one hall that says coin-op. Do not assume a hall's machines are free. Ask your residence hall director or check the hall's page. The cost of a double is in the [dorm versus off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/) and the [housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/).

## Which halls have laundry, and how is it set up?

A residence hall's machine count matters at busy times. Sheldon Court's basement laundry room has four washers and four dryers, a resident told The Cornell Daily Sun ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court)). The building houses 164 residents ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/sheldon-court)).

## What is the dorm laundry fund?

Cornell's Living Off Campus page carries a link labeled Your Laundry Fund ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/campus-living)). The page does not explain how it works. Ask your residence hall director before you load money onto any card.

## What is the alternative to using the machines?

Lazybones picks up laundry from $1.45 per pound ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships)). A 10-pound load is $14.50 at that rate (10 times 1.45).
