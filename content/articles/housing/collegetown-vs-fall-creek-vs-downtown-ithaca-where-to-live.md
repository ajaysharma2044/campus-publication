```glance
- Collegetown is 0.1 to 0.5 miles from campus; beds for 2027-28 run $800 (6BR) to $2,025 (1BR alone) a month.
- Downtown beds cost about 20% to 28% less than Collegetown for studios, 1BRs and 2BRs at one landlord.
- Fall Creek is 0.5 to 1 mile away and the quietest of the three; median cheapest spots run $828.33.
- Popular Collegetown units fill a year ahead; downtown and Fall Creek lease months later.
```

Pick Collegetown if walking to class matters most and you can sign about a year ahead. Pick downtown if you want lower per-bed rent and can ride the bus. Pick Fall Creek for quiet and a grad-heavy crowd. The three areas differ most in distance, price and when the leases fill, per [Cornell's neighborhood guide](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods).

## How do the three neighborhoods compare?

| | Collegetown | Fall Creek | Downtown and the Commons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | South of central campus: College Ave, Dryden Rd, Eddy St | Below campus toward the lake: Cascadilla Ave, Lake St, N Tioga St | West of campus at the bottom of the hill |
| Distance | 0.1 to 0.5 miles | 0.5 to 1 mile | 0.5 to 1.5 miles; the Commons is 1 mile |
| Walk | Up to 15 minutes; the lower part is uphill | 20 to 30 minutes, uphill | 20 to 30 minutes, uphill |
| Bus | Rarely needed | Frequent service | Route 10 serves downtown and campus |
| Price | Per-bed rent falls as the unit grows | Median cheapest spot $828.33 | Cheaper than Collegetown for studios to 2BRs |
| Crowd | Mostly undergrads | Many grad students | Mixed, with many grad students |
| Leases | About a year ahead | Spring for August | Spring for August |

Distances and walk times come from Cornell's neighborhood guide, which uses one paragraph for Fall Creek and downtown, so their shared walk time is one estimate. The Fall Creek bus ride is about 15 minutes from Hollister Hall, per a [Cornell graduate research group's guide](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html).

**Collegetown.** Cornell says the dense undergraduate population raises rent, noise and congestion there. Buildings run from luxury complexes to multi-unit houses and room rentals.

**Fall Creek.** Mostly subdivided houses, single-unit houses and apartments over shops, within walking distance of shopping and parks. A 1BR at 709 N Tioga St asks $1,600 and $1,700 for two units, with the landlord paying internet and water and the tenant paying electricity and garbage tags ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/cosy-quiet-comfortable-fall-creek-gem.-238258)). The Pinnacle at 121-125 Lake St. opened Aug. 1, 2026 with 77 apartments, at least 80% of them rented to graduate students, and its developer says rents sit 15% to 20% below newer Collegetown buildings ([Tompkins Weekly](https://www.tompkinsweekly.com/news/three-new-developments-offer-different-answers-to-ithacas-housing-needs-30cfa148)).

**Downtown.** Flat, close to the Commons, and the end of Route 10. A new building is not proof of a safe one: the City ordered the 181-apartment Asteri vacated from March 4 to 20, 2026 over fire and safety concerns ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/asteri-s-aftermath-displacement-dangerous-conditions-and-a-predatory-cash-for-keys-scheme)).

## How much does each one cost?

Rent in Ithaca is quoted per bed in most student buildings and per unit elsewhere, so compare like with like. Ithaca Renting posts per-bed prices for both [Collegetown](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) and [downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), which makes it the cleanest like-for-like test. The medians below take the midpoint of each printed range.

A Collegetown studio costs $370 more than downtown (21% less downtown), a 1BR costs $400 more (20% less) and a 2BR bed costs $385 more (28% less). Only at 6BRs is downtown higher, by $70 a bed. The gaps are our subtraction from [Ithaca Renting's price lists](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/). The downtown rows are mostly 2026-27 leftovers, and the 1BR and 3BR samples are small, so treat the gap as a range. Heat, water and trash come with downtown units, and electricity is included only at the Colonial Building, Commons Studios and Whiton Hall.

Across the whole inventory of Cornell-area buildings, the median cheapest per-person spot is $997.50 in Collegetown, $875 downtown, $828.33 in Fall Creek and $730 on South Hill ([off-campus marketplace listings](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing), Nov. 2026, 74, 44, 10 and 8 buildings). Fall Creek prices are the least certain, because few buildings post per-bed prices.

Every price needs one benchmark. Cornell's [2026-27 hall double](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) costs $13,744 a year with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included, which is $1,145.33 a month over 12 months.

Example: three friends compare a Collegetown 3BR bed at $1,243.75 with a downtown 3BR bed at $795. The gap is $448.75 a month each, or $5,385 a year, using the Ithaca Renting [Collegetown](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) and [downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) prices. A downtown share costs $9,540 a year ($795 times 12), which is $4,204 under the hall double.

## How do you get to campus from each one?

From Collegetown, walking wins. From Fall Creek and downtown, most people mix walking with the bus.

Bus rules after your first year, per [Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes):

- First-year degree students, including grads and transfers, ride TCAT free at all hours until Aug. 31 for fall entrants.
- Other registered students ride free after 6 p.m. weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday.
- A daytime weekday ride costs $1.50, and the OmniRide pass costs $200 a year.
- PhD students and graduate assistants get the OmniRide pass free.

A weekday round trip is two rides at $1.50, or $3.00. At that rate the pass pays for itself in about 67 weekday trips, or 13 to 14 weeks. No TCAT buses run from 1:30 to 4 a.m., so a late night downtown or in Fall Creek ends with a walk ([Cornell Sustainability](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting)). Cornell names Route 10 for downtown but does not list which routes serve Fall Creek, so check the TCAT trip planner for your address before you sign.

## When does each market sign?

Collegetown's popular units go first. Leasing for the next year opens in late August and September, when students arrive, and students report signing in October of sophomore year. A developer said in 2016 that he usually rents most units by the end of November ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/)). On Nov. 10, 2026, 14 of 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 on Ithaca Renting's page were already sold out.

The rest of the market moves later. Cornell's Off-Campus Living manager called it a myth that you must lock in housing the day you return in fall ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/)). Both are true: Collegetown's best units go early, while downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing still have supply in spring.

## Which side of the city line is each one on?

All three sit in the City of Ithaca, so two protections apply. A landlord must give current tenants [120 days' written notice](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/) before renewing, showing or re-letting a unit, unless the tenant initialed a waiver. City rentals also appear in Cornell's [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments), which shows sprinklers, alarms and construction type. Neither covers the Town of Ithaca, Lansing or Cayuga Heights.

Cornell's walk times for some other areas do not match its own distances ([Cornell neighborhoods](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)). At a 3 mph walking pace, which is our assumption, Belle Sherman's 1 to 2 miles take 20 to 40 minutes, not Cornell's "15-minute level walk." Plan from the distance.

| Area | Distance | Walk at 3 mph | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Belle Sherman | 1 to 2 miles | 20 to 40 minutes | Quieter, near East Hill Plaza |
| East Hill | 0.75 to 2 miles | 15 to 40 minutes | Partly in the Town of Ithaca |
| Cayuga Heights, Cornell Heights | 1 to 4 miles | 20 to 80 minutes | Cayuga Heights is a separate village |
| South Hill | 1.5 to 3 miles | 15 minutes downhill to downtown, then transit | Ithaca College students dominate |
| Northeast and Lansing | 4 miles; 5 to 10 miles | Bus or car | Lansing is about 20 minutes by bus |
| West Hill | 4 miles | Not practical on foot | Rural |

Sources: Cornell's neighborhood guide and the grad guide above.

Sources: Ithaca Renting's [Collegetown](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) and [downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) pages; the [Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/07/cornell-collegetown-construction/); [RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx); the [Dryden South availability page](https://drydensouth.com/availability/).

Two cautions apply outside the City. Cornell suggests asking NYSEG at 800-572-1111 for a home's average annual heating bill, especially for West Hill cottages. In the Town of Ithaca, a single-family home is limited to one family plus one boarder, a rule upheld in a [2017 case involving Ithaca College students](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html), which can block a group house.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
Decide group size and neighborhood before the November rush. Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown median is $1,246.25 a bed in a 4BR. If your group wants Collegetown, sign in September to November of sophomore year. If downtown or Fall Creek is fine, you can wait into spring. A group that shrinks costs the rest more: dropping from a 6BR to a 4BR median costs each person $446.25 a month.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
Skip the undergrad rush. A grad guide puts the search in March and April for an August start, with a few places left in June and July. Cornell's 2026-27 Ithaca budget allows [$19,962 a year](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students) for housing, or $1,663.50 a month ($19,962 divided by 12), which covers a $1,600 Fall Creek 1BR but not a Collegetown 1BR alone. Ask each landlord what share of tenants are undergrads. Our [grad housing guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/) covers Hasbrouck and other options.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Cheap beds exist in all three areas, but only in big units: a downtown 3BR bed at $795, a downtown 2BR at $992.50, and a Collegetown 6BR bed. A downtown 3BR share comes to $9,540 a year, $4,204 under the hall double. A Collegetown 1BR alone is $24,300 a year at the median, $10,556 above it.
```

```reader "If you're bringing a car"
The car changes the answer more than rent does. The City recommends the Dryden Road Garage to Collegetown residents, at $232 a month, or $2,784 a year ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)). Elsewhere in permit zones, a City residential permit costs $45 a year with a lease as proof ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)). A B commuter permit for campus costs $359.77 a year ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students)). The fact set does not say which Fall Creek and downtown blocks are permit zones, so ask the landlord whether a spot comes with the unit.
```

## What to do next

Pick the neighborhood first, then the group size. If it is Collegetown, start the search when classes begin in August. Read the [off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/) for the full market, from eligibility to move-out.
