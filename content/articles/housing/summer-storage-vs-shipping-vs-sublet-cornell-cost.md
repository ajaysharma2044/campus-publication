```glance
- Lease runs all summer? Leave your things in your room and sublet it furnished. Storage adds cost.
- Lease ends before fall? Store with Big Red Shipping and Storage, which picks up at your door.
- Shipping beats storage only if a one-way quote is under half the storage price.
- Mail a sublet request by May 1 for a June 1 start.
```

If your Ithaca lease runs through the summer, the cheapest plan is to leave your things in your room and sublet it furnished. You owe that rent either way. If your lease ends first, Big Red Shipping and Storage charges [$39 a small box and $49 a large one](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) for the summer.

## Which option costs least for a summer away?

Leaving your things in a sublet room costs least, because the sublet is worth far more than anything you save on boxes. Say you rent a bed in an Ithaca Renting 4-bedroom in Collegetown, where the median per-bed price is $1,246.25 a month for 2027-28 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). You go home for June, July and August with six large boxes and a mini fridge, and a subtenant pays you $900 a month. You owe $1,246.25 x 3 = $3,738.75 in summer rent whatever you choose. The sublet, less a $20 fee, brings back $2,680. Keeping your things in the room saves another $252 to $369 in storage. Shipping needs a real quote, covered below.

## Why does your lease decide the answer?

Most Ithaca leases run a full year and start before you come back. Ithaca Renting's leases run one year and start in June, July or August on fixed dates that can't be changed. At Dryden South, 205 Dryden Road, every 2027-28 lease starts June 1 and runs 12 months ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/)).

So a lease that starts in June, July or August makes you pay for the summer whether you stay or not. That rent is a sunk cost, meaning money you owe no matter what you decide. Paying again to store your things somewhere else only makes sense if your subtenant needs the space.

If your lease ends before you return, the math flips. Everything has to leave the apartment, and the choice is storage or shipping. That is also the dorm resident's position: Cornell's housing license offers no storage and bars residents from subletting or transferring their room ([Cornell housing license 2027-28](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). Dorm move-out is covered in the [move-out storage and shipping guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).

## Can you sublet with your things still in the room?

Yes, if your landlord approves the sublet. A sublet means someone else lives in your room and pays you, while you stay on the lease. Your things can stay as long as your sublease says what stays, where, and who pays if it is damaged.

In a building with four or more apartments, New York gives you a right to sublet with the landlord's written consent, which "shall not be unreasonably withheld" ([New York Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). You send the request by certified mail, return receipt requested. It must list the sublet dates, the subtenant's name and addresses, your reason, your address while away, any co-tenant or guarantor consent, and a copy of the sublease. The landlord can ask for more information within 10 days. The landlord must answer within 30 days, and silence counts as consent. In a house with three or fewer apartments, your lease alone decides whether you can sublet ([Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy Program](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

Either way, you stay responsible for the rent and any damage. If the subtenant stops paying, you still owe your landlord, and you can sue the subtenant in small claims court. That risk is the real cost of keeping your things in a sublet room, so lock valuables away or take them home.

Landlords add their own rules. Ithaca Renting requires approval before any sublet starts. Its Collegetown subtenants must be Cornell students, though summer subtenants can be graduate students from other universities. You can sublet only one term at a time, and nothing under a month. Its page lists a $15 sublet fee in one place and a $20 fee in another, so ask the Renting Office which applies. It also requires you to keep the electricity on over the summer. Ithaca Renting's downtown leases set a two-month minimum per sublet and require every other tenant on the lease to agree ([Ithaca Renting downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)).

A landlord can charge a sublet fee only if your lease lists one, and it must be reasonable. A credit or background check fee for the subtenant is allowed (Cornell Law guide above).

## What does a sublet bring in?

Any sublet beats an empty room once the income covers the fee, so price yours from real offers and listings for rooms like it. One Collegetown landlord, Modern Living Rentals, advertises sublet and roommate openings "from $900/person," with no period stated ([Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/)). That matches the sublet rate in the example above. Ithaca has no published typical summer sublet price.

Students hunting for a summer room use the guide to [finding summer housing from May to August](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/), the other side of your listing.

## What does storage cost if your things have to move?

For the load below, Storage Squad is the cheapest door-to-door option, then Uncle Marty's, then Big Red Shipping and Storage (BRSS). A self-storage unit costs less still if you have a car. BRSS is run by Student Agencies at 409 College Avenue. Its prices cover a 3 to 4 month term and include pickup, storage and delivery (BRSS pricing above). BRSS picks up free from a dorm, apartment or house ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)).

## When does shipping beat storage?

Shipping wins only when a one-way quote is under half of what storage would cost, because you pay to ship twice: home in May and back in August. BRSS ships through FedEx and sets the price at pickup from current FedEx rates, with extra fees for international shipments and customs paperwork. It posts no shipping prices, so get a quote before you choose.

For the load above, BRSS storage is $369, so a one-way shipping quote must come in under $184.50 to win. Per box, a large box stores for $49, so shipping it each way must cost under $24.50 (BRSS pricing above).

Carrying an extra box on the bus costs more than storing it. OurBus allows one suitcase up to 50 pounds free, and each extra bag is $30, prepaid ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/colleges/ithaca-ny-c-cornell-university)). A box carried home and back as an extra bag costs $60, more than the $49 to store it.

Shipping makes sense when you need the things over the summer, or when you aren't coming back.

```reader "If you're graduating or leaving Ithaca for good"
Storage only delays the problem, so the choice is shipping, carrying, selling or donating. Price one-way shipping only. If your lease runs past your last day, Cornell Law School's tenant guide says assigning the lease, which hands it over entirely, is generally better than subletting when you won't move back in, because it ends your liability for the new tenant's rent and damage. In a building of four or more units, the landlord can refuse an assignment without cause; if that refusal is unreasonable, you can be released from the lease on 30 days' notice.
```

```reader "If you're going abroad for a semester"
Your time away runs four or five months, past BRSS's 3 to 4 month term, so ask BRSS for a quote or use a month-to-month unit. A semester sublet uses up Ithaca Renting's one sublet term for that stretch. The [away-semester guide](/start/away-semester/) covers the rest of your checklist.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Shipping abroad costs the most once BRSS adds its international and customs fees. Store in Ithaca, or leave your things in a sublet room if your lease runs all summer. You must be present for a BRSS pickup, so book a slot before your flight date.
```

## FAQ

**Can I store things in my apartment and leave it empty?**
Yes, if your lease runs through the summer. Ithaca Renting still requires the electricity to stay on, at least enough to keep the fridge running, and charges its sublet fee if anyone uses the apartment over the summer, even for free.

## What to do next

Check your lease for its end date and its sublet clause. If it runs through the summer, start finding a subtenant in March and mail your request to the landlord by the date in the timeline above. If it ends, book storage by early April and get a shipping quote to compare. For the deposit on a lease that ends, see [moving out and getting your deposit back](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/).
