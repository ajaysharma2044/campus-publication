Fall 2027 move-in dates are not yet announced. Until they are, plan around the 2027 first day of classes, Aug. 23 ([Cornell registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar/2027-2028)).

## What do you do before move-in day?

Do these in order. The first two happen in the Housing and Dining Portal.

1. **Print your Move-In Ticket.** It comes from the Housing and Dining Portal and shows your assigned parking area.
2. **Book an arrival timeslot in the portal.** The timeslot is for arrival and check-in only. It does not limit how long you can unload.
3. **Decide how you arrive.** Uber and taxi riders go to A Lot on North Campus. Bus riders go straight to their building.
4. **Plan the car.** After unloading, you get 3 hours of short-term parking in the area printed on your ticket. Overnight parking is not allowed in the Jessup Road unloading spaces. The details by building are in the [move-in parking and unloading guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/).
5. **Leave the trailer at home.** In 2026, U-Hauls and trailers were turned away at North Campus.
6. **Check your building's own page.** Each hall has its own instructions. Balch Hall has an unloading zone with no staff help, and Cascadilla moves cars to the Williams Street Lot after unloading, at the upper lot at Williams Street and Stewart Avenue ([Cornell Cascadilla instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)).

## Where do you unload?

North Campus buildings have named zones. Jessup Road serves the low-rise halls marked LR6. The RPCC lot serves HR5, Jameson, Donlon and Ganędagǫ:. Program House Drive serves LR7, JAM, HILC, Ujamaa, Hu Shih and Mews/Loving. The Helen Newman lot serves CKB, and Balch uses its north dock.

South Campus works differently. Cascadilla unloads at the Eddygate entrance, then parks at the Williams Street Lot ([Cornell Cascadilla instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)). The Cascadilla service center was closed on first-year day, open 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. through the upper-level days and open 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. the Sunday after.

## When does staff unloading help end?

Staff unloading help ended at 8 p.m. on the Monday and at 11 a.m. on the Tuesday, for arrivals before 11. It ended at 5 p.m. on Friday and Saturday ([Cornell North Campus instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)). After those times you carry your own things, so arrive early in your slot.

## What changes in January?

Residence halls close Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, at 2 p.m. They reopen Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027, at 9 a.m. You can move back any time before classes start on Jan. 26, with an arrival timeslot in the portal. Staying past the closing time costs $60 per hour or part of an hour, and a late-departure request is due in the portal by Monday, Dec. 7 ([Cornell winter closing notice](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice)).

## How do you get to Ithaca and around campus?

Most first-years arrive by car, Uber or bus. The [first-year getting around guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-first-year/) covers buses and campus walking routes, and the [getting to Ithaca guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/) covers the airport and long-distance buses.

## What to do next

Check the Housing and Dining Portal in July for your assigned day, then print your ticket. The housing office can answer building-specific questions at housing@cornell.edu. Cornell's [new student resource hub](https://scl.cornell.edu/new-student-resource-hub) lists the To Do List, which went live Mar. 26 in the 2026 cycle, and the housing, health and placement-exam steps that follow in May and June.
