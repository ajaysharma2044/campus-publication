William Keeton House is a West Campus House at 4 Forest Park Lane that opened in August 2008. It holds more than 300 students in singles, doubles and 37 large suites, with no air conditioning. A double costs $13,744 for 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is William Keeton House?

It is one of Cornell's five West Campus Houses, built for upper-level undergraduates who want a small community with its own dean, dining room and events. Cornell's Facilities office lists Keeton Main at 4 Forest Park Ln. ([Cornell Facilities](https://findfacilities-prod.fcs.cornell.edu/facilitypage/3034)). The Cornell Chronicle dates the opening to August 2008 ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2009/08/west-campus-house-system-officially-complete)).

## What is it like to live in Keeton House?

The suites set Keeton apart. Cornell counts 37 of them, and they hold anywhere from four to ten students, while most suites in the other Houses hold five ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/room-selection-faqs)). Not every suite is offered in room selection.

Keeton also serves as a hub for nearby buildings. The House Office, laundry and dining for 1 Forest Park Lane are at Keeton ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/1-forest-park-lane)), and so is check-in for the Equity & Engagement Community at 2 Forest Park Lane ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-equity-and-engagement-community)). The dining room serves the nearby halls as well as Keeton residents.

Dining is the mixed part. Each Wednesday from 6 to 7 p.m., the dining room serves only House residents, fellows and guests, then opens to everyone at 7 p.m. ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms/west-campus-house-dinners)). Two Sun reviewers scored the five West Campus dinners during the week of Oct. 24, 2022. Keeton averaged 5.5 out of 10, with a swing from 8.5 on Monday to 0.5 on Friday, when the naan was stale and the burgers sold out ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/07/a-comprehensive-review-of-west-campus-dining/)). That is one week and two reviewers, so treat it as a snapshot.

Keeton does one thing the others do not list. Its dining room serves halal food on request: dinner seven days a week, plus lunch and brunch on Saturday and Sunday ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets)).

## What does William Keeton House cost?

Cornell prices rooms by type, not by House. A Keeton double costs the same as a double in any residence hall, and the House Meal Plan comes on top. The plan is $3,802 per semester, or $7,604 for the year, plus a $50 yearly fee, and it includes $400 in Big Red Bucks and 8 guest meals each semester ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Per semester | Per year | Year with House Meal Plan and fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double | $6,872 | $13,744 | $21,398 |
| Single | $7,783 | $15,566 | $23,220 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the House Meal Plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

The sources cited here list no separate rate for the larger suites, so check the rates page for the bedroom type you would get. Residents cannot change or cancel the House Meal Plan while they live in a West Campus House. [Is the Cornell meal plan worth it?](/cornell/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/) weighs the plan against cooking.

Off campus, a share of a Collegetown six-bedroom has a median asking rent of $9,600 over 12 months. That is $4,144 less than the double, before food, and the double includes utilities ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). ## How do you get a spot in Keeton House?

You rank it in room selection, and demand is high. About 20% of rising sophomores could select West Campus spaces in past years. Most lived on North and South Campus, and a student who did not select was assigned what was left ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/residential-programs/apply-housing/current-undergrad-application-process/room-selection-faqs)).

For 2027-28, rising sophomores apply from Feb. 1 to April 4, 2027, at 11:59 p.m. Timeslots come out April 7, and open room selection runs April 13 to 16 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). If a plan to rent instead is on the table, the [sophomore off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) covers it.

## What should you watch before you sign?

Plan for heat, because Keeton has no air conditioning. Plan for the cancellation schedule too. A returning junior or senior who cancels a 2027-28 license pays $50 through Jan. 31, $500 from Feb. 1 to March 31, $1,000 from April 1 to June 30 and $2,000 from July 1 to Aug. 15 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). The [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) turns your date into a number.

## What to do next

Pick a suite size, then price that room with the House Meal Plan. Rank Keeton alongside the other Houses, and keep a backup in mind, since most rising sophomores do not land in West Campus.
