Belle Sherman is a quieter residential area east of Collegetown, near East Hill Plaza and Judd Falls Plaza. Cornell puts it 1 to 2 miles from campus ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)). Some of it lies in the Town of Ithaca, where different zoning and permit rules apply than in the City.

## How far is Belle Sherman from campus?

Cornell pairs that distance with a 15-minute level walk, but the two numbers do not fit. At a walking pace of 3 miles an hour, or 20 minutes a mile, 15 minutes covers 0.75 mile, so the stated walk only works at the near end. The nearest listings in the area, Fairview Apartments and 303 Cornell St., sit 0.6 miles from Ho Plaza in a straight line, and Plaza East at 222 Summerhill Dr. sits 1.37 miles away ([listings feed](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing)). Plan on a bus or bike for the far end.

Two neighborhoods share the East Hill name. The Ithaca Times uses East Hill for the area between downtown and Collegetown, while Cornell's East Hill lies east of Collegetown ([Ithaca Times](https://www.ithaca.com/special_sections/newcomers_guide/ithaca-s-neighborhoods-have-subtly-distinctive-personalities/article_7186dd20-23c0-11ed-a9b6-1798d19eb7a1.html)). Check the address on a map before you rely on the name.

## What is Maplewood?

Maplewood is Cornell's graduate and professional student housing at 121 Veterans Place, built in 2017 and 2018 on a Cornell-owned site ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing)). It has 444 units and 872 beds in five apartment buildings, 21 townhouses and stacked flats ([LeChase](https://www.lechase.com/portfolio/greystar/)). Greystar manages it, and rent includes electricity, trash, internet and cable ([Greystar](https://www.greystar.com/maplewood-ithaca-ny/p_15697)). Each unit has in-unit laundry and the community is pet-friendly.

Greystar posts no prices. Cornell's veterinary college lists Maplewood at $1,075 to $1,330 for studio to four-bedroom units, without saying whether that is per bed or per unit, updated in February 2026 ([College of Veterinary Medicine](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/education/biomedical-biological-sciences-bbs-phd-program/prospective-students/our-community/housing-information)). Leaseholders pay a one-time $130.00 community amenity fee at move-in and need third-party renters insurance (Greystar). Our [graduate housing guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/) and [student family housing page](/cornell/housing/cornell-student-family-housing-ithaca/) explain who can apply.

## What else can you rent in Belle Sherman?

Fairview Apartments at 100 Fairview Square charge $1,363 to $1,514 per unit for a studio in The Manor and $1,495 to $1,602 for a one-bedroom, and the listing says rent covers all utilities except parking and pets ([listings feed](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing)). No lease year is printed, so confirm which year a quote covers. Coal Yard Apartments at 143 Maple Ave. list a price range of $1,240 to $3,750 and garage parking at $1,300 a year for 10 spaces ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/coal-yard-apartments-7734)). The graduate student guide says East Hill's newer complexes, such as Maplewood, have in-unit laundry ([Graduate Student Resources](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html)).

Ask each landlord to state in writing whether a price is per bed or per unit.

## What Town of Ithaca rules apply?

Parts of East Hill lie outside the City of Ithaca, in the Town, which calls itself a donut around the City. The Town provides no trash service ([Town of Ithaca](https://townithacany.gov/welcome-new-resident-guide/)).

The Town also limits who can share a house. A family is people related by blood, marriage or adoption, more than two unrelated people count as a family only if the Zoning Board of Appeals finds a functional equivalent, and a single-family home is limited to one family plus one boarder. A 2017 case involving Ithaca College students upheld the rule ([Drummond v. Town of Ithaca ZBA](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html)). A group of three or more students renting a single-family house in the Town should ask the landlord whether the house qualifies. Our [City, Town and Lansing guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) compares the rules.
