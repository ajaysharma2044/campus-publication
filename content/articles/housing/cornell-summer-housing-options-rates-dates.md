Cornell rents summer rooms in North Campus residence halls from June 1 to Aug. 11, 2027, at $57.00 a night for a double or $69.50 for a single, plus a $1.50 nightly fee ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus)). Applications open March 1. A co-op room, a sublet or a lease that starts in June are the other routes.

## What are your summer housing options near Cornell?

You have four routes. The right one depends on how many weeks you need and whether you want a lease.

| Option | Who it fits | What it costs | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cornell summer housing, North Campus | Any Cornell student, or a visiting student studying or working at Cornell | $57.00 or $69.50 a night, plus $1.50 | Two-week minimum; no refund for leaving early |
| A Cornell co-op | Students who want a house and shared meals | Ask co-ops@cornell.edu; no summer prices posted | Each house decides whether it has summer rooms |
| A sublet | Anyone who needs June and July only | Set by the tenant | You rely on someone else's lease |
| A 12-month lease starting in June | Students staying in Ithaca next year too | From about $700 a room a month (examples below) | You pay through the next spring |

Source: Cornell Student & Campus Life summer housing and co-op pages; Ithaca landlord listings linked below.

## How much does Cornell summer housing cost?

A double costs $58.50 a night once you add the $1.50 administrative fee, and a single costs $71.00 ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus)). Cornell prices summer housing only by the night, so your total depends on the dates you pick when you apply.

## When does Cornell summer housing open, and when should you apply?

Check-in starts at 2 p.m. on opening day, and checkout is 10 a.m. on closing day ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus)). You can arrive any day until 2 p.m. on July 27, the last arrival Cornell allows.

If you have lived in Cornell housing before, you apply through the Housing Portal. Everyone else creates an account on the summer application page. Submit at least 10 days before you arrive. [Cornell's FAQ](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus) says there is "generally enough housing for everyone who requests to stay," so the opening day is a start date, not a race.

## Who can live in Cornell summer housing, and what are the rooms like?

Any Cornell undergraduate or graduate student can apply. So can a student from another college who is taking classes, doing research or working in a Cornell facility. You must upload proof that you qualify, such as a class schedule or a job offer. High school students in Cornell's precollege programs and PSSP do not apply here; their programs place them. If you are weighing classes, see [whether Cornell Summer Session is worth it](/cornell/academics/cornell-summer-session-classes-transfer-credit-worth-it/).

The rooms are in suites. A suite is a locked door off the hallway that opens onto a few bedrooms, a shared common space and a bathroom used only by the people in that suite. Each resident gets an extra-long twin bed, desk, chair and dresser or wardrobe. Cornell supplies two sheets, a blanket, a pillow, towels and washcloths, and you wash them yourself. Each building has a shared kitchen with a refrigerator, stove and microwave, and free washers and dryers.

Suites are single gender; floors are mixed. Students 21 and older are grouped together when space allows. To room with a friend, both of you email summersessionhsg@cornell.edu. If you have accommodations through Student Disability Services, forward them to the same address (Cornell Student & Campus Life).

You check in at the 24-hour service center on the first floor of Robert Purcell Community Center. Return every key and card at checkout: a lost key costs $104.00 and a lost access card $25.00 (Cornell Student & Campus Life).

## What if your plans change?

Your contract covers every night you book. If you leave early, you get no prorated refund. Cornell refunds housing fees only if you officially withdraw from your program, and then it prorates the refund and keeps a $100 processing fee. The nightly administrative fees never come back ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus)).

To add nights, email the summer housing office. Anyone still in a room after the 10 a.m. checkout on closing day pays $60.00 for each hour or part of an hour (Cornell Student & Campus Life). So book the shortest stay you are sure of and add nights later: shortening a stay costs you money, and adding to one costs nothing extra.

## Do Cornell co-ops offer summer housing?

Some do. Cornell says its cooperative houses "often offer summer housing" and sends students to co-ops@cornell.edu or 607-255-3445 for options and rates ([Cornell cooperative housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). A co-op is a university-owned house where residents share the cooking and chores. Cornell posts no summer co-op prices, so email early in the spring and ask three things: which houses have summer rooms, the price per week or month, and whether house meals run in summer. The school-year co-op license ends at 2 p.m. May 23, 2027, so a summer co-op room is a separate agreement.

## Is a sublet or a June lease cheaper?

A room in town costs less per month than a campus summer room, but it comes with strings. A sublet means you rent from a tenant who is away, under their lease. A new lease ties you to Ithaca well past August.

**Sublets.** Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace lists sublets, and only people who prove a Cornell affiliation can see them and contact the subletters ([Cornell rental listings](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). Ithaca Renting, a large Collegetown landlord, makes every sublet register for approval, limits Collegetown subtenants to Cornell students, and allows summer sublets to graduate students from other universities too ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The full process, prices and safety checks are in [our summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/).

**Leases that start in June.** Some landlords start 12-month leases in early summer. At 310-312 S. Aurora St., downtown, a furnished seven-bedroom house lists at $4,900 a month, or $700 a room, from June 1, 2027, with utilities paid by tenants ([PPM Homes](https://www.ppmhomes.com/listings/detail/d9a5af7d-9b77-4e9a-83f0-a517e63e77cf)). At 408 Stewart Ave. in Collegetown, a furnished studio with utilities included lists at $1,200 a month from June 11, 2027, for the 2027-28 year ([CSP Management](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/PublicPages/ApartmentSearch.aspx)). Whiton Hall, a furnished rooming house at 222 S. Aurora St., rents premium rooms at $965 to $1,065 a month for 2026-27 on 12-month leases, with heat, electricity and Wi-Fi included ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=11)).

A room at the Aurora Street house costs $1,055.00 less a month than 30 nights in a campus double ($1,755.00 minus $700) ([PPM Homes](https://www.ppmhomes.com/listings/detail/d9a5af7d-9b77-4e9a-83f0-a517e63e77cf)). That saving holds only if you want the room for the full year.

## How do you eat on campus over the summer?

A meal plan is optional for summer residents, and Cornell recommends one. In summer 2026, the Summer Combo Plan cost $686 for 30 dining room meals plus $200 in Summer Bucks, from May 26 to Aug. 16 ([Cornell Dining summer meal plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/summer-meal-plans)). Added to a full summer in a double, that comes to $4,839.50 ($4,153.50 plus $686). Summer Bucks are a prepaid balance with no sales tax, and the Summer Bucks Plan starts with a $50 deposit. Ten more meals cost $157.00. With Summer Bucks, dinner cost $18.85 in 2026. Morrison Dining on North Campus stays open all summer, next to the summer halls. Cornell updates summer plans each April, so the 2027 prices come out after applications open.

Every summer hall has a shared kitchen, so the cheapest plan is a small Summer Bucks balance plus your own cooking.

```reader "If you're a graduate student"
Against the 2026-27 summer assistantship minimum of $12,228 ([Cornell Graduate School](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/)), a full summer in a campus single ($5,041.00) takes about 41 percent, and a double about 34 percent. If you are staying for the next year too, Hasbrouck Apartments on North Campus rents on 12-month contracts at $620 to $1,530 a month for 2026-27 ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)) and takes applications on a rolling basis ([Cornell graduate housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing)). Five co-ops, including Triphammer, Watermargin and Redbud in Von Cramm Hall, accept graduate students.
```

## FAQ

**Is there financial aid for summer housing?** Cornell's summer housing page sends students to the Office of Financial Aid for their own case. Ask before you book, because the housing contract binds you to every night you choose.

**What happens between summer checkout and fall move-in?** You need somewhere to stay or store your things for the gap. For 2026-27, fall move-in ran Aug. 17 to 22, 2026. Cornell has not posted 2027-28 move-in days in its license pages, so check them in the spring. For shorter breaks, see [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/).

## What to do next

Decide by February whether you want a campus room, a co-op room or a sublet, because each opens on a different clock. For a campus room, mark the 9 a.m. application opening on your calendar and have your proof of eligibility ready. For a co-op, email co-ops@cornell.edu before then.
