```glance
- The City of Ithaca's 120-day renewal notice, Certificate of Compliance, Cornell safety tool, $45 street parking permit, $6 trash tags and noise code apply only inside the city line.
- Cayuga Heights (a village), Lansing (a town) and the Town of Ithaca parts of East Hill and South Hill are outside it, even with an "Ithaca, NY 14850" address.
- In the Town of Ithaca, more than two unrelated people count as one family only if the Zoning Board of Appeals says so (Drummond, 2017).
- State law follows you everywhere: a one-month deposit cap and a 14-day return deadline.
- Good Cause Eviction covers the City (opted in July 2024), the only Tompkins County municipality on the State's opt-in list as of May 4, 2026.
- Code offices: City 607-274-6508, Town of Ithaca 607-273-1783, Lansing 607-533-7054, Cayuga Heights 607-257-5536 ext. 104.
```

Yes, it matters. The City of Ithaca's renter protections, including the [120-day renewal notice](https://ecode360.com/27390826), the [Certificate of Compliance](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) and Cornell's [building safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments), stop at the city line. Before you sign in Cayuga Heights, Lansing, Varna or the outer parts of East Hill and South Hill, ask which municipality the address is in and call that code office.

```reader "If you're a grad student"
The quieter, cheaper places grad students look at often sit on or past the line. Cayuga Heights is a separate village; parts of East Hill are in the Town of Ithaca; Lansing and Varna are outside the City. Lansing West (700 Warren Rd) showed 1BR units at [$1,038 and $1,193 in November 2026](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx), but no safety-tool record and no 120-day notice come with them. The mistake that costs most: assuming the City rules you read about cover you. Ask for the code office's inspection history instead.
```

```reader "If you're renting with a spouse, partner or children"
The Town of Ithaca's family rule is about unrelated people, so a married couple with children is one family anywhere. What changes outside the City is the paperwork: no Certificate of Compliance to look up, no safety-tool rating and no Good Cause Eviction right to renew. New York's [occupant law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F) still applies everywhere; a single-tenant lease allows your immediate family plus one more occupant. Before a 12-month lease on a Cayuga Heights or Lansing house, call that code office and ask whether a rental inspection exists there.
```

```reader "If you're an upper-year renting already"
Look at your lease's first page. In the City, a landlord must give you [120 days' written notice](https://ecode360.com/27390826) before renewing, showing your unit or signing new tenants, unless you initialed a bolded waiver. Outside the City no such rule exists, so nothing in City code stops a landlord showing your place to next year's group in September. Your deposit rights do not change: one month's rent at most, back with an itemized statement within [14 days](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). A non-renewal or a raise of 5% or more still needs [30 to 90 days' notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C) under state law. For a repair complaint, call the code office for your address, not the City's.
```

## The city line, rule by rule

Cornell's [neighborhoods page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) describes areas by name, not by municipality. The name on the listing tells you little. An "Ithaca, NY 14850" mailing address covers places outside the City: the Cayuga Heights code office itself sits at [836 Hanshaw Road, Ithaca NY 14850](https://cayugaheights.gov/departments/code-enforcement/).

## Which neighborhoods straddle the line

- **Collegetown, Fall Creek and downtown:** inside the City. Every City rule applies.
- **East Hill:** 0.75 to 2 miles from campus. Some addresses may lie in the Town of Ithaca, where the Town's family rule applies.
- **South Hill:** 1.5 to 3 miles, around Ithaca College. Parts are in the Town; the 2017 Drummond case under the Town's rule involved Ithaca College students.
- **Cayuga Heights:** 1 to 4 miles north. A separate village, outside the 120-day rule and the safety tool.
- **Lansing and Northeast:** 5 to 10 miles along Triphammer and Warren roads; Cornell recommends a bus or car. The Town of Lansing and the [Village of Lansing](https://www.villageoflansingny.gov/Newsletters/2025%20Spring%20Newsletter%20Final.pdf) each run their own code office.
- **Varna:** east along Dryden Rd, a hamlet in the [Town of Dryden](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2022/04/cornell-varna-fire-volunteers-protect-serve-learn), outside the City.

## Renewals and the 120-day rule

The City's rule passed April 6, 2022 and took effect May 31, 2022. It covers leases of nine months or longer, [as WSKG reported](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/). It is what keeps a Collegetown landlord from pushing you to re-sign in your first month. Outside the City, the clock that applies is the state's: 30, 60 or 90 days' notice if the landlord will not renew or raises rent 5% or more, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal).

Example: two grad students rent a Cayuga Heights duplex from August 1. In October the landlord asks them to commit to next year or lose the unit. In the City that request could not come before the 120 days' notice, unless they initialed the waiver. In the village, nothing in City code stops it. Their choice is simply whether to sign early.

## Safety checks: the certificate and the tool

Inside the City, a multiple dwelling needs a Certificate of Compliance, valid [3 years](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) (1 year for dorms, fraternities and sororities). The City's [Housing Inspections page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections) says inspections are required for all residential rentals in the City. To check one, call the Building Division at 607-274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org. The [Building Division](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/130/Building-Division) sits in City Hall, 108 E. Green St., 4th floor, open weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

The Cornell and City safety tool shows sprinklers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, construction type and property use for [City rentals only](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments). Cornell Off-Campus Living [checks listings against](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/) the City's certificate of compliance database. A Lansing or Cayuga Heights listing has no such check behind it.

A certificate check matters. In 2018 the tenants at 117 Thurston Ave learned [only after signing](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/) that its certificate had expired in November 2015. It then took 53 days from their first complaint to a fixed heater.

## Who can live together

The City's zoning calls a group of [four or more unrelated persons](https://ecode360.com/8393870) a "cooperative household".

The Town of Ithaca is stricter. Its "family" means people related by blood, marriage or adoption. More than two unrelated people count as a family only if the Zoning Board of Appeals finds them a functional equivalent. Single-family homes are limited to one family plus one boarder. A state court [upheld that rule in 2017](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html) in Drummond v Town of Ithaca ZBA, a case involving Ithaca College students.

Example: four friends find a four-bedroom house on the Town side of East Hill. Under the Town's definition, two unrelated people plus one boarder is the default for a single-family home. Before signing, they ask the Town code office (607-273-1783) whether four is legal at that address. If it is not, they look at City addresses instead.

## Parking, trash and noise

These are the rules a renter meets every week, and all three are City rules.

**Parking.** A City [residential permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) costs $45 a year, runs to July 31 and goes to renters in a permit area with a lease or utility bill plus registration. From November 1 to April 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., [odd/even parking](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations) applies when needed, and a car must move every 24 hours on weekdays. Central Collegetown has no street permits; the [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) sells a $232 monthly permit. None of these City rules reach a Lansing or Cayuga Heights street.

**Trash.** City trash takes one [$6.00 tag per can or bag](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags), or $36.00 for a sheet of 6, from January 1, 2026. Bags go out no more than 24 hours before pickup, and couches and mattresses may not sit outdoors ([exterior rules](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance)). Cayuga Heights collects household trash weekly with its own [Village tags](https://cayugaheights.gov/for-residents/household-yard-refuse/), $20.00 for a sheet of 12, each covering 15 lb. The Town of Ithaca does not collect, so residents [contract with a local hauler](https://townithacany.gov/faqs/does-the-town-provide-trash-and-recycling-pickup/). Lansing works the same way: [Tompkins County](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Recycling-Materials-Management/Trash/Trash-Tags) lists only the City and the villages of Cayuga Heights, Dryden and Trumansburg as offering collection. Anywhere in the county, a bag or can set out for pickup needs a tag, which haulers must sell; trash in a dumpster needs none. Ask the landlord whether the rent covers the hauler.

**Snow and noise.** City owners must clear the sidewalk within 24 hours after snowfall begins; a lease may pass that to you. The City's [noise code](https://ecode360.com/8391226) sets night hours from 10:00 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. The Town of Ithaca has a [separate ordinance](https://ecode360.com/8659282) whose daytime runs 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.

## Good Cause and legal help

New York's Good Cause Eviction law applies outside New York City [only where a municipality opts in](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal). Ithaca's Common Council [voted 8-2 to opt in](https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections) in July 2024. That gives City tenants a right to renew, with exceptions, and makes increases above the lower of 10% or 5% plus inflation presumed unreasonable.

Each year the state housing agency, DHCR, publishes the list of municipalities that have opted in. Its [2026 notice](https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction-law-notice-august-2026), current to May 4, 2026, names the City of Ithaca and no other Tompkins County city, town or village. A Town of Ithaca, Lansing, Cayuga Heights or Varna rental is therefore outside Good Cause unless that town or village opts in later. A state [non-renewal or 5%-plus increase notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C) must say whether the unit is subject to Good Cause.

Legal help also shifts. The tenant hotline (607-301-1560) gives advice with no geographic limits, but [full representation is only for City of Ithaca residents](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources).

## What stays the same everywhere

State law covers every address in Tompkins County. A deposit plus any rent advance is capped at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and it comes back with an itemized statement within 14 days or the landlord loses the right to keep any of it. [Application fees are banned](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). The [occupant law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F) lets you live with immediate family and one more occupant.

Town and City numbers come from the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance); the Village of Lansing number is its [village office](https://villageoflansingny.gov/). Cayuga Heights' [code page](https://cayugaheights.gov/departments/code-enforcement/) covers short-term rentals of 28 days or less. Outside the City, state rules still require the local code office to inspect multiple dwellings for fire safety and property maintenance at least every three years ([19 NYCRR 1203.3](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/19-NYCRR-1203.3)). Ask that office when a building was last inspected.

```reader "If you're a student with a car"
Outside the City, the City's $45 residential permit, the odd/even rule from November 1 to April 1 and the 24-hour move rule do not apply to your street. Inside the City, check the [parking rules](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations): a car unmoved for 72 hours may be towed. Central Collegetown has no street permits, so twelve months at the $232 garage rate is $2,784. A Cornell [B commuter permit](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students) is $359.77 a year wherever you live. The mistake that costs most: leaving a car on a City street over winter break, which a [2023 Cornell notice](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-11-16/winter-parking-transportation-information) says may be treated as abandoned and towed.
```

```reader "If you're an alum thinking about being a landlord"
Which side of the line you buy on sets your duties. In the City you need a current Certificate of Compliance for a multiple dwelling (valid 3 years), you give tenants 120 days' notice before re-letting, and Good Cause applies. In the Town of Ithaca, renting a single-family house to more than two unrelated students needs a [ZBA finding](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html). Deposit, fee and occupant law apply everywhere. The mistake that costs most: buying a Town house for four students and learning the occupancy is not legal.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
If you sign from abroad, the address is your only safety check. ## Common mistakes
```

- **Trusting "Ithaca, NY" on the listing.** A 14850 address can be in Cayuga Heights or the Town, so the City protections you counted on are missing.
- **Signing for four in a Town of Ithaca house.** Over two unrelated people needs a ZBA finding; a group that learns this after signing holds a lease for a house it may not legally share.
- **Looking up a Lansing or Cayuga Heights rental on the safety tool.** It covers City rentals only; a blank result is not a clean one.
- **Expecting 120 days' notice outside the City.** A landlord there can show your unit to the next group in the fall.
- **Calling the City Building Division about a Town apartment.** The complaint goes to the wrong office; call 607-273-1783 or 607-533-7054.
- **Assuming Good Cause protects a Lansing renewal.** Lansing had not opted in as of May 4, 2026; check the coverage line on the landlord's notice.

## FAQ

**Is Collegetown in the City of Ithaca?** Yes. Collegetown, Fall Creek and downtown are City neighborhoods, so the 120-day rule, the certificate and the [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) all apply.

**Do I need City trash tags in Cayuga Heights?** No. The [$6 City tags](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags) are for City collection; Cayuga Heights picks up trash with its own [Village tags](https://cayugaheights.gov/for-residents/household-yard-refuse/).

**Can a Town of Ithaca landlord legally rent to three unrelated students?** Only in a home where the Zoning Board of Appeals has found the group a functional equivalent of a family, under the rule [upheld in 2017](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html). Call the Town at 607-273-1783 to ask about a specific address.

**Does the City's noise permit apply outside the City?** No. The [City code](https://ecode360.com/8391226) covers City addresses; the Town of Ithaca has its own [noise ordinance](https://ecode360.com/8659282).

**Does Cornell treat a Lansing apartment differently?** Cornell's residential rules depend on your class year, not your address. The difference is the City-only safety tool and certificate check.
