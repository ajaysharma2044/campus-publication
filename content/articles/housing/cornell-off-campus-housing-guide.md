```glance
- First- and second-years must live in Cornell or affiliated housing; sign a lease without an approved exemption and you are billed for the full year anyway.
- Popular Collegetown units lease 8 to 16 months ahead; on Nov 8, 2026, 14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows for 2027-28 were sold out.
- Most Ithaca leases end July 31 and the next starts about August 1.
- Per bed for 2027-28 at one landlord: $800 in a 6-bedroom to $2,025 for a 1-bedroom alone; Cornell's $13,744 double equals $1,145 a month over 12 months.
- New York caps the deposit at one month's rent, bans application and cleaning fees, and wants the deposit back within 14 days.
- In the City of Ithaca, a landlord owes current tenants 120 days' notice before renewing or showing a unit, unless you initialed a waiver.
```

Off-campus housing at Cornell is a junior, senior and grad market: first- and second-years must live in Cornell or affiliated housing, and those who sign a lease without an approved exemption [will still be billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing). The popular Collegetown units for a junior-year lease go in the fall of sophomore year, [up to 16 months before move-in](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), while downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and grad housing lease into spring and summer. Measure every rent against Cornell's [$13,744 double for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), which is $1,145 a month over 12 months by our division; in Collegetown, mostly the biggest shares come in under it.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
The rule covers only this year, so you can sign now for junior year, and the rush is now. On Nov 8, 2026, [14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) for 2027-28 were sold out, the big houses among them. Set group size first: per-bed medians fall from $2,025 for a 1-bedroom alone to $800 in a 6-bedroom. Downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing lease into spring, so waiting is fine there. A backup Cornell room costs [$50 to cancel through Jan 31 and $500 from Feb 1](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). The costliest mistake: a rushed group that shrinks and leaves the rest owing the whole rent. Full plan: housing for sophomores.
```

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
Your lease most likely ends July 31. In the City of Ithaca your landlord owes you [120 days' written notice](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/) before renewing, showing or re-letting the unit, unless you initialed a bolded waiver on page one. A rent increase of 5% or more needs [30, 60 or 90 days' notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C), by how long you have lived there. Under Good Cause, a rise above the lower of 10% or 5% plus inflation is presumed unreasonable, but buildings with a 2009-or-later certificate of occupancy are exempt. Put repair requests in writing. At move-out, the deposit or an itemized list is due within 14 days. More in renewals, repairs and deposits.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer"
Entering as a sophomore, you live on campus for one academic year; a spring transfer stays that spring plus the next year, per [Cornell's transfer page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Entering as a junior or senior, or with two years on campus elsewhere, you are exempt, but you must request the exemption each year. You arrive after the Collegetown rush, so shop the leftover market: downtown per-bed medians of $992.50 for a 2-bedroom and $795 for a 3-bedroom, sublets and Lansing complexes. Your free first-year TCAT pass makes distance cheap. More in [Cornell transfer housing](/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/).
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
You do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment, per [Cornell International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). New York caps the deposit plus any rent paid in advance at one month's rent, so a demand for months up front runs into the law. Signing from abroad, check a City address on Cornell's safety tool and ask the City about its Certificate of Compliance before you pay. A landlord dispute does not affect your visa status, per [Cornell Graduate School staff](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). More in renting as an international student.
```

About 95% of graduate and professional students live off campus, per [Cornell's grad housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). ## Stage 1, years one and two: who may sign a lease

**The rule.** Cornell's two-year requirement covers full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-years, per its [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). Year one is a residence hall. Year two is a hall or approved affiliated housing: the eight university-owned co-ops, recognized fraternity and sorority houses, and houses run by a registered student organization. Cornell says first- and second-years [should not sign a lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process) without an approved exemption. A signed lease does not release you from the requirement.

**The exemptions:**

- Married students.
- Veterans discharged other than dishonorably, including service in a home country's military; ROTC does not count.
- Students coming to Ithaca with legal dependents.
- Students 21 or older by the start of the academic year of the exemption.
- Transfers with two years on campus elsewhere, or entering as a junior or senior.
- Commuters who have lived at least six months with a custodial parent or guardian within 25 miles of campus.

Request one in the Housing application while the exemption term is open, and not after you have signed a Cornell housing license. Documents may be required, and each exemption lasts one academic year. For 2026-27, every first- and second-year had to file the application by April 6, 2026, chapter-house residents included. For 2027-28, the application opens Feb 1, 2027 and closes [April 4 at 11:59 p.m.](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores), which is also the deadline for second-year exemption requests.

```tool can-i-live-off-campus-cornell-quiz```

**Co-ops and Greek houses** count as year-two housing. The eight co-ops hold 10 to 35 members each and select in [early November and early February](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing), each by its own process. A co-op double costs $3,487 to $4,981 a semester for 2026-27, against $6,872 for a hall double. A sophomore in a chapter house must be a member when the housing application is filed; in-house residents affirmed by March 15, 2026. Greek rent [covers the building's insurance, not your belongings](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities), so a renters policy still matters. More in Cornell co-ops and Greek houses and who can live off campus.

**Transfers** run on their own clock. New transfers live on campus for one academic year, and spring transfers stay for that spring plus the following year, per the [transfer page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). A spring transfer's first lease is about 18 months away. A new transfer cannot cancel a Cornell license unless exempt or accepted into affiliated housing before the Spring Housing Selection deadline.

## Stage 2, August to November: the Collegetown rush

A sophomore is here now. The requirement ends after year two, so a lease for junior year is yours to sign. If you want Collegetown, this month:

1. Agree on who you will live with, and how many.
2. Price units per bed at that size, against $1,145 a month.
3. Decide between Collegetown now and downtown, Fall Creek or Lansing later.
4. Check the building, read the lease (Stage 3), then sign.

### How early the popular units go

In nine signings the Sun reported from 2017 to 2025, all but one fallback came 8 to 16 months before an August move-in, mostly 10 to 11; our count from six stories. Ithaca Renting opened 2020-21 leasing on [Sept 12, 2019, after an overnight line](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/). A Sun columnist who signed in [October of sophomore year](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/) wrote of being late by Collegetown standards. The Ivy, a 52-unit building, [leased nearly all its units before construction finished](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026). On November 8, 2026, [14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) for 2027-28 were sold out, including the 8-bedroom at 105 Dryden Court and the 14-bedroom at 214 Dryden Rd.

The other side: in 2019 Off-Campus Living's manager called "you must sign the day you arrive" [a myth](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/). Both hold. Popular Collegetown units go early; downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing, sublets and grad housing keep supply much later. If you want a big Collegetown house or a new building, sign by November. If you want to save money or are unsure of your housemates, spring is a real option. More in [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).

First ask whether a price is per bed or per unit. Student buildings mostly quote per bed per month; aggregators and most 1-bedroom listings quote per unit; Cornell co-ops quote per semester. The medians below come from [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) for 2027-28, read Nov 8, 2026. They are one landlord's asking rents, furnished, heat included, electricity and parking extra.

| 4-bedroom | $1,246.25 | $14,955 | +$1,211 |

At the top, the Sun reported The Ivy's 3-bedrooms at $5,500 to $5,800 a month, $1,833 to $1,933 per person. More in Collegetown rent by unit size.

Size sets the risk too. Most local leases bind housemates ["jointly and severally"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): each owes the whole rent and damages if another stops paying. In 2020, Dery wrote that [large groups are likely to lose people](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus) and that individuals and small groups face higher prices.

Example: six friends plan a 6-bedroom at the $800 median. Two drop out, and the four left take a 4-bedroom at $1,246.25. Each pays $446.25 more a month, $5,355 more a year. If they stay in the 6-bedroom, they owe the two missing shares between them. Before signing, run Cornell's housemate questions: study hours, guests, cleaning, who handles money (student or parent), what happens if someone goes abroad. Agree that last one in writing. More in roommates and joint liability.

### Is it cheaper than the dorm?

Cornell's 2026-27 double is [$6,872 a semester, $13,744 a year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), including gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month. Over a 12-month lease that is $1,145 a month; over 10 months, $1,374. Living on campus also means a [required meal plan](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy); off campus, groceries replace it. Only big shared setups beat it on rent: 5- and 6-bedrooms, shared-suite rooms, downtown 2- and 3-bedroom shares and the co-ops.

Then add what the double includes. EnergySage puts the average Ithaca household electric bill at [about $280 a month](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/), a self-selected average that likely includes electric heat. **Example:** four friends compare a Collegetown 4-bedroom with two downtown 2-bedroom shares for 2027-28. In Collegetown, each pays the $1,246.25 median, $14,955 a year. The catch: a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk or a bus, and downtown rows that are mostly 2026-27 leftovers. More in is off-campus cheaper than a dorm and what a month off campus costs.

### Should you keep a Cornell room as a backup?

Cornell's room selection for 2026-27 ran [Oct 2 to 8, 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed). A returning junior or senior who cancels a 2027-28 room pays, per the [2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028): $50 through Jan 31, $500 from Feb 1 to Mar 31, $1,000 from Apr 1 to Jun 30, $2,000 from Jul 1 to Aug 15, then 50% of the fall room rate. ## Stage 3, the week you sign: the lease, the fees and the law

### Where the listings are

Cornell's [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) runs on College Pads, open only to people who prove Cornell affiliation. Off-Campus Living also runs an email list for housing, roommates and sublets; you join by emailing with the subject "Join" and a blank body. Admitted students without a NetID forward their acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu. Ithacarents.com takes posts only from Landlords Association of Tompkins County members. On any listing, check per bed or per unit, 10 or 12 months, the dates and which of heat, electricity, internet, trash, furniture and parking are included. More in where to find listings.

### Check the building before you pay

For a City address, ask the Building Division about the [Certificate of Compliance](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) at 607-274-6508. Then look the address up on the [Cornell and City safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments), which shows sprinklers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and construction type. It does not rate the landlord, and it covers City rentals only. In 2018, tenants at 117 Thurston Ave learned [after signing](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/) that its certificate had expired in 2015. They then waited 53 days from their first complaint to a fixed heater.

Cornell's [lease page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease) lists questions for a landlord: years in business, who repairs, the emergency contact, how long the last tenants stayed. Call a past tenant about repairs and the deposit. In a new building, ask the plan if it opens late: in 2016, Fontana left [more than 50 students with Aug 1 leases](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless) without an apartment into September.

Get every promise in writing. Before you sign, check the sublet clause, auto-renew terms, snow and trash duties, parking and the occupancy limit.

### The law in one table

New York law is better for tenants than most students assume. This page is general information, not legal advice.

| Item | The rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Security deposit | Deposit plus any rent advance at most one month's rent; pet deposits count | [GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) |
| Application fee | Banned | [RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) |
| Background or credit check | Lesser of actual cost or $20; waived with a check from the past 30 days | RPL 238-a |
| Late fee | Only after rent is more than 5 days late; at most the lesser of $50 or 5% of rent | RPL 238-a |
| Cleaning fee or non-refundable deposit | Not allowed | [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) |
| Move-in inspection | Landlord must offer one before you move in; defects you both list cannot come out of the deposit | GOL 7-108 |
| Deposit return | Within 14 days of move-out with an itemized statement, or the landlord forfeits the right to keep any | GOL 7-108 |

### The 120-day waiver box

The City rule took effect May 31, 2022, and covers leases of nine months or longer, as WSKG reported. The landlord's written notice must come [at least 120 days before](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/) it renews your lease or advertises the unit to new renters. The rule's sponsor, Patrick Mehler, counted from the lease start: a lease starting June 1 means deciding about renewal [by October 1](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/), and an August lease gives you until December. A landlord skips the notice only with a waiver explicitly written, bolded and initialed on the lease's first page. Mehler wrote that a discussion of why you are waiving the days should be your first question about any rental. More in the 120-day renewal rule.

## Stage 4, winter to summer: the later market

Most of the market is not Collegetown, and it moves later.

**Grad and professional students.** A grad guide says look [March to April for an August start](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html), with a few places left in June and July. It puts Fall Creek about 15 minutes by bus from Hollister Hall and Lansing about 20. Hasbrouck takes rolling applications. By our arithmetic, its unfurnished 1-bedroom is $16,980 a year, $7,776 below the [RentCafe Ithaca 1-bedroom average](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) of $2,063 a month, with utilities included. More in where grad students live.

**Transfers and late arrivals** shop the leftovers: downtown "available now" units, sublets and Lansing complexes. In November 2026, Lansing West offered rent free until January 2027 to applicants by Nov 14 who move in by Dec 1.

**International students.** Visa appointments cannot come more than 120 days before your program starts, per [International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students), so many leases are signed from abroad. If you cannot travel home over a break, you may ask [housing@cornell.edu](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources) about on-campus housing. Ithaca Renting includes heat and hot water at all its properties; elsewhere, ask. More in renting as an international student.

**Families.** Married students and students with dependents are exempt from the undergraduate rule, renewed each year. Hasbrouck family units run $1,475 to $1,530 a month on 12-month contracts, two adults at most. Example: on a private 2-bedroom at [RentCafe's Ithaca average](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) of $2,459, the first month plus a one-month deposit is $4,918 at signing. Spouses and partners with a CU ID can buy the OmniRide pass. More in housing for students with families.

**Visiting scholars and short stays.** The Sun reported that visiting scholars have [no guaranteed housing](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell) and that Cornell points them to the International Scholar House, a month-to-month furnished 5-bedroom. Ithaca Renting lists "Spring/Fall Semester 2027" rows. Avoid a 12-month lease for a six-month stay: you owe the rest unless it is sublet or re-rented. More in furnished short-term housing.

**Summer.** Most leases end July 31, but [Dryden South's](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961) all end May 31, 2027. Residence halls closed at [2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), and Cornell housing offers no storage. Take a sublet only with a written sublease and, in a building of four or more units, the landlord's written consent. More in summer housing and lease gaps.

## Stage 6, living there: repairs, renewal and rent increases

### Repairs

Put every repair request in writing with your name, address and the date; certified mail gives proof. Ithaca's heat season runs Sept 15 to May 31, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). From 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. it must be at least 68F inside when it is below 55F outside; overnight, at least 62F. Report bed bugs in writing and do not treat them yourself; the landlord must exterminate.

If the landlord ignores you, file with the City Building Division (607-274-6508), the Town of Ithaca (607-273-1783) or the Town of Lansing (607-533-7054). Courts typically cut rent about 10% to 20% for minor problems, about 30% for moderate ones and 50% to 60% for the worst. Withholding rent invites a nonpayment case, so call 607-301-1560 first. Since January 2024, a tenant who has lived in the unit 30 days and sent written notice can ask a court to order repairs. More in when a landlord won't fix heat or mold.

### Renewal and rent increases

Before you answer any renewal offer, check whether you initialed the 120-day waiver. State law then sets the notice for a non-renewal, or for a renewal with a rent increase of 5% or more, under [RPL 226-c](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C). Late notice keeps you on your existing terms until the period runs out.

| Time in the unit, or lease length | Notice owed |
|---|---|
| Under one year, and no lease of a year or more | 30 days |
| One to two years, or a lease of one to two years | 60 days |
| More than two years, or a lease of two years or more | 90 days |

Ithaca's Common Council voted 8-2 to [opt into Good Cause Eviction](https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections) in July 2024. A covered tenant has a right to renew. A rise above the lower of 10% or 5% plus inflation is [presumed unreasonable](https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction), which is not a hard cap. Among student housing, only university-run dorms are exempt as a category. What removes most student units: a certificate of occupancy from 2009 or later, exempt for 30 years under [RPL 214](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/214), and landlords who own one unit statewide. Owner-occupied buildings of 10 or fewer units are exempt too. The Ithacan, built in 2023, is exempt until 2053, the [Sun reported](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing). More in Good Cause Eviction in Ithaca.

```tool ithaca-good-cause-coverage-checker```

A good-faith complaint is protected. A landlord who refuses to renew, or raises rent unreasonably, within a year of one can face a [retaliation defense](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal), except in owner-occupied buildings with fewer than four units.

### Trash and snow

Trash goes out no more than 24 hours before pickup, and couches, mattresses and appliances may not be stored outdoors. Owners must clear the sidewalk [within 24 hours](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance) after snow starts falling, though a lease can pass that job to you. More in Ithaca renter rules.

## Stage 7, leaving mid-lease: sublets, time abroad and breaking a lease

You cannot simply walk away from a lease. Try these in order.

1. **A written early-termination agreement**, with the move-out date and what you owe. A termination fee may not hold up, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).
2. **A sublet.** In a building with four or more units, [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) lets you sublet with written consent that cannot be unreasonably withheld; in three or fewer units, the lease controls. Send the request by certified mail: the term, the subtenant's name and addresses, your reason, your address during the term, co-tenant and guarantor consent, and a copy of the sublease. The landlord may ask for more within 10 days and must answer within 30; silence is consent. You stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage.
3. **An assignment.** It generally ends your liability, but the landlord may refuse it without cause.
4. **Leaving, as a last resort.** Under [RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E), the landlord must try in good faith to re-rent at the lower of market or lease rent. Once the unit is re-rented, your debt stops growing.

**Example:** you mail a sublet request on March 1. The landlord may ask for more information within 10 days, so by March 11. If it asks nothing, it must answer within 30 days of the request, by March 31. Silence past that date counts as consent.

Housemates stay jointly liable for your share unless the landlord releases you, and [Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) will not intervene in a mid-year lease break. Example: a housemate leaves a 4-bedroom at the $1,246.25 median for a five-month semester abroad with no sublet; the share left to cover is about $6,231. Domestic violence victims can end a lease on 30 days' written notice, and so can service members called to active duty.

Cornell license holders cancel penalty-free for study away, leave, an internship or graduation by June 15 for fall or Dec 1 for spring, per the [2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027). Later requests cost $500. More in breaking or leaving a lease and [how to sublet](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/).

## Stage 8, move-out: the inspection and the 14-day deposit

Cornell's [move-out timeline](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/moving-and-out) has two checkpoints. Two weeks before: confirm the time, schedule the final inspection, reread the lease, change your address. One week before: schedule NYSEG and internet shutoff, clean, and give a forwarding address in writing.

Ask for the move-out inspection. Under [GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) it happens between two weeks and one week before the end, with 48 hours' written notice, and the landlord lists planned charges so you can fix them. Photograph every room on the way out. Do not leave a couch or mattress at the curb.

If money is withheld unfairly, send a demand letter, then try mediation or small claims at Ithaca City Court, up to $5,000. A willful violation can cost the landlord up to twice the deposit. ## Common mistakes

- **Signing a lease as a first- or second-year before an exemption is approved.** You are billed for the full Cornell year, $13,744 for a double, on top of rent.
- **Signing with a group formed in the first weeks of fall.** If it shrinks, those left owe the whole rent; a 6-bedroom group that ends up in a 4-bedroom pays $5,355 more a person a year.
- **Comparing a per-bed price with a per-unit price.** RentCafe's $3,414 Collegetown 2-bedroom is a whole-unit rent; Ithaca Renting's $1,377.50 is per bed.
- **Comparing 12 months of rent with Cornell's academic-year rate.** The double is $1,145 a month only over 12 months, and aid does not cover summer rent.
- **Keeping a backup Cornell room past Jan 31.** The fee jumps from $50 to $500, then $1,000 and $2,000.
- **Skipping the Certificate of Compliance and safety-tool check.** The 117 Thurston tenants found an expired certificate after signing and waited 53 days for heat.
- **Paying fees the law bans:** an application fee, a cleaning fee, a non-refundable deposit, or a deposit plus "last month" above one month's rent.
- **Initialing the 120-day waiver without asking why.** You give up notice before your unit is shown or re-let.
- **Withholding rent to force a repair** without advice. It invites a nonpayment case that starts with a 14-day demand.
- **Walking away from a lease.** Housemates stay liable, aid will not step in, and a sublet without the written steps is a substantial breach.
- **Skipping the move-out inspection and photos.** The landlord's list of charges becomes the only record.

## FAQ

**Can I live off campus as a sophomore at Cornell?** Only with an approved exemption, or in affiliated housing such as a co-op or recognized Greek house. Otherwise you are [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) even if you rent elsewhere.

**When should I start looking for a junior-year apartment?** For popular Collegetown units, in the fall of sophomore year: by Nov 8, 2026, [14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) for 2027-28 were sold out. Downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing lease into spring.

**How much is rent near Cornell?** At one landlord's 2027-28 Collegetown prices, a bed runs from an $800 median in a 6-bedroom to $2,025 for a 1-bedroom alone, per month. RentCafe's large-building average for a Collegetown 1-bedroom was [$2,342 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/) on Oct 30, 2026.

**Is off-campus housing cheaper than a dorm?** Sometimes. Under $1,145 a month per person on 12 months beats the $13,744 double. In Collegetown that means a 5- or 6-bedroom or bigger, or a room in a shared suite; a downtown 2- or 3-bedroom share also comes in under it.

**How much can a landlord ask for a deposit?** One month's rent at most, counting any rent paid in advance and any pet deposit, under [GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). It must come back within 14 days of move-out with an itemized statement.

**Can my landlord charge an application fee?** No. New York bans [application fees](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) and caps a background-check fee at $20 or actual cost, whichever is less.

**Does my landlord have to let me sublet?** In a building with four or more units, consent cannot be unreasonably withheld, and 30 days of silence counts as consent under [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). In three or fewer units, your lease decides.

**Is student housing exempt from Good Cause?** Only university-run dormitories. A private student apartment can still be exempt for another reason, most often a certificate of occupancy from 2009 or later.

**Do students ride TCAT free?** First-year degree students ride free all day. Everyone else rides free [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and on weekends](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), and otherwise pays $1.50 a ride or $200 a year for OmniRide.

**Does financial aid cover off-campus rent?** Need is calculated the same way, and you get a refund for living costs if aid exceeds Bursar charges. Aid does not cover [deposits, first and last month's rent, or rent in unregistered months](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus).
