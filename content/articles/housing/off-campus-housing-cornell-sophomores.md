```glance
- You cannot sign a lease for sophomore year without an approved exemption; Cornell bills the full year ($13,744 for a 2026-27 double) anyway.
- Popular Collegetown units for junior year go in September to November of sophomore year; 14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows for 2027-28 were sold out on Nov 8, 2026.
- Decide group size first: Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians run from $800 a bed in a 6BR to $2,025 for a 1BR alone.
- Downtown beds from the same landlord cost about 20% to 28% less, and that market keeps units into spring.
- A backup Cornell room costs $50 to cancel through Jan 31, then $500 from Feb 1.
- At signing, refuse application and cleaning fees and any deposit above one month's rent.
```

As a sophomore you must live in Cornell or affiliated housing this year, so the work now is the junior-year lease, and for Collegetown that lease is signed this fall: on November 8, 2026, [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) already showed 14 of its 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 as sold out. Settle your group and neighborhood by October, and sign this fall if you want a popular Collegetown unit; downtown and Fall Creek can wait for spring. If you keep a Cornell room as a backup, sign the lease by January 31, when [cancelling the room costs $50](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028).

```reader "If you're set on Collegetown"
Start in September. In our count of nine dated signings in Cornell Daily Sun stories from 2017 to 2025, all but one fallback fell 8 to 16 months before an August move-in, most 10 to 11. A columnist who signed in October of sophomore year [called himself late](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/) by Collegetown standards. The big houses go first: 214 Dryden Rd's 14-bedroom and 105 Dryden Court's 8-bedroom were [sold out for 2027-28](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) by Nov 8, 2026. The costly mistake is speed over people: signing with a group you barely know, then carrying their share when it shrinks.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Your two levers are group size and distance. A 6BR Collegetown bed at [Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 median](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) is $800 a month, $9,600 over 12 months. That is $4,144 under the [$13,744 Cornell double](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). A [downtown 3BR share](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) from the same landlord had a median of $795 a bed. A co-op double costs $6,974 to $9,962 for the 2026-27 academic year. Budget the first month and the deposit in cash: [Cornell aid does not cover](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) deposits, first or last month's rent, or summer rent.
```

```reader "If you're keeping a co-op or Greek house as the fallback"
Both count as Cornell-recognized housing. [Co-op selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing) starts in early November and again in early February, and each of the eight houses runs its own process; contact co-ops@cornell.edu or 607-255-3445. A 2026-27 co-op double runs [$3,487 to $4,981 a semester](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). The risk is a plan that collapses late. In a [2018 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), a group whose Greek housing fell through started hunting in February. They found a 14-person house that a resident described as falling apart at the seams.
```

```reader "If you're the parent paying"
Compare like with like. The $13,744 double covers two semesters and [includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). A 12-month lease at Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians costs $9,600 a year per person in a 6BR, $14,955 in a 4BR and $24,300 for a 1BR alone, before electricity. On most local leases [every housemate is liable](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) for the whole rent and damages, so a group that loses a member costs the rest. Without a NetID you can still call the City Building Division at [607-274-6508](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) to check a building's Certificate of Compliance.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year planning ahead"
Your sophomore year is settled: a residence hall, a co-op or a [recognized Greek house](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). The 2026-27 housing application was due April 6, 2026, and you file it even if you expect a chapter house. What you can do now is notice who you would actually live with, because the junior-year group forms in the first weeks of sophomore fall. A tip repeated in three Sun stories from 2018 to 2021: sign only with people you have lived near. One sophomore in a [2021 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/) chose Flora Rose House because she found the off-campus search too difficult.
```

## Why you can't sign for this year

Cornell's two-year rule covers [full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). In year two you may live in a residence hall, a recognized fraternity or sorority house, one of the university-owned co-ops, or other approved affiliated housing.

Cornell says first- and second-years [should not sign a lease unless they have an approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process). A lease does not release you from the rule. Students who neither meet the requirement nor get an exemption [will still be billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing), which is $13,744 for a 2026-27 double, on top of the rent.

The rule ends after year two. So the lease that matters is the junior-year one, and Collegetown fills it while you are still a sophomore. Exemptions exist (21 or older by the start of the year, married, a veteran, with dependents, or a local commuter), and they are requested in the Housing application during its open window, not after you sign a Cornell license.

**August and September.** The rush starts on arrival. Ithaca Renting opened its 2020-21 leasing at [9 a.m. on September 12, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/), after students slept on the sidewalk. Seven weeks into fall 2021, a landlord told students a unit was [already gone](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/).

**October.** Prelims arrive, and landlords told the Sun in 2018 that rising juniors [want to sign before them](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/). Cornell's fall room selection for 2026-27 ran [October 2 to 8, 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed). This is the month to choose: lease, Cornell room, or both for now.

**November.** One Collegetown developer said in 2016 that he usually rents [most units by the end of November](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/). The first co-op round starts in early November.

**January to April.** January 31 is the last $50 day to drop a Cornell room. Final 2027-28 Cornell rates are [posted in March 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). Downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing searches run through spring.

Not everyone has to rush. In 2019, Off-Campus Living's manager called "you must lock housing the day you return" [a myth](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/) that students and landlords both spread. Both things hold: Collegetown's popular units go early, and the wider market keeps supply much later.

## Pick the group size first

Per-bed rent falls steeply as the unit gets bigger. These are our medians of the midpoints of 38 priced 2027-28 rows on [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), read November 8, 2026. They include heat and hot water and furniture; most tenants pay electricity, and parking is extra.

| Unit size | Per bed a month (median) | Per person, 12 months | Against the $13,744 double |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | $1,765 | $21,180 | $7,436 more |
| 1BR alone | $2,025 | $24,300 | $10,556 more |
| 2BR | $1,377.50 | $16,530 | $2,786 more |
| 4BR | $1,246.25 | $14,955 | $1,211 more |
| 5BR (mean of 2 rows) | $1,102.50 | $13,230 | $514 less |
| 6BR | $800 | $9,600 | $4,144 less |
| Room in a shared suite | $895 | $10,740 | $3,004 less |

The double covers two semesters with utilities; the lease runs 12 months and does not. Over 12 months the double works out to $1,145 a month, or $1,374 if you sublet the summer and count only 10 months (our division of $13,744).

Example: a group of six plans a 6BR at the $800 median. Two drop out in November and the four left move to a 4BR at $1,246.25. Each pays $446.25 more a month, $5,355 more over the year.

That example is why the group comes before the apartment. Every housemate on most local leases is bound [jointly and severally](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): each owes the housemates' rent and damages for the whole term. A tenant who pays more than their share can [sue a co-tenant for contribution](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease), which is a lawsuit, not a refund.

In a 2020 Sun column, Dery wrote that large groups are [likely to lose people](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus) and that individuals and small groups face higher prices. Run Cornell's housemate questions before anyone signs: study hours, guests, cleanliness, whether the student or a parent handles money, and what happens if someone studies abroad.

Where to look: Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace shows listings only to people who prove Cornell affiliation, and the Off-Campus Living email list needs a NetID; both are on Cornell's [listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Ithaca Renting publishes full per-bed price lists. Ask every landlord whether a price is per bed or per unit.

## Collegetown now, or downtown later

Collegetown is up to a 15-minute walk; downtown and Fall Creek are a [20 to 30 minute uphill walk](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) or a bus. The same landlord prices the two very differently. These are our medians from [Ithaca Renting's downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) and its Collegetown page, both read November 8, 2026.

| Per bed a month | Downtown median | Collegetown median | Downtown saves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | $1,395 | $1,765 | $370 (about 21%) |
| 1BR | $1,625 | $2,025 | $400 (about 20%) |
| 2BR | $992.50 | $1,377.50 | $385 (about 28%) |

Two cautions. The downtown rows were mostly 2026-27 leftovers, possibly discounted, against 2027-28 Collegetown prices. And downtown includes heat, water and trash, but electricity only at the Colonial Building, Commons Studios and Whiton Hall.

Example: two friends compare a Collegetown 2BR at $1,377.50 a bed with a downtown 2BR at $992.50. Downtown saves each of them $385 a month, $4,620 over 12 months. Daytime buses then cost money: after year one, Cornell students ride TCAT free only [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and on weekends](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), and the OmniRide pass is $200 a year. The net saving is about $4,420 each.

![Dryden Road in Collegetown in 1985](2.webp)

Our advice: sign in the fall for Collegetown if walking matters most or your group is large and settled. Wait for spring and look downtown or in Fall Creek if a lower rent matters more, or your group is still forming. The comparison is on [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/).

## Keep a Cornell room as a backup?

You can hold a junior-year Cornell room while you look, then cancel it. In 2025, rising juniors and seniors [could cancel before check-in](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed). The price climbs on a fixed schedule under the [2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028).

| Cancel a returning junior's license | Fee |
|---|---|
| Through Jan 31 | $50 |
| Feb 1 to Mar 31 | $500 |
| Apr 1 to Jun 30 | $1,000 |
| Jul 1 to Aug 15 | $2,000 |
| After Aug 15, or no check-in by the first day of classes | 50% of the fall room rate |

Example: you take a Cornell double in October, then sign a Collegetown lease in December. Cancelling in January costs $50. Sign the same lease in March instead and the cancellation costs $500.

So a backup is cheap only if the lease is signed by January 31.

## What to refuse at signing

New York law sets the limits, and they apply in Collegetown as anywhere. Bring this table.

| At signing | The rule |
|---|---|
| Application or processing fee | [Not allowed](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) |
| Background or credit check | Actual cost or $20, whichever is less; waived if you bring a check from the past 30 days |
| Security deposit | The deposit plus any rent advance [cannot exceed one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108); no last month's rent on top |
| Non-refundable or cleaning fee | Unlawful |
| Late fee in the lease | Only after rent is 5 days late, capped at $50 or 5% of rent, whichever is less |

Expect the first month's rent and the deposit at signing, and none of it comes from aid.

Example: a junior-year lease in a 4BR at the $1,246.25 median. A deposit at the one-month cap is $1,246.25, so with the first month the payment at signing is up to $2,492.50 per person.

Get a written receipt for every payment. Before move-in, the landlord must offer an inspection; if you take it, defects on the signed record cannot come out of your deposit.

**The 120-day waiver.** In the City of Ithaca, a landlord must give current tenants [120 days' written notice](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/) before renewing, showing the unit or signing new tenants. A lease can waive this only with a statement that is written, bolded and initialed on page one. The rule's sponsor, Council member Patrick Mehler, wrote that [why you are waiving the days](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/) should be your first question. The waiver matters next year, when you decide whether to renew. Details are on [the 120-day rule](/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/).

The Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline, [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/), gives free advice before you sign.

## Check the building before you sign

For a City of Ithaca address, ask the City Building Division about the Certificate of Compliance: [607-274-6508 or ibd@cityofithaca.org](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Then look the address up on the Cornell and City [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) at safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu. It shows sprinklers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and construction type. It does not rate the landlord, and neither check covers the Town of Ithaca, Lansing or Cayuga Heights.

The cost of skipping it: in 2018 the tenants at 117 Thurston Ave learned after signing that the certificate [had expired in 2015](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/), then waited 53 days for heat. Visit in person, check two exits and the smoke detectors, and do not trust fresh paint.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing a lease for sophomore year itself.** Without an approved exemption, Cornell bills the full year anyway: $13,744 for a double, plus your rent.
- **Signing with a group formed in week two.** If six becomes four, each person in a 4BR pays $446.25 a month more than in a 6BR, $5,355 a year, and everyone stays liable.
- **Comparing a per-bed price with a per-unit price, or 12 months with two semesters.** The $13,744 double is an academic year with utilities; a lease is 12 months, mostly without electricity.
- **Holding a backup Cornell room into February.** Cancelling costs $500 from February 1 and $2,000 from July 1.
- **Paying a banned fee.** An application fee, a cleaning fee or a deposit above one month's rent is not allowed under New York law.
- **Skipping the Certificate of Compliance call.** The 117 Thurston tenants found an expired certificate after signing and went 53 days without heat.

## FAQ

**Can I tour or sign for junior year while I live in a dorm?** Yes. The rule limits where you live as a sophomore, not what you sign for the year after; only a lease for the current year needs an [approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process).

**Does financial aid change if I move off campus?** [Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) calculates need the same way off campus and stops billing housing through the Bursar. It does not cover deposits, first or last month's rent, or summer rent.

**What if someone in my group goes abroad junior spring?** They stay on the lease and liable unless the landlord agrees otherwise. The routes are a sublet, an assignment or an agreed early exit, covered in [breaking or leaving an Ithaca lease](/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).

**Will my parent need to co-sign?** Not confirmed: whether Collegetown landlords require guarantors is not published, and the [Cornell Law lease guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease) does not address guarantor terms. Ask the landlord before your group commits.

**Can I leave my sophomore Cornell room for a co-op or Greek house?** A second-year may cancel a Cornell license if [accepted to a Greek house, affiliated house or co-op](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) before the Spring Housing Selection deadline. Otherwise second-years cannot cancel.

**Is a 10-month lease available?** Some Collegetown units run 10 months from August 1; [Ithaca Renting's](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) leases are 12 months. Ask before comparing prices.
