```glance
- Co-op selection, called the mosey, runs in early November and early February; each house sets its own events.
- A chapter house takes members only, and you must belong when you file the housing application.
- Confirm a co-op or chapter plan by March 15, then still file the housing application by April 4.
- A co-op double costs $6,974 a year against $13,744 for a hall double; chapter rents vary by house.
```

Co-ops pick members through a house-by-house selection called the mosey, with house events and then a lottery. A chapter house takes members only, and you must belong when you apply. A chapter house counts as second-year housing if you confirm the plan on your Cornell housing application by March 15 ([Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

## Who can live in a co-op or chapter house?

Cornell's two-year residential requirement covers full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students. Second-years can meet it in a residence hall, a university-owned co-op, a recognized fraternity or sorority house, or other approved affiliated housing ([Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). First-years live in residence halls. If you think you qualify for an exemption instead, start with the [exemptions guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) or the eligibility quiz below.

## How do you get into a Cornell co-op?

Cornell owns eight co-ops: Prospect of Whitby, 660 Stewart, 302 Wait Avenue, Triphammer Cooperative, Von Cramm, Wait Terrace, Wari Cooperative and Watermargin. Each holds 10 to 35 members and runs its own selection ([Cooperative Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). The questions go to co-ops@cornell.edu or 607-255-3445.

The Cornell Daily Sun described entry in 2023 as a mosey: house events, then a lottery weighted by financial need and diversity factors ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/06/underserved-and-minority-students-find-belonging-and-support-in-co-op-housing/)). Cornell's co-op office suggested "mosey" as a gentler word than the "rush" that Greek organizations use ([Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/co-op-mosey-open-house-spring2024)). In the spring 2024 mosey, the open house was Sunday, Feb. 11, 2024, and decisions came Feb. 28 ([Co-op Mosey 2024](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/news-events/news/co-op-mosey-2024)). Most houses also fill open rooms on a rolling basis after that.

Triphammer asks applicants to attend two events and one cook group before the lottery ([Triphammer Cooperative](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/triphammer-cooperative)). Watermargin uses a random lottery to keep its mix of residents varied ([Watermargin](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/watermargin)). Wari has used interviews ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/11/open-house-day-highlights-unique-qualities-of-cornell-co-ops/)).

A co-op room comes with a license, which is Cornell's permission to live in the room, not a lease. For 2026-27 it requires full-time registration of at least 12 credit hours and carries no security or key deposit ([Co-op terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/co-op-housing-2026-2027-terms-and-conditions)).

## How do you get into a fraternity or sorority house as a sophomore?

Join the chapter first. A sophomore must be a member when the housing application is filed, and the chapter must be recognized by the university ([Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). A recognized chapter earns automatic approval if it has a live-in advisor and four upper-level officers by Jan. 1 each year.

Three steps follow:

1. Confirm your plan to live in the house on the housing application by the first deadline. Members whose chapters confirm them skip the April room selection for rising sophomores.
2. File the housing application anyway. For 2027-28 it opens Feb. 1 and is due April 4 at 11:59 p.m. ([Spring Housing Selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). Miss the confirmation and you finish the requirement in a Cornell hall.
3. Expect to live in. Nearly all chapters require members to live in for a semester or two, and members who live out can owe a "parlor" fee, which is the charge for using the house without living there ([Affiliated Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/qualified-off-campus-housing)).

Three non-Greek houses also qualify as affiliated housing: Alpha Zeta, the Center for Jewish Living and Seal and Serpent ([Qualified Off-Campus Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/qualified-off-campus-housing)). Cornell checks them for insurance, sprinklers and yearly city inspections. The check does not apply to recognized fraternities and sororities ([Requirements](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)).

```reader "If you're a sophomore choosing between a chapter house and a Collegetown lease"
A Collegetown lease for sophomore year does not end your Cornell bill if you are still required to live on campus. See the [sophomore guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) before you sign anything.
```

## What does each option cost?

A co-op double is the cheapest approved bed Cornell owns. A chapter house costs about what a hall room plus a meal plan does, with a wide spread from house to house.

| Option, 2026-27 | Per semester | Per academic year |
|---|---|---|
| Co-op double, Red Bud in Von Cramm | $3,487 | $6,974 |
| Co-op double, Triphammer or Watermargin | $4,317 | $8,634 |
| Co-op double, 660 Stewart or Wari | $4,981 | $9,962 |
| Residence hall double | $6,872 | $13,744 |
| Chapter live-in charge, median of 13 chapters, spring 2026 | $7,400 | Not published |

Co-op rates come from [Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates); yearly figures are two semesters. Chapter data come from the [Organization Scorecard](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/organization-scorecard).

A co-op double saves $3,782 to $6,770 a year against the hall double. The hall rate includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet, but hall residents must also buy a meal plan, and Bear Traditional is $3,528 per semester ([Undergraduate Meal Plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Four co-ops run a house meal plan: Whitby, Triphammer, Watermargin and 660 Stewart. Ask each house what its plan costs before you budget. For the full ranking of low-cost options, see the [cheapest housing guide](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/).

Chapters publish their own rates. Cornell's scorecard lists each chapter's self-reported live-in housing and dining charge for one spring semester. Among the 34 chapters that list rates, nonzero live-in charges run from $2,000 at Delta Kappa Epsilon (spring 2026) to $14,673 at Alpha Xi Delta (spring 2025). Beta Theta Pi lists $0. Live-out fees run from $0 to $4,600 at Sigma Chi. The median live-out fee is $1,152 for 23 fraternities and $602 for 11 sororities ([Organization Scorecard](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/organization-scorecard)).

Set a median of $7,400 against a hall double plus Bear Traditional, which is $6,872 plus $3,528, or $10,400 per semester. Only Alpha Xi Delta and Sigma Pi exceed a hall double plus a meal plan. Rent pays for housekeeping, utilities, internet, repairs and building insurance, but not your belongings ([Financial Obligations](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities)). The full comparison with the dorm is in the [off-campus vs dorm guide](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

## Can you cancel your Cornell room if you get in?

A second-year who is accepted to a Greek house, affiliated house or one of the eight co-ops before the Spring Housing Selection deadline can cancel a Cornell license. First-years and new transfers cannot ([Academic Year Housing License 2027-2028](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). Juniors and seniors who hold a hall room pay a fee that rises through the year: $50 through Jan. 31, then $500 from Feb. 1, and more in later months. The calculator shows the fee on your date.

## What are the houses like?

Co-op residents describe a close community, and the Sun reported in 2023 that underserved and minority students found belonging there ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2023](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/06/underserved-and-minority-students-find-belonging-and-support-in-co-op-housing/)). The buildings are older. In October 2024 the Sun reported that floors had collapsed in four of the eight co-ops in recent years and that Watermargin needed about $500,000 for a roof ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/)). Cornell required co-ops to standardize room rates in the same period. Visit a house and ask about its floors and roof.

Chapter life changed in fall 2026. President Michael I. Kotlikoff canceled all parties and social events in Interfraternity Council houses for the rest of 2026-27, announced Oct. 13, 2026, pending a review of fraternity life ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/10/breaking-cornell-suspends-all-fraternity-parties-social-events-for-2026-27-school-year)). A chapter loses the right to house second-years if it loses recognition, so ask your chapter about its status in writing.

## What to do next

1. Choose co-ops or a chapter, and list the houses that fit your class year.
2. Email co-ops@cornell.edu for each house's round dates, or ask your chapter treasurer for rent, board, dues and the parlor fee in writing.
3. Put both deadlines in your calendar.
4. If neither works out, the lease timeline in the [Collegetown lease guide](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) shows when to start looking.
