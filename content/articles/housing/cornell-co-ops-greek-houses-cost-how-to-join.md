```glance
- A co-op double costs $3,487 to $4,981 per semester for 2026-27, or $6,974 to $9,962 a year, against $13,744 for a Cornell dorm double.
- Co-op selection starts in early November and early February; each of the eight houses runs its own process.
- Greek house costs are not published; rent covers housekeeping, utilities, internet, repairs and building insurance.
- A sophomore must be a chapter member when filing the housing application, and affirm living in by March 15 (2026 date).
- Every first- and second-year files the housing application anyway (April 6, 2026), even if a chapter house is the plan.
- A second-year accepted to a co-op or Greek house before the Spring Housing Selection deadline may cancel a Cornell license.
```

Cornell's eight co-ops are the cheapest housing that satisfies the two-year residential requirement: a double runs [$3,487 to $4,981 per semester for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), against $6,872 for a dorm double. You get in through each house's own selection, which starts in [early November and early February](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing). A fraternity or sorority house also counts for sophomore year, but only for members, and Cornell publishes no dollar figures for it.

```reader "If you're a first-year deciding where to spend sophomore year"
First-years live in residence halls. For year two you have three choices: a residence hall, one of the eight co-ops, or a recognized chapter house. The hall double is $13,744 a year; a co-op double is $6,974 to $9,962 (our arithmetic: two semesters at the [2026-27 rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Try the early November or early February co-op round, and email co-ops@cornell.edu with questions. For a chapter house, you must be a member when you file the housing application, then affirm in it that you will live in the house by March 15 (the [2026 date](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). File the application by the April deadline whatever you plan. The costly mistake: signing a Collegetown lease for sophomore year, which still leaves you billed for the full Cornell year.
```

```reader "If you're a low-budget student"
The co-ops are the cheapest Cornell-approved beds. The Red Bud double in Von Cramm is $3,487 a semester and its triple $2,765, per the [2026-27 rate page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). A full year in that double is $6,974, which is $6,770 below a dorm double. The Cornell Daily Sun reported in 2023 that co-op entry ends in a [lottery weighted by financial need and diversity factors](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/06/underserved-and-minority-students-find-belonging-and-support-in-co-op-housing/). Four houses have meal plans, so check whether yours does before you budget for food. Greek houses: Cornell says costs are generally below dorm or off-campus costs but gives no numbers, so ask the chapter for its rent, dues and fees in writing.
```

```reader "If you're a co-op or Greek-house resident"
Your rent covers more than an apartment's. Greek rent pays for [housekeeping, utilities, internet, repairs and building insurance](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities), but not your belongings, so a renters policy still matters. Nearly all chapters require living in for a semester or two, and members living out may owe a parlor fee. Co-ops are Cornell-owned and aging: the Sun reported in 2024 that [floors had collapsed in four of the eight houses](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/). Report problems early. If you leave the house for junior year, the Collegetown market moves in the fall; a senior told the Sun in 2018 that one email to a landlord through her sorority network was enough.
```

Co-op singles come to $7,898 to $12,028 a year, below the $15,566 hall single.

What the hall price covers matters for the comparison. Cornell's rate includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month, and hall residents must also buy a meal plan. The Sun put the West Campus unlimited House Meal Plan at [$6,434 a year in 2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/); that figure is old. Program houses are residence halls and share the hall rate table, so they cost the same as any other dorm.

### Greek house costs

Cornell does not publish chapter prices. The [financial obligations page](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities) says each chapter sets its own dues and fees. It says rent covers housekeeping, utilities, internet, repairs and insurance on the building, though not on personal belongings. Cornell claims costs are generally below residence-hall or off-campus costs, without numbers. The page points to a per-chapter fee scorecard that chapters fill in themselves.

Not confirmed: any chapter's rent in dollars. Ask the chapter treasurer for rent, board, dues and the parlor fee before you commit.

## How co-ops work and how to get in

The eight co-ops are Prospect of Whitby, 660 Stewart, 302 Wait Avenue, Triphammer, Von Cramm, Wait Terrace, Wari and Watermargin. Cornell owns them and lists them as [qualified second-year housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/qualified-off-campus-housing). Each has 10 to 35 members.

1. **Watch for the round.** Selection starts in [early November and early February](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing). Each house runs its own process, so ask each one for its dates.
2. **Go to the events.** The Sun described entry in 2023 as a "mosey": house events, then a [lottery weighted by financial need and diversity factors](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/06/underserved-and-minority-students-find-belonging-and-support-in-co-op-housing/).
3. **Ask the office.** The co-op office is at co-ops@cornell.edu and 607-255-3445.
4. **Cancel your Cornell license if you get in.** A second-year accepted to a co-op before the Spring Housing Selection deadline may [cancel a Cornell housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). First-years and new transfers cannot.

Not confirmed: which four co-ops have meal plans, and what the plans cost.

Some co-ops also take graduate students; Cornell's [graduate housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) says to contact the houses.

### What the houses are like

Residents describe a real community. The 2023 Sun piece focused on underserved and minority students who found belonging in the co-ops. The buildings are another matter. In October 2024 the Sun reported [floors had collapsed in four of the eight co-ops](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/) in recent years, and that Watermargin needed about $500,000 for a roof. The same article reported Cornell had raised and standardized co-op rates. Cornell told the Sun it provides dedicated maintenance staff and a financial advisor. Visit the house, and ask about its floors and roof, before you commit.

## How Greek houses work and how to get in

Chapter houses are self-governed. The building may belong to the university, an alumni group or a national organization, per Cornell's [qualified housing list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/qualified-off-campus-housing). Nearly all chapters require members to live in for a semester or two. Members who live elsewhere may owe a "parlor" or living-out fee.

The rules for a sophomore are strict. From the [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy):

- You must be a member of the fraternity or sorority at the time of the housing application.
- You affirm living in the house in the Housing Application by March 15 (2026 date). Members confirmed by their chapter skip the April Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores.
- You still complete the Housing Application. For 2026-27 it was due April 6, 2026, even for students expecting a chapter house.
- The chapter must be recognized. Chapters are auto-approved if they meet the recognition policy and have a live-in advisor and four upper-level officers by January 1.

A house that holds second-years must also meet Cornell's [minimum standards](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing). Officers live on the property, sprinklers run throughout, and the house carries liability insurance naming Cornell. A City of Ithaca property must hold a current Certificate of Compliance; for fraternities and sororities the [City certificate lasts one year](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance).

Three other recognized houses appear on Cornell's list: Alpha Zeta at 214 Thurston Avenue, the Center for Jewish Living at 106 West Avenue, and Seal and Serpent at 305 Thurston Avenue.

The March and April dates are the ones Cornell published for 2026-27. Check the residential policy each winter for the next year's dates.

If you hold a Cornell license and change plans, the timing sets the fee. Second-years accepted to a co-op or chapter house before the Spring Housing Selection deadline may cancel. Returning juniors and seniors pay [$50 through January 31, $500 from February 1 to March 31, $1,000 from April 1 to June 30 and $2,000 from July 1 to August 15](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028).

Mid-year moves are harder. Cornell releases a spring housing contract only for graduating, going abroad or leaving the university. Moving into a fraternity small residence is [not an extenuating circumstance](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities).

Example: a rising junior keeps a Cornell room as a backup and enters a co-op's early November round. Accepted in November, they cancel for $50 before January 31 and pay $8,634 for a year in a Triphammer double. If they wait for the February round, cancelling costs $500.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
A co-op is an alternative to the Collegetown rush. The rounds start in early November and early February. A year in a Triphammer double is $8,634. One landlord's 2027-28 Collegetown 6BR median is [$800 a bed a month](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), or $9,600 over 12 months. Keep a Cornell room as a backup only until January 31, when cancelling costs $50; from February 1 it is $500. If a Greek house is your plan, line up a fallback: in 2018 the Sun reported a group whose Greek housing plans fell through and that [started a house hunt in February](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), ending in a 14-person house a resident called "falling apart at the seams."
```

```reader "If you're a first- or second-year who qualifies for an exemption"
You may rent off campus once Housing approves your exemption, and a co-op stays open to you either way. Request the exemption in the Housing application during the open window, and [not after you sign a housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). It lasts one academic year. Do not sign a lease before approval: Off-Campus Living says first- and second-years [should not sign a lease without an approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process), and a student who neither complies nor gets one is [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing). Compare a co-op double ($6,974 to $9,962 a year) with 12 months of rent, not 10: an $800 Collegetown bed is $9,600 a year.
```

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the Housing Application because a chapter house is the plan.** It is required anyway. Miss it and the requirement still applies, with a $13,744 hall double as the default bill.
- **Joining the chapter after you file.** A sophomore must already be a member when the application goes in, or cannot live in the house that year.
- **Signing a Collegetown lease for sophomore year.** Without an approved exemption you pay rent and the full Cornell year: $13,744 for a double.
- **Comparing a semester rate with a monthly rent.** A $4,317 co-op semester is $8,634 a year; a $800 bed over 12 months is $9,600.
- **Counting on a mid-year switch.** Moving into a fraternity small residence does not release a spring Cornell contract.
- **Assuming house insurance covers your things.** Greek rent covers building insurance, not personal belongings.
- **Missing the November co-op round while holding a backup room.** A February acceptance means a $500 cancellation instead of $50 for a returning junior or senior.

## FAQ

**Are program houses cheaper than other dorms?** No. Program houses share the [residence hall rate table](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), so a double is $6,872 a semester for 2026-27.

**Can graduate students live in a co-op?** Some co-ops take graduate students. Cornell's [graduate housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) says to contact the houses directly.

**Do co-ops include meals?** Four of the eight have [meal plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing). Ask each house what it charges and what residents cook themselves.

**Who inspects a fraternity or sorority house?** In the City of Ithaca, chapter houses need a Certificate of Compliance renewed [every year](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance). Cornell also requires sprinklers throughout and officers living on site.

**When will the 2027-28 co-op rates be final?** Final 2027-28 rates are [posted in March 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028); rates shown at Fall Housing Selection are projections subject to Board of Trustees approval.
