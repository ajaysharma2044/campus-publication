Ithaca Renting Company leases by the bed on one-year leases that start on a fixed June, July or August date, and it posts its prices online. Heat and hot water are included at every property, and electricity is the tenant's cost at most of them ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## What does Ithaca Renting rent?

Ithaca Renting lists 18 properties. In Collegetown they include Collegetown Plaza, Center and Court on Dryden Road, Aces on Eddy Street, 705 E. Buffalo St. and several Dryden Road houses. Downtown it manages Cityview, Commons West and the Old Cigar Factory, among others ([all properties](https://ithacarenting.com/all-properties/)). It has leased to more than 600 tenants near campus ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/)).

Its office is at 118 Prospect St., Suite 200. The Collegetown phone line is 607-272-3000 and the downtown line is 607-273-9462 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/all-properties/)).

## How does per-bed pricing work?

Each tenant signs for one bedroom and pays that bed's price. The price drops as the unit gets bigger, because tenants share kitchens and baths. The 2027-28 medians in Collegetown run from $800 per bed in a 6BR to $2,025 for a 1BR alone ([2027-28 dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)). Downtown beds are cheaper at the median in most sizes: $795 in a 3BR and $992.50 in a 2BR for 2026-27 ([downtown comparison](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/)). Building-by-building ranges are in the [Collegetown price guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/).

Ithaca Renting says prices can change until a lease is signed, so a posted price is an asking rent and not a quote.

## What do the lease dates and terms look like?

Leases last one year and begin in June, July or August. Each apartment has a preset start and end date that cannot be changed. You move in after noon on the start date and must vacate by 10 a.m. on the end date ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

Semester leases exist for some units ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). A Collegetown Court semester studio for spring or fall 2027 lists $1,635 to $1,735 per bed, against $1,565 to $1,640 for the standard 2027-28 studio. The semester price is higher because the term is shorter. The timeline tool below shows when each lease step falls.

## What is included in the rent?

Heat and hot water are included at all properties. Rent is for a furnished bed. Tenants pay electricity, except in the shared suites on floors two and three at 210 Dryden Rd. and in apartments rented by the room. The IRIS internet service is optional and costs extra ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Downtown, Whiton Hall at 222 S. Aurora St. includes electricity and Wi-Fi, and the Colonial Building and Commons Studios include electricity ([downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)).

Parking is a separate license for the full year, with a standard term of Aug. 15 to Aug. 14. There are no monthly or semester licenses. Tenants get priority, spaces are assigned, and the lots on Dryden Road, College Avenue and E. Buffalo Street hold more than 100 vehicles in all ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## When does leasing open, and why the lines?

Ithaca Renting opens each season's leasing on a set day. That day produced overnight lines in 2012, 2014 and 2019. In 2019 it opened 2020-21 leasing at 9 a.m. on Sept. 12 after students waited overnight ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/)). The company has said it opens late so current tenants can decide on renewing.

The company now posts 2027-28 prices openly. On Nov. 10, 2026, 14 of the 67 Collegetown rows were marked sold out. For the full picture of how early leases are signed, see the [when to sign guide](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) and its dataset.

## What are the sublet rules?

Collegetown sublets must be registered and approved, go to Cornell students only, and run one per term. Subletting for less than one month and Airbnb use are not allowed ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The page lists the sublet fee as $15 and also says a $20 fee applies if anyone uses the apartment over the summer, even when no money changes hands. Ask which one applies to you.

Summer subtenants can include graduate students from other universities. Tenants must keep the electricity on, at least enough to run the fridge. Downtown leases carry a sublet option with a two-month minimum, the written agreement of every named tenant, and Rental Office approval. A sublet does not release you from the lease ([downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)).

## What fees can Ithaca Renting charge?

Ithaca Renting does not post its deposit or application terms online, so ask for them in writing. State law sets the limits that apply to every landlord. The deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). A landlord cannot charge an application fee, and a background check costs the lesser of the actual cost or $20 ([RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)).
