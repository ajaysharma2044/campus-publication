Ganędagǫ: Hall (said "Gah-NEH-dah-go") is a suite-style residence hall on Cornell's North Campus that holds 564 students by Cornell Housing's resident count ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduates/residence-halls/ganedago-hall)). Cornell's page lists it for transfers and upper-level students. Its rooms are temperature-controlled.

## What does the name Ganędagǫ: mean?

It is a Cayuga word meaning "it's on the hill," the old name for the land where Cornell's main campus sits. Cornell chose it with Cayuga leaders and its American Indian and Indigenous Studies Program ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduates/residence-halls/ganedago-hall)).

## What is living in Ganędagǫ: like?

A suite means a locked door off the hall opens onto a few bedrooms, a common space and a bathroom that only the suite uses ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/residence-hall-configurations)). On North, only Ganędagǫ:, Toni Morrison Hall, Language House and the Latino Living Center are built this way. Most other North halls are corridor style, with bathrooms down the hall. In Ganędagǫ:, each suite is single-gender and each floor is mixed, and the rooms are singles and doubles ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduates/residence-halls/ganedago-hall)).

Ganędagǫ: is also the biggest residence building on North. Its 564 residents outnumber the next largest hall, Ruth Bader Ginsburg, which Cornell lists at 504 (Cornell Housing). Welliver, the construction manager, counts beds instead and lists Ganędagǫ: at 577 ([Welliver](https://www.buildwelliver.com/projects/integrated-acquisition-and-development-cornell-university-north-campus-residential-expansion)).

Cooling is the practical difference. Cornell's hall page lists temperature-controlled rooms. Twelve North buildings have no air conditioning in rooms, so this is one of the halls to ask for if late-August and May heat matters to you. The full list is in [North Campus dorms compared](/cornell/housing/cornell-north-campus-dorms-compared/).

The hall opened as part of a push to house every first-year and sophomore. Ganędagǫ: and Toni Morrison Hall opened in 2021 with 800 beds between them ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2022/08/cornell-welcomes-students-marks-milestone-residential-life)).

## What does a room in Ganędagǫ: cost?

Cornell prices rooms by type, not by building, so Ganędagǫ: costs what a double or single costs anywhere else on campus ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). The rate covers gas, electricity, trash pickup and internet up to 200 GB a month, and there is no security deposit.

A sophomore in a North hall can pick the Bear Traditional meal plan, up to 14 meals a week, at $7,056 a year plus the yearly administrative fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## How do you get a room in Ganędagǫ:?

Cornell lists Ganędagǫ: among North's upper-level halls, alongside Balch Hall, the north wing of Clara Dickson Hall, Toni Morrison Hall and the Townhouse Apartments ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduate-campus-housing)). Rising sophomores pick rooms in a timed online selection in the spring, and a student who does not pick is assigned what is left. Transfers are placed by Cornell.

You cannot cancel the license to move off campus as a sophomore unless you leave the university or win an exemption. The guide to [who can live off campus](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) explains each exemption. Returning juniors and seniors can cancel a 2027-28 license for $50 through Jan. 31, $500 from Feb. 1 to March 31, $1,000 from April 1 to June 30 and $2,000 from July 1 to Aug. 15 ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

## What have residents said about it?

In October 2021, first-year residents of Ganędagǫ: and Toni Morrison Hall told The Cornell Daily Sun they liked the spacious rooms, hardwood floors, cooling and heating. They also reported shower drains that flooded bathroom floors. Several said the heavy suite doors stayed shut, so they knew few people outside their suite. All said the good outweighed the bad ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/from-leaky-showers-to-lonely-suites-student-residents-voice-concerns-about-new-north-campus-dorms/)). The account is five years old, so ask a current resident before you pick the hall.

In January 2026, a sprinkler main pipe broke in a Ganędagǫ: stairwell, and Housing and Residential Life moved 46 residents to other campus buildings during the repairs ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/01/ganedago-hall-relocates-46-residents-following-sprinkler-main-pipe-break-in-stairwell)).

```reader "If you're staying over winter break"
Ganędagǫ: is one of the North halls Cornell keeps open over winter break in 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). Residents of open halls pay $125 for one week or $500 for the whole break, and apply in the Housing and Dining Portal between Oct. 1 and Dec. 1.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
The hall has no cheaper room type. Triples and one-room quads carry the lowest rate in Cornell's table, and this hall lists only singles and doubles. If price decides your year, compare a double here with a Collegetown share in the [cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/) before spring selection.
```

## Where do you unload at move-in?

Cornell sends cars to Ganędagǫ: through the Robert Purcell Community Center lot, with Jameson, High Rise 5 and Donlon, and the move-in page names that lot as the unloading zone ([Cornell Housing move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).
