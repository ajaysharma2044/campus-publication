Collegetown Terrace is a furnished apartment and townhome complex at 115 S. Quarry St. For 2027-28 it charges $605 to $2,265 per bedroom per installment, with 12 installments in the year ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/rates-floorplans/)).

## What is Collegetown Terrace, and who is it for?

It is a residential complex of studios and 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments and townhomes. The Scion Group manages it ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/management/)), and the site's sample housing agreement names the owners as VVA Phase I & II, LLC, VVA Phase III, LLC and VVA Phase IV, LLC ([sample agreement](https://collegetownterrace.com/sample_agreement.pdf)). The Cornell marketplace listing shows per-person leasing and roommate matching ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-terrace-236694)), so it suits a student who wants a private bedroom without finding a full group first.

## How far is Collegetown Terrace from campus?

The Cornell marketplace listing puts the walk to The Oval on campus at 1.2 miles, or 24 minutes ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-terrace-236694)). That is farther than the Dryden Road buildings. A shuttle bus runs from the complex to campus every 10 minutes on weekday mornings when classes are in session. In the afternoons it runs every 30 minutes, and the entrance gives quick access to TCAT city buses ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/faqs/)). In a 2012 Sun story from the complex's first year, another Collegetown landlord said tenants saw it as a little too far for rents of $1,000 to $1,200 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2012/10/01/as-collegetown-housing-rush-intensifies-students-sleep-outside-rental-agency/)). ## What does it cost?

Bedroom-only plans, which share a kitchen and sometimes a bathroom, top out at $1,115. A bedroom in a 2- or 3-bedroom apartment, cottage or townhome costs $1,075 to $1,405, since every rate is quoted per bedroom. Studios cost $1,309 to $2,005, and 1-bedroom apartments start at $1,605. Rates can change until the contract is fully signed ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/rates-floorplans/)). Over 12 installments, the cheapest bedroom comes to $7,260 and the priciest 1-bedroom to $27,180. A 2022 Sun opinion column put studios at up to $1,795 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/)).

The rate covers furnishings, high-speed internet, water and sewer, and community features such as the fitness center and shuttle ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/faqs/)). The sample agreement adds trash pickup to the included utilities. Each resident also pays a share of the apartment's electricity bill, plus a service fee of up to $72 up front or up to $6 per monthly bill ([sample agreement](https://collegetownterrace.com/sample_agreement.pdf)). Garage and surface parking cost extra ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/faqs/)).

To judge a rate, compare it per bed with the buildings in the [Collegetown rent-per-bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/) and in [Collegetown rent prices per bed](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/).

## How do you get a spot?

New applicants can lock in a rate by Nov. 15, 2026, if they fully sign the housing agreement while the offer runs ([Collegetown Terrace](https://www.collegetownterrace.com/)). Applying costs $20 ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/rates-floorplans/)). The standard term runs Aug. 19, 2027 to July 27, 2028, and limited alternate dates are available on request. Each resident must provide a qualifying guarantor, such as a parent, within 7 days of signing. The office can waive that in limited cases for a resident with enough income of their own ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/faqs/)). Collegetown's popular units go early, and one developer's Ivy building leased nearly every unit before it was built ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)).

## What should you watch for?

On the per-person contract, you pay only for your own bedroom, even if a roommate moves out or a bedroom sits empty ([Collegetown Terrace](https://collegetownterrace.com/faqs/)). Leaving early does not end your own bill: under the sample agreement, a resident who moves out still owes rent through the end of the term. A credit comes only once every open bedroom at the property is filled, minus a $650 re-marketing fee ([sample agreement](https://collegetownterrace.com/sample_agreement.pdf)). Read the whole agreement first: the [guide to reading an Ithaca lease](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) explains the clauses, and the [landlord reviews guide](/housing/how-to-check-an-ithaca-landlords-record/) covers what tenants report in Collegetown.
