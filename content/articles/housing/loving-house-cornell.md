Loving House is a Cornell program house with 30 residents on the first floor of Mews Hall, opened in 2019. Rooms are singles and doubles in mixed-gender pods. A program house is a themed residence hall, and a double costs $13,744 for 2026-27, the same as in any hall ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is Loving House?

It is one of the smallest program houses, and one of the newest. Cornell lists 30 residents ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/loving-house)). The house opened in 2019 on the first floor of Mews Hall ([Cornellians](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/program-houses/)).

## What does Loving House expect from residents?

Residents take part in the house. Cornell's program house expectations call for a retreat each fall and spring, and at least two Fellow-sponsored programs per semester. First-year and transfer residents also enroll in LWYL 1156, a course tied to the house ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates/program-houses/program-house-mission-and-expectations)). A Fellow is a faculty or community member linked to the house.

## What does Loving House cost?

Program houses share the residence hall rate table. A meal plan is required: Unlimited at $7,604 for first-years, or Bear Traditional at $7,056 for upper-level and transfer students, plus a $50 yearly fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

## How do you get into Loving House?

New students list a program house on the housing application. They apply within 10 days of getting a NetID, and halls not chosen are assigned by random auto-allocation ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)). Rising sophomores can list up to three program houses. For 2027-28, the application closes April 4, 2027, and program house priority selection is April 12 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

A program house counts as on-campus housing, so living there meets the two-year residential requirement. If you want to live off campus, check the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## Can you stay in Loving House over winter break?

Yes, and it costs nothing. Loving House and HILC residents stay free over winter break. Other open buildings charge $125 for one week or $500 for the full break. Cornell keeps Mews Hall open both years, and residents apply from Oct. 1 to Dec. 1 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). The [guide to staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) covers dining and transport while campus is quiet.

## What should you watch before you sign?

Check the cancellation schedule before you commit. The [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) turns your date into a number.

On move-in day, the Mews and Loving House entrance uses Program House Drive. In fall 2026 Cornell turned away U-Hauls and trailers ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)).
