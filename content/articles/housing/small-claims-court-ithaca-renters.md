If your landlord keeps money you are owed, you can sue in small claims court for up to $5,000 in Ithaca City Court, or up to $3,000 in a town or village court ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Small claims is a fast, informal court meant for people without lawyers. Send a demand letter first.

## What is small claims court, and what can a renter sue for?

Small claims court handles disputes over money, up to a cap ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Ithaca City Court, at 118 East Clinton Street, hears housing cases under the higher cap above. If your rental is outside the city, plan your claim around the lower town and village cap.

Three kinds of claim fit small claims:

- **A withheld security deposit.** New York law gives a landlord 14 days after you move out to return the deposit with an itemized statement. A landlord who misses the deadline forfeits the right to keep any part of it ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
- **A rent cut for bad conditions.** The Cornell Law guide puts rent reductions at about 10% to 20% for minor problems, about 30% for moderate ones and 50% to 60% for the most serious.
- **Money from a roommate or subtenant.** Each tenant on a lease can be held for the full rent, and a tenant who overpays can sue a co-tenant for contribution, which means a share of the cost ([Cornell Law lease guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)). An unpaid subtenant can be pursued for breach of contract in small claims ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

Small claims is not an eviction court. If a landlord is trying to remove you, see the [eviction guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-eviction-vacate-order-what-to-do/).

## What should I do before I file?

Try the cheaper routes first. The Cornell Law guide's recovery path for a deposit is a demand letter, then small claims, with mediation through the Community Dispute Resolution Center along the way ([Cornell Law deposit guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits)).

**The demand letter.** State what you are owed, why, and the date by which you want it paid. Attach your photos and your forwarding address. Send it by certified mail so you can prove delivery, and keep a copy.

**Mediation.** The Community Dispute Resolution Center offers neighbor, family and tenant mediation at 607-273-9347 ([Cornell resources](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/community-resources)). A mediator helps both sides reach a deal and keeps the talk out of a courtroom.

**The Attorney General.** Deposit complaints go to the state Attorney General after you have tried the landlord ([Cornell Law tenant guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits)). The Attorney General's tenants page has a complaint form and lists the office's main line, 1-800-771-7755 ([NY Attorney General](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants)).

## How strong is a deposit claim?

Strong, if you can show the landlord missed a rule. Run your lease through the checker to see which charges New York allows.

```tool ithaca-lease-fee-deposit-checker
```

Three points decide most deposit cases. First, the deposit and any advance rent cannot exceed one month's rent. Second, a landlord who willfully breaks the deposit law owes punitive damages of up to twice the deposit, and the landlord must prove that the amounts it withheld were reasonable ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). On a $1,200 deposit, twice the deposit is $2,400. Third, allowed deductions are limited to unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear, unpaid utilities owed to the landlord, moving and storing belongings left behind, and excessive cleaning. A landlord cannot deduct attorney's fees or late fees ([Cornell Law deposit guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits)).

Evidence wins these cases. Photos from move-in and move-out, a signed move-in condition record and your forwarding address on a letter carry more weight than memory. The full deposit rules are in our [deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/), and the [move-out guide](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/) shows how to build the record while you pack.

## Which landlord fees can I challenge?

Deposits are not the only money renters recover. Several New York rules cap or ban fees, and a charge above the cap is a claim you can bring.

| Charge | The rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Non-refundable deposit | Unlawful; the agreement is void | [Cornell Law deposit guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) |
| Application fee | A landlord may not demand any payment for processing an application | [RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) |
| Background or credit check | The lesser of actual cost or $20; waived if you supply a check from the past 30 days | [RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) |
| Late fee | Not allowed unless rent is more than 5 days late; capped at the lesser of $50 or 5% of monthly rent | [RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) |
| Replacement keys | No more than 110% of actual cost | [RPL 235-i](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-I) |

Deposits are also trust funds, so a landlord cannot mix them with its own money ([NY Attorney General](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants)). A new owner is liable for a deposit it knew about ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Take that into account if the building sold during your lease.

## What does filing cost, and how long does it take?

The Cornell Law guide does not list the filing fee, the forms or the hearing timeline. Ask the clerk at Ithaca City Court, 118 East Clinton Street, or call the Cornell Law tenants hotline at 607-301-1560 before you file. The hotline gives free advice, and full representation is for City of Ithaca residents ([Cornell Law tenants' rights guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/)).

## What if I share the lease?

Housemates on one lease can each be held responsible for the whole rent, so a claim against the landlord should name the group's facts clearly. Our guide to [roommates and joint liability](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) explains who owes what when someone leaves.

## What to do next

Send the demand letter with a payment deadline and book mediation ([CDRC](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/community-resources)) if the landlord does not answer. Before you file, call the court clerk or the hotline (607-301-1560) to confirm the fee and the right court for your rental address.
