```glance
- Lansing West, Triphammer, Winston Square and University Park sit about 2.2 to 2.4 miles from Ho Plaza.
- Triphammer Apartments include heat and free on-site parking; one-bedrooms start at $1,795.
- Lansing West takes cats only, and its rent-free offer ran through apply-by Nov. 14, 2026.
- Lansing is outside the City, so City rental rules and Cornell's safety lookup do not apply.
```

North of campus along Triphammer and Warren roads, large apartment complexes charge less than Collegetown but sit 2 to 3 miles out. Cornell says to expect larger complexes, lower rents and regular buses, with the Shops at Ithaca Mall, Triphammer Mall and Cayuga Mall nearby ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)). Most residents ride the bus or drive.

## How far is Lansing from campus?

Cornell lists Northeast at 4 miles and Lansing at 5 to 10 miles, with a bus or car recommended. Straight-line from Ho Plaza, most of the big complexes measure 2.2 to 2.7 miles ([listings feed](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing)), Pine Hill at 709 Triphammer Rd. is the closest at 1.1 miles, and the road distance is longer. A graduate student guide puts the bus ride from Lansing at about 20 minutes ([Graduate Student Resources](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html)). Our [transport guide for renters](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/) covers routes, and the TCAT page lists hours and free-ride rules ([TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/)).

## What is Lansing West like?

It is a large complex with plans named Otisco, Oneida, Cayuga, Keuka and Seneca and dynamic pricing that changes by unit and date ([RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx)). It is run by an affiliate of The Solomon Organization ([Lansing West](https://www.lansingwestapts.com/)). RentCafe showed one-bedroom units available now at $1,038 and $1,193 and a promotion of rent free until January 2027 for applications by Nov. 14 and move-in by Dec. 1, 2026. Pets are cats only, at $40 a month for one cat or $60 for two ([RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx)). Heat, water and internet are not stated, so ask what rent includes.

## What about Triphammer and Winston Square?

Triphammer Apartments run standard 11- to 12-month leases, with a monthly premium for shorter terms ([Triphammer Apartments](https://triphammerapts.com/faqs/)). Winston Square says it takes no guarantors or cosigners and charges $45.00 a month for one pet under 25 pounds ([Winston Square](https://winstonsquareapts.com/faq/)). That matters for a student without a U.S. credit history.

## Which rules apply in Lansing?

Lansing is a Town, not the City of Ithaca. The City's rental certificate and Cornell's safety lookup cover City rentals only, not Town, Lansing or Cayuga Heights ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)). For a code question, the Town of Lansing office is at 607-533-7054 ([Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Our [City, Town and Lansing guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) explains the differences.

## Do you need a car?

Parking is easy here: Triphammer lists free on-site parking ([Triphammer Apartments](https://triphammerapts.com/amenities/)) and 546 Warren Rd. includes off-street spots ([listings feed](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing)), so the question is whether you want to drive.
