```glance
- Studios cost the most per square foot: a 300 sq ft Collegetown studio costs $4.20.
- Two-bedrooms and larger units cost less per foot than one-bedrooms and studios.
- Most Collegetown landlords do not print unit sizes, so a per-foot price exists for only some buildings.
- To compare group houses, use price per bed instead.
```

Where a listing prints both a price and a size, the smallest Ithaca units cost the most per square foot. Most Collegetown landlords print prices without sizes, so this guide covers only the listings that show both.

## What does a square foot cost in Collegetown?

The most direct numbers come from a Collegetown landlord that prints both on its apartment search page. Collegetown rents per unit, per month, with the math shown:

| Building | Unit | Rent | Size | Rent per sq ft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 116 Osmun Pl | Studio (Apt 3) | $1,260 | 300 sq ft | $4.20 |
| 116 Osmun Pl | 1BR (Apt 1) | $1,450 | 475 sq ft | $3.05 |
| 408 Stewart Ave | 3BR (Apt 4) | $3,000 | 800 sq ft | $3.75 |

Source: [CSP Management apartment search](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/PublicPages/ApartmentSearch.aspx), 2027-28 academic year listings. Rent per square foot is rent divided by size, for example $1,260 / 300 = $4.20.

Both buildings are within half a mile of Ho Plaza by straight line ([CSP Management](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/PublicPages/ApartmentSearch.aspx)). A bigger unit does not always mean a lower price per foot: the 3BR at 408 Stewart Ave costs more per foot than the 1BR at 116 Osmun Pl.

Collegetown Terrace and 407 and 312 College Ave print sizes but not prices. Their 2x2, 3x3 and 4x4 layouts are 829, 1,212 and 1,545 sq ft ([Collegetown Terrace listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-terrace-236694)), which is about 414.5, 404 and 386 sq ft per bed. Their prices are listed as "Call For Price," so no per-foot price exists for them.

The pattern holds. The three 1BRs under 420 sq ft in the table cost $3.18 to $3.71 per foot ($1,435 for 387 sq ft, $1,400 for 400 sq ft and $1,305 for 411 sq ft; [Travis Hyde Properties](https://www.travishyde.com/availability)), while the 780 sq ft 2BR on Lake Street is the cheapest per foot in the table. Cayuga Apartments adds heat to the rent, which helps explain why a downtown 2BR near the top of the 2BR range still beats the small units. The point is that size, not neighborhood, drives most of the spread.

East Pointe at 15 E. Pointe Dr. lists four unit types, 2x2, 3x3, 4x4 and a 4x4.5 townhouse, and most show "Call For Price" ([East Pointe listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/east-pointe-236696)). It sits outside the core Collegetown area, and without posted prices it has no per-foot figure here.

## Why do averages from rent sites look so high?

Aggregator sites divide the average rent by the average size, and that inflates the number when tiny units are in the mix. RentCafe reports a Collegetown average of $2,815 at 375 sq ft, which is $7.51 per foot. Its 1BR figure is $2,342 at 433 sq ft, or $5.41, and its 2BR figure is $3,414 at 550 sq ft, or $6.21 ([RentCafe Collegetown](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/)).

Those are well above the listing figures above. For comparison, RentCafe's 2BR average downtown is $3,246 at 984 sq ft, or $3.30 ([RentCafe downtown](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/downtown-ithaca/)), and its Ithaca-wide 2BR figure is $2,459 at 902 sq ft, or $2.73 ([RentCafe Ithaca](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/)). RentCafe does not say which listings it counts, so use its numbers for direction, not for a budget.

A new building prices higher still. City Centre Ithaca's 508 sq ft Cayuga studio lists a base rent of $2,335 to $2,478, or $4.60 to $4.88 per foot, on a 15-month term ([City Centre Ithaca](https://citycentreithaca.com/floorplans/)).

## Is price per square foot the right way to compare?

Not for shared houses. A Collegetown group house rents by the bed, so the cost to you is your bedroom plus your share of common space. Ithaca Renting, the Collegetown landlord that publishes a full per-bed price list, shows a median of $800 per bed in a 6BR and $2,025 in a 1BR for 2027-28 ([Collegetown rent per bed, 2027-28](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)). That gap is much bigger than the square-foot gap, because shared living rooms and kitchens are cheap per person.

Use price per foot when you are choosing between whole apartments of different sizes. Use per-bed price for group houses. A dollar of rent buys much more room in a big shared unit than in a studio.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
Decide your group size first, then compare. A smaller group means a smaller share of common space and a higher cost per person. The [rent per bed guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/) lists what each unit size costs.
```

## What to do next

Ask each landlord for the unit's square footage and the lease year before you compare. Divide the monthly rent by the square feet, and compare only units of the same bedroom count. For group houses, compare per-bed price instead.
