Not if you walk to campus once a day. A downtown bed costs [$370 to $400 a month less](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/) than the same unit size in Collegetown at Ithaca Renting, and adds about 15 minutes each way. Over a school year, each hour of walking Collegetown saves you costs about $59 to $64.

```glance
- Collegetown costs $370 to $400 more per bed each month than downtown at the same landlord.
- One daily round trip, five days a week: you pay about $59 to $64 per hour saved.
- Pay for Collegetown if you go back and forth twice a day or come home after 1:30 a.m.
```

## How much more does a Collegetown bed cost?

On a 12-month lease, Collegetown costs $4,440 more for a studio, $4,620 more for a share of a 2-bedroom and $4,800 more for a 1-bedroom (Ithaca Renting). Those are median asking rents at one landlord with buildings in both neighborhoods, so the gap reflects location, not the company. In percentage terms the 2-bedroom gap is the steepest: Collegetown runs 38.8% above downtown, because the downtown price is lower.

Two things move that number. First, the two sides are different lease years. The Collegetown prices are the 2027-28 list, while downtown's are mostly 2026-27 leases or units available now. If downtown raises rents for 2027-28, the gap shrinks by that amount. Second, the rent covers different things. Collegetown tenants pay electricity, except in some units at 210 Dryden Rd., and pay extra for parking. Downtown rent includes heat, water and trash (Ithaca Renting). That makes the real gap larger than the rent alone shows.

## How much longer is the walk from downtown?

Plan on about 15 extra minutes each way. Cornell's housing office puts Collegetown 0.1 to 0.5 mile from campus, up to a 15-minute walk. It puts downtown 0.5 to 1.5 miles away and the Commons about 1 mile, a 20- to 30-minute uphill walk ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)).

Two Ithaca Renting buildings show the same thing. [Collegetown Plaza](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=1), at 111 Dryden Rd, sits 0.36 mile from Ho Plaza in a straight line. [Commons West](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=7), at 107 E. State St., sits 0.88 mile away. That is 0.52 mile farther. At 3 miles an hour, a normal walking pace on flat ground, 0.52 mile takes 10.4 minutes. The real route is longer than a straight line, and it climbs East Hill, so 15 minutes is a fair planning number.

Fall Creek gets the same 20- to 30-minute uphill estimate from Cornell, so the math below works for it too. The [neighborhood guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) compares the three areas on more than the commute.

## What does each hour of saved walking cost?

Your schedule sets the price. Say your Collegetown walk is 10 minutes and your downtown walk is 25, the middle of [Cornell's range](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods). That is 15 extra minutes each way, or 30 minutes a round trip. Five days a week, that is 2.5 hours. Over a 30-week school year (fall and spring semesters, a planning figure), that is 75 hours.

Divide the yearly premium (Ithaca Renting) by the hours. $4,440 / 75 = $59.20 an hour for a studio. $4,800 / 75 = $64.00 for a 1-bedroom. $4,620 / 75 = $61.60 for a 2-bedroom share. The hourly price runs high because you pay rent for 12 months but walk to class for about 30 weeks.

## Does the bus close the gap?

Mostly, and it costs little. Students past their first year ride all TCAT buses free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends. A daytime ride costs $1.50 cash. The OmniRide pass, Cornell's discounted unlimited TCAT pass, costs $200 a year ([Cornell Transportation and Delivery Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes)). Downtown plus OmniRide still saves $4,240 to $4,600 a year against Collegetown.

The bus has its own time cost: the wait. Buses run every 15 to 20 minutes from the Green Street stop behind Commons West (Ithaca Renting). Walk out without checking and you wait 7.5 to 10 minutes on average, half the gap between buses. Check the [TCAT real-time tracker](https://tcatbus.com/realtimeinfo) before you leave and you cut most of that. Ride times from Green Street to campus aren't posted, so time the trip in the tracker before you sign.

The [OmniRide break-even calculator](/tools/omniride-break-even-calculator/) tells you whether the pass beats paying cash for your schedule. The [TCAT guide for Cornell students](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) covers routes and free hours.

## When is paying for Collegetown worth it?

Closer wins in three situations.

You go home between classes for lunch, a nap or a shift. Two round trips a day halve the hourly price in the table above.

You come home very late. TCAT runs no buses from 1:30 a.m. to 4 a.m. ([Cornell Sustainability](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting)). If your lab, studio or job keeps you on campus past 1:30 a.m., downtown means a 20- to 30-minute uphill walk in the dark, or a ride you pay for.

You would fill the extra time with paid work or study you can't get otherwise. If an hour of your time is worth more to you than the hourly price for your schedule, Collegetown is a fair trade.

## When does downtown win?

Downtown wins if you walk to campus once a day, can ride the bus at night for free, and want to keep $4,000 or more a year. It also wins if you would otherwise pick a Collegetown studio. A downtown studio costs $1,395 a month, against $1,377.50 for a Collegetown 2-bedroom share (Ithaca Renting). Unit size moves the per-bed price about as much as the neighborhood does.

You can also live close for downtown prices by adding housemates. A bed in a Collegetown 6-bedroom has a median of $800 a month for 2027-28, against $795 for a downtown 3-bedroom share (Ithaca Renting). The catch is supply: 14 of the 67 Collegetown unit rows on Ithaca Renting's price page were sold out by Nov. 8, 2026, including large houses on Dryden Road.

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
The Collegetown prices above are the 2027-28 list you are shopping. Ask a downtown landlord for its 2027-28 rate before you compare, so you weigh next year's Collegetown price against next year's downtown one.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Registered PhD students and graduate assistants get a free OmniRide pass, so the daytime bus costs you nothing and downtown's only extra cost is time. If your lab keeps late hours, check the 1:30 a.m. bus gap against your schedule.
```

```reader "If you're keeping a car"
Distance matters less, but parking matters more. The student commuter (B) permit costs $359.77 a year and is valid in B lots at all times and in A lots after 2:30 p.m. ([Cornell student parking](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). B lots include the Vet College B, TRB, South and North Morrison and BTI lots, so you may still walk from the lot. Add the permit to the downtown side of your math, and add Collegetown's parking fee to the other side. Ithaca Renting sells parking as a separate license ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).
```

```reader "If you're paying your own way"
Downtown 2- and 3-bedroom shares come in under a Cornell double. A 3-bedroom share at $795 a month is $9,540 a year, against $13,744 for a 2026-27 double ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). That is $4,204 less, before utilities. No Collegetown studio, 1-bedroom or 2-bedroom at Ithaca Renting does that.
```

## FAQ

**Does Cornell's walk-time list work for Belle Sherman or Cayuga Heights?**
Use the distances instead. Cornell lists Belle Sherman at 1 to 2 miles but a 15-minute walk, and Cayuga Heights at 1 to 4 miles but up to 30 minutes. At 3 miles an hour, each mile takes 20 minutes, so plan on 20 to 40 minutes for Belle Sherman.

**Do these prices cover every Collegetown landlord?**
No. They are one landlord's asking prices, from 58 listings, and many Collegetown houses rent through other companies. Use them as a gauge of the location gap, not as the price you will pay.
