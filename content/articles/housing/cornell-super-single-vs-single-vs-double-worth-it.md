```glance
- A single costs $1,822 more a year than a double for 2026-27. A super single costs $2,851 more.
- The meal plan price does not change with your room, so the gap stays the same all-in.
- Cornell's aid budget counts housing at $13,744 and leaves out premium rooms. Plan to pay the upgrade yourself.
- A median Collegetown one-bedroom costs $7,705 a year more than a super single, before food.
```

Take the single if a room of your own is worth $911 more a semester to you. For 2026-27, a single costs $1,822 a year more than a double, and a super single costs $2,851 more ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Most students can skip the super single: its extra $515 a semester buys space, not privacy.

## What does each room type cost at Cornell for 2026-27?

A double costs $6,872 a semester, a single $7,783 and a super single $8,298 for 2026-27 (Cornell Housing). One rate table covers residence halls, program houses and the West Campus Houses, so a single costs the same in Cascadilla Hall as in Carl Becker House. Every rate includes gas, electricity, garbage pickup and internet up to 200 GB a month. There is no damage deposit. Cornell bills fall housing in August and spring housing in December ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is a super single?

A super single is a one-person room that Cornell prices as its own tier, above a standard single. Veterans House shows the difference best: every room in this 26-resident program house for transfer, upper-level and graduate students is a super single with a queen or extra-long twin bed ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/veterans-house)). A standard single, such as the 20 all-single rooms at 112 Edgemoor, comes with an extra-long twin bed ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/112-edgemoor)).

Ask Housing Operations what a specific super single measures before you pick it. The office is at 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368 ([Cornell Housing directory](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211065419/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory)).

## Is a single worth $1,822 more than a double?

Yes, if sleep, study or privacy is a real problem for you in a shared room. The single buys the one thing a double cannot: a door you control.

On West Campus, most of the 152 suites hold five people in three singles and a double, with a shared bathroom and sitting area, so a suite single gives you your own room inside a group of friends ([Cornell Room Selection FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/room-selection-faqs)). Near Collegetown, Cascadilla Hall has single and double rooms along its corridors for 366 residents ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/cascadilla-hall)). Schuyler House, between South Campus and downtown, is mostly singles for its 125 residents ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house)).

Air conditioning depends on the building, not the room type. Carl Becker House rooms have it ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system/carl-becker-house)). If heat keeps you up in September, pick the building first.

Stay in the double if you sleep well with a roommate and the upgrade would come out of loans or a campus job. A good roommate costs nothing extra.

## Is a super single worth $2,851 more than a double?

For most students, no. Over a standard single, it buys a larger room and, at Veterans House, a bigger bed.

It makes sense in two cases: you want the Veterans House community, where every room is a super single, or you need the space, such as for a larger bed or medical equipment. In the second case, ask Housing Operations about the room before you choose it.

## What does a year cost with the meal plan?

The meal plan costs the same whatever room you pick, so it doesn't change the upgrade math. First-year students must buy the Unlimited plan, $3,802 a semester or $7,604 a year. West Campus House residents must buy the House Meal Plan at the same price. Residents of 112 Edgemoor, Cascadilla Hall, Schuyler House, Sheldon Court, 301 College Avenue and 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive can choose the Collegetown Meal Plan at $7,056 a year. Every plan carries a $50 nonrefundable fee once a year ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

With the yearly fee, the Unlimited or House plan comes to $7,654 a year (Cornell Dining):

## Does financial aid cover a single?

Not in the standard budget. Cornell's undergraduate cost of attendance for 2025-26 lists housing at $13,744, the same figure as the 2026-27 double, and leaves out "premium on-campus or off-campus housing options" ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-to-attend)). Cornell Dining says aid fully covers the Unlimited plan for eligible students ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)); the premium for a single or super single sits outside that housing budget.

If you receive aid, treat the upgrade as money your family pays, and ask the Office of Financial Aid before you select whether your package changes. It is at 203 Day Hall, (607) 255-5145 ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).

## How does a super single compare with a Collegetown one-bedroom?

The super single is cheaper, even with meals. Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown one-bedrooms have a median asking rent of $2,025 a month, with heat, hot water and furniture included and electricity paid by the tenant at most units. Over a 12-month lease that is $24,300: $7,705 more than a super single ($24,300 minus $16,595) and $10,556 more than a double. A super single plus the Unlimited plan costs $24,249, $51 less than a year of rent alone, before food or power. The cheapest one-bedroom on the list, a standard unit at Collegetown Court, asks $1,625 to $1,725 a month for 2027-28, or $19,500 to $20,700 over 12 months, still above a campus super single ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

An apartment wins on other things: a kitchen, a full-size bedroom and a place to stay over the summer. For shared apartments against the dorm, see [whether off-campus housing is cheaper than a Cornell dorm](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

## Can you count on getting a single in room selection?

No. Cornell says choosing singles "is entirely dependent on what is available during your timeslot" ([Cornell Room Selection FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/room-selection-faqs)). A late timeslot can leave only shared rooms. In fall 2025, a transfer student with a 7:30 p.m. slot on the last day found only triples in Clara Dickson Hall and doubles in Sheldon Court left ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)).

West Campus is also hard to get. In past years, about 20% of rising sophomores were able to select West Campus spaces, so most suite singles go to upper-level residents (Cornell Room Selection FAQs).

Rising sophomores choose rooms for 2027-28 in spring 2027 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)):

## FAQ

**Will rates be the same in 2027-28?**
No guarantee. The rates shown at fall selection are projections that still need approval from the president and trustees, and Cornell posts final 2027-28 rates in March 2027 ([Cornell Housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

**Is a townhouse double a single?**
No. It is a double that Cornell prices at the single rate, $15,566 for 2026-27.

## What to do next

Decide whether the semester gap in the table above is worth it to you. If you receive aid, call Financial Aid before your timeslot. Rising sophomores should finish the housing application by April 4, 2027 (Cornell Housing), and pick a backup room type in case singles are gone.
