Leave candles, extension cords, toasters, air fryers, air conditioners, halogen lamps, hoverboards and any pet but fish at home. Cornell's fire marshal inspects dorm rooms and confiscates them ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/what-pack-what-not-bring)). The items students miss most are ordinary ones: a plain extension cord, a wall tapestry, an artificial plant.

```glance
- Leave home: candles, extension cords, toasters, air fryers, air conditioners, space heaters and hoverboards.
- Bring a UL-listed power strip with a breaker switch instead of any extension cord.
- A mini fridge must draw 7.3 amps or less to start and 1.4 amps or less running.
- Confiscated items are held seven days, then destroyed or donated.
```

## What can't you bring to a Cornell dorm?

Cornell bans anything that could start a fire, set off a false alarm or block an exit. The rules live in three places: the packing page above, the [Community Living Standards](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/community-rules-standards/community-living-standards-undergrads) for 2026-27, and the [2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028), the contract every resident signs. The Community Living Standards cover first-year and second-year halls, program houses, upper-level halls and the West Campus House System.

| Category | Banned in your room | Allowed instead |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Extension cords, multi-plug adapters, bed risers and headboards with built-in outlets, power strips plugged into each other | One UL-listed outlet strip with a breaker switch |
| Cooking | Toasters, toaster ovens, air fryers, rice cookers, hot pots, slow cookers, electric skillets, anything with an immersion coil, freestanding microwaves | Coffee maker with automatic shutoff; the microwave in your building's shared kitchen |
| Fridges | Microfridges you buy yourself; full-size fridges; a mini fridge over the power limit | A mini fridge within the limit, or a rented microfridge |
| Heating and cooling | Air conditioners of any kind, space heaters, humidifiers | A fan |
| Lighting | Halogen and open fluorescent bulbs; any lamp needing a bulb over 150 watts; non-LED "spider" lamps | LED lamps and UL-listed string lights, hung from hooks |
| Fire and fumes | Candles (wick or wickless), wax warmers, incense, fireworks, gasoline, propane, lighter fluid, spray paint | Battery-operated lights |
| Decor | Untreated tapestries, flags and fabric, curtains, room dividers, soundproofing panels, peel-and-stick wallpaper, artificial plants, natural evergreen trees and wreaths | Posters on picture moldings, tack strips or bulletin boards; metal trees |
| Furniture | Homemade or rented lofts, cinder blocks, extra furniture, waterbeds, upholstered pieces without a fire-code tag | A loft set up by Cornell's facilities staff |
| Batteries on wheels | Hoverboards anywhere; e-bikes, e-scooters and e-skateboards outside bike rooms | Storing and charging in the bike room |
| Animals | Any pet except fish | Fish in a tank of 10 gallons or less |
| Other | Firearms, ammunition, knives with blades over 4 inches, toy guns, drones, personal security cameras, barbells, pull-up bars, kegs | Weights under 30 lb |

Source: Cornell Student & Campus Life packing page, Community Living Standards 2026-27 and Housing License 2027-28.

## What does the room already have?

Every Cornell room comes with a Twin XL bed frame and mattress, a closet or wardrobe, a desk and chair, a dresser or drawers, drapes or blinds, and internet ([packing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/what-pack-what-not-bring)). That is why extra furniture is on the banned list. You cannot move Cornell's furniture out of the room without written permission, and Cornell has [no storage space](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) for its furniture or yours. Sheets must fit an extra-long twin, which is 36 inches wide and 80 inches long. Most buildings have no air conditioning and some have no mirror, so a fan and a mirror are worth packing.

## Which ordinary items break the rules?

Power strips, string lights, wall fabric and adhesive strips are the everyday items that break the rules.

Cornell bans extension cords and multi-plug adapters, including the cube kind that turns one outlet into three. It allows one outlet strip with a breaker switch and a UL label, which shows Underwriters Laboratories tested it for safety. You cannot plug one strip into another to make a chain (Cornell housing rules).

String lights need a UL label, and they must hang loosely from hooks. They cannot be taped to the wall, run within 18 inches of the ceiling, stretched across the room or plugged into each other. LED strip lights count as string lights (Cornell housing rules).

A tapestry, flag, bedsheet on the wall or fabric soundproofing panel needs a fire-retardant treatment applied by an approved outside vendor, with paperwork, before it enters the room. Decorations can cover no more than 10% of the wall (Cornell housing rules).

Nails, tacks, screws, tape, glue and "damage-free" adhesive strips are all banned on walls, doors and furniture. Cornell warns that products sold as safe for paint still peel it off. Each resident can post one 8.5-by-11-inch sheet of paper on the outside of the door (Cornell housing rules).

## Can I bring a mini fridge or a microwave?

Yes to a mini fridge that draws 7.3 amps or less to start and 1.4 amps or less running; no to a microwave in most rooms. The Community Living Standards allow 1.5 amps running, and the packing page says 1.4. Go by 1.4, which passes both. The amps are on the fridge's label.

A microfridge, a mini fridge and microwave built as one unit, is allowed only if you rent it from Cornell's vendor, [My MicroFridge](https://mymicrofridge.com/). It must plug straight into the wall. Order early if you want it in the room when you arrive. Stand-alone microwaves stay in the shared kitchen.

Small appliances with a sealed, thermostat-controlled heating element and automatic shutoff are allowed in kitchenettes only. A coffee maker with automatic shutoff is the one heating appliance allowed in a bedroom. Cornell confiscates any cooking appliance without a thermostat. Over any break longer than four days, empty the fridge, unplug it and leave the door open.

```reader "If you're living in the Townhouse Community or 301 College Avenue"
Your kitchen rules are looser. Townhouse residents can bring a microwave for the apartment kitchen, and both buildings have kitchens, so ask your building staff before buying a mini fridge. Units at [301 College Avenue](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue) have kitchenettes with a fridge and microwave. Beds can't be lofted in the Townhouse Community.
```

## Can I bring an air conditioner, fan or space heater?

Bring a fan. Window and portable air conditioners are banned, and so are space heaters and humidifiers. The space-heater ban covers any appliance with exposed coils or a water heater without automatic shutoff.

```reader "If you need an air conditioner or an animal because of a disability"
Ask Student Disability Services first. Cornell's facilities staff install an air conditioner for an approved accommodation; you can't bring your own. An emotional support animal needs Student Disability Services approval finalized before it arrives, and service animals are allowed. Mobility devices covered by the Americans with Disabilities Act are exempt from the battery ban.
```

## Can I bring a loft, bed risers or my own furniture?

No lofts you build or rent, and no cinder blocks. To raise, bunk or loft your bed, submit a maintenance request to Cornell's facilities staff; requests open Sept. 1. Beds can't be lofted at all in Barbara McClintock Hall, Ecology House, Ganędagǫ: Hall, Hu Shih Hall, Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall, Sheldon Court, Toni Morrison Hall or the Townhouse Community. Bed risers with built-in outlets are banned because they count as extension cords.

A couch, armchair or upholstered headboard needs a factory tag showing it meets New York or California fire codes. Without the tag, you need the maker's documentation or a professional fire-retardant treatment. A headboard must attach to the bed frame and contain no outlets. Keep a clear path from the bed to the door and window, with 36 inches of open floor in front of the door (Cornell housing rules).

## Can I bring an e-bike, scooter or hoverboard?

Leave the hoverboard at home; it is banned because of battery fires. E-bikes, e-scooters and e-skateboards are allowed on campus but cannot be stored, charged or even present anywhere in a residence hall except its bike room. In the bike room, plug the charger directly into the wall. Register every bike and scooter on campus. A regular bike can't live in your room either, except a folding bike that doesn't block the exit. Our [bike, e-bike and scooter guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-bike-ebike-scooter-rules-theft/) covers Cornell's size and power limits and how to avoid theft.

## Can I bring a pet?

Only fish. The tank must hold 10 gallons or less, every roommate must agree to it, and piranhas and other flesh-eating fish are banned. You are responsible for the fish over breaks. Cornell confiscates any other pet and can bring disciplinary action. Visiting pets can't enter any residential building either (Cornell housing rules).

## What else is banned in Cornell housing?

Cornell housing also bans weapons, smoking, underage alcohol, indoor sports gear and security cameras.

- **Weapons.** Firearms, BB guns, airsoft and paintball guns, ammunition, explosives and blades over 4 inches are banned. Skeet team members and hunters register and store guns with Cornell University Police in G-2 Barton Hall, with access around the clock. A kitchen knife over 4 inches can be used only in the community kitchen.
- **Smoking and vaping.** Both are banned in every Cornell residence. Smokers must stand at least 25 feet from any building.
- **Alcohol.** Students under 21 cannot have alcohol in Cornell housing. Kegs and other shared containers are banned for everyone, and Akwe:kon allows no alcohol at all.
- **Sports gear and gadgets.** Sports aren't allowed inside, so Nerf, water and dart guns, drones, barbells and pull-up bars stay home. Weights under 30 lb are allowed. Personal security cameras are banned in rooms.

## What happens if an inspection finds a banned item?

Staff take it. Cornell's fire marshal inspects rooms regularly and issues fines when a violation isn't fixed. A confiscated fire-safety item is held by your residence hall director for seven calendar days from the email notice. Pick it up within that window and ship it or take it off campus; after that, Cornell destroys or donates it. Alcohol, cannabis and weapons go to Cornell University Police or are thrown away. If an outside inspector fines Cornell because of your item, the [housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) makes you pay the fine and any damage. Shipping an item home works the same way as move-out shipping, covered in our [storage and shipping guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).

## FAQ

**Can I bring a car?** Cornell strongly discourages first-year students from bringing one, and first-years get a free TCAT bus pass. Our [first-year getting-around guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-cornell-first-year/) explains the bus and walking options. If you do drive up, the [move-in parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) covers where to unload.

**Do these rules apply to off-campus apartments?** No. An apartment's lease sets its own rules on smoking, pets, storage and alterations, so read that section before you sign. Cornell's [lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease) lists those restrictions among the terms to check.

**Is my stuff insured if it's damaged or stolen?** Not by Cornell. The housing license says the university's insurance doesn't cover personal property, and Cornell recommends renters insurance through GradGuard.

## What to do next

Before you order anything for the room, read the label for amps, a UL mark and an automatic shutoff. Those three checks clear most of the banned list. If you're on a tight budget, skip the microfridge rental and buy a compliant mini fridge or share one with your roommate; our [hidden costs guide](/cornell/money/cornell-first-gen-low-income-hidden-costs/) covers the other first-year expenses families don't plan for. Then work through the [first-year starting page](/start/first-year/).
