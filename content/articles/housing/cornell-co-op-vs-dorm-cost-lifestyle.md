A Cornell co-op room costs $3,782 to $6,770 a year less than a hall double, but you buy or cook your own food and share the housework. Pick a hall for a simple bill and a big social pool. Pick a co-op for a small house you run with your housemates ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)).

## What does each cost?

A co-op is a university-owned house of 10 to 35 members run by its residents. Cornell posts co-op rates per semester, and the hall double is $6,872 a semester ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

| Room | Co-op, per year | Hall, per year |
|---|---|---|
| Double | $6,974 (Redbud) to $9,962 (660 Stewart, Wari) | $13,744 |
| Triple | $5,530 (Redbud) | $12,644 |
| Single | $7,898 (Redbud) to $12,028 (Wari, top rate) | $15,566 |

Source: Cornell housing rates, 2026-27; yearly figures are two semesters. The hall double includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month.

The hall bill is not the full picture, because a hall resident must also buy a meal plan. Cornell's list of housing that carries the meal plan requirement names residence halls, program houses, the West Campus Houses, McGraw Place and the Townhouses, and it does not name co-ops ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Add Unlimited at $7,604 and the $50 fee to a hall double and the year is $21,398.

The co-op saving is then large, and food takes some back. At the highest co-op double ($9,962), you save $11,436 a year against that total, which is $953 a month for groceries over 12 months before the co-op costs more than the hall. At Redbud's $6,974 double, the break-even grocery budget is $14,424 a year, or $1,202 a month. These are our subtractions from Cornell's posted rates ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

For the whole range of options, including Collegetown rooms, see the [housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/) and our guide on [whether off campus is cheaper than a dorm](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/). If the cheapest bed is the goal, read the [cheapest off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/).

## How does daily life differ?

Hall life is built around services. Co-op life is built around the house itself.

**Food.** Four of the eight co-ops have a house meal plan, per Cornell's co-op page. Triphammer has mandatory vegetarian dinners Sunday to Thursday, and each resident joins a cook group of three or four people, one night a week ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/triphammer-cooperative)). At 660 Stewart, a house meal plan runs five nights a week with one cooking night and a mandatory staples program ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/660-stewart-avenue)). Wait Avenue Cooperative has no house meal plan but provides staples.

**Size.** The largest co-op is Redbud in Von Cramm Hall with 34 residents, and the smallest is Wari with 10. By comparison, Clara Dickson Hall has more than 460 residents ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/redbud-cooperative-von-cramm-hall)).

**Staff.** Co-ops have no live-in staff. Officers and liaisons are elected, and an Inter-Cooperative Union has one representative from each house ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/11/open-house-day-highlights-unique-qualities-of-cornell-co-ops/)).

**Perks.** Some houses add free laundry, parking and summer storage, such as Wait Avenue Cooperative.

## How do you get in?

Each house runs its own selection, in early November and early February. At Triphammer, applicants attend two events and one cook group, then enter a lottery. Watermargin uses a random lottery chosen to bring diversity. Rising sophomores must affirm a co-op by March 15, 2027, before the April 4 application deadline ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). A second-year accepted to a co-op before that deadline can cancel a hall license, and other second-years cannot.

Co-ops count as second-year housing under the residential requirement. Our [co-ops and Greek houses guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/) covers the application steps, and the [exemptions guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) covers living elsewhere.

## What should you watch for?

The houses are old and Cornell-owned. Four of the eight co-ops had floors collapse in recent years, and Watermargin needs about $500,000 for a roof, per The Cornell Daily Sun ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/10/21/co-op-residents-face-deteriorating-houses-increasing-rent-costs/)). Cornell also required co-ops to standardize room rates within and across houses, which raised some rents. Ask the house which repairs are pending before you apply.

The agreement is a license, not a lease, with no security or key deposit. The 2026-27 term runs from Aug. 21, 2026 to May 23, 2027, and residents must be registered full time with at least 12 credit hours ([Cornell co-op terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/co-op-housing-2026-2027-terms-and-conditions)). Only small caged pets and aquariums under 10 gallons are allowed. Check cancellation costs before you commit, using Cornell's fee schedule in the calculator below.

## What to do next

Pick two or three houses and email each one before the early-November or early-February selection window. Then decide whether to also rank a hall in the spring selection as a backup.
