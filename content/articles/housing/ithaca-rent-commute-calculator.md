One Cornell student's rent for a year in Ithaca runs from $7,150 for a share of a South Hill house to $29,940 for a deluxe one-bedroom in Collegetown Center. Your roommate count matters more than the building. Most beds cost more than Cornell's 2026-27 residence hall double, $13,744 for the academic year with utilities and internet included ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## How do I use the Ithaca rent calculator?

Pick a building, then a layout, then the number of people who will share the unit. The calculator gives your yearly cost, your monthly cost, and the gap to the Cornell double. A table under the result shows the same unit split among more or fewer people.

The building list has three parts. Two "typical bed" choices use the middle price of every listing at [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). Fifteen Ithaca Renting buildings follow, each with every layout it prices. The last group adds other landlords: Dryden South, The Ivy, 114 Stewart Ave., 117 Thurston Ave., 174 Kendall Ave., Lansing West and typical Fall Creek prices.

If your building is not on the list, pick the typical Collegetown or downtown bed and type in the price you were quoted. Say whether it is per bed or for the whole unit. A per-bed price is what one person pays. A whole-unit price gets split among your group.

The rest of the inputs are the lease length, whether electricity is in the rent, how you get to campus, and whether you keep a car. The calculator sets each one from the listing when it can, and you can change any of them.

## How much do roommates lower my rent?

Adding roommates is the biggest lever you have on rent. At Ithaca Renting, the 2027-28 median bed in Collegetown costs $2,025 a month in a one-bedroom and $800 in a six-bedroom ([Collegetown rent per bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)). That gap is $1,225 a month, or $14,700 over a 12-month lease.

Two effects stack. Landlords charge less per bed in bigger units. And every shared bill, from electricity to internet, gets split more ways.

Sharing a bedroom works too. Say two people split a standard one-bedroom at Collegetown Court, listed at $1,625 to $1,725 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The midpoint is $1,675 for the unit. Alone, rent and electricity at the upper bound come to $23,484 a year. Split two ways, each pays $11,742, which is $2,002 under the double.

New York law protects that choice in part. A landlord cannot limit a unit to the named tenant and immediate family. A tenant on a single lease can add one more occupant, and that person's dependent children, if the tenant lives there as a primary residence ([New York Real Property Law 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F)). Ask your landlord before you count an extra person, since the building's own occupancy limits still apply.

## What happens if a roommate drops out?

On a joint lease, the rest of the group owes the missing share. Cornell says most local leases bind housemates "jointly and severally," which means each person is responsible for the whole rent and for damages for the full term ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)).

Take Collegetown Plaza's premium four-bedroom with 1.5 baths, listed at $1,250 to $1,350 a bed ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The unit costs $5,200 a month at the midpoint. Four people pay $1,300 each in rent plus a quarter of the $282 electricity bill. That is $16,446 a year per person. If one person leaves and three cover the unit, each pays $1,733.33 in rent and $94 in electricity. Their year rises to $21,928, or $5,482 more each.

Put the calculator's head count at three to see this for your own unit. For what joint liability means and how to protect yourself, read [Cornell off-campus roommates and joint liability](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/).

## How does an off-campus year compare with a Cornell dorm?

On rent alone, a bed beats the double only in big shares or single rooms. The double costs $6,872 a semester and includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Over a 12-month lease, the rent that matches it is $1,145.33 a month (the double's yearly price divided by 12). If you sublet two summer months and pay 10, it is $1,374.40 (the yearly price divided by 10).

The two prices cover different stretches. The double covers the academic year. Most Ithaca leases run a full year, and some start in June, such as Dryden South and the Stewart Avenue eight-bedroom. Every row in the calculator's comparison adds electricity, since the double includes it.

The per-size picture across all Ithaca options is in the [Ithaca housing cost by living situation dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/). The full case for and against leaving the dorm is in [Is off-campus housing cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

## What does electricity add?

Electricity adds $70.50 a month per person in a four-person unit at the EnergySage average. Ithaca households who share bills with EnergySage spend about $282 a month on electricity ([EnergySage](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/)). The calculator uses the EnergySage figure in its totals.

Who pays depends on the building. At Ithaca Renting's [Collegetown buildings](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), tenants pay their own electricity. The exceptions are rooms rented one by one and the shared suites on floors 2 and 3 at 210 Dryden Rd. Downtown, the Colonial Building, Commons Studios and Whiton Hall include it ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)). Dryden South includes heat and hot water, and its pages do not say whether electricity or Wi-Fi are included ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/common-questions/)).

## What do the bus and a car add?

The bus adds nothing to $200 a year. TCAT's cash fare is $1.50 a ride. Cornell's OmniRide pass costs $200 a year, and PhD students and graduate assistants get it free ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes)). The pass pays off at 134 paid rides (the pass price divided by $1.50 is 133.3). New students in degree programs ride free.

Distance decides whether you ride at all. Cornell puts Collegetown 0.1 to 0.5 miles from campus, downtown 0.5 to 1.5 miles with a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk, and Lansing 5 to 10 miles, where it recommends a bus or car ([Cornell, Ithaca neighborhoods](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)). A two-bedroom at Lansing West, 700 Warren Rd., starts at $1,625 a month ([RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx)). Split two ways, that is $9,750 a year each in rent, before the commute.

A car costs far more. A monthly permit at the City's Dryden Road Garage is $232, or $2,784 a year, with no guaranteed space ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)). A Cornell B commuter permit for campus lots is $359.77 a year ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students)). Ithaca Renting sells parking as a separate license ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)), and Dryden South has no parking ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/common-questions/)).

```reader "If you're a parent"
Cornell aid does not cover security deposits, first and last month's rent, or rent in months your student is not registered ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)). New York caps a deposit at one month's rent ([New York General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Read the joint liability clause with your student before anyone signs.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Set your result against Cornell's 2026-27 Ithaca housing budget for graduate students, $19,962 a year, or $1,663.50 a month ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students)). Ithaca Renting pitches Collegetown Center, 151 Dryden Rd., to graduate students ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=461)). If your OmniRide pass is free, a cheaper place farther out adds nothing to your commute.
```

```reader "If you have a car"
Add parking before you compare buildings. The City's Dryden Road Garage monthly permit covers 24/7 access ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage)). At 117 Thurston Ave., parking is $55 a semester ([IthacaRents](https://www.ithacarents.com/4312-spacious-3-bedroom-in-historical-cornell-heights)). Pick your parking in the calculator and it adds the cost to your year.
```

## What to do next

Get the exact price and the head count for the unit you want, then run them through the calculator. Check whether electricity, heat and internet are in the rent. If your group could shrink, run the table at one person fewer and decide whether you could cover it.
