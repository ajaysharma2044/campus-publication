Varna is a hamlet on Dryden Road east of campus, and living there means riding a TCAT bus or driving. Ivy Ridge Townhomes lists an on-site stop with direct routes to Cornell, but it does not publish route numbers or travel times, so ask TCAT before you sign ([Ivy Ridge Townhomes](https://ivyridgetownhomes.com/)).

## What can you rent in Varna?

Ivy Ridge Townhomes, at 802-812 Dryden Rd., is the building students ask about. Its FAQ says the units are fully furnished, that rent includes internet, cable, trash, recycling, snow removal and landscaping, and that residents pay only electricity ([Ivy Ridge FAQ](https://ivyridgetownhomes.com/faqs/)).

The site calls the property "less than a mile" from campus and an easy commute ([Ivy Ridge FAQ](https://ivyridgetownhomes.com/faqs/)). The straight-line distance from Ho Plaza is 1.85 miles, and the road climbs, so plan on the bus. The site also states that it is not affiliated with Cornell.

Two details need a question before you apply. The footer says Campus Advantage manages the community, while the contact address and the "Apply Now" button point to Ithacor, a Cortland company. The ownership line is an unfilled template. Ask who the landlord of record is, and get the parking terms in writing.

## What do residents say about Ivy Ridge?

Veryapt, a review site, shows seven Ivy Ridge reviews averaging 6.4 out of 10. Reviewers were Johnson MBA students, and the reviews run from November 2019 to October 2025. They praised the Arboretum next door, pets and furnished options. They complained about dripping mini-split heat pumps, leaking pipes, slow management and limited parking ([Veryapt](https://www.veryapt.com/ApartmentReview-a23680-ivy-ridge-ithaca)).

Seven reviews are a small sample. Read them as a list of questions: who fixes a leak, how fast, and how many spaces each unit gets. If you are a graduate student choosing between buildings, the [graduate student housing guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/) compares the other neighborhoods.

## Which rules change outside the City of Ithaca?

Ivy Ridge sits on a Town of Dryden parcel, so the City of Ithaca's Certificate of Compliance, the three-year safety inspection permit, does not apply. Cornell's safety tool covers City of Ithaca rentals only, and it does not list Town, Lansing or Cayuga Heights buildings ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)). The [City versus town guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) sorts out which rules follow your address.

For a repair that goes unanswered, the code office is the Town of Dryden's, not the City's. Its number is 607-844-8888 ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).

## How does TCAT work from Varna?

Cornell students ride TCAT with their Cornell ID. The Cornell Johnson MBA page says rides are free the first year, then free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend ([Cornell Johnson MBA](https://business.cornell.edu/students/by-program/mba-students/living-in-ithaca/)). The TCAT cash fare is $1.50 a ride ([Ithaca College](https://www.ithaca.edu/id-office/tcat-information)). Cornell cut off free daytime rides for non-first-years in October 2024 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free)). The full rules are in the [TCAT guide for Cornell students](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/).

Here is what a daytime commuter pays. A round trip costs two daytime fares, or $3.00, and twenty weekday round trips come to $60.00. TCAT sells day and monthly passes, so compare its current prices at [tcatbus.com](https://www.tcatbus.com/ride/fare-pass-options/). Your own numbers go in the calculator below.

TCAT's phone line is 607-277-7433, and schedules are at tcatbus.com/bus-schedules ([Ithaca Tompkins International Airport](https://www.flyithaca.com/public-transportation)). Ask which route stops at Ivy Ridge, when the last bus leaves campus, and how the schedule changes in summer and winter break.

Ask the landlord about summer: PhD students and graduate assistants get a free OmniRide pass, the Cornell pass that covers all TCAT rides, according to [Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

## Is there another way in from Dryden?

Cortland Public Transit, a separate county bus system, serves Cornell, TC3 and Dryden, per Cornell Transportation's bus page. The page is undated and lists no schedule, so call Cortland Public Transit before you plan around it. Driving is the other option. The [car ownership guide](/getting-around/cornell-parking-guide-campus-collegetown-ithaca/) covers permits and parking costs.
