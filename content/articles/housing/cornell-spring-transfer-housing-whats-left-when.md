If you start at Cornell in January 2027, Cornell places you in upper-level campus housing for the spring and keeps you there through the 2027-28 year ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)). Your first off-campus lease starts in August 2028. Plan to sign it in 2027, while you are still in a residence hall.

Entering in fall or want every class year? See the [Cornell transfer housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/).

## Where do spring transfers live at Cornell?

You will land in an upper-level hall, not a first-year dorm. Cornell places transfers in Program Houses, South Campus halls and the West Campus House System, and transfers can also choose co-ops. It groups transfers together when it can. Transfers who count as first-years go to first-year residences. Every residence-hall resident must also carry a meal plan.

You do not pick the building. Unless you apply to a program house, Cornell sets your hall by random auto-allocation, meaning a computer assigns it and students do not rank halls ([Cornell Housing application process](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)). Apply within 10 days of getting your NetID, your Cornell login.

South Campus is the cluster of older halls in and near Collegetown, the student neighborhood just south of campus. Four of its halls take transfers:

Cascadilla Hall holds the Service Center for all the Collegetown-area halls. None of the three older halls has air conditioning in the rooms. 301 College Avenue is the newest, with kitchenettes holding a fridge and microwave ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue)).

West Campus is the other common landing spot. About 15% of West Campus House System residents are transfers, and residents can stay in the same house from sophomore to senior year ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system)). For a spring transfer with three semesters on campus, that means one house the whole time.

## Which rooms are left by January?

Expect a shared room in an older hall, not a single in a newer one. In the fall 2025 selection, a sophomore transfer with a 7:30 p.m. timeslot on the last day found only Clara Dickson triples and Sheldon Court doubles left ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)). Cornell posts no list of open spring rooms, so the end of the fall draw is the best guide.

You can still move after you arrive. One Prospect of Whitby co-president transferred from Hunter College, lived first in Cascadilla Hall and moved into Whitby, a co-op on Wait Avenue, that January ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/unexpected-delights-prospect-whitby)). A co-op is a small house where residents share cooking and chores. Co-ops run a recruiting round called Mosey each spring. In 2024 it opened Sunday, Feb. 11, decisions came Feb. 28, and most houses also took members on a rolling basis ([Co-op Mosey 2024](https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/news-events/news/co-op-mosey-2024)). Students in a residence hall who get a co-op spot must file a room change.

## What does spring 2027 on campus cost?

A double room with the Collegetown Meal Plan costs $10,450 for spring 2027. That is the $6,872 double rate per semester for 2026-27 ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)), plus the $3,528 Collegetown Meal Plan, plus the $50 yearly dining fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

The Collegetown Meal Plan, formerly the South Campus Meal Plan, gives up to 10 meals a week plus $500 in Big Red Bucks and $436 in City Bucks per semester. It is open to residents of Cascadilla, Sheldon, Schuyler, 301 College Ave., 112 Edgemoor and 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive. South Campus residents are enrolled in the Unlimited plan when they sign and can switch to the Collegetown plan ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

Across all three semesters, a double and the Collegetown plan come to $31,300 at 2026-27 rates. That is three rooms at $6,872 ($20,616), three meal plans at $3,528 ($10,584) and two yearly fees of $50 ($100). Cornell has not set 2027-28 rates, so the fall 2027 and spring 2028 parts will change ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

A co-op costs less for the room. Co-op doubles run $3,487 to $4,981 per semester for 2026-27, and some co-ops run their own house meal plans in place of Cornell Dining ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

```reader "If you're a parent paying the bill"
Cornell bills hall rent in August and December, so the spring 2027 room charge arrives in December 2026. There is no damage deposit. New transfers cannot cancel the housing license unless they stop attending or are accepted to a sorority, fraternity or university co-op ([2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)), so budget for all three semesters, not just the spring.
```

## When does each step happen?

Halls reopen for spring at 9 a.m. Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027, after closing at 2 p.m. Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026 ([Cornell winter closing checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-break-closing-checklist)). The spring term begins Jan. 25, 2027 ([Cornell Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)). Spring 2027 ends May 22 ([Cornell Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)), and your housing license runs through May 23.

Cornell publishes confirmation timing only for fall entrants, who hear from mid-June. Email housing@cornell.edu after you apply to ask when your spring assignment will post.

The junior and senior selection for 2027-28 ran in fall 2026, before you arrive ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process)). Email housing@cornell.edu by Feb. 1, 2027, to ask which process covers you.

## Can a spring transfer skip campus housing?

Yes, if you meet Cornell's exemption rule and get approval first. Cornell's residential policy says transfers who lived on campus for two years at their old school, or who enter as juniors or seniors, are not held to the two-year requirement ([Cornell residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). The transfer page says all new transfers live on campus for a year. Go by the policy, and file the exemption request so the answer is in writing.

You request it in the Housing application during the open application period, before you sign a housing license. An exemption lasts one academic year, and transfers must reapply each year. Cornell says first- and second-year students "should not sign a lease unless they have an approved exemption" ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process)). A student who skips campus without one "will still be billed for the full year" ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)). The full rules are in our guide to [residential requirement exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## When should you sign your first off-campus lease?

Sign your first lease between April and October 2027 for an August 2028 move-in. One Collegetown listing runs its 12-month lease from Aug. 1, so a first lease likely starts Aug. 1, 2028 ([Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095)). The most popular Collegetown units leave the market up to 16 months before move-in ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)). Sixteen months before that is April 1, 2027, about 10 weeks after you arrive. Rising juniors like to sign before October prelims, the midterm exams, students told The Sun.

You will be choosing roommates for 2028-29 after one semester in Ithaca. The market is wider than the rush suggests. Cornell's Off-Campus Living manager called the idea that you must lock housing the day you return in fall a myth, and a developer said there is no single date when everything is gone ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/)). Both hold: the best Collegetown units go early, and the wider city keeps listing later.

Start with the Off-Campus Living email list, which carries housing, roommate and sublet posts. It needs a NetID; send a message with the subject "Join" and a blank body ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). Our guide to [where to find Cornell listings](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) covers the rest, and the [Collegetown and downtown rent-per-bed data](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/) shows what a bed costs in each area.

Two summers fall between your housing terms: your spring license ends May 23, 2027, your 2027-28 license ends in May 2028, and the lease starts in August. If you stay in Ithaca for either summer, see the [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/).

## FAQ

**Do spring transfers ride TCAT buses free?**
Yes. New degree students, transfers included, get unlimited TCAT rides with a Cornell ID for their first year. For spring entrants the pass expires Dec. 31 ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). Arrival options are in [getting to Ithaca and around Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell/).

**Can a spring transfer live in a fraternity or sorority house?**
Transfers can join a chapter in their first semester, and formal recruitment runs the week before classes each January. Moving into a chapter house is not a reason Cornell releases a spring housing license.

## What to do next

Apply for housing within 10 days of getting your NetID. If you enter as a junior or senior, file the exemption request in the same application. Join the Off-Campus Living email list before you arrive, and set April 2027 as the month you start touring Collegetown for 2028-29. The [spring transfer start page](/start/transfer-spring/) lists the other first steps.
