```glance
- On most local leases, each roommate is responsible for the whole rent and all damage.
- A tenant who overpays can sue a roommate for the difference.
- Free mediation is available from the Community Dispute Resolution Center at 607-273-9347.
- Small claims courts handle disputes up to $3,000 in towns and $5,000 in Ithaca City Court.
```

When roommates on one lease disagree, the landlord can still demand the full rent from any of you. Settle it with a written agreement, a sublet, free mediation or, last, small claims. Cornell says most local leases bind housemates "jointly and severally," so each owes the others' rent and damages for the term ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)). This page is general information, not legal advice.

## What happens when a roommate stops paying?

You owe the shortfall. Each tenant can be held for the full rent, and a tenant who pays more than a fair share can sue the co-tenant for contribution ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)).

Here is the math for a four-person Collegetown 4BR at the 2027-28 median of $1,246.25 per bed. The unit costs $4,985.00 a month (4 x $1,246.25). If one roommate stops paying, the other three cover that bed's share, or $415.42 each, and the landlord's notice still goes to all of you ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

The nonpayment process moves on a clock. If rent is unpaid after 5 days, the landlord sends a certified-mail notice. A 14-day rent demand cannot be served until at least 6 days after rent is due, and you then have 14 days to pay or leave before a court filing ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/eviction-proceedings)). Paying inside that window avoids a filing. Then pursue the roommate for it. If a notice or court paper arrives, call the Tenants Advocacy hotline at 607-301-1560 the same day.

## What if a roommate damages the apartment?

The landlord can charge the deposit for damage beyond normal wear, and on a joint lease the deposit is shared, so everyone loses it together. A move-in inspection protects you: the landlord must offer one before you move in, and if it is accepted, both sides sign a written condition record and the landlord cannot keep the deposit for the defects listed ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Photograph the unit at move-in and move-out. Write how the deposit is split into a roommate agreement. The [deposit rules guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) and [move-out guide](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/) cover the 14-day return and the inspection.

## Can you make a roommate leave?

Not by yourselves. A roommate who signed the lease has the same right to live there as you do. Only the landlord can change who is on the lease, and any change should be in writing, signed by the landlord and every tenant. Call the Tenants Advocacy hotline at 607-301-1560 before you try.

A different case is a person who lives there but did not sign. State law says a tenant must give an occupant's name to the landlord within 30 days of move-in or of the landlord asking, and an occupant has no right to stay if the tenant leaves ([RPL 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F)). On a multiple-tenant lease, tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the number of tenants on the lease. A landlord must give a licensee, a person who lives there with permission but no lease, a 10-day notice to quit before an eviction.

## What if you are the one who wants out?

Your options are the same as for any tenant: a written release, a sublet or an assignment. Your roommates stay liable for your share until the landlord releases you, and you stay liable if a subtenant stops paying. The full walkthrough is in [breaking a lease in Ithaca](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/). For a summer or semester away, use the [sublet guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/). If a subtenant does not pay, the Cornell Law guide says you can pursue the subtenant for breach of contract in small claims, city, town or supreme court ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

## Who can help settle it?

The Community Dispute Resolution Center offers mediation for neighbor, family and tenant disputes at 607-273-9347 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/community-resources)). Cornell's Off-Campus Living, at 607-255-2310, offers guides on conflict resolution with roommates and landlords and one-on-one consultations, but it does not represent students in disputes ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease)).

## When does small claims make sense?

Small claims court takes disputes up to $5,000 in Ithaca City Court at 118 East Clinton St., and up to $3,000 in town and village courts ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). It fits a roommate who owes you money after you covered their share. Bring the lease, the payment records and your written messages. Send a written demand first, and try mediation before you file.

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
Agree on a written roommate plan before you sign: who pays what, who finds a subtenant, how the deposit is split and what happens if someone leaves. The lease will not protect you from a roommate, but a signed plan makes a small claim easy to prove.
```

## What to do next

1. Write down what each roommate owes and what you agreed.
2. Offer mediation with the Community Dispute Resolution Center.
3. Call 607-301-1560 if a notice or court paper arrives.
