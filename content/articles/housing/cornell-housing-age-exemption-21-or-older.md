```glance
- You qualify if you are 21 or older by the start of the academic year. Turning 21 later doesn't count.
- Rising sophomores must request the 2027-28 exemption by April 4, 2027, at 11:59 p.m.
- An exemption covers one academic year. A 21-year-old first-year files again for sophomore year.
- Sign a lease only after approval. Without it, Cornell bills the full year anyway.
```

If you will be 21 or older by the start of the academic year, Cornell exempts you from its campus housing rule for that year ([Cornell Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). The exemption isn't automatic. You request it in the Housing application, and Housing must approve it before you sign a lease.

## Who qualifies for the age exemption?

Cornell's two-year residential requirement makes full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students live in university housing and carry a meal plan while they live on campus. First-years go to residence halls. Second-years can choose a hall, one of the eight university-owned co-ops or a recognized fraternity or sorority house.

The age rule takes you out of that requirement. Cornell's wording is that students "21 and older by the start of the academic year" of the exemption are not subject to it. Age is one of six exemptions; the others cover married students, veterans, students with legal dependents, some transfers and local commuters. Our [guide to every exemption](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) covers those.

A second age line sits at the other end. Students who are 16 or younger at the start of the year are also exempt, but only if they live with a custodial parent or legal guardian for the whole year ([Cornell Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

## What if you turn 21 during the school year?

The exemption doesn't apply that year. Cornell measures your age on one day: the start of the academic year you are asking about.

Say you start sophomore year at 20 and turn 21 on Oct. 3. You are not exempt for sophomore year. By junior year the requirement no longer covers you, so the birthday never changes your housing ([Cornell Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

The birthday that matters falls before sophomore year starts. Say you turn 21 in March of your first year. You can request an exemption for sophomore year and rent off campus as a second-year ([Cornell Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

You can't switch partway through a year either. Cornell takes exemption requests during the open application period. It does not accept them after you sign a housing license, the contract for a campus room, or while that license is running. Cornell's residential policy does not print the exact date it uses as the start of the academic year. If your birthday falls in August, email housing@cornell.edu before you file and ask which date counts.

## How do you apply, and what documents do you need?

You apply online, inside the same Housing application every student uses.

Cornell says only that "supporting documentation may be required." It doesn't list which papers it accepts for the age exemption. Have a government ID that shows your birth date ready in case Housing asks.

```reader "If you're a first-year who is already 21"
The same exemption covers your first year, but the timing is different. Request it in your first-year Housing application, before you sign a housing license, because Cornell won't consider it after that.
```

## When is the 2027-28 deadline?

April 4, 2027, at 11:59 p.m. for rising sophomores. Cornell will not consider any request received after that ([Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

## Do you have to reapply every year?

Yes. Each exemption covers a single academic year, and returning students must reapply for every year they want one. That means at most two requests. A first-year who enters at 21 files twice, once for each year. A student who turns 21 during first year files once, for sophomore year ([Cornell Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

```reader "If you're a spring transfer"
Cornell's [transfer housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) says spring transfers live on campus that spring and the following academic year. It doesn't mention the age exemption. The residential policy lists age exemptions for all students, so request one in your Housing application before you sign a license. Get Housing's answer in writing. Our [transfer housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/) covers the rest of your first year.
```

## What happens if you sign a lease before approval?

You pay twice. Cornell tells first- and second-years they "should not sign a lease" without an approved exemption ([Housing Search Process](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process)). Students who neither meet the requirement nor hold an exemption "will still be billed for the full year" ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)).

Signing a campus license first is just as binding. First-years and second-years cannot cancel a housing assignment unless they leave the university, are approved for an exemption, or join an approved co-op or Greek house in time ([Housing License 2027-28](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

An unapproved lease sits on top of the campus year. A residence hall double costs $6,872 per semester for 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing Rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). The Unlimited meal plan costs $7,604 a year, plus a $50 administrative fee each year ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## What should you rent once you're approved?

Rent a shared bed, and expect to shop late. The exemption deadline falls in April, but much of Collegetown, the rental neighborhood one block from campus, leases the fall before. Landlord Nick Lambrou told The Cornell Daily Sun that rising juniors tend to sign before fall prelims, and one group signed for a 12-bedroom house at 410 Elmwood Avenue in April 2017, 16 months before moving in ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)).

If you turned 21 well before the deadline, file early, then shop as soon as approval arrives. If you are joining friends who sign in the fall, don't co-sign until you hold an approval. Our guide to [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) lays out the calendar.

Cornell sells debit plans to students who don't live in campus housing: Off-Campus Value at $1,824.60 a year, Flex 10/500 at $1,348 and Just Bucks at $1,000 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## FAQ

**Can I still live on campus if I'm 21?**
Yes. The age exemption is something you request, not a rule that removes you. If you don't file, you take part in room selection like any other first- or second-year.

**Who reviews the request?**
Housing Operations, at 1520 Clara Dickson Hall. Call 607-255-5368 or email housing@cornell.edu ([Housing and Residential Life Directory](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211065419/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory)). The office handles applications, cancellations and exemptions.
