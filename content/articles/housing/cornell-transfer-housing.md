```glance
- Fall transfers entering as first-years or sophomores live on campus for one academic year.
- Junior and senior transfers are exempt, but must request the exemption every year.
- Spring transfers live on campus for that spring and the next full year.
- A new transfer cannot cancel a Cornell license to move into an apartment, co-op or Greek house.
```

New Cornell transfers who enter as first-years or sophomores live on campus for one academic year, in a room Cornell assigns. A double costs [$13,744 for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). Transfers who enter as juniors or seniors are exempt and can rent off campus once the exemption is approved.

## Who has to live on campus?

Two things decide it: the class year you enter with and where you lived before. Cornell's [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) lists the exemptions, and any one of them lets a transfer rent off campus.

| Your situation at entry | On campus? | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Fall transfer, first-year or sophomore standing | Yes, one academic year | Apply for housing; sign no lease this year |
| Spring transfer, first-year or sophomore standing | Yes, that spring plus the next academic year | Apply for housing; first lease is about 18 months out |
| Entering as a junior or senior | No, once exempt | Request the exemption, then sign |
| Two years on campus at your previous school | No, once exempt | Request it; documents may be required |
| Married, with legal dependents, a veteran, or 21 or older by the start of the year | No, once exempt | Request it; ROTC does not count as service |

Request the exemption in the Housing application for the exemption term when it opens. Cornell does not accept requests after you sign a housing license. Each exemption covers one academic year, so a transfer must reapply every year.

Cornell does not post an exemption deadline for newly admitted transfers. The [April 4, 2027 deadline](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores) applies to rising sophomores. Call Housing Operations at 607-255-5368 before you plan around a date.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer"
You live on campus for 2026-27 and cannot sign a lease for this year. A lease does not release you: a student without an exemption is [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) anyway. You also cannot cancel the license for a co-op or Greek offer, because that release is [for second-years only](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). The catch is timing. You arrive in August as the Collegetown rush for your junior year starts, so meet people first, then decide.
```

```reader "If you're a junior or senior transfer"
You are exempt, but only after you request it in the Housing application during an open period, and never after you sign a Cornell license. Get approval in writing before you sign a lease. The exemption lasts one academic year, so a junior transfer files again for senior year. You arrive after the Collegetown rush, so shop the leftovers described below.
```

```reader "If you're a spring transfer"
You live on campus for your first spring and the following academic year. Arrive in January 2027 and you stay in Cornell housing through May 2028, so your first lease starts about August 2028. Collegetown leases sign 10 to 16 months ahead, so that search opens in late August 2027. Your free TCAT rides end Dec. 31, 2027, before you live off campus, so price the bus into a distant apartment.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
The rules match domestic transfers, but the timing is harder, because an exempt junior often signs a lease from abroad. Read the [international student guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-international-students/) for deposits, signing from abroad and breaks. If you check in to Cornell housing late, arrange it first: failing to check in by the first day of classes costs 50% of the fall room rate.
```

```reader "If you're married or have children"
You are exempt at any class year, but you must file in the Housing application and renew each year. Cornell's [Hasbrouck family units](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) run $1,475 to $1,530 a month and sit under graduate housing. Cornell's pages do not say whether an undergraduate family may apply, so ask Housing Operations.
```

## What does the on-campus year cost?

Activate your NetID, then apply in the Housing and Dining Portal. Cornell advises transfers to [apply within ten days of receiving the NetID](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process); confirmations begin in mid-June. Cornell picks the room, and students do not rank halls unless they apply to a program house.

Transfers go to upper-level halls: Program Houses, South Campus, the West Campus House System and the co-ops. Cornell groups transfers together when it can.

| 2026-27 Cornell room | Per academic year | Per month over 12 months |
|---|---|---|
| Triple or one-room quad | $12,644 | $1,053.67 |
| Double | $13,744 | $1,145.33 |
| Single or townhouse double | $15,566 | $1,297.17 |
| Super single | $16,595 | $1,382.92 |

Source: Cornell housing rates; monthly figures are the yearly rate divided by 12.

Residence-hall residents also need a meal plan. Cornell's eight co-ops hold 10 to 35 members each, run [their own selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing) in early November and early February, and charge $3,487 to $4,981 per semester for doubles.

The license is not a lease. New transfers [cannot cancel it](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) unless they stop attending or are exempt, and days outside the occupancy period cost $60 each.

## What has placement been like?

Placement news comes late. In 2018 The Cornell Daily Sun reported that transfers learned [on June 22](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus) whether they had campus housing. Some were placed far from the center of campus and said they felt isolated.

The next-year problem is real too. In fall 2025 the Sun reported that a sophomore transfer drew the last selection slot and found only leftover triples and doubles ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)). Housing told the paper housing is not guaranteed beyond the first two years.

## How does an exempt transfer find an apartment?

You are admitted months after the Collegetown rush, so a summer search covers what is left ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)). Most Ithaca leases end July 31 and restart around Aug. 1.

| Option | Price | Distance to campus |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown 3BR share, Ithaca Renting | [$795 per bed a month](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), 2026-27 median | 0.5 to 1.5 miles |
| Downtown 2BR share, Ithaca Renting | $992.50 per bed a month, 2026-27 median | Same |
| Collegetown 2BR share, Ithaca Renting | [$1,377.50 per bed a month](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), 2027-28 median | 0.1 to 0.5 miles |
| Dryden South single suite | [$1,395 per person a month](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), leases end May 31, 2027 | Next to campus |
| Lansing West 1BR, "now" units | [$1,038 and $1,193 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx) | 5 to 10 miles |

Every new degree student, transfers included, rides TCAT [free for the first year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Fall entrants keep that through Aug. 31 of the next year. After that, a daytime ride costs $1.50.

Say a junior transfer admitted in June wants a room for August. A Collegetown 2BR share costs $16,530 over 12 months ($1,377.50 times 12). A downtown 2BR share costs $11,910 ($992.50 times 12), which is $4,620 less. It is also $1,834 under the Cornell double ($13,744 minus $11,910). The downtown share wins unless walking to class matters more than $4,620 (prices from [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## How do I find roommates when I know no one?

Join Cornell's [Off-Campus Living email list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) for housing, roommates and sublets. Subscribe with the subject "Join" and a blank body. Admitted students without a NetID forward their acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu.

On a shared lease, each roommate can be liable for the whole rent. If a stranger you met in June leaves in October, the rest of you pay. Run through Cornell's [housemate questions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) first: study hours, guests, cleanliness, who pays the bills, and what happens if someone transfers.

## What mistakes cost transfers the most?

- **Signing a lease as a sophomore transfer for this year.** Cornell still bills the full year on top of 12 months of rent.
- **Forgetting to refile the exemption.** It lasts one academic year, so a junior who skips it for senior year falls back under the rule.
- **Signing a Cornell license before asking for the exemption.** Cornell takes no request after that.
- **Paying Collegetown prices for a summer leftover.** A downtown 2BR share runs $385 a month less per bed.
- **Signing with strangers from a rushed search.** One leaving roommate can leave the rest owing.

## FAQ

**Is housing guaranteed for transfers?** Cornell requires new transfers to live on campus and lists where it places them, but the transfer page does not use the word guarantee. Expect an assignment, not a choice.

**Can a transfer join a co-op or Greek house in the first year?** Cornell's transfer page lists co-ops among transfer placements, and each house runs its own selection, so email co-ops@cornell.edu. A new transfer cannot cancel the license for a Greek, co-op or affiliated house.

**Who answers housing questions?** [Housing Operations](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory) is at 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, housing@cornell.edu.
