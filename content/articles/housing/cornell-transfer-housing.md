```glance
- Fall transfers entering as first-years or sophomores live on campus for one academic year; a 2026-27 double costs $13,744.
- Transfers entering as juniors or seniors, or with two years on campus elsewhere, are exempt, but must request the exemption every year.
- Spring transfers live on campus for that spring and the next full year: their first lease is about 18 months away.
- A new transfer cannot cancel a Cornell license to move into an apartment, a co-op or a Greek house.
- Exempt transfers shop the leftover market: downtown shares at $795 to $992.50 per bed a month, and Lansing.
- First-year TCAT rides are free, so downtown or Lansing costs nothing extra to reach in year one.
```

New Cornell transfers who enter as first-years or sophomores live on campus for [one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), in a room Cornell assigns, at [$13,744 for a double in 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). Transfers who enter as juniors or seniors, or who lived on campus for two years at their old school, are exempt and may rent off campus, once the exemption is approved. Your class year at entry and your start term decide everything else.

```reader "If you're a sophomore transfer"
You live on campus for 2026-27 and cannot sign a lease for this year. A lease does not release you: a student without an exemption is [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) anyway, $13,744 for a double plus your rent. You also cannot cancel the license for a co-op or Greek offer; that release is [for second-years only](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). The catch is timing: you arrive in August just as the Collegetown rush for your junior year starts. On 2026-11-08, [14 of 67 Collegetown rows](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) at one landlord were already sold out. Meet people first, then decide.
```

```reader "If you're a junior or senior transfer"
You are [exempt](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), but only after you request it in the Housing application during an open period, and never after you sign a Cornell license. Get approval in writing before you sign a lease. The exemption lasts one academic year, so a junior transfer files again for senior year. You arrive after the Collegetown rush, so shop the leftovers: downtown 3BR shares at [$795 per bed a month](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), "now" units in Lansing, and sublets. The costly mistake is paying Collegetown prices for a late leftover when downtown runs about 20% to 28% cheaper per bed.
```

```reader "If you're a spring transfer"
You live on campus for [your first spring and the following academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Arrive in January 2027 and you stay in Cornell housing through May 2028; your first lease starts about August 2028. Collegetown leases sign 10 to 16 months ahead, so that search opens in late August 2027, your first fall. Your free TCAT rides [end December 31](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) of your start year, before you ever live off campus, so price the bus into a distant apartment.
```

```reader "If you're an international transfer"
The rules match domestic transfers; the timing is harder. Visa appointments can be no more than [120 days before your program starts](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students), so an exempt junior often signs a lease from abroad. You do not need an SSN to rent. Not confirmed: whether Ithaca landlords ask international students for a guarantor. New York caps a deposit plus any rent advance at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). In Cornell housing, check in by the first day of classes or arrange a late arrival: a no-show is billed [50% of the fall room rate](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). If you cannot fly home over breaks, ask [housing@cornell.edu](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources) about break housing.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer who is married or has children"
Married students and students [coming to Ithaca with legal dependents](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) are exempt at any class year. File in the Housing application and renew each year. You need a 2BR on a 12-month lease. The [Ithaca average 2BR asked $2,459 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) in October 2026; Lansing West listed 2BRs at [$1,825 and $1,870 and up](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx). Cornell's [Hasbrouck family units](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) ($1,475 to $1,530 a month) sit under graduate housing. Not confirmed: whether an undergraduate family may apply.
```

## Who must live on campus

The rule turns on two things: the class year you enter with, and where you lived before. Cornell's [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) lists the exemptions. Any of them lets a transfer rent off campus.

| Your situation at entry | On campus? | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Fall transfer, first-year or sophomore standing | Yes, one academic year | Apply for housing; no lease this year |
| Spring transfer, first-year or sophomore standing | Yes, that spring plus the next academic year | Apply for housing; first lease about 18 months out |
| Entering as a junior or senior | No, once exempt | Request the exemption, then sign |
| Two years on campus at your previous school | No, once exempt | Request it; documents may be required |
| Married, with legal dependents, a veteran, or 21+ by the start of the year | No, once exempt | Request it; ROTC does not count as service |

Request the exemption by logging in to the Housing application and applying for the exemption term when it is open. Cornell [does not accept requests](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) after you sign a housing license. Each exemption covers one academic year, and transfers must reapply every year.

Not confirmed: the exemption deadline for newly admitted transfers. The April 6, 2026 deadline Cornell posts applies to current first- and second-years.

## Your on-campus year: applying, placement and cost

Activate your NetID, then apply in the Housing and Dining Portal. Cornell advises transfers to apply [within ten days of receiving the NetID](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process); confirmations begin in mid-June. Cornell picks the room.

Transfers go to [upper-level halls](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates): Program Houses, South Campus, the West Campus House System and the co-ops. Cornell groups transfers together when it can. Some who qualify as first-years land in first-year halls.

| 2026-27 Cornell room | Per academic year | Per month over 12 months (our math) |
|---|---|---|
| Triple or one-room quad | $12,644 | $1,054 |
| Double | $13,744 | $1,145 |
| Single or townhouse double | $15,566 | $1,297 |
| Super single | $16,595 | $1,383 |
| Co-op double | $6,974 to $9,962 | $581 to $830 |

Hall rates include gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month, per the [rate page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). Residence-hall residents also need a meal plan. Co-op doubles run $3,487 to $4,981 a semester; the yearly figures double that. Cornell's eight co-ops hold 10 to 35 members each and run [their own selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing) from early November and early February (co-ops@cornell.edu, 607-255-3445).

The license is not a lease. You cannot walk away from it: new transfers [cannot cancel](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) unless they stop attending or are exempt. Days outside the occupancy period cost $60 each.

## What placement has looked like

Placement news comes late. In 2018 the Sun reported that transfers learned [on June 22](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus) whether they had campus housing. Some were placed far away, Schuyler House among them, and said they felt isolated. One transfer in that story advised spending about a week getting to know roommates before signing anything. That is one 2018 account, the only transfer-specific one on record, but the timing problem has not changed.

The next-year problem is real too. For 2026-27, Cornell's room selection ran October 2 to 8, 2025. The Sun reported in [November 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed) that a sophomore transfer drew the last selection slot and found only leftover triples and doubles. Housing said it would offer off-campus guidance and housing fairs to rising juniors and seniors without a room.

## Renting as an exempt transfer: the leftover market

You are admitted months after the Collegetown rush has picked over the stock. Popular Collegetown units sign 10 to 16 months before move-in. A summer search is a search of what is left.

| Option | Price (period, date) | Distance to campus |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown 3BR share, Ithaca Renting | [$795 per bed a month](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), 2026-27 median | 0.5 to 1.5 miles, 20 to 30 minute uphill walk |
| Downtown 2BR share, Ithaca Renting | $992.50 per bed a month, 2026-27 median | Same |
| Collegetown 2BR share, Ithaca Renting | [$1,377.50 per bed a month](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), 2027-28 median | 0.1 to 0.5 miles |
| Dryden South single suite, Collegetown | [$1,395 per person a month](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), leases end May 31, 2027 | Next to campus |
| Lansing West 1BR, "now" units | [$1,038 and $1,193 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx), November 2026 | 5 to 10 miles, bus or car |

Distances come from Cornell's [neighborhood guide](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods). Ithaca Renting's downtown rows include heat, water and trash. Downtown rows are mostly 2026-27 leftovers, so they may be discounted against Collegetown's 2027-28 prices.

Distance is cheaper for you than for anyone else. Every new degree student, transfers included, rides TCAT [free for the first year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). For fall entrants that runs to August 31 of the following year. After that, daytime weekday rides cost $1.50, or $200 a year for an OmniRide pass.

Example: a junior transfer admitted in June wants a room for August. A Collegetown 2BR share at $1,377.50 costs $16,530 for 12 months. A downtown 2BR share at $992.50 costs $11,910, which is $4,620 less and $1,834 under the Cornell double. The bus to campus is free all year. The downtown share wins unless walking to class matters more than $4,620.

## Finding roommates when you know no one

Join Cornell's [Off-Campus Living email list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) for housing, roommates and sublets. Subscribe with the subject "Join" and a blank body. It needs a NetID. Admitted students without one forward their acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu.

A shared lease usually makes every roommate liable for the whole rent. If a stranger you met in June leaves in October, the rest of you pay. Run through Cornell's [housemate questions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) first: study hours, guests, cleanliness, who pays the bills, and what happens if someone transfers or studies abroad.

Most Ithaca leases end July 31 and start again on or about August 1. A spring transfer's Collegetown search for August 2028 opens about August 2027, ten to 16 months ahead. Cornell warns that first- and second-year undergraduates [should not sign a lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process) without an approved exemption. A sophomore transfer signing in the fall for junior year should confirm with Housing that the lease year falls outside the rule before paying anything.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing a lease as a sophomore transfer for this year.** Cornell still bills the full year: $13,744 for a double, on top of 12 months of rent.
- **Forgetting to refile the exemption.** It lasts one academic year. A junior who skips it for senior year falls back under the rule.
- **Signing a Cornell license before asking for the exemption.** Cornell takes no exemption request after the license is signed, and a new transfer cannot cancel it.
- **Paying Collegetown prices for a summer leftover.** A downtown 2BR share at $992.50 a bed runs $385 a month less than a Collegetown one, about $4,620 a year.
- **Signing with strangers from a rushed search.** Each co-tenant can owe the whole rent if one leaves.
- **Arriving late without telling Housing.** Missing check-in by the first day of classes costs 50% of the fall room rate.

## FAQ

**Is housing guaranteed for transfers?** Cornell requires new transfers to live on campus and lists where it places them, but the [transfer page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) does not use the word guarantee. Expect an assignment, not a choice.

**Can I live in a fraternity or sorority house my first year?** No. A new transfer cannot cancel the Cornell license for a Greek, co-op or affiliated house; only second-years can, before the Spring Housing Selection deadline.

**Does my exemption carry over from year to year?** No. Each exemption covers one academic year, and transfers [must reapply each year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).

**Who answers housing questions?** [Housing Operations](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory), 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, housing@cornell.edu. [Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) is 607-255-2310 for lease and roommate questions.

**Can a transfer join a co-op?** Cornell's transfer page lists co-ops among transfer placements. Each house runs its own selection, so ask co-ops@cornell.edu before your application.
