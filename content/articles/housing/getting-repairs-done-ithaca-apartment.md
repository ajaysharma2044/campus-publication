To get a repair done, send the landlord a written request with your name, address, date and photos, then keep a log of every reply. New York law gives no strict repair deadline, so the written record is what makes the later steps work ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).

## What must a landlord fix?

Every residential lease carries the warranty of habitability, which is the landlord's implied promise that the home and shared areas are fit to live in and not dangerous to health or safety. You cannot waive it, and it does not cover conditions you caused ([RPL 235-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-B)).

The Cornell Law guide lists what counts: no heat, no hot or cold water, no electricity, plumbing failures, pests, mold, structural defects, lead paint, unsanitary conditions and broken landlord-provided appliances. Missing air conditioning does not qualify, and a code violation alone is not automatically a breach. When a court finds a breach, it has reduced rent by about 10% to 20% for minor problems, about 30% for moderate ones and 50% to 60% for the most serious, in the guide's experience ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).

Follow every phone call with a written note. Certified mail gives proof of delivery. Cooperative Extension's complaint steps match: identify the problem, record dates and photos, tell the owner, ask for a timeframe, follow up, then file a formal complaint ([Cooperative Extension Tompkins](https://ccetompkins.org/your-home-money/tenant-landlord-issues)).

Landlord entry is part of repairs. The lease sets the rules, and the guide describes reasonable notice, often 24 hours, at reasonable hours. A landlord has no right of entry the lease does not reserve, except in an emergency ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access)).

## What are the heat rules?

The heat season runs from Sept. 15 to May 31 in Ithaca, while the statewide season starts Oct. 1. From 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., the unit must reach at least 68F when it is under 55F outside. From 10 p.m. to 6 a.m., it must stay at 62F or higher whatever the outside temperature ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). If your heat is below those numbers, say so in the written request. Heat, mold and bed bugs have their own escalation path in [what to do when a landlord will not fix heat, mold or bed bugs](/cornell/housing/ithaca-landlord-wont-fix-heat-mold-bed-bugs/).

Bed bugs have a special rule. The landlord must exterminate even if you are suspected of bringing them, unless the landlord proves misconduct. Written notice goes to the units above, below and beside yours within 72 hours, without naming your unit. Treating them yourself can weaken a damages claim ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). The county's guidance asks for written landlord cooperation and licensed pest companies ([Tompkins County](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Whole-Health/Community/HNP/Bed-Bugs)).

## Where do you file a complaint?

Which code office handles your complaint depends on where the building is. The numbers below are from the Cornell Law guide.

| Municipality | Code office phone |
|---|---|
| City of Ithaca Building Division | 607-274-6508 |
| Town of Ithaca | 607-273-1783 |
| Town of Lansing | 607-533-7054 |
| Town of Dryden | 607-844-8888 |
| Village of Cayuga Heights | 607-257-5536, ext. 104 |

Source: [Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance); the Village of Cayuga Heights number is its code enforcement officer's line, and its page covers short-term rentals only.

The City also takes complaints through a "Report a Concern" form on the [Building Division page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/130/Building-Division). Ask for inspection reports in writing. Enforcement can take time: in 2018, tenants at one Collegetown house waited 53 days from the first complaint to a reported heater fix ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/)). Which municipality your address sits in is covered in the [City versus town guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/).

## Can you withhold rent or sue?

Withholding rent and repair-and-deduct are last resorts. Get free legal advice first, because withholding invites a nonpayment case that starts with a 14-day written notice ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Call the Tenants Advocacy hotline at 607-301-1560 or LawNY at 607-273-3667 ([Cornell Law Tenants' Rights Guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/)).

Another court route began in January 2024. After you have lived in the unit at least 30 days and sent written notice, you can file a petition asking a judge to order repairs. Small claims for money cover up to $5,000 in Ithaca City Court at 118 East Clinton St., and $3,000 in town and village courts ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).

## Are you protected if you complain?

A landlord cannot refuse to renew or raise the rent unreasonably because of a good-faith complaint within the past year. A tenant evicted within a year of a complaint can defend it as retaliation. The protection does not apply in owner-occupied buildings with fewer than four units ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal)). Whether your building is covered by Good Cause, the renewal law that adds protection, is a question for the checker.

## What to do next

1. Write the request today, with photos and a date.
2. Mark a follow-up date on your calendar.
3. File with the code office for your municipality if the landlord misses it.
