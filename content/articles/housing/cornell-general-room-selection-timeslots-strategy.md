```glance

- Apply in the Housing and Dining Portal by April 4, 2027, at 11:59 p.m.
- A block of up to five friends shares one timeslot, with no penalty for blocking.
- Each 20-minute slot holds about 65 to 70 students, so come with three ranked plans.
```

Cornell assigns every room selection timeslot at random, so applying early or joining a block does not move you up ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/room-selection-faqs)). Rising sophomores get their slots by email April 7, 2027, and pick in Open Room Selection, April 13 to 16. The housing application is due April 4 at 11:59 p.m.

## How are Cornell room selection timeslots assigned?

The housing platform draws them at random after the application closes. Individuals and blocks, Cornell's word for a roommate group that signs up together, have the same chance at an early slot. Applying early only helps the housing office estimate demand. You cannot trade a timeslot or hand yours to another student.

Slots come in 20-minute waves, and in past years about 65 to 70 students shared each one (Room Selection FAQs). Three waves an hour is 195 to 210 students an hour. If your slot starts two hours into a selection day, six earlier waves, or 390 to 420 students, have already picked that day.

Rising sophomores, the students finishing their first year, pick in the spring round. Every rising sophomore who must live on campus should sign up, including students going through fraternity and sorority recruitment. ## How do I pick a room with friends?

Form a block of up to five students before the application deadline. One person, the block leader, creates the block in the Housing and Dining Portal and sets a block username and password. The others join with those details. You can belong to only one block at a time.

Everyone in the block gets the same random timeslot. During that slot, the leader can place every member in any open bed in the same building. A block does not guarantee you all land in one room or even one building; it only gets you through the door together.

The size limit lines up with West Campus. Its new buildings hold 152 suites, and most are five-person suites with three singles, one double, a bathroom and a sitting area (Room Selection FAQs). A full block of five fits one of those suites exactly. A block of four or three will share a five-person suite with someone else, if one is still open at your slot.

Settle the block early, because it locks when the application closes. After that you cannot leave a block to join another. Your block's timeslot stays your timeslot even if the group falls apart. In that case, log in at the slot and choose a room for yourself.

## What happens during my timeslot?

You have four minutes to assign beds to the rooms in your cart, then five minutes to sign your housing license, Cornell's binding contract for the room.

```figure
  - label: Open the 2027-2028 Academic Year application and choose a building with open beds.
  - label: Add a room to your cart. Block leaders add rooms for every blockmate before saving.
  - label: Assign beds within four minutes. Block leaders assign blockmates now, because they cannot do it later.
  - label: Confirm the assignment and sign your housing license.
  - label: Choose your meal plan for fall 2027 and spring 2028. Your options depend on the building you picked.
  - label: Check that the Application Status page shows an assignment and a signed license.
```

Source: Cornell Student & Campus Life, Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores and Room Selection FAQs.

**Watch out:** Cornell asks students not to log in before the slot begins or from two devices at once, because repeat attempts can crash the system. Gather the block around one screen instead.

Blockmates the leader assigns get an email and have two hours to sign their own licenses (Spring Housing Selection page). Sign right away, because an unsigned license does not hold the room.

Rooms carry a gender label. "M" and "F" rooms are fixed. A dynamic room, marked "D," takes the gender of the first student who reserves a bed in it (Room Selection FAQs). A mixed-gender block can live together, but the system will not place them on its own. The block leader calls Cornell Housing at 607-255-5368 during the slot for an override.

## What is the best strategy for a late timeslot?

Plan three ranked options before your timeslot email, and make the third one something you would accept. Cornell tells students to build several housing plans. The tools are the Bed Availability Report, the Bed Availability by Room report and the floor plans, all inside the Housing and Dining Portal. Cornell keeps floor plans off the public web for residents' security, and the Housing Office has paper copies you can look at (Room Selection FAQs).

West Campus is the long shot. More students want it than it can hold, and in past years about 20% of rising sophomores picked a West Campus space (Room Selection FAQs). Most sophomores live on North and South Campus. If your slot falls late on April 15 or 16, plan around North and South first.

A late slot still gets you a bed. Cornell always opens more beds than there are students in the spring round, so every rising sophomore has some choice (Room Selection FAQs). You also do not have to log in at the exact minute. You can pick any time after your slot starts, until Open Room Selection closes on April 16.

Singles go first. Cornell says choosing singles depends entirely on what is left at your slot. A student going alone can take a single if any remain, a free bed in a partly filled room, or an empty double, knowing a stranger can claim the other bed.

The fall round for juniors and seniors shows what a late slot looks like. In October 2025, transfer student Ryan Lee had a 7:30 p.m. slot on the last day, and only triples in Clara Dickson Hall and doubles in Sheldon Court were left, he told [The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed). Sophomores have the bed guarantee that juniors and seniors lack, but the leftover rooms look much the same.

## Can I get a room before Open Room Selection?

Yes, in two cases, both on April 12. Program House Priority Selection comes first for students who want a program house, Cornell's theme communities such as Ujamaa or the Latino Living Center. You mark interest in up to three program houses on your application to get a priority slot. Every student who marks interest gets a separate early slot that day (Spring Housing Selection page).

Students with housing accommodations from Student Disability Services (SDS), Cornell's disability office, also pick that day. Students already approved do not need to reapply. Everyone else needs approval by April 5, 2027.

```reader
If you have an SDS housing accommodation
You get two timeslots: one for SDS Room Selection and one for Open Room Selection. Use the first one. Cornell does not promise a room that meets your needs if you skip the SDS slot. You can bring one roommate: pick your room, then call 607-255-5368 to have that student pulled in.
```

## What if I miss my timeslot or pick nothing?

If you know you will be busy, ask for a proxy, a Cornell Housing staff member who picks for you. Submit the request online ahead of time. The staff member chooses the best open space that matches your instructions, and you then have two hours to sign the license. Cornell lists academic conflicts as the reason for a proxy. If nothing matching your instructions is left, the proxy picks nothing and you choose from what remains.

If you never pick at all, Cornell assigns you a room from whatever is left after the round and bills it to your student account. That also happens to a rising sophomore who never applied: Cornell creates the application for you.

## Can I cancel after I sign?

No, with three exceptions. Rising sophomores cannot cancel a signed license unless they go abroad, withdraw from Cornell or take a leave of absence, with documents to prove it. Settle any plan to live elsewhere before the application deadline.

That includes an exemption from the second-year live-on rule, which Cornell accepts only through that deadline. Our guide to [Cornell residential requirement exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) covers who qualifies, and [off-campus housing for Cornell sophomores](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) covers what living off campus costs.

```reader
If you're joining a fraternity, sorority or co-op
Declare it on your housing application by March 15, 2027, and confirm with your house's leaders that you are on its roster. The housing office checks rosters from Sorority and Fraternity Life and the affiliated houses. Miss the date and you complete your second year in campus housing. Each co-op runs its own selection; our guide to [Cornell co-ops and Greek houses](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/) explains how to join.
```

```reader
If you're a rising junior or senior
Your round runs in the fall, not the spring, and campus housing is not guaranteed. For 2027-28 the application closed Sept. 27, 2026, and Open Room Selection was Oct. 8, followed by a waitlist ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)). You can cancel before move-in, but the fee rises with the date. If your fall selection did not go your way, see [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).
```

## FAQ

**Can I change my block leader?**
Yes. The leader can hand the role to another member in the portal any time before the application closes. After that, send a request through Cornell Housing's help page and staff will make the change.

**Will I know the room rates before I pick?**
Final rates for 2027-28 come out in March 2027, before the April round.

**Who can help during selection?**
The Room Selection Help Desk at 607-255-5368 takes calls during every timeslot. You can also send a chat in the Housing and Dining Portal or submit a housing ticket.
