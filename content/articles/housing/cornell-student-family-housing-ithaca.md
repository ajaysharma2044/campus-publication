```glance
- Cornell's Hasbrouck family units cost $1,475 to $1,530 a month for 2026-27, utilities included, for households of 2 to 4 with at most two adults.
- A private Ithaca 2BR averages $2,459 a month and a 3BR $3,039 (RentCafe, October 2026); Lansing West listed 2BRs at $1,825 and up.
- Budget about $4,918 at signing for a $2,459 2BR: first month plus a deposit, which New York caps at one month's rent.
- Spouses and partners with a CU ID can buy the $200 OmniRide pass; children 17 and under ride TCAT free.
- Married undergrads and those with legal dependents are exempt from the residential requirement, but must request it in the Housing application while it is open (the 2026-27 application deadline was April 6) and renew each year.
- Search March to April for an August start; apply to Hasbrouck early, since it takes rolling applications.
```

About 95% of Cornell graduate students live off campus, but the cheapest family option is Cornell's own: Hasbrouck family 2BR townhouses cost [$1,475 a month unfurnished or $1,530 furnished for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included. A private Ithaca 2BR averages [$2,459 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) before utilities, so apply to Hasbrouck in March or April and keep a private search running beside it.

```reader "If you're a student with a spouse, partner or children"
Hasbrouck is the number to beat: $1,475 to $1,530 a month on a 12-month contract, utilities in. Its family units hold 2 to 4 people with no more than two adults, so a household with a third adult needs a private rental. Privately, plan on $2,459 a month for an average 2BR and $4,918 at signing. Budget your partner's commute: a $200 OmniRide pass bought at 116 Maple Avenue, or a $359.77 B commuter permit for one car. Children ride TCAT free. The mistake that costs most is waiting for a cheap family unit to appear: The Cornell Daily Sun reported family housing as scarce in 2024.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
Cornell's 2026-27 Ithaca budget gives a grad student [$19,962 a year for housing](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students), about $1,663.50 a month by our division. A Hasbrouck family unit fits under that line; an average private 2BR at $2,459 runs about $795 over it. Start in March or April for an August lease; a few places remain in June and July. PhD students and graduate assistants get free OmniRide passes, so a cheaper unit in Lansing or Fall Creek adds no bus cost for you. Your partner pays $200 a year unless night routes 90 and 92 cover their trips.
```

```reader "If you're a first- or second-year with a spouse or children"
[Married students and students coming to Ithaca with legal dependents](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) are not bound by Cornell's two-year residential requirement. You still have to request the exemption in the Housing application while it is open, before you sign a Cornell housing license. The 2026-27 Housing application deadline was April 6, 2026 (Cornell's page does not say whether that date also applies to exemption requests, so confirm with Housing), and the exemption lasts one academic year, so you file again each spring. Cornell describes Hasbrouck as graduate and professional housing, so plan on a private 12-month lease. Sign it only after approval: a student who neither meets the requirement nor holds an exemption is [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing), $13,744 for a double.
```

```reader "If you're an international student bringing family"
You do not need a Social Security number to [rent an apartment or open a bank account](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Visa appointments come no more than 120 days before your program starts, so a lease is often signed from abroad. Before wiring money, check that the landlord asks for no more than one month's rent as deposit and advance combined, the New York cap. Ask whether heat is included. Not confirmed: how Ithaca landlords handle guarantors and missing US credit. A landlord dispute does not affect your visa status, [Cornell Graduate School staff say](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/), and the tenant hotline at (607) 301-1560 advises anyone, wherever they live.
```

## Cornell's family housing: Hasbrouck

Hasbrouck Apartments sit in the North Campus area and house graduate and professional students on [12-month contracts with rolling applications](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). Family and partner units hold 2 to 4 people, with no more than two adults per unit. Cornell runs three family rates for 2026-27, all per month, all on the [housing rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates).

Demand is real. Of Cornell's on-campus graduate households, [51% are family or partner households](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-students/your-cornell-community) and 49% single students. About 400 graduate students live on campus; about 95% live off it. The Cornell Daily Sun reported in 2024 that visiting scholars [compete with grad students for Hasbrouck and Maplewood](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell), and that family housing is scarce with high upfront costs.

Maplewood, Cornell's other graduate complex on East Hill, is run by Greystar: furnished studios to 4BRs, utilities included, in-unit washers and dryers, pet-friendly. Cornell's page gives no prices for it and lists no family units.

Not confirmed: whether Hasbrouck takes undergraduate families, its wait times, or whether it charges a deposit.

## Where families look, and what each area trades

Cornell's [neighborhoods page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) is the only official guide. Its walk times run optimistic, so go by its distances.

- **Lansing and Northeast**, 4 to 10 miles north on Triphammer and Warren roads: larger complexes, cheaper, near the malls, with regular buses. The [grad guide](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html) puts it about 20 minutes from campus by bus.
- **Fall Creek**, 0.5 to 1 mile below campus: lower rents, a frequent bus, a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk, and groceries that need a bus or car.
- **Cayuga Heights**, 1 to 4 miles north: a quiet faculty area with little walkable shopping. It is a separate village.
- **East Hill and Belle Sherman**, 0.75 to 2 miles east: quieter, with newer complexes such as Maplewood.
- **West Hill**, about 4 miles across the inlet: rural, cheaper, not walkable, and the heating warning below applies.

The city line matters for families. Ithaca's 120-day renewal rule, the Certificate of Compliance system and Cornell's [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) cover City of Ithaca rentals only. A Lansing, Cayuga Heights or Town of Ithaca address has none of them.

Schools: Cornell points families to the [Ithaca City School District and worklife@cornell.edu](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-students/your-cornell-community). Not confirmed: which neighborhoods feed which schools, and childcare cost or availability. Ask Work-Life before you sign.

## What you pay at signing

Expect the first month's rent plus a security deposit. New York caps the [deposit plus any rent advance at one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and pet deposits count toward that cap. The landlord must return what is left, with an itemized statement, within 14 days of move-out.

| Unit | First month | Deposit (cap) | Due at signing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average Ithaca 2BR | $2,459 | up to $2,459 | up to $4,918 |
| Average Ithaca 3BR | $3,039 | up to $3,039 | up to $6,078 |
| Lansing West 2BR | $1,825 | up to $1,825 | up to $3,650 |

The 3BR and Lansing West rows are our arithmetic on the same rule. New York also bars landlords from charging [any fee to process an application](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). A background or credit check may cost the actual cost or $20, whichever is less, and you get a copy. Late fees are capped at $50 or 5% of the rent, whichever is less.

Cornell aid will not help here. Its [off-campus aid page](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) says security deposits and first and last month's rent are not covered.

## Who may live with you

New York law protects a family's right to live together. Under [Real Property Law 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F), a landlord cannot limit occupancy to the tenant and immediate family. A lease in one tenant's name allows that tenant, immediate family, one more occupant and that occupant's dependent children.

On a lease with several tenants, tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the number of tenants named, not counting dependent children. You must give the landlord an occupant's name within 30 days of move-in or of being asked. An occupant has no right to stay if the tenant leaves.

The Town of Ithaca is stricter for unrelated people. Its zoning defines a family as people [related by blood, marriage or adoption](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html) and limits single-family homes to one family plus one boarder. That rule bites shared student houses, not a married couple with children.

## Getting your partner and children around

Your partner's commute is a real line in the budget. Spouses and partners with a CU ID can buy the OmniRide student pass at 116 Maple Avenue and ride [night routes 90 and 92 free](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). The pass costs $200 a year, prorated monthly, and expires August 31. A single TCAT ride costs $1.50, and [children 17 and under ride free](https://tcatbus.com/fare-pass-options).

| Rider | Bus cost | Car option |
|---|---|---|
| New Cornell degree student, year one | Free all year | B commuter permit $359.77 a year |
| PhD student or graduate assistant | Free OmniRide pass | GAs on full Ithaca assistantships can buy faculty and staff permits |
| Other student after year one | $1.50 a daytime ride or $200 OmniRide | B commuter permit $359.77 a year |
| Spouse or partner with a CU ID | $200 OmniRide; night routes 90 and 92 free | ask Transportation Services, 607-255-4600 |
| Child 17 or under | Free | none |

Permits and rates are on Cornell's [student parking page](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students). The B permit covers campus B lots, not parking at home.

## Heat: the cost families underestimate

A house with a yard can cost more than its rent says. Cornell warns that West Hill "cottages" are likely uninsulated with very high heating bills. It suggests asking NYSEG at (800) 572-1111 for the address's average annual heating cost before you sign, per the [neighborhoods page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods). NYSEG's [temporary rate increase took effect June 1, 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/05/15/nyseg-and-rge-win-temporary-rate-hikes-as-larger-increases-still-loom/), with a larger permanent case still pending.

Where the landlord supplies heat, the law sets a floor. In Ithaca the heat season runs September 15 to May 31. From 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. a unit must reach at least 68F when it is below 55F outside, and at least 62F overnight, per the [Cornell Law tenants' guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).

## Worked examples

Example: a PhD student, a spouse and a toddler, on one stipend. A Hasbrouck unfurnished townhouse costs $1,475 a month, $17,700 a year with utilities. That sits $2,262 under the $19,962 grad housing line. The spouse's OmniRide adds $200; the toddler rides free. An average private 2BR at $2,459 costs $29,508 a year before utilities, $9,546 over the line, with up to $4,918 due at signing.

Example: the same family, no Hasbrouck unit by June. A Lansing West 2BR at $1,825 costs $21,900 a year, $1,938 over the line, plus utilities. The student rides free; the spouse buys the $200 pass or drives on a B permit if eligible. Ask NYSEG for the heating history, and confirm which bills are yours.

Example: a married sophomore. File the married exemption in the Housing application while it is open (the 2026 application deadline was April 6), wait for approval, then sign a 12-month lease. Signing first risks a bill for the full year, $13,744 for a double, on top of rent.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for an affordable family unit to appear.** The Sun's 2024 report found family housing scarce. If Hasbrouck falls through in July, expect up to $4,918 at signing for an average 2BR.
- **Renting a rural cottage for the space.** Cornell warns West Hill cottages may be uninsulated with very high heating bills. Ask NYSEG for the history first.
- **Comparing Hasbrouck's rate with a bare rent.** Hasbrouck includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet. A bare $1,825 rent already costs $350 a month more than $1,475, before the first utility bill.
- **Paying an application fee.** Lansing West's RentCafe page lists a $20 application fee. New York bans payment for processing an application; only a background or credit check, at cost up to $20, is allowed.
- **Planning a third adult into Hasbrouck.** Its family units allow no more than two adults, so a live-in grandparent means a private lease.
- **Signing before an undergraduate exemption is approved.** The penalty is a full-year Cornell bill, $13,744 for a double.
- **Forgetting the partner's transport.** $200 a year for OmniRide, or $359.77 for a permit for an eligible student's car.

## FAQ

**Does Cornell financial aid cover the deposit?** No. Cornell's aid page says [security deposits and first and last month's rent are not covered](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), and aid will not intervene if you try to break a lease mid-year.

**Can my landlord refuse to let my partner move in?** Not on the grounds that they are not on the lease. Real Property Law 235-f lets a single tenant add [immediate family and one more occupant](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F); give the landlord the name within 30 days.

**Is Maplewood an option for a family?** Cornell's page describes furnished studios to 4BRs with utilities included and lists no family units or prices. Ask Cornell Housing directly.

**Who helps if the landlord will not fix the heat?** The tenant hotline at (607) 301-1560 advises tenants with [no income or geographic limits](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources); full representation is only for City of Ithaca residents. Put repair requests in writing.

**Can my partner park on campus?** Cornell's student permits are for students. Graduate assistants on full Ithaca assistantships can buy [faculty and staff permits](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students); ask Transportation Services at 607-255-4600 about a partner.

**Will I find a home my family can afford?** One grad student, Elias Beltran, told the Sun at a 2023 protest that his family [could not afford to rent in Ithaca](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/24/cornell-graduate-students-united-protests-for-competitive-wages-additional-university-support/). Hasbrouck and the Lansing complexes are the options that come in nearest the budget.
