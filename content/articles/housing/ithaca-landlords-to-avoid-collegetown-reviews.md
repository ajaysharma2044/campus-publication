```glance
- No current, independent list ranks Collegetown landlords; check the building and the owner yourself.
- Call the City Building Division at 607-274-6508 (8 a.m. to 4 p.m. weekdays) and ask if the address has a valid Certificate of Compliance.
- Cornell's safety tool shows sprinklers, alarms and construction type for City of Ithaca rentals only; it does not rate the landlord.
- Ask two past tenants Cornell's reference questions: repairs, deposit return, notice, would they rent again.
- Whoever the landlord is, New York caps the deposit plus any rent advance at one month's rent and bans application fees.
- Outside the City (Town of Ithaca, Lansing, Cayuga Heights, Varna), none of the City checks apply.
```

No current, independent review source ranks Collegetown landlords, so the useful answer is a method, not a blacklist. Before you sign, call the City of Ithaca Building Division at [607-274-6508](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) about the address's Certificate of Compliance, look it up on Cornell's [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments), and put Cornell's [reference questions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) to two past tenants. Those three steps take under an hour and catch the failure that cost the 117 Thurston tenants a heated house in 2018.

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
The junior-year Collegetown rush runs in your sophomore fall. On 2026-11-08, 14 of 67 Collegetown rows on [Ithaca Renting's page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) were already sold out for 2027-28. Speed is no excuse to skip the certificate call (607-274-6508) and two reference calls; both fit in an afternoon. The costly mistake is signing a lease for this sophomore year without an approved exemption: you are [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) of Cornell housing anyway, [$13,744](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) for a 2026-27 double.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying or co-signing"
You cannot see Cornell's listing marketplace without a NetID, but you can make the two checks that matter. Call the Building Division (607-274-6508) about the Certificate of Compliance, and look the address up on the [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments). Then check the money: the deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent under [GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and Cornell aid does [not cover](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) deposits or first and last month's rent. The mistake that costs most is assuming a new building opens on time: [Fontana's 2016 delay](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless) left over 50 students with Aug 1 leases and no home.
```

```reader "If you're a grad or professional student"
You shop later, usually March to April, so you have time to vet properly. Ask the landlord what share of tenants are undergrads, the question a [College Confidential reply](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/off-campus-housing-at-cornell-university/2002895) gave an incoming MEng student. Two grad buildings have reviews: Cliffside Terrace scores 7.4 out of 10 on 5 [veryapt reviews](https://www.veryapt.com/ApartmentReview-a16145-cliffside-terrace-apartments-ithaca), Ivy Ridge 6.4 on 7. Ivy Ridge sits in Varna, outside the City, so no Certificate of Compliance or safety-tool entry exists for it. Do not count on Cornell to mediate: Off-Campus Living [does not represent](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/) students in landlord disputes.
```

```reader "If you're an international student signing from abroad"
You will probably sign sight unseen, so the public checks replace the visit. You do not need an SSN to rent, per [Cornell International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Before you wire anything, email ibd@cityofithaca.org or call 607-274-6508 about the certificate, and use the safety tool. Cornell's marketplace shows only City listings with a current certificate ([Grad Tips](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/)). Refuse an application fee, which [RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) bans, and any prepayment that pushes deposit plus advance past one month's rent. Graduate School staff say a landlord dispute does not affect your visa or standing.
```

## Why there is no list of landlords to avoid

The published record on named Collegetown landlords is old or thin. The [2004 Sun feature](https://cornellsun.com/2004/12/02/good-bad-and-slummy-collegetown-landlords/) on good and bad landlords reported that one large company had a reputation for fighting over deposits; it is 22 years old. The [2013 Sun analysis](https://cornellsun.com/2013/05/02/collegetown-housing-market-scrutinized/) put 4,013 of an estimated 6,110 Collegetown bedrooms in the hands of a few landlords. No source gives market shares since then, a complaint database, or recent independent reviews of Ithaca Renting, Student Agencies, Lambrou or Urban Ithaca.

Size says nothing about quality in either direction. Treat the record below as a list of things to ask, not a verdict.

Not confirmed: current r/Cornell, r/ithaca or Google review counts for any Collegetown landlord.

## Step 1: check the Certificate of Compliance

The City of Ithaca requires a [Certificate of Compliance](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) for multiple dwellings: buildings with three or more units, or units housing six or more unrelated people (a City code snippet says five). Dormitories, fraternities and sororities are covered too. A multiple dwelling's certificate lasts three years; a fraternity's or sorority's lasts one. The owner must apply and schedule an inspection before the current one expires.

The inspection covers structure, fire protection, exits, heating, electrical and plumbing. The City page lists no public lookup. Call 607-274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org, and ask three things:

1. Does this address have a valid certificate, and when does it expire?
2. Are there open complaints or violations?
3. Is the occupancy on the lease legal for this unit?

If the certificate has lapsed, ask the landlord in writing when the inspection is booked, and wait for the answer before you sign.

The [Building Division](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/130/Building-Division) is at 108 E. Green St., City Hall, 4th floor, open 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. weekdays. Its main line is 607-274-6510.

## Step 2: look the address up on the safety tool

Cornell Off-Campus Living and the Building Division run an [online safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) for City rentals. It shows sprinklers, extinguishers, smoke and carbon monoxide notification, construction type, the applicable code and property use. It updates daily.

It launched in 2017 with [1 to 5 star ratings](https://news.cornell.edu/node/289146) built from Building Division data. The current page shows no scoring scale. It does not measure landlord quality, looks or neighborhood safety.

| Check | What it tells you | What it does not | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certificate call, 607-274-6508 | Valid certificate, expiry, open complaints | Repair speed, deposit habits | City of Ithaca only |
| Safety tool | Sprinklers, alarms, construction type | Landlord behavior; no score shown now | City of Ithaca only |
| Cornell marketplace | City listings must hold a current certificate to post | Anything about the owner | Cornell-affiliated viewers |
| Past tenants | Repairs, deposit return, notice, entry | Whether it was one bad year | Anywhere |

## Step 3: ask the landlord and two past tenants

Cornell's [lease page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) gives the questions. Use them word for word if you like; landlords expect them.

```figure

```

Ask the landlord how long they have been a landlord and how many properties they own. Ask how long the last tenants stayed and where they went, who does repairs, and how to reach them in an emergency.

Ask the references whether the landlord answered repair requests and handled late rent fairly. Ask whether they got their deposit back, whether the landlord gave proper notice before entering, and whether they would rent again. Cornell also suggests checking public records for code compliance, the Better Business Bureau, review sites and Chamber complaints, and visiting neighbors.

Those questions target the complaints that recur. The [Ithaca Tenants Union](https://cornellsun.com/2020/03/06/as-students-struggle-to-find-housing-three-ilr-juniors-form-new-ithaca-tenants-union/) formed in 2020 citing poor maintenance, one-sided leases, deposits above the legal cap and entry without 24 hours' notice.

## Step 4: visit and look past fresh paint

Two Sun pieces warn against judging by surface. A [2017 column](https://cornellsun.com/2017/08/21/kankanhalli-not-clickbait-my-apartment-tried-to-kill-me) described a blind that fell and nearly hit the writer on day one. A [2021 story](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/) found pandemic virtual tours hid drawbacks. On a visit, check for two exits and working smoke detectors, ask what heats the unit, and knock on a current tenant's door.

## What going wrong has looked like

Three dated cases show what the checks are for.

```figure
kind: timeline
title: Collegetown and downtown landlord cases on record
rows:
  - {label: "Fontana opens late; 50+ students with Aug 1 leases have no home", date: "Aug 2016"}
  - {label: "117 Thurston tenants wait 53 days for heat", date: "Sept 27 to Nov 19, 2018"}
  - {label: "City Council makes renting an unsafe unit a misdemeanor", date: "Mar 2019"}
  - {label: "City orders about 200 Asteri residents out", date: "Mar 4 to 20, 2026"}
```

**117 Thurston Ave, 2018.** The tenants learned only after signing that the certificate had [expired in November 2015](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/). They went about three days without hot water, and heat fell below the legal minimum. It took 53 days from their first complaint letter (Sept 27) to a heater reported fixed (Nov 19). One tenant slept in a campus library. The departing students said the Building Department could then only send a written warning.

In 2019 the City Council [stiffened the penalties](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/03/city-council-votes-to-stiffen-penalties-for-noncompliant-landlords). Renting an unsafe unit became a misdemeanor, with up to a $500 fine and 30 days. Landlords owe displaced tenants double the monthly rent, prorated per day.

**Fontana Apartments, 327 Eddy St, 2016.** The building [opened late after five delays](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless). Over 50 students with Aug 1 leases were left without a home. The landlord offered $30 a day or a room at the Trip Hotel.

**Asteri, downtown Commons, 2026.** The City ordered about 200 residents out of the 181-unit Vecino Group building on [March 4, 2026](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/asteri-s-aftermath-displacement-dangerous-conditions-and-a-predatory-cash-for-keys-scheme) and lifted the order March 20. A $2,000 cash-for-keys offer was called "predatory" by a city official and the Tenants Union.

## The lease terms that protect you with any landlord

A good landlord and a bad one sign under the same law. Knowing it does more than any rating.

- **Deposit:** the deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent. The landlord must return it with an itemized statement within 14 days of move-out or lose the right to keep any of it ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
- **Fees:** no application fee; a background check costs at most $20 ([RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)).
- **Move-in inspection:** the landlord must offer one before you move in. A signed condition record stops deductions for listed defects.
- **The 120-day waiver:** in the City, a landlord must give current tenants 120 days' written notice before renewing, showing or re-letting, unless a [bolded, initialed waiver](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/) sits on page one. The rule's sponsor wrote that your first question should be [why you are waiving](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/) the days.

Example: four friends look at a Collegetown 4BR from a small owner at $1,250 per person, $5,000 a month for the unit. The owner asks each for a $1,250 deposit, last month's rent of $1,250 and a $100 application fee. The deposit plus advance comes to $2,500 each, two months' rent. The cap is one month, so $1,250 each is over the line, $5,000 for the group. The $100 fee is banned outright; a credit check could cost $20. Ask for both in writing, then make the certificate call before anyone signs.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing on leasing day without the certificate call.** The 117 Thurston tenants found the expired certificate after signing, then waited 53 days for heat.
- **Trusting a score with five reviews.** Cliffside's 7.4 rests on 5 reviews and Ivy Ridge's 6.4 on 7; one tenant can swing either.
- **Reading the safety tool as a landlord rating.** It shows fire protection for City rentals; it says nothing about repairs or deposits.
- **Paying a deposit plus last month in advance.** Together they cannot exceed one month's rent; on a $1,250 share, the excess is $1,250 a person.
- **Initialing the 120-day waiver without asking why.** You give up the notice window before your unit is shown to others.
- **Assuming a new building opens on time.** Fontana's 2016 tenants got $30 a day or a hotel room.
- **Expecting Cornell to step in.** Off-Campus Living does not represent students in disputes; call the hotline at 607-301-1560 instead.

## FAQ

**Can I see a landlord's code violations online?** Not on the City's certificate page, which lists no public lookup. Call 607-274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org; [Cornell's listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) gives the same contacts.

**Do Collegetown landlords require a parent guarantor?** Not confirmed: no source covers guarantor practice in Ithaca, so ask before signing. If you do guarantee, a later sublet request needs your consent too ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).

**Does Cornell endorse any landlord?** No. Cornell says it [does not endorse](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) any landlord or listing site and takes no responsibility for disputes.

**Is a single-family house covered by the certificate rule?** The City's [Housing Inspections page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections) says inspections are required for all residential rentals in the City, while the certificate page names buildings of three or more units. Ask the Building Division about the specific address.

**What if my landlord keeps my deposit past 14 days?** The landlord forfeits the right to keep any of it, and a willful violation can cost up to twice the deposit ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Send a demand letter, then use small claims, up to $5,000 in Ithaca City Court.

**Should I avoid a landlord because it owns a lot of Collegetown?** No. The [2013 count](https://cornellsun.com/2013/05/02/collegetown-housing-market-scrutinized/) shows who owned the stock, not who treats tenants well. Judge the building and the references.
