```glance
- Most students cannot rent until junior year, and that lease is signed in the fall of sophomore year.
- Only big shared units come in under Cornell's double on a 12-month basis.
- New York caps the deposit plus any rent advance at one month's rent.
- Cornell aid does not cover the deposit, first and last month's rent, or summer rent.
```

Off campus at Cornell costs more than the [$13,744 residence hall double for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), unless your child shares a big unit. Most students cannot rent until junior year. Before anyone signs, cap the deposit at one month's rent, check the building's City safety record, and know that every housemate can owe the whole rent.

## When can my child live off campus?

Cornell's [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) requires full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students to live in Cornell or affiliated housing. Year two can be a residence hall, one of the eight university-owned co-ops or a recognized fraternity or sorority house.

First- and second-years [should not sign a lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process) without an approved exemption. A student who neither meets the requirement nor gets one [will still be billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing), on top of rent.

Exemptions are narrow: 21 or older by the start of the academic year, married, coming with legal dependents, a veteran who is not in ROTC, a transfer entering as a junior or senior, or a commuter living with a parent within 25 miles. Each lasts one academic year (Cornell residential policy).

So the first lease usually covers junior year. The timing is awkward because that lease is signed during sophomore fall, while your child still lives in Cornell housing. Collegetown leases have been signed as early as 15 to 16 months before move-in ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)). The wider market, including downtown and Lansing, has units later.

## Is off campus cheaper than the dorm?

Only in big shared units. Rent is quoted per bed per month, usually on a 12-month lease, while Cornell's rate is per student for the academic year. To compare, multiply the per-bed price by 12 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## What does financial aid cover off campus?

Cornell's [aid page](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) says need is calculated the same as on campus. The Bursar stops billing housing, your child pays the landlord, and a refund for living costs is issued if aid exceeds Bursar charges.

Three things are not covered: security deposits, first and last month's rent, and rent in months your child is not registered. On a 12-month lease, June and July, and sometimes August, come out of savings or a sublet. Financial Aid will also not step in if your child tries to break a lease mid-year. Questions go to Financial Aid at 607-255-5145 ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).

Say a student on aid takes a downtown three-bedroom share at $795. The year costs $9,540, well under the double. Two or three summer months add $1,590 to $2,385, and the deposit plus first month add $1,590 at signing. That is cash the family has to find (prices from [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)).

## What is a landlord allowed to charge?

New York caps a security deposit plus any rent advance at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), so a deposit and last month's rent together cannot exceed it. The deposit comes back with an itemized statement within 14 days of move-out, or the landlord loses the right to keep any of it. The landlord must offer a move-in inspection before move-in; if your child accepts, both sign a written condition record.

Landlords cannot charge [any application fee](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). A background or credit check costs the lesser of the actual cost or $20. Late fees are allowed only when rent is more than 5 days late, capped at the lesser of $50 or 5% of monthly rent ([NY Real Property Law 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)). Non-refundable deposits and separate cleaning fees are [unlawful](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits). The [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) has the details.

## What happens if a housemate leaves?

Cornell says most local leases bind housemates ["jointly and severally"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): each is responsible for the others' rent and damages for the whole term. A tenant who overpays can sue a co-tenant for contribution ([Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)). That is a lawsuit among friends, not a refund from the landlord.

Example: your child signs a four-bedroom at $1,246.25 a bed with three friends. In January one friend leaves school and stops paying. The three who remain owe that rent, about $415.42 each a month on top of their own. From February through July, that is about $2,493 each ($415.42 times 6; rent from [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

If you are asked to co-sign, the Cornell Law guide does not address guarantors, meaning people who promise to pay if the tenant cannot. Ask the landlord in writing what the guarantee covers and for how long before you sign. A tenant's sublet request in a building of four or more units must include any [guarantor's consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). The [roommate liability guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) covers groups that shrink.

## How can I check a building is safe?

Cornell's [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) lets only people who prove Cornell affiliation contact landlords. Two checks are open to anyone.

```figure
kind: steps
title: A parent's safety check before paying a deposit
rows:
  - {label: "Call the City Building Division about a current Certificate of Compliance"}
  - {label: "Look the address up on Cornell's safety lookup (City of Ithaca rentals only)"}
  - {label: "Ask the landlord about repairs and an emergency contact"}
  - {label: "Ask past tenants whether their deposits came back"}
```

A Certificate of Compliance is the City of Ithaca's safety permit for rentals. Buildings with three or more units need one ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)). Call the Building Division at 607-274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org and ask whether the address has a current certificate ([Cornell listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)).

The safety lookup at safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu shows sprinklers, extinguishers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and construction type. It covers City of Ithaca rentals only, not the Town of Ithaca, Lansing or Cayuga Heights, and it does not measure landlord quality or neighborhood safety ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)).

Cornell's [lease page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease) suggests asking how long the last tenants stayed, who does repairs, and how to reach the landlord in an emergency. Read [reviews of Collegetown landlords](/housing/how-to-check-an-ithaca-landlords-record/) too.

## Who helps in a dispute?

Off-Campus Living offers consultations but [does not represent students](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/) in landlord disputes. The Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline, 607-301-1560, gives free tenant advice ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/)). The [lease reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) shows which clauses to check before anyone signs.

## What mistakes cost families the most?

- **Signing for sophomore year.** Your child pays rent and is billed for the Cornell room anyway.
- **Comparing 12 months of rent with the academic-year rate.** A $1,246.25 bed is $14,955 over 12 months, not the $12,462.50 that 10 months suggests.
- **Paying a deposit plus last month's rent.** Together they cannot exceed one month's rent.
- **Keeping a backup Cornell room too long.** A returning junior's cancellation costs $50 through Jan. 31, $500 from Feb. 1, $1,000 from April 1 and $2,000 from July 1 ([Cornell housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

## FAQ

**Can my child cancel a Cornell room after finding an apartment?** Returning juniors and seniors can, at the fees above. First-years and second-years cannot, unless they stop attending, are exempt or, for second-years only, are accepted to a co-op or Greek house in time.

**Does Cornell housing take a security deposit?** No. Cornell housing is a license, not a lease, and carries no damage deposit.

## What to do next

1. Confirm whether your child qualifies for an exemption before any lease talk.
2. Set a signing target with the timeline tool and a per-person budget from the table.
3. Run the certificate call and the safety lookup before any deposit moves.
