## What sets Flora Rose House apart?

Rose is the House built last. Its building was finished a year before the House program began, and for that first year it served as an ordinary dorm. The five Houses opened between August 2004 and August 2009, and Cornell called the whole project finished two years early and within budget ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2009/08/west-campus-house-system-officially-complete)).

Sharon Sassler is House Professor-Dean, and Jeff Godowski is Assistant Dean ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system/flora-rose-house)). About 225 residents live in the four Gothic halls. Lyon Hall, one of them, is for women only.

## What is living in Rose like?

The setup mirrors Cook: a main building with suites, plus older halls. Rose Main has 16 suites for five or six people, and the Gothics hold the rest, including triples and quads. No room is cooled (Cornell Housing).

Cornell's pages carry no recent reviews of Rose. One sophomore told The Cornell Daily Sun in 2021 that she chose Flora Rose House because the off-campus search felt too difficult ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/)). That tells you why students end up here, not what living here feels like.

For the other four Houses side by side, see [West Campus Houses compared](/cornell/housing/cornell-west-campus-houses-compared/).

## What does a year in Rose cost?

Cornell charges by room type, so Rose costs what any same-type room costs ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Every House resident must take the House Meal Plan, with all meals in ten residential dining rooms, and cannot change it while living on West ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## How do you get a room in Flora Rose House?

Sophomores pick rooms in spring. For 2027-28 the application opens Feb. 1, 2027, and closes April 4, with open room selection April 13 to 16 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). About 20% of rising sophomores have landed a West Campus space in past years. A student who does not select is assigned what is left ([Cornell Housing FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/residential-programs/apply-housing/current-undergrad-application-process/room-selection-faqs)).

The residential requirement applies to sophomores, and the [sophomore off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) lists the exemptions. Returning juniors and seniors can cancel a 2027-28 license for a fee that grows with each deadline ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

Rose does not state a transfer share, so ask Housing how many transfers it takes.

## What should you watch for?

Ask which building you would live in before you rank Rose. Lyon is women only, and the main building and the Gothics differ in room types.

## What to do next

Decide whether a triple's lower rate or a suite matters more to you. List Rose with two other Houses in spring selection, and set a reminder for the Feb. 1 opening.
