Alice Cook House was the first of Cornell's five West Campus Houses. It opened in August 2004 and holds [nearly 400 students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system/alice-cook-house) in a modern main building and four Gothic stone halls. Rooms have no air conditioning, and residents buy the House Meal Plan.

## What sets Alice Cook House apart?

A House is Cornell's version of a residential college, a dorm with its own faculty leaders and programs. Raina Plowright is Cook's House Professor-Dean, and Dylan Stratton is Assistant Dean. Signature programs include Make-Something-Mondays, a weekly hands-on project, and Fun Fridays ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system/alice-cook-house)).

About 120 residents live in Cook Main, and more than 250 live in the four Gothic halls. Cook says about a fifth of its residents are transfers, and Cornell puts the West Campus average at about 15% ([West Campus House System](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system)).

## What is living in Cook like?

Your room depends on your building. Cook Main has suites of five to seven people. The Gothic halls are the older buildings and have the only triples and quads. No building has air conditioning.

The Gothics carry small costs. In 2020, a North Baker resident told The Cornell Daily Sun her building had no water fountains and no laundry room, and maintenance requests waited for weeks. That was fall 2020, during the pandemic, so ask a current resident how the building runs now ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/11/students-recount-social-struggles-limited-food-options-and-spiders-while-living-on-west-campus)).

For the other four Houses side by side, see [West Campus Houses compared](/cornell/housing/cornell-west-campus-houses-compared/).

## What does a year in Cook cost?

Cornell charges by room type, not by building ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). The meal plan is where West differs. The House Meal Plan is required for residents of the West Campus House System and gives access to all meals in the ten residential dining rooms ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## How do you get a room in Alice Cook House?

Most rising sophomores do not land on West. Cornell says about 20% of rising sophomores have gotten a West Campus space in past years, and a student who does not select is assigned what is left ([Cornell Housing FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/residential-programs/apply-housing/current-undergrad-application-process/room-selection-faqs)). For 2027-28 the application opens Feb. 1, 2027, and closes April 4, and open room selection runs April 13 to 16 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

The two-year residential rule applies. For the exemptions, read the [sophomore off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/). Returning juniors and seniors can cancel a 2027-28 license for a fee that grows with each deadline ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).

## What should you watch for?

The Gothic halls and Cook Main are one House on paper but two living situations. Ask which building you are placed in before you rank Cook, and tour both if you can.
