Before you sign, check an Ithaca landlord's record three ways: confirm the building's Certificate of Compliance with the City Building Division, look the address up on Cornell's safety tool, and call two past tenants with Cornell's reference questions. The City's inspection checks the building's structure, fire protection, exits, heating, electrical and plumbing systems against the housing, building and zoning codes ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)).

This page is general information, not legal advice.

## How do you check a Certificate of Compliance?

Every rental in the City of Ithaca needs a housing inspection, from a one-family house to a fraternity ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections)). A certificate lasts three years for a building with three or more units or a unit with six or more unrelated residents, and one year for a dormitory, fraternity or sorority. The owner must schedule the next inspection before the current certificate expires ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)).

Then call the Building Division at 607-274-6508 ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)) or email ibd@cityofithaca.org. Ask three questions:

1. Does this address have a valid certificate, and when does it expire?
2. Are there open complaints or violations?
3. Is the occupancy on the lease legal for this unit?

The office is at 108 E. Green St., City Hall, fourth floor, open 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays ([Building Division](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/130/Building-Division)). For copies of a building's inspection and complaint records, file a Freedom of Information Law request with the City, online or on paper ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/182/Freedom-of-Information-Law-Requests)). If the certificate has lapsed, ask the landlord in writing when the inspection is booked and wait for the answer before you sign.

The stakes are real. In 2018, tenants at 117 Thurston Ave. learned after signing that the certificate had expired in November 2015. It took 53 days from the first complaint to a reported fix of the heater ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/)). If an order to vacate reaches your building, see [what to do after an eviction or vacate order](/cornell/housing/ithaca-eviction-vacate-order-what-to-do/).

## What does Cornell's safety tool show?

Cornell Off-Campus Living and the Building Division run an online tool for City rentals. It shows sprinklers, extinguishers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, construction type, the applicable code and property use, and it updates daily ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)). Cornell uses those items to determine a safety score for each property. The tool launched in 2017 with one-to-five-star safety ratings built from public City data ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2017/09/campus-living-rates-safety-ithaca-city-rentals)). It does not measure landlord quality, looks or neighborhood safety, and it covers City rentals only.

| Check | What it tells you | What it misses |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate call | Valid certificate, expiry, open complaints | Repair speed, deposit habits |
| Safety tool | Sprinklers, alarms, construction type | Landlord behavior |
| Past tenants | Repairs, deposit return, notice to end the lease | Whether it was one bad year |

Source: City of Ithaca, Cornell Student and Campus Life.

## What should you ask the landlord and past tenants?

Cornell's lease page gives the questions, and landlords expect them. Ask how long the landlord has been in business, how many properties they own, how long the last tenants stayed and where they went, who does repairs, and how to reach them in an emergency. Ask references whether the landlord answered repair requests and handled late rent fairly, whether they got their deposit back, whether the landlord gave proper notice to end the rental agreement, and whether they would rent again ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease)).

These questions target the complaints that recur. The Ithaca Tenants Union formed in 2020 citing poor maintenance, one-sided leases, deposits above the legal cap and entry without 24 hours' notice ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2020/03/06/as-students-struggle-to-find-housing-three-ilr-juniors-form-new-ithaca-tenants-union/)). The deposit plus any advance rent cannot exceed one month's rent ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)), and a landlord cannot charge an application fee ([RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)). Test any lease against both caps with the checker.

## What should you look for on a visit?

Look past fresh paint. Inspect the actual unit you will lease, check for two exits and a smoke detector (required by law), and write needed repairs into the lease ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations)). Talk to the neighbors and find out how long people stay. Cornell also suggests checking the Better Business Bureau and the City or County Chamber of Commerce for complaints ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease)). If the problem is already underway, see [what to do when a landlord will not fix heat, mold or bed bugs](/cornell/housing/ithaca-landlord-wont-fix-heat-mold-bed-bugs/).

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
The Collegetown rush runs in your sophomore fall, and speed is no reason to skip the certificate call. Both calls fit in an afternoon. Skipping them costs more than a missed unit.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying or co-signing"
Off-Campus Living's listings site is free to use for those with Cornell credentials ([Cornell Graduate School](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/)), but you can make the two City checks yourself. Then check the money against the caps above. Financial aid cannot help with a security deposit or first and last month's rent ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
You shop later, so you have time to vet. Ask what share of tenants are undergraduates. Rules outside the City are in the [City versus town guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/).
```

```reader "If you're an international student signing from abroad"
You probably sign sight unseen, so the public checks replace the visit. You do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment ([Cornell Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students)). A landlord dispute does not affect your visa or your status at Cornell, according to Denise Thompson, a manager at Cornell Off-Campus Living ([Cornell Graduate School](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/)).
```

## What to do next

1. Call 607-274-6508 and ask the three certificate questions.
2. Run the address through the safety tool.
3. Read the lease with the [lease reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) before you sign.
