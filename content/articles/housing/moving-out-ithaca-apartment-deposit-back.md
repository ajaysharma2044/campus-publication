- Your landlord owes you the deposit, or an itemized list of charges, within 14 days.

```

Ask your landlord in writing for a move-out inspection, which New York law sets between two weeks and one week before the lease ends, with at least 48 hours' written notice ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Fix what it flags, photograph every room, and leave a forwarding address. The deposit then comes back on a state deadline, covered below.

This page is general information, not legal advice.

## What is the move-out timeline?

Most Ithaca leases end on the last day of July, tied to the student calendar ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing)). For a lease ending then, the dates below follow from the return deadline and the inspection window.

Cornell's Off-Campus Living office lays out a [two-stage checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/moving-and-out):

1. **Two weeks before.** Confirm your move-out date and time, schedule the final inspection, reread the lease and change your address with the Post Office.
2. **One week before.** Schedule disconnection of electric, gas, phone, internet, cable and garbage service if they are in your name. Clean. Give the landlord a forwarding address.

On move-out day, photograph every room, every appliance and the inside of the fridge and oven, with dates, and return every key. For a lease that ends May 31, move every date back two months.

## How do you ask for the move-out inspection?

When you or the landlord gives notice that the tenancy is ending, the landlord must tell you in writing that you can request an inspection. If you ask, it happens in that one-week window, and the landlord lists the charges it plans to make, which gives you a week to fix them. The landlord does not have to hold one if you give less than two weeks' notice ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).

Email your move-out date at least three weeks ahead and ask for the inspection in the same message. That gives you a dated record. Be there for it.

If you accepted a move-in inspection and both of you signed a condition record, the landlord cannot keep deposit money for the defects on it. Bring that record and your move-in photos.

## What can a landlord deduct from the deposit?

| Charge | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Unpaid rent | Yes |
| Damage you caused beyond normal wear | Yes |
| Unpaid utilities owed to the landlord | Yes |
| Moving and storing belongings you left behind | Yes |
| Excessive cleaning and trash removal | Yes |
| Routine cleaning to ready the unit for the next tenant | No |
| A separate cleaning fee | No |
| Late fees or attorney's fees (since June 14, 2019) | No |
| Replacement keys | Up to 110% of actual cost |

Source: [Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy security deposit guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits); the key cap is from [Real Property Law 235-i](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-I).

The landlord must prove that withheld amounts are reasonable, and a lease clause that waives these rules is void. Cleaning is where sources split. Cornell's own move-out page says ["charges for cleaning services will be deducted"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/moving-and-out), while the Cornell Law guide says a separate cleaning fee is banned and only cleaning beyond routine turnover can come out. Go by the law. Clean the unit back to its move-in condition and photograph it. The full rules, including the cap and banned fees, are in the [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/).

In a building with six or more units, the deposit sits in an interest-bearing New York account, and the landlord can keep a 1% yearly administrative fee ([NY Attorney General](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants)).

## What do you do with trash and furniture?

In the City of Ithaca, every bag or can of trash needs a tag. Tags cost $6.00 each or $36.00 for a sheet of six, effective Jan. 1, 2026, with one tag per container up to 35 pounds ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags)). Garbage can go out no more than 24 hours before pickup, and the City bars couches, mattresses and appliances stored outdoors ([City exterior property rules](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance)). The [trash tags, snow and noise guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/) has pickup days and where to buy tags.

Furniture and bulk items can go to the county's [Recycling and Solid Waste Center at 122 Commercial Ave.](http://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Recycling-Materials-Management), open Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. A high-visibility vest is required to enter. Call the center for fees before you load a truck.

Anything you leave inside, the landlord can move and store at your cost. Out at the curb, it becomes a City violation.

## What about utilities, mail, keys and parking?

- **Electric and gas.** Schedule the shutoff a week ahead with NYSEG at [800-572-1111](https://www.nyseg.com/support/moving-help). Ask for a final meter reading on your move-out date. The [winter utilities guide](/cornell/money/ithaca-utilities-cost-winter-heating-bill-apartment-students/) explains what the bills look like.
- **Internet.** Call your provider a week out and return any equipment it lent you.
- **Mail.** File the Post Office change of address two weeks out, and give the landlord your forwarding address in writing, since that is where the deposit goes.
- **Keys.** Hand back every key and get a dated note or email that you did.
- **Parking.** City residential parking permits are valid through July 31 and cost $45 per year ([City Clerk](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)).

## When must the landlord return the deposit?

Within 14 days after you vacate, the landlord must return the deposit, or the rest of it with an itemized statement of each deduction. A landlord who misses the deadline forfeits the right to keep any part of it. If the building changed hands, the new owner is liable for a deposit it knew about. A willful violation can bring punitive damages of up to twice the deposit ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).

Say your rent is $1,200 a month for your share. The deposit can be no more than one month's rent, so at most $1,200 ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Your lease ends at the end of July. You email for an inspection on July 10, the landlord sets it for July 20 with two days' notice, and you patch the two nail holes it flags. By Aug. 14 you should have $1,200, or a smaller check with a list. If nothing arrives, the landlord has lost the right to keep any of it. If a court finds the withholding willful, the damages can reach twice the deposit, $2,400.

Older City code text gives 30 days, but the state's deadline controls ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).

```

The [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) puts the demand letter first: it says the deposit is wrongfully withheld and that you will sue in small claims by a set date. The Community Dispute Resolution Center handles tenant disputes ([Cornell Campus Living Resources](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/community-resources)). Ithaca City Court hears small claims up to $5,000, and town and village courts top out at $3,000 ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Free advice at any step: the Cornell Law tenant hotline at [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/).

```reader "If you're a junior or senior leaving a July 31 lease"
If you graduate in May, decide early whether you are subletting June and July or paying for them, and do the inspection before you leave town if you will not be back. Skipping the inspection and photos is the mistake that costs most, because then the landlord's list of charges is the only record. See [renting as a junior or senior](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-juniors-seniors-renting/) for the rest of the year.
```

```reader "If you're going abroad or away for a semester"
If a subtenant lives in your room, you [stay liable for your obligations under the lease](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), so a subtenant's unpaid rent or damage can end up charged against your deposit. Photograph the room before you hand it over and again when the subtenant leaves. If you leave a joint lease early while housemates stay, get a written agreement with them and the landlord on how your share comes back.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
Lease dates rarely line up. Some leases end May 31, such as [all Dryden South leases in 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), and most end July 31, with the next one starting about Aug. 1. Plan storage for the gap. Cornell's vendor, Big Red Shipping and Storage, [picks up free at apartments](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options); ask it for a storage quote. Commercial self-storage is the other route. Your deposit clock starts when you leave, not when your next lease starts.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Give the landlord a written forwarding address that will still work in August, and ask in writing how the refund will be paid if you are leaving the United States. Do the inspection before your flight, not after. The Cornell Law hotline, 607-301-1560, can still advise you after you leave Ithaca.
```

```reader "If you're a parent who paid the deposit"
Cornell aid [does not cover security deposits](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), so the refund is your money coming back. Check that your student gave the landlord a forwarding address in writing, and mark the return date. The deposit is a trust fund that the landlord cannot mix with its own money. If your student signed with housemates, each one can be held liable for the rent and damage across the whole unit ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)).
```

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Skipping the inspection.** You lose the week to fix the landlord's list.
- **No dated photos.** A charge for a stain or hole becomes your word against the landlord's.
- **No forwarding address in writing.** The check goes to an empty apartment.
- **Leaving a couch or mattress at the curb.** The City bars them outdoors, and left inside, the landlord can deduct for removal.
- **Paying a flat cleaning fee.** A separate cleaning fee is banned.

## FAQ

**Can the landlord keep my deposit for last month's rent?** New York caps the deposit plus any rent advance at one month's rent. If you skip the last month's rent, you still owe it, and the landlord deducts it as unpaid rent.

**My roommate damaged the kitchen. Can the landlord charge my share?** On most Ithaca joint leases, yes, because each tenant can be held for the whole unit. A housemate who pays more than their share can sue the other for contribution.

**Do Town of Ithaca or Lansing rentals follow the same rules?** The deposit law is state law, so it applies across New York. The trash tag and curb rules are City of Ithaca rules, so check your own town.

## What to do next

Count back from your lease end date and email the landlord for an inspection. Then read your lease for any notice clause with the [lease-reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/), and photograph the apartment on the last day.
