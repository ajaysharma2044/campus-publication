```glance
- Ask in writing for a move-out inspection; it happens between two weeks and one week before the lease ends, with 48 hours' written notice.
- For a July 31 lease end, that window is July 17 to July 24 (our count).
- The landlord owes you the deposit, or an itemized list of charges, within 14 days of move-out: by August 14 for a July 31 exit.
- Miss the 14 days and the landlord loses the right to keep any of it; a willful violation can cost up to twice the deposit.
- Two weeks out: change your address and book the inspection. One week out: schedule NYSEG and internet shutoff, clean, and give a forwarding address in writing.
- In the City of Ithaca, trash needs a $6 tag per bag, and couches and mattresses may not be left outdoors.
```

Ask your landlord in writing for a move-out inspection, which New York law sets [between two weeks and one week before the lease ends, with at least 48 hours' written notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Fix what it flags, photograph every room on the way out, and leave a forwarding address. The landlord then has [14 days to return the deposit or send an itemized statement](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), so a July 31 exit means mid-August.

```reader "If you're a junior or senior leaving a July 31 lease"
Most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), so your inspection window is July 17 to 24 and your deposit is due by August 14. If you graduate in May, decide early whether you are subletting June and July or paying for them, and do the inspection before you leave town if you will not be back. The mistake that costs most is skipping the inspection and photos: then the landlord's list of charges is the only record. Your City residential parking permit also [expires July 31](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance).
```

```reader "If you're going abroad or away for a semester"
If a subtenant lives in your room while you are away, you [stay liable for your obligations under the lease](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), so a subtenant's unpaid rent or damage can end up charged against your deposit. Photograph the room before you hand it over and again when the subtenant leaves. If you leave a joint lease early while housemates stay, get a written agreement with them and the landlord on how your share of the deposit comes back. Not confirmed: how a deposit is split when one co-tenant leaves before the lease ends.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
Lease dates rarely line up. Some leases end May 31 ([Dryden South's all do in 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)); most end July 31 and the next one starts about August 1. Plan storage for the gap: Cornell's vendor, Big Red Shipping and Storage, [picks up free at apartments](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options) but posts no prices, and SpareFoot's Ithaca page lists a 10x10 self-storage unit at [about $93.39 a month](https://sparefoot.com/Ithaca-NY-self-storage.html), and its other Ithaca pages show higher figures, so treat it as a floor, not a promise. Your deposit clock starts when you leave, not when your next lease starts.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Give the landlord a written forwarding address that will still work in August, and ask in writing how the refund will be paid if you are leaving the US. A dispute over your deposit [does not affect your visa status or standing](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/), Cornell Graduate School staff say. The Cornell Law tenant hotline, 607-301-1560, [gives advice with no income or geographic limits](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources), so you can still call after you leave Ithaca. Do the inspection before your flight, not after.
```

```reader "If you're a parent who paid the deposit"
Cornell aid [does not cover security deposits](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), so the refund is your money coming back. Check that your student gave the landlord a forwarding address in writing, and mark the 14-day date: August 14 for a July 31 exit. The deposit is a [trust fund the landlord may not mix with its own money](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants), and a landlord who misses the deadline loses the right to keep any of it. If your student signed with housemates, [each one is usually liable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease) for rent and damage across the whole unit.
```

Cornell's Off-Campus Living office sets out a [two-stage checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out):

1. **Two weeks before.** Confirm your move-out date and time, schedule the final inspection, reread the lease, and change your address with the Post Office.
2. **One week before.** Schedule disconnection of electric, gas, phone, internet, cable and garbage service if they are in your name. Clean. Give the landlord a forwarding address.
3. **Move-out day (our addition; Cornell's page has no move-out-day step).** Photograph every room, every appliance and the inside of the fridge and oven, with dates. Return every key.

For a May 31 lease, move every date back two months: inspection window May 17 to 24, deposit due by June 14.

## Asking for the move-out inspection

When you or the landlord gives notice that the tenancy is ending, the landlord must [tell you in writing that you can request an inspection](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). If you ask, it happens between two weeks and one week before the end, and the landlord gives you at least 48 hours' written notice. The landlord then lists the charges it intends to make, which gives you a week to fix them yourself.

Check your lease for a notice clause, then email your move-out date at least three weeks ahead and ask for the inspection in the same message. That gives you a dated record. Be there for it. If you give less than two weeks' notice, the landlord no longer has to hold one.

The move-in record matters here. If you accepted the move-in inspection and both of you signed a condition record, the landlord [cannot keep deposit money for the defects on it](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Bring that record and your move-in photos to the move-out inspection.

## What the landlord can and cannot deduct

| Charge | Allowed? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Unpaid rent | Yes | [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) |
| Damage you caused beyond normal wear | Yes | Same |
| Unpaid utilities owed to the landlord | Yes | Same |
| Moving and storing belongings you left behind | Yes | Same |
| Excessive cleaning and trash removal | Yes | Same |
| Routine cleaning to ready the unit for the next tenant | No | Same |
| A separate cleaning fee | No | Same |
| Late fees or attorney's fees (since June 14, 2019) | No | Same |
| Replacement keys | Up to 110% of actual cost | [RPL 235-i](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-I) |

The landlord [must prove that withheld amounts are reasonable](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and any lease clause waiving these rules is void.

Cleaning is where sources split. Cornell's own move-out page says ["charges for cleaning services will be deducted"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out). The Cornell Law guide says a separate cleaning fee is banned and only cleaning beyond routine turnover can come out. Trust the law. Clean the unit back to its move-in condition, and photograph it, so there is nothing excessive to charge.

If your building has six or more units, the deposit sits in an interest-bearing New York account, the landlord may keep a 1% yearly administrative fee, and [you can take the interest at the end of the lease](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants).

## Trash, furniture and what you leave behind

In the City of Ithaca, every bag or can of trash needs a [$6.00 tag (or $36.00 for a sheet of six)](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags), effective January 1, 2026, with one tag per container up to 35 pounds. Garbage may go out [no more than 24 hours before pickup](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance), and the City bars couches, mattresses and appliances stored outdoors. Tenants share responsibility for litter.

## Utilities, mail and keys

- **NYSEG.** Schedule the shutoff a week ahead. The customer line is [(800) 572-1111](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods). Ask for a final meter reading on your move-out date, so the next tenant's power is not on your bill.
- **Internet.** Call your provider a week out, and return any equipment it lent you.
- **Mail.** File the Post Office change of address two weeks out. Give the landlord your forwarding address in writing a week out, since that is where the deposit or statement goes.
- **Keys.** Hand back every key, and get a dated note or email that you did.
- **Parking.** City residential permits [expire July 31](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance).

## The 14 days after you leave

The landlord must return the deposit, or the rest of it with an itemized statement of each deduction, [within 14 days after you vacate](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). A landlord who misses the deadline forfeits the right to keep any part of it. If the building changed hands while you lived there, the new owner is liable for a deposit it knew about.

Example: a senior pays $1,246 a month for one bed in a Collegetown 4BR. That is the median per-bed asking rent across four [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) 4BR listings for 2027-28, by our midpoint count. The deposit can be no more than one month's rent, so at most $1,246. The lease ends July 31. The senior emails for an inspection on July 10, the landlord sets it for July 20 with two days' notice, and the senior patches two nail holes it flags. By August 14 the senior should have $1,246, or a smaller check with a list. If nothing arrives by August 14, the landlord has lost the right to keep any of it. If a court finds the withholding willful, punitive damages can reach twice the deposit, $2,492.

Not confirmed: a 2026 LeaseLenses blog post reads [City Code Chapter 258](https://www.leaselenses.com/blog/new-york-ithaca-security-deposit-ordinance/) as allowing up to three times a wrongly withheld deposit; it has not been checked against the code. The state's 14 days controls either way.

## If the deposit does not come back


1. **A demand letter.** The [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) puts this first. State the amount, the 14-day rule and the date you moved out, and attach your photos.
2. **Mediation.** The Community Dispute Resolution Center handles tenant disputes at [607-273-9347](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources).
3. **Small claims.** Ithaca City Court, 118 East Clinton Street, hears claims [up to $5,000](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance); town and village courts top out at $3,000.
4. **The Attorney General.** After you have tried the landlord, file a complaint at [1-800-771-7755](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants).

Free advice at any step: the Cornell Law tenant hotline, [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/).

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the inspection.** You lose the week to fix the landlord's list, and its charges become the only record.
- **No dated photos.** Without them, a charge for a stain or hole is your word against the landlord's.
- **No forwarding address in writing.** The deposit check goes to an empty apartment in August.
- **Leaving a couch or mattress at the curb.** The City bars them outdoors, and its Solid Waste group [issues violation notices](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/239/Trash-Collection) for misplaced trash. Left inside, the landlord can deduct for removal.
- **Giving under two weeks' notice.** The landlord no longer has to hold a move-out inspection, so you lose the list of charges you could have fixed.
- **Paying a flat cleaning fee.** A separate cleaning fee is banned; only cleaning beyond routine turnover can come out of the deposit.

## FAQ

**Does my landlord have to give me interest on the deposit?** Only in a building with six or more units, where it must sit in an [interest-bearing account](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants), less a 1% yearly fee.

**Can the landlord keep my deposit for last month's rent?** You cannot have paid both: New York caps the deposit plus any rent advance at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Skip the last month's rent and you still owe it; the landlord deducts it as unpaid rent.

**My roommate damaged the kitchen. Can the landlord charge my share?** On most Ithaca joint leases, yes: each tenant is liable for the whole unit. A housemate who pays more than their share [can sue the other for contribution](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease).

**Do Town of Ithaca or Lansing rentals follow the same rules?** The deposit law is state law, so it applies everywhere in New York. The $6 trash tag and the curb rules are City of Ithaca rules; check your own town.

**Is it 14 days or 30 days?** Fourteen. Older City code text gives 30 days, but the [state's 14 days](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) controls.
