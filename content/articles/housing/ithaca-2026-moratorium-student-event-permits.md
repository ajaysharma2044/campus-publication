Ithaca's Common Council voted 9-1 on Oct. 7, 2026, to withhold new permits for events run by Cornell groups, fraternities and sororities. The measure covered assembly, special-event, Commons and noise permits ([Ithaca Times](https://www.ithaca.com/news/ithaca/common-council-sets-moratorium-on-cornell-event-permits-following-gang-rape-allegations/article_6c84a345-d112-483a-98bc-316f3b64f0a1.html)). The City let it lapse on Nov. 5, 2026, rather than extend it ([Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nys/central-ny/politics/2026/11/05/ithaca-moratorium-cornell-student-permits-end)).

This page is general information, not legal advice.

## What did the moratorium do?

A moratorium is a temporary pause. Here, the City stopped issuing new permits of four kinds to Cornell groups, fraternities and sororities: assembly, special event, Commons and noise ([Ithaca Times](https://www.ithaca.com/news/ithaca/common-council-sets-moratorium-on-cornell-event-permits-following-gang-rape-allegations/article_6c84a345-d112-483a-98bc-316f3b64f0a1.html)). Constitutionally protected activities, such as religious exercise, free speech, peaceful assembly and petitioning, were exempt ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/1510)). The Ithaca Times headline ties the vote to gang rape allegations. The Cornell Daily Sun described the permit pause as primarily affecting Greek organizations ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/10/breaking-cornell-suspends-all-fraternity-parties-social-events-for-2026-27-school-year)).

## How does it relate to Cornell's own action?

Six days after the vote, on Oct. 13, 2026, President Michael I. Kotlikoff canceled all parties and social events in Interfraternity Council houses for the rest of 2026-27. Any fraternity that holds an in-house party or social event faces an immediate emergency suspension. The directive also calls for a review of whether and how fraternity life continues, with an independent review led by Yates and investigators from the New York State Police ([Cornell Presidential Actions](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/policies-and-procedures/presidential-actions)).

The two actions are separate. The City's measure limited permits the City issues. Cornell's directive limits what chapters can do under the university's rules. Cornell's scorecard shows that some earlier suspensions run on set dates: Phi Sigma Kappa becomes eligible to return in fall 2027, Zeta Psi on the first day of fall 2027, Phi Delta Theta in spring 2028 and Delta Tau Delta in spring 2029. Chi Phi is listed on interim suspension ([Cornell Organization Scorecard](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/organization-scorecard)).

## Is the moratorium still in effect?

No. Mayor Robert Cantelmo told the Common Council the City would let it lapse "for the time being." He said Cornell had agreed to two of the City's three demands. The open one is a permanent handover of sex crime investigations to the Ithaca Police Department or another government agency ([607 News Now](https://607newsnow.com/news/258852-ithaca-mayor-allows-cornell-moratorium-to-end-for-time-being/)). If Cornell does not hand those cases over, the City will reconsider the moratorium ([Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nys/central-ny/politics/2026/11/05/ithaca-moratorium-cornell-student-permits-end)).

## What does it mean for renters near campus?

The pause named Cornell and its affiliated fraternities, sororities and student organizations ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/1510)). A City staff member said it was not meant to stop a small gathering with a Bluetooth speaker ([14850.com](https://www.14850.com/101647462-ithaca-moratorium-cornell-events/)). Anyone whose amplified sound carries outside a building between 10 p.m. and 7:30 a.m. needs a City noise permit. Apply at least 72 hours ahead ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/528/Noise-Permit)). For the city's other noise, trash and snow rules, see the [trash tags, snow and noise guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/).

If you plan to live in a chapter house in 2027-28, the suspensions and the review of fraternity life matter to your housing plan. The [off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/) covers how Greek housing fits the residential requirement, and a lease clause on parties and noise is worth a close read, as the [lease-reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) shows.
