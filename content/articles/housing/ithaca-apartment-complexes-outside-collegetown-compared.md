```glance
- Downtown's Ithaca Renting complexes price a bed from $695 to $1,595, with heat, water and trash included.
- The Lake Street corridor is the closest alternative, 0.4 to 0.8 miles from Ho Plaza.
- Lansing, Varna and the Northeast sit 1.9 to 2.7 miles out and need a bus or a car.
- Always check which prices are per bed and which are per unit.
```

Outside Collegetown, the lowest-priced beds cluster downtown, in [Ithaca Renting's buildings](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/). Closer options sit along Lake Street, and cheaper-per-unit ones lie east and north, at the cost of a longer ride to campus.

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Cornell's own Maplewood Apartments, at 121 Veterans Place, hold [444 units and 872 beds](https://www.lechase.com/portfolio/greystar/) with electricity, trash, internet and cable in the rent. Furnished studios to 4-bedrooms are managed by [Greystar](https://www.greystar.com/maplewood-ithaca-ny/p_15697). The [grad housing guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/) covers Hasbrouck and the rest of the grad market.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
Compare by the year. At the Old Cigar Factory's 3-bedroom rate, a bed costs $8,340 to $9,540 over 12 months, against [$13,744 for a Cornell double](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). Add the bus. The [cheapest housing guide](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/) ranks every low-cost option.
```

## How do the areas compare?

Cornell gives the distances. The table adds what each area's complexes charge and include.

| Area | Distance from campus | Typical complexes | What to expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown | 0.5 to 1.5 miles; 20 to 30 minute uphill walk | Cityview, Commons West, Old Cigar Factory, Whiton Hall, City Centre, Asteri | Heat and water usually in rent; bus every 15 to 20 minutes |
| Lake Street corridor | 0.44 to 0.81 miles straight line | Lake Street Apartments, The Pinnacle, Auden Ithaca, Cliffside Terrace | Furnished options and shuttles |
| East Hill and Belle Sherman | 0.75 to 2 miles | Coal Yard Apartments, Summerhill Townhouses | Townhouses and larger units |
| Lansing and Northeast | 2 to 3 miles straight line | Lansing West, Triphammer Apartments, The Meadows | Car or bus; garages and parking |
| Varna | 1.85 miles straight line | Ivy Ridge Townhomes | Furnished townhomes |

Source: [Cornell neighborhoods page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) and the listing pages linked below; straight-line distances are to Ho Plaza from the Ithaca marketplace inventory. The [Collegetown, Fall Creek and downtown guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) covers the first three areas in depth.

## What do the downtown complexes cost?

Ithaca Renting runs most of the larger downtown buildings and prices by the bed, for 2026-27 unless noted. Heat, water and trash are included at its downtown properties, and electricity is included only at Whiton Hall, Colonial and Commons Studios, per its [downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/).

| Complex | Address | Price per bed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cityview | 118 Prospect St | Studio $1,400 to $1,595; 2BR $940 to $1,045; 3BR $815 to $895 | Furnished; one block from the Commons; parking extra |
| Commons West | 107 E State St | Studio $1,295 to $1,495; 2BR $975 to $1,025; 1BR $1,700 to $1,725 | Furnished or unfurnished; sublet option |
| Old Cigar Factory | 108 to 114 E State St | 3BR $695 to $795; budget studio $1,175 to $1,275 | Under 5 minutes from the Green and Seneca Street stops |
| Whiton Hall | 222 S Aurora St | Room $965 to $1,065 | Electricity and Wi-Fi included; furnished |

Source: [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), with property pages for [Cityview](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=6), [Commons West](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=7), the [Old Cigar Factory](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=9) and [Whiton Hall](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=11).

Cityview adds two rules: no pets, and the first month and the deposit due before move-in. Heat is tenant-controlled at the Old Cigar Factory, and internet and parking cost extra there.

Other downtown buildings price by the unit or the month. [City Centre](https://citycentreithaca.com/), a Greystar building at 301 E. State St., lists a studio at [$2,475.75 to $2,618.75 a month on a 15-month term](https://citycentreithaca.com/floorplans/), with utilities not included per its [FAQ](https://citycentreithaca.com/faqs/). [Cayuga Place](https://livehhcayuga.com/cayuga-place/floor-plans) at 131 E. Green St. includes water, sewer and trash, and charges $75 a month per unit for internet and $75 a month per bedroom for furniture.

Asteri, at [118 E. Green St.](https://www.asteriithaca.com/contact), publishes no prices. The city ordered about 200 residents out on March 4, 2026, over a blocked stairwell among more than 40 violations, and lifted the order March 20, per [the Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/asteri-s-aftermath-displacement-dangerous-conditions-and-a-predatory-cash-for-keys-scheme). A $2,000 cash-for-keys offer to residents who left within five days drew criticism as predatory from a city official and the Ithaca Tenants Union.

## What about the Lake Street corridor?

Lake Street runs between West Campus and downtown, so it is the nearest non-Collegetown choice. Travis Hyde's [Lake Street Apartments](https://www.travishyde.com/west-campus/lake%20st) are furnished and sit across from TCAT Stop 5. Travis Hyde's [availability page](https://www.travishyde.com/availability) lists a 2-bedroom at 106 Lake St for $2,070 a month and a 1-bedroom at 108 Lake St for $1,400.

Two newer buildings sell shuttles. [The Pinnacle](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/the-pinnacle-237571) at 121 Lake St lists prices from $1,600 to $3,800 and a commute shuttle. [Auden Ithaca](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/auden-ithaca-7919) at 210 Lake St is fully furnished with a private shuttle, internet and cable included, and a $689 to $1,389 range. Neither listing says whether those prices are per bed or per unit, so ask before you compare.

[Cliffside Terrace](https://liveherehousing.com/cornell/cliffsideterrace), 405 Lake St, has 36 units and states 0.54 miles west of Cornell, a 21-minute walk and a 3-minute drive.

## What about East Hill, Lansing and Varna?

These cost more in time and sometimes less in rent.

**East Hill.** [Coal Yard Apartments](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/coal-yard-apartments-7734) at 143 Maple Ave lists a $1,240 to $3,750 range, with most units "Call For Price" and garage parking at $1,300 a year. [Summerhill Townhouses](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/summerhill-townhouses-7826) at 222 Summerhill Lane list 3-bedroom townhouses at $2,700 to $2,750 per unit, unfurnished, with a washer and dryer in each and a bus about every 15 minutes outside.

**Lansing and the Northeast.** [Lansing West](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx) lists base rents from $1,302 for a 1-bedroom, $1,625 for a 2-bedroom and $2,072 for a 3-bedroom. Its special offered rent free until January 2027 for applications by Nov. 14, 2026. The listing also posts a $20 application fee, but New York's [fee law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) bars any payment for processing an application, so ask the landlord to drop it. [Triphammer Apartments](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/triphammer-236697) at 5 Cinema Dr list rents of $1,695 to $1,750. [The Meadows at Ithaca](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/the-meadows-at-ithaca-236704), at 100 Graham Rd, lists rents of $1,725 to $2,495 for its 1- to 3-bedroom townhomes, with electric, gas, internet and trash included. The [Lansing and Cayuga Heights guide](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/) explains how the rules change outside the City.

**Varna.** [Ivy Ridge Townhomes](https://ivyridgetownhomes.com/), 802 Dryden Rd, hold [108 bedrooms, finished in September 2019](https://ithacating.com/2019/09/08/802-dryden-road-construction-update-9-2019/). The units are fully furnished and priced on request. The FAQ says it is less than a mile to campus, while map coordinates put it 1.85 straight-line miles from Ho Plaza, so measure it yourself.

## How do I get to campus from these complexes?

Downtown buildings list bus stops within five minutes at 15 to 20 minute intervals. Further out, the bus or a car decides the trip. ## How should I choose between them?

Four questions sort the list quickly.

**Who pays for electricity?** Maplewood includes it. City Centre leaves utilities out of base rent, and at Cayuga Place the resident pays electricity. Downtown Ithaca Renting buildings include heat, water and trash but not electricity in most cases.

**Is it furnished?** Cityview, Whiton Hall, Lake Street Apartments, Auden and Ivy Ridge list furnished units. Summerhill Townhouses are unfurnished, so add the cost of a bed and a couch.

**How long is the lease?** City Centre lists terms of 12 to 15 months on its floor plans. Cayuga Place offers 10-month options at an additional premium, per its leasing site. Match the term to your stay, or the extra months become the real cost.

**Where does the car go?** Triphammer Apartments advertise free on-site parking, and Coal Yard garage spaces cost $1,300 a year. Cityview lists parking for rent at $50.

## What to do next

Pick the area first, then the complex. For each one you like, ask in writing whether the price is per bed or per unit, which utilities it includes and what the lease term is.
