```glance
- In a building with 4 or more units, New York law says your landlord cannot unreasonably refuse a sublet; in 3 or fewer units, your lease decides.
- Send the request by certified mail, return receipt, with six items: term, subtenant's name and addresses, reason, your address while away, co-tenant and guarantor consent, a copy of the sublease.
- The landlord has 10 days to ask for more information and 30 days to answer; no answer counts as consent.
- You stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage; only an assignment ends that, and a landlord can refuse an assignment without a reason.
- A sublet fee is allowed only if your lease lists it and it is reasonable; a background check costs at most $20.
- List free on Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace or the Off-Campus Living email list; mail the request 2 to 3 months before you leave.
```

Yes, you need your landlord's written consent, but in a New York building with four or more units the landlord [cannot unreasonably withhold it](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), and silence for 30 days after your certified-mail request counts as a yes. In a house with three or fewer units, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease), so read its sublet clause first. Either way you remain on the hook for rent and damage while the subtenant lives there.

```reader "If you're going abroad or away for a semester"
Mail the sublet request two to three months before you leave, so the 10-day and 30-day clocks run out while you are still in Ithaca. Put your address abroad in the letter; the statute asks for where you will live during the sublet. Get your housemates' written consent first, because the request must include it. If no subtenant turns up, your share is still due: a five-month semester at the [Ithaca Renting Collegetown 4BR median of $1,246.25 per bed for 2027-28](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) is about $6,231 (our arithmetic), and your housemates are jointly liable for it. The costly mistake is handing over keys without the written steps, which the law calls a [substantial breach](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B).
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
If you are taking someone's sublet, get a [written sublease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) and ask to see the landlord's written consent. Cornell says most leases require landlord approval. Check the end date of the main lease: Dryden South's leases [all end May 31, 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), so a sublet there cannot run into June. Asking prices are thin: Modern Living advertises sublets ["from $900 per person"](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/) with no period stated, and one Dryden South suite was listed at [$1,395 per person for Fall 2026](https://www.apartments.com/dryden-south-sublets-available-fall-2026-ithaca-ny/yeje6qs/). The mistake that costs most is paying a subletter who never asked the landlord. Not confirmed: what a subtenant can do if that landlord objects.
```

```reader "If you're a senior whose lease runs past graduation"
Most Ithaca tenants' leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), so a May graduate pays June and July unless someone takes the room. At the [Collegetown 4BR median of $1,246.25 per bed for 2027-28](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), that is about $2,493 for two months (our arithmetic). Mail the request by mid-March so the landlord's 30 days end before May. If you are leaving for good, ask about an assignment, which ends your liability, but the landlord may [refuse it without cause](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). The mistake is waiting until May, when 40 days of legal clocks eat most of June.
```

```reader "If you're an alum who owns an Ithaca rental"
In a building of four or more units you may ask for more information within 10 days and must answer in writing within 30; [silence is consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). Refuse only for a reason you can defend: a tenant facing an unreasonable refusal may sublet anyway and recover costs and attorney fees if you acted in bad faith. A lease clause waiving these rules is void. You can charge a sublet fee only if the lease lists it and it is [reasonable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease), and a background check is capped at [$20 or actual cost](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). In a house of three or fewer units, your lease sets the rule.
```

## Does your landlord have to agree?

It depends on how many residential units your building has. Count them before anything else.

| Your building | Your right to sublet | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| 4 or more residential units | A legal right, with consent that [cannot be unreasonably withheld](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) | RPL 226-b; a lease clause waiving it is void |
| 3 or fewer units | No statutory right | [Your lease](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) |
| Public housing, units with statutory admission criteria, co-op proprietary leases | Excluded from 226-b | Program rules or the proprietary lease |

A common belief is that a landlord can always say no. That is wrong for buildings of four or more units. You still have to ask in writing there. The right limits how the landlord can refuse; it does not let you skip the request.

Cornell's own lease checklist lists the [ability to sublet and the conditions for a sublet](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) as terms to read before you sign. Off-Campus Living answers lease questions at 607-255-2310.

## Sublet or assignment: which one you want

A sublet keeps you as the tenant. An assignment hands the whole lease to someone else. The difference is who owes the rent if things go wrong.

| | Sublet | Assignment |
|---|---|---|
| Who stays liable to the landlord | You, for the subtenant's rent and damage | Generally not you, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) |
| Can the landlord refuse? | Not unreasonably, in 4+ unit buildings | Yes, [without cause](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) |
| Your remedy if the refusal is unreasonable | Sublet as requested; costs and attorney fees if the landlord acted in bad faith | Release from the lease on 30 days' notice, and nothing more |
| Fits | A summer, a semester abroad, an internship | Leaving Ithaca for good |

If neither works, the third route is a written early-termination agreement with the move-out date and the amount owed. The [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) calls it the simplest route. If you leave anyway, the landlord must try in good faith to [re-rent the unit](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) at the lower of market or lease rent. That is a different page: [breaking an Ithaca lease early](/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).

## How to ask: the request, step by step

```figure
  - {label: "Mail the request certified, return receipt requested"}
  - {label: "Answer any request for more information sent within 10 days"}
  - {label: "Get the answer in writing, or wait out the 30 days"}
  - {label: "Sign the sublease, photograph the unit, hand over keys"}
```

1. **Read the lease.** Find the sublet clause and any listed fee. In a small house this clause is the whole rule.
2. **Get co-tenant and guarantor consent.** The [statute](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) wants their written consent in the request. On a joint lease, Cornell says each housemate is [responsible for the others' rent and damages](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire), so they have a stake in who moves in. A parent who co-signed is a guarantor and signs too.
3. **Find a subtenant and write the sublease.** Put in the dates, rent, deposit and who pays utilities.
4. **Mail the request.** Use certified mail, return receipt requested. Email is a courtesy copy, not a substitute.
5. **Answer follow-ups fast.** The 30-day clock restarts from the date you supply extra information.
6. **Keep the receipt.** It proves when the clock started if the landlord stays silent.

The request must contain these six items, per [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B):

- the term of the sublease;
- the subtenant's name and addresses;
- your reason for subletting;
- your address during the sublet;
- the written consent of any co-tenant or guarantor;
- a copy of the proposed sublease.

The landlord may ask for more information [within 10 days](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). The answer is due within 30 days of your request, or of the extra information, whichever is later. A landlord who never answers is deemed to have consented.

Example: a junior leaving for a spring semester abroad mails the request on October 1. The landlord can ask for more information until about October 11. Say the landlord asks on October 8 and the junior replies on October 15. The answer is then due by about November 14. With no answer by then, the sublet is approved. Mailed in December instead, the same request could still be open when the flight leaves.

Not confirmed: which refusal reasons count as reasonable. If you get a refusal, call the Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline at [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) before you sublet anyway.

## What it costs to sublet

The landlord may charge a sublet fee [only if the lease allows it, and it must be reasonable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). A background or credit check on the subtenant is allowed. State law caps that check at the [lesser of $20 or actual cost](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A), and it must be waived if the applicant brings a check from the past 30 days.

Listing is free. Sublets were [free to list on Cornell's service in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/), when landlords paid $30. The Graduate School calls the marketplace [free to anyone with Cornell credentials](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/).

Not confirmed: a typical Ithaca summer sublet rent, or the usual discount below lease rent. No Ithaca landlord publishes a standard sublet fee either; ask yours in writing.

## After the subtenant moves in

You are still the tenant. If the subtenant stops paying, the landlord comes to you. Your claim is against the subtenant, and Ithaca City Court hears small claims [up to $5,000](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).

Before you leave, Cornell's [tenant checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) says to notify contacts, forward mail, sort out utilities, and clean and secure the apartment. Take dated photos at handover. They are your record if the deposit is docked for damage the subtenant caused.

## Common mistakes

- **Handing over keys on a verbal "sure."** A sublet without the written steps is a [substantial breach of the lease](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), which puts your deposit and your next reference at risk.
- **Mailing the request too late.** Ten days plus thirty, plus a follow-up, can run past six weeks. A request sent in late April may not clear before a June 1 start.
- **Skipping a housemate's consent.** The request must include it. Without it, the request does not follow the statute's steps, and the sublet risks being a breach.
- **Choosing an assignment because it sounds cleaner.** A landlord can refuse it without cause, and your only remedy is release on 30 days' notice.
- **Paying a sublet fee the lease never mentions.** It is allowed only if the lease lists it; the check on the subtenant is capped at $20.
- **Assuming Cornell will step in.** Cornell Financial Aid [will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in a mid-year lease break, and the Cornell Law guide does not cover study abroad.

## FAQ

**Can my landlord charge me for the subtenant's credit check?** Yes, up to the [lesser of $20 or actual cost](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A), with a copy of the check and the invoice.

**I hold a Cornell housing license, not a lease. What do I do?** Cornell's own license deadlines apply. For 2026-27, Cornell cancels without penalty for study away, leave or an internship if you ask by [June 15 for fall or December 1 for spring](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027); later requests cost $500.

**What if my landlord refuses for no good reason?** In a 4+ unit building you may sublet as requested, and recover costs and attorney fees if the landlord [acted in bad faith](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). Call the Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline at [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) first.

**Does a dispute with my landlord affect my visa?** Cornell Graduate School staff say a landlord dispute [does not affect international students' visa status](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/) or standing.

**Who do I call with a lease question?** Off-Campus Living at [607-255-2310](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease). This page is general information, not legal advice.
