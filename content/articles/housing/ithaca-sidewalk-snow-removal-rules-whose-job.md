The City of Ithaca puts sidewalk snow on the property owner: all snow and ice must come off the sidewalk in front of the property within 24 hours after snowfall begins ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance)). A lease can move that job to the tenants.

This is general information, not legal advice.

## Whose job is the sidewalk in Ithaca?

The owner's. City Code 178-3 says the owner of a property must clear the adjacent sidewalk, and each day the walk stays uncleared counts as a separate violation. Under Chapter 178-10, the owner or agent owes a civil penalty of $40 for a first sidewalk violation at a property within 12 months, $60 for a second and $100 for each one after that ([City of Ithaca Code, Chapter 178](https://ecode360.com/8389191)).

The city does not clear most sidewalks itself. In September 2023, Public Works Superintendent Mike Thorne said the city lacks the staff to clear sidewalks or curb cuts. He estimated the cost at about $4,000 per mile per winter ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2023/09/snow-removal-or-no-removal/)). Syracuse clears about 125 miles on a $650,000 yearly budget, which works out to $5,200 per mile ($650,000 divided by 125) (Ithaca Voice, same story).

## What counts as cleared?

Clear the full paved width. On a corner lot, the duty covers the portion to the curb and the ramps ([City of Ithaca exterior property maintenance](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance)). Corner owners clear both sidewalks ([City of Ithaca plowing guide](https://cityofithacany.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12559)).

Plows can push snow back onto a walk you already cleared, and then you clear it again. Where ice will not come off, the city FAQ allows sand or ashes, followed by clearing when practical ([City of Ithaca FAQ](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/faq?cat=46)).

## What happens if nobody shovels?

The city can send a crew and bill the owner. The Superintendent of Public Works can clear the walk, with a minimum charge of $50 per plowing ([City of Ithaca FAQ](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/faq?cat=46)).

## How does the city plow the streets?

Sidewalks are yours, but the streets are the city's. Ithaca plows about 65 miles of road, nearly 150 lane-miles. Emergency lanes come first, then the highest-volume roads, with steep roads prioritized and small residential streets last. Restoring parking lanes can take weeks, and salt loses its effect below 20F. The city says it averages 65 inches of snow a year, and it runs a volunteer app called Snow Angels for neighbors who help clear walks ([City of Ithaca plowing guide](https://cityofithacany.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12559)).

Residential side streets near Collegetown get plowed late, so plan on a pile at the end of your walk. That pile is why the re-clearing rule matters: the plow can bury a walk you finished an hour earlier.

## What can a tenant do when the walk is not cleared?

Start with the lease. If it assigns the job to tenants, the work is yours. If it does not, send the owner a dated written note with a photo, the same habit that works for repairs. The city's code puts the duty on the owner, so a written note shows the owner knew ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance)).

Say you live in a three-bedroom house on a corner lot. Snow starts Tuesday at 6 p.m. The sidewalk along both streets must be clear by Wednesday at 6 p.m., ramps and curb included. If the plow buries the ramp Wednesday night, clear it again.

| Item | Rule | Who carries it |
|---|---|---|
| Sidewalk in front of the property | Clear the full paved width after snowfall starts | Owner, unless the lease says otherwise |
| Corner lot | Both sidewalks, the curb portion and the ramps | Owner, unless the lease says otherwise |
| Plow piles on the walk | Clear again | Whoever holds the duty |
| Ice that will not come off | Sand or ashes, then clear when practical | Whoever holds the duty |

Source: City of Ithaca exterior property maintenance page, plowing guide and snow FAQ.

## Can a lease make the tenant shovel?

Yes. The code puts the duty on the owner, but a lease can pass it to tenants, and Cornell lists "snow removal" among the items to check before you sign ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease)). Our [lease reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) shows where to look.

Some buildings bundle it in the rent. A listing for 407 College Ave. in Collegetown includes snow removal along with internet, water and hot water ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/407-college-ave-collegetown-s-best-kept-secret-237329)).

```reader "If you rent a house with roommates"
Ask who shovels before you sign, and write the answer into the lease or an email. If the lease assigns the job to tenants, split it by a shared schedule, because the city counts the walk as one property. Snow that falls overnight needs clearing inside the same deadline as snow that falls at noon. If your lease says nothing, the code still points at the owner, so send the owner a written note when the walk is buried.
```

```reader "If you're a landlord"
The duty is yours unless the lease says otherwise, and corner lots carry two sidewalks. Our [landlord rules guide](/cornell/housing/becoming-ithaca-landlord-student-rental-rules/) covers the rest of the city's rental requirements. Hire a plow service or name the tenant in writing.
```

## What if you live outside the City of Ithaca?

The facts above are City of Ithaca rules. The Town of Ithaca, outside the Village of Cayuga Heights, puts the job on both the owner and the occupant: snow and ice must come off the sidewalk in front of the property within 24 hours of falling. If nobody clears it, the Town can do the work and bill the owner the actual cost plus 50% for overhead ([Town of Ithaca Code, Chapter 230](https://ecode360.com/8660754)). In Cayuga Heights or Lansing, call the village or town clerk, and read our [guide to which municipality covers your address](/cornell/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/).

## Who clears sidewalks on campus?

Cornell Grounds clears campus roads, lots, steps and sidewalks, except those posted as not receiving winter maintenance, and Building Care salts entrances, steps and ramps. Report a slippery spot to 607-255-5322 ([Cornell Facilities and Campus Services](https://fcs.cornell.edu/services/repair-maintenance/snow-removal)).

## What else changes when it snows?

Parking rules change on the same nights. Our [winter parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) covers odd/even days and campus lots. Trash, noise and tag rules are in the [renter rules guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-trash-tags-snow-noise-rules-renters/).
