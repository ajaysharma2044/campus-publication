```glance
- Put the parties, exact dates, rent, deposit and utilities in writing, and sign it before any money moves.
- Attach a copy to the landlord consent request; the law requires one.
- Take dated photos at handover. They decide who owes for damage.
- You stay liable to the landlord, so write what the subtenant owes you.
```

A sublet agreement should name both people, give the exact start and end dates, state the rent and how it is paid, set the deposit and its return date, and say who pays utilities. In a building with four or more units you must also send a [copy of the proposed sublease](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) to the landlord.

## Why does a sublet need a written agreement?

Cornell tells tenants to get a [written sublease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus), and it warns that original tenants can remain responsible after a sublet. If the subtenant stops paying, the landlord looks to you. A written agreement is your proof of what the subtenant promised. The [how-to guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) covers the landlord's consent steps.

The Cornell Law guide says an unpaid subtenant can be pursued for breach of contract in [small claims, city, town or supreme court](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). A court will look for the written terms.

## What goes in the agreement?

Cornell's list of lease terms to check is a good model, because a sublease is a small lease. Work down this list.

**The parties**
- Full legal names and addresses of you and the subtenant. The landlord's consent request must list the [subtenant's name and addresses](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) and your address during the sublet.
- The address and unit number of the apartment, and the name of the landlord.

**The dates**
- Move-in and move-out dates. Check them against the main lease, which cannot be exceeded.
- The sublet's term as the landlord will see it. Ithaca Renting sets a [two-month minimum](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) on downtown sublets and bans stays under a month in Collegetown.

**The money**
- Rent for each month, the due date, the late penalty and how it is paid. Cornell's list names [rent, due date and late penalty](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) as terms to read.
- A receipt for every payment, signed and dated.
- Any sublet fee the landlord charges and who pays it. A landlord can charge one only if the lease allows it and it is reasonable.

**The deposit**
- The amount, who holds it, the conditions for keeping any of it, and the return date. New York caps a landlord's deposit plus any advance rent at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and a landlord must return the remainder with an itemized list within 14 days. Those rules govern the landlord's deposit. Write a similar timetable for yours and ask the landlord to confirm it.
- The deductions you can take: unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear, unpaid utilities, and moving and storing belongings left behind ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits)).

**Utilities and extras**
- Who pays electricity, gas and internet, and how the bills are split. Ithaca Renting asks summer tenants to [keep the electricity on](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), with the fridge running at the least.
- Parking, laundry, pets and guests, taken from the main lease. Cornell's list adds the minimum and maximum number of occupants.

**The room and its contents**
- Which furniture stays, and its condition.
- Dated photos of every room at handover, attached to the agreement. Cornell's move-out advice also says to take photos.

**What happens if something goes wrong**
- Early termination: put the move-out date and any money owed in writing. The Cornell Law guide calls a written termination the [simplest route](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) out of a lease.
- Repairs: say who reports problems and to whom. The Cornell Law guide advises repair requests [in writing, with name, address and date](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).

**The signatures**
- The subtenant, you, and every housemate on the lease who must consent. A request needs [written consent from any co-tenant or guarantor](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). The [roommates guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) explains why they sign.
- A copy of the landlord's written consent, or the dated certified-mail receipt if the landlord never answered.

## What should you not put in the agreement?

Do not promise rights the main lease does not give you. Do not waive the landlord's rights: a lease clause that waives the statute is void. Do not include a clause that lets the subtenant sublet again. Ithaca Renting allows [one sublet per term](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) in Collegetown.

## How do you use it?

Read it aloud with the subtenant. Attach it to the consent request if the building has four or more units. Sign after the landlord answers. Walk through the unit together, take photos and hand over the keys. At the end, inspect again, compare the photos and settle the deposit. The [lease-reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) shows what a main lease holds, and the [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) covers the money.

## What to do next

Copy the clause list into a document, fill it in with the subtenant and send it with your consent request. If the sublet falls through, the [early-termination guide](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/) covers your options.
