A House Dinner is a weekly sit-down meal in each of Cornell's five West Campus Houses, held Wednesdays from 6 to 7 p.m. for residents, staff, fellows and invited guests. It is the best-known part of house life ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms/west-campus-house-dinners)).

## What is a West Campus House Dinner?

Cornell Dining calls it an upscale meal, specially prepared by the House Chefs and Cornell Dining teams, in a festive setting that can feature music, guest speakers or events tied to House programs or holidays. Everyone in the House community comes: all residents, staff, house fellows, guests-in-residence and distinguished visitors.

The point is community. The dinners give the House Professor-Dean, the Assistant Dean, the house fellows and the graduate and undergraduate fellows a regular chance to meet residents, share news and celebrate what the House has done (Cornell Dining). The [guide to House Professor-Deans](/cornell/housing/cornell-house-professor-dean-what-they-do/) explains who they are.

## Who can attend, and when?

The five dining rooms open at 6 p.m. on Wednesdays, and each House welcomes its own residents, fellows and invited guests. At 7 p.m. the rooms reopen to others, and non-residents can join then or eat at another dining room on campus (Cornell Dining). Check [Cornell Dining's hours page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms) for menus and times at every eatery.

The dining rooms are Becker House Dining Room, Jansen's Dining Room in Hans Bethe House, Keeton House Dining Room, Rose House Dining Room and a dining room in Alice Cook House.

## What does the House Meal Plan cost?

The House Meal Plan costs $3,802 per semester, or $7,604 a year, plus a $50 annual fee. It covers all meals in the ten residential dining rooms and includes $400 in Big Red Bucks and eight guest meals per semester. West Campus residents are enrolled automatically, and the plan cannot be changed or cancelled while you live on West ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## What else is house life?

Each House has a House Office for mail and keys, a large common room, lounges, study rooms, a library, laundry and seminar rooms. Residents get priority for one-credit "Learning Where You Live" courses. Students help run the House through House Councils and other resident leaders ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system)).

Cornell says Houses have historically been about 65% sophomores, 25% juniors and 10% seniors, with about 15% transfer students. A sophomore can stay through continued occupancy to live on West as a junior and senior (Cornell Student & Campus Life, [West Campus House System](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/west-campus-house-system)).

## Can you keep the dinners if you move off campus?

Partly. Off-campus students who lived in the House System for at least one semester can buy the House Affiliate plan, which costs $724 per semester, or $1,448 a year. It gives three meals a week in the West Campus dining rooms plus $100 in Big Red Bucks per semester. You apply by email to dining@cornell.edu (Cornell Dining). The plan is for former residents only.

If you plan to cook instead, the [off-campus meal plan or cook-yourself cost guide](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) compares the options. For the rules on leaving campus housing, see the [residential requirement exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).
