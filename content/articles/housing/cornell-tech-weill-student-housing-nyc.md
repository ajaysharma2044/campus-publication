Cornell budgets $28,856 a year for housing for a graduate student in New York City in 2026-27, which is $8,894 more than the $19,962 it budgets for Ithaca ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students)). You choose your own apartment, because the residential requirement covers Ithaca-based students.

## How much does housing cost for a Cornell Tech or Weill student?

Cornell's budget line is the one number the university publishes for New York City. Housing is $28,856 a year, or $2,404.67 a month (28,856 divided by 12). The Ithaca line is $1,663.50 a month, so New York is $741.17 higher each month. Cornell's graduate budget for New York City also lists these 2026-27 lines ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students)):

| Line | New York City | Ithaca |
|---|---|---|
| Housing | $28,856 | $19,962 |
| Food | $6,404 | $5,768 |
| Transportation | $1,364 | $3,754 |
| Books | $2,464 | $2,464 |
| Personal | $4,658 | $4,196 |

Cornell's NYC subtotal on that page does not match its own lines, so use the individual lines. Transportation is the one line lower in New York. The budget is an allowance, not a rent quote, so check real listings against it.

## Does Cornell house students in New York City?

Cornell's housing pages in this guide's sources do not list Cornell Tech or Weill residence options, rents or application dates, so ask your program's student affairs office for the current list and its deadlines before you search privately. The two-year residential requirement covers full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students ([Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). Students based in New York City are not part of it.

The full Ithaca-side picture, including Hasbrouck and Maplewood, is in the [grad student off-campus guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/). If you are still choosing a path, start from the [grad student start page](/start/grad-student/).

## How do you get between New York City and Ithaca?

Cornell's Campus-to-Campus bus, called C2C, stops at Weill Medical at 1300 York Avenue, with the main entrance across from 69th Street. Riders wait in the lobby ([C2C schedule](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-departure-schedule)). The trip takes about 4.5 to 5 hours. A NetID fare is $93.50 one way and $187 round trip, and the public fare is $101 ([C2C rates](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates)).

Two changes matter. The stop at 64th Street and Third Avenue, which served the Tech campus, was discontinued on Aug. 1, 2026, and Cornell opened a new reservation system on Nov. 4, 2026 that requires everyone to make a new account ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4)). Check the current stop list before you book, and read the [C2C booking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-c2c-bus-booking-fares-rules/) for steps and fees.

Commercial buses cost less. OurBus lists Ithaca to New York from $37.40 ([OurBus](https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny)). The [NYC to Cornell bus guide](/cornell/getting-around/nyc-to-cornell-bus-c2c-ourbus-flixbus-greyhound/) compares the carriers.

## What lease rules apply in New York City?

State rules cover the whole state. A security deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent, and the landlord must return the balance with an itemized statement within 14 days after you move out ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). A landlord cannot charge a fee to process an application. A background or credit check costs the lesser of its actual cost or $20 ([Real Property Law 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)).

The Good Cause Eviction law took effect on April 20, 2024, and it applies outside New York City only where a municipality opts in ([Cornell Law tenants' guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal)). Ithaca's own 120-day renewal notice rule and its Certificate of Compliance system do not apply in New York City. City-specific rules, such as broker fees and rent-regulated units, are not covered here, so confirm them with the city's tenant resources before you pay a broker.

## Should you buy renters insurance?

Cornell's housing checklist says to buy renter's insurance ([Checklists and Considerations](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations)). Prices and Cornell's preferred insurer are in the [renters insurance guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-renters-insurance-cost/).
