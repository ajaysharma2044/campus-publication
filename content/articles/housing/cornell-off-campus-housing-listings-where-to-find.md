```glance
- Start with Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace on College Pads: only Cornell-affiliated users can see it, and City listings need a current Certificate of Compliance.
- Join the Off-Campus Living email list (NetID required) for rooms, roommates and sublets; admitted students without a NetID forward their acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu.
- Ithaca Renting is the one big landlord with a full per-bed price list; on 2026-11-08, 14 of its 67 Collegetown rows for the coming year were sold out.
- Modern Living and Student Agencies post little or no pricing, so you call or email them.
- Read every listing for per bed or per unit, 10 or 12 months, what is included, and the lease dates; "Call For Price" and expired dates are common.
- Cornell's 2026-27 double works out to $1,145 a month over 12 months: the number to beat.
```

Start with Cornell's [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) on College Pads, open only to people with a proven Cornell affiliation, and the Off-Campus Living email list for rooms and sublets. Then go to landlord sites: [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) publishes per-bed prices for every building, while most other Collegetown landlords make you ask. Collegetown's August units go first, so search the fall before if you want to walk to class; downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and sublets last into spring and summer.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning a junior-year lease"
Your search runs this fall, not next spring. On 2026-11-08 Ithaca Renting already showed 14 of 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 as sold out, including its 8- and 14-bedroom houses. Decide your group size first, because the price list falls from a $1,765 median per bed for a studio to $800 in a 6-bedroom. Fill a group through the Cornell marketplace and the email list; get prices from landlord sites. The costly mistake is quoting a per-unit rent to roommates as if it were per person, or the reverse. Downtown and Fall Creek leftovers stay open into spring, so if Collegetown is gone, you have not lost the year.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
You arrive after the Collegetown rush, so shop the leftover market. A sophomore transfer must live on campus for a year. A junior or senior transfer is exempt but must [request the exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) in the Housing application while it is open, and again each year. Join the Off-Campus Living list before you have a NetID by forwarding your acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu. Then check Ithaca Renting's downtown page (2-bedroom median $992.50 per bed, 3-bedroom $795) and complexes such as Lansing West, which listed one-bedrooms at $1,038 and $1,193 in November 2026. Your free first-year TCAT pass makes a cheaper downtown or Lansing unit workable. The mistake that costs most: signing with strangers you met that week, since each of you owes the whole rent.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
[About 95% of Cornell grad and professional students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) live off campus, and you shop months after the undergrads. The Reed Group's unofficial grad guide says start March to April for an August lease, with a few places left in June and July. If you want quiet, ask every landlord what share of tenants are undergrads. Measure listings against Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments: rolling applications, 12-month contracts, a 1-bedroom at [$1,415 a month unfurnished](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) for 2026-27 with utilities and internet included. The common error is comparing a per-unit one-bedroom rent with a per-bed price: Ithaca Renting's Collegetown one-bedroom median is $2,025 for a person living alone.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
You may sign from abroad and sight unseen, so lean on the channels that screen listings. The Cornell marketplace requires proof of Cornell affiliation, and its City of Ithaca listings need a current Certificate of Compliance. Before you pay, run the address through the City and Cornell safety tool. Cornell International Services says you [do not need an SSN](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students) to rent an apartment. New York caps the deposit plus any rent paid in advance at one month's rent and bans application fees, so a request for three months up front or an "application fee" is a reason to stop. Not confirmed: how Ithaca landlords handle guarantors for students without US credit.
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar, postdoc or here for one semester"
You want furnished space by the month or semester, which most listings are not. Ask Cornell first: it offers some visiting faculty and affiliates [temporary, academic-year or 12-month space in Hasbrouck Apartments](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/visiting-faculty-affiliate-campus-housing) as space allows (gradhousing@cornell.edu). The Sun reported in 2024 that the [International Scholar House](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell) is a furnished five-bedroom rented month to month. Private semester supply shows up as "Spring/Fall Semester 2027" rows on Ithaca Renting and Spring '27 leases on the Ithaca College portal. The expensive mistake is signing a 12-month lease for a six-month stay: you [owe the rest](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) unless the unit is sublet or re-rented.
```

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
Summer housing is mostly sublets from students whose leases run to July 31. Look on the Cornell marketplace and the Off-Campus Living email list. Modern Living advertises sublets "from $900 per person" without a stated period, so ask whether that is monthly. Check the end date: Dryden South's leases all end May 31, 2027, which leaves a June and July gap. Get the landlord's written consent and a written sublease. In a building with four or more units, the landlord [cannot unreasonably refuse](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) a sublet; in a smaller house, the lease decides. Not confirmed: a typical summer sublet price in Ithaca.
```

Cornell names the marketplace, the email list, Ithacarents.com and the row 6 outlets on its [rental listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), and takes no responsibility for disputes with any landlord there.

### 1. The Cornell marketplace on College Pads

The platform is "College Pads, now part of StarRez." Only people who prove a Cornell affiliation can see listings and contact landlords and subletters. From 2026-27, a [City of Ithaca property must have a current Certificate of Compliance](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/) to post. The Graduate School's [renting tips](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/) add that listings are free to Cornell-affiliated people and checked against the City's certificate records. Properties in the Town of Ithaca, Lansing or Cayuga Heights fall outside that check. In 2019, posting a sublet was free and landlords paid $30 a listing, per a [2019 Sun report](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/).

### 2. The Off-Campus Living email list

You need a NetID to join; an admitted student without one forwards the acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu. To subscribe, email the list address on Cornell's [rental listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) with the subject "Join" and a blank body. To leave, send "Leave" in the body with a blank subject. Questions go to Off-Campus Living at [607-255-2310](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease).

### 4. The Ithaca College portal

The Cornell marketplace shares its platform with Ithaca College's portal. Cornell listings sit under an "ithaca-college-ny" path, and the same listing ID shows on both. Some Collegetown units appear there, such as [209 Stewart Ave](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095), five rooms at $850 each a month "all in." It also carries [Spring '27 roommate-matching leases from Foundation Housing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-ny/listing/spring-27-and-full-year-leases-available-for-roommate-matching-foundation-housing-96176), useful if you arrive mid-year.

## How to read a listing

Rent near Cornell is quoted per bed in most student buildings and per unit almost everywhere else. Cornell's 2026-27 residence hall double costs [$13,744 for the year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included. Spread over 12 months that is $1,145 a month, or $1,374 over 10.

Say a four-person group finds a 4-bedroom listed at "$5,000" with no unit stated. Per unit, that is $1,250 each, close to Ithaca Renting's $1,246.25 median per bed. Per bed, each of them would owe four times that median. One email to the landlord settles it.

## Check the listing before you pay

For a City of Ithaca address, run it through the [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) that Off-Campus Living and the City Building Division run. It shows sprinklers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and construction type, updated daily. It covers City rentals only and rates fire safety, not the landlord. For the Certificate of Compliance, Cornell's listings page points you to the Building Division at (607) 274-6508 or ibd@cityofithaca.org.

Then check the money. The [deposit plus any rent paid in advance cannot exceed one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108).

## Common mistakes

- **Quoting a per-unit price as per person.** At Ithaca Renting's medians, a 4-bedroom share is $1,246.25 a month and a 1-bedroom alone is $2,025: confusing the two moves your budget by hundreds a month.
- **Trusting the headline price.** 407 College Ave advertises $995, but its range runs to $2,890 and most units say "Call For Price."
- **Chasing a stale listing.** A unit whose start date has passed, or a post with a 555 number, wastes days in a market where 14 of Ithaca Renting's 67 Collegetown rows were sold out by November 8.
- **Skipping the City check.** Listings in the Town of Ithaca, Lansing and Cayuga Heights fall outside the Certificate of Compliance rule and the safety tool; screen those yourself.
- **Paying a banned fee.** A non-refundable application fee is illegal in New York, and a deposit over one month's rent is above the cap.
- **Missing the lease dates.** A lease ending May 31 leaves two summer months unhoused; most Ithaca leases end [July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing).

## FAQ

**Can I see the Cornell marketplace before I have a NetID?** You need to prove Cornell affiliation to see listings and contact landlords. For the email list, forward your acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu, per Cornell's [listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources).

**Can a sophomore sign a lease found on these sites?** For junior year, yes. For the current sophomore year, only with an approved exemption under Cornell's [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).

**Does Cornell recommend any landlord?** No. Cornell does not endorse any listed landlord or site, and Off-Campus Living staff told the Sun in 2019 it does not represent students in landlord disputes.

**Where do grad students find quieter places?** The same marketplace, plus Fall Creek and downtown; the [grad guide](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html) puts Fall Creek about 15 minutes by bus from Hollister Hall and Lansing about 20.

**Is Facebook or Craigslist safe to use?** The grad guide names Facebook groups, Craigslist and Apartments.com as places to look. None of them checks the Certificate of Compliance, so run the address through the safety tool yourself.
