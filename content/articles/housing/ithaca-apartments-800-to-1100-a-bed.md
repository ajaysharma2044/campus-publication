A bed for $800 to $1,100 a month in Ithaca means sharing. In Collegetown, that buys a bed in a five- to ten-bedroom apartment or a room in a shared suite. Downtown, it buys a share of a two- or three-bedroom. Every price in this band costs less over a 12-month lease than a Cornell double ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What does $800 to $1,100 a bed get you in Collegetown?

Only the big shares and the suite rooms fit; smaller layouts cost more per person. At Ithaca Renting, the median four-bedroom bed is $1,246.25 a month for 2027-28 and the median two-bedroom bed is $1,377.50. The full price list by unit size is in our [Collegetown rent per bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/).

Ithaca Renting prices are per bed, per month, for a one-year lease. Heat and hot water are included at every one of its buildings. Tenants pay their own electricity, except in the shared suites on floors 2 and 3 at 210 Dryden Road and in apartments rented room by room. Its IRIS internet service costs extra ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

These are 2026-27 prices. Ithaca Renting has posted only one downtown layout for 2027-28, a Commons West studio, so ask the rental office for next year's rate before you plan around any unit above (Ithaca Renting).

Heat, water and trash removal are included at every downtown building. Electricity is included only at Whiton Hall, the Colonial Building and Commons Studios, and Whiton Hall adds Wi-Fi. Compare a Whiton Hall room against a cheaper bed plus its power bill.

The trade is distance. Cornell puts downtown and the Commons 0.5 to 1.5 miles from campus, a 20- to 30-minute uphill walk. Collegetown is 0.1 to 0.5 mile away, up to a 15-minute walk ([Cornell neighborhoods guide](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)). For how the neighborhoods compare beyond price, see [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown Ithaca](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/).

## What about Fall Creek and Cornell Heights?

North of campus, the band mostly buys a room in a shared house, with utilities included in some listings. Fall Creek sits 0.5 to 1 mile from campus, a 20- to 30-minute uphill walk with frequent buses. Cornell Heights is up to a 30-minute level walk (Cornell neighborhoods guide).

- **117 Thurston Ave.:** a three-bedroom at $815 per person, with the lease starting Aug. 1, 2027. Heat and Wi-Fi are included ([IthacaRents](https://www.ithacarents.com/4312-spacious-3-bedroom-in-historical-cornell-heights)).
- **410 University Ave.:** single bedrooms in a five-bedroom house at $850, with electricity, gas, internet and water included (Ithaca College marketplace).
- **832 N. Aurora St.:** a room in a four-bedroom at $875, with electricity, heat, internet and water included (Ithaca College marketplace).
- **Kelvin Place Apartments, 219 Kelvin Place:** rooms listed at $795 to $825, with all utilities, laundry and parking included ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/kelvin-place-apartments-237474)).

Ask each landlord which lease year the price is for.

## Is $800 to $1,100 a bed cheaper than a Cornell dorm?

Yes, on rent alone, across the whole band. A residence hall double costs $13,744 for the 2026-27 academic year, or $6,872 per semester, with utilities and internet included (Cornell housing rates). Spread over a year, that is $1,145.33 a month, above every price in the band.

The gap shrinks fast at the top of the band. A $1,100 bed saves $544 over a year ($13,744 minus $13,200), and the double includes electricity while most of these leases do not. At $800, the saving is $4,144 (Cornell housing rates).

Summer cuts the other way. Most of these leases run 12 months. If you find a subtenant and pay only 10 months yourself, the break-even with the double rises to $1,374.40 a month (the double's cost divided by 10, from [Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What costs come on top of the rent?

Plan for electricity, internet, a deposit and, from downtown, the bus. Which ones you pay depends on the building.

New York caps a security deposit, plus any rent paid in advance, at one month's rent ([N.Y. General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). In a shared apartment, that cap applies to the whole unit's rent, so the landlord can split the deposit among housemates. Cornell financial aid does not cover security deposits or first and last month's rent ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)), so that money has to come from savings.

Most Ithaca group leases are "joint and several." That means each housemate is responsible for the whole rent and any damage, not just their own share ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)). A cheap bed in a six-bedroom is only cheap if all six people pay.

The bus costs $1.50 a ride. Registered Cornell students ride TCAT free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day Saturday and Sunday, and new students ride free for their first year ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes)). Say you live downtown and ride up the hill and back each weekday morning and afternoon. That is $3.00 a day (two rides at $1.50), or $15.00 for a five-day week.

## When do beds in this range get signed?

The big Collegetown shares in this band sign first. On Nov. 10, 2026, 14 of the 67 rows on [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) were already sold out for 2027-28. ## FAQ

**Is a listed price per bed or per apartment?**
Ithaca Renting says its prices are per bed. At other landlords, ask in writing whether a price is per bed or per apartment before you split the rent with housemates.

**Can I find a single room in this range without a group?**
Yes. Rooms in shared suites at 210 Dryden Road and Collegetown Plaza, the rooms at 301 Dryden Road and Whiton Hall downtown are all rented one person at a time. Room-by-room rentals at Ithaca Renting include electricity.
