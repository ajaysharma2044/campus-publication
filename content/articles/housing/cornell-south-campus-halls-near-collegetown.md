Jeanelle Wu wanted a double on West Campus for her sophomore year, where most of her classmates were headed. Cornell put her somewhere else: a double with no roommate in Sheldon Court, a 1903 building above Collegetown Bagels on College Avenue. She was let down at first. By April 2026 she was telling other students to choose it on purpose ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court)).

Wu wrote that she could find little about the building before she moved in. South Campus is Cornell's name for a small set of residence halls for juniors, seniors and transfers, most of them in or near Collegetown, the student neighborhood just south of campus. These halls are Cornell housing in every way that counts. You sign a license, not a lease. You carry a meal plan, and the bill goes to your Cornell account. But the buildings sit on the same blocks as the apartments students sign for a year ahead. South Campus is how a Cornell student lives in Collegetown without a landlord.

The four older halls hold 675 students between them, and 301 College Avenue adds Cornell-run apartments. A double costs $6,872 per semester for 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)), and the meal plan comes on top. Against that sits Collegetown's private market, where a bed in a shared apartment is quoted by the month and the lease runs a full year.

## Seven buildings, one name

Cornell's housing office says its "four upper-level residence halls" are on South Campus, then lists seven buildings under that heading ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduate-campus-housing)). The list is the better guide. It names Cascadilla Hall, 301 College Avenue, 112 Edgemoor, Schuyler House, Sheldon Court, 109 McGraw Place and 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive.

Four of them are the old core. Cascadilla Hall and Sheldon Court stand in Collegetown near the Schwartz Center for the Performing Arts. Schuyler House sits just below Collegetown, toward downtown Ithaca. 112 Edgemoor is a small house beside Cascadilla Gorge. The fifth, 301 College Avenue, is an apartment building Cornell bought in 2025. The last two sit across the gorge, by West Campus (Cornell Housing).

Every one of them is open to transfer and upper-level students ([Cornell residence halls](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls)). Cornell also places new transfer students in these halls, grouping them together when it can ([Cornell transfer housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)).

## Cascadilla Hall: older than the university

Cascadilla Hall began as something else. Samantha Nivison, the first woman to practice medicine in Tompkins County, led a plan to build a "water cure" sanitarium there and to train women doctors. Ezra Cornell invested. When the money ran out, he bought out the other investors and finished the building ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2013/09/19/ezras-oracle-sept-20-2013)). That made it the first building of the university he was founding. The same column notes that Andrew Dickson White, Cornell's first president, thought very little of it, calling it something close to a smelly poorhouse.

Cornell dates the building to 1864. It took in students and faculty when the university opened in fall 1868 and became Cornell's first residence hall when ownership passed to the university in June 1869 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/cascadilla-hall)). Ithaca was not ready for that first class of 412 students. Most of them roomed downtown and climbed to campus through the city cemetery ([Cornellians](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/article/collegetown-history)).

Today Cascadilla is the largest South Campus hall, with 366 residents. Rooms are singles and doubles along corridors, and the floors are mixed gender. There are lounges, study areas, a kitchen, a piano room and laundry. Cornell pitches it to students who want a "calm, self-directed" place rather than constant activity (Cornell Housing).

Cascadilla also runs the Service Center for the Collegetown-area halls. Your mail, your packages and your move-in keys come through this one building.

## Sheldon Court: "Cornell's First Dormitory" over the bagel shop

Sheldon Court opened in 1903. Charles L. Sheldon Jr., Class of 1901, ran it and advertised it as "Cornell's First Dormitory," a claim Cascadilla could dispute. Cornell bought it in 1955 and kept the name ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/sheldon-court)). Part of the ground floor held the Triangle Bookshop, which Sheldon founded, until the university bought the building in the 1950s and the shop moved across the street ([Cornellians](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/article/collegetown-history)).

The building holds 164 residents in singles and doubles. It has a TV lounge with a pool table, study lounges, kitchens, laundry and a piano room (Cornell Housing).

Wu's account in the Sun covers daily life there:

- **Rooms:** many residents end up with a double to themselves, because others switch out. Her double felt small for two, and corner rooms are bigger.
- **Noise:** walls between rooms block sound, but hallway doors do not. Lower rooms facing College Avenue are loud by day and late at night.
- **Bathrooms:** each floor's bathrooms have three showers, three sinks and three toilets. She found them clean and never crowded.
- **Elevator:** Sheldon has one, unlike the West Campus Gothic halls, which helps on move-in day. It breaks down often, so she suggests asking for a lower floor.
- **Food and buses:** there is no dining hall on South Campus. The nearest is about a 12-minute walk away on West Campus. The College and Oak bus stop is across the street.

Sheldon also turns up as a leftover room. During fall 2025 room selection, a sophomore transfer with a 7:30 p.m. slot on the last day found only Clara Dickson Hall triples and Sheldon Court doubles left ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)). He told the Sun a lease did not make sense for him that year.

## Schuyler House and 112 Edgemoor: the quiet edges

Schuyler House was built in 1910 and opened as a residence hall in 1981. It is named for George W. Schuyler, Cornell's first treasurer ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house)). It sits between Collegetown and downtown Ithaca, so it is a longer walk to class than the Collegetown halls. Cornell puts the walk to Central Campus at 15 to 20 minutes. TCAT buses stop at the door three to 11 times an hour, and the ride takes about five minutes.

To make up for the distance, every Schuyler resident gets a free TCAT bus pass. That is worth up to $200 a year, the price of Cornell's annual OmniRide pass ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). Other students past their first year ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend, and pay $1.50 a ride on weekday daytime trips.

Schuyler has 125 residents. Most live in singles, with some doubles and a few triples, on corridors with shared bathrooms. Each floor has a kitchen, and the building has lounges, TV rooms, a study room and laundry (Cornell Housing).

The building's distance has costs beyond the walk. In August 2023, Cornell closed Schuyler's mailroom and sent residents to Cascadilla for packages ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2023/08/schuyler-house-service-center-closed-after-move-in-residents-directed-to-cascadilla-hall)). A Cornell official said too few packages arrived at Schuyler to justify the desk. Residents told the Sun the walk to Cascadilla took about 10 minutes each way, uphill. Others worried about carrying large packages on those hills in winter. Cornell's Schuyler page still sends mail to 115 Cascadilla Place.

112 Edgemoor is Cornell's smallest South Campus hall. It houses 20 students, all in single rooms, with shared single-sex bathrooms, a kitchen and coin-operated laundry ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/112-edgemoor)). It sits in a quiet neighborhood beside Cascadilla Gorge, between West Campus and South Campus. Its mail goes through the Willard Straight Hall Service Center, not Cascadilla.

Beginning in 2027, 112 Edgemoor becomes home to Veterans House, a program house for students who want to support the military community. A program house is a themed hall built around a shared interest. Veterans House has its own extra application during room selection, so the building will fill through that process (Cornell Housing).

## 301 College Avenue: the building Cornell bought out of foreclosure

301 College Avenue had a short, rough history as a private building. Developers John Novarr and Phil Proujansky bought it for $15 million in June 2017. Tompkins Community Bank foreclosed on it in February 2024 over unpaid mortgage payments. Cornell then bought it for the same price in March 2025, according to a Tompkins County deed filing ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/03/cornell-acquires-301-college-avenue-for-15-million-in-collegetown)). At the time, the building had 26 apartments, from studios to six bedrooms, and a Jason's Deli convenience store. A Cornell spokesperson said the goal was to help revive Collegetown's economy.

That aim has roots. In 2007, Ithaca's Common Council adopted a Collegetown Vision Statement to guide the area's growth, and Cornell put in $90,000 toward the planning. Cornell also wrote Collegetown into its own campus master plan as one of its districts (The Cornell Daily Sun).

Six months after the purchase, Cornell put the building into room selection for 2026-27. Its availability report listed 12 apartment units, from a one-person studio to six-bedroom apartments ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/301-college-avenue-available-for-student-housing-in-2026-2027-academic-year)). Ryan Lombardi, vice president for Student and Campus Life, said Cornell planned to fold it into its housing.

It opened in August 2026 with 43 students while work went on around them ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)). Cornell expects it to reach its full 105 residents by fall 2027. Before opening, Cornell replaced flooring, paint and furniture on the ground and second floors. It also added a new sprinkler and fire alarm system, card access and new locks. Jason's closed around the time students arrived.

Resident Eleanor McNamara, Class of 2028, told the Sun she liked apartment living and her room. She also found a dirty unit and a refrigerator running at about 55 degrees, well above the 40 degrees food safety requires. Her suitemate's repair request got it fixed a few days later. Her kitchen had no stove. The lobby was unfinished, and construction outside started at 7:30 a.m. about six days a week. An anonymous resident advisor said the building opened "a little bit prematurely," with one live-in RA and early trouble with keys (The Cornell Daily Sun).

On paper, each unit has a kitchenette with a fridge and microwave, plus shared laundry ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue)). Cornell's housing license says the building has full-size refrigerators ([Cornell housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). Residents still pay for a meal plan, even with a kitchen.

## The West Campus edge: 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive and 109 McGraw Place

Two South Campus buildings are nowhere near Collegetown. 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive sits at the base of Libe Slope, steps from Noyes Community Recreation Center and the West Campus houses ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/104-mary-ann-wood-drive)). It has doubles on corridors, with women on the first floor and men on the second. It got new flooring, updated bathrooms and common areas renovated in 2026. Its rooms have no carpet. For food, William Keeton House, 104West! and Okenshields are close by.

109 McGraw Place was built in 1965 as a fraternity house and has new flooring, windows and heating. It is closed for the 2026-27 year ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates/residence-halls/109-mcgraw-place)). Cornell has not said whether it reopens for 2027-28, so check the bed availability report before you count on it.

Cornell's dining terms also group the Equity & Engagement Community with South Campus ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)). That program house is at 2 Forest Park Lane, near West Campus ([Cornell move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-equity-and-engagement-community)). You reach it through program house selection, not South Campus selection.

## Why Cornell keeps dorms in Collegetown

The pressure on these halls comes from Cornell's own rules. Starting in fall 2022, Cornell required sophomores, not just first-years, to live in university or affiliated housing ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/)). The North Campus expansion made room for that, with about 800 new beds for sophomores and 1,200 for first-years. Tim Blair, executive director of housing and residential life in 2021, called the rule part of building a "cohesive community."

That rule fills campus beds with first- and second-years. Juniors and seniors come after. Cornell says plainly that on-campus housing "is not guaranteed beyond the first two years," and in fall 2025 it changed selection so rising juniors and seniors could no longer keep the same room. It also let them cancel before move-in, so it could pull students off the waitlist ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)). One rising senior told the Sun the process felt more competitive than in past years.

South Campus is where that squeeze lands. The halls are built for upper-level students who, in Cornell's words, "have found their place" and want a quieter, more independent home. The Sun counts 301 College Avenue as Cornell's fourth Collegetown residence. It adds apartment-style rooms, the kind students otherwise rent from private landlords. For how that private market works by neighborhood, see [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/).

## What a year costs

A South Campus room costs the same as any Cornell residence hall room of its type: Cornell posts one rate table for its residence halls, program houses and West Campus houses, with no separate South Campus prices. Rates include gas, electricity, garbage pickup and internet up to 200 GB a month, and there is no security deposit. Undergraduates are billed in August for fall and in December for spring (Cornell Housing rates).

Source: Cornell Housing rates and Cornell Dining undergraduate meal plans, 2026-27.

The meal plan is where South Campus differs from the rest of campus. Residents can take the Collegetown Meal Plan, formerly called the South Campus Meal Plan. It costs $3,528 per semester for 2026-27. It gives up to 10 meals a week in the 10 dining rooms, $500 in Big Red Bucks and $436 in City Bucks each semester, and four guest meals ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Big Red Bucks are Cornell's tax-free dining dollars. City Bucks are a prepaid balance for food at off-campus restaurants. A $50 administrative fee is added once a year.

A full year in a South Campus double costs $13,744 for the room, $7,056 for the Collegetown plan and the fee, or $20,850. A single comes to $22,672 the same way. The Unlimited plan costs $7,604, so taking it instead of the Collegetown plan raises a double to $21,398. The Collegetown plan saves $548 and trades unlimited swipes for 10 a week plus City Bucks (Cornell Dining).

Compare that with a Collegetown lease. A bed in a four-bedroom apartment rents for a median of $1,246.25 a month at Ithaca Renting for 2027-28, or $14,955 over a 12-month lease ([EduExplained cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). That rent covers no food. Most of those units also bill electricity to the tenant. The four-bedroom share costs $5,895 less than the South Campus double with its plan. It wins only if a year of groceries and electricity costs you less than that, or $491.25 a month. A six-bedroom share at the $800 median, $9,600 a year, leaves far more room. For the full comparison, read [Is off-campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/) and the [Collegetown rent price guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/).

The comparison is not only money. The 2026-27 Cornell license ran from move-in in mid-August to May 23, 2027 ([Cornell licenses](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses)), while the lease math above counts a full year of rent. A South Campus room comes with furniture, a resident advisor and Cornell maintenance. As 301 College Avenue showed, that last one does not always mean fast.

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
The cheapest South Campus bed is a Schuyler House triple at $6,322 per semester. Schuyler's free bus pass also saves you up to $200 a year in fares. Pair the room with the Collegetown plan rather than Unlimited and you save another $548. Cornell's Unlimited plan is the one financial aid fully covers for eligible students, so ask the aid office how your package treats the Collegetown plan before you switch.
```

## Eating without a dining hall

No South Campus building has its own dining room. Sheldon Court's nearest dining hall is about 12 minutes away on foot, on West Campus. Residents of 301 College Avenue walk to West Campus or Okenshields to eat ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)). Most halls offer shared kitchens instead.

Cornell's housing license requires a meal plan of every student in undergraduate campus housing. When you sign for a South Campus room, Cornell enrolls you in the Unlimited plan by default. You can then switch to the Collegetown plan if you qualify. Once you hold the Collegetown plan as a South Campus resident, you cannot cancel it. If you move to other campus housing mid-year, you must pick a comparable plan ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

One line in the dining terms ties the South Campus meal plan rule to students who entered before fall 2021. The housing license has no such limit, and residents of 301 College Avenue told the Sun they had to enroll. Plan on a meal plan (Cornell housing license).

You can reduce a meal plan, if you are eligible, until Sept. 9, 2026, for fall and Feb. 3, 2027, for spring. You can add or upgrade at any time. Changes are prorated by the week, with no penalty (Cornell Dining terms).

## Getting a room

Juniors and seniors pick rooms in the fall for the following year. For 2027-28, the application ran from Sept. 1 to Sept. 27, 2026 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)). Cornell treats "South Campus Residence Halls and 301 College Avenue Apartments" as one area. Students already living there had first pick in Same Area selection on Oct. 6 and 7. Everyone else could choose from what was left in Open Room selection, starting Oct. 8. After that, a waitlist opened for students without a room.

```figure

rows:

  - {label: "Get a random timeslot by email on Sept. 30"}
  - {label: "Current South Campus residents choose in Same Area selection, Oct. 6 to 7"}
  - {label: "Everyone else chooses in Open Room selection, from Oct. 8"}

```

Source: Cornell Housing, Fall Housing Selection for Rising Juniors and Seniors.

Timeslots are random, and Cornell says blocking with friends gives no advantage or disadvantage. A room is held for four minutes while you assign beds. Signing the license makes it binding. A student who missed the Sept. 27 deadline gets no timeslot and goes straight to the waitlist (Cornell Housing).

```reader "If you're a sophomore"
You pick next year's room in the spring, not the fall. Sophomores do land on South Campus: Wu lived in Sheldon Court as one. For 2027-28, the rising sophomore application opens Feb. 1 and closes April 4, 2027, and open selection runs April 13 to 16 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). You cannot cancel your second-year license except in a few cases, so pick a South Campus room only if you would live in it.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
You must live on campus for one academic year. A spring transfer stays for that spring and the following year. If you enter as a junior or senior, or had two years of campus housing at your last school, the requirement does not apply to you.
```

## Moving in and out

All South Campus check-ins run through the Cascadilla Service Center. For 2026, it was closed Aug. 17, open 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. from Aug. 18 to 22, and open 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Aug. 23 ([Cornell move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)). Your reserved timeslot covers arrival and check-in, not unloading. Students who arrive after 7 p.m. call the number posted at their building to get in and pick up keys the next day.

Each building unloads differently:

- **Cascadilla Hall:** unload at the Eddygate entrance, then the driver moves the car to the Williams Street lot.
- **Sheldon Court:** unload through the Dryden Road Garage entrance at 114 Dryden Road, then move to the Williams Street lot ([Cornell move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-sheldon-court)).
- **Schuyler House:** unload at the building, then park in the spaces farthest from the door, with no overnight parking ([Cornell move-in instructions](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-schuyler-house)).

For move-out, Cornell warns there is no parking between the gorge and Cascadilla Hall, and fire lanes are off limits. It points Cascadilla and Sheldon residents to the City of Ithaca garage just south of Cascadilla and the Williams Street Garage at Williams Street and Stewart Avenue ([Cornell loading information](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/important-loading-and-parking-information)). Sheldon has a few spots at its loading dock. Our [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) covers the rest of campus.

```reader "If you're bringing a car"
The move-in parking above is for unloading day only. Line up a year-round spot before you bring a car. Our guide to [Collegetown and downtown parking](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/) covers the city garages and permits.
```

```reader "If you need air conditioning or an elevator"
Five of the halls have no air-conditioned rooms (see the list under the building table). At Sheldon, ask for a lower floor, since its elevator breaks down often. Rising sophomores with accommodations approved by Student Disability Services by April 5, 2027, get a separate room selection on April 12.
```

## Changing your mind: cancelling the license

A Cornell housing license is not a lease. It is a revocable permission to occupy a room, and it creates no landlord-tenant relationship ([Cornell housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). It binds you from the moment you accept the room in the portal.

Cornell changed its cancellation policy for licenses starting August 2026 ([Cornell cancellation policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-license-cancellation-policy)). Returning juniors and seniors can now cancel before they check in, for a fee that rises through the year. First-years, new transfers and second-years cannot cancel unless they leave Cornell, win an exemption or (for second-years) get into a Greek house, affiliated house or co-op in time.

Source: Cornell housing license 2027-28; half-fall-rate amounts use 2026-27 rates, since 2027-28 rates are final in March 2027.

That schedule makes a South Campus room a workable backup. A junior who holds a room while hunting for a lease pays the lowest fee in the table to drop it through Jan. 31. If you fail to check in by the first day of classes without arranging a late arrival, Cornell cancels the room and bills you half the fall rate (Cornell housing license).

## What comes next

South Campus will look different in fall 2027. 301 College Avenue is expected to be full, with 105 residents instead of 43. 112 Edgemoor becomes Veterans House. 109 McGraw Place, closed for 2026-27, has no announced plan. And the rates themselves are not final. The 2027-28 prices shown at selection are projections, and Cornell posts final rates in March 2027 (Cornell housing license).

Two open questions matter most for anyone choosing these halls. The first is whether 301 College Avenue's second year runs smoother than its first. The second is how many juniors and seniors Cornell can house at all, as it balances upper-level demand against incoming classes. If you want a South Campus room, the waitlist and next fall's selection are the places to watch. If you would rather cook for yourself, [see whether you can live off campus](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/) and compare costs before you sign anything.

## Before you pick a South Campus room

- Check the building's walk to your classes and to a dining hall. Schuyler is 15 to 20 minutes from Central Campus on foot.
- Decide whether you can live without air conditioning in the four historic halls.
- Price the Collegetown plan against Unlimited, and ask the financial aid office how each counts.
- At 301 College Avenue, ask Cornell what is still under construction and whether your unit has a stove.
- Know your cancellation deadline before you sign, and put Jan. 31 on your calendar if the room is a backup.
- Plan your mail: Cascadilla, Sheldon, Schuyler and 301 College Avenue packages all go through Cascadilla Hall.
