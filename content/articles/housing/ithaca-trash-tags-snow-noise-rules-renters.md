```glance
- Trash goes out no more than 24 hours before pickup; couches, mattresses and appliances may not be stored outdoors.
- Recycling is picked up every other week on your trash day; the county's 2026-2027 guidelines took effect July 1, 2026.
- Sidewalks must be cleared the full paved width within 24 hours after snow starts falling; the code puts it on the owner, and a lease can pass it to you.
- Night noise hours run 10 p.m. to 7:30 a.m.; a party must not disturb people 25 feet away, and outdoor amplified sound at night needs a permit filed 72 hours ahead.

```

If you rent in the City of Ithaca and trash is not in your lease, you buy a [$6.00 tag for every can or bag](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags) (2026 price) and set it out no more than [24 hours before pickup](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance). Sidewalk snow must be cleared within 24 hours after a snowfall begins, which the code puts on the owner unless your lease hands it to you. Quiet hours run [10 p.m. to 7:30 a.m.](https://ecode360.com/8391226), and the host of a party answers for noise heard 25 feet away.

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
You pay for tags only if trash is not included: a household setting out two bags a week spends $624 a year at $6 each (our arithmetic). Check your lease for snow removal before the first storm; the owner's duty under City code can be passed to tenants, and the City can clear the walk and bill at least $50 a plowing. The costliest mistake comes at move-out: a couch or mattress left at the curb breaks the City's outdoor storage rule, and trash still needs a tag per bag. Book a disposal run to the county center at 122 Commercial Ave instead.
```

## Who does what: owner, tenant, City

City code names the owner for most outdoor duties. Your lease decides how much of it lands on you. Cornell's [lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) lists "snow removal" as a term to find before you sign, and the same goes for trash.

| Duty | What the City requires | Who the code names | What to check in the lease |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trash | Tag on every can or bag; out no more than 24 hours before pickup | Owners and tenants (litter) | Is trash included, or do you buy tags? |
| Recycling | Every other week on the trash day | Not stated | Bins supplied, and where they are stored |
| Sidewalk snow | Full paved width within 24 hours after snow starts | Owner | Is snow removal passed to tenants? |
| Grass | No taller than 9 inches | Owner | Who mows a house lot |
| Parking | Designated spaces only, up to the number allowed | Not stated | How many spaces, and any fee |
| Noise | Night limits 10 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. | The person in charge of a party | Quiet-hours clause, guest rules |

The sources: trash, set-out, litter, snow, grass and parking rules are on the City's [exterior property maintenance page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance); noise is in [Chapter 240](https://ecode360.com/8391226).

Some student buildings fold these jobs into the rent. Ithaca Renting's [Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) lists trash as included at Collegetown Center, Plaza and Court, and its [downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) includes heat, water and trash. The [407 College Ave listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/407-college-ave-collegetown-s-best-kept-secret-237329) bundles recycling and snow removal.

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing your first lease"
Add two questions to your list before you sign: "Is trash included?" and "Who clears the sidewalk?" Cornell's lease checklist names snow removal as a lease term. If trash is not included, budget for tags: $6.00 each in 2026, and a four-person house putting out two bags a week spends $156 each a year (our arithmetic). Some leases charge a flat fee instead; the 209 Stewart Ave listing splits $100 a year five ways, $20 each. Neither cost is large, but a house lease that also passes on snow duty means shovels, salt and a rota from November on.
```

## Trash tags: price, weight and where to buy

From January 1, 2026, a City trash tag costs [$6.00, or $36.00 for a sheet of 6](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags). One tag covers one can or bag up to 35 lbs, and tag sales are final. The City's [collection page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/239/Trash-Collection) caps a container at 32 gallons and 70 lbs. The price is set for the year each January 1, so check it again in 2027.

## Setting trash out without a violation notice

The City's [exterior maintenance rules](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance) set four habits:

1. Put garbage out no more than 24 hours before pickup.
2. Store lidded containers at the side or rear of the house, not the front.
3. Tag every loose bag.
4. Keep appliances, couches and mattresses indoors. Owners and tenants are both responsible for litter on the property.

The City's Solid Waste group [issues violation notices](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/239/Trash-Collection) for trash, yard waste and recycling placed wrongly or left untagged. Not confirmed: the fine amounts, which sit in City Code 178-10.

Your pickup day comes from the City's Resident Services Search Tool, linked from the [tag page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags). Questions go to Streets and Facilities, 607-272-1718.

```reader "If you're a parent helping with move-in or move-out"
Bring the furniture problem home with you, not to the curb. The City bars storing couches, mattresses and appliances outdoors, and every trash bag needs a $6.00 tag in 2026. The county's Recycling and Solid Waste Center at 122 Commercial Ave is open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., and has required a high-visibility vest since August 1, 2026; its fees are not posted online. If you drive up for move-in, park only in the property's designated spaces, never on the lawn or sidewalk. From November 1, overnight street parking may switch to odd and even sides.
```

## Recycling: every other week, and what changed July 1, 2026

Recycling is collected [every other week on your trash day](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags). Tompkins County sets what goes in the bin, and its [2026-2027 Curbside Recycling Guidelines](http://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Recycling-Materials-Management) took effect July 1, 2026.

| Item | Curbside from July 1, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Paper cups | Accepted, empty, with straws and lids removed |
| Polystyrene (foam) cups | Not accepted |
| Food and beverage cartons | Not accepted |
| Food scraps | Not curbside; free drop-off at county Drop Spots |

The county's Recycling and Solid Waste Center is at [122 Commercial Ave](http://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Recycling-Materials-Management). The drop-off is open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.; the office is open weekdays, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Wear a high-visibility vest there, a rule since August 1, 2026. Not confirmed: the center's fees, which are not listed online. Call the county at (607) 273-6632 before you load the car. (The City's page lists 607-273-5700; the county's own page gives 273-6632.)

Yard waste runs April 1 to November 30, or until snow, on the weeks opposite recycling. It takes its own tags, $9 for a sheet of 6, and [no plastic bags](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/239/Trash-Collection): bundles up to 4 feet, branches under 2 inches, 50 lbs at most.

## Snow: the 24-hour sidewalk rule, and who the lease puts it on

City Code 178-3 makes property owners clear the adjacent sidewalk of snow and ice, the [full paved width, within 24 hours after a snowfall begins](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance). On a corner lot that includes the stretch to the curb and the ramps. FingerLakes1 reported the rule in effect in [February 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/02/14/ithaca-sidewalk-snow-rules-in-effect/).

The City's [snow FAQ](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/faq?cat=46) adds three points:

- Clear it again if a plow pushes snow back onto the walk.
- Where ice cannot be removed, sand or ashes are allowed until clearing is practical.
- The Superintendent of Public Works may clear the walk and bill the owner, at least $50 per plowing.

The code names the owner. A lease can still pass the shovel to you, which is why Cornell's [lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) lists snow removal as a term to find. If yours does, ask in writing who pays a City clearing bill.

Example: a lease passes snow duty to tenants, and a storm starts at 9 p.m. on a Friday before winter break. The walk must be clear by 9 p.m. Saturday. If everyone has left town, the City can clear it and bill the owner $50 or more. Check whether your lease lets the owner pass that bill on.

```reader "If you're an alum who owns or is buying an Ithaca rental"
The sidewalk is your duty under City Code 178-3: the full paved width within 24 hours after snow begins, re-cleared after plows, with the City able to clear it and bill at least $50 a plowing. You can pass it to tenants in the lease, but students leave for winter break, so plan who covers late December and January. You also answer for side or rear container storage, grass under 9 inches, and parking only in designated spaces. Including trash in the rent, as Ithaca Renting does downtown, removes the tag question from tenants.
```

## Noise hours, parties and the amplified-sound permit

City Chapter 240 sets daytime as [7:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. and nighttime as 10:00 p.m. to 7:30 a.m.](https://ecode360.com/8391226). Residential limits are 60 dBA by day and 50 dBA at night, under 2018 amendments.

| Rule (City of Ithaca) | Day | Night |
|---|---|---|
| Hours | 7:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. | 10:00 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. |
| Residential sound limit | 60 dBA | 50 dBA |
| Party noise | Must not disturb people 25 feet or more away | Same |
| Vehicle audio | Not plainly audible at 50 feet | Not plainly audible at 25 feet |
| Outdoor amplified sound | Permit page covers nights only | Permit needed, filed 72 hours ahead |

For a party, the [code](https://ecode360.com/8391259) puts the duty on the person in charge: keep it from disturbing people 25 feet or more away. Agree before the party which housemate that is.

Amplified sound heard outside between 10:00 p.m. and 7:30 a.m. needs a [City noise permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/528/Noise-Permit). Apply online at least 72 hours ahead; the page lists no fee. The permit does not excuse disorderly conduct under Penal Law 240.20.

Outside the city line the rules change. The Town of Ithaca has its [own noise ordinance](https://ecode360.com/8659282), with daytime running 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. City trash tags and the City snow code also stop at the line; see [City of Ithaca or not](/housing/city-of-ithaca-vs-town-lansing-cayuga-heights-renters/).

```reader "If you're a graduate student who wants quiet"
The City's night limit is 50 dBA from 10:00 p.m. to 7:30 a.m., and a party host must keep noise from disturbing people 25 feet away. Those rules give you something to cite, but they will not make a Collegetown block quiet. [GradCafe posters in 2014](https://forum.thegradcafe.com/topic/51153-cornell-english-phd-help-with-housing-pretty-please) called Collegetown loud and undergraduate and pointed to East Hill and downtown instead. Ask a landlord what share of tenants are undergrads before you sign. If you rent in the Town of Ithaca, its own ordinance applies, with daytime ending at 9:00 p.m.
```

## Parking at the house, and the winter street rules

On the property, park [only in designated spaces](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance), up to the number allowed for that property. Not on the grass, not on the sidewalk. Each violation carries a civil penalty for each day. Not confirmed: the penalty amounts in City Code 178-10.

On the street, the City's [winter rule](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/727/Parking-Regulations-Violations) runs November 1 to April 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m.: odd side on odd dates, even side on even dates. It is enforced when needed, such as snow events, and marked 24-hour streets are exempt. Alerts come through TC SIREN, or text "SIREN" to 67283. Year round, cars must move at least once every 24 hours on weekdays, and a car not moved in 72 hours may be towed at the owner's expense.

A [residential parking permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) costs $45 a year and runs to July 31. Renters in a permit area can get one with a current lease; landlords cannot get them for tenants.

For a move-in visit, City meters cost [$2.50 an hour](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/705/Parking-Information), 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Monday through Saturday, and are free on Sundays. The [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) at 118 Dryden Road charges $2.25 an hour with an $18.00 daily maximum.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving a couch or mattress at the curb at move-out.** The City bars storing them outdoors, and the Solid Waste group issues violation notices. Take it to the county center at 122 Commercial Ave instead.
- **Setting trash out the night before a holiday week.** More than 24 hours before pickup breaks the set-out rule.
- **Untagged bags.** Every can or bag needs a $6.00 tag in 2026, and untagged trash draws a violation notice.
- **Signing a house lease without reading the snow clause.** If the lease passes the duty, a missed storm can bring a City clearing bill of at least $50 per plowing.
- **Leaving for winter break with nobody on the shovel.** The 24-hour clock runs whether or not you are in Ithaca.
- **Outdoor music after 10 p.m. with no permit.** The permit needs 72 hours' notice, so a same-day party cannot get one.
- **Parking guests on the lawn.** Parking is limited to designated spaces, with a civil penalty per day.

## FAQ

**Do I need trash tags if my landlord includes trash?** No. Tags pay for City pickup, so a lease that includes trash, like Ithaca Renting's [downtown buildings](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), covers it. Some houses charge a yearly fee instead, such as $100 split among five tenants at [209 Stewart Ave](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095).

**Can I put a bag of trash in a recycling bin to skip a tag?** No. The Solid Waste group issues [violation notices](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/239/Trash-Collection) for wrongly placed or untagged trash and recycling.

**What do I do with a broken TV or old microwave?** Keep it indoors, since appliances may not be stored outdoors, and take it to the county center at 122 Commercial Ave. Call [(607) 273-6632](http://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Recycling-Materials-Management) first, because fees are not posted online.

**Who do I call about a missed pickup?** Streets and Facilities, 607-272-1718, listed on the City's [tag page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags).

**Do the City rules apply in Cayuga Heights or Lansing?** No. City tags, the City snow code and Chapter 240 stop at the city line, and the Town of Ithaca has its [own noise rules](https://ecode360.com/8659282).
