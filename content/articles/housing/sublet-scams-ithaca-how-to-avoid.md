```glance
- Never send money for a sublet until you have seen the unit and the landlord's written consent.
- A fake listing copies a real one at a lower rent and takes your deposit.
- A fake subtenant sends a check for too much and asks you to refund the extra.
- Report losses at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and tell your bank at once.
```

Do not send money for a sublet until you have seen the unit and the landlord has approved the sublet in writing. That one rule stops most scams. The Federal Trade Commission counted nearly 65,000 rental scam reports from January 2020 to June 2025, with about [$65 million lost and a median loss of $1,000](https://ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/12/rental-scams-hit-home-65-million-reported-losses).

## What do sublet scams look like?

Scams hit both sides of a sublet. A seeker loses a deposit to a fake listing. A tenant loses money to a fake subtenant.

| Scam | How it works | Your defense |
|---|---|---|
| Copied listing | A scammer copies a real listing, cuts the rent and collects deposits | View the unit in person; confirm with the landlord |
| Overpayment check | A "subtenant" sends a check for more than the rent and asks for a refund | Never refund before the check clears at your bank |
| Sublet without consent | A tenant takes your money for a unit the landlord never approved | Ask for the landlord's written consent |

Source note: [FTC](https://ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/12/rental-scams-hit-home-65-million-reported-losses); [University of Illinois Police](https://police.illinois.edu/fake-sublessee/); [Ithaca Times, 2010](https://www.ithaca.com/news/local_news/ithaca-landlords-tenants-battle-craigslist-rental-scams/article_fa700738-0769-5add-9329-897f05256e6d.html).

The [FTC found](https://ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/12/rental-scams-hit-home-65-million-reported-losses) that about half of reported rental scams in the 12 months to June 2025 began with a fake Facebook ad, and 16 percent began on Craigslist. People ages 18 to 29 were three times more likely to report losing money than older adults. That is the age of most Cornell students.

Ithaca has seen this before. In May 2010, scammers [copied local Craigslist listings with cut rents and took deposits](https://www.ithaca.com/news/local_news/ithaca-landlords-tenants-battle-craigslist-rental-scams/article_fa700738-0769-5add-9329-897f05256e6d.html). Landlords Association of Tompkins County president Herb Dwyer said newcomers from overseas were hit hardest. One landlord's listing was reposted at half its rent. The association set up Ithacarents.com for its own members.

The overpayment version is just as simple. In 2023 a student subletting at the University of Illinois [received a $3,850 check](https://police.illinois.edu/fake-sublessee/), refunded the "subtenant" to two accounts, and the check never cleared.

## How do you check a sublet before you pay?

```figure
kind: steps
title: Checking a sublet before you pay
rows:
  - {label: "Find the listing on Cornell's marketplace or the Off-Campus Living list"}
  - {label: "View the unit in person, or on a live video tour you control"}
  - {label: "Confirm the landlord's written consent with the landlord directly"}
  - {label: "Check the building with the City Building Division"}
  - {label: "Read the whole sublease, and pay only against a receipt"}
```

1. **Start where Cornell screens people.** Cornell limits its marketplace to people who prove Cornell affiliation, for security. Cornell also tells renters to [view properties in person and read the entire lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) before paying. The [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) covers the other channels.
2. **Ask who the landlord is, then call the landlord yourself.** A tenant in a building with four or more units needs the landlord's [written consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). Find the landlord's number from the building's own site, not from the sublet ad.
3. **Check the building.** Cornell names a City Building Department page for Certificate of Compliance property information. You can also call the City Building Division at 607-274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org. The Cornell and City [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) shows sprinklers, extinguishers and alarms for City rentals.
4. **Watch the money.** New York caps a landlord's security deposit and any rent paid in advance at one month's rent, and it bars a last month's rent on top of a deposit ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). The statute speaks of landlords, so ask the landlord before you pay a subtenant's deposit, and stop at any request above one month.
5. **Check the landlord's reputation.** The [landlord check guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-landlords-to-avoid-collegetown-reviews/) covers how to judge a landlord before you commit.
6. **Get a receipt for every payment.** The [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) explains what you can recover and when.

Red flags to stop on: a rent far below nearby listings, a "landlord" who is abroad and cannot show the unit, a request to pay by gift card or wire, and pressure to decide before you see anything.

## How do you protect yourself if you are the one subletting?

Screen replies before you share the lease or the address. Meet in person. Reject any check larger than the rent and any request to forward the difference. Wait until the funds clear at your bank, not until the bank shows a balance. Get the landlord's consent first, because a sublet without it is a substantial breach.

```reader "If you're an international student"
You cannot tour from abroad, so you rely on paper. Use Cornell's marketplace, get the landlord's written consent before you wire money, and keep a receipt. Cornell Graduate School staff say landlord disputes [do not affect your visa status or standing](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/), so ask for help early. The [guide for international students](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-international-students/) covers renting without a Social Security number and arriving in August.
```

## What do you do if you were scammed?

Call your bank first and ask it to stop or recall the payment. Report the loss at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Then call Off-Campus Living at [607-255-2310](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease), which gives lease advice but does not represent students in disputes. For a dispute with a real landlord, the Cornell Law tenants hotline answers questions at [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/).
