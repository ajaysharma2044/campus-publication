```glance
- The number to beat: $1,145 a person a month over 12 months ($1,374 over 10) equals Cornell's $13,744 double for 2026-27, utilities and internet included.
- A shared Collegetown 4BR costs about $1,285 to $1,373 a person a month all in, on a $1,246.25 median bed for 2027-28.
- A shared downtown 2BR costs about $1,081 to $1,236 a person a month, bus pass included, on a $992.50 median bed.
- Add-ons per person: electricity $17 to $280, internet $5 to $120, renters insurance $5 to $27, and water and sewer about $20 to $81 if the lease leaves it out, depending on how many share.
- The bus: free after 6 p.m. and on weekends after year one; otherwise $1.50 a ride or $200 a year for OmniRide.
- Trash tags cost $6 each from January 1, 2026, if trash is not in the rent; a car adds $359.77 a year for a Cornell B permit.
```

With rent, electricity, internet, insurance and the bus counted, a month costs about $1,285 to $1,373 a person in a shared Collegetown 4BR and $1,081 to $1,236 in a shared downtown 2BR (our arithmetic on [Ithaca Renting asking rents](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) read November 8, 2026). Compare that with $1,145, which is Cornell's [$13,744 double for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) spread over 12 months. Big shares (6BR and up, shared-suite rooms, downtown 3BR splits) come in under it; a 1BR alone costs about twice as much.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
You cannot sign for this year without an approved exemption. For junior year, settle group size first, because it moves the monthly number more than anything else. At Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians a 4BR bed is $1,246.25 a month and a 6BR bed is $800, a gap of $446.25 a month, or $5,355 a year. All in, the 4BR runs about $1,285 to $1,373 a person, $140 to $228 over the $1,145 break-even. The 6BR comes in $251 to $318 under. The mistake that costs most is a group that shrinks after signing: on most local leases each housemate is [jointly and severally liable](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) for the whole rent. Timing is on [its own page](/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying the rent"
Compare a 12-month lease with the double spread over 12 months ($1,145 a month), not with the academic year. A 12-month lease bills the summer, so a 4BR bed at the $1,246.25 median charges at least $2,492.50 for June and July that the [$13,744 double](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) never bills. Cornell aid is calculated the same way off campus and can refund living costs, but it does not cover the [security deposit, first and last month's rent, or rent in unregistered months](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). Plan to front about two months of rent at signing, since New York caps the [deposit plus any rent advance at one month](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Food is separate: Cornell's [2025-26 budget lists $7,604](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-to-attend) for it.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
The cheapest month in the sample is a bed in a big unit. A Collegetown 6BR at the $800 median runs about $827 to $894 a person all in; a downtown 3BR at the [$795 median](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) runs about $845 to $955. Both beat the $1,145 break-even by $190 or more a month. A co-op double is cheaper still: $6,974 to $9,962 for the 2026-27 academic year, on Cornell's rate page. Aid will not pay the deposit or the first month, and it does not cover summer on a 12-month lease, so save for both or line up a sublet. The most expensive mistake is a Collegetown 1BR alone: $2,025 a month at the median, $10,556 a year above the double before a single bill.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Cornell's 2026-27 Ithaca budget allows [$19,962 a year for housing](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students), which is $1,663.50 a month. A downtown 1BR at Ithaca Renting's $1,625 median comes to about $1,764 to $2,052 with electricity, internet and insurance, so most solo grads either share or look at Hasbrouck. Hasbrouck's [unfurnished 1BR is $1,415 a month](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included. PhD students and graduate assistants get a [free OmniRide pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes); master's and professional students pay $200 a year or $1.50 a ride after year one. Assuming you ride free like a PhD student is the common slip.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
You do not need a Social Security number to [rent an apartment or open a bank account](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Budget the signing money from home: New York caps the deposit plus any rent advance at one month's rent, so a 4BR bed at $1,246.25 means about $2,492.50 up front for the deposit and first month. Every new degree student rides TCAT free with a Cornell ID in year one, so a cheaper downtown room costs nothing extra to reach until the pass expires. Ask whether heat is in the rent before you sign from abroad. Ithaca Renting includes heat at its Collegetown and downtown buildings; a unit without it can push the electricity line toward the $280 upper bound.
```

```reader "If you're a student with a car"
Parking is the line that changes most. A Cornell [B commuter permit is $359.77 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students), about $30 a month, but it covers campus lots only. When the City set up residential permits in 1998, it [left out R-3 zones such as central Collegetown](https://news.cornell.edu/node/278473), so the overnight fallback there is the [Dryden Road Garage at $232 a month](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage), $2,784 a year with no guaranteed space. In permit zones outside central Collegetown, a City [residential permit is $45 a year](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance). In a shared unit, a garage permit adds more to the month than electricity, internet and insurance combined.
```

```reader "If you're a family"
Hasbrouck's family 2BR townhouse costs [$1,475 a month unfurnished or $1,530 furnished](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with utilities and internet in the rate. The private alternative is RentCafe's [Ithaca 2BR average of $2,459](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) (October 30, 2026), which comes to about $2,593 to $2,859 a month once a full household electricity bill and internet are added. Signing a $2,459 unit means about $4,918 up front for the first month and a one-month deposit. A spouse or partner with a CU ID can buy the OmniRide pass at 116 Maple Avenue. Budget for that pass or a second parking permit; families often forget the partner's commute.
```

```reader "If you're a first- or second-year with an exemption"
Your monthly numbers are the same as everyone else's, but only after Housing approves the exemption. Request it in the Housing application [during the open window](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), not after you sign a Cornell license; the 2026-27 deadline was April 6, 2026, and it lasts one year. Signing a lease first is the costly mistake: a student without approval is [billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) of Cornell housing, $13,744 for a double, on top of the rent. That adds the equivalent of $1,145 a month to whatever the lease costs.
```

## How much rent costs per bed

Rent in student buildings is quoted per bed per month, and it falls as the unit gets bigger. Ithaca Renting's [Collegetown list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), read on November 8, 2026, gives the cleanest sample: 38 priced rows for 2027-28, one landlord, asking rents. Its [downtown list](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) is mostly 2026-27 units, so treat those as leftovers that may be discounted.

At both, heat and hot water are in the rent and units come furnished. Electricity is usually the tenant's bill. Downtown, it is included only at the Colonial Building, Commons Studios and Whiton Hall. Trash is included at Collegetown Center, Plaza and Court and at the downtown buildings. Parking is extra.

These are the three bills a typical Ithaca Renting tenant pays on top of rent, plus water and sewer where a lease leaves them out. Each varies with the unit and the plan, so each comes as a range.

**Electricity.** EnergySage puts the [average Ithaca bill at about $280 a month](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/) at about 1,116 kWh (page updated November 1, 2026). That is a self-selected household average and probably includes homes with electric heat, so treat it as an upper bound. PowerOutage.us shows [$104.20 at 698 kWh](https://poweroutage.us/electricity-rates/ny/ithaca). Split four ways, that is $26 to $70 a person; split two ways, $52 to $140. NYSEG's [temporary rate increase took effect June 1, 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/05/15/nyseg-and-rge-win-temporary-rate-hikes-as-larger-increases-still-loom/), with a typical electric bill impact of 0.2%, and a larger permanent decision is still pending.

**Internet.** Aggregator listings give a [range of $30 to $120 a month](https://ispreports.org/internet-service-providers-ithaca-ny/), with Spectrum's cheapest plan about $30 for 100 Mbps. Many listings fold internet into the rent: the [709 N Tioga St listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/cosy-quiet-comfortable-fall-creek-gem.-238258) in Fall Creek has the landlord paying internet and water, while the tenant pays electric and buys garbage tags.

**Renters insurance.** Cornell names GradGuard as its [preferred insurer](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus), covering theft, fire, smoke and vandalism on and off campus. SafeButler collected [Ithaca quotes of $5 and $10 a month](https://www.safebutler.com/cheap-best-renters-insurance-ithaca-ny) for renters aged 18 to 24 (September 11, 2026). Insure.com's New York average is [$328 a year, about $27 a month](https://www.insure.com/renters-insurance/average-renters-insurance-cost-in-new-york/), for $40,000 of property and $300,000 of liability cover. The range is $5 to $27 a month.

**Water and sewer.** Ithaca Renting includes water downtown, but some leases do not. The City of Ithaca bills water and sewer quarterly at a combined [$20.18 per unit in 2026](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/293/Rates-Metering-Billing) ($10.83 water, $9.35 sewer), a unit being 100 cubic feet, or 748 gallons. The city's minimum-charge table, labeled 2025, sets a small meter's quarterly minimum at $242.16 for 12 units, about $80.72 a month, or $20.18 each for four people (our arithmetic).

## The bus and trash tags

**The bus.** New degree students ride TCAT free with a Cornell ID in their first year. After that, Cornell students ride free [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Daytime weekday rides have cost [$1.50 each](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/03/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free/) since non-first-years lost free daytime access in November 2024. The OmniRide pass is $200 a year, valid through August 31, and refundable through March 31 for a $40 fee.

At $1.50 a ride, the pass pays off after 134 rides. Two daytime rides every weekday reach that in about 13 to 14 weeks, less than a semester (our arithmetic). A Collegetown tenant who walks pays nothing. A downtown tenant faces a 20 to 30 minute [uphill walk](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods), so the pass is worth $16.67 a month spread over a year.

**Trash tags.** In the City of Ithaca, a tag costs [$6.00, or $36.00 for a sheet of six](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags), from January 1, 2026. Each can or bag up to 35 pounds needs one, and trash goes out weekly. A house putting out one bag a week uses four or five tags a month, $24 to $30, or $6 to $7.50 each for four people (our arithmetic). Some leases bill trash as a yearly fee instead: [209 Stewart Ave](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095) splits $20 of county solid waste and $80 of garbage a year among five tenants, $20 each.

## Two worked months

Both examples use the medians above and every add-on at its low and high end.

Example: four friends share a Collegetown 4BR for 2027-28 and walk to campus. Rent is $1,246.25 each, with heat included (and trash, in the Plaza buildings that make up three of the four 4BR rows). Electricity adds $26.05 to $70, internet $7.50 to $30, and insurance $5 to $27. The month comes to $1,284.80 to $1,373.25 each. Over 12 months that is $15,418 to $16,479, which is $1,674 to $2,735 more than the double.

Example: two grad students who are not PhD students share a downtown 2BR and buy OmniRide passes. Rent is $992.50 each, with heat, water and trash included. Electricity adds $52.10 to $140, internet $15 to $60, insurance $5 to $27, and the pass $16.67. The month comes to $1,081.27 to $1,236.17 each. Over 12 months that is $12,975 to $14,834, between $769 under and $1,090 over the double.

| Line, per person a month | Collegetown 4BR share | Downtown 2BR share |
|---|---|---|
| Rent (median per bed) | $1,246.25 (2027-28) | $992.50 (mostly 2026-27) |
| Electricity | $26.05 to $70 | $52.10 to $140 |
| Internet | $7.50 to $30 | $15 to $60 |
| Renters insurance | $5 to $27 | $5 to $27 |
| Bus | $0 (walk) | $16.67 (OmniRide) |
| Trash | included at Plaza; else about $6 to $7.50 in tags | included |
| Total | $1,284.80 to $1,373.25 | $1,081.27 to $1,236.17 |
| Against $1,145 | $140 to $228 more | $64 less to $91 more |

The downtown share is cheaper by about $137 to $204 a month, and the bus is what you trade for it. To run your own unit, rider status and car, use the calculator.

## What a car adds

| Parking option | Price | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell B commuter permit (campus lots only) | $359.77 a year | about $30 |
| Dryden Road Garage monthly permit | $232 a month | $232 |
| City residential permit (outside central Collegetown) | $45 a year | $3.75 |

The B permit is limited and sold first come, first served; it does not permit parking at a residence. Over 12 months the garage costs $2,784, 7.7 times a B permit (our arithmetic on the [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) and [Cornell permit](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students) prices).

For a shared 4BR, no, by $140 to $228 a month. For a 6BR, a shared-suite room or a downtown 3BR, yes. Students disagree on the trade. A [2021 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/) quoted one student who said her off-campus rent was cheaper than living on campus unless she lived in a triple, and another who called the required meal plan a dealbreaker, while a [2019 Sun columnist](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/21/pietsch-the-rent-is-too-damn-high/) called rent too damn high. The meal plan is the swing: it is required on campus, and off-campus students can drop to a [debit plan from $1,000 a year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans).

## Common mistakes

- **Comparing 12 months of rent with the academic-year double.** A 4BR bed at $1,246.25 charges $2,492.50 for June and July alone.
- **Leaving electricity out.** At the upper bound it adds $70 a person a month in a 4BR, $840 a year.
- **Taking a 1BR alone in Collegetown.** At the $2,025 median that is $24,300 a year, $10,556 more than the double.
- **Signing before an exemption is approved.** The student is billed $13,744 for a Cornell double on top of the rent.
- **Assuming the bus is free all day.** After year one, daytime rides are $1.50; 134 of them cost as much as the $200 pass.
- **Bringing a car to central Collegetown with no spot.** The garage costs $2,784 a year.
- **Counting on aid for move-in money.** The deposit and first month are not covered; on a $1,246.25 bed that is $2,492.50.

## FAQ

**Can I see past heating or electric bills before I sign?** Cornell suggests asking NYSEG at [(800) 572-1111](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) for a unit's average annual heating bills, especially for West Hill cottages.

**Where do I buy trash tags?** City Hall at 108 E. Green St., Ace Hardware, the Cornell Campus Store, GreenStar, P&C Fresh, Tops and [Wegmans](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags) all sell them; sales are final.

**Does financial aid send me money for rent?** Aid is calculated as it is on campus; the Bursar stops billing housing, and if aid exceeds Bursar charges, Cornell [refunds the difference](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) for living costs. You pay the landlord yourself.

**Will Ithaca rents be lower next year?** The latest figures show little movement. RentCafe's large-building Ithaca average was $2,263 on October 30, 2026, down 0.59% from $2,277, and Ithaca Renting says rents [may change until a lease is signed](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/).

**Is OmniRide worth it if I live in Collegetown?** Usually not. Evening and weekend rides are already free, so only daytime weekday trips count toward the 134-ride break-even.
