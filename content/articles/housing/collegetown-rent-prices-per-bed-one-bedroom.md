```glance
- A Collegetown bed for 2027-28: $800 a month in a 6BR, $2,025 for a 1BR alone.
- Only 5BR and larger units, and shared-suite rooms, beat Cornell's $13,744 double over 12 months.
- Smaller Collegetown landlords ask less for whole units: a median $1,650 for a one-bedroom.
- Big units go first: 14 of Ithaca Renting's 67 Collegetown rows were sold out by Nov. 8, 2026.
```

For 2027-28, a Collegetown bed gets cheaper as the unit gets bigger. At Ithaca Renting, which posts its full price list, the median is $800 a month for a bed in a six-bedroom, $1,246.25 in a four-bedroom and $2,025 for a one-bedroom alone ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Smaller landlords nearby ask less for whole studios and one-bedrooms.

## What does a bed in Collegetown cost for 2027-28, by unit size?

The price falls with every bedroom you add. Collegetown buildings quote rent "per bed," the monthly price each person pays for their own bedroom in a shared unit. A one-bedroom or studio has one bed, so its per-bed price is the whole rent.

The table uses [Ithaca Renting's](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) 38 priced Collegetown rows for 2027-28. The landlord prints each price as a range, so each row's midpoint stands for it. The median is the middle price in each group. Where a size has only two listings, the table gives their average instead.

| Unit size | Listings | Median per bed, a month | Range listed | 12 months of rent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | 7 | $1,765 | $1,565 to $1,950 | $21,180 |
| 1BR | 8 | $2,025 | $1,625 to $2,545 | $24,300 |
| 2BR | 5 | $1,377.50 | $1,145 to $1,495 | $16,530 |
| 3BR | 2 | $1,243.75 (average) | $1,185 to $1,345 | $14,925 |
| 4BR | 4 | $1,246.25 | $895 to $1,395 | $14,955 |
| 5BR | 2 | $1,102.50 (average) | $925 to $1,275 | $13,230 |
| 6BR | 5 | $800 | $695 to $1,120 | $9,600 |
| 10BR | 1 | $775 | $725 to $825 | $9,300 |
| Room in a shared suite | 4 | $895 | $695 to $995 | $10,740 |

Source: Ithaca Renting Collegetown price page, 2027-28 rows. Every row, midpoint and calculation is in the [Collegetown rent per bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/).

Group size moves the price more than the building does. Four friends who take a four-bedroom instead of two two-bedrooms save $131.25 each a month ($1,377.50 minus $1,246.25), or $1,575 a year. Six friends in a six-bedroom pay $446.25 less each than in the four-bedroom ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

The spread inside one size comes from the building and the finish level ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The cheapest one-bedrooms are Collegetown Court standard units at 208 Dryden Road, $1,625 to $1,725. The most expensive are Collegetown Center deluxe units at 151 Dryden Road, $2,445 to $2,545. A studio costs $260 less than a one-bedroom at the medians.

The cheapest beds on the list are the six-bedrooms at Aces Apartments on Eddy Street, from $695, and the standard rooms at 210 Dryden Road, $695 to $810. Electricity is included on the second and third floors of 210 Dryden Road, which makes those the lowest all-in prices on the list ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
Settle your group before you tour, because a group that shrinks after signing pays more per person. Dropping from a six-bedroom to a four-bedroom costs each person $5,355 a year at the medians. Sign only for junior year. A sophomore who moves off campus without an approved exemption "will still be billed for the full year" for campus housing ([Cornell residential requirements](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)).
```

## Is a Collegetown bed cheaper than a Cornell dorm?

Only in the biggest units. A residence hall double costs $13,744 for the 2026-27 academic year, with utilities and internet included ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Spread over a 12-month lease, that is $1,145.33 a month.

Source: Ithaca Renting Collegetown medians times 12; Cornell housing rates.

Every size from studio to four-bedroom costs more than the double over 12 months. The five-bedroom, six-bedroom, 10-bedroom and shared-suite rooms cost less. The gap narrows if you sublet the summer and count only 10 months of rent. Then the double works out to $1,374.40 a month, almost exactly the two-bedroom median. The comparison still favors the double on bills, since most Collegetown leases leave electricity to the tenant. The full comparison is in [Is off-campus housing cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).

```reader "If you're a parent paying the rent"
Price every option per person per year, then add electricity. Cornell aid does not cover security deposits, first and last month's rent, or rent in months your student is not registered ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)). New York caps a security deposit plus any rent paid in advance at one month's rent, so a landlord cannot demand first month, last month and a deposit ([N.Y. General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
```

## What do other Collegetown landlords charge?

Less, for whole studios and one-bedrooms. Smaller landlords listing 2027-28 units on Dryden Road, College Avenue, Linden Avenue, Stewart Avenue, Osmun Place and Ferris Place ask a median $1,650 for a one-bedroom and $1,260 for a studio. That is $375 and $505 a month below [Ithaca Renting's](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) medians.

| Unit size | Listings | Median per bed, a month | Range | Ithaca Renting median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | 5 | $1,260 | $1,150 to $2,190 | $1,765 |
| 1BR | 8 | $1,650 | $1,450 to $3,000 | $2,025 |
| 2BR, per person | 5 | $1,150 | $875 to $1,400 | $1,377.50 |
| 3BR, per person | 4 | $1,174.17 | $1,000 to $1,933.33 | $1,243.75 (average) |

Source: Asking rents listed by [CSP Management](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/PublicPages/ApartmentSearch.aspx) (116 Osmun Pl.; 400-404, 406 and 408 Stewart Ave.), [PPM Homes](https://www.ppmhomes.com/availability) (101 Dryden Rd.; 119 Stewart Ave.), [Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/) (205 Dryden Rd.), [210 Stewart Ave.](https://www.ithacarents.com/4525-210-stewart-avenue-2br-unit----2026/27), [215 College Ave.](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/215-college-ave/13334029) and [The Ivy](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/325-college-ave/13257148). Whole-unit rents are divided by the number of bedrooms; The Ivy's three-bedroom figure is the $5,500 to $5,800 a month its first leases carried, reported by [The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026), divided by three.

The sample is small, so read each row as a handful of real prices, not a market average. A few listings set the ends of each range. The cheapest studio is [CSP's](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/PublicPages/ApartmentSearch.aspx) Apartment 5 at 408 Stewart Ave., $1,150 a month. The dearest one-bedroom is at [The Ivy](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/325-college-ave/13257148), $3,000 a month, available Aug. 17. A one-bedroom at [215 College Ave.](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/215-college-ave/13334029) asks $2,550, available Aug. 15.

Big shared houses sit close to Ithaca Renting's prices. A six-bedroom house on Ferris Place asks [$5,100 a month](https://www.ithacarents.com/3331-beautiful-6-bdrm-house-near-cornell--downtown-ithaca) from Aug. 8, 2027, or $850 a person, with internet, heat, electricity and trash split by the housemates. An eight-bedroom at 114 Stewart Ave. asks [$900 a person](https://www.ithacarents.com/3763-114-stewart-avenue----2027/28-school-year-in-center-of-collegetown) from June 10, 2027. Four-bedrooms at 119 Stewart Ave. ($4,000 for the unit) and 211 Linden Ave. ($1,050 a bedroom) work out to $1,000 and $1,050 a person.

Compare what each price includes before you compare the price. [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) furnishes every unit and pays heat. Some cheaper listings, such as the two-bedrooms at 406 Stewart Ave. ([CSP Management](https://cspmgmt.managebuilding.com/Resident/PublicPages/ApartmentSearch.aspx)), leave all utilities to the tenant.

## How much does a one-bedroom cost in Collegetown?

Expect a price between the two medians above for 2027-28. The low end comes from small landlords, the high end from [Ithaca Renting's](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) newer buildings. Other one-bedrooms near campus cost less if you can live farther from the Engineering Quad.

RentCafe's figure runs higher for two reasons. It averages whole-unit rents from buildings with 50 or more units only, which leaves out the small landlords and houses that make up much of Collegetown ([RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/)). Its Collegetown one-bedroom, $2,342, is $317 above Ithaca Renting's median. Downtown is the cheaper swap at the same landlord. The median of [Ithaca Renting's downtown listings](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) is $1,625 for a one-bedroom, $1,395 a bed for a studio and $992.50 a bed for a two-bedroom. The [Collegetown vs downtown rent dataset](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/) has the full comparison. In Fall Creek, a one-bedroom at 709 N. Tioga St. lists at [$1,600 with internet and water included](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/cosy-quiet-comfortable-fall-creek-gem.-238258).

## What does The Ivy cost?

The Ivy, at 325 College Ave., sets the top of the market. Its 52 units opened with leases from Aug. 17, 2026, and its three-bedrooms rented for $5,500 to $5,800 a month ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)). Split three ways, that is $1,833.33 to $1,933.33 a person, or $22,000 to $23,200 a year. The cheapest unit the Sun reported was a basement two-bedroom at $1,595 a person, against a full price of $4,000.

An optional utility package at [The Ivy](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/325-college-ave/13257148) adds $150 a person a month.

## What does Collegetown rent include?

It depends on the landlord. Electricity is the bill most leases leave to you.

| Listing, 2027-28 | Included | You pay |
|---|---|---|
| Ithaca Renting, Collegetown | Furniture, heat, hot water; trash at Collegetown Court, Plaza and Center | Electricity at most units; parking |
| 116 Osmun Pl. (CSP Management) | Furniture, heat, electricity, internet, trash | Cooking gas in the studio and two-bedroom |
| 400-404 Stewart Ave. (CSP Management) | Internet, trash; one-bedrooms furnished | Electricity |
| Dryden South, 205 Dryden Rd., suites | Furniture, heat, hot water | Electricity, wifi |
| Six-bedroom house, Ferris Place | Furniture, in-home laundry | Internet, heat, electricity, trash, split by housemates |
| The Ivy, 325 College Ave. | Building amenities | Utilities, unless you buy the $150 package |
| Cornell double (2026-27) | Utilities and internet | Meal plan |

Source: landlord listings and Cornell housing rates, linked above.

The average Ithaca household electric bill is about [$280 a month](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/) (EnergySage). That average covers whole homes, so treat it as a ceiling for a student apartment. Split four ways, it is $70 a person. A four-bedroom bed at the median plus that $70 comes to $15,795 a year ($1,316.25 times 12), which is $2,051 more than the double.

Parking is extra at Ithaca Renting. A monthly permit at the city's Dryden Road Garage costs [$232](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) (City of Ithaca), or $2,784 a year. The rest of a monthly budget, from bus passes to trash tags, is in [What a month off campus costs at Cornell](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/).

## How fast do Collegetown units rent?

Fast, and the biggest units go first. By Nov. 8, 2026, 14 of the 67 rows on [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) (20.9%) were marked sold out for 2027-28, nine months before an August move-in. The sold-out rows included the eight-bedroom at 105 Dryden Court, the 14-bedroom at 214 Dryden Road and the Collegetown Plaza deluxe six-bedroom. At [Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/), all 10 four-bedroom suites for 2027-28 were marked rented by Nov. 10, 2026, at $1,360 to $1,575 a person. Only its two studios, at $1,990 and $2,190, were still open.

The prices in this guide are asking rents and can change until you sign. On Nov. 8, 2026, Ithaca Renting's home page listed the Collegetown Center deluxe studio at $1,935 to $2,035, while its Collegetown page listed $1,830 to $1,950. When to sign, and when waiting is fine, is covered in [When to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).

```reader "If you're a spring transfer student"
These 2027-28 prices are a guide, not your market. Spring transfers live on campus for their first spring semester and the following academic year ([Cornell transfer housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)). If you arrive in January 2027, your first Collegetown lease would start in 2028, and you would sign it during the fall 2027 rush.
```

## Have Collegetown rents gone up?

New luxury buildings have pushed the top of the market up. [RentCafe's](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/) Collegetown average fell 2.59% in the year to Oct. 30, 2026, to $2,815 from $2,890.

| Item | 2019 (for 2020-21 leases) | 2027-28 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ithaca Renting 3BR, per person | $1,160 | $1,192.50 (Collegetown Court) to $1,295 (705 E. Buffalo St.) | +2.8% to +11.6% |
| Most expensive 1BR | $2,445 (312 College Ave.) | $2,545 (Collegetown Center deluxe) | +4.1% |
| Most expensive 3BR, whole unit | $4,150 (312 College Ave.) | $5,500 to $5,800 (The Ivy, 2026-27) | +32.5% to +39.8% |

Source: [The Cornell Daily Sun, Sept. 19, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/); Ithaca Renting; The Cornell Daily Sun, December 2025. Not adjusted for inflation.

## FAQ

**Are these the prices people actually pay?**
They are asking rents, the prices landlords list before anyone signs. Signed Collegetown rents are not public, so ask each landlord for the final rent in writing before you sign.

**Is a price per person or for the whole unit?**
It varies by landlord. Ithaca Renting, Dryden South and The Ivy's bedroom prices are per person. CSP Management and most marketplace listings quote the whole unit. Ask before you split a price with roommates.

## What to do next

1. Agree on your group size and budget per person, with electricity added.
2. Check each landlord's price basis and what the rent includes, using the table above.
3. Look up the landlord's record in [Ithaca landlords to avoid](/housing/how-to-check-an-ithaca-landlords-record/) before you tour.
4. If a one-bedroom alone is over your budget, run the commute calculator above against a downtown or Fall Creek address.
