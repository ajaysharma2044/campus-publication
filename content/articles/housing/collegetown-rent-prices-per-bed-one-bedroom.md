```glance
- A one-bedroom alone in Collegetown: $2,025 a month at the median of one landlord's eight 2027-28 listings (range $1,625 to $2,545); RentCafe's large-building average is $2,342 (Oct 30, 2026).
- A bed gets cheaper as the unit grows: $1,377.50 in a 2BR, $1,246.25 in a 4BR, $800 in a 6BR, $895 for a room in a shared suite (2027-28 medians).
- Cornell's 2026-27 double costs $13,744 with utilities, or $1,145 a month over 12 months. Only 5BR and larger units and shared-suite rooms come in under it.
- The newest building, The Ivy, works out to $1,833 to $1,933 per person a month in a 3BR.
- Ithaca Renting's prices include heat and furniture; electricity and parking are extra. A Dryden Road Garage permit is $232 a month.
- On Nov 8, 2026, 14 of Ithaca Renting's 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 were already sold out.
```

A one-bedroom alone in Collegetown asks about $2,025 a month for 2027-28, the median of [Ithaca Renting's eight priced 1BR listings](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) on Nov 8, 2026. A bed in a big shared unit costs far less: $800 a month at the median for a 6BR at the same landlord. Aggregator averages run higher, up to [$2,342 for a Collegetown 1BR on RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/), because they count only large buildings.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
Pick your group size before you tour. It moves the rent more than the building does. At Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians, a 1BR alone is $2,025 a month, a 4BR share $1,246.25 and a 6BR share $800. Popular units sign in the fall of sophomore year, and the big houses go first: on Nov 8, 2026, 14 of 67 rows were sold out, including the 8BR at 105 Dryden Court and the 14BR at 214 Dryden Rd. The costly mistake is a group that shrinks after signing. Dropping from a 6BR to a 4BR costs each person $446.25 a month, or $5,355 a year. Do not sign a lease for your current sophomore year without an approved exemption: Cornell [still bills the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing).
```

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
Use the 2027-28 medians below as a check on your renewal offer. Compare like with like: a per-bed price against your share, not against the whole unit's rent. If your landlord quotes a 4BR share well above $1,246.25 or a 1BR well above $2,025, ask what the extra buys (newer building, electricity, parking). Check the lease end date too. Dryden South's leases [all end May 31, 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), two months before the usual July 31. A senior who graduates in May pays two fewer months there. For how much a landlord may raise the rent, see [the Good Cause guide](/housing/ithaca-good-cause-eviction-rent-increase-students/).
```

```reader "If you're a grad student who wants a one-bedroom"
Skip Collegetown unless the walk matters most. Its 1BR median is $2,025 a month at Ithaca Renting; the same landlord's downtown 1BR median is [$1,625](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/). A Fall Creek 1BR at 709 N Tioga St was listed at [$1,600 and $1,700](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/cosy-quiet-comfortable-fall-creek-gem.-238258), internet and water included. Cornell's Hasbrouck 1BR is [$1,415 a month unfurnished](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with utilities and internet in. Cornell's 2026-27 graduate budget allows [$19,962 a year for housing](https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students), which is $1,663.50 a month by our division. The mistake: comparing a bare rent with Hasbrouck's all-in rate.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying the rent"
Put every price on a year, per person. At Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians, a year costs $9,600 in a 6BR, $14,955 in a 4BR, $16,530 in a 2BR and $24,300 in a 1BR alone. The Ivy's 3BRs run $22,000 to $23,200. Cornell's double is [$13,744 for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with gas, electricity and internet included, so add electricity to every lease that excludes it. Cornell aid [does not cover the security deposit or first month's rent](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). New York caps the deposit plus any rent paid in advance at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), so refuse a demand for "first, last and deposit".
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
The number to beat is $1,145 a month, Cornell's double spread over 12 months. The cheapest Collegetown beds at Ithaca Renting for 2027-28 are Aces 6BR rooms from $695, the 10BR at 202 Dryden Rd at $725 to $825, and shared-suite rooms with a $895 median. Downtown, the same landlord's 3BR median is $795 a bed. A [2025 Sun columnist](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/maia-on-the-mic-the-collegetown-housing-hustle) put older Collegetown houses at about $800 to $1,300 per person, many in poor shape. The mistake: forgetting the extras. A 4BR share plus a $70 slice of electricity comes to $2,051 a year more than the double.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
A sophomore transfer cannot rent yet: new transfers [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) unless exempt. A junior or senior transfer is exempt but arrives after the Collegetown rush. By Nov 8, 2026, 14 of Ithaca Renting's 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 were gone. Look at leftovers instead. The same landlord's downtown 2BR median is $992.50 a bed and its 3BR $795. A Dryden South suite sublet for fall 2026 was listed at [$1,395 per person](https://www.apartments.com/dryden-south-sublets-available-fall-2026-ithaca-ny/yeje6qs/). Lansing West showed 1BR units available now at [$1,038 and $1,193](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx). [Free TCAT in your first year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) makes downtown and Lansing workable. The mistake is paying Collegetown prices for a picked-over unit.
```

## Per-bed or per-unit: why the numbers disagree

Collegetown rents are quoted two ways. Student buildings, Ithaca Renting among them, quote per bed per month. Aggregators such as RentCafe quote per unit per month. A 1BR is the one size where the two match, because one person rents the whole unit.

Even there the sources split. RentCafe put the Collegetown 1BR at $2,342 on Oct 30, 2026. Its separate [1BR listings page showed $2,396](https://www.rentcafe.com/1-bedroom-apartments-for-rent/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown) in October 2025. RentCafe's Ithaca-wide 1BR is [$2,063](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/).

Trust the per-bed list for a student lease. RentCafe draws on Yardi Matrix data from buildings with 50 or more units. That leaves out the small landlords and houses that make up much of Collegetown. Its square footages also look wrong: a 148 sq ft studio and a 550 sq ft 2BR suggest a small sample weighted to a few new buildings.

| RentCafe, Oct 30, 2026 | Collegetown rent a month | Collegetown sq ft | Ithaca-wide rent | Ithaca-wide sq ft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | $2,006 | 148 | $1,772 | 335 |
| 1BR | $2,342 | 433 | $2,063 | 638 |
| 2BR | $3,414 | 550 | $2,459 | 902 |
| All units | $2,815 | 375 | $2,263 | 724 |

On these figures Collegetown runs 13.5% above the Ithaca average for a 1BR and 38.8% above for a 2BR, by our arithmetic. RentCafe's Collegetown average fell 2.59% from $2,890 a year earlier.

Two other 2027-28 1BR asks fit the same band. A 1BR at 215 College Ave was listed at [$2,550 from Aug 15, 2027](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/215-college-ave/13334029). Example: four friends compare a 4BR with two 2BRs. At the medians, the 4BR is $1,246.25 each and a 2BR $1,377.50 each. The 4BR saves each person $131.25 a month, or $1,575 over 12 months. Six friends in a 6BR at $800 save $446.25 each against the 4BR.

Ithaca Renting's own pages disagree with each other. On the same day, its home page showed a Collegetown Center deluxe studio at $1,935 to $2,035, while the Collegetown page showed $1,830 to $1,950. Prices may change until you sign.

## The Ivy and the top of the market

The Ivy at 325 College Ave has 52 units, with leases from Aug 17, 2026. Nearly all were leased before construction finished, [the Sun reported](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026). Its 3BRs rent for $5,500 to $5,800 a month. Split three ways, that is $1,833 to $1,933 per person, or $22,000 to $23,200 a year: 1.60 to 1.69 times the Cornell double.

The cheapest unit was a basement 2BR at $1,595 per person, against a full price of $4,000. One student offered a 20% early-interest discount stayed in her current building. She was concerned that The Ivy's units were smaller than her current apartment and that she did not know what the interior would look like.

For a 2027 start, listings show [3BR bedrooms at $1,900 to $1,950](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/325-college-ave/13257148), about $300 more per bedroom on higher floors. An optional utility package adds $150 per person a month.

## What each price includes

A $100 gap in rent can vanish once you add what the lease leaves out.

| Listing | Price | Included | Not included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ithaca Renting, Collegetown (2027-28) | Per bed, table above | Heat, hot water, furniture; trash at Collegetown Center, Plaza and Court | Electricity at most units (included on floors 2 and 3 of 210 Dryden Rd and in room-rented units); parking |
| The Ivy, 325 College Ave | $1,833 to $1,933 per person (3BR) | Rooftop terrace, wellness center, study rooms | Utilities, unless you buy the $150 package |
| 209 Stewart Ave, 5BR | [$850 per room](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095), Aug 2026 to July 2027 | Heat, water, electricity, WiFi | $20 county solid waste and $80 garbage a year, split five ways |
| Dryden South, 205 Dryden Rd | $1,395 to $1,990 per person, 2026-27 | Heat, hot water, furniture | Electricity not listed as included |
| Cornell double, 2026-27 | $13,744 a year | Gas, electricity, garbage, internet | Meal plan |

Electricity is the big unknown. EnergySage puts the average Ithaca household bill at [$280 a month](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/), which likely includes electric heat. Treat it as a ceiling. Split four ways, that is $70 each. A 4BR share at the median plus that $70 comes to $15,795 a year, $2,051 above the double.

Parking is extra at Ithaca Renting. The City's Dryden Road Garage sells a [monthly permit for $232](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage), which is $2,784 over 12 months.

## Common mistakes

- **Comparing a per-unit price with a per-bed price.** RentCafe's $3,414 Collegetown 2BR is the whole unit. Ithaca Renting's 2BR median is $1,377.50 a bed, or $2,755 for the unit, $659 less.
- **Comparing 12 months of rent with Cornell's academic-year rate.** A 1BR at $2,025 costs $24,300 over 12 months, $10,556 more than the $13,744 double. Aid does not pay rent in unregistered months.
- **Leaving out electricity and parking.** Up to about $70 a person a month for electricity in a 4BR, and $232 a month for a garage permit.
- **Waiting for a big house.** The 8BR at 105 Dryden Court and the 14BR at 214 Dryden Rd were sold out for 2027-28 by Nov 8, 2026, nine months before move-in.
- **Signing with a group that later shrinks.** A move from a 6BR to a 4BR adds $5,355 a year per person at the medians.
- **Treating a listed range as the final price.** Ithaca Renting's home page and Collegetown page quoted different ranges for the same unit on the same day.

## FAQ

**Is Collegetown rent going up?** Not much at the large buildings. RentCafe's Collegetown average fell 2.59% to $2,815 in the year to Oct 30, 2026. Old-stock per-bed prices at one landlord are up 2.8% to 11.6% since 2019.

**How much do I pay when I sign?** Usually the first month plus a deposit. The deposit plus any rent paid in advance cannot exceed [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), so a $2,025 1BR means at most $4,050 up front. Cornell aid [covers neither](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus).

**What if we only pay rent for 10 months?** Then the double works out to $1,374 a month, not $1,145. On a 12-month lease, such as Dryden South's, that view only holds if you sublet the summer.

**Can I find a Collegetown 1BR under $1,700?** At Ithaca Renting, only the low end of the Collegetown Court standard 1BR, at $1,625 to $1,725 for 2027-28. Downtown and Fall Creek have more 1BRs near $1,600.

**Are these the prices people actually pay?** No. Every figure here is an asking rent. Ithaca Renting's price is the only full list available, and its ranges may change until you sign.

**Is a studio cheaper than a 1BR?** By about $260 a month at Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 medians, $1,765 against $2,025.
