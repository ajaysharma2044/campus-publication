```glance
- Only students returning to Cornell campus housing for spring semester can stay over winter break.
- Apply in the Housing and Dining Portal between Oct. 1 and Dec. 1.
- It costs $125 for a one-week option or $500 for the full break.
- HILC, Loving House and Veterans House residents stay in their own rooms at no extra cost.
```

You can stay on campus over Cornell's winter break if you are returning to campus housing for spring semester and you apply between Oct. 1 and Dec. 1 in the Housing and Dining Portal. Most students pay $125 for one week or $500 for the whole break ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). Everyone else must leave by 2 p.m. on Dec. 20, 2026.

```reader "If you're an international student"
If visa issues make it hard to travel home over breaks, Cornell invites you to email the housing office confidentially at housing@cornell.edu to ask about staying on campus ([Cornell Global](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources)). Write well before the application deadline. If you'll stay every winter, ask for the Holland International Living Center (HILC), where break housing is free.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate student"
The winter closing applies to undergraduate residences. Hasbrouck Apartments, Cornell's graduate and family housing, rents on 12-month contracts, from $620 to $1,530 a month for 2026-27 ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). You don't need a break application to stay there.
```

```reader "If you live off campus"
You don't qualify for break housing. Your own apartment is your break housing. If you keep a car in Ithaca, read the [city's winter parking rules](/cornell/getting-around/ithaca-winter-parking-rules-cornell-odd-even/) before you leave it on a street.
```

## Who can stay in Cornell housing over winter break?

Any student returning as a resident of on-campus housing for spring semester can apply. So a senior who graduates in December, a student moving to Collegetown in January, and a student leaving for a spring abroad program cannot stay.

Where you live in fall decides how you stay. Cornell keeps 10 buildings open over the 2026-27 break:

- Ganędagǫ: Hall
- High Rise 5
- Holland International Living Center (HILC), in Low Rise 8
- Hu Shih Hall
- Loving House
- Mews Hall
- Toni Morrison Hall
- Townhouse Apartments
- Cascadilla Hall
- Veterans House

If you live in one of these, you stay in your own room. If your building closes, you can still apply. Cornell places you in a Residential Flex Space, a spare room held open in one of the open buildings, so you move buildings for the break. Cornell calls these spaces "extremely limited," so apply as soon as the window opens.

## How much does winter break housing cost?

The price depends on your building, not on how many nights you stay.

HILC and Loving House are free because individual gifts to those communities cover the cost. Veterans House is free because its license already runs through the break. Residents of all three must still file the application so Cornell knows they are staying.

Cornell does not prorate the fees, so a student who stays 10 days of the full break pays the same as one who stays all 30 nights from closing to reopening. That makes the full break $16.67 a night ($500 divided by 30), and a one-week option $17.86 a night ($125 divided by 7).

## How do you apply for winter break housing?

You apply in the Housing and Dining Portal, even if your own building stays open.

```figure

title: Applying for Cornell winter break housing
rows:
  - {label: "Open the Housing and Dining Portal between Oct. 1 and Dec. 1."}
  - {label: "Complete the Winter Break housing application, even if your building stays open."}
  - {label: "Pick one option: the week after fall term, the week before spring term, or the full break."}
  - {label: "Pay the fee for that option, unless you live in HILC, Loving House or Veterans House."}
  - {label: "If you live in a closing building, apply for the full break and wait for a Flex Space assignment."}
```

Cornell doesn't say whether it takes late applications. If you miss the deadline, email housing@cornell.edu or call Housing Operations at 607-255-5368 before you book anything that depends on a bed.

All undergraduate residences close at 2 p.m. on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026 ([Winter Break 2026 Closing Checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-break-closing-checklist)). Only students with prior approval for break housing can enter residential buildings until they reopen at 9 a.m. on Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027. Students who can't leave by the 2 p.m. deadline must ask for a later departure in the portal by Monday, Dec. 7, with their name, room number and reason. Without one, the charge is $60.00 an hour.

If you go home, you can't get into your room during break, so take your passport, medications and anything else you'd need. You can move back in any time between reopening and the start of classes on Jan. 26, but you must book an arrival timeslot in the portal.

## What changes for the 2027-28 winter break?

The list of open buildings changes for 2027-28. High Rise 5 and Cascadilla Hall drop off. Jameson Hall and 301 College Avenue join. The other eight stay the same: Ganędagǫ: Hall, HILC, Hu Shih Hall, Loving House, Mews Hall, Toni Morrison Hall, the Townhouse Apartments and Veterans House.

That matters when you pick a room for next year. A student who knows they'll stay every winter can choose an open building and keep their own room, instead of hoping for a Flex Space.

```reader "If you're an incoming student"
When you apply for housing for the coming year and expect to stay over winter break, select a "Winter Break Housing" option on your housing application. The housing team will try to place you in one of the open buildings from the start.
```

## Can you eat on campus over winter break?

Fall meal plans end when the fall semester closes, and they don't carry into break. During break you can eat at the dining rooms and retail spots that stay open, paying with Big Red Bucks (BRBs), the prepaid dining dollars on your meal plan. Cornell Dining usually closes every location during the week the university itself is closed. That week, you can spend City Bucks, the meal plan money accepted at local businesses, at Ithaca restaurants that are open.

Save some BRBs and City Bucks from fall if you plan to stay. For buses, groceries and the rest of a break in Ithaca, read our guide to [staying in Ithaca over Cornell breaks](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/), and see the [TCAT guide](/cornell/getting-around/tcat-free-for-cornell-students-hours-routes/) for routes and free hours.

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
If you'll stay every winter, pick HILC, Loving House or Veterans House when you choose a room, and break housing costs nothing. Otherwise budget for the full-break fee plus food once your meal plan ends. Our guide to [hidden costs for first-generation and low-income students](/cornell/money/cornell-first-gen-low-income-hidden-costs/) covers where to ask for help with costs like these.
```

## Should you stay or go home?

Stay if the full-break fee and a month of food cost less than a round trip. Decide early: the application closes nearly three weeks before the halls do. If you're going home, our guide to [getting home for winter break 2026](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-winter-break-travel-home-2026/) covers buses, flights and the C2C bus.
