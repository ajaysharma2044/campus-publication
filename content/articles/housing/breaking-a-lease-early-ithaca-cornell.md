```glance
- Try a written early-termination agreement first, then a sublet, then an assignment. Walking away is last.
- In a building with four or more units, a landlord cannot unreasonably refuse a sublet.
- If you leave anyway, the landlord must try in good faith to re-rent the unit.
- Housemates on a joint lease stay liable for your share until the landlord releases you.
```

Cornell warns that a local lease cannot be broken without consequences, and the consequence is money. You leave cleanly with the landlord's written release, a sublet or an assignment, and the reason does not matter: study abroad, a transfer, a leave of absence or a job ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)).

## What are your four options?

Cornell Law's tenants' guide calls a written agreement with the landlord the simplest route. Each later option leaves you more exposed ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

| Option | What you need | Do you still owe rent? | Do housemates owe your share? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early-termination agreement | Landlord's signature on a move-out date and amount | Only what the agreement says | Only if it releases you; ask for that in writing |
| Sublet | Written consent; a legal right in buildings of four or more units | Yes, you stay liable for the subtenant | Yes, if the subtenant stops paying |
| Assignment | Written consent; the landlord can refuse without cause | No, once assigned | The new tenant takes your place |
| Leave without a deal | Nothing | Yes, until the landlord re-rents | Yes, unless the landlord releases you |

## How do you get a written early-termination agreement?

Ask the landlord to end the lease on a set date, and get it in writing with the move-out date and the money owed ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). On a shared unit, the agreement should release you by name, or your housemates still owe your share. Read any termination fee closely: the guide says such fees can be treated as liquidated damages, which means a preset penalty a court does not always enforce. Call the Tenants Advocacy hotline at 607-301-1560 before you pay a large one.

## How does a sublet work?

A subtenant lives in your room and pays rent, but you stay on the lease. In a building with four or more residential units, New York law gives you a right to sublet with the landlord's advance written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). In a building of three or fewer units, the lease controls, so a house renter should read the sublet clause first.

Per [RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), send the request by certified mail with return receipt. It lists the sublet term, the subtenant's name and addresses, your reason, your address during the term, any co-tenant's or guarantor's consent and a copy of the proposed sublease. The landlord can ask for more information within 10 days and must answer within 30 days of the later of your request or that extra information. Silence counts as consent. If the landlord refuses unreasonably, you can sublet as requested and recover costs and attorney fees if the landlord acted in bad faith. A sublet without these steps is a substantial breach, and you stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage.

## What is an assignment?

An assignment hands the rest of your lease to a new tenant. You need written consent, and the owner can refuse without cause. If the refusal is unreasonable, your only remedy is release on 30 days' notice. After an assignment, the new tenant carries the rent and damage, not you. The Cornell Law guide prefers assigning over subletting if you do not plan to move back in ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).

## What if you leave with nothing signed?

The landlord must take reasonable and customary steps, in good faith, to re-rent at the lower of fair market value or your lease rent. A new lease ends the old one and offsets the damages, and the duty cannot be waived ([RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E)). Until a new tenant's lease starts, you owe rent. Your deposit comes back on the usual schedule in the [deposit rules guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/), and the [move-out guide](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/) covers the walk-through.

Cornell Financial Aid will not intervene in a mid-year attempt to break a lease ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).

## What do your housemates owe?

Most local leases bind housemates jointly and severally, which means each is responsible for the housemates' rent and damages for the whole term. A tenant who overpays can sue a co-tenant for contribution ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)). Here is the size of that exposure. A five-month semester in a Collegetown 4BR at the 2027-28 median of $1,246.25 per bed is $6,231.25 (5 x $1,246.25) ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Agree in writing before you sign who finds the subtenant and who pays if none turns up. The full liability picture is in the [roommate liability guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/).

## Are there legal exits?

Two situations let you end a lease by notice ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

| Situation | How it works |
|---|---|
| Domestic violence | Written notice ends the lease in 30 days; documentation within 25 days; prepaid rent returned within 10 days; Advocacy Center of Tompkins County, 607-277-5000 |
| Active military duty | Written notice after duty starts; effective 30 days after the next rent due date |

```reader "If you're studying abroad with housemates"
Your share does not pause while you are gone. Send the sublet request by certified mail two to three months before you fly, so the landlord's questions and answer fit before your flight.

A license is not a lease, so the rules above do not apply. For study away, leave, an internship or graduation, you can cancel penalty-free by June 15 for fall or Dec. 1 for spring, and later requests cost $500 ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027)). ```reader "If you're on a one-semester stay"
The costly mistake is signing a 12-month lease for a six-month stay. Look for semester units, which Ithaca Renting lists for spring and fall 2027. A month-to-month tenancy ends on one month's notice, and the Cornell Law guide describes it as limited legal protection. If you are a spring transfer, the [transfer housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/) covers your options.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
A program ending early does not end the lease. Get a written termination or a signed sublease before you fly home, and give the landlord your address abroad. A landlord dispute does not affect your visa or standing, per Graduate School staff ([Cornell Graduate School](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/)).
```

```reader "If you're a parent or guarantor"
The Cornell Law guide's lease page does not cover guarantors, so the guaranty you signed sets your exposure. A sublet request must include any guarantor's consent, so expect to sign.
```

## What to do next

1. Email the landlord for a written release with a move-out date and amount.
2. If that fails, send the sublet request by certified mail.
3. Call 607-301-1560 before you stop paying rent.
