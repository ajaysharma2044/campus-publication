```glance
- Try these in order: a [written early-termination agreement](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease), a sublet, an assignment. Walking away is the last resort.
- In a building with four or more units, the landlord [cannot unreasonably refuse a sublet](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). They get 10 days to ask for more information and 30 days to answer; silence is consent.
- If you leave anyway, the landlord must try in good faith to [re-rent at the lower of market or your lease rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E). A new lease ends what you owe.
- Housemates on a joint lease [owe your share](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) until the landlord releases you: about $6,231 for a five-month semester in a Collegetown 4BR (our math on a 2027-28 median).
- Cornell license holders cancel penalty-free for study away, leave or an internship by [Dec 1, 2026 for spring 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027) or June 15, 2027 for fall 2027. Later requests cost $500.
- Cornell Financial Aid [will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in a mid-year lease break.
```

Cornell warns that a local lease [cannot be broken without consequences, usually monetary](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire), so you leave cleanly only with the landlord's written release, a sublet or an assignment. If you go without one, the landlord must try to [re-rent your unit at the lower of market value or your lease rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E), and you owe rent until a new lease starts. On a joint lease your housemates [stay liable for your share](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) the whole time, so settle the plan with them in writing first.

```reader "If you're studying abroad with housemates"
Your share does not pause while you are gone. At [Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown 4BR median](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) of $1,246.25 per bed a month, a five-month semester is $6,231 (our arithmetic), and three housemates would split it if you vanish. Agree in writing before you sign who finds the subtenant and who pays if none turns up. Send the sublet request by certified mail two to three months before you fly. The landlord has 10 days to ask for more and 30 to answer. Subletting without the written steps is a [substantial breach](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B).
```

```reader "If you're leaving Cornell for good"
Ask the landlord first for an [early-termination agreement](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) that names the move-out date and the exact amount owed. If they refuse, request an assignment: it hands over the whole lease and generally ends your liability. If the landlord refuses an assignment unreasonably, [you can be released on 30 days' notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). If you leave with nothing signed, the landlord must try to re-rent, and you owe until a new tenant's lease starts. [Financial Aid will not step in](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). Call the free [Tenants Advocacy hotline](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/), 607-301-1560, before you go.
```

```reader "If you hold a Cornell housing license"
A license is not a lease, so the rules above do not apply. For study away, leave, an internship or graduation, cancel penalty-free by [June 15 (fall) or Dec 1 (spring) for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027), and [June 15, 2027 or Dec 1, 2027 for 2027-28](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). A later request costs $500. First-years, new transfers and second-years cannot cancel unless they are no longer attending or are exempt. Going abroad in spring 2027? Your deadline is Dec 1, 2026.
```

```reader "If you're on a one-semester stay"
The costly mistake is signing a 12-month lease for a six-month stay: the remaining months are owed unless you sublet or the landlord [re-rents the unit](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E). Look for semester rows instead; [Ithaca Renting lists "Spring/Fall Semester 2027" units](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). A month-to-month tenancy generally ends on [one month's notice](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal). Cornell notes that units with no lease offer flexibility but [limited legal protection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations), so get the terms in writing.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
A program ending early does not end the lease. Your housemates stay liable for your share, and [Cornell aid will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). Before you fly home, get a written termination agreement or a signed sublease, and give the landlord your address abroad. Cornell Graduate School staff say a landlord dispute [does not affect your visa status or standing](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). They also advise contacting [Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) (607-255-2310) before you break a lease. The tenant hotline, 607-301-1560, gives advice with [no income or geographic limits](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources).
```

```reader "If you're a parent or guarantor"
When a housemate leaves a joint lease, each remaining tenant [can be held for the full rent](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease). A tenant who overpays can sue the co-tenant for contribution. The Cornell Law guide's lease page does not cover guarantors, so the guaranty you signed sets your exposure. A sublet request must include [any guarantor's consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), so expect to sign. Financial aid does not cover the gap: Cornell says it [will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in a mid-year lease break. More in the [parent's guide](/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-for-parents/).
```

## The four ways out, in the order to try them

The [Cornell Law School tenants' guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) calls a written agreement with the landlord the simplest route. Each later option leaves you more exposed.

| Option | What you need | Do you still owe rent? | Do housemates still owe your share? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early-termination agreement | The landlord's signature on a move-out date and amount | Only what the agreement says | Only if the agreement releases you; ask for it in writing |
| Sublet | Written consent; a right to it in buildings of 4+ units | Yes: you stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage | Yes, if the subtenant stops paying |
| Assignment | Written consent; the landlord may refuse without cause | Generally no, once assigned | The new tenant takes over your place on the lease |
| Leave without a deal | Nothing | Yes, until the landlord re-rents | Yes, unless the landlord releases you |

### 1. A written early-termination agreement

Ask the landlord to end the lease on a set date. Get it in writing with the [move-out date and the money owed](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). If you share the unit, have the agreement release you by name, or your housemates still owe your share.

Read any termination fee in your lease closely. The Cornell Law guide says such fees may be treated as liquidated damages and are [not always enforceable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Call the hotline (607-301-1560) before you pay a large one.

### 2. A sublet

A subtenant lives in your room and pays rent, but you stay on the lease. In a building with four or more residential units, [New York's RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) gives you a right to sublet with the landlord's advance written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. In a building of three or fewer units, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease), so a house renter should read the sublet clause first. Cornell's own page notes that ["most leases require landlord approval"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) to sublet.

The request goes by certified mail, return receipt requested. It lists:

- the sublet term and the subtenant's name and addresses;
- the reason you are leaving and your address during the term;
- the written consent of any co-tenant or guarantor;
- a copy of the proposed sublease.

The landlord may ask for more information within 10 days. They must answer within 30 days of the later of your request or that extra information. [Silence counts as consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). If the landlord refuses unreasonably, you may sublet as requested and recover costs and attorney fees if they acted in bad faith.

A sublet fee is allowed only if the lease permits it and the fee is reasonable; [background or credit check fees are allowed](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Advertise on the [Cornell Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), which only Cornell-affiliated people can view, and on the Off-Campus Living email list. Sublets were [free to list in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/). Asking prices give a rough bar: [Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/) advertised sublets "from $900 per person" in November 2026, with no period stated. Not confirmed: what discount Ithaca sublets usually take.

The full sublet process, including summer sublets, is in [how to sublet an Ithaca apartment](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/).

### 3. An assignment

An assignment hands the rest of your lease to a new tenant. After it, the original tenant is [generally not liable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) for the new tenant's rent or damage. The catch: the landlord [may refuse without cause](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). If the refusal is unreasonable, your only remedy is release from the lease on 30 days' notice. That release is itself a way out, so put the assignment request in writing.

### 4. Leaving without a deal

If you move out with nothing signed, [RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) requires the landlord to take reasonable and customary steps, in good faith, to re-rent the unit. The target rent is the lower of fair market value or your lease rate. A new lease ends yours and offsets the damages. The party seeking damages carries the burden of proof, and a lease clause waiving this duty is void.

You still owe rent for the months the unit sits empty. On a joint lease, the landlord can collect it from any of your housemates.

## What your housemates owe when you leave

Cornell's own page says most local leases bind housemates ["jointly and severally"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire). Each is responsible for the others' rent and damages for the whole term. The same page lists a question for housemates: what happens if one takes leave, transfers or studies abroad. It gives no answer, so your group has to write one.

A housemate who covers your share can [sue you for contribution](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease). The landlord can still collect the full rent from any one of you first.

The collision is built into the calendar. A [2018 Cornell Daily Sun report](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/) described desirable Collegetown leases leaving the market up to 16 months before move-in. A [2016 Sun article](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/20/students-housing-search-hampers-study-abroad) reported the housing search getting in the way of study-abroad planning. You often sign before you know where you will be.

Example: four juniors sign a Collegetown 4BR for 2027-28 at the [Ithaca Renting median](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) of $1,246.25 per bed a month. One leaves for a five-month spring semester abroad with no sublet and no release.

## If you hold a Cornell housing license

Cornell housing is a [revocable license, not a lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028), so the sublet and re-rent laws do not apply. Cancellation runs on Cornell's own dates.

| Your reason | Deadline | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Study away, leave, internship or graduation, fall 2026 | June 15, 2026 (passed) | $0; later requests $500 |
| Same, spring 2027 | Dec 1, 2026 | $0; later requests $500 |
| Same, fall 2027 | June 15, 2027 | $0; later requests $500 |
| Same, spring 2028 | Dec 1, 2027 | $0; later requests $500 |
| Returning junior or senior, any other reason | Through Jan 31 | $50 |
| | Feb 1 to Mar 31 | $500 |
| | Apr 1 to Jun 30 | $1,000 |
| | Jul 1 to Aug 15 | $2,000 |
| | After Aug 15, or not checking in by the first day of classes | 50% of the fall semester room rate |

Sources: the [2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027) and the [2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). For a double at [$6,872 per semester in 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), the 50% charge is $3,436 (our arithmetic).

First-years, new transfers and second-years cannot cancel, unless they are no longer attending or exempt. Second-years accepted to a Greek house, affiliated house or one of the eight co-ops before the Spring Housing Selection deadline can also cancel.

## The away plan, by date

The steps below assume a spring semester abroad. Shift each one by six months for fall.

```figure
kind: timeline
title: Leaving an Ithaca lease for a spring semester away (spring 2027 dates)
rows:
  - {label: "Before signing: agree in writing with housemates what happens if one goes away", date: "Fall before the lease"}
  - {label: "Sublet request by certified mail, two to three months before a January departure", date: "Oct to Nov 2026"}
  - {label: "Landlord may ask for more information", date: "Within 10 days"}
  - {label: "Landlord must answer; silence is consent", date: "Within 30 days"}
  - {label: "Cornell license holders: last penalty-free day", date: "Dec 1, 2026"}
  - {label: "Two weeks out: change address, confirm move-out", date: "Two weeks before leaving"}
  - {label: "One week out: utilities, cleaning, forwarding address", date: "One week before leaving"}
```

1. **Before you sign.** Write down with your housemates who finds a subtenant and who pays if none appears. Cornell's [housemate questionnaire](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) raises the question; your group answers it.
2. **Two to three months out.** Send the sublet request. Leave room for the 10-day and 30-day clocks, which can run 40 days or more if the landlord asks for more (our arithmetic). Leaving in January 2027? Send it this month.
3. **No subtenant yet?** Ask for an assignment or a written termination agreement. Do not hand over keys on a handshake.
4. **Insurance.** Cornell's preferred insurer is GradGuard, whose policy lists [worldwide property coverage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships) and personal liability; no premium is published. Check whether a subtenant's things are covered under your policy or theirs.
5. **Mail and utilities.** Cornell's [tenant best practices](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) say to notify contacts, forward mail, manage utilities, and clean and secure the apartment. If the subtenant takes over NYSEG or internet, put that in the sublease.
6. **Leaving for good.** Use Cornell's [move-out timeline](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out): two weeks out, confirm the date and change your address; one week out, schedule utility shutoffs and give a forwarding address in writing.
7. **Storage.** [Big Red Shipping and Storage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), Cornell's vendor, picks up free at apartments and posts no prices. [Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny) shows $172 a month labeled "2025 Avg." and asks for bookings by early April. A 10x10 unit averages about [$93 a month on SpareFoot](https://sparefoot.com/Ithaca-NY-self-storage.html) (July 2026).

## Special exits: domestic violence and military service

Two groups can end a lease by notice alone, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease):

| Who | How | When it takes effect | Help |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic violence victims (RPL 227-c) | 30 days' written notice; documentation within 25 days | 30 days after notice; prepaid rent back within 10 days | Advocacy Center of Tompkins County, (607) 277-5000 |
| Service members | Written notice after active duty starts | 30 days after the next rent due date | Tenants Advocacy hotline, 607-301-1560 |

The guide does not address study abroad or leaving school. It refers those tenants to the [Tenants Advocacy hotline](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/), 607-301-1560.

## Common mistakes

- **Subletting on a handshake.** A sublet without the written steps is a [substantial breach of the lease](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), which gives the landlord grounds against you.
- **Leaving housemates to absorb your share.** At the 4BR median that is about $6,231 for a semester, or $2,077 each for three housemates (our arithmetic).
- **Missing June 15 or Dec 1 on a Cornell license.** The penalty-free window closes and a [late request costs $500](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027).
- **Paying a termination fee without asking.** Such fees [may not be enforceable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) as liquidated damages; one call to 607-301-1560 is free.
- **Counting on financial aid.** Cornell Financial Aid [will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in a mid-year lease break, so the rent comes from you.
- **Signing a 12-month lease for one semester.** The months you are gone are owed unless sublet or re-rented.

## FAQ

**Does my landlord have to let me sublet while I'm abroad?** In a building with four or more units, yes, unless the refusal is reasonable; [silence after 30 days counts as consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). In a house of three or fewer units, the lease decides.

**I graduate in May, but my lease runs to July 31. What are my options?** Sublet June and July with the same certified-mail request, or ask the landlord for a written early end date. If you have not signed yet, some buildings end earlier: every current Dryden South lease [ends May 31, 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961).

**Can my landlord keep charging me after they find a new tenant?** No. Under [RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) a new lease ends your old one and offsets the damages.

**Do I need to give notice at the end of my lease if I am not coming back?** Not at the end of a fixed term. A month-to-month tenancy generally needs [one month's notice](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal).

**Will Cornell help me negotiate with my landlord?** Off-Campus Living offers one-on-one consultations but, per staff in 2019, [does not represent students in disputes](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/). Graduate School staff advise [contacting Off-Campus Living before you break a lease](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/) (607-255-2310).

**Can a landlord charge my subtenant a fee?** Only if the lease allows it and the fee is reasonable; [credit or background check fees are allowed](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).
