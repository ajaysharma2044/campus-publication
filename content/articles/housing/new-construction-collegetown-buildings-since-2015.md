Collegetown's newest buildings are also its most expensive. The Ivy, an eight-story building at 325 College Ave. with 52 units, began leases on Aug. 17, 2026, and nearly every unit was leased before construction finished ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)). It followed a 2016 wave of three buildings.

## What opened in Collegetown in 2016?

Three buildings added nearly 200 bedrooms in 2016: 307 College Ave., 327 Eddy St. and 205 Dryden Rd. ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/)). County records list 307 College Ave. as Collegetown Crossing, built in 2016.

327 Eddy St., Fontana Apartments, opened late after five delays. More than 50 students with Aug. 1 leases had no home, and the landlord offered them $30 a day or a room at the Trip Hotel on Sheraton Avenue ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless)).

205 Dryden Rd., Dryden South, offers 2x2, 3x3, 4x4 and 4x4.5 townhouse layouts at $1,395 to $1,990 per person, furnished, with heat and hot water included. All current leases end May 31, 2027 ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)).

## What does The Ivy cost?

Its three-bedroom units cost $5,500 to $5,800 a month, and the cheapest unit was a basement two-bedroom at $1,595 per person, or $3,190 for the unit. A utility package is optional at $150 per person a month (The Cornell Daily Sun). One student offered a 20 percent early-interest discount stayed in her own building anyway, because the cheapest unit had less space than her apartment.

Compare that with the median Collegetown bed in the same year: $800 a month in a six-bedroom and $2,025 in a one-bedroom for 2027-28 ([Collegetown rent per bed](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)). Our [one-bedroom and per-bed price guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/) shows the whole range.

## What is 301 College Avenue?

It is Cornell housing, not a private apartment building. Cornell bought it for $15 million in March 2025 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/03/cornell-acquires-301-college-avenue-for-15-million-in-collegetown)). The building has 12 residential units, from studios to six-bedrooms, and Cornell housed 43 students there in August 2026, with room for 105 residents by fall 2027 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/301-college-avenue-available-for-student-housing-in-2026-2027-academic-year)).

It opened partly finished. A resident told the Sun in September 2026 that the building felt "incomplete" and "rushed" ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)). Residents are on the Collegetown Meal Plan, $3,528 per semester for 2026-27 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

## How early do new buildings fill?

Before they open. Collegetown leases are signed 8 to 16 months before an August move-in, mostly 10 to 11 months ahead, according to Sun stories from 2017 to 2025. Our [lease timing guide](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) explains the calendar. On Nov. 8, 2026, 14 of 67 Ithaca Renting rows for 2027-28 were already sold out ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

```reader "If you're signing for a building that is not finished"
Ask in writing what the landlord pays if the building opens after your lease start. In 2016 the Fontana students got $30 a day or a hotel room, and a delayed opening is a risk you take on with any pre-leased building.
```

## What if a new building fails inspection?

New does not mean safe. Asteri, a downtown building that opened in August 2024, received a vacate order on March 4, 2026, over a blocked stairwell among more than 40 violations, and the order was lifted March 20 ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/asteri-s-aftermath-displacement-dangerous-conditions-and-a-predatory-cash-for-keys-scheme)). Our [vacate order guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-eviction-vacate-order-what-to-do/) lists your rights if that happens to your building.
