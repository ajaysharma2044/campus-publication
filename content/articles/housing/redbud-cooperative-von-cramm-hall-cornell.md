Redbud Cooperative in Von Cramm Hall is the largest of Cornell's eight co-ops, with 34 residents at 623 University Ave. A co-op is a Cornell-owned house run by its residents. Redbud has the lowest rates of the eight (Cornell Housing), and it takes graduate students as well as undergraduates.

## What is Redbud Cooperative?

Cornell's page lists 34 residents, singles, doubles and triples, and nightly shared dinners. The co-op was founded in 1956. Its name, Redbud, commemorates a former wooded area. Residents are undergraduate and graduate students, and many are international or exchange students ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/redbud-cooperative-von-cramm-hall)). Cornell's rate table spells the name "Red Bud in Von Cramm."

Cornell's overview names four houses with a house meal plan: Prospect of Whitby, Triphammer, Watermargin and 660 Stewart. Redbud is not one of them ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). Ask the house how its nightly dinners are paid for.

## What does Redbud cost?

Redbud's rates are the lowest in the co-op system. For 2026-27 a double is $3,487 per semester, which is $6,974 for two semesters. A triple is $2,765 per semester, or $5,530 for the year, and a single is $3,949 per semester ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

A hall double costs $13,744 for the year, so the Redbud double is $6,770 less. The hall triple costs $12,644, so the Redbud triple is $7,114 less. Those are room rates only, and any dinner costs are extra. The full comparison with leases is in [Is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/), and a student on a tight budget can compare more options in [cheapest off-campus housing near Cornell](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/).

## How do you get into Redbud?

Each co-op runs its own selection, and selection starts in early November and early February ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). A Cornell Daily Sun report described entry as a "mosey," a set of events followed by a lottery weighted by financial need and diversity ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/06/underserved-and-minority-students-find-belonging-and-support-in-co-op-housing/)). Write to co-ops@cornell.edu or call 607-255-3445 for Redbud's current steps.

Rising sophomores must state their co-op plan on the 2027-28 application by March 15, 2027. If you miss that date, you complete the requirement on campus ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). A co-op counts as affiliated housing, which our [affiliated housing page](/start/affiliated-housing/) explains. For other ways around the requirement, see the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What are the license terms?

All eight co-ops share one license, with no security deposit and a full-time student requirement. The rules are in [Cornell co-ops compared](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-all-eight-compared/). How the process works across houses is in our guide to [co-ops and Greek houses](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/).

## What to do next

Email co-ops@cornell.edu, ask Redbud about its dinners and selection dates, and compare the rate with a hall.
