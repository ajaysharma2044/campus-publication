Catherine Commons is a large, furnished apartment building at 210 College Ave. in Collegetown, with 356 apartments and 483 beds, 206 of the apartments studios ([Welliver](https://buildwelliver.com/projects/integrated-acquisition-development-corp-iad-catherine-commons-210-college-avenue)).

Cornell's marketplace lists prices from $1,615 to $2,479 without saying per bed or per unit, so confirm the basis before you compare it ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/catherine-commons-236692)).

## What is Catherine Commons?

The contractor, Welliver, lists 356 apartments in the building: 206 studios, 64 one-bedrooms, 45 two-bedrooms and 41 three-bedrooms. It totals 483 beds, about 270,000 square feet, and 2,600 square feet of retail. The architect was IKON.5 ([Welliver](https://buildwelliver.com/projects/integrated-acquisition-development-corp-iad-catherine-commons-210-college-avenue)).

It is a studio-heavy building. If you want a private bedroom and your own kitchen, it fits. If you want a cheap bed in a big shared house, it does not: the per-bed rents in the [2027-28 Collegetown dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/) for large shared units are lower.

## Why was it built, and what did it change?

The Planning and Development Board approved the project on March 23, 2022. It replaced eleven two-story houses at Catherine Street and College Avenue. The Cornell Review reported about 360 units and a net gain of 339 bedrooms, with completion scheduled for August 2024 ([The Cornell Review](https://www.thecornellreview.org/cornells-construction-crunch-ithacas-housing-crisis-and-the-catherine-commons/)).

The two sources disagree on who built it. Welliver names Integrated Acquisition & Development as the owner and developer. The Cornell Review names Novarr Mackesey. Ask the leasing office who the landlord of record is before you sign.

## What does Catherine Commons cost?

The marketplace price range carries no basis, and every floor plan is marked "Call For Price." The page says neither per bed nor per unit, nor the lease year. A Sun columnist wrote that higher-end Collegetown apartments run from $1,450 for a three- or four-bedroom to upward of $2,300 for a studio, but the column does not name Catherine Commons ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/maia-on-the-mic-the-collegetown-housing-hustle)). Do not treat it as this building's price.

Ask the leasing office for the application fee and every other fee in writing, and add them to the rent.

For price context across the neighborhood, see [Collegetown rent prices per bed](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/) and the [Collegetown versus downtown comparison](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/).

## How do you get a spot, and what should you watch?

The Cornell marketplace listing shows availability as "Ask," with no lease dates, so contact the leasing office at 607-270-3637, the number printed there ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/catherine-commons-236692)).

Test the commute before you commit. The listing shows 7 minutes from The Oval.
