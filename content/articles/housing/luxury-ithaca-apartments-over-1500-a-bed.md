Most Ithaca beds that cost more than $1,500 a month are studios and one-bedrooms near Cornell, plus shares in a few new towers. At [Ithaca Renting's three Collegetown high-rises](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), 2027-28 studios and one-bedrooms ask $1,565 to $2,545 a bed. The newest buildings, led by The Ivy at 325 College Ave., charge that much even when you share.

## How much do The Ivy, The Bear and The Summit cost?

These three are the top of the market. Modern Living Rentals runs all three, and developers Nick Robertson and Charlie O'Connor built The Ivy.

**The Ivy, 325 College Ave.** The eight-story building at the corner of Dryden Road has 52 units, from studios to seven-bedrooms. Leases began Aug. 17, 2026, and nearly every unit was leased before construction finished. The Sun reported 3BRs at $5,500 to $5,800 a month in December 2025, while the [Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/07/cornell-collegetown-construction/) put the top 3BR at $4,900 in July 2026. The cheapest unit was a basement 2BR at $1,595 per person, or $3,190 for two ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)). Split three ways, the 3BR is $1,833.33 to $1,933.33 per person ($5,500 / 3 and $5,800 / 3). Studios start at $2,595 a month ([Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/07/cornell-collegetown-construction/)).

A one-bedroom, Unit 205, listed at $2,700 per person, and comparable units on the 8th floor at $3,300. Utilities cost extra unless you buy the optional package, $150 per person a month ([Rentler listing](https://www.rentler.com/places-for-rent/ny/ithaca/325-college-ave/13257148)). The building has a fitness center, sauna, tanning booth, private study rooms and an in-unit washer and dryer, and every unit comes furnished ([Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/the-ivy)).

The Ivy sells out early. Its one-bedrooms were spoken for in May 2026, before tours, and about a quarter of the apartments were leased for the following year shortly after it opened ([Tompkins Weekly](https://www.tompkinsweekly.com/news/three-new-developments-offer-different-answers-to-ithacas-housing-needs-30cfa148)).

**The Bear, 215 College Ave.** Tompkins Weekly puts The Bear at 84 units and 150 beds, and the [Ithaca Voice](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/07/cornell-collegetown-construction/) lists studios at $2,850 a month and 3BRs at $6,000. That 3BR is $2,000 per person ($6,000 / 3). It is scheduled to open for the 2027 academic year.

**The Summit, 201 Oak Ave.** It has 46 units and about 88 bedrooms. Studios and one-bedrooms start at $2,700, and a 4BR rents for $7,200 a month, split four ways in the table above, according to the same Ithaca Voice report.

More is coming. Robertson and O'Connor plan an eight-story building at 309 College Ave., the old East Hill fire station site, with 77 units and 112 bedrooms ([Ithaca Times](https://www.ithaca.com/news/ithaca/former-collegetown-fire-station-site-slated-for-16-5m-apartment-development/article_daeb3b07-ae00-497b-9e13-ac15184dc19e.html)).

## What do Collegetown Center, Plaza and Court charge?

These are Ithaca Renting Company's three Dryden Road high-rises, and they are the only luxury-tier buildings that post every price. All three are furnished, with heat, hot water and trash removal in the rent. Tenants pay their own electricity. Parking is a separate one-year license, starting at $2,450 a year for an uncovered space in the lower Dryden lot (Ithaca Renting).

**Collegetown Center, 151 Dryden Rd.** Built in 1997, it is the priciest of the three. The landlord pitches it to graduate students and "the more mature student," with a lobby concierge desk staffed 9 a.m. to 11 p.m., air conditioning and a fitness room ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=461)). Covered parking in the Dryden Road lot is for Center residents only.

**Collegetown Plaza, 111 Dryden Rd.** Its studios and one-bedrooms sit in the middle of the range. The Plaza also has the group's cheapest beds, in its 6BRs, so friends on different budgets can live in one building.

**Collegetown Court, 208 Dryden Rd.** It is the entry point to the tier, with the cheapest studios of the three.

## What about Dryden South and Eddygate?

**Dryden South, 205 Dryden Rd.** A six-story building from 2016 next to the Breazzano Center, it has two studios and ten four-bedroom suites. For 2027-28, Studio 101 asks $1,990 per person a month and Studio 102 asks $2,190. Three suites cross the line: Suites 502 and 602 at $1,575 and the Suite 601 penthouse at $1,525. All ten suites are already rented. Every lease starts June 1 and runs 12 months ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/)).

The suites include heat and hot water and come furnished. Studio tenants pay their own heat, air conditioning, electricity and Wi-Fi, and there is no parking ([Cornell off-campus marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)).

**Eddygate, 110 Dryden Rd.** Travis Hyde Properties prices Eddygate by the unit, not the bed. One-bedrooms list at $2,055, $2,165 and $2,395 a month. Two-bedrooms run $2,850 to $3,150, which is $1,425 to $1,575 each when two share. Units are furnished, with water, sewer and recycling included. Tenants pay electricity, a $175 trash fee and a $240 Wi-Fi fee per person each year, and parking in the city garage behind the building is $218 a month ([Travis Hyde listings](https://www.travishyde.com/availability)). Most Eddygate listings show a June 16, 2027 availability date.

**400 College Ave.** PPM Homes lists unit 204, a 481-square-foot furnished one-bedroom, at $2,000 a month, with heat, water and sewer included ([PPM Homes](https://www.ppmhomes.com/availability)).

## Is downtown luxury cheaper than Collegetown?

Mostly, yes. The exception is Theory Ithaca. Downtown sits about 0.6 to 0.9 miles from Ho Plaza in a straight line, and the walk back is uphill.

**Theory Ithaca, 445 E. State St.** It is the most expensive building in this roundup. Its listing on the [Ithaca College housing marketplace](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/listing) prints per-bed rents from $2,015.66 in a 3BR to $3,449.00 in a one-bedroom, with no lease year. Cornell's marketplace shows the same $2,015.66 to $3,449.00 range but says "call for price" on each plan ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/theory-ithaca-239997)). That listing describes private bathrooms, balconies, a 24-hour fitness center, a rooftop terrace and pet-friendly units, with 12-month, academic-term and short-term leases. Ask for a written quote with the lease year before you compare it with anything else.

**Ithaca Renting downtown.** Only its one-bedrooms cross the line. Commons West, 107 E. State St., lists a premium one-bedroom at $1,700 to $1,725 a bed, and 154 The Commons lists one-bedrooms at $1,525 to $1,625, both for 2026-27 ([Ithaca Renting downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)). Those are leftovers from the current lease year, so ask what the 2027-28 price will be.

Two more listings cross the line. PJ Apartments, 109 Dewitt Pl., lists a one-bedroom at $1,795 a bed, and a two-bedroom at 405 S. Albany St. lists at $1,550 a bed. Neither prints a lease year (Ithaca College marketplace).

## Which high-end buildings do not post prices?

Several of the buildings students think of as luxury publish no per-bed price you can compare. Call them before you rule them in or out.

- **Lux & Lofts, 201 College Ave.** 106 units across The Lux on Summit Street and The Lofts on College Avenue, one to five bedrooms. Every plan says "ask" ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/lux-lofts-236685)).
- **Collegetown Terrace, 115 S. Quarry St.** The listing shows "price from $605 to $2,265" with no lease year and no per-bed or per-unit label ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-terrace-236694)).
- **The Outlook, 815 S. Aurora St.** On South Hill, 1.26 miles from Ho Plaza, with a resident shuttle. The listing shows $1,800 to $3,700 with no basis printed ([Cornell marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/the-outlook-240145)).
- **Catherine Commons, 210 College Ave.** A 2025 Sun column put higher-end Collegetown rents at "$1,450 for a three or four bedroom to upwards of $2,300 for a studio" without naming buildings ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/maia-on-the-mic-the-collegetown-housing-hustle)).

## What should you check before signing a luxury lease?

Higher rent does not change the rules. In New York, a security deposit cannot be more than one month's rent, and the landlord must return it with an itemized list within 14 days after you move out ([New York General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). At Dryden South's Studio 102, the most a landlord can hold is $2,190.

Then check three things that vary by building:

1. **What the rent covers.** Ithaca Renting includes heat; Eddygate includes water; Dryden South's studios include neither heat nor electricity. Write down every monthly and yearly fee.
2. **The lease dates.** Dryden South starts June 1, The Ivy started Aug. 17 and Ithaca Renting sets a date per apartment. Two buildings with the same monthly rent can cost different amounts for the months you live there.
3. **The city's safety certificate.** Look up the address on the [Cornell and City of Ithaca safety lookup](https://safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu/), which shows whether a building's Certificate of Compliance, the city's periodic safety inspection permit, is current. Ask the landlord for the current certificate before you sign.

## FAQ

**Can I rent a luxury studio for one semester?**
Yes, at Ithaca Renting. Collegetown Center lists a spring 2027 semester studio at $1,665 to $1,800 a bed, and Collegetown Court lists fall and spring 2027 semester studios at $1,635 to $1,735. Theory Ithaca's listing also offers academic-term and short-term leases.

**When do these buildings fill up?**
Early: The Ivy, Dryden South and Ithaca Renting's Collegetown list were all filling for 2027-28 by November 2026 (details above). For the full calendar and when waiting is safe, see [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).

## What to do next

Decide first whether you will share, since the two-bedroom ceiling above is where the tier begins. If you want to live alone, price Collegetown Court's studios first, then call Lux & Lofts and Collegetown Terrace for written per-bed quotes before you commit to The Ivy or Collegetown Center.
