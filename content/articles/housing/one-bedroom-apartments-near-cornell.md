```glance
- Collegetown's 2027-28 one-bedroom median at Ithaca Renting is $2,025 a month; downtown's is $400 lower.
- Fall Creek, Cornell Heights, Lansing and South Hill list one-bedrooms from $795 to $1,900.
- A one-bedroom has one bed, so its per-bed price is the whole rent.
- Newer buildings add costs: electricity, internet, trash and parking often sit outside the rent.
```

If you want a one-bedroom close to campus, expect to pay the most in Collegetown and the least in Fall Creek, Cornell Heights and the outer areas. Ithaca Renting's [2027-28 Collegetown list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) shows the highest one-bedroom prices of any area, and downtown runs lower.

## What does a one-bedroom cost in Collegetown?

The median of its 2027-28 one-bedroom rows is $2,025 a month, which is $24,300 over 12 months. ## Why is a one-bedroom so expensive per person?

A one-bedroom has no one to split the rent with. At Ithaca Renting, the 2027-28 Collegetown median falls as units get bigger: $1,246.25 per bed in a 4-bedroom and $800 in a 6-bedroom, per the [Collegetown list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). A bed in a 6-bedroom is $9,600 over 12 months, and the one-bedroom is $24,300. Cornell charges [$13,744 for a residence hall double](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) for the academic year.

So a one-bedroom suits a grad student, a couple or anyone who wants privacy and can pay for it. If cost matters more, a bed in a larger unit costs far less at the Collegetown medians.

## Can two people share a one-bedroom?

New York's roommate law lets a single-tenant lease cover the tenant, immediate family, one additional occupant and that occupant's dependent children, if the tenant or the tenant's spouse lives there as a primary residence, per [RPL 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F). The tenant must give the occupant's name within 30 days of move-in or of the landlord asking. Landlords can still limit occupancy to comply with law or code, so ask about the building's limit.

Cornell's graduate housing sets its own rule: Hasbrouck's partner and family units hold two to four people, with no more than two adults per unit, per the [grad housing page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing).

## When do one-bedroom leases start?

Start dates vary by building. Ithaca Renting's Collegetown leases run one year and start in June, July or August on set dates for each apartment. Most Ithaca leases end July 31, per [the Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), and Dryden South's current leases all end May 31, 2027. Individual listings use their own dates: the CSP Management units list openings from Jan. 8, 2027 to Aug. 12, 2027, and the Farm Street units listed 2026 starts.

Ask what end date a new lease carries, because a short first term can drop you back into the market in spring.

## How far is each area from campus?

Cornell puts Fall Creek at 0.5 to 1 mile, a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk with a frequent bus. Cornell Heights and Cayuga Heights run 1 to 4 miles. South Hill runs 1.5 to 3 miles, a 15-minute downhill walk to downtown and then transit. Lansing sits 5 to 10 miles out, where Cornell recommends a bus or a car, per its [neighborhoods page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods).

The two Farm Street listings say both bus stops are under a five-minute walk, with campus service about every 10 minutes during peak hours. Edgecliff sits on TCAT Route 10. The [guide to the three nearest areas](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) compares them for a one-bedroom renter.

## What should I check before I sign?

Compare total cost, not rent alone. Ask four questions of each listing.

**What is the real monthly cost?** Add electricity, internet, trash and water where they are extra. Farm Street adds $33 a month per person for water.

**What is the lease year?** Listings that print no year may show last year's price. Ask for the lease start and end dates in writing.

**Is it furnished?** Furnished one-bedrooms cost more to start and less to move into. Edgecliff, Kelvin Place and Farm Street 2W are furnished.

**How will I commute?** A cheaper rent in Fall Creek or on South Hill buys a bus ride or a longer walk, and the extra minutes repeat every school day. Use the calculator to weigh a cheaper rent against a longer trip.

## What to do next

Pick the neighborhood you can commute from, then shortlist two buildings. Ask each for a written quote with utilities, the lease dates and the deposit, which cannot exceed one month's rent under New York law.
