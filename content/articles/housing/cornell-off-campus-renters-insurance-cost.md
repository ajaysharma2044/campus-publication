```glance
- Cornell does not require renters insurance off campus; its off-campus checklist simply says to buy it.
- Whether your landlord requires it is in your lease. No public source says whether Collegetown landlords ask for proof.
- Ithaca and New York quotes run about $5 to $27 a month (about $60 to $328 a year), depending on the source and the coverage.
- GradGuard is Cornell's preferred insurer, sold through the Housing and Dining Portal; Cornell publishes no price for it.
- Greek house rent includes building insurance, not your belongings, so chapter-house residents need their own policy too.
- Liability cover matters most: your deposit is capped at one month's rent, and housemates share the bill for damage.
```

Cornell does not require renters insurance for off-campus housing, but its off-campus checklist tells renters to ["Purchase renter's insurance"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations), and your lease may require it. Ithaca quotes run from about [$5 a month for a basic policy for a student aged 18 to 24](https://www.safebutler.com/cheap-best-renters-insurance-ithaca-ny) to [$328 a year ($27 a month) for a fuller New York policy](https://www.insure.com/renters-insurance/average-renters-insurance-cost-in-new-york/). Buy one before move-in with personal liability included: it covers the two things a lease leaves on you, your belongings and damage you cause.

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
Open your lease and search for "insurance". If it names a minimum, buy at least that; if it says nothing, insurance is still your call, not your landlord's. Cornell's checklist says to buy a policy, and quotes in Ithaca run about $5 to $27 a month. The cover that protects your deposit is personal liability: GradGuard, Cornell's preferred insurer, includes [liability if you damage your residence](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships). On most local leases you and your housemates answer for each other's damage. The costly mistake is renewing for a senior year without checking that last year's policy renewed with it.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying the bills"
Cornell recommends renters insurance and names GradGuard, which covers [theft, fire, smoke, vandalism and more, on and off campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus). Cornell does not publish its price; Ithaca quotes run about $5 to $27 a month. Before you buy, ask your own insurer whether your home policy reaches your student's apartment. Then read the lease for an insurance clause. Joint liability means your child can owe for a housemate's damage, so check the policy includes personal liability. Cornell aid [does not cover the deposit](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), so a damage claim against it comes out of family money.
```

## Is renters insurance required off campus?

Not by Cornell. Its off-campus checklist lists ["Purchase renter's insurance"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations) as a step, the same way it lists reading the lease. No Cornell page sets it as a condition of living off campus.

Your landlord can require it. A lease is a contract, and an insurance clause in it binds you like the rent clause does. Read the lease before you sign; the [lease guide](/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) covers the other clauses to check.

Not confirmed: whether Collegetown or downtown landlords commonly require proof of renters insurance. No landlord page, news report or student account says either way.

Cornell's own advice is split across three pages, and none gives a price:

| Cornell page | What it says about insurance |
|---|---|
| [Off-campus checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations) | "Purchase renter's insurance" as a step; no requirement, no price |
| [Living off campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) | GradGuard is the preferred insurer, for students on and off campus; covers theft, fire, smoke, vandalism and more |
| [Housing vendor partnerships](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships) | GradGuard's features and sign-up in the Housing and Dining Portal; no premium shown |

### GradGuard, Cornell's preferred insurer

Cornell names [GradGuard College Renters Insurance](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) as its preferred insurer. The [vendor page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships) lists its features:

- no rate increase when you file a claim
- no sub-limits on electronics
- no depreciation penalty
- worldwide property coverage
- personal liability if you damage your residence
- sign-up in the Housing and Dining Portal

Not confirmed: GradGuard's premium for a Cornell student. Cornell publishes none. "Preferred" is Cornell's word, not a requirement, so a quote from any insurer is fine if it meets your lease.

## What a policy covers that your lease and your house don't

A lease protects the landlord. A renters policy protects you. Two gaps matter in Ithaca.

**Your belongings.** A landlord's building insurance pays for the building. Cornell's Greek housing page is explicit: chapter rent covers [housekeeping, utilities, internet, repairs and building insurance](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities), not personal belongings.

**Damage you cause.** New York caps a security deposit, plus any rent paid in advance, at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). A landlord may deduct [tenant-caused damage beyond normal wear](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits). Damage that costs more than the deposit is still owed. Cornell says most local leases bind housemates ["jointly and severally"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire), so each is responsible for the others' rent and damages for the term. Liability cover is the part of a policy that can pay that bill.

| Risk | Who pays without your own policy | What a renters policy adds |
|---|---|---|
| Fire or smoke damages the building | Landlord's or chapter's building insurance | Nothing needed |
| Fire, smoke or theft destroys your laptop and clothes | You | Property cover |
| You or a housemate damages the unit | Your deposit first, then you and your housemates | Personal liability (GradGuard lists it) |
| The City orders the building vacated | Landlord owes relocation pay under City rules | Depends on the policy; ask |

On the last row: in March 2026 the City ordered about 200 Asteri residents out of their downtown building on [March 4 and lifted the order March 20](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/asteri-s-aftermath-displacement-dangerous-conditions-and-a-predatory-cash-for-keys-scheme). Since a 2019 City Council vote, landlords owe displaced tenants [double the monthly rent, prorated per day](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/03/city-council-votes-to-stiffen-penalties-for-noncompliant-landlords). That is the landlord's duty, not your insurer's. The [vacate-order guide](/housing/ithaca-eviction-vacate-order-what-to-do/) covers it.

```reader "If you're in a co-op or Greek house"
Your house's insurance is not yours. Greek chapter rent covers [building insurance, not personal belongings](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities). Houses that hold second-years must carry [commercial liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) naming Cornell, which protects the house, not your laptop. GradGuard covers students on and off campus. Not confirmed: what the eight Cornell-owned co-ops' own insurance covers for residents; ask co-ops@cornell.edu or [607-255-3445](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing). The mistake is assuming that "insurance is in the rent" means your things.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Buy the policy before or on arrival, alongside the move-in inspection. If you signed a lease from abroad, sight unseen, read it again for an insurance clause and any minimum cover. GradGuard sells through the Housing and Dining Portal, which you reach with your NetID, and its vendor page lists [worldwide property coverage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships), which matters if you carry a laptop home over break. You do not need an SSN [to rent an apartment](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Not confirmed: whether other insurers ask for one. The costly mistake is assuming your landlord's insurance covers your things. It does not.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
About [95 percent of Cornell grad and professional students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) live in non-university housing, so this decision is yours from year one. Start the policy on your lease start date and match its term to a 12-month lease. If you live with a partner, ask the insurer whether one policy can cover you both. If you own more than a student's laptop and clothes, price the higher-cover quotes: [$328 a year buys $40,000 of property and $300,000 of liability](https://www.insure.com/renters-insurance/average-renters-insurance-cost-in-new-york/) in one New York average. The mistake is letting a policy lapse between leases.
```

```reader "If you're on a tight budget"
The cheapest Ithaca quotes are $5 and $10 a month for renters aged 18 to 24 ([SafeButler, September 2026](https://www.safebutler.com/cheap-best-renters-insurance-ithaca-ny)). That is $60 to $120 a year. Cornell aid works the same off campus: the Bursar stops billing housing and you get a [refund for living costs](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) if aid exceeds Bursar charges, so insurance comes out of that refund. Aid does not cover the deposit. If a housemate's party damages the unit, joint liability can put the bill on you beyond the deposit. A basic policy with liability cover is cheaper than one damage claim.
```

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming the landlord's insurance covers your things.** It covers the building. A fire leaves you replacing everything you owned out of pocket.
- **Assuming "insurance included" in Greek house rent means you.** Chapter rent covers [building insurance, not personal belongings](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities).
- **Buying property cover with no liability.** Damage beyond normal wear comes out of a deposit capped at one month's rent, then out of you and your housemates jointly.
- **Comparing quotes that measure different things.** A $5 basic quote and a $328 policy with $40,000 of property cover are not the same product.
- **Signing the lease before reading the insurance clause.** A required minimum is a lease term like the rent; missing it puts you in breach.
- **Letting the policy lapse at renewal.** A new 12-month lease does not renew your insurance for you.

## FAQ

**Does a Cornell dorm policy cover me off campus?** GradGuard, Cornell's preferred insurer, is offered for [students on and off campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus). Check that your policy's address moved with you.

**Do I need my own policy if my roommates have one?** Ask the roommate's insurer whether you are named on it. If you are not, assume you are not covered and buy your own.

**Can my landlord keep my deposit for damage instead?** Only for damage beyond normal wear, and the deposit plus any prepaid rent cannot exceed [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). The [deposit guide](/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) covers the 14-day return rule.

**Does renters insurance count in Cornell's cost of attendance?** It has no line of its own: the [2025-26 undergraduate list](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-to-attend) shows housing, food, books, personal and transportation. Off-campus aid refunds [living costs](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) when aid exceeds Bursar charges, and insurance comes out of that.

**Is GradGuard the cheapest option?** Unknown: Cornell publishes no GradGuard price. Its own blog gives an average of [$13 a month](https://gradguard.com/blog/?p=14522), above the cheapest Ithaca student quotes.
