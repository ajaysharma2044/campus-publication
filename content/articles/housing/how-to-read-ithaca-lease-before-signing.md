```glance
- A signed lease binds you before any money changes hands; get every promise in writing first.
- Page one: if there is a bolded line to initial waiving the City's 120-day renewal notice, ask why before you initial it.
- Deposit plus any prepaid "last month" cannot exceed one month's rent; application and cleaning fees are banned; a credit check costs $20 at most.
- Most leases make each housemate liable for the whole rent: in a 4BR at $1,246.25 a bed, one empty room costs the rest $14,955 a year.
- Check the sublet, renewal, snow, trash, parking and late-opening clauses; auto-renewal needs a reminder 15 to 30 days before your opt-out date.
- Free advice before you sign: the Cornell Law tenant hotline, 607-301-1560.
```

Read every clause before you sign, because a signed lease binds you even before money changes hands, as a state assistant attorney general told a [2012 Collegetown meeting](https://cornellsun.com/2012/09/11/new-york-state-official%e2%80%88landlords-students-must-be-reasonable-people/). The checks that cost students most are the 120-day waiver on page one, a deposit or fee above New York's caps, joint liability for the whole rent, and the sublet, renewal and late-opening clauses. Before you sign, call the free [Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline at 607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/).

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
You can sign for junior year, but not for the rest of sophomore year without an approved exemption: a student who skips the requirement [will still be billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing). Read three clauses hardest. First, the 120-day waiver box. Second, joint liability: if a housemate drops out, the rest pay. A group that shrinks from a 6BR to a 4BR pays $446.25 more a person a month, $5,355 a year, at the medians in [Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) (our arithmetic). Third, the sublet clause, if anyone may go abroad. Timing is on the [when-to-sign page](/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying or co-signing"
Expect first month's rent plus a deposit at signing; Cornell aid [does not cover deposits, first or last month's rent, or summer rent](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). At the 4BR median that is about $2,492.50 per person (our arithmetic). The deposit plus any prepaid rent must stay at one month or less. Read the joint liability clause as if a housemate could leave: each tenant can owe the full rent. Without a NetID, you can still ask the City Building Division (607-274-6508) whether the [Certificate of Compliance](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) is current. Not confirmed: whether Collegetown landlords require a parent guarantor, or on what terms.
```

```reader "If you're a junior or senior renewing or re-renting"
A renewal lease is a new lease; read it the same way. Check whether you initialed the 120-day waiver last time and whether this one asks again. A landlord who raises rent 5% or more owes [30, 60 or 90 days' notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C) by tenure, and the notice must say whether Good Cause covers the unit. The landlord [may top up your deposit](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants) to the new monthly rent. Seniors: check the end date. Most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), so a May graduate pays June and July or sublets.
```

```reader "If you're an international student signing from abroad"
You do not need an SSN: Cornell International Services says [you can rent an apartment without one](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). A request to prepay several months runs into the one-month cap if it is framed as a deposit or rent advance. Before wiring money for a unit you cannot see, check a City address with the Building Division and the [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments). Ask whether heat is included. Cornell Graduate School staff say a landlord dispute [does not affect your visa or standing](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). The hotline at 607-301-1560 [advises with no income or geographic limits](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources). Not confirmed: how Ithaca landlords handle a guarantor for a student with no US credit history.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student renting outside Collegetown"
Look at the address first. Cayuga Heights, Lansing, Varna and the Town of Ithaca parts of East Hill are outside the City. There, no 120-day rule, Certificate of Compliance check or Cornell [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) applies. State deposit, fee, sublet and roommate law still does. On a house, read the utilities clause closely and ask NYSEG for the heating history. If quiet matters, ask the landlord what share of tenants are undergraduates.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
A sophomore transfer cannot sign for the first Cornell year: all new transfers [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). A junior or senior transfer can sign once the exemption is approved, and must reapply each year. Arriving in summer, you are likely signing with people you met weeks ago, so the joint liability clause is your biggest risk. Check the start date on "available now" units and accept the move-in inspection.
```

## Who can sign, and where the City rules stop

First- and second-year undergraduates [should not sign a lease unless they have an approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) from Housing and Residential Life. Exemptions last one academic year, so a transfer or returning student reapplies each year.

Then check the address. The 120-day rule, the Certificate of Compliance and Cornell's [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) cover City of Ithaca rentals only. The Town of Ithaca, Lansing and Cayuga Heights are outside them. New York State law (deposits, fees, sublets, roommates) applies everywhere.

For a City address, ask the Building Division whether the [Certificate of Compliance](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) is current (607-274-6508). In 2018 the tenants at 117 Thurston Ave learned only after signing that theirs had [expired in November 2015](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/); they then waited 53 days for a fixed heater.

## The lease, clause by clause

Cornell's [lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) names the terms to find. Here they are with the rule that sits beside each one.

| Clause | What to look for | The rule beside it |
|---|---|---|
| Page one | A bolded line you initial to waive 120 days' notice | Valid only if written, bolded and initialed on page one (City of Ithaca) |
| Rent | Per person or per unit; due date; 10 or 12 months | No legal rule; the lease decides |
| Late fee | Amount and when it starts | Only after rent is more than 5 days late; at most $50 or 5% of rent, whichever is less |
| Deposit | Amount, where it is held, return date | Deposit plus any prepaid rent at most one month's rent; back within 14 days with an itemized list |
| Fees | Application, cleaning, "non-refundable" deposit | All banned; credit check at most $20 |
| Who is liable | "Jointly and severally" | Each tenant can owe the whole rent |
| Occupants | Names, maximum number | Tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the tenants on the lease |
| Sublet | Allowed, with consent, or banned | 4+ units: consent cannot be unreasonably withheld; 3 or fewer: the lease controls |
| Renewal | Auto-renewal and its opt-out date | Landlord must remind you in writing 15 to 30 days before the opt-out date |
| Entry and keys | Notice before entry; key charges | No entry right unless the lease reserves it; keys at most 110% of cost |
| Repairs | Who fixes what; emergency contact | Habitability cannot be waived |
| Snow, trash | Who shovels; who buys tags | The owner's duty under City code unless the lease shifts it |
| Parking | Included, extra, or none | Often extra; City permits go to residents, not landlords |
| Furniture | Inventory list | Photograph it at move-in |
| Start date | What happens if the unit is not ready | No rule; get the plan in writing |

## Page one: the 120-day waiver

In the City of Ithaca, a landlord must give current tenants [at least 120 days' written notice](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/) before renewing your lease, showing the unit or signing new tenants. The rule took effect May 31, 2022, and covers leases of nine months or longer, as reported.

The waiver is allowed only if it is "explicitly written, bolded and initialed" on the lease's first page. The Sun column by the rule's sponsor, Patrick Mehler, says [both parties must choose to waive](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/). He wrote that asking why you are waiving the days should be your first question about renting somewhere.

If you initial it, your landlord can renew, show or re-let your unit without the 120-day window. Leave it blank if you can, or get the reason in writing. The [120-day rule page](/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/) covers the counting and the city line.

## Rent: per person or per unit, and for how many months

Check whether the number is per bed or for the whole unit before you quote it to housemates. Two Sun pieces, from 2019 and 2022, pass on this tip; one is Nguyen's [2022 column](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/).

Example: The Ivy listed its 3BRs at [$5,500 to $5,800 a month](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026), a per-unit price. Split three ways, that is $1,833 to $1,933 per person a month (our arithmetic). Ithaca Renting quotes per bed: its 2027-28 Collegetown 4BRs have a median of $1,246.25 per bed a month.

Then check the term. Most Ithaca leases [expire July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing). A 12-month rent is not comparable to Cornell's [2026-27 double at $13,744](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) for the academic year, which equals $1,145 a month over 12 months (our arithmetic). Check what the rent includes too. Ithaca Renting includes heat and hot water, but tenants pay electricity at most of its Collegetown buildings, and parking is extra.

## The money due at signing

New York caps the deposit and bans most fees. The [deposit law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) says a security deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent; pet deposits count toward it. The [fee law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) bans application fees and caps a background check.

| Charge | Legal limit in New York | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Security deposit plus any "last month" | One month's rent in total | Refuse anything above it |
| Application or processing fee | Not allowed | Do not pay |
| Background or credit check | Actual cost or $20, whichever is less; waived with your own check from the past 30 days | Ask for the copy and invoice |
| Cleaning fee or deposit | Not allowed as a separate charge | Do not pay |
| Non-refundable deposit | Unlawful | Do not pay |
| Late fee | Only after 5 days; lesser of $50 or 5% of rent | Check the clause matches |
| Replacement key | At most 110% of actual cost | Check the clause |
| First month's rent | Normal at signing | Get a written receipt |

The [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) adds that non-refundable deposits are unlawful and a separate cleaning fee is prohibited. In buildings with six or more units, the deposit goes in a New York interest-bearing account and the [landlord must tell you the bank](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants) in writing. Replacement keys are capped at [110% of actual cost](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-I).

A demand for $1,246.25 each as a deposit plus $1,246.25 each as "last month" totals $9,970, twice the cap. First month's rent on top is normal: $1,246.25 each.

Accept the move-in inspection the landlord must offer after signing; defects you both list cannot come out of the deposit.

## Joint liability and who may live there

Cornell says most local leases bind housemates ["jointly and severally"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): each is responsible for the others' rent and damages for the whole term. A tenant who overpays can [sue a co-tenant for contribution](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease), but the landlord can collect from anyone on the lease.

Example: in that 4BR, one housemate leaves in August and pays nothing. The other three owe the missing $1,246.25 a month, $14,955 over 12 months (our arithmetic), until the landlord releases the leaver or the room is filled.

On occupants, New York's [roommate law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F) says that on a multiple-tenant lease, tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the number of tenants named. You must give an occupant's name within 30 days of move-in or of the landlord asking. More on groups is on the [roommates and joint liability page](/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/).

## Sublets, renewal and auto-renewal

The sublet clause matters if anyone may study abroad or leave for a summer. In a building with four or more units, you have a [legal right to sublet](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) with the landlord's written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. In a building of three or fewer units, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). A sublet fee is allowed only if the lease allows it and it is reasonable. You stay liable for your subtenant.

```tool ithaca-sublet-request-timeline```

For auto-renewal, the landlord must give written notice of the clause [15 to 30 days before your opt-out deadline](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal). Without a clause, an expired lease continues month to month unless proper notice is given.

At renewal, a landlord who will not renew, or raises rent 5% or more, owes [30, 60 or 90 days' notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C) depending on how long you have lived there. Ithaca [opted into Good Cause Eviction](https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections) in July 2024. In covered units, an increase above the lower of 10% or 5% plus CPI is [presumed unreasonable](https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction). Buildings with a certificate of occupancy from January 1, 2009 or later are [exempt for 30 years](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/214), which covers most new Collegetown buildings.

## Entry, repairs, snow, trash, parking and furniture

**Entry.** A landlord has [no right to enter](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access) if the lease does not reserve it, even for repairs, except in emergencies. Reasonable notice is often 24 hours. Look for the notice period in writing.

**Repairs.** Every New York lease carries a [warranty of habitability](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-B), and a clause waiving it is void. Find the emergency contact and the person who handles repairs.

**Snow.** City code makes the owner [clear the sidewalk](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance) the full paved width within 24 hours after a snowfall begins. A lease may pass that duty to you. The City can clear it and [bill at least $50 per plowing](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/faq?cat=46). If the lease makes you responsible, ask who pays that bill.

**Trash.** City trash needs a [$6.00 tag per can or bag](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags) from January 1, 2026, unless your landlord includes trash. Some leases pass on fees: the [209 Stewart Ave listing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095) splits a $20 county fee and $80 garbage fee a year among five tenants, $20 each (our arithmetic).

**Parking.** Ithaca Renting lists parking as extra. Your other options are the [Dryden Road Garage at $232 a month](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) or Cornell's B commuter permit at [$359.77 a year](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students). A City [residential permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) costs $45 a year where your block has one; landlords cannot get it for you.

**Furniture.** For a furnished unit, ask for an inventory list. Cornell's checklist says to send the landlord an inventory and photos after move-in.

## New buildings: the late-opening clause

Fontana Apartments at 327 Eddy St opened late in 2016 after five delays. Over 50 students with August 1 leases were [left without a home](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless); the landlord offered $30 a day or a room at the Trip Hotel. In August 2026, Cornell's 301 College Ave opened with residents reporting [no stoves, an unfinished lobby and construction from 7:30 a.m. six days a week](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed), per the Sun. That building is campus housing, not a lease, but the lesson carries.

If the building is new or under renovation, ask before you sign: what happens if the certificate of occupancy is late, where you stay, who pays, and whether rent stops. Get the answer into the lease.

## Common mistakes

- **Trusting a verbal promise.** Once signed, the lease binds you before you pay; a spoken promise about furniture or a start date may not count.
- **Initialing the 120-day waiver without asking why.** You give up the notice window before your unit is shown or re-let.
- **Paying a deposit plus "last month".** On a $4,985 4BR that can mean $9,970 up front instead of $4,985.
- **Signing jointly with a group that shrinks.** One empty room at the 4BR median costs the remaining housemates $14,955 a year.
- **Quoting a per-unit price as per person.** At The Ivy, $5,500 a month is $1,833 each for three.
- **Skipping the Certificate of Compliance check.** The 117 Thurston tenants found an expired certificate after signing, then waited 53 days for heat.
- **Assuming a new building opens on time.** Fontana's tenants got $30 a day or a hotel room.

## FAQ

**Can I negotiate a lease clause before I sign?** Yes. Any term is [negotiable before signing](https://cornellsun.com/2012/09/11/new-york-state-official%e2%80%88landlords-students-must-be-reasonable-people/), so ask for changes in writing and sign only the final version.

**Can a landlord charge me a fee to apply?** No. New York bars any payment for processing or accepting an application; a background check costs [at most $20](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A).

**When do I get my deposit back?** Within 14 days after you move out, with an itemized statement. A landlord who misses it [forfeits the right to keep any of it](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108).

**Does my landlord have to let me sublet?** In a building of four or more units, consent cannot be unreasonably withheld, and silence for 30 days counts as consent. In a smaller building, the lease controls.

**Do I need renters insurance?** Cornell's checklist says to [buy renter's insurance](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations). Whether your landlord requires proof depends on the lease, so look for an insurance clause.

**Can my housemate move a partner in?** Only within the roommate law: on a joint lease, tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the number of tenants named.
