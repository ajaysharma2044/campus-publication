```glance
- Read page one first: a bolded, initialed line can waive the City's 120-day renewal notice.
- Deposit plus any prepaid rent cannot exceed one month's rent, and application and cleaning fees are banned.
- Most leases make every housemate liable for the whole rent, so one empty room costs the rest.
- Free advice before you sign: the Cornell Law tenant hotline, 607-301-1560.
```

Read every clause before you sign, because a signed lease binds you even before money changes hands, as a state assistant attorney general told [a 2012 Collegetown meeting](https://cornellsun.com/2012/09/11/new-york-state-official%e2%80%88landlords-students-must-be-reasonable-people/). Start with page one, then the deposit and fees, the joint liability clause and the sublet clause. Free help is a call away at 607-301-1560.

```reader "If you're a sophomore signing for junior year"
You can sign for junior year, but not for the rest of sophomore year without an approved exemption. Read three clauses hardest: the page-one waiver, joint liability and the sublet clause if anyone may study abroad. Cornell's own license charges a juniors-and-seniors cancellation fee that climbs from $50 to $2,000 through the summer, so a private lease is the cheaper place to settle your plans only if the sublet clause lets you out.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying or co-signing"
Ask whether the lease binds you as well as your student. Joint liability means the landlord can collect the full rent from any one signer. Cornell's checklist tells renters to get written receipts for rent and deposits. Which Collegetown landlords demand a guarantor, and on what terms, is not published anywhere we can cite, so ask each landlord in writing before your student signs.
```

```reader "If you're an international student signing from abroad"
You do not need a Social Security number: Cornell Global Learning says you can [rent an apartment without one](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Before wiring money for a unit you cannot see, check a City address with the Building Division and ask whether heat is included. Cornell Graduate School staff say a landlord dispute does not affect your visa or standing, per [their renting tips](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/).
```

```reader "If you're a grad student renting outside Collegetown"
Look at the address first. The 120-day rule and the Certificate of Compliance apply only inside the City of Ithaca, so check which municipality a house in Cayuga Heights, Lansing or the Town of Ithaca sits in. State deposit, fee, sublet and roommate law applies everywhere.
```

This page is general information, not legal advice.

## Who can sign, and where the City rules stop

First- and second-year undergraduates [should not sign a lease unless they have an approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process) from Housing and Residential Life. Exemptions last one academic year, so a transfer or returning student reapplies each year, per the [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). A student who skips the requirement still gets billed for the full year.

Then check the address. The City of Ithaca's rental rules, including the Certificate of Compliance (the city's safety inspection permit for rentals), are a City of Ithaca program, so confirm the address is inside the city first. Cornell's [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) shows fire protection and smoke alarms for City rentals. To check a certificate, call the Building Division at [607-274-6508](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance).

The reason to check: tenants at 117 Thurston Ave learned after signing that their certificate had [expired in November 2015](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/). They then waited 53 days between their first complaint and a fixed heater.

## What should I look for in each clause?

Cornell's [lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease) lists the terms to find: rent, due date, late penalty, deposit, termination, sublet, parking, extra costs, restrictions, occupancy dates and renewal notice. The table pairs each with the rule beside it.

| Clause | What to look for | The rule beside it |
|---|---|---|
| Page one | A bolded line you initial to waive 120 days' notice | Valid only if written, bolded and initialed on page one |
| Rent | Per person or per unit; due date; term | The lease decides |
| Late fee | Amount and start date | Only after 5 days; the lesser of $50 or 5% of monthly rent |
| Deposit | Amount, where it is held, return date | One month's rent at most, with any prepaid rent; back in 14 days |
| Fees | Application, cleaning, "non-refundable" deposit | Application fees banned; credit check at most $20 |
| Who is liable | "Jointly and severally" | Each tenant can owe the whole rent |
| Occupants | Names, maximum number | Tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the tenants named |
| Sublet | Allowed, with consent, or banned | Four or more units: consent not unreasonably withheld |
| Renewal | Auto-renewal and opt-out date | Written reminder 15 to 30 days before the opt-out date |
| Entry and keys | Notice before entry; key charges | Keys at most 110% of cost |
| Repairs | Who fixes what | Habitability cannot be waived |
| Snow, trash, parking | Who shovels, buys tags, pays | The lease can shift City duties to you |

Source: the linked statutes and guides in the sections below.

## What is the page-one waiver?

In the City of Ithaca, a landlord must give current tenants at least 120 days' written notice before renewing a lease, showing the unit or signing new tenants, per [WSKG's report](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/). The rule passed April 6, 2022, and took effect May 31, 2022.

A lease can waive it only if the waiver is "explicitly written, bolded and initialed" on the first page. The rule's sponsor, Alderperson Patrick Mehler, wrote in the Sun that [both sides must choose to waive](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/), and that "why you are waiving the days" should be your first question. If you initial the line, your landlord can renew, show or re-let your unit without the notice window. Leave it blank if you can, or get the reason in writing. The [120-day rule page](/cornell/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/) explains the rule in full.

## Is the rent per person or per unit?

Check before you quote the number to housemates. A listing at [209 Stewart Ave](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095) shows $850 per room per month for a five-bedroom, which is $4,250 for the unit. Ithaca Renting also prices per bed: its 2027-28 Collegetown 4-bedroom median is [$1,246.25 per bed](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), or $4,985 for the unit.

Then check the term. A 12-month rent is not comparable to Cornell's [2026-27 double at $13,744](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) for the academic year, which works out to $1,145.33 a month over 12 months ($13,744 divided by 12). Ask what the rent includes. The 209 Stewart listing includes heat, water, electric and WiFi, and splits a $20 county fee and an $80 garbage fee among five tenants.

## What can a landlord charge at signing?

New York caps the deposit and bans most fees. The [deposit law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) says a security deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent, and pet deposits count toward it. The [fee law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) bans application fees and caps a background or credit check at the lesser of actual cost or $20.

| Charge | Legal limit in New York | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit plus "last month" | One month's rent in total | Refuse anything above it |
| Application or processing fee | Not allowed | Do not pay |
| Credit check | Lesser of actual cost or $20 | Ask for the copy and invoice |
| Cleaning fee or deposit | Prohibited as a separate charge | Do not pay |
| Late fee | After 5 days; lesser of $50 or 5% of rent | Check the clause matches |
| Replacement key | 110% of actual cost | Check the clause |

Source: [Cornell Law security deposit guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) and [RPL 235-i](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-I).

At the 4-bedroom median above, the deposit plus prepaid rent can total $4,985 for the unit under the [one-month cap](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). A demand for a deposit and a "last month" from each of four people totals $9,970, twice the cap. First month's rent on top is normal.

In buildings with six or more units, the landlord must [tell you the bank](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants) holding your interest-bearing deposit. After signing but before you move in, the landlord must [offer a move-in inspection](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and defects you both list cannot come out of the deposit. Your deposit is due back within 14 days of moving out with an itemized statement, or the landlord forfeits the right to keep any of it. The [deposit rules page](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) covers deductions.

## Who is liable if a housemate leaves?

Cornell says most local leases bind housemates ["jointly and severally"](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): each is responsible for the others' rent and damages for the whole term. A tenant who overpays can [sue a co-tenant](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease), but the landlord can collect from anyone on the lease.

In that 4-bedroom, if one housemate leaves and pays nothing, the other three owe the missing share every month. Over 12 months that is $14,955. The [roommates and joint liability page](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) covers how to protect yourself.

On occupants, New York's [roommate law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F) says a tenant must give an occupant's name soon after move-in or when the landlord asks. Occupants get no right to stay if the tenant leaves.

## Can I sublet, and what happens at renewal?

In a building with four or more units, you have a [right to sublet](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) with the landlord's written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. The request goes by certified mail and silence after the landlord's response window counts as consent. You stay liable for your subtenant. In a smaller building, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). The [sublet guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) walks through the request.

For auto-renewal, the landlord must give written notice of the clause [15 to 30 days before your opt-out deadline](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal).

At renewal, a landlord who will not renew, or who raises rent 5% or more, owes [30, 60 or 90 days' notice](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C) by how long you have lived there. Ithaca [opted into Good Cause Eviction](https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections) in July 2024. In covered units, a yearly increase above the lower of 10% or 5% plus the inflation rate is [presumed unreasonable](https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction). A building with a certificate of occupancy dated Jan. 1, 2009 or later is [exempt for 30 years](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/214). The [Good Cause guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-good-cause-eviction-rent-increase-students/) explains who is covered.

## What about entry, repairs, snow, trash and parking?

**Entry.** A landlord has [no right to enter](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access) if the lease does not reserve it, except in emergencies. Reasonable notice is often 24 hours, so look for the notice period in writing.

**Repairs.** Every New York lease carries a [warranty of habitability](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-B), and a clause waiving it is void.

**Snow and trash.** City code puts [sidewalk snow clearing](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance) on the owner within 24 hours after a snowfall begins, though a lease can pass the duty to you. City trash needs a [$6.00 tag per can or bag](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags), so ask whether the landlord includes trash.

**Parking.** Ithaca Renting sells parking as a separate full-year license. The City's [Dryden Road Garage](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/718/Dryden-Road-Garage) costs $232 a month.

## What if the building is new?

Fontana Apartments at 327 Eddy St opened late in 2016 after five delays. More than 50 students with Aug. 1 leases were [left without a home](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless), and the landlord offered $30 a day or a room at the Trip Hotel. Before you sign for a new or renovated building, ask what happens if the certificate of occupancy is late, where you stay, who pays and whether rent stops. Get the answer into the lease.

## In what order should I sign?

```figure

title: Signing an Ithaca lease, in order
rows:
  - label: Confirm you may sign (sophomores need an approved exemption)
  - label: For a City address, call the Building Division at 607-274-6508 about the certificate
  - label: Read page one for a bolded 120-day waiver and ask why it is there
  - label: Check rent per person or per unit, the term and what is included
  - label: Refuse application and cleaning fees and any deposit over one month's rent
  - label: Read the joint liability, sublet, renewal, snow, trash and parking clauses
  - label: Get every promise, including a late-opening plan, in writing
  - label: Sign, pay, and get written receipts
```

A lease with a written-changes clause generally bars oral changes, and an oral lease over one year is barred by the Statute of Frauds, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease). Off-Campus Living (607-255-2310) offers one-on-one consultations, but staff said in 2019 that it [does not represent students](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/) in landlord disputes.

## FAQ

**Can I negotiate a clause before I sign?** Yes. Ask for changes in writing and sign only the final version.

**Do I need renters insurance?** Cornell's [checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations) says to buy it. Whether your landlord requires proof depends on the lease, so look for an insurance clause.

**Can my housemate move a partner in?** Only within the roommate law above, and only if the lease allows the extra occupant.

## What to do next

Call 607-301-1560 with any clause you do not understand, then sign. The [Cornell Law hotline page](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/) has an intake form.
