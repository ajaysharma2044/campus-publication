```glance
- A room swap trades your assignment with another student's. It costs nothing.
- 2027-28 swap windows: Feb. 1 to 11 for rising juniors and seniors, June 21 to July 1 for all.
- Both students must agree, in three steps, or nothing changes.
- A regular room change during the year costs $100 and is never guaranteed.
```

When Cornell housing is full, a direct swap gets you a different room: you trade assignments with a student who wants yours, in the Housing and Dining Portal. For 2027-28, swaps are free and open twice: Feb. 1 to 11 for rising juniors and seniors, and June 21 to July 1 for all class years ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/room-change-processes-cornell)).

## What is a Cornell room swap?

A room swap is a trade of housing assignments between two students. You take their bed, and they take yours. Housing staff don't look for a space for you. The two of you find each other, and the portal records the trade.

That is why swaps keep working when the halls are full. A swap needs no empty bed, because nobody gains or loses a space. For 2026-27, Cornell told incoming students it would be full and would take no room change requests before arrival. It pointed anyone who wanted a different room to the direct swap, open to any student with a signed housing license from mid-June to mid-July ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/residential-programs/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process)). A housing license is the contract you sign for a campus room. It works like a lease but gives you fewer rights.

The swap is also the only legal way to hand your room to someone. The 2027-28 license bars you from letting anyone not assigned by the university share your room, and from trying to "assign or transfer" it yourself ([Academic Year Housing License 2027-2028](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). A private deal between friends, without the portal, breaks that rule.

## When can you swap rooms for 2027-28?

You can swap only inside the two windows above. Each opens and closes at 10 a.m. ([Room Change Processes](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/room-change-processes-cornell)).

The February window is only for rising juniors and seniors. To use it, you must have picked a room and signed your 2027-28 license during Fall Housing Selection, or have received a waitlist assignment before the swap opens. Fall Housing Selection ended with Open Room Selection on Oct. 8, 2026, and a waitlist opened right after it ([Fall Housing Selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)).

The summer window runs 10 days. Rising sophomores have rooms by then, because their Open Room Selection runs April 13 to 16, 2027 ([Spring Housing Selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). Incoming first-years get their housing confirmations starting in mid-June, so the summer window is their first and only swap chance before move-in.

## Who can you swap with?

You can swap only within your group. First-year students can swap only with other first-years. Sophomores, juniors, seniors and new transfer students can all swap with each other. A first-year can't trade a North Campus double for a sophomore's room on West Campus.

The rule follows Cornell's two-year residential requirement for first- and second-year students ([Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). If you're a second-year hoping to leave campus entirely, a swap won't do it. That takes an approved exemption, covered in our guide to [Cornell's residential requirement and exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## How do you request a direct swap?

You set up a swap in the Housing and Dining Portal, where a Room Swap link appears on the navigation bar during each window.

Source: Cornell Housing and Residential Life, Room Change Processes at Cornell.

A swap needs a request, the other student's acceptance, and then your own final acceptance. Until all three happen, both of you keep your original rooms. Listing your preferences doesn't commit you to anything. You can turn down any request you get, and you never have to finish a swap you started.

A swap happens only when someone wants exactly what you have, and Cornell doesn't guarantee one.

## What does a swap cost compared with a room change?

A swap is free in both 2027-28 windows. A room change, where housing staff move you into an open space, costs $100 once it is completed ([Room Change Processes](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/room-change-processes-cornell)).

A room change is slower and less certain. For 2026-27, the application is open Sept. 21 to Dec. 1, 2026, for fall, and from the day the February swap opens to May 1, 2027, for spring. If you applied in the fall, you don't need to apply again for spring. After you apply, you meet with your Residence Hall Director (RHD), the staff member who runs your building, or with your West Campus house office. Staff try mediation or other fixes first. Housing then offers moves based on when you applied, your situation, your preferences and what is open. If no email offer arrives, staff haven't found a space. Hasbrouck Apartments residents email housing@cornell.edu instead.

Timing matters for juniors and seniors. The February swap opens on the same day the cancellation fee rises from $50 to $500 ([Housing License Cancellation Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-license-cancellation-policy)). If you would rather leave campus than keep your current room, decide by Jan. 31. Waiting to see whether a swap works costs $450 more ($500 minus $50) if it fails and you cancel. Our [Cornell housing license cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) shows the fee for any date.

## What can go wrong with a room swap?

Three mistakes sink swaps, and Cornell can also move you on its own.

1. **Missing the window.** Each window closes at 10 a.m. on its last day.
2. **Not accepting the last step.** The student who sent the request has to accept once more after the other student says yes. Check the portal after your partner responds.
3. **Trading outside the portal.** Moving into a friend's room without a recorded swap breaks the license rule above. It also leaves you billed for the room you were assigned.

The 2027-28 license lets housing staff consolidate rooms, combining two half-empty rooms into one to free a whole room ([Academic Year Housing License 2027-2028](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). Staff try to keep you in the same building before move-in, and on the same floor after it. If your roommate leaves, staff first let you find a roommate yourself. After that, they can ask you to take a new roommate by a set date or move in with another resident.

## FAQ

**Can I swap rooms in the middle of the semester?**
Cornell's 2027 swap windows are in February and summer. During the year, you move through the room change application. Ask Housing Operations whether any in-term swap is available before you count on one.

**Does a swap change my housing bill?**
Under the 2027-28 license, you pay the housing charges for the room assigned to you, and they go on your Bursar account ([Academic Year Housing License 2027-2028](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). Before you accept a trade between room types, such as a double for a single, ask Housing Operations how your charges will change.

## Who do you contact about a swap?

Housing Operations answers swap questions at 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368 or housing@cornell.edu, open 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays ([Housing and Residential Life Directory](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211065419/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory)).
