```glance
- Ithaca Renting prices some furnished spring 2027 studios and rooms for the semester, not the year.
- Short-term leases exist, but most Ithaca leases run 12 months, so look for "short term" on the listing.
- A month-to-month tenancy ends with one month's notice, which is the cleanest way out.
- Spring transfers must live on campus first, so off-campus rules apply to you later.
```

For one semester, rent a furnished room or studio on a semester or short-term lease, or take a sublet, because a 12-month lease leaves you paying for months you are gone. Ithaca Renting posts [Spring Semester 2027 studios at Collegetown Center from $1,665 to $1,800](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=4).

```reader "If you're a spring transfer student"
Check whether you can live off campus at all. Cornell requires spring transfers to [live on campus for the incoming spring semester and the following academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). So a one-semester off-campus stay applies to you only with an exemption. The [transfer housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/) lists the exemptions and the dates.
```

```reader "If you're coming back from study away or an exchange"
Cornell's 2027-28 license lets students cancel for study away without a penalty if they tell Housing by [June 15, 2027 for fall or Dec. 1, 2027 for spring](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). Otherwise the fee is $500. If you leave a private lease instead, the leaving-early rules apply, covered in the [breaking a lease guide](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar or postdoc"
Cornell offers some visiting faculty and affiliates temporary housing in Hasbrouck Apartments as space allows, and [does not guarantee it](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/visiting-faculty-affiliate-campus-housing). Our [scholar housing guide](/cornell/housing/visiting-scholar-postdoc-furnished-housing-ithaca/) covers furnished options for stays of several months.
```

## How much does one semester cost?

A furnished studio for spring 2027 costs about as much as a Cornell residence hall double for the same semester. Cornell charges [$6,872 per semester for a double](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) in 2026-27. At Collegetown Center's spring studio price, four months at $1,665 per bed is $6,660, and at $1,800 it is $7,200. The four-month figure is an example, because Ithaca Renting prices spring rows per bed per month and prints no end date.

## Which kinds of lease fit a single semester?

Four kinds of arrangement fit a semester. Each moves the risk somewhere different.

**A semester lease.** Ithaca Renting's spring and fall rows are priced for the term, so you pay only for it. This is the lowest-risk choice, and the stock is thin. Plaza also lists a Fall Semester single room at $950 to $1,050 per bed.

**A short-term lease.** [Cayuga Place](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/cayuga-place-237022) lists 3-month short-term leases that end July 31, 2027. [Ravenwood Apartments](https://www.travishyde.com/west-campus/ravenwood) is furnished and sits one block from Cornell's West Campus; ask whether it offers a short-term lease. Expect a premium over the 12-month rate.

**A sublet.** You take over part of someone's lease. The leaver stays liable, and the landlord must agree. The [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers where sublets are posted. Semester sublets cluster in January and August, when students leave for study away.

**A roommate-matching lease.** Foundation Housing posted [Spring '27 leases for roommate matching](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-ny/listing/spring-27-and-full-year-leases-available-for-roommate-matching-foundation-housing-96176) on the Ithaca portal. The listing shows a price from $1,400 to $1,600, and individual units say Call For Price, so ask the landlord for the total, fees and deposit in writing.

## What else should the quote include?

Ask about four extras before you compare prices.

**Utilities.** Ithaca Renting's buildings include heat and hot water, and tenants pay electricity at most of them. At 407 College Ave, tenants pay electric, plus annual internet and trash fees per room.

**Furniture.** Ithaca Renting's Collegetown rows are furnished.

**Parking.** Ithaca Renting sells parking only as a separate full-year license, with no monthly or semester option. A student bringing a car for one term should plan another lot, or leave the car home.

**Deposit.** The cap and the return deadline apply to short leases too. New York allows a deposit plus any rent advance of one month's rent at most, and requires the balance back within 14 days of moving out, per the [deposit law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108).

## What if I can only find a 12-month lease?

You can leave early, but the cost depends on how. Cornell Law's tenant guide says [early termination by agreement is the simplest route](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease): get the move-out date and money owed in writing. Termination fees can be treated as liquidated damages and are not always enforceable. A landlord who loses a tenant early must also take reasonable steps to re-rent, per [RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E).

A tenant who stays after a fixed term ends continues month to month unless proper notice is given, per [Cornell Law's renewal guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal). A month-to-month tenancy needs about one month's notice to leave. If you only need a place for the last weeks, a month-to-month lease after a short term is cleaner than a 12-month commitment.

Expect to sign for the full term in a building like [Dryden South](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), whose current leases all run to May 31, 2027. Fairview Apartments lists 10-month leases for August 2027, which still leaves you paying for more than a semester.

## When should I start looking?

Start looking in December for a spring arrival, because that is when semester rows and sublets appear, per [Ithaca Renting's Spring/Fall Semester 2027 listings](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). Spring classes begin Jan. 25, 2027. The Collegetown rush for 12-month leases runs months ahead of August, and it has little room for a student who needs only one term. ## What to do next

1. Confirm that you may live off campus this term.
2. List your start and end dates, then ask each landlord for a semester or short-term quote in writing.
3. Ask what the quote includes: furniture, heat, electricity, internet and trash.
4. Get the exit terms in the lease before you pay.
