Pet owners have more choices in Ithaca than the dorm rules suggest. Collegetown Terrace lists cats and dogs as allowed ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/collegetown-terrace-236694)). Lansing West calls itself a cat-friendly community. Four downtown buildings ban pets, and most others charge monthly pet rent.

## Which Ithaca apartments allow pets?

Fourteen buildings below state a pet policy on their own pages or on Cornell's off-campus housing marketplace. The policies differ by species and size, so read the row for your animal before you tour.

Ask for the fee and any weight or breed rule in writing before you apply. The Student Agencies Building's FAQ links a 2024-2025 lease, so confirm its pet terms for the year you would sign.

## Which buildings ban pets?

Cityview, the Old Cigar Factory, the Ivy Building and Whiton Hall all say no pets. They are listed among the downtown buildings on [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)'s site, and their Cornell marketplace pages carry the ban ([Old Cigar Factory](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/the-old-cigar-factory-10219), [Whiton Hall](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/whiton-hall-10220), [Ivy Building](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/ivy-building-10216)). These are the cheapest bed prices downtown, so a pet owner who wants a low rent has to look elsewhere.

Individual landlords set their own rules too. Lambrou Real Estate lists no pets at its North Fulton Street and West Court Street apartments, and PPM Homes bans cats and dogs at its eight-bedroom house at 129 College Ave. (PPM Homes).

## What does pet rent cost?

Where a price is posted, it is in the table above. True Renew Homes lists $45 a month for a pet at its Columbia Street apartment ([Ithaca Rents](https://www.ithacarents.com/4564-furnished-1bdrm-apt-at-columbia-st.-just-a-20-min-walk-to-cornell)). Over a 12-month lease, that range adds $540.00 to $600.00 (12 months at $45.00 and at $50). The Student Agencies Building's first year costs $850.00 ($250 plus $600.00).

Fairview Apartments on East Hill includes every utility except parking and pets, so a pet there is an extra charge by design (Cornell marketplace listing).

## Do pet deposits count toward the deposit limit?

Yes. New York caps a security deposit, plus any rent advance, at one month's rent, and pet deposits count toward that cap ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). A landlord who wants one month's rent as a deposit cannot add a separate pet deposit on top. A monthly pet rent is a different charge from a deposit, and the cap does not name it. Our [security deposit guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-security-deposit-rules-tenants/) covers the full rule, and the checker below tests a lease's fees against it.

## Can you keep a pet in a Cornell dorm or co-op?

Co-op licenses for 2026-27 allow only small caged pets and aquariums under 10 gallons ([Co-op Housing terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/co-op-housing-2026-2027-terms-and-conditions)). Cats and dogs belong in an off-campus apartment.

## What should you check in the lease before you sign?

Cornell's own list of lease terms to check names pets twice: under extra costs and under restrictions ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease)). Get the species, weight and breed rules, the one-time fee, the monthly rent and any damage terms in the lease itself, not in an email. Our guide to [reading an Ithaca lease](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) shows where each clause sits.
