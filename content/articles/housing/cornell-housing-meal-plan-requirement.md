```glance
- Living in Cornell housing means carrying a Cornell Dining meal plan, in any class year.
- First-years and West Campus residents pay $7,604 for 2026-27 and cannot drop their plan.
- Upper-level students elsewhere on campus can choose a $7,056 plan instead, saving $548 a year.
- Co-ops, Greek chapter houses and off-campus apartments carry no Cornell Dining requirement.
```

Yes. Every Cornell undergraduate who lives in campus housing must enroll in a Cornell Dining meal plan, whatever their class year ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). The building sets the plan: first-years carry Unlimited, West Campus residents the House Meal Plan, and most other upper-level residents can pick a cheaper plan. Co-ops, Greek chapter houses and off-campus apartments require none.

```reader "If you're a first-year"
Your Unlimited plan is fixed, so the choice that matters is where you live as a sophomore. A co-op or a Greek house meets the two-year housing rule without a Cornell Dining plan. West Campus locks in the House Meal Plan; a North or South Campus hall leaves some room to trim. Look at co-ops early: each house runs its own selection, starting in early November and early February.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer"
If you live in a residence hall, you must have a meal plan, and new transfers live on campus for at least one academic year ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)). You start on Unlimited and can reduce only to Bear Traditional. You cannot cancel. Spring transfers carry the plan for their first spring and the full year after it. More on rooms and timing is in the [transfer housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-transfer-housing/).
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
The required plan is not always an out-of-pocket cost. Cornell says the Unlimited plan is fully covered by financial aid for eligible students ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). If your student moves to a cheaper plan or a co-op to save money, check how the aid package changes before they switch.
```

## Does Cornell require a meal plan if you live on campus?

Yes, and the rule follows the building, not the class year. Cornell's two-year residential requirement for first- and second-year students includes a required meal plan ([Cornell residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). But the dining rule reaches further. It covers every undergraduate who entered Cornell in fall 2021 or later and lives in on-campus housing, so a junior or senior in a residence hall carries a plan too.

Cornell Dining lists the housing that carries the requirement:

- North and South Campus residence halls
- The West Campus House System
- Program houses, Cornell's theme-based halls
- McGraw Place Residences
- The Townhouse Apartments

Cornell's stated reason is nutrition. The residential policy says students keep a meal plan to have access to proper nutrition and to guard against food insecurity, meaning not having reliable access to enough food.

The sophomore half of the rule is recent. All first-years and sophomores had to live in university or affiliated housing starting in fall 2022 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/)). Before that, some sophomores moved to Collegetown partly to escape the meal plan. One told the Sun, "Having an unlimited meal plan would have been a waste."

Every undergraduate in a South Campus hall needs a plan. Cornell applies the South Campus rule to students who entered before fall 2021 and the campus-wide rule to everyone who entered in fall 2021 or later, so every class year is covered ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

## Which meal plan do you have to buy?

Your building decides. Cornell enrolls you in Unlimited or the House Meal Plan when you sign a housing contract. Upper-level and transfer students outside West Campus can then reduce to Bear Traditional or, in Collegetown, the Collegetown Meal Plan.

| Where you live in 2026-27 | Plan you must carry | Can you change it? |
|---|---|---|
| First-year residence hall | Unlimited | No reducing or canceling |
| West Campus House System: Alice Cook, Carl Becker, Hans Bethe, William Keeton, Flora Rose | House Meal Plan | No change or cancel while you live there |
| Collegetown halls: 112 Edgemoor, Cascadilla Hall, Schuyler House, Sheldon Court, 301 College Avenue, 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive | Unlimited, or the Collegetown Meal Plan | Reduce to Collegetown by the deadline; no canceling |
| Other upper-level halls, program houses, Townhouses, McGraw Place | Unlimited, or Bear Traditional | Reduce to Bear Traditional by the deadline; no canceling |
| One of the eight co-ops | None from Cornell Dining | Some houses run their own plan |
| Greek chapter house | None from Cornell Dining | SFL Supplemental is optional |
| Off-campus apartment | None | Any plan is optional |

Source: [Cornell Dining terms and conditions, 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027) and Cornell Dining's undergraduate meal plan page.

Each plan buys a different mix of swipes and Big Red Bucks, the dollars for tax-free food at Cornell's cafés, grills and convenience stores, one Big Red Buck to $1 (Cornell Dining).

If you are a sophomore in a North Campus hall, confirm with Cornell Dining through its help ticket form, in writing, before the reduce deadline.

## What does the House Meal Plan mean on West Campus?

It means a fixed plan for as long as you live there. Every resident of the five houses is enrolled in the House Meal Plan and cannot change or cancel it. If you move from West into other campus housing during the year, you must pick a comparable plan.

West Campus is also hard to get as a sophomore. In past years, about 20% of rising sophomores were able to choose West Campus House System spaces ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/residential-programs/apply-housing/current-undergrad-application-process/room-selection-faqs)). Most live on North and South Campus instead.

The price has climbed from $6,434 a year in November 2021 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/)) to $7,604 for 2026-27: up $1,170, or 18.2%.

## What about the Collegetown halls and 301 College Avenue?

Residents of Cornell's Collegetown halls must carry a plan, even though Cornell runs no dining hall in Collegetown. You are enrolled in Unlimited when you sign, and you can switch to the Collegetown Meal Plan, formerly called the South Campus Meal Plan. Once on it, you cannot cancel it.

The Collegetown plan is the only one with City Bucks, a balance you spend at participating off-campus restaurants and food stores in Ithaca ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks/city-bucks-faqs)). The swipes still have to be used on campus. From Sheldon Court, above Collegetown Bagels, the nearest dining hall is about a 12-minute walk on West Campus ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court)).

301 College Avenue shows how the rule works at its edge. Cornell bought the building and opened it as campus housing in August 2026. Its units have kitchenettes with a refrigerator and microwave ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue)). They have no stoves, and residents told the Sun in September 2026 that they still had to enroll in a meal plan ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/301-college-ave-opens-for-student-housing-resident-describes-it-as-incomplete-rushed)). The 2026-27 dining terms list 301 College Avenue among the Collegetown halls whose residents must carry a plan ([Cornell Dining terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

## Do co-ops and Greek houses require a meal plan?

No. Both count as second-year housing under the residential rule, and neither is on Cornell Dining's list of housing that carries a plan. For how a sophomore gets in, see the [co-op and Greek house guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/).

**Co-ops.** Cornell owns eight co-ops, and four of them run their own house meal plans ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). These are house plans, cooked by residents, not Cornell Dining plans.

| Co-op | House meal plan |
|---|---|
| Triphammer Cooperative, 150 Triphammer Road | Mandatory; vegetarian dinners Sunday through Thursday, and each resident cooks one night a week |
| Prospect of Whitby, 228 Wait Avenue | All-vegetarian house meal plan |
| Watermargin, 103 McGraw Place | Vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes |
| 660 Stewart Avenue | Optional; dinners five nights a week with one cooking night, plus a mandatory food staples program |
| Redbud in Von Cramm Hall, Wait Avenue Cooperative (302 Wait Ave.), Wait Terrace (308 Wait Ave.), Wari Cooperative | No house meal plan |

Sources: [Triphammer](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/triphammer-cooperative), [Prospect of Whitby](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/prospect-whitby), [Watermargin](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/watermargin) and [660 Stewart Avenue](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/660-stewart-avenue) house pages, Cornell Student & Campus Life.

[Cornell's rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) lists co-op room charges; for a house's meal plan charge, email co-ops@cornell.edu or the house before you compare costs. 660 Stewart residents can also buy a Cornell Dining plan and eat at the West Campus houses next door.

**Greek chapter houses.** Cornell offers an optional SFL Supplemental plan, also called Bear Supplemental, with 127 meals and $500 Big Red Bucks per semester. Members living off campus can enroll. Members living on campus qualify only if their chapter requires out-of-house members to carry a plan of at least seven meals a week. Cornell Dining publishes two prices for it: $2,167.51 per semester ($4,335.02 a year) with its undergraduate plans, and $2,088.77 ($4,177.54) on the [SFL Supplemental page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/bear-supplemental). Budget for the higher figure and confirm the charge with Cornell Dining.

## What does a year of room and required meals cost?

A standard double plus the required plan comes to $20,850 to $21,398 for 2026-27. The double is $6,872 per semester, or $13,744 for the year ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## When can you change or cancel a meal plan?

Only by the semester deadline, and only if your housing allows it. A plan becomes binding when you sign the dining application or are automatically enrolled. It covers the full academic year and is billed in July and January. You can upgrade at any time; eligible students reduce in the Housing and Dining Portal.

First-year, sophomore and transfer students cannot cancel a plan. Moving mid-year does not get you out of one either. A student who moves into a co-op during the year cannot cancel, but can switch to a similar or smaller plan by the deadline. A student who joins a fraternity or sorority in the spring cannot cancel, but can switch to SFL Supplemental if the chapter qualifies.

Cornell can cancel a plan if you withdraw, take a leave, get a medical leave, or leave for an internship, graduation or study away. It can also cancel for other grounds it judges adequate. You ask through a written petition on the Cornell Dining help ticket form. Refunds are prorated by the week. New York State tax law bars credits for missed meals, including meals missed because of special dietary needs.

Cornell publishes no separate dietary or medical exemption from the requirement. A student with a medical diet should file a petition through Cornell Dining's help ticket form well before the Sept. 9 or Feb. 3 deadline and ask for a written answer.

## What if you live off campus?

No plan is required, and several are sold only to you: debit plans, which give a set number of meals per semester plus Big Red Bucks, and Bear Choice and Bear Basic, which on campus are limited to students enrolled before summer 2021 (Cornell Dining).

| Optional plan for off-campus students | What you get | 2026-27 price |
|---|---|---|
| Bear Choice | Up to 10 meals a week, $500 Big Red Bucks per semester | $3,075 per semester; $6,150 per year |
| Bear Basic | Up to 7 meals a week, $500 Big Red Bucks per semester | $2,489 per semester; $4,978 per year |
| Off-Campus Value | 45 meals and $150 Big Red Bucks per semester | $912.30 per semester; $1,824.60 per year |
| Flex 10/500 | 10 meals and $500 Big Red Bucks per semester | $674 per semester; $1,348 per year |
| Just Bucks | $500 Big Red Bucks per semester | $500 per semester; $1,000 per year |
| House Affiliate | 3 meals a week on West Campus, $100 Big Red Bucks per semester; only for former West Campus residents | $724 per semester; $1,448 per year |

Source: Cornell Dining, undergraduate meal plans. The $50 yearly fee applies to each.

Whether a plan beats cooking depends on how often you would eat on campus. ## Common myths about the meal plan rule

**"A kitchen gets you out of it."** The Townhouse Apartments and McGraw Place Residences have their own kitchens and are still on the list. So is 301 College Avenue.

**"Co-ops are campus housing, so they come with a Cornell plan."** They count toward the housing rule but carry no Cornell Dining requirement.

**"I can drop the plan once I move."** Not mid-year; the rules for moving are above.

**"An exemption from the housing rule is an exemption from the meal plan."** Only because you then live off campus. The meal plan rule attaches to campus housing, so an exempt student who moves off campus owes no plan. The exemptions themselves are in the [residential requirement guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).
