Akwe:kon is a 35-resident Cornell program house on North Campus, opened in 1991, with singles and doubles and no air conditioning. A program house is a themed residence hall organized around a shared community. Rooms cost the same as any hall: $13,744 for a double in 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is Akwe:kon?

It is one of 13 program houses on Cornell's current list ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upperlevel-undergraduate-campus-housing)). It is among the smallest, with 35 residents. It opened in 1991. Rooms are singles and doubles, and none has air conditioning ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/akwekon)).

## What has been reported about Akwe:kon?

The Cornell Daily Sun reported on Dec. 2, 2025, that American Indian first-time first-year enrollment fell from 10 in 2023 to 2 in 2024 and 4 in 2025. Student leaders told the Sun that non-Indigenous students now fill overflow rooms. One student said guest rooms and a TV room were converted into student rooms. Housing and Residential Life told the paper that vacancies are filled to meet needs ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/even-if-our-numbers-are-lower-were-still-here-indigenous-student-engagement-persists-despite-enrollment-drops)).

If the community matters to you, ask the house staff who lives there now before you list it.

## What does Akwe:kon cost?

Program houses share the residence hall rate table, so the room price depends on the room type and not on the house. Every undergraduate in a program house must also buy a meal plan. First-year students take Unlimited at $7,604 for the year. Upper-level and transfer students can choose Bear Traditional at $7,056, and both carry a $50 yearly fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Per year | With cheapest allowed meal plan and fee, upper-level | With Unlimited and fee, first-year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double | $13,744 | $20,850 | $21,398 |
| Single | $15,566 | $22,672 | $23,220 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

Cheaper Cornell options exist. Double rooms across the eight co-ops run $6,974 to $9,962 for two semesters, and the dataset lists each one ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). ## How do you get into Akwe:kon?

New students list a program house on the housing application. They apply within 10 days of getting a NetID, and halls not chosen are assigned by random auto-allocation ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)). Rising sophomores can list up to three program houses. For 2027-28, the application closes April 4, 2027, and program house priority selection is April 12 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

At a March 10, 2026, town hall, administrators said most program houses have no formal application process. Placement uses preferences, availability, accommodations and roommate groups ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/bsu-town-hall-addresses-housing-financial-aid-and-federal-policy-concerns)).

A program house counts as on-campus housing, so living there meets the two-year residential requirement. Students who want to live off campus instead should check the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What should you watch before you sign?

Heat comes first, since there is no air conditioning. Cancellation comes second: the [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) turns your date into a fee.

Policy is the third item. A Department of Education letter dated Feb. 14, 2025, cited race in dormitories and set a Feb. 28 compliance date ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/02/bsu-holds-emergency-meeting-as-affinity-groups-program-houses-are-threatened-by-department-of-education-guidelines)). At the March 2026 town hall, President Michael I. Kotlikoff said the federal settlement changes no policies.

Move-out uses the Akwe:kon lot for loading ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/important-loading-and-parking-information)). The sources cited here list no move-in unloading zone for Akwe:kon, so check the [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) and your move-in email.

## What to do next

Decide on your room type, price it with the meal plan, and ask Housing how many Akwe:kon rooms are open.
