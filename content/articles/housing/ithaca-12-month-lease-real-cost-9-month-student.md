```glance
- Three empty summer months on a 12-month lease cost $3,738.75 at the Collegetown 4BR median.
- Cornell's $13,744 double equals $1,145.33 a month over 12 months, or $1,374.40 over 10.
- A June-July sublet at $750 a month brings that 4BR share to $13,455, under the double.
- A 10-month lease beats a 12-month one if its premium is under 20% and you can't sublet.
```

A 12-month Ithaca lease makes a student who leaves after spring term pay for about three months they never use. At the median Collegetown 4BR bed of $1,246.25 a month for 2027-28, that is $3,738.75, and your real cost per month lived rises to $1,661.67. A summer sublet or a 10-month lease can win most of it back ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## How many months do you actually pay for and not use?

Most Ithaca student leases run a full year on the landlord's dates. The listing for 209 Stewart Ave., for example, runs Aug. 1, 2026, to July 31, 2027 ([Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095)). Ithaca Renting, which runs many Collegetown buildings, writes one-year leases that start in June, July or August on a preset date that cannot be changed ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

A student here for the academic year lives in the apartment from late August to the end of spring term, about nine months. The other three months are June, July and the empty weeks at each end of the year.

Where those empty months fall depends on the start date. Dryden South, at 205 Dryden Road, starts every 2027-28 lease on June 1 ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/)). Its lease ends May 31, at the end of spring term, so the empty months come first, in the summer before you arrive.

## What do the empty months cost?

## How much does a summer sublet win back?

A June-July sublet wins back what your subtenant pays for those two months. In a sublet, someone else lives in your room and pays you rent while you stay on the lease, and your landlord still bills you the full rent.

Ithaca has no posted summer sublet prices, so treat the sublet rent as your own estimate. Modern Living Rentals advertises sublets "from $900 per person" without saying for what period ([Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/)). Test a few prices below your lease rate.

A 2BR share at the median can't get there through a sublet. Its gap to the double is $2,786 (16,530 - 13,744), or $1,393 a month for two months. That is more than the $1,377.50 rent itself. A 6BR bed at $800 already costs less than the double, so a sublet is a bonus ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

## What rules decide whether you can sublet?

The size of your building decides it. In a building with four or more apartments, New York law gives you the right to sublet with your landlord's advance written consent, and the landlord cannot refuse without a good reason ([N.Y. Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). In a house with three or fewer units, your lease controls subletting ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

The law sets the steps and the clock:

1. Send your request by certified mail with return receipt. Include the sublet dates, the subtenant's name and home and business addresses, your reason, where you will live, any roommate's or guarantor's written consent and a copy of the sublease.
2. Your landlord has 10 days from mailing to ask for more information.
3. Your landlord must answer within 30 days of the later of your request or the extra information.
4. If the landlord never answers, the law treats that as consent.
5. You stay responsible for the rent and any damage your subtenant causes.

Landlords add their own rules on top. Ithaca Renting requires its office to approve every Collegetown sublet first, allows one sublet term at a time, and bans sublets under one month, graduation weekend rentals and Airbnb. Its page lists a $15 sublet fee and a $20 fee when anyone uses the apartment over the summer, even if no money changes hands. Ask the office which fee applies to you. For its downtown buildings, each sublet must last at least two months and every other tenant on the lease must agree ([Ithaca Renting downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)).

## Is a 10-month lease cheaper?

A 10-month lease is cheaper when its monthly premium is smaller than the share of summer rent you would otherwise lose. A few Ithaca landlords offer them. Lambrou Real Estate leases its 24-bedroom house at 140 College Ave. for the 2027-28 school year on a 10-month lease, August to June ([Lambrou Real Estate](https://www.lambrourealestate.com/140-college-ave-24-bed)). Some apartments at 226 Linden Ave. come with 10- or 12-month leases, and the price varies by length ([Off-Campus Housing Marketplace listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/226-linden-avenue-8104)). Downtown, Cayuga Place on East Green Street offers a 12-month standard lease, with 10-month options "at an additional premium" ([Cayuga Place](https://livehhcayuga.com/cayuga-place/floor-plans)).

Ask each landlord for the 10-month price, since none of these pages posts it, and compare with this rule: a 10-month lease wins if its monthly rent, times 10, is less than your 12-month cost after any sublet income ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) for the 12-month baseline).

If you're sure you won't find a subtenant, a 10-month lease up to 20% more a month still saves money. If you can sublet easily at a good price, the 10-month premium has to be small to win. A 10-month lease also removes the risk of a subtenant who doesn't pay or damages the place ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

## How else can you cut the cost of the empty months?

Pick a bigger shared unit. The three empty months cost $2,400 in a 6BR at the median, against $6,075 for a 1BR alone. The [cheapest off-campus options near Cornell](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/) list the beds with the lowest rents.

Read the sublet clause before you sign. A lease that bans sublets in a house of three or fewer units locks in the full summer cost.

Don't plan to walk away at the end of spring term. Leaving early breaks the lease, and you would owe rent until the landlord re-rents your room. See [breaking a lease early in Ithaca](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/) before you try it.

## FAQ

### What if my subtenant stops paying?

You still owe your landlord the full rent, because the law keeps you responsible under a sublet. You can sue an unpaid subtenant for breach of contract in small claims court ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). Collect the summer rent before the sublet starts if you can.

### Is handing over the whole lease better than subletting?

Handing over the whole lease is called an assignment, and it ends your liability for the new tenant's rent and damage. Your landlord can refuse an assignment without giving a reason. If you don't plan to move back in, Cornell's law clinic recommends assigning over subletting.

### Can I get free help reading my lease's sublet clause?

Yes. The Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline at 607-301-1560 reviews leases for free ([Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). Call before you sign.

## What to do next

Find your lease's start date, end date and sublet clause. Multiply your monthly rent by the months you won't live there. If that number matters to your budget, mail your sublet request by April 15 ([N.Y. Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)), or ask your landlord for a 10-month price and compare it with the table above.
