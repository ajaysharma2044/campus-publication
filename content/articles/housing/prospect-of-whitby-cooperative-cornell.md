Prospect of Whitby is a Cornell-owned co-op at 228 Wait Ave. with 19 residents. Its house meal plan is all vegetarian. It takes transfer and upper-level undergraduates, at rates well below a residence hall (Cornell Housing).

## What is Prospect of Whitby?

A co-op is a university-owned house run by its residents. Cornell lists Whitby at 228 Wait Ave. with 19 residents, 7 singles and 6 doubles ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/prospect-whitby)). A Cornell feature says the house was founded in 1965 in the former Alpha Xi Delta sorority house ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/unexpected-delights-prospect-whitby)).

The same feature, from November 2023, describes a co-president who transferred in, lived first in Cascadilla Hall and moved to Whitby in January (Cornell Housing). That is one example of a mid-year move-in.

## What does Prospect of Whitby cost?

For 2026-27 a double is $4,318 per semester, or $8,636 for two semesters. A single is $4,889 to $5,213 per semester ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

A hall double costs $13,744 for the year, so the Whitby double is $5,108 less. Whitby is one of four houses that Cornell lists with a house meal plan ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). Cornell's pages do not give the plan's price, so email prospectofwhitby@cornell.edu before you compare totals. For leases, see [Is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/) and [cheapest off-campus housing near Cornell](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/).

## How do you get into Prospect of Whitby?

Each co-op runs its own selection, and selection starts in early November and early February (Cornell Housing). Write to co-ops@cornell.edu or call 607-255-3445 for Whitby's steps, since Cornell's house page does not list them.

Rising sophomores must state their co-op plan on the 2027-28 application by March 15, 2027 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). A co-op counts as affiliated housing, explained on our [affiliated housing page](/start/affiliated-housing/). To live off campus instead, check the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

Whitby does not list graduate students as eligible. Five other co-ops do: Wait Avenue, Wait Terrace, Triphammer, Watermargin and Redbud ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing/redbud-cooperative-von-cramm-hall)).

## What should you watch?

If you eat a vegetarian diet by choice, the meal plan is a plus. If you do not, ask how the plan handles other food before you commit.

All eight co-ops share one license, with no security deposit and a full-time student rule. See [Cornell co-ops compared](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-all-eight-compared/) and our guide to [co-ops and Greek houses](/cornell/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/).

## What to do next

Email prospectofwhitby@cornell.edu with questions about meals, rooms and selection.
