```glance
- First- and second-years must live in Cornell residence halls or approved affiliated housing (co-ops, recognized Greek houses) in 2026-27.
- Exempt: 21 or older by the start of the year, married, with legal dependents, veterans (not ROTC), junior or senior transfers, and commuters within 25 miles.
- Request the exemption in the Housing application while it is open; the 2026-27 application was due April 6, 2026.
- An exemption lasts one academic year. Reapply every year.
- Sign a lease without an approved exemption and Cornell still bills the full year: $13,744 for a 2026-27 double.
- Juniors and seniors are free to rent, so the junior-year lease you sign as a sophomore needs no exemption.
```

Yes: Cornell requires full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students to live in [university housing for two years](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), and year two can be a residence hall, a co-op or a recognized Greek house. You are exempt only if you are 21 by the start of the year, married, coming with legal dependents, a veteran, a junior or senior transfer, or a local commuter, and the exemption must be approved before you sign a lease. Students who skip that step [will still be billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing).

```reader "If you're a first-year"
You cannot rent off campus for sophomore year unless you hold an exemption. Your three choices are a residence hall ($13,744 for a 2026-27 double), one of the eight co-ops, or a recognized Greek house if you are a member when you file. File the Housing application by the April deadline (April 6 in 2026) even if you expect a chapter house. The costly mistake is signing a Collegetown lease for sophomore year with friends: you pay the rent and the full Cornell bill.
```

```reader "If you're a sophomore"
This year is settled: you live in Cornell or affiliated housing. Next year is not. Juniors are outside the rule, so a junior-year lease signed this fall needs no exemption. The rush for it runs now: on November 8, 2026, [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) already showed 14 of 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 sold out. If you keep a Cornell room as a backup, cancelling it as a returning junior costs $50 through January 31 and $500 from February 1.
```

```reader "If you're a first- or second-year who qualifies for an exemption"
You may rent like an upperclass student, but only after Housing approves the exemption. Request it in the Housing application while the exemption term is open; Cornell may ask for documents. Do not sign a Cornell housing license first, because requests are not accepted after one is signed. The exemption covers one academic year, so a sophomore exempt by age files again. ROTC does not count as military service.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer"
All new transfers [live on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Spring transfers stay for that spring plus the next academic year. You are exempt if you enter as a junior or senior, or if you lived on campus two years at your old school. A sophomore transfer cannot cancel a Cornell license unless exempt or accepted to a co-op, Greek or affiliated house in time. Exempt transfers reapply each year.
```

```reader "If you're a student with a spouse, partner or children"
Married undergraduates are not subject to the requirement, and neither are students coming to Ithaca with legal dependents. You still request the exemption in the Housing application, during the open period, and you request it again each year. Plan a family-size, 12-month lease after approval. For units, prices and who may live with you, see the [family housing guide](/housing/cornell-student-family-housing-ithaca/).
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
A first- or second-year's lease does not replace the Cornell bill. Without an approved exemption, Cornell bills the full year anyway, $13,744 for a 2026-27 double, on top of rent you may have guaranteed. Ask to see the approval before you pay a deposit. A junior-year lease is different: it can be signed in sophomore fall with no exemption, and that rush starts as soon as your student arrives in August.
```

```reader "If you're in a co-op or Greek house"
Both count as second-year housing. A sophomore must be a member of the fraternity or sorority when filing the Housing application, and must affirm living in by the March deadline (March 15 in 2026). File the April application anyway. A second-year accepted to a co-op or Greek house before the Spring Housing Selection deadline can cancel a Cornell license. Moving into a fraternity small residence does not release a spring contract.
```

## The rule: who it covers and where they can live

Cornell's [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) sets a two-year residential requirement for full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students. Living on campus comes with a required meal plan.

- **Year one:** first-year and transfer students who need the requirement are assigned to university residence halls.
- **Year two:** a residence hall, or approved university-affiliated housing. Cornell defines that as housing owned or controlled by a registered or recognized student organization, recognized fraternity and sorority houses, and the university-owned co-ops.

The sophomore half is recent. The Cornell Daily Sun reported that all first-years and sophomores had to live in university or affiliated housing [starting in fall 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/). Opponents quoted then wanted independence and lower costs.

Cornell's housing search page is blunt: first- and second-years [should not sign a lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process) unless they have an approved exemption. A signed lease does not release you from the requirement.

## Who is exempt

These are the exemptions on the [residential policy page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) for 2026-27, in Cornell's terms.

| Exemption | Who qualifies | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 21 or older by the start of the academic year of the exemption; or 16 or younger at the start of the year and living with a custodial parent or guardian all year | Turning 21 in October does not count for that year |
| Married | Married students are not subject to the requirement | File it anyway, every year |
| Dependents | Students coming to Ithaca with legal dependents | Documents may be required |
| Veteran | Served in active military, naval or air service and discharged under conditions other than dishonorable; includes international students who served in their home country's military | ROTC participants do not qualify |
| Transfer | Two years of on-campus residence at the previous school, or matriculating as a junior or senior | A sophomore transfer with less is not exempt |
| Commuter | Lived at least six months at a permanent residence with a custodial parent or guardian within 25 miles of campus | Housing may contact you to discuss it |

Anyone not on that list stays in Cornell or affiliated housing for two years.

## How to get an exemption approved

```figure
kind: steps
title: Getting an exemption, in order
rows:
  - {label: "Check the list: age, married, dependents, veteran (not ROTC), transfer, commuter"}
  - {label: "Log in to the Housing application and choose the exemption term when it opens"}
  - {label: "Upload any supporting documents Housing asks for"}
  - {label: "Do not sign a Cornell housing license while you wait"}
  - {label: "Sign a lease only after the approval arrives"}
  - {label: "Reapply next year: an exemption covers one academic year"}
```

1. **Request it in the Housing application.** You log in and apply for the exemption term when it is available for spring and fall. Housing accepts requests during the open application period. It does not accept them after you complete a signed housing license, or during the license period.
2. **Meet the deadline.** All first- and second-year students had to complete the 2026-27 Housing Application by [April 6, 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) through the Housing and Dining Portal, including students expecting to live in a chapter house.
3. **Expect a document request.** Supporting documentation may be required. For commuters, Housing and Residential Life may contact you to discuss it.
4. **Repeat every year.** Exemptions last a single academic year. Transfers and returning students must reapply for each year they want to be exempt.

Questions go to [Housing Operations](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory): 607-255-5368 or housing@cornell.edu.

Not confirmed: Cornell's pages give no separate exemption window for new transfers, and do not say whether a fall request can cover the next academic year.

## What signing first costs

The penalty is plain. A student who neither meets the requirement nor gets an exemption [will still be billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing). For 2026-27 a residence hall double is [$6,872 per semester, $13,744 for the year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month included.

Example: a non-exempt sophomore joins a Collegetown 4BR at the 2027-28 per-bed median of $1,246.25 a month on [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown list](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) and signs it for sophomore year. Our arithmetic: 12 months of rent is $14,955. Add the $13,744 double Cornell still bills, and the year costs $28,699 for one bed in use.

## Who can cancel a Cornell room, and what it costs

Under the [2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028), first-years, new transfers and second-years cannot cancel. The exceptions: they are no longer attending, they are exempt, or (second-years only) they were accepted to a Greek house, an affiliated house or one of the eight co-ops before the Spring Housing Selection deadline.

| Student | Can cancel? | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| First-year | Only if leaving Cornell or exempt | Not set out as a fee schedule |
| New transfer | Only if leaving Cornell or exempt | Not set out as a fee schedule |
| Second-year | Also if accepted to a co-op, Greek or affiliated house before the Spring Housing Selection deadline | Not set out as a fee schedule |
| Returning junior or senior | Yes | $50 through Jan 31; $500 Feb 1 to Mar 31; $1,000 Apr 1 to Jun 30; $2,000 Jul 1 to Aug 15; then 50% of the fall room rate |

A spring release is narrower still. Cornell's Greek life page says it is only for graduating, going abroad or leaving the university, and that moving off campus or into a fraternity small residence is [not an extenuating circumstance](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities).

## Co-ops and Greek houses as your second year

Both satisfy year two. The eight co-ops are Prospect of Whitby, 660 Stewart, 302 Wait Avenue, Triphammer Cooperative, Von Cramm, Wait Terrace, Wari Cooperative and Watermargin, and Cornell lists them as [second-year housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/qualified-off-campus-housing). Each house holds 10 to 35 members and runs its own selection, which starts in [early November and early February](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing) (co-ops@cornell.edu, 607-255-3445). For 2026-27, co-op singles run $3,949 to $6,014 and doubles $3,487 to $4,981 per semester, by house, on the [rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates).

Greek houses have three rules for sophomores on the [residential policy page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy):

- You must be a member of the chapter when you file the housing application.
- You affirm living in by March 15 (the 2026 date); confirmed members skip the April Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores.
- A chapter is auto-approved only if it meets the recognition policy and has a live-in advisor and four upper-level officers by January 1.

Costs and joining rules are on the [co-ops and Greek houses page](/housing/cornell-co-ops-greek-houses-cost-how-to-join/).

The exemption runs on Cornell's spring calendar. The Collegetown market does not. Room selection for 2026-27 ran [October 2 to 8, 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed), and popular Collegetown units for the next August go in the fall before.

That leaves a gap for an exempt sophomore. If you will turn 21 before sophomore year starts, the best Collegetown units for that year are leased while you are a first-year, months before the April deadline. Two ways through: wait for approval and shop the later market, or treat Cornell housing as the safe default. Off-Campus Living's manager called the idea that you must find housing early [a myth](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/), and downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing keep supply later. Timing by market is on [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).

Example: a first-year who took two gap years turns 21 in June 2027. That student qualifies by age for 2027-28, files in the spring 2027 Housing application, and signs a lease after approval. For 2028-29 the student is a junior and needs no exemption.

## What students say

In a 2021 Sun story, one sophomore decided to stay on campus and planned to join Rose Scholars in Flora Rose House; she said [Ithaca renting is really difficult](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/). The same story reported that only seven weeks into the fall semester many Cornellians had already signed leases for the next year. Staying in Cornell housing for year two is the rule, but for some it is also the easier path.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing a lease for sophomore year without approval.** Cornell bills the full year anyway: $13,744 for a 2026-27 double, plus your rent.
- **Signing a Cornell housing license, then asking for an exemption.** Requests are not accepted after the license is signed, and a second-year cannot cancel just to move off campus.
- **Skipping the April application because you expect a chapter house.** Every first- and second-year had to file by April 6, 2026.
- **Joining a chapter after you file.** A sophomore must be a member when the application goes in, or cannot live in the house.
- **Counting ROTC as military service.** It does not qualify for the veteran exemption.
- **Forgetting to reapply.** An exemption covers one academic year; next year you are back under the rule.
- **Keeping a backup room past January 31 as a rising junior.** The cancellation fee jumps from $50 to $500, then $1,000 on April 1.

## FAQ

**Can I join a fraternity or sorority in the spring and live in the house as a sophomore?** Only if you are a member when you file the Housing application, per the [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy); confirmed members then skip the April Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores.

**Does the rule apply to part-time students?** No. It covers full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students, per the [residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).

**Can a spring transfer rent off campus in their first fall?** No. Spring transfers live on campus for the incoming spring and the [following academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates), so the first lease is about 18 months out.

**Does a meal plan come with year two?** Living on campus includes a required meal plan. Four of the eight co-ops [have meal plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing); all of them count as year-two housing.

**Where do I ask about my own case?** Housing Operations, 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, housing@cornell.edu, weekdays 9:00 to 4:00.
