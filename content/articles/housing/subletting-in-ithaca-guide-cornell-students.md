```glance
- In buildings of 4 or more units, landlords cannot unreasonably refuse a sublet; in smaller houses, the lease decides.
- Mail your sublet request by certified mail at least 41 days before the subtenant moves in.
- A sublet leaves you liable for the subtenant's rent and damage; only an assignment or a release ends that.
- Two empty summer months in a Collegetown four-bedroom bed cost about $2,600 at 2027-28 rents.
```

In the fall of 2016, Nora Rabah had a spring semester in Copenhagen ahead of her and an Ithaca lease she could not leave behind. To go, she needed someone to take over the lease. She told The Cornell Daily Sun the trip depended on it. She found her subletter ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/20/students-housing-search-hampers-study-abroad)).

Not everyone does. Marina Markott, then the director of Cornell Abroad, told the paper that some students drop out of study abroad when they cannot find a subletter for their Collegetown apartment. She also said Cornell has no count of the families still paying rent on empty apartments while a student is overseas, and she called paying two rents "untenable" for most families (Cornell Daily Sun).

That is the problem a sublet solves. Most Ithaca leases end July 31, on a calendar built around the school year ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing)). Cornell students leave for summers, semesters abroad, engineering co-ops and jobs after graduation. A sublet, a short rental of your room to someone else while your own lease stays in force, fills the gap. New York gives tenants in larger buildings a strong legal right to one. In small houses, the lease is the only rule. Everything else comes down to timing, price and trust.

The stakes are real money. Cornell's residence halls closed at 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)). In 2018-19, Cornell counted 789 study abroad semesters among its undergraduates ([Cornell International Affairs](https://global.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/IA_YearInReview_20182019.pdf)). A bed in a Collegetown Plaza premium four-bedroom lists at $1,250 to $1,350 a month for 2027-28 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). At the $1,300 midpoint, five empty months cost $6,500. Cornell's financial aid office does not cover rent for months when you are not a registered student, and it will not step in when a student tries to break a lease mid-year ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)). And the money that changes hands draws scammers: renters reported a median loss of $1,000 to the Federal Trade Commission ([FTC](https://ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/12/rental-scams-hit-home-65-million-reported-losses)).

The step-by-step consent letter has its own page, [how to sublet an Ithaca apartment and get landlord consent](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/). For the whole off-campus picture, start with [the Cornell off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/).

## Why Ithaca has a sublet season

The mismatch starts at signing. On Nov. 10, 2026, as it leased for 2027-28, Ithaca Renting already marked 14 of the 67 Collegetown listings on its page as sold out (Ithaca Renting). The same day, Cornell opened applications for fall 2027 and full-year 2027-28 study abroad programs. First-round decisions come out Jan. 29, and second-round decisions roll out until March 11 ([Cornell Education Abroad](https://abroad.globallearning.cornell.edu/apply/deadlines)). A junior who signs a lease in November does not yet know where they will be next fall.

Cornell Abroad flagged the problem in 2016. Markott said her office stresses early housing plans at first-year information sessions, because leases can be signed well before abroad applications are due (Cornell Daily Sun, 2016).

Landlords set their own dates. Ithaca Renting's Collegetown leases run one year, starting in June, July or August, and each apartment's start and end dates are set in advance and cannot be changed (Ithaca Renting). At Dryden South, 205 Dryden Road, every lease starts June 1 and runs 12 months ([Dryden South](https://drydensouth.com/availability/)). A student who wants only the school year has to rent the full year and fill the rest.

Seasons shape who is looking. Markott said most Cornell students go abroad in spring. That puts more spring sublets on the market and helps students who leave in the fall, when fewer rooms compete for subtenants. One sophomore told the paper she expected to sublet for much less than her rent, because so many spring-abroad students would be competing with her (Cornell Daily Sun, 2016).

## The law: a right that took two tries

New York's sublet law is Real Property Law section 226-b. The state first passed it in 1975. That version covered tenants in buildings with four or more units. If a landlord unreasonably refused a sublet, the tenant's remedy was to be released from the lease. A 1981 law review article argued that this was all the law gave: a way out, not a right to sublet without consent ([Fordham Urban Law Journal](https://ir.lawnet.fordham.edu/ulj/vol9/iss3/16)).

The law on the books today goes further. In a building with four or more residential units, you can sublet with your landlord's written consent, given in advance, and the landlord cannot unreasonably withhold it. You ask by certified mail, return receipt requested. The landlord can ask for more information within 10 days. The landlord must answer within 30 days of your request or of the extra information, whichever is later, and silence counts as a yes. If the refusal is unreasonable, you can sublet anyway, and you can recover your costs and attorney fees if the landlord acted in bad faith ([N.Y. Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).

Three more parts of the statute matter to students. A lease clause that waives these rights is void. A sublet that skips the steps counts as a substantial breach of your lease, which is a serious enough violation to put your tenancy at risk. And the law does not cover public housing or co-op apartments owned through shares (RPL 226-b).

Below four units, the statute drops away. In a building with three or fewer residential units, your lease controls whether you can sublet ([Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy Program](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). Count every apartment at your address, not just yours, before you decide which rules apply.

An assignment works differently. With an assignment, someone else takes over your whole lease and you step out of it. The statute lets a landlord refuse an assignment without giving a reason. If the refusal is unreasonable, the landlord must release you from the lease on a 30-day notice from you, and that release is your only remedy (RPL 226-b). The Cornell Law guide's summary says a landlord cannot unreasonably deny a request to sublet or assign. The statute's own text is narrower for assignments, so go by the statute: expect that a landlord can say no to an assignment.

A 2019 law changed what happens when no sublet comes through. New York's Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act, signed June 14, 2019, made landlords try to re-rent an apartment before chasing a former tenant for the remaining rent ([Legal Assistance of Western New York](https://www.lawny.org/node/393/new-protections-ny-state-renters)). The Cornell Law guide says the landlord must make documented, good-faith efforts to re-rent at the market rate or your rate, whichever is lower. Once the landlord re-rents, your lease ends and you owe no more rent (Cornell Law guide).

The law also limits what a sublet can cost you in fees. A landlord can charge for a background or credit check on your subtenant, capped at the actual cost or $20, whichever is less. The landlord must waive it if the subtenant brings a check done in the past 30 days ([N.Y. Real Property Law 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)). Any other sublet fee is allowed only if your lease lists it, and it must be reasonable (Cornell Law guide).

## Every way to cover a lease you will not use

A sublet is one of six routes. The right one depends on whether you are coming back, how big your building is and what your landlord will sign.

| Route | What it is | Can the landlord say no? | Who owes the rent while you are gone | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sublet, building of 4+ units | Someone rents your room for a set term; your lease stays in force | Only for a reasonable cause; silence after 30 days is consent | You, if the subtenant does not pay | A summer, a semester abroad, a co-op term |
| Sublet, house of 3 or fewer units | Same deal, under your lease's sublet clause | Yes, if the lease says so | You | Same, if the lease allows it |
| Assignment | Someone takes over your whole lease | Yes, without a reason; an unreasonable refusal releases you on 30 days' notice | The new tenant, not you | Leaving Ithaca for good |
| Early-termination agreement | You and the landlord sign a written end date and settle what is owed | Yes; it is a negotiation | Whatever the agreement says | Leaving for good, with a landlord who will deal |
| Moving out without a replacement | You leave; the landlord must try to re-rent | Not applicable | You, until the unit is re-rented at market or your rate | The last resort |
| Cancelling a Cornell housing license | Cornell releases your room; you cannot sublet it | Cornell decides; free by its study-away deadlines | Nobody, if you cancel on time | Juniors and seniors in Cornell housing |

Sources: [N.Y. Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B); [Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy Program, Breaking a Lease](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease); [Cornell Academic Year Housing License 2027-28](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028).

The Cornell Law guide calls a mutual termination agreement the most straightforward way out. It says to put the new move-out date in writing, along with whether either side owes the other money. A fixed termination fee can be treated as liquidated damages, a sum agreed in advance, and courts do not always enforce those (Cornell Law guide). For the full comparison of exits, read [breaking an Ithaca lease early](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).

## Season by season

### Summer

Summer is the biggest sublet season, and the math is simple. A lease that ends in late July means a student who leaves after spring exams still owes June and July. At the Collegetown Plaza midpoint above, that is two full months of rent for a room nobody lives in, priced out in the cost table below (Ithaca Renting; Cornell Daily Sun, 2024).

The landlord rules fit a two-month summer neatly. Ithaca Renting's downtown leases require at least two months per sublet, and both of its portfolios ban sublets shorter than a month (Ithaca Renting). A sublet that covers both summer months clears both limits.

Timing is the part people miss. The landlord gets 10 days to ask a question and a 30-day window to answer after you reply. Add one day for silence to become consent, and you need 41 days (10 + 30 + 1) (RPL 226-b). For a sublet that starts June 1, 2027, mail the request by April 21, 2027. The free tool below counts the dates for your own move-in day.

```tool ithaca-sublet-request-timeline```

Keep the power on. Ithaca Renting tells tenants who sublet, store things or return in the fall not to disconnect electricity, and its internet service runs for the full lease, sublets included (Ithaca Renting).

![College Avenue in Collegetown in summer](slot:summer)

Your graduation weekend is not a sublet. Ithaca Renting bans graduation rentals and Airbnb stays outright (Ithaca Renting, downtown). The City of Ithaca requires a separate short-term rental permit for that kind of use ([City of Ithaca Housing Inspections](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections)).

For students staying in Ithaca over the summer, the [summer in Ithaca start page](/start/summer-in-ithaca/) collects every guide for that stretch.

### A semester abroad

A semester abroad is the expensive case. A fall semester away, August through December, is five months of rent. A spring semester, January through the end of spring exams, is about five more. At the same Collegetown midpoint, either one costs the $6,500 above (Ithaca Renting).

The calendar also works against you. Fall 2027 first-round decisions arrive Jan. 29, 2027. Spring 2028 first-round decisions arrive July 13, 2027 (Cornell Education Abroad). If you apply in that first round, you will know by mid-July, which leaves time to mail a request well ahead of a January start.

Subtenants are wary of single semesters. Rabah told the Sun that potential subletters hesitated because they could not have the apartment for the whole year (Cornell Daily Sun, 2016). Landlords also sell single semesters: Ithaca Renting lists Collegetown studios by the semester for spring and fall 2027. Its Collegetown Court semester studio lists at $1,635 to $1,735, while the same building's standard studio on a 2027-28 lease lists at $1,565 to $1,640 (Ithaca Renting). That is $70 more at the low end and $95 more at the high end. A subletter can compare your price against the landlord's own semester rate, and so should you.

Your housemates are the other party to settle with. Cornell says most local leases bind housemates "jointly and severally," which means each of you is responsible for the others' rent and damages for the whole term. Its housemate questionnaire asks what happens when a housemate decides to study abroad ([Cornell Housemate Compatibility Questionnaire](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)). Answer it before anyone signs. The [joint liability guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) covers what each housemate owes if a share goes unpaid, and the [away-semester start page](/start/away-semester/) lists every guide for a term away.

### Co-op and internship terms

Cornell Engineering co-ops take a full fall or spring semester. Students must work full time, at least 37.5 hours a week, in a paid engineering job. Forms for spring 2027 co-ops are due Dec. 14, 2026 ([Cornell Career Services](https://career.cornell.edu/resources/engineering-co-operative-education-experience-co-op/)).

A co-op paycheck changes the math. The average co-op salary in 2022-23 was $6,078 a month, and about half of the surveyed Class of 2019 co-op students reported some housing help from their employer (Cornell Career Services). An unsublet Collegetown bed at the midpoint above is 21.4% of that average monthly pay ($1,300 / $6,078). You still owe it, on top of rent wherever you work.

If you hold a Cornell housing license, cancel it instead. Cornell lets students leave without penalty for study away, including the Engineering Co-op, an academic internship or Cornell in Washington. For 2026-27, the deadlines are June 15, 2026, for fall and Dec. 1, 2026, for spring. The 2027-28 license sets the same two dates a year later. Requests after those dates cost $500 ([Cornell 2026-27 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027); Cornell 2027-28 license). The co-op office describes the deadlines as late June and late November. The license dates are the binding ones, so go by them.

### Graduation and leaving for good

Seniors face the summer problem with no fall to come back to. The Cornell Law guide says assigning is the better choice if you do not plan to move back in, because an assignment ends your liability (Cornell Law guide). Ask early, because your landlord can refuse an assignment, and the release that follows an unreasonable refusal is not instant.

Cornell housing works differently for seniors. Graduating students in Cornell housing are told each spring of an extended stay through the day after Commencement, at no extra charge (Cornell 2027-28 license).

## Doing it right as the tenant

Read your lease's sublet clause first; the consent how-to linked above lists every item the request letter needs. Cornell's lease checklist lists the ability to sublet and the conditions for a sublet as terms to find before you sign ([Cornell, Signing the Lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/signing-lease)). Our guide to [reading an Ithaca lease before signing](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/) shows where the clause hides.

Then get your housemates on board. The Cornell Law guide lists co-tenant consent among the items your letter must cover, and the statute adds any guarantor (Cornell Law guide; RPL 226-b). Ithaca Renting requires every other tenant named on the lease to agree (Ithaca Renting, downtown).

Write the sublease down. Cornell's tenant page says subtenants should receive a written sublease setting out their rights and duties, and it notes that the original tenant can remain responsible ([Cornell Tenant Best Practices](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus)). Put the dates, rent, deposit, utilities and keys in it. Ithaca Renting asks that returned keys for a sublet be labeled with the subtenant's name and dates (Ithaca Renting, downtown).

Collect a deposit as if you were the landlord. New York caps a landlord's deposit at one month's rent and requires the balance or an itemized statement within 14 days of move-out ([N.Y. General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Whether that rule binds a tenant who sublets is a question for a lawyer. Following it costs you nothing and avoids a fight.

If the subtenant stops paying, you still owe the landlord. Your claim against the subtenant is a breach-of-contract case in small claims court or another local court (Cornell Law guide). The Tenants Advocacy hotline is 607-301-1560, and Cornell's Off-Campus Living office answers lease questions at 607-255-2310 (Cornell Law guide; Cornell, Signing the Lease).

## Taking a sublet

As a subtenant, your landlord is the tenant who sublets to you, and your right to stay depends on that tenant's lease. Check the channel and the paperwork before you pay.

Start with the channel. Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace lets only people who can prove a Cornell affiliation contact landlords and subletters, which Cornell says is for security. Cornell's Off-Campus Living email list, open to NetID holders, carries sublets too ([Cornell Rental Listings & Resources](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). Sublets were free to list on Cornell's service in 2019, when landlords paid $30 a listing ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/)).

Then check the paperwork. Ask how many units the building has. In a building of four or more, ask for the landlord's written consent, or a dated copy of the request plus proof that the 30-day window passed. In a smaller house, ask to read the sublet clause. If the landlord is Ithaca Renting, ask to see the approved sublet form, since its Collegetown sublets are open only to Cornell students (Ithaca Renting).

Check the end date of the main lease too. At Dryden South, current leases end May 31, 2027 (Dryden South marketplace listing). No sublet can outlast the lease it comes from. The [May-to-August sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers the subtenant's summer search in full.

```reader "If you're coming to Ithaca for one semester or a short stay"
You have two markets, not one. A sublet is the cheap end if you find one through a Cornell channel. A landlord's own semester lease is the safe end: Ithaca Renting lists a spring 2027 single room at 210 Dryden Road at $695 to $795 and fall 2027 single rooms at Collegetown Plaza at $950 to $1,050. A semester lease puts your name on a real lease. A sublet leaves you depending on someone else's. Price both before you pay a deposit to anyone.
```

## Scams: the oldest trick in the sublet market

Ithaca has fought this for years. In May 2010, the Ithaca Times reported scammers copying real Craigslist rental listings, cutting the rent and asking for a deposit up front. Herb Dwyer, then president of the Landlords Association of Tompkins County, said newcomers from overseas who could not visit an apartment first were hit hardest. Landlord Deena Schwartz found her own house reposted with her photos, a new email address and the rent cut in half. Dwyer stopped using Craigslist and set up Ithacarents.com, where only association members can post ([Ithaca Times](https://www.ithaca.com/news/local_news/ithaca-landlords-tenants-battle-craigslist-rental-scams/article_fa700738-0769-5add-9329-897f05256e6d.html)). Cornell still lists Ithacarents.com and still notes that only association members can post there (Cornell Rental Listings & Resources).

Cornell police warned students in 2010 about fake listings in which a "landlord" asks for money wired to an overseas account. Then-Chief Kathy Zoner said the department saw few such reports from Cornell students each year ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2010](https://cornellsun.com/2010/12/01/police-warn-students-to-look-out-for-holiday-internet-scams/)). Ithaca police have warned of a version where the caller hears that many people want the apartment and must send money by Western Union at once to hold it ([WHCU](https://1037qcountry.com/news/025520-ithaca-police-warn-of-nyseg-craiglist-scams/)).

The national numbers have grown. From January 2020 through June 2025, people reported nearly 65,000 rental scams to the FTC, with about $65 million lost. In the 12 months ending June 2025, about half of the reported scams started with a fake Facebook ad and 16% with a fake Craigslist listing. People aged 18 to 29 were three times more likely than other adults to report losing money (FTC). The FTC names the common patterns:

- a demand for an application fee, deposit or first month's rent to "hold" a place before you have seen it;
- a request for a screenshot of your credit score, or a link to a $1 credit check that signs you up for a paid membership;
- a request for your Social Security number, license photo or pay stubs before you agree to rent;
- a listing copied from a real one, with new contact details and a rent far below the market;
- a "self-guided tour" with a lockbox code, so you never meet anyone.

Subletters face a mirror-image scam. In December 2023, University of Illinois police described a student who was subletting an apartment. A stranger sent a $3,850 check to cover the whole sublet, then said they had changed their mind and asked for the money back. The student sent it to two accounts, and the original check never cleared ([University of Illinois Division of Public Safety](https://police.illinois.edu/fake-sublessee/)). Wait for any check to clear fully before you return money to anyone.

Cornell's own advice is short: view a property in person and read the whole lease before you sign or pay (Cornell Rental Listings & Resources). Report a scam at ReportFraud.ftc.gov (FTC). The [sublet scams guide](/cornell/housing/sublet-scams-ithaca-how-to-avoid/) lists the checks to run before you pay and what to do if you lose money.

```reader "If you're an international student"
You are the renter the 2010 scams targeted: someone who cannot see the room before paying. Use only Cornell's marketplace or the Off-Campus Living list, where the other person has to prove a Cornell link. Ask a friend in Ithaca to see the room for you. Never wire money or send a Social Security number to hold a room you have not seen. If you are the one leaving, a subtenant who offers to overpay by check is a warning sign, not a bonus.
```

## What comes next

Three things will shape the sublet market over the next two years.

The first is the calendar collision. Collegetown leases for 2027-28 were selling before fall 2027 study abroad applications even opened (Ithaca Renting; Cornell Education Abroad). Unless one of those calendars moves, every junior who signs early will keep making a bet on where they will be.

The second is the semester lease. Ithaca Renting sells Collegetown studios and rooms by the semester. Each one is a room a short-stay renter can take straight from a landlord instead of from a student, so student sublets now compete with the landlord's own price.

The third is trust. Cornell's marketplace already limits contact to people with a Cornell affiliation, and Ithaca Renting's Collegetown sublets are open only to Cornell students. The open question is whether more of the market moves into closed channels like these, where both sides know who they are dealing with.
