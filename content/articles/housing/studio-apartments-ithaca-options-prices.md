## What does a studio cost in Collegetown?

[Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) prices Collegetown studios per bed for 2027-28. A studio holds one bed, so each figure below is the whole monthly rent. Court, Center, Plaza and Dryden South include heat, hot water and furniture.

| Building | Studio price per month, 2027-28 |
|---|---|
| Collegetown Court, 208 Dryden Rd | $1,565 to $1,640 standard; $1,645 to $1,715 premium |
| Collegetown Center, 151 Dryden Rd | $1,695 to $1,750 standard; $1,715 to $1,890 premium; $1,830 to $1,950 deluxe |
| Collegetown Plaza, 111 Dryden Rd | $1,725 to $1,825 premium; $1,755 to $1,775 deluxe |
| Dryden South, 205 Dryden Rd | $1,990 and $2,190 for two studios |

Source: [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) and the [Dryden South listing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961).

The median across seven priced Ithaca Renting studio rows is $1,765 a month, or $21,180 over 12 months. Cornell's 2026-27 double costs [$13,744 for the academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). The [2027-28 rent dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/) shows every row behind the median. For the 1-bedroom version of this question, see the [1-bedroom price guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/).

Property pages: [Court](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=5), [Center](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=4) and [Plaza](https://ithacarenting.com/property-details/?pid=1).

**407 College Ave.** [Travis Hyde Properties](https://www.travishyde.com/college-town/407%20college%20ave) lists furnished studios at $1,945 to $2,140 a month. Tenants pay electric, and the listing adds $240 for internet and $175 for trash a year per room.

**Newer towers.** Four buildings sit at the top of the market.

- [The Ivy](https://ithacavoice.org/2026/07/cornell-collegetown-construction/), 325 College Ave: studios from $2,595 a month, in 52 units and 81 beds.
- The Summit, 201 Oak Ave: studios and 1-bedrooms from $2,700, in 46 units.
- The Bear: studios from $2,850, in 84 units, [scheduled to open for the 2027 academic year](https://www.tompkinsweekly.com/news/three-new-developments-offer-different-answers-to-ithacas-housing-needs-30cfa148).
- [Catherine Commons](https://buildwelliver.com/projects/integrated-acquisition-development-corp-iad-catherine-commons-210-college-avenue), 210 College Ave: 356 apartments, 206 of them studios. Its listing posts a range of $1,615 to $2,479 and no per-unit prices.

Collegetown Terrace, at 115 South Quarry St, advertises [rates starting at $605](https://www.collegetownterrace.com/) with no period stated, and asks for a rate lock by Nov. 15, 2026. Ask which unit that price covers.

## What does a studio cost downtown?

Downtown is cheaper per bed. Ithaca Renting's median studio is [$1,395 downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), against the Collegetown median above, a difference of $370 a month, or $4,440 over 12 months. The downtown figures are mostly 2026-27 prices. The [rent comparison dataset](/datasets/collegetown-vs-downtown-rent-per-bed-2026/) shows the full gap.

| Building | Studio price per month (2026-27) | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Old Cigar Factory, 108 to 114 E State St | $1,175 to $1,275 budget | Heat, hot and cold water, trash |
| Colonial Building, 109 to 113 E State St | $1,225 to $1,325 budget | Heat, water, electricity, trash |
| Commons Studios, 212 E State St | $1,275 to $1,375 budget | Heat, water, electricity, trash |
| Commons West, 107 E State St | $1,295 to $1,495 standard | Heat, water, trash |
| Cityview, 118 Prospect St | $1,400 to $1,595 standard | Heat, water, trash |
| Carey Building, 314 to 320 E State St | $1,855 (unit 406) | Furnished; heat and water |

Source: [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), plus [Travis Hyde Properties](https://www.travishyde.com/downtown-ithacaf4ba8d70/carey%20building) for the Carey Building.

The three Ithaca Renting buildings that include electricity, per its [downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), are Colonial, Commons Studios and Whiton Hall. Downtown is a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk to campus, per Cornell, so factor in a bus pass or the commute tool below.

New downtown buildings charge more. [City Centre](https://citycentreithaca.com/), a Greystar building at 301 E. State St., lists studios at $2,475.75 to $2,618.75 a month on a 15-month term, utilities not included. The Ithacan at 210 E Green St lists studios at $2,549 to $2,699, and Ironworks at 430 W State St lists $2,072 to $3,943, both on the marketplace. Theory Ithaca, at 445 E State St, lists a Studio A at $2,789 per bed.

## What does a studio price leave out?

Check four extras before you compare. **Utilities:** the Collegetown Ithaca Renting buildings include heat and hot water, and tenants pay electricity at most of them, so an advertised studio rent is not the full monthly bill. **Fees:** New York's [fee law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A) bars application fees and caps a credit check at the lesser of its cost or $20, so decline a larger charge. **Parking:** Ithaca Renting sells parking only as a separate full-year license. **Term:** a 15-month lease at City Centre costs three months more than a 12-month one.

Cornell also owns studios. [301 College Ave](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/301-college-avenue) is campus housing, not a lease, with four floors of units from studios to six-bedrooms and shared laundry. It is open to transfer and upper-level students.

## How do I choose?

Match the studio to your budget and your commute.

**Pick Collegetown** if a short walk matters more than the $370 monthly premium over downtown. **Pick downtown** if you want a managed building with utilities included, and accept the bus. **Pick Highland Road or Lake Street** if you want the lowest rent within about a mile. Whatever you pick, check whether the price covers a 12-month lease or a shorter term, and whether electricity is in it. The commute tool weighs rent against travel.
