```glance
- Popular Collegetown units sign 8 to 16 months before an August move-in, mostly in September or October.
- On Nov 8, 2026, 14 of Ithaca Renting's 67 Collegetown rows were already marked sold out, including the big houses.
- Downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and grad housing keep supply into spring; grads look March to April.
- A backup Cornell room for junior year costs $50 to cancel through Jan 31, then $500 from Feb 1.
- Sophomores cannot sign for sophomore year without an approved exemption, or Cornell bills the full year ($13,744 for a double).
- Neither the 2013 nor the 2022 notice law moved the Collegetown signing window.
```

If you want a popular Collegetown unit, sign in September or October, about 10 to 11 months before an August move-in: every dated signing in the Cornell Daily Sun from 2017 to 2025 but one fallback came [8 to 16 months ahead](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/). Downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and graduate housing fill months later, so waiting into spring is fine there and usually cheaper. Whichever you choose, keep a Cornell room as a backup only until January 31, when cancelling still costs [$50](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028).

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
Decide group size and neighborhood first, then the date. For Collegetown, the window is September to November of sophomore year. A columnist who signed in October of sophomore year [called himself late](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/) by Collegetown standards. For downtown, Fall Creek or Lansing, February to April works. Before you sign early, check a City address's Certificate of Compliance with the [Building Division](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) (607-274-6508). Keep your Cornell room from fall selection until the lease is signed, then cancel by Jan 31 for $50. The costly mistake is a group thrown together in the first weeks of fall: every housemate is [liable for the whole rent](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) if one drops out.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year"
You cannot rent for sophomore year. Second-years live in a residence hall, a co-op or a recognized Greek house, and a student who signs a lease anyway is [still billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing). Your first lease starts in August after sophomore year. The Collegetown rush for it runs from September to November of sophomore year. Use this year to find people you would actually live with. The tip students repeat most is to sign only with people you have lived near, because groups formed in a hurry [shrink](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus).
```

```reader "If you're a transfer"
A sophomore transfer [lives on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). A spring transfer lives on campus for that spring and the following year, so the first lease is about 18 months away. A junior or senior transfer is exempt, must request the exemption each year, and arrives after the rush: in 2018, transfers learned [on June 22](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus) whether they had campus housing. Shop the leftover market in spring and summer: sublets, and downtown 2BR shares at a median of [$992.50 per bed](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) at Ithaca Renting. No NetID yet? Forward your acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu to join the housing email list. Do not sign with strangers in a hurry; each owes the whole rent.
```

```reader "If you're a first- or second-year with an exemption"
Get the exemption approved before you sign. First- and second-years should not sign a lease without one. You [request it in the Housing application](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy) during the open period, not after signing a housing license. For 2026-27 the deadline was April 6, 2026, and an exemption lasts one academic year. The Collegetown rush runs in the fall, before that spring deadline, so an exempt student who wants Collegetown faces a gap. Ask Housing about timing before you sign anything. Signing before approval means a bill for the full year: $13,744 for a 2026-27 double.
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
If the group wants Collegetown, money is due in September to November of sophomore year: usually the first month's rent plus a deposit. If your student holds a Cornell room as a backup, the last $50 cancellation day is Jan 31. New York caps the deposit plus any rent paid in advance at [one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Cornell aid [does not cover](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) the deposit, first and last month's rent, or summer rent. The costliest decision is a big group that later shrinks: the remaining tenants carry the rent. If your student can wait for downtown, the same landlord's per-bed prices run about 20% to 28% below Collegetown. Not confirmed: whether Collegetown landlords require a guarantor.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Your visa appointment [cannot be more than 120 days](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students) before your program starts, so you will likely sign from abroad. You do not need a Social Security number to rent. Undergrads follow the same calendar as everyone else; grads follow the March to April market. A 2021 Sun story reported that [virtual tours hid drawbacks](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/), so get the address and ask the City Building Division (607-274-6508) about its Certificate of Compliance before paying. Never send more than one month's rent in total for deposit and advance rent: New York caps the two together at one month.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
You can skip the undergrad rush. Start looking in March or April for an August lease; a few places [remain in June and July](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html). [GradCafe posters in 2014](https://forum.thegradcafe.com/topic/51153-cornell-english-phd-help-with-housing-pretty-please) said June was not too late, because year-long leases start at the end of July. Cornell's Hasbrouck apartments take rolling applications on 12-month contracts. About 95% of graduate and professional students live in non-university housing. The costly mistake is signing in the fall for a Collegetown 1BR at the $2,025 median, among mostly undergraduate neighbors.
```

## When Collegetown leases actually get signed

The Cornell Daily Sun holds the only dated record. Our count of first-person signings in Sun stories from 2017 to 2025 comes to 9, in 6 stories, with months counted to an August move-in.

|---|---|---|---|

| Nov 2018 | 10-bedroom house, North Quarry St | May 2017 | about 15 |

Apart from the one fallback, every signing falls 8 to 16 months ahead, mostly 10 to 11. It is one outlet, and reporters seek out early signers, so read it as the shape of the market, not a median. The rush has also crept earlier. In 2013 Cornell's assistant dean said signing had moved from January to [late October or November](https://cornellsun.com/2013/09/12/despite-new-law-housing-rush-persists-in-collegetown/), with the August and September push only four to six years old.

The current listings agree. On November 8, 2026, [Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) marked 14 of its 67 rows sold out (20.9%); 39 of the 67 rows were tagged 2027-28. The sold-out rows include the big houses (105 Dryden Court, 8 bedrooms; 214 Dryden Rd, 14 bedrooms), the Court deluxe 5BR, the Plaza deluxe 6BR and every whole-unit layout at 210 Dryden Rd. That is nine months before an August 2027 move-in.

The big houses go first. That matters because size sets the price: at Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown rates, our median of its listed ranges is $800 per bed for a 6BR, $1,246.25 for a 4BR, $1,377.50 for a 2BR and $2,025 for a 1BR alone.

## Is "no need to rush" wrong?

No. It describes a different market. In 2019 Off-Campus Living's manager called the idea that you must lock in housing the day you return [a myth](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/) that students and landlords both spread, and said students can find housing later. A developer said in 2016 that there is no single clearing date, though he usually rents most of his units [by the end of November](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/).

Both are true. Popular Collegetown units, big houses and new buildings go in the fall. The wider market keeps supply into spring and summer.

| Market | When to look | What is left late | Price on Nov 8, 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Popular Collegetown units and big houses | September to November, 10 to 11 months ahead | Few; 14 of 67 Ithaca Renting rows already sold out | 6BR $800, 4BR $1,246.25 per bed (2027-28 medians) |
| Other Collegetown units | Fall to winter | Studios, 1BRs, rooms in shared suites | Studio $1,765, room in a shared suite $895 per bed |
| Downtown | February to summer | "Available now" units at [Ithaca Renting downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) | 2BR $992.50, 3BR $795 per bed (mostly 2026-27) |
| Lansing complexes | Spring to summer, some mid-year | Move-in specials | [Lansing West](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx) 1BR "now" units at $1,038 and $1,193 |
| Grad and professional housing | March to April for August | A few places June to July | Varies by building |

Example: four sophomores want to live together for junior year. If they take a Collegetown 4BR in October, each pays about $1,246.25 a month at the 2027-28 median. If they wait until February and split two downtown 2BRs, each pays about $992.50 at the downtown median. That is $253.75 less a month, or $3,045 over a 12-month lease (our arithmetic). The trade is a [20 to 30 minute uphill walk](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) or a bus ride, and fewer units to choose from. If they hold Cornell rooms as a backup, cancelling in February costs $500 each.

## Why the notice laws did not move the window

Ithaca has tried twice to slow the rush. In April 2013 the City required [60 days' written notice](https://cornellsun.com/2013/09/12/despite-new-law-housing-rush-persists-in-collegetown/) before renewing, showing or signing new tenants. The clock started at the lease start, so for a June lease showings could begin in late August. The rush carried on.

In 2022 the City raised the notice to [120 days](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/), effective May 31, 2022. A landlord must give current tenants that notice before renewing, showing the unit or signing new tenants, unless the tenant initials a bolded waiver on the lease's first page. The rule's sponsor wrote that why you are waiving the days [should be your first question](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/). His examples count from the lease start: for an August lease, he put the renewal decision at about December.

The signing record shows no shift after either law. Ithaca Renting was marketing 2027-28 units nine months ahead in November 2026. Possible reasons, none confirmed: new or vacant units have no current tenant to notify, tenants may have initialed waivers, or the count starts differently than it seems.

Not confirmed: whether the 120 days count forward from the lease start or back from its end, and so the earliest date a landlord may market a City unit. No official earliest signing date exists for Collegetown. The rule is City of Ithaca code, so it does not reach the Town of Ithaca, Lansing or Cayuga Heights.

## The backup Cornell room and the January 31 line

Cornell's fall room selection for the next year ran [October 2 to 8 in 2025](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed), in the middle of the Collegetown rush. A rising junior or senior can hold that room while hunting. Cancelling gets more expensive as the year goes on, per the [2027-28 housing license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028):


The backup is cheap only if your lease is signed by January 31. If you plan to wait for downtown in February or later, budget $500 to cancel, or skip the backup. The room itself is the benchmark: a 2026-27 double costs [$13,744 a year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) with gas, electricity, garbage and internet, which equals $1,145 a month over a 12-month lease (our arithmetic). Rates shown at fall selection are projections; final 2027-28 rates are posted in March 2027.

First-years and sophomores are on a different footing. They cannot cancel a license unless they leave Cornell, are exempt, or (sophomores only) are accepted to a Greek house, an affiliated house or a co-op before the Spring Housing Selection deadline.

- **Late August to September.** Searching starts on arrival. Ithaca Renting opened 2020-21 leasing at 9 a.m. on [September 12, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/) after an overnight line.
- **October.** Prelims arrive, and landlords say rising juniors want to [sign before them](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/).
- **November and February.** The [co-ops](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing) run selection in early November and early February; each house runs its own.
- **February to April.** Downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing searches. In 2018 a group whose Greek plans fell through started hunting in February and found a 14-person house.
- **March to June.** The graduate market. Hasbrouck takes [rolling applications](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing).

## Common mistakes

- **Signing for sophomore year without an exemption.** Cornell bills the full year anyway: $13,744 for a 2026-27 double, on top of rent.
- **Keeping a backup Cornell room past January 31.** Cancelling costs $500 from February 1 and $2,000 from July 1.
- **Signing with a group formed in the first weeks of fall.** A Sun columnist wrote that large groups are [likely to lose people](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus), and each housemate on most local leases is [jointly and severally liable](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) for the whole rent. Dropping from a 6BR to a 4BR at the medians costs $446.25 a person a month, $5,355 a year (our arithmetic).
- **Paying Collegetown prices for a late leftover.** The same landlord's Collegetown per-bed prices run 25% to 39% above its downtown prices for studios, 1BRs and 2BRs (our comparison of medians).
- **Initialing the 120-day waiver in the rush without asking why.** You give up the notice before your unit is shown or re-let.
- **Counting on a new building to open on time.** Fontana, at 327 Eddy St, left [more than 50 students](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless) with August 1 leases in a hotel in 2016. Ask an unbuilt building what happens if it is late.

## FAQ

**What if I didn't get a Cornell room in fall selection?** After the October 2025 selection, Housing and Residential Life said it would offer [off-campus guidance and housing fairs](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed) to rising juniors and seniors without rooms. Downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing keep units into spring.

**Do prices drop if I wait?** Some late units carry specials. Lansing West offered rent free until January 2027 to applicants by November 14 who moved in by December 1, 2026. No source tracks Collegetown discounts.

**What do I pay at signing?** Expect the first month's rent and a deposit. The deposit plus any advance rent cannot exceed one month's rent, and New York bans [application fees](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A).

**Can a transfer join the Off-Campus Living email list before arriving?** Yes. An admitted student without a NetID forwards the acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu, per Cornell's [listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources).

**When did sophomores have to start living on campus?** The rule has covered sophomores [since fall 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/09/as-cornell-requires-university-housing-for-sophomores-students-weigh-options/). A lease signed for sophomore year without an approved exemption still brings the full-year bill.

**Can I renew my current apartment instead of hunting?** Yes, if the landlord offers it. In the City, the landlord must give you 120 days' written notice before renewing or showing your unit to others, unless you initialed the first-page waiver.
