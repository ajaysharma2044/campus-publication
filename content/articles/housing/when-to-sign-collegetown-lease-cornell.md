```glance
- Dated Collegetown signings run 8 to 16 months before an August move-in, mostly 10 to 11 months.
- On Nov. 8, 2026, 14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows were already sold out.
- Downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and grad housing keep supply into spring; grads look in March or April.
- A backup Cornell room costs $50 to cancel through Jan. 31, then $500 from Feb. 1.
- Camp-outs in 2012 and 2014 show the rush is old, and the 2013 and 2022 notice laws did not move it.
```

If you want a popular Collegetown unit for 2027-28, sign in the fall of 2026, about [10 to 11 months](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/) before an August move-in. Downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and graduate housing fill later, so waiting into spring is fine there. Whichever you choose, keep a Cornell room as a backup only until Jan. 31, when cancelling still costs $50.

## When do Collegetown leases actually get signed?

The Cornell Daily Sun holds the only dated record. Nine signings in six Sun stories, from 2017 to 2025, were counted by calendar month to an August move-in. Eight fall 8 to 16 months ahead, and five of the nine fall in September or October. The ninth is a fallback: a 14-person group whose Greek housing plans fell through began hunting in February of the move-in year ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2018](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)).

| Nov. 2018 | 10-bedroom house on North Quarry St., signed May 2017 | about 15 |
| [Sept. 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/) | Ithaca Renting opening day for 2020-21 leases | about 11 |
| [Oct. 2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/) | Students already searching for 2022-23 housing, story dated Oct. 13 | about 10, from the story date to August |

| [Dec. 2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026) | The Ivy: nearly all 52 units leased before completion | 8 or more |

The table shows six of the nine cases; all nine, with the method and a CSV, are in the [lead-times dataset](/datasets/collegetown-lease-signing-lead-times-2017-2025/). The sample is one outlet, and reporters seek out early signers, so read it as the shape of the market, not a median. The current listings agree. On Nov. 8, 2026, nine months before an August 2027 start, Ithaca Renting marked 14 of its 67 Collegetown rows as sold out (20.9%) ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Big houses go first, because one signature fills many beds.

## Is the rush really that early, or is it a myth?

Both are true. In 2019 Off-Campus Living's manager called the idea that you must sign the day you arrive a myth that students and landlords both spread ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/)). A developer said in 2016 that he usually rents most units by the end of November ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2016](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/)). Popular units, big houses and new buildings go in the fall. The wider market keeps supply for months.

The rush is older than either notice law. Around 15 students camped outside Ithaca Renting's Dryden Road office in the fall of 2012 ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2012](https://cornellsun.com/2012/10/01/as-collegetown-housing-rush-intensifies-students-sleep-outside-rental-agency/)), and more than 150 camped on Dryden Road in 2014 ([Cornell Daily Sun column, Aug. 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/)). In early September 2022, Student Agencies was among the first to open leasing for 2023-24 ([Cornell Daily Sun, Sept. 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/12/hurt-for-housing-begins-early-causing-stress-for-students/)). Ownership is concentrated too: in 2013, eight landlords owned about two-thirds of Collegetown's bedrooms ([Cornell Daily Sun, May 2013](https://cornellsun.com/2013/05/02/collegetown-housing-market-scrutinized/)), and the [who owns the buildings](/cornell/housing/collegetown-landlords-who-owns-the-buildings/) guide shows today's managers and owners. New buildings have not ended the early rush either: the [new construction guide](/cornell/housing/new-construction-collegetown-buildings-since-2015/) tracks what has opened since 2015, and The Ivy, which leased nearly all its units before it opened, is in the table above.

Say four sophomores want junior-year housing. At the [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) medians above, a Collegetown 4BR in October costs each about $1,246.25 a month, and two downtown 2BRs split four ways in February cost each about $992.50. The gap is $253.75 a month, or $3,045 over 12 months. The trade is a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk or a bus ride, and fewer units to choose from.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
Decide group size and neighborhood first, then the date. For Collegetown, the window is September to November of sophomore year. For downtown, Fall Creek or Lansing, February to April works. The [sophomore guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) lays out the whole year. Keep your Cornell room from fall selection until the lease is signed.
```

```reader "If you're a first-year"
You cannot rent for sophomore year without an approved exemption, and a student who signs anyway is still billed for the full year, $13,744 for a 2026-27 double. Your first lease starts in August after sophomore year, and the rush for it runs from September to November of sophomore year. Use this year to find housemates you have lived near.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer"
A sophomore transfer lives on campus for one academic year, and a junior or senior transfer is exempt but must request the exemption. Shop the leftover market in spring and summer, when sublets and downtown shares are open. If you have no NetID yet, forward your acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu to join the housing email list.
```

```reader "If you're a first- or second-year with an exemption"
Get the exemption approved before you sign. For 2027-28 the application and exemption deadline is April 4 at 11:59 p.m. ([Spring Housing Selection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)). The Collegetown rush runs in the fall, before that deadline, so ask Housing about timing before you sign anything.
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
Money is due at signing, usually the first month's rent plus a deposit. New York caps the deposit plus any rent paid in advance at one month's rent ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). Cornell aid does not cover deposits or first and last month's rent ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)). The costly mistake is a big group that later shrinks, because the people who stay carry the rent, as the [roommates guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) explains.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
Cornell's International Services says you do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment ([International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students)). Undergraduates follow the same calendar as everyone else. Get the address and ask the City Building Division, 607-274-6508, about its Certificate of Compliance before you pay anything.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Skip the undergraduate rush. Start looking in March or April for an August lease, with a few places left in June and July. Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments take rolling applications on 12-month contracts, and about 95% of graduate and professional students live off campus ([Graduate Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing)).
```

## Did Ithaca's notice laws change the timing?

No. A city law passed in April 2013 and in effect from June 15 required 60 days' written notice before renewing, showing or signing new tenants, and the rush carried on ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2013](https://cornellsun.com/2013/09/12/despite-new-law-housing-rush-persists-in-collegetown/)). The City raised the notice to 120 days effective May 31, 2022. A landlord must give current tenants that notice before renewing, showing the unit or signing new tenants, unless the tenant initials a bolded waiver on the first page of the lease ([WSKG](https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/)). Signings after 2022 fall in the same window as signings before it. The [120-day guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/) explains how the count works and what the waiver gives up. No official earliest signing date exists for Collegetown.

## What does a backup Cornell room cost to cancel?

Cornell's fall room selection for the next year ran Oct. 2 to 8 in 2025, in the middle of the Collegetown rush. A rising junior or senior can hold that room while hunting. Cancelling gets more expensive as the year goes on ([Academic Year Housing License 2027-2028](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)):

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Signing with a group formed in the first weeks of fall.** Dropping from a 6BR to a 4BR at the medians costs each person $446.25 a month, or $5,355 over 12 months (1,246.25 minus 800).
- **Keeping a backup room too long.** Cancelling costs more each time the fee schedule steps up, and the last step is $2,000.
- **Counting on a new building to open on time.** Fontana, at 327 Eddy St., left more than 50 students with Aug. 1 leases in a hotel in 2016 ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2016](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2016/09/delayed-collegetown-apartment-opening-leaves-e2-80-88students-homeless)).
- **Initialing the 120-day waiver without asking why.** You give up the notice before your unit is shown or re-let.

## What to do next

1. Choose your group size and neighborhood by October.
2. Tour and sign by November if you want Collegetown, or plan February to April for downtown.
3. Read the [off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/) for rules, costs and the full calendar.
