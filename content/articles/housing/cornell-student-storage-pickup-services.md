Four companies collect student belongings at Cornell: Big Red Shipping and Storage (BRSS), Storage Squad, Storage Scholars and Uncle Marty's. Storage Squad lists small items at [$36 for a four-month summer term](https://storagesquad.com/summer-storage/cornell-university/). Each company prices a different unit, so compare your own load, not the headline number.

## Which pickup storage service costs the least?

It depends on what you store. BRSS and Storage Squad charge once for the term. Uncle Marty's charges every month. Storage Scholars charges per item per month and sets a four-month minimum. The table shows the posted prices.

| Service | Posted prices | Term | What is included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big Red Shipping and Storage | Small box $39; large box $49; Blue Moving Bag $65; small item $49; medium $75; large $100; giant $150 | 3 to 4 months | Pickup, storage and delivery |
| Storage Squad | Small $36; standard $45 (includes a 24x17x15 Mega Box or cube fridge); large $65; extra-large $75; oversized adds $20 | 4 months | Free supplies; reservation fee not posted |
| Storage Scholars | $14 to $40 per item a month; boxes-only packages $150 to $300; full dorm room $300 to $500 | 4-month minimum | Pickup and delivery; surcharge up to $300 for moves off the school schedule |
| Uncle Marty's | $15 per regular item a month (up to 50 lb); $35 large; $60 giant or bicycle; $15 climate control | One-month minimum, then billed every two weeks | Free in-store drop-off; curbside pickup or delivery $25 for up to 10 items plus $2.50 each; $15 per access |

Source note: [BRSS price list](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) (no year printed), [Storage Squad](https://storagesquad.com/summer-storage/cornell-university/), Storage Scholars' [Ithaca page](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny) and [pricing blog](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing), [Uncle Marty's](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage).

Here is the math for one small box over a four-month summer. BRSS is $39 and Storage Squad is a little lower. Uncle Marty's charges [$15 a month for a regular item](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/storage), which is $60 over four months, before pickup and delivery. A full summer order is larger: Storage Scholars says a [typical order is about $915](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing), and Storage Squad says [its average student pays about $400](https://storagesquad.com/summer-storage/cornell-university/).

Posted base prices leave out some fees. BRSS lists extra charges for overweight, oversized and fragile items, late reschedules and missed appointments, and does not post the amounts. Ask each company for a total before you book.

## How does each service work?

**Big Red Shipping and Storage.** Cornell calls it the [contracted vendor](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options) for over 40 years. It offers free in-room pickup at a dorm, apartment or house and stores items in a climate-controlled facility ([Cornell Student and Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options)). You book at bigredboxes.com or call 607-524-3130. Cornell chose it by bid in 2003, when a small box cost [$15 for the summer](https://cornellsun.com/2003/05/02/students-seek-storage-options).

**Storage Squad.** Cornell students started it in Collegetown, and it has operated since 2011. In March 2020 it moved about 40,000 boxes for more than 2,000 students, and its founders sold it in January 2021 ([Cornell Chronicle](https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2021/04/ilr-alumni-sell-storage-business-seven-figures)). It offers a Room Return service for $750, covering up to 25 items.

**Storage Scholars.** A national company that serves more than 200 campuses. Its Ithaca page says it covers East Hill and Collegetown with pickup and delivery. Keyed residence-hall access is only at partner schools, so plan on meeting the driver.

**Uncle Marty's.** A printing and shipping store with locations at 206A Dryden Rd and 704 W. Buffalo St. A bicycle counts as a giant item at $60 a month, so a bike costs more here than a box.

## What does Cornell provide?

Cornell offers no storage and its housing staff cannot give pickup crews access to rooms, so [you must be present](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options). Items left behind after the buildings closed at 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026, were donated. BRSS offers "Free Box Days" to students who show proof of an appointment. Free storage for residents in 2020 was a [pandemic response](https://cornellsun.com/2020/03/13/local-storage-services-left-boxed-in-after-new-cornell-paid-solution/).

## When do you have to book?

Slots run out. [Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny) asks for bookings by early April. Uncle Marty's guarantees a delivery slot for [move-out bundles](https://www.unclemartysoffice.com/move-out-bundles) ordered before May 1. BRSS has limited slots, and it marks an order "confirmed" 48 hours before the appointment.

```reader "If you're an international student"
Shipping home is a different service. BRSS lists extra fees for international shipments and customs paperwork and does not post them, so ask for the amount in writing. If you fly home, the [airport and rides guide](/cornell/getting-around/flying-home-from-cornell-airports-rides/) covers the trip itself.
```

```reader "If you're keeping your lease through summer"
You may not need storage at all. If a subletter takes your room furnished, your things stay put. The [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers how to find one safely.
```

## Which service should you pick?

Pick BRSS or Storage Squad if you have a handful of boxes and one large item, because one price covers the term. Pick Uncle Marty's if your summer is short, since billing is monthly after a one-month minimum. Pick Storage Scholars if you store a whole dorm room and book early. The [move-out guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/) puts BRSS next to self-storage.

## What to do next

Count your boxes and sort large items by weight. Get a written total from two companies. Book by early April, and keep the confirmation. For winter break, the [breaks guide](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) covers who can stay, and the [before-break travel checklist](/tools/before-break-travel-checklist/) lists what to settle before you leave.
