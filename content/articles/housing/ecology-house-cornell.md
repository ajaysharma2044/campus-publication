A double costs $13,744 for 2026-27, the same as in any residence hall ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is Ecology House?

It is Cornell's environment-themed program house, a residence organized around a shared interest instead of a building type. The community was established in 1971. Its building, Hurlburt House, opened in 1953 as the Cornell Heights Residential Club and became student housing in 1966 ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/ecology-house)).

## What sets Ecology House apart from other program houses?

Its rooms. Most North Campus program houses have corridor rooms or clusters that share a bathroom. Ecology House is a corridor building where each room has a private bath ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/residence-hall-configurations)), and every room is a double. Roommates share a heating and air conditioning unit ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/ecology-house)).

The sources cited here do not describe Ecology House's events, facilities or distance to class. Check Cornell's page for those details, or email the house staff.

## What does Ecology House cost?

Program houses share the residence hall rate table. Since every Ecology House room is a double, the double rate is the one that applies. A meal plan is required: Unlimited at $7,604 for first-years, or Bear Traditional at $7,056 for upper-level and transfer students, plus a $50 yearly fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Student | Room per year | Meal plan per year | Year with fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-year | $13,744 | $7,604 | $21,398 |
| Upper-level or transfer | $13,744 | $7,056 | $20,850 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the plan and the $50 yearly fee to the room rate.

Off campus, a share of a Collegetown six-bedroom has a median asking rent of $9,600 over 12 months, before food ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). That is $4,144 less than the double, though the double includes utilities. ## How do you get into Ecology House?

New students list a program house on the housing application. They apply within 10 days of getting a NetID, and halls not chosen are assigned by random auto-allocation ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)). Rising sophomores can list up to three program houses. For 2027-28, the application closes April 4, 2027, and program house priority selection is April 12 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

A program house counts as on-campus housing, so living there meets the two-year residential requirement. If you want to live off campus, read the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What should you watch before you sign?

Check the cancellation schedule before you commit. The [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) turns your date into a number.

Plan your move too. Move-out loading uses the Ecology House lot ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/important-loading-and-parking-information)). The sources cited here list no move-in unloading zone for Ecology House, so check the [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) and your move-in email.
