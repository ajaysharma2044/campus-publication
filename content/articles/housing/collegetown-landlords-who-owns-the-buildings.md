A small group of management companies runs much of Collegetown: Modern Living Rentals, Lambrou Real Estate, Urban Ithaca, Ithaca Renting Company and PPM Homes among them. Ask who the owner of record is before you sign; the [Tompkins County assessment roll](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/assessment/documents/assessment-rolls/city-final-2026.pdf) lists owners by parcel.

## Who manages the most Collegetown buildings?

| Manager | What it runs | Office |
|---|---|---|
| Modern Living Rentals | The Ivy, The Bear, The Summit and 309 College Ave. | 200 E. Buffalo St. |
| Lambrou Real Estate | 37 properties: 15 apartment buildings, 16 houses of 3 to 24 bedrooms, 6 downtown units | 405 Eddy St. |
| Urban Ithaca | "Over 120 apartments and houses," studios to 11 bedrooms | 307 College Ave. |
| Ithaca Renting Company | 18 properties in Collegetown and downtown; per-bed prices posted | |
| PPM Homes | Residential and commercial properties, managed since 1992; rent on request | 116 W. Buffalo St. |

Source: [Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/), [Lambrou Real Estate](https://www.lambrourealestate.com/apartments), [Urban Ithaca](https://www.urbanithaca.com/), [Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/all-properties/) and [PPM Homes](https://www.ppmhomes.com/).

Student Agencies, founded in 1894 and the oldest independent student-run company in the nation, is based at 409 College Ave. and also operates Big Red Shipping & Storage ([Student Agencies](https://www.studentagencies.com/)). The Student Agencies Building at that address shows all units leased for 2027-28 ([The Student Agencies Building](https://www.thestudentagenciesbuilding.com/)).

## Who owns the buildings the managers run?

A manager and an owner are not always the same. The 2026 county assessment roll shows owners of record, and the pattern is concentrated. Jason Fane, or Fane Enterprises Inc., is owner of record for 20 parcels, and ENP Associates, L.P. for 13. The roll lists owners of record by parcel, so match a manager's listing to its owner by address ([Tompkins County](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/assessment/documents/assessment-rolls/city-final-2026.pdf)).

Charlie O'Connor, owner of Modern Living Rentals, developed The Ivy with Nick Robertson. The Ivy at 325 College Ave. has 52 units, and nearly all were leased before construction finished ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)).

Many buildings sit in single-purpose companies. A Collegetown Crossing LLC owns 307 College Ave. The LLC name tells you little, so ask the manager which company signs the lease ([Tompkins County](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/assessment/documents/assessment-rolls/city-final-2026.pdf)).

## How concentrated is the market?

In 2013, eight landlords held about $153 million in assessed Collegetown property and owned 4,013 of an estimated 6,110 bedrooms. John Novarr alone owned 1,559 bedrooms, and Novarr and Fane each held more than $38 million in property. The others were the Avramis family, Travis Hyde, the Lambrou family, Pam Johnston, the Lower family and Matoula Halkiopoulus ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2013/05/02/collegetown-housing-market-scrutinized/)).

## How does each landlord run the leasing season?

Leasing dates differ by landlord, and the Sun has recorded several. Ithaca Renting opened 2020-21 leasing at 9 a.m. on Sept. 12, 2019, after an overnight line, and the Sun described it as one of the largest local firms, leasing to over 600 tenants near campus ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/)). In 2016, George Avramis said his buildings were 70 to 75 percent leased by late September, and Josh Lower of Urban Ithaca said he usually rents most units by the end of November ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/)). PJ Apartments dropped its 24-hour notice policy in September 2013 for first-come, first-served showings ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2013/09/12/despite-new-law-housing-rush-persists-in-collegetown/)).

Dates move every year, so use [when to sign a Collegetown lease](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) for the current window.

```reader "If you're a parent paying the rent"
Ask for the owner of record and the manager in writing, and ask which company signs the lease. A 2026 county roll lookup by address confirms the owner in minutes. Do not treat a manager's size as a quality signal in either direction.
```

## What does it cost with each landlord?

Ithaca Renting posts a full per-bed price list on its [Collegetown page](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), and its 2027-28 medians are in the [per-bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/) and the [price guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/). With Lambrou, Urban Ithaca and PPM Homes, rent is on request: ask for a written quote per bed and per unit.

## How do you check a landlord before signing?

Call the City Building Division about the building's certificate, read the lease, and speak to past tenants. The [landlord checking guide](/housing/how-to-check-an-ithaca-landlords-record/) walks through it. To find listings from landlords outside this list, use the [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/).

## What to do next

1. Look up your building's owner of record on the county roll.
2. Ask the manager for lease dates and price per bed in writing.
3. Run the certificate check before you pay a deposit.
