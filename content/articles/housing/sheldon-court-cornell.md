## What is Sheldon Court?

It is a corridor-style South Campus hall with a TV lounge and pool table, kitchens, laundry and a piano room ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/sheldon-court)).

## What is it like to live at Sheldon Court?

The building has history. Charles L. Sheldon Jr., Class of 1901, operated it as "Cornell's First Dormitory." The Triangle Bookshop occupied part of the ground floor until Cornell bought the building in 1955 ([Cornellians](https://alumni.cornell.edu/cornellians/article/collegetown-history)).

A Cornell Daily Sun feature from April 17, 2026, describes daily life. It counts five floors plus a basement. Each bathroom has three showers, three sinks and three toilets. The basement laundry has four washers and four dryers. The writer says the elevator keeps breaking down, and many residents get a double to themselves. The College & Oak bus stop is across the street ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court)).

The trade-off is food. The same feature puts the nearest dining hall about a 12-minute walk away, on West Campus, and notes College Avenue noise and the building's age. A resident quoted there still recommends the dorm ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court)).

## What does Sheldon Court cost?

Cornell prices rooms by type, not by hall. The Collegetown Meal Plan, the new name for the South Campus plan, adds $3,528 per semester, or $7,056 for the year, plus a $50 yearly fee. It allows up to 10 meals a week and includes $500 in Big Red Bucks and $436 in City Bucks each semester, plus 4 guest meals ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Per semester | Per year | Year with Collegetown Meal Plan and fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double | $6,872 | $13,744 | $20,850 |
| Single | $7,783 | $15,566 | $22,672 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the meal plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

Renting in the same neighborhood costs more per person at small sizes. A Collegetown 1BR has a median asking rent of $24,300 over 12 months, which is $10,556 more than the double ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). ## How do you get a room at Sheldon Court?

You rank it in room selection, and its rooms can last until the end. During fall 2025 selection, a sophomore transfer with a last-day timeslot found only Clara Dickson triples and Sheldon Court doubles available. Housing and Residential Life told the Sun that housing "is not guaranteed beyond the first two years" ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)). If you want it, pick an early timeslot. If you want to rent, start with [off-campus housing for Cornell sophomores](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/).

## How does move-in and parking work?

Sheldon residents unload through the Dryden Road Garage entrance at 114 Dryden Rd. and then park at the Williams Street Lot ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)). A permit for that lot cost about $700 in 2023 and sold out within hours ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students)). The [parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/) lists the garages, and [getting around Ithaca without a car](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/) covers the bus from College & Oak.
