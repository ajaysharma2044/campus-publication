Ujamaa Residential College is a Cornell program house with 144 residents in Low Rise 10 on North Campus. It was founded in 1972. A program house is a themed residence hall, and rooms here cost the same as in any hall (Cornell Housing).

## What is Ujamaa Residential College?

Cornell lists 144 residents at Ujamaa ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/ujamaa-residential-college)). The house began in 1972, in a building that opened in 1970 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/ujamaa-residential-college)).

Low Rise 10 uses the layout shared by Low Rises 8 to 10: clusters of one double, one triple and two singles, with one shared bathroom and no air conditioning ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/loving-house)). That puts seven people on one bathroom. Cornell's list of North Campus buildings without room air conditioning includes all the Low Rises ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/first-year-undergraduates/residence-halls/mary-donlon-hall)).

## Who actually lives in Ujamaa?

The Cornell Daily Sun reported in May 2026 that a resident advisor there estimated about 30% of 2025-26 residents were Black. A former resident put the figure at about two-thirds in 2023 ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/05/oq5rriymdyfz)). The same story reported complaints of heating and water problems and mold. A university spokesperson told the paper that students are placed in Ujamaa either by saying they are interested or through general housing assignment.

At a March 10, 2026 town hall, administrators said placement depends on preferences, open rooms, disability accommodations and roommate groups. President Michael I. Kotlikoff said the federal settlement changes no Cornell policies ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/bsu-town-hall-addresses-housing-financial-aid-and-federal-policy-concerns)). The pressure behind that question started in February 2025, when a Department of Education letter cited the use of race in dormitories and set a two-week compliance window ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/02/bsu-holds-emergency-meeting-as-affinity-groups-program-houses-are-threatened-by-department-of-education-guidelines)).

If you want the community, list Ujamaa and write to the house before you apply. Ranking it first does not guarantee a bed.

## What does Ujamaa cost?

Ujamaa uses the residence hall rate table. A double is $6,872 per semester, or $13,744 per academic year ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). The cluster layout matters here, because a triple saves $1,100 a year against a double, and a single costs $1,822 more than a double (15,566 minus 13,744).

Every resident buys a meal plan: Unlimited at $7,604 for first-years, or Bear Traditional at $7,056 for upper-level and transfer students, plus a $50 yearly fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Room per year | Upper-level with Bear Traditional and fee | First-year with Unlimited and fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Triple | $12,644 | $19,750 | $20,298 |
| Double | $13,744 | $20,850 | $21,398 |
| Single | $15,566 | $22,672 | $23,220 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates and Cornell Dining for 2026-27. Each total adds the room rate, the meal plan and the yearly fee.

## How do you get a spot in Ujamaa?

List Ujamaa as a preference on the housing application. New first-year and transfer students apply within 10 days of getting a NetID, Cornell's student login. Unless you list a program house, your hall is set by random auto-allocation ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)).

Rising sophomores can list up to three program houses. For 2027-28, the application closes April 4, 2027 at 11:59 p.m., and Priority Program House Selection is April 12 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

Juniors and seniors had a round from Sept. 1 to Sept. 27, 2026. Cornell warns that program house beds may not be left in later stages ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)). A program house meets the two-year residential requirement. To live off campus instead, see the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What should you watch before you sign?

Ask about the building's condition. Residents told the Sun in May 2026 about heating, water and mold problems at Ujamaa (Cornell Daily Sun), so ask housing@cornell.edu what has been repaired.

Low Rise 10 is not on Cornell's list of buildings open over winter break ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). Plan where you will stay between semesters.

Juniors and seniors who sign for 2027-28 can cancel for $50 through Jan. 31, 2027, and the fee then rises ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). The [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) turns your date into a number.

On move-in day in fall 2026, Ujamaa residents unloaded at Program House Drive, and U-Hauls and trailers were turned away ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)). The [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) has the rest.
