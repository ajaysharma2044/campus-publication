Cascadilla Hall is a 366-resident South Campus hall at 115 Cascadilla Place for transfer and upper-level students. It was built in 1864 and has singles and doubles on corridors, with no room air conditioning. A double costs $13,744 for 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is Cascadilla Hall?

It is a corridor-style hall, which means rooms open onto a shared hallway. Floors are mixed-gender. Residents share a kitchen, a piano room and laundry. The hall also houses the service center for all Collegetown-area halls, where mail and packages go ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/cascadilla-hall)).

## What makes Cascadilla different from other halls?

Its age and its story. The hall began as a planned water-cure sanitarium led by Samantha Nivison, the first woman to practice medicine in Tompkins County. Ezra Cornell invested, bought out the other investors and finished the building, and Andrew Dickson White criticized the project harshly ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2013/09/19/ezras-oracle-sept-20-2013)).

Today its practical difference is the service center. Residents of Sheldon Court, Schuyler House and 301 College Avenue all route mail through the hall too. The sources cited here give no walking time from Cascadilla to Central Campus, so test the walk yourself before you rank it. Its sibling Sheldon Court is covered in [Sheldon Court](/cornell/housing/sheldon-court-cornell/).

## What does Cascadilla Hall cost?

Cornell prices rooms by type, not by hall. A Cascadilla double costs what a double costs in any residence hall. The Collegetown Meal Plan, the new name for the South Campus plan, adds $3,528 per semester, or $7,056 for the year, plus a $50 yearly fee. It allows up to 10 meals a week and includes $500 in Big Red Bucks and $436 in City Bucks each semester, plus 4 guest meals ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Per semester | Per year | Year with Collegetown Meal Plan and fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double | $6,872 | $13,744 | $20,850 |
| Single | $7,783 | $15,566 | $22,672 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the meal plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

Compare that with renting. A Collegetown studio has a median asking rent of $21,180 over 12 months, which is $7,436 more than the double ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). ## How do you get a room in Cascadilla Hall?

You rank it in room selection. South Campus halls and 301 College Avenue form one selection area for rising juniors and seniors. Blocks can hold up to five students, and Cornell holds a room for four minutes ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)).

Cornell places new transfers in upper-level halls such as this one. If you plan to rent after sophomore year instead, read [off-campus housing for Cornell sophomores](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/).

## How does move-in and parking work?

Cascadilla unloads at the Eddygate entrance. After unloading, move your car to the Williams Street Lot, the upper lot at Williams Street and Stewart Avenue. The 2026 service center hours were 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Aug. 18 to 22 and 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Aug. 23 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-cascadilla-hall)).

A permit for the Williams Street lot cost about $700 in 2023 and sold out within hours ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students)). The [Collegetown and downtown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/) lists the garages, and [getting around Ithaca without a car](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/) covers the bus.

## What should you watch before you sign?

Cascadilla has no room air conditioning. It stays open over winter break in 2026-27, when Cornell sells a one-week stay at $125 or the full break at $500 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)).

## What to do next

Price a double or single with the Collegetown Meal Plan, then walk the route to your first class. Rank Cascadilla alongside Sheldon Court and Schuyler House.
