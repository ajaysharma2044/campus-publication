```glance
- Find housemates through Cornell's Off-Campus Living email list, the Cornell marketplace and the people you already know.
- Most local leases bind housemates jointly and severally: each owes the whole rent and damages.
- A 6BR bed has a $800 median; a 4BR bed, $1,246.25. A group that shrinks pays the difference.
- Put in writing what happens if a housemate studies abroad, takes leave or drops out.
```

Start with Cornell's Off-Campus Living email list and its Off-Campus Housing Marketplace, both on its [rental listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Most local leases make housemates jointly and severally liable, so if one person stops paying, the rest owe the full rent. Pick people you would trust with your share.

## Where do you find roommates for a Collegetown lease?

**The Off-Campus Living email list.** Cornell runs it for housing, roommates and sublets, and it requires a NetID. Subscribe by sending a message with the subject "Join" and a blank body. Admitted students without a NetID forward the acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)).

**The Off-Campus Housing Marketplace.** It is a College Pads listing service, and only people who prove Cornell affiliation can view and contact landlords and subletters. Cornell advises viewing a property in person and reading the whole lease before you pay anything ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). The [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) covers every source.

**The Ithaca College portal.** It runs on the same platform and carries roommate-matching leases from Foundation Housing for spring 2027 and full years ([Ithaca College portal](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-ny/listing/spring-27-and-full-year-leases-available-for-roommate-matching-foundation-housing-96176)). A landlord who matches strangers still leaves you with strangers on your lease, so read whether it is a joint lease or by the room.

**Your own networks.** A senior told the Sun in 2018 that one email to a landlord through her sorority network was enough ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2018](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)).

Off-Campus Living, at 607-255-2310, offers one-on-one consultations and guides on conflict with roommates and landlords ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease)).

## How does group size change the rent per person?

Per-bed rent falls steeply as the unit grows. These are Ithaca Renting's Collegetown medians for 2027-28, from 38 priced rows. Heat and hot water are included at most units, and electricity and parking mostly are not ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

| Unit | Rows | Median per bed, a month | Per person, 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1BR alone | 8 | $2,025 | $24,300 |
| 2BR | 5 | $1,377.50 | $16,530 |
| 4BR | 4 | $1,246.25 | $14,955 |
| 6BR | 5 | $800 | $9,600 |
| Room in a shared suite | 4 | $895 | $10,740 |

Big houses go first. On Nov. 8, 2026, Ithaca Renting showed 14 of 67 Collegetown rows sold out for 2027-28, so a large group has to move early. The [timing guide](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/) shows when.

Say six sophomores plan a 6BR for junior year, and two drop out. The four left rent a 4BR. At the medians above ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)), each pays $446.25 more a month, or $5,355 more over a 12-month lease (1,246.25 minus 800, times 12).

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
You pick housemates in the fall of sophomore year, about a year before move-in. Decide group size first, then look for people. Sign with people you have lived near, because a group thrown together in the first weeks of fall can lose members, and whoever stays owes the whole rent.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
You may know nobody to room with. Join the Off-Campus Living list now, and forward your acceptance letter if you have no NetID. A sophomore transfer lives on campus for one academic year, so the first lease is further off than it looks ([Residential Policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
You do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment ([International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students)). A housemate you met online can leave you owing their share, and Cornell Financial Aid will not step in on a mid-year lease break ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying or co-signing"
On a joint lease your student owes the whole rent if a housemate stops paying. Your exposure as a guarantor depends on what you sign. Cornell Law's lease guide does not address guarantors ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)), so read the guaranty to see whether it covers one share or the whole unit.
```

## What does joint and several liability mean?

It means the landlord can hold any one tenant for the whole rent. Cornell says most local leases bind housemates "jointly and severally": each is responsible for housemates' rent and damages for the term, and breaking the lease has consequences, mostly in money ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)). A tenant who overpays can sue a co-tenant for contribution, and small claims go up to $5,000 in Ithaca City Court ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)). Mediation is at the Community Dispute Resolution Center, 607-273-9347 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources)).

Say four friends sign a 4BR at the median, about $4,985 for the unit (1,246.25 times 4). One leaves in January with no replacement. The other three owe the full $4,985, about $1,661.67 each, or $415.42 more a month apiece. An oral lease over one year is barred by the Statute of Frauds, and a written-changes clause bars most oral changes, so get any promise to let someone out in writing ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)).

## What should you ask a possible housemate before you sign?

Cornell's housemate questionnaire covers study hours, music and TV, silence, smoking, smells, alcohol, guests and cleanliness. It asks how each of you handles a bad mood and confronting people, who manages expenses (student or parent), whether you will share a car and whether there is a legal limit on occupants. It also asks what happens if a housemate takes leave, transfers or studies abroad, and it gives no answer ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)).

New York law gives three answers. Write your group's version down before signing:

1. **Sublet.** In a building with four or more units, a tenant can sublet with the landlord's consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. The request goes by certified mail. The landlord has 10 days to ask for more information and 30 days to answer, and silence is consent ([Real Property Law 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). In buildings of three units or fewer, the lease controls ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). The person going away stays liable for the subtenant.
2. **Assign.** An assignment ends the leaver's liability, but the landlord can refuse without cause.
3. **Leave anyway.** The landlord must try in good faith to re-rent at the lower of market or lease rent, and a new lease ends the old debt ([Real Property Law 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E)). Until then, housemates stay jointly liable. The [early-exit guide](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/) covers the options.

## Who can legally live in the unit?

On a single-tenant lease, New York's roommate law allows the tenant, immediate family, one additional occupant and that occupant's dependent children. On a lease with several tenants, tenants plus occupants cannot exceed the number of tenants named, not counting dependent children. You must give the landlord an occupant's name within 30 days of move-in or of the landlord asking, and an occupant has no right to stay if the tenant leaves ([Real Property Law 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F)).

Ask whether the occupancy is legal for the address. The City of Ithaca defines a cooperative household as four or more unrelated persons sharing a unit ([City code](https://ecode360.com/8393870)). A rental unit with five or more unrelated people needs a Certificate of Compliance under the code ([City code](https://ecode360.com/8390192)). The City's web page says six or more, so go by five. The Town of Ithaca treats more than two unrelated people as a family only if the Zoning Board of Appeals finds they are a functional equivalent, a rule upheld in 2017 ([Drummond v Town of Ithaca ZBA](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html)). The lease clauses to check are in the [lease-reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/).

## What to do next

1. Join the Off-Campus Living list and post what group size you want.
2. Meet each possible housemate twice, and go through Cornell's questions with each one.
3. Write your plan for a dropout, study abroad or sublet, and have everyone initial it.
4. Build your dates with the timeline tool, then read the [off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-guide/).
