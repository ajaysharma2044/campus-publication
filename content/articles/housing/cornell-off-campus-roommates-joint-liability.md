```glance
- Find housemates on Cornell's Off-Campus Living email list (NetID needed), the Cornell marketplace, or Ithaca College's portal, which carries roommate-matching leases.
- Most Ithaca leases bind housemates "jointly and severally": each of you owes the whole rent and damages if someone stops paying.
- If one person leaves a 6BR and the group rents a 4BR instead, the per-bed median rises from $800 to $1,246.25 a month: $446.25 more each (Ithaca Renting, 2027-28).
- New York law caps people in the unit at the number of tenants on a multi-tenant lease; name any occupant to the landlord within 30 days.
- Check legal occupancy: the City treats 4 or more unrelated people as a "cooperative household"; the Town of Ithaca allows more than 2 unrelated only by zoning-board finding.
- Write down, before signing, what happens if someone studies abroad. Cornell asks the question and gives no answer.
```

Cornell's main roommate channels are the Off-Campus Living email list (NetID needed) and the Off-Campus Housing Marketplace, both on its [rental listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Most local leases make housemates [jointly and severally liable](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): if one person stops paying, the rest owe the full rent. Pick people you would trust with your share, and put in writing what happens if someone leaves.

```reader "If you're a sophomore planning junior year"
You will pick housemates around October of sophomore year, a year before move-in. Decide group size first: Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians run $800 per bed in a 6BR, $1,246.25 in a 4BR and $2,025 for a 1BR alone ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The costly mistake is a big group formed in the first weeks of fall. Dery's 2020 Sun column said large groups are likely to lose people ([Dery](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus)), and whoever stays owes the whole rent. Sign with people you have lived near.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
You probably know nobody to room with. Join the Off-Campus Living list now; if you have no NetID yet, forward your acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). A sophomore transfer cannot rent yet: new transfers live on campus for one academic year unless they enter as juniors or seniors ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). In a 2018 Sun story, one transfer advised spending about a week getting to know roommates before committing ([Sun, 2018](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus)).
```

```reader "If you're already renting and a housemate is leaving"
The person leaving stays liable unless the landlord releases them, and so do you. The cleanest fix is a replacement: under [RPL 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F), people living in the unit cannot exceed the number of tenants on the lease, so one in for one out fits the count. Give the landlord the new occupant's name within 30 days of move-in. An occupant has no right to stay if the tenants leave. If you end up covering a share, you can sue for contribution ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease)) in Ithaca City Court small claims, up to $5,000.
```

```reader "If you're going abroad or away for a semester"
Your housemates stay jointly liable for your share unless the landlord releases you. In a building of four or more units you have a right to sublet with consent that cannot be unreasonably withheld; send the request by certified mail, and the landlord must answer within 30 days ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). In a house of three or fewer units the lease controls. Leaving them your 4BR share for a five-month semester costs them about $6,231 (our arithmetic: $1,246.25 x 5). Settle this before signing.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
You may be picking housemates and signing from abroad. You do not need an SSN to rent ([Cornell Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students)). A housemate you met online can leave you owing their share. If you leave at the end of a program, the rest of the group owes yours, and Cornell Financial Aid [will not intervene](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) in a mid-year lease break. The deposit plus any rent paid ahead cannot exceed one month's rent ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)), even when a group pays up front.
```

```reader "If you're a parent paying or co-signing"
On a joint lease your student owes the whole rent if a housemate stops paying. Your exposure as guarantor depends on what you sign, and the [Cornell Law lease guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease) is silent on guarantors, so read the guaranty for whether it covers one share or the whole unit. A sublet request needs any guarantor's consent ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). Cornell's housemate questions ask whether the student or a parent manages expenses; answer that before the group signs. Not confirmed: whether Collegetown landlords require guarantors.
```

**The Off-Campus Living email list.** Cornell runs it for housing, roommates and sublets; it needs a NetID. Subscribe with the subject "Join" and a blank body; leave with a blank subject and "Leave" in the body ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). Admitted students without a NetID forward their acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu.

**The Off-Campus Housing Marketplace.** The [marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/) is a College Pads listing service. Only people who prove Cornell affiliation can view and contact landlords and subletters ([listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources)). Listings are checked against the City's Certificate of Compliance database ([Graduate School, 2024](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/)).

**The Ithaca College portal.** It runs on the same platform as Cornell's marketplace and carries [roommate-matching leases from Foundation Housing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-ny/listing/spring-27-and-full-year-leases-available-for-roommate-matching-foundation-housing-96176) for spring 2027 and full years. Strangers matched by a landlord are still strangers on your lease, so read whether it is joint or by the room.

**Your own networks.** A senior in a [2018 Sun story](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/) said one email to a landlord through her sorority network was enough.

Off-Campus Living offers one-on-one consultations and a guide to roommate conflict; its line is 607-255-2310 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease)).

Example: four friends sign a 4BR priced at the median, about $4,985 a month for the unit (our arithmetic: $1,246.25 x 4). One leaves in January with no replacement. Joint and several liability means the other three owe the full $4,985, about $1,661.67 each, or $415.42 more a month apiece.

## Joint and several liability, in plain terms

Cornell says most local leases bind housemates [jointly and severally](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire): each is responsible for housemates' rent and damages for the whole term. Breaking the lease has consequences, usually monetary.

Under the [Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease), the landlord can hold any one tenant for the full rent. A tenant who overpays can sue a co-tenant for contribution. In Ithaca City Court, 118 East Clinton Street, small claims go up to $5,000 ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). Mediation for roommate disputes is at the Community Dispute Resolution Center, 607-273-9347 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources)).

The same guide gives two rules that bite groups. An oral lease over one year is barred by the Statute of Frauds. A written-changes clause generally bars oral changes, so get any "we'll let you out if someone leaves" in writing.

## Occupants, replacements and the 30-day rule

New York's roommate law, [RPL 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F), sets who can live in the unit beyond the named tenants.

| Your lease | Who may live there |
|---|---|
| One tenant on the lease | The tenant, immediate family, one additional occupant and that occupant's dependent children, if the tenant or spouse lives there as a primary residence |
| Several tenants on the lease | Tenants plus occupants (not counting dependent children) cannot exceed the number of tenants on the lease |

Three details catch groups. You must give the landlord an occupant's name within 30 days of move-in, or of the landlord asking. An occupant gets no right to stay if the tenant leaves. And a landlord may still restrict occupancy to comply with law or code.

A replacement who moves in as an occupant is not on the lease, so the departed tenant stays liable. Taking someone off the lease takes an assignment, and the landlord [may refuse one without cause](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). After an assignment, the original tenant is generally no longer liable for the new tenant's rent or damage ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

## How many unrelated people can legally share

Before a group of five or six signs, ask whether the occupancy is legal for that address.

| Where | The rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| City of Ithaca | A "cooperative household" is four or more unrelated persons, excluding minor dependent children, sharing a dwelling unit | [City code (search snippet)](https://ecode360.com/8393870) |
| City of Ithaca | A rental unit with five or more unrelated persons needs a certificate of compliance (code); the City's web page says six or more | [City code](https://ecode360.com/8390192); [City page](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance) |
| Town of Ithaca | More than two unrelated persons count as a family only if the Zoning Board of Appeals finds a functional equivalent; a single-family home is one family plus one boarder | [Drummond v Town of Ithaca ZBA, 2017](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html) |

The Town rule was upheld in 2017 in a case involving Ithaca College students. Lansing and Cayuga Heights addresses fall under their own codes.

Not confirmed: the City of Ithaca's definition of "family" and any Collegetown occupancy cap.

## The housemate questions to answer before you sign

Cornell's [housemate questionnaire](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire) covers study hours, music and TV, silence, smoking, smells, alcohol, guests and cleanliness. It asks how each of you handles a bad mood and confronting people, who manages expenses (student or parent), whether you will share a car, and whether there is a legal limit on occupants. It also asks what happens if a housemate takes leave, transfers or studies abroad, and gives no answer. The clash is old: a 2016 Sun story reported the housing search [getting in the way of study-abroad plans](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/20/students-housing-search-hampers-study-abroad).

New York law gives three answers. Write your group's version down before signing:

1. **Sublet.** In a building with four or more units, the tenant may sublet with consent that cannot be unreasonably withheld. Send a certified-mail request; the landlord may ask for more within 10 days and must answer within 30, and silence is consent ([RPL 226-b](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)). In three units or fewer, the lease controls ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)). The person going abroad stays liable for the subtenant.
2. **Assign.** Ends the leaver's liability, but the landlord may refuse without cause.
3. **Leave anyway.** The landlord must make good-faith efforts to re-rent at the lower of market or lease rent, and a new lease ends the old debt ([RPL 227-e](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E)). Until then, housemates stay jointly liable.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing with a group formed in the first weeks of fall.** Dery wrote in 2020 that large groups are likely to lose people. Losing two from a 6BR costs each remaining person $446.25 a month at 2027-28 medians.
- **Believing a spoken promise.** A written-changes clause generally bars oral changes, so "we'll let you out" is not a release.
- **Moving in a friend without telling the landlord.** RPL 235-f gives 30 days to name an occupant.
- **Packing six unrelated people into a Town of Ithaca house.** More than two unrelated need a zoning-board finding.
- **Leaving for a semester with no written plan.** Housemates cover about $6,231 for a five-month 4BR share (our arithmetic), and Cornell aid will not step in.
- **Assuming the leaver is off the hook once someone moves in.** Only an assignment or a landlord's release ends their liability.

## FAQ

**Can the landlord stop us from adding a roommate?** Not by limiting occupancy to tenants and family; [RPL 235-f](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F) voids that. On a multi-tenant lease, the total still cannot exceed the number of tenants on it.

**Can a sophomore sign onto our lease for this year?** Only with an approved exemption. Sophomores must live in Cornell or affiliated housing ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)), and one who signs without an exemption is [still billed for the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing).

**Will Off-Campus Living settle a fight with my housemates?** It offers consultations and conflict-resolution guides. For mediation, call the Community Dispute Resolution Center at 607-273-9347 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources)).

**Can I get free advice on a group lease before signing?** Yes: the Cornell Law Tenants Advocacy hotline is 607-301-1560 ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources)).

**Does Cornell aid cover a housemate's unpaid share?** No. Aid does not cover deposits, first or last month's rent, or rent in unregistered months ([Cornell Financial Aid](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus)).
