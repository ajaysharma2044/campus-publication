```glance
- Collegetown leases are usually one year long and start in June, July or August.
- Dates differ by unit: The Ivy began Aug. 17, 2026, and a 114 Stewart Ave. lease starts June 10, 2027.
- Ten-month leases, August to June, exist on some units, sometimes at a premium.
- Under a 12-month lease you pay for summer; a sublet is the usual way to recover it.
```

Collegetown leases usually run [one year](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), starting in June, July or August, and a lease such as 209 Stewart Ave.'s runs Aug. 1 to July 31. The key date is per apartment, not per neighborhood, so read the exact start and end on the lease before you compare prices. Some units start in June, some in mid-August, and a few offer 10 months.

## Why do many Collegetown leases run August to August?

Landlords sell a year, not a semester, and the year starts when the last tenants leave and the next arrive. The 209 Stewart Avenue listing is one example, with a full rental year in the table below ([209 Stewart Ave.](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095)). Cornell's own calendar fits it. Cornell's 2026 move-in dates were Monday, Aug. 17, and Aug. 21 and 22 ([Move-In, North Campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)), so a student who arrives in the third week of August finds a lease already running.

Ithaca Renting says its leases are one year long and start in June, July or August on preset dates for each apartment ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

Two lessons follow. A lease can end before August, as the current Dryden South leases do ([Dryden South](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)), so an early end can mean moving out with no summer housing. And a lease can start before August, as at 114 Stewart, so a June start means paying for June and July rent before your own year begins.

## Are 10-month leases available?

On some units. The 140 College Avenue house is offered for 2027-28 on a 10-month lease, August to June, furnished ([140 College](https://www.lambrourealestate.com/140-college-ave-24-bed)). Cayuga Place at 131 East Green Street, downtown, has a standard 12-month lease and 10-month options at an additional premium; ask the leasing office for the amount ([Cayuga Place](https://livehhcayuga.com/cayuga-place/floor-plans)). Some apartments at 226 Linden Avenue are available on 10-month or 12-month leases, and the price varies by length ([226 Linden Ave.](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/226-linden-avenue-8104)). At the other end, one City Centre studio is listed on a 15-month term ([City Centre](https://citycentreithaca.com/floorplans/)).

A 10-month lease does not mean a 10-month price. Say a Collegetown 4BR bed costs $1,246.25 a month, the 2027-28 median ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)), or $14,955 a year. If you use 10 of the months, the effective cost is $1,495.50 a month ($14,955 divided by 10). A shorter lease that costs more per month can still beat a long one you cannot sublet.

## What does the July 31 end date mean for your summer?

You pay for the summer whether you are in Ithaca or not. A sublet is the way most students recover it. Ithaca Renting requires Collegetown sublets to be registered and approved, limits subtenants to Cornell students (summer sublets can go to graduate students from other universities), allows one per term and bars Airbnb and stays under one month ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). The steps are in the [how to sublet guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/), and the [summer sublet search guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) covers finding housing for the summer months.

Three July 31 effects are easy to miss. A residential parking permit from the City of Ithaca is valid only through the end of July and cannot be bought for part of a year ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance)). A landlord must return a deposit with an itemized statement within 14 days after you move out, which is mid-August for an exit at the end of July ([General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)). And the City's 120-day renewal notice counts toward the end date.

## When does the 120-day notice fall?

Ithaca's rule requires a landlord to give current tenants 120 days' written notice before renewing, showing the unit or signing new tenants ([City of Ithaca code](https://ecode360.com/27390826)). Counting 120 days back from a lease end on the last day of July lands on about April 2. Ask the landlord which date your lease counts from. The [120-day guide](/cornell/housing/ithaca-120-day-lease-renewal-notice-rule/) walks through the rule.

## How does a lease line up with Cornell's calendar?

Cornell's own housing runs a different clock. For 2026-27 a residence-hall license ran from move-in in August 2026 through Dec. 20 and resumed Jan. 19 for the spring term ([Cornell Housing rates and licenses](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses)). If you hold a Cornell room as a backup for the Collegetown lease, the fee schedule is in the [Collegetown lease guide](/cornell/housing/when-to-sign-collegetown-lease-cornell/).

## What to do next

1. Find the exact start and end dates on the lease, not the listing.
2. Check whether the unit offers 10 months and what the premium is.
3. Plan the summer: a sublet, or a 12-month cost you accept.
4. Read the clauses on dates, renewal and subletting in the [lease-reading guide](/cornell/housing/how-to-read-ithaca-lease-before-signing/).
