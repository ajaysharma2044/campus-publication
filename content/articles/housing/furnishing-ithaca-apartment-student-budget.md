The cheapest way to furnish an Ithaca apartment is to rent one that comes furnished, as most Collegetown and downtown student buildings are. At Ithaca Renting's downtown buildings, taking the furniture out "does not change the rent" ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)). Then buy only what the lease leaves out.

## Which Ithaca apartments come furnished?

Most of the big student landlords near Cornell furnish their apartments. Ithaca Renting Company's Whiton Hall, Commons Studios and Colonial Studios apartments are furnished, and so are most of its other downtown units; call its Rental Office to confirm a specific unit. In Collegetown, its Collegetown Court building at 208 Dryden Road is furnished ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=223)).

Other furnished buildings include Dryden South at 205 Dryden Road ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)) and The Ivy at 325 College Ave., which also has an in-unit washer and dryer ([Modern Living Rentals](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/the-ivy)). Travis Hyde Properties furnishes Eddygate, TVs included ([Travis Hyde](https://www.travishyde.com/college-town/eddygate%20apartments)), and Ravenwood at 516 University Ave. the same way ([Travis Hyde](https://www.travishyde.com/west-campus/ravenwood)).

Some landlords sell furniture as an add-on. Coal Yard Apartments at 143 Maple Ave. lists "furnished packages" ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/coal-yard-apartments-7734)). Ask what a package costs before you sign.

Some units come unfurnished. Heritage Park TownHouses at 225 Pennsylvania Ave. rents two-bedrooms unfurnished for $1,420 a month plus utilities, for leases starting in August 2026 ([IthacaRents](https://www.ithacarents.com/1616-225-pennsylvania-ave)).

For what each building charges per bed, see [Collegetown rent prices per bed and per one-bedroom](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/).

## What does "furnished" include in an Ithaca apartment?

A furnished Ithaca bedroom has a bed, desk, chair and dresser, sometimes a bookcase. The living room and kitchen vary by building, so get the list in writing.

## Is a furnished apartment worth paying more for?

Yes, when the premium is small, because furniture you own costs money twice: once to buy it and again to store or move it each summer. In many Ithaca buildings the premium is zero.

The clearest price gap is at Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments for graduate students. A one-bedroom costs $1,490 a month furnished and $1,415 unfurnished on a 12-month contract for 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). That is $75 a month, or $900 over 12 months. A family two-bedroom townhouse costs $1,530 furnished and $1,475 unfurnished, a gap of $55 a month, or $660 a year.

In Fall Creek, True Renew Homes lists a furnished one-bedroom at 312 Farm St., Apt 2W, for $1,545 a month ([IthacaRents](https://www.ithacarents.com/4385-cute-furnished-1-br-apt-in-fall-creek-that-is-closest-to-cornell)). Its unfurnished one-bedroom next door at 310 Farm St., Apt 1E, lists for $1,535 ([IthacaRents](https://www.ithacarents.com/4563-unfurnished-sun-filled-1br-in-fall-creek-1e-walk-to-cornell--commons)). The units differ, but the gap is $10 a month.

Storage costs more than any of those gaps. Big Red Shipping & Storage charges $150 per "giant" item for a three- to four-month term, and it counts a dresser, a mattress, a bed frame, a desk and a couch as giant items ([Big Red Shipping & Storage](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing)). Storing a bed frame, mattress, desk and dresser for one summer costs $600 (four items at $150).

## What should you buy, and where do you get it used?

Buy only what the landlord's list leaves out, and buy the big pieces used. For most furnished units that means bedding, a lamp, kitchen gear, a shower curtain and cleaning supplies. If your unit is unfurnished, a bed and mattress come first, then a desk and chair.

Cornell's own move-out in May is one source of free used furniture. Items students leave in campus housing without a resident present are donated to Dump & Run, and usable items go to Project ReCollect bins in residence hall lobbies ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out)). Dump & Run's sale dates and prices aren't on that page; contact Dump & Run about its next sale before you shop elsewhere.

Students leaving furnished Collegetown apartments sell what they bought at the same time. Most Ithaca leases end July 31, so late July is when the most used furniture changes hands. For more ways to cut spending, see [how to save money at Cornell](/cornell/money/how-to-save-money-at-cornell-student-discounts-ithaca/).

## How do you keep used furniture from bringing bed bugs?

Inspect any used mattress, couch or upholstered chair before it comes inside, and report any bugs in writing right away. New York law puts the cost of extermination on the landlord. The landlord must exterminate even if it suspects you brought the bugs in, unless it can prove misconduct ([Cornell Tenants Advocacy Practicum](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). The landlord must also notify the units above, below and beside yours in writing within 72 hours.

**Watch out:** treating bed bugs yourself with store-bought products can weaken a later claim for damages. Call the Tenants Legal Hotline at (607) 301-1560 if a landlord refuses to act.

## Do you need renters insurance for your furniture?

Yes. Renters insurance covers your own belongings if they are stolen or damaged, and Cornell's checklist for off-campus renters says to buy it ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists)). Your landlord's insurance covers the building, not your desk or laptop. Cornell points students to GradGuard College Renters Insurance, which covers theft, fire, smoke and vandalism on and off campus ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus)). Ithaca quotes average about $76 a year, or about $6 a month ([SafeButler](https://www.safebutler.com/cheap-best-renters-insurance-ithaca-ny)). For the rest of a monthly budget, see [what a month off campus costs](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/).

## What should you do with the landlord's furniture on move-in day?

Photograph every piece the landlord provides, and send the landlord a written list of its condition. Cornell's checklist tells renters to give the landlord an inventory and photos after move-in. A torn mattress or broken desk drawer you did not report can come out of your deposit at move-out. The [deposit guide](/cornell/housing/moving-out-ithaca-apartment-deposit-back/) covers how to get it back.

## What do you do with your furniture when you move out?

Sell it, store it, or haul it away, but do not leave it on the curb or the porch. City of Ithaca rules bar storing discarded couches, mattresses, tables, chairs and appliances anywhere outside on the property ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance)).

To dispose of it yourself, the Tompkins County Recycling and Solid Waste Center is open Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., and has required a high-visibility vest since Aug. 1, 2026 ([Tompkins County](http://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Recycling-Materials-Management)). Fees aren't posted online; call the county's recycling office, at 122 Commercial Ave., before you load a car. For summer storage and shipping options, see [move-out storage and shipping](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/).
