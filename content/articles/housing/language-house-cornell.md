The Language House is a Cornell immersion community of more than 50 upper-level residents in suites in Toni Morrison Hall. For 2026-27 it offers Spanish, French, Mandarin, Korean and American Sign Language. Room rates follow the shared table, with a double at $13,744 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is the Language House?

It is a place to live in a language you are learning. It was founded in 1984 with German, French and Spanish. It moved to Toni Morrison Hall in 2023. Cornell's page says the house's future location is still being decided ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/language-house)).

## Which languages does the Language House offer?

Five for 2026-27: Spanish, French, Mandarin, Korean and American Sign Language. The Cornell Chronicle reported on April 1, 2025, that American Sign Language was added for 2025-26. Arabic, Farsi, Japanese, Italian and Russian have been offered in past years ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2025/04/language-house-opens-students-learning-asl)). The lineup changes, so confirm the languages for the year you apply.

## How is it different from other program houses?

Cornell itself says the Language House is not a traditional program house ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates/program-houses)). It is for upper-level students, and its rooms are suites, not the cluster rooms of the Low Rises. Cornell lists the house as temperature-controlled ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/loving-house)).

## What does the Language House cost?

Program houses share the residence hall rate table. The sources cited here do not say which room types the suites contain, so check the rates page for yours. A meal plan is required: Bear Traditional at $7,056 for upper-level and transfer students, plus a $50 yearly fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Room per year | Year with Bear Traditional and fee |
|---|---|---|
| Triple | $12,644 | $19,750 |
| Double | $13,744 | $20,850 |
| Single | $15,566 | $22,672 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

Off campus, a share of a Collegetown five-bedroom has a median asking rent of $13,230 over 12 months, before food. That is $514 less than the double ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). [Is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/) adds food and utilities.

## How do you get into the Language House?

Rising juniors and seniors need an additional application beyond room selection. For 2027-28, applications ran Sept. 1 to Sept. 27, 2026, and program house priority selection was Oct. 1. Program house beds may not be available in later stages ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/fall-housing-selection-rising-juniors-and-seniors)).

Check Cornell Housing's selection page each September for the next application dates and the Language House form.

A program house counts as on-campus housing, so living there meets the two-year residential requirement. If you want to live off campus, check the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What should you watch before you sign?

Ask where the house will be located in future years, since Cornell lists it as undecided. Check the cancellation schedule before you commit. The [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) turns your date into a number.

The sources cited here list no move-in unloading zone for Toni Morrison Hall, so check the [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) and your move-in email.

## What to do next

Decide whether your language is on the current list, then ask Housing about the extra application and the suite layouts.
