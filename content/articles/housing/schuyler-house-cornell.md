## What is Schuyler House?

It is a small hall with 125 residents. The building dates to 1910 and has housed students since 1981. It is named for George W. Schuyler, Cornell's first treasurer. Rooms are mostly singles, with some doubles and a few triples, and each floor has a kitchen ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/residence-halls/schuyler-house)).

## How far is Schuyler House from campus?

Cornell lists a 15 to 20 minute walk to Central Campus. The TCAT bus, Ithaca's public bus line, runs 3 to 11 buses an hour and takes about 5 minutes. Cornell gives all residents a free TCAT bus pass, which is the main thing that sets this hall apart. Check the bus times for your own class schedule, and read [getting around Ithaca without a car](/cornell/getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/) for fares and winter service.

## What does Schuyler House cost?

Cornell prices rooms by type, not by hall, so a single here costs what a single costs elsewhere in the residence halls. Since most rooms are singles, the single rate is the one to plan around. The Collegetown Meal Plan, the new name for the South Campus plan, adds $3,528 per semester, or $7,056 for the year, plus a $50 yearly fee. It allows up to 10 meals a week and includes $500 in Big Red Bucks and $436 in City Bucks each semester, plus 4 guest meals ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Per semester | Per year | Year with Collegetown Meal Plan and fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | $7,783 | $15,566 | $22,672 |
| Double | $6,872 | $13,744 | $20,850 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the meal plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

The bus pass has a dollar value. If you rented in Collegetown and rode TCAT to campus, you would pay for the ride yourself. The [Collegetown rent per bed guide](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/) gives rents by unit size, and [Is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm?](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/) adds food and utilities.

## How do you get a room in Schuyler House?

You rank it in room selection. South Campus halls are one selection area, so a sophomore or upper-level student can rank Schuyler next to Cascadilla and Sheldon. For 2027-28, rising sophomores apply by April 4, 2027, and the full process is in [off-campus housing for Cornell sophomores](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-sophomores/) alongside the rent option. The sources cited here have no sign-up numbers for this hall alone, so ask Housing and Residential Life how many rooms open each year.

## How does mail and parking work?

Schuyler's own service center closed on Aug. 21, 2023, and residents were sent to Cascadilla Hall. A Cornell official cited low package volume. One resident put the walk at about 10 minutes each way, up a hill ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2023/08/schuyler-house-service-center-closed-after-move-in-residents-directed-to-cascadilla-hall)). Cornell's page still lists the mail address as 115 Cascadilla Place.

For permits, read the [Collegetown and downtown parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/parking-collegetown-downtown-ithaca-garages-permits/).

## What to do next

Price a single with the Collegetown Meal Plan, then ride the bus from Sage Place at the hour of your first class. Compare Schuyler with Cascadilla Hall and Sheldon Court.
