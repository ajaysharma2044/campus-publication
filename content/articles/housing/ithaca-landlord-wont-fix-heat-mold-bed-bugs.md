```glance
- Ithaca heat season runs Sept 15 to May 31: at least 68F from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. when it is under 55F outside, and 62F overnight.
- Every repair request goes in writing (email, text or letter) with your name, address and the date; keep photos and a log.
- Bed bugs: report in writing and don't self-treat; the landlord must exterminate and notify neighboring units within 72 hours.
- If the landlord stalls, call code enforcement: City of Ithaca 607-274-6508, Town of Ithaca 607-273-1783, Town of Lansing 607-533-7054.
- Courts typically cut rent about 10% to 20% for minor problems, about 30% for moderate ones and 50% to 60% for the worst.
- Don't withhold rent before calling the free tenant hotline (607-301-1560); it invites a 14-day rent demand and a nonpayment case.
```

Every New York lease carries a [warranty of habitability](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-B), so failed heat, mold and bed bugs are the landlord's to fix. Report the problem in writing, then file with the code office for your address, and call the free Cornell Law tenant hotline at [607-301-1560](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/tenants-rights/) before you hold back any rent. Since January 2024 you can also ask a court to [order the repair](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance) after 30 days in the unit and a written notice.

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
The fear is real: students report skipping repair requests so they won't lose a renewal ([Cornell Daily Sun, 2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/07/23/ithaca-tenants-union-and-solidarity-slate-join-regional-efforts-toward-housing-equity/)). The law covers you. A landlord may not refuse to renew or raise rent unreasonably because of a good-faith complaint made in the past year, and an eviction within a year of one can be defended as [retaliation](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal). The exception is an owner-occupied building with fewer than four units. The costly mistake is a phone call with no email after it: without a written record, an abatement claim is your word against the landlord's.
```

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Check which side of the city line you live on. Fall Creek and downtown addresses go to the City Building Division at [607-274-6508](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/130/Building-Division). Varna, Lansing and Cayuga Heights are outside the City, so they go to their own code office; the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance) lists the Town of Ithaca (607-273-1783), the Town of Lansing (607-533-7054) and every other town in the county. Cornell's [safety tool](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments) covers City rentals only. At [Ivy Ridge](https://www.veryapt.com/ApartmentReview-a23680-ivy-ridge-ithaca) (802 Dryden Rd, Varna), 7 reviews by Johnson MBA students from 2019 to 2025 describe dripping mini-splits, leaking pipes and slow management.
```

```reader "If you're living with a spouse, partner or children"
Mold and lead paint both fall under [habitability](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance), and for mold the guide says to keep photos, any test results and medical records. If a child's or partner's health is affected, those records are your evidence. Night heat matters most for a family: the floor is 62F from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m. regardless of the outside temperature. Report a failure the same night by text or email, not just a call. Moving out over mold is usually not justified unless the landlord ignores a serious health risk; get advice at 607-301-1560 before you leave.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
A landlord dispute does not affect your visa status or standing, Cornell Graduate School staff [say](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). New York also bars harassment of immigrant tenants, such as [denying heat or hot water](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/immigrant-tenant-rights). With a lease, you cannot be evicted for immigration status absent a substantial lease or code violation. The tenant hotline gives advice with no income or geographic limits; full representation is for City of Ithaca residents only ([Cornell International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources)). The mistake that costs most is stopping rent to force a fix. It starts a nonpayment case.
```

```reader "If you're a parent"
The repair request should come from the tenant, with their name, address and the date, so have your student send it and copy you. You can call the City Building Division yourself at 607-274-6508, Monday to Friday. Cornell's Off-Campus Living office does not represent students in landlord disputes, its manager [said in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/). The free help is the Cornell Law hotline (607-301-1560), LawNY at (607) 273-3667 and mediation at the Community Dispute Resolution Center, 607-273-9347 ([Cornell's resource list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/campus-living-resources)). Talk your student out of withholding rent.
```

```reader "If you need disability accommodations"
Cornell tells grad students with disability housing needs to [register with Student Disability Services first](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). If a feature your lease relies on fails, such as the elevator a building like [Dryden South](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961) lists, report it in writing the same day with photos and how it affects you, and ask for a timeframe. The hotline (607-301-1560) can say whether it counts as a habitability problem. Not confirmed: the rules on reasonable accommodations from private Ithaca landlords.
```

## What counts as the landlord's problem

The warranty is implied in every residential lease. The home and shared areas must be fit to live in and not dangerous to life, health or safety, and a lease clause waiving that is [void](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-B). Conditions you caused do not count.

The [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance) lists what it covers: no heat, hot or cold water, electricity or plumbing; pests; mold; structural defects; lead paint; unsanitary conditions; and broken appliances the landlord supplied. Missing air conditioning generally does not qualify. A code violation alone is not automatically a breach.

| Problem | The rule in Ithaca | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| No heat | Sept 15 to May 31; 68F from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. when under 55F outside; 62F from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m. | Note the indoor temperature and time; tell the landlord in writing that it is below the legal minimum |
| Bed bugs | Landlord exterminates even if you are suspected of bringing them, unless it proves misconduct; written notice within 72 hours to units above, below and beside, and a common-area posting | Report in writing; don't spray or treat yourself |
| Mold | No standalone rule; treated as a habitability problem | Photos, any tests, medical records |
| Utility cut-off (landlord pays) | At least 18 days' posted notice in apartment buildings, 15 days mailed in two-family houses, 30 days for heat from Nov 1 to Apr 15 | Keep the notice; call the hotline |

Sources: [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance); [Tompkins County bed-bug guidance](https://www.tompkinscountyny.gov/All-Departments/Whole-Health/Community/HNP/Bed-Bugs).

The county's bed-bug advice, revised May 2021, adds written landlord cooperation, integrated pest management, licensed pest companies, and no pesticides on the body. Self-treating can also [weaken a damages claim](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).

The problems are not rare. Poor maintenance is the second most repeated complaint in Cornell student accounts in the Sun and on review sites: 12 distinct articles and reviews from 2017 to 2026, by our count. A 2022 Sun columnist described spiders, clogged tubs and leaking sinks in a first apartment, and a friend's rental with mold and a burst pipe that [collapsed a wall](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/).

## How to get it fixed, step by step

```figure
kind: steps
title: Getting a repair made in Ithaca
rows:
  - label: Write to the landlord with your name, address, the date and the problem
  - label: Photograph it, log every contact and ask for a timeframe
  - label: Follow up in writing; send certified mail if you need proof
  - label: File with the code office for your address and ask for the inspection report
  - label: Call the free tenant hotline at 607-301-1560
  - label: After 30 days in the unit and written notice, petition the court for a repair order
```

1. **Write it down.** A letter, text or email with your name, address and the date. Follow every phone call with writing. Certified mail gives proof of delivery. There is [no strict statutory repair deadline](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance), so ask the landlord for a timeframe.
2. **Keep a record.** Cornell Cooperative Extension's steps are to record dates, times and photos, log each attempt, tell the owner, ask for a timeframe, follow up, and then [file a formal complaint](https://ccetompkins.org/your-home-money/tenant-landlord-issues).
3. **Let them in.** A landlord usually needs reasonable notice to enter, often 24 hours, at reasonable hours. It has no right to enter for repairs unless the lease reserves one, but it can enter without notice in an emergency ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access)). Code inspectors also need entry with reasonable notice.
4. **Call code enforcement.** There is no fixed waiting period: once the landlord's timeframe passes, or the heat is below the minimum, file. The office depends on your address, not your landlord's.
5. **Get legal advice before you hold back money.** The hotline is free; intake is also online.

| Your address | Code office | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| City of Ithaca (Collegetown, Fall Creek, downtown) | Building Division, 108 E. Green St., City Hall 4th floor, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. weekdays | 607-274-6508 (main line 607-274-6510) |
| Town of Ithaca (parts of East Hill) | Town code office | 607-273-1783 |
| Town of Lansing | Town code office | 607-533-7054 |
| Town of Dryden | Town code office | 607-844-8888 |

Sources: [City Building Division](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/130/Building-Division); [Cornell Law guide's code-office list](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).

City code inspectors handle housing complaints, and the Building Division takes them through its online "Report a Concern" form. Ask for any inspection report in writing.

## What a court can give you

Since January 2024, under the Tenant Dignity and Safe Housing Act, a tenant can file a Petition for Judgment Directing Repairs. You must have lived in the unit at least 30 days and sent written notice ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).

Courts can also reduce, or abate, the rent. From its court experience, the guide puts typical abatements at about 10% to 20% for minor problems, about 30% for moderate ones and 50% to 60% for the most serious.

Example: a senior pays $1,200 a month for a Collegetown room and the heat fails for a full January month. If a court treated that as among the most serious problems, the guide's range points to $600 to $720 back for that month. The same tenant with a dripping faucet might see $120 to $240. These are our multiplications of the guide's typical ranges, not a promise of any award.

## Why withholding rent is the last resort

Repair-and-deduct and rent withholding are [last resorts](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). Withholding invites a nonpayment case. That starts with a 14-day written rent demand, which can be served at least 6 days after rent is due; you then have 14 days to pay or leave before the landlord can file ([eviction guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/eviction-proceedings)).

If you are told to hold rent, keep it ready to pay. The 117 Thurston tenants in 2018 said they held their rent in a Tompkins Trust account ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/)).

In 2018 the tenants of [117 Thurston Ave](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/) went about 3 days without hot water and lived with heat below the legal minimum. They learned only after signing that the certificate of compliance had expired in November 2015. It took 53 days from their September 27 letter to a heater reported fixed on November 19. One tenant slept in a campus library. The students who left said the Building Department could then only send a written warning.

The City stiffened penalties in March 2019. Renting an unsafe unit became a misdemeanor, with a fine of up to $500 and 30 days, and landlords owe displaced tenants [double the monthly rent](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/03/city-council-votes-to-stiffen-penalties-for-noncompliant-landlords), prorated per day. If the City orders your building vacated, see the eviction and vacate order guide.

## Common mistakes

- **Only calling.** A phone call leaves no record, and an abatement claim rests on dated written requests.
- **Spraying for bed bugs yourself.** It can weaken a damages claim, and the landlord has to pay for extermination anyway.
- **Withholding rent first.** It hands the landlord a 14-day rent demand and a nonpayment case.
- **Calling the wrong office.** A Varna or Lansing address goes to a town code office, not the City Building Division.
- **Leaving over mold without advice.** Moving out is usually not justified unless the landlord ignores a serious health risk; otherwise the rent stays owed.
- **Staying quiet before renewal.** The 2021 Sun reported students avoiding requests for this reason, yet retaliation is a defense for a year after a good-faith complaint.

## FAQ

**Does my landlord have to provide air conditioning?** Generally no; lack of air conditioning does not usually breach the [warranty of habitability](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).

**Can I change the locks after a break-in if the landlord won't fix the door?** Tenants generally cannot change locks without consent, per the [landlord access guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/landlord-access). Ask in writing and call the hotline.

**I live in Lansing. Does the Sept 15 heat date apply?** The [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance) gives the statewide season as Oct 1 to May 31 and Ithaca's as Sept 15 to May 31. Ask your town code office which one it enforces.

**Can Cornell's safety tool tell me about a building's repair history?** No. It shows sprinklers, extinguishers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and construction type for [City rentals](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments), and does not rate the landlord.

**Who pays the exterminator if the landlord says I brought the bed bugs?** The landlord does, unless it proves tenant misconduct, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).
