- First try: International Scholar House, a furnished 5-bedroom rented month to month, open since September 2023, at $1,500 a month per room with utilities included.
- Second: Hasbrouck grad apartments, as space allows. Furnished 1BR $1,490 a month for 2026-27; ask gradhousing@cornell.edu about temporary stays.

Cornell does not guarantee housing to visiting scholars, so start with the two Cornell-run options: the furnished, month-to-month [International Scholar House](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell) and the [Hasbrouck apartments](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/visiting-faculty-affiliate-campus-housing), which take visiting faculty and affiliates as space allows. If both are full, a furnished semester sublet near Collegetown (one ran [$1,395 per person a month for Fall 2026](https://www.apartments.com/dryden-south-sublets-available-fall-2026-ithaca-ny/yeje6qs/)) beats signing a 12-month lease you will leave halfway through.

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar, postdoc or one-semester student"
You need months, not a year, and usually furniture. Email gradhousing@cornell.edu before you arrive: Cornell says Hasbrouck takes [temporary, academic-year or 12-month stays](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/visiting-faculty-affiliate-campus-housing) for some visiting faculty and affiliates, but only as space allows. Ask your host department about International Scholar House, the furnished month-to-month 5-bedroom. If both are full, look for spring sublets in December and January, when students leave to study abroad. The costly mistake is a 12-month lease for a six-month stay: you owe the remaining rent unless the landlord [re-rents the unit](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) or you sublet it. On a month-to-month rental, give [one month's notice](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) before you leave.
```

```reader "If you're an international visiting scholar or student"
You do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment, per [Cornell International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). You will likely sign from abroad, so check the deposit: in New York, the deposit plus any rent paid in advance [cannot exceed one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and [application fees are banned](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). A professor told the [Cornell Daily Sun in 2024](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell) that some visiting scholars get only $600 to $800 a month from their home governments. That covers Hasbrouck's $620 regular single in a shared townhouse, not a studio. [Graduate School staff say](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/) a dispute with a landlord does not affect your visa status. Cornell employees, including temporary employees and affiliates, [ride any TCAT bus free](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes) with a valid CU photo ID.
```

```reader "If you're coming with a spouse, partner or children"
The Sun reported family housing for visiting scholars as scarce, with high upfront costs. Hasbrouck's family 2-bedroom townhouse costs [$1,530 a month furnished or $1,475 unfurnished](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) for 2026-27, and a family 2-bedroom apartment on the third floor $1,500. Units hold [2 to 4 people, with no more than two adults](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). The average Ithaca 2-bedroom in large buildings asks [$2,459 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/) (RentCafe, October 2026). On a private lease, deposit plus first month is about $4,918 at signing (our arithmetic: one month's deposit, the legal cap, plus the first month). Apply to Hasbrouck early: it takes applications on a rolling basis. A partner with a CU ID can buy the [OmniRide student bus pass](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) at 116 Maple Avenue.
```

```reader "If you're a postdoc or new grad student here for a full year"
A year is long enough to use the regular grad market. Hasbrouck runs 12-month contracts with rolling applications: studio $1,355 a month, furnished 1-bedroom $1,490, furnished large single in a 2-bedroom townhouse $910 (2026-27). Maplewood, Cornell's Greystar-managed grad complex, is furnished with utilities included. A grad guide says to [look March to April](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html) for an August start, so you do not need to join the undergraduate rush the fall before. New degree students [ride TCAT free](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) for their first year, and Cornell employees, including temporary employees and affiliates, ride free with a CU photo ID.
```

### 1. International Scholar House

Since September 2023, Cornell has pointed visiting scholars to International Scholar House, a furnished 5-bedroom rented month to month, according to the [Cornell Daily Sun's 2024 report](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell). It is the only Cornell option built for stays of a few months. The house, at 401 Thurston Avenue, is [reserved for visiting international faculty and staff](https://new.express.adobe.com/webpage/6hZiqbHaVwOIg). A furnished room costs $1,500 a month with all utilities included, and bedding and towels are provided. Residents share a kitchen, living rooms and a laundry room. With five bedrooms, it fills fast, so write to intl_scholar_house@cornell.edu or call 607-254-3462 as soon as your appointment is confirmed.

### 2. Hasbrouck Apartments

Hasbrouck is Cornell's graduate and professional student community. Cornell's [visiting faculty page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/visiting-faculty-affiliate-campus-housing) says it houses "some visiting faculty members and Cornell affiliates" for temporary, academic-year or 12-month stays, as space allows, and that housing is not guaranteed. You apply through Cornell's housing application, following the graduate student instructions. For a temporary stay, write to gradhousing@cornell.edu.

### 3. Maplewood Apartments

Maplewood is managed by Greystar for Cornell. Every unit, from studio to 4-bedroom, is [furnished with utilities included](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing), with a private bathroom per bedroom, an in-unit washer and dryer, and pets allowed. The Vet College's apartment list puts Maplewood at "$1075- $1330" for studio to 4-bedroom, [updated February 2026](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/education/biomedical-biological-sciences-bbs-phd-program/prospective-students/our-community/housing-information). Greystar charges a [$130 one-time amenity fee per leaseholder](https://www.greystar.com/maplewood-ithaca-ny/p_15697) at move-in and requires renters liability insurance. For lease terms, call the leasing office at (607) 821-0248.

### 4. Semester sublets

A student leaving for a semester often wants someone to take over the rent. Dryden South at 205 Dryden Road advertised [suites at $1,395 per person for a Fall 2026 sublet](https://www.apartments.com/dryden-south-sublets-available-fall-2026-ithaca-ny/yeje6qs/). The building is [furnished, with heat and hot water included](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), and its leases run 12 months, all ending May 31, 2027. A sublet makes you the original tenant's subtenant, so the landlord's written consent matters: in buildings with four or more units, a tenant may sublet with the [landlord's consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). In smaller buildings, [the lease decides](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).

### 5. Semester leases

A few landlords lease by the semester. Ithaca Renting's Collegetown list includes ["Spring/Fall Semester 2027" rows](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/); its Collegetown units are furnished, with heat and hot water included and electricity mostly extra. Foundation Housing lists [Spring '27 roommate-matching leases](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-ny/listing/spring-27-and-full-year-leases-available-for-roommate-matching-foundation-housing-96176) on the Ithaca College portal.

### 6. Nightly and weekly rentals

Useful for your first week while you look. Inside the City of Ithaca, [short-term rentals need a separate permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections) from the one for ordinary rentals. In the Village of Cayuga Heights, a rental of 28 days or less needs an [annual permit under Local Law 2022-4](https://cayugaheights.gov/departments/code-enforcement/). Ask the host for the permit.

### 7. A 12-month lease

Ithaca Renting's downtown units, mostly for 2026-27, are marked "Available Now" in places, on [12-month leases](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), with a median studio at $1,395 a month and a 1-bedroom at $1,625. These suit a stay of a year. For six months, they cost you six months of rent you will not use, unless you find someone to take over.

## Lease, no lease, or month to month

| Arrangement | How you leave | What protects you |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-term lease | No notice needed at the end; leaving early, you owe rent until the unit is re-let | The written lease; the landlord's duty to try to re-rent |
| Month to month | Generally one month's notice | After 30 days, the landlord needs formal notice and a court case to remove you |
| Sublet | Ends with the sublease term | The sublease; the original tenant stays liable to the landlord |
| Oral deal, no term | At will | Little; get it in writing |

Cornell's own advice is that a one-year lease gives [a certain amount of legal protection](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations), while a unit without a lease gives flexibility with limited protection. A month-to-month tenant [generally gives one month's notice](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease) to leave. A fixed-term lease that runs out without proper non-renewal notice [continues month to month](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal). After 30 days, a month-to-month tenant can be removed only with [formal notice and a court case](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/immigrant-tenant-rights). An oral lease with no term is at will, and an oral lease for more than a year [is unenforceable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease).

If you leave a fixed lease early, New York requires the landlord to [take reasonable steps to re-rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E) the unit. A new tenant's rent offsets what you owe.

Example: a visiting scholar arrives January 1 for six months and signs a 12-month downtown studio at the $1,395 median. Twelve months is $16,740. Leaving June 30 leaves six months, $8,370, owed until the landlord re-rents or a subtenant takes over. A furnished Hasbrouck 1-bedroom at $1,490 for the same six months would cost $8,940, if Hasbrouck can offer a temporary stay.

## What you can be charged up front

The deposit plus any rent paid in advance [cannot exceed one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) in New York. That cap matters to scholars asked to prepay a semester. Application fees are banned, and a credit or background check [costs no more than $20](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). The landlord must return the deposit, with an itemized list of deductions, within 14 days after you leave.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing a 12-month lease for a six-month stay.** On a $1,395 studio, that is $8,370 owed for months you are gone, unless the unit is re-rented or sublet.
- **Assuming Cornell will house you.** Housing for visiting scholars is [not guaranteed](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell); the Sun reported that they compete with graduate students for Hasbrouck and Maplewood.
- **Taking a room on a handshake.** An oral deal with no term ends at will, and you have little to prove what you were promised.
- **Taking a sublet the landlord never approved.** A sublet that skips the legal steps is a [substantial breach](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) of the original lease. Ask to see the landlord's written consent before you pay.
- **Prepaying several months to win a unit.** Deposit plus advance rent cannot exceed one month's rent. On a $1,395 studio, $1,395 is the most a landlord may hold.

## FAQ

**Does a visiting scholar need an SSN to rent?** No. Cornell International Services says you [do not need an SSN](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students) to open a bank account, rent an apartment or get a credit card.

**Will a dispute with my landlord affect my visa?** Cornell Graduate School staff say landlord disputes [do not affect international students' visa or standing](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). They advise contacting Off-Campus Living as soon as a problem starts. The Ithaca [tenant hotline, (607) 301-1560](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources), gives advice with no income or geographic limits.

**Can I bring my family into Hasbrouck?** Hasbrouck's family and partner units hold [2 to 4 people, with no more than two adults](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). Family 2-bedrooms run $1,475 to $1,530 a month for 2026-27.

**Do visiting scholars ride the bus free?** Cornell employees, including temporary employees and affiliates, [ride any TCAT route free at any time](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes) with a valid CU photo ID. New degree students ride free for their first year, and PhD students and graduate assistants get free OmniRide passes.

**How much is a 1-bedroom if I stay a full year?** RentCafe's October 2026 average for an Ithaca 1-bedroom in large buildings is [$2,063 a month](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/). Hasbrouck's unfurnished 1-bedroom is $1,415 with utilities included.
