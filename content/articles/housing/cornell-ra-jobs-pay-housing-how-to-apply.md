A Cornell resident advisor (RA) gets a free single room and a taxable stipend that starts at $3,930 a semester, in exchange for about 20 hours of work a week ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-position-description)). At 2026-27 room rates, a first semester's room and stipend are worth about $11,713. The 2027-28 application has closed.

## What does a Cornell RA get paid?

An RA is a student who lives in a residence hall and looks after the people on their floor. Cornell pays RAs in three parts. They get a single room, a stipend paid twice a month, and a MicroFridge (a small fridge and microwave in one unit) if the room has no standard fridge. The stipend is taxed. It is cut if you arrive late or leave early. You can't trade any part of the package for cash or a different room.

The stipend rises the longer you serve as an RA in Housing and Residential Life (HRL), the office that runs Cornell's dorms. Only fall and spring semesters as an HRL RA count. Time as an RA in the West Campus House System or at another school does not.

## What is the free room worth?

The room is a single, and Cornell charges other students $15,566 a year for a single in 2026-27 ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Room rates for 2027-28 are not yet announced, so the math below uses the 2026-27 price.

The better comparison is what you would pay without the job. Most students on campus live in a double. If you'd otherwise take one, the RA job saves you its full rent and hands you a single too.

**By the numbers.** Say you are in your second year as an RA and would otherwise live in a North Campus double with the Bear Traditional meal plan. That costs $20,850 a year: $13,744 for the room, $7,056 for the plan and the $50 yearly dining fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). If you bought the same plan and paid the fee as an RA, you would pay $7,106. Your $8,060 in second-year stipends covers it with $954 left. The swing is $21,804 a year ($20,850 + $954), before taxes and any change to your aid. With the Unlimited plan at $7,604 plus the fee, $406 of the stipend is left.

Measured per hour, the cash alone is thin. The fall appointment runs Aug. 4 to Dec. 20, 2027, about 20 weeks. At the weekly average that is about 400 hours. The first-semester stipend works out to about $9.83 an hour. Add the fall half of the single's price, $7,783, and the semester package is $11,713, or about $29.28 an hour. The room is the real pay ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-position-description)).

## How does it compare with renting in Collegetown?

An RA pays no rent. A bed in a Collegetown 4-bedroom has a median asking rent of $1,246.25 a month for 2027-28, or $14,955 over a 12-month lease ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). That is $1,211 more than the campus double, and the RA job wipes out all of it. The full set of options, from a $5,530 co-op triple up, is in our [Ithaca housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/), and the [cheapest off-campus housing guide](/cornell/housing/cheapest-off-campus-housing-cornell/) ranks them.

**Watch out.** The timing works against students who want both a lease and an RA job. Popular Collegetown leases leave the market up to 16 months before move-in ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)). Cornell sends most fall RA offers from March. If you sign a lease in the fall and then get the job, you owe the rent on both counts: you must live in your RA room, and Cornell says it can't help you change an off-campus lease ([RA FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-faqs)). If you want the RA job, don't sign a lease you can't sublet.

A Cornell housing license works differently. If you signed one for next year and accept an RA job, Housing cancels it and gives you a new license for your RA room. If you decline the job, or lose eligibility, you still owe the license you signed. Cornell advises every applicant to go through regular housing selection anyway, because applying is no guarantee.

## How does the RA job affect financial aid?

Cornell adds the free room to your aid record as a grant, and the stipend is taxable income. If you get aid, Cornell reviews your package after you accept and can revise it. You could then owe a refund back, or owe the bursar for a balance your old package covered. Cornell places a hold on your account while Financial Aid and the bursar adjust, so a refund can be delayed. Talk to the Office of Financial Aid before you apply, and again before you accept an offer.

```reader "If you're on a large aid package"
Your gain can be much smaller than the $21,804 swing above. If your aid already pays most of your room, the room grant can replace aid you were getting instead of adding to it. Ask Financial Aid for a written estimate of your package with and without the RA job before the 4-business-day acceptance window starts. Our [first-gen and low-income costs guide](/cornell/money/cornell-first-gen-low-income-hidden-costs/) covers the other bills aid doesn't touch.
```

## What does the job involve?

The weekly hours go to residents, meetings and an on-call rotation. They include two hours a week of Community Hours, when you're on hand for residents, and one-on-one "RA Chats" with them. They include a mandatory team meeting on Wednesdays from 5:15 to 6:45 p.m. They include meetings with your supervisor, a Residence Hall Director (RHD), a full-time staff member who lives on campus.

On-call runs 7 p.m. to 8 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and 24 hours on weekends. The RA on call must be in the building from 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. and reach any call in person within 15 minutes. You can sleep while on call, as long as you answer the phone. Calls cover lockouts, noise and roommate conflicts ([RA FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-faqs)).

The job also takes your breaks. RAs move in Aug. 3, 2027, train Aug. 4 to 13 and help with move-in after that. They stay for fall closing on Dec. 19 and 20, 2027, return Jan. 16, 2028, and leave May 24 or 25, 2028. RAs must be present whenever their community stays open, including university breaks and holidays, and they share coverage ([Cornell Housing and Residential Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-position-description)).

**Watch out.** Other commitments are limited. RAs can't also be Orientation Leaders, work at the Tatkon Center, serve as a Resident Language Fellow, sit on the Residence Hall Association executive board or hold a West Campus live-in leadership role. Any other job needs your supervisor's approval first.

## Who can apply?

You must be a full-time Cornell undergraduate taking at least 12 credits each semester. You need at least two semesters of college finished before the job starts, not counting winter or summer terms. That means first-years can apply in their first fall, before they have a Cornell GPA. You need a 2.5 GPA each semester and overall, checked during selection and through the job. You must attend one information session, pass a background check and disclose any past or current conduct charges ([RA FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-faqs)).

Cornell has about 200 RAs. Cornell publishes no count of openings each year. Placement follows the department's needs, so you can land in an area you didn't rank. RA jobs cover North and South Campus first-year, upper-level and program house communities, not West Campus, Hasbrouck or the co-ops ([RA FAQs](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection/resident-advisor-faqs)).

```reader "If you're a first-year"
With no Cornell GPA when you apply, yours is checked at the end of fall and spring. If you get an offer and finish spring below 2.5, you lose the job.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
F-1 and J-1 students may work up to 20 hours a week while classes are in session, and going over even once is a serious violation of status ([Office of Global Learning](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/employment-and-taxes/campus-work)). The RA job's average can fill that whole limit, so ask International Services before you add a second campus job. You also need a Social Security number for the background check, and you must apply for it in person. J-1 students need an on-campus employment letter first. Our [F-1 campus jobs guide](/cornell/money/cornell-international-student-jobs-on-campus-f-1-how-to-find/) covers the rest.
```

## How do you apply to be a Cornell RA?

You attend one information session, then submit an online application. For 2027-28, applications opened Oct. 13, 2026, were due by Oct. 27 for priority review and closed Nov. 3 ([RA selection page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection)). Late applications were read only if HRL still needed RAs. One application covered both the spring 2027 opening and the full 2027-28 year.

Students with a class conflict could ask for help through a form by Nov. 2 ([RA selection page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection)).

## Can you be an RA in the summer or over winter break?

Yes. Summer Session RAs and Pre-Collegiate Summer Scholars Program (PSSP) RAs, who live with high school students in Cornell's summer program, are hired separately. One application covers both. The 2026 round opened April 3, 2026, with an April 17 priority deadline, and paid a room plus a stipend ([Summer Session RA selection](https://web.archive.org/web/20251209224105/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/student-employment/summer-session-resident-advisor-ra-selection)). Cornell says to check back during the spring 2027 semester for the 2027 round. You can apply whatever happened in the academic-year cycle. Our [Ithaca summer jobs guide](/cornell/money/cornell-summer-jobs-ithaca-students-stay-for-summer/) lists other ways to stay for the summer.

Winter Break RA jobs exist, but only current RAs can apply.

```reader "If you're a graduate student"
You can't be an undergraduate RA, and an RA who graduates and returns as a grad student loses eligibility. Your version is the Graduate Community Advisor at Hasbrouck Apartments. The 2025-26 posting paid a two-bedroom apartment and a $7,000 first-year stipend for 5 hours a week, year-round, with applications due Feb. 10, 2025 ([Cornell Housing](https://web.archive.org/web/20251209224004/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/student-employment/graduate-community-advisor-positions)). Watch that page in January. At the graduate level, "RA" usually means research assistant, a different job: its 2026-27 minimum stipend is $36,684 for nine months ([Cornell Graduate School](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/)).
```

## FAQ

**Can an RA hold another job?** Yes, if your work-hour limit leaves room beyond the RA job and your supervisor approves. Every RA duty still comes first. Our [Ithaca part-time jobs guide](/cornell/careers/ithaca-part-time-jobs-for-cornell-students/) lists campus options, and the [off-campus jobs guide](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-jobs-ithaca-students-part-time/) covers work in town.

**Can I apply while studying abroad?** Yes. If you're invited, you interview by video during the regular interview days. The job itself must be done in person.

**What if I quit or lose the job midyear?** You call Cornell Housing for a new room. If you still owe the live-on requirement, Housing assigns you one. If you don't, Housing tries to find a room and can put you on the wait list.

## What to do next

If you're a first-year or sophomore aiming at 2028-29, book a Financial Aid appointment in September 2027 and ask what the room grant would do to your package. Then watch the RA selection page for the October information sessions. If you want a summer RA job, check the Summer Session page in March 2027 ([RA selection page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-cornell-housing/student-employment-cornell-residential-life/resident-advisor-ra-selection)).
