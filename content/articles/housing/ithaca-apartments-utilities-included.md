Only a handful of Ithaca apartments bundle every utility into the rent. Kelvin Place, a Cornell Heights building for graduate students, lists electricity, heat, hot water, internet, trash and water in its rent ([Cornell Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/kelvin-place-apartments-237474)). Elsewhere, expect to add an electric bill.

## Which Ithaca apartments include all utilities?

These listings say all utilities are in the price. Prices are as listed; ask the landlord which lease year a quote covers.

## Which landlords cover only heat and hot water?

Most Collegetown and downtown rents cover heat and hot water and little else. Ithaca Renting, the largest Collegetown landlord, includes heat and hot water at all its units, trash at some, and electricity only at a few ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). Downtown, its buildings include heat, water and trash, and electricity only at the Colonial Building, Commons Studios and Whiton Hall ([Ithaca Renting downtown](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/)). Electricity is also included in the shared suites on floors 2 and 3 of 210 Dryden Rd. and in units rented by the room ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)).

Dryden South at 205 Dryden Rd. includes heat and hot water (Cornell marketplace, [Dryden South](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961)).

East Pointe charges a monthly utility fee that covers internet, cable, water, sewer, trash and recycling, and residents pay electricity to NYSEG based on use ([East Pointe FAQ](https://eastpointeapartment.com/faqs/)). A listing that says "utilities included" can still leave out electricity, so read the line item.

## How much does electricity add?

EnergySage puts the average Ithaca electric bill at about $282 a month, or $3,384 a year, at about 1,125 kilowatt-hours, with a price near 25 cents per kilowatt-hour against 21 nationally ([EnergySage](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/electricity-cost/ny/tompkins-county/ithaca/)). The figure comes from bills shared by Ithaca residents requesting quotes on its marketplace, so a one-bedroom may sit below that household average. Split five ways, that is $56 each.

Our [utilities and winter heating guide](/cornell/money/ithaca-utilities-cost-winter-heating-bill-apartment-students/) covers bills by season and how to ask NYSEG for a building's history.

## How does that compare with a dorm?

A Cornell residence hall double costs $13,744 a year, and the rate includes gas, electricity, garbage and internet up to 200 GB a month ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Our [monthly cost guide](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/) puts both side by side.

In Collegetown, the median 6BR bed is $800 a month for 2027-28 with electricity extra, and Kelvin Place bundles everything into one rent ([rent per bed dataset](/datasets/collegetown-rent-per-bed-2027-28/)). The Collegetown [one-bedroom and per-bed prices](/cornell/housing/collegetown-rent-prices-per-bed-one-bedroom/) show where else a bundled price beats a base rent plus bills.

## What if the heat fails?

If heat is included, the landlord owes it. In Ithaca the heat season runs Sept. 15 to May 31, and from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. the inside temperature must be at least 68 degrees F when it is below 55 degrees F outside (Cornell Law School Tenants Advocacy, [building issues](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)).

## What to do next

Before you sign, ask the landlord for a written list of what is included, utility by utility, and for the last year of electric bills for the unit. Then add a year of electricity to the base rent and compare buildings on that total.
