```glance
- Maplewood, the Ivy, the Summit, The Dean, City Centre and Aces list in-unit washers and dryers.
- Collegetown Center, Plaza and Court share laundry on a floor or in the building.
- Aces' in-unit 6-bedrooms list lower per-bed prices than Plaza's shared-laundry 6-bedrooms.
- Confirm in-unit laundry for the exact unit; a listing may describe only some units.
```

If you want a washer and dryer inside your apartment, look first at Cornell's Maplewood Apartments, the newer Collegetown towers and the downtown buildings built in the last decade. A listing's laundry line sometimes covers one building and not every unit, so ask about the exact apartment.

```reader "If you're a grad student"
Maplewood is Cornell's graduate housing on East Hill, and Cornell lists [furnished studios to 4-bedrooms with in-unit laundry](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing), utilities included, pets allowed. Cornell does not post prices there. A Cornell vet school page lists a range of [$1,075 to $1,330 for studios to 4-bedrooms](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/education/biomedical-biological-sciences-bbs-phd-program/prospective-students/our-community/housing-information), updated in February 2026, with no per-bed or per-unit basis. The [grad housing guide](/cornell/housing/off-campus-housing-cornell-grad-students/) covers Hasbrouck too.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate student with a partner or children"
Laundry matters more with a household. Maplewood's in-unit machines fit a family, but check unit sizes first. Cornell's [student family housing guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-student-family-housing-ithaca/) covers Hasbrouck's family units, and the Cornell fact sources used here do not state whether Hasbrouck units have in-unit laundry, so ask the housing office before you apply.
```

The cheapest large houses are the ones to look at, as the [Collegetown medians](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/) show.

## Where else can I find in-unit laundry?

East Hill, South Hill and the north side have in-unit options at lower prices, with a longer trip to campus.

- **Westview Terrace**, 8 Country Club Rd: a [2-bedroom unit](https://www.travishyde.com/listings/detail/9dc6c8ea-ccb9-4242-8aad-1995082e539a) with an in-unit washer and dryer, furnished or unfurnished. Tenants pay electric.
- **Summerhill Townhouses**, 222 Summerhill Lane: [3-bedroom townhouses at $2,700 to $2,750](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/summerhill-townhouses-7826), unfurnished, with a washer and dryer in each.
- **Triphammer Apartments**, 5 Cinema Dr: 1- and 2-bedroom homes at [$1,695 to $1,750](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/triphammer-236697), with in-home washer and dryer connections.
- **The Outlook**, 815 S Aurora St, on South Hill: [fully furnished with in-unit laundry](https://www.outlookithaca.com/) and a private resident shuttle, from $1,800 to $3,700 on the Cornell marketplace.
- **Winston Square**, 601-1 Winston Court: a [washer and dryer in every unit](https://winstonsquareapts.com/).

Cornell's neighborhoods page puts South Hill 1.5 to 3 miles from campus, and Lansing 5 to 10 miles out. The [guide to areas](/cornell/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) compares the three nearest neighborhoods, and the commute adds a bus ride.

## How much does a month cost with in-unit laundry?

Add electricity, because washers and dryers raise the bill. Ask whether the lease includes electricity, gas for the dryer and a water charge. At Winston Square the utilities-included list covers heat, gas for the stove and dryer, trash and pest control. At Westview Terrace the tenant pays electric. The [monthly cost guide](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/) puts rent, utilities and food in one budget.

## Is in-unit laundry worth paying for?

It saves time and a weekly trip, and the price gap is small or zero in some buildings, as the Aces and Plaza comparison shows. The alternative is shared laundry. At Collegetown Center the machines are on the sixth floor, and at Plaza on the fifth, so a load means an elevator ride in a building with a concierge desk and a fitness room. Downtown buildings such as Commons West use coin or credit-card machines in the building.

A pickup service is the other option. Lazybones, a vendor on Cornell's [housing vendor page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/housing-vendor-partnerships), charges from $1.45 per pound. A 15-pound load is $21.75 ($1.45 times 15), so a student who sends one load a week spends $87 a month at that rate. Compare that with the rent gap between a building with in-unit machines and one without.

## How do I confirm laundry for my unit?

Ask three questions in writing. Is the washer and dryer inside the apartment, or in the building? Is it included in the rent, or a coin or card machine? Who repairs it if it breaks? A marketplace listing tags "Washer & Dryer" for some units and "Laundry On-Site" for others, so the tag alone does not settle it. The [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) explains where to find and compare listings.

## What to do next

Pick the neighborhood first, then shortlist three buildings with in-unit laundry. Ask each for the exact unit, its price and the laundry setup in writing.
