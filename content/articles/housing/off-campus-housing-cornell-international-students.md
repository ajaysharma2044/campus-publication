```glance
- New York caps a deposit plus any prepaid rent at one month's rent.
- Application fees are banned; a credit check costs at most $20.
- Before you wire money, confirm the unit's Certificate of Compliance and run Cornell's safety lookup.
```

You do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment in Ithaca, [Cornell International Services says](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Because you may sign before you visit, check the building before any money leaves your account.

## What documents do I need to rent?

None that you cannot get. Cornell International Services says you do not need an SSN to open a bank account, rent an apartment or get a credit card. A landlord can run a background or credit check, but the fee is capped at the lesser of the actual cost or $20, and the landlord must give you a copy of the check and the invoice. If you bring your own check from the past 30 days, the fee is waived ([New York Real Property Law 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)).

Cornell does not publish how Ithaca landlords treat renters with no US credit history or whether they ask for a US guarantor, meaning someone who promises to pay if you cannot. Ask each landlord in writing before you sign what they accept instead.

Undergraduates have one more question: permission. First- and second-years must live in Cornell or affiliated housing and [should not sign a lease without an approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).

## What can a landlord charge at signing?

Under [General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), the security deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent. A pet deposit counts toward the cap, and a last month's rent cannot be added on top. A landlord who asks you to prepay six months because you have no credit history is asking for more than the cap allows.

| Charge | Legal in New York? | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|
| First month's rent | Yes | Normal practice |
| Security deposit | Yes | Deposit plus any rent advance capped at one month's rent |
| Last month's rent on top of a deposit | No | Counts toward the same cap |
| Application or processing fee | No | Banned |
| Credit or background check | Yes | Lesser of actual cost or $20 |
| Late fee | Yes, limited | After rent is 5 days late; lesser of $50 or 5% of monthly rent |
| Cleaning fee separate from the deposit | No | Banned per the Cornell Law guide |

Source: [Real Property Law 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A), GOL 7-108 and the [Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits).

Take a bed in a downtown two-bedroom share at $992.50 a month, the [Ithaca Renting downtown median](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/). The most a landlord can collect at signing is the first month plus a one-month deposit, plus a credit check.

The deposit is due back with an itemized statement within 14 days after you move out, and a landlord who misses that deadline loses the right to keep any of it. ## How do I check a unit from abroad?

Use public records. Start inside Cornell's own channel. The Off-Campus Housing Marketplace shows listings only to people who [prove Cornell affiliation](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), and its listings are checked against the City's Certificate of Compliance records. A Certificate of Compliance is the city's safety permit for rentals; buildings with three or more units need one. The [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) compares the places to look.

| Check | How, from abroad | What it does not tell you |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate of Compliance | Call 607-274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org with the address | Anything outside the City of Ithaca |
| Cornell safety lookup | safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu shows sprinklers, extinguishers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and construction type | Landlord behavior or neighborhood; Town of Ithaca, Lansing and Cayuga Heights |
| Heating history | Ask NYSEG, 800-572-1111, for the average annual heating bill | Whether the heat works this winter |
| Free lease review | Tenant hotline, 607-301-1560 | Anything outside the lease text |

Source: Cornell's [safe living page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments), [neighborhood guide](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods) and [legal resources page](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources).

## Who covers heat?

Ask before you sign. Ithaca's heat season runs Sept. 15 to May 31, the stretch when a landlord must supply heat ([Cornell Law tenants guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance)). The lease says who pays for it. Cornell suggests asking NYSEG for the average heating bill, especially for West Hill cottages.

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments take rolling applications on 12-month contracts. A studio is $1,355 a month and an unfurnished one-bedroom is $1,415 for 2026-27 ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). Compare a utilities-included rate with a bare rent before you choose. Cornell's guide for grads says to contact Off-Campus Living as soon as a problem starts ([Cornell Graduate School](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/)).
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar, postdoc or short-stay student"
Visiting scholars have [no guaranteed Cornell housing](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell). The International Scholar House is a month-to-month furnished five-bedroom. The costly mistake is a 12-month lease for a six-month stay. In buildings with four or more units you may sublet with the landlord's [written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B).
```

## What happens over breaks and between leases?

Students worried about not being able to travel home during breaks can email housing@cornell.edu about [on-campus housing during breaks](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources).

New degree students ride TCAT free in the first year with a Cornell ID. The pass [expires Aug. 31 for fall entrants](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) and Dec. 31 for spring entrants. A cheaper downtown unit costs nothing extra to commute from in year one. The [international arrival guide](/cornell/getting-around/getting-to-cornell-international-students/) covers the trip itself.

## Does a landlord dispute affect my visa?

No. Cornell Graduate School staff say landlord disputes do not affect an international student's visa or standing. State law also protects you: a tenant with a lease cannot be evicted because of immigration status unless there is a substantial lease or code violation ([Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/immigrant-tenant-rights)). The tenant hotline, 607-301-1560, gives advice with no income or geographic limits.

## Does financial aid pay the deposit?

No. Cornell aid [does not cover deposits](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), first or last month's rent, or rent in months you are not registered.

## What to do next

1. Ask every landlord in writing what they accept without US credit history.
2. Confirm the certificate and run the safety lookup before you wire money.
3. Get the lease reviewed at 607-301-1560, then sign.
