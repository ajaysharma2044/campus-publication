- New York caps a deposit plus any prepaid rent at one month's rent, so "pay six months up front" runs into the law.
- Application fees are banned; a credit or background check costs at most $20, and is free if you bring one from the past 30 days.
- Before you send money from abroad: confirm the City Certificate of Compliance (607-274-6508) and look the address up on Cornell's safety tool.
- Ask whether heat is in the rent; Ithaca's heat season runs Sept 15 to May 31.

You do not need a Social Security number to rent an apartment in Ithaca, [Cornell International Services says](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). At signing, New York lets a landlord take a security deposit plus any rent advance [worth no more than one month's rent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), and bans application fees. Because you will likely sign before you can visit, check the building's City certificate and Cornell's safety tool before any money leaves your account.

```reader "If you're an international student"
Your visa appointment can be no more than [120 days before your program starts](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students), so you will probably sign sight unseen. Expect to pay first month's rent plus a deposit of up to one month's rent; Cornell aid [does not cover deposits or first and last month's rent](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). If you cannot fly home over breaks, ask housing@cornell.edu [about on-campus housing](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources). Your first year of [TCAT buses is free](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). The mistake that costs most: wiring a deposit for a unit nobody has checked. Confirm the certificate and the safety tool first.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate or professional student"
About [95% of Cornell grad students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) live off campus, and your market runs later than the undergraduate rush: one grad guide says [start looking March to April for an August start](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html), with a few places left in June and July. Cornell's Hasbrouck Apartments take rolling applications on 12-month contracts: a studio is [$1,355 a month and an unfurnished 1BR $1,415 for 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates), with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included. PhD students and graduate assistants ride TCAT free every year; other master's students ride free in year one only. The costly mistake: comparing a utilities-included rate with a bare rent.
```

```reader "If you're a transfer student"
A transfer entering as a junior or senior is exempt from Cornell's residential requirement and may sign a lease, but must [request the exemption each year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). A sophomore transfer [lives on campus for one academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates) and should not sign. Admission comes after Collegetown's best units are gone, so you shop the leftover market: downtown "available now" units, where Ithaca Renting's 2BR median is [$992.50 a bed per month](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), and sublets. Free first-year TCAT makes a downtown address workable. The costly mistake: signing as a sophomore transfer, since Cornell [still bills the full year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing) of campus housing.
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar, postdoc or short-stay student"
Visiting scholars have [no guaranteed Cornell housing](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell); the International Scholar House is a month-to-month furnished 5BR, open since September 2023. Ithaca Renting lists [semester rows for spring and fall 2027](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). A month-to-month tenant [generally gives one month's notice](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). The costly mistake: a 12-month lease for a six-month stay. The rest is owed unless the unit is re-let, though the landlord [must try in good faith to re-rent it](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E). The one-month deposit cap and fee bans protect you the same as anyone.
```

## Documents: what you need and what you don't

Cornell International Services is direct: you [don't need an SSN](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students) to open a bank account, rent an apartment or get a credit card. A landlord may run a background or credit check, but New York caps the fee at [the lesser of actual cost or $20](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A). The landlord must give you a copy of the check and the invoice. If you bring your own check from the past 30 days, the fee is waived.

Not confirmed: how Ithaca landlords screen renters with no US credit history, and whether they require a US guarantor. Ask each landlord in writing before you sign what they accept instead.

Undergraduates have one more document question: permission. First- and second-years must live in Cornell or affiliated housing and [should not sign a lease without an approved exemption](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). Exemptions last one academic year.

## Money: what a landlord may ask for at signing

The deposit cap is the rule that matters most when you pay from abroad. Under [General Obligations Law 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108), the security deposit plus any rent advance cannot exceed one month's rent. A pet deposit counts toward the cap, and a "last month's rent" cannot be added on top. A landlord who asks you to prepay six months because you have no credit history is asking for a rent advance the cap counts.

| Charge | Legal in New York? | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|
| First month's rent at signing | Yes | Normal practice |
| Security deposit | Yes | Deposit plus any rent advance capped at one month's rent ([GOL 7-108](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)) |
| Last month's rent on top of a deposit | No | Counts toward the same one-month cap |
| Several months' rent prepaid | Not beyond the cap | A rent advance counts toward one month |
| Application or processing fee | No | Banned ([RPL 238-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A)) |
| Credit or background check | Yes | Lesser of actual cost or $20; waived with your own check from the past 30 days |
| Non-refundable deposit | No | [Unlawful; the agreement is void](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) |
| Cleaning fee | No | Banned as a separate fee |
| Late fee | Yes, limited | Only after rent is 5 days late; lesser of $50 or 5% of monthly rent |

Example: an international master's student takes a bed in a downtown 2BR share at $992.50 a month, Ithaca Renting's [downtown 2BR median](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) read in November 2026. At signing the most the landlord can collect is the first month plus a deposit of one month.

A demand for six months up front would be $5,955 on that bed, six times the cap. Pay by a method that leaves a record, and get a written receipt for every payment, as [Cornell's lease checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease) advises.

The deposit comes back with an itemized statement [within 14 days after you move out](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). A landlord who misses that deadline forfeits the right to keep any of it. In buildings with six or more units, the deposit must sit in a New York interest-bearing account. ## Checking a unit from abroad

You cannot walk through the apartment, so let the public records do it. Start inside Cornell's own channel. The Off-Campus Housing Marketplace shows listings only to people who [prove Cornell affiliation](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), and its listings are [checked against the City's Certificate of Compliance database](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). The Off-Campus Living email list covers housing, roommates and sublets. It needs a NetID; an admitted student without one forwards the acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu.

| Check | How, from abroad | What it does not tell you |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate of Compliance | Call (607) 274-6508 or email ibd@cityofithaca.org with the address; required for buildings with 3+ units, valid 3 years ([City of Ithaca](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance)) | Anything outside the City of Ithaca |
| Cornell safety tool | safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu: sprinklers, extinguishers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, construction type, updated daily ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments)) | Landlord behavior, looks or neighborhood; Town of Ithaca, Lansing and Cayuga Heights |
| Landlord references | Ask how long they have rented, who repairs, how fast, and whether deposits came back ([Cornell checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease)) | Only what past tenants choose to say |
| Heating history | Ask NYSEG, (800) 572-1111, for the average annual heating bill ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods)) | Whether the heat works this winter |
| Free lease review | [Tenant hotline](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources) (607) 301-1560; [Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/signing-lease), 607-255-2310 | Nothing outside the lease text |

## Heat: get it in the lease

Ithaca winters decide your real rent. The City's heat season runs [Sept 15 to May 31](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). From 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. the inside must reach at least 68F when it is below 55F outside, and 62F at night regardless.

Who pays for that heat varies by lease. Ithaca Renting [includes heat and hot water at all its properties](https://ithacarenting.com/all-properties/); electricity is extra except in a few buildings. Elsewhere, ask. Cornell warns that West Hill "cottages" are [likely uninsulated, with very high heating bills](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods). NYSEG bills rose under [temporary rates from June 1, 2026](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/05/15/nyseg-and-rge-win-temporary-rate-hikes-as-larger-increases-still-loom/), with a larger permanent case still pending.

Undergraduates who want Collegetown face an earlier clock: its desirable units can lease [up to 16 months before move-in](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), so the hunt for a junior-year lease happens in sophomore fall. Grad students and transfers shop later.

After you sign but before you move in, the landlord must [offer a move-in inspection](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Accept it. Both sides sign a condition record, and the landlord cannot later keep deposit money for defects listed on it.

## Breaks and the months between leases

Most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing) and the next starts about August 1, so a 12-month lease covers winter and summer breaks. A Cornell housing license does not: it charges [$60 a day outside the occupancy period](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). Students who cannot travel home may ask housing@cornell.edu about [on-campus housing during breaks](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources).

New degree students, including grad, PhD and transfer students, ride TCAT free all year with a Cornell ID. The pass [expires Aug 31 for fall entrants](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes) and Dec 31 for spring entrants. After that, rides are free only after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend, unless you buy the $200-a-year OmniRide pass. So a cheaper downtown or Fall Creek unit costs nothing extra to commute from in year one.

## Your status and a landlord dispute

A disagreement with a landlord does not touch your visa. Cornell Graduate School staff say landlord disputes [do not affect international students' visa or standing](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/), and advise contacting Off-Campus Living as soon as a problem starts.

State law protects you too. A tenant with a lease [cannot be evicted for immigration status](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/immigrant-tenant-rights) absent a substantial lease or code violation, and harassment such as denying heat or hot water is barred. The tenant hotline, (607) 301-1560, gives advice [with no income or geographic limits](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources); full representation is only for City of Ithaca residents.

## Common mistakes

- **Prepaying months of rent to make up for no credit history.** The cap counts rent advances, so the deposit and any prepaid rent together stop at one month. On a $992.50 bed, six months up front is $5,955, or $4,962.50 over the cap.
- **Wiring a deposit before checking the building.** The 117 Thurston tenants found an expired certificate after signing, then waited 53 days for heat.
- **Paying an application fee.** It is banned. A credit check costs $20 at most.
- **Skipping the move-in inspection.** Without the signed condition record, any defect can become a deduction from your one-month deposit.
- **Renting a cheap West Hill cottage without the heating history.** Cornell warns the bills there run very high; NYSEG can tell you the average.
- **Signing as a sophomore without an exemption.** Cornell still bills the full year of campus housing.

## FAQ

**Who do I call if the heat fails in January?** Tell the landlord in writing first, with the date and address. If nothing happens, call the City Building Division at [(607) 274-6508](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance); the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance) warns against withholding rent without advice.

**Does my deposit earn interest?** In buildings with six or more units, yes: it must sit in a New York interest-bearing account, and you are told the bank in writing, per the [Cornell Law guide](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits).

**Can I sublet if I go home for the summer?** In buildings with four or more units, you may sublet with the landlord's [written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B); in smaller buildings the lease decides.

**Will Cornell help if my landlord keeps the deposit?** Off-Campus Living advises but [does not represent students](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/) in landlord disputes. The [deposit route](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits) is a demand letter, then small claims, with mediation at the Community Dispute Resolution Center.

**Does financial aid pay the deposit?** No. Cornell aid [does not cover deposits](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), first or last month's rent, or rent in months you are not registered.
