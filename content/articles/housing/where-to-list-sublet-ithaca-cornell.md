Post your sublet on Cornell's [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), where only people who prove Cornell affiliation can view listings and contact you. Add the Off-Campus Living email list. Use public sites last.

## Where can you post an Ithaca sublet?

| Channel | Who sees it | Cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Off-Campus Housing Marketplace | Only people with proven Cornell affiliation | Sublets were free to list in 2019 | Most sublets |
| Off-Campus Living email list | Cornell NetID holders | Free | Roommate and sublet notices |
| Your landlord's own sublet process | Applicants the landlord approves | Fee set by the landlord | Ithaca Renting buildings |
| Public sites and newspapers | Anyone | Varies by site | Reaching non-Cornell subtenants |

Source note: [Cornell's listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources); [Cornell Daily Sun, 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/).

## How does Cornell's marketplace work?

The marketplace is a College Pads service, now part of StarRez, and Cornell limits it to people who prove their affiliation. Cornell says the limit exists for security. Cornell also tells renters to [view a property in person and read the whole lease](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) before they pay. The Graduate School calls the marketplace [free to anyone with Cornell credentials](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). In 2019 landlords paid $30 to list, and sublets were free.

## How do you use the Off-Campus Living email list?

You need a NetID, per [Cornell's listings page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Admitted students without one can forward their acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu. To subscribe, email the list with "Join" as the subject and a blank body. To leave, send a blank subject and "Leave" in the body. The list carries housing, roommate and sublet notices, and Off-Campus Living answers questions at 607-255-2310.

## What if your landlord runs its own sublet process?

Then use it first, because an unapproved sublet can put you in breach. Ithaca Renting's Collegetown page says sublets must be [registered and approved](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/), go to Cornell students only and run one per term. The listed fee is $15 on one line and $20 on the next, which the page says applies if anyone uses the apartment over the summer, even without payment. Summer subtenants can include graduate students from other universities. Its [downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) sets a two-month minimum. Keep the electricity on in either.

Other landlords show sublets on their own sites. Modern Living Rentals advertises [sublet and roommate openings from $900 per person](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/) and does not state the period. Dryden South links a [Sublets page](https://drydensouth.com/availability/) from its availability page. Ask your landlord whether it posts tenants' sublets.

## Should you use public sites?

You can, with care. Public sites reach people outside Cornell, including summer interns and researchers. They also reach scammers. The Federal Trade Commission reports that about half of rental scam reports in the 12 months to June 2025 started with a fake [Facebook ad](https://ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/12/rental-scams-hit-home-65-million-reported-losses), and 16 percent started on Craigslist. Ithaca saw the same pattern earlier: in 2010 scammers [copied local Craigslist listings with cut rents](https://www.ithaca.com/news/local_news/ithaca-landlords-tenants-battle-craigslist-rental-scams/article_fa700738-0769-5add-9329-897f05256e6d.html) and took deposits. Cornell also names the Cornell Daily Sun, the Ithaca Journal and the Ithaca Times as outlets but does not endorse them. Ithacarents.com is for members of the Landlords Association of Tompkins County, so a tenant cannot list there.

## What should the listing say?

Give the dates, the monthly rent, what is included, whether the room is furnished and how many people share the unit. Add your building's address only after a screened reply. Never post the lease. Whatever the channel, wait until your landlord has approved the sublet. In a building with four or more units, the request goes by certified mail, and the [how-to guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) covers it.

```reader "If you're leaving for a semester abroad"
Post early. Cornell Abroad's director told the Sun in 2016 that [some students drop out of study abroad](https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/20/students-housing-search-hampers-study-abroad) when they cannot find a subletter, and Cornell does not know how many families pay rent on vacant apartments.
```

```reader "If you're a graduate student looking for a summer subtenant"
Graduate students from other universities can take summer sublets at Ithaca Renting's Collegetown buildings. Cornell's Graduate School says [a few places remain for graduate students from June to July](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/), so graduate students are a real source of summer subtenants.
```

## How do seekers read your listing?

They compare you with every other sublet. The [guide for people finding housing](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/) shows what they check, and the [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) covers the channels from their side. If nobody answers, a written agreement ending the lease can be cheaper than months of unpaid rent: see [breaking a lease early](/cornell/housing/breaking-a-lease-early-ithaca-cornell/).

## What to do next

Get your landlord's approval. Post on the marketplace and the email list on the same day. Answer replies from Cornell addresses first, and meet people in person before you sign anything.
