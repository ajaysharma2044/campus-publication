A 5x5 self-storage unit near Cornell costs $84 a month, and a 10x10 runs from $122 to $222, according to [Bolt Storage's price lists](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny). Every facility is a car ride from campus. Without a car, a pickup service costs less for a few boxes.

## What does self-storage near Cornell cost?

Price depends on size, on whether the unit is climate controlled (kept at a steady temperature), and on whether it is drive-up (you park at the door and load straight in). The table lists what each facility posted in Nov. 2026 ([Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny)).

|---|---|---|---|

| Bolt Storage, 53 Pinckney Rd | none posted | $128 | 5x8 or 5x10 $106; 10x20 $234; drive-up only; free lock |

Source note: Bolt Storage facility pages ([Dryden Road](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny), [Pinckney Road](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/ithaca-ny), [Ridge Road](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/lansing-ny)); [SpareFoot](https://sparefoot.com/Ithaca-NY-self-storage.html) and [StorageSeeker](https://www.storageseeker.com/self-storage/NY/dryden/storage-sense-ithaca/215442) for Storage Sense; [Neighbor](https://neighbor.com/self-storage-near-me/new-york/ithaca) for 4-Season Storage and the [U-Haul price](https://www.neighbor.com/facility/new-york/freeville/bolt-storage/1401-dryden-rd/14857). Neighbor also lists J.C. Leasing units from $50 a month, with no date.

Posted prices run above the averages. SpareFoot, a unit-search site, puts the average 10x10 at $93.39 a month and the average unit booked in the last 180 days at $107.19 ([SpareFoot](https://sparefoot.com/Ithaca-NY-self-storage.html)). [SelfStorage.com](https://www.selfstorage.com/self-storage/new-york/ithaca/24-hour-access/) lists an average 10x10 at $152.75. The $44 10x10 on SpareFoot is a promotion, and the cheapest 10x10 with 24-hour access there is $179. Ask what the rent becomes after the first month.

A 5x5 is 25 square feet (5 × 5). A 10x10 is 100 square feet, four times the floor space. At Bolt's Dryden Road prices the 10x10 drive-up unit is 75 percent more than the 5x5 for four times the room, so the larger unit is the better deal per square foot if you can fill it ([Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny)).

## How much does a summer of storage cost?

Rent is month to month, and a summer is four months, May through August. A drive-up 5x5 costs $336 for the summer and a climate-controlled one costs $384, four months at the monthly rates in the table. Bolt's Dryden Road site asks for [10 days' notice before you move out](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny), so give notice before you leave for fall.

Self-storage also needs transport. A pickup-truck rental costs [$30 to $60 a day](https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/college-storage-cost-calculator-pricing), and you need one trip in and one trip out. That adds $60 to $120, which puts a 5x5 at $396 to $456 for the summer. Big Red Shipping and Storage charges [$39 for a small box](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing) with pickup, storage and delivery included. Eleven small boxes cost $429, inside that range. So below about ten boxes a pickup service costs less, and above that the unit does. The [move-out options guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/) compares the pickup services in full.

## Which facility is closest, and what are the terms?

Bolt Storage's Dryden Road site is the closest posted: about 6 minutes from Cornell, with a 24-hour gate, a free lock and month-to-month rent. It also requires insurance, either your own policy or a plan bought when you rent ([Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny)). The Pinckney Road and Lansing sites are drive-up only.

Addresses for two facilities disagree across listing sites. SpareFoot puts Storage Sense at 1395 Danby Road, while StorageSeeker places it in the Dryden area. Neighbor lists 4-Season Storage at 1395 Dryden Road, with [hours of 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. and the phone number 607-533-7333](https://www.neighbor.com/facility/new-york/ithaca/4-season-storage/1395-dryden-rd/9693). Call before you drive out.

## Do you need a car to use self-storage?

Yes, or a way to borrow one. Nothing in the table picks up. If you do not own a car, rent a truck for the two trips or use a pickup service. Whether a car is worth owning at all is a separate decision, covered in [having a car at Cornell](/cornell/getting-around/having-a-car-at-cornell-student-guide/).

```reader "If you're an international student"
Bolt Storage lets you [rent and pay online from abroad](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny), so you can reserve a unit before you fly. You still need someone to carry your things to the facility. A pickup service solves that, because a driver collects boxes from your apartment.
```

## When should you book?

Cornell does not store belongings. The [housing license says](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027) storage space is not available. Residence halls closed at 2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026, and [items left behind were donated](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options). Pickup companies book up: [Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny) asks for summer bookings by early April. Self-storage units have no posted deadline, so reserve once you know your move-out date.

If someone sublets your place furnished, your furniture can stay and you skip the unit. That route is in the [summer sublet guide](/cornell/housing/summer-sublet-ithaca-finding-housing-may-august/). For winter break, the same facilities work, and the [breaks guide](/cornell/getting-around/staying-in-ithaca-over-cornell-breaks/) covers who can stay in Cornell housing instead.

## What to do next

Count your boxes and your large items. Under about ten boxes, price a pickup service first. Over ten, or with furniture, call Bolt's Dryden Road site and ask about the rent after the first month, the insurance plan and the notice period.
