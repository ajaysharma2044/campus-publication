Just About Music, or JAM, is a 166-resident Cornell program house in Low Rise 9 on North Campus. Its cluster rooms have no air conditioning. A program house is a themed residence hall, and rooms cost the same as in any hall: $13,744 for a double in 2026-27 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

## What is Just About Music?

It is the music-themed program house. Low Rise 9 opened in 1970, and JAM has lived there since 2000. The house has 166 residents ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/just-about-music-jam)).

## What is the room layout?

Each room group in JAM holds one double, one triple and two singles, with one shared bathroom, and JAM rooms have no air conditioning ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/program-houses/just-about-music-jam)). Adding the beds gives seven students per bathroom.

## What does JAM cost?

Program houses share the residence hall rate table. A meal plan is required: Unlimited at $7,604 for first-years, or Bear Traditional at $7,056 for upper-level and transfer students, plus a $50 yearly fee ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

| Room type | Room per year | Upper-level with cheapest plan and fee | First-year with Unlimited and fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Triple | $12,644 | $19,750 | $20,298 |
| Double | $13,744 | $20,850 | $21,398 |
| Single | $15,566 | $22,672 | $23,220 |

Source: Cornell Housing rates for 2026-27 and Cornell Dining. Totals add the plan and the yearly fee to the room rate.

A Sun column from April 29, 2026, argues that all dorms should cost the same ([The Cornell Daily Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/unequal-vs-inequitable-housing-why-all-dorms-should-cost-the-same)). Ask the house whether any fee applies this year.

Off campus, a room in a Collegetown shared suite has a median asking rent of $10,740 over 12 months, before food ([2026-27 housing cost dataset](/datasets/ithaca-housing-cost-by-living-situation-2026-27/)). ## How do you get into JAM?

New students list a program house on the housing application. They apply within 10 days of getting a NetID, and halls not chosen are assigned by random auto-allocation ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/new-undergrad-application-process)). Rising sophomores can list up to three program houses. For 2027-28, the application closes April 4, 2027, and program house priority selection is April 12 ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/apply-housing-campus/current-undergraduate-application-process/spring-housing-selection-rising-sophomores)).

A program house counts as on-campus housing, so living there meets the two-year residential requirement. If you want to live off campus, check the [exemption rules](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).

## What should you watch before you sign?

Winter break matters. Cornell's 2026-27 open list includes the HILC and Loving House buildings but not Low Rise 9, so ask Housing what is available if you stay ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing)). The [cancellation fee calculator](/tools/cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator/) covers the cost of changing your mind.

On move-in day, JAM unloads at Program House Drive. In fall 2026 Cornell turned away U-Hauls and trailers ([Cornell Housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus)). The [move-in day parking guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-in-day-parking-unloading/) has the full plan.

## What to do next

Price a bed type with your meal plan, then ask the house about its music space and any yearly fee.
