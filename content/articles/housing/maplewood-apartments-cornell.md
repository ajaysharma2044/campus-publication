Maplewood Apartments is a furnished apartment community for Cornell graduate, professional and faculty residents at 121 Veterans Place. Greystar manages it on land Cornell owns. Rent includes electricity, trash, internet and cable, but no price list is posted, so call (607) 821-0248 for a quote ([Greystar](https://www.greystar.com/maplewood-ithaca-ny/p_15697)).

## What is Maplewood Apartments?

Maplewood is one of two Cornell-owned graduate housing options. The other is Hasbrouck Apartments on North Campus. Cornell's graduate housing page lists Maplewood as furnished studios through 4-bedroom units on East Hill. Each unit has a private bath, an in-unit washer and dryer, and a free fitness center, and pets are allowed ([Cornell Student & Campus Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing)).

The scale figures come from the builder: 444 units and 872 beds, in five apartment buildings plus 21 townhouses and stacked flats on a 17-acre site ([LeChase](https://www.lechase.com/portfolio/greystar/)).

## How did Maplewood get built?

Cornell replaced an older complex with the same name. Maplewood Park opened in 1989 and held 394 units for 480 residents. Cornell announced in spring 2015 that it would close at the end of July 2016 because repairs cost too much. On Jan. 28, 2016, a trustee committee chose EdR, a student-housing developer, to finance, build and manage the replacement while Cornell kept the land ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2016/02/board-gives-green-light-new-grad-student-housing)). Cornell's Garrett pledged to prioritize graduate housing in the new buildings ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/02/03/trustees-approve-plans-expanding-graduate-student-housing/)).

The new buildings were built in 2017 and 2018 ([LeChase Construction](https://www.lechase.com/portfolio/greystar/)). EdR merged into Greystar in 2018, which is why Greystar runs the complex today ([Cornell Assemblies](https://assembly.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/gpci_-_housing_dec_2020.pdf)).

## What does Maplewood cost?

Cornell does not publish a Maplewood rate, and Greystar's page lists none either. The only public number is on the Cornell College of Veterinary Medicine housing page, which lists Maplewood at "$1,075- $1,330" for studio through 4-bedroom units, updated in February 2026. The page does not say whether that is per bed or per unit, or for what period, and notes that ranges vary with specials and availability ([Cornell vet college](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/education/biomedical-biological-sciences-bbs-phd-program/prospective-students/our-community/housing-information)).

Ask Greystar for a written quote for your exact unit, and ask whether it is per bed or per unit before you compare it with anything else. For a point of comparison on campus, Cornell's own Hasbrouck rates run from $620 for a regular single in a 2-bedroom townhouse to $1,530 for a furnished family 2-bedroom townhouse per month on a 12-month contract ([Cornell housing rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)).

The move-in cost to plan for is a one-time $130.00 community amenity fee per leaseholder at move-in, plus renters liability insurance bought from a third party (Greystar).

For how these costs fit a graduate budget, see the [monthly rent budget for Cornell graduate students](/cornell/money/cornell-grad-student-off-campus-budget-monthly-rent-ithaca/) and the [stipend and cost-of-living guide](/cornell/money/cornell-grad-student-stipend-cost-of-living-ithaca-enough/).

## Who can live at Maplewood and how do you get a unit?

Greystar markets Maplewood to graduate, faculty and professional students. Cornell's graduate housing page does not publish Maplewood prices, a lease start date or an application window, so ask Greystar at the number above for all three.

Visiting scholars have no guaranteed Cornell housing and compete with graduate students for units like these. The [visiting scholar and postdoc housing guide](/cornell/housing/visiting-scholar-postdoc-furnished-housing-ithaca/) covers furnished options for short stays.

## What should you watch for?

Two events in Maplewood's short history are worth knowing.

In August 2018, 106 students could not move in on Aug. 20 because the buildings were not ready. The delay ran four to six weeks. EdR offered affected residents three choices: stay with a friend or at an Airbnb with a $200 daily gift card and prorated rent, take an EdR-paid hotel plus $100 daily, or cancel the lease without penalty ([Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/08/12/delay-in-maplewood-housing-opening-affects-over-100-cornell-graduate-students/)).

A December 2020 graduate student assembly report found that Hasbrouck and Maplewood together house 15 to 20 percent of graduate students, about 5 percent of them in Cornell-owned units. It said some Maplewood residents renewed partly because they lacked other options, and it recorded complaints of staff entering units without notice and only six guest parking spots (Cornell Assemblies). That report is from 2020, so ask current residents whether those points still hold.
