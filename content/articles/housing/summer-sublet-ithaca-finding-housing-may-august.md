```glance
- Cornell dorms closed at 2 p.m. Sunday, May 17, 2026, and a Cornell room costs $60 a day outside its occupancy period.
- Most Ithaca leases end July 31 and the next starts about August 1; some end May 31 (Dryden South, all leases, 2027).
- Take a sublet only with a written sublease and, in a building of 4 or more units, the landlord's written consent.
- Summer sublet prices are thin: Modern Living advertises sublets "from $900 per person"; no typical summer rate is published.
- Storage for June and July: a 10x10 unit runs about $93 to $153 a month; Big Red Shipping and Storage picks up free.
- Summer deadlines: City parking permits expire July 31, Cornell permits August 15, OmniRide and first-year bus passes August 31.
```

Most Cornell students staying in Ithaca from May to August either keep a 12-month lease, take a sublet, or bridge a few weeks between a dorm and an August 1 lease. Cornell dorms closed at [2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), and most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing). If you sublet, get a written sublease and see the landlord's consent before you pay, because a sublet without it is a breach by the tenant you are paying.

```reader "If you're staying in Ithaca for the summer"
Your housing runs out on May 17 if you live in a dorm, and a Cornell license charges [$60 a day outside the occupancy period](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028), with no storage. Look for a June and July sublet on the [Cornell marketplace or the Off-Campus Living email list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Before you pay, read the written sublease and ask the tenant for the landlord's written consent. If your fall lease starts August 1, book storage early: [Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny) asks for bookings by early April. The costly mistake: moving into a sublet the landlord never approved. Under [New York law](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) that is a substantial breach by your subletter, and you have no lease of your own.
```

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
Check your end date first. Most leases end July 31, but some end May 31: every current [Dryden South](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961) lease ends May 31, 2027. A May 31 end leaves June and July uncovered. A July 31 end and an August 1 start leave about a day, so line up a mover and storage. Ask for a move-out inspection one to two weeks before the end. The landlord then has [14 days to return your deposit](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) or itemize it. Your $45 City parking permit [expires July 31](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance) and you need a new one at a new address. If you are the one subletting, see [how to sublet and get landlord consent](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/).
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
If you cannot travel home, you may ask [housing@cornell.edu](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources) about on-campus housing during breaks. A 12-month lease already covers the summer. If you take a sublet from abroad, you cannot see the unit first. Use the [Cornell marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), where only people who prove Cornell affiliation can contact subletters. Get the sublease and the landlord's consent in writing before you wire money. You do [not need an SSN](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students) to rent. In your first year, free TCAT rides last to [August 31](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so a cheaper sublet downtown costs no bus fare. The costly mistake: paying a stranger for a room you have not seen, with nothing in writing.
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar, postdoc or short-stay student"
You need months, not a year, and sublets are the main short supply. Cornell's other options have limits. International Scholar House is a [month-to-month furnished 5BR](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell), and visiting scholars have no guaranteed housing. Hasbrouck takes [rolling applications](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) on 12-month contracts, from [$620 to $1,530 a month in 2026-27](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). A month-to-month tenant generally gives [one month's notice](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). The costly mistake: signing a 12-month lease for a three-month stay and owing the rest.
```

## Your ways to cover May to August

| Your situation | What covers the summer | What it costs you | The risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12-month lease, August 1 to July 31 | Your own apartment | Rent all summer; Cornell aid does not cover [rent in unregistered months](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) | None for housing; you pay rent even if you leave |
| Dorm now, off-campus lease from August 1 | A June and July sublet, plus storage | Sublet rent, storage, two moves | A sublet the landlord never approved |
| Lease ends May 31, next starts August 1 | A sublet for about 61 days | Sublet rent, storage | Same; also no place for your furniture |
| Lease ends July 31, next starts August 1 | Your current lease | About a day of gap; a mover | Deposit and move-out on the same weekend |
| New arrival for a summer job or research | A sublet or a short lease | Sublet rent, often furnished | Paying before you see the unit |

Start dates vary by building. [209 Stewart Ave](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095) runs August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027. [The Ivy](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026) leases started August 17, 2026, which stretches the gap to more than two weeks for anyone leaving a July 31 lease.

Not confirmed: whether Cornell rents rooms to students staying for summer jobs or research, and at what rate. Students who cannot travel home may ask housing@cornell.edu about on-campus housing during breaks.

- **May 17.** Dorms closed at 2 p.m. in 2026. Anything you leave for a pickup company while you are absent is [donated after buildings close](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options). Be there for the pickup.
- **May 31.** Ithaca's heat season runs [September 15 to May 31](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). Lack of air conditioning generally does not breach the warranty of habitability, so a hot summer sublet is not a repair claim.
- **June 15.** The penalty-free deadline to cancel a fall Cornell license for study away, leave, an internship or graduation was [June 15, 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027). A fall internship elsewhere is a reason to cancel by then.
- **July 31.** City residential parking permits cost [$45 a year, expire July 31, and come with no partial-year option](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance). Landlords cannot get one for you.
- **August 15 and 31.** Cornell parking permits are [valid through August 15](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students). OmniRide passes and first-year TCAT access [expire August 31](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).

## How to take a sublet safely

```figure
kind: steps
title: Taking an Ithaca summer sublet, in order
rows:
  - {label: "Find it on the Cornell marketplace or the Off-Campus Living list"}
  - {label: "Ask how many units the building has"}
  - {label: "Ask for the landlord's written consent (4+ units) or the lease's sublet clause (3 or fewer)"}
  - {label: "Read and sign a written sublease with dates, rent and utilities"}
  - {label: "Pay only after consent, with a receipt; photograph the room on move-in"}
```

1. **Find it where Cornell checks people.** The [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources) shows subletters only to people who prove Cornell affiliation. The Off-Campus Living email list carries housing, roommates and sublets; send "Join" in the subject with a blank body. Listings are [checked against the City's Certificate of Compliance database](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). Sublets were [free to list in 2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/).
2. **Count the units.** In a building with four or more units, a tenant has a [legal right to sublet](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) with the landlord's advance written consent, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. In a building of three or fewer units, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Cornell's tenant page says [most leases require landlord approval](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus).
3. **Ask to see the consent.** In a 4+ unit building the tenant asks by certified mail. The landlord may ask for more information within 10 days and must answer within 30. Silence counts as consent, so a dated copy of the request plus 30 days of silence is the proof.
4. **Get a written sublease.** Cornell tells tenants to use one. Put in the dates, rent, deposit, utilities, keys and what furniture stays.
5. **Know what a sublet fee can be.** A landlord may charge one [only if the lease allows it and the fee is reasonable](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Background check fees are allowed.

If your subletter has not sent the request yet, this tool shows how the 10-day and 30-day clocks fall:

```tool ithaca-sublet-request-timeline```

A sublet is not an assignment. With an assignment the whole lease passes to the new tenant, and the [landlord may refuse without cause](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). For a summer, you want a sublet.

## Bridging the gap: storage and two moves

A dorm-to-apartment summer means storing a room's worth of things for June and July. A [Cornell license offers no storage space](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028) for personal belongings.

| Option | Price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Big Red Shipping and Storage](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options) | No published price | Cornell's vendor; free pickup at a dorm, apartment or house; (607) 524-3130 |
| [Storage Scholars](https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny) | $172 a month, "2025 Avg." (not stated as Ithaca-specific) | Pickup and delivery in East Hill and Collegetown; book by early April |
| [SpareFoot](https://sparefoot.com/Ithaca-NY-self-storage.html) average, 10x10 | About $93.39 a month | You move it yourself |
| [SelfStorage.com](https://www.selfstorage.com/self-storage/new-york/ithaca/24-hour-access/) average, 10x10 | About $152.75 a month | You move it yourself |
| [4-Season Storage](https://www.neighbor.com/facility/new-york/ithaca/4-season-storage/1395-dryden-rd/9693), 1395 Dryden Rd | From $115 a month | You move it yourself |

Example: a rising junior has a lab job from June 1 to July 31. The dorm closes May 17 and the Collegetown lease starts August 1. A 10x10 unit for two months costs about $187 to $306 at the SpareFoot and SelfStorage.com averages (our math). A sublet at $900 a month, Modern Living's "from" figure treated as monthly for illustration, costs $1,800 for June and July. The 15 days from May 17 to June 1 still need a place. A Cornell room is no bridge: 15 days at $60 a day is $900 in fees.

Example: a senior's lease ends May 31 and the next one starts August 1. That is June 1 to July 31, 61 days. Moving out on May 31 also starts the deposit clock: [14 days](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108) for the deposit or an itemized statement. Cornell's [move-out checklist](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out) says to schedule the final inspection two weeks out and the NYSEG and internet shutoff one week out.

## Getting around Ithaca in summer

After the first year, Cornell students ride TCAT free only [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). A daytime weekday ride is [$1.50](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/03/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free/). The OmniRide pass is $200 a year and expires August 31, so a pass bought for the school year covers the summer. A sublet farther from campus costs bus fare on every weekday commute without one.

Car owners: a City residential permit runs only to July 31 and is issued to one plate at one address, with a lease or utility bill as proof. A summer sublet in a permit area may not come with a document in your name; ask before you bring a car.

## Common mistakes

- **Paying before you see the landlord's consent.** In a 4+ unit building a sublet without the legal steps is a [substantial breach](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B) by your subletter. You hold no lease of your own to fall back on.
- **No written sublease.** An oral deal leaves the dates, rent and utilities to memory. Cornell's [tenant page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus) says to get it in writing.
- **Leaving dorm boxes for a pickup you skip.** Items left while you are absent are donated after buildings close at 2 p.m. on move-out Sunday.
- **Overstaying a Cornell room.** $60 a day outside the occupancy period; a week is $420.
- **Signing a 12-month lease for a summer stay.** The remaining months are [owed unless the place is sublet or re-rented](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E); for a three-month stay that is nine months of rent at risk.
- **Forgetting the July 31 permit.** A City permit has no partial-year version, so a car at a new address needs a new $45 permit.

## FAQ

**Does my summer sublet landlord owe me air conditioning?** No. The Cornell Law guide says lack of air conditioning [generally does not breach habitability](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). Ask whether the unit has a window unit before you sign.

**Can I rent an Ithaca place by the week for the summer?** Short-term rentals need a [separate City permit](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections). Nightly or weekly prices are not published.

**Is June too late to find a fall lease as a grad student?** Not always. A Cornell grad guide says to start looking March to April for August, and that [a few places remain June to July](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html).

**Will Cornell financial aid pay my summer rent?** No. Cornell aid does not cover [rent in months you are not registered](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus), nor deposits or first and last month's rent.

**What if my subletter's landlord never answers?** In a building of four or more units, a landlord who does not answer within 30 days of the request has [consented](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B). Ask your subletter for the dated certified-mail receipt.
