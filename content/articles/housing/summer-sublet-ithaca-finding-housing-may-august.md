```glance
- Cornell residence halls closed in mid-May 2026, and most Ithaca leases run to late July.
- A sublet needs a written sublease and, in a building of four or more units, the landlord's consent.
- No source posts a typical summer sublet rent. The cost table shows the closest asking prices.
- Storage for the gap runs from $36 for one small box to $84 a month for a unit.
```

For a summer internship or research stay, take a furnished sublet found on Cornell's marketplace or the Off-Campus Living email list, and get a written sublease before you pay. In a building with four or more units, the tenant must also have the landlord's [written consent](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B), or the sublet breaks the lease and you have no lease of your own.

## What are your options between May and August?

Cornell residence halls closed at [2 p.m. on Sunday, May 17, 2026](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/moving-out/storage-shipping-options), and most Ithaca leases [end July 31](https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing). Your route depends on where you live now and where you live in August.

| Your situation | What covers the summer | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| 12-month lease, Aug. 1 to July 31 | Your own apartment | You pay rent all summer; Cornell aid does not cover [rent in months you are not registered](https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus) |
| Dorm now, off-campus lease from Aug. 1 | A June and July sublet, plus storage | Sublet rent, storage, two moves |
| Lease ends May 31, next starts Aug. 1 | A sublet for 61 days (June 1 to July 31) | Same, and no place for your furniture |
| Lease ends July 31, next starts Aug. 1 | Your current lease | About a day of gap; deposit and move-out the same weekend |
| New arrival for a summer job or research | A furnished sublet or a short lease | Paying before you see the unit |

Start dates vary by building. Dryden South leases all [end May 31, 2027](https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961), so a sublet there cannot run into June.

## Where do you find a summer sublet?

Start on the [Off-Campus Housing Marketplace](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources), which shows subletters only to people who prove Cornell affiliation. The Off-Campus Living email list carries sublets and roommate notices too: send "Join" as the subject with a blank body. Landlords post their own: Modern Living Rentals advertises [sublet and roommate openings from $900 per person](https://www.modernlivingrentals.com/), and Dryden South has a sublets page that said ["Nothing Currently Available"](https://drydensouth.com/availability/). The [listings guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-housing-listings-where-to-find/) covers each channel, and June is the main sublet season.

## How do you take a sublet safely?

```figure
kind: steps
title: Taking an Ithaca summer sublet, in order
rows:
  - {label: "Find it on the Cornell marketplace or the Off-Campus Living list"}
  - {label: "Ask how many units the building has"}
  - {label: "Ask for the landlord's written consent (4+ units) or the lease's sublet clause (3 or fewer)"}
  - {label: "Read and sign a written sublease with dates, rent and utilities"}
  - {label: "Pay only after consent, with a receipt; photograph the room on move-in"}
```

1. **Count the units.** In a building with four or more units a tenant has a legal right to sublet, with the landlord's advance written consent. In a smaller building, [the lease controls](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Cornell's tenant page says [most leases require landlord approval](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus).
2. **Ask to see the consent.** The tenant sends the request by certified mail. Silence from the landlord counts as consent after the deadline, so a dated copy plus the waiting period is the proof.
3. **Get a written sublease.** Put in the dates, rent, deposit, utilities, keys and what furniture stays.
4. **Know the fee rule.** A landlord can charge a sublet fee only if the lease allows it and it is reasonable.

The tenant's side, step by step, is in the [how-to-sublet guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/). To see how the clocks fall, use the tool:

A Cornell room is no bridge. The 2026-27 housing license says storage is not available, and it charges [$60 a day outside the occupancy period](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-and-residential-life-and-faculty-involvement-campus/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027). Fifteen days from May 17 to June 1 would cost $900. City residential parking permits [expire July 31 with no partial-year option](https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance), so a car at a new address needs a new permit.

## How do you store things for the gap?

Cornell offers no storage, and items left behind when the buildings closed were donated. The prices below are posted for a typical summer; the [move-out options guide](/cornell/getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/) compares them fully.

| Option | Posted price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Big Red Shipping and Storage | $39 for a small box (3 to 4 months) | Cornell's vendor; free pickup |
| Storage Squad | $36 for a small item (4 months) | Pickup; reservation fee not posted |
| Bolt Storage, 1401 Dryden Rd | From $84 a month for a 5x5 | You move it yourself |

Source note: [BRSS](http://www.bigredboxes.com/BRSS_Pricing), [Storage Squad](https://storagesquad.com/summer-storage/cornell-university/), [Bolt Storage](https://www.boltstorage.com/self-storage/freeville-ny).

```reader "If you're a junior or senior already renting"
Check your end date first. A May 31 end leaves June and July uncovered. A late-July end and an Aug. 1 start leave about a day, so line up a mover and storage. Ask for a move-out inspection one to two weeks before the end. The landlord then has 14 days to return your deposit or itemize it. If you are the one subletting, see the [how-to-sublet guide](/cornell/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) above.
```

```reader "If you're an international student"
If you cannot travel home, ask [housing@cornell.edu](https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources) about on-campus housing during breaks. If you take a sublet from abroad, you cannot see the unit first, so use the Cornell marketplace and get the sublease and the landlord's consent in writing before you wire money. You do [not need a Social Security number](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students) to rent.
```

```reader "If you're a visiting scholar, postdoc or short-stay student"
You need months, not a year, and sublets are the main short supply. International Scholar House is a [month-to-month furnished 5-bedroom](https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell), and visiting scholars have no guaranteed housing. Hasbrouck takes [rolling applications](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing) on 12-month contracts. Signing a 12-month lease for a three-month stay leaves nine months of rent to cover. The [scholar housing guide](/cornell/housing/visiting-scholar-postdoc-furnished-housing-ithaca/) lists furnished options.
```

## What mistakes cost the most?

- **Paying before you see the landlord's consent.** A sublet without the legal steps is a substantial breach.
- **No written sublease.** Cornell's tenant page says to get it in writing.
- **Leaving dorm boxes for a pickup you skip.** Items left while you are absent are donated.
- **Overstaying a Cornell room.** A week outside the period costs $420.
- **Signing a 12-month lease for a summer stay.** Ask about a sublet first.

## FAQ

**Does my summer sublet landlord owe me air conditioning?** No. The Cornell Law guide says lack of air conditioning [does not breach habitability in general](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). Ask whether the unit has a window unit before you sign.

**Is June too late to find a place as a grad student?** No. Cornell's Graduate School says [a few places remain for graduate students from June to July](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/).

**Can I sublet to someone from another university?** At Ithaca Renting's Collegetown buildings, [summer sublets can go to graduate students from other universities](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). Its [downtown page](https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/) sets a two-month minimum.

## What to do next

Decide your route from the table. Look for a sublet in March and April, and book storage by early April. Ask the landlord for written consent before you pay anything.
