Cornell Summer Session is worth it when you need specific credits fast and your college has confirmed they count, because tuition is steep: [$2,013 per credit for 2027](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/tuition-rates-and-fees), so a three-credit course is [$6,039](https://sce.cornell.edu/courses/tuition/tuition). If the goal is only to bank credits, a cheaper course elsewhere can transfer, but only if your own college approves it first. The decision turns on which college you are in, so start there.

## What Summer Session is

Summer Session offers in-person courses in Ithaca, online classes, courses at Shoals Marine Laboratory and study abroad, in [3-, 6- and 8-week sessions](https://sce.cornell.edu/courses/). The 2026 dates, per the [advisor memo](https://sce.cornell.edu/fa-advisors-summer/), were:

Enrollment for 2026 [opened March 16](https://sce.cornell.edu/fa-advisors-summer/), and each session has its own tuition, refund and add/drop deadlines. Expect a similar pattern for 2027, but check the Summer Academic Calendar. The advisor memo also caps credits: up to four in a three-week session, eight in a six-week session, ten in an eight-week session and fifteen for the whole summer, and exceeding them takes a petition the memo says is not advisable.

## What it costs

Tuition is the same per credit however you split it. The Bursar lists [Winter and Summer Sessions 2027 at $2,013 per credit](https://bursar.cornell.edu/students-parents/tuition-rates-and-fees). The SCE page applies that rate to the [fall 2026 through summer 2027 period](https://sce.cornell.edu/courses/tuition/tuition) and adds course fees, which vary, and possible health insurance or a health fee.

Financial aid is the catch. SCE says [there is no financial aid available from the School of Continuing Education](https://sce.cornell.edu/fa-advisors-summer/), and tells students to ask their own financial aid office whether aid covers any Summer Session tuition. Ask before you enroll. Tuition is also owed for every class you are enrolled in [even if you do not attend](https://sce.cornell.edu/fa-advisors-summer/), so drop right away if plans change.

## Will the credit count? Rules by college

Always confirm with your own college before paying. What we could verify:

| College | Taken at Cornell Summer Session | Taken elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| Arts & Sciences | Can fulfill college requirements, including the [FWS and foreign language requirements](https://as.cornell.edu/transferring-credits) | [C or better](https://as.cornell.edu/transferring-credits); departmental approval strongly recommended before enrolling; form for each course; cannot count toward the foreign language or distribution requirements |
| Engineering | Not covered on the page we read | Form with syllabus, [department and advisor approval](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/transfer-credit-application-process); transfers if you earn a C or better; reviews take about two weeks |
| CALS | Not covered on the page we read | [Pre-approval recommended](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/transfer-non-cornell-credit); check the Transfer Course Database first; maximum of 60 external credits |

For other colleges (ILR, Human Ecology, Hotel, AAP) we could not open a current official rule, so ask your advising office.

Details that trip people up in Arts & Sciences:

- Summer sessions at Cornell or elsewhere [do not count toward the residence requirement](https://as.cornell.edu/transferring-credits), but students may earn up to 12 credits in one summer. That is stricter than the 15-credit SCE memo limit, so the A&S number is the one to plan around if you are in that college.
- You [will not be granted more transfer credit](https://as.cornell.edu/transferring-credits) than the course earned at the offering institution.
- The offering school must send an official transcript to Student Services after the course. CALS likewise [accepts only official transcripts sent directly from the institution](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/transfer-non-cornell-credit).
- Arts & Sciences says a transfer GPA is not computed into your Cornell GPA. That cuts both ways: a bad grade does not hurt it, a good one does not help it.

## Taking a summer course in Ithaca or at home instead

Tompkins Cortland Community College lists [$236 per credit tuition for New York residents with a Certificate of Residence and $485 for others](https://tompkinscortland.edu/tuition), plus a technology fee of $23 and a student services fee of $34 per credit hour. The page does not date those rates or give a summer rate, so confirm the summer total. On tuition alone, three credits would be $708 against $6,039 at Cornell.

Two cautions, both from the college rules above. Cheaper only counts if it transfers, and the course must match something your college accepts, backed by a syllabus. Credit also transfers as credit, not as the Cornell course: A&S [does not allow transfer credit toward its distribution requirements](https://as.cornell.edu/transferring-credits), which is the kind of requirement Summer Session can fill.

We found no strong r/Cornell consensus on Summer Session versus community college, so treat the decision as math plus your college's rules.

## Which to choose

- **You need distribution or FWS credit in Arts & Sciences:** Cornell Summer Session, since A&S says it can fulfill those requirements.
- **You need elective or general credits only:** a pre-approved course elsewhere is far cheaper, if your college approves it in writing.
- **You are in Engineering:** get the form signed first; Engineering reviews [in the order received](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/transfer-credit-application-process), so submit well ahead of the course start.
- **Aid-dependent:** ask financial aid first; SCE offers none.
- **Staying in Ithaca:** see [summer money options](/money/cornell-summer-jobs-ithaca-students-stay-for-summer/) and, if you need housing, [how to sublet an Ithaca apartment](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/).

## Steps for a course elsewhere

1. Pick the course and save its description and syllabus.
2. Get your college's pre-approval (department approval in A&S, department and advisor in Engineering).
3. Enroll and earn at least a C.
4. Have the school send an official transcript to your college.

## FAQ

**Is Summer Session a lighter workload?** Not per credit. The pace is compressed into 3, 6 or 8 weeks, so a four-credit load in three weeks is dense; the memo's limits are described as roughly a full semester's worth of credits.

**Can I take summer classes without being enrolled at Cornell?** Yes, SCE runs visiting-student options, but financial aid is not available to visiting students this term, per the [tuition page](https://sce.cornell.edu/courses/tuition/tuition).

**Can I plan fall courses around this?** Summer credit can change your fall load. See our guides on [course enrollment strategy](/academics/cornell-course-enrollment-strategy-pre-enroll-tips/) and [getting into a full class](/academics/how-to-get-into-a-full-class-at-cornell-waitlist/).
