Take a class S/U when it is a free elective or a distribution requirement outside your major and you want to explore without risking your GPA. Do not take it for anything that counts toward your major, or for prerequisites you will need for a professional school. Cornell sets the basic meaning, and each college sets its own limits, so check yours before you switch. Here is how it works and how to decide.

## How S/U grading works

At the ILR School, [S means a grade of C- or better and U means D+ or lower](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-grades-and-notations). ILR also says a U counts as an F for academic status but [does not affect your grade point average](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-grades-and-notations). Other colleges publish their own wording, so confirm how your college treats a U before relying on that.

The option is only there if the course offers it. Some courses are letter-only, and some are S/U-only.

## The rules we could verify, by college

| College | What its page says |
|---|---|
| Arts & Sciences | You [may elect S/U if the course does not also count toward major requirements or serve as a prerequisite for admission to the major](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/course-enrollment). S/U courses [can fulfill college distribution requirements](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/course-enrollment). |
| ILR | The option is available only in [ILR electives and out-of-college electives](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-grades-and-notations). No more than 8 S/U credits in a semester, and at least 105 letter-grade credits toward the degree. |
| Human Ecology | S/U [cannot fulfill distribution or major requirements](https://human.cornell.edu/academics/policies/requirements) unless a course is S/U-only. A maximum of 12 S/U credits can apply toward graduation. The page covers students who matriculated in fall 2024 or earlier. |

We could not confirm current published S/U limits for Engineering, CALS, AAP or Hotel from their own pages, so ask your college's student services office. Do not rely on a friend in another college.

## Deadlines

ILR says you cannot change your grading option after [the 57th calendar day of University instruction, with no exceptions and no appeals](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-grades-and-notations). Arts & Sciences points to its academic calendar for deadlines. After the deadline, A&S students may petition only to [change from S/U to letter graded, and only to count the course toward a major or minor that requires a letter grade](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/petitions-policies). Add, drop and grade-option dates are set each semester, so check the current calendar.

## Decision by situation

These are our recommendations, based on the rules above.

- **A free elective or a distribution course outside your major (Arts & Sciences or ILR):** a good use of S/U. The risk is a C- or lower, and the reward is trying a hard class without a GPA hit.
- **Anything counting toward your major or minor:** do not. In Arts & Sciences and Human Ecology it is barred by the rules above, and in Arts & Sciences the fix after the deadline is a petition back to a letter grade.
- **Heading to grad school:** Cornell's Graduate School said, about spring 2020, that it would [respect students' and institutions' decisions about S/U grading](https://cornellsun.com/2020/05/12/students-left-in-the-dark-over-grades-as-su-deadline-approaches/). That was a pandemic statement, not a standing policy. In a pandemic-free semester, ask the programs you want.
- **Pre-med or other pre-health:** keep prerequisites as letter grades. Cornell's pre-health office does not give an S/U rule on its [academic preparation page](https://prehealthadvising.cornell.edu/academic-preparation); it says to check with advisors and the programs you target. Each school sets its own policy, so ask them before switching any prerequisite.
- **A course where you are not sure of your grade:** decide before the deadline, not after. In spring 2020, [several students told the Cornell Daily Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2020/05/12/students-left-in-the-dark-over-grades-as-su-deadline-approaches/) they could not tell whether to switch because professors had not posted grades. Ask your instructor where you stand.

## Common mistakes

- Switching a course that your major quietly requires. Check your degree tracker first.
- Assuming a U is harmless everywhere. At ILR it does not change your GPA but does count as an F for academic status.
- Missing the grade-option deadline, which at ILR has no exceptions.
- Taking a prerequisite for a later course S/U without checking that the next department accepts it.

## FAQ

**Does an S affect my GPA?** At ILR, S/U grades are outside the GPA calculation, and a U ["will not affect grade point average"](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-grades-and-notations). Check your own college's page.

**Can I take a distribution course S/U?** In Arts & Sciences, yes. In Human Ecology, no unless the course is S/U-only.

**What if I want to go back to a letter grade?** Before the deadline, change it in your enrollment. After it, Arts & Sciences allows a petition only for courses that count toward a major or minor.
