Cornell's library hours page lists these as 24-hour spaces for the week of November 2 to 6, 2026: the [Engineering Library, the Catherwood first-floor study space, the Law Library (Law community only), the Mann Library lobby, Physical Sciences (Clark) and the Uris Library Tower Entrance and Cocktail Lounge](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours). Most require a Cornell ID card after the building closes. Fall 2026 study days are [December 8 to 10, and final exams run December 11 to 19](https://as.cornell.edu/index.php/registrar), so these are the rooms to know before then. Hours can change, and the library says so on its own pages, so check the hours page the day you go.

## The 24-hour spaces, by campus area

| Space | Where | Access, per the library |
|---|---|---|
| Uris Library Cocktail Lounge and Tower Entrance | Central | [Cornell ID required](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours) |
| Physical Sciences (Clark) Library | Central | Listed as [study space open 24/7](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries); no ID note on that page |
| Engineering Library | Engineering Quad | [Card access](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours) |
| Carpenter Hall first-floor reading room | Engineering Quad | Current Cornell faculty, staff and students [via ID card, 24/7, except holidays and campus closings](https://engineering.library.cornell.edu/study-space/) |
| Duffield Hall atrium | Engineering Quad | The Engineering Library page says [the atrium is open 24/7](https://engineering.library.cornell.edu/study-space/) |
| Mann Library lobby, Mann 103 and Mann 112 | Ag Quad | [Open 24/7 to the Cornell community via card swipe](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space) |
| Catherwood first-floor study space | Ives Hall | Listed as [24 hours on the hours page](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours) |
| Law Library | Myron Taylor Hall | [Card access for the Law community](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours) |

Two notes on naming. The Mann page currently calls Mann 112 the "contactless pickup area," while the 2022 Sun article described it as a [group and individual study room with printers](https://cornellsun.com/2022/03/17/mann-library-extends-study-space-hours-to-24-7/). And the Engineering Library page describes the Carpenter reading room and the Duffield atrium rather than the whole library, while the hours page lists the Engineering Library itself as 24 hours with card access. If you are heading out at 2 a.m., use the hours page.

## Finals-week changes to expect

The library hours page does not mention finals specifically, so do not assume longer hours. What it does show:

- **Holiday closings.** The page says the [24/7 spaces close at 4:00 p.m. on November 24](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours), and the Tower Entrance and Cocktail Lounge close at 5 p.m. on December 24.
- **Other libraries run set hours.** For example, the page lists Olin at 8am to midnight Monday to Thursday, Uris at 8am to 11pm, and Mann at 8am to 10pm, so only the spaces above stay open all night.

Past years are only a rough guide. In December 2021, the Sun reported that Mann [extended its hours by two hours when the study period began](https://cornellsun.com/2021/12/07/as-finals-season-approaches-libraries-continue-adapting-to-student-needs/), and a 2019 article quoted Bonna Boettcher, director of Olin and Uris Libraries, as saying library hours go from five days a week to seven as finals approach. Those are old reports, not this year's schedule. The same 2019 piece described some Uris gallery-floor spaces as ["24/5"](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/25/library-director-outlines-alternate-24-hour-study-spaces-besides-cocktail-lounge) (Sunday through Thursday nights), which is a reminder to read the day-by-day listing.

## ID rules and what happens late at night

You need a current Cornell ID for almost all of these. The Mann page says access is [by card swipe for the Cornell community](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space). The 2019 Sun article said that after midnight the Mann lobby needed an ID swipe, and that security took over at 1 a.m. and asked for a Cornell-issued ID; that is 2019 reporting, so treat it as a reason to carry your card, not as a current rule. Guests without a card should not count on getting in.

## If you are...

- **On Central Campus:** Uris Cocktail Lounge or Clark. The Sun's March 2026 guide by Jeanelle Wu calls them [two 24-hour study spaces on Central Campus](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/your-guide-to-a-successful-all-nighter).
- **An engineer:** Carpenter Hall's reading room or the Duffield atrium.
- **On the Ag Quad:** Mann's lobby, 103 or 112. In 2022, student Julia VanPutte '24 told the Sun that [being kicked out of a space while stressed adds to the stress](https://cornellsun.com/2022/03/17/mann-library-extends-study-space-hours-to-24-7/), which is the case for knowing where the all-night rooms are before you need one.
- **Wanting group space:** the 2021 Sun piece said some study rooms could be [booked through the LibCal app](https://cornellsun.com/2021/12/07/as-finals-season-approaches-libraries-continue-adapting-to-student-needs/); check whether that still applies.

## Late-night food and the fallback

The same 2026 Sun guide lists [Bear Necessities (Robert Purcell Community Center) open till 2 a.m., Jansen's Market (Noyes) till 1 a.m., and the Collegetown 7-Eleven open 24 hours](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/your-guide-to-a-successful-all-nighter). If a space is full or closed for a holiday, an Ithaca cafe is not something we could verify as open late, so treat the library list as your plan A and B. For medicine or supplies at odd hours, see our guide to [pharmacies near campus and late options](/health-safety/pharmacy-near-cornell-campus-ithaca/).

## Common mistakes

Our advice, drawn from the sources above:

- Relying on a 2019 to 2022 article for hours. The spaces and rules have shifted since.
- Forgetting your ID. Nearly every 24-hour space is card access.
- Planning an all-nighter around December 24 or November 24, when the 24/7 spaces close in the afternoon.

## FAQ

**Is the Cocktail Lounge open 24 hours?** The hours page lists the [Uris Tower Entrance and Cocktail Lounge as 24 hours, Cornell ID required](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/hours).

**Is Olin open all night?** Not on the hours page; it lists Olin as 8am to midnight Monday to Thursday.

**When are finals?** [December 11 to 19, 2026, after study days December 8 to 10](https://as.cornell.edu/index.php/registrar); the page notes dates are subject to change.
