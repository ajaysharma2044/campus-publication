For a late night, go to the Cocktail Lounge in Uris Library: Cornell describes it as [available for quiet and group study 24 hours a day, 7 days a week](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces), with a current Cornell ID needed after building hours. For true quiet, the Clark Physical Sciences Library and the Engineering Library are the other two libraries the [library hours page](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) lists as open around the clock. For groups, Mann 112 and the reservable rooms in Olin, Uris and Mann are the places to start. Hours change by semester and around finals, so check the linked pages before you walk across the Arts Quad.

| You need | Go to | Why, and the source |
|---|---|---|
| Late night, any hour | Uris Cocktail Lounge | [24/7 for quiet and group study with a Cornell ID after hours](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces) |
| Quiet, late | Clark Physical Sciences Library | [Study space open 24/7](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) |

## Late night

**Uris Cocktail Lounge.** Cornell calls it [very popular, with panoramic views of Libe Slope](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces). It is the best-known overnight room. A 2022 Cornell Daily Sun piece by Lena Thakor, a freshman in Arts and Sciences at the time, [calls it the spot for a "grinder"](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/) and mentions sunset views.

**Other overnight rooms.** For the week of November 8 to 14, 2026, the library's [hours feed](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/api_hours_grid.php?format=json&weeks=1) listed five more spaces at 24 hours: the Uris tower entrance (Cornell ID required), the Engineering Library (card access), the Mann lobby and contactless pickup area, the Catherwood Library first-floor study space, and card access for the Law community. It listed the Physical Sciences Library in Clark Hall as "Study space open 24/7". Mann's [Find a Space page](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space) adds that the lobby, Mann 112 and Mann 103 are open 24/7 to members of the Cornell community by card swipe. Older lists ran longer: in 2019 Bonna Boettcher, then director of the Olin and Uris Libraries, [named the Uris gallery floor (three computer labs, the Kirby Room and the Fiske)](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/25/library-director-outlines-alternate-24-hour-study-spaces-besides-cocktail-lounge) among the 24-hour options, and a [December 2021 Sun article](https://cornellsun.com/2021/12/07/as-finals-season-approaches-libraries-continue-adapting-to-student-needs/) placed the Catherwood space on the first floor of Ives Hall.

## Quiet

**Clark Physical Sciences Library.** The library hours page [lists study space open 24/7 at 293 Clark Hall](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/). The 2019 Sun article called it a [quiet late-night option open seven days a week](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/25/library-director-outlines-alternate-24-hour-study-spaces-besides-cocktail-lounge) and gave the room as 290 Clark Hall; use the library's address.

**Engineering Library.** In Carpenter Hall and [open 24 hours by card access](https://spaces.library.cornell.edu/api_hours_grid.php?format=json&weeks=1). The 2022 Sun writer was not impressed by the looks and noted [few amenities beyond private conference rooms](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/).

**Olin Library.** Olin has [numerous study rooms and carrels](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces). The 2022 Sun piece describes it as the place for a ["lone wolf"](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/) working in quiet among the stacks.

## Groups

**Mann 112.** Mann's [Find a Space page](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space) lists the lobby, the contactless pickup area in Mann 112 and Mann 103 as open 24/7 to members of the Cornell community by card swipe. When Mann Library [moved Mann 112 to 24/7 operation in March 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/03/17/mann-library-extends-study-space-hours-to-24-7/), the Sun described group and individual study space, comfortable seating and C.U. Print printers. Mann's site says [some spaces are first come, first served and others can be reserved](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/). The Help Desk is at mann_circ@cornell.edu or 607-255-3296.

**Reservable rooms.** Olin and Uris have [large and small study rooms for both quiet and group study](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces). Mann's [individual and group study rooms](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space) can be reserved for up to four hours at a time, and Cornell graduate students can book the graduate study room for up to eight. Book before finals.

## Coffee and food nearby

Olin is home to the [Amit Bhatia Libe Cafe](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces); its hours are in the table above. Mann Library has the [Mann Cafe](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/cafes-food-courts-coffeehouses), a Cornell Dining cafe on the Ag Quad. The independent Manndible Cafe that older posts mention [served its last day on May 21, 2021](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/manndible-cafe-closing). The 2022 Sun writer named Gates Hall her favorite building, with [a Gimme Coffee on the ground floor, plenty of light and steady quiet](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/). A cafe is better for atmosphere than for output.

## If you are...

- **A night owl:** Cocktail Lounge first, Clark if it is full or too social. Bring your ID.
- **In a group project:** book a room early, especially in finals weeks.
- **Easily distracted:** skip the cafes and A.D. White; pick Clark or an Olin carrel.
- **Studying on a budget:** our guide to [cheap textbooks](/money/cornell-textbooks-cheap-where-to-buy-used-ithaca/) covers cutting course costs.

## Common mistakes

- Trusting a 2019 or 2022 list for 2026 hours. Check the library site.
- Forgetting your Cornell ID after hours.
- Assuming a room is free. Study rooms can be reserved, so book ahead.

## FAQ

**Is there a 24-hour library at Cornell?** Yes: the [Cocktail Lounge](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces), [Clark](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) study space, the [Engineering Library](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) and the [Mann lobby](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space) are listed as around the clock.

**Do I need an ID?** For the Cocktail Lounge after building hours, [a current Cornell ID card](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces).

**Can I reserve a room?** Yes at Mann, through its [Find a Space page](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/find-a-space); Olin and Uris describe their rooms on the [spaces page](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces).
