For a late night, go to the Cocktail Lounge in Uris Library: Cornell describes it as [available for quiet and group study 24 hours a day, 7 days a week](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces), with a current Cornell ID needed after building hours. For true quiet, the Clark Physical Sciences Library and the Engineering Library are the other two libraries the [library hours page](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) lists as open around the clock. For groups, Mann 112 and the reservable rooms in Olin, Uris and Mann are the places to start. Hours change by semester and around finals, so check the linked pages before you walk across the Arts Quad.

One caution on the pages we found: the library site showed most libraries as "Closed" when we looked, so we could not confirm regular opening hours for Olin, Uris or Mann. Where a spot is described below, the source and its date are stated.

## Late night

**Uris Cocktail Lounge.** Cornell calls it [very popular, with panoramic views of Libe Slope](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces). It is the best-known overnight room. A 2022 Cornell Daily Sun piece by Lena Thakor, a freshman in Arts and Sciences at the time, [calls it the spot for a "grinder"](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/) and mentions sunset views.

**Other overnight rooms.** In 2019, Bonna Boettcher, then director of the Olin and Uris Libraries, [named the Uris gallery floor (three computer labs, the Kirby Room and the Fiske), the Engineering Library and the Mann lobby](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/25/library-director-outlines-alternate-24-hour-study-spaces-besides-cocktail-lounge) as other 24-hour options, adding that the Mann lobby needs a Cornell ID swipe after midnight. A [December 2021 Sun article](https://cornellsun.com/2021/12/07/as-finals-season-approaches-libraries-continue-adapting-to-student-needs/) listed the Cocktail Lounge, extra gallery-level spaces, the Mann lobby and a first-floor space in Ives Hall run by Catherwood Library as 24/7 that semester. Those are past-semester lists, so treat them as leads and confirm.

## Quiet

**Clark Physical Sciences Library.** The library hours page [lists study space open 24/7 at 293 Clark Hall](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/). The 2019 Sun article called it a [quiet late-night option open seven days a week](https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/25/library-director-outlines-alternate-24-hour-study-spaces-besides-cocktail-lounge) and gave the room as 290 Clark Hall; use the library's address.

**Engineering Library.** In Carpenter Hall and [listed as open 24 hours](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/). The 2022 Sun writer was not impressed by the looks and noted [few amenities beyond private conference rooms](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/).

**Olin Library.** Olin has [numerous study rooms and carrels](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces). The 2022 Sun piece describes it as the place for a ["lone wolf"](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/) working in quiet among the stacks.

## Groups

**Mann 112.** In March 2022 Mann Library [moved Mann 112 to 24/7 operation](https://cornellsun.com/2022/03/17/mann-library-extends-study-space-hours-to-24-7/), with group and individual study space, comfortable seating and C.U. Print printers. Late-night building access needs a student ID card. That was the 2022 policy. Mann's own site now says only that its lobby is [open 24 hours](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/) and that some spaces are first come, first served while others can be reserved, so confirm Mann 112 before relying on it. The Help Desk is at mann_circ@cornell.edu or 607-255-3296.

**Reservable rooms.** Olin and Uris have [large and small study rooms for both quiet and group study](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces). In 2021 the library said some rooms could be [reserved through the LibCal app](https://cornellsun.com/2021/12/07/as-finals-season-approaches-libraries-continue-adapting-to-student-needs/), which is worth doing before finals.

## Coffee and food nearby

We could not verify hours for Collegetown or downtown Ithaca cafes from an official source, so we do not list them. What we can say from the 2022 Sun piece: Libe Cafe goes with Olin, Gates Hall has [good coffee, light and quiet](https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/14/what-your-favorite-study-spot-says-about-you/) (the writer's favorite building), and a cafe is better for atmosphere than for output. The 2019 Sun article places the Manndible Cafe next to the Mann lobby.

## If you are...

- **A night owl:** Cocktail Lounge first, Clark if it is full or too social. Bring your ID.
- **In a group project:** book a room early, especially in finals weeks.
- **Easily distracted:** skip the cafes and A.D. White; pick Clark or an Olin carrel.
- **Short on gear:** the Mann 112 page notes printers; see our guide to [cheap textbooks](/money/cornell-textbooks-cheap-where-to-buy-used-ithaca/) to cut course costs.

## Common mistakes

- Trusting a 2019 or 2022 list for 2026 hours. Check the library site.
- Forgetting your Cornell ID after hours.
- Showing up on a Saturday morning at Olin without a plan: an admissions blogger, as quoted in search results, said students queue for window seats. We could not open that page, so treat it as hearsay.

## FAQ

**Is there a 24-hour library at Cornell?** Yes: the [Cocktail Lounge](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces), [Clark](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) study space and the [Engineering Library](https://www.library.cornell.edu/libraries/) are listed as around the clock.

**Do I need an ID?** For the Cocktail Lounge after building hours, [a current Cornell ID card](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces).

**Can I reserve a room?** Some, via the library's booking tools; see [Mann](https://mann.library.cornell.edu/) and [Olin and Uris](https://olinuris.library.cornell.edu/use/spaces).
