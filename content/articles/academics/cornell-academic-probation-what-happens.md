Cornell University in Ithaca mostly does not use the word probation. A student who misses the standards for good academic standing gets an academic action, usually a warning, after a college committee reviews the record. If you do not get back to good standing, the next steps are a required leave of absence or, in serious cases, withdrawal from the university. Separately, your financial aid has its own academic test, and you can fail it even while your college treats you as fine. The details differ by college, so find yours below. (Search results for this question often show Cornell College in Iowa, a different school.)

## The ladder, in order

```figure
kind: steps
title: How academic action escalates at Cornell
rows:
  - label: You miss a good-standing standard at the end of a semester
  - label: Your college committee reviews your record
  - label: Warning or elevated warning
  - label: Not back in good standing next term: required leave
  - label: Serious or continued distress: withdrawal
```

1. **Review.** In Arts & Sciences, students who miss any criterion have their records [reviewed by the Academic Records Committee at semester's end](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/policies), which "may result in an academic action such as warning or elevated warning."
2. **Warning.** One law-firm explainer says a warning is [recorded on your academic record but does not appear on your transcript](https://studentdisciplinedefense.com/academic-progression-at-cornell-university). CALS says a warning [does not appear on the permanent academic record](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/cals-academic-policies), but the college must report it when verifying good standing for a prior semester.
3. **Required leave.** Students in more serious difficulty [may be put on a required leave for one semester or one year, or withdrawn](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/policies).
4. **Withdrawal.** Reserved for serious cases. ILR describes required withdrawal as [expulsion from the School with no expectation of re-registration](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-standing).

## What counts as good standing, by college

| College | Main standards |
|---|---|
| [Arts & Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/policies) | 12 academic credits completed, semester GPA of 2.0, admitted to a major by the end of the summer after sophomore year, progress toward the major, two First-Year Writing Seminars in the first four semesters |
| [ILR](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-standing) | 12 credits, a 2.0 term average with at least 8 letter-graded credits, no failing grades, 2.0 cumulative |
| [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/cals-academic-policies) | 2.0 semester and cumulative GPA, 12 credits, at least one CALS class per semester until required CALS credits are done, reasonable progress in the major |
| [Engineering, not yet in a major](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/registrar/registrar-frequently-asked-questions/) | 12 credits, 2.0 semester and cumulative GPA, no F, U or UX, minimum grades in required math courses |

Once an engineer affiliates with a major, [the major department sets the criteria](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/registrar/registrar-frequently-asked-questions/) and the Engineering Undergraduate Handbook has the specifics. Other colleges publish their own pages; check yours.

## What changes while you are on warning

- **A credit cap.** In Arts & Sciences, students on warning or elevated warning from the previous term [may enroll in a maximum of 18 credits](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/policies); students in good standing may take up to 22. Plan [enrollment](/academics/cornell-course-enrollment-strategy-pre-enroll-tips/) with that in mind.
- **A recovery test.** ILR says a student who [does not restore good standing during a semester on warning](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-standing) can be required to take a leave of one or two semesters.
- **Harsher triggers at ILR.** A semester average below 2.0 after a prior warning, failing two or more courses in a semester, or a term average below 1.0 can each lead to required leave.
- **CALS support.** The CALS committee can also place a student in the Academic Success Program, and its leave decisions weigh cumulative GPA, recent semester GPA and credits completed, with [no numeric cutoffs published](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/cals-academic-policies).

## If you are put on required leave

- **You cannot take Cornell classes.** You may take courses at other U.S. institutions and [petition to transfer the credits](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/leave-absence-and-withdrawal). In Arts & Sciences those credits can count toward the 120 for graduation but not toward the 100 required A&S credits or language and distribution requirements. Study abroad on leave earns no credit.
- **Money and housing keep running.** The A&S page says you stay responsible for [tuition and charges through the leave date, and housing and dining charges can continue until you stop using those services](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/leave-absence-and-withdrawal). It tells students to talk to the Bursar, Financial Aid and Housing before leaving, and notes that insurance eligibility may be affected.
- **Coming back takes paperwork.** In Arts & Sciences you submit the Return from Leave form and show that every return condition is met, including Bursar holds. For pre-enrollment, [requests are due March 1 for fall or October 1 for spring](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/policies). CALS students petition for readmission, with deadlines of [July 15 for fall and December 1 for spring](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/cals-academic-policies), must clear incompletes and holds, and are told that readmission is not guaranteed. ILR students must [petition the Committee on Academic Standards and Integrity](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/academic-standing).
- **Leaves expire.** A&S says a leave [expires after five years](https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/leave-absence-and-withdrawal), and CALS says leaves over five years mean withdrawal and reapplication.

For scale, a 2003 Cornell Daily Sun article reported that the Arts & Sciences committee usually reviewed 120 to 160 records a term and required [25 to 35 students to take a leave](https://cornellsun.com/2003/04/16/colleges-monitor-students-academics/). That is old, so treat it as a sense of proportion, not current data.

## Financial aid is a separate test

Aid uses [Federal Satisfactory Academic Progress](https://finaid.cornell.edu/policies/federal-satisfactory-academic-progress) (SAP), reviewed at the end of the academic year after final grades post. The undergraduate standards:

```figure
kind: cost_table
title: Undergraduate SAP standards for aid
rows:
  - {label: Cumulative GPA, value: "At least 2.0"}
  - {label: Pace, value: "Earn at least 67% of attempted credits"}
  - {label: Maximum timeframe, value: "150% of program length (180 attempted credits for a 120-credit degree)"}
```

Withdrawals and failures count as attempted credits. Failing SAP means losing aid eligibility, with written notice from the Office of Financial Aid. You can appeal for reasons such as a death in the family, injury or illness; the page says lack of awareness of SAP is not a valid reason, third-party documentation is required, and decisions take about two weeks. An approved appeal gets one semester of aid probation. Paying out of pocket for a semester does not by itself restore eligibility; you have to meet the standards. Read [our guide to aid appeals](/money/cornell-financial-aid-appeal-success/) and [why aid offers drop](/money/cornell-financial-aid-decreased-why-aid-offer-lower-than/).

## A recovery plan (our advice)

- Meet your college advising office as soon as the warning arrives, before the next enrollment period.
- Do not drop below 12 credits to protect a GPA; the credit minimum is itself a standard in every college above.
- Ask about Incompletes carefully: CALS lists INC grades as a warning reason.
- If illness or a family crisis drove the semester, start documentation now; SAP appeals require it.
- Check aid and housing before agreeing to any leave.

## FAQ

**Does Cornell have academic probation?** Arts & Sciences, ILR, CALS and Engineering pages use warning, elevated warning, required leave and withdrawal. Aid probation exists, but only after an approved SAP appeal.

**Will a warning show on my transcript?** Per the law-firm explainer, no, but required leaves are [noted on the transcript](https://studentdisciplinedefense.com/academic-progression-at-cornell-university). This is a secondary source; confirm with your college.

**Can I appeal a decision?** The same explainer says decisions can be challenged, for example by requesting a grade change or an Incomplete, and that exceptions are occasionally made in extraordinary cases. Ask your advisor which route your college uses.

**Where are the exact rules for my college?** The college pages linked above, and the Cornell University Catalog. We could not load the catalog's leave page, so we cite the college pages directly.
