Cornell's free tutoring is spread across several offices, and which one you use depends on your course and college. Undergraduates in any college can use the Learning Strategies Center (LSC) for [free drop-in group tutoring](https://lsc.cornell.edu/help-with-classes/help-with-cornell-courses/lsc-tutoring). Duffield Engineering undergraduates, and Biological and Environmental Engineering majors, also get [two free hours a week of tutoring](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/tutoring/). Math students can use the Math Support Center, and anyone writing a paper can use the writing centers. Paid tutors fill the gaps, at about $28 an hour on average in Ithaca.

## Learning Strategies Center (all undergraduates)

The LSC says its resources are [free and available to all undergraduate students at Cornell](https://lsc.cornell.edu/help-with-classes/help-with-cornell-courses/). Its tutoring is drop-in and group-based, the tutors are [Cornell undergraduates](https://lsc.cornell.edu/help-with-classes/help-with-cornell-courses/lsc-tutoring), and it is not one-on-one. The LSC also says tutors will not do your homework or re-teach course material, so go having reviewed the material and tried the assignments, with specific questions. Groups can be large, and busy periods may mean a wait.

The [Fall 2026 course list](https://lsc.cornell.edu/help-with-classes/help-with-cornell-courses/) includes:

- **Chemistry:** CHEM 2070, 2080, 2090, 2510, 3570, 3580, 1560, 1570
- **Math:** MATH 1106, 1110, 1120
- **Physics:** PHYS 1112, 2213 and other PHYS 1xxx/2xxx courses
- **Biology:** BIOMG 1350, BIOG 1440, with limited tutoring for BIOMG 2800/2801
- **Statistics:** including STSCI/ILRST 2100 and 2110, ECON 3130 and PSYCH 2500
- **Finance and accounting:** AEM 1200, 2210, 2240, 2241, 2420 and several HADM courses
- **Languages:** ASL, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Spanish

Economics is marked "Not offered" and a few entries are marked as not offered in the current term, so check the page for your exact course. Times and locations live in a spreadsheet linked from the page. Questions go to learningstrategiescenter@cornell.edu.

During the end of Spring 2026, the LSC also had [general chemistry and calculus tutors daily on study days and exam week](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/05/cornell-offers-academic-and-wellness-resources-to-students-during-finals-week), according to the Cornell Daily Sun. The Sun quoted Amy Godert, director of the LSC and executive director of academic student success programs.

## Engineering: Tutors-on-Call and workshops

Duffield Engineering undergraduates and any student majoring in Biological and Environmental Engineering get [two hours of free tutoring each week](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/tutoring/). The listed courses are MATH 1910, 1920, 2930, 2940; CHEM 2080, 2090; CS 1110, 1112, 2110; PHYS 1112, 2213, 2214; ENGRD 2020/MAE 2020; and ENGR 2700. Extra tutors may be available for distribution courses; ask eng-tutoring@cornell.edu.

1. Read the tutoring guidelines on the Duffield page.
2. Use its "Request a Tutor" link.
3. Book early. The page says tutors are busy around prelims and finals.

Tutors will not help with homework or projects. Use course office hours for those.

Engineering also runs [Academic Excellence Workshops](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/academic-excellence-workshops/): one-credit, graded S/U, two hours a week, led by peer facilitators, for core math, CS and chemistry courses. They start in the third week of classes, and missing more than three means a U. Enroll like any course; our [enrollment strategy guide](/academics/cornell-course-enrollment-strategy-pre-enroll-tips/) covers timing.

## Math Support Center

The Math Department describes [free student-led tutoring for all math courses](https://math.cornell.edu/math-support-center-msc) in 256 Malott Hall. Hours are on a schedule linked from that page, and we could not confirm them directly. The Sun's finals piece also lists it as [open to students in any undergraduate math course](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/05/cornell-offers-academic-and-wellness-resources-to-students-during-finals-week).

## Writing help

The Cornell Writing Centers are [free for the whole Cornell community](https://knight.as.cornell.edu/cornell-writing-centers). Book through WC Online or walk in. Appointments are 60 minutes, you can book up to two a week, and you should cancel at least 8 hours ahead, because three missed appointments disable the account. Fall 2026 hours:

- Sunday to Thursday, 7 to 9 pm: 108 Uris Library, 403 Olin Library, B06 Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall, plus online shifts
- Monday to Thursday, 3 to 5 pm: 178 Rockefeller Hall
- Fall break (October 8 to 13) and Thanksgiving break (November 20 to 29) have no tutoring
- Last day of the full schedule is Monday, December 7; limited exam tutoring is December 8 to 11, 3 to 5 pm, in Rockefeller 178 only

Graduate students, professional students and postdocs can also use [ELSO tutoring](https://elso.as.cornell.edu/elso-tutoring) for writing, presentations and pronunciation. Sessions are 45 minutes. The ELSO page does not state a price, so confirm with elso@cornell.edu.

## Paid tutors in Ithaca

If your course is on no list, or you want one-on-one time, a private tutor is the fallback. [Care.com reports an average posted rate of about $28 an hour](https://www.care.com/private-tutors/ithaca-ny) for private tutors in Ithaca as of November 2026 (its page shows $28.00 in one place and $28.20 in another, and the 16 profiles shown average $25). Rates vary by tutor and subject, so treat it as a starting point and ask for a trial hour.

The Sun's finals piece also lists Student Agencies' Cornell Group Study and Cornell Course Tutoring, described as [an external, student-run nonprofit](https://cornellsun.com/article/2026/05/cornell-offers-academic-and-wellness-resources-to-students-during-finals-week). We found no published price, so ask them.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until the night before a prelim. The LSC says [not to wait until the day before a test](https://lsc.cornell.edu/help-with-classes/help-with-cornell-courses/lsc-tutoring).
- Expecting homework answers. Neither the LSC nor Engineering tutors will give them.
- Assuming a course is covered. Check the lists above; Economics, for example, is not offered at the LSC.
- Skipping the early-semester window. If you are fighting for a seat in a class, see [how to get into a full class](/academics/how-to-get-into-a-full-class-at-cornell-waitlist/) before you worry about tutoring.

## FAQ

**Is Cornell tutoring really free?** LSC tutoring, Tutors-on-Call, the Math Support Center and the Writing Centers are each described as free on the pages linked above. Engineering workshops are one-credit courses, and their page does not state a cost.

**How many hours can I get?** Engineering caps Tutors-on-Call at two hours a week. The Writing Centers allow two appointments a week. The LSC page states no limit.

**Can I get tutoring in the evening?** The Writing Centers run Sunday to Thursday evenings. For LSC and Math Support Center times, check their posted schedules.

**Who do I contact if I am unsure?** The LSC's address is learningstrategiescenter@cornell.edu, and it can point you to the right office.
