Cornell's drop deadline for a regular semester course is the 57th day: [October 19 for fall 2026 and March 22 for spring 2027](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). Drop before it and nothing shows on your transcript. Drop after it and you get a W, on a schedule each college sets. A Senate proposal to move the free drop to day 11 is still pending.

## The dates for 2026-27

Regular full-semester courses, per the [Registrar's academic calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar). The calendar changes each year, so check it for 2027-28.

Seven-week courses run on their own clock. Fall 2026's 7-week 2 session had a drop deadline of [November 11](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), and its last day of instruction is December 4. Spring 7-week 1 closes drops February 22 and 7-week 2 closes them April 14. The calendar also notes that the drop deadline is the last day to change grading basis, so the two decisions share one date.

## What happens at each stage

1. **Add period.** The Arts & Sciences registrar says regular courses can be added [during the first 15 calendar days](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies). The Registrar's calendar gives September 8 for fall 2026 and February 8 for spring 2027.
2. **Through the 57th day: drop, no record.** Arts & Sciences says a course dropped within the first 57 days [comes off the academic transcript](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies), as long as no academic integrity issue is pending.
3. **After the 57th day: a W.** Arts & Sciences says a course withdrawn after that point shows a W where the grade would normally appear, and that the W [is a matter of record and cannot be petitioned](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies). Arts & Sciences students petition the college to withdraw, and the page says petitions may not be submitted after the published deadlines except in exceptional circumstances. The [A&S important dates page](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/important-dates) shows a W first appearing October 20, 2026 and March 23, 2027, with the last petition date for regular classes December 7, 2026 (fall) and May 11, 2027 (spring).

## College by college

Colleges agree on the 57th-day idea but word the W rules differently. Each row links the college's own page.

| College or school | Free drop (no W) | W window | Source note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arts & Sciences | First 57 days | After day 57, by petition: W first appears Oct 20, 2026 (fall) and Mar 23, 2027 (spring); last petition date Dec 7, 2026 and May 11, 2027 | [A&S policies](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies), [A&S important dates](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/important-dates) |
| Engineering | University deadline, Oct 19, 2026 for fall regular courses, without a petition | By petition after that: W first appears Oct 20, 2026; absolute last day to drop by petition Dec 7, 2026, with a W on the transcript | [Engineering dates](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/students/undergraduate-students/curriculum/dates-and-deadlines-eng-undergraduates-and-meng) |
| CALS | Before the drop deadline | Request a W by the last day of classes; no exceptions after it | [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/withdrawing-course) |
| Dyson | University drop deadline; individual courses can set their own add/drop dates | Petition to the Dyson Office of Student Services through the withdrawal deadline; "no drop" and early-drop courses excluded; the W is permanent and does not affect GPA | [Dyson, Courses of Study](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/applied-economics-management/) |
| Nolan School of Hotel Administration | University dates, but some HADM classes set earlier add and drop deadlines, listed on the Class Roster | By petition for extenuating circumstances, reviewed by the Nolan School Academic Review Committee | [Nolan, Courses of Study](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/hotel-administration/) |
| Brooks School of Public Policy | University dates | Last day of instruction for full-semester and 7-week classes | [Brooks](https://www.publicpolicy.cornell.edu/?p=23333) |
| ILR | Before the official drop deadline | Only by petition to the Faculty Committee on Academic Standards and Scholarships | [ILR](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/dropping-course) |
| AAP | University calendar dates; the AAP page points to that calendar for add, drop and withdrawal deadlines | University calendar withdrawal deadline; petitions go in before the act, signed by the faculty advisor | [AAP](https://aap.cornell.edu/student-services/academic-policies) |
| Bowers CIS | Your enrolling college's rules | Your enrolling college's rules; forms and petitions go through its registrar (Arts & Sciences, CALS, Engineering or the Graduate School) | [Bowers CIS](https://bowers.cornell.edu/offices/registrar) |
| Veterinary Medicine (DVM) | One week at the start of each distribution period, for enrollment and grade option changes | After the first week of instruction: a W plus a $100 fee | [Vet College policy](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/003-Add-DropPolicy.pdf) |
| Graduate School | By the posted drop date | Last day of classes | [Graduate School](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academic-progress/enrollment-status-progress/enrolling-in-courses/) |

A few things stand out. CALS, Brooks and the Graduate School give you a W by request right up to the last day of classes. Arts & Sciences, Engineering and Dyson want a petition for a late W, and [Engineering's fall 2026 petition window](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/students/undergraduate-students/curriculum/dates-and-deadlines-eng-undergraduates-and-meng) runs from the day after the drop deadline to the last day of instruction. ILR makes you petition and says that [not doing well in a course, by itself, is not regarded as a reason](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/dropping-course) to be allowed to drop. The [Vet College](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/003-Add-DropPolicy.pdf) adds a $100 fee to any drop after the first week of instruction, and charges the same fee for a late add.

Short or modular courses can run on different deadlines, according to [Brooks](https://www.publicpolicy.cornell.edu/?p=23333) and [Arts & Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies), and [Dyson](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/applied-economics-management/) and [Nolan](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/hotel-administration/) say individual classes can set their own add and drop dates, so read the class notes on the roster.

## What a W costs

- **GPA:** [CALS says](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/withdrawing-course) a W does not affect GPA but an F does, and that neither earns credit while both count as attempted credits. [Engineering](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/registrar/leave-of-absence-and-withdrawal/) and [Dyson](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/applied-economics-management/) say W grades do not affect GPA.
- **Aid, visa, athletics:** Engineering warns that W grades [may negatively impact financial aid packaging, international visa status and athletics eligibility](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/registrar/leave-of-absence-and-withdrawal/). CALS asks you to consult an advisor first if dropping puts you below 12 credits, which matters most for aid recipients, veterans, international students and athletes.
- **Retakes:** ILR says that if you retake a course, [the W stays on your record](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/academic-policies/dropping-course) and the second attempt gets a grade.

If aid is on the line, read [our guide to financial aid appeals](/money/cornell-financial-aid-appeal-success/) and talk to Student Financial Services before you drop.

## The pending change

On March 24, 2026 the Faculty Senate's Educational Policy Committee posted a [resolution to revise the add and drop deadlines](https://deanoffaculty.cornell.edu/?p=64112). For full-semester courses it proposes:

- add without petition through the end of day 10
- drop with no transcript notation, and grade option change, through the end of day 11
- late drop with a W from day 12 through the Friday of Week 9 by self-service, then by college petition from the Monday of Week 10

In November 2026 the Dean of the Faculty site still lists the resolution under [Pending Matters](https://deanoffaculty.cornell.edu/?p=64112). Its suggested timeline called for a Senate vote in spring 2026 and new deadlines in fall 2026, but the Registrar's 2026-27 calendar keeps the 57th-day deadlines, so plan around those. The resolution's tables put the self-service W cutoff at Week 9; one later section says Week 8.

Cornell has loosened the deadline before. In spring 2021 it let students drop through the last day of instruction, May 14, [without a transcript notation](https://cornellsun.com/2021/04/13/citing-widespread-stressors-cornell-extends-drop-deadline-to-last-day-of-classes), a one-semester exception.

## If you are...

After the fall 2026 drop deadline of [October 19](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), a full-semester fall class can only come off your schedule with a W: by petition in Arts & Sciences, Engineering and Dyson, or by request in CALS, Brooks and the Graduate School, through the last day of instruction, December 7.

- **Before the drop deadline and unsure:** decide now. Dropping is free of any record; waiting turns it into a W.
- **Past the deadline in CALS, Brooks or the Graduate School:** you can still take a W until the last day of classes. Do not stop attending before the form is processed.
- **Past the deadline in Arts & Sciences, Engineering, Dyson or ILR:** you need to petition. Start with your advising office.
- **Below 12 credits after a drop:** talk to an advisor first about aid, visa and athletics consequences. [Dyson](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/applied-economics-management/) requires that meeting before it processes the petition.

## FAQ

**Is a W the same as an F?** No. CALS says a [W does not affect GPA, but an F does](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/withdrawing-course).

**Can I get a W removed?** Arts & Sciences says its W is [a matter of record and cannot be petitioned](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies), and [Dyson](https://courses.cornell.edu/business/applied-economics-management/) calls its W permanent.

**Does the grading option deadline match the drop deadline?** Yes, on the [Registrar's calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar) for regular courses.
