Build your schedule in the Student Center shopping cart before your appointment, enroll in the first minutes of your window, and keep backups. If a popular class is full, do not panic: the best-known student advice is to wait for add/drop, when seats often open. For spring 2027, [pre-enrollment runs November 10 to 20, 2026](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), in order of class year, with graduate students first, then seniors, juniors, sophomores and first-years. If you are reading this on November 7, you have days, not weeks.

## Spring 2027 timeline by class year

Each pre-enrollment appointment [runs 3 days and begins at 7:30 AM](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment) for all class rankings. Your exact start and end are in the Enrollment Dates section of [Student Center](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment), so confirm them there.

Per the [academic calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar), seniors and Vet Med fourth-years go November 11 to 13, juniors and Vet Med third-years November 16 to 18, sophomores and Vet Med second-years November 17 to 19, and first-years and Vet Med first-years November 18 to 20. Graduate, professional and Cornell Tech students go November 10 to 12. Add/drop then opens January 19 for graduate students and seniors, January 20 for juniors, January 21 for sophomores and January 22 for first-years. The add deadline for regular sessions is February 8, and the drop and grading basis deadline is March 22. The same calendar lists seven-week and project sessions with their own dates.

The registrar says continuing students pre-enroll [for fall in late April and for spring in late November](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment). Dates change each year, so check the calendar every term.

## Before your appointment

1. **Plan with the Scheduler.** The registrar's guide says to use the Class Roster [Scheduler](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment) to plan your semester and to review your proposed schedule with your advisor. Cornell says [each advisor approaches enrollment differently](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment), so ask yours what they expect.
2. **Fill the shopping cart.** You can [place requests in the Shopping Cart before your appointment](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment). It does not save a seat: [you still have to complete enrollment once your appointment is active](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-questions), and class details can change after you add a class to the cart.
3. **Plan backups.** Cornell warns that [course offerings are subject to change](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment). Line up alternates, and avoid time conflicts, which Student Center rejects.
4. **Check for holds.** They appear at the top right of Student Center, and [some require action](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment).
5. **Get permission numbers early.** For a consent class, the instructor or department gives you [a permission number you enter in step 1](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment); numbers should not be shared.
6. **Know your credit cap.** See the next section.

## Credit caps by college

[Colleges set their own maximum credits per semester](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment), and your limit shows in the Enrollment Dates area of Student Center. Some colleges lower the cap during pre-enrollment, so check both numbers. Four examples, checked in November 2026:

| College | Cap per semester | Going over |
|---|---|---|
| [Arts and Sciences](https://as.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/registrar/policies) | 22 credits for students in good standing (18 if on warning status from the previous term) | Petition; more than 22 only in serious extenuating circumstances |
| [CALS](https://cals.cornell.edu/undergraduate-students/cals-student-services/academic-resources/enrollment) | 22 credits; 18 during pre-enrollment | See the CALS enrollment page |
| [Duffield Engineering](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/registrar/) | 20 credits | Petition, never more than 24; petitions are not approved for first-years |
| [ILR](https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/current-students/undergraduate-academic-policies/18-credits-semester) | 18 credits | Only listed situations (for example one extra physical education course); no petition otherwise |

Engineering's cap was [lowered from 23 to 20 starting fall 2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/03/17/college-of-engineering-reduces-credit-limit-citing-mental-health-concerns/), per The Cornell Daily Sun. The [Duffield registrar page](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/registrar/) lists a student in their first year among those who will not be approved to exceed 20 credits. For other colleges, ask your college registrar. If a request pushes you over, Student Center shows ["Unable to add this class - term maximum exceeded"](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-errors).

## On the day

- **Use one window.** Cornell says [not to log into Student Center from multiple windows, browsers or devices](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment), which can leave requests stuck in "pending". Wait for the success or error message before refreshing.
- **Clear your cache first** and [quit other browser windows](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment).
- **Look for the green check.** In step 3, [a green checkmark means you are enrolled](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment).
- **You are [logged out after more than 30 minutes of inactivity](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment).**
- **Cannot enroll at all?** Check holds first, then contact your college registrar.

## Restricted seats, waitlists and full classes

All courses have enrollment caps, and [faculty or departments may enforce specific restrictions](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment). The error for a restricted seat reads ["Available seats are reserved. You do not meet the enrollment group criteria to enroll in this class at this time"](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-errors). The page gives no direct fix, so ask the department whether seats open later.

Only [a limited number of classes have online waitlists](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment). Open the section details: [under Class Availability, a wait list capacity of 0 means there is no wait list](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/a-guide-to-enrollment). If one exists, the registrar's FAQ describes a ["Wait list if class is full" checkbox](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-questions) next to the wait list option. To leave it, [drop the class](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-questions). For the full rules, see [how to get into a full Cornell class](/academics/how-to-get-into-a-full-class-at-cornell-waitlist/).

## When to wait for add/drop

Katherine Yao, a junior in Arts and Sciences when [writing in The Cornell Daily Sun in November 2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/11/14/yao-the-best-way-to-pre-enroll-is-waiting-until-add-drop/), said large classes such as CS 1110 and MATH 1110 were ones she predicted would open up during add/drop, so first-years and sophomores can wait for spots. The column also points to Coursegrab for seat alerts and says schedules shift during the semester anyway. It is one student's view from 2021, so check how your class behaved in past terms. Our advice:

- Enroll in required, small or restricted classes at your first opportunity.
- For large intro courses with several sections, take any decent section and swap later.
- If you are a first-year or sophomore with a thin schedule, pre-enroll in what you can, then check Student Center regularly once add/drop opens. Remember your window: the first-year start is the last of the four.
- Do not hold a seat you will not use. Drop it before the deadline.

We could not open the registrar's gateway-course page when we checked on November 7, 2026, so this section rests on the 2021 column plus the [add/drop dates](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar) above. Departments also set their own rules, so check your department's enrollment page. See also [the add/drop deadline and when a W shows up](/academics/cornell-add-drop-deadline-w-on-transcript/).

## Common mistakes

- Treating the shopping cart as a reservation. It is not one.
- Using two devices and getting stuck in "pending".
- Skipping backups for a class that might be restricted.
- Missing the add deadline: after it passes, [contact your college or school registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-questions).

## FAQ

**When does spring 2027 enrollment start?** November 10 for graduate students, November 11 for seniors, and November 18 for first-years. See the timeline above.

**Can I change my schedule after pre-enrollment?** Yes. With an active enrollment appointment, you can [drop, swap or edit grading options in Student Center](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-questions), unless consent is needed, the class is closed, there is a time conflict, or you would go over your credit maximum.

**What if I get an error I do not recognize?** Check the [common errors list](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-errors), then email the registrar at coursenroll@cornell.edu, an address given on the [Common Enrollment Questions page](https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/enrollment/common-enrollment-questions), or contact your college registrar.
