You can earn money in Ithaca over the summer from an on-campus student job, a paid or funded internship, an off-campus seasonal job, or by subletting your own room, and the numbers only work if you plan housing and food first. Cornell's student wage scale starts at [$16.00 an hour for Level I in 2026](https://www.studentemployment.cornell.edu/jobs/wages-and-classifications/student-wage-scale), while campus summer housing costs [$57.00 a night for a double or $69.50 for a single](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus), plus a $1.50 nightly admin fee.

The net picture, as an illustration (our arithmetic, not a Cornell figure): 10 weeks at 40 hours and $16.00 is $6,400 before tax. A double room for all 71 nights of the summer window is about $4,154, roughly 65 percent of that $6,400 before tax and before food. That leaves about $2,246, and the [$686 Combo meal plan](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/summer-meal-plans) alone takes it to about $1,560, before tax and any other spending.

**What to do now (November):** the Pathways deadline for this cycle passed on October 19, so the next steps are browsing Handshake and booking a Career Services advisor, then watching for the Summer Experience Grant to open on February 1 (based on the 2026 cycle).

Hourly pay is not the whole picture. Whether a campus job offers 40 hours a week is something the hiring unit decides, so ask before you plan around it.

## Where to apply

- **Cornell Alumni Affairs and Development, Reunion student staff:** the [Reunion jobs page](https://alumni.cornell.edu/students/work-with-us) lists class clerk, van driver and set-up worker roles, with 100+ student positions. The page we read covers Reunion June 10 to 13, 2027, with clerk training June 7 to 13. Check the page for current pay and eligibility.
- **Cornell Small Farms Program:** the [student positions page](https://smallfarms.cornell.edu/about/interns/now-hiring-student-positions/) describes full-time summer roles from mid-May to mid-August and school-year roles. The version we read was for 2025 hiring, so confirm the current round with the program.
- **Cornell Career Services:** [Handshake and other boards](https://career.cornell.edu/channels/search-for-a-job-internship/) for campus and local postings.
- **Off campus:** see [off-campus jobs for Cornell students](/careers/ithaca-part-time-jobs-for-cornell-students/).

## What staying costs

Summer housing is in [designated residence halls on Cornell's North Campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus). It runs from 2:00 pm June 1 to 10:00 am August 11 in 2027, with a two-week minimum stay. You must upload documentation of your eligibility for summer housing. Applications open at 9 am on March 1, and the page asks you to submit at least 10 days before arrival.

For the full window of 71 nights (our count), that is about $4,154 for a double or $5,041 for a single, including the $1.50 admin fee. The page gives nightly rates only, so confirm totals with the housing office at summersessionhsg@cornell.edu. Cornell's page says to contact co-ops@cornell.edu about [summer co-op options and rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/summer-housing-campus); it lists no co-op prices.

Food is separate. In summer 2026 the [Combo Plan cost $686](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/summer-meal-plans) for 30 dining room meals plus $200 in Summer Bucks, valid May 26 to August 16. Summer Bucks prices were $10.80 for breakfast, $15.25 for lunch (Monday to Saturday) and $18.85 for dinner. A meal plan is optional but recommended per the housing page. The 2027 plans were not posted when we checked. For the academic-year version of this math, see [our meal plan breakdown](/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/).

## Steps to work through

1. **Now to January:** Browse [Handshake and the other job boards](https://career.cornell.edu/channels/search-for-a-job-internship/) on Career Services, and book an advisor appointment.
2. **February:** Decide whether your job is paid or unpaid. If unpaid or minimally paid, get a written offer and apply for the grant, since the requirements ask for [an offer you have not yet accepted](https://experience.cornell.edu/opportunities/summer-experience-grant/requirements).
3. **By March:** Apply for summer housing once it opens, or line up a lease or co-op room.
4. **April to May:** Ask your campus employer for expected hours and confirm your start date.

## What students have done

An A&S profile describes one student's [summer around Ithaca](https://as.cornell.edu/news/spotlight-summer-opportunities-work-around-ithaca): a student employee job in Undergraduate Admissions, volunteer debate coaching at the Finger Lakes Juvenile Residential Center, a Kids' Shakespeare camp week with the Ithaca Shakespeare Company, and a residence hall mentor and teaching assistant role at the Cornell International Summer Debate Camp. The page does not say which roles were paid.

The Cornell Daily Sun reported in 2022 on two international students who [stayed for on-campus jobs](https://cornellsun.com/2022/06/09/international-students-share-summer-plans-in-ithaca-and-abroad/). Hawi Humnessa Tolera '25, who works for Conference and Event Services, said she had to earn money because plane tickets home were unaffordable. Nick Mugirashaka '25, who works at the Service Center, said staying let him take a class and earn money. The article is from 2022, so treat the details as one year's experience.

## If you are...

- **An international student:** F-1 students are normally limited to on-campus work [done for and paid by Cornell](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/employment-and-taxes/f-1-cpt). Cornell's CPT page says a student not registered for classes in the summer may work full-time or part-time under CPT, which needs approval before you start. Apply for CPT at least three business weeks before the start date, since approval cannot be backdated. Talk to International Services before accepting anything, and see [our F-1 on-campus jobs guide](/money/cornell-international-student-jobs-on-campus-f-1-how-to-find/).
- **Taking a summer class:** The grant requirements list summer courses as ineligible, and the Pathways program lists [taking classes](https://as.cornell.edu/careers/pathways-internship-program) as ineligible.
- **Looking for off-campus work:** See [off-campus jobs for Cornell students](/careers/ithaca-part-time-jobs-for-cornell-students/).
- **On financial aid:** If summer costs are the problem, read [how aid appeals work](/money/cornell-financial-aid-appeal-success/).

## Common mistakes

Our advice, drawn from the figures above:

- Waiting for spring to look. The housing application and first grant deadline both fall on March 1 in the cycles we checked.
- Counting stipends: the [grant page says](https://experience.cornell.edu/opportunities/summer-experience-grant/requirements) any stipends are deducted from the amount you request.
- Budgeting rent only. A double for 71 nights (about $4,154) plus the $686 Combo plan comes to about $4,840 before any other spending (our arithmetic).

## FAQ

**Do campus jobs pay more in summer?** The wage scale lists [hiring ranges by level](https://www.studentemployment.cornell.edu/jobs/wages-and-classifications/student-wage-scale), not by season. Student Employment's [dates page](https://studentemployment.cornell.edu/important-dates) lists no summer-specific rules, so ask sesemp@cornell.edu or 607-255-5145 about summer hours.

**Is summer housing guaranteed?** The page says room assignments begin in April and does not use the word guarantee.

**Can I just stay in my apartment?** If your lease runs through summer, yes. If you are leaving, see [our sublet guide](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/).
