Yes, if you share costs or live in Cornell's cheaper units, and tightly if you rent a one-bedroom alone on the open market. The 2026-27 minimum for a TA, GA, RA or GRA is [$48,912 for 12 months ($36,684 for the nine-month academic year plus $12,228 for summer)](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/), effective August 21, 2026. That is about $4,076 a month before taxes. Tuition and the Student Health Plan are paid by your funding package, so rent is the number that decides how comfortable you are. The Tompkins County Workers' Center puts the [2026 living wage for a single adult at $25.08 an hour](https://www.tcworkerscenter.org/2026/04/tompkins-county-living-wage-updated-now-25-08-hour-detailed-study/), which is about $52,166 over a 2,080-hour year (our multiplication). The 12-month stipend sits about $3,250 under that line.

The 12-month figure is [$1,364 above 2025-26](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/2025-26-stipend-rates/) ($47,548), a rise of about 2.9 percent (our arithmetic). University Fellowships pay the same nine-month rate, but the [summer amount is $7,779](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/), so the 12-month total is $44,463. Cornell Tech pays $60,719 for 12 months.

Doctoral funding packages include [tuition, fees, Student Health Plan (SHP), and a stipend](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/policies/funding/). Students on assistantships and fellowships are [enrolled in the SHP at no cost](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/policies/health-insurance/). The SHP page lists [$4,092 for 2026-27 for fully funded graduate students](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/node/177) on the bursar bill, so you are being spared that amount, and it is the amount a dependent costs: a spouse, domestic partner or one child is $4,092 a year, two or more children $8,184.

Two things are not obviously "included". The summer stipend is not guaranteed: the Graduate School says [summer stipends are additional](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/policies/funding/) and some programs offer them, so ask your field whether you are funded for summer. And taxes differ: assistantship pay goes through payroll with [taxes withheld](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/policies/funding/), while fellowship stipends are paid as a refund to your student account with [no taxes withheld and are typically taxable](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/policies/funding/), so save for April.

## Rent decides everything

Rent is where the budget breaks. Cornell's graduate housing at Hasbrouck is on a 12-month contract, and the 2026-27 rates per month on the [housing rates page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates) are below. Cornell says housing rates include gas, electricity, garbage pickup and internet up to 200 GB a month. For the market there are two sources that disagree. One [Zumper snapshot of October 2026](https://www.zumper.com/rent-research/near-cornell-university-ny) shows a one-bedroom averaging $1,695 and a studio $1,725; the studio is above the one-bedroom, which points to a thin sample, and the page also mentions Trumansburg, so read it as a rough guide, not a quote. [RentCafe's Collegetown figures as of October 30, 2026](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/) are higher: studio $2,006, one-bedroom $2,342, two-bedroom $3,414. Collegetown is the priciest walkable area, so expect cheaper listings farther out (see the [neighborhood comparison](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/)).

| Option | Monthly rent | Per year | Share of $48,912 | Left per month (before tax) |

| Hasbrouck regular single, 2-bedroom townhouse, furnished | $620 | $7,440 | 15% | about $3,456 |

The percentages and the "left" column are our arithmetic from the figures above. The Congressional Research Service, in a May 2026 report, defines a [cost-burdened household as one spending more than 30 percent of income on housing costs](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R48945.html), the benchmark HUD uses. Thirty percent of $48,912 is about $1,223 a month, which only the shared townhouse room clears. The 2023 Sun op-ed discussed below argued about affordability in these terms.

## What is left after rent

Rent is not the only bill. Cornell publishes no graduate-wide breakdown of food and other costs that I could find, so this is a rough estimate built from the closest 2026-27 Cornell figures. The [Johnson MBA budget](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/graduate/mba/tuition-financing/) lists meals at $5,768, transportation at $3,754 and miscellaneous at $4,196 for the fall/spring term, and it does not say how many months that covers. As a cross-check, Cornell Global Learning gives international graduate students a [living-expense estimate of $37,418 for 2026-27, or $3,118 a month](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/tuition-and-living-expenses).

| Item (estimate) | Basis | Rough monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Groceries | $5,768 over 12 months, or 9 months if the term only | about $480 to $640 |
| Phone and everything else | $4,196 miscellaneous over 12 or 9 months | about $350 to $470 |
| Transport | Free if you use a bus pass (below); the $3,754 MBA line likely assumes more | $0 to a few hundred |

That puts non-rent costs near $830 to $1,110 a month before any car, travel or dependents (our estimate, not a Cornell figure). Take the Hasbrouck studio: about $2,721 left before tax, minus roughly $830 to $1,110, leaves about $1,600 to $1,900 before taxes on assistantship pay. Against the market one-bedroom, about $1,270 to $1,550. That is the real answer to "enough": workable, with little room for a car, a large move or a dependent. For cheaper food see our guide to [cheap groceries in Ithaca](/money/cheap-groceries-ithaca-ny-students/), and for winter bills the [utilities guide](/money/ithaca-utilities-cost-winter-heating-bill-apartment-students/).

## The summer gap

If your program funds you only for the nine-month year, the same 12-month rent is paid from [$36,684](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/), about $3,057 a month spread over the year. A $1,355 studio is then 44 percent of income, and a $1,695 market one-bedroom 55 percent. Plan for that before you sign a 12-month lease, and read our guide on [how to sublet an Ithaca apartment](/housing/cornell-summer-sublet-how-to-sublet-apartment-ithaca/) if you will be away.

## Costs the stipend rate hides, and costs it avoids

- **Union dues.** The Graduate School's page on union payments says the union's website states dues are [1.44 percent of a graduate student worker's stipend](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/about/union-representation/union-dues-fees-or-charity-payments-know-your-options). On the 2026-27 minimum, $48,912 x 1.44% is about $704 a year, or about $59 a month (our arithmetic). The page says the university deducts from payroll only if you sign an authorization form; otherwise you can pay the union directly. A challenge to the union is pending at the National Labor Relations Board, [the Sun reported in March 2026](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/graduate-student-asks-nlrb-to-question-graduate-student-union-s-legality), with no ruling reported.
- **Charity option.** The same page says students with a sincerely held religious, moral or ethical objection can [make a monthly charitable contribution equal to union dues](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/about/union-representation/union-dues-fees-or-charity-payments-know-your-options) instead. It lists the American Heart Association, the American Cancer Society and the United Way as eligible charities.
- **Bus.** The Graduate School's union page says [graduate student workers in Ithaca will receive free TCAT bus passes](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/about/union-representation), and a September 2025 message there says doctoral students are eligible for a TCAT OmniRide pass for all years of study. You request it by emailing transportation@cornell.edu. The page does not list further conditions, so confirm yours with Transportation Services.
- **Dental, vision and fitness.** The same page describes a [$544 benefits adjustment](https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/about/union-representation) in the Ithaca minimum stipend for fall 2025 and fall 2026, covering dental and vision insurance costs and access to Cornell Fitness Centers. It is framed as applying to graduate student workers, not fellows, so ask your department what your appointment gets.
- **Dependents.** A partner or child on the SHP adds the $4,092 per person figure above, and a one-income household is a very different budget from the single-person one in this article.

## What students say

Ewa Nizalowska, a Ph.D. student in Government, argued in a [2023 Sun guest column](https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/23/guest-room-eight-percent-is-not-enough/) that the 8 percent raise Cornell announced that year did not keep up with Ithaca costs. She also cited a study showing Tompkins County rents rose 12.3 percent. Cornell's side at the time, in its [announcement of the raise](https://news.cornell.edu/node/325842), was that it was the largest jump in stipends since 2006 and that 1.4 percent of graduating doctoral students in 2022 had borrowed during their studies, against a national average of 38.5 percent for doctoral research graduates. Both are three years old, so use them as the argument, not as current numbers.

## If you are...

- **Single and cost-conscious:** take a shared townhouse room or a roommate in a market two-bedroom, and request the TCAT pass if you are a doctoral student.
- **Single and want your own place:** a studio or one-bedroom leaves roughly $2,400 to $2,700 a month before tax, food and everything else, and about $1,300 to $1,900 once the non-rent estimate above is taken out. Workable, not roomy.
- **Funded for nine months only:** treat the summer as unfunded, ask your field about summer support, and consider [off-campus work](/careers/ithaca-part-time-jobs-for-cornell-students/), after checking your appointment's hour limits.
- **Supporting a partner or child:** add $4,092 a year per dependent for the SHP, and expect to need a second income.

## Common mistakes

Our advice, from the numbers above:

- Signing a 12-month lease without checking that you are funded for summer.
- Comparing the stipend to rent alone and forgetting food, phone and taxes, which come out of the gross figure used here.
- Assuming a fellowship pays the same take-home as an assistantship, when no tax is withheld on it.
- Skipping the [neighborhood comparison](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/) before choosing a lease.

## FAQ

**Is the stipend the same in every department?** Cornell says base rates are the same for fellowships and assistantships and [do not vary by discipline](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/). Your program may pay more than the minimum, and Cornell Tech has its own rate.

**What is the most I can earn?** If any part of a stipend comes from Cornell-held funds, the cap is [$55,025 for the academic year and $18,341 for summer](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/).

**How many hours is the job?** Standard assistantships [average 15 hours a week, with up to 5 more permitted](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/stipend-rates/).

**What about food?** Our guide on [cooking versus a meal plan](/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/) compares the two approaches.
