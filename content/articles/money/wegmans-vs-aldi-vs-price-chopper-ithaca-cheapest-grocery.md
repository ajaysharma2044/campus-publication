Aldi was about $57 cheaper than Wegmans on a 50-item basket in a [September 2025 Northeast survey](https://www.marketreportblog.com/2025/09/the-cheapest-grocery-stores-in-northeast.html), though none of the surveyed stores were in Ithaca. Wegmans is the store with a documented bus trip from campus, and we found no Price Chopper in Ithaca. Nobody has published a current Ithaca price comparison and we did not price a basket ourselves, so treat this as the best-supported answer rather than a measured one. If you want one rule: buy staples at Aldi when you have a ride, and use Wegmans for everything else.

## Is there a Price Chopper in Ithaca?

We found no Ithaca store. The Cornell Daily Sun's [2022 list of Ithaca groceries](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/) names two Aldi stores, a Wegmans, a Trader Joe's, a Tops, two GreenStar stores and Anabel's Grocery, and does not mention Price Chopper. A 2024 column by Sun columnist Carlin Reyen [calls Tops and P&C Fresh](https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/22/a-cheapskates-guide-to-navigating-cornell) some of the most affordable options near campus. Confirm on Price Chopper's own store locator before you plan a trip around it.

## What price data exists

The best dated comparison is a [September 2025 Market Report survey](https://www.marketreportblog.com/2025/09/the-cheapest-grocery-stores-in-northeast.html) that priced 50 staples at 30 Northeast stores in late August and early September 2025. None of the four stores below were in Ithaca, and the post notes that many chains charge different prices at different locations. Use it for ranking, not for dollar amounts. Tops is a sister store to Price Chopper, per the same post.

Aldi ranked first of 30, Wegmans 12th, Tops 18th and Price Chopper 21st, per the same survey. The gap between Aldi and Wegmans was about $57 on a 50-item basket, and Aldi beat the next-cheapest store in the survey by $15. The post also says Aldi had a large number of substitutions, more likely a generic version than the name brand, and lacks the full selection of a larger store, so the Aldi ranking is not a like-for-like match with the other stores. That ranking suggests Aldi is cheapest in Ithaca too, but it is an inference, not a local measurement.

A local source stops short of naming a winner. Cornell Health's affordable-food page [tells students to try different stores](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food) in the Ithaca area to find the best prices and to buy generic or store brands.

## Getting there without a car

Price only matters if you can get to the store. The Sun's 2022 article says [Route 30 reaches Tops and Route 15 reaches Wegmans and Trader Joe's](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/). It reported Route 15 running once an hour on weekdays, with the fastest trip to Wegmans taking about half an hour. That is a 2022 snapshot; check TCAT's current timetable at tcatbus.com before relying on it.

We could not open TCAT's fare and route pages (the site refused our automated requests), and Aldi's store locator did not list Ithaca addresses when we loaded it, so we cannot give you a verified current fare, Aldi street address or the TCAT route that serves Aldi. Look up all three on aldi.us and tcatbus.com before you go. The Sun's 2022 list does say Ithaca had two Aldi stores.

The same article quotes two students on the trade-offs. Katie Go '22, an Anabel's Grocery employee, said that when she lived in Collegetown, the nearby grocery options cost more than Wegmans or Walmart, which were hard to reach without a car. Alexandra Michael '23 said she and her roommate went three weeks without grocery shopping because they had no car, and called unreliable transportation the biggest factor. Reyen's 2024 column calls the trip to Tops and P&C Fresh without a car a logistical nightmare and mentions a TCAT shortage that left her stranded in Lansing.

## Wegmans basics

The Ithaca Wegmans is at [500 South Meadow Street, open 6 AM to midnight seven days a week](https://www.wegmans.com/stores/ithaca-ny), per its store page. Late hours help if you take the bus after a lab or a shift.

## Which store for which student

Our advice, from the facts above:

- **You have a car or a friend with one:** do a monthly Aldi run for staples and a Wegmans stop for what Aldi lacks.
- **You rely on TCAT:** Wegmans is the store with a documented route from campus. One big trip beats several small ones, because the bus ride is the real cost.
- **You live in Collegetown with no ride:** Anabel's Grocery in Anabel Taylor Hall is [student-run, offers discounted groceries and accepts SNAP](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food), and it runs during the academic year.
- **You are comparing against the meal plan:** see [Is the Cornell meal plan worth it?](/money/cornell-meal-plan-worth-it/) and [meal plan or cook yourself](/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/).

## How to price your own basket in 20 minutes

1. Write your ten most-bought items: eggs, milk, bread, rice, pasta, chicken, a vegetable, a fruit, peanut butter, one snack.
2. Look up the unit price (per ounce or per pound) of the store brand of each at Wegmans and Aldi on the stores' own sites or shelf labels.
3. Add the bus time to the price, then decide whether a car-free trip is worth it.

## FAQ

**Is Wegmans cheaper than Aldi?** In the 2025 Northeast survey, no: its basket was about $57 higher. Individual items can differ.

**Is Tops cheaper than Wegmans?** In the same survey Tops was higher than Wegmans, but those were stores outside Ithaca.

**Is a car worth it for groceries alone?** Probably not; see [Do you need a car at Cornell?](/tools/should-i-bring-a-car-cornell-ithaca-cost/).
