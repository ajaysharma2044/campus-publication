A Cornell meal plan saves money only if you eat close to its weekly cap. At full use, the 2026-27 plans cost $12.51 to $15.98 per swipe, against a $16.80 walk-in lunch ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)). First-years have no choice, because Unlimited is required. Everyone else should buy the smallest plan they will honestly use.

## What does each 2026-27 meal plan cost?

Undergraduate plans run from $2,489 to $3,802 per semester for 2026-27 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). A swipe is one entry to a dining room, and Big Red Bucks (BRBs) are dollars on your Cornell ID for campus food. Each BRB is worth $1 and carries no tax ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)).

| Plan | Per semester | Per year | Meals | BRBs per semester | Guest meals per semester |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited | $3,802 | $7,604 | All meals in service periods | $400 | 8 |
| Bear Traditional | $3,528 | $7,056 | Up to 14 a week | $400 | 4 |
| Bear Choice | $3,075 | $6,150 | Up to 10 a week | $500 | 4 |
| Bear Basic | $2,489 | $4,978 | Up to 7 a week | $500 | 4 |

Source: [Cornell Dining undergraduate meal plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), 2026-27. Each plan adds a non-refundable $50 fee once per academic year.

The same page lists plans for special groups. The House Meal Plan costs the same as Unlimited and is for West Campus houses. The Collegetown Meal Plan costs the same as Bear Traditional and adds $436 in City Bucks per semester. Three debit plans (Off-Campus Value, Flex 10/500 and Just Bucks) are for students who live off campus, and each is covered below.

## How much does one swipe really cost?

Subtract the BRBs, because you would have spent that money on campus food anyway, and add half the annual fee for each semester. That gives a net price for the swipes alone. Divide by the swipes you actually use. This table assumes 18 weeks per semester, which matches the fall term of Aug. 17 to Dec. 20, 2026 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

| Meals eaten per week | Unlimited | Bear Traditional | Bear Choice | Bear Basic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | $27.20 | $25.02 | $20.63 | $15.98 |
| 10 | $19.04 | $17.52 | $14.44 | Over the cap |
| 14 | $13.60 | $12.51 | Over the cap | Over the cap |

Net semester price: Unlimited $3,427, Bear Traditional $3,153, Bear Choice $2,600, Bear Basic $2,014. For example, Bear Traditional is ($3,528 - $400 + $25) divided by 252 swipes (14 a week for 18 weeks), using the [2026-27 plan prices](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans).

Each plan's best case is its cap. Cap plans reset every Thursday, so a missed meal cannot be used later. Unlimited has no cap, so it only gets cheaper per swipe the more you eat.

## How many meals a week do I need to eat to break even?

Compare each plan with what the same meals would cost in BRBs. Take a student who eats breakfast, lunch and dinner Monday to Saturday, plus brunch and dinner on Sunday. At BRB prices that week costs 6 x ($10.80 + $15.25 + $18.85) + ($16.85 + $18.85) = $305.10. Divided by 20 meals, the average meal costs $15.26 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates)).

Read the figure against the caps. Bear Traditional wins if you eat 12 or more meals a week. Bear Choice wins if you eat 10 every week. Bear Basic cannot break even, though it is the cheapest way to buy the swipes you will realistically use. Unlimited needs more than 12 meals a week, and its 8 guest meals are worth up to $150.80 per semester at the BRB dinner price. That bonus is not in the break-even. The break-even uses the [BRB price list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates).

## What do the numbers miss?

Unused swipes vanish. Unused meals do not carry over, and the plan week runs Thursday to Wednesday ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)). Use only 7 of 10 Bear Choice swipes a week, and its $14.44 swipe becomes $20.63.

Location matters. A February 2025 Sun column said Okenshields was the only Central Campus place that took swipes, and that plan holders often ran out of BRBs before midsemester ([The Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus)). A March 2026 Sun column said the BRB allotment has stayed about flat while prices rose, and asked Cornell for more BRBs per plan ([The Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/we-want-more-big-red-bucks)). Both are student opinion, so check your own class schedule against the ten dining rooms.

BRBs have an end date, and Cornell's pages disagree on it. The undergraduate plans page says May 27, 2027, and the deadlines page says May 31, 2027 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms)). Plan to spend them by the earlier date. BRBs are not refundable, and they carry from fall to spring but not into the next year.

Breaks also cost you. The plan does not cover winter intersession, and Cornell Dining closes from Dec. 25, 2026, to Jan. 1, 2027 ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining)).

## Which plan is right for my situation?

```reader
If you're a first-year
You do not choose. Unlimited is required, and first-years cannot reduce or cancel ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Cornell says financial aid fully covers the plan for eligible students. Use the guest meals and spend the BRBs. If money is tight, read [first-gen and low-income hidden costs](/cornell/money/cornell-first-gen-low-income-hidden-costs/) for what aid does not cover.
```

```reader
If you live in a West Campus house
The House Meal Plan is required and cannot be canceled while you live there ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms)). The two-year residential requirement for first-years and sophomores includes a meal plan. If you think you qualify to live elsewhere, read the [residential requirement exemptions](/cornell/housing/cornell-residential-requirement-exemptions-live-off-campus/).
```

```reader
If you're a transfer student or live on campus as a junior
You can reduce from Unlimited to Bear Traditional, and nothing else, by the published deadline. The fall deadline of Sept. 9, 2026, has passed. The spring deadline is Feb. 3, 2027, and the move saves $548 a year ($7,604 minus $7,056). Reducing makes sense if you rarely eat 12 or more meals a week. After the deadline you can upgrade but not reduce.
```

```reader
If you live off campus
Bear Choice and Bear Basic are open to every off-campus student, and the debit plans are for students with no other plan ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Off-Campus Value costs $912.30 per semester for 45 meals and $150 in BRBs, which is $17.50 a swipe at full use. Flex 10/500 costs $674 for 10 meals and $500 in BRBs, or $19.90 a swipe. Just Bucks is $500 of BRBs with no meals. Compare them with cooking in [meal plans for off-campus students](/cornell/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/), and add rent from [is off campus cheaper than a Cornell dorm](/cornell/housing/is-off-campus-cheaper-than-cornell-dorm/).
```

If you pay a student's bills, a plan is the biggest dining line. See [how much spending money to give a Cornell student](/cornell/money/cornell-parent-how-much-spending-money-should-i-give-my/) for what to add on top.

## What mistakes cost the most?

Waiting to fix a plan is the first. Reductions close early in each semester, and after the deadline you can only upgrade. Changes take effect on Thursdays, and credits post to your Bursar account in 7 to 10 business days ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)).

Counting BRBs as free is the second, because they are only worth their face value if you spend them. Buying the cap you hope to hit is the third. Count the meals you ate last month, not the ones you plan to eat. Expecting a refund is the fourth: credits are prorated weekly and the fee does not come back ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)).

## FAQ

**Can I have a plan for one semester only?** No. Plans last a full academic year and renew automatically for spring ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs)).

**Can I use my swipe for a friend?** No. Swipes and BRBs are for the student only. Guest meals cover friends.

**What if I study abroad or take a leave?** First-year, sophomore and transfer students cannot cancel a plan. Tell the Dining Contracts Office by Jan. 6, 2027, to avoid spring charges ([Cornell Dining](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027)).

**How do I change my plan?** Use the Housing and Dining Portal or submit a Cornell Dining ticket. You can upgrade at any time.
