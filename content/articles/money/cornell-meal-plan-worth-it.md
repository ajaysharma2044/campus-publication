As a money saver, a Cornell meal plan is worth it only if you will eat in the dining rooms close to the maximum the plan allows. The official 2026-27 rates work out to roughly $15 to $19 a swipe at full use once you subtract the Big Red Bucks, and a Monday to Saturday lunch at the door costs [$16.80, tax included, on Cornell's 2026-27 dining room price list](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates). A February 2025 Sun column had put [the door lunch at $15.50](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus), so prices have moved. For most students the choice is made for them: the [Unlimited plan is required for first-year undergraduates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) at $7,604 for the year. If you do have a choice, the smaller the plan you can honestly use, the better the math.

## The 2026-27 plans

Prices are per semester and per year for 2026-27, from [Cornell's undergraduate meal plan page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). They change each year.

| Plan | Swipes | Big Red Bucks per semester | Guest meals per semester |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited | All meals in designated service periods | $400 | 8 |
| Bear Traditional | Up to 14 a week | $400 | 4 |
| Bear Choice | Up to 10 a week | $500 | 4 |
| Bear Basic | Up to 7 a week | $500 | 4 |

All of these carry a [non-refundable $50 administrative fee once each academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). The page also lists the House Meal Plan for West Campus residents ($3,802 per semester, same terms as Unlimited), the Collegetown Meal Plan ($3,528 per semester, up to 10 a week, $500 BRB plus $436 City Bucks) and three off-campus debit plans covered below.

## The break-even math

Big Red Bucks (BRBs) are [dollar-for-dollar and tax-exempt](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs), so we treat them as cash you would have spent on campus food anyway. That makes the real price of the swipes the semester price minus the BRBs:

- Unlimited: $3,802 - $400 = $3,402
- Bear Traditional: $3,528 - $400 = $3,128
- Bear Choice: $3,075 - $500 = $2,575
- Bear Basic: $2,489 - $500 = $1,989

**Cost per swipe at different usage levels.** We assume a 15-week semester of meals, which is our assumption, not a Cornell figure. Cap plans are use-it-or-lose-it each week, so a dash means the plan does not allow that many.

| Meals eaten per week | Unlimited | Bear Traditional | Bear Choice | Bear Basic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 (105 a semester) | $32.40 | $29.79 | $24.52 | $18.94 |
| 10 (150 a semester) | $22.68 | $20.85 | $17.17 | - |
| 14 (210 a semester) | $16.20 | $14.90 | - | - |

**How many swipes you need to break even.** If a dining-room meal is worth $10 to you (what you might spend cooking or grabbing food in Collegetown, our assumption), the numbers are above what any capped plan allows. The second row uses the official [$16.80 door price for lunch](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates) as the value of a meal. The same page lists a Big Red Bucks price of $15.25 for lunch, for students paying in BRBs instead of swiping.

| Value of one meal | Unlimited | Bear Traditional | Bear Choice | Bear Basic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $10.00 | 341 swipes | 313 | 258 | 199 |
| $16.80 | 203 swipes | 187 | 154 | 119 |

Read that against the weekly caps. Over 15 weeks Bear Traditional allows 210 swipes, so it breaks even at the door price only if you use about 187 of them, or roughly 12 to 13 a week. Bear Choice allows 150 and needs 154. Bear Basic allows 105 and needs 119. Neither breaks even against $16.80, though Bear Basic is by far the cheapest way to buy the swipes you will realistically use: its $4,978 year is [$2,626 less than Unlimited's $7,604](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). Bear Choice and Bear Basic are [open to on-campus undergraduates only if they enrolled at Cornell before Summer 2021](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans), so on-campus students today cannot buy them.

Two caveats. First, the door price is for Monday to Saturday lunch; [dinner is $20.80](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates), so dinner swipes are worth more, and prices change each year. Second, every figure above assumes you spend all your BRBs. [BRBs are non-refundable and expire at the end of spring](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs). Cornell's two pages give different final dates: the plan page says [May 27, 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) and the deadlines page says [May 31, 2027](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms). Plan for the earlier date.

## What the numbers do not capture

**Unused swipes vanish.** [Unused meals do not carry over each week](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs), and the plan week runs Thursday to Wednesday. A 2019 Sun opinion column by Nile Jones, then a junior, wrote that she [struggles to eat more than 10 meals a week and that unused swipes cost students $1,000 to $2,000 a year](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/04/jones-stop-forcing-west-campus-residents-to-buy-unlimited-meal-swipes). That is her estimate, from an older price list.

**Where swipes work matters.** A 2025 column by Maia Mehring, a sophomore, said [Okenshields is the only Central Campus dining option that accepts swipes](https://cornellsun.com/2025/02/25/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus), and that students often run out of BRBs mid-semester. Both are her claims.

**Skipping the plan is possible off campus.** Andrea Miramontes Serrano, then a freshman, wrote in 2020 that her [Saturday grocery trip cost $147](https://cornellsun.com/2020/10/26/a-skidmore-medal-topped-with-runny-pasta-freshman-year-with-zero-meal-plan) and that she had spent about $400 on food since arriving. She was writing about a 2020 plan, and first-years cannot skip the plan today. A 2011 College Confidential thread, now dated, had several posters [advising off-campus students against meal plans](https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/big-red-bucks-and-or-meal-plans-worth-it/1145525), with one poster calling plans a "ripoff." For the cooking side of the math, see [meal plan or cook yourself](/money/cornell-off-campus-meal-plan-or-cook-yourself-cost/).

## Which situation are you in?

- **First-year:** no decision. Unlimited is required, and [first-years may not reduce or cancel](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms). Cornell says [financial aid fully covers it for eligible students](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). Use the guest meals and spend the BRBs.
- **West Campus House resident:** the House Meal Plan is [required, and these residents cannot cancel it](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms).
- **Transfer student or on-campus junior:** you may [reduce from Unlimited to Bear Traditional by the published deadline](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms). The fall 2026 deadline, September 9, 2026, has already passed (today is November 7, 2026). The next reduction deadline is February 3, 2027, for spring. If you rarely eat 13 or more meals a week, the [$548 per year difference between the two ($7,604 vs $7,056)](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) is real money.
- **Off campus:** Bear Choice and Bear Basic are [open to all students living off campus](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans). On-campus undergraduates can buy them only if they enrolled at Cornell before Summer 2021. The debit plans (Off-Campus Value, Flex 10/500, Just Bucks) are the low-commitment end. Off-Campus Value is $912.30 per semester for 45 swipes and $150 BRB, which is $762.30 net, or $16.94 a swipe at full use. Flex 10/500 is $674 for 10 swipes and $500 BRB, or $17.40 a swipe. Just Bucks is $500 for $500 BRB. Debit plans require that you live off campus and have no other meal plan. Compare with your rent and location in [Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown](/housing/collegetown-vs-fall-creek-vs-downtown-ithaca-where-to-live/).

## Common mistakes

Our advice, from the rules above:

- Assuming you can fix it later. Reductions close [a couple of weeks into each semester](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs); you can upgrade any time but not reduce after the deadline.
- Counting BRBs as free. They are only worth their face value if you spend them before they expire.
- Buying the cap you hope to hit. Count the meals you ate last month, not the ones you plan to eat.
- Expecting a refund. Credits for approved changes are [prorated weekly](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs), and the $50 fee is not refunded.

## FAQ

**Can I have a plan for one semester only?** No. [All Dining meal plans are for a full academic year](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs) and renew automatically for spring.

**Can I use my swipe for a friend?** No. Swipes and BRBs are [for the individual student](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs); each plan includes 4 or 8 guest meals per semester.

**What if I study abroad or take a leave?** Spring plans can be cancelled by students not at the university that semester, and a [leave of absence is prorated](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs) from the later of the last date of usage or the leave date.

**How do I change my plan?** Use the [Housing and Dining Portal](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/apply-meal-plan-or-change-your-meal-plan) to upgrade or reduce, or submit a cancellation request ticket if you are eligible.
