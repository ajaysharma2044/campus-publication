Without a car, shop at Anabel's Grocery on campus for staples and ride TCAT to Wegmans for the big trip. With a car, Wegmans, Aldi or Tops along Route 13/Meadow Street is the standard answer. The useful ranking is cost against effort: the cheapest store is not much use if getting there takes half your afternoon. Here is how the options work by situation.

## The short version

| If you... | Start here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Have no car and want cheap staples | Anabel's Grocery | On campus, student-run, [accepts SNAP/EBT](https://www.anabelsgrocery.org/) |
| Have no car and want a full shop | TCAT to Wegmans | [Route 32 to Route 15](https://cornellsun.com/2023/05/03/culinary-autonomy-cooking-for-yourself) |
| Have a car | Wegmans, Aldi or Tops, along Route 13/Meadow Street | Largest selection, [all on the same corridor](https://postdocs.cornell.edu/postdoc-life/essentials-in-ithaca/) |
| Live in Collegetown and need something tonight | GreenStar, 307 College Avenue | Walkable, but a [Collegetown shopper told the Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/) it costs more |
| Cannot afford food right now | Cornell Food Pantry | Free and confidential |

## Anabel's Grocery (campus, no car)

Anabel's is a [student-run grocery in Anabel Taylor Hall](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food) that operates during the academic year. The store's own site lists it in [Room 127 at 548 College Ave](https://www.anabelsgrocery.org/), open Wednesday to Friday noon to 7 p.m. and Saturday noon to 3 p.m., and mentions seasonal closures, so check its posts before you walk over.

On price, the NYC Food Policy Center's [snapshot of Anabel's](https://www.nycfoodpolicy.org/food-policy-snapshot-anabels-grocery-cornell-university/) reports that shoppers saved 18 percent on groceries on average during the fall 2021 semester, measured against what it calls the leading local grocery store. The store itself says its produce is priced [at or below Wegmans](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/), per the 2022 Cornell Daily Sun, which also says selection is limited. Treat Anabel's as the place for staples, not a replacement for a supermarket.

Cornell Health's page carried a [November 10, 2025 note](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food) that Anabel's was offering a 50 percent discount to all customers during the government shutdown. Anabel's own site describes a 50 percent discount as a [one-day promotion](https://www.anabelsgrocery.org/). Do not plan a budget around it.

## Wegmans by bus (no car, full shop)

Wegmans is at [500 South Meadow Street and open daily from 6 a.m. to midnight](https://www.wegmans.com/stores/ithaca-ny). Kyle Roth, a Human Ecology sophomore writing for the Sun's Dining Department column, describes the ride: [take Route 32 to the East Seneca Street stop on the Commons, transfer to Route 15, and get off at Wegmans](https://cornellsun.com/2023/05/03/culinary-autonomy-cooking-for-yourself). The return Route 15 usually arrives about an hour later. Check [TCAT's site](https://tcatbus.com) for the current Route 15 timetable before you go.

The catch is frequency. The 2022 Sun article says [Route 15 runs once an hour on weekdays and takes about half an hour to Wegmans](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/). Alexandra Michael '23 told the Sun that [a roommate and she went three weeks without grocery shopping](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/), mainly because they had no car and TCAT felt unpredictable. Schedules change, so check the timetable before you plan around the hour.

**Fares.** [New students ride TCAT free for their first year with a Cornell ID](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting). After that, Cornell says students ride free after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends, and can buy discounted passes at other times. The postdoc guide puts the [normal fare at $1.50](https://postdocs.cornell.edu/postdoc-life/essentials-in-ithaca/).

Our advice: batch your shopping into one trip a week or fortnight and bring a bag you can carry on a bus. Free rides apply [after 6 p.m. on weekdays and on weekends](https://sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting), but the Sun says Route 15 runs weekdays only, so confirm on TCAT's timetable that Route 15 runs on the day and time you plan to go.

## Aldi and Tops

The Sun article counts [two Aldi stores and one Tops in Ithaca](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/). The postdoc guide places [Wegmans, GreenStar, Tops and Aldi along Route 13/Meadow Street](https://postdocs.cornell.edu/postdoc-life/essentials-in-ithaca/). Aldi is a good fit if a friend with a car is going, which the Sun reports is how [many students without cars manage](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/).

The same article calls Tops the [nearest grocery store to campus by bus](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/), served by Route 30, and says that, as of its November 2022 reporting, service on that route had been cut by staffing problems.

## Price Chopper (P&C Fresh)

P&C Fresh's own site lists its Ithaca store at [315 Pine Tree Road, open 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.](https://www.pandcfresh.com/contact-us/), with holiday hours set by the store.

## GreenStar in Collegetown and downtown

[GreenStar has locations at 307 College Avenue (open daily 8 a.m. to 11 p.m.), 770 Cascadilla Street and 215 N. Cayuga Street](https://www.greenstar.coop/). In 2022, Katie Go '22, who worked at Anabel's, told the Sun that without a car the [only supermarkets in Collegetown were GreenStar or 7-Eleven](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/) and that they cost more than Wegmans or Walmart.

If you use SNAP, the Sun reported in August 2024 that [Double Up Food Bucks, which lets SNAP users get more produce for their money, was being brought to every Ithaca GreenStar branch](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/08/savings-forecasted-to-bloom-with-double-up-food-bucks-at-all-greenstar-locations). Cornell's Bronfenbrenner Center described the same [expansion to GreenStar's three Ithaca locations](https://arc.bctr.cornell.edu/2024/08/09/new-york-nutrition-incentive-program-expands-access-to-healthy-produce) as a way to increase SNAP benefits. Use GreenStar for the late-night gap, not the weekly shop.

## Free food if money is tight

- [Cornell Food Pantry](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/food-security), 109 McGraw Place: free, confidential food and personal care items for Cornell undergraduate and graduate students, plus staff and faculty.
- [Free Food Fridge](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food), Anabel Taylor Hall room 120: donated leftovers from Cornell events.
- Swipe Out Hunger: donated dining hall meal swipes for students, per [Cornell Health](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food).
- Cornell Health nutritionists can build a plan for affordable food; call [607-255-5155](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food).
- [Cayuga Health Community Cupboard](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food): free local farm-fresh food Monday to Friday at the Cayuga Health entrance at the Ithaca Mall.

## Common mistakes

- Planning around Anabel's hours without checking them. They are limited, and the store closes seasonally.
- Assuming the bus is free. It depends on your year and the time of day.
- Assuming Route 15 runs when you want to shop. The Sun says it is weekdays only.
- Doing the weekly shop at a Collegetown store. It is for emergencies, going by the 2022 Sun.
- Not asking about SNAP. Anabel's takes it on campus, and Cornell Health points students to [SNAP eligibility](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food).

## FAQ

**What is the cheapest grocery store in Ithaca?** The NYC Food Policy Center snapshot reports an average 18 percent saving at Anabel's in fall 2021, and Cornell Health suggests [trying different stores, buying store brands and using rewards programs](https://health.cornell.edu/services/nutrition-services/affordable-food).

**Is Ithaca expensive for groceries?** The Sun cited BestPlaces.net putting Ithaca's grocery prices [0.8 percent above the New York average and 4.6 percent above the national average](https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/30/lack-of-nearby-grocery-stores-leave-students-with-limited-options/) in 2022.
