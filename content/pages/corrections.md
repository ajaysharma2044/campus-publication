If something on a page is wrong, we want to know, and we will fix it quickly. A guide is only useful if the price, the date and the rule are right.

## How to report an error

Email [OWNER: fill in corrections email] with:

1. The page (its address or title).
2. The sentence or number you believe is wrong.
3. If you can, the official page that contradicts it. A link is enough.

Reports from students, parents, university staff, landlords and businesses are all welcome. You do not need to prove the point; we will open the sources and check.

## What happens next

- We check the claim against the official source within a few days. If a source has changed since we read it, that counts as an error on our side, and we fix the page.
- A confirmed error is corrected on the page, and the "Updated" date changes. A correction that changes a substantive fact (a price, a deadline, a rule) is also noted at the end of the article, with the date.
- We do not quietly change a fact and leave an old date on the page.
- If a reader's name or words appear on a page in error, we remove or correct them as soon as the report is confirmed.

## Disagreements about advice

Disagreements about advice ("I would have picked the other bus") are not corrections, but we still read them. Write to the editor through the [contact page](/contact/).

## Why there is no comment form

Keeping corrections to email means we can reply to you, ask a follow-up question, and keep no comment data on the site. Your email is used only to answer your report. See the [privacy page](/privacy/).
