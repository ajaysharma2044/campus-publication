EduExplained answers the practical questions that come with going to Cornell and living in Ithaca: where to live, how to get to campus from JFK with two suitcases, what a meal plan really costs, which bus to NYC is worth the money, what parents should book for move-in weekend.

Each guide starts from a real question people search for. We read the pages that currently answer it, then research the facts from official sources (the university, the city, transit agencies, airlines, bus companies, landlords) and the experience from students who have done it (student newspapers, public forums, listings and reviews). A guide is published only when it would serve a reader better than the best page already out there.

## What you can expect on every page

- The first paragraph answers the question. The details follow in the order you need them.
- Every hard fact (a price, a date, a fee, a deadline, a rule, a phone number, an address) links inline to the official page it came from. If we could not source a fact, it is not on the page.
- Student experience is paraphrased and attributed to where we read it, with a link. We never invent quotes, people, ratings or numbers.
- Our own advice is marked as advice and follows from the facts.
- An "Updated" date on every guide, and a Sources list at the end.

## How the site is funded

The site is funded by display advertising and by affiliate links on pages where a product or service genuinely helps the reader (renters insurance, bus tickets, storage, textbooks). Advertisers and affiliate programs never see an article before it runs and never change a fact, a comparison or a recommendation. Pages with affiliate links say so at the top.
