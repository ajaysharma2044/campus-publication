This page describes how a Footnote Daily guide is researched, written, checked and funded. It is the standard we hold every page to, and the one you can hold us to.

## Which questions we answer

We write about questions people actually search for: housing, getting to and around Ithaca, classes, money, daily life, careers, and what parents and alumni need. Before writing, we read the pages that currently rank for the question. We publish only when our page would serve the reader better than the best of them, by combining scattered official pages, adding real costs in one table, adding the student side of the story, or giving a clear recommendation by situation.

## Where facts come from

Hard facts, meaning prices, dates, fees, deadlines, rules, phone numbers and addresses, come from official sources: the university, the city and county, transit agencies, airlines, bus companies, landlords' own sites and other primary pages. Each one links inline to the page it came from, the first time it appears, and the page is listed under Sources with the date we read it.

Listings, booking pages, schedules, public course reviews and public salary or employer data count as data sources and are linked the same way.

## How we use student experience

What living in a dorm, riding a route or taking a course is actually like matters, so we read student newspapers, public forums, dorm tours and local news. We paraphrase, attribute and link that material ("several 2025 threads on a student forum say..."). It is experience, never the source of a hard fact. We never invent quotes, people, ratings or numbers, and we never copy more than a short phrase from any source.

## Our own advice

We give advice when it follows from the facts, and we say so ("If you need a single, apply in the first window"). We say which option we would pick for which situation. We do not pretend a judgment call is a rule.

## How a page is checked

Every page is reviewed by someone who did not write it. The reviewer opens every cited source and checks every hard fact against it, then compares the page against the best page currently ranking for the question. A page with a factual mismatch is fixed or withdrawn, not published. Code checks run on every page before it goes live: complete sourcing, title and description lengths, working internal links, live source URLs, and a test for copied passages.

## Dates and updates

Every guide shows the date it was last updated. Prices and dates carry their period and year. When a rule or price changes, we update the page and the date; a correction that changes a substantive fact is also noted on the [corrections page](/corrections/).

## Images

We use only photographs we have the right to publish: openly licensed photos (public domain, Creative Commons licenses that allow commercial use, Unsplash and Pexels licenses), photos with written permission, or our own. Every photo carries a caption with the photographer's credit and a link to the license. Charts, timelines and step diagrams are drawn by code from the facts in the article. We do not publish AI-generated images that look like photographs of real places.

## Advertising and affiliate links

Footnote Daily is funded by display advertising and by affiliate links. When a page links to a product or service through an affiliate program (renters insurance, apartment essentials, bus or train tickets, storage, moving, textbooks), the page says so at the top, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We add a link only where the product or service genuinely helps with the question on the page.

Advertisers and affiliate programs do not see articles before publication, do not choose topics, and do not change a fact, a comparison, an order or a recommendation. If we ever run sponsored content, it will be labeled "Sponsored" and kept separate from the guides.

## Independence

Footnote Daily is not affiliated with Cornell University, the City of Ithaca or any landlord, carrier or business it writes about. We hold no agreements that give any of them a say in what we publish.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or about a specific page, go to the editor through the [contact page](/contact/). Errors go to the [corrections page](/corrections/).
