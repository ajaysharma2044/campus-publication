## What we collect, and why

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, page requested, browser type, time) to serve pages and defend against abuse. These are kept for a short period by the provider and are not used to identify readers.

**Analytics.** When analytics is enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and understand which guides are read and on what kind of device. In regions where consent is required, analytics cookies are set only after you choose to allow them in the consent banner. We do not collect your name, email address or precise location through analytics, and Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

**Advertising.** When advertising is enabled, ads are served by an advertising network (Google AdSense, or a network such as Mediavine or Raptive). Ad networks and their partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure ads and, with your consent, to personalize them. Where consent is required, the banner lets you decline personalized advertising; you will still see ads, but not ones based on your browsing. Google explains its use of data on its [advertising privacy page](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Other networks' policies are linked from the consent banner when they are in use.

**Consent banner.** The consent banner is provided by a Google-certified consent management platform. It stores your choice in a cookie so it can honor it on later visits. You can change your choice at any time through the banner's own settings control, which stays available on every page.

**Affiliate links.** Some pages contain affiliate links, marked by a note at the top of the page. When you click one, the retailer or program may set a cookie to record that you came from our site so we can earn a commission. We do not receive your personal details from them.

**Email you send us.** If you email a correction or question, we keep the message as long as needed to deal with it, and we use your address only to reply.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not build reader profiles, run fingerprinting to recover blocked analytics, or infer sensitive characteristics. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and the site is not directed at them.

## Your choices and rights

- Decline non-essential cookies in the consent banner, or change your choice later through its settings control.
- Block or clear cookies in your browser; every page works without them.
- Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- If you are in the EU, UK, California or another region with privacy rights, you may ask what personal data we hold about you (in most cases, none beyond an email you sent us), ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Email [OWNER: fill in privacy email].
