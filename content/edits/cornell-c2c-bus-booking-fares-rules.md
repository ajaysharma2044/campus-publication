## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. Cross-block repetition of a minor tip. "If your bus is full, check other times" appeared three times (first-year block, step 3, FAQ). Proposed rule: give a minor tip one home outside the reader blocks. Only a cost-of-error warning (a forfeit or a fee) gets repeated per block.
2. Example paragraphs restate the paragraph before them. The missed-bus paragraph ended with the exact point the sophomore example then makes. Proposed note: when a worked example follows, the rule paragraph states the rule only, and the example carries the consequence.
3. Sourcing sentences that only point at a page ("Stop details come from the stop locations page"). Proposed note: put the link on the fact itself, or on a table caption, rather than in a standalone sentence about where the facts came from.
