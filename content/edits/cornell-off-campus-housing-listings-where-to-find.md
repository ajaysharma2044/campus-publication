## Left alone on purpose (what looked like a problem and why it is not)
- Two "Not confirmed:" lines (international, summer). They are separate gaps in separate reader blocks, and each reader reads only their own block. They are one line each.

## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. Old fee or price facts repeated in reader blocks read as current. Example: "sublets were free to post in 2019" sat in the summer block. Rule: a fact older than two years appears once, dated, in the detail section, not in a reader block.
2. A note under a mixed figure ("Those are our medians...") should say which rows it describes. This figure mixes Ithaca Renting medians with the Cornell double row.
3. "Fast fall market", "late searchers" and similar phrases should carry the page's own sold-out count. The page already had "14 of 67 by 2026-11-08", and the writer used it in the glance box but not in the mistakes list.
