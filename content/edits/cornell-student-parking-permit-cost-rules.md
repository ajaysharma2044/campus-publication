## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. Counted-voice hedges: writers stack the method into the sentence ("two-thirds to four-fifths of the arguments, by our two counts of 8 to 10 posters"). Rule: put the sample size with the source, the method in parentheses at the end.
2. Section openers that restate the heading ("Parking without a permit" -> "You can skip the annual permit and pay as you go."). Exemplar note: the first sentence under a heading carries the first fact.
3. A fact sentence that also appears in the intro gets repeated verbatim with its link in the matching H2 (families-page line). Rule: the H2 adds what the intro did not say.
