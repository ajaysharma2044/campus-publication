## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. A numbered list that repeats a `steps` figure. Here the figure and the list said the same five things. Rule: if a page has a steps figure, the prose under it adds only what the figure cannot hold, or nothing.
2. Our own arithmetic gets narrated ("by our sum"). The math stays, but the sentence should state the total and show how it adds up ("That costs $10.45: the $10 day rate plus one $0.45 fee"). Worth adding "by our sum / by our math" to the research-narration list.
3. Every reader block repeats the same list of times or prices. Rule: a reader block carries the one number that changes this reader's plan (Tuesday 11 a.m. for an accessibility reader), not the full schedule already in the glance box.
