## For the style guide
1. Reader blocks that end with a "The mistake that costs most is..." line: two of the four blocks ended this way, and each restated the deadline. A rule worth adding: a reader block ends on the action, not on a framed warning.
2. Section openers that restate the glance box ("Every college sets its own scores..."). The swap test catches these. An exemplar note could say that a section's first sentence carries its first number.
3. The same event details (time, room) appeared in full in both a reader block and a body section. Suggested rule: logistics live in one place, and the other mention gives only the date.
