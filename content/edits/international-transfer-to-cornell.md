## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. "Our advice:" and "Our sums" are first-person narration that the Part 7 list misses. Evidence: "Our advice: if there is any chance..." and "Our sums add those official prices". Suggest adding "our advice", "our sums" and "our take" to the narration list. Keep "by our math" with the arithmetic shown.
2. Step lists repeat their bold heading in the first sentence. Evidence: "**October 1, 2026 onward: start the CSS Profile.** File it as soon as possible after October 1". Suggest a rule: the body of a step adds what the heading cannot.
3. Writers frame a source as a sentence of its own ("The rules come from the X page and the Y page."). Suggest folding the source into the lead-in of the table or list it introduces.
