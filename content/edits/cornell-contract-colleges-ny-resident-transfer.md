## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. One rule, one body home. The AEM rule appeared four times (reader block, table, body paragraph, mistakes). Rule: outside reader blocks and the glance box, a rule is stated once in the body plus at most once in Common mistakes; the body copy is the one that carries the link.
2. Put the caveat on the number, not around the paragraph. "In the latest count ... It is the newest by-college count Cornell has published" bracketed a paragraph with the same caveat. Exemplar note: attach the dating caveat as an appositive on the date ("In fall 2021, the newest by-college count Cornell has published, ...").
3. Link the fact, not the publisher. "The Cornell Chronicle reported in 2024 that Cornell admits ..." makes the source the subject. Rule: the linked text is the claim; attribution goes in parentheses when the reader needs the date.
