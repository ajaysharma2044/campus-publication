## For the style guide (at most 3 patterns worth a rule or an exemplar note, with the evidence)
1. Colon openers that restate the sentence after them ("Your bus pass is free: Cornell gives ... free TCAT OmniRide passes"; "You can rent without a US Social Security number: ... you don't need an SSN"). Two of these turned up in six reader blocks. Worth an exemplar note: put the fact in the linked sentence and drop the label.
2. Common mistakes lines that end with an instruction already implied by the line ("Add the power bill before choosing."). The mistake's name plus the number is enough.
3. Back-references like "the same landlord" across neighborhood paragraphs that each name a different source. Name the landlord every time, because readers jump straight to their neighborhood.
