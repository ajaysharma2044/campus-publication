This tool builds your own dated list of the travel, parking, bus-pass and break-housing deadlines between August 2026 and December 2027. You tell it when you started at Cornell, whether you have a car, and which breaks you will spend in Ithaca; it keeps only the dates that apply to you, in order, marks the ones already passed, and names the next one. Where Cornell has not yet published a 2026-27 date that we could find (residence closing, finals charters, move-out), the row says "not yet published" and shows last year's date as a guide.

It is for fall 2026 first-years and transfers, January 2027 starters, returning students with a car on or off campus, and anyone staying in Ithaca over Thanksgiving, winter break or the summer, including international students who cannot fly home for short breaks.

## The inputs

- **When did you start at Cornell?** Fall 2026 as a first-year, fall 2026 as a transfer, January 2027 (spring start), or before fall 2026. This sets your free TCAT dates: fall entrants ride free from fall registration to Aug 31, 2027; spring entrants from Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2027; returning students only after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend.
- **Do you have a car?** No car; a car parked on campus while you live in university housing (resident permit); or a car while you live off campus (commuter B permit or a city street). This adds the permit sale, the city odd/even season, the campus winter rule, the refund deadline and permit expiry.
- **Staying over Thanksgiving, winter break or summer 2027?** Staying swaps the travel rows (peak days, last C2C bus) for the break-housing, mail and holiday rows.
- **Hide dates that have already passed.** The list compares each date with your device's date.

## Every number and how the tool uses it

There is no money math beyond two small steps. The Thanksgiving fare rise is the plain average of three routes: (27.9 + 34.2 + 37.9) / 3 = 33.3%. Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November, so the tool computes Thu Nov 26, 2026 and the Wednesday before it, Nov 25, the peak day OurBus names. The C2C gap is the day after the last "2026" departure to the day before the first "2027" departure: Dec 24 to Jan 5.

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- "Not yet published" means we found no 2026-27 date from Cornell as of Nov 8, 2026; the row shows the most recent precedent (2024, 2025 or 2026) instead. Check the academic calendar and housing notices before you book.
- The North Campus move-in year is inferred: the page prints weekdays that match 2026, not a year.
- The permit sale date and the Aug 16 to Aug 15 permit year come from a search summary of a page that returned an error to us.
- The Thanksgiving fare rise was measured on Binghamton, Rochester and DC routes from one aggregator on Nov 8, 2026, two to three weeks out. NYC was not sampled, so applying it to NYC is an assumption.
- Whether the campus winter rule applies every night from Dec 1 to Apr 1 or only when snow is forecast is not clear on Cornell's pages; the tool says "when snow is forecast."
- The transfer TCAT rule rests on a Cornell Sun reporter's statement; confirm with Transportation Services at 607-255-4600.
- Academic calendar dates come from the [Registrar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar): no classes Nov 25 to 29, 2026; fall finals end Dec 19; spring term begins Jan 25, 2027; no classes Mar 27 to Apr 4, 2027 (the tool puts the Friday peak day on Mar 26); spring finals May 15 to 22. The page lists no winter closure dates.
- Not in the brief: the 2026 winter residence closing time; spring 2027 move-in dates; the fall 2026 registration date; the 2027 move-out date; any fall 2026 Student Assembly charter; holiday TCAT service on Thanksgiving and in the closed week; Thanksgiving 2026 dining hours; the 2027-28 permit sale date; tow and impound costs; summer 2027 housing.
- The tool stores nothing and sends nothing; it runs in your browser.

## How to read the result

The line at the top says how many dates apply to you, how many are still ahead, and the next one. Each row gives the date in bold, then what happens and what to do. A "passed" tag means the date is behind you (your device's date). A "not yet published" tag means the date shown is last year's, or a rough window like "mid-January", because Cornell's 2026-27 date was not found. Rows with a date and no tag come from a 2026-27 page and can be planned around. Peak travel days are where fares rise and buses sell out, so book those first.
