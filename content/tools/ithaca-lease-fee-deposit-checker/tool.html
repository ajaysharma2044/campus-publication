Your result

If this box does not update, check by hand: deposit plus advance plus pet deposit must be no more than one month's rent (GOL 7-108). Application and cleaning fees are not allowed. A background check is at most $20, or $0 if you bring one from the past 30 days, and a late fee is allowed only after 5 days, at most $50 or 5% of rent (RPL 238-a). Key copies are at most 110% of cost (RPL 235-i). Your deposit or an itemized statement is due 14 days after you move out.
