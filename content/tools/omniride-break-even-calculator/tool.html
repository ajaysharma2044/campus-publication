Cornell's prices: OmniRide $200 a year, cash fare $1.50 a ride, refund until Mar 31 less $40. From Cornell's bus passes page, as of Nov 11, 2026.

A transfer who starts in January should pick "spring".
Count each boarding. Rides after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend are free, so leave them out.
30 weeks is a rough academic year. Add summer weeks if you stay in Ithaca. Spring entrants: count only weeks from Jan 1 to Aug 31 after your free year.

How it works: paid rides a week times weeks times $1.50 is your pay-as-you-go cost. If that is more than $200, buy OmniRide. The pass pays for itself at 134 paid rides (200 / 1.50 = 133.3). First-years, transfers in their first year, PhD students and graduate assistants already ride free.
