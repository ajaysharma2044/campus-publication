Your lease and your summer

Ithaca Renting leases run one year and start in June, July or August on fixed dates. If yours ends before you come back, you must move everything out.
Three is June, July and August. BRSS prices cover a 3 to 4 month term.

BRSS ships by FedEx at rates set at pickup, so ask BRSS or FedEx for a quote first. Leave blank to skip shipping.

How it works: BRSS storage is the sum of its per-box and per-item prices. A self-storage unit is its monthly price times the months away. Shipping is your quote, twice if you come back. If your lease runs all summer, your rent is owed either way, so leaving your things in the room costs nothing extra, and a sublet earns back part of the rent.
