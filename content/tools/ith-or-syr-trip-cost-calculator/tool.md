This calculator adds up the whole round trip from each airport: the airfare you found, plus what it costs to get there and back. Driving means the parking for the days you are away. The bus means two fares per person. A taxi means two of your quotes. It then tells you which airport is cheaper and by how much. When a price is a range (the OurBus fare to Syracuse), the totals and the difference are ranges too.

It is for the student who flies home and has to pick an airport for each break, the international student connecting through JFK or Dulles, the student with a car deciding where to leave it, the grad student on a work trip, and the parent buying the tickets.

## The inputs

- **Days away.** How long the car sits at the airport, counting each started 24 hours. Friday morning to Sunday night is 3.
- **People flying.** Airfares and bus fares are per person. Parking and taxis are per car.
- **Round-trip airfare from ITH and from SYR, per person.** You enter these; the calculator holds no airfares, because none are published in a form we can date. Use the same travel dates for both.
- **How you get to ITH.** Drive and park in the long-term or short-term lot, TCAT Route 32 or 72, a taxi (enter your quote, each way), or a drop-off.
- **My TCAT pass covers the ride.** Tick it if your pass covers the airport routes and you pay no fare; check your pass's hours first.
- **How you get to SYR.** Drive and park in the Red or Purple economy lot, the Blue Lot or the garage; OurBus from Ithaca; a car service (enter your quote, each way); or someone drives you.

## The numbers and the math

**ITH parking.** Long-term is $8 a day (5 to 24 hours) and $40 a week. Short-term is $9.50 a day and $48 a week. The calculator charges whole weeks at the weekly rate and the leftover days at the daily rate, but never more than one more weekly rate. So 7 days long-term is $40, 3 days is 3 x $8 = $24, 6 days is $40 (not 6 x $8 = $48), and 14 days is 2 x $40 = $80.

**SYR parking.** The daily maximum is $14 in the Red and Purple economy lots, $16 in the Blue Lot and $22 in the garage. SYR shows no weekly rate, so the calculator charges each day at the daily maximum: 7 days in economy is 7 x $14 = $98, in the garage 7 x $22 = $154.

**OurBus to SYR.** Fares run from $46.70 to $62.10 each way, so a round trip is 2 x $46.70 = $93.40 to 2 x $62.10 = $124.20 per person. The ride takes 1 h 25 min to 2 h 05 min. A Sunday sample on 2026-11-08 found $48 and $51 seats from the airport back to Ithaca.

**TCAT to ITH.** $1.50 each way, so $3 a round trip per person, or $0 on a pass that covers it. Route 32 runs weekdays and Route 72 weekends, between the airport and Ithaca Commons.

**Taxi or car service.** Your quote x 2. No current fare is published for either airport. An older, undated ITH page listed $12 base within 1 mile of the airport and a $15 minimum beyond, plus $1.50 a bag. One commercial transfer site puts a Syracuse airport to Cornell taxi at about $200; that figure is unverified, so the calculator does not use it.

**The total.** Airfare x people, plus ground cost. The difference is SYR total minus ITH total; with a range, the low end is SYR's lowest minus ITH's highest.

**Worked example.** One person, 7 days, no airfares entered. Driving: ITH long-term $40 against SYR economy $98, so Ithaca is $58 cheaper on parking alone. Your SYR flight has to be more than $58 cheaper than the ITH one to make Syracuse the better deal. By bus: TCAT $3 against OurBus $93.40 to $124.20, so Ithaca is $90.40 to $121.20 cheaper on the ride.

## Assumptions and what this does not cover

- The ITH parking page does not say how stays shorter than a week are charged. Using the lower of the daily or weekly total is our estimate, and the result says so.
- SYR may have a weekly rate that its page does not show; if it does, a long SYR stay costs less than this says.
- SYR's pre-booked parking adds a non-refundable $2.99 booking fee ([syrairport.org](https://www.syrairport.org/)); the calculator assumes you pay at the lot.
- The round trip uses the same way there and back. If you take OurBus out and get a ride home, halve the bus cost by hand.
- Not in the brief: airfares for either airport, gas and tolls for the drive to Syracuse, current taxi fares from Cornell to either airport, bag fees, and SYR shuttle or rideshare prices. The calculator leaves them out or asks you for them.
- TCAT pass coverage of Routes 32 and 72, and pass hours, vary by pass; tick the box only if yours covers the trip.

## How to read the result

The top line says which airport is cheaper and by how much. Below it, each airport shows its total, split into airfare and ground cost, and the ground time each way. A range means the OurBus fare could land anywhere from $46.70 to $62.10; "too close to call" means the answer flips inside that range.

Price is half the choice. ITH has 2 scheduled nonstops, so one cancellation can mean a long wait for the next flight; SYR lists 27 destinations but sits about 1 h 15 min away by car. If the totals are within a few dollars, the extra flights out of Syracuse or the 10-minute ride to Ithaca's airport should decide it, not the calculator.
