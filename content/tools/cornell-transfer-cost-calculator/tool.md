A new Cornell transfer must live on campus for one academic year, and that year costs $96,318 billed for a hall double with the Unlimited plan at endowed or non-New York tuition, before aid, at 2026-27 prices. This calculator adds up your version: tuition for your college and residency, your room, the meal plan your hall comes with, the health plan if you keep it, and Cornell's own estimates for books and personal costs. It shows the billed total, the full cost, the cost a month, and what Cornell's income lines for aid would mean for you.

```tool cornell-transfer-cost-calculator
```

## Who it is for

- **Sophomore and junior transfers** who must live on campus for the required year ([new transfer housing](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)) and want the number before they commit.
- **Parents** who will pay the July and January bills and want to know what goes on them and what does not.
- **Students on a tight budget** comparing a hall triple or a co-op room against a hall double, and checking the $125,000 and $75,000 aid lines.
- **New York residents** choosing between a contract college at $49,816 and an endowed college at $73,946.
- **International transfers**, who pay $73,946 at every college and must file the CSS Profile by March 15, 2027 to get any aid.

## The inputs

- **College you are applying to.** Endowed: Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Hotel Administration, AAP. Contract: CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks, Dyson. The college matters only together with residency.
- **Where you live.** A New York State resident at a contract college pays $49,816. Everyone else pays $73,946, including international students at a contract college.
- **Room.** The six residence hall room types at their yearly rate, or one of the eight co-ops by room size at its semester rate. Halls are assigned by random auto-allocation from your preferences, so the room type is a request.
- **Meal plan.** It follows the hall. Most halls take the Unlimited plan; the West Campus houses (Alice Cook, Carl Becker, Hans Bethe, William Keeton, Flora Rose) require the House Meal Plan; Cascadilla, Sheldon Court, Schuyler, 112 Edgemoor, 301 College Ave and 104 Mary Ann Wood Dr use the Collegetown Meal Plan ([meal plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)). Picking a co-op switches this to "no Cornell meal plan counted"; picking a hall switches it back to Unlimited.
- **Student Health Plan.** Keep it or waive it with comparable coverage. If you do not respond, Cornell enrolls you and bills you ([SHP costs](https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27)).
- **How many years.** One (the required year) or two, with the same room and 2026-27 prices both years. A sophomore transfer spends at least four semesters at Cornell.
- **Family income (optional).** Up to $75,000, $75,001 to $125,000, or over $125,000. It changes only the aid note under the result.

## The numbers and the math

**Billed by Cornell** = tuition + mandatory fees + housing for the year + meal plan for the year. Co-op rates are posted per semester, so a co-op year is the semester rate x 2.

**Full cost of attendance, Cornell's way** = billed + books $1,234 + personal $2,182. This is how Cornell builds its own budget, and it leaves out health insurance and travel ([cost of attendance](https://finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend)).

**Your total** = full cost of attendance + the health plan if you keep it. A fall entrant pays $1,705 for fall (coverage from Aug 1) and $2,046 for spring: $3,751 in year one. Year two is the returning-student premium of $4,092.

**Per month** = your total / 9, the months of one academic year (fall classes begin Aug 24, 2026 and spring finals end May 22, 2027, per the [academic calendar](https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar)). For two years, divide by 18.

**Two years** = every yearly line x 2, plus $3,751 + $4,092 = $7,843 for the health plan if kept.

Worked examples:

- **Default (non-New York, hall double, Unlimited, health plan kept):** 73,946 + 1,024 + 13,744 + 7,604 = $96,318 billed. Add 1,234 + 2,182 = $99,734, which matches Cornell's published budget. Add 1,705 + 2,046 = $103,485. Divided by 9: $11,498 a month.
- **New York resident at CALS, hall triple, Unlimited, health plan waived:** 49,816 + 1,024 + 12,644 + 7,604 = $71,088 billed; + 3,416 = $74,504; $8,278 a month.
- **New York resident at CALS, Redbud co-op double, health plan waived:** 49,816 + 1,024 + (3,487 x 2 = 6,974) = $57,814 billed; + 3,416 = $61,230, with food not counted.
- **Two years, default choices:** 96,318 x 2 = $192,636 billed; 99,734 x 2 = $199,468; + 7,843 = $207,311; $11,517 a month over 18 months.

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- **These are 2026-27 prices.** Anyone entering in fall 2027 pays 2027-28 prices, which Cornell confirms before its early April 2027 aid offers ([aid office](https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants)). Tuition rose 3.76% for 2026-27; that is history, not a forecast, so the tool does not project it.
- **Cornell's cost table carries a 2025-26 heading** but uses 2026-27 tuition, the 2026-27 double rate and the 2026-27 Unlimited price, so the tool treats its fees, books and personal figures as 2026-27.
- **You do not choose your hall.** Rooms come by random auto-allocation from your preferences, except Program Houses. Transfers are placed in upper-level areas (West Campus houses, South Campus, Program Houses, co-ops), and the housing pages describe a requirement, not a guarantee.
- **Co-op food is not counted.** Budget for it separately.
- **Year two uses the same room.** After the required year you may live off campus, which changes the housing and food lines.
- **Left out:** travel (Cornell lists it as "varies"), the $50 meal plan administrative fee, optional dental and vision plans, and health plan rates for a spouse, partner or children ($4,092 or $3,751 for one dependent; $8,184 or $7,502 for two or more children).
- **Aid lines are Cornell's statements, not a quote.** "Typical assets" is Cornell's phrase; your aid comes from the CSS Profile and FAFSA. International students' admission is need-aware, so ask the aid office whether the lines apply to you.
- Books use Cornell's $1,234 estimate.

## How to read the result

The bold line is your total for the year with everything Cornell charges or estimates. Under it, the table splits that total into three layers. **Billed by Cornell** is what appears on the Bursar bill: tuition billed in early July and January, housing in July or August and December, meals in July and January. **Full cost of attendance, Cornell's way** adds books and personal costs; it is built the way Cornell's aid office builds its budget ($99,734, or $75,604 at NY contract tuition, for a hall double), and for families under $75,000 Cornell says grants, scholarships and work-study cover that cost of attendance. **Your total** adds the health plan, which sits outside that budget: if your family plan covers you, waiving it saves $3,751 in year one.

The monthly figure spreads the total over the nine months you are in Ithaca. Compare it with rent plus food at home, not with a 12-month lease. When a co-op single has a price range, every line it touches shows the range, low to high.
