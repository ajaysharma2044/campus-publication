Enter your year, term, transfer, exemption and market to get the August your first lease can start and the dated steps up to it. The steps cover when to look and sign, co-op rounds, the Cornell housing application and exemption deadline, the Collegetown rush, the last cheap day to drop a backup Cornell room and the lease start.

The answer depends on four things. Are you still under Cornell's two-year rule? Did you transfer, and in which term? Do you have an exemption? Do you want Collegetown, where popular units go [8 to 16 months ahead](/datasets/collegetown-lease-signing-lead-times-2017-2025/), or the rest of Ithaca, which moves months later?

## Who it is for
- First-years planning sophomore housing (residence hall, co-op or Greek house) and their first lease after that.
- Sophomores deciding now, in fall 2026, on a 2027-28 Collegetown lease.
- Transfers, including spring transfers with their roughly 18-month wait.
- First- and second-years who qualify for an exemption.
- Graduate and professional students, who shop a later market.
- International students signing from abroad.
- Co-op and Greek-house residents.
- Parents, who can enter their child's details.

## The inputs
- **Undergraduate or graduate.** Graduate and professional students are not under the two-year rule. Their search runs March to April for an August start.
- **Your year at Cornell now (fall 2026).** "Not at Cornell yet" means you start next term (January or August 2027). For grads, the class-year choices do not change the result.
- **Start term.** Fall (August) or spring (January). It matters most for transfers and for when free first-year TCAT ends.
- **Transfer.** Entering as a sophomore means one year on campus, or for a spring transfer, the spring plus the next academic year. Entering as a junior or senior, or with two years in campus housing elsewhere, makes you eligible for an exemption, which you request every year.
- **Exemption.** None, "I think I qualify" (filed or not, not yet approved), or approved. Exemptions cover first- and second-years who are married, veterans (not ROTC), with dependents, 21 or older by the start of the year, qualifying transfers, or commuters living with a parent within 25 miles ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).
- **Where you want to live.** Collegetown, or elsewhere (downtown, Fall Creek, East Hill, Lansing).
- **Backup Cornell room.** Whether you plan to hold a Cornell room while you look. This adds the cancellation fee steps.
- **Coming from abroad.** Adds the visa timing line.

## The numbers and rules behind it
**When your first lease can start.** First- and second-year undergraduates live in Cornell or affiliated housing unless exempt. They "should not sign a lease unless they have an approved exemption" ([Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process)). One who signs anyway "will still be billed for the full year" ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)), which is $13,744 for a 2026-27 double ([rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). The tool works out the first August you are free to rent:
- Sophomore, junior or senior now, any grad student, or an underclass student with an exemption: Aug 2027.
- First-year now: Aug 2028.
- Sophomore transfer starting fall 2027 or spring 2027: Aug 2028. Spring transfers live on campus "for the incoming spring semester and the following academic year" ([transfer page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)), about 18 months.
- First-year starting fall 2027: Aug 2029.

Most 12-month leases run Aug 1 to July 31 ([209 Stewart Ave listing](https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/209-stewart-avenue-235095)).

**When to sign in Collegetown.** The record holds 9 dated signings from 6 Cornell Daily Sun stories, 2017 to 2025. Eight fell 8 to 16 months before an August move-in, mostly 10 to 11. The ninth was a fallback group that began hunting in February, 6 months out ([2018](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), [2019](https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/), [2021](https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/), [2022](https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/12/hurt-for-housing-begins-early-causing-stress-for-students/), [2022 opinion](https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/), [2025](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/12/the-ivy-to-establish-new-luxury-housing-in-collegetown-opens-fall-2026)). Months are counted by calendar month back from August. For a lease starting Aug 2027: 16 months back is April 2026, 11 is Sept 2026, 10 is Oct 2026 and 8 is Dec 2026. The tool compares that window with today. On Nov 8, 2026 an Aug 2027 lease is 9 months out: inside the window, near its late end. That day, 14 of 67 Ithaca Renting Collegetown rows were already marked sold out; 39 of the 67 are tagged 2027-28 ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)). A developer said in 2016 he usually rents most units by the end of November ([Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/)).

**When waiting is fine.** In 2019 Off-Campus Living's manager called "you must sign the day you arrive" a myth ([Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/)). For undergraduates looking downtown, in Fall Creek or in Lansing, the tool shows February to April of the move-in year. A 2018 group whose Greek plans fell through began hunting in February ([Sun](https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/)). For grads it shows March to April, with a few places left in June and July ([grad guide](https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html)). Hasbrouck takes rolling applications on 12-month contracts ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing)).

**The backup room.** A returning junior or senior who cancels a Cornell license pays $50 through Jan 31, $500 Feb 1 to Mar 31, $1,000 Apr 1 to June 30 and $2,000 July 1 to Aug 15. After Aug 15 the fee is 50% of the fall semester room rate ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). At the 2026-27 double of $6,872 a semester, that is 0.5 x 6,872 = $3,436. Study away, leave, internship or graduation cancellations are free until June 15 (fall) and Dec 1 (spring). Room selection for 2026-27 ran Oct 2 to 8, 2025 ([Sun](https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)).

**Co-ops and Greek houses.** Co-op selection starts in early November and early February, and each house runs its own process ([Cornell](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing)). A co-op double costs $3,487 to $4,981 a semester in 2026-27 ([rates](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates)). A sophomore in a chapter house must be a member when the housing application is filed and affirmed living in by Mar 15 in 2026. Every first- and second-year filed the 2026-27 housing application by Apr 6, 2026, exemption requests included ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

**Transfers and international students.** In 2018 transfers learned on June 22 whether they had campus housing ([Sun](https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus)). New degree students get unlimited TCAT rides in year one. The privilege ends Aug 31 of the following year for fall entrants and Dec 31 of the same year for spring entrants ([Cornell Transportation](https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes)). A visa appointment can be no more than 120 days before your program starts, and you need no SSN to rent ([International Services](https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students)).

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover
- The Collegetown window comes from one newspaper's dated stories. Reporters seek out early signers, so treat it as the pattern for popular units, not a measured median. No 2026 leasing-season story was found.
- Cornell's 2027 dates for the housing application, the Greek affirmation and room selection are not posted. The tool shows the 2026 or 2025 date with a "last year's date" badge. For a backup room in 2028-29 or later, it assumes the cancellation schedule shared by 2026-27 and 2027-28 carries on.
- The tool does not compute the City's 120-day renewal notice date. When the count starts is unresolved; see the 120-day rule guide.
- Not in the brief: how the two-year rule counts a first-year (not a transfer) who starts in January; when new transfers file an exemption; how Housing handles an exempt sophomore's fall request for the next year. The tool says so and points you to housing@cornell.edu.
- Not in the brief: spring-start off-campus leasing beyond "mostly sublets and semester leases"; a dated grad market for Collegetown buildings; Greek house costs.
- Junior or senior transfers starting in spring: the residential policy exempts you, but the transfer page says spring transfers live on campus for the spring and the next year. The tool shows Aug 2027 and asks you to confirm.
- Nothing you enter is stored or sent. The tool reads your device's date to mark steps that have passed.

## How to read the result
The first lines give your answer: the August your first lease can start, and when to look and sign for it. In Collegetown that means a month range and how far today sits from it. Elsewhere it means the spring window. Lines about your situation follow: the full-year billing trap, the exemption order (approval first, lease second), the spring transfer wait or the backup-room cutoff. Below that is a dated list in calendar order. "Passed" marks a step that is already behind you. "Last year's date" means Cornell has not posted the new one. If the list says you are past the Collegetown window, the wider market (downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing, leftover units) is your plan. It is not a failure.
