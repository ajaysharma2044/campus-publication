This calculator gives one person's yearly cost to live in a named Ithaca building. Pick the building and the layout, then say how many people share the unit. It returns your share of the rent and electricity for the months you pay, adds a year of bus fares and parking, and sets the rent and electricity against Cornell's 2026-27 residence hall double, $13,744. A table under the result shows the same unit split among more or fewer people.

```tool ithaca-rent-commute-calculator
```

## Who it is for

Sophomores pricing a junior-year lease, and parents checking that number against the dorm bill. Juniors and seniors comparing a renewal with a building they have not lived in. Grad students weighing Collegetown Center, downtown and Lansing: with a free OmniRide pass, a cheaper place farther out adds nothing to the commute. Students on a tight budget looking for the share that beats $1,145.33 a month. First-year transfers, who ride TCAT free, and anyone with a car who has to count overnight parking.

## The inputs

- **Building.** Two "typical bed" choices use the medians of Ithaca Renting's 38 Collegetown and 20 downtown listings. Fifteen Ithaca Renting buildings follow with every priced row they list. Then come six buildings or areas from other landlords: Dryden South, The Ivy, 114 Stewart Ave., 117 Thurston Ave., 174 Kendall Ave. and Lansing West, plus typical Fall Creek prices.
- **Unit layout.** The rows that building lists, with the price range. Ithaca Renting and Dryden South price per bed. The Ivy 3BR, 117 Thurston, 174 Kendall, Lansing West and Fall Creek price the whole unit.
- **People sharing the unit.** Resets to the bedroom count when you change the layout. Change it to see two people in a 1BR, or a 4BR shared by three.
- **Rent to use.** The listed price, or a per-bed or whole-unit price you were quoted.
- **Months of rent.** Set from the listing (11 for 174 Kendall Ave., 12 for the rest). Pick 10 if you will sublet two summer months.
- **Electricity included.** Set from the listing: on for Whiton Hall, the Colonial Building, Commons Studios and the 210 Dryden Rd. rooms, off elsewhere.
- **Bus status, paid rides a week, weeks you ride.** First-year students ride free. PhD students and graduate assistants get OmniRide free. Everyone else pays for weekday rides before 6 p.m. The 30-week default is our assumption.
- **Car and parking.** None, a space at your building (price not counted), a City residential permit or the Dryden Road Garage monthly permit, plus an optional Cornell B commuter permit.

## The numbers and the math

- **Unit rent.** Per-bed rows: the midpoint of the listed range times the bedrooms. Whole-unit rows: the listed price. "Typical" rows use the median, or the mean where a size has only two listings.
- **Your rent** = unit rent / people.
- **Electricity**, if not included: household bill / people. The range is $104.20 to $282 a month; totals use $282, the upper bound.
- **Rent and electricity for the year** = (your rent + electricity) x months.
- **Against the double** = that yearly total - $13,744.
- **Break-even rent** = $13,744 / months: $1,145.33 over 12, $1,249.45 over 11, $1,374.40 over 10.
- **Bus a year.** Zero if you ride free. Otherwise the smaller of (rides a week x weeks x $1.50) and the $200 OmniRide pass, which pays off at 134 rides (200 / 1.50 = 133.3).
- **Car a year.** City permit $45, or the garage at $232 x months, plus $359.77 for a B permit.
- **Your year** = rent and electricity for the year + bus + car.

A worked example: Collegetown Plaza's premium 4BR with 1.5 baths lists at $1,250 to $1,350 a bed for 2027-28. The midpoint is $1,300, so the unit is $5,200 a month. Four people pay $1,300 each. Electricity adds $282 / 4 = $70.50. Over 12 months that is $1,370.50 x 12 = $16,446, which is $2,702 more than the double.

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- All rents are asking prices, not what tenants paid. Ithaca Renting prints a range for each layout; the tool uses the midpoint and shows the range.
- Downtown rows are mostly 2026-27 leftovers; Collegetown rows are 2027-28. The Ivy figures are for leases that began Aug. 17, 2026.
- Lansing West prices units dynamically. The figures are the lowest base rents shown in November 2026.
- Electricity is a household average split evenly. EnergySage's figure comes from bills its users share, so $282 is treated as an upper bound.
- The double covers the academic year. A 12-month lease covers the summer too, and the tool counts no sublet income.
- Heat is included at every Ithaca Renting building and Dryden South suite. At 174 Kendall Ave. tenants pay heat, which the tool does not add.
- Internet, renters insurance, trash fees, furniture and deposits are left out. [What a month off campus costs](/cornell/housing/what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell/) covers them.
- Landlords set how many people a unit may hold. Ask before you count an extra person.

## How to read the result

The bold line is your year and your month, all in. Under it, the rent line shows the bed price, the unit total and your share, with a link to the listing. **Against the double** says how far your rent and electricity sit above or below $13,744. **Break-even** shows the monthly rent that matches the double for your lease length. The bus and car lines follow, then warnings for that building.

The table at the bottom keeps the unit and changes the head count. Read it to see what one more roommate saves you, or what losing one costs the rest of the group. Two examples show the swing.

Two people splitting a standard one-bedroom at Collegetown Court, listed at $1,625 to $1,725 a month ([Ithaca Renting](https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/)), pay $1,675 for the unit at the midpoint. Alone, rent plus $282 of electricity comes to $23,484 a year. Split two ways, each pays $837.50 in rent and $141 in electricity a month, $11,742 a year, which is $2,002 under the double.

In the other direction, take the Collegetown Plaza premium 4BR from the worked example above, $16,446 a year for each of four people. If one leaves and three cover the unit, each pays $1,733.33 in rent and $94 in electricity a month, and the year rises to $21,928, or $5,482 more each. On a joint lease the rest of the group owes the missing share: Cornell says most local leases bind housemates "jointly and severally," which means each person is responsible for the whole rent and for damages for the full term ([Cornell Off-Campus Living](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/tenant-best-practices/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire)). Set the head count to three to see this for your own unit; [Off-campus roommates and joint liability](/cornell/housing/cornell-off-campus-roommates-joint-liability/) covers what that clause means and how to protect yourself.

The date in the small print is when the prices were read. Asking prices change until a lease is signed, so check the listing before you sign.
