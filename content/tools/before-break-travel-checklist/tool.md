This checklist turns your situation into a dated to-do list for Thanksgiving 2026 or winter break 2026-27. Tell it the break, whether you are leaving or staying, how you travel, where your car sits and whether you are storing things, and it lists what to do now, before you leave, on travel day and on the way back, with the next deadline and how many days away it is. It also prices leaving a car at the Ithaca or Syracuse airport for the days you are away.

It is for first-years booking their first trip home, students with a car on campus or on a city street, off-campus renters, students who fly home, international students and anyone staying in Ithaca for the break.

## The inputs

- **Which break**: Thanksgiving (Thursday Nov 26, 2026) or winter break 2026-27. Winter adds the C2C two-link split, campus mail ending and residence closing.
- **Leaving or staying**: staying swaps the travel items for break housing, food and errands.
- **How you travel**: the Cornell Campus-to-Campus (C2C) bus to NYC, a commercial bus (OurBus, FlixBus, Greyhound and others), a plane, or your own car.
- **If flying: from which airport**: ITH, SYR or a NYC airport. Used only when you pick plane.
- **Where you live**: university housing or off campus. Housing decides the mail, closing and break housing items.
- **Your car in Ithaca**: none, in a campus lot with your permit, or on a city street.
- **Days away**: 1 to 40, used only for the airport parking estimate.
- **Storing or shipping things**: adds the storage item.

## Every number and how the tool uses it

C2C: since Nov 4, 2026 every rider needs a new account. Signing in with Cornell single sign-on gives the $93.50 NetID fare; a plain account pays $101 one way. Departures through Dec 23, 2026 book on the "2026 Travel Reservations" link, and departures from Jan 6, 2027 on the "2027" link. Cancelling at least 8 hours ahead costs $10 a seat and inside 8 hours $30. Riders board 10 minutes early with matching photo ID and carry at most 2 suitcases below deck plus 2 small carry-ons. A rider with a car can email for a B Lot fee exemption (rule effective Oct 10, 2024) or buy temporary B Lot parking for up to 14 days.

Commercial buses: OurBus names the Wednesday before Thanksgiving (Nov 25, 2026) as a peak day. In a sample read on Nov 8, 2026, Thanksgiving-week lowest fares ran 27.9% to 37.9% above earlier November days on three routes, a mean of 33.3%. Wanderu showed Ithaca-NYC averaging about $41 in January against about $66 in November.

Airport parking, the one formula: ITH long-term = (whole weeks x $40) + the lesser of (leftover days x $8) and $40. SYR economy = days x $14 daily maximum. Example for 5 days: ITH 0 x 40 + min(5 x 8, 40) = $40; SYR 5 x 14 = $70. For 14 days: ITH 2 x 40 = $80; SYR 14 x 14 = $196.

Housing and mail: winter break housing closes applications Dec 1, 2026 and costs $125 per one-week option or $500 for the full break. Campus mail and packages stop Dec 23 and resume Jan 7, 2027. In 2025, undergraduate residences closed at 2 p.m. Sunday Dec 21.

## Assumptions and what it does not cover

- The ITH parking page does not say how stays under a week are charged; the tool repeats the $8 daily rate and caps a partial week at the $40 weekly rate. SYR may have a weekly rate its page does not show, so 7 or more days at SYR may cost less than the tool says.
- The Thanksgiving fare rise comes from Binghamton, Rochester and DC, read 2 to 3 weeks out. NYC was not sampled, and fares can rise further as the dates near.
- "Days from today" uses your device's date. The checklist itself is fixed to facts read on 2026-11-08.
- Not in the brief: Cornell's 2026-27 academic calendar, so the last day of December finals and the 2026 residence closing date; the 2026 Thanksgiving dining hours; TCAT holiday service; whether late winter housing applications are taken; current campus-to-ITH taxi fares; airline bag fees; Big Red Shipping and Storage prices; an off-street place to leave a car all break other than your own permit area; tow and impound costs; visa and travel-signature timing for international students.
- No free Student Assembly finals charter or Cornell Thanksgiving airport shuttle was found for 2026 as of Nov 8.

## How to read the result

The first line counts your items and names the next date that applies to you, with how many days away it is. Then come up to four groups. "Do now" is anything that sells out or has a deadline: accounts, peak-day tickets, housing applications, storage slots. "Before you leave" covers the car, cancellations, mail and residence closing. "Travel day" covers boarding and bags. "Coming back" is the storm plan. If you are staying, the groups cover the break instead. Each item links to the page its fact came from, so check the official page before you pay.
