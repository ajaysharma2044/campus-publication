The rooms

Measure each bedroom: length x width in feet. Count a private bathroom's floor as part of that bedroom. The example sizes are a setup to replace.

How it works: shared space (the apartment minus the bedrooms) is split evenly. Each bedroom's own floor is paid by the person in it. Then each perk moves money: the people who have it pay more, the people who don't pay less, and the total stays the same.
