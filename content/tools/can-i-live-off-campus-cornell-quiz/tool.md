This quiz tells you whether Cornell lets you sign an off-campus lease for a given academic year, and what to file first. You answer for the year the lease covers: your class year, whether you are a transfer, your age on the first day of that year, marriage, dependents, military service, whether you live with a parent near Ithaca, and any Greek or co-op plans. It gives one of four answers: free to rent; not yet, because you qualify for an exemption that must be approved first (it names which); no, because that year belongs in Cornell or affiliated housing; or probably, because you will not be full-time in Ithaca. For the "not yet" and "no" answers it also prices the mistake of signing early: Cornell bills the full year anyway, and the lease is still owed. The rules are Cornell's 2026-27 policy, read on 2026-11-08.

## Who it is for

- **First-years** who want to know what years two and three allow.
- **Sophomores** weighing a lease against the two-year rule, and those headed for a chapter house or a co-op.
- **First- and second-years who may qualify for an exemption**: 21 or older, married, with dependents, veterans, local commuters.
- **Transfers**, whose answer depends on the year they enter and how long they lived on campus before.
- **Students with a spouse or children**, and **parents** checking before they pay a deposit.

## The inputs

| Input | What it means |
|---|---|
| Your year when the lease starts | First-year, sophomore, junior, senior or later, or graduate and professional. |
| Full-time and based in Ithaca | The requirement covers full-time, Ithaca-based students. Untick it if that year you will not be one. |
| Transfer student | No; starting the fall the lease starts; starting the spring before; or transferred in an earlier year. |
| Years on campus at your previous school | Two years qualifies a transfer for an exemption. |
| Age on the first day of that academic year | 21 or older qualifies; so does 16 or younger while living with a custodial parent or guardian all year. |
| Military service | Veterans discharged under conditions other than dishonorable qualify, including service in a home country's military. ROTC does not. |
| Living with a parent or guardian | A permanent home with a custodial parent or guardian within 25 miles of campus for at least six months is the commuter exemption. |
| Greek or co-op plans | A chapter house (member now or not yet), one of the eight Cornell co-ops, or another approved affiliated house. |
| Signed a Cornell housing license | Changes what you can still request or cancel. |
| Married; legal dependents | Each is its own exemption. |
| Rent per person and lease length | Used only to price signing before you are allowed. Default $1,246.25 a month for 12 months. |

## The rules and numbers

The two-year requirement covers full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students. Year one is a residence hall. Year two is a residence hall or approved affiliated housing: a house owned or controlled by a registered or recognized student organization, a recognized fraternity or sorority house, or one of the eight university-owned co-ops ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)). All new transfers live on campus for one academic year; spring transfers live on campus for the incoming spring and the following academic year ([transfer page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates)).

The exemptions: married; coming to Ithaca with legal dependents; veteran (not ROTC); 21 or older by the start of the academic year, or 16 or younger and living with a custodial parent or guardian all year; a transfer with two years of on-campus living at the previous school or matriculating as a junior or senior; a commuter living at least six months at a permanent home with a custodial parent or guardian within 25 miles. You request one in the Housing application while the exemption term is open (spring and fall), not after a housing license is signed, with documents if asked. It lasts one academic year. For 2026-27 every first- and second-year had to complete the Housing application by April 6, 2026, chapter-house residents included; Greek residents affirmed by March 15, 2026, and a sophomore must be a chapter member when the application is filed ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).

First- and second-year undergraduates "should not sign a lease unless they have an approved exemption" ([search process](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/living-off-campus/housing-search-process)), and those who neither meet the requirement nor get an exemption "will still be billed for the full year" ([requirements page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)).

The formula for the cost of signing anyway: Cornell's room rate for the year + (rent per person x lease months). With the defaults: $13,744 + (12 x $1,246.25) = $13,744 + $14,955 = $28,699.

## Assumptions and what it does not cover

- It applies Cornell's 2026-27 policy as read on 2026-11-08. The 2027-28 policy may move dates; check the Housing portal for that year.
- The "billed for the full year" figure uses the double rate. Your bill would be the rate of the room Cornell assigns, from $12,644 to $16,595 on the 2026-27 table.
- Housing decides every exemption. A match in this quiz is not an approval.
- Cornell's transfer page says all new transfers live on campus for one academic year, while the residential policy exempts transfers entering as juniors or seniors or with two years on campus elsewhere. The quiz follows the policy and tells transfers to get approval in writing.
- First- and second-years with a signed Cornell license cannot cancel it unless no longer attending, exempt, or accepted to a Greek house, affiliated house or co-op before the Spring Housing Selection deadline ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).
- Not in the brief: the 2027-28 application deadline; whether an unmarried partnership counts as married; how Housing handles a fall request for the next year, which matters to an exempt sophomore facing the fall Collegetown rush; when new transfers file; the 2026-27 meal plan price; what Greek houses cost.

## How to read the result

- **Yes** means the requirement does not reach you that year. Nothing to file. If you hold a Cornell license, the fee schedule shows what cancelling costs.
- **Not yet** means you match at least one exemption. Request it in the Housing application, wait for approval, then sign. Do it again next year.
- **No** means that year is a residence hall (year one) or a hall, co-op or approved Greek or affiliated house (year two). The cost line shows what signing anyway would cost: Cornell's full-year bill plus every month of the lease.
- **Probably** means you said you will not be full-time in Ithaca, which the requirement is written around. Confirm with Housing Operations (1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, housing@cornell.edu, per the [Housing directory](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211065419/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory)) before you sign.
