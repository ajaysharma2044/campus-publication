This checklist turns your move-in into a dated to-do list. Tell it whether the place is in the City of Ithaca, your lease start date, which bills you pay, how many people split them, whether you have a car and where it sleeps, your Cornell status, and three yes-or-no facts (someone living there who is not on the lease, arriving from abroad, a new building). It sorts the steps into before move-in day, move-in day, the first 30 days and later dates, computes the deadline to name an extra occupant and a prorated Cornell parking permit price, and gives a per-person range for the bills you add on top of rent.

## Who it is for

Juniors and seniors signing a new lease, sophomores moving into their first apartment next August, transfers and new grad students arriving for a fall start, international students setting up from abroad, students moving in with a partner or children, students with a car, and parents who are helping on move-in day.

## The inputs

- **Where the place is.** The City of Ithaca (Collegetown, downtown, Fall Creek, Belle Sherman) has its own trash tags, residential parking permits, winter street rules and Certificate of Compliance. Outside the City (Town of Ithaca, Lansing, Cayuga Heights, Varna), none of those apply, and the tool says what to ask the landlord instead.
- **Move-in date.** The day your lease starts. Defaults to August 1, 2027, the usual Ithaca start. It dates the occupant deadline, the permit proration and whether winter parking rules are already on.
- **Electricity, internet, trash.** Whether each is in the rent or on you.
- **People splitting the bills.** Used only to divide the household bill ranges.
- **Car.** None, a landlord's lot, a City street or a City garage.
- **Your Cornell status.** New degree student (first-year, transfer or new grad), PhD student or graduate assistant, or other continuing student. It sets what you pay for the bus and, for assistants, which parking permits you can buy.
- **Someone not on the lease, arriving from abroad, new building, a helper driving in.** Each adds its own steps.

## The numbers and formulas

**Occupant deadline** = move-in date + 30 days (RPL 235-f also allows 30 days from when the landlord asks).

**Prorated Cornell B permit** = $359.77 x days left to August 15 / 365. Permits run August 16 to August 15 and prorate daily. If 31 or fewer days are left, the tool tells you to buy next year's permit when sales open in early August instead (Tuesday, August 4, 2026 at 9 a.m. for 2026-27). This proration is our arithmetic on Cornell's stated rule, not a Cornell quote.

**Bills on top of rent, per person per month:**
- low = $5 insurance + ($104.20 electricity + $30 internet) / people
- high = $27 insurance + ($280 electricity + $120 internet) / people

Electricity and internet drop out when the rent includes them. For four people paying both, that is $5 + 134.20 / 4 = $38.55 low and $27 + 400 / 4 = $127 high a month. Trash is shown separately at $6 a bag, because the number of bags varies.

## Assumptions and what it does not cover

- The bill range is an estimate. Every electricity, internet and insurance figure comes from a commercial site, not from real Ithaca student bills. EnergySage's $280 is a self-selected household average that probably includes electric heat, so treat it as a ceiling. NYSEG raised temporary rates on June 1, 2026, and a larger permanent increase is still pending ([FingerLakes1](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/05/15/nyseg-and-rge-win-temporary-rate-hikes-as-larger-increases-still-loom/)).
- The trash tag price is the 2026 price. The City sets it each January 1, so a 2027 move-in may pay a different price.
- The tool checks your choices against the rules. It does not read your lease, which can shift snow removal, trash or parking duties onto you.
- Not in the brief: which internet providers serve your address; how far ahead NYSEG needs a start request; the 2027-28 Cornell permit sales date; which City blocks are residential permit zones; trash, parking and code rules in the Town of Ithaca, Lansing and Cayuga Heights; whether Ithaca landlords require proof of renters insurance; water and sewer billing.

## How to read the result

The bold line counts your steps and how many have a date, then gives what the bills on top of rent cost each person a month as a low-to-high range. Under it, the steps run in order: before move-in day, move-in day, the first 30 days, and later dates. Do the inspection and the insurance before you get the keys; the inspection record and your photos are what protect your deposit at move-out. A permit price marked as our estimate is prorated by day from Cornell's annual price; the cashier's figure is the one that counts. Change any input and the list rebuilds.
