This tool turns the fall 2027 transfer cycle into your own list of dated steps, from today to the end of your first Cornell semester in December 2027. You pick your situation (sophomore or junior, four-year or community college, or Transfer Option) and the Cornell college you are applying to, and say whether you are international, a New York resident, or already lived on campus for two years elsewhere. It returns the steps that apply to you in order, with the course list and GPA signal for your college, the aid forms and codes for your status, the housing or lease steps, the July bill at 2026-27 prices, the health plan waiver, move-in and the exam credit request. It counts the days to your next fixed deadline from your device's date. Every row whose 2027 date Cornell has not posted shows last cycle's date and a "2026 pattern" tag (or 2024 or 2025).

It is for anyone applying to transfer into Cornell for fall 2027: sophomores and juniors from four-year schools, community college students, Transfer Option holders, Engineering and computer science applicants, international students, New York residents aiming at a contract college, and the parents who pay the bill and drive to move-in.

## The inputs

- **Your situation.** Sophomore entry from a four-year college (one year done); junior entry from a four-year college (two years done); community college, sophomore entry; community college, junior entry (an associate's or four semesters); or Transfer Option holder. Junior entry exempts you from the on-campus year and swaps the housing rows for a lease row; it also adds the junior course lists and eligibility limits. Transfer Option swaps in its own application, deadline pattern and decision timing.
- **Cornell college.** One of nine. It sets the course and GPA row, the portfolio note on March 15, the step right after admission, the June email, the credit cap and the exam credit thresholds. CALS, Human Ecology, ILR, Brooks and Dyson are contract colleges, which matters only with the New York box.
- **International.** Swaps the US aid row for the CSS-only rule (no aid ever if you miss March 15), adds tax withholding, translations and the F-1 or J-1 record transfer. It overrides the New York box, because international students pay non-New York tuition.
- **New York State resident.** Adds HESC to the aid row (code 0174 at a contract college, 0175 at an endowed one) and uses contract tuition in the July bill when your college is a contract college.
- **Lived on campus two years elsewhere.** Exempts you from the on-campus year, like junior entry.
- **Show steps for parents and family.** Adds the early May family orientation notice, Family Orientation day and the Cashnet note on the bill. On by default.

## Every number and how the tool uses it

The tool does little arithmetic. The countdown is the number of days from your device's date to March 15, 2027 or April 15, 2027, whichever is next. The July bill row picks contract tuition ($49,816) only when you are a New York resident, not international, and applying to a contract college; otherwise it shows $73,946. The billed year for a hall double with the Unlimited plan is tuition plus fees plus housing plus food at 2026-27 prices: $73,946 + $1,024 + $13,744 + $7,604 = $96,318, and $49,816 + $1,024 + $13,744 + $7,604 = $72,188 for a New York contract student. The co-op double range is twice the per-semester rate: Redbud $3,487 x 2 = $6,974 to 660 Stewart and Wari $4,981 x 2 = $9,962. The hall double per month is $13,744 / 9 months = $1,527. The year-one health plan for a fall entrant who does not waive is $1,705 + $2,046 = $3,751.

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- The cycle is fall 2027 entry, the one open on Nov 11, 2026. External transfer is fall only.
- A "2026 pattern" row shows the fall 2026 date because the 2027 date was not posted on Nov 11, 2026. The June college emails come from a page written for 2024, the July bill example is from 2024, and the room selection window is from 2025; those rows say so. Recheck each against Cornell's pages as summer 2027 nears.
- Prices are 2026-27. You will pay 2027-28 prices, which Cornell sets in late March and confirms before early April 2027 aid offers.
- The housing pages describe a requirement to live on campus and say transfers "may be placed" in upper-level halls; they do not promise a room.
- The countdown uses your device's date.
- The tool stores nothing and sends nothing; it runs in your browser.

## How to read the result

The top line says who the list is for, how many steps it holds, whether you have a required year in campus housing, how many days remain to your next fixed deadline, and how many rows use last cycle's dates. Each row gives the date or window in bold, then what to do. A row with no tag carries a date Cornell has published for this cycle, so you can plan around it now. A "2026 pattern" tag (or 2024 or 2025) means the date is last cycle's: expect the 2027 date near it, and check before you book travel or count on it. March 15 and April 15 are the two that end applications; the July bill, the health plan waiver and the exam credit request are the ones that cost money if missed.
