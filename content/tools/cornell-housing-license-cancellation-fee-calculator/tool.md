This calculator tells you what Cornell charges to cancel a room on the date you cancel. Cornell housing is a license, not a lease, and its cancellation fee climbs with the calendar: $50 through Jan 31, then $500, $1,000, $2,000, and after Aug 15 half the fall room rate. Pick your status, the license year, what you are cancelling, why, the date and your room. The tool returns the fee, the penalty-free deadline if your reason qualifies, or a plain "you cannot cancel".

```tool cornell-housing-license-cancellation-fee-calculator
```

## Who it is for

- **Sophomores holding a junior-year room as a backup** while they hunt for a Collegetown lease. On that license you count as a returning junior, and the backup costs $50 to drop only through Jan 31.
- **Juniors and seniors** deciding between a Cornell room and a lease for next year.
- **Students going away** for a semester or a year: study away, leave, internship or graduation cancels free by June 15 (fall) or Dec 1 (spring).
- **Parents** paying for the room who want the dollar figure before their child signs a lease.
- **Students moving into a Greek house, affiliated house or co-op**, where the rule depends on class year.

## The inputs

- **Your status in the year the license covers.** First-year, new transfer, second-year, or returning junior or senior. The dollar schedule is written for returning juniors and seniors. First-years, new transfers and second-years cannot cancel unless they are no longer attending, hold an exemption, or (second-years only) were accepted to a Greek house, affiliated house or one of the eight co-ops before the Spring Housing Selection deadline ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)).
- **License year.** 2026-27 (the year under way) or 2027-28 (rooms chosen in fall 2026). Both licenses carry the same fee schedule; only the dates move by a year.
- **What you are cancelling.** The whole year from fall, or spring only.
- **Why.** Moving off campus; a Greek, affiliated or co-op acceptance; study away; leave; internship; graduation; or an approved exemption (first- and second-years only).
- **Date you cancel.** Defaults to Nov 8, 2026.
- **Room type and fall room rate.** The rate matters only after Aug 15, when the fee is half of it. The menu fills in 2026-27 residence hall rates per semester; type your own for a 2027-28 rate.

## The numbers and the math

For a returning junior or senior cancelling the whole year to move off campus, the fee is a lookup by date. For the 2027-28 license: $50 through Jan 31, 2027; $500 from Feb 1 to Mar 31, 2027; $1,000 from Apr 1 to Jun 30, 2027; $2,000 from Jul 1 to Aug 15, 2027; after Aug 15, 2027, 50% of the fall semester room rate. For a 2026-27 double that last step is $6,872 x 0.5 = $3,436. For the 2026-27 license, subtract one year from every date.

Study away, leave, internship and graduation have their own deadlines: June 15 to cancel fall (and the year) and Dec 1 to cancel spring, both in the year the license starts. Before the deadline the fee is $0. After it, the request costs $500.

Moving off campus is not a reason Cornell accepts for releasing only the spring semester ([Sorority and Fraternity Life](https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/sorority-fraternity-life/join/financial-obligations-sororities-fraternities)).

The menu's per-semester rates other than the double are half the yearly rate, our arithmetic: single and townhouse double $15,566 / 2 = $7,783; super single $16,595 / 2 = $8,297.50; triple or one-room quad $12,644 / 2 = $6,322. The double's published semester rate, $6,872, is exactly half of $13,744.

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- 2027-28 room rates are posted in March 2027; until then the tool uses 2026-27 rates, and rates shown at Fall Housing Selection are projected and subject to Board of Trustees approval ([2027-28 license](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028)). Type the final rate when it is out.
- The "cannot cancel" answer for first-years, new transfers and second-years has a cost behind it: students who neither meet the residential requirement nor hold an exemption "will still be billed for the full year" ([requirements page](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing)), $13,744 for a 2026-27 double.
- Exemption requests are not accepted after a signed housing license is completed or during the license period; exemptions last one academic year ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).
- For first- and second-years, the license allows cancelling when "no longer attending". Whether study away or an internship counts is for Housing Operations to say: 1520 Clara Dickson Hall, 607-255-5368, housing@cornell.edu ([directory](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211065419/https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/contact-us/housing-and-residential-life-directory)).
- A returning junior or senior moving into a Greek house or co-op is treated as an ordinary cancellation on the dated schedule.
- A first- or second-year cancelling on a Greek, affiliated or co-op acceptance or on an approved exemption should ask Housing Operations what, if anything, the release costs before signing with the house.
- The 2027 Spring Housing Selection date is not yet announced. For 2026-27, students living in a sorority or fraternity house affirmed it in the Housing Application by March 15, 2026, and the Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores ran in April ([residential policy](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy)).
- Co-op residents sign a separate Cooperative Academic Year Housing License, binding for the whole term. It allows cancellation for withdrawal, leave, an academic internship, graduation or study in absentia, requested by written petition in the housing portal, with a per diem charge for days occupied; releases for other reasons are exceptional and may carry a cancellation fee ([co-op terms](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/co-op-housing-2026-2027-terms-and-conditions)). The dated schedule above is for the academic-year residence hall license, so ask Housing Operations what a co-op cancellation costs.
- Other license charges, such as $60 a day outside the occupancy period and the $500 revocation fee, are not cancellation fees and are left out.

## How to read the result

The bold line is the answer: a dollar fee, $0 with the days left before a penalty-free deadline, or "you cannot cancel". For a dated fee, the next line says when it rises and how many days you have at the current price, and the last line shows the whole schedule with your step in bold. If you are a sophomore keeping a junior-year room as a backup, the line to watch is Jan 31: after it, dropping the room costs at least $500. The note under the result gives the date of the numbers and what is estimated. Questions about your own license go to Housing Operations (housing@cornell.edu, 607-255-5368).
