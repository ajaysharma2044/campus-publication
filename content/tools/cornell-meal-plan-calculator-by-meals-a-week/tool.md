This calculator prices every Cornell Dining plan you are allowed to buy in 2026-27 against the number of dining-room meals you actually eat. You pick where you live and who you are, enter your meals a week, and choose which meals they are. It shows each plan's cost to you for a semester, what each swipe you use really costs, and how many meals a week the plan needs before it beats paying per meal. The cheapest plan for your numbers comes first.

```glance
- Off campus, 1 to 9 dining-room meals a week: Just Bucks costs least.
- At 10 to 12 meals a week, Bear Choice wins; at 13 or 14, Bear Traditional.
- At 15 or more meals a week, Unlimited is cheapest, down to $9.52 a swipe.
- Baseline for that ranking: 2026-27 prices, a mixed week of meals, 18 weeks a term, Big Red Bucks counted at $1.
```

## Who it is for

Sophomores, juniors and transfers on campus choosing between Unlimited and Bear Traditional. Collegetown-hall residents weighing the Collegetown Meal Plan. Off-campus undergraduates deciding whether any plan is worth it. Graduate and professional students, who may buy every plan plus a $50 graduate plan. First-years and West Campus House residents have no choice, so for them the tool shows what each swipe costs at their meal count. Parents can use it to check a bill.

## The inputs

- **Where you live and who you are.** This sets the plans you may buy, from Cornell Dining's undergraduate, graduate, rates and FAQ pages:
  - First-years: Unlimited only, which cannot be reduced or canceled.
  - Upper-level and transfer students in other on-campus housing: Unlimited or Bear Traditional. The FAQ names transfers and on-campus juniors; the rates page says upper-level students. Sophomores should confirm with Cornell Dining.
  - West Campus House System: House Meal Plan only.
  - 112 Edgemoor, Cascadilla Hall, Schuyler House, Sheldon Court, 301 College Ave. and 104 Mary Ann Wood Dr.: Unlimited or the Collegetown Meal Plan.
  - Off-campus undergraduates: every traditional plan, the three debit plans or no plan.
  - Graduate and professional students: the same, plus the Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan.
- **Dining-room meals a week.** Each entry to one of the ten residential dining rooms uses one swipe. The default is 10.
- **Which meals.** A mix of all three, lunch and dinner only, or mostly dinners. This sets the price of a meal without a swipe.
- **Weeks of meals.** Fall meals run from lunch Aug. 17 to brunch Dec. 20, 2026, and spring meals from lunch Jan. 19 to dinner May 28, 2027 (terms and conditions). Each is about 18 weeks, the default.
- **Big Red Bucks setting.** Ticked (the default), the Big Red Bucks and City Bucks in a plan count as $1 each, as if you would spend them on food anyway. Unticked, they count only toward dining-room meals beyond your swipes.
- **House Affiliate and SFL boxes.** These add the House Affiliate plan (off-campus students who lived in the West Campus House System for at least a semester) and the SFL Supplemental plan (members of recognized fraternity and sorority chapters; on campus only if the chapter requires a plan of at least 7 meals a week).

## The numbers and the math

Each plan's cost to you for a semester is: plan price + $25 (half the $50 yearly fee) − the Big Red Bucks and City Bucks it includes + any meals beyond its swipes at the Big Red Bucks price. With no plan, every meal is paid at the door price, tax included.

The price of one meal without a swipe uses a 20-meal week: breakfast, lunch and dinner Monday to Saturday, plus Sunday brunch and dinner. At Big Red Bucks prices that week costs 6 × ($10.80 + $15.25 + $18.85) + $16.85 + $18.85 = $305.10, or $15.26 a meal. At door prices it is 6 × ($11.90 + $16.80 + $20.80) + $18.55 + $20.80 = $336.35, or $16.82 a meal. Lunch and dinner only averages $17.16 in Big Red Bucks and $18.93 at the door. Mostly dinners uses $18.85 and $20.80.

Break-even is the plan's net cost divided by the price of a meal paid in Big Red Bucks. For Unlimited over 18 weeks: ($3,802 + $25 − $400) / 18 = $190.39 a week, and $190.39 / $15.26 = 12.48 meals a week.

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- The 20-meal week behind the mixed average is an assumed pattern, not a Cornell figure. Pick "lunch and dinner" or "mostly dinners" if that fits you better.
- Counting Big Red Bucks and City Bucks at $1 is a best case. City Bucks work only at partner businesses off campus and are taxed.
- Meals beyond your swipes are priced in Big Red Bucks. Any plan holder can add Big Red Bucks at get.cbord.com/cornell.
- Guest meals are not counted in any total. Eight Unlimited guest meals are worth up to $150.80 a semester at the $18.85 dinner price.
- Shabbat, holiday and special meals at 104West! can cost extra, and the tool does not price them.
- Weeks inside the term when you are away still cost the full plan price. Lower the weeks input to see what that does to your cost per swipe.
- Financial aid is not modeled. Cornell Dining says the Unlimited plan is fully covered by financial aid for eligible students; ask the financial aid office how a smaller plan changes your package.
- Deadlines are not modeled. Reduce or cancel by Sept. 9, 2026, for fall, and reduce by Feb. 3, 2027, for spring ([Cornell Dining deadlines](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms)). After a deadline you can still upgrade, but not reduce.

## How to read the result

The bold line names the cheapest plan you may buy and how much it saves against the next one. The table lists every plan open to you, cheapest first, with your semester cost, the cost of each swipe you use, and the break-even point. "Never" in the break-even column means that even at full use, the plan's swipes cost more than paying in Big Red Bucks for the meal mix you chose. When two plans sit within a few dollars, pick the one that fits the weeks you are least sure about: a weekly plan loses unused swipes every Wednesday night, while a debit plan's meals last the semester. Double each total for the year.

In the six Collegetown halls, the Collegetown Meal Plan beats Unlimited at 12 or fewer meals a week if you spend its City Bucks in full, and at 11 or fewer if you don't. Each meal a week above its 10 swipes adds $274.68 a semester at Big Red Bucks prices ([Cornell Dining plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).

SFL Supplemental's 127 meals a semester work out to about 7 a week, and for an off-campus member it is the cheapest choice from about 7 to 12 meals a week. House Affiliate is the best value for anyone who qualifies, as long as you eat its 3 weekly meals on West Campus; it stays cheapest from 3 to 12 meals a week ([Cornell Dining plans](https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans)).
