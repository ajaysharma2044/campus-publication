This tool works out the dates in a New York sublet request. You enter how many units are in your building, the day your subtenant moves in, and the day you mail the request. It gives you the last day to mail, the last day the landlord can ask you for more information, the day the landlord's answer is due, and the day silence turns into consent. It also lists the seven things the request must contain. If your building has three or fewer units, it tells you instead that your lease, not the law, decides whether you can sublet.

```tool ithaca-sublet-request-timeline
```

## Who it is for

- Juniors and seniors going abroad or away for a semester while housemates stay on a joint lease.
- Students leaving Ithaca for the summer who want someone in the room from June to July.
- Seniors whose lease runs to July 31, past May graduation, with two months to fill.
- The student taking the sublet, who can check that the tenant asked the landlord the right way.

## The inputs

- **Units in the building.** Count every residential unit at your address, not only your apartment. A Collegetown house split into three apartments is three; a 20-unit building is 20. The law's sublet right starts at four. Choose "Not sure" to see both answers.
- **The day your subtenant moves in.** The first day of the sublet term. The tool counts back from it to tell you when to mail.
- **The day you mail the request.** The day the certified letter goes out, or the day you plan to send it.
- **Did the landlord ask for more information?** The landlord gets 10 days to ask. If they did, enter the day you sent the answer: the 30-day clock then runs from that day.

## The numbers and the math

New York Real Property Law 226-b gives tenants in buildings with four or more residential units a right to sublet with the landlord's written consent, given in advance, which cannot be unreasonably withheld. The request goes by certified mail, return receipt requested. The landlord may ask for more information within 10 days. The landlord must answer within 30 days of the later of your request or the extra information. If the landlord does not answer, the silence counts as consent. In a building with three or fewer units, the lease controls.

The math is addition of calendar days:

- Last day for the landlord to ask for more: mailing date + 10.
- Answer due: the later of the mailing date and the day you sent the extra information, + 30.
- Consent by silence: the answer date + 1, the first day after the deadline has passed with no answer.
- Latest mailing date if the landlord asks nothing: move-in date − 31 (30 + 1).
- Safer mailing date: move-in date − 41 (10 + 30 + 1). This covers a landlord who asks a question on day 10 and an answer you send the same day.

A worked example with the tool's defaults. You mail on April 1, 2027, and the subtenant moves in June 1, 2027. The landlord can ask for more until April 11. The answer is due May 1, which is a Saturday. If none comes, silence is consent from May 2. To be safe you should have mailed by April 21 (June 1 − 41), and April 1 clears that. May 1 (June 1 − 31) is the last day that works with no questions.

What the request must contain, under the same law: the term of the sublet; the subtenant's name; the subtenant's addresses; your reason for subletting; your address during the sublet; the written consent of any co-tenant or guarantor; and a copy of the proposed sublease.

What else the law and the Cornell Law guide say:

- If the landlord refuses without a reasonable reason, you may sublet as requested, and you can recover costs and attorney fees if the landlord acted in bad faith ([RPL 226-B](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B)).
- Subletting without following these steps is a substantial breach of the lease. You stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage. A lease clause that waives these rights is void (same source).
- An assignment, handing the whole lease to someone else, is different. The landlord may refuse it without cause. After an assignment you are generally not liable for the new tenant's rent or damage ([RPL 226-B](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B); [Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).
- A sublet fee is allowed only if the lease allows it and the fee is reasonable. Background or credit check fees are allowed ([Cornell Law](https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease)).

## Assumptions and what the tool does not cover

- The tool counts calendar days from the day you mail. The statute counts the 10 days "after the mailing" of the request and the 30 days from the mailing of the request or of the extra information, whichever is later, so delivery time does not move the dates. Mail early anyway.
- Not in the brief: what happens when a deadline falls on a weekend or holiday. The tool flags weekend dates and moves nothing.
- Not in the brief: what counts as a reasonable refusal. The law says consent cannot be unreasonably withheld; it does not list reasons in our sources.
- Not in the brief: whether a landlord's request for information sent after day 10 restarts the clock. The tool restarts the 30 days from the day you sent the information, whenever the landlord asked.
- Not in the brief: typical sublet rents or discounts in Ithaca. Two asking prices were seen on Nov 8, 2026: Modern Living sublets "from $900 per person" and a Dryden South Fall 2026 sublet at $1,395 per person. The tool does not price your sublet.
- Not in the brief: any student account of sublet scams in Ithaca.
- The tool covers private leases only. Cornell housing licenses have their own cancellation deadlines, which this tool does not model.
- This is a date calculator, not legal advice. For your lease, call the hotline above.

## How to read the result

The bold line at the top is your mailing deadline: the safer date first, then the last date that works if the landlord asks nothing. Under it, a dated list runs from your mailing day to the day silence becomes consent. The last line compares that day with your move-in date. If it says the 30 days run past move-in, don't let the subtenant move in on silence. Get the landlord's yes in writing or move the start date. If you chose three or fewer units, the result says "Your lease controls," and the dates don't apply; read your sublet clause and get any yes in writing. The checklist ticks are for you; nothing is saved or sent. The date under the result is the day we read the law.
