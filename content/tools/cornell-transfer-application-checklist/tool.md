This checklist builds the list of everything your Cornell transfer application needs for fall 2027 entry. Pick the college and major you are applying to and tick what fits you. The tool sorts every piece into four groups: before you apply, the first deadline (March 15, 2027), the second deadline (April 15, 2027) and after you are admitted. Each line says who completes the piece (you, your family, an official at your school, an instructor, each school's registrar or Cornell) and when it is due. Nothing is computed or estimated: it is Cornell's rules, matched to your situation.

## Who it is for

Sophomore and junior applicants from four-year and community colleges, Transfer Option holders, international applicants, students applying for aid or through EOP or HEOP, veterans and anyone out of school two or more years, and students who will need disability accommodations or accessible housing.

## The inputs

- **College and major.** You apply to one college and, in most colleges, one major. The choice adds that college's extras: ILR's three writing pieces, the Dyson and Hotel resume, AAP's personal statement and B.F.A. portfolio, the Landscape Architecture portfolio, Fashion Design's portfolio and Design Index, the design supplement for Fashion Design Management and Design and Environmental Analysis, and Engineering's required course block. It also flags closed programs: B.Arch. (closed to external transfers for fall 2027), Global and Public Health Sciences (no transfers) and Global Development (sophomores only).
- **Transfer Option holder.** Swaps the Common App for the Transfer Option application, drops the fee, and lists that college's Transfer Option extras.
- **International.** Switches the aid line to the international CSS Profile rules and adds translations and the F-1 or J-1 record transfer.
- **Applying for aid.** Adds the CSS Profile and FAFSA, the Cornell Aid Estimate and the automatic fee waiver note.
- **New York resident.** Adds HESC with the right code for your college (not shown for international applicants).
- **EOP or HEOP.** Adds the OADI step: the HEOP Transfer Certification form or an email to stateprograms@cornell.edu, depending on the college. It also turns on the aid forms, which EOP and HEOP require.
- **Out of school two or more years, or a veteran.** Lets a personal or employer reference replace the Academic Evaluation, and adds the Veterans Admissions Team, Veterans House and the credit limits on old courses and military training.
- **Disability accommodations.** Adds registration with Student Disability Services before the housing application.
- **Taking courses this spring.** Adds the Mid-Term Report, which is required when you have spring courses.

## The rules and numbers

Two deadlines govern a regular application. By March 15, 2027: the Transfer Common App, the $85 fee or waiver, and any portfolio or design supplement. By April 15, 2027: everything else (College Report, Academic Evaluation, transcripts, Mid-Term Report). Aid forms are due March 15, 2027, not April 15. ILR wants the Mid-Term Report by March 15 if possible. The Transfer Option application was due March 15 in 2026, and its page shows "Application Available Winter 2027" for the next round.

Who completes what: you write the essay (250 to 650 words), the Writing Supplement and the Mid-Term Report; an official at your current school (registrar or dean) completes the College Report, which confirms standing and is not a recommendation; one instructor of a full-credit college course writes the Academic Evaluation; each school sends its own transcript.

After admission: housing within 10 days of getting your NetID; the Student Health Plan waiver by July 31 (2026 date) or $1,705 for fall plus $2,046 for spring, which is $1,705 + $2,046 = $3,751 in year one; the written exam credit request by the last day of instruction of your first Cornell semester (December 7 in 2026).

## Assumptions and what it does not cover

- The cycle is fall 2027 entry, the only one open: external transfer is fall only. The March 15 and April 15, 2027 deadlines and the aid deadline are published for this cycle. The Transfer Option deadline, the health plan waiver date and the exam credit date are fall 2026 dates; the Human Ecology and Dyson after-admission dates come from a page written for 2024. The tool labels each one.
- On the Transfer Option route, the central page names March 15 for the application and aid and lets the Mid-Term Report arrive later. The tool lists the College Report and transcripts with the application.
- Where Cornell's pages disagree, the item says so. The central admissions page requires a resume for Hotel; the Hotel page lists resumes only for first-year applicants, so the tool says to send one. ILR's guidelines ask for an academic recommendation from a college professor; the central page lets applicants out of school two or more years use a personal or employer reference, so the tool says to ask ILR first.
- It lists pieces, not admission odds, GPA targets or prerequisite course lists beyond Engineering's required block. The housing line assumes the one-year on-campus rule; juniors and students with two years on campus elsewhere are exempt.

## How to read the result

The bold line counts your items, splits them by deadline, and tells you how many you complete yourself. Under it, each group lists its pieces in order. Read the "Who" first: anything that depends on someone else (the College Report, the Academic Evaluation, transcripts) needs a request weeks before April 15, 2027. Anything marked "you and your family" is the aid paperwork, due March 15, 2027 with the application, not with the April materials. An item dated "(2026 date)" or "(2024 pattern)" is last cycle's date; recheck it on the linked page when Cornell posts the 2027 version. Change any input and the list rebuilds.
