# Data shape to presentation form

| The data looks like | Use | Example | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 to 6 options, same attributes | comparison table | ITH vs SYR vs ELM airport: flights, distance, cost to campus | a paragraph per option before the table |
| prices by category | cost table with period | rent per bed by unit size, 2027-28 | prices scattered through prose |
| steps in order | numbered steps with deadline or cost | how to appeal a financial aid decision | bullets (they hide order) |
| dates across a year | timeline | when Collegetown leases sign, month by month | a list of dates in a sentence |
| one value changing over years | line chart | bikeshare price per minute, 2023 to 2026 | a history table with mostly blanks |
| a reader's own numbers decide | calculator | split rent and utilities among roommates; when a bus pass pays off | a table of every possible combination |
| key stats for one entity | stat band (4 to 6 values) | a college's admit rate, net price, transfer acceptance | a stat in every paragraph |
| rule plus exceptions | rule sentence, then an exceptions list | who must live on campus, then who is exempt | burying exceptions in a long sentence |
| myth vs fact | two-column table | "student housing is exempt from Good Cause" vs the actual exemptions | a paragraph that repeats the myth first |
| answer differs by situation | "If you're..." sections | repayment options by when you borrowed | one long section with "however, if..." clauses |
| a single key number | one sentence | the deposit cap is one month's rent | a figure or table for one value |
