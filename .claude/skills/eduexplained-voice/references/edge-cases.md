# Edge cases from the November 2026 audit

Each pattern below appeared across our published pages. The count shows how widespread it was. When you meet one of these situations, apply the fix exactly.

| Pattern | Found in | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| One fact stated 6 or more times | 64 of 108 pages | State it once in full; refer back at most once. Reader blocks and the summary never restate it. |
| Opening over 60 words | 105 of 108 pages | Answer plus one supporting fact. Move everything else into sections. |
| Opening with 3 or more links | 32 pages | One link in the opening, at most. Other sources get linked where their facts appear. |
| Research narration ("per X's page", "read on Nov 8", "says") | 654 phrases, 106 pages | Link the fact; name the source only when who said it matters (rules, laws, disputes). |
| Process talk ("we found", "our arithmetic", "we could not confirm") | 229 phrases, 84 pages | Delete. Present our math as fact with its inputs shown. |
| "Not confirmed:" label | 176 lines, 63 pages | One plain sentence: what is unknown, what to do. |
| Negative opening ("Nobody publishes...", "There is no list...") | 12 pages | Open with what the reader can do. Mention the gap once, lower down. |
| Time-bound text ("this week", "if you are reading this in November") | 21 phrases | Anchor to a date: "by Nov. 15," "before the Jan. 31 deadline." |
| Planner notes on the page ("pages that rank", "AI answers give") | several pages | Rewrite as reader-facing advice or delete. |
| Money missing its cents digit ("$46.7 to $62.1") | 7 pages, from one bad fact row | Write "$46.70 to $62.10." The fact file validator now blocks this at the source. |
| Trivia that serves no decision (2023 trip counts, a three-year price history) | several pages | Keep a fact only if it changes what the reader does or understands. History stays when it explains why old pages are wrong, in one sentence. |
| Several pages answering the same question (part-time jobs in two topics; three car pages; three "what does a month cost" pages) | 6 groups | Not a writing fix: the architect assigns one page per intent. If your packet's coverage map overlaps another live page, stop and report it. |
| Hypothetical examples ("a hypothetical junior...") | common | Keep worked examples; drop the word "hypothetical." Use a concrete setup: "Say you live in Collegetown and ride 12 minutes each way." |
