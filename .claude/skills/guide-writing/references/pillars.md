# Writing a pillar section

Pillars are written in parallel: several guide writer sessions each draft one section at the same time, then the editor stitches them into one page. This keeps a 5,000-word pillar from being the slow job that holds everything up.

When your packet has a `section` field:
- Write only your section, starting with its `##` heading. No page opening, no frontmatter, no FAQ.
- Your section's first paragraph answers its main question, in 50 words or fewer.
- State the facts assigned to your section. If you need a fact assigned to another section, refer to it by name ("the 120-day rule, explained below") instead of restating it, and list it in `cross_refs` in your output so the editor can check the order.
- Link to the detailed guide for your use case where the packet's `links` include one. The pillar answers; the guide goes deep.
- Match the voice exactly; the editor will smooth seams, but sections written in different registers are expensive to fix.
