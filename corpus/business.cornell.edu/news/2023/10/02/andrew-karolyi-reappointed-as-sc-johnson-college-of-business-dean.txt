Andrew Karolyi Reappointed as SC Johnson College of Business Dean
Andrew Karolyi, Professor of Finance and Global Business (JGSM).
Andrew Karolyi
, will continue to lead the Cornell SC Johnson College of Business, as the Charles Field Knight Dean for a second term. Provost Michael I. Kotlikoff made the official announcement on October 2, recognizing Karolyi’s outstanding leadership, dedication, and commitment to our institution.
