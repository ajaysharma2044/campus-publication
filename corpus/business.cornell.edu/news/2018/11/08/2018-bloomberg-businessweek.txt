Johnson MBA ranked #10 by Bloomberg Businessweek
Johnson has moved up in the 2018 Bloomberg Businessweek rankings to #10 in the United States, a three-point gain from 2017.
“I join you in applauding our improved ranking,” said Mark Nelson, the Anne and Elmer Lindseth Dean, in an email to the Johnson community. Nelson noted the investments the school has made in curriculum improvements, diversity programming, and new faculty hires are reflected in how Johnson is ranked.
“More fundamentally,” he says, “this good news is the result of the hard work of everyone in our community, including students, staff, faculty, and alumni. We have continued to see rankings improvements in The Economist,
the Financial Times
, and US News & World Report. It is a very exciting time to be part of Johnson!”
