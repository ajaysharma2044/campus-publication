The Nolan School offers hospitality business degrees and programs to fit any career stage, from undergraduates to professionals and executives.
Undergraduate program
Our undergraduate program blends top-tier business and hospitality education with engaging experiential learning. Become head chef at The Establishment, a real restaurant, and build industry connections at the student-run Hotel Ezra Cornell conference. Leverage curriculum in finance, banking, real estate and analytics to tailor your ideal career path in hospitality.
Professional and executive-level programs
Our graduate programs combine business education with hospitality specialization to accelerate your career. Choose from a Master of Management in Hospitality, an Executive Master of Management in Hospitality, a Master of Professional Studies in Real Estate through the Rubacha Department of Real Estate or a Cornell-Peking MMH/MBA.
Research-focused programs
Our highly-ranked M.S. and Ph.D. programs offer research-focused training in hospitality management. Students collaborate with faculty on practical challenges and build the skills needed for high-level positions in academia, policy and industry.
