Enrollment Verification
Option 1:
Request the verification online via
HomerConnect
.
Click on
Request Enrollment Verification in the menu on the left side of the page
.
Enter the most current term you are registered for.
Fill out the required information & submit.
To have one emailed, enter email address in the
ISSUE TO
field.
Leave all address fields blank.
There is no fee for this service.
These reports are manually processed at the end of each business day and will be delivered by the Office of the Registrar in the method you have requested.
Option 2:
Request the verification through the
National Student Clearinghouse
.
There is a fee for this service.
View your enrollment histories at your institution.
Print out proof-of-enrollment certificates for use with health insurers and others.
