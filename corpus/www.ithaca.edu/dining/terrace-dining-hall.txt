Welcome to Terrace Dining Hall
Terrace Dining Hall offers something for everyone on campus and is conveniently located on the ground floor of the Terrace 1 residence hall. Use your meal swipe card for breakfast, lunch, and dinner while you’re on your way to and from classes.
