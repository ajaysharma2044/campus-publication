A MEAL SWIPE EXCHANGE POINT
Most people equate meal plans with “swipes”; you swipe your ID card to eat at a dining hall, right? Well, the exchange program lets you “swipe” in more places than just the dining hall – 7, 14, or 21 times a week! In essence, you are “exchanging” a swipe at a dining hall for an opportunity to get your “swipe” meal at any five retail eateries across campus using your GrubHub app or café kiosk, and it won’t cost you extra. Just make sure you add your ID card to the payment options on GrubHub and choose from the selections provided on the “Exchange Menu" below!

All of our exchange meals are made fresh daily on campus and availability is subject to change.
