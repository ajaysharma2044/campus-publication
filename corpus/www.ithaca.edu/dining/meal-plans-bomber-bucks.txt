Meal Plans
From busy students who live on and off campus to our hard-working faculty and staff, we have a meal plan for everyone who is a part of the Ithaca College community. Meal plans are not active during Thanksgiving Break, Winter Break and Spring Break.
Student Meal Plans
Our dining packages fit every student's active life at Ithaca College. Meal swipe options campus-wide give our students flexibility, convenience, and more robust dining opportunities.

Faculty & Staff Meal Plans
Our staff and faculty can enjoy various options from our dining halls at a price that can't be beaten! A block of 10 meal swipes is just $80; payment is made simply through payroll deductions.
