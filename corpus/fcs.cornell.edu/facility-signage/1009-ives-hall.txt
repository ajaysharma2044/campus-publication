1009 - Ives Hall
Location
121 Tower Rd, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Accessible entrances are at the 1st floor southwest and southeast ends of the building off of the lower courtyard. The 2nd floor has two entrances at the north side on the east and west ends of the building off of Tower Road Plaza. ADA door operator is located at the 2nd floor northeast entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
Elevators are located at the east side which goes to the 6th floor with 4th & 5th floors locked off and the west side which goes from the basement through the 3rd floor. Catherwood Library has two elevators that go from the 1st to the 5th floors with the 1st floor being locked off. The 3rd, 4th and 5th floors can be locked off as well.
Building-Specific Info
The building is open this Fall Semester:
Monday, Tuesday and Thursday; 7:30AM - 8:30PM
Wednesday; 7:30AM - 9:40PM
Friday; 7:30AM - 5:00PM
Saturday and Sunday; closed
The Catherwood Library is closed this Fall Semester.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms are on the 1st floor and are located on the east and west ends of building. Restrooms on the 2nd floor are located on west end of building. Restrooms on the 6th floor are located at the east end on the south side.  Restrooms within the Catherwood Library space; there is a 2nd floor Women's restroom and a 3rd floor Men's restroom. 4th and 5th floors have restrooms for Library staff only. The Catherwood Library is closed this Fall Semester.
