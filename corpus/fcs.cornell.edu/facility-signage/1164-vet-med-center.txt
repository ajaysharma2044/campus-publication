1164 - Vet Med Center
Location
930 Campus Rd, Town of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Door 92, card access with NO auto opener.  Very heavy doors.
Directional Flow Considerations
This is the main public corridor to the Vet Complex from Campus Road.  It primarily leads you directly to the Atrium and café.  Elevator and stair access to the VMC are immediately east and west of the entrance.  This is not the hospital entrance.
Building-Specific Info
There is a directional map at this entrance.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
There are both men's and women's restrooms directly down the main corridor on the west.
