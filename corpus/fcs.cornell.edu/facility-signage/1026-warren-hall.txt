1026 - Warren Hall
Location
137 Reservoir Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
North entrance located off Reservoir Avenue.
Directional Flow Considerations
You will be entering at the basement level.  You will go through two sets of double doors, and into a lobby area.  Proceed ahead and you will be at the elevator.  This elevator provides access to floors B-4.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
All restrooms in this facility are ADA accessible.  Restrooms are located on the east and west sides of the facility.  A mother's room is located on the Fourth Floor, east side of the facility.
