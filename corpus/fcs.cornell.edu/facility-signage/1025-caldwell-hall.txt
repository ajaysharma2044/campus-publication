1025 - Caldwell Hall
Location
121 Reservoir Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
North entrance located off Reservoir Avenue.
Directional Flow Considerations
You will enter the building off Reservoir Avenue at the basement level.  Immediately upon entering there is a accessible lift that will take you to the first floor.  You can reach floors 1-4 using the elevator to the left of the main lobby area, starting at floor 1.   This elevator is not accessible with a wheelchair.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms are located in the basement, and in levels 1 and 2.  All restrooms are ADA accessible.
