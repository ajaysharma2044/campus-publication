1007R - ILR Research
Location
140 Garden Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Accessible entrances are at the 2nd floor northwest side off of the upper courtyard and the 3rd floor off of Garden Ave. ADA door operators are located at each entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
The elevator is located in the center of the building on the north side.
Building-Specific Info
This building is locked down for ILR Faculty and Staff only via card access.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms on 2nd floor are located on the east side near the elevator. 3rd and 4th floor restrooms are located in center of building on south side.
