1007A - King Shaw Hall
Location
140 Garden Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Accessible entrances are at the 2nd floor west side off of the upper courtyard and the 3rd floor off of Garden Ave. An ADA door operator located at each entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
There is an elevator located in the center of the building on east side.
Building-Specific Info
The Building is open this Fall Semester:
Monday - Thursday; 7:30AM - 5:15PM
Friday; 7:30AM - 1:00PM
Saturday & Sunday; closed on the 3rd floor entrance
COVID test site on the 2nd floor entrance. Doors are open Monday - Friday; 7:30AM - 4:30PM
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms are on the 2nd floor and are located on the north end across from the Dolgen Hall entrance and the south end within the ILR Research building. The 3rd floor restrooms are located at the north entrance on the east side. The 4th floor Women's restroom is located on north end east side and the Men's restroom is located on south end east side. The 5th floor restrooms are located on the north end with Women's at end of the north corridor and the Men's on the west side.
