1080S - Kennedy Hall
Location
215 Garden Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
South Entrance through Trillium Dining.
Directional Flow Considerations
Enter the main entrance to Trillium Dining and proceed thru two sets of double doors to enter a narrow hallway.  Follow the hallway to an open area and turn right.  Two elevators are located on your left.  These elevators access floors B-4.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
ADA accessible restrooms are located in the north side of Kennedy Hall on floors 1-4.
