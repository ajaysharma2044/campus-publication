1170 - CVM Center
Location
606 Tower Rd, Town of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Door 26, card access and an auto opener.
Directional Flow Considerations
This is the main entrance from Tower Road into the Vet Complex. There are multiple directions you can go from this entrance to enter the entire complex.  Multiple stairs and elevator access are available.
Building-Specific Info
There is one color coded directory just to the south of this entrance.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
There are both men's and women's restrooms to the east and north of this entrance.
