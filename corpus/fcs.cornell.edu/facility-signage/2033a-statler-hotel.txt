2033A - Statler Hotel
Location
130 Statler Dr, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
For access to the Terrace restaurant, please use the Clark Courtyard entrance between Uris and Statler. For access to the Statler Hotel please use the main entrance off of Statler Drive on the East side of the building.
Directional Flow Considerations
Enter through Terrace Restaurant and exit out the Terrace patio. Traffic will flow both ways through this entrance. Guest will be given key access to use the elevators for entering the guest tower. The staircase on the north floor.
Building-Specific Info
Only the Terrace is open for public access. The 2nd floor is completely restricted. Floors 1 and 3-9 are for hotel guests and limited support staff.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
On the Terrace level there is a multi occupant men's room and women's room.
