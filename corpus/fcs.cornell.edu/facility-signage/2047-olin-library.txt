2047 - Olin Library
Location
161 Ho Plaza na, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
For quiet study reservations, enter the facility using the card reader next to the southernmost door on main entrance from Ho Plaza. Exit the facility using the 2 northernmost doors at the main entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
A welcome desk inside the entrance will be staffed to provide directional assistance during building open hours, Sun-Thur 9am–9pm, Fri 9am–5pm, Sat 10am–5pm.
Building-Specific Info
Only the 1st floor and B level are currently  accessible to the public.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
The public restrooms on the B level have an occupancy of 8 for the men's and 7 for the women's. There is also a single occupancy all gender restroom on the B level.
