1007E - Dolgen Hall
Location
140 Garden Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Accessible entrances are at the 2nd floor southwest side off of the upper courtyard and the 3rd floor off Garden Ave. An ADA door operator is located at each entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
An elevator located in the center of the building on the south side.
Building-Specific Info
This building is locked down for ILR Faculty and Staff only via card access.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms on the 2nd floor are located on the east side across from the entrance near King-Shaw Hall and one is in the center of the building on north side. the 3rd and 4th floors are located in the center of building on the north side.
