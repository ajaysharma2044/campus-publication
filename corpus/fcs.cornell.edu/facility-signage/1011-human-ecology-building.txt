1011 - Human Ecology Building
Location
37 Forest Home Dr, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Enter from Forest Home Garage or the west entrance from the pedestrian bridge and Terrace Plaza. Enter from the southwest door from the Terrrace Courtyard.
Directional Flow Considerations
Corridor flow is counter clockwise from entrances, with the exception of the NS corridors on the west end which is bi-directional. Northwest stair is down only. Southeast Stair is up only.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Toilet room occupancy is unrestricted.
