2010 - Uris Library
Location
160 Ho Plaza na, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
For quiet study reservations, enter the facility using the northern door on the main entrance up the stairs from Ho Plaza. Please call (607)254-6241 for access via the tower entrance without stairs. Exit the facility using the southern door at the main entrance or the Cocktail Lounge exit on the 4B level. For contactless pick up items, use the card reader on the southern door, facing west inside the tower entrance lobby. Exit the facility using the northern door through the tower entrance lobby.

Building-Specific Info
The stack tower is not currently accessible to the public.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
The men's room on the first floor has an occupancy of 11. The women's room on the B level has an occupancy of 5. The women's room on the B level has an occupancy of 4. The men's and women's restrooms on the 4B level both have an occupancy of 2. There is also a single occupancy all gender restroom on the 4B level and 2 single occupancy all gender restrooms on the first floor.
