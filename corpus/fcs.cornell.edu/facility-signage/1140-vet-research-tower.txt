1140 - Vet Research Tower
Location
618 Tower Rd, Town of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Door 23, card access and auto opener.
Directional Flow Considerations
This is the main entrance to the VRT from Tower Road. There are multiple directions you can go from this entrance.  There are elevators to access VRT to the west, there stairs to level 2 VRT to the east, there is access to ECRF and the CARE Offices to the south and there is a corridor leading to the CVM Center and VEC to the east.
Building-Specific Info
There is a directory in the VRT lobby area.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
There is an existing men's room (now a gender neutral during COVID, just to the east of the entrance.
