1024 - Bailey Hall
Location
230 Garden Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Patrons with limited mobility can enter Bailey Hall through the entrance at the southwest corner of the building (to the left of the main entrance of the building). Performers with limited mobility can enter through an entrance in the northeast corner of the building. There is an elevator in the lobby for patrons’ use and an elevator in the northeast part of the building for performers’ use. A small lift in the auditorium can be used to transfer guests to and from the orchestra level.
Building-Specific Info
There are hearing assist devices available through the Box Office. There is no charge for the use of these devices, however some form of collateral is require during pick up to ensure the units safe return to the Box Office.
