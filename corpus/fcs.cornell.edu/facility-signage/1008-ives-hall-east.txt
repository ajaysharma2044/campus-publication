1008 - Ives Hall East
Location
121 Tower Rd, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
An accessible entrance is at the 2nd floor South on the side off of the parking lot.
Directional Flow Considerations
The entrance leads into the stairwell. To access the elevator go through the interior door, straight ahead and turn left and then left again.
Building-Specific Info
This building is locked down for ILR Faculty and Staff only via card access. Access to the 1st floor Catherwood Library study space is through the 1st floor of Ives Hall. Access to the study space is via card access during Ives Hall building hours and at this time only ILR students can access the space.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms on 1st floor are located in the center of building on the East side and all gender specific restrooms are at the South end of the building on the West side. 3rd floor restrooms are for faculty and staff only and are located on northeast end of building.
