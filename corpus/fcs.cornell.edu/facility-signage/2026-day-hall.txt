2026 - Day Hall
Location
144 East Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Accessible entrances are located at the NW corner (adjacent to Day Hall parking lot), NE (off Day Hall plaza) and SE (near loading dock).
Directional Flow Considerations
There is no directional flow: all persons are requested to stay to the right when travelling in any hallway.
Building-Specific Info
The building is currently restricted to only those employees with authorized card access.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Accessible bathrooms are located as follows:
Women = basement (W), 3rd floor (E & W);
Men = 1st floor (W) and 3rd floor (E)
