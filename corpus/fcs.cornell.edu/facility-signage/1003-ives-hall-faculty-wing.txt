1003 - Ives Hall Faculty Wing
Location
133 Statler Dr, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Accessible entrances are at the 1st floor West side off of Statler circle, the North side under the breezeway and the 2nd floor East side off of the parking lot. An ADA door operator is located at each entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
The building is "L" shaped and has an East/West wing and a North/South wing. The elevator is located in the center of the building.
Building-Specific Info
This building is locked down for ILR Faculty, Staff and Graduate Students only via card access.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms on 1st, 2nd and 3rd floors are located on the North/South wing, East side of the corridor. In the basement restrooms are located across from the elevator on the South side.
