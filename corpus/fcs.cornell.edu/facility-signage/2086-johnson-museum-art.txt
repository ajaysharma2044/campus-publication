2086 - Johnson Museum of Art
Location
114 Central Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
There will be an electronic sign directing students to the correct entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
There is a clockwise rotation on each floor and in each gallery.
Building-Specific Info
Main entrance (Appel Lobby) for AAP classes and Museum staff. Wing entrance (Gussman Lobby) for all other scheduled classes.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
There are two (2) single occupant restrooms located on floor 2L of the main building. There are two (2) single occupant restrooms located in the basement level of the wing.
