1029 - Fernow Hall
Location
226 Mann Dr, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
North entrance located off Reservoir Avenue.
Directional Flow Considerations
You will be entering the facility at the basement level off Reservoir Avenue.  You will enter through two sets of double doors into a lobby area.  To the left of the stairwell is an elevator that will take you to all floors in the building.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms are located on the east side of the facility.   All restrooms are ADA accessible.
