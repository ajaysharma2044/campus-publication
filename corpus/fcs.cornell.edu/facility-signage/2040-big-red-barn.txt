2040 - Big Red Barn
Location
135 Presidents Dr, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Front door on the side of AD White House.
Directional Flow Considerations
One way in the front door (AD White house side) and out the back door (Mallott Hall side) unless the person needs wheelchair access, in which case in and out would happen through ramp in front or side greenhouse entrance.
Building-Specific Info
When the building opens back up, signage will be placed indicating the direction guests should take.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
One person using the restroom at a time and others waiting in a formed line outside of the door.  The floor will have indicators of where to stand to social distance.
