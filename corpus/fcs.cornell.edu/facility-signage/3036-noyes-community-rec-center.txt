3036 - Noyes Community and Rec Center
Location
306 West Ave, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
Enter the facility through the ground level entrance on the West side of the building facing Forest Park Lane. Exit the facility through the upper level entrance on the East side of the building facing West Avenue.
Directional Flow Considerations
Flow moves from lower level to upper level for Jansen's Store, upper toilet room, and study use.
Flow moves clockwise on the ground level if using the ground level bathroom facilities or multipurpose room.
Flow moves one-way using the basketball gymnasium for PE courses, entering through the basketball gym entrance and exiting through the emergency exit tunnels to the West side of the building toward Forest Park Lane.
Staircase connecting the ground floor to the upper level in one-way up.
Building-Specific Info
Unique signage to the building: all patrons must have a CUID and must check in at the desk on the ground level to access the building. Do not kick ADA door activators. The walkway from the upper level lobby to the sky lounge may only have one patron at a time. Please wait for individuals to clear the walkway before using it.
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Upper level - two single-use toilet rooms. Lower level - one men's and one women's bathroom/locker room, 3 individuals maximum occupancy. No use of locker banks or shower facility.
