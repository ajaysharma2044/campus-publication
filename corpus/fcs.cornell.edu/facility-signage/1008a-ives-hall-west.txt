1008A - Ives Hall West
Location
121 Tower Rd, City of Ithaca, Tompkins
Preferred Entrances
To access Ives Hall West you need to enter through Ives Hall. Accessible entrances are at the 1st floor northwest end off of the lower courtyard and the 2nd floor has two entrances at the north side on the east and west ends of the building off of the Tower Road Plaza. An ADA door operator is located at the 2nd floor northeast entrance.
Directional Flow Considerations
To access Ives Hall West you need to enter through Ives Hall. The elevator is located on the west side.
Building-Specific Info
The Building is open this Fall Semester:
Monday, Tuesday and Thursday; 7:30AM - 8:30PM
Wednesday; 7:30AM - 9:40PM
Friday; 7:30AM - 5:00PM
Saturday & Sunday; closed
Restroom Protocols for this Building
Restrooms are located in Ives Hall on the 1st and 2nd floors. The 1st floor restrooms are located on the east and west ends of the building and restrooms on the 2nd floor are located on the west end of the building.
