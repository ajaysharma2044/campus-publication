Transportation and Delivery Services
Cornell University’s Department of Transportation and Delivery Services facilitates smart and sustainable movement of people, packages, and vehicles across Cornell campuses and beyond. We are committed to offering and encouraging alternatives to the single-occupant vehicle, while supporting sustainability initiatives for the campus, adjacent neighborhoods, and the environment.
