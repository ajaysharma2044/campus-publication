Facilities Management
Building Care Department
The Building Care Department consists of Custodial Services and Table and Chair services. We provide cleaning and waste services for faculty, staff, students and visitors. Our department provides a clean, safe and environmentally sustainable campus.
In 2020, The Building Care Department was honored to receive the Employee Assembly Appreciation Award in recognition of the tireless everyday efforts of our staff in providing a safe, clean, and healthy environment for our students, faculty and staff.
Scope of Services
Daily cleaning and disinfection service
Trash and Recycling removal
Table and Chair services
Event preparation and cleanup
