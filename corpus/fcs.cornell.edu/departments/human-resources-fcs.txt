Welcome to the Facilities and Campus Services Office of Human Resources.
FCS-HR is committed to fostering a collaborative and inclusive work environment where each individual is treated in accordance with our values of Truth, Respect, Excellence, Teamwork, and Integrity.
FCS-HR provides advice to all FCS staff and supervisors pertaining to such things as facilitation of Medical Leaves, training, performance management, HR policies, work/life balance, workforce planning, diversity, and engagement.
If you cannot find the appropriate information using one of the links below, please contact your departmental human resources representative.
