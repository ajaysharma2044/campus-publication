Engineering & Project Management
is responsible for the management and execution of the university's capital program. We provide engineering and project management leadership and services for planning, designing, constructing, and maintaining the university’s facilities and infrastructure. Our team of architects, engineers, project managers, and subject-matter experts serve as stewards of the built environment. Our team is organized around the following core functions:
Facilities Engineering
Engineering & Architectural Design Services
Technical Stewardship
Project Management
Capital Project Management
