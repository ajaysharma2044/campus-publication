Welcome!
The office of Facilities Contracts has been delegated responsibility to efficiently manage the creation, execution, and management of facilities related contracts for projects which require the use of labor and services for the purpose of maximizing financial and operational performance and minimizing risk.
These services typically include contracting and support for:
Architectural/Engineering design services
Commissioning
Construction services for both capital and non-capital projects
Facilities related consulting services
Feasibility studies
Renovations
Testing services
