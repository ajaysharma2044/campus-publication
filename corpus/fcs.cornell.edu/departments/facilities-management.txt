Facilities Management
Facilities Management (FM) provides maintenance, grounds, and custodial services to the entire campus.
As a key steward of the University’s facilities, FM is responsible for facility assessments, skilled trades, general construction, building care, grounds, recycling, asbestos, and the management of planned, corrective, and preventive maintenance.
Facility Management maintenance operations
insure Cornell University’s ability to meet its mission through our vision to harness the energy, talent and creativity of the Facilities Management team to provide consistent service to all facilities that results in a safe, attractive and functional campus. Our goal is to provide these services in a timely and cost-effective manner with minimal disruption to faculty, staff, and students.
