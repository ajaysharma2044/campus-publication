FETCHED: 2026-11-06

It's Permit Renewal Season!
Annual permit holders
with a permit expiring June 30
will have the opportunity to renew their parking commitment for the new fiscal year between June 5 - 30, 2023. For information about the permit renewal process, visit the
Transportation website
.
Commuters are encouraged to use this time to re-examine their
transportation and parking options
, especially if they require only limited, occasional access to campus. Employees can take advantage of deeply discounted
Daily Decision
parking using ParkMobile; utilize the
Commuter Alternative Program
; or choose on-demand,
short-term
mobile payment and pay station parking.
