FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Intersection of Garden & Campus Closed January 7 - 14, 2020.
Underground utilities work will necessitate a partial closure of the intersection of Garden Ave. and Campus Rd. January 7 - 14, 2020. Barricades will be placed in the westbound lane of Campus Rd. just past the entrance to Hoy Garage, and in the northbound lane of Garden Ave. at the intersection with Campus Rd.
Traffic through the intersection will be allowed eastbound-only on Campus Rd., and southbound-only on Garden Ave. Detour signs will be in place to direct traffic around the work zone.
