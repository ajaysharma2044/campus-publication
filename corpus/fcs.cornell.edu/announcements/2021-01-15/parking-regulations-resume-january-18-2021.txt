Parking Regulations Resume January 18, 2021
Enforcement of parking permit and payment requirements will resume on
January 18, 2021
. Commuters should take time to
examine their transportation options
, especially if they require only
limited, occasional access
to campus.
A Lot
permit holders may continue to park until
February 7, 2021
, in the following locations (
see map
) with their permit displayed:
University Ave Lot
Oxley Lot (Route 366)
120 Maple Ave Lot
EHOB/EHP/CISER Complex Lots
Crescent Lot (Campus Road)
Peterson Lot (across from Stocking Hall)
TRB Lot (East Campus)
Everyone should comply with
Winter parking restrictions
, in effect until April 1. Please contact Cornell Transportation (607-255-4600,
transportation@cornell.edu
) if you have any questions.
