Special Event Transportation and Parking
Multiple events occurring in May and June, along with several planned construction projects, will impact traffic circulation and parking availability.
May 11, 2022, marks the return of
Slope Day
, and two
Cornell Commencement
ceremonies are taking place on May 28.
Alumni Reunion
weekend kicks off on June 9. Ongoing and upcoming construction and utility projects will have further impacts on transit users and permit holders.
Please
plan your commute
with these events in mind. Alternatives to parking on campus include using a park-and-ride, working remotely (if approved to do so), sharing the ride, taking transit, or cycling or walking to campus.
Visit
CUInfo
or
TCAT's
website and check your Cornell.edu email for special alerts and notifications from Transportation Services. Event-related road and parking lot closures can be found on the
Event Parking & Transportation
page.
