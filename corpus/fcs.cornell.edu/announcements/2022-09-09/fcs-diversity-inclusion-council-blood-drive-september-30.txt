FCS Diversity & Inclusion Council Blood Drive September 30
The FCS Diversity and Inclusion Council are
sponsoring a blood drive
to support those with sickle cell disease.
The September 30 blood drive will be held at Willard Straight Hall from 9:30 AM – 2:30 PM. All are invited to participate. Eligible donors
(*Donors who self-identify as Black or African American)
may be screened for the Sickle Cell Trait.
To make an appointment, download the Blood Donor App, call 1-800 RED CROSS, visit redcrossblood.org, or use the link below to access our specific blood drive sign-up:
9/30 Cornell FCS Diversity and Inclusion Council blood drive  9:30 AM - 2:30 PM
