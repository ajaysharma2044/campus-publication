FCS United Way chili cookoff, bake off and vendor fair results
Chefs will compete for the FCS chili cook-off trophy!
The Facilities and Campus Services 8th (somewhat) annual United Way chili cook-off, dessert bake-off, and craft & vendor fair was a great success on November 16, raising funds for the United Way of Tompkins County.
The chili cook-off and bake-off competition was in the HSB atrium. The craft and vendor fair went on throughout the halls, conference rooms, and lunchroom of Humphreys.
Over $1,200 was raised for the United Way of Tompkins County!
Be sure to join us next year!
