Parking Holiday December 23-24, 2019
In order to relieve some of the burden and stress on the Cornell community members who will be on campus December 23 and 24, Cornell Transportation Services will not enforce regular parking permit restrictions,
with the exceptions of a few areas
, on those two days. People may park in most permitted or Parkmobile spaces for free during this period.
Transportation Services will continue to enforce fire lanes, loading zones, no parking areas, accessible spaces, reserved spaces, Sage Hall Lot, Myron Taylor Lot, and ILR Lot.
Winter holiday break parking rules
will be in effect December 25 - January 1. Regular parking enforcement will resume January 2, 2020.
Please contact Cornell Transportation (607-255-4600,
transportation@cornell.edu
) if you have any questions, and have a safe and happy winter break!
