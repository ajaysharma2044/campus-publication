Homecoming Celebration Sept. 23-24, 2022
Parking Lot and Road Closure Information
Cornell University is preparing for the 2022 Homecoming weekend. Activities related to this celebration will
impact parking lots and traffic flow
on Campus Road beginning Wednesday, September 21, 2022.
Homecoming activities begin Friday, September 23, 2022. Visit
https://alumni.cornell.edu/come-back/homecoming/
for event information and registration.
