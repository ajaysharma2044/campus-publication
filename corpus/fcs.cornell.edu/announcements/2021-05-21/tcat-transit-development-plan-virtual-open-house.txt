TCAT Transit Development Plan Virtual Open House
The public is invited to a
virtual open house
on Tues., May 25, Noon to 1:30 p.m. or 5:30 p.m. to 7 p.m. to weigh in on a proposed system redesign as TCAT moves toward finalizing its Transit Development Plan (TDP) 2020-2021.
County residents, including both riders and non-riders, are encouraged to participate in either an afternoon or evening session to get an overview of the draft produced by TCAT’s TDP consulting firm.
