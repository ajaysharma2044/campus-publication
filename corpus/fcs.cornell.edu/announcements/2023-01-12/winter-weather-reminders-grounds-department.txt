Winter Weather Reminders from the Grounds Department
We have a lot of ground to cover...
After a heavy snowfall, the Cornell University
Grounds Department
works hard to clear 15 miles of campus roads, 61 miles of sidewalks, and over 100 acres of parking lots. It can take seven to twelve hours for Grounds to complete one clean-up cycle (assuming it stops snowing). Please
help crews do their job
as efficiently as possible and stay safe this winter by
following a few simple guidelines
.
Overnight Parking
To aid in snow removal, overnight parking is
restricted between December 1 and April 1
anytime that it is snowing or threatening to snow. If you must leave your vehicle on campus on a night when restrictions are in place (it’s snowing, or snow is forecast), use one of the areas designated specifically for
winter overnight parking
.
Parking After a Snowstorm
If you arrive on campus after a major snowstorm and find that your lot isn’t plowed,
proceed to the A lot on North Campus, or the B lot near the Vet College
. These large-capacity lots are the first to be cleared and are available to all commuter permit holders during the snow removal period.
If possible,
park your car next to other vehicles
, or at the end space if you’re first in the row. Try to park as close to other cars as is safe so the plow crews can do their job.
Winter Safety Tips and Resources
When walking on snowy or icy surfaces is unavoidable,
Walk Like a Penguin
to
prevent slips and falls
. Take note that some campus paths and spaces within parking lots will not be plowed. These areas are clearly signed and should be avoided.
If you observe a winter safety issue anywhere on campus, call
607-254-COLD (2653)
to report it and get it fixed.
