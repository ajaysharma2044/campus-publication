Campus-to-Campus Service Reduction Spring 2020
Beginning March 23, 2020, Campus-to-Campus will reduce service to two daily round-trips by eliminating the early morning departures from Ithaca and New York City. This reduction in service will allow for more efficient operation and conservation of university resources.
There is no higher priority to the Cornell University and Campus-to-Campus community than the safety of our customers and employees. The coaches are cleaned after every trip using an EPA approved disinfectant in the lavatories and in the cabin, and a deeper cleaning occurs nightly.
As a reminder, if you are ill, or traveling while in quarantine status, please reschedule your trip and do not ride the Campus-to-Campus bus service. This precaution is for the safety of all passengers and drivers. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and thank you in advance for your cooperation.
