Construction Impacts Summer 2019
Cornell’s 2019 construction season promises to be a busy one and a long one as well. A number of small projects slated to begin this spring and summer, along with the anticipated start of the
North Campus Residential Expansion
, could have significant impacts on traffic circulation, pedestrian access, and parking.
Visit
Construction Impacts
for an interactive project map and list of resources to help you stay informed. For web and email alerts, visit
CUINFO
and subscribe to the Special Conditions listserv.
