August 23 & 24 Parking Changes for New Student Move-In
In order to facilitate move-in activity on North and West Campus, on Aug. 23 and 24 a portion of the A Lot and the University Ave. lot will be reserved for vehicle queuing and staging. On West Campus, West Ave. will be designated as one-way, with traffic traveling from University Ave. to Campus Rd.
On these days, permit holders can continue to park in their designated areas (A permit holders can access the westernmost bays of A Lot, near Northcross Rd.), or may choose to park in the following areas if convenient to their work location: Hoy Road parking garage (rooftop level), Outer Hasbrouck (North Campus), Peterson Lot (near Dairy Bar), or TRB Lot (near Vet College).
We appreciate your cooperation and understanding as we work to accommodate student needs. If you have questions about the move-in process, please visit
https://scl.cornell.edu/
. For parking and transit questions, visit
parking.cornell.edu
.
