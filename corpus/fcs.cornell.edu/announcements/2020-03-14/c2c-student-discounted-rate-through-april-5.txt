C2C Student Discounted Rate Through April 5
Let Cornell owned and operated Campus-to-Campus bus service transport you to New York City with a special rate for students. Effective immediately through Sunday, April 5, student fares will be reduced to $50 one-way, a discount of $32.50.
Our stops include:
Cornell Club (44th and Madison) – near Grand Central Station and blocks away from the Port Authority
F Train to Tech Campus (64th and 3rd)
Weill Cornell Medical College (69th and York
)
Availability for reduced fee is limited and cargo space will also be a squeeze.
We will attempt to accommodate all luggage, but please be efficient in packing and be prepared to hold items on your lap.
On-line reservations can be made at
https://c2cbus.ipp.cornell.edu
