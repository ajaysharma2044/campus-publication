UPDATE: FCS Parking Impacts Dec. 4-5
NEW PROJECT DATES ANNOUNCED
Access to the parking lots located on Route 366 near Dwyer Dam (Oxley Lot) and the Central Heating Plant (Humphreys Service Building) will be very limited Dec. 4-5 due to a utility project involving crane work. Traffic on Route 366 may be impacted as well; please use care if traveling through the area.
HSB Lower Lot: Dec. 4-5
Oxley Lot: Dec. 5
