Hoy Garage & Alumni Field Parkmobile Rate Discount through February 4
Transportation Services has introduced a temporary rate reduction for
Parkmobile
parking in both the Hoy Road Parking Garage and the Alumni Field Lot on Tower Road. The discounted rates for hourly and daily mobile-payment parking are in effect through February 4, 2022.
Hoy Garage, Parkmobile Zone 4121 is $1/hour or $3.65/day. Alumni Field is $.75/hour or $1.65/day. A Parkmobile convenience fee of $.35 is added to all transactions.
To get started with Parkmobile, download the Parkmobile App, or simply text the word
PARK
to 77223. More information about
Parkmobile
and
Daily Decision
parking can be found on the
parking.cornell.edu
site.
