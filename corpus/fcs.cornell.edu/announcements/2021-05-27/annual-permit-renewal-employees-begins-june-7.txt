TITLE: Annual Permit Renewal for Employees Begins June 7 | Facilities and Campus Services
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Annual Permit Renewal for Employees Begins June 7
Annual permit holders
with a permit expiring June 30
will have the opportunity to renew their parking commitment for the new fiscal year between June 7 - 30, 2021. For information about the permit renewal process, visit the
Transportation website
.
Commuters are encouraged to use this time to re-examine their transportation and parking options, especially if they require only limited, occasional access to campus. Employees can take advantage of deeply discounted
Daily Decision
parking using ParkMobile; utilize the
Commuter Alternative Program
; or choose on-demand,
short-term
mobile payment and pay station parking.
Employees
who do not have a current parking permit
and wish to obtain a new one can make a request using the
Permit Request Form
.
For assistance, contact
Transportation Services
to schedule a consultation with a customer service agent.
