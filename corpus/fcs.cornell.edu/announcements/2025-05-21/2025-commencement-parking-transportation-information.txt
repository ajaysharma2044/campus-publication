2025 Commencement Parking and Transportation Information
May
Commencement
Ceremonies
The university will host two
commencement
ceremonies
on May 24. To keep everything running smoothly for all graduates and guests, please:
Review parking options and shuttle routes.
Comply with all traffic control signage and directions.
Accessible Parking
Accessible parking accommodations
must be requested via the Office of
Commencement
Events.
Shuttle Transportation
Park and Ride Shuttle Service Saturday
Free campus bus service
will be available from 7:30 a.m. until approximately 9 p.m. and will travel between the parking lots noted on the map. Bus frequency is approximately every ten to fifteen minutes.
Buses are fully accessible and will travel from the pick-up locations on North Campus, Central Campus, B Lot, and then on to the Crescent Lot behind the stadium.
Parking & Road Closures
Thursday, May 22
Senior Convocation Event
Garden Ave. will be closed between Campus Road and Tower Road, from 10 a.m. - 3 p.m.
The Crescent Lot will close from 5 p.m. Thursday - 5 p.m. Sunday.
Any vehicles remaining after 5 p.m. will be towed from the parking lot.
Friday, May 23 Events (
Cornell Campus Map
)
Campus general parking lots are open to all guests attending individual
college events
throughout the day and evening. Please abide by individual space restrictions, ADA-designated spaces, and life safety zones.
The Sage South parking lot at Sage Hall will close at 5 p.m. Friday, 12 p.m. Monday, May 26

Saturday, May 24
Road Closures
Traffic will be prohibited on:
Campus Road between College Avenue to Wing Drive from 7 a.m. - 5 p.m.
Hoy Road will be closed from Campus Road to Hoy Parking Garage's lower entrance from 7 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
Garden Ave. will be closed between Campus Road and Tower Road, from 7 a.m. - 5 p.m.
Feeney Way will be closed between Campus Road and Tower Road, from 7 a.m. - 5 p.m.
