NEW: Find Facility Information Page Coming Soon!
Hello, Find Facility Information users,
We are excited to announce the new Find Facility Information page is launching on
Tuesday, September 19th, 2023
. Some of the enhancements include a modernized look and feel, new and advanced search filters and a mobile friendly experience.
In addition to the new version of this site, there is also a new URL:
https://findfacilities-prod.fcs.cornell.edu/searchfacilities
.
The current URL will go away entirely as of October 1st, 2023.
While navigating the new layout, feel free to reference the user’s guide that can be found in the top right corner of the Find Facility Information landing page, labeled “
Tips for using this tool
.”
If you require access to restricted areas related to this page please refer to the
request access page
.
Please direct any questions, comments, or concerns to
findfacilityinfo@cornell.edu
where we will address these as soon as possible.
Thank you, The Find Facility Information Project Team
