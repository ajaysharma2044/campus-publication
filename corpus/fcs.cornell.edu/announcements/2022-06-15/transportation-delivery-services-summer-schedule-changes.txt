Transportation & Delivery Services Summer Schedule Changes
In harmony with President Pollack's recent announcement, all
Transportation & Delivery Services
operations will close at 11 am on June 24, July 1, 8, 15, 22, and 29. Campus parking permit and payment requirements will be suspended on these dates except in reserved, MT, and accessible spaces and life safety zones. There will be no afternoon campus mail or courier delivery service.
The
Campus-to-Campus
executive coach service will adhere to its regular schedule through July 1 and have a service break from July 2 through July 31, resuming service on August 1.
Enjoy the
Summer of 2022 Employee Appreciation
!
