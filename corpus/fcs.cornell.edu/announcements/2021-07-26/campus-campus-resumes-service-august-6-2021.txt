Campus-to-Campus Resumes Service August 6, 2021
Campus-to-Campus
(C2C) will restart its popular executive coach service between the Ithaca and New York City campuses by August 6. The service, which was suspended in spring 2020 in response to the COVID-19 pandemic and related travel restrictions, will ease into operations with a reduced-frequency daily service limited to members of the Cornell University community.
C2C has enacted several proven
safety protocols
including implementing a mask requirement (regardless of vaccination status), performing regular cleaning of high-touch surfaces and installing enhanced filtration systems.
Initially, reservations will be limited to passengers with a Cornell NetID, and service will be reduced to two round trips per day, six days per week. Service will begin at full passenger capacity, and bookings are available through the end of September 2021.
To learn more about the Campus-to-Campus executive coach service or to
book a trip
, visit
c2cbus.com
.
