Event Parking & Transportation Impacts Oct. 29, 2021
Multiple events occurring on Oct. 29, 2021, along with a number of ongoing construction projects and road closures may impact traffic circulation and parking availability.
Oct. 29 marks the return of
Family Weekend
when the campus opens its doors to parents and family members, many of whom will be visiting Ithaca for the very first time.
Employee Celebration
is also taking place the evening of Oct. 29, as are
multiple sporting events
.
Please
plan your commute
with these events in mind, and avoid driving on campus if possible.
