FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Parking Restrictions Suspended Nov. 3, 2023
In keeping with the spirit of the Provost's announcement, permit restrictions and payment requirements are suspended on Friday, November 3, 2023. To avoid disrupting emergency vehicle and essential worker access, please avoid parking in fire lanes, loading zones, time-limited spaces, no parking areas, and accessible, MT, and reserved spaces.
