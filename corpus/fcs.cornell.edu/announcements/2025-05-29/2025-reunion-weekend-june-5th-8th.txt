2025 Reunion Weekend June 5th-8th
Get Ready to Celebrate —
Cornell Reunion Weekend 2025!
Over
7,000 alumni and guests
are gearing up to return to campus for an unforgettable Reunion Weekend from
Thursday, June 5 through Sunday, June 8
. It’s a special time to reconnect, reminisce, and make new memories at our beloved Cornell University!
Important Parking Info for Reunion Weekend
To make room for our returning alumni and the amazing vendors helping to make this event so memorable, several parking lots on North Campus will be reserved during Reunion Weekend:
Robert Purcell Lot, Program House Drive, and Appel Commons Lot
will be set aside for guest move-in and parking from
Thursday, June 5 to Sunday, June 8
.
The
A Lot
will be reserved for reunion classes housed on North Campus starting
Friday, June 6 through Sunday, June 8
.
Friday, June 6 Parking Accommodations
Permit and payment requirements will be
suspended campus-wide on Friday, June 6
, except in the lots mentioned above, as well as accessible and reserved parking (including MT spaces) and life safety zones.
Plan Ahead & Get Around Smart!
We encourage everyone to plan their commute with Reunion Weekend in mind and avoid driving on campus if possible. Consider these great alternatives:
Use a park-and-ride
Work remotely (if your schedule allows)
Carpool with friends
Take the TCAT bus (running on a summer schedule—visit
tcatbus.com
for routes and schedules)
Bike or walk to campus
Let’s all make this Reunion Weekend a smooth and joyous celebration! See you on campus!
