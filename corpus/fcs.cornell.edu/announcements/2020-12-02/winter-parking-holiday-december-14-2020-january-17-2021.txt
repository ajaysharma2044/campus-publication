Winter Parking Holiday December 14, 2020 - January 17, 2021
In order to relieve the burden on Cornell community members who must be on campus during the staff winter break period, Transportation Services will not enforce regular parking permit restrictions,
with the exception of a few areas
, December 14, 2020, through January 17, 2021. Parking permits and pay station/mobile payments will not be required in most campus parking spaces during this period.
Cornell Transportation Services
will continue to enforce
fire lanes, loading zones, time-limited spaces, no parking areas, and accessible, MT, and reserved spaces.
Please comply with
Winter parking restrictions
, in effect from December 1 to April 1.
Please contact Cornell Transportation (607-255-4600,
transportation@cornell.edu
) if you have any questions, and have a safe and happy winter break!
