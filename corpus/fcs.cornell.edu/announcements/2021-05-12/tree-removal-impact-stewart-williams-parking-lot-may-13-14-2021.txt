Tree Removal to Impact Stewart-Williams Parking Lot May 13-14, 2021
Emergency tree removal will impact parking in the SW lot located at the intersection of Stewart and Williams Streets on May 13 and 14, 2021. The north end of the lot (nearest the gorge) will be blocked for crane set-up and tree lay down, closing approximately 25 spaces.
All vehicles must be removed from the affected area by 3 pm on May 12
. Permit holders are being instructed to park in the Edgemoor Lane and South Avenue lots during the closure.
Questions about parking can be directed to
transportation@cornell.edu
.
