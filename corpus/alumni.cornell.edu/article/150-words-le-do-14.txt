150 words: Le Do ’14
By
AAD Staff
November 4, 2014
Not even a month after he graduated from the School of Hotel Administration, Le Do was back on campus for Reunion. “To be honest, I still feel like a student, and I always will,” said Le, an active class council member for the past three years and a recently elected alumni class officer.
Now working for a real estate company in Houston, Texas, Le plans to apply to Cornell Law School within three years. He dreams of founding a not-for-profit educational institution in Vietnam—his country of origin—in the future.
He stays in touch with Cornell in many ways, including managing his class’s Facebook page, attending the most recent Homecoming celebration, and contributing
his spirited rendition
of the alma mater for the
Cornell Global Choir
.
Le sees his recording as a ballad for his beloved university. “I want to share her beautiful attributes with everybody,” he explained.
