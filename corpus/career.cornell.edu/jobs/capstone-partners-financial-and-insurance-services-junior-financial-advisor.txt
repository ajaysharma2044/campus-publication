Junior Financial Advisor
Credence Financial Group is seeking a motivated, detail-oriented, and client-focused
Junior Financial Advisor
to support senior advisors in delivering comprehensive financial planning and wealth management services. This role provides an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in client relationship management, financial planning, investment analysis, and business development.
The ideal candidate is eager to grow within the financial services industry, possesses strong analytical and communication skills, and is committed to helping clients achieve their financial goals.
Key Responsibilities
Client & Advisor Support
Assist senior advisor(s) in preparing for client meetings and reviews.
Gather, organize, and analyze client financial information to support the planning process.
Prepare financial planning recommendations, reports, presentations, and investment review materials.
Serve as a primary point of contact for client service requests and account inquiries.
Support account opening, transfers, beneficiary updates, and other service-related transactions.
Coordinate with custodians, insurance carriers, and other financial institutions to facilitate client needs and resolve issues.
Maintain accurate client records, notes, and documentation in CRM and planning systems.
Follow up on client action items, outstanding requirements, and implementation activities.
Financial Planning & Investment Support
Assist in developing financial plans covering retirement, insurance, estate, education, and wealth accumulation goals.
Support investment research, portfolio reviews, and asset allocation analysis.
Prepare illustrations, proposals, and planning scenarios using financial planning software.
Stay current on market trends, investment products, insurance solutions, and industry developments.
Participate in client meetings and take detailed notes to support follow-up and implementation.
Business Development & Operations
Assist with prospect onboarding and new client relationship development.
Help coordinate client seminars, educational events, and marketing initiatives.
Prepare reports, spreadsheets, and operational documentation.
Identify opportunities to improve client experience and internal processes.
Collaborate with advisors, operations staff, and external partners to ensure efficient service delivery.
Qualifications & Requirements
1-3 years of experience in financial services, wealth management, banking, insurance, or a client-facing financial role preferred.
Strong interest in financial planning, investment management, and wealth advisory services.
Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
Ability to build rapport and maintain professional client relationships.
Experience with Microsoft Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Teams.
High level of integrity and ability to handle confidential financial information.
Self-motivated with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
FINRA Series 7 and Series 66 licenses, or willingness to obtain them, preferred.
California Life & Health Insurance License, or willingness to obtain, preferred.
