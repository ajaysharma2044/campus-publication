Office of Career Development
Cornell’s Jeb E. Brooks School Office of Career Development provides students with a comprehensive suite of services to get you up and running for your internship and job prospects. Our services include career advising, career exploration and assessment, interview preparation, and career development workshops and courses. Career Development is committed to equipping you with the tools you need to put your degree to work in the competitive job market, whether you’re a current student or an alum.
