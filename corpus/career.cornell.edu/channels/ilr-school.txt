ILR School Career Development Mission Statement
The ILR School Career Development empowers students and alumni to navigate career exploration, pursue meaningful opportunities, and develop the skills and confidence needed for lifelong professional success. Through individualized support, experiential learning, and strong employer and alumni partnerships, we prepare the ILR community to lead and thrive across diverse industries and sectors.
