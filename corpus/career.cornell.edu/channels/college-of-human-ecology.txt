Office of Career Development
Human Ecology’s Career Development staff provide a welcoming environment for students to explore career paths, connect with professionals, and build industry-ready skills.  Our specialized staff and student career assistants offer dedicated career support to prepare students to make an impact in their careers and within human lives.
