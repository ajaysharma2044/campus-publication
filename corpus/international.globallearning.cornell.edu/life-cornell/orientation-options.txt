Orientation Options
For Students
Welcome to Cornell!
The International Services team is here to
help you settle in
and find out what you need to know to succeed at Cornell. We offer several pre-orientation and orientation options for international students. We look forward to meeting you!
Prepare for Undergraduates
Prepare is the International Services' preorientation program for incoming international undergraduates. You’ll learn about the U.S. academic system, Cornell's student life and traditions, and campus opportunities for the international community. Prepare takes place annually in August, during the week before
Cornell's new student orientation
.
Orientation for Graduate and Professional Students
Our international graduate and professional student orientation takes place biannually in August (just before the start of the fall semester) and January (just before the start of the spring semester). This half-day orientation covers a variety of topics: U.S. immigration and tax, transitioning to a new academic culture, academic expectations, health and safety, and more.
