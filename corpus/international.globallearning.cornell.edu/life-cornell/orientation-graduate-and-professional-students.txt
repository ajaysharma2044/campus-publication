For Students

The Office of Global Learning team is here to
help you settle in
and find out what you need to know to succeed at Cornell. For international graduate and professional students starting at Cornell, we offer a robust in-person orientation. We look forward to meeting you!
International Graduate and Professional Student Orientation, hosted by International Services, gives you an early start on your Cornell experience by introducing you to important resources for international students and helping you connect with your classmates from around the world.
