Submit Your Visa Request
Ready to submit your visa request?
This page provides the know-how you need to complete Step 1 of the
Sponsorship Process
and then manage the remaining prearrival steps. Before you read more, please review the sponsorship process and who is responsible for each step.
In Step 1, your department or unit confirms the academic appointment, funding, employment eligibility, and employment dates with the scholar. Then you or an authorized representative of your department creates a record for the scholar if needed and completes the first section of the visa request in myStatus.
What is myStatus?
MyStatus is Cornell's
international case management platform. It is integrated with the Department of Homeland Security's
Student and Exchange Visitor Information System
(SEVIS)
for federal compliance.
International Services uses myStatus to process visa requests, manage immigration records, and maintain compliance for all Cornell-sponsored international scholars and students. We also rely on it to communicate with our international population and generate reports.
Department administrators play a key role in myStatus by initiating most visa requests—such as J-1, H-1B, O-1, and permanent residency requests—on behalf of incoming and continuing international scholars. In myStatus, you can review the department's current and closed cases and see your scholars' immigration documents.

The incoming employee needs a myStatus record before you can open a visa request. If your new scholar already has a seven-digit employee ID assigned during Cornell onboarding, you can skip this step. Use the employee ID number to start the visa request.

3
Complete and submit the request forms.
In myStatus, you'll find request options for various visa types and situations. Select the appropriate request for your scholar, and complete all forms within the request. After you submit the department forms, you'll get an email from myStatus acknowledging receipt, and the employee will get a myStatus invitation to complete the scholar section.

H-1B Request
All types of H-1B petitions require similar information from the department. Departments submit initial H-1B requests on behalf of employees who are new to H-1B status (for example, currently in F-1 or J-1 status), and portability requests for those already working in H-1B status for a different employer. H-1B extension and amendment requests are for employees who are already in H-1B status at Cornell. 4
Pay any required USCIS fee.
Visa petitions submitted to U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) require a filing fee. In some cases, the fee must be paid by the department; in others, the fee may be paid by either the department or scholar.
At this stage, you must pay any required fee or notify the scholar to pay it. Find information about current fees for J-1s on
Invite or Extend J-1 Visitors
and fees for H-1Bs and other visa types in the pages under
Employment Visas
.
If the department is paying: Submit a "USCIS fee payment request" in addition to your visa request. You’ll be asked to select fees and provide an account number.
If the scholar is paying: The department needs to notify the scholar of the obligation to pay, as well as the fees and amounts. Scholars drop off or mail a signed authorization for credit card transactions (Form G-1450) to our
campus office for scholars
.
Steps 2–4: After the myStatus Request
Please review the remaining prearrival steps of the
Sponsorship Process
. Most visa requests have two sections. The department completes one section of the visa request form (Step 1).
Submitting it triggers a myStatus invitation to the scholar to complete the second section of the request (Step 2). Most scholars can bring dependents—spouses and unmarried children under the age of 21—to join them in the United States. Scholars provide information about their dependents in their portion of the myStatus visa request.
When both of these steps are complete in myStatus, International Services begins processing your request (Step 3). We'll keep you updated throughout the processing and reach out if we need further information or evidence from you or your scholar.
For
J-1 scholars
: International Services creates a DS-2019.
For
J-1 student interns

For

After International Services shares these visa documents in myStatus, the scholar reviews them for accuracy (Step 4)—concluding the steps that must be completed before the new employee travels to campus.
Mandatory Consultation for Permanent Teaching Positions
Don't forget your new employee's
consultation with International Services
. This meeting is required for all
foreign nationals accepting permanent teaching positions—including tenure-track faculty, lecturers, instructors, and other teaching staff with an expectation of permanent employment.
The department is responsible for scheduling the consultation with International Services, department representatives, and the incoming scholar (with spouse or partner, if desired). It should be held shortly after the job offer is accepted.
