Invite Visitors and Interns
Cornell departments and units bring a variety of international scholars and students—including visiting researchers and speakers, undergraduate research interns, and graduate and undergraduate nondegree students—to campus for short academic visits, typically under one year.

Pathways for Visitors and Interns
B-1/B-2 Business and Tourist Visitors
B-1/B-2 visitor
status best serves s
peakers, short-term independent researchers
, and people planning short visits to one or several U.S. universities. Note that some Cornell colleges or departments may have policies that preclude this choice for liability reasons.
Pluses and Minuses for B-1/B-2 Visitors
Can be reimbursed for expenses (travel, food, lodging)—but be careful about recording exactly what is being reimbursed.
Must have accident and medical insurance.
Will not qualify for university programs as visitor.
Cannot be paid to work on campus, with the exception of B-1s at Cornell for nine days or less.
Might not meet university requirements for volunteers
. Generally, if a person is not allowed to work in the United States due to a visa restriction, then he or she is also prohibited from working as a university volunteer.
Cannot register for classes at Cornell.
Might have trouble obtaining B-1/B-2 visa stamp at U.S. consulates or gaining entry to the U.S. as B-1/B-2 or visa waiver visitor to come to Cornell to do research.
J-1 Research or Short-Term Scholars (PhD or graduate)
The J-1 research scholar and short-term scholar categories are
J-1 exchange visitor visas
for those who will be given academic appointments or appointed as research interns.
The J-1 research scholar and short-term scholar categories are meant for people qualified to perform advanced teaching and research. Graduate students must be actively working toward a master's degree or PhD.
Pluses and Minuses for J-1 Research or Short-Term Scholars
Can work on campus up to 40 hours per week (paid or unpaid).
Can be reimbursed for expenses (travel, food, and lodging).
Must have an academic appointment. Interns may be appointed for no more than 12 months.
Must have a bachelor’s degree and be working on a master’s or higher degree. For some positions, a doctorate is required.
Must have sufficient
financial support
.
Must have
health insurance
if temporary or part-time less than 20 hours (not benefits-eligible).
J-1 Student Interns (undergraduate)
The J-1 student intern program gives international students currently pursuing undergraduate degrees outside the United States the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in their fields at Cornell. J-1 interns work alongside Cornell faculty members in internships that may extend up to 12 months. Carefully review the
J-1 Student Interns
page if you are considering this status for an intern.
Pluses and Minuses for J-1 Student Interns
May be paid or unpaid. To be employed, however, the student intern must receive approval from his or her home institution's dean or academic advisor.
Must pay an administrative fee.
Must complete a minimum of 32 hours per week of internship activity, with no more than 20 percent clerical work.
Cannot displace American workers or fill a labor need. The internship must exist solely to assist the intern in achieving educational objectives and must consist of work-based learning, rather than ordinary employment or unskilled labor.
F-1 Nondegree Students (graduate or undergraduate)
Visit the
Nondegree Students
page for a range of nondegree pathways, including the International Research Internship Program (IRIP) managed by the School of Continuing Education.
Nondegree students
must obtain F-1 visa status, unless already in the United States in a status that allows enrollment. The visiting student cannot be in B-1/B-2 status.
Pluses and Minuses for F-1 Nondegree Students
Can work on campus for up to 20 hours per week (paid or unpaid).
Can be reimbursed for travel expenses (travel, food, and lodging).
Must be registered full-time at Cornell during all semesters, including summer.
Must pay tuition.
Must pay for their own accident and medical insurance.
