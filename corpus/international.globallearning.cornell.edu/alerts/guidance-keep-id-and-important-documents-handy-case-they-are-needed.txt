Guidance: Keep ID and Important Documents Handy in Case They Are Needed
January 31, 2025
Detailed information on
what to do if external (non-Cornell) law enforcement arrives
is now on the Information on Executive Orders website. This is a rapidly evolving situation, and we understand that our students and scholars are concerned about what documentation to have on hand.
We recommend that all students carry some form of photo ID (i.e., school ID, driver's license). If you are an international student or scholar and in the U.S. in F-1, J-1, or other visa status, we additionally recommend that you have an image or PDF of your I-94 record of entry on your phone or know how to
access it online
.
Know where to locate these
emergency numbers
:
International Services: +1 607-255-5243 (8:30 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.)
Cornell University Police Department (CUPD): +1 607-255-5111
If arrested or held at a U.S. Border, call our
International SOS hotline
at +1 215 942-8478 and reference Cornell's membership number: 11BSCA827281.
The
Get Advice
page has these numbers and additional contact information.
We will continue to monitor the situation and provide additional guidance as new information becomes available.
