Guidance: Order Expands H-1B Program Oversight
September 22, 2026
The White House has issued two related measures that may impact Cornell employees on H-1B visas and hiring units in coming weeks and months.
This alert focuses on a new executive order, “Enhancing Program Integrity and Interagency Coordination in the Administration of the H-1B Nonimmigrant Visa Program.”
Please also review our
updated alert
on the administration's renewal of a $100,000 H-1B fee requirement.
The fee is currently blocked due to legal challenges.
Read the
White House fact sheet
announcing the two measures.
"Enhancing Program Integrity" Order
According to the White House, the September 18
executive order
is designed to reduce H-1B abuses, strengthen oversight, and increase coordination across federal agencies. It requires the agencies reviewing H-1B applications to consider whether the employer sponsoring an H-1B petition has recently laid off U.S. workers in similar jobs.
More to Come
International Services is closely monitoring agency actions responding to this executive order. We will update this alert as agencies issue new guidance about how they plan to implement the order.
We will also communicate directly with Cornell’s H-1B visa holders about any immediate impacts. In the meantime, please
reach out
if you have questions.
