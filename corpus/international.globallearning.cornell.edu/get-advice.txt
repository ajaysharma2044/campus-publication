Get Advice
To streamline your case, please contact us by opening a ticket. We are also available to support you in person and on Zoom.
Get in Touch: Open a Ticket
International Services' ticket system is the best way to get advice
in most cases. When you open a ticket, you'll find information you need quickly and get connected with an advisor if you need individual help.
Your questions and requests are important to us.
Urgent questions
are answered as soon as possible. Advisors respond to all other tickets in the order received. Submitting multiple tickets for the same request does not speed up the response. Thanks for your patience!
Open a Ticket
Cornell Tech Students:
For the fastest response from a Cornell Tech advisor,
open a ticket
with Student & Academic Affairs.
Appointments
Advisors offer 10-minute advising appointments for international students, in person at 300 Caldwell Hall or by Zoom. Please schedule
no more than one appointment per day
so that we can accommodate as many students as possible. Extended appointments (longer than 10 minutes) are available upon request by
opening a ticket
.
Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m–12:00 p.m. and 2:00–4:00 p.m. ET

Emergency Help
Cornell provides resources! Find out how to get the help you need at any hour.
Stopped at the Border
: Our International SOS hotline is here to help you navigate the situation.
Victim of Violence
: The Cornell University Police Department (CUPD) is here to support you.
Urgent Questions
The following questions are considered urgent:
Status or document (e.g. I-20 or DS-2019) expiration within a week
Return to the U.S. within three days and travel signature or document required
Financial Emergencies
Limited funding support may be available to international students experiencing a health care or family emergency.
Email us
for additional information. Eligible expenses:
International travel expenses due to serious illness or death in the immediate family
Emergency medical expenses not covered by insurance
Essential and emergency dental work

Request Travel Signatures
If your I-20 or DS-2019 travel signature has expired:
Request a travel signature online
, and we will mail you the electronically signed document. Please note that a digital I-20 or DS-2019 with an electronic signature is fully acceptable for your travel needs. You do not need to make an appointment to request a travel signature.
