Transfer from Cornell
For Students
If you are an F-1 or J-1 student, and you want to study at a different U.S. college or university, you’ll need to transfer your immigration status to your new school.
This transfer is required even if you are graduating, if you plan to enter a degree program (for example, a law school or Ph.D. program, for example) at a new school. The only exception is summer-only programs during your time at Cornell.

Be sure to discuss any interim travel or work plans you have with one of our advisors, as these plans may affect your transfer procedure.
Are there additional timing factors I should consider?
You should choose a date to release your SEVIS record to your new school based on your last day of enrollment at Cornell, including any final exams and papers. We cannot release your SEVIS record until you have finished your academic program at Cornell.
If you are on F-1 OPT/OPT STEM or J-1 Academic Training and transferring to a new school, your transfer release date must be after your last day of employment, as your work authorization will end as of the transfer release date.
If you'll be traveling outside the country between programs, make sure to give your new school enough time to create your new I-20, as you will need it to return to the U.S. Talk to your new school to find out how long their processing will take.
3
Be sure you meet your deadline!
It’s best to start the transfer process earlier, but F-1 and J-1 students receive a grace period.
If you are graduating from Cornell, and you plan to go on to another program at another school, you must discuss your transfer with International Services no later than the end of this grace period:
F-1: No more than 60 days after graduation
J-1: No more than 30 days after graduation
We need to enter your transfer information into the SEVIS database before the end of your grace period. Your release date can be later.
4
Complete the Transfer Out form
.
Please complete the Transfer Out form to have your current SEVIS F-1 or J-1 student record transferred from Cornell University to another SEVP certified institution.
