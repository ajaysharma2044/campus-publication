Alerts
Alerts keep you informed about evolving U.S. immigration and travel policies and provide time-sensitive guidance for Cornell's international community.
Current Travel Advisory
November 4, 2026
If you're planning international travel, be aware that U.S. federal travel restrictions continue to evolve. Read our updated guidance to prepare for your fall and winter travel.
Guidance: DHS Proposes New OPT Fees
October 8, 2026
DHS has proposed substantial new fees to be paid by universities for all initial and follow-on F-1 OPT applications. Cornell opposes the proposal and will submit a comment during the public comment period.
Guidance: Order Expands H-1B Program Oversight
September 22, 2026
A recent executive order increases interagency review and scrutiny of employers’ layoff history during the H-1B filing process.
Update: H-1B Fee Increase Extended
September 22, 2026
A new presidential proclamation renews a $100,000 H-1B fee payable by employers. The fee is currently blocked due to legal challenges and is not in effect.
Guidance: Rule Ending D/S Temporarily Blocked
September 15, 2026
The rule ending duration of status will not take effect on September 15. Your D/S remains intact while a preliminary injunction blocking implementation is in place.
Proposed OPT Fee Rule Awaits Publication
September 14, 2026
A proposed rule requiring a fee for F-1 OPT cleared White House review last week. We will share more details after it is published.
Guidance: DHS Proposes Eliminating Grace Period for Employment Visas
September 11, 2026
A proposed rule would eliminate the current 60-day grace period for nonimmigrants on visas including H-1B, O-1, TN, and E-3.
Guidance: F-1 CPT Eligibility Advisory
August 31, 2026
August SEVP messages significantly narrow the past interpretation of CPT eligibility criteria. We have contacted students with pending CPT applications.
Update: DHS Ends Duration of Status
July 27, 2026
DHS has replaced duration of status (D/S) with fixed periods of stay for F and J visa holders, effective September 15. Read a detailed analysis and guidance representing Cornell immigration experts’ current understanding of the new rule.
Guidance: USCIS Policy Memo for Green Card Applicants
May 27, 2026
USCIS has issued a policy memo related to the final step of the permanent residency application process. The policy does not affect green card eligibility.
Guidance: D/S Changes Expected
May 11, 2026
The rule ending duration of status (D/S) will be published soon. We will share specific details and guidance by email when the final rule is published.
Guidance: Proposed Prevailing Wage Change for H-1B, E-3, and EB-2 Workers
April 2, 2026
A proposed federal rule would significantly increase required wage levels for employers sponsoring H-1B, E-3, and EB-2 workers. We will share additional guidance if the rule takes effect.

Update: New and Continuing Travel Ban
February 4, 2026
A significant expansion of the travel ban took effect on January 1. USCIS has also paused processing of new and pending immigration applications from the 39 countries, creating delays and uncertainty.
Guidance: New Immigrant Visas Paused for 75 Countries
January 15, 2026
The U.S. will stop issuing new immigrant visas to people applying from 75 countries. Most of Cornell’s international community is not affected by this measure.
Update: Visa Interview Scheduling and Screening
December 5, 2025
U.S. embassies and consulates are following enhanced protocols for screening new F/J visa applicants’ digital footprints. H-1B applicants will be screened beginning Dec. 15.
Guidance: Apply for Visas in Your Home Country
September 19, 2025
Applicants for nonimmigrant visas (including F/J) should schedule visa interview appointments in their country of nationality or residence, according to new instructions from the State Department.

Guidance: Scheduling Pause for Visa Appointments
