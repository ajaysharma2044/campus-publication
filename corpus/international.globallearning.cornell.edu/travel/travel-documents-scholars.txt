For Faculty/Staff
When you travel as a Cornell faculty or staff member, you’ll need to carry various documents to prove your immigration status and allow reentry if you leave the United States.
If you’re leaving the United States and you need a new visa stamp,
find out what you need to do
. If you have further questions,
reach out to us
.
J-1 Documents
Valid passport
Valid entry visa stamp (Canadians excepted)
Valid DS-2019 showing J-1 status,
signed for travel
by an International Services advisor within the last year. (A travel signature is valid for 12 months or until the DS-2019 end date, whichever is earlier.)
Letter from your Cornell host department/unit confirming continued employment
J-1 Dependent (J-2) Documents
The spouse or children of an J-1 scholar traveling with the scholar need the following documents to reenter the United States in J-2 status:
Valid passport
Valid J-2 entry visa stamp
Valid DS-2019 showing J-2 status, with a valid
travel signature
. A travel signature is valid for 12 months or until the DS-2019 end date, whichever is earlier.
Proof of financial support
If the J-2 dependents of an J-1 scholar are traveling without the scholar, they need all the above documents, plus the following copies of the J-1 scholar's documents:
J-1 entry visa stamp
Most recent
I-94 arrival record
as proof of J-1 status
DS-2019

H-1B/O-1 Dependent (H-4/O-3) Documents
The spouse or children of an H-1B/O-1 scholar traveling with the scholar need the following documents to reenter the United States in dependent status:
Passport valid beyond H-1B/O-1 end date
Valid H-4/O-3 entry visa stamp (Canadians excepted)
Proof of relationship with H-1B/O-1 visa holder (e.g., marriage certificate, birth certificate, adoption certificate)
If you filed an
I-539 application
: Form I-797, H-4 Approval Notice from USCIS
If traveling without the scholar H-1B/O-1 scholar, they need all the above documents, plus the following copies of the H-1B/O-1 scholar's documents:
H-1B/O-1 visa entry stamp (Canadians excepted)
Most recent
I-94 arrival record
as proof of H-1B/O-1 status
I-797 approval notice for H-1B/O-1 status
