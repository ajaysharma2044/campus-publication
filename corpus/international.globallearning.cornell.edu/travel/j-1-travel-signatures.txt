For Faculty/Staff
If you are a J-1 exchange visitor and you plan to travel outside the United States during your time at Cornell, you need a valid DS-2019 with travel signature to reenter the country.
To obtain a new signature, either come to the office
during advising hours
with your original DS-2019 (as well as your J-2 dependents' original DS-2019s if they also will need a travel signature) OR request a reprint of your DS-2019 via our
I-20/DS-2019 reprint request form
. You will select whether you want to pick up the reprint in person, have it securely sent as a PDF, or have it express mailed (payment instructions are included in the request link). Express mail is required for international shipments.
Travel signatures are valid for twelve (12) months for J-1 professors and research scholars and for six (6) months for short-term scholars. You may be able to travel multiple times on one travel signature.
If your
J-1 program is extended
, we will give you a new DS-2019 with travel signature. If you have questions about your travel signature, here's how you can
get advice
.
Other Visa Types Don’t Need Travel Signatures
You don’t need to worry about travel signatures if your visa type is H-1B, O-1, TN, or E-3.
