Work Authorization for International Students
Part of the Work Talks series, this presentation for international students discusses the work authorization process for F-1 and J-1 students as you get close to graduation, as well as options for summer employment while you are still enrolled.
Visas After Graduation
This presentation for international students provides an overview of possible options for working long-term in the U.S. after graduation, including becoming a permanent resident (green card holder), if you desire. Working with the law firm of Miller Mayer, we offer the presentation every fall and spring semester.
Check out our Visas After Graduation page.
International Student Entrepreneurs and Immigration
This presentation is for international students with an interest in founding a business or company in the U.S. Anna Angel of Miller Mayer answers questions about how to safely navigate U.S. immigration rules as an entrepreneur.

OPT with STEM extension
