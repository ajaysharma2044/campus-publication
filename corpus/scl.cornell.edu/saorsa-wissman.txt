Saorsa Wissman (she/her) is the Program Assistant for the Office of Spirituality and Meaning-Making (OSMM). She coordinates the office for OSMM, manages scheduling and operations for Anabel Taylor and Sage Chapel, and supervises the OSMM student office workers.
Saorsa is a queer, crip, pagan who recently completed an M.Div. from Union Theological Seminary, including two years of fieldwork as an interfaith chaplain at Colgate University. For the past year, she served in a dual role as both administrative and interfaith program coordinator at Hamilton College. Saorsa is passionate about supporting programming, events, and logistics for the OSMM as she believes this is crucial for building spiritual community and well-being at Cornell.
