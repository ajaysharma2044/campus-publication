Santi Tabares Erices
2023 Emerging Leader
Santi Tabares Erices is an undergraduate in the College of Agriculture and Life Science majoring in Environment & Sustainability at Cornell University with a concentration in Environmental Biology and Applied Ecology. He has worked on mammalian torpor and cultural evolution in lyrebirds and is interested in physiology and chemical ecology in birds, reptiles, and amphibians. Outside of class, Santi enjoys birding, herping, rock climbing, and baking.
