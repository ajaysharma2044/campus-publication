Kiara Walker is expected to graduate May 2024 from Binghamton University with a Masters in Music. She has just over five years of experience working in higher education. After graduating with a Bachelor of Arts from Valdosta State University, she moved to New York City to work for New York University as a  StudentLink Representative. She then shifted gears and started working at The Juilliard School as the Schedule and Program Associate for the Vocal Arts Department. While completing her masters course work, she held an office position in the Binghamton University Music Department. In her down time, Kiara enjoys outdoor activities such as hiking, snowboarding, and traveling with her dog Indy.
