Emmett Mitterando
Summer Super Lead Facilitator
Emmett Mitterando is passionate about experiential learning and holds an AS in Outdoor Recreation. He has been with CTLC since 2022 after moving to Ithaca from NJ. Emmett is a Wilderness First Responder and a Certified Level I Instructor with the American Association of Snowboard Instructors. Now living in Newark Valley, Emmett loves spending time with his dogs, chickens, and ducks. During the winter, he can be found at the nearby mountain teaching snowboarding and coaching with Greek Peak Adaptive Snowsports. In working with the adaptive program and CTLC, Emmett wishes to increase his repertoire in instructing participants of all abilities, ages, and backgrounds in new outdoor experiences.
