Campus Activities
Welcome to Campus Activities at Cornell!
The Campus Activities Office is your hub for out-of-classroom experiences that help you grow, connect, and thrive at Cornell. We support your involvement in student organizations, leadership development, and campus traditions—creating opportunities that promote personal growth, creativity, and community.
Through our office, you can:
Connect with 1,000+ student organizations
Get guidance on everything from organization registration to leadership transitions
Attend skill-building Leadership Workshops each semester
Participate in iconic campus events like ClubFest, Slope Day, and Movies on the Arts Quad
We’re here to help you:
Build a sense of belonging and community
Explore diversity and develop intercultural understanding
Enhance your leadership, interpersonal, and self-awareness skills
Prioritize well-being and social responsibility
Prepare for your role as an engaged global citizen
Whether you're joining a club, starting a new initiative, or just looking to get involved, Campus Activities is here to support your journey at Cornell—every step of the way.
