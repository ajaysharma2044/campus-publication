Alumnae Panhellenic Advisory Council
The Alumnae Panhellenic Advisory Council (APAC) is a volunteer group of alumnae each specializing in a specific area of interest for the benefit and consultation of sorority alumnae advisors on the Cornell University campus.
Objectives
To keep sorority alumnae advisors at Cornell University aware of current trends,
To promote and be an advocate for the sorority system at Cornell University,
To improve the sorority image on the Cornell University campus, and
To stimulate a continuing interest in Panhellenic affairs and development on the Cornell University campus.
