College & School Links
The information, forms, and to-do's on this site are for all new students. There may be additional tasks that your college needs you to do. Many of the college sites below are updated over time, so check them often. Your college will also contact you via your Cornell email address.
College of Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS)
Got questions? Of course you do, so we've made it easy to get answers. First, relax and enjoy your time with family and friends before the semester begins. Second, begin to check your new Cornell email and Chatter regularly! We'll be in touch to provide you with insider info on everything from course enrollment to where to find the best ice cream in Ithaca. Welcome to CALS!

College of Architecture, Art, and Planning (AAP)
Welcome to the extraordinary AAP community of designers, architects, and scholars! During the summer, the AAP Office of Student Services will be your primary contact. This office provides information, support, and programming to encourage student success from matriculation through graduation. Our staff is committed to fostering an environment that values creativity, critical thinking, and respect of diverse perspectives enabling AAP students to achieve their fullest potential. SC Johnson College of Business
Welcome to the SC Johnson College of Business! As a college we strive to create a student experience that is flexible, collaborative, and cross-disciplinary as we build on the strengths of three world-class schools at Cornell—Dyson, the Hotel School, and Johnson. New undergraduate students will be supported by dedicated staff based in Dyson and Hotel—important information from each School’s Office of Student Services follows.
For all students, information will be sent to your Cornell email address—check your email often and read emails in their entirety.
Charles H. Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management
Welcome to the Dyson community! The Dyson Office of Student Services and Advising team will reach out via your Cornell email in June to share information about our Discover Dyson Summer Series. This program includes a weekly email, virtual sessions to prepare students for course enrollment, and other information to allow students to feel comfortable as they join us in the fall. Students will also have time in mid-July to meet with an Academic Advisor.

College of Human Ecology
Welcome to the College of Human Ecology!
Please visit the site below to verify your major between June 8-30. Our site includes very specific advice for creating your first semester Cornell course schedule. Be sure to read through the site before course enrollment begins. Each department in the college has provided detailed scheduling information, so base your selections on that information. You will finalize your choices during Orientation after participating in the college briefing and mandatory department advising meetings.
