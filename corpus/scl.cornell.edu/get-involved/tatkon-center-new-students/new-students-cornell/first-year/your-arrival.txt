Your Arrival
We are excited to welcome you to Cornell!
Move-In
New Student move-in will occur on August 19 and 20. New students will select a move-in date in June. Look for email communications from Housing and Residential Life with specific details about move-in including instructions for printing your unloading permit.
Learn more about Cornell move-in
.
Shipping Items to Cornell
If you wish to ship items to Cornell before your arrival, please review the instructions for
addressing mail and packages
. There are two service providers who can have items placed in your room if you order before their deadlines. Learn more about
pre-arrival shipping deadlines
.
Family Orientation
Parents and families are invited to join us for Family Orientation and are welcome to explore campus on August 19 & 20.
