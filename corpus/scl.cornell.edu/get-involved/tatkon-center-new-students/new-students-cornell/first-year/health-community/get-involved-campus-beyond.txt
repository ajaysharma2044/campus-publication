Get Involved: Campus & Beyond
Life outside the classroom at Cornell is rich and varied. As you become more acquainted with the campus community, take advantage of the many extracurricular opportunities Cornell has to offer.
You may opt to explore something totally new or continue being involved in a long-time interest, or both. Joining campus organizations, volunteering, and participating in special programs will help you connect with others and learn more about yourself and your new community.
