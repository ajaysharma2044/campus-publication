Fitness, Intramurals & Recreation
Recreational Services
Recreational Services offers fitness, wellness, sports and open recreation activities for the Cornell community. This includes intramural sports and recreation. Recreational services is a wonderful resource if you are looking for fun, social, and low-cost ways to stay active as a Cornellian.

Cornell Outdoor Education (COE)
Cornell Outdoor Education
is the largest and most comprehensive collegiate outdoor education program in the country. Programs, classes, and leadership opportunities abound.
Club Sports
There are dozens of
club sports
at Cornell. Some sports are highly selective and others welcome students who are interested in trying something new.
Physical Education
Anyone can enroll in a
physical education class
to improve their fitness level and to learn new skills. Dozens of courses from water skiing and fly fishing to soccer and tennis are offered throughout the year. It is a
graduation requirement
for undergraduate students to complete two semesters of physical education.
Tips & Topics
Get outside
The
Cornell Botanic Gardens
offer miles of trails and beautiful gardens and green space for walking, running, hiking, or just relaxing.
Safe swimming
Cornell’s gorges are beautiful and safe for hiking–when on designated trails—but
not for swimming
. There are numerous
safe and scenic swimming options
on or near campus.
