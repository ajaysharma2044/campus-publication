Health Insurance
Student Health Insurance
Cornell requires that all active students have health insurance that provides access to comprehensive care.
All full-time and/or degree-seeking students must either confirm enrollment in the Student Health Plan (SHP) or, if eligible, waive this coverage by providing proof of other insurance meeting University requirements. With
limited exceptions
, students who do not confirm or waive coverage will be enrolled in the Student Health Plan by default, and the
charge
for this coverage will be posted to their bursar account.
Waivers in SHP are reverified each semester, and students may be required to provide updated insurance information to maintain their waiver or be enrolled in SHP. Some students
returning from leave
may also be required to reapply for a waiver.
The
Student Health Plan
provides excellent coverage for
medical services
and
prescriptions
in Ithaca (including at
Cornell Health
), New York City, nationally, and
internationally
.
