Alexies Benitez
2023 Emerging Leader
Alexies is an undergraduate student at Cornell, originally from Santa Ana, CA. She is in the College of Human Ecology while studying Human Biology Health and Society in the Division of Nutritional Sciences. On campus, she is the Co-Conference Chair of BBMTA, the president of Cornell Pre-SOMA, a teacher assistant for the general chemistry courses, member of the Cornell ACLU Association, and Cornell Speech and Debate. Alexies loves to learn about social factors, and how those make an impact on a person's life. In her free time, Alexies likes to watch sunsets/sunrises, spend time in nature, cook, and listen to mystery podcasts. Alexies loves to be involved in the community and spend time with her friends and family.
