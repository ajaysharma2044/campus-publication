Summer Dining on Campus
Looking for a meal or snack while you're on campus for Summer Sessions, a conference, concert, or lecture, or just visiting? From breakfast sandwiches to salads, grab-n-go to smoothies, sandwiches and wraps to delicious breakfast buffets, there's something for every taste at our Cornell Dining eateries, which include dining rooms, cafés, coffee houses, food courts, and convenience stores. You're certain to find something that you crave.
Check
Cornell Dining Now
to see what's open when near you.
Over the course of the summer, the following eateries are open to the general public, summer event guests, students, staff, and faculty. Eateries on campus will accept credit cards and Summer Bucks. Only the Cornell Dairy Bar accepts cash.
Please note that hours vary
over the course of the summer with academic sessions and scheduled events, and are subject to change. Many eateries will be closed or have limited hours on holidays such as Memorial Day, Juneteenth, and Independence Day. Check
Cornell Dining Now
from day to day to see what's open when near you.
Questions? Need special accommodations? Email us at
dining@cornell.edu
.
