Winter Break Dining
The Fall Meal Plan period ends with
brunch on Sunday, December 20, 2026
.  Students remaining on campus through Winter break can use, or add to their Fall BRB balances for use at various cafés, coffee shops, and convenience stores through
Thursday, December 24th.
Cornell Dining will be shut down from
Friday, December 25, 2026
through
Friday, January 1, 2027
. During this period, students are welcome to add funds to their
City Bucks
account. Cornell Dining's City Bucks program allows you to enjoy pay for meals or groceries off campus, or delivered to your door, with a simple swipe of your Cornell ID card.
From
January 4, 2027 through January 19,2027,
students can continue to use their Fall BRBs at
the various cafes, coffee shops and convenience stores that will be open, until
Spring Meal Plan dining begins with lunch on Tuesday, January 19, 2027.
During break, please check
Cornell Dining Now
for the most up-to-date list of open locations and hours.
