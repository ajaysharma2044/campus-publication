No administrative fee!
Big Red Bucks can be used in any Cornell Dining facility to purchase your own meals, but not to purchase meals for guests. Any BRB balance carries over from the fall semester to the spring semester. Any unused meal swipes do not carry over. You will automatically be billed for each semester's initial deposits. Any Big Red Bucks balance must be used by May 31, 2027. A nonrefundable $50 administrative fee will be charged upon enrollment and once each academic year, except for the Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan.
