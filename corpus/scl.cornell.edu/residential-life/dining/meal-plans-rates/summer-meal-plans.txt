Summer Meal Plans
Whether you're on campus to take classes, conduct research, or attend a conference or seminar, Cornell Dining has something for everyone!
If you are staying in on-campus housing as a part of a summer conference or program, please consult with your Facilitator or Conference Planner regarding the Meal Plans available to you.
Summer Meal Plans – 2026
Summer Combo Plan - $686
Includes 30 dining room meals and $200 in Summer Bucks. You may take advantage of meals at our designated dining room locations based on hours of operation. Summer Bucks may be used to purchase food items in retail locations or meals at our designated dining room locations.
Plan begins with breakfast on Tuesday May 26 and ends with dinner on Sunday August 16.
Summer Bucks Plan - $50
Summer Bucks are a sales tax free, pre-paid declining balance account, valid from May 26 through August 16. Summer Bucks may be used to purchase food items in retail locations or meals at our designated dining room locations. There is an initial minimum deposit of $50. There is no minimum for subsequent deposits.
Purchases made with Summer Meal Plans and Summer Bucks are exempt from New York State sales tax. It is to your advantage to maintain an adequate account balance, as purchases made with cash or credit card will be charged sales tax.
Purchased plans are nonrefundable and nontransferable, and must be used by August 16, 2026.

Cornell Dining takes food allergies, intolerances and other
special dietary needs
seriously. Our culinary teams are trained on safe preparation of allergen free meals and we are passionate about finding safe options for our students. In our Residential Dining Locations, we label for the major allergens (soy, egg, milk, gluten, peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, fish and sesame) that are inherently found in our menu options. We also identify areas of high risk for our students with food allergies including foods produced in shared oil, and at high risk for cross contact including baked goods and ice cream. We have purple allergy awareness signage throughout our dining facilities to help identify areas of high risk. You can
learn more about our process
. If you or your child is sensitive or allergic to an item that we do NOT label for, or if Cross Contact is a concern, please fill out the form below at least 14 days prior to the camper’s arrival date so that we can provide appropriate options for you/your child.
