Student Assembly Dining Committee
The Dining Services Committee of
Cornell's Student Assembly
works to represent student interests in the Cornell food system. The Chair works with the Director of Cornell Dining to select relevant administrators and staff to be present at meetings. Members collectively review the policies and initiatives of Cornell Dining, and make recommendations to Student Assembly and Dining leadership for improving existing policies or integrating new ideas. The committee consists of at least one faculty member, two Dining student workers and/or Dining Student Sustainability Coordinators, one graduate/professional student, and additional undergraduate students. Additionally, the Director of Dining (or a designee) and the Budget Director of Dining shall serve ex-officio. The chair shall be elected by the Assembly.
The Student Assembly Dining Committee has advocated for and helped bring to fruition:
Availability of
nutrition and allergen information
for retail eateries
An improved
reusable beverage rewards program
to incentivize use of reusable mugs
An option to
pre-order to-go meals
in exchange for a meal swipe
A new
primary coffee vendor
for Cornell Dining's cafés and coffee shops
If you're interested in joining the Student Assembly Dining Committee,
which meets monthly during the academic year, contact Cornell Dining Administrative Coordinator Maggie Jones ’19 at
mij24@cornell.edu
