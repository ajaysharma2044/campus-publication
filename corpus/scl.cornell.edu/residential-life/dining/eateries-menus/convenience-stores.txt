Convenience Stores
Stop by one of our convenience stores to pick up a snack, Grab-n-Go meal, groceries, or toiletries. Along with their main retail sections,
Bear Necessities
includes a popular grill, and
Jansen's Market
offers specialty coffees, desserts, smoothies, and bubble tea. Within The Cornell Store,
Café Jennie
has a range of convenience items and products from Cornell alumni-owned companies, as well as a full-service café.
North Campus locations:
Bear Necessities
– a convenience store & grill in Robert Purcell Community Center
West Campus locations:
Jansen's Market
– a convenience store and coffee shop in Noyes Community Recreation Center
