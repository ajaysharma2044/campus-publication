Nutrition & Special Diets
As part of Cornell Dining's purpose to Nourish the Future, students have free access to meet with the Cornell Dining Nutrition team to discuss how to best navigate our dining rooms and cafés with specific dietary needs.
Our team members review all menus served and help to make sure students have access to healthy menu items.
To support our mission, we are committed to serving housemade, high-quality food that follows our guidelines:
Chef-driven menus: Our culinary team includes award-winning Chefs that are passionate about food and are creative in their menus.
We use canola oil, not soy or peanut oil, in our fryers.
A large variety of vegetarian and vegan options are offered at every meal.
We choose high-quality ingredients with few additives and allergens.
We serve locally produced
Cornell Dairy
milk, yogurt, cheese, and ice cream.
We serve local apples and apple cider from the
Cornell Orchards
seasonally.
We accommodate special dietary needs in a supportive and sensitive manner.
We label all menu items in our
residential dining rooms
for
major allergens
.
We train all employees to serve food safely through food safety and AllerTrain food allergen training programs.
We take
suggestions and recommendations
from our student customers seriously.
We work closely with the
Student Assembly Dining Committee
to help guide our decisions.
