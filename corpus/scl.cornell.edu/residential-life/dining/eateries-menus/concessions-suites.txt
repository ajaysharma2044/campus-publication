Concessions & Suites
Cornell Dining is now responsible for concession operations at Cornell Athletics facilities, serving men's and women's hockey and men's and women's basketball fans at Lynah Rink and Newman Arena, football and lacrosse at Schoellkopf Field, plus track & field and more. There's a full selection of snacks, meals, and beverages at the various locations, ranging from coffee and soda to local hot cider, hot pretzels and fresh Cornell Dairy ice cream to burgers, hot dogs, street tacos, and calzones. Concession stands take major credit cards, MealChoice, and Big Red Bucks.
