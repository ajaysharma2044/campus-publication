Kosher Resources on Campus
Located next to the Center for Jewish Living building on the south edge of west campus,
104West!
is Cornell's kosher and multicultural dining room. Menus are prepared under the supervision of STAR-K (meat and pareve) and STAR-D (dairy) Kosher Certifications, and Jewish dietary laws are strictly followed with the direction of a resident "Mashgiach," or kosher-food supervisor.
Hours and menus at 104West!
Shabbat dinner times vary over the course of the year, based on sunset times. Save money and help us plan by making advance reservations for Shabbat dinners and holiday meals.
Kosher meal reservations
There is a dedicated Kosher station at Morrison Dining in Toni Morrison Hall on north campus, open for lunch and dinner daily. (Holidays and breaks might affect the station's operations.)
Hours and menus at Morrison Dining
In addition, grab-and-go sandwiches, wraps, and salads made in our 104West! kitchen are distributed to retail eateries around campus. Find them anytime in your favorite café's cooler!
