Halal Resources
There is a dedicated Halal station at Morrison Dining in Toni Morrison Hall on north campus, Halal meals are available upon request at Keeton House Dining Room, and Cornell Dining offers Halal-friendly meals that are available for pickup at Willard Straight Hall.
Read about Cornell Dining's Halal Resources
Kosher Resources
Located next to the Center for Jewish Living building on the south edge of west campus,
104West!
is Cornell's kosher and multicultural dining room, with prepared under the supervision of STAR-K (meat and pareve) and STAR-D (dairy) Kosher Certifications. There is also a dedicated Kosher station at Morrison Dining, and prepared Kosher FreshTake items such as salads and sandwiches are available at several retail locations on campus.
Read about Cornell Dining's Kosher Resources
Religious Observances
Cornell Dining makes resources available to students and other guests following traditional religious observances such as Ramadan, Lent, and Passover. For help navigating these, reach out to the
Cornell Dining Nutrition
team.
Yom Kippur is September 20th – 21st, 2026
Ramadan is February 7th – March 8th, 2027
Lent is February 10th – March 25th, 2027
Passover is April 21st – 29th, 2027
For additional assistance
For assistance with religious observances not covered here, please reach out to the
