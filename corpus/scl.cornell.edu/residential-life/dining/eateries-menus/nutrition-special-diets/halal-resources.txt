Halal Resources on Campus
There is a dedicated Halal station at Morrison Dining in Toni Morrison Hall on north campus, open for lunch and dinner daily. (Holidays might affect the station's operations.)
Hours and menus at Morrison Dining
Halal meals are available upon request at Keeton House Dining Room, at dinner seven days a week and at lunch and brunch on Saturday and Sunday.
Cornell Dining offers Halal-friendly meals that are available for pickup at Willard Straight Hall. Choose from a variety of Take Us Home menu items that are Halal certified and follow extensive food handling and procurement practices.
Pre-order your meal on GET
Additionally, all foods in Cornell Dining's residential dining rooms are labeled for alcohol and pork.
Food allergies and special diets
During Ramadan each year, we offer extended hours in select dining rooms, take-out containers, and pre-fast meal boxes available for pickup the night before.
