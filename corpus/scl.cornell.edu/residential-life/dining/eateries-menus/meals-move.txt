Meals on the Move
Meals on the Move is designed to add convenience for our meal plan holders, increase variety available, and reduce wait times during peak meal periods on Central Campus. Students are able to pick up a breakfast or lunch from Crossings Café on North Campus, Rose House Dining Room on West Campus, or Weill Hall Atrium on Central Campus in the morning and enjoy it at a time conducive to their schedule.
Order your Meals on the Move breakfast or lunch
We've added breakfast offerings and a central campus pickup location thanks to feedback from students who need to be on central campus first thing in the morning for a variety of reasons, such as team practices, lab or study sessions, jobs, or early classes.
Meals on the Move breakfasts and lunches are available Monday through Friday when classes are in session. Order by 10:30am the day before you'd like to pick up your meals.
Frequently Asked Questions
How does the Meals on the Move program work and what are the advantages?
The Meals on the Move program allows students to pre-order to-go meals in exchange for a meal swipe. This program is perfect for busy students who need a convenient solution for their morning or midday meals while still being able to use their meal swipes.
How much do the meals cost?
The price for a meal is one meal swipe, deducted from your meal plan on the morning of your order pickup.
Who can use this program?
Most undergraduate student meal plans that have meal swipes available can take advantage of this program. That includes Unlimited, Bear Traditional, Bear Choice, Bear Basic, House Meal Plan, SFL Supplemental, South Campus Meal Plan, Flex 10/500, Off Campus Value, and the Dining Student Employees Meal Plan.
What do the meals come with and what is the menu?
The Meals on the Move program features several menu items that you can choose from. Each breakfast is one of a variety of breakfast bowls. Each lunch includes your chosen entrée (sandwich, salad, or grain bowl) along with a bag of chips, a piece of whole fruit, and an optional bottle of water.
How and when can I place an order?
Orders can be placed online by visiting
mealsonthemove.scl.cornell.edu
.
Beginning Friday afternoons, eligible meal plan holders will be able to order up to a week’s worth of meals for daily pickup on this platform.
You can also order meals daily, as long as you place your order before 10:30 a.m. for next-day pick-up. Meals on the Move will be available Monday through Friday while classes are in session.
Where do I pick up my meal?
Meals will be available for pickup at three convenient locations – Crossings Café in Toni Morrison Hall on North Campus, Flora Rose House Dining Room on West Campus, or Weill Hall Atrium on Central Campus. Meals can be picked up between 7:30 and 10:30am.
