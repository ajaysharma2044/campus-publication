Order Ahead
Ordering in advance with GET Order, our mobile ordering service, helps you reduce your wait time in our eateries and be more efficient with your time. You can place your order hours ahead if you're sure of your schedule, or simply before walking over. Just skip the line and grab your meal from the pickup station.
Access GET Order at
get.cbord.com/cornell
or search for "CBORD GET" in the app store
More details about GET and a tip for Android users:
Manage Your Account
The following eateries offer GET Order mobile ordering service for made-to-order coffee drinks, sandwiches, and more: Amit Bhatia Libe Café, Bear Necessities, Big Red Barn, Bus Stop Bagels, Café Jennie, Cornell Dairy Bar, Crossings Café, Goldie's Café, Jansen's Market, Mann Café, Martha's Café, Mattin's Café, and Novick's Café.
When you order ahead, just prepay with Big Red Bucks (BRBs), MealChoice, or a credit card using the GET app. If you're picking up other items at the same time, you can pay for them by scanning your digital ID with GET My Card at the cashier.
Having trouble with GET?
Android users may run into a glitch with two-step login using DUO with the GET app. More details and a tip at our
Manage Your Account page
.
If you don't have a smartphone, just use a web browser on any computer or mobile device to place your order before heading to the eatery to pick it up. There is no fee for using GET Order to place your order.
