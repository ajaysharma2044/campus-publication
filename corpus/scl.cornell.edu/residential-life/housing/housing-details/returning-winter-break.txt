Returning from Winter Break
Winter Break: December 20, 2026 – January 19, 2027
Undergraduate residences reopen on
Tuesday, January 19, 2027,
at 9:00 AM ET Students may move back in at any time before classes begin on Tuesday, January 26 and
must sign up for an arrival timeslot in the Housing and Dining Portal.
Please
remember to check in on the Housing and Dining Portal after you've arrived
, as this ensures your continued building access. Failure to do so within 10 days of arrival will result in revocation of access. Don't let this happen!
1.       Log in to the
Housing and Dining Portal
with your Cornell NetID
2.       Click the "Undergraduate" tab or button
3.       Click "Continue" for your 2026-27 Academic Year application
4.       Navigate to the Spring Check-in page and click "Check In"
Self-check-in is a necessary action step in maintaining your building access.
