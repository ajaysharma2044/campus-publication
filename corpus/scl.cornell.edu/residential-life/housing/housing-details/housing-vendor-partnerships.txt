Housing and Residential Life Vendor Partnerships
At Cornell, we believe in forging strong relationships with industry-leading vendors to enhance our residential community's experience and capabilities. Through our strategic partnerships with select companies, we provide our students with cutting-edge technology, exclusive resources, and preferential pricing on essential products and services. These carefully cultivated relationships help us maintain our commitment to excellence while delivering exceptional value to our campus community.
Big Red Shipping and Storage
Big Red Shipping and Storage (BRSS) is Student and Campus Life’s preferred shipping and storage vendor, and they offer pre-arrival services to ease the busy trip to Ithaca. They receive your items, place them in storage, and put them into your room before you arrive on campus! Big Red Shipping and Storage offers move-in, move-out and summer storage services to Cornell University students. Learn more about
storage and shipping options
or contact
Big Red Shipping and Storage
to sign up.
CSC Laundry
Forget waiting in line, counting coins, and stressing about the status of your laundry loads. Monitor the status of your hall's laundry machines using the
CSCPay Mobile app
. The app allows you to see balances and manage your account, check washer and dryer availability, and get notified when your wash or dry cycle finishes. Laundry has never been easier or more convenient!
Download the app
for free in the App Store (iOS) or in the Google Play Store (Android).
DormCo
The Cornell Store and Cornell Housing & Residential Life collaborate with DormCo to provide a convenient solution for ordering room essentials. DormCo offers high-quality bedding and over 5,000 budget-friendly products to help make your room or apartment one of a kind. On-campus students: let us do the heavy lifting! Order by the summer deadline and we’ll put your order in your room before you arrive!
View details and order.
GradGuard
GradGuard College Renters Insurance is designed for students. Your rates won't go up if you file a claim, there are no sub-limits on electronics coverage, and there are no penalties for depreciation. Coverage protects physical items you bring to college including cell phones, laptops, clothing, bicycles, textbooks, gaming systems, furniture, and jewelry (up to the policy limits) from things like theft, fire, smoke, vandalism, wind, sprinkler system discharges, and more.
Unique features include worldwide property coverage (your belongings, including rented property, can be protected from covered perils anywhere in the world), and personal liability coverage (if you unintentionally damage your place of residence, GradGuard can help.)
Learn more about GradGuard
and sign up for coverage in the
Housing and Dining Portal
.
Lazybones - Bulk Laundry & Dry Cleaning Service
Did you know you can bring your laundry to select Campus Life Service Centers and have it returned clean and folded the next day? Cornell partners with Lazybones to offer this affordable service to busy students. You can select a package by the semester or pay by the pound, and can bring your laundry to the
North Campus Service Center
and
Hasbrouck Service Center
.
Laundry service from Lazybones starts at $1.45 per pound, and dry cleaning is priced per item. If you have any questions about the service, which is available from the first week of classes through the end of the semester, please visit
www.MyLAZYBONES.com
or contact
wecare@mylazybones.com
or
877-215-2105.
My MicroFridge
A combination refrigerator and microwave unit is an ideal space-saving solution for students living in residence halls, offering two essential appliances in one compact footprint. This convenient setup allows students to easily store fresh food and beverages while having the ability to quickly heat up meals or leftovers, all within arm's reach of their study space. Not only does this combination save valuable floor and counter space, but it also provides students with the basic amenities needed to maintain a flexible eating schedule around their busy academic and social lives.
Microfridges may be rented and placed in your room and can be ordered directly from
My MicroFridge
. Order early and have the unit placed in your room before you arrive.
