Rates, Amenities, & Licenses
Your signed housing license is a legally binding agreement between you and the Housing & Dining Contracts Office.
Please note that you will only be released from your license under certain circumstances, at the discretion of the Office of Housing and Residential Life, and usually with an associated fee. Refer to your contract Terms and Conditions below for details.
