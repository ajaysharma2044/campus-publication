Laundry Services
There are clothing washers and dryers in each residential building or community.
Forget waiting in line, counting coins, and stressing about the status of your laundry loads. Monitor the status of your hall's laundry machines using the
CSCPay Mobile app
.
Laundry Quick Facts
Our laundry machines don’t take coins. Students can add funds to their laundry account by following the instructions in the
CSCPay Mobile app
or inserting a credit card directly. Credit card payments will be assessed a 25-cent fee per swipe. Paying with the app doesn't incur a fee per use.
Washers are $2.00, and dryers are $1.55 per cycle.
Do not overload the washers & dryers. Clothing needs to be able to move freely to clean properly.
Place clothing loosely in machines, leaving about 5" of space from the top. If clothing is overloaded, clothing can be damaged.
Do not wash or dry comforters or large bulky items in these machines.
Is doing laundry new to you? CSC Serviceworks shares many
helpful laundry tips
on their website.
CSC Serviceworks also offers Laundry Basics videos on how to use a
front-load
or
top-load
washer. Watch the one that matches the equipment you're using!
Bulk Laundry & Dry Cleaning Service
Did you know you can bring your laundry to the selected Campus Life Service Centers and have it returned clean and folded the next day? Cornell works with Lazybones to offer this affordable service to busy students. Lazybones has been keeping students clean since 1993.
You can select a package by the semester or pay by the pound. You can bring your laundry to the
North Campus Service Center
and
Hasbrouck Service Center
.
Laundry service from Lazybones starts at $1.45 per pound, and dry cleaning is priced per item. If you have any questions about the service, which is available from the first week of classes through the end of the semester, please visit
www.MyLAZYBONES.com
or contact
wecare@mylazybones.com
or
877-215-2105
.
