Project ReCollect
Cornell's Project ReCollect program, formerly known as Dump & Run, collects items from students leaving campus in May, and resells them to students moving in each August. In addition to diverting about 50 tons of reusable items from the waste stream yearly, the annual ReCollect sale raises about $80,000 each year for local charitable organizations, primarily Cops, Kids & Toys, to assist with local families during the holiday season.

May 2026 Collection

give it away!
Donations Accepted:
Clothing & shoes
Furniture & organizational units
Working appliances & electronics
Household items (dishes, clothing hangers, cleaning products, etc.)
Decorations & toys
Sporting goods
New toiletries
Books
Non-perishable food
We cannot accept donations of inkjet printers, mattresses, underwear, trash, anything broken, or opened food packaging.
