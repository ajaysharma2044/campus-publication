Move-In Instructions for 1 Forest Park Lane
Please read this entire page for detailed Move-In instructions, and review everything carefully so you can finalize your travel plans for a successful arrival. It is to your advantage to arrive when directed and follow our Move-In instructions precisely.
Before You Arrive
Select a Move-In timeslot in the
Housing and Dining Portal
. Your reserved timeslot is for arrival and check-in only, not unloading.
Review
Prior to Your Arrival
, and
Transportation & Parking
pages.
Print your Move-In Ticket (available in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1) and have ready to scan upon arrival.
Please have your printed Move-In Ticket on your dashboard (with a cell phone number) throughout your stay on campus.
Move-In Instructions by Arrival Date and Time
Your arrival process will depend on whether you arrive during assisted Move-in hours, House Office business hours or after the House Office is closed.

Tuesday, August 18
Arrival between 8:00a.m.-11:00a.m.
Arrival Location:
University Avenue Parking Lot (627 University Ave)
Unloading Location:
1 Forest Park Lane Unloading Zone
Arrive on campus and proceed to the
University Avenue Parking Lot
(627 University Avenue)
for queueing.
Proceed directly to your residence hall unloading zone. You will sign out a cart or carts and unload your vehicle.
You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
Once your vehicle is unloaded, proceed to your building check-in location to receive your keys, student ID, and other move-in materials.
Follow directions to your 3-hour short-term parking. Information about your designated parking area can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.

Arrival Location:

Keeton House Office
Proceed directly to your residence hall unloading zone. You will sign out a cart or carts and unload your vehicle.
You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
Once your vehicle is unloaded, proceed to your building check-in location to receive your keys, student ID, and other move-in materials.
Follow directions to your 3-hour short-term parking. Information about your designated parking area can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the

Arrival on campus after 8:00p.m.
Arrival Location: 1 Forest Park Lane Unloading Area
The House Office will be closed
When you get to your building, call the posted number for access to your room.  The GRF on call will assist you with accessing your room so you may unload your belongings.
Staff unloading assistance will not be available. You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
You will not receive your keys and/or student ID at this time
.
Once unloading is complete, move the vehicle to the end of the University Ave Parking Lot. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.
Friday-Saturday, August 21-22
Arrival between 9:00a.m. – 5:00p.m.
Arrival Location:
University Avenue Lot, 627 University Ave
Unloading Area:

Arrive on campus and proceed to your residence hall unloading zone. You will sign out a cart or carts and unload your vehicle. You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.

Not arriving by vehicle?
Arriving by Uber/Taxi
- Please plan to arrive at your designated timeslot and proceed with check-in.
Arriving by Bus
- Students who are arriving by bus directly to North Campus/Robert Purcell Community Center should proceed to their assigned building for check-in.
