Move-In Instructions for Sheldon Court
These Instructions Apply To
The instructions below apply to the following South Campus residences:
Sheldon Court
Please read this entire page for detailed Move-In instructions, and review everything carefully so you can finalize your travel plans for a successful arrival. It is to your advantage to arrive when directed and follow our Move-In instructions precisely.
Before You Arrive
Select a Move-In timeslot in the
Housing and Dining Portal
.
Your reserved timeslot is for arrival and check-in only, not unloading.
Review
Prior to Your Arrival
, and
Transportation & Parking
pages.
Print your Move-In Ticket (available in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1) and have ready to scan upon arrival.
Please have your printed Move-In Ticket on your dashboard (with a cell phone number) throughout your stay on campus.
Move-In Instructions by Arrival Date and Time
Your arrival process will depend on whether you arrive during Service Center hours or after the Service Center is closed.
South Campus Cascadilla Service Center
Service Center Hours
Monday, August 17: Closed
Tuesday, August 18 - Saturday, August 22: 8:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m.
Sunday, August 23: 10:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.
Tuesday, August 18
Arrival on campus 8:00 a.m.- 11:00 a.m.
Arrival Location:
Sheldon Court Unloading Zone (use Dryden Road Garage entrance, 114 Dryden Road)
Check-in Location:
Sheldon Court
Proceed directly to your residence hall unloading zone.  You will sign out a cart or carts and unload your vehicle.
Staff unloading assistance will be available. You and your guests will be responsible for moving your belongings to your room.
Once your vehicle is unloaded, proceed to your building check-in location to receive your keys, student ID, and other move-in materials.
Once unloading is complete, the driver will be asked to move the vehicle to Williams Street Lot. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.
Arrival on campus 11:30 a.m.- 7:00 p.m.
Arrival Location:
Cascadilla Hall
Check-in Location:
Cascadilla Service Center
Proceed directly to the
Cascadilla Service Center
to check in and pick up your keys/ID (if applicable). You will be able to park temporarily in the Cascadilla Circle.
You will then proceed to your building for unloading. You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
Once unloading is complete, the driver will be asked to move the vehicle to Williams Street Lot. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.
Arrival on campus after 7:00 p.m.
Arrival Location:
Sheldon Court Unloading Zone
The Cascadilla Hall Service Center will be closed
Proceed to
your residence hall unloading zone
.
When you get to your building, call the posted number for access to your room.
Staff unloading assistance will not be available. You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
You will not receive your keys and/or student ID at this time
.
Once unloading is complete, move the vehicle to the Williams Street Lot. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.
The following day, visit the
Cascadilla Hall Service Center
during business hours to check-in, get keys and your ID.
Arrival to Campus Friday, August 21 - Saturday, August 22
Arrival on campus 9:00 a.m.- 7:00 p.m.
Arrival Location:
Cascadilla Hall
Check-in Location: Cascadilla Service Center
Proceed directly to the Cascadilla Service Center to check in and pick up your keys/ID (if applicable). You will be able to park temporarily in the Cascadilla Circle.
You will then proceed to your building for unloading. You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
Once unloading is complete, the driver will be asked to move the vehicle to the end of the parking lot. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the Housing and Dining Portal after August 1.
Arrival on campus after 7:00 p.m.
Arrival Location:
Sheldon Court Unloading Zone
The Cascadilla Hall Service Center will be closed
Proceed to
your residence hall unloading zone
.
When you get to your building, call the posted number for access to your room.
Staff unloading assistance will not be available. You and your guests will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room.
You will not receive your keys and/or student ID at this time
.
Once unloading is complete, move the vehicle to the end of the Williams Street Lot. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.
The following day, visit the
Cascadilla Hall Service Center
during business hours to check-in, get keys and your ID.

- Please plan to arrive at your designated timeslot and proceed with check-in.

- Students who are arriving by bus directly to North Campus/Robert Purcell Community Center or West Campus should proceed to their assigned building for check-in.
