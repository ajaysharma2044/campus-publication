Early Arrival - North and South Campus
Early arrival must be requested and approved in advance. Only pre-approved requests are allowed to move in early.
To request an early arrival, submit a request through the Housing and Dining Portal in your 2026-27 Academic Year application.
Please read this entire message carefully before making final travel arrangements. Following the instructions below and arriving at your designated time will help ensure a smooth and efficient move-in process.
For information about what you should do before arriving on campus, please visit the
Prior to Your Arrival
page.
Arrival and Move-In Instructions
Check-In Location
All students must check in at the
North Campus Student Check-in Desk
, located on the first floor of the Robert Purcell Community Center (RPCC) to receive their Cornell ID and keys. Temporary parking is available in designated RPCC parking spaces while you pick up your key and ID. Long-term parking is not available at RPCC.

Arrival During Service Center Hours
Arrival Location:
RPCC Parking Lot
Check-in Location:
North Campus Student Check-in Desk (RPCC 103-105)
Proceed directly to
RPCC room 103-105
to check in and pick up your keys/ID (if applicable).
Proceed to your residence hall unloading area.
You and your guests are responsible for unloading and moving belongings to your room.
Once unloading is complete, move your vehicle to a designated Move-in parking lot to make space for other arrivals.
If you have purchased an academic year parking permit, please follow the temporary move-in parking instructions provided by Transportation Services.
Arrival After 7:00p.m.
Arrival Location: Your Residence Hall
The North Campus Service Center will be closed
Proceed directly to your residence hall’s unloading area.
Call the staff member on-call phone number posted at your building entrance.
The staff member on-call will assist you with accessing your room so you may unload your belongings.
You and your guests are responsible for unloading and moving belongings to your room. Staff unloading assistance will not be available.
Once unloading is complete, move your vehicle to a designated
Move-in parking lot
to make space for other arrivals.
You will not receive your keys or Cornell ID during after-hours arrival.
You must return to the North Campus Service Center the following morning to officially check in and obtain your keys/ID.
If You Cannot Call Within the United States
If you do not have a phone capable of making U.S. calls, use a
Blue Light Emergency Phone
located near your residence hall.
Press the button to connect with Cornell Police and tell them which residence hall you are trying to access. They will contact the RA on your behalf.
Vehicle Unloading and Parking: North Campus
To help manage move-in traffic, designated unloading areas are available throughout North Campus. Please use only the designated unloading locations and do not drive on pedestrian walkways unless specifically designated as an unloading route.
Move-in bins and carts will be available at residence halls on a first-come, first-served basis.
No Parking zones, including fire lanes and emergency access routes, will be strictly enforced.
