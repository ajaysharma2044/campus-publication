Move-In Instructions for West Campus
These instructions apply to...
The instructions below apply to the following West Campus residences:
Alice Cook House:
Cook Main, Boldt Tower, Boldt Hall, North Baker, Baker Tower
Carl Becker House:
Becker Main
Hans Bethe House:
Bethe Main, McFaddin Hall
William Keeton House:
Keeton Main, 1 Forest Park
Flora Rose House:
Rose Main, South Baker, Lyon, Founders, Mennen Hall
Please read this entire message for detailed move-in instructions, and review everything carefully so you can finalize your travel plans for a successful arrival. It is to your advantage to arrive when directed and follow our move-in instructions precisely.
Before you Arrive
Select a Move-In timeslot in the
Housing and Dining Portal
.
Your reserved timeslot is for arrival and check-in only, not unloading.
Review
Prior to Your Arrival
, and Transportation & Parking pages.
Print your Move-In Ticket (available in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1) and have ready to scan upon arrival.
Move-In Instructions by Arrival Date and Time
Tuesday, August 18
Arrival between 8:00 a.m. – 11:00 a.m.
Arrival Location: University Avenue Parking Lot (627 University Ave)
Arrive on campus and proceed directly to the University Ave Lot where your vehicle will be queued for Move-In. Please have your Move-In Ticket on your dashboard with a cell phone number.
Once checked in, you’ll be guided to your building’s unloading zone.
When you arrive at your designated unloading zone, you will sign out a bin or bins and unload your vehicle. We will provide assistance to unload your vehicle, but please know that you and your guests will be responsible for moving your belongings to your room.
You will check-in at your
House Office
, and pick up your keys/ID (if applicable).
Once your vehicle is unloaded, follow directions to your 3-hour short-term parking. Information about your designated parking can be found on the printable Move-In Ticket in the
Housing and Dining Portal
after August 1.
Students with a paid parking permit will be able to park temporarily in the Anna Comstock lot and/or in the University Ave lot until Sunday 8/23 when West Campus parking re-opens.
Arrival after Timeslot Hours and when House Offices are Closed
Arrival Location: Your West Campus House Lobby
On Tuesday, your House Office will be able to help you check-in and get your key/ID, but there will not be any assistance with unloading your car or getting belongings to your room.
If
House Offices
are closed when you arrive, you will need to call the Graduate Residence Fellow (GRF) on-call number located on your West Campus House main doors for assistance with checking-in.
You will be able to temporarily park to unload in the areas listed below.
The GRF on call will assist you with obtaining your keys/ID (if applicable).
Please know that you (and guests) will be responsible for unloading and moving your belongings to your room. Please have your Move-in Ticket on your dashboard with a cell phone number.
Unloading locations:
Along West Avenue:
Across from Baker Tower: parking spaces designated as loading zones
Across from Founders & Lyon: the pull-off loading zone on the Slope side
Near Bethe & Keeton:
Mary Ann Wood Drive (just behind William Keeton House): parking spaces designated as loading zones
Forest Park Lane (just behind Noyes Community Recreation Center): parking spaces designated as loading zones
Stewart Ave
Paid parking spaces along road, free after 6pm and on Sundays
