Prior to Your Arrival on Campus
Below is a list of steps you will need to take before your arrival on campus this August to ensure your move-in goes as smoothly as possible.
1. Select Your Move-In Timeslot
You must select a Move-In arrival timeslot via the
Housing and Dining Portal
for the day and time you plan to arrive to campus.
Deadline to select your timeslot: Monday, August 10
Selecting a Move-In date and timeslot helps ensure a smooth move-in experience and minimize any traffic issues or wait times.
We appreciate your assistance in adhering to your selected Move-In timeslot.
As you make plans, if you find that your Move-In date and time no longer work for you, please log in to the
Housing and Dining Portal
to select a new date prior to your August deadline.
Please note that the Move-in arrival timeslot you select is when you need to arrive to your designated check-in location.
Your 30-minute timeslot is just your arrival window, not unloading.
However, we ask that you move your vehicle as soon as you unload, so other students and their families can also get close to their building.
There will be plenty of time, up to 3 hours, to unpack throughout the day before your 3-hour parking expires.
2. Print Your
Move-In
Ticket
Your Move-In Ticket, a printable pass that contains important information about your Move-In arrangements, will need to remain on your vehicle’s dashboard throughout your Move-In period to show that you’re authorized to park on campus.
Print your Move-In Ticket from the
Housing and Dining Portal
prior to arrival, beginning in early August.
Access your Move-In Ticket by returning to the Fall Move-In page of your 2026-2027 Academic Year application.
You must have at least one cell phone number written on the pass.
You will also find directions to parking locations in the Housing and Dining Portal as part of your Move-In Ticket.
3. Register Your Bicycle or Micromobility Device
If you plan to bring a bicycle
, e-bike, scooter, or other micromobility device
to campus, you must register it with Cornell Transportation Services. Please register before your arrival using the
Micromobility Registration Form
.
Once registered, you can get a Bike Room key from your Service Center or House Office, if applicable. Note, you will need to show proof of bike registration.
Please be advised that all personal electronic (battery-powered) modes of recreational transport, including, but not limited to, e-bikes, e-scooters, e-skateboards, and hoverboards are
prohibited from being charged or stored in any University residential building outside of designated Bike Rooms
.
4. Submit Your Student ID Photo
For New Students Only:
You are required to
submit a photo online for your Student ID Card
. If you have not done so already, be sure to submit your photo
as soon as possible
so that your Student ID will be available when you arrive. Your Student ID Card is used for accessing your building and dining meal plan, so not having your Student ID ready when you arrive will require additional steps as a part of your move-in and could negatively impact your experience.
If your Student ID is not available at Move-In, you will need to go to the Office of the University Registrar in 245 Day Hall, open Monday - Thursday, 11:00am - 1:00pm and 2:00pm - 3:30pm, to obtain your student ID after you arrive on campus.
5. Review Your Personal Property Insurance
If your belongings are not already covered on a family insurance policy, we strongly suggest you consider
personal property insurance
. Per Section XVII of the Housing License, the University is not liable for any loss or damage to personal property. Cornell University’s preferred provider is GradGuard. More information is available at
GradGuard.com
.
