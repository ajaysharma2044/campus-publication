Residence Hall Configurations
Cornell University has a wide variety of residence halls in its housing portfolio, but in general, the buildings fall into one of three types of room configurations: Corridor-style, Suite-style, and Apartment-style. Below you will find a descriptions of each style as well as the building that it applies to.

Bathroom Configurations
Bathroom setups vary by residence hall, but within corridor-style configurations, most fall into one of two common styles. Some residential areas, including the West Campus House System, may feature a combination of both. All bathrooms are stocked and cleaned by Cornell University Building Care staff.
Shared Bathroom with Private Shower and Toilet Rooms
This configuration includes a shared sink and vanity area connected to separate shower and toilet rooms, each with its own locking door.
Students can choose the level of privacy that best meets their needs. The main bathroom door may be locked for exclusive use of the entire bathroom area or left unlocked, so multiple students can use different areas simultaneously. Shower and toilet rooms are enclosed and lockable for privacy.
In mixed-gender residence halls, students work with together with residence hall staff to determine whether these bathrooms will be designated as all-female, all-male, or remain ungendered spaces.
Traditional Single-Gender Community Bathroom
These bathrooms are designated for one gender and when shared by a floor or corridor, are intended for multiple users at a time.
Most include multiple sinks, toilet stalls, and shower stalls. Each toilet stall is enclosed with a lockable door, and shower stalls are enclosed with either a door or curtain to provide privacy, while shared sink and vanity areas support daily routines.
In some smaller residence halls, where single-gender community bathrooms are not possible, a single-user bathroom is available and may include a sink, toilet, and shower within one private room secured by a lockable door.
Note: Specific bathroom configurations by building will be available on each individual residential community page soon.
Suite-Style
Lockable door off the hallway that leads to a grouping of individual bedrooms, common space, and a bathroom that are private to the individuals who live in the suite.

West Campus:
