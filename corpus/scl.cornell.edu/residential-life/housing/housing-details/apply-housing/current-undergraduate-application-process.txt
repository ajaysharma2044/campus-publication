The 2027-28 Rising Sophomore Room Selection will occur in Spring 2027.
Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores is when students receive a timeslot to reserve a room in an upper-level residence hall or house.
Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores
On-campus housing guarantees
All first- and second-year undergraduate students who are studying full-time on the Ithaca campus are required to live on campus, or in affiliated housing that has been approved to fulfill the
residential requirement
, through the spring semester of their second academic year of enrollment.
Juniors and seniors are not guaranteed on-campus housing. Rising juniors and seniors who would like to live on campus for the 2027-2028 academic year are strongly encouraged to apply to and participate in their fall selection process.
Students who are on leave, but subject to the residential requirement, are encouraged to complete a Housing application to ensure they are able to fulfill the residential requirement upon return.
Cooperative Houses
University-owned Cooperative Houses manage their own occupancy. Students interested in Cooperative Houses should reach out to individual houses to inquire about vacancies and learn more about co-op living. An open house, called Co-op Mosey, will be held on in Spring 2027 for residency during the 2027-2028 academic year. Read about
Cooperative Housing
