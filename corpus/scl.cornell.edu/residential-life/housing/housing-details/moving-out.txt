Moving Out
May 2026
Undergraduate students
Undergraduate residence halls close for the spring semester at 2:00pm on Sunday, May 17, 2026.
All residents should select a move-out timeslot. Please
Log in to the Housing and Dining Portal
to provide your planned move-out date and time.
Please read carefully for important check-out process information.
Closing Checklist for Residents
Complete the Spring Semester Closing Notice and Checklist posted on your suite/room/exterior door.
Dispose of all trash and recycling in the proper building dumpsters. (Charges apply for excess trash left behind.)
Donate usable items to the Project ReCollect collection area in your lobby.
Clean your bathroom and kitchen (if applicable).
Place all items “borrowed” from dining rooms in the collection bins — do not throw them away.
Remove all decorations from walls, doors, and furniture.
Remove personal food from shared refrigerators
Defrost, clean, unplug, and prop open your MicroFridge.
Vacuum or sweep your floor.
Clean any marks on walls or doors that you can.
Close and lock windows; close drapes/shades; turn off lights.
Lock your room/suite/apartment door.
Turn in *all keys* at the Service Center or House Office before you leave
(*room, suite, front door, bike room, alternative access*)
