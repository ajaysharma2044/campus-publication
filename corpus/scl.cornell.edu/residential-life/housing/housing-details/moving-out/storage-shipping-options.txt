Storage & Shipping Options
Thinking about what to do with your personal belongings for the summer as you prepare to move out?
If you want to leave items in Ithaca or ship them home, we recommend using
Big Red Shipping and Storage (BRSS)
, our contracted vendor. Their prices are the most affordable in Ithaca! With over 40 years of experience as Student and Campus Life's preferred vendor, the professional team at Big Red Shipping & Storage is committed to providing excellent customer service, convenience, and consistency to Cornell students and the greater Ithaca area! BRSS operates year-round.
If you scheduled a pick-up time with BRSS or any  other company to ship and/or store your belongings, you
MUST
be present in your room for your appointment. No Housing and Residential Life or University staff can grant access into your building or room, or assist with facilitating the pick-up. If you are not present or unable to keep your scheduled appointment, you will need to reschedule your pick-up time. If a resident is not present during a pick-up appointment, all items left for the company will be donated after the building closes at 2:00PM on Sunday, May 17, 2026.
Storage and Shipping Options
Full Service Storage
Schedule an appointment and BRSS will come pick up your packed boxes and take them to storage. They can deliver supplies or even pack your items if finals week is too stressful!
Full Service Shipping
Pack your items, and BRSS will pick them up during your scheduled appointment time and ship them to your desired location. If supplies are needed, they can deliver those too!
Book Your Appointment
BRSS has limited availability and timeslots, so book your appointment now before they fill up!
How to secure your preferred pickup date and timeslot:
Sign up online at:

free

Storage items will be stored in a climate-controlled facility, and shipping items will be sent to your desired location.
BRSS will be given permission to be in residence halls to assist you, but you must be present. Pickup and Delivery to-and-from your room is FREE!
