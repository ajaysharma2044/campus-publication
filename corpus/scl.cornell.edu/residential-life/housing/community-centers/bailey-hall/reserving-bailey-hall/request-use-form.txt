Request for Use Form
This form must be submitted at least six (6) weeks prior to the requested event date. Requests are subject to availability and appropriateness of structure. Once the form has been submitted, you will be contacted via email, telephone, and/or written notice to confirm the event date. Until you receive notification, the event request is considered tentative.
