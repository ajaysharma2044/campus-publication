Affiliated Housing for the Second-Year Housing Requirement
Cornell has a 2-year
residential requirement
** for full-time, Ithaca-based campus, first-year and second-year undergraduate students, including a required meal plan. In the second year of residency, students may live in residence halls, and are permitted to live in approved, university-affiliated housing, including Sorority and Fraternity, Co-operative Houses, and others. These organizations offer unique communities and experiences aligned with their missions. Each organization manages its own intake and application process, so please contact them directly for further information.

Sorority & Fraternity Life
Cornell sororities and fraternities are self-governed organizations. Groups that are fortunate enough to enjoy a chapter house are responsible for the operation and upkeep of the house. The buildings are owned either by the university, an alumni group, or a national organization. Funding for the operations of these buildings comes from student rents. In most cases the rent is billed through the university Bursar system. Room and board fees vary.
Chapter houses are expensive to maintain and operate. All members benefit from having a chapter house, therefore, all members must contribute to their buildings operating budget. Nearly all chapters have a minimum requirement to live in their chapter house for at least a semester or two. In addition, there are parlor fees, or living out fees.
