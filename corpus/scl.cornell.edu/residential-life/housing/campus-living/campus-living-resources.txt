Community Resources
Numerous Cornell and Ithaca community resources support those living off campus. Cornell services are available to all students regardless of where they live.
Health & Safety Resources
Students can access safety and health support from local law enforcement,
Cornell University Police
,
Cornell Health
, and
Student Support and Advocacy Services
. These services offer emergency contacts, counseling, crime reporting, and housing safety information. Additional community resources are listed below.
Ithaca Area Resources
2-1-1 Tompkins/Cortland Helpline
A local helpline for housing and human services resources in the Ithaca area.
Phone: 2-1-1
City of Ithaca Building Division
Handles complaints about unsafe living conditions and inspects rental properties.
Phone: 607- 274-6508
Cooperative Extension of Tompkins County
A community resource for advice on local housing, rental rights, and community services.
Phone: 607- 272-2292
Community Dispute Resolution Center
CDRC helps neighbors, families, tenants, and others needing support with everyday disputes using the mediation process to facilitate communication and resolve conflicts.
Phone: 607-273-9347
Tenants Resources
NY State Attorney General - Tenants Resources
Ithaca Tenants' Union
Offers tenants’ rights education, advocacy, and support for renters in Ithaca.
Upstate NY Tenants’ Rights Guide
Landlord Issue Form
Legal Assistance of Western New York LAWNY Ithaca
Offers free legal assistance for housing disputes and tenant rights.
Phone: (607) 273-3667
Tenant Advocacy Practicum
, Cornell Law School
A student-run legal aid group that helps tenants with landlord disputes and housing issues.
Tenants Advocacy Program (TAP) Intake Form
