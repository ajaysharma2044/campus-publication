Student Employment with Cornell Residential Life
The departments within Campus Life Enterprise Services employ hundreds of Cornell students year-round. We hire for fall semester, spring semester, and summer.
We seek students with a wide variety of experience levels, and support our student employees in growing their skills.
Each department manages their own hiring. Follow the links to read about positions, application instructions, and timeframes.
