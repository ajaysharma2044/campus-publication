Emergencies
Life Safety Emergencies
For life safety emergencies including fire, activated fire alarm, activated fire extinguisher, gas odor, etc. please call CUPD at 607-255-1111.
Facilities Emergencies
Facilities emergencies include:
Broken elevator
Card access or door latch failure
Broken windows
Plugged toilet in a location with only one toilet
No heat in room or apartment
No hot water in room or apartment
No electricity in room or apartment
Unusual loud noises coming from the mechanical room
Flooding
Vomit or other bodily fluids
What To Do
Students
Please alert your building staff or call Customer Service at 607-255-5322, Monday-Friday, 8:00am-4:00pm.
After hours and during weekends, students should contact their on-call RA or staff person.
Staff members
In the case of a Facilities emergency (flood, broken elevator, gas odor, etc.), please call the Student & Campus Life Facilities Management Office at 607-255-0328, Monday-Friday, 8:00am-4:00pm.
After hours and during weekends, residential staff (RHDs, RAs, HADs, GRFs) should refer to your SCL Facilities Management Service & Emergency Guide for contact information. Other staff members may call EMCS (Energy Management and Control System) at 607-255-5322. Please only call EMCS if something poses a threat to personal health and safety, or to property.
