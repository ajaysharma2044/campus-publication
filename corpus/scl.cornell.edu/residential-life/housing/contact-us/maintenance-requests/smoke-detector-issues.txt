Smoke Detector Issues
Troubleshooting Smoke detectors
All residential rooms / apartments are equipped with smoke detectors. (Apartments in Hasbrouck and the Townhouse Apartments also have carbon monoxide detectors; please scroll down for more information).
Smoke detector troubleshooting
If this happens
You'll hear or observe
And you should do this
Smoke alarm
Alarm sounds loudly
Lights may flash, depending on the device
Evacuate immediately, and call CUPD at 607-255-1111
Low Battery/ Malfunctioning Device
Alarm chirps (short beep) periodically
Call CUPD at 607-255-1111 to have a new battery installed or your device serviced.
Troubleshooting carbon monoxide detectors
Townhouse residents
Carbon monoxide and smoke detector troubleshooting for Townhouse residents
If this happens
You'll hear or observe
And you should do this
Smoke alarm
Smoke alarm sounds loudly
Evacuate your apartment immediately and call CUPD at 607-255-1111.
Smoke alarm - low battery
Smoke alarm chirps (short beep) periodically
Call CUPD at 607-255-1111 to have a new battery installed.
Carbon monoxide alarm
Detector alarm sounds with 4 loud beeps, then a 5-second pause (pattern repeats)
Red light will turn on
Evacuate your apartment immediately and call CUPD at 607-255-1111.
Carbon monoxide detector - low battery
Device chirps (short beep) once every 30 seconds
Call CUPD at 607-255-1111 to have a new battery installed.
Malfunctioning devices
Devices beep or display lights in a manner not described above
Call CUPD at 607-255-1111 to have the device serviced.
Hasbrouck residents
Carbon monoxide and smoke detector troubleshooting for Hasbrouck residents
If this happens
You'll hear or observe
And you should do this
Smoke alarm
Alarm sounds with 3 long beeps, followed by a pause (pattern repeats)
Light at bottom flashes red.
Evacuate your apartment immediately and call CUPD at 607-255-1111.
Carbon monoxide alarm
Detector alarm sounds with 4 loud beeps, then a 5-second pause (pattern repeats)
Red light will turn on
Evacuate your apartment immediately and call CUPD at 607-255-1111.
Low battery
Device chirps (short beep) once every 60 seconds.
Light at bottom flashes green
Call CUPD at 607-255-1111 to have a new battery installed.
Malfunctioning Device
Device chirps (short beep) 3 times every 60 seconds
Light at bottom flashes green
Call CUPD at 607-255-1111 to have the device serviced.
