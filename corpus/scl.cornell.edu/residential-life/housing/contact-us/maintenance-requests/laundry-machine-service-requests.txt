Laundry Machine Service Requests
Problems with a washer or dryer
Visit
www.cscserviceworks.com
and use their Service Request tool, or use the
CSC ServiceWorks Service app
to report a problem with a washer or dryer in a residential laundry room. Make sure you have the machine ID handy.
Other issues in a laundry room
To report leaks, flooding, electrical issues, or other issues in a laundry room, please
submit a maintenance request
.
You must have a Cornell NetID to enter your request.
Residents without a NetID should contact their
service center
.
Staff/Faculty: You do NOT need to include an account number
– or any other information in fields not marked with a star – in order to submit a request.
