2027-2028 Resident Advisor (RA) Selection Process Overview
Information about the 2027-2028
Resident Advisor position can be found on the Housing and Residential Life
Residential Advisor (RA) Position Description

Application
You must submit a 2027-2028 Resident Advisor (RA) application if you are interested in the following positions:
2027 Spring RA position
and
2027 Fall – 2028 Spring RA position
2027 Fall – 2028 Spring RA position.
Applications are submitted through
Qualtrics
. You can find instructions around the application process within the Qualtrics application. The application opens on October 13, 2026 by 5:00PM ET, and the link will not be active before that time.
The application will include the following questions: applicant information, eligibility questions, preferences, and four (4) short essay questions. Each short essay question should be completed in 450 words or less.
Note:
Submission of application does not guarantee an interview or position.
Once your application is submitted, you will be presented with PDF copy to download as proof of submission. Please download this PDF for your own record.
All application submissions are final and cannot be edited once submitted.
If you believe there was an error in the information you provided for the applicant information, eligibility questions, and preferences, please email
hrl-sl-selection@cornell.edu
with the selection.
Application Questions
Question One
As a Resident Advisor, your role is to build an inclusive community by facilitating individual and group interactions to create a sense of belonging, regardless of individual ideas, perspectives, or identities.
How do you define community? Give an example of a time you built a community or developed meaningful relationships with your peers. What specific actions did you take? What did you learn from that experience?
How would you apply what you learned to the Resident Advisor position? Provide specific examples of what this would look like.
Question Two
As a Resident Advisor, your role is to promote social responsibility by addressing conflicts and concerns, building relationships, setting expectations, helping residents identify harm, and repairing relationships. Through this process, residents learn about their own values and how to incorporate their values into decision making while keeping personal and community impact in mind.
Give an example of a time you addressed or engaged in conflict.  How did you identify the conflict? What actions did you take to address the conflict? What did you learn from that experience?
How would you apply what you learned to the Resident Advisor position? Provide specific examples of what this would look like.
Question Three
As a Resident Advisor, your role is to foster an inclusive community through intentional interactions with residents with diverse personal and social identities (gender, religion, race, orientation, to name a few), backgrounds, and experiences. Through this work, Resident Advisors role model and promote a sense of belonging.
Give an example of how you have intentionally built relationships or engaged with individuals who have different social identities, backgrounds, or experiences. What specific actions did you take? What skills did you use to ensure a sense of belonging? What did you learn from that experience?
How would you apply what you learned to the Resident Advisor position to ensure a sense of belonging while navigating differences? Provide specific examples of what this would look like.
Question Four
What skills do you possess that you will apply to the Resident Advisor position?
How does that connect to the type of student population (first-year, upper-level) and/or the program houses you indicated interest in within the application? (If you ranked program houses, state why you are interested in the individual Program House[s] you ranked.)
Note: Indicating preference does not guarantee placement in a specific area of campus or specific residential community.

Interview and Status Updates
2027 Spring RA position and 2027 Fall – 2028 Spring RA position
Interviews for candidates who applied and are eligible for a
2027 Spring and 2027 Fall-2028 Spring RA position
will occur in mid to late November 2026.
Interview invites will be sent out on a rolling basis after the priority deadline and will continue until mid-November 2026. If an applicant is extended an interview invite, applicants will have 2 business days to sign up for a 30-minute interview time slot upon receiving an interview invite.
Interviews will occur Monday through Friday between 9 AM ET and 5 PM ET. Virtual interview options will be provided Monday through Thursday, and in-person interview options will be provided on Friday. Interview dates will be included in the interview invitation. Please monitor your Cornell email account for updates.
Final position status updates will be sent out on a rolling basis starting the second week of December and will continue throughout the month of December for the Spring 2027 RA position.  Status updates will pause between December 22, 2026, and January 19, 2027, due to Winter break. Status updates for the 2027 Fall – 2028 Spring RA position will be sent out on a rolling basis starting March.
If offered a position, candidates will have 4 business days to accept or decline an offer. If we do not hear back from the candidate after an offer has been made, they will be removed from the selection process and will have to apply during the 2027-2028 Resident Advisor selection cycle to be considered for a future position.
We encourage you to speak to the Office of Financial Aid prior to status updates being sent out.
Note:
An interview invite does not guarantee a position. We ask that you wait for a status update regarding interview invitations and final decisions post interviews as inquiries can further delay the process.

Additional Information and Resources
It is highly recommended that you speak to a representative from the Office of Financial Aid prior to applying and interviewing for the position. The remuneration for the position is room and a semi-monthly stipend, and this may affect your personal financial aid. Information can be found on the
Office of Financial Aid
website.
Should you be offered a position,
Housing and Residential Life will notify the Office of Financial Aid of your appointment.  Please note that the room is added as a grant and that the semi-monthly stipend is taxable.  If you receive financial aid, your package will be reviewed and may be revised.  This means that you may be required to repay a refund and/or make a payment to the bursar account for balances no longer covered under your original aid package.
If you receive a refund during a semester that you are a Resident Advisor, you should contact bursar to confirm that it is valid prior to utilizing the funds. The refund could have been dispersed when it shouldn’t have been due to Financial Aid and the Bursar still working with Housing and Residential Life to make necessary adjustments after a position offer is made and accepted.
Financial Aid and the Bursar will have been made aware of your position offer and will place a hold on your account.  Should you not accept the Resident Advisor position and are meant to receive a refund, disbursal may be delayed.
If you are an international student, it is recommended that you connect with the Office of Global Learning to learn about eligibility requirements. Information can be found on the
Office of Global Learning
website.
If you are interested in resources available to you regarding interview preparation, we encourage you to visit the
Career Services
website.
