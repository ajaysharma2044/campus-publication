Commencement Housing On Campus
Commencement Housing 2026
Residence Hall Accommodations on Friday, Saturday and Sunday Nights for Families and Guests
We're excited to welcome families, friends and guests of graduating students to campus for the May 2026 Commencement. On-campus housing in the residence halls on North Campus and West Campus will be offered for guests during the 2026 Commencement Weekend.
How to make reservations
Students, families, and guests
may reserve housing for Commencement Weekend starting in early January, 2026 at the
Commencement Housing Reservation site
.
