Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall
Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall is a modern residence hall designed to help students build connections, explore new opportunities, and become active members of the Cornell community. As part of the North Campus residential experience, it is an excellent fit for students who enjoy meeting new people, trying new activities, and immersing themselves in the social, academic, and cultural opportunities that shape their college experience.
Designed to foster interaction and community, Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall features modern living spaces along with spacious lounges, study areas, and nearby cafés that encourage collaboration, conversation, and connection.
Living in Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall gives students the opportunity to develop lasting friendships, build a strong sense of belonging, and establish a foundation for academic, personal, and social success at Cornell.
More About Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall
The hall is named in honor of Ruth Bader Ginsburg '54, the trailblazing U.S. Supreme Court Justice and Cornell alumna whose lifelong commitment to equality, justice, and public service left an enduring impact on the nation. Her legacy serves as an inspiration for students as they begin their own journeys of intellectual growth, leadership, and civic engagement at Cornell. Residents benefit from a vibrant community where connection, exploration, and shared experiences are central to daily life.
Residents are supported by a dedicated Residence Hall Director, Faculty-in-Residence member, and Resident Advisors who host programs and activities that help students meet new friends, explore campus resources, and find their place within the university community.

Affiliated Faculty
Kristen Underhill, Professor of Law, is the Faculty-in-Residence for Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall. She lives in an apartment in the building with her partner, Brian Richardson, Faculty Fellow in Residence and Assistant Professor of Law, and regularly spends time with student residents of the Ruth Bader Ginsburg community.
Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hall also has a number of Faculty and Community Fellows that provide students with opportunities to explore and cultivate their academic, intellectual, and cultural interests. You will find them attending events in your building, eating with students in the dining room, and leading on and off campus trips to lectures, plays, films, concerts, and regional points of interest.
