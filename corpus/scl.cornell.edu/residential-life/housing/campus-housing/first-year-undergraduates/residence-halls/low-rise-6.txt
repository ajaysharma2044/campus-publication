Low Rise #6
Low Rise 6 is part of a welcoming residential neighborhood on North Campus, where students can enjoy both the energy of the broader Cornell community and the comfort of a close-knit living environment.
The building's thoughtfully designed common spaces, including a main lounge, study room, and kitchen, encourage everyday interaction and community building. Residents gather to share meals, study together, participate in programs hosted by residential staff and faculty members in residence, watch films, enjoy informal conversations, and build lasting friendships. These shared experiences help students develop a sense of belonging while balancing social engagement with academic success.
Living in Low Rise 6 offers students the opportunity to become part of a supportive residential community, connect with peers from diverse backgrounds and interests, and take advantage of all that North Campus has to offer. The result is a living experience that fosters personal growth, meaningful relationships, and a strong connection to the Cornell community.
More About Low Rise 6
Opened in 1970, Low Rise 6 is one of a series of modern brick-and-glass residences connected by pedestrian pathways and situated alongside several of Cornell's Program Houses. While the building does not have a formal namesake, it holds a unique place in Cornell's residential history, having briefly served as housing for students participating in the Committee on Special Educational Projects (COSEP) during its first semester of operation.
As part of the North Campus community, Low Rise 6 is ideal for students who want to meet new people, explore a wide range of academic and extracurricular opportunities, and remain connected to the vibrant undergraduate experience. Residents benefit from living in a community that values connection, discovery, and shared experiences while providing a comfortable space to establish friendships and find their place at Cornell.
"My favorite part about Low Rise #6 is the community it provides. The way the units are set up makes it so easy to meet and hang out with everyone." — Zoe G.

Faculty Involvement
Stephanie Thomas, Senior Lecturer in Economics, is the Faculty-in-Residence for the Low Rises. She lives in an apartment in Low Rise 6 and regularly spends time with residents of the Low Rise community.
The Low Rises also have a number of Faculty and Community Fellows that provide students with opportunities to explore and cultivate their academic, intellectual, and cultural interests. You will find them attending events in your building, eating with students in the dining room, and leading on and off campus trips to lectures, plays, films, concerts, and regional points of interest.
