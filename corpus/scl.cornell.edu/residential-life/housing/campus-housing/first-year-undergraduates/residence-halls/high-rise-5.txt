High Rise #5
High Rise 5 offers students a dynamic residential experience at the center of Cornell's North Campus community. Opened in 1971 as part of the university's expansion of undergraduate housing, the building has long served as a welcoming home for students seeking connection, engagement, and an active campus experience.
The residence features a corridor-style layout with multi-bedroom units and kitchens that encourage students to build friendships and connect with neighbors. One of the building's most distinctive features is its top-floor Skylounge, which offers sweeping views of North Campus, the university golf course, and Fuertes Observatory while providing a unique space for studying, socializing, and relaxing.
More About High Rise 5
High Rise 5 is an excellent fit for students who enjoy meeting new people, exploring new interests, and staying closely connected to the broader Cornell community. Located just steps from the Robert Purcell Community Center and Morrison Dining, residents are immersed in the social, cultural, and academic opportunities that make North Campus a hub of undergraduate life.
Living in High Rise 5 gives students the opportunity to develop meaningful relationships, participate in a vibrant residential community, and enjoy easy access to campus resources and activities. Whether gathering with friends in a lounge, attending community programs, or exploring everything Cornell has to offer, residents gain a strong sense of belonging and a supportive home base for their undergraduate journey.

Faculty Involvement
High Rise 5 and Jameson Hall have a number of Faculty and Community Fellows that provide students with opportunities to explore and cultivate their academic, intellectual, and cultural interests. You will find them attending events in your building, eating with students in the dining room, and leading on and off campus trips to lectures, plays, films, concerts, and regional points of interest.
