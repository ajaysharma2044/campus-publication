Mews Hall
Mews Hall offers students a welcoming and engaged residential experience at the heart of Cornell's North Campus community. Located adjacent to Appel Commons, residents enjoy convenient access to dining, study spaces, recreation, and campus resources while becoming part of a vibrant community designed to help students make the most of their Cornell experience. Mews is an excellent fit for students who enjoy meeting new people, exploring new opportunities, and staying connected to the social and academic energy of campus life.
Mews Hall is also home to Loving House, one of Cornell's Program Houses. Loving House provides an intentional and supportive residential community centered on LGBTQIA+ identities, inclusion, and allyship. Through community-building, dialogue, and educational programming, residents have opportunities to explore topics related to identity, sexuality, gender expression, cultural competency, and social systems while contributing to a welcoming environment grounded in mutual respect and acceptance.
More About Mews Hall
Opened in 2001 as part of Cornell's North Campus Residential Initiative, its name derives from a British term for a cluster of residences arranged around a small street or courtyard, reflecting the building's distinctive design.
Residents of Mews benefit from a strong sense of connection and belonging fostered through a wide variety of programs and traditions. Activities such as trips to New York City and the United Nations, ice skating events, community barbecues, weekly study breaks, and other social and educational programs help students build friendships, explore new interests, and create lasting memories. Through these experiences, students develop meaningful relationships, expand their perspectives, and gain confidence navigating university life.
Whether students are seeking the broad connections and opportunities of North Campus or the shared purpose and support of a themed residential community, Mews Hall offers a home where residents can build community, explore their identities and interests, and establish a strong foundation for success and belonging at Cornell.
