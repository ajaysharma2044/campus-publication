Conference Housing On Campus
Cornell Conference and Event Services
hosts more than 100 conferences, events, and academic meetings each year, with an annual attendance of 15,000 participants coming to campus
From the beginning of June through mid-August, conference facilitators can work with Conference and Event Services to provide accommodations for their attendees. On- or off-campus, Conference and Event Services can offer your group a variety of housing options. They can help connect you with area hotels or your conference guests can stay in University housing right on Cornell's scenic campus, close to event facilities, and Cornell Dining's world-class eateries.
Visit the
Cornell Conference Services website
, or
contact a conference planner
in that office, for more information.
