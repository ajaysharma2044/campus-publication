104 Mary Ann Wood Drive
104 Mary Ann Wood Drive offers a residential experience that balances independence, convenience, and connection. Located at the base of Libe Slope, students are just steps from Noyes Community Center, the West Campus House System, and Central Campus, giving residents easy access to academic resources, dining, cultural events, and campus activities while enjoying a quieter living environment.
As part of the South Campus community, 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive is an excellent fit for students who have established their friendships, interests, and routines at Cornell and are seeking a more self-directed residential experience. Residents value a calm and respectful atmosphere that supports academic focus, personal well-being, and the flexibility to invest more deeply in the relationships, organizations, and pursuits that matter most to them.
More About 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive
The residence offers a unique combination of proximity and independence, with modern enhancements including newly installed flooring, updated bathrooms, and common areas that were renovated in 2026. Students benefit from being close to the intellectual, cultural, and culinary opportunities of West Campus while enjoying a residential setting with its own distinct character. This balance allows residents to remain connected to the broader Cornell community while shaping a lifestyle that aligns with their individual goals and priorities.
Living at 104 Mary Ann Wood Drive provides students with the space and flexibility to deepen their academic interests, strengthen meaningful connections, and enjoy the independence that comes with upper-level living, all within a supportive and conveniently located residential community.

Laundry Information
Laundry located on-site.
Payment for use of washers and dryers is through a Campus Life debit account accessed by downloading the CSCPay Mobile App to add funds to your laundry account. You can download the app from the App Store or Google Play. The recommended starting amount is $25. Subsequent deposits can be made at any time. The balance in your laundry account will remain active as long as you live in University housing. Please note that any balance remaining after you move out of University housing cannot be refunded.
