Schuyler House
Schuyler House offers a unique residential experience for students who want the independence and neighborhood feel of off-campus living while still enjoying the convenience and advantages of on-campus housing.
Schuyler House is approximately a 15- to 20-minute walk from Central Campus. For added convenience, frequent TCAT buses stop directly at Schuyler House, with service ranging from three to eleven buses per hour depending on the time of day, providing a quick five-minute ride to campus. To make getting around even easier, all Schuyler House residents receive a complimentary TCAT bus pass.
With its combination of accessibility, independence, and strong connection to both Cornell and the broader Ithaca community, Schuyler House is an ideal home for students seeking the best of both on-campus and off-campus living.
More About Schuyler House
Built in 1910 but opened as a residence hall in 1981, Schuyler House is named after George W. Schuyler, Cornell’s first treasurer.  Nestled just below Collegetown, Schuyler House provides easy access to Cornell's academic core while immersing residents in the vibrant culture, dining, and energy of both Collegetown and downtown Ithaca.
Its location makes it easy to stay connected to campus life while enjoying the character of one of Ithaca's most dynamic neighborhoods. Students are within reach of classes, campus dining locations, and university resources, while also benefiting from nearby local restaurants, shops, and community attractions.

Mostly singles, with some doubles and a few triples; rooms arranged along corridors with shared bathrooms on each floor
Lounges, TV rooms, a study room, kitchen facilities on each floor, and a laundry room
Bedrooms contain, for each occupant: an extra-long twin bed, a closet and a bureau (or wardrobe/bureau combination), a desk and desk chair, a wastebasket, and floor carpeting
Wireless Internet access
A free bus pass for all residents
Schuyler House rooms do not have air conditioning.
