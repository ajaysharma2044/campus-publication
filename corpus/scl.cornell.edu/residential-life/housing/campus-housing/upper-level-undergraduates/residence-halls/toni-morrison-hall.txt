Toni Morrison Hall
Toni Morrison Hall offers an engaging residential experience that supports academic success, personal growth, and community involvement. Named in honor of Toni Morrison, the first African American woman to receive the Nobel Prize in Literature, the hall celebrates the legacy of one of Cornell's most distinguished alumnae.
Designed with innovative floor plans and a contemporary aesthetic, Toni Morrison Hall creates an environment that encourages connection, collaboration, and engagement. The building's layout intentionally draws residents beyond their individual rooms into welcoming common spaces that support group study, social interaction, and community programming. Spacious lounges and nearby cafés provide natural gathering places where students can build friendships, exchange ideas, and participate in a vibrant residential community.
More About Toni Morrison Hall
A hallmark of life in Toni Morrison Hall is its strong tradition of student leadership and self-governance. The student-led Community Council plays an active role in shaping the residential experience by organizing a wide variety of programs and events. Activities range from trips to local theaters and seasonal outings such as apple picking to educational discussions and workshops on topics including women in the workplace, navigating academic life at Cornell, sexuality and relationship development, and body image.
Toni Morrison Hall offers students a dynamic and supportive community where they can connect with peers, pursue leadership opportunities, and engage in meaningful conversations, all while honoring the legacy of a literary icon whose work and influence continue to inspire generations.

Affiliated Faculty
Toni Morrison Hall has a number of Faculty and Community Fellows that provide students with opportunities to explore and cultivate their academic, intellectual, and cultural interests. You will find them attending events in your building, eating with students in the dining room, and leading on- and off-campus trips to lectures, plays, films, concerts, and regional points of interest.
