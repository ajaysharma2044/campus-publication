112 Edgemoor
112 Edgemoor offers a small, close-knit experience for students in a residential neighborhood beside Cascadilla Gorge and conveniently situated between West Campus and South Campus. The residence provides easy access to the dining, academic, cultural, and social opportunities of both campus and Collegetown while maintaining a peaceful, neighborhood feel.
Beginning in 2027, 112 Edgemoor will be home to Veterans House, a Program House for students interested in learning more about, and supporting, the military community. While the focus of the program house is to support Cornell’s student veterans, having a diverse residential program fosters an atmosphere that not only benefits student veterans but also creates a greater awareness of the unique challenges they face.
More About 112 Edgemoor
Living at 112 Edgemoor gives students the best of both worlds: the independence and tranquility of a smaller residential setting alongside convenient access to the resources, opportunities, and vibrant communities that make Cornell unique. Residents gain a strong sense of belonging, meaningful connections, and the flexibility to shape a Cornell experience centered on what matters most to them.
Community Features:
Open to transfer and upperlevel students
20 residents
Located between West Campus and South Campus
All single rooms, with shared single-sex bathrooms
Lounges/common areas, a kitchen, and laundry facilities (with coin-operated washers/dryers)
Bedrooms contain, for each occupant: an extra-long twin bed, a closet and a bureau (or wardrobe/bureau combination), a desk and desk chair, a wastebasket, and floor carpeting
Wireless Internet access
112 Edgemoor rooms do not have air conditioning.

Faculty Involvement
South Campus Residence Halls have a number of Faculty and Community Fellows that provide students with opportunities to explore and cultivate their academic, intellectual, and cultural interests. You will find them attending events in your building, eating with students, and leading on and off campus trips to lectures, plays, films, concerts, and regional points of interest.
