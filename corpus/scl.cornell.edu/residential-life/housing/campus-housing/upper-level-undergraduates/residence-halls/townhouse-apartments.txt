Townhouse Apartments
Townhouse Apartments offer students the independence of apartment-style living paired with the convenience, resources, and community of living on campus.
Located on North Campus, the Townhouses are well suited for students who value both independence and community. Residents enjoy the flexibility of living in four- and six-person apartments featuring shared bedrooms, full kitchens, living rooms, and bathrooms while remaining connected to the broad social, academic, and extracurricular opportunities that define the Cornell experience.
Living in the Townhouses is ideal for students who are looking for greater independence, a more self-directed living experience, and the opportunity to build meaningful connections with roommates, neighbors, and the broader Cornell community. Residents gain the benefits of apartment living without sacrificing access to campus amenities, creating a residential experience that supports personal growth, and community engagement.
More About the Townhouse Apartments
Opened in 1986, the Townhouse Apartments was developed to expand Cornell's housing options and accommodate growing residential needs while creating a living environment that offers students greater autonomy and responsibility. Consisting of eight townhouse clusters, identified as Townhouses A through H, the community was intentionally designed to provide a more independent residential experience while maintaining strong connections to campus life.
The Townhouse Apartments strike a unique balance between privacy and engagement. Residents have access to a dedicated community center that serves as a hub for programs, events, studying, and socializing, while the nearby Robert Purcell Community Center, Morrison Dining, and Bear Necessities convenience store provide additional resources just steps away. This combination of apartment-style living and campus connectivity allows students to develop life skills such as shared responsibility, communication, and independent decision-making while still enjoying the support of a residential community.

Affiliated Faculty
The Townhouses Apartments have a number of Faculty and Community Fellows that provide students with opportunities to explore and cultivate their academic, intellectual, and cultural interests. You will find them attending events in your building, eating with students in the dining room, and leading on and off-campus trips to lectures, plays, films, concerts, and regional points of interest.
