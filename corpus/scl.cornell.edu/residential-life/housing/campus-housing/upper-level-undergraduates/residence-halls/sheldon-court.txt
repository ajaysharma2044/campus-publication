Sheldon Court
Sheldon Court combines a rich tradition of student living with the independence and convenience that define Cornell's South Campus community. Located next to the Schwartz Center for the Performing Arts in the heart of Collegetown, Sheldon Court places residents just steps from popular restaurants, coffee shops, shops, and gathering places while offering easy access to campus resources and opportunities.
Sheldon Court reflects the character of Cornell's South Campus community, where students who have found their place at Cornell can enjoy a more independent and self-directed residential experience. The community is especially appealing to students who value a quieter atmosphere, have established friendships and campus connections, and are looking for a living environment that supports focus, personal well-being, and meaningful engagement.
More About Sheldon Court
Opened in 1903, Sheldon Court is one of Cornell's earliest purpose-built student residences.  Built in the Italianate style, the residence was operated by Charles L. Sheldon, Jr. '01, M.A. '02, who proudly promoted it as "Cornell's First Dormitory" and Cornell ultimately purchased the building in 1955, retaining its original name. For more than a century, it has provided generations of Cornellians with a place to live, learn, and build community close to the center of campus life.
Today, residents enjoy close proximity to the intellectual, cultural, and culinary opportunities of West Campus and Collegetown while benefiting from a welcoming community atmosphere. Through residential programming, shared experiences, and everyday interactions, Sheldon Court provides opportunities for students to strengthen their connections to Cornell and make the most of their upper-level undergraduate experience.
