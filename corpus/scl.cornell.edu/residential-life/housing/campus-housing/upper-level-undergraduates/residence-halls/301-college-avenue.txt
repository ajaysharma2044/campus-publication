301 College Avenue
301 College Avenue offers students the independence of apartment-style living with the convenience of remaining connected to Cornell and the vibrant Collegetown neighborhood. Located in the heart of Collegetown, residents are steps from restaurants, cafés, shops, and campus resources while providing a comfortable home base for students who have established their routines, friendships, and interests at Cornell.
Living at 301 College Avenue allows students to enjoy the best of both worlds: the independence and responsibility of apartment-style living alongside the support and connection of the Cornell residential community. Residents gain valuable experience in managing a more autonomous lifestyle while remaining close to the people, resources, and opportunities that matter most to their Cornell experience.
More About 301 College Avenue
As part of the South Campus community, 301 College Avenue is especially well suited for students seeking a more self-directed residential experience. Residents value the flexibility to focus on academics, leadership opportunities, career preparation, personal interests, and meaningful relationships while enjoying a respectful and independent living environment. The building features four floors of residential units, ranging from studios to six-bedroom units with kitchenettes, accommodating a variety of living preferences and roommate configurations to meet different needs and lifestyles, and supports students who are looking for greater autonomy and the opportunity to shape their daily lives in ways that align with their goals and priorities.
