Wait Avenue Cooperative
302 Wait Avenue
Wait Avenue Cooperative is one of the university's cooperative residences, a collaborative and independent community governed by its student residents.
Located at 302 Wait Avenue on north campus, Wait Avenue Co-op provides a family-like atmosphere for 14 women, a diverse mixture of undergraduate and graduate/professional students. Residents enjoy socializing, watching movies, and sharing meals together.
The house is more than 100 years old – one of the oldest buildings on north campus – and full of character, with a spacious front porch. One or two house managers handle daily house operations, and all members share in house maintenance responsibilities.
For more information, contact
waitavenue@cornell.edu
.
Community Features:
Open to all female-identifying transfer, upper-level, and graduate/professional students
14 residents (all women)
Located at 302 Wait Avenue on north campus
Single and double rooms, with shared bathrooms
A living room with a TV, a full kitchen, a front porch, free laundry, free parking, and summer storage
Bedrooms contain, for each occupant: a bed, a closet and a bureau (or wardrobe/bureau combination), a desk and desk chair, a desk lamp, and a wastebasket
Internet access included
