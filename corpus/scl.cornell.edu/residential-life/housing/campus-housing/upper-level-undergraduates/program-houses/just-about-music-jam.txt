Just About Music (JAM)
Low Rise 9, home to the Just About Music (JAM) Program House, is a vibrant residential community, providing a welcoming home for students whose interests range from performing and songwriting to music production, listening, and exploring the role of music in everyday life. Residents come from a wide variety of academic disciplines, not just music majors. What unites them is a shared enthusiasm for music, creativity, and collaboration.
Living in Low Rise 9 gives students the opportunity to deepen their connection to music, develop leadership and event-planning skills, express themselves creatively, and form lasting relationships with peers who share their interests. Whether performing on stage, recording original work, attending community events, or simply discovering new music with friends, residents help create a welcoming and dynamic community that feels like home.
More About Just About Music (JAM)
Opened in 1970 as part of Cornell's North Campus residential expansion, the building remains officially known as Low Rise 9, though it has been closely associated with JAM since the program house was established there in 2000. As a Program House, JAM offers students the opportunity to build friendships and community through common interests while contributing their own talents, perspectives, and ideas.
Residents enjoy access to unique resources, including a concert stage with professional sound and lighting, a recording studio, a 24-hour piano and practice room, and shared instruments. Community traditions such as monthly Open Mic nights, listening parties, social events, craft nights, and student-led programs create opportunities for students to perform, collaborate, and showcase their creativity. At the same time, established quiet hours and dedicated study spaces help maintain a balanced environment that supports both artistic pursuits and academic success.
JAM is an ideal fit for students who want to live in a community built around a common passion while still enjoying the energy and opportunities of the broader North Campus experience.

Faculty Involvement

How to Apply
Rising junior and rising senior students may select to live in a program house as part of the fall Housing Selection process while rising sophomores can select during their General Room Selection process in the spring. New students can indicate their preference to live in a program house on their New Student Housing Application.
*Please note in some years when housing space is limited and there are available spaces in the Just About Music (JAM) residence hall, some students who did not preference housing in JAM may be placed there. While the JAM community is intended for all students who share in a passion or appreciation for music, all students of all identities and interests are welcome. Regardless of their housing preference, all students residing in JAM will have the opportunity to connect with each other, build community, and grow. Finally, given the theme of JAM, all residents are expected to be thoughtful of the intention of the space and engage with every student in a respectful and open-minded manner.
