Risley Residential College
Prudence Risley Hall is one of Cornell's most distinctive residential communities, where creativity, tradition, and artistic exploration come together in a setting unlike any other on campus. Located on North Campus, Risley is home to the Risley Residential College, Cornell's first Program House, and welcomes students who are passionate about the arts and creative expression.
Its castle-like architecture has long made it a campus landmark. Today, Risley brings together students from diverse backgrounds and disciplines who share a passion for creating, learning, and contributing to a dynamic residential community.
Risley is also home to the Risley Dining Room, Cornell's AllerCheck™ approved gluten-free, tree nut-free, and peanut-free dining facility.
More About Risley Residential College
Opened in 1913, the building was named for Prudence Risley (1778-1865), the mother of financier Russell Sage. As Cornell's first expansion north of Fall Creek Gorge, Risley helped shape the future growth of North Campus and established a residential presence beyond the university's original academic core. In 1970, Risley became home to Cornell's first Program House, fostering a community rooted in creativity, artistic expression, inclusion, and tradition.
As part of North Campus, Risley offers students access to the energy and opportunities of campus life while providing a smaller, interest-based community centered on shared creative pursuits. Residents contribute as artists, performers, organizers, advocates, and collaborators, helping shape the culture and traditions of the community.
More than a residence hall, Prudence Risley Hall is a creative community where students turn their passions into shared experiences, build meaningful connections, and contribute to one of Cornell's most unique residential traditions.
Risley Dining Room, located in Prudence Risley Hall and modeled after the Christ Church Refectory at Oxford University, serves as a central gathering space for residents and visitors.
Community Features
Open to first-year, transfer, and upper-level students
194 residents
Located on North Campus, near Robert Purcell Community Center and Appel Commons
Single and double rooms arranged on corridors, with all-gender bathrooms above the first floor.
A dining hall, community lounges, and kitchens on every floor
Bedrooms contain, for each occupant: an extra-long twin bed, a closet and a bureau (or wardrobe/bureau combination), a desk and desk chair, a wastebasket, recycle bin, and carpet on 2nd and 3rd floors. (Eventually all carpet from bedrooms will be removed.)
Wireless Internet access
Risley Residential College rooms do not have air conditioning.

How to Apply
Rising junior and rising senior students may select to live in a program house as part of the fall Housing Selection process while rising sophomores can select during their General Room Selection process in the spring. New students can indicate their preference to live in a program house on their New Student Housing Application.
