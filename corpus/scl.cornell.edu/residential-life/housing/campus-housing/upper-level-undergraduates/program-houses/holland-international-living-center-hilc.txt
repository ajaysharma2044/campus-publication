Holland International Living Center (HILC)
Low Rise 8, home to the Jerome H. Holland International Living Center (HILC), offers students a unique opportunity to build a globally minded community while enjoying the energy and connections of North Campus.
HILC is an ideal fit for students who are curious about the world, enjoy learning from people with different backgrounds and perspectives, and value cross-cultural exchange. International and domestic students live together in a supportive community centered on global citizenship, cultural exploration, and mutual respect.
More than a residence hall, HILC serves as a home away from home where residents gain a deeper appreciation for diverse perspectives, build a strong support network, and become part of a welcoming community committed to global understanding, respect, and connection.
More About the Jerome H. Holland International Living Center (HILC)
Opened in 1970 as part of Cornell's North Campus expansion, the building itself remains officially known as Low Rise 8, but it has been home to the International Living Center since its first year of operation. In 1985, the program house was renamed in honor of Jerome H. Holland '39, M.S. '41, a distinguished Cornell alumnus, educator, diplomat, university president, and advocate for international understanding, human rights, and education.
Residents share traditions, discuss international issues, and create meaningful connections that broaden their understanding of the world while helping international students feel at home at Cornell.
As both a Program House and part of the broader North Campus community, HILC combines a strong sense of shared purpose with access to the social energy and opportunities that define the undergraduate residential experience. Through cultural celebrations, discussions, educational programs, and student-led activities, residents develop intercultural communication skills, global awareness, leadership experience, and lasting friendships that extend across cultures and continents. A student-run council helps shape community life, ensuring there are ample opportunities for both meaningful dialogue and social engagement.

How to Apply
Rising junior and rising senior students may select to live in a program house as part of the fall Housing Selection process while rising sophomores can select during their General Room Selection process in the spring. New students can indicate their preference to live in a program house on their New Student Housing Application.
*Please note in some years when housing space is limited and there are available spaces in the Jerome H. Holland International Living Center (HILC), some students who did not preference housing in HILC may be placed there. While the HILC community is intended for all students interested in cross-cultural exchange, all students of all identities and interests are welcome. Regardless of their housing preference, all students residing in HILC will have the opportunity to connect with each other, build community, and grow. Finally, given the theme of HILC, all residents are expected to be thoughtful of the intention of the space and engage with every student in a respectful and open-minded manner.
