Language House
The Language House, Cornell's language immersion residence, provides a unique living-learning community that helps students become comfortably fluent in one of the languages offered for the academic year.  Languages for the 2026-2027 academic year include Spanish, French,  Mandarin, Korean and American Sign Language (ASL).
Language House is currently in Toni Morrison Hall, but future location is still being determined.
NOTE: The Language House is not a traditional Program House.
Community Features
Open to upper-level students
50+ residents
Single and double rooms arranged in suites with shared bathrooms. Suites in Language House are gender-inclusive.
Lounges, study areas, shared kitchen spaces, a music practice room, and laundry facilities
Bedrooms contain, for each occupant: an extra-long twin bed, a wardrobe and three-drawer bureau, a desk and desk chair, a desk lamp, a wastebasket, and a recycling bin
Wireless Internet access
Language House rooms in Toni Morrison Hall are temperature-controlled.
