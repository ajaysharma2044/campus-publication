FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Two-year residential requirement for full-time Ithaca first- and second-year students, with a required meal plan. Transfers who have not met the residential requirement elsewhere must live on campus for one academic year; spring transfers for the incoming spring and the following academic year; transfers with two years of on-campus residence at a previous school, or entering as juniors or seniors, are exempt. Individual exemptions via the housing application during open periods, one academic year at a time.
