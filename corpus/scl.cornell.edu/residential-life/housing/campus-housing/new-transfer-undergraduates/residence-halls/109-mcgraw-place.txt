109 McGraw Place
109 McGraw Place offers a distinctive residential experience that combines independence, community, and convenience. Located adjacent to West Campus, the residence provides easy access to academic buildings, dining options, and campus resources while maintaining the quieter, self-directed character that defines the South Campus community.
As part of the South Campus community, 109 McGraw Place is ideal for students who value independence, a respectful atmosphere, and the flexibility to shape their Cornell experience around academic, professional, and personal goals. Residents benefit from a setting that encourages personal recharge and stronger connections while remaining closely linked to the broader campus community.
109 McGraw Place will be closed for the 2026-2027 Academic Year
More About 109 McGraw Place
Originally built in 1965 as a fraternity house, 109 McGraw Place features a modern lodge-style design distinguished by its tan brick exterior, projecting balconies, deep cornice, and distinctive window detailing. Today, with recent modern updates, including new flooring, updated windows, and a new building and water heating system, the building serves students who have established their place at Cornell and are seeking a living environment that supports focus, balance, and meaningful engagement with the people and activities most important to them.
Living at 109 McGraw Place offers greater autonomy while maintaining engagement with campus life. Residents enjoy a quieter residential environment, convenient access to West Campus amenities, and a supportive community where they can deepen friendships, pursue their interests, and make the most of their Cornell experience.

Laundry Information
Payment for use of washers and dryers is through a Campus Life debit account accessed by downloading the CSCPay Mobile App to add funds to your laundry account. You can download the app from the App Store or Google Play. The recommended starting amount is $25. Subsequent deposits can be made at any time. The balance in your laundry account will remain active as long as you live in University housing. Please note that any balance remaining after you move out of University housing cannot be refunded.
