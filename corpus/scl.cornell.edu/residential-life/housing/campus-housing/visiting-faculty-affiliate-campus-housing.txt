Visiting Faculty & Affiliate On Campus Housing
Housing Options
Cornell provides on-campus housing to some visiting faculty members and Cornell affiliates who require temporary, academic-year, or 12-month accommodations.
Apartments are offered as space allows in
Hasbrouck Apartments
, a graduate and professional student community.
To apply for housing, visit
Apply for Housing
and follow the
instructions for graduate and professional students
.
To apply for temporary housing, contact
gradhousing@cornell.edu
.
On-campus housing is not guaranteed for visiting faculty members and special guests, but every effort is made to meet your request.
