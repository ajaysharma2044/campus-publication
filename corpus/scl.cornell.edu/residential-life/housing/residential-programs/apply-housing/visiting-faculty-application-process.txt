Visiting Faculty Application Process
Visiting faculty and Cornell affiliates seeking on-campus housing are placed in one of Cornell's
graduate and professional student communities
.
To apply for housing, please refer to the information and instructions for graduate and professional students found at
Application Process: Graduate & Professional Students
.
To apply for temporary housing, contact the
Housing & Dining Contracts Office
to speak with a housing coordinator.
On-campus housing is not guaranteed for visiting faculty members and special guests, but every effort is made to meet your request.
