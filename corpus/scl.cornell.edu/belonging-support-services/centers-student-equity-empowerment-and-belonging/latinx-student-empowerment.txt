Latinx Student Empowerment
The Latinx Student Empowerment office is a dedicated space to acknowledge and celebrate the rich diversity of the Latinx student experience at Cornell.
As a unit within the
Centers for Student Equity, Empowerment, and Belonging
, this office's purpose is to facilitate a supportive community for all students.
This office offers guidance to student organizations, coordinates and implements programming, and serves as a support network for all students.
