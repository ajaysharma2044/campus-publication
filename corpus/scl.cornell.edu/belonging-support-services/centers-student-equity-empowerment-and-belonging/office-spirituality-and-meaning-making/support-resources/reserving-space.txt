Reserving space
Sage Chapel and Anabel Taylor Hall are nonsectarian spaces that are religious and spiritual in nature. Priority is given to events that are religious or spiritual in nature, as well as musical events in the chapels, and events that support student wellness, equity, empowerment, and belonging.
Conference and Event Services
manages all weddings, remembrance services, private events, and requests from outside groups and parties.
Scheduling Procedure
Submit reservation requests through the
Scheduling@Cornell
website, where you can also find information describing rooms and capacity.
You may also contact the OSMM scheduler at 607.255.6002 or
swissman@cornell.edu
with any questions.
For full consideration, please submit your reservation request at least 10 business days before the scheduled event. CURW groups may reserve spaces up to one year in advance. Other groups may reserve spaces after July 15 for Fall Semester and after November 15 for Spring Semester.
Groups are responsible for their own setup and for returning the space to its original setup and condition after their event. This includes closing windows. For events with food, groups are required to carry out their trash to the dumpster behind Anabel Taylor or the dumpster in the Day Hall parking lot for Sage Chapel. We do not provide room setup and offer minimal tech support. Please contact CIT for more advanced audio/visual support and Facilities for setup assistance.
Please note you are not allowed to use candles in any of these facilities unless you have a fire permit from the fire department. Contact Environmental Health and Safety at
askehs@cornell.edu
or 607.255.8200.
Scheduling Priority
OSMM, CURW, and Music Department have priority in Sage Chapel and Anabel Taylor Chapel. The Alternative Testing Program has priority in the Auditorium (ATH 230) on weekdays during the academic year. OSMM, CURW, and student organizations affiliated with the Interfaith Council at Cornell have priority in all other spaces in Anabel Taylor Hall.
Major religious holidays (e.g. High Holy Days, Ramadan Iftars, Holy Week) take precedence over other events.
Regularly scheduled events normally take precedence over other special events, although this is subject to negotiated exceptions.
Campus events that are religious or spiritual in nature, or that support wellness, equity, and belonging, take precedence over other events.
Meetings and events for Cornell students and personnel take precedence over non-University activities.
Events for non-University groups and individuals must be sponsored by a University group or department or reserved through
Conference and Event Services
.
OSMM reserves the right to adjust or cancel reservations when this is reasonable to preserve public safety, to best serve Cornell students, and to honor the religious and spiritual nature of our shared spaces.
Available Spaces
Sage Chapel has an occupancy of 650 and is suitable for weddings, memorials, baptisms, lectures, film screenings, and concerts. You will need to make arrangements with the OSMM office or Conference and Event Services to access the AV Room or to play the front concert piano. Permission to use the organs requires prior approval from the University organist, Annette Richards, Ph.D., who can be reached at annette.richards@cornell.edu or 607-255-7102.
Anabel Taylor Hall Chapel (ATH 112) has a total of 120 available chairs and an upright Steinway piano. There is a small sound system with available microphones. Permission to use the organs requires prior approval from the University organist, Annette Richards, Ph.D., who can be reached at annette.richards@cornell.edu or 607-255-7102.
The Founders Room (ATH 114) has tables and chairs available to accommodate 49 people. This room has a baby grand piano. The large wooden table and other furniture should not be moved from their current locations.
The Edwards Room (ATH G14) is an open room with no set-up and 49 available chairs. There is a white board in this space and two tables.
The One World Room (ATH B21) is a large open room that accommodates 250 with available sound system. The room includes chairs and a limited number of tables.
The Auditorium (ATH 230) accommodates 160 with tables and chairs. There is an available sound system, projector, and connections for your computer and devices. There is a baby grand piano on the stage.
ATH 314 is a seminar room with tables and chairs that accommodates 29. There is a white board in this space that you may use.
Services of Remembrance
The Office of Spirituality and Meaning-Making plans an annual Service of Remembrance to honor and remember departed members of the Cornell community. If you have questions about this annual event, please contact
osmm@cornell.edu
.
Sage Chapel and Anabel Taylor Hall Chapel are available to family and friends of deceased alumni who would like to hold a service of remembrance on campus. Our office is unable to provide support for these services. Please contact
Conference and Event Services
.
Weddings
Congratulations on your engagement and your decision to celebrate your marriage on the Cornell campus. Sage Chapel and Anabel Taylor Hall Chapel are both suitable venues for your wedding.
Conference and Event Services
manages all weddings. Please contact
weddings@cornell.edu
or 607-253-8368.
