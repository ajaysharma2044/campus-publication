Mathew Hall
Associate Dean of Students & Director of the LGBT Resource Center
mh2667@cornell.edu
626 Thurston Ave. Ithaca, NY 14850 (third floor)
Mathew Hall (he/they), M.Ed., serves, since August 2025, as the Associate Dean of Students and Director of the LGBT Resource Center and is part of the larger staff of the Centers for Student Equity, Empowerment, and Belonging. In his role, Mat provides oversight and leadership to the work of the LGBT Resource Center and supports its innovative and welcoming programming, services, and support for all LGBTQ+ students. Mat’s professional philosophy is one of holistic, student-centered support across the academic lifecycle.

Tina Coyne
Assistant Director of the LGBT Resource Center
tc748@cornell.edu
626 Thurston Ave Ithaca NY 14850 (third floor)
Tina Coyne (she/they) is the Assistant Director of the LGBT Resource Center. In this role she focuses on implementing educational and community focused programming, running the center, and providing direct support to students. Tina centers their experiences serving as a former K-12 art educator and queer organizational director in their work, often taking creative and anarchist approaches to support LGBTQ+ student development. She fondly refers to her role in higher education as part-time educator and full-time queer party planner.
