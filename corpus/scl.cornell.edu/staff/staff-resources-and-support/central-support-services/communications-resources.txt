Communications Resources
Overview of Responsibilities
Student & Campus Life Communications and Marketing tells the story of the Division of Student & Campus Life and its multiple units and programs to the Cornell University community through the creation, implementation, and assessment of effective integrated marketing communications.
Services Available
SCL Communications and Marketing supports the Division’s internal stakeholders to reach their full potential and convey our mission of inspiring transformation. Our office’s core focus is to strategically craft and disseminate SCL’s collective story about its impact on the Cornell student experience.
Resources
SCL Brand and Identity Guidelines
Student & Campus Life (SCL) is a division comprised of a system of multiple units, offices, and program areas unified and connected by a single mission to inspire transformation. The SCL Identity Architecture is to be used as outlined by all SCL units, offices, and program areas to serve our mission by supporting enhanced brand recognition, increased message alignment, and divisional connection with our student, parent/family, staff, alumni, and donor audiences.​
Please connect directly with the team below to discuss how to use the SCL Identity Architecture system in your office's materials and messaging.
