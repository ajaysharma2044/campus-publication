Ticket Purchase Policy
The purchased ticket is good only for the event and seat/section for which it is issued (if event is reserved seating). Tickets are not transferable unless indicated in event details. The ticket is a limited revocable license and may be revoked and admission refunded at the sole discretion of the sponsoring organization. Cornell University and/or the sponsoring organization reserves the right, without refund of any portion of the ticket price, to refuse admission or eject any person whose conduct is deemed disorderly or fails to comply with the terms and conditions contained herein.
The sponsoring organization restricts the resale of their ticket(s) for higher than face value (plus fees) plus the greater of 10% of the ticket price or $5.00. Tickets purchased or obtained from CornellTickets.com may not be resold or given away as part of a package (i.e. "Purchase [item A] and receive this ticket free."). Unauthorized or unlawful distribution, resale or attempted resale is grounds for seizure and cancellation without compensation. Tickets obtained from unauthorized sources may be lost, stolen or counterfeit, and if so are void. The ticket cannot be replaced if lost, destroyed or stolen. BY COMPLETING A PURCHASE AT CORNELLTICKETS.COM, YOU ARE AGREEING TO ABIDE BY THE TICKET PURCHASE POLICY.
Cornell University is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged property. With the exception of approved media, any recording devices (included still photography, video, and/or audio) are prohibited inside the venue. Smoking is prohibited inside all buildings.
In the event of cancellation, refunds will be available for three weeks only. Refund information will be available at CornellTickets.com. If event is postponed and rescheduled for another date within three (3) months of scheduled date, the ticket shall be valid for the rescheduled date, and refunds will not be issued under any circumstances.
The ticket is not subject to any refund (unless show is cancelled, as outlined above) and shall bear no cash value. If issued complimentary, this ticket shall not be exchangeable. No refunds or exchanges, except as provided herein. Event date & time subject to change. All rights reserved.
