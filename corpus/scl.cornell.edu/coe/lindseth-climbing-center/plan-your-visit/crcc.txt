Cornell Rock Climbing Club
Cornell Rock Climbing Club (CRCC) is a student-run organization whose goal is to help a community of climbers who can train together,
regardless of skill and ability
. We aim to help open up the doors to training for climbing, and make it more accessible to everyone.
We hold weekly practices at the Lindseth Climbing Center located in Bartels Hall. Some events we've had recently are movie nights at Cayuga Climbs and climbing socials. Past outdoor trips include Little Falls, NY; the Gunks; Red River Gorge, KY; and Rumney, NH.
Any Cornell undergrad and grad student may join. We welcome anyone who wants to learn about climbing, develop skills, meet new friends and belay partners, and/or train and compete. Whether you've been climbing for years, or are just now starting out, we want you to join us on our journey to building community and helping all who are interested improve their climbing ability.
