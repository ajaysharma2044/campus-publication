Olivia Murray is the Administrative Assistant for Student Support and Advocacy Services and Office Manager of the Elizabeth Chapman Staley Center. Olivia assists in coordination and team logistics for the team, as well as managing the physical space of the Dean of Students Suite. Olivia also serves as the administrator for the Student-Parent Dependent Care Grant.
Olivia studied Political Science and Public Administration, earning her bachelor's degree in 2020 from SUNY Geneseo. While at her alma mater, Olivia was the Manager of the MacVittie College Union, an intern for the Alcohol and Other Drugs Program, and a dedicated executive member of Geneseo Outing Club. She is delighted to be back in her hometown, working for Cornell in a capacity that draws upon her experiences and passion for supporting students through their transformative college years. When not at work, Olivia loves to hike all over New York, knit, and cook for her friends and family.
