Jaime Panos is an Associate Dean of Students within Student Support and Advocacy Services. Jaime provides support and resources to students who are navigating difficult times and oversees the Administrator on-Call program. Jaime is committed and passionate about creating safe, welcoming spaces and supporting the holistic development and growth of students. Previously, she served in numerous capacities within Residence Life at Elmira College, most recently as the Assistant Dean of Students. Outside of work, Jaime enjoys spending time on Cayuga Lake, being with family and friends and spoiling her two cats.
