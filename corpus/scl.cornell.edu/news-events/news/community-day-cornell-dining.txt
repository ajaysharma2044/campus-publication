Updated schedule for Cornell Dining on Friday, November 3rd
November 2, 2023
As Provost Michael Kotlikoff, Vice President for Student and Campus Life Ryan Lombardi, and Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer Christine Lovely announced on Wednesday, Cornell University is taking a community day on Friday, November 3rd, in recognition of the extraordinary stress of the past few weeks.
Some Cornell Dining eateries will be open extra hours, and several eateries will be closed for the day on Friday, to accommodate students and to allow as many staff members as possible to take the day off.
North Star Dining Room
will open for lunch on Friday from 10:30am-2:30pm.
Amit Bhatia Libe Café, Café Jennie, Franny's, Green Dragon, Mann Café, and Rusty's
will be closed on Friday.
Grab-and-go sushi will be available just at Bear Necessities and Jansen's Market on Friday.
Please check
now.dining.cornell.edu
for the latest schedule and menu updates.
About Cornell Dining
Cornell Dining operates over 30 on-campus eateries -- which include residential dining rooms, cafés, coffeehouses, food courts, and convenience stores -- and serves more than 23,000 meals a day to members of the Cornell community. Cornell University is consistently ranked in the Princeton Review's top ten for best campus food among all colleges and universities in the country! That high rating comes from customer surveys, and reflects Cornell Dining's commitment to serving high-quality foods that are healthy and creatively prepared with genuine care for a diverse community. For more information, visit dining.cornell.edu.
