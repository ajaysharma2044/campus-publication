Inside Higher Ed: Cornell's Let's Talk Offers Informal Counseling Consultations
November 30, 2023
Many colleges and universities are
scaling mental health services
and resources for students to accommodate greater need on campus. However, a summer 2023
Student Voice survey
by
Inside Higher Ed
and College Pulse found two-thirds of students haven’t used a campus mental health resource. Among students who rate their mental health as poor, half
have yet to utilize
campus services.
