Co-op Mosey Open House
October 18, 2023
Interested in moving to a Co-op for Spring 2024?
The Co-op Mosey, an afternoon of open houses of the university-owned cooperative residences will be held
Saturday, November 4, 2023 from 1:00pm - 5:00pm ET
.
For more information about cooperative living see the
Cooperative Living webpage
. The webpages for each house list: types of rooms and amenities in each house; the number of residents; whether the house has a meal plan; the address and location; and contact information for the house.
Applying for residency is handled independently by each house. Most houses will host additional events up until
November 21st
Decision notifications will be sent
Wednesday, November 29
th
, 9:00 p.m.
Students who have been accepted into a co-op from a residence hall are required to place a room change through their Housing Portal, stating the name of the co-op in which they have been accepted.
P.S.  The term "mosey" for the co-op open houses is a tradition; it was suggested as an alternative to the term "rush" used in the Greek letter organization community.
