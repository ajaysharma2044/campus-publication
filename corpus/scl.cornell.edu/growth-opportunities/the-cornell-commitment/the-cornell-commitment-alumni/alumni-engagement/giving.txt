Giving
Donations to the Cornell Commitment Programs
We greatly appreciate any donation to our programs and thank you in advance for thinking of our office.  Donations will help support:
post-graduate leadership or service experiences
welcome reception for freshmen and their families
faculty networking dinner for RCPRS
emergency funds for students
food, lodging and transportation for alumni engagement on campus
project funding for students
If you would like to make a gift with a credit card, you may use the following link:
www.giving.cornell.edu/give
, or call 1-800-279-3099 to speak with a member of our giving staff.
If you would prefer to mail a check, it may be sent to:
