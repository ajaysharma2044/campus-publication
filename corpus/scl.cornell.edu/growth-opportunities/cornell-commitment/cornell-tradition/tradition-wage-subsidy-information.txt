Tradition Wage Subsidy Information
The wage subsidy is available to Cornell Tradition students who do not receive work study or who have exhausted their work study for the year.  The wage subsidy must be renewed annually for each academic year.
Students
: Fill out the wage subsidy form below and submit it electronically. The Commitment office will then contact your supervisor. Please note that the Cornell Tradition wage subsidy benefit is for academic year wages only.
Wage Subsidy Form
Supervisors
: An email should have been sent to you from the Cornell Commitment office containing a wage subsidy application form. Please have your payroll representative complete this form and return it to the Commitment office.
