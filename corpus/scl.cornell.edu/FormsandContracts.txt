Forms & Contracts
The forms and contracts below are the only official forms student organizations need to process payments. Additional information will be required to process a payment request and students will be prompted to enter that information when submitting a payment request.
Travel Waivers apply to both Independent and University Organizations
Travel Waivers (for any organization travel)
Travel waivers are needed for any type of student organization travel. Please review the above link for the proper form process.
Only University Student Organizations can use Cornell Fleet Services as a transportation resource.
General Student Organizations
Service Engagement Agreement
(Performer, Entertainer, or Service Provider) - This agreement should be used anytime a general organization utilizes a service. It is an agreement of services between the student organization (not Cornell University) and the vendor.
Sports Official Form
- The form should be utilized anytime an organization uses an official, judge, referee, etc.
University Student Organizations
Photography-Artist-Illustrator-Intellectual Property License Addendum (PDF)
– Mandatory addendum that defines Cornell’s right to use the work while the photographer, artist, or illustrator retains ownership. The service provider must sign it.
Writing, Editing, Indexing, Videography–Work for Hire Addendum (PDF)
– Required to establish work for hire to ensure Cornell ownership of work product. The service provider must sign it.
