Alice Hu
2023 Emerging Leader
Alice Hu is in the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences at Cornell University, majoring in Biological Sciences and minoring in Business. On campus, she is part of the Cornell Chapter of Girls Who Code, a Physics undergraduate TA, Basic Rock Climbing Instructor, and a member of the Pre-Professional Program. After graduation, she hopes to go to medical school and become a pediatrician. When she isn't studying, you can find her rock climbing, curled up in a corner reading, or trying some new recipes.
