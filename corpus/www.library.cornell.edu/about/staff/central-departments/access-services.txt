TITLE: Access Services – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-07

Access Services includes circulation, interlibrary services, offsite storage, document delivery, scanning, reserves, stacks management, building coverage, patron billing, and other unit-specific tasks such as equipment loan or patron carrels and studies.
