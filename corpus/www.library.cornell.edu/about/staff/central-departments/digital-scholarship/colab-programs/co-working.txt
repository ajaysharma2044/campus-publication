What is co-working?
Co-working is the practice of sharing a physical or digital workspace with others. At the Digital CoLab, co-working hours are a time set aside for you to work on—and find support for—digital scholarship.
It can be hard to find time to make progress on digital projects during the semester, especially in isolation. Through co-working, we offer each other structure, community, and space to focus.
You can be as quiet or as talkative as you want to be! Come ask a question and bounce, or set up your stuff and stay a while.
