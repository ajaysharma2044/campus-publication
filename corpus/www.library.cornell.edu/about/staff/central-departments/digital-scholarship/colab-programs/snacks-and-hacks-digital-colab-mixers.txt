TITLE: Digital CoLab Mixers – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

The Digital CoLab occasionally hosts mixer events, often called “Snacks and Hacks,” to help Cornell community members interested in digital scholarship mingle in an informal setting.
Mixer events are open to anyone from the campus community. See a list of upcoming and past events below:
Upcoming mixers
Snacks and Hacks: A Spooky Digital CoLab Mixer
Thursday, October 1, 2026
4:30-6pm
Olin Library 701
The Digital CoLab at Olin Library is excited to host our first event of the season! Connect with community members from around campus interested in digital scholarship and digital humanities. We’ll have seasonal snacks to help break the ice. Who knows––you might just meet a potential collaborator or a new friend at this “spirited” social event. All are welcome.
Past mixers
Book Launch Party
Friday, October 3, 2025
3:00-4:30pm
Olin Library 703
Come celebrate with us! The Digital CoLab at Olin Library is excited to host a book launch party to celebrate the publication of librarians Iliana Burgos and Whitney Kramer’s new book: Text and Data Mining Literacy for Librarians. We’ll have snacks, activities, and more! The party is also a great chance to meet future collaborators or find new friends in the Cornell community! All are welcome.
