TITLE: Audiovisual Digitization – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

We have deep expertise in the details of legacy media, including magnetic tape, cartridge-based, machine-readable technology, as well as the principles, design, and function of the playback machines we need to digitize your content at international standards.
We can handle any audiovisual media you might have, from old cassettes to digital files on your computer or hard drive. We currently digitize several formats in our AV preservation lab, and we work closely with external vendors to minimize cost with media types we don’t. Email us at
dcaps@cornell.edu
for more information about our audiovisual digitization services.
Formats
We can digitally preserve the following formats:
VHS/SVHS
U-Matic (3/4”)
Cassette
Vinyl LP (33rpm)
1/4” open reel audio
DV/MiniDV
all formats of non-tape based, digital-born audio and video
