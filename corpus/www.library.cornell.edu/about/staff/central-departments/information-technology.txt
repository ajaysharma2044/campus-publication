TITLE: Information Technology – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Cornell University Library Information Technology (CUL-IT) is responsible for user-facing and internal IT systems in support of the Library’s mission. CUL-IT activities include: support for individual and public computers; IT security; IT project management; core services such as the library catalog; management and integration of external services; design, development and maintenance of websites and software applications; user experience design and accessibility; scholarly communications; and repository systems for access and preservation.
CUL-IT staff are part of
IT at Cornell
, collaborate closely with other library units, and engage in national and international library IT initiatives. As part of the IT at Cornell model, CUL-IT Director Debra Howell has a dual reporting relationship to Chief Information Officer Ben Maddox and to Associate University Librarian Simeon Warner.
