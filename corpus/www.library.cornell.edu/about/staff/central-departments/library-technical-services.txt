TITLE: Library Technical Services – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-07

Library Technical Services (LTS) supports Cornell University’s mission by keeping the collections vital and current by licensing and purchasing new materials, describing and organizing them for efficient use, as well as preparing our rare and unique archival items to preserve the past.
We provide metadata consultative services to Cornell researchers through
Digital Consulting and Production Services (DCAPS)
and
Digital Scholarship Services
and more broadly to the profession. For other LTS services see
Technical Services Contacts
.
