TITLE: Digital Consulting and Production Services (DCAPS) – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Digital Consulting and Production Services (DCAPS) is a team of experts that provide services that support digital access to collections for teaching, research, and scholarship. DCAPS is a single-point of service for those wishing to create digital collections and resources. DCAPS is comprised of members of Digitization and Conservation Services, Metadata Design and Operations, Digital Scholarship, Cornell University Library Information Technology, and experts in copyright and accessibility.
