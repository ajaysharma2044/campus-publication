TITLE: Web Accessibility Assistance – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Cornell University Library seeks to provide an accessible and “barrier-free” environment and equal access to library services and collections for all library users and adhere to
University Policy 5.12 Web Accessibility Standards
.
We accommodate reasonable requests for assistance in obtaining, accessing, and making use of library resources, materials, and services.
Some services require advance notice to ensure staff availability. For specific accommodations, please contact
libaccessibility@cornell.edu
or
Ask a Librarian
.
Along with support from librarians and staff, there are campus partners with additional resources to help support the best possible user experience:
Accessibility Information at Cornell
Cornell Student Disability Services
IT@Cornell
Cornell Center for Teaching Innovation
Office of Civil Rights
About the Readability Widget
