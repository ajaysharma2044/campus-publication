Getting started with your research
ABI/INFORM
– business articles and industry research.
Capital IQ
– company profiles, financials, deals, ratios, private equity.
IBISWorld
– industry research.
Mintel
– consumer market research reports and products.
MSCI ESG Direct
– ESG reports on companies and industries.
PitchBook
– private equity investors, start-ups, deals, research.
Statista
– market and industry statistics and studies.
WRDS
– data for academic research. Includes Compustat, CRSP, IBES, OptionMetrics, RavenPack, and more.
On-campus resources
These databases can ONLY be accessed on the Ithaca campus from these libraries:
Bloomberg – at Management, Mann, and Nestlé libraries. Requires account.
RS Means – Fine Arts and Nestlé.

tjo65@cornell.edu
), Business and Economics Librarian.

hotellibrary@cornell.edu
.
Do you have questions related to fields of engineering or computer information science, including patent information and databases? Please email the Engineering Library at
engrref@cornell.edu
.

lawref@cornell.ed
u.
Are you a member of the Cornell Tech community? Please email the Cornell Tech Library team at
library@tech.cornell.edu
and we’ll be happy to assist you.
Part of the Cornell entrepreneurship ecosystem? Contact the entrepreneurship team at
eship-library@cornell.edu
for assistance finding accurate data to size potential markets, determine emerging trends, and research support to understand the competitive landscape for your startup.
