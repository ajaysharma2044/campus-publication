TITLE: Alumni, Parents, and Friends – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-07

With collections, spaces, and expertise encompassing all fields of study, Cornell University Library is the intellectual alma mater for all Cornell alumni as well as a beacon for all champions of lifelong learning. We invite you to stay engaged with the Library through our special events and to enjoy collections and services that we offer to alumni and friends like you. We also hope you will continue to share our commitment in advancing Cornell’s mission of academic excellence and social impact. Together, we make a difference not only in fields of study on campus but in communities transformed by the work of Cornell’s scholars.
Give to the Library
Give a gift that helps Cornell University Library do the greatest good on campus and beyond. The Cornell Fund for the University Library supports a range of vital areas, including building collections in all disciplines, employing student workers, enhancing and maintaining study spaces, and growing the teaching and research expertise of our librarians who serve the Cornell community as well as scholars and library patrons who visit from all over the world.
Give your gift today
Cornell University Library no longer accepts unsolicited book donations for our general collections; these are materials dropped off or sent without prior discussion. This change supports our commitment to sustainable stewardship and ensures we can build collections that best meet current research and teaching needs. While any unsolicited items we receive will be redirected to other organizations, we still welcome advance conversations about materials that may align with the Library’s priorities.
For more information about how and where you can make the most impact for the Library, please contact Michelle Houle Hitz ’98, Director of Development, Library Alumni Affairs and Development, Cornell University at mmh11@cornell.edu.
