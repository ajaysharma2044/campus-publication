Cornell University Library Advisory Council (CULAC) Mission Statement
The Cornell University Library Advisory Council works with the
University Librarian
to highlight Cornell University Library’s mission to foster inquiry, enable scholarship, and preserve knowledge through innovative services, inclusive spaces, and outstanding collections. Members will act as advisors, supporters, promoters and advocates for the Library.
Our goal is to build a knowledgeable network from both inside and outside Cornell to secure the Library’s position as a leader in resources and technology as we focus on our
One Library for One Cornell Strategic Plan Framework
and our strategic directions of catalyzing research and discovery; advancing student success; and sustainable stewardship.
