FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Cornell students, faculty and staff can check out materials on-site at the thirteen BorrowDirect partner institutions through
BorrowDirect Plus
:
Brown University
University of Chicago
Columbia University
Dartmouth College
Duke University
Harvard University
Johns Hopkins University
Massachusetts Institute of Technology
University of Pennsylvania
Princeton University
Stanford University
Yale University
Cornell University
Are you visiting Cornell from a BorrowDirect institution?
Submit your
Library Card application
.
Visit the Olin Library Service Desk, Monday through Friday, 8:00am to 3:30pm.
Present a government issued photo ID card (passport, driver’s license).
In order to verify your status, you will be asked to log into your ILLiad
login page at your home institution.
Your Cornell Library Card will:
Be valid for one year from the issuing date.
Allow physical access and borrowing of circulating books and DVDs at all Cornell libraries.
Allow you to check out up to twenty items for 8 weeks.
Items can be returned at either Cornell or your home institution’s library.
