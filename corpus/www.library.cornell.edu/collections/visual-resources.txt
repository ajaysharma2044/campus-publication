Image Databases
We provide the Cornell community with access to a wealth of
image databases
, including licensed resources, such as Artstor/JSTOR, and other collections of visual resources.
Cornell University Library’s Digitized Images
DCAPS
digitizes and showcases materials from our collections and from archives across Cornell University. You can search, browse, filter results, zoom, geo-locate, read, and download. Explore
our digital collections
.
Online Exhibits
Our online exhibits showcase materials to spark the imagination and provide resources for scholarship. Curators and archivists across Cornell University Library have drawn from their collections, including
Mann Library
,
Olin and Uris Libraries
,
Kroch Asia Collections
, the
Kheel Center
, and
Rare and Manuscript Collections
, to create these exhibits. Also view
online exhibits across the Library
.
Audiovisual Repositories
eCommons
eCommons
is Cornell’s general-level institutional repository. It provides long-term access to a broad range of Cornell-related digital content of enduring value.
MediaSpace
Cornell University Library’s
MediaSpace
provides short- to medium-term access to a range of audiovisual content related to Cornell University Library.
Streaming Video
An assortment of licensed and open access
video resources
.
Additional Image Resources at Cornell
eMuseum
eMuseum
is the virtual portal to the Herbert F. Johnson Museum of Art’s permanent collection of more than 40,000 works from around the world and across time.
Cornell Photos Image Library
Cornell Photos Image Library
holds thousands of images of Cornell’s people, events, spaces, and buildings. Log in with Cornell net ID.
Macaulay Library
Cornell’s Laboratory of Ornithology sponsors the
Macaulay Library
, a scientific archive of over 80 million photographic, audio, and video files.
Cornell Fashion & Textile Collection
Housed in the College of Human Ecology, the
Cornell Fashion & Textile Collection
contains more than 10,000 items of apparel, accessories, and flat textiles.
