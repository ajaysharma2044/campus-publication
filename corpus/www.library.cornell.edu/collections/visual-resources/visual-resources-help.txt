TITLE: Visual Resources Help – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Cornell University Library offers Cornell faculty, students and staff assistance with creating, using, and finding images. We also welcome questions regarding images owned or licensed by Cornell from researchers not affiliated with Cornell.
If you are reporting a technical problem or requesting technical help, please let us know which operating system you are using and whether you are attempting to gain access to a collection from on or off campus.
If you are inquiring about the value of an image in your own collection, please be aware that Cornell librarians are not permitted to perform appraisals and adhere to the
ACRL code of ethics for special collections librarians
.
