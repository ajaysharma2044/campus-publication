TITLE: Research Guides – Cornell University Library
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Research guides, sometimes referred to as LibGuides, are tools created by library staff to help patrons navigate the tricky world of library research. Subject guides are intended to give a broad overview of a subject or skill, while course guides are developed in partnership with faculty for specific course offerings. If you’re stuck, library research guides are a good place to start.
