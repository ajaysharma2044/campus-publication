Academic Dishonesty
Every student’s work is expected to represent personal efforts. In cases involving allegations of plagiarism (see “
Plagiarism
” in the Ithaca College student handbook) or any other form of academic dishonesty, the procedures set out in the Ithaca College “
Student Conduct Code
” (part of the Ithaca College student handbook) are followed. All unresolved differences (as well as repeat offenses) are referred to the Conduct Review Board for hearing in accordance with the code.
For more information, see
7.1.4
in the Student Policies.
