FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Cornell Health provides leadership for and collaborates with students groups and campus partners on the following popular initiatives. Let's Meditate and Body Positive Cornell are free and open to all, and are designed to help support the health and well-being of our campus community.
Well-being Coaching
Well-being Coaching is a free service available to Cornell undergraduate, graduate, and professional students to help them set and pursue their well-being goals.
Well-being coaches are trained to partner with students in one-on-one, evidence-based conversations structured around the changes they want to make in their lives. Coaches work to create a strengths-based and collaborative conversation space for students to self-identify ways to get unstuck, maximize their personal and professional potential, and become the best versions of themselves.

Well-Being Coaching page

Let's Meditate
Let’s Meditate is a guided mindfulness meditation series sponsored by Cornell Health, in collaboration with numerous campus partners.
Half-hour Let's Meditate sessions are held daily Monday-Friday at various campus locations throughout the fall and spring semesters (and less frequently during University breaks).
Let's Meditate is free and open to all Cornell community members.
Learn more – and see the current schedule – on our
Let's Meditate page
.
Nature Rx
Nature Rx @ Cornell is a campus-wide initiative, represented by many individuals from around the university who have come together with a shared passion for how connection with nature can support every Cornellians' well-being.
Nature Rx consists of three components:
An oversight committee led by faculty, staff, and student members who manage the university's
Nature Rx website
that spotlights natural places on and near campus
A student organization that leads weekly walks and other events designed to enable and encourage students to get out in nature
The clinical application of Nature Rx by medical and mental health providers at Cornell Health
Learn more on our
Nature Rx page
.
Body Positive Cornell
Body Positive Cornell (BPC) is supported by Cornell Health's
Collaborative Health and Eating Program (CHEP)
and
Skorton Center for Health Initiatives
staff, and is conducted in collaboration with Cornell Athletics, Cornell Fitness Centers, Cornell Minds Matter, Panhellenic, and Cornell Wellness.
Peer-led BPC groups are offered in the fall and spring semesters, and meet once a week for 8 weeks. Participants support each other in cultivating body appreciation, increasing self-compassion, and improving overall well-being.
Groups are open to undergraduate students, graduate/professional students, and staff members of all genders, sexes, identities, and bodies.
Learn more on our
Body Positive Cornell page
.
Refresh: Cornell's Sleep Program
Refresh is an eight-week, evidence-based self-help program that provides information and strategies proven to help improve college students’ sleep.
Refresh is free and open to undergraduate, graduate, and professional students.

Refresh page
