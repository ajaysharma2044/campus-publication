TITLE: Community-Based Services | Cornell Health
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Available to all undergraduate, graduate, & professional students
Cornell Health offers the following services and support at numerous campus locations, as well as by Zoom, to compliment the clinical services provided at our Ceriale Center for Cornell Health building on Ho Plaza.
These services are designed to "meet students where they are" and reduce barriers to receiving care, addressing unique challenges students may encounter within their respective colleges, schools, and rigorous academic disciplines.
The recent expansion of the Well-being Coaching and Embedded Therapist programs was made possible by a
generous gift
from the Melissa and John V. Ceriale Family Foundation.
Well-being Coaching
Well-Being Coaches work one-on-one with students to help them identify their strengths, values, and purpose, navigate barriers, and pursue your well-being goals.
Learn more about Well-being Coaching
.
Embedded Therapist Services
Cornell's embedded therapists are
Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS)
counselors who provide ongoing mental health support from “satellite” offices located in the academic colleges and other campus locations.
Learn more about Embedded Therapist Services
.
Let's Talk
Students can consult informally with a CAPS counselor during “Let’s Talk” drop-in hours – offered weekly at various campus locations and by Zoom.
Learn more about Let's Talk
.
CAPS Community Liaisons
Community Liaisons are CAPS staff members who work directly with student communities on campus that face additional barriers and stigma about accessing mental health care.
Learn more about CAPS Community Liaisons
.
