Cornell has joined a growing number of U.S. college campuses offering embedded therapist services, in which ongoing mental health support is provided from “satellite” offices located in academic colleges and other campus locations.
The goal is to supplement the
Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS)
offerings at Cornell Health with convenient, on-site services tailored to a specific student audience.

Connecting with an embedded therapist:
Embedded therapist services are available to all students.
(Exception: Cornell Tech embedded therapist services are only available to students from Cornell Tech and other New York City-based programs.)
Students may drop by an embedded therapist's Let's Talk hours, or schedule a CAPS Access Appointment to request counseling with an embedded therapist. Visit the site-specific links above to learn more.
About the embedded therapist model
Embedded therapist services progresses Cornell's health-promoting campus goals as stated in the
Mental Health Review
and supported by our signing of the Okanagan Charter to become a
Health Promoting Campus
.
Benefits to students:
Provides students with additional access points for clinical support services beyond the Cornell Health building
Facilitates proximity and availability conducive to students' busy schedules
Increases awareness of mental health services due to promotion within embedded locations
Enables the embedded therapist to gain familiarity with the culture of a college/area, and awareness of how current events affect students in their embedded communities
Gives students access to tailored services rooted in the identified needs of the embedded community, building trust and reducing stigma among students seeking care
Benefits to campus partners:
Embedded therapists are a visible presence and a direct liaison for consultation with faculty and staff
Increases access, decreases stigma, and affirms a commitment to mental health and well-being
Reinforces mental health literacy within embedded sites and offers education around prevention and accessing mental health resources
Embedded therapists can lend their expertise and provide feedback to campus partners based on emerging needs of students
Support from Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS):
Embedded therapists can consult with CAPS colleagues who have cultural or clinical specialties
Embedded therapists can connect students to a network of community referral options, ensuring that the appropriate level of treatment is available for students
In the event that the embedded therapist is unavailable, support and care is available from CAPS Urgent Care Counselors and Assistant Director staff
In the event of a crisis or urgent support, tCAPS staff will be available for walk-ins at the Cornell Health facility
The embedded therapist is able to include CAPS staff in workshops, activities, and outreaches within the campus community, drawing on the expertise of the full CAPS staff
