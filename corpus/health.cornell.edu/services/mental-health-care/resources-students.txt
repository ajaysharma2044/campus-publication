About these resources
The
articles, videos, podcasts, exercises, and online resources
below
have been hand-picked by our staff to provide information for students interested in self-help strategies, and support for students who may be waiting to see a counselor or other health care provider.
We also invite you to explore our
Health Topics section
and our
Fact Sheet Library
for additional resources.
