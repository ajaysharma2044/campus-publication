CAPS support at Martha Van Rensselaer Hall
Students in Human Ecology and Public Policy – as well as other students taking classes in Martha Van Rensselaer (MVR) – can now access
Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS)
support on site at 2221 Martha Van Rensselaer Hall.
MVR is one of CAPS'
Embedded Therapist
sites through which ongoing mental health support is provided from satellite locations across campus. The goal is to supplement CAPS’ offerings at Cornell Health with convenient, on-site services tailored to specific student audiences.

"Let's Talk" drop-in consultation
Let’s Talk is a drop-in service that allows students to consult informally with a Cornell Health counselor without an appointment. Consultations are offered first-come, first-served, and are typically about 20-25 minutes in length. (Note that Let's Talk is not a substitute for counseling, and not appropriate for urgent concerns or mental health emergencies.) Learn more about
Let's Talk
.

Individual counseling
MacKenzie provides single-session therapy and short-term
individual counseling
from her office at 2221 Martha Van Rensselaer Hall, as well as by Zoom.
Students wishing to schedule a counseling appointment with MacKenzie should first schedule a
CAPS access appointment
. During this free 25-minute appointment, you can discuss your concerns with an access counselor, and request a counseling appointment with MacKenzie.
To schedule a CAPS access appointment:
Log in to
myCornellHealth
using your Cornell NetID and password, and select "Appointments"
Or call Cornell Health during business hours to speak with a scheduler:
607-255-5155
Follow-up counseling appointments may be scheduled with MacKenzie directly, or by calling
607-255-5155
.
Students may also ask to schedule counseling appointments with
other Cornell Health therapists
. Learn more on our
Individual Counseling
page.
