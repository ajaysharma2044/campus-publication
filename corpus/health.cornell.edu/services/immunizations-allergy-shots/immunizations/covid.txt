FALL 2026 INFORMATION
The New York State Department of Health recommends that all adults (age 18 and older) be vaccinated against COVID. Vaccination is a critical tool to prevent serious illness and transmission during the peak months of respiratory illness season, which typically runs from October to May.
See the latest COVID vaccination information from the NYS Department of Health
.
Getting your COVID booster at Cornell
New this year, COVID boosters will be offered (in limited supply) to eligible individuals at
Cornell's on-campus flu vaccination clinics
. The cost of COVID vaccination at a flu vaccination clinic will depend on your insurance.
COVID boosters are also available by appointment at Cornell Health. There is no charge for students to receive COVID boosters. To schedule a COVID vaccination appointment, please call us during business hours at
607-255-5155
.
Getting your COVID booster off campus
COVID boosters are now available to New Yorkers (age 3+) at local pharmacies, without a prescription from a medical provider. Insurance coverage may vary; contact your insurance provider for details.
Please call your pharmacy of choice directly to make sure they have COVID vaccine in stock, and to schedule an appointment.
