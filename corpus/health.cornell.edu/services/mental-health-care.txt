Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS) at Cornell Health provides
drop-in consultation,
individual counseling, and group counseling
to help you “live well to learn well” here at Cornell.
Our staff members come from diverse clinical and cultural backgrounds with expertise in responding to the wide range of mental health concerns faced by university students.
>
Meet our CAPS staff
About our services:
CAPS services are available to undergraduate, graduate, and professional students studying in Ithaca or on the nearby Geneva campus, with select services available to Cornell Tech and some other NYC-based students (see
Who we serve
).
Services are available in person and through
telehealth
(Zoom); note that students must be on or near campus to receive telehealth services. Phone appointments may be available, when needed.
CAPS SERVICES
Section break
“Let’s Talk” drop-in consultation
Informal drop-in consultations with a CAPS counselor for students who are uncertain if counseling is a good fit for them, want to know more about available support, or are just in need of a one-time brief conversation with a provider. Offered Monday-Friday at different campus locations and by Zoom.
How to access:
Drop-in; offered at various campus locations & by Zoom
Cost:
No charge
See the Let’s Talk schedule
Individual counseling
Short-term individual therapy focused on treating common mental health conditions and concerns.
How to access:
Schedule an
access appointment
(see below) to determine if this level of care is a good fit for you
Cost:
$10 / visit
Learn more about individual counseling
Group counseling
Therapy groups focused on a variety of clinical issues and/or identity-specific experiences. Groups meet weekly (some in person and some by Zoom), and run from 5 weeks to semester-long.
How to access:
Schedule a brief
group orientation
if you know what group you would like to join, or schedule an
access appointment
(see below) to explore what options might best fit your needs
Cost:
No charge
See our current group offerings
Access appointments: how to initiate care
Scheduling an access appointment is the first step to connecting with many CAPS services.
During this
free 20-minute Zoom appointment
, a Cornell Health counselor will ask you questions, listen to your concerns, and match you with appropriate care. Learn more about
what to expect from an access appointment
.
To schedule an access appointment:
Log in to
myCornellHealth
using your Cornell NetID and password, and select Appointments.
You may also call
607-255-5155
during business hours to schedule.
For follow-up CAPS appointments:
Appointments may be made directly with your CAPS provider. Or call us to schedule at
607-255-5155
.
How to connect directly with a specialist:
Schedule an ADHD Triage Appointment
to request ADHD medication management services at Cornell Health or explore off-campus ADHD evaluation.
Connect with the CAPS Sexual Violence Response Counselor
for confidential support related to a recent sexual assault or unwanted sexual contact.
Schedule an AOD Support Session
if you would like to meet with a counselor about an alcohol or other drug (AOD) concern.
Connect with a CAPS Referrals Manager
for help finding long-term counseling or specialty care options off-campus, in Ithaca or in New York State.
