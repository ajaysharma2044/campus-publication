Pregnancy testing
Students seeking pregnancy testing can acquire a test in one of two ways:
At-home pregnancy testing
At-home pregnancy tests are available for sale at the
Cornell Health pharmacy
and in our
24/7 self-care vending machine
(as well as at other pharmacies and stores). Free pregnancy tests are also available at the
Gender Equity Resource Center
.
Home pregnancy tests can detect a positive pregnancy as early as 10 days after conception, or around the time of a missed menstrual period; however, for a more accurate result, wait until after you’ve missed a period to take a test. These types of tests are approximately 99% effective when used correctly.
Pregnancy testing at Cornell Health
Pregnancy testing is also available at Cornell Health (free for students studying on the Ithaca campus). Walk-in appointments are often available, but we recommend calling ahead to schedule an appointment (
607-255-5155
) or scheduling online through
myCornellHealth
.
In-office pregnancy tests are conducted by taking a urine sample that is sent to Cornell Health's lab. It takes about an hour to get results. You can wait for the results, or a nurse can call you once the report is back. (Pregnancy blood tests are also offered when clinically indicated.)
Pregnancy options
Discovering you’re pregnant may be welcome news. It may also be an unwelcome surprise, or somewhere in the middle. Cornell Health supports your right to choose what happens next, and can provide you with the resources and support you need to take the next steps.
Continuing a pregnancy
If you want to continue your pregnancy – whether that means becoming a parent or finding an adoptive family – you may benefit from early-pregnancy information and resources. We can provide you with prenatal care information and help you connect with OB-GYN practices and midwifery services, either in Ithaca or in the location you desire. We can also help you connect with adoption resources if that is of interest to you.
View our
Early Pregnancy Guidelines

To speak with a referrals nurse for information about local OB-GYN practices, midwifery services, or adoption resources, call us at
607-255-5155

Ending a pregnancy
If you want or need pregnancy termination, our medical providers can offer consultation, advise you on your options, and provide or connect you with care.
Abortion is legal in New York State from conception through the 24th week of pregnancy. After 24 weeks, abortion is legal if someone’s health or pregnancy is at risk. Both medication abortion and surgical ("in-clinic") abortion are available in New York, depending on the number of weeks the pregnancy has progressed.
View our
Termination of Pregnancy
fact sheet for more information.
To get care, call us at
607-255-5155
during business hours and ask to speak with a nurse about pregnancy options. The nurse will ask you questions to learn more about the stage of the pregnancy and your personal preferences, and will either schedule you to meet with a medical provider at Cornell Health, or will refer you to an off-campus provider for care.
