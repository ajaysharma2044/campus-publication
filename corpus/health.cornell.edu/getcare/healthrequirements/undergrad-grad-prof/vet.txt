TITLE: Rabies Vaccination Requirement | Cornell Health
FETCHED: 2026-11-06

For new College of Veterinary Medicine students
Information for Fall 2026 students
In addition to completing Cornell's standard
New Student Health Requirements
, students entering Cornell's
College of Veterinary Medicine
must receive – or provide documentation that they have received – the
two-dose rabies vaccine
.
Rabies vaccine clinic at Cornell Health
All new CVM students who have not satisfied the rabies vaccine requirement
must attend Cornell Health's rabies vaccine clinic (whether or not you have received your rabies vaccine and/or submitted your documentation).
How to check if you've satisfied this requirement:
Log in to
myCornellHealth
using your Cornell NetID and password
Go to "Clearances & Requirements" in the menu bar
The status of "Veterinary Rabies Vaccine" must be "Satisfied"
If your "Veterinary Rabies Vaccine" item is "Not Satisfied" or reads "No Data," that means you have NOT satisfied the requirement and you must attend Cornell Health's rabies vaccine clinic.
Clinic dates & times:
Saturday, August 15:
9:00 am – 12:00 pm
Saturday, August 22:
9:00 am – 12:00 pm
Location:
Cornell Health, 110 Ho Plaza, Level 4
You will be scheduled into a timeslot
to receive your first vaccine dose on Saturday, August 15, and your second dose on Saturday, August 22.
If you are unable to attend the rabies vaccine clinic during your scheduled timeslot(s)
, please call Cornell Health at
607-255-5155
and select option #2 to reach our nursing staff.
Already received your rabies vaccine?
Before August 15, please upload documentation of your two-dose rabies vaccination:
Log in to
myCornellHealth
using your Cornell NetID and password
Go to “Clearances & Requirements” in the menu bar
Select "Veterinary Rabies Vaccine" to upload your documentation
If on August 15 your "Veterinary Rabies Vaccine" status still reads "No Data" or "Not Satisfied"
(see
how to check
, above), you must attend the rabies vaccine clinic on August 15 during your timeslot. During your visit, Cornell Health's nursing staff can review your documentation and confirm that you've met the vaccination requirement.
