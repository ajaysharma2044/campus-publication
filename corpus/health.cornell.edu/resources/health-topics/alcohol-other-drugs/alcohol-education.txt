Welcome to Cornell University!
As part of your introduction to Cornell, the university expects all incoming first-year and transfer students to complete an online alcohol education program. This program is designed specifically for Cornell students – those of you who choose not to drink for a variety of valid reasons, and those of you who do drink alcohol.
Students who have been enrolled in the Alcohol Education Canvas course should access the course through their New Student To Do List.
