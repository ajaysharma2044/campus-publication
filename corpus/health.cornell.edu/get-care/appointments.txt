Schedule an appointment
Appointments are required for most Cornell Health services. Students can schedule appointments
online
,
by phone
, or
by coming in to Cornell Health
.
Schedule online:
Most appointments can be scheduled online through our secure patient portal.
Log in to myCornellHealth
using your Cornell NetID & password and click "Appointments."

We reserve some same-day and next-day appointments for students with time-sensitive medical or mental health concerns. These appointments may not be available through myCornellHealth;
please call us
during
business hours
for the first available appointment:
607-255-5155
.
Don't see an appointment type that meets your needs?
Please call us to speak with a scheduler (see below).

Urgent / time-sensitive concerns
We reserve some same-day and next-day appointments for students with time-sensitive medical or mental health concerns.
These appointments may not be available to those scheduling online
.
If you are in urgent need of care:
Call us during
business hours
:
607-255-5155
Or come in to Cornell Health
and speak with a staff member at our Level 4 front desk
Students in need of urgent medical care
will be triaged by a nurse who will provide care and/or
schedule you into the next available medical appointment if additional care is required. (If a medical appointment is not available to meet a time-sensitive concern, students may be referred to one of
Ithaca's urgent care clinics
.)
Students in need of urgent mental health care
will be scheduled into a same-day appointment with a CAPS Urgent Care Counselor.

Pre-appointment questionnaires
Pre-appointment questionnaires are
required
for most appointments
(not required for allergy and immunization visits).
You must complete your pre-appointment questionnaire(s)
before
your visit.
Log in to myCornellHealth
and click "Appointments."
If questionnaires are not completed before your visit, you may be asked to reschedule.
If a questionnaire you receive does not seem to match your health concern, call us during business hours (
607-255-5155
) to discuss.
CAPS appointments:
You will have a
separate
brief Wellbeing Assessment survey to complete 10 minutes before your appointment. Please check in early enough before your appointment to complete this survey.
Note:
Occupational Medicine
patients will receive questionnaire information by email with instructions for accessing questionnaires through myCority, not myCornellHealth.
When / where to check in for your appointment
Appointments at Cornell Health:
Plan to arrive / check in 10 minutes prior to your appointment to complete the check-in process before your appointment begins (CAPS clients must also complete a brief Wellbeing Assessment survey).
When you get to our building, you may check in on the level (floor) where your appointment is scheduled. If you're not sure where to go, check in at the main desk on Level 4.
If you arrive more than 5 minutes late for your appointment, you may be asked to reschedule.
Telehealth appointments:
Check in 5 minutes prior to your appointment to complete the check-in process before your appointment begins (CAPS clients must also complete a brief Wellbeing Assessment survey).
To access your secure Zoom link,
log in to myCornellHealth
, go to “Appointments,” then click “Appointment Check In.”
If you check in more than 5 minutes late for your appointment, you may be asked to reschedule.

Call

607-255-7217

607-255-6960

Online:
log in to myCornellHealth
(not available to Occupational Medicine patients)
*If you have already completed your pre-appointment questionnaire
, please CALL if you would like to reschedule your appointment so you do not have to complete a new questionnaire.
Late arrival & missed appointment policies
Late arrivals:
If you arrive more than 5 minutes late for your appointment, you may be asked to reschedule.
Missed appointments:
A $25 fee will be charged for missed appointments.
Other appointment policies
Support person policy:
Students may have a support person present during clinical appointments, provided that the presence of the support person helps facilitate care in the judgment of both the patient/client and the provider, and there are no safety concerns. The provider will meet briefly with the patient/client first before the support person can join the appointment.
Recording / photography policy:
To protect patient/client privacy, recording of any kind (photographic, video, audio) is prohibited at Cornell Health.
Personal fragrance policy:
Please limit the use of personal fragrances before Cornell Health appointments.
Cornell Health is a scent-free environment in support of students and staff who have allergies or other medical conditions triggered by scent.
