Transportation
Ambulance:
In a physical or mental health emergency, call 911 for ambulance transportation to the hospital.
Cost:
If you are enrolled in a
Cornell student health plan (SHP)
, ambulance transports are covered at no cost. If you have other insurance, there may be a $100 or more charge.
