Cornell Health
We're located at
110 Ho Plaza
, at the corner of Campus Road and College Avenue on Cornell’s central campus. [
see building hours
]
Our main entrance is on Ho Plaza
, with additional entrances on Campus Road and beneath our building underpass off of Campus Road. The main entrance and underpass entrance are both
wheelchair-accessible
.
Parking:
There are 10 paid parking spaces reserved for Cornell Health patients/clients located beneath our building underpass, and just past the underpass toward Willard Straight Hall. Two are
accessible-permit spaces
.
Parking and Transportation
manages all parking on central campus. The fee is $3/hour (plus a $.35 transaction fee), payable through
Parkmobile
.
(You may also call
877-727-5730
and pay over the phone. If you need a phone, please come inside to the main lobby information desk on Level 4.)
