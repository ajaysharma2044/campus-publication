Cornell Health provides medical care, mental health care, and support services for students on Cornell's Ithaca campus. Our 250+ staff members serve more than 80% of Cornell's undergraduate, graduate, and professional students each year, and support the health and well-being of the whole community through campus-wide initiatives and campaigns.
