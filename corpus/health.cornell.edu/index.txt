Fall Flu Vaccine Clinics
Protect yourself from getting sick this fall by getting your (free!) flu shot. Our
fall flu vaccine clinics
are held at different campus locations throughout the semester. Sign up for a clinic today!
CAPS Drop-In Consultation
Want to talk? Consult informally with a Cornell Health counselor during
Let's Talk
drop-in hours, offered weekdays at 9 different campus locations and by Zoom. No appointment necessary.
Well-being Coaching
Need help getting unstuck? Cornell's
Well-being Coaches
can help you identify your strengths and values, and create and work toward your well-being goals. Free and available to all students!
