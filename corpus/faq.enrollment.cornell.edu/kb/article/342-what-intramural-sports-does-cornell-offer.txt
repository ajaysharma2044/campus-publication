What intramural sports does Cornell offer?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
15655
views
Cornell is home to one of the largest intramural sports programs in the nation, with 30+ teams and leagues. Since the program is open to the entire university community, students may find faculty and staff participating together. In addition, Cornell offers the most comprehensive collegiate
outdoor education program
in the country. To learn more, please visit Cornell's
Intramural Sports
page. Our
Community & Belonging
and
Health & Wellness
pages may also be helpful.
