When should I submit official test scores?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
5577
views
Official test scores for SAT or ACT can be submitted at any point in the admissions process, but must be received by the stated deadlines for matriculating students. Official scores will be required for matriculating students from any exam score they self-reported during the admissions process. For Early Decision and QuestBridge Matched matriculating students, scores must be received by March 1. For Regular Decision matriculating students, scores must be received by May 15. To learn more, visit our
Standardized Testing Policy
webpage. The
How to Apply
page may also be a helpful resource.
