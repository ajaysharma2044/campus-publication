As an international student, how will my application be read?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14259
views
All applications are reviewed by the specific Cornell college or school to which a student has applied. The evaluation process is based on where a student currently attends school, rather than by citizenship status. International students attending the same high school are evaluated as a group, as are applicants from the same state (U.S.) or country.
