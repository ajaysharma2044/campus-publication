Are in-person campus visits available to admitted students?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
16456

In addition to virtual events, Cornell Days is a one-day visit program in Ithaca, NY, with consistent programming options across the four dates offered. Newly admitted first-year students and families are invited to campus to connect with their college or school, tour campus, and enjoy a meal in one of our many Cornell Dining eateries.
