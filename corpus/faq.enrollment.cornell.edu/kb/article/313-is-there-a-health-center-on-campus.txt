Is there a health center on campus?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
32241
views
To support a healthy community,
Cornell Health
is conveniently located on campus and is available to students with mental or physical health concerns any time of day or night, 365 days a year. Students can call Cornell Health to talk with a health care provider about a mental or physical health concern, or to make an appointment to come by in person. To learn more about Cornell's commitment to supporting the well-being of our community, visit our
Health & Wellness
page.
