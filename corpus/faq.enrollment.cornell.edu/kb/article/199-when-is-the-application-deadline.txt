When is the application deadline?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
442938
views
The
first-year early decision
application deadline is November 1. Applicants are notified in mid-December of Cornell’s decision.
The
first-year regular decision
application deadline is January 2. Applicants are notified in early April of Cornell’s decision.
The
fall semester transfer
application deadline is March 15. Applicants are notified in May and June of Cornell's decision.
For additional information about deadlines, please visit Cornell's
How to Apply
webpage.
