Which programs require a portfolio or design supplement as part of the application process?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
18819
views
Portfolio or design supplement submission requirements are specific to the major that you apply to. The following majors require a portfolio or design supplement:
College of Agriculture and Life Sciences
Landscape Architecture: a portfolio is required. See the
CALS website
for details, format, and submission instructions.
College of Architecture, Art and Planning
Architecture: a
portfolio and video interview
are required; must be submitted via Cornell
AAP SlideRoom portal
.
Art: a
portfolio
is required; must be submitted via Cornell
AAP SlideRoom portal
.
College of Human Ecology
Design & Environmental Analysis: a
Design Challenge
is required; must be submitted via the
CHE SlideRoom portal
.
Fashion Design & Management:
Fashion Design option: a
portfolio and Fashion Design Index
are required; must be submitted via the
CHE SlideRoom portal
.
Fashion Design Management option: a
Design Case Study
is required; must be submitted via the
CHE SlideRoom portal
.
