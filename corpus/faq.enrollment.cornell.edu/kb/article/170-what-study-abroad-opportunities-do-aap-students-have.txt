What study abroad opportunities do AAP students have?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
13592
views
AAP hosts two off-campus programs:
Cornell in Rome
and
AAP NYC
. All undergraduate students are eligible to apply to Cornell in Rome; B.Arch. and U.R.S. students are eligible to apply to AAP NYC.  U.R.S. students often take advantage of Cornell's many study abroad opportunities worldwide through the
Office of Global Learning
. For B.Arch. and B.F.A. students, it is most common to participate in AAP programs only, as studios in these programs count towards graduation requirements just as Ithaca studios do.
