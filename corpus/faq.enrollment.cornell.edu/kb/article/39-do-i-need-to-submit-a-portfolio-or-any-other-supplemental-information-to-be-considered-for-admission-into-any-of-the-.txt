Do I need to submit a portfolio, or any other supplemental information, to be considered for admission into any of the Human Ecology programs?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14694
views
First-year and transfer applicants to the Design & Environmental Analysis and Fashion Design & Management majors are required to submit
design supplements
. Carefully review the information and guidelines for the current admissions cycle.
Fashion Design & Management applicants can
email
the department with questions about the program and required design supplement.
Design & Environmental Analysis applicants can contact
DEA’s Academic Programs Coordinator
with questions about the program and required design supplement.
Students applying to majors that do not require a portfolio can submit a few select pieces via the supplemental materials link when they receive access to their Cornell University applicant portal. These pieces will not be evaluated by department faculty.
