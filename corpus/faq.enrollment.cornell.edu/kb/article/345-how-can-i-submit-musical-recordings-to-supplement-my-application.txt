How can I submit musical recordings to supplement my application?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
27685
views
The colleges that consider supplemental music submissions as part of their application process are: the College of Agriculture & Life Sciences, the College of Architecture, Art and Planning, the College of Arts & Sciences, the College of Engineering, the College of Human Ecology, and the Cornell SC Johnson College of Business. Undergraduate applicants with exceptional talent may submit optional supplemental materials for review by the Department of Music. Although you are not required to major in music, Cornell expects that you will only submit materials if you plan to be significantly engaged in music while at Cornell.
For more detailed instructions, please visit the
Department of Music
website.
