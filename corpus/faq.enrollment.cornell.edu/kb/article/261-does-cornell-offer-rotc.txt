Does Cornell offer ROTC?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14759
views
Educating military leaders at Cornell has been a tradition since the university was founded, and Cornell formally established a
Reserve Officer Training Corps (ROTC)
unit in 1917. Today, Cornellians can participate in Army, Navy/Marine Corps, and Air Force ROTC units.
To learn more about our military training and education programs, please visit the
Cornell ROTC
page.
