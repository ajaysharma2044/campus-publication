Are Cornell Jeb E. Brooks School of Public Policy undergraduates charged contract or endowed tuition rates?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
9076
views
The Cornell Brooks School of Public Policy is a hybrid academic unit that partners with contract and endowed colleges to offer undergraduate programs. New York State residents enrolled in Bachelor of Science programs in the Brooks School receive the New York State resident contract college tuition rate. More information about tuition rates and expenses can be found on the
Cornell financial aid website
