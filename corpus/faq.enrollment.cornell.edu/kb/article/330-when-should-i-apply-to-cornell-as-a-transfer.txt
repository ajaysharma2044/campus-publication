When should I apply to Cornell as a transfer?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
70747
views
Cornell accepts transfer students who have completed two to four semesters at another college or university. Applicants in their first college semester are not eligible to apply. Candidates are encouraged to apply when they feel they have a strong application and should follow the guidelines and deadlines outlined on the
How to Apply: Transfer Applicants
webpage. The transfer application deadline for fall semester is March 15 and applications are considered for the fall semester only.
