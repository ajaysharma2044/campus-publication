Does Cornell offer a pre-med program?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
20276
views
Students who are interested in pursuing a health career find great opportunities at Cornell. In fact, nearly one out of every six Cornell undergraduates intends to pursue a career in medicine. Students may study in any of
Cornell's undergraduate schools and colleges
and graduate well prepared for further medical career studies and options. The resources available in the
Health Professions Advising Center (HPAC)
are accessible to all pre-Health students across the university.
To learn more, please visit Cornell’s
Preparing for a Health Career
section.
