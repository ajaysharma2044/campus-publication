What is the architecture studio experience like?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
10608
views
Design studios at each level immerse students in design culture, helping them develop the skills and intellectual tools to solve problems. Students learn to communicate ideas through models and graphics.
Our studios encourage exploration, iteration, and collaboration. Our students learn from their peers, as well as professors, in an open and challenging environment that encourages critical thinking.
