Who can I contact with questions about Cornell Human Ecology?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
13965
views
The College of Human Ecology Admissions Office offers the best guidance for major, college, and admissions information. Prospective undergraduate first-year or transfer students can call our office at 607-255-5471 or
e-mail us
with questions. Individual appointments are not offered.
Our
Human Ecology Ambassadors
can answer questions about student life in the College of Human Ecology during the academic year.
Those interested in Design & Environmental Analysis can email the
DEA’s Academic Programs Coordinator
with questions; those interested in Fashion Design & Management and Fiber Science can
email the department
with questions.
Contacting faculty directly is discouraged.
