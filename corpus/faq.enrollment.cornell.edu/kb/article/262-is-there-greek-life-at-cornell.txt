Is there Greek life at Cornell?
Author:
Katy Lemon
23221
views
Cornell has a vibrant Greek community of 60+ fraternities and sororities with about 1/3 of the undergraduate student population participating. Collectively the fraternities and sororities are active in community service and the social life of the campus.
To learn more, please visit the
Sorority & Fraternity Life
website.
