How does Cornell consider U.S. citizens abroad and international students attending school in the U.S.?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
15668
views
The application and requirements are the same for all students. All applications are reviewed by the specific Cornell
undergraduate college or school
to which a student has applied. The selection committees read applications based on where you currently attend school, rather than by your citizenship status. Your application will be read with others from the school you attend and those from the same state (U.S.) or country. Please see the
How to Apply
and
Preparing for Your Cornell Application
pages for more information.
