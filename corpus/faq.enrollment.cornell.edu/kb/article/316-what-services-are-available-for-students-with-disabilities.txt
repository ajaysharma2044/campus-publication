What services are available for students with disabilities?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
10318
views
Cornell is committed to equality of educational opportunity for all students. Cornell's
Student Disability Services (SDS)
office facilitates services and accommodations so that Cornell students with disabilities enjoy the same exceptional opportunities as their peers, with the greatest degree of independence possible. To learn more, please visit the
SDS website
.
