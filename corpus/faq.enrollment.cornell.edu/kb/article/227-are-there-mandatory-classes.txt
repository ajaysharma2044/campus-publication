Are there mandatory classes?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
16742
views
The Cornell curriculum is flexible and students can fine tune their schedules to have the educational experiences they are looking for. That said, each of the undergraduate colleges and schools has its own academic requirements. In addition,
all
first-year students participate in Cornell’s award-winning
First-Year Writing Seminar
(FWS) program. To learn more about these offerings, please visit the
John S. Knight Institute for Writing in the Disciplines
information on our Courses of Study webpage.
