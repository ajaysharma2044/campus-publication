Is it easy to take other classes outside of AAP?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
11392
views
Yes! Students from all of our majors can easily take classes outside of AAP. Many students choose to complete a minor in another area, and some students choose to take classes that they find interesting. AAP encourages our students to take advantage of classes outside of AAP and explore the opportunities that a world-class university has to offer!
