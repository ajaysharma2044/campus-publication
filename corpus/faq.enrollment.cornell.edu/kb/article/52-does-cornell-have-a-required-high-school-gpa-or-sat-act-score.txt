Does Cornell have a required high school GPA or SAT/ACT score?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
35168
views
Cornell does not use any single formula for its admission decisions, and there is no GPA or SAT/ACT cutoff for admission. Effective for students applying for Fall 2026 or beyond, first-year applicants to all undergraduate colleges and schools will be required to submit standardized test scores. For more information, see our
Standardized Testing Policy
and the
How to Apply
page.
