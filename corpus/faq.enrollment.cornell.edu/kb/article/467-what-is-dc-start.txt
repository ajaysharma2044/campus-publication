What is DC Start?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
2722
views
DC Start is a distinctive residential academic experience offered to first year Brooks School undergraduate students in their fall semester. Housed at the Brooks School’s Wolpe Center in close walking distance to Dupont Circle in Washington D.C., the program offers an inspiring, immersive public policy learning experience to public policy and health care policy majors each fall.
