Is the SC Johnson College of Business a New York state contract college?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
8137
views
The Cornell SC Johnson College of Business houses two undergraduate schools. One of them, the Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management, is a New York State contract school, which means that students from New York State pay
in-state tuition
.
The other school, the Nolan School of Hotel Administration, is an endowed school.
