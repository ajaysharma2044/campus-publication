What is the status of my application for admission?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
30235
views
When we receive your application, we will email you about establishing an online
applicant portal
. This email message will be your confirmation that we have received your application. Using your secure applicant portal page, you will be able to track your application materials, upload supplemental materials to your application, and update your contact information as needed. Once you've submitted your application, we will keep you posted on its status.
