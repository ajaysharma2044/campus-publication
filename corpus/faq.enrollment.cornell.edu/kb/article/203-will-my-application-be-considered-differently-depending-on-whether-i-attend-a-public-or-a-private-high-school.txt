Will my application be considered differently depending on whether I attend a public or a private high school?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
18230
views
Cornell looks for students who have taken the greatest advantage of opportunities available to them within the context of their school, public
or
private, therefore we feel it is most fair to compare students who are given the same advantages within a single school rather than compare students coming from two very different schools. We do not have a set quota for the number of applicants we accept from any school.
