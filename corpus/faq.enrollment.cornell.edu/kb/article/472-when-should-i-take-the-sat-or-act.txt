When should I take the SAT or ACT?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
2395
views
Exams should be completed before the application deadline you are applying under. For Early Decision, the latest test dates would occur in October, for Regular Decision, the latest test dates would occur in December. To learn more, visit our
Standardized Testing Policy
page. The
How to Apply
page may also be a helpful resource.
