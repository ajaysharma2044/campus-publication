How do I self-report my SAT/ACT scores?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
8270
views
Students should self-report their SAT or ACT test scores on their Common Application or QuestBridge application. For any additional scores from exam dates that fall after a student has submitted an application, students can self-report their scores using the form in their Applicant Portal.
