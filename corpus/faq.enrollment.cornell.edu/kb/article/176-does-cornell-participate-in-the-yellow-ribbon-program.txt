Does Cornell participate in the Yellow Ribbon program?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
15695
views
The Yellow Ribbon GI Education Enhancement Program is a provision of the Post-9/11 Veterans Educational Assistance Improvement Act of 2010. Cornell University participates in the Yellow Ribbon program for an unlimited number of eligible undergraduate students. Please submit a copy of your Certificate of Eligibility from the VA to the School Certifying Official (SCO) at
[email protected]
. Undergraduate students do not need to submit a separate application for the Yellow Ribbon Program.
