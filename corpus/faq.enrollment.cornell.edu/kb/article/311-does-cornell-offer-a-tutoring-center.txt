Does Cornell offer a tutoring center?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
13363
views
Cornell students have access to a variety of programs and services to ease their transition to college and help them flourish. One such resource is the
Tatkon Center for New Students
, which offers study skills workshops, writing walk-in services, student mentors, and drop-in professional help with any problem, big or small. The
Learning Strategies Center (LSC)
, the central academic support unit for undergraduates, is also a helpful resource. In a typical year, more than 20,000 student visits are made to LSC office hours, tutorials, consultations, workshops, and courses.
