Does Cornell offer credits for military training?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
11586

While all potential transfer credits are evaluated, Cornell is not typically able to award credits for military training experiences. For more information about transfer credit at Cornell, please visit the
Credit Evaluation Services
page within the
University Registrar
website.
