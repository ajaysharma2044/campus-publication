What are the College of Arts and Sciences admissions requirements?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
20029
views
Please see our
requirements website
for details.
In addition to the
Common Application
, complete applications must include:
Official Secondary/High School Transcript followed by a Mid-Year Report when it becomes available
Counselor Recommendation and School Report
Two Teacher Evaluations
Cornell Writing Supplement
$85 application fee or fee waiver
International students: You may be asked to submit results from an
English language proficiency exam
The submission of
SAT/ACT
test results is required for all first-year applicants to the College of Arts & Sciences, including Questbridge, Early Decision, and Regular Decision.
If you are applying as a first-year student, A&S requires the following high school coursework:
4 units of English
3 units of mathematics (including pre-calculus)
3 units of science
3 units of one foreign language (for heritage speakers or students who have initiated a foreign language sequence before high school, completion of at least the third level (e.g. Spanish 3)).
Also recommended: an additional unit of advanced mathematics and science
