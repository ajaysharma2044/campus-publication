Is public transit available near campus?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14700
views
Tompkins Consolidated Area Transit (TCAT) serves Ithaca and the surrounding area. Cornell students receive free, unlimited rides on all TCAT buses after 6 p.m. on weekdays, and anytime Saturday and Sunday. New Cornell students enrolled in an undergraduate degree program receive a free bus pass for their first academic year. Many Cornellians find that public transit is a convenient, economical, and sustainable way to get around.
To learn more, please visit the
Student OmniRide Bus Pass
website.
