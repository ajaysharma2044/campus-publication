I’m not currently enrolled in a college or university. Do I still submit the Mid-Term Report?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14477
views
The Mid-Term Report is required. Applicants not currently enrolled at a college or university should submit the required Mid-Term Report with a note explaining that they are not currently enrolled. Please note that the Mid-Term Report is only available after you’ve submitted your application and logged in to your Applicant Portal. For more information, see the
How to Apply: Transfer Applicants
webpage.
