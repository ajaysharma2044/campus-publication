What is the application fee?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
21624
views
The application fee for both first-year and transfer students is $85.
Applications
must
be submitted with a nonrefundable $85 application fee or fee waiver in order to be considered.
Cornell University has an enduring commitment to equity and access for all students. If the application fee would cause financial hardship for your family, you may be eligible for a fee waiver.
For information about fee waivers, please visit the
How to Apply
webpage.
