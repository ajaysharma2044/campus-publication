What language programs does Cornell offer?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
17095
views
Over 50 languages, both modern and ancient, are taught in classrooms at Cornell and students can study another 50 or so in language labs on campus. In addition to the Romance languages, courses include:
Bengali, Burmese, Finnish, Persian/Farsi, Sanskrit, Swahili, Ukrainian, Urdu, and Yoruba
. With such a wide range of languages available through the
College of Arts and Sciences Language Program
, Cornell students have many options. Students can further their language skills through a variety of study abroad and experiential learning opportunities. Please visit the
Education Abroad
page to learn more.
