As an admitted student, can I change my college/school?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
12450
views
Newly admitted students may not change the college or school to which they were admitted before arriving in the fall. Students will need to complete at least two semesters in the college or school to which they are admitted to fully explore options. There are often multiple ways to pursue your academic interests and goals at Cornell.
