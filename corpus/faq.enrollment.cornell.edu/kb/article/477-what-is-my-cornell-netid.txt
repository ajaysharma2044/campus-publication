What is my Cornell NetID?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
9078
views
Your NetID is a unique identifier that, combined with a strong passphrase, provides access to non-public Cornell resources and information.
Activate Your NetID, Email, and Two-Step Login
After the university has received your enrollment response and deposit, you will receive an email from "
[email protected]
" at your admissions email address that your NetID was created and what to do next.
Sometimes this email goes to spam/junk folders, so check there if you do not find it in your Inbox
. It can take up to two business days for your NetID to be created after your deposit is processed.
After you receive the activation email, you will sign in with your Application Portal login information to
activate your NetID and email
.
Don't forget to set up
Two-Step Login
so you can access services vital to getting started as a new student. This two-factor authentication service prevents the use of stolen Cornell passwords by requiring confirmation from something a third party does not have, such as a mobile phone.
Review the
IT checklist
before you arrive to select an email system to access your Cornell email, register for campus Wi-Fi, set up a password recovery email, and more.
Please activate your NetID immediately.
Your email will be activated with your NetID. Your email address will look something like this—
[email protected]
—where "ewe1" is your Cornell NetID.
After you activate your NetID, it will be your permanent account, and you will use it to log in to Cornell services moving forward. NetIDs carry both additional privileges and additional responsibilities. Use a strong, unique
passphrase
with your NetID and keep it private.
