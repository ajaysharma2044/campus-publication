Author:

To prepare for studying within CALS, each academic major has key foundation courses that competitive transfer candidates need to complete or have in progress at the time of their application. View the required coursework and other transfer resources on the
Transfer Applicant
webpage. As with first-year applicants, we conduct a holistic admissions process for transfer applicants.
