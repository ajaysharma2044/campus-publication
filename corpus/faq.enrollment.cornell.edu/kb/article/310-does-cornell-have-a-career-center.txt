Does Cornell have a Career Center?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
15432
views
There is great support in place at Cornell to help students reach graduation and provide opportunities to be successful after graduation.
Cornell Career Network
provides a range of services to help students identify their skills, explore career options, and gain workplace experience. Cornell Career Network also assists students applying to graduate and professional school. Students who are just beginning to explore their options for the future, seeking help with their resume, or looking for a summer job or internship, can look to Cornell Career Network for support.
