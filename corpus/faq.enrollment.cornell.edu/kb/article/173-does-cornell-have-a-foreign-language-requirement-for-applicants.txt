Does Cornell have a foreign language requirement for applicants?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
32737
views
Some of Cornell's colleges and schools require or recommend foreign language coursework at the high school level. Prospective students are encouraged to review the
college and school admissions requirements
for detailed information. Applicants who lack required foreign language experience will still be considered for admission. However, they should include a letter explaining this deficiency so their application is given every consideration. This explanation can be submitted via the Supplemental Materials section of the
Applicant Portal
.
