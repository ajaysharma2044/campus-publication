Are first quarter grades required for early decision applicants to Cornell University?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
33705
views
As an early decision applicant, first quarter grades and the Midyear Report are not required and will not appear on your application checklist.
For more information about Cornell's application requirements, please see the
How to Apply
page.
