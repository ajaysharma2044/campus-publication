Can I study away from Ithaca as a Nolan/Dyson student?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
6082
views
Yes, as a student in either the Nolan School of Hotel Administration or the Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management, you can participate in study away programs. Cornell offers various opportunities for students to
study abroad
or at other campuses within the United States. We encourage students to work with their academic advisor as soon as possible to plan their study away experience and ensure it aligns with their academic goals and degree requirements.
