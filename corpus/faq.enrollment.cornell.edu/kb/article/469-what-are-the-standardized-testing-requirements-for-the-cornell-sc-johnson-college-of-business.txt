What are the standardized testing requirements for the Cornell SC Johnson College of Business?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
4005
views
SAT or ACT test scores are required of all first-year applicants, but are not required or expected for transfer applicants. Applications will be reviewed using unofficial or self-reported scores, but official test scores will be required for all matriculating students. There is no minimum required test score that must be achieved to be reviewed for admission. However, we do seek students who show strength in quantitative coursework and testing. For more information regarding testing, review the
Standardized Testing Policy
.
