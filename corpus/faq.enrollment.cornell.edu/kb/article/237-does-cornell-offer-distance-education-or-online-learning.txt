Does Cornell offer distance education or online learning?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
22733
views
Cornell offers continuing education, summer college for high school students, online learning, professional studies and more. To learn more, please visit
Cornell University’s School of Continuing Education
(SCE)
. You may also want to check out
CornellX
, where you can browse free online courses (MOOCs) in a variety of subjects. Additionally, Cornell’s external education unit,
eCornell
, provides executive development and online certificate programs in various fields, including leadership, marketing, technology, and more.
