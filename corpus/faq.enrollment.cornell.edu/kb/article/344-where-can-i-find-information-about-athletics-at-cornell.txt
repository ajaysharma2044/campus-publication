Where can I find information about athletics at Cornell?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
13022
views
Cornell has a lot to offer when it comes to fitness and athletics. The university fields 37 varsity teams, one of the largest intramural sports programs in the country, and the most comprehensive collegiate outdoor education program in the country. Students have access to four fitness centers on campus as well.
To learn more, please visit our
Health & Wellness
webpage.
