Does Cornell require personal interviews?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
128342

Video interviews are required for individuals applying to our Architecture program located in our College of Architecture, Art, and Planning. More information about the submission process can be found by going to the
AAP website
.
Personal interviews are not required, nor available, for admission to any of Cornell's other undergraduate level programs.
