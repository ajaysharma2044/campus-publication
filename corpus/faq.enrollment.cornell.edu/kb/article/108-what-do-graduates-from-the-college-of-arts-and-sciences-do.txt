What do graduates from the College of Arts and Sciences do?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
11898
views
A
survey of College of Arts & Sciences students in the Class of 2025
found that 66.6% of graduates are employed, 28.8% are attending graduate school, 1.3% are seeking employment, and 3.3% are traveling/volunteering/preparing for graduate school. Top employment sectors for our graduates include Financial Services (23%), Technology (21%), Consulting/Professional Practice (10%), Human Healthcare Services (9%), Education (7%), Law (6%), Communications/Media (4%),  Government (3%), and Nonprofit Organizations (3%). Top fields for graduate study include Computer Science, Engineering, Law, Business, Biological and Life Sciences, and Medicine.
