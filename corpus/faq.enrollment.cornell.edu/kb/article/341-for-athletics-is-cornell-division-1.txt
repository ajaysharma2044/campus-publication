For athletics, is Cornell Division 1?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
42921
views
Cornell University has an NCAA Division I athletics program in 37 varsity sports and is a member of the Ivy League athletic conference. In addition, Cornell is home to one of the largest intramural sports programs in the country, and the most comprehensive collegiate outdoor education program in the United States. You’ll find four fitness centers on campus as well. To learn more, please visit the
Cornell University Athletics
and
Recreation & Sports
webpages.
