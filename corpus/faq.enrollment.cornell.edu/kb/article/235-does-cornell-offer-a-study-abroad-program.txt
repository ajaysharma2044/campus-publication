Does Cornell offer a Study Abroad program?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
18462
views
Cornell University has always had a
global outlook
, and we offer programs for
study abroad
and international experiential learning in over 40 countries. Students may choose to study abroad at a university, conduct research or fieldwork, engage in service learning, or participate in an internship. In addition, Cornell students have the opportunity to study off-campus in the United States through the Cornell-in-Washington Program in D.C. or the Shoals Marine Lab in Maine. Undergraduates receiving financial aid will continue to do so if they study off campus or abroad through an approved Cornell program.
To learn more, please visit the
Global Learning
website.
