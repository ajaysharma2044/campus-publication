What are the hours of operation on campus?
Author:

14776
views
Cornell is in operation around the clock throughout the academic year. During the day, students are attending classes, working in one of the
research facilities
on campus, or enjoying a meal at one of the 30 dining locations on campus. (
Cornell Dining
was ranked by Princeton Review as one of the top 10 among U.S. colleges and universities for best campus food!) After class and into the evening, students take a break from their studies to participate in one of the more than
1,300 student organizations
on campus, watching a film at the
Cornell Cinema
, or studying in one of the campus
librar
ies
.
Visit the
Community & Belonging
webpage for more information.
