Is there a separate application process for adult students and veterans?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
12553
views
Adult students and veterans don’t apply for undergraduate admission through a separate pathway at Cornell. Depending on their academic history, they follow the traditional pathway for
first-year applicants
(never matriculated at a college and earned fewer than 12 credits after high school) or apply as
transfer applicants
. We recognize, however, that adult applicants and veteran applicants may have special circumstances and questions about the process, so you are encouraged to reach out to us at
[email protected]
or
[email protected]
. The
Applying as a Veteran
page may also be helpful.
