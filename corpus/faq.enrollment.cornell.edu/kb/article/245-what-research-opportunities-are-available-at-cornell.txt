What research opportunities are available at Cornell?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
15823
views
As a world-renowned research university, Cornell is committed to providing undergraduate students with research opportunities. Research is conducted across all academic departments, including humanities and arts. Undergraduates will find both individual research opportunities and team projects; some begin research their first year at Cornell.
