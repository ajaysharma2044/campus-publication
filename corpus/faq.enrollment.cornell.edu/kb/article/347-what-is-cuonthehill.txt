What is CUontheHill?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
11668
views
CUontheHill is Cornell’s private admitted student community. Admitted students to the incoming class will receive an invitation to join the network after being offered admission to Cornell. On CUontheHill, students can connect with current Cornellians, staff, and alumni.
