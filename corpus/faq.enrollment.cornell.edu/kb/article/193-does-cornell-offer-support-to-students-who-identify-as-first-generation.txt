Does Cornell offer support to students who identify as first-generation?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
15406
views
Cornell is committed to fostering an inclusive and welcoming environment for first-generation students. We consider you a first-generation college student if you come from a family in which neither of your parents or guardians has obtained a four-year college degree. To learn about the support available to students who identify as first-generation, please visit the
Student Life
website. The section of our
How to Apply
page for
First-Generation College and Low and Moderate Income Applicants
may also be a helpful resource.
