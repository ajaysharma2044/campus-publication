Can I still apply even if I don’t meet the minimum requirements as outlined in the Human Ecology Admissions Requirements?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
13743
views
First-Year Students
Competitive applicants will have met all of the
first-year admissions requirements
. Applicants who have curricular gaps may provide an explanation/context for the gaps in the “Additional Information” section of the Common Application. Their school counselor may include this information in their reference letter.
Review CHE’s
first-year application tips
for additional information.
Transfer Students
Prospective transfer students need to complete
specific coursework requirements
to be competitive in the
transfer admissions process
. These courses should be completed, or in progress, at the time the application is submitted. Applicants who have curricular gaps can provide an explanation/context for the gaps in the “Additional Information” section of the Common Application.
Completion of these requirements positions incoming transfers to graduate on time and pursue their
College of Human Ecology
major with focus and confidence.
Review CHE’s
transfer application tips
for additional information.
