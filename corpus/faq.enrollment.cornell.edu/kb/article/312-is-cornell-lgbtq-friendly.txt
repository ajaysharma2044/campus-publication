Is Cornell LGBTQ+ friendly?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14533
views
There are a number of resources available at Cornell to support the LGBTQ+ community. Twenty-plus undergraduate and graduate LGBTQ+ student groups on campus, a variety of resources for people interested in the study of human sexuality and gender, and events and programs designed for first-year students help provide a community that is open and welcoming for all. The
LGBTQ Resource Center
page is a great starting point to learn more.
