Does Cornell have a student newspaper?

Founded in 1880, The
Cornell Daily Sun
is the oldest continuously independent college daily newspaper in the country. It is staffed almost entirely by Cornell students, operating out of an office building in downtown Ithaca. The Cornell Daily Sun is free on newsstands and available online, providing the Cornell community with all of the latest campus news.
