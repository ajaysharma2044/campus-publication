Does Cornell have an Honors Program?
Author:
Katy Lemon
21511
views
Several of the academic programs at Cornell offer students the opportunity to participate in an Honors Program and obtain an honors distinction if certain requirements are met. To learn more about the college or school-specific Honors Programs that may be available, please explore the website for the
undergraduate college or school
of interest.
Other programs of interest may be the
Hunter R. Rawlings III Cornell Presidential Research Scholars
, the
Meinig Family Cornell National Scholars
, and the
Cornell Tradition
. These Cornell Commitment programs aim to recognize, reward, and encourage further development of a select group of Cornellians who exemplify the university’s commitment to excellence, work, and service.
To learn more, please visit the
Cornell Commitment
website.
