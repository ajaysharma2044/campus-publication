I just started attending a university in my home country, but I want to apply to Cornell as a first-year student. Is this possible?
Author:
Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
14295
views
You must apply to Cornell as a transfer student if you’ve graduated from high school and have earned 12 or more credits at another college or university since then. If you are enrolled as a full-time student at another institution, you are also considered a transfer applicant.
Please be sure to review the
How to Apply
webpage for more information about Cornell's application process and requirements.
