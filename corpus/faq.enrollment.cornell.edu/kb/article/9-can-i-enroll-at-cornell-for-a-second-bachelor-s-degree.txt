Can I enroll at Cornell for a second bachelor’s degree?
Author:
Katy Lemon
24221
views
Students who have previously earned a bachelor’s degree are not permitted to enroll for a second bachelor’s degree at Cornell. If you already have a bachelor’s degree and you wish to pursue further study, please visit the
Cornell University Graduate School
website or the appropriate academic department for advice about extramural courses or graduate studies.
