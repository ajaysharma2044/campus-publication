Stellic Log-In Issues
If you have attempted these troubleshooting methods and are still unable to log-in, please contact your
Home College or School Student Services Office
.
If you have not received an email notification from CALS indicating that Stellic is available to you, then Stellic is not yet accessible to you and you will not be able to log-in.
Make sure you are using the following web address for logging into Stellic:
https://stellic.cornell.edu/
For instructions on clearing your browser’s cache, please see
Clear Your Browser’s Cookies, Cache, and History
.

This issue is caused by a bad cookie and can be removed. You may wish to remove this cookie without deleting history by selectively deleting only the Stellic-related cookie (stellic.cornell.edu or cornell.stellic.com).
