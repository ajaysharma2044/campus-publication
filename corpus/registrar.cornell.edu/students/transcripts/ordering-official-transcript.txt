Ordering Official Transcript
Official transcript services are provided in partnership with Parchment. All requests must be submitted by the current or former student. Requests submitted by parents or any unauthorized parties will not be honored. During peak processing times; May to June, August to mid-October, and December to February, processing and mail delivery times may take additional time. For any issues using Parchment, please contact Parchment.

Parchment Multi-Factor Authentication
Starting July 1st all Parchment Users will be required to set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for their Parchment Account. When you log in you will be required to enter a one-time access code that will be sent to the email address that you have registered with Parchment. If you are having a problem with accessing your account, please contact Parchment directly.
The transcript ordering site now includes the capability of adding attachments to both paper and electronic copies of your transcript. Accepted file types include .pdf, .jpg, .doc, .docx, .tiff, .txt, and .png. Please note that all attachments are delivered as submitted. If you require any part of the form to be completed by a college official, please work with the appropriate office(s) prior to uploading and submitting your attachments.
Students and alumni applying to medical schools can submit secure pdf electronic transcripts from our online ordering site directly to AMCAS (American Medical College Application Service).
Begin your order as specified above. After selecting Order Now from the welcome page, select the AMCAS eTranscript option. Enter your 8-digit AAMC ID & 7-digit Transcript ID (required). The two IDs may be found in your AMCAS application. The AAMC ID should be visible in the main menu and the Transcript ID is visible in the Schools Attended Summary. The IDs are also located on the AMCAS transcript form.
Please note that transcripts cannot be held until grades or degrees are finalized. Orders are processed as soon as possible.
The Office of the University Registrar is committed to maintaining the integrity, accuracy, and security of every current and former student’s academic record. As part of our ongoing efforts to enhance services and ensure the long-term sustainability of our operations, we charge a fee for official transcript requests. This fee supports:
The secure processing and delivery of official transcripts
Maintenance and enhancement of our academic records systems
Continued investment in technology and infrastructure to ensure timely and accurate recordkeeping
Compliance with institutional and federal standards for data integrity and privacy
We understand the importance of transparency and accountability in all financial matters. This fee structure has been carefully considered to balance service quality with fiscal responsibility, without impacting staffing or reducing support for our students and alumni. All transcripts are processed through our ordering partner, Parchment. The official transcript fee includes Parchment professing fees.
