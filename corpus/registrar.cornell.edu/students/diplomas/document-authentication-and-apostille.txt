Document Authentication and Apostille
Closed for in-person services
The Office of the University Registrar is closed for in-person services through Friday, October 9. Please email
univreg@cornell.edu
with your questions and we will work to assist you. Thank you for your understanding and patience.
Governments and agencies may require that diplomas, transcripts, and enrollment/degree certificates be certified with an Apostille. The University Registrar’s Office only provides step one of this process.
An official in the Office of the University Registrar must first authenticate the academic document. A notary from the office will then notarize the official's signature.
Processing will take an estimated 12 weeks.
Step 1
To request a document be processed for apostille:
Electronic documents: Email
univreg@cornell.edu
with the following:
Scanned copy of the document(s) (eDiplomas and eTranscripts will not be accepted, only a PDF file that can be printed)
Full name
Date of birth
Current mailing address (including country)
Phone number
Email address
Original document(s): Mail them to the following address, with the same information as above:
Cornell University
Office of the University Registrar
245 Day Hall
Ithaca, NY 14853
The Office of the University Registrar will process the necessary certifications and verifications and return them to you via postal mail.
We are not able to assist with expedited shipping.
If an apostille is required, the following steps need to be completed by the student or someone acting on their behalf.
Step 2
Once you receive the notarized document(s), you will need to obtain a
Notarial Certificate
from the Tompkins County Clerk. The following is needed:
Completed
Notarial Certificate
The notarized document(s)
A check/money order for $3.00 (per document) made payable to Tompkins County Clerk
Step 3
Once you receive the certified document(s), proceed to process with the
New York State Department of State Apostille services
.
All payments must be in U.S. funds drawn from a U.S. institution or bank.
