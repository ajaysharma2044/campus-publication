Locating a Class Number
Within the Class Roster:
Class Numbers (4-5 characters in length) are located under
Enrollment Information
, to the left of the Component Type and Section Number.
Within your Student Center:
Option 1
To determine the Class Number for a class that you wish to drop, you could review
This Week’s Schedule
on your
Student Center
.
Option 2
To determine the Class Number for a class that you wish to add, you can perform a Class Search by clicking on
Search
or
Search for Classes
from your
Student Center
homepage.
Select the appropriate
Subject
(Animal Science) from the drop down, enter the correct
Course Number
(1500), and click
Search
.
The
Section
(class section) and the
Class
(class number) will be returned on the
Search Results
screen. From this screen, you can opt to add the class section to your shopping cart by clicking
Select
and following the prompts.
