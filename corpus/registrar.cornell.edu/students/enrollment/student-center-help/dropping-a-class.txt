Dropping a Class
You may drop a class directly through your
Student Center
during either pre-enrollment or open enrollment (prior to the start of each semester). If your enrollment window has closed or an enrollment deadline has passed, please reach out to your
College or School Registrar
for further guidance.
To start, after logging in to your
Student Center
, click on
Enroll
in your
Student Center
.
Select
Drop
under the
Enroll
tab.
Step 1 - Select classes to drop
Select the term in which you will be changing your enrollment.
Select the course from which you would like to drop and click
Drop Selected Classes
.
Step 2 - Confirm your selection
Select
Finish Dropping
.
Step 3 - View Results
If you have successfully dropped the class, you will receive a success
message
and a green check mark in the
status
field.
If you have been unsuccessful in dropping the class, you will receive an error
message
and a red X in the
status
field.
