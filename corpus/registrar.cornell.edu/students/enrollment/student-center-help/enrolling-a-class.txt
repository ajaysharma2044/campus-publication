Enrolling in a Class
You may add a class directly through your
Student Center
during either pre-enrollment or open enrollment (prior to the start of each semester). If your enrollment window has closed or an enrollment deadline has passed, please reach out to your
College or School Registrar
for further guidance.
To start, after logging in to your
Student Center
, click on
Enroll
.
Select
Add
under the Enroll tab.
Step 1 - Select classes to add
Select the term in which you will be changing your enrollment.
Enter the Class Number in
Enter Class Nbr
and then select
Enter
.
Confirm that you will be enrolling in the appropriate grading basis and number of units on the
Enrollment Preferences
page and click
Next
.
Once you select
Next
, the class will appear in your shopping cart. Select
Proceed to Step 2 of 3
to continue with your enrollment request.
Step 2 - Confirm classes
Select
Finish Enrolling
.
Step 3 - View Results
A successful enrollment attempt will produce a success
message
and green check mark in the
status
field.
An unsuccessful enrollment attempt will produce an error
message
and a red X in the
status
field.
