About
The Office of the University Registrar (OUR) supports students, faculty, and staff through the educational experience. Our office manages the student information system, implements tools to bring efficiencies across campus, and provides central services including ID cards, diplomas, and transcripts, and collaborates with college and school registrar offices for student success.
