Call for Submissions:
Biddy Martin Graduate Prize & LGBT Studies Undergraduate Prize
The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, & Transgender Studies program will be accepting submissions for the 2026 Biddy Martin Graduate Prize and 2026 LGBT Studies Undergraduate Prize.
Submission Guidelines:
Entrants for either prize must be currently enrolled students at Cornell University. Works submitted for consideration must be your original work produced at any time during your tenure at Cornell. Entrants may submit an essay, artwork, article, thesis chapter, dissertation chapter, etc for consideration.
A faculty committee will conduct a blind review (with submitters’ names and identifying markers removed from entries by the LGBT Program Manager).
Deadline:
Applications are due April 24th, and winners are notified in May each year. Send submissions to
lgbtstudies@cornell.edu
.
