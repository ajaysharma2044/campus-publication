About Summer Experience Grants
The Summer Experience Grant (SEG) is a collection of summer funding awards that provide financial support for students to complete summer unpaid or minimally paid career-related experiences. SEG supports students with funding for living expenses, transportation, and professional clothing so that these essential experiences are available to all of our students, including those who may otherwise not be able to afford them. The selection process is competitive, as we receive a large number of high-quality applications for a limited amount of funding. Administration of the grant is coordinated by University Career Development. For information on eligibility and the application, please see University Career Development's
Summer Experience Grant website
. After reviewing the website, feel free to contact
seg@cornell.edu
with any further questions.
College of Arts & Sciences Students
Undergraduate students in the College of Arts & Sciences will be considered for A&S specific awards. Visit the eligibility criteria portion of the website above and take the
eligibility screening
to determine if your experience qualifies.
A&S funds have a maximum award of $6,000 per student. Students residing in university housing (at Cornell or elsewhere) and/or students on financial aid with high need may exceed the $6,000 limit. These applications will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Students should ensure that their budget form and application are detailed and thorough.
In addition to funding available for all eligible experiences there is specific funding available for:
students on financial aid,
first-generation college students,
Higher Education Opportunity Program (HEOP) participants,
students who are traveling internationally,
experiences at Weill Cornell Medicine,
experiences in the field of journalism/communication, with a preference for students from
The Cornell Daily Sun
, and
experiences in think tanks/government in Washington, DC (funded by the Berger Grant).
