Jason Simms, Assistant Professor, Performing & Media Arts

Design in performing and media arts

The Hive, a social distancing performance and gathering venue

Adjunct Faculty, Theatre Department, Montclair State University, 2018-2020
Visiting Lecturer and Design Mentor, Department of Theatre and Dance, Williams College, 2018-2019
Adjunct Faculty, Theatre Department, Fordham University, 2017
Guest Artist, Drama Department & Vocal Arts, The Juilliard School, 2013-2019

Guest Artist, Yale Dramatic Association, Yale University, 2011
Graduate Assistant, Undergraduate Drama, NYU, Tisch School of the Arts, 2006-2009
Academic background:
M.F.A., Design for stage and film, NYU Tisch School of the Arts, 2009
B.F.A., Performance production, Cornish College of the Arts, 2005
Last book read:
“Between the World and Me” by Ta-Nehisi Coates
In your own time/when not working:
I tend to my plants, gardening, enjoying nature and keep fish aquariums.
Courses you’re most looking forward to teaching:
Terrarium Imagined: World Building Through Allegory; Scenic and Lighting Design Studio I
What most excites you about Cornell:
I am so excited to be in a place where so many cross-disciplinary possibilities exist. The sharing of information and discoveries across each college and department excites me.
