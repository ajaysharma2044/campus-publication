Youth Employment Program (YEP) - 2026-2027 School Year
Applications are now open on a rolling basis!
The Youth Employment Program (YEP) serves youth ages 14-20 in the 14850 zip code with job placements and career readiness education through one-on-one support and workshops. Main work experience period: October 2026 – June 2027.
Income eligibility requirements: Family receive at least one form of public assistance or is income eligible at 200% of the Federal Poverty Line.
YEP Interest Survey
Summer Youth Employment Program (SYEP) - Summer 2027
Application currently closed; and will reopen Spring 2027.
The Summer Youth Employment Program (SYEP) is a six-week paid work opportunity for youth ages 14–20 to gain real-world work experience, build job skills, and explore career interests. Participants are placed at local worksites and take part in workshops that help develop financial literacy, resume building, and leadership skills. Work experience July through August.
Income eligibility requirements: Family receive at least one form of public assistance or is income eligible at 200% of the Federal Poverty Line.

The JobLink Program
JobLink is a year-round program for Tompkins County young people ages 14-24
JobLink serves to support and equip young people with the skills and services to gain and maintain employment along a career path of interest
Participants may be eligible for a temporary work placement for up to 480 hours at $16.00/hour to improve employable skills in a supportive environment
Learn more about JobLink by watching our short
info video on YouTube
JobLink is funded federally under the Workforce Innovation Opportunity Act (WIOA)
