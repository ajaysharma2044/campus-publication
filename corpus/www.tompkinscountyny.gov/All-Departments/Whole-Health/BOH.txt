The Tompkins County Board of Health is established under NYS Public Health Law
Article 3, Title III, Section 343
.
Meetings
Meetings are the 4th Tuesday of the month at Noon, except for Nov. & Dec., which are combined and held the first Tuesday in December.
All meetings are
open to the public
in the Rice Conference Room at Whole Health,
55 Brown Rd., Ithaca
.
Meetings will be recorded and the video will be uploaded to YouTube after the meeting.
(Links in the dropdown sections below.)
Meetings are not livestreamed.
