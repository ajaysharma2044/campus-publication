FETCHED: 2026-11-06

Office for the Aging
Minor Home and Safety Repair Program
Through a contract with
INHS
, this county-wide program provides minor home repairs, especially those related to health and safety, for older adult homeowners age 60+.
Types of work that can be done through this program include:
installing handrails and grab bars
patching loose flooring
installing wheelchair ramps
fixing or replacing deteriorating steps
improving lighting
alleviating trip hazards
fixing leaky faucets
For more information or to apply please contact INHS at 607-277-4500.
