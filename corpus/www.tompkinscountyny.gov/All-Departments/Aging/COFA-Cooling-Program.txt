Office for the Aging
COFA Cooling Program
The 2026 COFA Cooling Program is closed for this year.
Eligible individuals age 60+, or caregivers of eligible individuals 60+, can receive an air conditioner through the Office for the Aging. The COFA Cooling Program opens once the HEAP cooling assistance program has ended and will close September 30th. This program covers the cost of an air conditioner for eligible older adults.
Eligibility:
Must meet the current income guidelines for HEAP:
Household Size
Maximum Gross Monthly Income
1
$3,473
2
$4,542
3
$5,611
Cannot have a working air conditioner or the air conditioner that the individual has must be 5 years or older.
Cannot have received an air conditioner from this program in the past 5 years.
Must have a physicians note specifying that the individual has a medical condition that is exacerbated by the heat.
Individual must have the means to retrieve the air conditioner from the Office for the Aging.
The type of air conditioner shall be determined on a case by case basis and shall not exceed $500. The COFA Cooling Program closes on September 30th or whenever funding runs out, whichever happens first. For more information or to apply for the COFA Cooling Program please call our office at
