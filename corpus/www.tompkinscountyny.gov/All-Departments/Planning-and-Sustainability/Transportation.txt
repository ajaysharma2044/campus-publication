Planning and Sustainability
Transportation
The Tompkins County Comprehensive Plan aims to create a transportation system that is efficient, affordable, and environmentally friendly. To achieve this, the County seeks to maintain and improve its existing transportation network, reduce reliance on fossil fuels, promote alternative modes of transportation like biking, walking, and public transit, support land use patterns that facilitate efficient movement, ensure the smooth flow of freight, maintain airport services, and address the transportation needs of vulnerable populations. Tompkins County Transportation Coordinated Plan (2026-2029)
The
Tompkins County Transportation Coordinated Plan (2026-2029)
identifies cost-effective approaches to close transportation gaps and improve coordination among local providers, with a focus on residents who face the greatest barriers to getting around, including people with disabilities, older adults, and those with limited income. Drawing on socio-economic data, an inventory of existing services, and input from transportation providers, human services agencies, and local government, the plan lays out goals and actions to reduce duplication, make better use of available resources, and guide how federal and state funding is prioritized by the County, public transit operators, and project partners in the years ahead
Mobility Vision Plan for Tompkins County (2024-2027)
In 2024, the County Legislature approved the
Mobility Vision Plan for Tompkins County (2024-2027)
(PDF, 408KB)
, a comprehensive strategy for improving transportation in the county. The Mobility Vision prioritizes customer-focused mobility management, aiming to provide equitable access to transportation for all residents. This plan addresses the social determinants of health by breaking down transportation barriers, ensuring fair access to essential destinations like jobs, healthcare, and education.
Mobility Management Programs
Mobility management is more than just providing modes of transportation — it's making sure that transportation is accessible, affordable, and free from unnecessary stress for all Tompkins County residents. You could have an infinite supply of buses, bikes, or cars, but if people can't afford a bus pass, feel unsafe riding their bikes, or don't have a driver's license, no matter how many vehicles you have, people still won't be able to utilize them or get from point A to point B.

Transportation Equity Coalition
Mission
: To advance transportation equity by advocating for and implementing accessible, affordable and reliable transportations solutions that engage historically unrepresented communities, ensuring their full participation and autonomy in mobility.
Vision
: An equitable transportation system where all individuals, regardless of background or circumstance, have seamless access to affordable, reliable, and autonomous travel options that enhance quality of life and opportunities.
The Transportation Equity Coalition is a group of transportation providers, human service providers, and Tompkins County residents working to advance transportation equity in Tompkins County.
