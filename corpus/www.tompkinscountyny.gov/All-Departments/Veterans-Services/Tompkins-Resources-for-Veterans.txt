Tompkins’ Resources for Veterans
Departments across Tompkins County government offer services and benefits for local Veterans. Please read on to learn more about these perks for living in Tompkins County.

County Clerk
The Tompkins County Clerk offers Veterans the opportunity to file a copy of their original DD214 with the office. The office will provide a Veteran certified copies of their discharge papers, free of charge, if/whenever needed.
Veterans who file their original DD214 with the County Clerk’s office also get to register for Return the FAVOR (Find & Assist Vets of Record). Through this program, registered merchants offer discounts to enrolled Tompkins County Veterans who patronize their business.
Return the FAVOR Program
Qualified Veterans, those who were honorably discharged or received a general discharge under honorable circumstances, can have the word "Veteran" printed on the upper left hand of their NYS driver license, learner permit or non-driver photo ID card.
