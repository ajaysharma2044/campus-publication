Sheriff's Office
Inmate Visitation
Visitation days are Tuesday and Saturday between the hours of 8:30 A.M. and 4:15 P.M. These visits will be allowed in one-hour blocks in either contact or non-contact visitation areas. An inmate has the right to refuse any visit. All new inmates have the right to a 20 minute non-contact initial visit within 24 hours of confinement unless regular visitation hours fall within that same period.
8:30 AM-9:30 AM
9:45 AM-10:45 AM
11:00 AM-12:00 PM
1:15 PM-2:15 PM
3:15 PM-4:15 PM
It is requested that visitors contact the Corrections Division at 257-5316 and reserve a visiting time. The visiting room consists of 6 contact and 2 non-contact visits. Calls will be taken on Sundays from 9:00am-2:00pm and 7:00pm-10:00pm and Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays between the hours of 7:00 pm-10:00 pm. No reservations will be taken beyond the next available visiting day. (Example: Visitor can not call on a Monday to reserve a time for a Saturday visit.)
To ensure you are not turned away due to a full visitation room, it is recommended to schedule a visitation period.
Visitors will be responsible for arriving at their visit on time. Visitors should arrive 15 minutes prior to their reserved time. Once the hour has commenced, no visitors will be allowed in. No split visits.
Properly identified persons shall mean a person who presents adequate proof as to his/her identity. Adequate proof shall consist of the following:
Valid State Drivers License with photo and D.O.B.
Current Passport
Sheriff's ID with photo and D.O.B.
Valid State issued Non-driver's I.D. with photo and D.O.B.
Commissary money (for inmate accounts) will be accepted from the public on Sundays between the hours of 7:30 am and 10:30 am and Thursdays 3:00 pm-5:00 pm. All other commissary money will be accepted using the Cashier Machine in the lobby that has been provided by the Commissary Vendor.
People wishing to drop off money using the cash machine will need a valid driver’s license along with the inmate’s PIN number or must know the inmate’s name and date of birth. Officers will not give out this information.
In addition to a valid ID, visitors under the age of 18 years are required to be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Proof required for the child will be a birth certificate and/or court documents.
