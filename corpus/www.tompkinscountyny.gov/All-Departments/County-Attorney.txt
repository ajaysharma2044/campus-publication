County Attorney
County Attorney
The County Attorney's Office is the sole legal advisor for Tompkins County and provides legal opinions and guidance to the Legislator, County Departments and staff. It represents the County as a government entity in civil litigation, bankruptcy, foreclosure, solid waste and health department matters, capital projects and County property transactions, and acts as prosecutor of juvenile delinquency and PINS cases in Family Court.
