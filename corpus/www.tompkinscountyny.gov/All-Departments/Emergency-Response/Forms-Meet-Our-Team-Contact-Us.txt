The 9-1-1 Communications Center and the public safety agencies we serve are operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Much of the 9-1-1 activity received occurs after business hours. When a first responder is sent to a business or other premise, it is often difficult to locate an emergency contact to come and assume responsibility for the property. You will find a link on this page where you can obtain a form to complete and submit emergency contact for your business or property. You can also use this form to notify us of a new business, an address change, or other information that would help us help you.
