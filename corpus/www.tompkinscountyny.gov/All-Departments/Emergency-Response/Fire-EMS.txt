The Tompkins County Rapid Medical Response (RMR) program was selected the 2026
Central New York EMS Agency of the Year
by CNYEMS. As the winner of the regional award, the program is automatically nominated for New York State EMS Agency of the Year. Winners of that award will be announced later this fall. Congratulations to the Tompkins EMS team!
