Virtual Meeting on the Tompkins County Resiliency and Recovery Plan
Published on May 10, 2022
Join us for a Virtual Meeting on the
Tompkins County Resiliency and Recovery Plan.
Learn about the integrated plan that outlines specific steps our local governments, agencies, businesses, and residents throughout the County can take to build resiliency.
Date:
Wednesday, May 18, 2022
Time:
7:00 – 8:30 pm
Register:
https://bit.ly/TompkinsCo_VirtualMtg
Tompkins County Department of Planning & Sustainability is leading development of a countywide Resiliency and Recovery Plan. This plan aims to reduce the risks associated with hazards and the changing climate, as well as to better prepare for long-term recovery from disaster events.
