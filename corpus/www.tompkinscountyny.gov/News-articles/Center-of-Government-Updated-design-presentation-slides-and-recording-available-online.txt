Center of Government: Updated designs and recording available online
Published on July 09, 2026
At the June 15 Legislature meeting, HOLT architects presented
updated interior and exterior renderings and floor plans (view slides) (PDF, 4MB),
reflecting legislative direction to reduce the building size to 45,000 square feet.
The resulting programming changes include County Administration, Human Resources, and Planning and Sustainability departments not moving into the new Center of Government. Under this revised plan, County Administration and Human Resources would return to the Old Jail building on East Court Street. The Planning and Sustainability department would remain at the Governor Daniel D. Tompkins building on East Court Street.
A synopsis of the presentation and comments is included in the

About the Project
The proposed project involves constructing a 45,000-square-foot Center of Government designed to centralize County services, enhance staff equity, improve operational efficiency, eliminate reliance on leased space, and support long-term sustainability. The project will involve the removal of three structures in the DeWitt Park Historic District (128 E. Buffalo St. and 300 and 308 N. Tioga St.). The project is expected to be completed in 2029.
The project page
tcgov.co/cog
was created to provide transparency and timely updates to residents as this initiative progresses. It includes information on the project background, vision and goals, timeline, funding, and the departments involved.
