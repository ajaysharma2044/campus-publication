Linderman Creek Polling Site Moved
Published on November 05, 2024
The Tompkins County Board of Elections is alerting voters whose polling site is at the Linderman Creek Community Building at 201 Cypress Court (
ITH-10
) that the General Election polling site has moved to a community room located at (101 Conifer Circle) within the same apartment complex. Directional signage has been adjusted and now points to the new site.
The polling site was moved following a vehicle accident. Ithaca Fire Department and the Tompkins County Sheriff’s Office responded to the scene and are reporting no injuries. Tompkins County can confirm no malicious intent related to the accident.
Voters who plan to vote at this polling site are being asked to wait to arrive until after 2pm as election equipment is securely moved. It is not anticipated that voting hours will change further at this time.
