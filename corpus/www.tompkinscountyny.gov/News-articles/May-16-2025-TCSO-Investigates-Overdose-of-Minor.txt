May 16, 2025 - TCSO Investigates Overdose of Minor
Published on May 16, 2025
The Tompkins County Sheriff's Office is investigating a recent incident of a minor experiencing an adverse reaction after using a vape pen. The content of the vape pen and its source are currently not confirmed by law enforcement.
We also have not determined when an illicit substance may have been placed within it.
