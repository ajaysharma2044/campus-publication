October 26, 2026 - DWI Arrest
Published on October 26, 2026
On 10/26/26, at 3:36 AM, deputies responded to a one car collision on Cinema Drive in the Village of Lansing, NY. Upon arrival, they observed a vehicle with heavy front-end damage. The investigation revealed that the vehicle had left the roadway and struck a street sign and then a tree.
The driver, Jiayi Luo, age 25, of Long Island City, NY, was charged with Driving While Intoxicated. He was additionally cited for Failure to Keep Right and Speed Not Reasonable and Prudent.
Luo was scheduled to appear in the Town of Lansing Court on 11/24/26, at 2:00 PM.
All charges are allegations only and all defendants are presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.
