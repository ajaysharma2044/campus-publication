Juneteenth Closing and Recycling Pickup Schedule
Published on June 12, 2026
The Tompkins County Department of Recycling and Materials Management (TCRMM) office will be
closed
Friday, June 19th, and will
reopen
Monday, June 22nd, during regular hours. However, the Recycling and Solid Waste Center (RSWC) will remain open as usual on
Friday, June 19
th
.
There will be
curbside recycling collection
on Friday, June 19
. Recycling pickup will occur as usual for the duration of the week.
Residents can use the
“Find My Recycling Day”
tool to receive curbside collection reminders via phone, text or email. The tool automatically adjusts for holidays when there is no collection. Enter your address and click “Get a Reminder.”
About Tompkins County Recycling and Materials Management
Tompkins County Recycling and Materials Management develops and implements sustainable strategies that maximize diversion with the 4Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, and Rethink) as a focal point. Learn more at
RecycleTompkins.org
, and follow us on
Facebook
and
Instagram
.
