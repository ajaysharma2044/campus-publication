Martin Luther King Jr. Day Closing and Recycling Pickup Schedule
The Tompkins County Department of Recycling and Materials Management (TCRMM) office will be
closed
Monday, Jan. 19, and will
reopen
Tuesday, Jan. 20, during regular hours. The Recycling and Solid Waste Center (RSWC) will remain
open
on Monday, Jan. 19.
There will be
curbside recycling collection
on Monday, Jan. 19
. Recycling pickup will follow the normal schedule during the holiday week.
Residents can use the “Find My Recycling Day” tool at
https://tcgov.co/MyRecyclingDay
to receive curbside collection reminders via phone, text or email. The tool automatically adjusts for holidays when there is no collection. Enter your address and click “Get a Reminder.”
About Tompkins County Recycling and Materials Management
Tompkins County Recycling and Materials Management develops and implements sustainable strategies that maximize diversion with the 4Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, and Rethink) as a focal point. Learn more at
RecycleTompkins.org
, and follow us on
Facebook
and
Instagram
.
