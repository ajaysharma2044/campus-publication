Early Voting Open Through Sunday, Election Day is Tuesday, November 7
Published on November 03, 2023
First Annual Tompkins County
“I Voted” Sticker
Being Handed Out to Voters
Polls are open for early voting through Sunday, November 5 in Tompkins County, election day is Tuesday, November 7.
For early voting, voters can go to the Town of Ithaca Town Hall (215 N Tioga St, near the Post Office), or to the Tompkins County Crash Fire Rescue Building (72 Brown Rd., near the airport). Both sites are open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Friday through Sunday, November 5th. Any Tompkins County voter can visit either site for early voting, and encouraged to remember daylight saving time clock adjustments on Sunday morning.
On Election Day, November 7, 2023, voters may vote at their regular poll sites from 6:00 AM until 9:00 PM. If you are not sure where your polling place is, visit
www.votetompkins.com
, call 607-274-5522, or check your registration (with polling place information) at the NYS Board of Elections
https://voterlookup.elections.ny.gov/
All Tompkins County voters will receive an
“I Voted” sticker
featuring the winning design from a contest engaging local students.
