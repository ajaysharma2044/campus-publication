County offices closed for Juneteenth
Published on June 17, 2026
Tompkins County government offices will be closed on Friday, June 19, 2026, in observance of Juneteenth.
Regular office hours will resume on Monday, June 22. Residents are encouraged to plan accordingly for any county services that may be affected by the holiday closure.
Juneteenth commemorates the end of slavery in the United States and honors the ongoing pursuit of freedom, equity, and opportunity for all.
