Overview
Join us for an Executive Master of Human Resource Management (EMHRM) information session on
Thursday, November 12, at 12 p.m. ET.
You'll get an overview of the curriculum, learn how the program combines online coursework with on-campus residential sessions and hear how Cornell faculty and senior HR practitioners bring real-world expertise into the classroom.
We'll also review the admissions process, share application tips and answer your questions about the EMHRM experience.
We look forward to seeing you there!
