Immigration Research Shapes Labor and Employment
Immigrant workers contribute to the economy and society of the United States, yet even “documented” immigrant workers face precarious working conditions.
Researchers across the ILR School examine how immigration status intersects with race, gender, national origin and the local political context to shape worker outcomes.

24th Annual ILR Labor Roundtable
The ILR Labor Roundtable is an annual event that brings together a wide range of representatives from labor leadership, unions, and social justice organizations to campus for dynamic, in-depth conversations with students. Through these discussions, students gain exposure to current trends in the labor movement and the fundamental role that it plays in bringing about social change. This event is open to all students. Please register by logging into your existing Handshake account to attend.
