Business at ILR
To succeed in the world of business, you must understand its most important component — the people. The ILR School is the world leader in studying the intersection of economic, legal, management, international and historical issues that impact people in the workplace.
Entrepreneurs, top executives, financial analysts and nonprofit leaders who have graduated from ILR credit their success to our interdisciplinary and applied curriculum. Unique courses not found within many business programs, such
as Conflict Resolution, Technological Change at Work
and
Managing Creativity
are why ILR students stand out to a wide range of employers like Google, Goldman Sachs, Deloitte and Disney.
