MILR Alumni Stories
MILR alums benefit from opportunities to network with HR leaders in some of the world’s top companies. They are also able to connect with their cohort of peers, who often become lifelong friends and advocates. Students credit the program with changing the way they think and work in HR, establishing themselves as trusted partners to organizational leaders and landing their dream jobs.
