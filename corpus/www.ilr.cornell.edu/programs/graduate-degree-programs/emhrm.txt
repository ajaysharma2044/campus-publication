Executive Master of Human Resource Management
EMHRM is a 15-month hybrid program
for HR professionals with 8+ years of experience
A Master’s for HR professionals. Take the next step in your career!
Check your eligibility
Apply today
Through a hybrid learning environment, EMHRM students engage with peers across the HR field, develop strategies for challenges that matter now and learn from faculty deeply engaged in both HR research and practice.
With a blend of online coursework, immersive residencies and applied capstone work, students strengthen their strategic leadership, expand their impact and build lasting relationships that extend well beyond the program.
