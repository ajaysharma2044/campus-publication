Overview
A deep, practical understanding of conflict management can position you uniquely within your enterprise. Managed well, conflict can be a source of creativity and innovation in the workplace.
It’s part of our mission to bring all stakeholders to the table in decision-making processes, giving us a unique perspective on conflict in the workplace. Over our 75 year history we have built the leading faculty studying workplace issues, so our professional programs use world-class research as their foundation. Our instructors and program directors collaborate with industry leaders to build real time examples and experience into the programs.
