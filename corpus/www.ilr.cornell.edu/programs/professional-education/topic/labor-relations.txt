Overview
The Cornell ILR School was created to study and practice labor relations. This program consists of skilled-based interactive workshops for both management and union representatives. Learn, practice and receive feedback from experts with decades of experience. Develop the confidence and ability to design strategy, engage stakeholders, negotiate, administer the CBA, resolve disputes and operate effectively in a union environment. Network with other union and management representatives from all industries across the US and around the world.
