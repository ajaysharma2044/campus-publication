Learn how to be a strong CHRO and lead from the top seat.

HR Leadership: Enhance Your Influence and Impact
Lead With Influence. Drive Change. Achieve Results.
Today's HR leaders face a range of organizational and workforce challenges to lead transformational change and drive business results.  HR leaders must understand the business, navigate complex relationships, and foster consensus to drive meaningful outcomes. This program provides HR leaders with tools and strategies to advise and support senior business leaders and influence the actions of the leadership teams they support to enable organizational results.

Cancellation Policy
All cancellations requests must be received in writing by ILR Customer Service. Full refunds of paid program fees can be obtained up to 30 days before the program start date. Any cancellations or requests for transfers and substitutes received 30 days or less from the program start are subject to the fees as described below.
More than 30 days' notice: 100% refund
30 to 14 days' notice: 50% refund
Less than 14 days' notice: no refund
To make a request for a cancellation, transfer or substitution, please contact ILR Customer Service at 1-866-470-1922 or
