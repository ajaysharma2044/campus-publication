Course
Strategic, well-executed organization design is critical to navigating complexity and maintaining agility. The pressure for organizations to continually adapt has never been greater. Market shifts, digital disruption, and new ways of working make change a constant—and the cost of misalignment steep. Whether it's a full-scale transformation or a targeted structural adjustment, effective organizational design is essential.
HR professionals and organizational effectiveness professionals are expected to guide the business through this complexity, align design with strategy, and lead change that drives real results. This hands-on, two-day workshop provides you with the tools and confidence to translate strategy into design - and design into action.
Participants are asked to bring a real organizational design or change project – current, upcoming, or past - to the workshop. You’ll apply the concepts directly to your case, learn from peers tacking similar challenges, and leave with a practical action plan you can implement immediately.
Grounded in proven frameworks and real-world examples, this workshop walks you through the full organization design process—from diagnosing issues to developing design criteria, selecting structural models, and enabling successful implementation. You’ll also explore how organization design connects to strategy, culture, and sustainable change.
Key outcomes
Lead an optimal end-to-end organizational design process
Evaluate the current state and identify design gaps
Apply the STAR model to align structure, people, rewards and processes
Build design criteria tied to strategy and key organizational capabilities
Compare organizational models to support strategic objectives
Learn how to integrate and activate the new design across the system
Anticipate design risks and manage unintended consequences
Use practical change tools to engage stakeholders and drive adoption
Who should enroll
HR professionals, organizational design practitioners, and change leaders responsible for advising the business or leading transformation
Best suited for those with foundational knowledge of organizational design—such as structural models, roles, and reporting lines—looking to deepen their expertise and consulting skills
Sample titles: HR Managers, HR Generalists, HR Business Partners, Organizational Effectiveness Consultants
