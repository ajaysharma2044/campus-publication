Payment and Fees
Registration Fees
Registration fees vary depending on the course. Your registration fee includes all course materials, a continental breakfast, lunch, and refreshment breaks. Prices may change without notice.
A 15% discount is available for government and not-for-profit organizations.
Payment
Payment or payment guarantee (such as a purchase order) is expected at the time of registration. If a PO or other guarantee of payment will be submitted from your organization, your manager must sign the registration form to take responsibility of payment. You may pay by credit card or check. Please make checks payable to CORNELL UNIVERSITY ILR. All prices and dates are subject to change.
Substitution and Cancellation
Substitutions of registrants can be made at any time, unless a course has a prerequisite or pre-work. Cancellations and transfers are subject to a 25% charge unless
received in writing five business days
before the course. When an organization's representative approves an employee registration, that organization becomes responsible for cancellations, transfers, substitutions, and payments. The full course  fee will be charged for any registration that is not cancelled in writing.
In the event this workshop is cancelled or has a date change, Cornell ILR is not responsible for any penalty incurred by purchasing a non-refundable airline ticket or related travel or personal costs.
Enrollment and Attendance
Enrollment Limits
Spaces for each course is limited, so courses quickly fill up. We recommend early registration. The number of participants from the same organization may be limited, depending on the course.
Participation
Our courses are highly interactive in nature and involve adult-learning techniques. So that everyone has the best chance to benefit from the experience, attendance at the entire workshop is required.
Schedule and Accommodation
Schedule
Check-in, coffee and review of materials begins half an hour before the course starting time. Courses finish between 4:30 p.m. and 5:00 p.m. depending on the course and location.
Hotel Accommodation
The registration fee does not include hotel accommodations. We will send you a list of area hotels along with your registration confirmation.
Special Accommodation
If you need specific, ADA accommodations, please let the training center know at least two weeks before your course starts.
