24th Annual ILR Labor Roundtable
The ILR Labor Roundtable is an annual event that brings together a wide range of representatives from labor leadership, unions, and social justice organizations to campus for dynamic, in-depth conversations with students. Through these discussions, students gain exposure to current trends in the labor movement and the fundamental role that it plays in bringing about social change. This event is open to all students. Exhibit Opening: Images and Texts of Labor in China - A Thirty Year History (1993-2023)
Since 2016, the China labor collection at Kheel Center in the ILR School’s Catherwood Library, has amassed a vast audio-visual archive consisting of photographs, films, brochures, oral interviews, poetry, and other materials on labor in the People’s Republic of China between the years 1993-2023. This exhibition is a curation of this invaluable collection, made available for the first time to the Cornell and greater Ithaca community. Come and learn about the multifaceted lives of China’s workers: their struggles, their efforts to determine their own working conditions through strikes and protests, and their self-expression in the form of documentation, poems, videos and more.
Acknowledgements: Cornell Kheel Center, ILR School; Department of Asian Studies; The East Asia Program; Department of History of Art & Visual Studies; the China Asia Pacific Studies Program; and the Asian Labor Institute

Worker Precarity in Journalism, Academia and Computer Programming
Collective Representation as a Response to Worker Precarity
What do part-time or underemployed workers without health benefits, pensions or job security who work split shifts at a number of different locations each week have in common? They all hold a university degree, often with at least one master’s degree and/or a doctorate, but find themselves in precarious, insecure work.
