2025-26 New York at Work
We are excited to share with you the sixth annual New York at Work report, produced by Cornell University’s New York State School of Industrial and Labor Relations (ILR).
Established by the New York State Legislature in 1945 to address the great workplace conflicts of the day and to promote better labor-management relations, the ILR School is guided by a commitment to social and economic justice and to improving the lives of New York’s working people. Today, we continue to be the world’s leading school focused on labor, management and the economy.
