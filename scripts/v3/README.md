# Running the v3 pipeline (docs/v3/spec.md; v2 still owns files, lint, render, images and deploy)

One topic at a time, in this order; `<topic>` is a slug from `scripts/v3/topics.yml` (`off-campus-housing`, `getting-around`, `money`, ...); its articles live under `content/articles/<dir>/`.

1. Research: `scripts/v3/research.sh <topic> [--lanes A,B,C,D,E] [--workers 5] [--model sonnet]`. Five lanes (official, market, voice, competition, analysis) write raw, sourced, dated notes to `intel/<topic>/raw/<lane>*.md` plus `raw/demand-questions.md` (the topic's queue rows). Best started overnight; several topics may run at once.
2. Brief: `scripts/v3/brief.sh <topic> [--model opus] [--max-turns 200] [--check-model sonnet] [--skip-check] [--resume]`. One opus session merges every raw file into `intel/<topic>/brief.md`: 11 numbered sections (`## 1. The topic in one page` to `## 11. Change log`), 15,000 to 40,000 words, every fact row with Source URL, Applies to and Checked; it writes the skeleton first and fills it with Edit. Under 15,000 words, one more "expand" session adds depth from the lanes. Then one sonnet fact-check session per lane (five in parallel) opens the sources, fixes the brief in place and appends its findings to `intel/<topic>/brief-check.md`. The script prints the word count, each section's presence and size and the check totals; `--resume` keeps an existing brief (only expand and check), otherwise the old brief is moved to `build/runs/v2/brief/`. A full re-verification of a finished brief (the spec's `reverify.sh`) is `scripts/v3/brief.sh <topic> --resume`: no synthesis, the five lane checks again, findings appended.
3. Readers: `scripts/v3/readers.sh <topic> [--model opus] [--min-readers 10]`. One session reads the brief and the demand rows and writes `intel/<topic>/readers.md`: one `## Reader: <name>` section per reader type with a `Key:` line and the six fields (Situation, Questions in their words, Decisions with numbers, Timeline, Mistakes and cost, Brief facts that matter). Prints the count; fewer than 10 is a failure.
4. Plan: `scripts/v3/plan.sh <topic> [--model opus] [--limit-spokes 30]`. One session writes `intel/<topic>/plan.md`: a table `kind | slug | title | question | primary_query | type | readers | brief_sections | notes` with exactly one pillar, 10 to 30 spokes, reader guides, tools, datasets, start-here paths and rewrites of this topic's existing articles (an article under another topic's dir is that topic's rewrite; slugs are unique across the site). The script adds a row per planned page to `queue/queue.csv` (id `q-<dir>-NNNN`, question = the title, verdict `plan`, angle `kind=...; slug=...; readers=...; type=...`, status `proposed`): one row per slug and topic, ever (a slug with any earlier plan row, rejected or published included, is skipped and the row named; a rejected page is retried with `queue.py set-status <id> approved --force`), tools, datasets and start pages already on disk are skipped too, and it prints the summary Ajay reads.
5. Approve: Ajay reads `intel/<topic>/plan.md` (about ten minutes), fixes rows by hand if needed (edit a line, delete a row), then `scripts/v3/plan.sh <topic> --approve`: no new session; proposed rows are synced from their edited plan lines, rows removed from the plan are left proposed and named, and the rows in the plan (plus `requeue_v2.py`'s rewrite rows for the topic) flip to `approved`.
6. Write and edit: `scripts/v3/write.sh <topic>` (one opus session per approved plan row writes and edits the article from the brief, the reader map and the plan row; pillars and reader guides get two drafts and a pick-best read; every 10th article gets a cold editor read; tools and datasets are built from their rows too). 7. Check: `scripts/v3/check.sh <topic>` (sonnet; only facts not in the brief, plus our own math; `content/checks/<slug>.json`). 8. Publish: `scripts/v2/publish.sh` (lint, render, deploy; `reports/to-read.md` for Ajay's spot check). `scripts/v3/run.sh <topic>` loops write, fix, check and publish until the topic has no approved rows left. `scripts/v3/requeue_v2.py <topic> [--approve]` queues the topic's published v2 pages as rewrites (spec: every v2 page goes through steps 5 to 7). Start-here pages (`content/start/<reader>.md`) have no build step yet: their plan rows are queued and skipped by `write.sh`, and the pages are written by hand from the plan's notes until a step exists. Never run `scripts/v2/write.sh` on plan rows.

Every step: `--model` and `--workers` as flags, log in `build/runs/v2/<step>-<ts>.log`, prompts sent in `build/runs/v2/prompts/`, session results in `build/runs/v2/sessions/`; stop cleanly with `scripts/v2/stop.sh` (`.run/STOP`), and a usage limit pauses and resumes on its own (`scripts/v2/lib/sessions.py`).
Prompts: `scripts/v3/prompts/*.md`. Quality rules live there, in `docs/v2/style.md`, `docs/v2/exemplar.md` and the briefs; never in code.
Notes: `brief.py` lifts the Edit tool for its own sessions only (sessions.py disallows Edit for every v2 step); its allowed-tools rule names the topic's intel folder, but the repo's `.claude/settings.json` allows Edit everywhere, so the prompts (not the rule) are what keep edits inside `intel/`. `plan.py` extends queue.py's topic list from `topics.yml` at run time (dining, transfer, health). The plan's question, brief_sections and notes columns stay in `plan.md`, where the writer reads them by slug. A reader section the session wrote without a `Key:` line gets one from its name (`readers.sh` adds it and says so).
Tests (no real sessions): point `CLAUDE_BIN` at a stub that writes the expected files and run each step with `--intel-dir <tmp>/intel` (and `plan.sh --queue <tmp>/queue.csv --content-dir <tmp>/content`).
