Waiver rules (https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/keep-your-own-insurance/can-you-opt-out-shp; no date):
- Domestic degree-seeking students at Ithaca, AgriTech or Cornell Tech may opt out with coverage meeting the criteria (criteria on a separate page, not read).
- International students generally cannot waive (exception: US-based employer or embassy plan). PhD students generally cannot waive (exception if program does not fund SHP and they have other qualifying insurance). Non-PhD grad student workers whose funding includes SHP cannot waive.
- International waivers must not be county-limited, must include out-of-network, not be a reimbursable plan, and cover repatriation of remains.
- Deadline July 31 for exception requests (international US-employer/embassy, PhD unfunded proof, studying-away students). General waiver deadline: not on this page (see "Waiver/Opt-Out Deadlines" page, not read).
- Contact: studentbenefits@cornell.edu, (607) 255-6363, business days 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, closed 1:00 to 2:00 PM; Zoom office hours Wednesdays 12:00 to 1:00 PM.

## 12. City Bucks (off-campus food spending with the Cornell ID)
Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks (FETCHED 2026-11-06)
- Registered students automatically have an account; add money at get.cbord.com/cornell. Ithacatogo.com now accepts City Bucks.
- Balance carries semester to semester while registered (subject to cancellation/leave refund terms). Usable for food for you and guests at participating off-campus businesses; 21+ may buy alcohol; subject to NYS sales tax; not for tobacco or lottery; disputes to vendor within two weeks with receipt.
- Participating grocery stores on the page: Greenstar Natural Foods Market, 307 College Avenue, 607-882-2667; P&C Fresh, 315 Pine Tree Road, 607-272-5836; Tops Friendly Markets, 2300 Triphammer Road, 607-257-4952; 7-Eleven, 409 College Ave, 607-256-9672. Restaurants include D.P. Dough 408 College Ave, Papa John's 435 Franklin St, Five Guys 222 Elmira Rd, McDonald's (372 Elmira Rd and 2350 N Triphammer Rd), Monks on the Commons, Texas Roadhouse, Auntie Anne's, Wings Over Ithaca 121 Dryden Rd. Wegmans and Aldi are NOT on the list.
- Note: the meal-plan page's Collegetown plan includes $436 City Bucks per semester.
