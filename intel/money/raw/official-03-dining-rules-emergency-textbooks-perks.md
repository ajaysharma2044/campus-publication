## 18. Graduate School perks and discounts page
Source: https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/student-experience/student-perks-and-discounts/ (published June 6, 2018; last modified Oct 26, 2026 per page metadata). No dollar amounts on the page. Items (all as described by the page):
- Free: computing/data workshops, IT training, library workshops, Big Red Barn events (free with snacks and coffee, discounted beer/wine), Johnson Museum admission, equipment loans from Cornell Recreation, lap swimming at Helen Newman and Teagle, outdoor courts, Botanic Garden trails.
- Discounted: Cornell Music, Schwartz Center tickets, Cornell Cinema, Cinemapolis, men's hockey student tickets, Big Red Sports Pass (unlimited sports except men's hockey, https://www.bigredtix.com/), Outdoor Education classes, Lindseth Climbing Center, Cornell Store tech and cell products (three Ithaca locations), Campus to Campus bus (https://fcs.cornell.edu/content/c2c-rates), Short Line bus to NYC/NJ/Long Island discounted weekend fare, skating at Lynah Rink or Cass Park for City/Town of Ithaca residents.
- TCAT: page says students get unlimited rides on all TCAT buses in their first year; in later years rides after 6 p.m. weekdays and all day weekends with CU ID at the farebox; doctoral students and graduate assistants eligible for free Omniride passes (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-omniride-passes). **Needs verification against TCAT and Transportation pages** (see section on transit).
- Funding: Research Travel and Conference Grants (http://gradschool.cornell.edu/costs-funding/travel-funding-opportunities). Personal finance links: GradSense, CashCourse. External: StudentRate.com.
