## 17. Financial aid appeals
Source: https://www.finaid.cornell.edu/appeal-application (read 2026-11-08; no page date)
| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| What it is | request to reconsider CSS Profile/FAFSA information used to set expected family contribution |
| Accepted reasons (page) | errors on application (income, assets, family size); big family changes (parental abandonment/estrangement, death or incarceration of parent, trafficking, refugee/asylee status); circumstances beyond family control (involuntary job loss, large unreimbursed medical costs, one-time emergency income/expenses, unreimbursed disaster costs) |
| Usually denied (page) | voluntary income reduction, recent divorce/separation, consumer debt, discretionary spending, anticipated or very recent job loss, one-time income; cannot seek merit aid, negotiate, or dispute methodology |
| Documentation | required for each circumstance; incomplete appeals cannot be considered; no document list on page |
| Order of steps | fix application errors in CSS/FAFSA and email finaid first (may not need appeal); otherwise submit requested documents, then appeal after preliminary estimate or offer |
| Deadline | none stated |
| Review | in order received; typical response 10 to 15 business days |
| Outcome | approval lowers EFC and may raise grant/scholarship; may not add grant aid; denial possible |
| First-years/transfers | ask for deposit extension through the Admission Portal if the decision depends on the appeal |
| Contact | 203 Day Hall; finaid@cornell.edu; (607) 255-5145 |
Demand file also lists the form PDF https://finaid.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/2025-12/Financial-Aid-Appeal.pdf (not opened).

## 18. Emergency money

| Fund | Who | Covers | Max | How |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CU Emergency Fund (FASE) | enrolled students, urgent unanticipated costs | costs above COA: shipping, moving, storage, some tech (not laptop/tablet purchases) | typically $500 per academic year; priority to high need | application form; cuemergencyfund@cornell.edu |
| Laptop help | undergrads | laptops via separate loans, Undergraduate Student Appeal Form with invoice/receipt | generally up to $1,250 per device | same page |
| Students Helping Students (SHS) | registered undergrads with need | emergency travel, fire/disaster living costs, stolen goods, uncovered emergency medical/dental | not stated | counselor in 203 Day Hall or finaid@cornell.edu; anonymous review by Student Assembly Financial Aid Review Committee |
| Renewal Fee Emergency Funds | enrolled students renewing status (international) | employment authorization and biometric renewal fees | up to $495; may add Syracuse field-office travel; no repayment | application page; (607) 255-5145 |
