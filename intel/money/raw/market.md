Off-campus budget page: https://finaid.cornell.edu/special-circumstances/living-campus (checked 2026-11-08)
- No dollar amounts on the page (and no academic year). Refers to an "off campus housing and dining budget" and points to the Off-Campus Housing Office and finaid@cornell.edu, (607) 255-5145.
- Rules (paraphrased): need is calculated the same as on campus; Bursar doesn't bill off-campus housing so you pay the landlord; aid cannot cover security deposits, first/last month rent, or rent for months not enrolled; Family Contribution is unchanged when moving off campus; page advises counting trash fees, cable/phone/internet, utilities; Financial Aid won't intervene in breaking a lease or campus housing contract.
- Cornell Sun 2025-09 piece on COA topping $100k for some students (headline per demand file): https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/financial-gymnastics-cornell-s-cost-of-attendance-surpasses-100k-for-some-students-in-2025-2026 (NOT yet read).
