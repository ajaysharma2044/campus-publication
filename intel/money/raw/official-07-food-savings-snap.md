## 28. SNAP for college students (New York)
Source: web search results summarizing https://otda.ny.gov/programs/snap/ and https://otda.ny.gov/programs/snap/qanda.asp; the direct fetch of the FAQ failed with a connection error on 2026-11-08, so the pages themselves were NOT read. Treat as unverified.
- Students aged 18 to 49 enrolled at least half-time generally must meet an exemption; listed ones include working an average of 20 hours a week in paid employment, or participating in a state/federally financed work-study program during the regular school year (approved at time of application and anticipated to actually work), disability, single parent full-time with a child under 12, receiving TANF.
- The COVID-era exemptions ended for recertifications after June 30, 2023 (OTDA 23-ADM-09: https://otda.ny.gov/policy/directives/2023/ADM/23-ADM-09.pdf, not read).
- Income limit tables and application route: not found.
- Cornell Food Pantry page confirms SNAP participation is one way to qualify for the pantry.
