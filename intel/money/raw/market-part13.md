## 51. SNAP rules for college students (secondary/advocacy, not USDA or NY OTDA)
Search results only; none opened (blocked or not read). Conflicts: one source cites 20 hours/week for less-than-half-time students, another 30.
- Half-time or more students generally need a student exemption; H.R. 1 (2025) did not change the student rule, per sources summarized by Georgetown (https://feed.georgetown.edu/?p=9156) and Benefits USA (https://benefitsusa.org/en/blog/snap-college-student-exemption-2026-after-obbb).
- H.R. 1 raised the ABAWD work-requirement age cap from 54 to 64 and removed the homelessness exemption (same sources); NYC Food Policy Center says it also tightened "qualified work activity": https://www.nycfoodpolicy.org/how-snap-cuts-in-h-r-1-threaten-college-student-food-security/ (403 to my fetch).
- Introduced April 9, 2026: H.R. 8246 (OHH SNAP Act); not law (https://congress.wiki/map/legislation/ohh-snap-act-of-2026-119hr8246).
- Work-study counting and New York-specific rules: NOT FOUND (looked at the above plus Cornell Food Pantry page, which lists SNAP recipients as eligible there).
Note: this matters because the Cornell Food Pantry (200% FPL or SNAP etc., section 11), GreenStar FLOWER (13%, section 34) and Double Up all key off SNAP.
