## 25. Work-study detail
Source: https://finaid.cornell.edu/work-study (no date)
- Eligible undergraduate applicants with need are offered work-study funds, "typically $5,000 per academic year."
- Federal Work-Study pays 70% of the hourly wage for FWS-eligible positions; most Cornell student jobs are FWS-eligible; funding is limited, some eligible students get no offer.
- Paid through university payroll. All enrolled undergrads in good standing (no registration, bursar or other holds) may work student jobs with or without FWS. Cornell does not place students; students search and apply.
- Page does not give a summer or term "student contribution" expectation. (The Student Employment FWS page quotes "$5,000, funded by Cornell and FWS" as a standard work expectation: https://studentemployment.cornell.edu/federal-work-study-for-students.)
- Arithmetic using official numbers (computed here): $5,000 at the Level I minimum $16.00 is about 312 hours across an academic year, before tax; real take-home is lower after withholding.
