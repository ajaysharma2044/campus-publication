## C3. Two questions from my own searches
### Q29. Is the bus free for Cornell students (TCAT)? 
Top results (search order): cornellsun.com/2024/11/03 "non first-year Cornell students no longer able to access TCAT for free" (2024-11-03); sustainable.cornell.edu/focus-areas/transportation/bus-commuting; news.cornell.edu/node/327352 (TCAT and Cornell contract, date not shown); masstransitmag.com four-year contract press release; cornellsun.com/2022/10/24 "students rally for free TCAT". Says: first year free; later free evenings and weekends; OmniRide $200 per year. Misses: price on official page, how to buy at the time of year, who benefits from OmniRide. TYPICAL AI ANSWER (simulated): "Yes, Cornell students ride TCAT free with a student ID." Wrong: only first-years all day; others only evenings and weekends.
Gap: accurate "when is it free, when to buy the $200 pass, break-even math vs $1.50 cash fare" (verify current fare).

Top results: international.globallearning.cornell.edu us-income-tax-basics; file-your-return-sprintax; finance.cornell.edu/tax/foreignnationals; plus many other universities (Brandeis, CMU, Columbia TC).
