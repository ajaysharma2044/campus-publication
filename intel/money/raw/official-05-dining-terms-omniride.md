**Contradictions inside Cornell Dining's own pages (flag for writers):**
1. Terms page says "Pricing has not yet been finalized for the 2026-2027 term" while the plans page lists 2026-27 prices (page fetched same day, 2026-11-06). Treat the plans page as current; the sentence is leftover wording.
2. BRB last-use date: undergraduate plans page "May 27, 2027"; graduate plans page "May 31, 2027"; terms page "noon Monday, May 31, 2027". The spring meal program ends with dinner Friday May 28, 2027.
3. Who may not cancel: FAQ page says "First-year and transfer students"; terms page says "First-year, sophomore, and transfer students".
4. Terms page says collegetown debit plans excluded for Campus Life housing; plans page says debit plans for those not in on-campus housing and not in another plan. Same meaning.
