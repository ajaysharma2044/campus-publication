## 2. Saving tips (Sun "Cheapskate's Guide", same URL, 2024-04-22; one author's advice)
- Textbooks: check syllabi on Canvas early, search the library, request via BorrowDirect (free delivery/pickup). Author's own estimate: about $450/yr at the Cornell Store for ~10 titles a semester.
- Zeus cafe: iced coffee cheaper in a reusable cup; free hot-water tap if you bring tea bags; author says to ask kitchen staff about leftovers near closing (unverified as policy).
- Listservs for catered events (free food); paid research studies, often above minimum wage; Dump & Run at O-week for free household items; ask for financial help.

## 9. International students: banking, SSN (official page snippets; for lane A)
- Cornell Global Learning says an SSN is not needed to open a bank account, rent or get a credit card; lists Ithaca Community Bank and M&T (no SSN), Tompkins Trust student account (up to age 25), CFCU debit/online without SSN or ITIN; Bank of America needs in-person opening, nearest branch Syracuse (per search summary).
