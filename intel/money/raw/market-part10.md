## 40. Graduate meal plans (same price as undergrad, plus a cheap one)
Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/graduate-meal-plans
- Traditional plans open to grad/professional students at the same 2026-27 prices as section 1 (Unlimited $3,802/sem, Bear Traditional $3,528, Bear Choice $3,075, Bear Basic $2,489; debit: Off-Campus Value $912.30, Flex 10/500 $674, Just Bucks $500).
- Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan: $50 per semester, includes $50 Big Red Bucks and a 5% discount at the Atrium Cafe, Big Red Barn, Bear Necessities and Vet Med Cafe; no admin fee (other plans carry the $50 fee). Must enroll in a plan before adding BRB funds. BRB balance must be used by May 31, 2027 (note: the undergrad page says May 27, 2027; the two official pages differ by four days).
- Special Shabbat/holiday meals at 104West may cost extra; reservations at kosher.scl.cornell.edu.
Conflict noted: BRB expiry May 27, 2027 (undergraduate page) vs May 31, 2027 (graduate page).

## 43. City Bucks FAQ details
Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/city-bucks/city-bucks-faqs
- Off-campus only; taxable purchases (NY sales tax applies, unlike BRB which is tax-free on campus).
- Bursar billing for City Bucks is disabled if more than $500 of Bursar-billed deposits in 30 days.
- Parents can fund by credit card at get.cbord.com/cornell.
- Balance must cover the whole purchase or the card is declined (no split with another payment).
- Graduating seniors: balances of $35 or more are refunded if they cancel before last day of spring classes.
- Contact citybucks@cornell.edu; lost ID line 607-255-8582 (8 to 4:30 M to F).
- Differences from section 31 page: that page said no set amount; both agree on carry-over while registered.
