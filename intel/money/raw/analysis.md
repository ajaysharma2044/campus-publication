## Analysis 1: Effective price of one dining-room meal on each Cornell plan

**Question.** What does one swipe really cost on each plan, and how does it change with how often a student eats in the dining rooms?

**Method.** Net plan price = semester price minus Big Red Bucks (BRB) included. BRBs are valued at face value ($1 each per the dining terms, https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2027-2028, line stating each BRB equals $1), which is the most generous treatment because BRBs only work at Cornell Dining and any balance left on May 27, 2027 is lost. Cost per meal = net price divided by meals. The $50 yearly fee is left out of per-meal numbers and added in Analysis 2.

**1a. Fixed meal counts (no calendar assumption needed).**

| Plan | Price/sem | BRB/sem | Net | Meals/sem | Net per meal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SFL Supplemental | 2,167.51 | 500 | 1,667.51 | 127 | 1,667.51 / 127 = 13.13 |
| Off-Campus Value | 912.30 | 150 | 762.30 | 45 | 762.30 / 45 = 16.94 |
| Flex 10/500 | 674 | 500 | 174 | 10 | 174 / 10 = 17.40 |

Check: 13.13 x 127 = 1,667.5; 16.94 x 45 = 762.3; 17.40 x 10 = 174.

**1b. Plans counted "up to N per week" (needs weeks per semester).**

Calendar proxy: official fall 2025 calendar (2026-27 not found), Aug 25 to Dec 20.
- Days Aug 25 to Dec 20 inclusive: Aug 25-31 = 7, Sep = 30, Oct = 31, Nov = 30, Dec 1-20 = 20; total 118 days; 118 / 7 = 16.86 weeks (includes study period, exams, fall break and Thanksgiving, when dining rooms may be closed or reduced; not checked).
- Instruction only, Aug 25 to Dec 8: 7 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 8 = 106 days = 15.14 weeks.
- I therefore show two columns: 15 weeks (instruction) and 16.86 weeks (whole term). The truth for a given student sits between or below, depending on break closures. This is an estimate, not a measured count of open service days.

Cost per meal if the student uses every meal the plan allows:

| Plan | Net/sem | Max meals/wk | Meals at 15 wk | $/meal at 15 wk | Meals at 16.86 wk | $/meal at 16.86 wk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear Basic | 2,489 - 500 = 1,989 | 7 | 105 | 1,989 / 105 = 18.94 | 118.0 | 1,989 / 118.0 = 16.86 |
| Bear Choice | 3,075 - 500 = 2,575 | 10 | 150 | 2,575 / 150 = 17.17 | 168.6 | 2,575 / 168.6 = 15.27 |
| Bear Traditional | 3,528 - 400 = 3,128 | 14 | 210 | 3,128 / 210 = 14.90 | 236.0 | 3,128 / 236.0 = 13.25 |
| Unlimited (3 meals x 7 days = 21/wk maximum) | 3,802 - 400 = 3,402 | 21 | 315 | 3,402 / 315 = 10.80 | 354.1 | 3,402 / 354.1 = 9.61 |

Unlimited by how often the student actually eats in dining rooms (16.86-week basis; at 15 weeks multiply by 16.86/15 = 1.124):
- 14 meals/wk (2 a day): 3,402 / (14 x 16.86 = 236.0) = 14.41
- 10 meals/wk: 3,402 / 168.6 = 20.18
- 7 meals/wk: 3,402 / 118.0 = 28.83

**Result.** Among fixed-count plans, effective cost is 13.13 to 17.40 per meal (3 plans). On weekly-count plans, a student using every allowed meal pays about 13 to 19 per meal on the Bear plans and about 10 to 11 on Unlimited; a student using only 7 a week on Unlimited pays about 29 per meal. Sample: 8 plans, 2026-27 prices, calendar proxy from 2025-26.

**Caveats.** Meals per week can include dining rooms that close on weekends or breaks; "designated service periods" apply. The 21 a week maximum assumes three meals per day, 7 days, which is an assumption (the page says "all meals during designated service periods"). The Sun and other outlets may have published their own per-swipe math; not checked in this lane.

## Analysis 2: Unlimited vs. cheaper plans, and the yearly cost of food off campus

**Question.** For an upper-level student off campus, what does each food route cost for the year, and where does Unlimited stop making sense?

**Method.** Yearly cost = 2 x semester price + $50 fee (plans), or semester price x 2 for debit plans (fee also applies to "any of these meal plans", the page does not exclude debit plans, so I add it for every option). Break-even uses the net-of-BRB difference and the 16.86-week proxy.

| Option | Calculation | Yearly cost (incl. $50 fee) | Meals/yr included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited | 7,604 + 50 | 7,654 | unlimited |
| Bear Traditional | 7,056 + 50 | 7,106 | up to 14/wk |
| Bear Choice (off campus) | 6,150 + 50 | 6,200 | up to 10/wk |
| Bear Basic (off campus) | 4,978 + 50 | 5,028 | up to 7/wk |
| Off-Campus Value | 1,824.60 + 50 | 1,874.60 | 90 (45 x 2) |
| Flex 10/500 | 1,348 + 50 | 1,398 | 20 |
| Just Bucks | 1,000 + 50 | 1,050 | 0 (food money only) |

Break-even, Unlimited vs Bear Basic per semester: net difference = 3,402 - 1,989 = 1,413. Unlimited adds up to (21 - 7) x 16.86 = 236 extra swipes, so if all were used, the extra swipes cost 1,413 / 236 = 5.99 each. If the student really eats 14 a week (extra 7 a week = 118 extra swipes), the extra swipes cost 1,413 / 118 = 11.97 each. Rule of thumb for a fact checker: Unlimited beats Bear Basic only if the student would otherwise spend more than about 12 dollars per extra meal AND eats 14 a week in dining rooms.

Break-even, Unlimited vs Off-Campus Value per semester: 3,402 - 762.30 = 2,639.70. Unlimited gives 354 - 45 = 309 extra swipes at the 16.86-week maximum: 2,639.70 / 309 = 8.54 each at full use of all 21 meals/week; at 14 a week the extra is 236 - 45 = 191 swipes: 2,639.70 / 191 = 13.82 each.

**Result.** The yearly range for Cornell Dining routes is $1,050 (Just Bucks) to $7,654 (Unlimited), a spread of 7,654 - 1,050 = $6,604. The cost-of-attendance page assumes the full Unlimited plan ($7,604) as "Food", so a student who chooses a smaller plan or cooks will be below the official budget for food. Grocery cost to compare against is NOT measured in this pass (see Gaps).

**Caveats.** Taking Bear Basic or Choice off campus is allowed for all off-campus students; on campus only for students enrolled before Summer 2021, which is now almost nobody (current first-years and second-years must carry Unlimited-type plans; the exact rule for juniors and seniors on campus not re-checked). Estimates depend on the calendar proxy.

## Analysis 5: What a campus job could add (arithmetic, not a measured earning)

**Question.** How much could a student earn at the posted 2026 wage scale for a plausible schedule?

**Method.** Earnings = hours per week x weeks x hourly rate. Hours and weeks are assumptions chosen to show the range; the student-employment page names no maximum hours.

Weeks worked: instruction weeks proxy of 15 per semester (calendar above) = 30 weeks per academic year (estimate).

| Level | Rate (min) | Rate (max) | 8 hrs/wk x 30 wk = 240 hrs: min | max | 12 hrs/wk x 30 = 360 hrs: min | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 16.00 | 17.75 | 240 x 16.00 = 3,840 | 240 x 17.75 = 4,260 | 360 x 16.00 = 5,760 | 360 x 17.75 = 6,390 |
| II | 16.25 | 19.05 | 3,900 | 4,572 | 5,850 | 6,858 |
| III | 16.85 | 20.60 | 4,044 | 4,944 | 6,066 | 7,416 |
| IV | 18.00 | 25.75 | 4,320 | 6,180 | 6,480 | 9,270 |

Check examples: 240 x 16.25 = 3,900; 240 x 19.05 = 4,572; 360 x 16.85 = 6,066; 360 x 25.75 = 9,270.
