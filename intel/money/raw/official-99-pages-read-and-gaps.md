## Contradictions and changes to flag
1. 2026-27 cost of attendance: both finaid COA URLs show empty 2026-27 sections; only 2025-26 ($99,734 endowed; $75,604 NY resident at a contract college) was available on 2026-11-08. Do not state a 2026-27 total.
2. BRB last-use date: May 27, 2027 (undergrad plans page) vs May 31, 2027 (grad plans page) vs noon Monday May 31, 2027 (terms page).
3. Who cannot cancel a meal plan: first-year and transfer (FAQ) vs first-year, sophomore and transfer (terms).
4. Dining terms say 2026-27 pricing "not yet finalized" while the plans page shows prices.
5. Night-route access for partners: routes 90/92/93 (sustainability page) vs 90 and 92 (OmniRide page).
6. SHP page lists spring-2027 new-student coverage start as "1/1/2026" (probable typo for 1/1/2027).
7. Bursar paying-your-bill page lists no card fee; installment plan page states a 2.85% / 4.25% Cashnet fee on cards.
8. Cornell Tech page (© 2022) mentions a 10-hour recommendation for master's students; International Services page (fetched 2026-11-06) states 20-hour maximum only.
9. Grad perks page says TCAT free in first year and after 6 p.m. weekdays/weekends afterwards; matches the two Cornell transportation pages. Student Assembly resolution SA R69 (2025-26) shows ongoing pressure to expand it (search snippet only).
10. Changed from previous year: grad stipend 9-month minimum rose from $35,661 (2025-26) to $36,684 (2026-27), +$1,023 (2.9%, computed here). 12-month: $47,548 to $48,912. Cornell Tech: $59,029 to $60,719.
