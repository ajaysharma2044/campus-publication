## 9. Federal Work-Study
Source: https://studentemployment.cornell.edu/federal-work-study (page got via fetch; many details missing)
- FWS pays 70% of the wage with the employer covering 30% (page); take-home is the same regardless of funding type; no award amounts, hour caps, pay schedule or deadlines on the page. Cornell Work-Study is offered in an aid package alongside or instead of FWS. Checked 2026-11-08.
