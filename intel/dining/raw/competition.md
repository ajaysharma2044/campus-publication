**CONTRADICTIONS between two official Cornell pages (record for the brief):**
1. Terms page (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms/cornell-dining-terms-and-conditions-2026-2027) says "pricing for 2026-2027 has not been finalized", while the plans page (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans) titled "2026-2027 Undergraduate Meal Plans" lists specific prices. A search-tool summary also surfaced older 2025-26 figures ($3,664 Unlimited/semester; Collegetown $3,400, 10 meals, $500 BRB, $436 City Bucks) which are exactly what third-party guides still quote. So 2026-27 Unlimited rose $3,664 to $3,802 (+$138, about 3.8%) and Collegetown $3,400 to $3,528 (+$128, about 3.8%) if the 2025-26 figures are right (2025-26 figures came from the search summary, not a page I read; verify before use).
2. Last day to use BRBs: plans page says May 27, 2027; terms page says Mon May 31, 2027 (noon for debit plans). Spring meals end May 28, 2027 per terms page.
3. The terms page says all 2026-27 on-campus undergrads who entered Fall 2021 or later must have a plan; plans page says Bear Choice/Basic are for on-campus undergrads enrolled before Summer 2021 and all off-campus students. Consistent, but a reader skimming sees "Collegetown plan is required" vs "Collegetown plan cannot be cancelled" differently from the terms page's "automatically enrolled in the Unlimited plan" for South Campus. Flag: terms page still says "South Campus Meal Plan", plans page says "Collegetown (formerly South Campus)".
