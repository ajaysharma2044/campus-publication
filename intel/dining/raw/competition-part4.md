3. part 2, Q2: the line about weeks ("need to check weeks") is unfinished. The correct approach: count serving weeks from the terms page dates. Fall: Mon Aug 17 2026 lunch to Sun Dec 20 2026 brunch is 18 weeks (126 days after Aug 17 to Dec 20 inclusive is 126 days = 18 weeks, my arithmetic). Spring: Tue Jan 19 2027 lunch to Fri May 28 2027 dinner is about 19 weeks (130 days). Total about 37 weeks (my arithmetic, verify before publication; the plan week resets Thursdays, and breaks inside the term, e.g. fall break and spring break, may reduce usable weeks, which no page quantifies). Rough per-week cost on Unlimited: $7,604 / 37 about $205 per week; Bear Basic $4,978 / 37 about $135 per week for at most 7 meals, or about $19 per meal if all 7 are used (arithmetic only; meal count and weeks need confirming). Do not publish these without the week count verified.
4. part 2, Q20 wrote Wegmans as possibly 24 hours; part 3 resolved: the official Wegmans page says 6 AM to midnight daily.
5. part 3, Q27: "The Sun 2025 piece conflicts with Cornell's own claim" is my framing; the Sun piece is one student's opinion. Keep it attributed.
