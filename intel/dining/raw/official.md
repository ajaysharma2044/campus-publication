## 2. Undergraduate meal plan prices, 2026-27

All from https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans (corpus, applies to 2026-27, checked 2026-11-08). Dollar amounts are per semester / per year.

| Plan | Price | Swipes | Big Red Bucks per semester | Bonus guest meals per semester | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited | $3,802 / $7,604 | All meals in service periods at the ten residential dining rooms | $400 | 8 | Required for first-years |
| Bear Traditional | $3,528 / $7,056 | Up to 14 per week | $400 | 4 | Upper-level, transfer |
| Bear Choice | $3,075 / $6,150 | Up to 10 per week | $500 | 4 | On-campus students enrolled before summer 2021; all off-campus students |
| Bear Basic | $2,489 / $4,978 | Up to 7 per week | $500 | 4 | Same as Bear Choice |
| House Meal Plan | $3,802 / $7,604 | All meals at the ten dining rooms | $400 | 8 | Required for WCHS |
| House Affiliate | $724 / $1,448 | 3 per week at any West Campus dining room | $100 | not stated | Off-campus students who lived at least one semester in WCHS; apply by emailing dining@cornell.edu with subject "House Affiliate meal plan" with name, NetID, Cornell ID card number |
| Collegetown Meal Plan (formerly South Campus) | $3,528 / $7,056 | Up to 10 per week | $500 BRB plus $436 City Bucks | 4 | Residents of 112 Edgemoor, Cascadilla Hall, Schuyler House, Sheldon Court, 301 College Ave, 104 Mary Ann Wood Dr; apply at the Housing and Dining Portal |
| SFL Supplemental (Bear Supplemental) | $2,167.51 / $4,335.02 | 127 meals per semester | $500 | not stated | Sorority & Fraternity Life members off campus; on campus only if chapter requires a plan of at least 7 meals/week for out-of-house members |

Debit plans (same page): only for students not living in on-campus housing and not on another plan. You must enroll in a plan before adding funds to BRB.

| Debit plan | Price | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Off-Campus Value | $912.30 / $1,824.60 | 45 meals per semester, $150 BRB, 4 bonus meals |
| Flex 10/500 | $674 / $1,348 | 10 meals per semester, $500 BRB |

Note: "Unlimited plan is fully covered by financial aid for eligible students" is stated on the same page (source above). Specific aid rule not given on that page; see aid pages later in this file if found.

Note: extra charges may apply for some Shabbat, holiday and special meals at 104West!; reservations at kosher.scl.cornell.edu.
