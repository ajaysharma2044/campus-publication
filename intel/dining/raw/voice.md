| S1 | "Meal swipes belong on central campus" (opinion; Maia Mehring, Sun Lifestyle staff writer, ILR student) | 2025-02-25 | https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/02/meal-swipes-belong-on-central-campus | Author says meal-plan students pay roughly $4,896 to $7,132 a year yet have few convenient swipe-accepting meals on Central Campus. Author says undergrad plans carry about 400 to 500 BRB per semester that can run out by mid-semester. Notes a $15.50 lunch door price for non-plan guests (Mon to Sat). | S2 | "Cornell's abandonment of vegetarian students and where they can eat instead" (opinion column; a vegetarian junior) | 2025-03-10 | https://cornellsun.com/2025/03/10/cornells-abandonment-of-vegetarian-students-and-where-they-can-eat-instead | Author says Morrison and North Star (Appel) options were repetitive, West Campus breakfast/lunch often plain vegetables or rice and broccoli, tofu bland. Says mandatory unlimited plan has no reduced or flexible option for dietary restrictions and that feedback to the head chef changed nothing. Cites $7,132/yr plan and about $400 extra on delivery and local meals. Recommends Moosewood (cross-contamination-safe, vegan), Thompson and Bleecker, Dos Amigos (late hours), Koko, Zocalo (about 20 min by bus), Halal Brothers (open to 2 a.m. weekends, 10 p.m. weekdays per author), Sangam, Plum Tree. Tips: ask restaurants to modify dishes, check hours before late trips. Hours are the author's claims; verify with the venues. |

Adams, A&S junior; co-signed by Basic Needs Coalition, Friendship Donations Network, Cornell Hunger Relief) | 2023-05-09 | https://cornellsun.com/2023/05/09/guest-room-dear-cornell-youre-breaking-the-law-and-students-are-going-hungry | Authors argue Cornell Dining wastes edible food and is not following NYS food donation/scrap recycling law (effective 2022-01-01 for large generators). Claim: Cornell removed the food insecurity question from the 2023 survey. Describe dining as an "enterprise service" earning money. Claims first-years and West Campus residents are placed on the unlimited plan. |
