## Part 9. Derived numbers (mine; arithmetic only, from market.md Parts 1 and 3)

Per-swipe cost, assuming a 15-week fall term is NOT verified (term length and weeks not read from an official source); so I record only plan facts that need no assumption:
- Unlimited $7,604 per year includes $800 of BRB per year ($400 x 2) so the swipes component is $6,804 for the year (7,604 minus 800), before the $50 fee. Official BRB value: 1 BRB = $1.
- Flex 10/500: $674 per semester for 10 swipes + $500 BRB; the swipe portion is $174 per semester, about $17.40 per swipe (674 minus 500, divided by 10).
- Off-Campus Value: $912.30 - $150 = $762.30 for 45 swipes = about $16.94 per swipe.
- Compare BRB dinner price $18.85 and lunch $15.25 (Part 3). These are my calculations; label as "derived" if used.
- Just Bucks: $500 per semester = $500 BRB, no premium paid (price equals value) apart from the $50 yearly fee.
- Collegetown plan includes $436 City Bucks per semester (an official number), usable at off-campus partners (taxable, per City Bucks terms).
