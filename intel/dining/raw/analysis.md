## Analysis 1. What does one meal cost on each plan?

**Question.** A student asks "is the Unlimited plan worth it, and what does a swipe really cost on the smaller plans?"

**Method.**
1. Net plan cost per semester = semester price, minus BRB (valued at $1 each, which assumes every BRB is spent), plus half the $50 fee ($25).
2. Swipes per semester = weekly cap times weeks W. For Off-Campus Value and Flex the swipes are stated per semester.
3. Cost per swipe = net plan cost divided by swipes, assuming every swipe is used.
4. W is not published as a number. I use two values to bracket it. W = 18 is the spring length (below). W = 15 is my low assumption for a shorter fall; this is an assumption, not a Cornell figure.

Spring length: Jan 19 to May 28, 2027 inclusive = 13 (Jan 19-31) + 28 (Feb) + 31 (Mar) + 30 (Apr) + 28 (May 1-28) = 130 days = 18.6 weeks. I rounded down to 18.

**Data and calculation.**

| Plan | Net cost/semester (price - BRB + $25) | Swipes at W=18 | $/swipe W=18 | Swipes at W=15 | $/swipe W=15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear Traditional (14/wk) | 3,528 - 400 + 25 = 3,153 | 252 | 12.51 | 210 | 15.01 |
| Bear Choice (10/wk) | 3,075 - 500 + 25 = 2,600 | 180 | 14.44 | 150 | 17.33 |
| Bear Basic (7/wk) | 2,489 - 500 + 25 = 2,014 | 126 | 15.98 | 105 | 19.18 |
| Off-Campus Value (45/sem) | 912.30 - 150 + 25 = 787.30 | 45 | 17.50 | 45 | 17.50 |
| Flex 10/500 (10/sem) | 674 - 500 + 25 = 199 | 10 | 19.90 | 10 | 19.90 |
| Unlimited | 3,802 - 400 + 25 = 3,427 | depends on use | see Analysis 2 | | |

Check: 3,153 / 252 = 12.51; 2,600 / 180 = 14.44; 2,014 / 126 = 15.98; 787.30 / 45 = 17.50; 199 / 10 = 19.90.

**Result.** If a student uses every swipe, the weekly-cap plans cost about $12.50 to $16.00 per swipe over an 18-week term, and the debit plans cost $17.50 to $19.90. Sample size: 6 plans, one academic year (2026-27), 2026-11-08.

**Caveats.**
- "Every swipe used" is the best case. Unused weekly meals are lost (FAQ), so real cost per swipe used is higher.
- BRBs at $1 is also the best case. Unspent BRBs expire at the end of spring and are non-refundable.
- I did not verify whether a swipe is worth the same at breakfast, lunch and dinner, or what service periods count. The plan page says "designated service periods" only. Hours are in other lanes.
- Guest meals (4 or 8) are not given a dollar value here.
- Whether the fee is $50 per year spread over two semesters is my reading ("once each academic year"). Billing of the fee by semester not confirmed.

## Analysis 2. How many meals a week do you need to eat on Unlimited to beat paying per meal?

**Question.** At what weekly usage does Unlimited cost less than buying the same meals in a dining room?

**Method.**
1. Build a typical week at the BRB price list. Assumption: a student eats breakfast, lunch and dinner Monday to Saturday and brunch plus dinner on Sunday (20 meals). This is an assumed pattern, not observed behavior. Dining-room meal availability on those days is from the price page (lunch Mon-Sat, Sunday brunch); I did not verify every room serves all of them.
2. Average meal price = weekly total / 20.
3. Break-even meals/week = Unlimited net weekly cost / average meal price.

**Calculation.**
- Week at BRB prices: 6 x (10.80 + 15.25 + 18.85) = 6 x 44.90 = 269.40. Sunday: 16.85 + 18.85 = 35.70. Week total = 305.10. Average = 305.10 / 20 = **$15.255 per meal**.
- Week at door prices (tax included): 6 x (11.90 + 16.80 + 20.80) = 6 x 49.50 = 297.00. Sunday: 18.55 + 20.80 = 39.35. Week total = 336.35. Average = 336.35 / 20 = **$16.82 per meal**.
- Unlimited net per semester = 3,427 (from Analysis 1). Weekly: 3,427 / 18 = 190.39; 3,427 / 15 = 228.47.
- Break-even at BRB prices: 190.39 / 15.255 = 12.48 meals/week (W=18); 228.47 / 15.255 = 14.98 (W=15).
- Break-even at door prices: 190.39 / 16.82 = 11.32 (W=18); 228.47 / 16.82 = 13.58 (W=15).

Effective cost per meal on Unlimited, by meals eaten per week:

| Meals/week | $/meal W=18 (3,427 / (18 x n)) | $/meal W=15 (3,427 / (15 x n)) |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 19.04 | 22.85 |
| 14 | 13.60 | 16.32 |
| 17 | 11.20 | 13.44 |
| 20 | 9.52 | 11.42 |

**Same test for the capped plans (W=18, BRB prices, $15.255 average):**

| Plan | Net weekly cost | Break-even meals/week | Cap | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear Traditional | 3,153 / 18 = 175.17 | 11.48 | 14 | beats a la carte if you eat 12+ of the 14 |
| Bear Choice | 2,600 / 18 = 144.44 | 9.47 | 10 | beats a la carte only if you use about 10 of 10 |
| Bear Basic | 2,014 / 18 = 111.89 | 7.33 | 7 | cost per swipe ($15.98) is above the average dining-room price ($15.26) even when all 7 are used |

**Result.** On these assumptions Unlimited beats paying per meal at about 12 to 13 meals a week (18-week term) at BRB prices, or about 11 to 12 at door prices; with a 15-week term the figure is 14 to 15 and 13 to 14. Bear Basic and Bear Choice save nothing against a la carte at the average meal; their value comes only if you skew to dinners ($18.85) or avoid skipped swipes. Sample: 4 meal prices, 1 academic year, computed 2026-11-08.

**Caveats.**
- The Unlimited plan also includes 8 guest meals per semester, worth up to 8 x $18.85 = $150.80 at BRB dinner prices if used for dinners (my multiplication). Not counted above, so Unlimited is slightly better than shown if a student uses guest meals.
- A 20-meal week is high. A student who skips breakfast (14 meals: 6 x 2 lunch/dinner + 2 Sunday) would average (6 x (15.25 + 18.85) + 35.70) / 14 = (204.60 + 35.70) / 14 = 240.30 / 14 = $17.16. Break-even at 18 weeks becomes 190.39 / 17.16 = 11.1 meals/week.
- Unlimited is required for first-years and cannot be changed (FAQ), so for them this is a cost-per-meal figure, not a choice.

## Analysis 3. Gap between fall and spring dining

**Question.** For how long is there no dining-room meal service over winter break?

**Method.** Count days between the last fall dining-room meal and first spring meal, from the winter break page.

**Calculation.** Last fall meal: brunch Sun Dec 20, 2026. First spring meal: lunch Tue Jan 19, 2027. Days from Dec 20 to Jan 19: 11 (Dec 21-31) + 19 (Jan 1-19) = 30 days after Dec 20, i.e. about 4.3 weeks. Dining closed fully Fri Dec 25, 2026 to Fri Jan 1, 2027 per the same page; cafes and stores open on some days for fall BRB use through Thursday Dec 24 and again Jan 4-19.

**Result.** About 30 days between the last fall dining-room meal and the first spring one (source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining, fetched 2026-11-06, applies winter 2026-27). Sample: 1 break.

**Caveats.** Applies to residents who stay over break. Housing availability over break is covered in other lanes. I did not check whether fall BRB leftovers can be used in the open cafes between Jan 4-19 at which specific locations (the page lists "open cafes").
