## 9. Meals on the Move (pre-order to-go meal for one swipe)

Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/meals-move (corpus, 2026-27)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| What | Pre-order a breakfast or lunch; costs one meal swipe, deducted on the morning of pickup |
| Order site | mealsonthemove.scl.cornell.edu |
| Order timing | Order by 10:30am the day before; from Friday afternoon you can order up to a week ahead |
| Days | Monday to Friday while classes are in session |
| Pickup | 7:30 to 10:30am at Crossings Cafe (Toni Morrison Hall, North), Rose House Dining Room (West), or Weill Hall Atrium (Central) |
| Eligible plans | Unlimited, Bear Traditional, Bear Choice, Bear Basic, House Meal Plan, SFL Supplemental, South Campus Meal Plan, Flex 10/500, Off Campus Value, Dining Student Employees Meal Plan |
| Menu | Breakfast bowls; lunch = entree (sandwich, salad or grain bowl) plus chips, whole fruit, optional water |
| Allergens | Labeled on boxes with a NetNutrition QR code; no customization in the ordering system; questions to diningnutrition@cornell.edu |
| Note | Page says "Crossings Cafe on North Campus" in the intro and "Flora Rose House Dining Room" in the FAQ for the same West pickup (Rose House) |

## 10. Dietary needs and allergies

Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/nutrition-special-diets/food-allergies-special-diets (corpus, page undated; mentions November 2025 certification)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Training | Culinary team and managers trained yearly with AllerTrain |
| Certification | FARECheck Silver (at least 90% of the workforce trained in food allergy safety), received as of November 2025 |
| Allergen labeling | All items in residential dining rooms labeled for milk, egg, fish, tree nuts, peanuts, shellfish, soy, sesame; also gluten and coconut; also labeled for alcohol and pork |
| Kosher | 104West! Multicultural Dining Room is Star K certified (also STAR-D dairy per the kosher page); mashgiach supervises; also accommodates Halal and Seventh-day Adventist diets |
| Dedicated Halal and Kosher stations | Morrison Dining, lunch and dinner daily (holidays may affect) |
| Halal on request | Keeton House Dining Room, dinner seven days a week and lunch and brunch Saturday and Sunday |
| Shabbat and holiday meals | Dinner times vary with sunset; reserve in advance at kosher.scl.cornell.edu (kosher page); extra charges may apply |
| Kosher grab-and-go | Sandwiches, wraps, salads made in the 104West! kitchen are in coolers at retail eateries |
| Vegan and vegetarian | Plant Forward options at all locations (Menus of Change partnership); labeled at stations; dedicated vegan fryers where possible; look for "shared oil" sign; retail items searchable on NetNutrition |
| Gluten-free | GF bread, cereals, desserts offered across campus; house-made foods |
| Gluten, peanut and tree-nut free | Risley Dining sources without gluten, peanuts and tree nuts (coconut allowed); AllerCheck approved by MenuTrinfo; Risley GF/PF/TNF grab-and-go sold in retail eateries with a purple seal |
| INDIGO Kitchen | "Individualized Dining on the Go": safe meals for students with allergies or medically diagnosed needs; run with Registered Dietitian Nutritionists |
| Who to contact | Dining Nutrition Office: 108 Appel Commons, (607) 255-9280, diningnutrition@cornell.edu |
| Disclaimer | Cornell says contamination risk exists and manufacturers can change formulas without notice |

Applies to: 2026-27. Checked 2026-11-08.
