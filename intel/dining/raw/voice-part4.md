| D1 | "Hidden meal plan hotspots on campus" (Sun lifestyle; Eirian Huang, Kira Walter) | 2024-09-18 (upd. 2025-03) | https://cornellsun.com/2024/09/18/hidden-meal-plan-hotspots-on-campus/ | After the Sept. 2 2024 strike ended, 33 dining units were back to normal. 104 West! (kosher hall by Center for Jewish Living; weekdays 11-2 and 5-7; takes swipes, BRBs, Cornell currency, credit; reviews cited call its food among campus's best). McCormick's at Moakley House (golf-course tavern; wings, poutine, burgers; weekdays 11-6, Sat to 5, Sun 9-2; BRBs). Big Red Barn (former carriage house, popular with grad students; hot-pressed sandwiches, soups; 8-3 daily; BRBs). Straight From The Market (Willard Straight; hot bar, salads; 11-6; takeout $8/lb; BRBs). Gimme! Coffee in Gates (weekdays 11-3; cash and credit only, no BRB). Says Starbucks' Cornell contract ends June 2025. Tip: hours vary, check first. |
| D2 | "Unlimited meal plan, unlimited fury" (Sun opinion; first-person West Campus sophomore) | 2019-11-20 | https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/20/unlimited-meal-plan-unlimited-fury | Forced unlimited house plan with housing; downhill walk between classes impractical; dry, bland breakfast (author: only Bethe and Becker serve hot breakfast); most halls close about 7:30-8 p.m.; one swipe about $16 vs $7-$10 elsewhere so eating out feels like wasting a swipe; $3,047/semester cited; $28.56 BRB left by midsemester; 10 or 7 meals/week alternatives mentioned. 2019 numbers, stale. |
| D3 | "Inocciduous thoughts: things I've noticed about food on West Campus" (Sun opinion; Amber Krisch, says she has worked with Cornell Dining) | 2019-02-05 | https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/02/inocciduous-thoughts-things-ive-noticed-about-food-on-west-campus | 35 observations. Unlimited plan goes unused when students are on Central Campus; midday gap with no hall open; limited declining balance. Dairy-free milk widely available but often sweetened; some items labeled vegan contained dairy (her experience); praises Keeton staff; dining workers help with dietary needs when treated respectfully. |
| D4 | "Dining halls as a vegan" (Sun; Melanie Metz, Human Ecology senior) | 2021-10-24 (upd. 2022-10-05) | https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/24/dining-halls-as-a-vegan/ | Risley best for dietary variety (vegetarian hot station, vegan stir-fry and poke; says fully gluten-free); Keeton good grain salads; Rose creative, not always vegan; Appel many stations. Complaints: badly made vegan grilled cheese, vegan waffles gone, some days thin vegan options. Tips: check Eatery app menus; ask staff. |
| D5 | "A comprehensive review of West Campus dining" (Sun; Margaret Haykin, pescetarian, and Catherine Zhang) | 2022-11-07 (upd. 2022-11-16) | https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/07/a-comprehensive-review-of-west-campus-dining/ | Dinners the week of Oct. 24, 2022, scores /10 averaged: Hans Bethe 8, Cook 6.6, Rose 6.5, Keeton 5.5, Becker 2.5. Real story is day-to-day swing: Cook 2 Monday, 10 Tuesday; Rose 10 Monday, 3 Thursday; Keeton 8.5 Monday, 0.5 Friday (stale naan, sold-out burgers). Bethe closes earliest (7:30). Two reviewers, one week: anecdote. |
| D6 | "The freshman dining hall experience" (Sun; Grace Kim, Hotel School freshman) | 2020-09-18 | https://cornellsun.com/2020/09/18/the-freshman-dining-hall-experience/ | Pandemic era: about 30 min check-in queue plus about 30 min inside at RPCC and Appel; workaround is eating at 11:30 a.m. or 6 p.m. COVID-specific; do not generalize. |
| D7 | "The art of meal prepping" (Sun lifestyle; Giuliana Keeth, A&S senior) | 2025-09-22 | https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/09/the-art-of-meal-prep | Moved off campus; first week overwhelming (planning, waste). Fix: recipes sharing ingredients (shakshuka, tomato pasta), spreadsheet planning, shop on a light class day, cook on weekend; lunch at a cafe with friends as a middle step. No prices or stores. |
| D8 | "Culinary autonomy: cooking for yourself" (Sun; Kyle Roth, Human Ecology sophomore) | 2023-05-03 | https://cornellsun.com/2023/05/03/culinary-autonomy-cooking-for-yourself | Cost is a reason students skip unlimited. Tips: batch cook; dorm common kitchens (stovetops, shared fridges) are low-pressure practice. Groceries: Walmart, Wegmans, Target by bus (author: Route 15 to Wegmans and Walmart from the Commons; Route 30 to Target at Cayuga Mall); nearer: Anabel's, GreenStar, Cornell Food Pantry. Check routes against TCAT. |
