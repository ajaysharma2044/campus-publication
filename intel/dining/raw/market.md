Rules on the same page (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans):
- 2-year residential requirement for first- and second-years, with a required plan; applies to students who began Summer 2021 or later.
- Billed in two parts, on the bursar bill in July and January.
- Non-refundable $50 administrative fee once per academic year (graduate "Graduate and Professional Student Meal Plan" is exempt, see below).
- BRBs buy your own meals, not guest meals. Fall debit balance carries to spring; unused swipes do not.
- BRB expiry date as stated on this page: May 27, 2027.
- The page says the Unlimited plan is fully covered by financial aid for eligible students (claim by Cornell Dining, not verified against financial aid pages).
- Shabbat, holiday and special meals at 104West! can carry extra charges (reservations at kosher.scl.cornell.edu).

Summer 2026 comparison, https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/summer-meal-plans: Summer Bucks price identical to BRB prices above (breakfast $10.80, lunch $15.25, dinner $18.85, brunch $16.85); door pricing there is listed before tax ($11.02, $15.56, $19.26, $17.18 plus tax). Note the two pages present door prices differently (tax included vs plus tax) and the numbers differ.

Other plan rules (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs; fetched 2026-11-06):
- Plan week runs Thursday to Wednesday; unused weekly swipes do not carry over; changes take effect Thursdays.
- Swipes and BRBs are for the student only; guest meals (4 or 8 per semester) are for guests.
- First-years may not change or cancel; transfers and on-campus juniors may only reduce to Bear Traditional; WCHS students may not change or cancel.
- After the deadline you can upgrade but not reduce, by request ticket.
- Plans are for a full academic year; cost adjustments post to the bursar account in 7 to 10 business days; credits prorated weekly.
- 1 BRB = $1, tax-exempt, declining balance; usable at Cornell Dining locations (not non-Dining eateries) and many vending machines; food only; non-refundable, non-transferable; expire at end of spring; friends or family can add funds at get.cbord.com/cornell.
- Sick tray: email from another student, copying dining@cornell.edu, with name, card ID, allergies; one meal deducted from weekly balance.

## Part 4. Providers, locations and what each offers (Cornell Dining)

Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/dining-rooms and https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/cafes-food-courts-coffeehouses (fetched 2026-11-06; checked 2026-11-08). - Central: Amit Bhatia Libe Cafe (Olin Library), Atrium Cafe (Sage Hall), Big Red Barn (grad/professional community center), Bus Stop Bagels (Kennedy Hall), Cafe Jennie (The Cornell Store), College of Veterinary Medicine Cafe (Schurman Hall), Cornell Dairy Bar (Stocking Hall), Goldie's (Physical Sciences Building), Green Dragon (Sibley Hall), Mann Cafe (Mann Library), Martha's Cafe (Martha Van Rensselaer Hall), Mattin's Cafe (Duffield Hall), Rusty's (Uris Hall), Trillium (food court, Kennedy Hall).
- (Count of named non-dining-room units on that page: 4 North + 14 Central = 18. My earlier "28" above is wrong; correct count is 18.)

Mobile ordering (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/order-ahead): GET Order at get.cbord.com/cornell, no fee; at Libe Cafe, Bear Necessities, Big Red Barn, Bus Stop Bagels, Cafe Jennie, Dairy Bar, Crossings, Goldie's, Jansen's Market, Mann, Martha's, Mattin's, Novick's (13 units). Meals on the Move (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/eateries-menus/meals-move): costs one swipe; pre-order by 10:30am the day before; pickup 7:30 to 10:30am Mon-Fri while classes are in session at Crossings Cafe, Flora Rose House Dining Room, or Weill Hall Atrium; ordering at mealsonthemove.scl.cornell.edu; eligible plans include Unlimited, Bear Traditional/Choice/Basic, House, SFL Supplemental, South Campus, Flex 10/500, Off Campus Value; lunch = entree (sandwich, salad or grain bowl) + chips + fruit + optional water; breakfast = a breakfast bowl.

## Part 5. Calendar and timing (official)

Winter break (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining; fetched 2026-11-06; applies to winter 2026-27):
- Fall meal plan period ends with brunch Sunday Dec 20, 2026.
- Fall BRBs usable at cafes/coffee shops/convenience stores that stay open through Thursday Dec 24.
- Cornell Dining shut Fri Dec 25, 2026 through Fri Jan 1, 2027 (City Bucks can still be funded).
- Jan 4 to Jan 19, 2027: Fall BRBs usable at open cafes/coffee shops/stores.
- Spring dining begins with lunch Tuesday Jan 19, 2027.
- Gap: no dining-room meals between Dec 20 brunch and Jan 19 lunch, roughly 4 weeks (my reading of the dates).

Other dates (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms): Sept 9, 2026 fall reduce/cancel deadline; Feb 3, 2027 spring reduce deadline; May 28, 2027 swipes end; May 31, 2027 BRBs last day (contradiction noted above).
Swipe Out Hunger (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/food-security/swipe-out-hunger): open Sept 21 to Dec 8, 2026; students donate guest meals; request free meals via application handled by the FGLI office (Barnes Hall 200, M-F 9-5). Cornell Food Pantry at 109 McGraw Place (free to students, staff, faculty). Page cites "approximately 1 in 3 college students" food insecure (generic national claim, no source on page).
