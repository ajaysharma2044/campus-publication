Note the two pages present door prices differently (tax included vs plus tax) and the numbers differ.

Other plan rules (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/meal-plan-faqs; fetched 2026-11-06):
- Plan week runs Thursday to Wednesday; unused weekly swipes do not carry over; changes take effect Thursdays.
- Swipes and BRBs are for the student only; guest meals (4 or 8 per semester) are for guests.
- First-years may not change or cancel; transfers and on-campus juniors may only reduce to Bear Traditional; WCHS students may not change or cancel.
- After the deadline you can upgrade but not reduce, by request ticket.
- Plans are for a full academic year; cost adjustments post to the bursar account in 7 to 10 business days; credits prorated weekly.
- 1 BRB = $1, tax-exempt, declining balance; usable at Cornell Dining locations (not non-Dining eateries) and many vending machines; food only; non-refundable, non-transferable; expire at end of spring; friends or family can add funds at get.cbord.com/cornell.
- Sick tray: email from another student, copying dining@cornell.edu, with name, card ID, allergies; one meal deducted from weekly balance.

## Part 5. Calendar and timing (official)

Winter break (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/winter-break-dining; fetched 2026-11-06; applies to winter 2026-27):
- Fall meal plan period ends with brunch Sunday Dec 20, 2026.
- Fall BRBs usable at cafes/coffee shops/convenience stores that stay open through Thursday Dec 24.
- Cornell Dining shut Fri Dec 25, 2026 through Fri Jan 1, 2027 (City Bucks can still be funded).
- Jan 4 to Jan 19, 2027: Fall BRBs usable at open cafes/coffee shops/stores.
- Spring dining begins with lunch Tuesday Jan 19, 2027.
- Gap: no dining-room meals between Dec 20 brunch and Jan 19 lunch, roughly 4 weeks (my reading of the dates).

Other dates (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/deadlines-terms): Sept 9, 2026 fall reduce/cancel deadline; Feb 3, 2027 spring reduce deadline; May 28, 2027 swipes end; May 31, 2027 BRBs last day (contradiction noted above).
Swipe Out Hunger (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/about-dining/food-security/swipe-out-hunger): open Sept 21 to Dec 8, 2026; students donate guest meals; request free meals via application handled by the FGLI office (Barnes Hall 200, M-F 9-5). Cornell Food Pantry at 109 McGraw Place (free to students, staff, faculty). Page cites "approximately 1 in 3 college students" food insecure (generic national claim, no source on page).

## Part 13. Sales tax and a closure precedent

Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/january-morrison-dining-reduced (Cornell Dining news; Spring 2026 context; year inferred):
- Morrison Dining ran limited service for deep cleaning, Jan 4 to 12, 2026, with reduced door prices: breakfast $8, lunch $12, brunch $12, dinner $16.
- BRBs carry no sales tax; credit card, CornellCard or MealChoice add 8% New York State sales tax (as stated on the page). So: eating with BRB saves 8% versus cash/card at the same menu price (Cornell's statement; the dining room "door price" on the plans page is shown tax included).
- Spring 2026 meal plan dining began with lunch Tuesday Jan 13, 2026 (the day residence halls opened). Compare spring 2027: Jan 19, 2027.
