## Part 13. Sales tax and a closure precedent

Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/news-events/news/january-morrison-dining-reduced (Cornell Dining news; Spring 2026 context; year inferred):
- Morrison Dining ran limited service for deep cleaning, Jan 4 to 12, 2026, with reduced door prices: breakfast $8, lunch $12, brunch $12, dinner $16.
- BRBs carry no sales tax; credit card, CornellCard or MealChoice add 8% New York State sales tax (as stated on the page). So: eating with BRB saves 8% versus cash/card at the same menu price (Cornell's statement; the dining room "door price" on the plans page is shown tax included).
- Spring 2026 meal plan dining began with lunch Tuesday Jan 13, 2026 (the day residence halls opened). Compare spring 2027: Jan 19, 2027.
