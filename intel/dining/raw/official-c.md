## 16. Cornell Dining scale and recognition (claims by Cornell)

Cornell states it ranked #2 in the Princeton Review campus food list that year, that Cornell Dining runs over 30 eateries and serves more than 23,000 meals a day (2023 figures). The Princeton Review result is based on student surveys (165,000 students at 389 schools, per the page). This is a 2023 item; the current-year ranking was not checked here.
