- BRB amounts: S1 says about 400 to 500 per semester; S18 gives no current number. Official plan page decides.
- Annual cost: $4,896 to $7,132 appears in S1; $7,132 repeated in S2 (same author network, not independent). Treat as one source.
- Big Red Food Court: S15 (owners expect BRB) versus University spokesperson (not eligible) in the same article.
- Swipe Out Hunger status: two Cornell pages disagree (voice.md section 4).
- RPCC Marketplace "permanently closed" (S14, 2022); status in 2026 unchecked.
