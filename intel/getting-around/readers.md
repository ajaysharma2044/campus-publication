## Reader: First-year (fall entrant, on campus)
Key: first-year
### Situation
Entered fall 2026 and lives in university housing, most on North Campus. Most have no car: the families page says first-years are "discouraged" from bringing one, though limited permits exist (https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). Rides TCAT free at all hours with a Cornell ID from fall registration to Aug 31, 2027 (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). NYC is the biggest home destination; a student guide puts about 31% of first-years from NYC (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/busses-trains-and-planes-getting-to-and-from-cornell). Parents often pay for trips home. Knows nothing yet about stops, booking links or peak days.
### Questions in their words
- "Is TCAT free for Cornell students and which routes should I use?" (q-getting-around-0008)
- "Can freshmen have a car at Cornell and how much does parking cost?" (q-getting-around-0007)
- "Do I need a car at Cornell?" (q-getting-around-0012)
- "What is the best way to get from NYC to Cornell by bus?" (q-getting-around-0001)
- "How do I book a bus for Thanksgiving break and when do tickets sell out?" (q-getting-around-0024)
- "Where do I park on Cornell move-in day?" (q-getting-around-0025)
- Forum posters told an incoming freshman to skip the car because parking is scarce and TCAT and walking cover it (College Confidential, 2016: https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/should-i-bring-a-car/1811430).
### Decisions with numbers
- Bring a car or not: for this reader, no. A resident permit is $752.98 a year, $2.06 a day, the price of about 502 TCAT cash rides, and a first-year rides TCAT free anyway (brief 2.14, 10.13; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). The ND permit is "extremely limited and capped" to North Campus residents (same URL). Whether a first-year may register a car at all is not stated on the permit page; the families page says "discouraged, limited permits" (brief 8.2).
- Buy OmniRide or not: no. The free first-year pass covers all hours until Aug 31 of the next year, with no extensions (brief 2.10; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes).
- NYC by C2C or a commercial bus: C2C is $93.50 one way with a NetID from 217 Jessup Rd (North Campus) with two suitcases below deck (brief 2.2, 2.3; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-rates). OurBus runs 4 to 6 daily campus-to-Manhattan trips from North and West Campus since Sept 1, 2026, "from" $25 to $33 (brief 2.4; https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/09/8kl4l6fodjwk ; https://www.ourbus.com/routes/ithaca-ny-to-new-york-ny). Four round trips cost $748 on C2C against $264 at the OurBus floor (brief 10.18). Pick C2C for the East Side (Weill at 1300 York Ave, Cornell Club at 6 E 44th St) or two checked bags; pick OurBus or FlixBus for the West Side and price (brief 6.2).
- C2C account: since Nov 4, 2026 every rider needs a new account; sign in with Cornell single sign-on or pay the $101 public fare (brief 2.1, 2.2; https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2026-10-22/new-campus-campus-reservation-system-launches-november-4).
- When to book Thanksgiving: now. OurBus names the Wednesday before Thanksgiving as a peak day (https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269), and Thanksgiving-week floors ran about 33% higher in a Nov 8 sample (brief 10.9). Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Thu Nov 26 (brief 4).
- Which bag plan: OurBus guarantees one large bag per ticket; extra bags $30 prepaid (search summary); Greyhound includes one carry-on and one checked bag (brief 2.4, 2.5; https://www.cornellsun.com/2024/02/01/got-baggage-inside-cornells-luggage-transport-woes ; https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/ithaca-ny-new-york-ny).
### Timeline
- After Aug 1: print the Move-In Ticket from the Housing and Dining Portal; it names the parking area (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-campus/move-instructions/move-instructions-north-campus).
- Mon Aug 17, Tue Aug 18, Fri Aug 21 to Sat Aug 22: North Campus move-in (year inferred as 2026; same URL).
- Fall registration: free TCAT starts.
- Sept 1, 2026: OurBus daily campus service began.
- Oct 1: winter break housing applications open (https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/winter-break-housing).
- Nov 4, 2026: new C2C booking system; new account needed.
- Nov 9, 2026: OurBus downtown stop moves to 235 S. Cayuga St (https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/06/ourbus-moves-ithaca-stop-to-south-cayuga-street-monday-as-tcat-shifts-stops/).
- Nov 23 to 27: Thanksgiving week; plan the return around storms (Dec 1-2, 2019 storm: https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/12/before-classes-were-cancelled-cornellians-attempted-to-return-to-ithaca-at-all-costs).
- Dec 1: winter break housing deadline.
- Last day of December finals: OurBus peak day. Residences closed 2 p.m. Sun Dec 21 in 2025; the 2026 date is not in the brief.
- Dec 23: last C2C departure on the "2026" link; Jan 6, 2027: first on the "2027" link (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service).
- May finals: free Student Assembly charters to ITH and SYR if funded again (brief 2.12).
- Mid-May: buildings close for move-out (2 p.m. Sun May 17 in 2026).
- Aug 31, 2027: free first-year TCAT ends.
### Mistakes and cost
- Signing in to C2C with a plain account: $101 instead of $93.50, $7.50 a trip (brief 10.1).
- Booking Thanksgiving late: about a third more on the floor fare (brief 10.9), or a sold-out bus.
- Missing the C2C bus: the full $93.50 fare is kept once the bus leaves its last stop; the return leg is not cancelled automatically (brief 2.2; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/campus-campus-bus-service/c2c-policies-regulations).
- Cancelling C2C inside 8 hours: $30 a seat instead of $10 (same URL).
- Going to the old 710 W State St terminal or the old OurBus East Seneca stop (brief 2.6, 7.4).
- Bringing a car: $752.98 for the permit plus $50 per ticket for parking without a permit or payment (brief 2.15).
- Assuming the free pass carries into sophomore year: from Sept 1, 2027 daytime weekday rides cost $1.50 (brief 2.10).
### Brief facts that matter

## Reader: Transfer or spring entrant
Key: transfer-spring
### Situation
A transfer arriving in fall or January, or any student whose first term starts in spring. Transfers count as first-years for TCAT per the Cornell Sun's Nov 2024 report (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free). A spring entrant's free TCAT runs Jan 1 to Dec 31 of the same year, not to Aug 31 (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Many transfers know college life but not Ithaca, may live off campus, and may already own a car. A January arrival lands inside the campus winter overnight rules (Dec 1 to Apr 1) and the city odd/even season (Nov 1 to Apr 1).
### Questions in their words
- "Is TCAT free for Cornell students and which routes should I use?" asked as a transfer (q-getting-around-0008)
- "Do I need a car at Cornell?" (q-getting-around-0012)
- "What is it like driving to Ithaca in winter?" for a January move (q-getting-around-0016)
- "Where can I park near Collegetown and overnight in Ithaca?" (q-getting-around-0015)
- "Who can ride the Cornell Campus-to-Campus (C2C) bus and how do I book it?" for a January trip (q-getting-around-0017)
- Students reported Cornell IDs denied at fareboxes during free periods after the fall 2024 change (Cornell Sun: https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/03/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free/).
### Decisions with numbers
- Buy OmniRide or not: no in the first year. Transfers ride free any time per the Sun reporter's statement (brief 2.10, 4.2); confirm with Transportation Services at 607-255-4600 because the GM is not quoted on transfers (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2024/11/non-first-year-cornell-students-no-longer-able-to-access-tcat-for-free).
- When the free pass ends: a fall transfer keeps it to Aug 31 of the next year; a spring entrant loses it Dec 31 of the same year, with no extensions, and then pays $1.50 per daytime weekday ride or $200 a year for OmniRide (brief 2.10).
- Permit bought mid-year: prices prorate daily, resident from Sept 1, commuter from Aug 16; a prorated refund needs the permit returned by Mar 31 (brief 2.14; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). No transfer-specific permit rule exists in the brief.
- January drive or bus: C2C trips from Jan 6, 2027 book on the separate "2027" link at https://c2cbus.transportation.cornell.edu/ (brief 2.1). Storms have cancelled buses and flights in January; in Jan 2019 Coach USA cancelled NYC departures and the governor banned buses on most highways (https://cornellsun.com/2019/01/20/canceled-flights-and-impromptu-housing-winter-storm-frustrates-students-travel-plans-to-cornell/).
- Overnight parking on arrival: when snow is forecast from Dec 1 to Apr 1, park 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m. only in the designated bays (A Lot west and mid bays, B Lot east and west bays, Forest Home garage, Hoy Road garage except the roof, and others) (brief 2.16; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules).
- Spring move-in dates and transfer orientation transport: not in the brief.
### Timeline
- Fall transfer: same as the first-year timeline, with the permit sale on Tue Aug 4, 2026 at 9 a.m. if bringing a car (https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809).
- Spring entrant: Nov 1 city odd/even season already running; Dec 1 campus winter rules start; Jan 1 free TCAT starts; Jan 6, 2027 first C2C departures on the "2027" link; mid-January arrival (recess ended Jan 13 in 2026; https://scl.cornell.edu/sub/sub/residential-life/housing/housing-details/winter-closing-notice); Mar 31 permit refund deadline; Apr 1 winter rules end; Dec 31 free TCAT ends.
### Mistakes and cost
- Buying OmniRide while the first-year pass covers you: $200, refundable only until Mar 31 less $40 (brief 2.10).
- A spring entrant assuming the pass runs to Aug 31: $1.50 a daytime ride from Jan 1 of the following year.
- Booking a January C2C trip on the "2026" link: that link covers departures only through Dec 23, 2026 (brief 2.1).
- Parking overnight outside the winter bays when snow is forecast: $50 winter-rules fine (brief 2.15).
- Leaving a car on a city street over break: ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned (brief 2.16).
### Brief facts that matter
2.10 (pass dates by entry term; transfer line); 4.2 (spring start and transfer paragraphs); 2.14 (proration, Mar 31 refund); 2.16 (winter bays, odd/even); 2.1 (two C2C links); 7.2 (Jan 2019 storm); 8.2 (2026-27 break dates unknown).

## Reader: Upper-year undergrad without a car
Key: upper-year-no-car
### Situation
Sophomore, junior or senior, on campus or in Collegetown, with no car. Since fall 2024 rides TCAT free only after 6 p.m. on weekdays and all weekend; otherwise $1.50 a ride or a $200 OmniRide pass (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Walks most places, takes the bus home, may pay their own way. Knows the campus but often still believes TCAT is free all day.
### Questions in their words
- "Is TCAT free for Cornell students and which routes should I use?" (q-getting-around-0008)
- "Do I need a car at Cornell?" (q-getting-around-0012)
- "Is Ithaca CarShare worth it for Cornell students?" (q-getting-around-0014)
- "How much does Ithaca Bikeshare cost and is it free for Cornell students?" (q-getting-around-0019)
- "Are Uber and Lyft reliable in Ithaca or should I use a taxi?" (q-getting-around-0010)
- Students waited long because Google Maps times were wrong, then paid for an Uber (Cornell Sun 2022: https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/06/tcat-implements-massive-service-cut-due-to-driver-shortage/); student government called buses late and unreliable in March 2026 (https://assembly.cornell.edu/resolutions/student-assembly/sa-r69-2025-2026).
### Decisions with numbers
- OmniRide or pay as you go: the $200 pass pays off after about 134 paid daytime weekday rides, about 4.5 a week over an assumed 30-week year (brief 10.14). Below that, pay $1.50 a ride; the farebox caps a day at $3 (search summary of the 9/4/26 TCAT page, brief 2.11; https://www.tcatbus.com/ride/fare-pass-options/). OmniRide is prorated monthly, refundable until Mar 31 less $40, expires Aug 31, and can take 24 hours to activate (brief 2.10).
- Carshare plan: Just In Case is $30 a year plus $9 an hour; It's My Car is $310 a year plus $6 an hour; both add a $20 sign-up, mileage of 42 to 52 cents and 20% in taxes on usage. Below about 93 hours a year, Just In Case is cheaper (brief 2.20, 10.17; https://www.ithacacarshare.org/students-commuters ; https://www.ithacacarshare.org/rates). Eligibility: 18 or older, license held 2 or more years; under 21 needs a spotless record and an own policy with $300,000 liability (https://www.ithacacarshare.org/eligibility).
- Bikeshare for short hops: $1.50 unlock plus $0.25 a minute, so a 20-minute trip is $6.50 before tax; day pass $25, month $60; no student discount found (brief 2.19, 10.17; https://www.ithacabikeshare.org).
- Late nights: Route 92 Blue Light bus runs 6:30 p.m. to 2:30 a.m. year-round and is free in the evening hours for this reader (brief 2.10; https://scl.cornell.edu/families/cornell-family-resource-hub/parking-transportation). Uber is hard to get downtown late at night per a 2022 student account; current waits and prices are not in the brief.
- A year of getting around: with OmniRide and four NYC round trips at bus floors, $416 to $464; with C2C, $948 (brief 10.18).
### Timeline
- Aug 31: the first-year pass ended for rising sophomores; buy OmniRide at MyParking.Cornell.Edu if the math says so.
- Weekdays before 6 p.m.: paid rides. After 6 p.m. and weekends: free.
- Nov 9, 2026: TCAT East Seneca stops reshuffled downtown (brief 2.6).
- Mar 31: last OmniRide refund (less $40).
- Peak travel days: Wednesday before Thanksgiving, last day of December finals, the Friday spring break begins (https://www.ourbus.com/blog/?p=6269).
### Mistakes and cost
- Believing TCAT is free all day: $1.50 per daytime weekday ride (brief 7.4).
- Buying OmniRide and riding rarely: $200, or $40 lost on a refund before Mar 31.
- Paying cash past 134 daytime rides a year: every ride after that costs $1.50 that the pass would have covered.
- Trusting Google Maps times over Bus Tracker or MyStop: missed buses and paid rides (brief 6.6).
- Joining Carshare under 21 without an own insurance policy: the application cannot be approved (brief 2.20).
### Brief facts that matter
2.10 (after-6 p.m. rule, OmniRide terms); 2.11 (fares, passes, cap); 2.19 (Bikeshare prices); 2.20 (Carshare plans, eligibility); 6.6 (riding TCAT as a student); 10.14, 10.17, 10.18; 7.1 themes 1, 9, 11, 14 and 19.

## Reader: Student with a car in university housing
Key: car-on-campus
### Situation
An upper-year (or the rare first-year with a permit) living in university housing in a permit area: FH (Hasbrouck), ND (North), SW (South) or WD (West). Pays $752.98 a year for a resident permit (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). Drives home or to the airport, deals with snow and breaks, and still hunts for spots: in 2023 a Williams St permit holder called parking a daily "treasure hunt" (Cornell Sun: https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2023/09/williams-street-parking-lot-struggles-to-accommodate-students). Usually parents help pay.
### Questions in their words
- "Can freshmen have a car at Cornell and how much does parking cost?" (q-getting-around-0007)
- "Where can I park near Collegetown and overnight in Ithaca?" (q-getting-around-0015)
- "What is it like driving to Ithaca in winter?" (q-getting-around-0016)
- "Do I need a car at Cornell?" (q-getting-around-0012)
- "Where do I park on Cornell move-in day?" (q-getting-around-0025)
- Students with cars feared icy hills and snowbanks burying cars and said tickets and ParkMobile add up (Cornell Sun 2022: https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/).
### Decisions with numbers
- Buy a permit: be online when sales open (Tue Aug 4, 2026 at 9 a.m., first come first served; valid Aug 16, 2026 to Aug 15, 2027) (https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809). Register the plate in MyParking.Cornell.edu first, which is required even for short-term parking (brief 2.13). The resident permit is $752.98, prorated daily from Sept 1; ND is capped to North Campus residents (brief 2.14). Resident students are not eligible for a free A or E permit.
- Where it works: the signed area plus North Campus A Lot after 2:30 p.m. (ND: A Lot at all times). Elsewhere, pay by ParkMobile with a $0.45 fee per transaction; commuter lots are paid Mon to Fri 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. including breaks (brief 2.13; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/short-term-parking-options).
- Winter nights: resident-permit holders park in their own lots as usual; the designated-bay rule (Dec 1 to Apr 1, 5 p.m. to 7:30 a.m., when snow is forecast) governs everyone else (brief 2.16; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/winter-break-parking-rules).
- The car over winter break: leave it in your permit area on campus; never on a city street, where it can be ticketed, towed and treated as abandoned; odd/even runs Nov 1 to Apr 1, 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., with towing possible after 72 hours (brief 2.16; https://ecode360.com/8395630).
- Drive to an airport: ITH long-term parking is $8 a day or $40 a week; SYR economy is about $98 a week at the $14 daily max (brief 10.2; https://www.flyithaca.com/parking ; https://www.syrairport.org/parking/).
- Taking C2C: email c2cbus@cornell.edu with plate and dates for free B Lot parking (2024 rule), or buy temporary B Lot parking up to 14 days (brief 2.2; https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/12488).
- Out-of-state plates and license: students from other states normally need not exchange their license; NY-registered vehicles need NY insurance (search summary, brief 2.23; https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/exchange-out-of-state-driver-license).
- Is it worth it: a year with a permit and two ITH weekly stays costs $832.98 before gas, insurance and tolls (brief 10.18); the permit price alone buys about 59 to 70 hours of It's My Car carshare (brief 10.17).
### Timeline
- Jul 1: annual rates set.
- Tue Aug 4, 9 a.m.: permit sales open; in 2023 the Williams St lot sold out within hours.
- Aug 15: old permit expires. Aug 16: new permit valid. Sept 1: resident proration starts.
- Nov 1: city odd/even season. Mid-November: Cornell's annual winter parking notice (2023 example: https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-11-16/winter-parking-transportation-information).
- Dec 1 to Apr 1: campus winter overnight rules.
- Mar 31: last day to return the permit for a prorated refund.
- May move-out: posted loading docks and lots by area (brief 2.18).
### Mistakes and cost
- No permit or payment: $50, plus $10 if unpaid in 10 days (brief 2.15; https://cornellsun.com/2023/09/04/on-campus-parking-violation-fines-increase-for-first-time-since-2014/).
- Fire lane, accessible space or life-safety zone: $150 with automatic towing; reserved space $150; grass $100; winter rules $50; over time limit $35; boot release $150.
- Missing the sale morning: no permit for the year (Williams St sold out within hours in 2023).
- Car on a city street over break: towed and possibly impounded; the tow and impound cost is not in the brief.
- Returning the permit after Mar 31: no refund.
### Brief facts that matter
2.13 (plate registration, ParkMobile, commuter-lot hours); 2.14 (permit types, prices, sale, refunds); 2.15 (fines); 2.16 (winter bays, break rule, odd/even); 6.4 (permit steps and the unofficial part); 10.2, 10.13, 10.17, 10.18; 7.2 cars and parking.

## Reader: Off-campus renter (Collegetown, downtown, Fall Creek, West Hill)
Key: off-campus
### Situation
An upper-year or grad student on a lease off campus. Not eligible for resident permits; may buy the student commuter (B) permit, use the city residential permit, pay a city garage, or go without a car. Commutes by foot, Route 10 or other TCAT routes. West Hill and Cayuga Heights renters lost routes 14S and 83, suspended since fall 2023 (https://cornellsun.com/2024/04/25/facing-operator-and-bus-shortages-tcat-board-prolongs-suspension-of-routes-14s-and-83/). Pays rent and travel themselves or with family help.
### Questions in their words
- "Where can I park near Collegetown and overnight in Ithaca?" (q-getting-around-0015)
- "Can freshmen have a car at Cornell and how much does parking cost?" asked about the commuter permit (q-getting-around-0007)
- "Is TCAT free for Cornell students and which routes should I use?" (q-getting-around-0008)
- "Do I need a car at Cornell?" (q-getting-around-0012)
- "What is it like driving to Ithaca in winter?" (q-getting-around-0016)
- A West Hill resident described a transfer workaround with an up-to-55-minute unsheltered wait after 14S and 83 were suspended (Ithaca Journal via Yahoo: https://www.yahoo.com/news/bus-riders-bemoan-aging-fleet-181118830.html); snowbanks buried Collegetown street-parked cars (Cornell Sun 2022: https://cornellsun.com/2022/11/21/dont-let-it-snow-students-express-concerns-over-driving-conditions-as-temperatures-fall/).
### Decisions with numbers
- Parking on campus: the B commuter permit is $359.77 (about $30 a month), prorated daily from Aug 16, valid in B Lot always and A Lot after 2:30 p.m.; commuter lots are Vet College B, TRB, South and North Morrison and BTI (brief 2.14; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking-campus/parking-students). Or Daily Decision from "as little as $1/day" ($0.65 permit plus $0.35 ParkMobile fee) in select zones; rates updated Aug 1, 2026, so verify (brief 2.13; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/getting-around/commuter-support-services).
- Parking at home: the city residential permit is $45 a year, valid to July 31, no partial-year price, visitor pass $10; residents only (a landlord cannot get it for you); bring the registration and a lease or utility bill to the City Clerk, 108 E Green St (brief 2.17, 6.5; https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance). Whether a Cornell permit works on Collegetown streets is not in the brief.

## Reader: Cyclist, e-bike or scooter rider
Key: cyclist
### Situation
Rides a bike, e-bike or e-scooter to class, possibly year-round, on a hilly campus with about 7 miles of bike lanes and 2 miles of shared paths (Cornell staff column: https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/guest-room-from-bikes-to-buses-how-to-sustainably-navigate-campus-this-fall). Theft is common. Winter riding has no official guidance.
### Questions in their words
- "Can you bike in Ithaca in winter and what is it like?" (q-getting-around-0009)
- "How much does Ithaca Bikeshare cost and is it free for Cornell students?" (q-getting-around-0019)
- "Is TCAT free for Cornell students and which routes should I use?" asked about bike racks (q-getting-around-0008)
- "Who can ride the Cornell Campus-to-Campus (C2C) bus and how do I book it?" asked about bringing a bike home (q-getting-around-0017)
- Students lost a $600 bike after about ten unlocked minutes in Collegetown and a $2,025 e-bike despite a cheap lock; one now uses a cable, a U-lock and two AirTags (Cornell Sun 2024: https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/14/this-happens-all-the-time-campus-police-report-more-than-40000-in-bike-and-scooter-thefts-this-semester-so-far).
- A 2026 Reddit commenter called the campus bike lanes a handful that start and stop (mirror, search summary: https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1t9kvn2/snapshots/2026-05-11T03%3A14%3A47.558916Z).
### Decisions with numbers
- Register: free with Cornell; the main cycling page urges it without saying it is mandatory (brief 2.19; https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/alternative-transportation-options/cycling-campus).
- Lock and park: designated racks only; bikes locked to railings, signposts, trees or poles can be impounded; lock frame and wheel with a U-lock. Fall 2024 saw 51 bike and scooter thefts worth at least $41,177 with four bikes recovered intact (brief 2.19). Impound fees are not in the brief.
- E-bike or scooter: an e-bike must have a motor of no more than 750 W, be no wider than 36 in and have working pedals; an e-scooter must be under 100 lb with a max of 20 mph. Do not charge batteries in residential rooms, offices or academic buildings; indoor storage only in sprinklered bike rooms or with a building coordinator's written permission (brief 2.19).
- Own bike or Bikeshare: Bikeshare is $1.50 plus $0.25 a minute (20 minutes is $6.50), $25 a day, $60 a month, with a $1.50 fee for trips not ending at a hub; Class 1 pedal-assist up to about 16 mph; Cornell requires bikeshare bikes be parked at racks (brief 2.19). Winter operation, station count and student discounts are not in the brief.
- Winter: no official winter guidance; the old student advice is gear and good brakes and a separate winter bike because salt wears a bike down (2007); avoid the Slope while it is snowing and stay off metal bridge panels (2023); put the bike on a TCAT front rack (free) for the climb (brief 6.11). Which paths are cleared is not in the brief.
- Path rules: white means bike lane, yellow means shared and yield, red or unmarked means dismount; no riding in parts of the Botanic Gardens and Six Mile Gorge (brief 2.19).
- Taking the bike home: C2C carries a bike only in a bike box, counted as one suitcase (brief 2.2).
### Timeline
- Move-in: register the bike and buy a U-lock before the first week.
- November to March: winter riding; TCAT snow routes chosen by dispatch.
- Move-out: bike rooms and storage; the brief has no bike-storage-over-summer rule.
### Mistakes and cost
- A cheap lock or no lock: losses of $400 to $2,025 in the 2024 stories.
- Locking to a railing or tree: impound.
- Charging an e-bike battery in a dorm room: against the guidance document.
- Riding the Slope in snow: the student tip is to avoid it.
### Brief facts that matter
2.19 (registration, parking, e-bike rules, theft, Bikeshare); 6.11 (biking steps, winter); 2.10 (TCAT bike racks); 2.2 (C2C bike box); 7.2 bikes; 7.3 tips; 8.2 (winter path clearing unknown).
