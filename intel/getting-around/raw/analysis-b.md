### Analysis A: cost of parking a car at ITH for a trip (computed from the table)
Question: what does it cost to leave a car at ITH for a break trip?
Method: apply the published rates; for stays over a day assume each full 24 h costs the 5-24 hr daily max, and use the weekly rate for 7 days. This assumes the daily rate repeats; the page does not say how multi-day stays are charged beyond "weekly", so treat 2-6 day figures as estimates.
- 7 days long-term: $40.00 (published). Short-term: $48.00 (published).
- 3 days long-term (estimate): 3 x $8.00 = $24.00. Short-term: 3 x $9.50 = $28.50.
- 14 days long-term (estimate): 2 x $40 = $80.00.
- Weekly rate per day: long-term $40/7 = $5.71; short-term $48/7 = $6.86.
- A four-hour pick-up/drop errand: long-term $5.00 vs short-term $7.00.
Result: long-term lot saves $8/week (17%) over short-term. Date 2026-11-08, caveat: page undated.

### Analysis B: how much has the TCAT adult fare changed, 1999 to 2026?
Question: how much has the TCAT base fare changed over time?
Method: compare the 1999 single-zone fare ($0.75) with the current adult cash fare ($1.50) from the TCAT snippet; adjust no inflation figure here (a checker should apply BLS CPI if wanted).
Calculation: $1.50 / $0.75 = 2.0, so the nominal fare doubled over 27 years (1999 to 2026). Average increase if compounding: 2^(1/27) - 1 = about 2.6% per year.
Caveat: the current fare is from a search snippet of a page that returned 403; 1999 fare is single-zone. Not an official year-by-year series.
