### Analysis E: what finals-week shuttle spending implies per charter, and what it does not
Question: how much did Student Assembly spend on finals-week airport shuttles in 2025-26?
Data: R18 $7,853.32 (fall; term assumed from the resolution year, not verified) and R83 $10,668.87 (spring).
Calculation: $7,853.32 + $10,668.87 = $18,522.19 total approved across the two resolutions; spring/fall = 10,668.87 / 7,853.32 = 1.36.
Result: about $18.5k approved for 2025-26, spring about 36% larger than R18. Riders and trips not stated on either page, so no per-rider cost can be computed. Caveat: approved amounts, not actual spend; R18 timing inferred.
