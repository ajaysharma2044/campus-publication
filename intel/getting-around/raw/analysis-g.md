## 25. Analyses

### Analysis J: walking time from Collegetown and downtown to central campus (estimate)
Question: how long is the walk?
Method: published distance divided by an assumed walking speed of 3.0 mph (my assumption, not from a source). Distances: Cornell's housing guide describes Collegetown as 0.1 to 0.5 miles from campus (reported via search snippet of scl.cornell.edu ithaca-neighborhoods page; page itself 404 on fetch); Rome2rio gives Ithaca Commons to Cornell as 2 miles (third-party, approximate, https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ithaca-Commons/Cornell-University); a Cornell Sun guide says the Collegetown walk through the Arts Quad is 15 to 20 minutes each way (https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/10/embrace-your-inner-intellectual-go-on-a-midnight-walk).
Calculation: minutes = miles / 3.0 x 60. 0.1 mi = 2 min; 0.5 mi = 10 min; 2.0 mi = 40 min.
Result: Collegetown 2 to 10 minutes by formula; the Sun's own 15 to 20 minutes (a route through Arts Quad, hills) exceeds the formula, so use the Sun figure for a realistic range. Commons about 40 minutes on foot by formula. With Route 10 every 6 to 12 minutes (Summer 2024 snippet), expected wait is half the headway: 3 to 6 minutes (arithmetic assumption of random arrival).
Caveats: hills not modeled; distances secondary; no official TCAT riding times read.

### Analysis K: 2026-27 cost of getting around, three student profiles (arithmetic on sourced prices)
Inputs: OmniRide $200/year (FCS passes page); resident permit $752.98 and B permit $359.77 (FCS students page, analysis-b); Carshare "It's My Car" $355/year at $6/hour (handbook summary, UNVERIFIED, summer 2024); bus: OurBus Ithaca-NYC "from $33" and 2 x 33 = $66 round trip at the advertised floor; ITH weekly parking $40 long-term.
Profile 1: first-year, no car, 4 round trips to NYC by OurBus at the floor fare: TCAT $0 + 4 x $66 = $264.
Profile 2: upper-year, no car, buys OmniRide, 4 NYC round trips: $200 + $264 = $464. Versus not buying and riding free evenings/weekends only: $264 plus $1.50 per daytime weekday ride; break-even pass vs cash: 200 / 1.5 = 133 daytime rides.
Profile 3: upper-year with a resident permit and 4 round trips by car, ignoring gas and insurance: $752.98 + 0 = $752.98 before gas; adding two ITH weekly parking stays $80 = $832.98.
Profile 4 (Carshare use): $355 + 20 application + 20 hours x $6 = $495 (hours assumed for illustration).
Result: ranges from $264 to $833 depending on profile; every NYC fare is the advertised floor and likely understated (typical averages $45 to $82 per Analysis F: 4 round trips at mean $61.91 each way = 8 x 61.91 = $495.28 instead of $264).
Sample size: none; scenario arithmetic, not a measurement. Gas, insurance, tolls, C2C ($6 surcharge plus unknown base fare) excluded.

### Analysis L: OurBus capacity change since Sept 2026
Data: before Sept 1, 2026 campus stops were mainly offered around major breaks (Sun article); after, 4 to 6 daily Manhattan trips; OurBus's own route page lists 9 NYC buses/day across all Ithaca stops.
Calculation: 4 to 6 campus trips per day x 7 days = 28 to 42 campus-stop departures per week.
Result: 28 to 42 weekly Manhattan departures serving campus stops, per the press-release figures; not verified against a timetable. Caveat: company claim reported by the Sun.
