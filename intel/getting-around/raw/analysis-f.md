Analysis H: how many scheduled passenger flights reached ITH on Saturday 2026-11-07?
Method: count rows with distinct origin and time; treat rows with the same time and origin as one aircraft (codeshare numbers).
Count: JFK 12:23 (1), JFK 22:29 (1), IAD 19:02 (1), IAD 23:44 (1) = 4 aircraft arrivals; 9 board rows reduce to 5 distinct (date,time,origin) entries over two days, 4 of them on Nov 7.
Result: 4 arrivals on one Saturday, 2 from JFK and 2 from IAD; no Newark (EWR), Charlotte (CLT) or other origins appeared on this board for these two days. n = 1 day. Not a schedule; charters and private aviation excluded by the board.
Caveats: third-party board; one weekend day; the later IAD arrival is flown by CommuteAir (United Express) and the JFK ones by Endeavor (Delta Connection). This agrees with the airport's airlines page (Delta JFK, United IAD) in analysis-b.md section 5 and with the Cornell Sun's statement (2026-09-02) that ITH lost its direct Newark flight in 2025. American CLT: no evidence of operation.
