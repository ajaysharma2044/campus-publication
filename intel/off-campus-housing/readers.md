## Reader: Sophomore planning a junior-year lease
Key: sophomore
### Situation
A full-time, Ithaca-based second-year who must live in a residence hall, a co-op, a recognized Greek house or other approved affiliated housing this year (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). The requirement ends after year two, so the junior-year lease is free to sign, but the Collegetown rush for it runs in the fall of sophomore year; the brief calls the junior-year lease "a decision made around October of sophomore year" (brief 6.1). Has already lost free daytime TCAT (first-year passes expire Aug 31 for fall entrants: https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Usually no car. Often parents pay; aid is calculated the same off campus and the Bursar stops billing housing (https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus). Knows little about leases, per-bed pricing or Ithaca law; the Sun reports students reviewing three leases during prelims (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus).

- "Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown Ithaca: where should I live?" (q-housing-0003)
- "How much is rent for a one-bedroom in Collegetown?" (q-housing-0011)

- Sun reporting has students searching from the day they arrive in August, a landlord telling them a unit was already gone seven weeks into fall 2021, and pressure to choose roommates a year ahead (https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/ ; https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/12/hurt-for-housing-begins-early-causing-stress-for-students/).
- A columnist who signed in October of sophomore year wrote that he was late by Collegetown standards (https://cornellsun.com/2022/08/04/nguyen-the-tale-of-cornells-broken-housing-market/).
### Decisions with numbers
- Sign in the fall rush or wait: it depends on where. For popular Collegetown units, sign in September to November of sophomore year: every dated signing in the Sun record from 2017 to 2025 except one fallback falls 8 to 16 months before move-in, mostly 10 to 11 (brief 10.6), and on 2026-11-08 Ithaca Renting already showed 14 of 67 Collegetown rows for 2027-28 sold out, including the big houses (https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/). For downtown, Fall Creek, Lansing and leftover units, waiting into spring is fine, because that market keeps supply later; Off-Campus Living's manager called "you must lock housing the day you return" a myth (https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/ ; brief 7.4).
- Group size: decide it first, because per-bed rent falls steeply with size. Ithaca Renting's 2027-28 Collegetown medians: 1BR alone $2,025, 2BR $1,377.50 per person, 4BR $1,246.25, 6BR $800, room in a shared suite $895 (brief 10.9; https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/).
- Collegetown or downtown: the same landlord's downtown per-bed prices run about 25% to 39% below Collegetown (studio $1,395 vs $1,765; 1BR $1,625 vs $2,025; 2BR $992.50 vs $1,377.50), at the cost of a 20 to 30 minute uphill walk or a bus (brief 10.10; https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/ ; https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods).
- Off campus or stay with Cornell: the 2026-27 residence hall double is $13,744 with gas, electricity, garbage and internet included, which equals $1,145 a month over 12 months or $1,374 over 10 (brief 10.3; https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates). Only 6BR, 10BR, shared-suite rooms and (on a two-row mean) 5BR Collegetown units come in under that before electricity (brief 10.9).
- Keep a Cornell room as a backup or not: room selection for 2026-27 ran Oct 2 to 8, 2025 (https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed). A returning junior who cancels pays $50 through Jan 31, $500 Feb 1 to Mar 31, $1,000 Apr 1 to Jun 30, $2,000 Jul 1 to Aug 15, then 50% of the fall room rate (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). A backup room is cheap only if the lease is signed by Jan 31.
- Who to sign with: every housemate on most local leases is "jointly and severally" liable for the whole rent and damages (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire). Use Cornell's housemate questions (study hours, guests, cleanliness, who handles money, what happens if someone goes abroad) before signing.
- What to refuse at signing: no application fee, no non-refundable or cleaning fee, a background check of at most $20 (waived with a check from the past 30 days), a deposit plus any rent advance of at most one month's rent (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A ; https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108).
- The 120-day waiver: in the City a landlord must give current tenants 120 days' written notice before renewing, showing or re-letting, unless the tenant initialed a bolded waiver on page one (https://ecode360.com/27390826 ; https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/). The rule's sponsor wrote that a discussion about why you are waiving the days should be your first question (https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/).
- Screening: for a City address, check the Certificate of Compliance (607-274-6508, ibd@cityofithaca.org) and the safety tool at safety.offcampusliving.cornell.edu; neither covers the Town of Ithaca, Lansing or Cayuga Heights (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources ; https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments).
### Timeline
- August, sophomore fall: Collegetown leasing for junior year opens as students arrive; Student Agencies opened 2023-24 leasing in early September 2022 (https://cornellsun.com/2022/09/12/hurt-for-housing-begins-early-causing-stress-for-students/).
- September: Ithaca Renting opened 2020-21 leasing at 9 a.m. Sept 12, 2019 after an overnight line (https://cornellsun.com/2019/09/19/cornell-students-sleep-on-sidewalk-for-cheaper-more-desirable-leases/).
- Early October: Cornell fall room selection (Oct 2 to 8 in 2025); prelims, which landlords say rising juniors want to beat (https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/).
- Early November: co-op selection round (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/upper-level-undergraduates/cooperative-housing).
- End of November: a developer said in 2016 he usually rents most units by then (https://cornellsun.com/2016/09/26/landlords-say-collegetown-housing-hassle-unlikely-to-change/).
- Jan 31: last day to cancel a junior-year Cornell license for $50.
- February to April: downtown, Fall Creek and Lansing searches; second co-op round in early February.
- Early August before junior year: Cornell parking permit sales (Aug 4, 2026 at 9 a.m.: https://fcs.cornell.edu/node/17809); lease starts on or about Aug 1; take the landlord's move-in inspection and photos (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108).
### Mistakes and cost
- Signing a lease for the current sophomore year without an approved exemption: the student is billed for the full year of Cornell housing anyway, $13,744 for a double, on top of rent (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing ; brief 2.3).
- A group formed in the first weeks of fall that shrinks: Dery wrote that large groups are likely to lose people and that individuals and small groups face higher prices; housemates stay jointly liable (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2020/10/dery-cornell-should-actively-help-house-students-off-campus). Moving from a 6BR median ($800) to a 4BR ($1,246.25) costs $446.25 a person a month, $5,355 a year (our arithmetic on brief 10.9).
- Comparing a per-bed price with a per-unit price, or a 12-month rent with Cornell's academic-year rate (brief 1, mistake 2). Aid does not cover rent in unregistered months (https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus).
- Keeping a Cornell backup room past Jan 31: $500 to $2,000 to cancel.
- Skipping the COC check: the 2018 tenants at 117 Thurston Ave learned after signing that the certificate had expired in 2015, then waited 53 days for heat (https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/25/cornell-student-abandons-house-sleeps-in-library-after-months-long-dispute-with-landlord/).
- Paying a banned fee or a deposit above one month.

## Reader: Junior or senior already renting
Key: upper-year-renter
### Situation
Holds a 10- or 12-month lease, most ending July 31 (https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing), and is deciding whether to renew, move or leave Ithaca. Pays rent, may pay NYSEG and internet, buys trash tags if trash is not included. Rides TCAT free only after 6 p.m. on weekdays and on weekends (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). A senior's lease often runs past May graduation. Knows the market but usually not the law, which the brief calls better for tenants than most students assume (brief 1).
### Questions in their words
- "What does Ithaca's Good Cause Eviction law mean for student renters?" (q-housing-0022)
- "What are the security deposit rules for renting in Ithaca?" (q-housing-0005)
- "What can I do if my Ithaca landlord won't fix mold, bed bugs or no heat?" (q-housing-0018)
- "How do I sublet my apartment in Ithaca and does my landlord have to agree?" (q-housing-0004)
- "Do I need renters insurance if I live off campus at Cornell and what does it cost?" (q-housing-0009)
- "What do I need to do to move out?" (q-housing-0015, which ranks for dorms; off-campus move-out is the gap: brief 9)
- Students report being pressed to renew within weeks of moving in (2012 and 2022 reports: https://cornellsun.com/2012/09/11/new-york-state-official%e2%80%88landlords-students-must-be-reasonable-people/ ; brief 6.8) and avoiding repair requests for fear of not being renewed (https://cornellsun.com/2021/07/23/ithaca-tenants-union-and-solidarity-slate-join-regional-efforts-toward-housing-equity/).
### Decisions with numbers
- Renew early or wait: in the City the landlord must give 120 days' written notice before renewing, showing or re-letting your unit, unless you initialed a first-page waiver (https://ecode360.com/27390826). The count start is unresolved: counting back from a July 31, 2027 end gives April 2, 2027, while the sponsor's examples count forward from the lease start (brief 10.5). Do not publish a date; tell the reader to check whether they initialed a waiver.
- A rent increase at renewal: an increase of 5% or more, or non-renewal, needs 30, 60 or 90 days' notice depending on tenure; late notice keeps the old terms until the period runs (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-C). Under Good Cause (Ithaca opted in July 2024), an increase above the lower of 10% or 5% plus CPI is presumed unreasonable (https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction ; https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections).
- Is my building covered by Good Cause: not if its certificate of occupancy is from 2009 or later (exempt 30 years), if the landlord owns only one unit in the state, if it is owner-occupied with 10 or fewer units, or if the rent is above 345% of fair market rent (about $4,402 for a 1BR on the 2023 FMR) (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/214 ; https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction-law-notice-february-2025 ; https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing). Private student apartments are not exempt as a category; only university dorms are. The landlord's 226-c notice must say whether the unit is covered and why not.
- Repairs: put every request in writing. Heat season in Ithaca runs Sept 15 to May 31; inside it must be at least 68F from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. when it is below 55F outside, and 62F overnight (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance). Courts typically abate about 10% to 20% for minor problems, about 30% for moderate and 50% to 60% for the worst (same URL). Withholding rent invites a nonpayment case with a 14-day notice; get free advice first (607-301-1560). After 30 days in the unit and written notice, a tenant may petition for a repair order (since January 2024).
- A complaint is protected: refusing to renew or raising rent unreasonably within a year of a good-faith complaint can be defended as retaliation, except in owner-occupied buildings under four units (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal).
- Getting the deposit back: request a move-out inspection; it happens between two weeks and one week before the end, with 48 hours' written notice. The landlord must return the deposit or an itemized statement within 14 days or forfeit the right to keep any of it; a willful violation can cost up to twice the deposit (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). Deductions: unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear, unpaid utilities owed to the landlord, removing left belongings, excessive cleaning but not routine turnover cleaning (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/security-deposits). Small claims up to $5,000 at Ithaca City Court, 118 East Clinton St.
- The senior's last summer: a lease ending July 31 after May graduation leaves June and July to sublet (see away-semester) or to pay. Some buildings end May 31 (Dryden South, all leases end May 31, 2027: https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961).
- Renters insurance: Cornell's checklist says buy it; GradGuard is Cornell's preferred insurer with no price published; Ithaca quotes run about $5 to $27 a month depending on source (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations ; https://www.safebutler.com/cheap-best-renters-insurance-ithaca-ny ; brief 3.7). Whether landlords require it: not in the brief.
- Ending a lease for domestic violence: 30 days' written notice, documentation within 25 days, prepaid rent back within 10 days; Advocacy Center of Tompkins County (607) 277-5000 (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).
### Timeline
- August to September: a landlord may raise renewal for the following year within weeks of move-in; check for a waiver. Heat season starts Sept 15.
- December: the rule's sponsor put the renewal decision for August leases "about December" (his example: https://cornellsun.com/2022/04/18/mehler-student-tenants-rights-shifting-in-your-favor/).
- Any month: Good Cause renewal notice falls 30 to 90 days before lease end (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/216).
- Two weeks before move-out: confirm time, schedule the final inspection, change address. One week before: schedule NYSEG and internet shutoff, clean, give a forwarding address in writing (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/moving-and-out).
- May 31: heat season ends; some leases end.
- July 31: most leases end; City residential parking permits expire (https://www.cityofithacany.gov/731/Permit-Issuance).
- By mid-August: deposit or itemized statement due (14 days after a July 31 exit).
### Mistakes and cost
- Initialing the 120-day waiver without asking why: loses the notice window before the unit is shown or re-let.
- Withholding rent before getting advice: a 14-day rent demand and a nonpayment case (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/eviction-proceedings).
- No written repair record: weakens any abatement claim; self-treating bed bugs can weaken a damages claim (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance).
- Skipping the move-out inspection and photos: the landlord's list of charges is the only record.
- Leaving couches or mattresses at the curb: barred by City rules; trash needs a $6 tag per bag and goes out no more than 24 hours before pickup (https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance ; https://www.cityofithacany.gov/168/Trash-Yard-Waste-Collection-Tags).
- Accepting a cash payment to leave a building the City has ordered vacated: City rules owe displaced tenants double the monthly rent prorated per day; Asteri residents were offered $2,000 to leave in five days and release claims, which a city official and the Tenants Union called predatory (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/03/asteri-s-aftermath-displacement-dangerous-conditions-and-a-predatory-cash-for-keys-scheme).

## Reader: Transfer student
Key: transfer
### Situation
Admitted after the Collegetown rush for the year has already picked over the stock. The rule depends on class year: all new transfers live on campus for one academic year; spring transfers live on campus for the incoming spring semester and the following academic year (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). A transfer matriculating as a junior or senior, or with two years of on-campus residence at the previous school, is exempt from the residential requirement and must request the exemption each year (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). The transfer page links Off-Campus Living with no deadlines or contacts (brief 2.1). Rides TCAT free for the first year (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Usually knows no one to room with.
### Questions in their words
- "How does housing work for Cornell transfer students?" (q-housing-0001)
- "When do Cornell students sign Collegetown leases and when should I start looking?" (q-housing-0006)
- "How do I find roommates for an off-campus apartment at Cornell?" (q-housing-0019)
- "Collegetown vs Fall Creek vs downtown Ithaca: where should I live?" (q-housing-0003)
- "Does Cornell require sophomores to live on campus?" (q-housing-0017)
- In 2018 transfers learned on June 22 whether they had campus housing; some were placed far away and felt isolated; one advised spending about a week getting to know roommates before signing (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus).
- A sophomore transfer in fall 2025 got only leftover triples and doubles at the last selection slot (https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed).
### Decisions with numbers
- Can I live off campus at all: a sophomore transfer, no; one year on campus, and a new transfer cannot cancel a Cornell license unless exempt or accepted into affiliated housing before the Spring Housing Selection deadline (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). A junior or senior transfer, yes, once the exemption is approved. When a new transfer files the exemption: not in the brief beyond "during the open application period" (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).
- Spring transfer: the first off-campus lease is about 18 months away (spring plus the next academic year), so the fall rush to watch is the second fall on campus (brief 4.3).
- Where to look as a junior or senior transfer arriving in summer: the leftover market, not the Collegetown rush: downtown "available now" units at Ithaca Renting (downtown 2BR median $992.50 per bed, 3BR $795: https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/), sublets (Dryden South Fall 2026 sublet $1,395: https://www.apartments.com/dryden-south-sublets-available-fall-2026-ithaca-ny/yeje6qs/), and Lansing and Northeast complexes (Lansing West showed 1BR "now" units at $1,038 and $1,193 and rent free until January 2027 for a Dec 1 move-in: https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx).
- Distance is cheaper for a transfer than for anyone else: free TCAT all year makes downtown (20 to 30 minute uphill walk) or Lansing (5 to 10 miles, bus) workable in year one (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods).
- Roommates: join the Off-Campus Living email list for housing, roommates and sublets; an admitted student without a NetID forwards the acceptance letter to offcampusliving@cornell.edu (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources). Remember joint and several liability with strangers (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire).
### Timeline
- Admission and housing notice: late spring to June (June 22 in 2018).
- April 6, 2026 was the housing application deadline for first- and second-years; whether new transfers follow it: not in the brief.
- Summer: off-campus search in the leftover market; lease starts on or about Aug 1 (or later; "available now" units).
- Fall, first year at Cornell: if the student will want Collegetown next year, the rush is now (brief 4.1).
- Aug 31 (fall entrants) or Dec 31 (spring entrants) of the following year: free TCAT ends.
- Each year: reapply for the exemption.
### Mistakes and cost
- A sophomore transfer signing a Collegetown lease: billed for the full year of campus housing anyway, $13,744 for a double, plus rent (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing ; https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates).
- Forgetting to reapply for the exemption: it lasts one academic year only.
- Signing with strangers met in a rushed search: each owes the whole rent if one leaves.
- Paying Collegetown prices for a late leftover when a downtown share is about 25% to 39% cheaper per bed (brief 10.10).
### Brief facts that matter

## Reader: International student
Key: international
### Situation
Undergrad or grad from abroad. Visa appointments cannot be more than 120 days before program start (https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students), so a lease is often signed from abroad and sight unseen. No SSN and often no US credit history. International undergrads follow the same two-year residential requirement; most international grads rent off campus (see grad-student). May stay in Ithaca over breaks. Often pays from family funds or a stipend; a professor said some visiting scholars get only $600 to $800 a month from home governments (https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell). Unfamiliar with upstate winters and heating costs.
### Questions in their words

### Decisions with numbers
- Do I need an SSN: no; Cornell International Services says you do not need one to open a bank account, rent an apartment or get a credit card (https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/life-cornell/faq-international-students). Guarantor and credit-history practice in Ithaca: not in the brief.
- How much to pay up front: New York caps the security deposit plus any rent advance at one month's rent (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108). A request to prepay several months is worth asking about under that cap (brief 9); expect first month's rent plus a deposit at signing (brief 6.5).
- Sight unseen or wait: no Cornell page offers arrival-week temporary housing (brief 4.5). The Cornell marketplace shows only listings from people who prove Cornell affiliation, and City listings must have a current Certificate of Compliance (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/rental-listings-resources ; https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/). Check the address on the safety tool before paying. A 2021 Sun story found virtual tours hid drawbacks (https://cornellsun.com/2021/10/13/just-months-after-move-in-next-years-hectic-housing-search-begins/).
- 12-month lease or a Cornell license: a 12-month lease covers breaks; a Cornell license charges $60 a day outside the occupancy period (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028). Students who cannot travel home may ask housing@cornell.edu about on-campus housing during breaks (https://global.cornell.edu/resources/immigration-advice-and-resources).
- Heat in the lease or not: Ithaca Renting includes heat and hot water at all properties (https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/); Cornell warns West Hill cottages may be uninsulated with very high heating bills and suggests asking NYSEG (800) 572-1111 for average annual heating costs (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods).
- A dispute with a landlord: Cornell Graduate School staff say it does not affect visa status or standing (https://www.gradschool.cornell.edu/announcements/grad-tips-renting/). With a lease, a tenant cannot be evicted for immigration status absent a substantial lease or code violation (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/immigrant-tenant-rights). The tenant hotline (607) 301-1560 gives advice with no income or geographic limits; full representation is only for City residents (https://international.globallearning.cornell.edu/maintaining-your-status/legal-resources).
- Bus or car: new degree students ride TCAT free all year with a Cornell ID (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes), so a cheaper downtown or Fall Creek unit costs nothing extra to commute in year one.
### Timeline
- Up to 120 days before program start: visa appointment.
- March to April (grads) or the fall before (undergrads wanting Collegetown): search and sign.
- Before arrival: confirm move-in date (most leases start about Aug 1), what is furnished, who sets up NYSEG and internet.
- Arrival: take the move-in inspection and photos; buy renters insurance.
- Winter break: ask housing@cornell.edu if a campus option is needed; a car left on City streets over break may be treated as abandoned (https://fcs.cornell.edu/announcements/2023-11-16/winter-parking-transportation-information).
- Aug 31 of the following year: free TCAT ends.
### Mistakes and cost
- Wiring a deposit for a unit you cannot see or check: no Ithaca sublet or rental scam story was found (voice.md gaps), so the safeguard is the COC check, the Cornell marketplace and the one-month cap.
- Paying an application fee: banned in New York (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A).
- An unheated or poorly insulated unit: NYSEG raised temporary rates from June 1, 2026, with a larger permanent case pending (https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/05/15/nyseg-and-rge-win-temporary-rate-hikes-as-larger-increases-still-loom/).
- Assuming a lease can be walked away from at the end of a program: housemates stay liable and Cornell aid will not intervene (https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus).

## Reader: Graduate or professional student
Key: grad-student
### Situation
About 95% of graduate and professional students live in non-university housing; about 400 live on campus (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). Not subject to the undergraduate residential requirement. Shops in a later market than undergrads. Usually wants quiet and some distance from undergraduate Collegetown. The 2026-27 Ithaca cost-of-attendance housing line is $19,962 a year, described as covering "comfortable, but modest, housing" (https://finaid.cornell.edu/graduate-students ; https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/tuition-rates/cost-of-attendance/). PhD students and graduate assistants ride TCAT free; other master's and professional students do not after year one. Many have a car.

Our arithmetic: $19,962 / 12 = $1,663.50 a month for housing in the budget.
- Bus pass: PhD students and graduate assistants get free OmniRide passes; everyone else after year one pays $1.50 a daytime weekday ride or $200 a year for OmniRide, which pays off after 134 rides, about 13 to 14 weeks of weekday commuting (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes ; brief 10.4).
- Parking: graduate assistants on full Ithaca assistantships can buy faculty and staff permits; others buy the B commuter permit at $359.77 if eligible (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/parking/parking-students).
- Quiet: ask what share of tenants are undergrads; Cliffside Terrace (405 Lake St) reviewers, all tagged Cornell Law, praise quiet and fast repairs and complain of limited bus and taken parking spots (https://www.veryapt.com/ApartmentReview-a16145-cliffside-terrace-apartments-ithaca).

- March to April: search for an August start; apply to Hasbrouck (rolling).
- June to July: a few places remain.

- Year one: free TCAT for all new degree students; from year two, free only for PhD students and assistants.
- Mar 31: last day for a prorated refund on a parking permit or OmniRide.
### Mistakes and cost
- Renting in Collegetown for convenience and getting undergraduate noise: the most repeated grad tip (brief 7.2).
- Ivy Ridge (802 Dryden Rd, Varna) reviews by Johnson MBA students describe dripping mini-splits, leaking pipes, slow management and limited parking, 6.4/10 on 7 reviews (https://www.veryapt.com/ApartmentReview-a23680-ivy-ridge-ithaca).

- Master's students assuming they ride free like PhD students: $1.50 a ride or $200 a year.
- Assuming Cornell will mediate: Off-Campus Living does not represent students in landlord disputes (https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/).

Key: student-family
### Situation
Usually a grad or professional student, sometimes an undergrad. Undergrads who are married or coming to Ithaca with legal dependents are exempt from the residential requirement (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). Of Cornell's on-campus grad households, 51% are family or partner households (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-students/your-cornell-community). The Sun reported family housing as scarce with high upfront costs (https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell). Household income is often one stipend.

- A grad student at a 2023 protest said he cannot afford to rent a home for his family (https://cornellsun.com/2023/03/24/cornell-graduate-students-united-protests-for-competitive-wages-additional-university-support/).
### Decisions with numbers
- Hasbrouck family unit or private: Hasbrouck family 2BR apartment (3rd floor) $1,500 a month; family 2BR townhouse $1,530 furnished or $1,475 unfurnished; 12-month contracts; units hold 2 to 4 people with no more than two adults (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates ; https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). Private averages: RentCafe Ithaca 2BR $2,459, 3BR $3,039 (https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/); Lansing West 2BR $1,825 and $1,870+, 3BR $1,694 (https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/ithaca/lansing-west-apartments/default.aspx).

Tell the landlord an occupant's name within 30 days. The Town of Ithaca limits single-family homes to one family plus one boarder, which matters for shared houses, not for a family (https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html).
- Schools: Cornell points families to the Ithaca City School District and worklife@cornell.edu (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-students/your-cornell-community). Which neighborhoods feed which schools: not in the brief. Childcare: not in the brief.
- Partner's commute: spouses and partners with a CU ID can buy the OmniRide student pass at 116 Maple Ave and ride night routes 90 and 92 free (https://fcs.cornell.edu/departments/transportation-delivery-services/sustainable-transportation-options/bus-services-privileges-passes). Children 17 and under ride TCAT free (https://tcatbus.com/fare-pass-options).
- Undergrad parent: file the dependents exemption in the Housing application during the open window; it lasts one year (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).
### Timeline
- March to April: search for an August start (grad timing: https://reedgroup.github.io/GraduateStudentResources/LifeAtCornell.html); apply to Hasbrouck (rolling).
- Before signing: confirm heat, laundry and parking; ask NYSEG for heating history on older houses.
- On move-in: name any occupant not on the lease within 30 days.
- Each April (undergrads): renew the dependents or married exemption (April 6 in 2026).
### Mistakes and cost
- Waiting for an affordable family unit to appear: the Sun reports scarcity and high upfront costs; a deposit plus first month on a $2,459 2BR is $4,918 at signing (our arithmetic: one month's deposit cap plus first month; brief 2.7, 3.1).

- Not budgeting for the partner's bus pass or a second car's parking.

### Situation
Pays their own way or relies on aid; an upper-year undergrad or a grad with a thin stipend. ## Reader: Student going abroad or away for a semester
Key: away-semester
### Situation
A junior or senior with a 12-month lease, or a student holding a Cornell license, who will study abroad, take leave or do a semester internship. Housemates stay behind on a joint lease. The Sun reported the housing search getting in the way of study-abroad plans (https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/20/students-housing-search-hampers-study-abroad). Cornell's housemate questionnaire asks what happens if a housemate studies abroad and gives no answer (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus/study-abroad/housemate-compatibility-questionnaire).
### Questions in their words
- "Can I break my Ithaca lease early if I study abroad or leave for a semester?" (q-housing-0025)
- "How do I sublet my apartment in Ithaca and does my landlord have to agree?" (q-housing-0004)
- "What are my options for storing my stuff and what does it cost?" (q-housing-0010)
- "Do I need renters insurance if I live off campus?" (q-housing-0009)
- "Does my landlord have to let me sublet if I'm abroad?" (from the section 9 sublet myth: brief 7.4)
### Decisions with numbers
- Cornell license holders: cancel penalty-free for study away, leave or internship by June 15 (fall) or Dec 1 (spring) for 2026-27, and June 15, 2027 or Dec 1, 2027 for 2027-28; later requests cost $500 (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027 ; https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028).
- Sublet or not: in a building with four or more units the tenant has a right to sublet with consent that cannot be unreasonably withheld; in three or fewer units the lease controls (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B ; https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Send the request by certified mail, return receipt, with term, subtenant name and addresses, reason, your address abroad, co-tenant and guarantor consent and a copy of the sublease. The landlord may ask for more within 10 days and must answer within 30; silence is consent. You stay liable for the subtenant's rent and damage.
- Sublet or assign: an assignment ends your liability, but the landlord may refuse without cause, and the only remedy for an unreasonable refusal is release on 30 days' notice (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B).
- Leaving without a sublet: ask for a written early-termination agreement with the date and amount; a termination fee may be unenforceable as liquidated damages; if you leave, the landlord must make good-faith efforts to re-rent at the lower of market or lease rent, and a new lease ends your debt (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E ; https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease). Housemates remain jointly liable for your share unless the landlord releases you.
- Where to advertise: the Cornell marketplace and the Off-Campus Living email list (sublets were free to list in 2019: https://cornellsun.com/2019/02/06/students-and-landlords-both-perpetuate-myths-about-housing-university-panelist-says/). Market sublet prices: Modern Living "from $900 per person"; Dryden South Fall 2026 sublet $1,395 (brief 3.2, 3.3). Typical sublet discounts: not in the brief.
- Storage: Big Red Shipping and Storage (Cornell's vendor, free pickup at apartments, no published price); Storage Scholars $172 a month labeled "2025 Avg." (not Ithaca-specific); self-storage 10x10 about $93 (SpareFoot) to $153 (SelfStorage.com); 4-Season Storage from $115 (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/moving-out/storage-shipping-options ; https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny ; https://sparefoot.com/Ithaca-NY-self-storage.html).
- Before leaving: notify contacts, forward mail, manage utilities, clean and secure the unit (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/living-campus).
### Timeline
- The fall before signing: agree with housemates, in writing, what happens if someone goes abroad.
- Two to three months before leaving: sublet request by certified mail (10-day and 30-day clocks).
- June 15 or Dec 1: Cornell license penalty-free cancellation.
- Early April: Storage Scholars asks for bookings (https://www.storagescholars.com/cities-we-serve/ithaca-ny).
- During the away term: rent still due unless a sublease, assignment or release is in place.
### Mistakes and cost
- Subletting without the written steps: a substantial breach of the lease (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B).
- Assuming aid helps: Cornell Financial Aid will not intervene in a mid-year lease break (https://finaid.cornell.edu/living-campus).
- Missing June 15 or Dec 1 on a Cornell license: $500.
- Leaving housemates to cover a share: a semester at the 4BR median is about $6,231 (our arithmetic: $1,246.25 x 5 months; brief 10.9).
- Study abroad is not addressed in the Cornell Law guide, which refers to the hotline 607-301-1560 (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).

## Reader: Parent paying for or co-signing housing

## Reader: First- or second-year who qualifies for an exemption
Key: exempt-underclass
### Situation
A first- or second-year who meets an exemption: married or partnered; a veteran discharged other than dishonorably (including service in a home country's military, not ROTC); with legal dependents; 21 or older by the start of the academic year (or 16 or younger living with a custodial parent); a transfer with two years on campus elsewhere or entering as a junior or senior; or a commuter who has lived at least six months with a custodial parent or guardian within 25 miles of campus (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy). May rent like an upperclass student, but only after approval.

Visiting scholars have no guaranteed housing and compete with grad students for Hasbrouck, Maplewood and co-ops (https://cornellsun.com/2024/11/12/high-costs-and-limited-support-plague-visiting-scholars-and-fellows-at-cornell). Usually wants furnished space. Often arrives mid-year.
### Questions in their words
- "Where can a visiting scholar or postdoc find furnished short-term housing near Cornell?" (q-housing-0023)
- "How do I sublet my apartment in Ithaca?" (q-housing-0004)
- "Can I break my Ithaca lease early if I leave for a semester?" (q-housing-0025)
- "Where should a grad student live?" (q-housing-0014)
- The Sun reported family housing as scarce with high upfront costs and some scholars' home stipends at $600 to $800 a month (same 2024 URL).
### Decisions with numbers
- Cornell options: International Scholar House, a month-to-month furnished 5BR, since September 2023 (2024 Sun URL); Hasbrouck takes rolling applications on 12-month contracts (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). Its price: not in the brief.
- Semester supply: Ithaca Renting lists "Spring/Fall Semester 2027" rows (https://ithacarenting.com/collegetown/); the Ithaca College portal lists Spring '27 roommate-matching leases (https://offcampushousing.ithaca.edu/city/ithaca-ny/listing/spring-27-and-full-year-leases-available-for-roommate-matching-foundation-housing-96176); Dryden South had a Fall 2026 sublet at $1,395 (https://www.apartments.com/dryden-south-sublets-available-fall-2026-ithaca-ny/yeje6qs/); downtown "available now" units (https://ithacarenting.com/downtown/).
- Lease or no lease: Cornell says a one-year lease offers some legal protection and units without a lease give flexibility but limited protection (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations). A month-to-month tenant generally gives one month's notice (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/breaking-a-lease).
- Short-term rentals: need a separate City permit (https://www.cityofithacany.gov/145/Housing-Inspections). Nightly or weekly rental prices: not in the brief.
- Getting around: spring entrants who are degree students ride TCAT free to Dec 31 of the following year; visiting scholars' transit status: not in the brief.
### Timeline
- Before arrival: apply to International Scholar House or Hasbrouck; search sublets and semester leases.
- December to January: spring-semester supply (sublets from students going abroad).
- Leaving: give one month's notice on a month-to-month; a fixed-term lease needs none at its end.
### Mistakes and cost
- Signing a 12-month lease for a six-month stay: the remaining months are owed unless sublet or re-rented (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E).
- Renting a room with no lease and no written terms: limited protection; an oral lease over a year is unenforceable (https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/understanding-your-lease).

## Reader: Alum curious about being an Ithaca landlord
Key: alum-landlord
### Situation
A Cornell alum, often a former Collegetown tenant, who owns or is thinking of buying a rental near campus. Needs the City and state duties, the student leasing calendar and the market. Zillow's typical Ithaca home value was $355,495 through August 2026 (search summary, not re-verified: https://www.zillow.com/ithaca-ny/home-values/). Purchase costs, taxes and returns: not in the brief.
### Questions in their words
- "What does Ithaca's Good Cause Eviction law mean?" (q-housing-0022)
- "What are the security deposit rules for renting in Ithaca?" (q-housing-0005)
- "How do I sublet and does my landlord have to agree?" (q-housing-0004), from the landlord's side
- "Which Collegetown landlords are good and which should I avoid?" (q-housing-0012)
- "What can I do if my landlord won't fix mold, bed bugs or no heat?" (q-housing-0018), from the landlord's side
- At the 2024 Good Cause hearing small landlords objected to the one-unit exemption threshold and predicted $2,000 to $5,000 in legal costs for non-renewals (their claim: https://www.wamc.org/2024-07-12/ithaca-is-the-latest-upstate-city-to-opt-into-good-cause-renter-protections ; https://cornellsun.com/2024/07/10/landlords-and-advocates-grapple-with-good-cause-eviction-law-at-public-hearing).
### Decisions with numbers
- Certificate of Compliance: required for buildings with three or more units, or units with five or six or more unrelated occupants depending on the source; valid 3 years for multiple dwellings; fees from $100 (one-family) and $175 (two-family) to $300 for 3 to 8 units and up to $2,500 for 251+ units; $10 per violation found, $50 per uncorrected violation, re-inspection $75 an hour, $75 missed-appointment charge (https://www.cityofithacany.gov/146/Certificate-of-Compliance ; https://www.cityofithacany.gov/147/Certificate-of-Compliance-Fees ; brief 8.1). The Cornell marketplace requires a current COC to post a City listing (https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/).
- Renewal timing: give current tenants 120 days' written notice before renewing, showing or re-letting, unless the tenant initialed a bolded first-page waiver (https://ecode360.com/27390826). Disputes can go to the City's housing court (https://wskg.org/ithaca-landlords-must-now-give-renters-120-days-before-renewing-leases/).
- Good Cause: Ithaca's small-landlord exemption is one unit statewide (state default 10); buildings with a certificate of occupancy from 2009 on are exempt for 30 years; owner-occupied buildings of 10 or fewer units are exempt; increases above the lower of 10% or 5% plus CPI are presumed unreasonable; to claim the small-landlord exemption the landlord must disclose owners and unit counts (https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction-law-notice-february-2025 ; https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/214 ; https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction).
- Deposits: at most one month's rent including any advance; held in trust, not mixed with your money; in buildings of six or more units, an interest-bearing New York account with a 1% annual administrative fee allowed; return with an itemized statement within 14 days or forfeit it; willful violations up to twice the deposit; offer a move-in inspection and, on notice, a move-out inspection (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108 ; https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/tenants).
- Fees: no application fee; background check at most $20 or cost; late fee only after 5 days, at most $50 or 5%; replacement keys at most 110% of cost (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A ; https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-I).
- Sublets and early exits: in 4+ unit buildings consent to a sublet cannot be unreasonably withheld and silence for 30 days is consent; if a tenant leaves early you must try in good faith to re-rent (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/226-B ; https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/227-E).
- Safety and penalties: renting an unsafe unit is a misdemeanor (up to $500 and 30 days), and displaced tenants are owed double the monthly rent prorated per day (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2019/03/city-council-votes-to-stiffen-penalties-for-noncompliant-landlords).
- Snow: owners must clear the adjacent sidewalk within 24 hours after snowfall begins; the City may clear and bill at least $50 a plowing; a lease may pass the duty to tenants (https://www.cityofithacany.gov/136/Exterior-Property-Maintenance ; https://www.cityofithacany.gov/m/faq?cat=46).
- Renting to second-years as affiliated housing: a Registered Student Organization, sprinklers throughout, at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate liability insurance naming Cornell (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/requirements-qualified-university-recognized-campus-housing).
- Town of Ithaca property: more than two unrelated people count as a family only if the Zoning Board of Appeals finds a functional equivalent (https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2017/2017-ny-slip-op-30471-u.html).
- Market rents: Collegetown per-bed medians $800 (6BR) to $2,025 (1BR) at one landlord for 2027-28; most leases end July 31 (brief 10.9, 2.6).
### Timeline
- Before the current COC expires: apply via OpenGov and pay the fee; one 30-day extension allowed.
- 120 days before re-letting (count start unresolved, brief 10.5): written notice to current tenants.
- Sept 15 to May 31: heat minimums in Ithaca.
- 30 to 90 days before lease end: Good Cause and 226-c renewal or increase notices.
- Within 14 days of move-out: deposit and itemized statement.
- By Aug 1 each year: DHCR usually publishes the CPI figure behind the rent standard.
### Mistakes and cost
- Letting the COC lapse: the 117 Thurston tenants found an expired certificate after signing, and the case drove the 2019 penalties (brief 5.2).
- Missing the 14-day deposit deadline: the whole deposit is forfeit.
- Charging an application or cleaning fee: banned.
- Offering cash for keys after a City vacate order: the Asteri $2,000 offer was called predatory and the displacement rule owes double rent (brief 2.24, 6.14).

## Reader: Student who needs disability accommodations
Key: accessibility
### Situation
Needs a unit with step-free access or other features, and an accessible commute up Ithaca's hills. Cornell tells grad students with disability housing needs to register first with Student Disability Services (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing). The brief has little else for this reader; fair-housing accommodation rules are not in it (the Cornell Law discrimination page returned 404: brief 8.3).

- Elevators: Dryden South and Collegetown Court list elevators (https://offcampus.housing.cornell.edu/city/ithaca-college-ny/listing/dryden-south-50961 ; https://ithacarenting.com/unit-details/?uid=223); Sheldon Court (Cornell) has had a broken elevator (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2026/04/the-misunderstood-dorm-sheldon-court).
- Safety features: the safety tool shows sprinklers, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and construction type for City rentals (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/housing-search-process/safe-living-environments).
- Reasonable accommodations or modifications from a private landlord, and accessible-unit counts: not in the brief.
### Timeline
- Before searching: register with Student Disability Services.
- Early August: AP permit purchase alongside permit sales (Aug 4 in 2026).
