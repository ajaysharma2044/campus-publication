### Building issues and maintenance
URL: https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/building-issues-maintenance (page dated Feb 2026 via user agreement; image Oct 2025)

| Fact | Value | Applies to | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warranty of habitability | Implied in every residential lease; covers serious problems not minor ones; lack of A/C generally does not qualify; code violation alone not automatically a breach | all | 2026-11-08 |
| Typical abatement (court experience per guide) | About 10-20% minor, ~30% moderate, 50-60% most serious | all | 2026-11-08 |
| Heat season | Statewide Oct 1 to May 31; in Ithaca Sept 15 to May 31 (per guide) | all | 2026-11-08 |
| Heat minimums | 6am-10pm: if outside below 55F, inside at least 68F. 10pm-6am: at least 62F regardless | all | 2026-11-08 |
| Utility shutoff notice (landlord-paid utilities) | Posted notice at least 18 days (apartment buildings); mailed at least 15 days (two-family houses); at least 30 days for heat shutoffs Nov 1 to Apr 15 | all | 2026-11-08 |
| Mold | No standalone section; listed as habitability issue; keep photos, tests, medical records; moving out usually not justified unless serious health risk and landlord ignores requests (constructive eviction) | all | 2026-11-08 |
| Bed bugs | Habitability violation; landlord must exterminate even if tenant suspected of bringing them, unless landlord proves tenant misconduct; written notice within 72 hours to units above, below, beside; common-area posting within 72 hours; tenants should report in writing and avoid self-treatment | all | 2026-11-08 |
