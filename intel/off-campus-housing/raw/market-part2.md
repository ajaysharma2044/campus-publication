Note: co-op rates quoted are per semester; ten-month equivalent roughly 2x, so a Triphammer double is $8,634 for the year (my arithmetic on the figures above, not published by Cornell). Cornell housing is a revocable license, not a lease; "security and key deposits will not be required" (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2026-2027, crawl 2026-11-06).

Cancellation fees on Cornell housing licence for returning juniors/seniors (same licence page, 2026-27): through Jan 31 $50; Feb 1-Mar 31 $500; Apr 1-Jun 30 $1,000; Jul 1-Aug 15 $2,000; after Aug 15, 50% of fall semester room rate. Penalty-free cancellation for study away/leave/etc. by June 15, 2026 (fall) and Dec 1, 2026 (spring); late request $500. Daily charge $60 for occupancy outside the stated period. This matters to anyone weighing an off-campus lease against a Cornell housing licence they have already signed. Dollar totals above are as written on the page.

Graduate housing facts (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/graduate-professional-student-campus-housing, crawl 2026-11-06): about 95% of Cornell's graduate and professional students live in non-university-owned housing; Maplewood Apartments managed by Greystar (furnished studio-4BR, all utilities, in-unit washer/dryer, private bath); Hasbrouck accepts families (max two adults per unit; units fit 2-4 people); Cornell's Off-Campus Housing Marketplace is "powered by College Pads, now part of StarRez".

## 9. Residential requirement and exemptions (official, matters for who is off-campus)

Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy (crawl 2026-11-06; policy text refers to AY 2026-27 deadlines)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | Full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year students; meal plan required when in on-campus housing |
| Year 2 options | residence halls; university-affiliated housing (recognized frats/sororities, University-owned co-ops, affiliated off-campus housing). A sophomore in a Greek house must be a member at time of application |
| Exemptions (each for one academic year; reapply yearly) | married; veteran/military (not ROTC); coming with legal dependents; age 21+ by start of the academic year, or 16 and under living with custodial parent; transfer with two years of on-campus residence at prior school, or entering as junior/senior; commuter (6+ months at permanent residence with custodial parent/guardian within 25 miles) |
| How to apply | Through the Housing application, in the open application window; not after a signed housing licence |
| AY 2026-27 dates on page | Housing application due April 6, 2026; confirm in-house Greek living by March 15, 2026; Spring Housing Selection for Rising Sophomores in April |
Implication: junior/senior transfers and 21+ students have no requirement and are the group actually in the open market early; sophomores may sign an off-campus lease only if exempt or in affiliated housing. Pair this with the lease timing in part 1 section 5. For 2027-28, see corpus page https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/academic-year-housing-license-2027-2028 (not read).

## 15. Gaps and stale data (what the public data lacks)

1. No public median or distribution of rent per bedroom for Collegetown; the one automated source (RentCafe) counts only 50+ unit buildings and reports whole-unit averages.
2. Listing aggregators block automated reads; the Cornell/Ithaca College portal pages are readable but many show "Call For Price".
3. No dated record of when each landlord opens leasing. Evidence: Sun 2022 (early Sept), 2018 headline (up to 16 months before move-in), Nov 2026 listings already showing Aug 2027 starts.
4. 120-day notice rule: confirmed from WSKG April 2022 (effective May 31, 2022). Not confirmed against current code text or any amendment.
5. Fall Creek, downtown, South Hill, Lansing rent samples; parking, internet and renters-insurance prices; storage prices; TCAT upper-level pass price; international student and family-specific housing costs outside Cornell: all NOT FOUND.
6. Cornell's Housing Rates page has no date stamp; the neighborhoods page repeats blocks; Cornell's license notes final rates were due March 2026 and earlier numbers were projected.
7. Possible contradiction to resolve: RentCafe Collegetown $2,815 (Oct 30, 2026) vs search summary of $2,985 for April 2026; and the leaselenses page's 14-day vs 30-day deposit deadline.
