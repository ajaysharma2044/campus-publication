### Lease-review checklist Cornell gives (signing-lease page)
Questions for landlord: how long a landlord, how many properties, how long last tenants stayed and where they went, who repairs, preferred contact method (general/emergency). References: when rented, how long, why moving, repair/late-rent responsiveness, deposit return, respect, proper notice to end agreement, would rent again. Other screening: public records for code compliance, BBB/review sites/Chamber complaints, visit neighbors, read the whole lease. Lease terms to check: rent amount/due date/late penalty/price changes; deposit amount/conditions/return date; termination, sublet ability and conditions; parking; extra costs (utilities, pets, guests, parking, furnishings, cleaning); restrictions (smoking, noise, storage, pets, alterations, parties); occupancy dates, renewal notification requirements, min/max occupants; damages; changes to agreement; laundry; inspection (incl. "Certificate of Compliance (Ithaca City only)"); cleaning; snow removal; owner/manager names and emergency contacts. Points to NY AG Tenants Rights Guide.

### Checklists page
URL: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/checklists-and-considerations. Smoke detector required by law; two means of egress; arrange utilities/phone before move-in; inventory + photos to landlord after move-in; buy renter's insurance; written receipts for rent/deposits; rooms in a student or private house tend to be cheapest; one-year lease "offers a certain amount of legal protection"; units without a lease offer flexibility but limited legal protection.
