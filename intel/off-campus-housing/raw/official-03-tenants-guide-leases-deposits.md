## 11. Lease renewal, rent increases, Good Cause Eviction
URL: https://sites.lawschool.cornell.edu/tenants-advocacy/lease-renewal

| Fact | Value (per guide) | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Renewal is the norm; expired lease continues month-to-month unless proper non-renewal notice (RPL 232-c) | 2026-11-08 |
| Auto-renew clause | Landlord must give written notice of clause 15 to 30 days before tenant's opt-out deadline (GOL 5-903) | 2026-11-08 |
| Notice to refuse renewal or raise rent by more than 5% | Less than 1 year: 30 days; 1-2 years: 60 days; more than 2 years: 90 days (longer of lease term or occupancy). Improper notice means month-to-month on existing terms | 2026-11-08 |
| Tenant notice to leave | None at end of 12-month lease; month-to-month needs one month | 2026-11-08 |
| 120-day Ithaca rule | NOT mentioned by this guide (guide section 4.2-4.3 read via summary). Remains unconfirmed; see section 7 | 2026-11-08 |
| Good Cause Eviction | State law effective April 20, 2024; applies only where municipality opted in; Ithaca has opted in (per guide). Gives right to renew with exceptions (landlord's own residence, demolition, leaving rental market, proven legal ground e.g. nonpayment or uncured violation after 10 days' written notice) | 2026-11-08 |
| Exclusions | Small landlords (in Ithaca: owns only one rental unit), owner-occupied buildings with 10 or fewer units, condos/co-ops, hotel rooms, dorms, units under other rent regulation | 2026-11-08 |
| Rent increase presumption | Presumed unreasonable above lower of (5% + regional CPI change) or 10%; DHCR usually publishes CPI figure by August 1 | 2026-11-08 |
| Retaliation | No refusal to renew or unreasonable increase for good-faith complaint within past year; not applicable to owner-occupied buildings with fewer than 4 units | 2026-11-08 |

### Cross-check flag
Section 7 (search summary) said the local law's rent standard is "inflation plus 5%, max 10%"; the guide agrees (lower of 5%+CPI or 10%). The "120 days" city notice rule is the only open contradiction, and only vendor-sourced.
