Source: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-living/qualified-off-campus-housing (crawl 2026-11-06)
- Cornell's 2-year residential requirement covers full-time, Ithaca-based first- and second-year undergrads, with a required meal plan. In year two students can live in residence halls or approved university-affiliated housing (fraternity/sorority, co-ops, others).
- Eight university-owned co-ops named: Prospect of Whitby, 660 Stewart, 302 Wait Avenue, Triphammer Cooperative, Von Cramm, Wait Terrace, Wari Cooperative, Watermargin.
- Other affiliated houses listed with addresses: Alpha Zeta (214 Thurston Ave), Center for Jewish Living (106 West Ave; (607) 219-3019), Seal and Serpent (305 Thurston Ave).
- Greek houses: rent usually billed via the Bursar; minimum live-in of a semester or two in most chapters; "parlor"/living-out fees exist.
