### Contradiction with section 7 (search summary of old code)
Section 7 reported inspection every 5 years for 1-2 family units and every 3 years for 3+ units or 5+ unrelated occupants, with certificates up to 5 years. The current city COC page says COC is for 3+ unit buildings or units with 6+ unrelated persons (not 1-2 family houses by default), valid 3 years, with fraternities/sororities/dorms valid 1 year. The city's Housing Inspections page separately says inspections are "required for all residential rental dwellings located within the City of Ithaca" (short phrase), with no intervals. So even the city's two pages are not aligned on whether 1-2 family rentals need a COC. Do not state a rule for single-family student houses until the code text is read.
