### Part 0. Contradictions and errors on Cornell's own neighborhoods page (high value gap)
- Fall Creek, Downtown Ithaca and The Commons entries carry identical copy (all "at the base of East Hill", "20 to 30-minute uphill walk"); Belle Sherman and East Hill identical ("15-minute level walk"); Lansing and Northeast identical. Cayuga Heights and Cornell Heights are merged in one paragraph. Fall Creek is called "west of campus" but described as "base of East Hill". Page gives no rents, no bus route numbers, no lease-timing guidance. URL: https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/housing-search-process/ithaca-neighborhoods
- Two URL families exist for the same Cornell content (/campus-living/ and /campus-housing/, /sub/ and /sub/sub/ variants), so Cornell's own pages compete with each other in search (see Q9, Q15, Q17).
- Third-party site (live-cornell.pages.dev, unofficial) places Belle Sherman "east of Collegetown"; Cornell page text places it "south of campus" and also "just east of Collegetown" (same entry). Minor contradiction.
