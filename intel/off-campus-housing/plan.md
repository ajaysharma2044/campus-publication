# Content and data plan: Off-campus housing in Ithaca for Cornell students
This plan covers off-campus housing for Cornell students. It runs from who may rent at all, through the Collegetown rush, per-bed prices, neighborhoods and the city line, leases, deposits, the 120-day rule, Good Cause, repairs, sublets, leaving early, moving out, co-ops and Greek houses, to landlords' duties. The pillar is cornell-off-campus-housing-guide.
Rows: 1 pillar, 21 spokes, 8 reader guides, 8 tools, 4 datasets (from brief section 10), 18 start-here paths (one per reader key) and 4 rewrites. The map has nine "high" readers. Eight get a new guide. The transfer guide is the rewrite of /housing/cornell-transfer-housing/, which already answers that question.
Five questions belong to other topics' pages, which this plan only links to. Storage (q-housing-0010) goes to /getting-around/cornell-move-out-storage-shipping-options/. The monthly cost of living (q-housing-0027) is this plan's spoke what-a-month-off-campus-costs-cornell (the money topic's cost-of-living page is rejected and unpublished). Commute and parking by area go to /getting-around/getting-around-ithaca-off-campus-renters/. Utility bills go to /money/ithaca-utilities-cost-winter-heating-bill-apartment-students/. The grad monthly budget goes to /money/cornell-grad-student-off-campus-budget-monthly-rent-ithaca/. The dorm-only demand rows (0007, 0016, 0020, 0021, 0024, 0026) are out of scope.
No lane is incomplete, but four areas are thin:
- City Code 258-10. Its text was seen only as a search snippet (eCode360 returned 403). This affects the 120-day spoke, the when-to-sign spoke and the landlord spoke.
- The DHCR Good Cause thresholds. Their PDF could not be read. This affects the Good Cause spoke and its checker.
- Student voice. Reddit could not be read, and nothing was found on sublets, international guarantors or deposits after 2020.
- Accessibility. The brief has almost nothing, so that reader gets a start-here path only.
