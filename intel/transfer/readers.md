## Reader: Sophomore transfer from a four-year college

Key: transfer

### Situation
In the first year at a four-year college elsewhere, with two semesters done by fall 2027 entry; applying for sophomore standing. Must live on campus for one academic year unless they already lived on campus two years elsewhere, which a sophomore has not (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/new-transfer-undergraduates). Usually 19 to 20 years old (average age 20 in the 2021 profile, https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf). Family pays, with need-based aid only. Knows college life but not Cornell's college-by-college system. Size: 578 of 674 fall 2021 transfers (85.8%) came from four-year schools (IRP 2021 counts, brief 10.6); 625 transfers enrolled in fall 2025 in all (https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cornell-Common-Data-Set-2025-26.pdf). How many enter as sophomores rather than juniors: not in the brief.

### Questions in their words
- "What's Cornell's transfer acceptance rate?" (Q9.1; forum guesses range 7% to 20%)
- "What GPA do I need to transfer to Cornell?" (Q9.2)
- "When is the transfer deadline, and is there a second deadline?" (Q9.3, Q9.18)
- "Is housing guaranteed for transfers, and do I have to live on campus?" (Q9.11; voice theme housing, 9 sources, e.g. https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus)
- "How do transfers make friends when first-years already bonded?" (Q9.21; voice theme isolation, 10 sources, e.g. https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/05/a-letter-to-incoming-transfer-students-what-no-one-tells-you)
- "Who fills out the College Report and the Mid-Term Report?" (Q9.24)
- "Will my classes count, and why can't I enroll before add/drop?" (Q9.5; voice theme credits and closed classes, https://cornellsun.com/2023/01/26/spring-transfers-start-cornell-journey-mid-year-and-prepare-for-challenges-ahead/)

### Decisions with numbers
- Apply now or wait a year: apply in the spring of the first year. A first-semester student cannot apply, and Cornell takes students with two to four semesters done (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/330-when-should-i-apply-to-cornell-as-a-transfer/). Sophomore entry loses no credits under the 60 cap; a 90-credit junior loses 30 and spends ten semesters in college (brief 10.4; https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits).
- Which college: you apply to one college and usually one major (https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants). Global Development (CALS) takes sophomores only, so it is open to this reader and closed to juniors (https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants). Engineering standing for a sophomore is first or second semester (https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements).
- Grades: admitted transfers are "generally" 3.3 or better (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/); judge against your college's number: CALS minimum 3.0 (3.5 Biological Sciences), ILR typical 3.4, Human Ecology competitive at 3.5, AAP minimum 2.7, Engineering none stated (brief 2.4).
- Courses this year: take the sophomore prerequisites now. Human Ecology wants 23 credits (8 lab science, 6 writing, 3 statistics, 3 precalculus or calculus, 3 intro psychology) (https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer); ILR wants a composition course, intro economics, math and a science-and-technology course (https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf); Brooks wants 15 credits (https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/undergraduate/apply/transfer-applicants/external-transfers/). Take them for letter grades: CALS and Human Ecology refuse pass/fail (brief 6.4).
- Deadlines: March 15, 2027 for the Transfer Common App, the $85 fee or waiver and any portfolio; April 15, 2027 for the College Report, Academic Evaluation, Mid-Term Report and transcripts; CSS Profile and FAFSA also March 15, 2027 (https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants; https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants).
- Housing: you must live on campus for one year in upper-level housing (South Campus, West Campus House System, Program Houses, co-ops); apply on the Housing and Dining Portal within ten days of getting a NetID; no fee, no deposit (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/apply-housing/new-undergrad-application-process). The pages describe a requirement and "may be placed," not a guarantee (brief 2.9). The only building you choose by name is a Program House; everything else is random auto-allocation. A co-op double costs $6,974 to $9,962 a year against $13,744 for a hall double (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/housing-rates).
- Cost of the required year before aid: $96,318 billed for a hall double with the Unlimited plan at endowed or non-NY tuition; range $95,218 (triple) to $99,169 (super single) (brief 10.1; https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters).
- Year two: free to rent after the required year. Desirable Collegetown leases sign up to 16 months ahead (https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/), so start looking in the first fall; or enter upper-level selection with current students (Oct 2 to 8 windows in 2025, https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed).
- Health plan: waive with comparable coverage by July 31 (2026 pattern) or pay $1,705 for fall plus $2,046 for spring, $3,751 in year one (https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27; https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/node/179).

### Timeline
- Fall 2026 (first semester elsewhere): not yet eligible; take prerequisites; FAFSA opens October 1, 2026 (brief 4.1).
- January to March 2027: line up the College Report official and one instructor for the Academic Evaluation (brief 6.2).
- March 15, 2027: application, fee, portfolio; CSS Profile and FAFSA.
- April 15, 2027: everything else, including the self-completed Mid-Term Report.
- Late April to June 2027: rolling decisions (https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants); Cornell Aid Estimate soon after admission. CDS reply date July 1; no deposit deadline found (brief 2.2).
- Within ten days of the NetID: housing application.
- Early to mid-June (2024 pattern): college emails about credit and enrollment; mid-June housing confirmation or guaranteed waitlist; room swaps mid-June to mid-July.
- July: fall tuition bill (early July), housing bill July or August; SHP waiver by July 31 (2026 pattern).
- August 1: last day to change the move-in slot.
- Mid-August (2026 pattern: transfers moved in Aug 18, NSO Aug 18 to 23): orientation with transfer small groups (https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-student-orientation).
- First week of classes: enroll during add/drop; add deadline about two weeks in (Sep 8 in 2026) (https://registrar.cornell.edu/calendars-exams/academic-calendar).
- Last day of fall instruction (Dec 7 in 2026): written request for AP, IB or A-Level credit to the college registrar (https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).
- First fall: start the year-two housing search.

### Mistakes and cost
- Sending only the March 15 pieces and missing April 15: an incomplete file; the College Report is a standing form from your school's official, not a recommendation, and the Mid-Term Report is yours to fill in (brief 6.2).
- Assuming AP credit carries over: it does not automatically; missing the first-semester written request forfeits it (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/332-as-a-transfer-student-will-i-automatically-receive-ap-ib-or-a-level-examinations-credit-if-it-was-accepted-at-my-previous-institution/; https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services).
- Missing the ten-day housing window: earlier years show the cost; in 2016 transfers who applied after the then July 1 deadline were among about 30 placed in converted North Campus lounges (https://cornellsun.com/2016/10/03/transfer-students-forced-to-live-in-north-campus-lounges/). No lounge reports since 2022 were found (brief 6.5).
- Signing a private lease for the required year: the license binds new transfers, who cannot cancel unless they leave Cornell or win an exemption (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/housing-details/rates-amenities-licenses/12-month-undergraduate-housing-license-2026-2027); a lease on top would double the rent.
- Not waiving the health plan by the deadline: $1,705 billed for fall (brief 10.7).
- Waiting until spring of year one to look for a year-two apartment: the desirable Collegetown stock is gone (brief 3.8).

## Reader: Junior-entry transfer

Key: transfer-junior

### Situation
Has four semesters (or an associate's degree, or about 60 credits) done and would arrive as a junior. Exempt from the residential requirement as a matriculating junior (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy), so housing becomes a private lease signed after an April to June admission. Cornell discourages junior transfer from four-year schools (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/323-can-i-transfer-to-cornell-in-my-junior-year-from-another-college/). Size: not in the brief (no standing split is published).

### Questions in their words
- "Can I transfer to Cornell as a junior?" (Q9.16)
- "How many of my credits will transfer?" (Q9.5)
- "Can I still graduate in four years total?" (Q9.16)
- "Where do transfer juniors live off campus, and when do I start looking?" (Q9.29)
- "What extra courses do juniors need?" (Q9.23)
- "Do I have to live on campus if I'm a junior?" (Q9.11; the 2025 sophomore transfer who looked off campus but did not want a lease, https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/11/new-changes-to-fall-housing-selection-leave-some-rising-juniors-seniors-disappointed)

### Decisions with numbers
- Junior or sophomore entry: for a student with 90 credits, 60 transfer, 30 are lost and college takes ten semesters; at 75 credits, 15 are lost and nine semesters; the four-semester residence rule means overloading cannot shorten it; the extra year costs $74,970 in endowed tuition and fees before housing and aid (brief 10.4; https://as.cornell.edu/registrar/transferring-credits). Up to 60 credits nothing is lost.
- Eligibility by college: CALS bars anyone with more than four semesters at a four-year school, and a CALS applicant with four semesters or an associate's must enter as a junior and cannot choose sophomore entry (https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants). Hotel bars current juniors and seniors (https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/). Engineering takes juniors only in their first semester of junior standing (https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements). Global Development takes sophomores only.
- Courses: juniors need more. ILR juniors need two composition courses, micro and macroeconomics, math, a science-and-technology course and at least two ILR elective credits (https://www.ilr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files-d8/2026-02/transfer-guidelines-fa-26-final.pdf). Human Ecology juniors add microeconomics, a design studio, and organic chemistry for pre-health (https://www.human.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer). A&S juniors need the intended major's prerequisites (https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Animal Science juniors are expected to have met CALS chemistry and math (snippet; https://cals.cornell.edu/animal-science/degrees-programs/undergraduate-studies/transfer-curriculum).
- Caps: Engineering allows 33 credits per full-time year and 66 in all, so two years elsewhere fill it; Architecture 64 with 90 credits required at Cornell (https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Exam credit sits inside the 60 cap, so AP credit fills it faster (brief 10.4).
- Housing: exempt; choose between a lease and voluntary campus housing. Collegetown large-building averages: studio $2,006, one-bedroom $2,342, two-bedroom $3,414 a month (updated Oct 30, 2026, https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/ny/ithaca/collegetown/). A hall double works out to about $1,527 a month over nine months (brief 10.8). Per-bedroom snippets ran $785 to $1,950 (unopened). Whether exempt transfers can get campus housing, and on what terms: not in the brief.
- Meal plan if off campus: Bear Choice ($6,150), Bear Basic ($4,978), Off-Campus Value ($1,824.60), Flex 10/500 ($1,348), Just Bucks ($1,000) are open to off-campus students (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/dining/meal-plans-rates/undergraduate-meal-plans).

### Timeline
- Fall of the second year elsewhere: confirm junior-level prerequisites are done or in progress; FAFSA from October 1, 2026.
- March 15, 2027: application and aid forms; April 15: other materials.
- Late April to June 2027: decision. As soon as admitted: start the lease search, because desirable leases go up to 16 months ahead (https://cornellsun.com/2018/11/07/desirable-collegetown-leases-leave-market-up-to-16-months-before-residents-move-in/) and the brief has no plan for a student admitted in June (brief 9 Q29).
- Early July: tuition bill. August: move-in (lease start dates: not in the brief).
- First Cornell semester: exam credit request by the last day of instruction.

### Mistakes and cost
- Transferring with 75 to 105 credits: 15 to 45 credits lost and one to three extra semesters, each year about $74,970 in tuition and fees (brief 10.4).
- Applying to CALS after more than four semesters at a four-year school: ineligible.
- Waiting for a decision before looking at housing: the best Collegetown stock is already signed; voice: transfers in 2018 felt nervous and unsafe living off campus (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2018/12/isolated-vulnerable-and-nervous-transfer-students-express-frustration-over-not-being-able-to-live-on-campus).
- Assuming exemption is automatic for a sophomore who lived on campus one year: the exemption needs two years on campus elsewhere or junior standing; individual exemptions are requested through the housing application one year at a time (https://scl.cornell.edu/residential-life/housing/campus-housing/residential-policy).

### Brief facts that matter
2.1 (FAQ 323 junior warning), 2.6 (CALS standing, Engineering standing, ILR and Human Ecology junior lists, Hotel eligibility), 2.7 (caps, residence minimums), 2.9 (exemption), 2.11 (off-campus meal plans), 3.8 and 5.3 (rents, 16-month leases), 4.6 (exempt junior), 10.4 (credit-loss table), 10.8 (hall versus rent).

## Reader: Community college transfer

Key: transfer-community-college

### Situation
At a two-year college, often in New York (34 of 96 community college transfers in 2021 were from NY), often working long hours, sometimes first-generation; believes Cornell is out of reach or unaffordable (https://www.news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/05/community-college-transfers-flourish-cornell). Size: Cornell says about a quarter of transfers come from community colleges (https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/325-does-cornell-prefer-transfer-applicants-from-certain-colleges-or-types-of-colleges/); the only official count is 96 of 674 fall 2021 transfers, 14.2% (https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf); Cornell admits about 100 a year (Chronicle 2024).

### Questions in their words
- "Is there a guaranteed transfer from community college to Cornell?" (Q9.6)
- "How does community college to Cornell work?" (Q9.28)
- "Do they even take community college students, or do you need a perfect GPA?" (voice: 2014 College Confidential thread, https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/what-are-my-chances-for-transferring-to-cornell/1642313; CollegeVine, https://www.collegevine.com/faq/182748/advice-for-transferring-from-community-college-to-cornell)
- "Will my community college or online classes transfer?" (Q9.5)
- "Can I afford Cornell coming from community college?" (Q9.7; Chronicle 2024 counselor on the cost misconception)
- "Is the TC3 path a direct path?" (Q9.6; https://cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/agricultural-science-students-at-tc3-now-have-a-direct-transfer-path-to-cornell-cals)

### Decisions with numbers
- Odds: no preference by school type; admitted transfers are generally 3.3 or better (FAQ 325). Forum talk of near-perfect GPAs is voice, not policy.
- Pathway or open application: there is no general guaranteed transfer program (brief 2.20). TC3's Sustainable Farming and Food Systems A.A.S. gives "priority consideration," not a guarantee, for CALS Agricultural Sciences, with a 3.0 GPA and B or better in all transfer courses; one year at TC3 (two semesters plus summer) then four semesters at Cornell (https://cals.cornell.edu/news/2025/08/cornell-cals-and-tc3-establish-transfer-pathway-for-agriculture-students). SUNY Broome's business A.S. leads to the online part-time SCE Bachelor of Professional Studies, not the residential degree: 45 to 60 transferable credits, at least four years past high school, applications January 2027, first cohort August 2027 (https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/06/cornell-sce-suny-broome-create-transfer-pathway-new-online-bachelors-degree). SUNY Broome 2017, SUNY Orange 2014, CCSF 2012 and Pathways to Success 2006 to 2008 agreements: current status unverified (brief 2.20).
- Standing: in CALS, an associate's degree or four semesters means junior standing, and the student cannot choose sophomore entry (https://cals.cornell.edu/education/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants).
- Course choice: credit cannot be denied because a course was at a community college or online, but it must be about 80% similar, C or better, from a regionally accredited school (https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). Chemical Engineering says to apply as a sophomore if possible because some sophomore courses are not offered at most two-year colleges (https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer-applicants/requirements). Math below precalculus, study skills, orientation and remedial courses never transfer.
- Recommendation: one instructor of a full-credit course writes the Academic Evaluation (https://admissions.cornell.edu/apply/transfer-applicants).
- Money: fee waiver applied automatically if the fee is a burden and you apply for aid; CSS fee waived for AGI up to $100,000; families under $125,000 with typical assets get tuition covered by grants, under $75,000 the full cost (https://finaid.cornell.edu/apply-aid/prospective-students-or-first-time-aid-applicants; https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/03/board-trustees-approves-2026-27-budget-parameters).
- Support after arrival: a CALS transfer student success navigator (similar roles in ILR and Human Ecology), PAVER and the CC STEP group (Chronicle 2024).

### Timeline
- First community college year: pick courses that map to the target college's list, for letter grades.
- Second semester (sophomore entry) or fourth semester (junior entry): apply by March 15, 2027 with aid forms; April 15 for the rest.
- TC3 pathway: no deadlines or credit counts published (brief 2.20); same central dates apply.
- After admission: send syllabi when asked; keep them until the Cornell degree audit is right (brief 6.4).
- August: orientation; first semester: exam credit request by the last day of instruction.

### Mistakes and cost
- Treating TC3 or an old SUNY agreement as a guarantee: no backup applications, and a denial with nothing else lined up (brief 1, mistake 2).
- Taking courses pass/fail or below C: no credit in CALS or Human Ecology; C- fails in Engineering (brief 6.4).
- Finishing an associate's and wanting sophomore entry to CALS: not allowed; junior standing is fixed.
- Not applying for aid because Cornell looks too expensive: the 2024 Chronicle counselor called this a common misconception; in fall 2021, transfers with Cornell need-based grants averaged $44,712 (https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Profile2021-Fall-Transfers.pdf).
- Confusing the SCE online BPS pathway with admission to a residential college: a different degree and program.

### Brief facts that matter
2.4 (FAQ 325: no preference, quarter share, 3.3), 2.7 (community college and online rule, 80% test), 2.15 (aid thresholds), 2.20 (all pathways with status), 3.3 (2021 counts: 96 community college), 3.5 and 10.6 (14.2% versus a quarter), 5.4 (sending schools), 6.8 (route steps), 7.7 (forum odds talk), 7.9 (myths).

## Reader: Transfer Option holder

Key: transfer-option

### Situation
Applied as a first-year and got a Transfer Option instead of admission: an invitation to apply for sophomore transfer after one full-time year elsewhere, not an offer of admission, and not deferrable (https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Spending that year at another school (one 2025 writer used Ithaca College). Anxious about whether it is guaranteed. Size: the number of offers is not published (https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants).

### Questions in their words
- "What is the Transfer Option, and is it guaranteed?" (Q9.20; College Confidential decision-night thread, https://talk.collegeconfidential.com/t/cornell-class-of-2024-transfer-option/2087946)
- "How do I apply under the Transfer Option, and do I redo the whole Common App?" (Q9.27; Reddit mirror, https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qbvg0g/snapshots/2026-01-16T10%3A01%3A13.578438Z)
- "What GPA do I need to keep my Transfer Option?" (Q9.2; Koppelman, https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2023/7/3/using-the-cornell-transfer-option-read-this-first)
- "Can I take a gap year instead?" (Q9.20)
- "When do TO decisions come out?" (Q9.18)
- "Is there a group chat for TO admits?" (Reddit 2026 snippet; admissions Q&A on a TO Discord, https://admissions.cornell.edu/node/6748)

### Decisions with numbers
- Guarantee: the central page does not say admission is guaranteed and gives no number of offers; A&S calls it "not an offer of admission" (https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants; https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Plan as if it is a strong but conditional invitation. A 2025 IC-to-Cornell student wrote that it was described to him inconsistently (https://www.cornellsun.com/article/2025/08/from-both-sides-of-the-hill-an-ic-transfers-perspective).
- The year elsewhere (A&S): two full-time semesters or three quarters at a regionally accredited degree-granting school; summer does not count; broad liberal arts and sciences, not business, engineering or pre-professional programs; at least one writing-intensive composition course encouraged; a 3.5 GPA every semester with no grade below B-; A&S cannot pre-approve courses; moving toward a more selective or sequential major may hurt (https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). A gap year does not meet this. Other colleges set their own conditions on pages no lane opened; a consultant says most CALS TO majors need 3.0 and Biological Sciences 3.5 (voice).
- Which application: only offer holders use the Transfer Option application, with their earlier portal credentials; no application fee (https://admissions.cornell.edu/transfer-option-applicants).
- Materials: all college transcripts, the final high school transcript, College Report, Mid-Term Report and college extras: CALS resume, course schedule, AP scores and two essays; Human Ecology resume, AP scores, a 600-word essay and design supplement; SC Johnson resume; ILR AP scores; A&S none (same URL).
- Deadline: March 15 for the application and aid (2026 pattern; the page shows "Application Available Winter 2027"); the Mid-Term Report may come later (same URL).
- After entry (A&S): at least 60 Cornell credits and four semesters in residence (https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process). Housing: one required year on campus like any sophomore transfer.

### Timeline
- Spring 2026 (first-year cycle): offer received; it cannot be deferred.
- Fall 2026: full-time first semester elsewhere; 3.5 and no grade below B- for A&S.
- Winter 2027: application opens ("Application Available Winter 2027").

Key: transfer-engineering

### Situation

### Decisions with numbers
- Aid now or never: file the CSS Profile (code 2098) after October 1, 2026 and by March 15, 2027; generally no fee waiver; an applicant who does not apply by the deadline cannot receive undergraduate aid later (https://finaid.cornell.edu/first-year-and-transfer-students-international; https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-international-applicants). Asking for aid makes admission need-aware; Cornell says it meets 100% of need for those admitted who applied. No merit, athletic or talent aid (snippet).

- Credit from abroad: generally accepted from Ministry-of-Education-recognized institutions; remedial, ESL and international military courses never transfer; English courses are reviewed by Literatures in English (https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services). A-Level and IB transcripts go to the college registrar (https://scl.cornell.edu/get-involved/tatkon-center-new-students/new-students-cornell/transfer/course-enrollment).
- Cost: endowed or non-NY tuition $73,946; billed year $95,218 to $99,169 (brief 10.1). An international student is non-NY at a contract college too.

- Housing: the one-year rule applies the same way (unless exempt).

### Timeline
- October 1, 2026 onward: CSS Profile as soon as possible.
- March 15, 2027: application and CSS Profile; April 15 other materials with translations.
- Early April 2027: aid offers "tentatively," for those already admitted.
- Late April to June: decision; then SEVIS transfer (timing not in the brief), housing within ten days of the NetID.
- Mid-August (2026 pattern): move-in and orientation.

### Mistakes and cost
- Skipping the CSS Profile to look stronger in a need-aware pool, then needing aid later: aid is closed for the whole undergraduate career.
- Assuming a grant above tuition is tax-free: 14% withheld without a treaty.
- Counting on ESL or remedial courses for credit: none transfers.
- Not budgeting for the health plan: $3,751 in year one if not waived (brief 10.7); whether international students can waive: not in the brief.

## Reader: Transfer with a partner or children

Key: student-family

- "Is there support for student parents?" (Q9.26)

- Health plan dependents (2026-27): spouse or partner, or one child, $4,092 from July or $3,751 from August; two or more children $8,184 or $7,502 (https://studenthealthbenefits.cornell.edu/shp-costs-billing-important-dates/shp-costs-dates-2026-27).

## Reader: Current Cornell student switching colleges

Key: internal-transfer

### Situation
Already enrolled at Cornell and wants another Cornell college; a separate track whose GPA rules rank in searches for external transfer. Includes students who entered Cornell as external transfers and now want to switch. Size: not in the brief.

### Questions in their words
- "How do I switch colleges inside Cornell?" (Q9.13)
- "What GPA do I need for internal transfer?" (Q9.2)
- "Can I switch to Hotel or AAP for spring?" (Q9.13)
- "I transferred into A&S; can I move to another college?" (Q9.13)
- "Is internal transfer easier than external?" (voice: https://cornellsun.com/2019/11/26/internal-transfers-finding-a-new-home-within-cornell/)

### Decisions with numbers
- A&S internal: cumulative 3.0 and 3.0 in 12 credits in the application semester; earliest after the second semester, latest end of the first semester as a junior; apply at internaltransfer.cornell.edu/apply; the 2026 deadline was May 1 (https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/admissions-requirements-and-process).
- Hotel internal: fall May 1, spring December 1 (11:59 pm ET); Cornell GPA 3.0; first- or second-year only; ECON 1110 or equivalent; essay up to 650 words; students who came from other institutions are not eligible; office hours November 18, 2026 (https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate/bs-hotel-administration/application-requirements/).
- AAP internal: spring December 1, fall May 1; GPA 2.7; first-years may apply at the end of the second semester; portfolio as PDF (https://aap.cornell.edu/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/).
- External transfers in A&S: may apply to another college only after two semesters at Cornell; A&S re-evaluates credits; strongly discouraged (same A&S URL). Engineering transfers must stay in their major one year; CALS transfers cannot add AEM.

### Timeline
- November 18, 2026: Hotel office hours. December 1, 2026: Hotel and AAP spring deadlines. Early January 2027: Hotel spring decisions. May 1, 2027 (2026 pattern): fall deadlines at Hotel, AAP and A&S.

### Mistakes and cost
- Following external-transfer rules (March 15, Common App): wrong process.
- An external transfer applying to Hotel internally: ineligible.

### Brief facts that matter
2.6 (A&S moving college later), 2.21 (whole section), 4.6, 6.9, 9 Q13.
