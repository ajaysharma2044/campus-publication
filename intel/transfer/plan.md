# Content and data plan: Transferring to Cornell
This plan covers external transfer to Cornell for fall 2027 entry: eligibility, the application, each college's rules, credit, money, housing as it applies to transfers, orientation and the first semester, and the community college and Transfer Option routes. The pillar is "Transferring to Cornell: the complete guide for fall 2027". There are 48 rows: 1 pillar, 20 spokes, 6 reader guides, 5 tools, 3 datasets, 13 start-here paths and 0 rewrites. No /transfer/ article exists yet.
Transfer housing already has a page, /housing/cornell-transfer-housing/, and the housing topic owns it, so this plan links to it and adds no housing spoke. Moving between Cornell colleges is answered by /academics/how-hard-is-it-to-transfer-colleges-within-cornell/, so internal transfer gets a start-here path but no guide.
The demand file has no rows, so primary queries come from the competition lane's live searches (brief section 9, cited as Q9.n). Some rows rest on thin or snippet-only facts and must be checked before writing: the Engineering guide (whether CS 1110/1112 is required, brief 8.1 #11), the Hotel spoke (the resume conflict, 8.1 #7), the Transfer Option guide (CALS, Dyson and Nolan Transfer Option FAQ pages were never opened), the international guide (SEVIS transfer steps and English test minimums were not read) and the veterans spoke (GI Bill versus aid comes from a snippet).

## Open questions for research
- transfer, parent: Cornell publishes no admit rate by college or by major, and no graduation, retention or GPA outcomes for transfers. The CDS graduation rate leaves transfers out (brief 8.2, 8.3).
- transfer-option: conditions on the CALS, Dyson and Nolan Transfer Option FAQ pages were not opened (403, 404 or not tried). The fall 2027 Transfer Option deadline has not been posted. What happens to a holder who misses a condition is known only from 2020 forum posts (2.5, 7.5, 8.3).
- international: the F-1 and J-1 SEVIS transfer steps and timing (Global Learning pages exist but were not read); TOEFL, IELTS and Duolingo minimums for transfers; whether international students can waive the health plan (2.18, 8.3).
- transfer-engineering: whether CS 1110 or 1112 is a required component (8.1 #11). The Engineering registrar form that may cap credit taken after enrolling at 18 was not opened (8.3).
- transfer, low-budget: whether co-op residents must buy a meal plan, and how many co-op rooms go to new transfers (8.2, 10.3).
- transfer, parent: where non-exempt transfers are actually housed since 2022, and whether placement works as a guarantee in practice. No first-hand account after 2022 was found (7.2, 8.2). The housing topic's /housing/cornell-transfer-housing/ needs the same answer.
- transfer-junior: whether exempt transfers can get campus housing, and on what terms. There is no off-campus rent sample of 15 or more listings, and no plan for a junior admitted in June (3.8, 8.3, 9 Q29).
- transfer, parent: the enrollment deposit deadline for transfers. Only the CDS July 1 reply date was found (2.2, 8.2).
- transfer-spring: when external spring entry ended. Spring 2023 existed; snippets say spring 2026 was closed (2.19, 8.2).
- veteran-nontraditional: how the GI Bill and an aid package combine (from a snippet only), and whether military service counts toward the housing exemption (2.15, 6.5).
- accessibility: medical criteria for housing and meal plan exemptions, and whether accommodations from a prior school carry over (readers.md).
- student-family: whether having a family is grounds for exemption from the one-year rule; family rentals outside Collegetown (2.9, 3.8).
- transfer: the prompts for each college's Writing Supplement are not in the brief beyond ILR's word counts, which is why there is no essay-only spoke (2.3, 9 Q10).
- transfer, ny-resident: the Hotel resume requirement (8.1 #7); the CSS Profile wording for transfers on the aid page (8.1 #30); how much state aid HESC adds (readers.md).
- transfer: Brooks, AAP, Dyson and Bowers have too little transfer detail for spokes of their own. Dyson's external page was never found, AAP closed B.Arch. transfers for fall 2027, and Brooks gives only its prep courses (2.6, 8.3). The rules dataset covers them for now.
- internal-transfer: internal-transfer rules for Engineering, CALS, Human Ecology, ILR and Brooks are not in the brief (2.21).
- transfer: transfer student organizations and Greek recruitment for transfers were not found. Data on transferring out exists only in one 2019 column (7.8, 8.3).
