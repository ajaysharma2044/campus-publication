### 4.1 University transfer credit policy (Registrar)
Source: https://registrar.cornell.edu/students/credit-evaluation-services (undated policy text).

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Accreditation | US institution must be regionally accredited; credit only with syllabus and official transcript |
| Equivalency | About 80% similar in scope, content and learning outcomes; two external courses may equal one Cornell course; department that owns the Cornell course decides from the syllabus; cannot be denied because of community college setting or online delivery |
| Retake | May retake an equivalent course at Cornell but receives credit for only one |
| Database | Transfer Evaluation System (TES) with a public "TES Equivalency Database" being built; determinations reviewed every three years |
| Age | Courses seven or more years old are generally not eligible; department may decide otherwise |
| No equivalent | College or program decides how it counts |
| Minimum grade | C; a college or major may require up to B for major courses; pass/fail accepted with a written statement of C or better |
| Quarter credit | Times two thirds |
| Cap | Up to 60 credits including exam credit; Engineering up to 66 (123 to 137 credits to graduate); Architecture up to 64 (154 credits, five years) |
| Residence | At least 60 credits at Cornell (Architecture 90) |
| After matriculation | Need prior approval before taking outside courses |
| Not transferable | Orientation, study skills, career development; math below precalculus; specialized or proprietary credit; non-credit remedial; MOOCs (colleges may allow exceptions) |
| Exams | Duplicate credit: only the transfer course counts; external transfers must have scores sent and submit a written request to the college registrar by the last day of instruction of their first Cornell semester |
| CASE | Departmental Cornell Advanced Standing Exams may give credit or placement |
| International | Generally accepted from Ministry-of-Education-recognized institutions; remedial, ESL, international military courses not transferable; English courses reviewed by Literatures in English |
| Transcript | Transfer courses, credits and grades are not on the Cornell transcript; the institution name and total transfer credits are; transfer GPA not in Cornell GPA |
| Internships | No credit unless part of an approved program |
| Study abroad | Office of Global Learning approved fall or spring programs do not count toward the cap |
