# Privacy
What we collect, what we do not, and how long we keep it.

We measure which pages and tools are used, in aggregate, with short-lived identifiers and a coarse device class. We do not collect email addresses, precise location, raw search text that might contain personal details, or the text of correction requests in analytics. We do not fingerprint browsers to recover blocked analytics, and we do not infer sensitive attributes. Choosing your situation ("transfer student", "parent") is voluntary and is used only to order the page's suggestions.

Advertising, when enabled, loads only after you consent. Without consent every page works identically. Raw first-party events are kept for 90 days and aggregates for 13 months. There are no accounts and no sale of personal data.
