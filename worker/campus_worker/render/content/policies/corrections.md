# Corrections
How to report an error and what happens when you do.

Use the correction link at the bottom of any page. Tell us the page, the fact, and if you can, the official source that contradicts it. You may leave an email address if you want a reply; it is stored separately from analytics and deleted once the request is closed.

Reports are rate-limited and sanitised before anyone or anything reads them. A confirmed material error triggers a correction on the page, a re-check of every page that depends on the same fact, and a dated note in that page's history. We do not silently change a substantive fact behind an old "verified" date.
