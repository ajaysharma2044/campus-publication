How this site works

Every number carries a date

Facts show when we last verified them and the source we used. “Unknown” means no source states it, not that the answer is no.

Pages are school-specific

Coverage is labeled by institution. We do not generalize from one campus to another, and we say when a topic does not apply.

Tools use the same records

The calculator and comparisons are built from the verified fact tables, so a corrected rate updates the guide, the comparison and the total at once.
