# Content and data plan: Getting to Ithaca and getting around it
This plan covers getting to Ithaca and getting home: buses to NYC and beyond, the ITH, SYR and ELM airports, TCAT, cars and parking, bikes, shared cars and bikeshare, taxis, breaks and storage. Every reader on the map gets a path. The pillar is getting-to-ithaca-and-around-cornell.
Rows: 1 pillar, 22 spokes, 9 reader guides (one for each "high" reader), 7 tools, 4 datasets (from brief section 10), 15 start-here paths (one per reader key) and 1 rewrite (the rejected winter-biking page). Demand row 0012 ("Do I need a car?") is already answered by /money/should-i-bring-a-car-cornell-ithaca-cost/, which the money topic rewrites. Here it gets only the spec's quiz, and that quiz links to the money page.
No lane is incomplete, but three areas rest on thin research and need a recheck before writing:
- TCAT facts. tcatbus.com returned 403 to every lane, so pass prices and the $3 cap come from search summaries. This affects the TCAT spoke, the OmniRide calculator and the pillar's TCAT section.
- Student voice is old or missing for Boston, Philadelphia, DC and Toronto, for Uber and taxis (2017 data), and for winter biking (2007 and 2023).
- The newest sourced taxi fares are undated or from 2017.
