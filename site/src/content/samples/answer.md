This is sample content. A quick answer page gives the **whole answer in the first paragraph**, in 40 to 60 words, so a reader or an AI search engine can take it without scrolling: the number, the rule, the date, and the one condition that changes it, each [cited to its source](https://example.org/sample-source-1).

## Why the answer is what it is

Two to four short H2 sections give the context: where the rule comes from, who it applies to, and what students report in practice, [attributed to where it was read](https://example.org/sample-source-2).

## What changes the answer

A second section covers the exceptions. Keep each section to a few paragraphs; a quick answer runs 300 to 900 words in total.

## What to do next

The last section points to the action, and the page ends with related questions, the Sources list and a link to the parent pillar guide.
