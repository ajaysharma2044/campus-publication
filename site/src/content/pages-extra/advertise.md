EduExplained is funded by display advertising. This page says what that means for readers and what we offer advertisers.

## For readers

- Ads are labeled "Advertisement" and sit in fixed spaces so the page never jumps while you read.
- No ad appears above a guide's title or above its short answer, inside a table or step list, or in the way of the Sources.
- Advertisers never see a guide before it is published and never influence what a guide recommends. The [editorial standards](/editorial-standards/) and the [privacy policy](/privacy/) cover the rest.

## For advertisers

Display placements are sold through our advertising partner once one is configured; direct sponsorships of a topic hub or a data page are available for organizations that serve Cornell students and families in Ithaca. Sponsorship is labeled on the page and never changes a guide's content, order or recommendations.

- Audience: students, parents and alumni of Cornell University researching a specific decision (where to live, what a meal plan costs, how to get to Ithaca).
- Placements: sidebar, in-content and leaderboard units on guides and hubs.
