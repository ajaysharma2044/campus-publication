## Secondary and historical context (never policy)
- Cornell Sun, 2018-12-04: reported that Cornell guaranteed first-year housing but not transfer housing at that time.
- Cornell Sun, 2021-09-10: reported the two-year live-on requirement applying to first-year and transfer students from that semester.
- Cornell Sun 2012-09-04, 2013-08-29, 2016-10: reported transfers placed in converted lounges in some years.
- Student Assembly R47 (2019-20) and a 2020 Assembly presentation: administration said it hoped to fulfil all transfer housing applications once the North Campus expansion was complete but would not commit; the presentation cited 645 admitted transfers, 637 applying for housing, 436 assigned (one past year; not current).
- News.cornell.edu 2026-03: Board of Trustees approved 2026-27 budget parameters including housing and dining increases (no figures in snippet).
