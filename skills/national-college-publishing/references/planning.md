# Planning the brief (role E)
`briefs.build_entity_profile_brief` assembles from the database: reader decision, verified answer, unique contribution, critical and supporting claim ids, unresolved facts (unknowns listed by field), exceptions (not-applicable fields), structure, next tasks, overlapping pages, and a public-safe evidence packet (no excerpts). It is `ready` only when required evidence exists and no critical claim is blocked.
Audience guide test: `briefs.audience_guide_justified` — a separate guide only when an eligibility/deadline/process/rate claim for that audience differs from the general one. Otherwise improve the existing page and record the rejection.
A keyword list is not a brief. The planner model may refine wording and propose headline candidates; it cannot add claims.
